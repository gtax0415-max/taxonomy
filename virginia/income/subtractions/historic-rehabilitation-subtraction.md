# Historic Rehabilitation (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 53)
## Description
To the extent included in federal adjusted gross income, any gain or income recognized by a taxpayer in connection with the Virginia Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit is allowed as a subtraction on the Virginia return, using code 53.
## What to enter
- Gain or income recognized in connection with the Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit and included in federal AGI
- Enter code 53 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Questions
- Are you involved in a historic rehabilitation project that generated Virginia credits?
- Did you recognize gain or income on your federal return in connection with those credits?
## Common Errors
- Leaving credit-related gain in Virginia income
- Subtracting amounts not included in federal AGI
## Prompt
- I recognized income on my K-1 from a historic rehabilitation credit deal.
