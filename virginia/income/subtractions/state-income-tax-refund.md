# State Income Tax Refund or Overpayment Credit (Form 760, Line 6)
## Description
A state income tax refund, or an overpayment credited to the next year, is sometimes reported as income on the federal return. Virginia removes whatever was reported as income federally on Form 760, Line 6.
## What to enter
- The amount of any state income tax refund or overpayment credit that you reported as income on your federal return
- If none was reported federally, enter nothing; there is nothing to remove
## Questions
- Did you receive a Form 1099-G for a state income tax refund?
- Did you report any part of it as income on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you apply a prior-year overpayment to your estimated tax instead of taking a refund?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the whole refund when only part (or none) was taxable federally
- Missing an overpayment credit that was reported federally even though no check was received
## Prompt
- I got a Virginia refund last year and it showed up on my federal return.
