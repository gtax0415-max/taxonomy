# Federal and State Employees (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 39)
## Description
A low-paid federal or state employee may subtract salary from that job. Any individual who qualifies as a federal or state employee earning $15,000 or less in annual salary from all employment can subtract up to $15,000 of the salary from the state or federal job, using code 39.
## Amount
- Eligibility: annual salary from ALL employment of $15,000 or less
- Subtraction: up to $15,000 of the salary from the federal or state job, and never more than the actual salary received
- If both spouses on a joint return qualify, each may claim the subtraction
## Mutually exclusive items
If you claim this subtraction, you cannot claim the Credit for Low-Income Individuals or the Refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit. Compare the benefit of each before choosing.
## Questions
- Do you work for the federal government or the Commonwealth of Virginia?
- What was your total salary from all jobs in 2026?
- Are you considering the Virginia low-income credit or earned income credit instead?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it when total salary from all employment exceeds $15,000
- Subtracting more than the salary actually received from the government job
- Claiming it together with the low-income credit or refundable Virginia EIC
## Prompt
- I work part-time for the state and earned about $12,000 total.
