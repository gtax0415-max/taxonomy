# Interest on Federally Exempt U.S. Obligations (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 10)
## Description
Some interest or dividends are exempt from federal income tax but are not exempt from Virginia tax. Because they are not in federal adjusted gross income, Virginia requires them to be added back using addition code 10. This is the reverse of Schedule ADJ, Line 4, which subtracts U.S. obligation income that is in federal AGI but exempt from Virginia tax.
## What to enter
- The amount of interest or dividends exempt from federal income tax, but taxable in Virginia
- LESS related expenses
- Enter code 10 in the code box on Line 2b or 2c, followed by the amount
## How it differs from nearby lines
| Item | Federal | Virginia | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Interest on other states' obligations | Exempt | Taxable | Schedule ADJ, Line 1 |
| Federally exempt interest or dividends taxable in Virginia | Exempt | Taxable | Code 10 |
| U.S. obligation income exempt from state tax | Taxable | Exempt | Schedule ADJ, Line 4 |
## Questions
- Did you receive interest or dividends that were exempt on your federal return?
- Where did they come from, and were they from another state's bonds (Line 1) or some other federally exempt source?
- Did you have expenses related to this income?
## Common Errors
- Reporting other-state municipal bond interest here instead of on Line 1
- Confusing this addition with the Line 4 subtraction for U.S. Treasury interest
- Not reducing the amount by related expenses
## Prompt
- I have interest that was tax-free federally but my preparer says Virginia taxes it.
