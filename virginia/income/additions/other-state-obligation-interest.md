# Interest on Obligations of Other States (Schedule ADJ, Line 1)
## Description
Interest on bonds issued by other states and their localities is usually exempt from federal income tax, so it is not in federal adjusted gross income. Virginia taxes it. A Virginia resident adds it back on Schedule ADJ, Line 1. Interest on Virginia's own state and municipal obligations is not added; if any Virginia obligation income is in federal AGI it is subtracted instead (subtraction code 20).
## What to enter
- The amount of any interest on obligations of other states not included in federal AGI that is taxable in Virginia
- LESS related expenses
- The form line reads: interest on obligations of other states, exempt from federal income tax but not from state tax
## How to compute
1. Start from federal tax-exempt interest and exempt-interest dividends.
2. Remove the portion from Virginia obligations (Virginia does not tax it).
3. Remove the portion from U.S. obligations, which is handled elsewhere (see code 10 if federally exempt but Virginia taxable).
4. Subtract related expenses.
5. Enter the result on Line 1. It is included in Schedule ADJ, Line 3 and Form 760, Line 2.
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds, or mutual funds that pay exempt-interest dividends?
- Does the fund or broker report what share of the exempt income came from Virginia issuers?
- Did you have expenses related to earning this interest?
## Common Errors
- Adding back the Virginia portion of a national municipal bond fund, which Virginia does not tax
- Forgetting the addition entirely because the interest is not on the federal return's taxable lines
- Not reducing the addition by related expenses
- Putting this interest on Lines 2b-2c with a code instead of on Line 1
## Prompt
- I have tax-free muni bond interest from a New York bond. Does Virginia tax it?
- My municipal bond fund paid exempt-interest dividends.
