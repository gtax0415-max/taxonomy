# Additions to Federal Adjusted Gross Income (Schedule ADJ, Lines 1-3)
## Description
Virginia starts from federal adjusted gross income (Form 760, Line 1) and adds back income that federal AGI leaves out but Virginia taxes. Every addition is reported on Schedule ADJ, Lines 1 through 2c, totaled on Schedule ADJ, Line 3, and carried to Form 760, Line 2. Form 760, Line 3 is Line 1 plus Line 2. This folder covers each addition line and each addition code for tax year 2026.
## How the lines flow
- Schedule ADJ, Line 1 = interest on obligations of other states, less related expenses
- Schedule ADJ, Line 2a = conformity additions (worksheet Part A bonus depreciation + Part B other conformity additions = Part C)
- Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c = other additions, each entered as a 2-digit code followed by the amount
- Schedule ADJ, Line 3 = Lines 1 through 2c → Form 760, Line 2
- Form 760, Line 3 = Form 760, Line 1 + Line 2
## More than two addition codes: Schedule ADJS
Schedule ADJ has room for only two coded additions (Lines 2b and 2c). If you claim more than two, use the supplemental Schedule ADJS to give the code and amount for each addition in excess of two (ADJS Lines 1-10, totaled on ADJS Line 11). Include the ADJS Line 11 amount in the total of all additions on Schedule ADJ, Line 3, fill in the oval at the bottom of page 1 of Schedule ADJ, and enclose Schedule ADJS with the return. Use additional Schedules ADJS if necessary. Schedule ADJS is also used with Schedule 763 ADJ.

## Note on code numbers
Addition code 20 (Pass-Through Entity Tax Add Back) and subtraction code 20 (Income from Virginia Obligations) share a number but are different items on different lines. Check the line (2b-2c for additions, 6b-6d for subtractions) as well as the code.
## 2026 context
For 2026 Virginia has a fixed IRC conformity date of December 31, 2025, and conforms to most of the 2025 federal Budget Reconciliation Act (P.L. 119-21), with listed exceptions that drive the Line 2a conformity additions, including a new section 179 addition for 2026. See conformity-additions.md.
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds or bond funds issued by states other than Virginia?
- Do you have depreciable business or rental assets, or did you elect a section 179 deduction?
- Did you receive a lump-sum retirement distribution taxed on federal Form 4972, or a trust accumulation distribution (Form 4970)?
- Are you an owner of a partnership or S corporation that elected to pay the Virginia pass-through entity tax, or that had a federal partnership audit?
- Do you claim the Virginia Food Donation credit, or hold a first-time home buyer savings account?
