# Taxable Income and the 3% Tax
## Description
Louisiana taxes all taxable income at one flat rate.

## Amount
- Taxable income (Line 10) = Line 7 minus Lines 8 and 9D, not below zero
- Tax (Line 11) = Line 10 × 3%, rounded to the whole dollar; no tax table or brackets

Example: Louisiana AGI $65,676, joint standard deduction $25,676 → taxable income $40,000 → tax $1,200.

## Status for 2026
3% applies for 2026. No rate change was enacted in the 2025 or 2026 sessions; proposals to cut the rate from 2027 did not become law.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:32, as amended by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (flat 3% from 2025)
- Line numbers are from the 2025 form

## Required Information
- Louisiana AGI, standard deduction, Line 9D

## Questions
- What is your Louisiana taxable income?

## Common Errors
- Using the pre-2025 brackets (1.85%, 3.5%, 4.25%)
- Letting taxable income go negative

## Prompt
- What is the Louisiana income tax rate?
