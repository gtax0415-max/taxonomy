# Louisiana Standard Deduction
## Description
Every filer takes the standard deduction for their filing status. Since 2025 it replaces the old personal and dependency exemptions.

## Amount
- 2026: $12,838 (single, married filing separately); $25,676 (joint, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse)
- 2025: $12,500 and $25,000
- Indexed each year by CPI-U starting in 2026

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:294(B), enacted by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session
- $12,838 / $25,676 equals the 2025 amounts increased by 2.7%, the same adjustment that produced the confirmed 2026 retirement exemption ($12,324). LDR has published only preliminary amounts ($12,875 / $25,750, used in the 2026 withholding tables) and said the final figures could differ slightly. The "RIB 26-019" previously cited could not be located; confirm against the 2026 IT-540
- Louisiana's head of household amount equals the joint amount, unlike federal law

## Required Information
- Filing status

- Using the preliminary withholding-table amount on the return
