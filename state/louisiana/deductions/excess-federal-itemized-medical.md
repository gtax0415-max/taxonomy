# Excess Federal Itemized Deductions (Medical Only)
## Description
Louisiana allows one itemized deduction: the amount by which the federal medical and dental deduction exceeds the federal standard deduction. No other itemized deduction carries over.

## Amount
- 9A: total federal itemized deductions (Schedule A Line 17)
- 9B: federal medical and dental deduction (Schedule A Line 4)
- 9C: federal standard deduction for the filing status, basic amount only (no additional age or blind amount)
- 9D: 9B minus 9C, not less than zero

2026 federal basic standard deduction (Rev. Proc. 2025-32): $16,100 single or separate, $32,200 joint or surviving spouse, $24,150 head of household (2025: $15,750 / $31,500 / $23,625).

Example: a joint filer with a $37,008 federal medical deduction → 9D = $37,008 − $32,200 = $4,808.

## Eligibility
- Itemized on the 2026 federal return
- Medical deduction larger than the federal standard deduction

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(3) (medical-only rule since 2022)
- Higher 2026 federal standard deductions make this rarer

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A Lines 4 and 17
- Filing status

## Questions
- Did you itemize on your federal return?
- How large was your federal medical deduction?

## Common Errors
- Entering total itemized deductions minus the standard deduction on 9D
- Including the additional age or blind amount in 9C
- Using 2025 federal standard deduction amounts
