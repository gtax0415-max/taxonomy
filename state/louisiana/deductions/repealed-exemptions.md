# Personal, Dependent, Age, and Blind Exemptions (Repealed)
## Description
Louisiana no longer has personal or dependency exemptions, or the extra $1,000 deductions for being 65 or older, blind, or for each dependent. Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session replaced them with the standard deduction beginning in 2025.

## Status for 2026
- Still repealed; no exemption amounts for 2026
- Dependents are still listed on the return, and the age box still matters for the 65+ retirement exemption (code 06E)
- A 2025 bill to add an extra standard deduction for those 65+ (SB 240) did not pass

Example: a blind filer gets no separate deduction.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:294, as amended by Act 11 (effective December 4, 2024, for 2025 onward)

## Required Information
- Filing status (the standard deduction replaces all exemptions)
- Dependents and ages, for credits and the retirement exemption

## Questions
- Are you 65 or older or blind? (Relevant only for the retirement exemption and federal figures)

## Common Errors
- Looking for a dependent exemption
- Adding $1,000 for age or blindness
