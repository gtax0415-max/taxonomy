# Interest and Dividends from Other States' Obligations
## Description
Interest and dividends on bonds of states other than Louisiana (and their local governments) are federally tax-exempt but taxable to Louisiana residents, so they are added back.

## Rules
- Add if all apply: you file as a Louisiana resident; the obligation is from another state or its subdivisions; it was acquired on or after January 1, 1980
- Interest on Louisiana and Louisiana local-government obligations stays exempt
- For municipal bond funds, add only the share of exempt-interest dividends from non-Louisiana obligations (use the fund's state breakdown)

Example: $7,800 of other-state municipal bond interest → add $7,800.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(c) (additions) and (9)(b) (Louisiana obligations exempt). Unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- Tax-exempt interest and exempt-interest dividends by state
- Acquisition dates for older bonds

## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds or municipal bond funds?
- Which states issued them?

## Common Errors
- Adding back Louisiana bond interest
- Adding the whole fund dividend instead of the non-Louisiana share

## Prompt
- I have tax-free muni bond interest. Is it taxed in Louisiana?
