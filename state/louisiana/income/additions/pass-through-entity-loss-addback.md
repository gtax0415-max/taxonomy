# Add Back of Pass-Through Entity Loss
## Description
When a partnership, LLC, or S corporation elects to pay Louisiana tax at the entity level, its owners exclude the entity's income from their own returns. If the entity has a loss instead, the owner adds that loss back so it is not deducted twice.

## Rules
- Add back the owner's share of the Louisiana net operating loss reported at the entity level
- The matching exclusion for income from an electing entity is a Schedule E exempt-income code

Example: a partner's $4,000 share of an electing partnership's loss → add $4,000.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.14, as amended by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session
- From 2026, Act 382 of the 2025 Regular Session treats S corporations as pass-through entities for Louisiana purposes
- LDR requires its pass-through exclusion form attached; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Entity name and election status
- Owner's share of the Louisiana loss

## Questions
- Do you own part of a business that elected to pay Louisiana tax at the entity level?
- Did that entity have a loss in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Deducting the electing entity's loss on the individual return
- Excluding income from an entity that did not elect

- My partnership pays Louisiana tax itself and had a loss. What do I do?
