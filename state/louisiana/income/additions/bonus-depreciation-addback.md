# Add Back of Federal Depreciation Previously Accelerated via State Bonus
## Description
Since 2025 Louisiana lets individuals elect 100% bonus depreciation (and immediate deduction of research costs) on qualified property, claimed as code 32E. Because the full cost was already deducted for Louisiana, the federal depreciation on that same property in later years is added back.

## Rules
- Add back each later year's federal depreciation or amortization on property fully expensed under the Louisiana election
- On sale, recapture follows federal rules (as of January 1, 2024), and any federal loss carryforward from the sale is added back

Example: equipment fully expensed for Louisiana in 2025, federal depreciation of $3,000 in 2026 → add $3,000.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.25, enacted by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- Federal 100% bonus depreciation returned under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act for property acquired after January 19, 2025; where federal and Louisiana both expense the asset fully, there is little or nothing to add back
- LDR requires its bonus depreciation form attached; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Assets expensed for Louisiana and the year
- 2026 federal depreciation on each

## Questions
- Did you elect Louisiana bonus depreciation in 2025?
- Are you still depreciating those assets federally?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the add-back
- Adding back depreciation on assets not expensed for Louisiana

## Prompt
- I took Louisiana bonus depreciation last year. What do I do this year?
