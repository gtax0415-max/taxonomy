# Annual Retirement Income Exemption for Taxpayers 65 or Older (Code 06E)
## Description
Each person age 65 or older may exempt a set amount of taxable pension, annuity, and IRA income not already exempted under another code.

## Amount
- 2026: $12,324 per person age 65 or older (2025: $12,000)
- Indexed each year by CPI-U starting in 2026
- Figured per spouse on a joint return: each spouse 65+ exempts up to the limit of their own retirement income
- Income counted: federal Form 1040 Lines 4b and 5b, less amounts exempted under codes 02E–05E; Social Security is handled separately (code 07E, fully exempt)

Example: a 71-year-old with a $28,000 private pension exempts $12,324.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:44.1(A), as amended by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session
- The $12,324 figure comes from LDR's proposed rule (LAC 61:I.1311, Louisiana Register, June 2026); final adoption not confirmed. LDR posts the amount each January
- Enter the payer's name on the form

## Required Information
- Each spouse's age and taxable retirement income
- Amounts already exempted under other codes

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older in 2026?
- Did you receive pension, annuity, or IRA income?

## Common Errors
- Using $6,000 (pre-2025) or $12,000 (2025)
- Combining both spouses' limits against one spouse's income
- Double-counting income already exempt as government retirement
