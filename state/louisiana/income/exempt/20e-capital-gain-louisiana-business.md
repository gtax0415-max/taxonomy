# Capital Gain from Sale of a Louisiana Business (Code 20E)
## Description
Former exclusion of net capital gain from selling a Louisiana nonpublic business held at least five years. Repealed for 2025 onward; in 2026 it survives only for installment payments on sales made before 2025.

## Status for 2026 — transition only
- Repealed by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session for taxable periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- LDR still allows it for: installment payments recognized after 2024 on sales executed before January 1, 2025; and sales whose purchase agreement was signed and all conditions met before January 1, 2025, even if closing came later
- No exclusion for sales agreed on or after January 1, 2025

Example: a 2026 installment payment on a 2023 sale of a family business qualifies.

## Notes
- Authority: former R.S. 47:293(9)(a)(xvii). The transition rule comes from LDR's FAQs and instructions, not the Act text
- LDR requires its net capital gain worksheet attached; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Sale date and agreement date
- 2026 gain recognized on the installment
- Holding period and Louisiana nexus of the business

## Questions
- Did you sell a Louisiana business before 2025 on an installment basis?
- When was the purchase agreement signed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a 2025 or 2026 sale
- Claiming gain on publicly traded stock

## Prompt
- I'm receiving installment payments from selling my business in 2023.
