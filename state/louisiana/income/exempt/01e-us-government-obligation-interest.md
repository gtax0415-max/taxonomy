# Interest and Dividends on U.S. Government Obligations (Code 01E)
## Description
Interest on U.S. Treasury bonds, notes, bills, and savings bonds is taxable federally but exempt from state tax, so Louisiana subtracts it.

## Rules
- Subtract interest from U.S. government obligations included in federal AGI
- Mutual fund dividends qualify only for the portion the fund identifies as coming from U.S. obligations
- No dollar limit
- Not exempt: interest on Ginnie Mae and Fannie Mae securities and similar agency-guaranteed pass-through obligations, and interest on federal tax refunds (general state rule; confirm specific holdings)

Example: Treasury bond interest of $1,200 included in AGI → $1,200 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: federal law (31 U.S.C. §3124); unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- 1099-INT box 3 amounts
- Fund percentages for mutual funds

## Questions
- Do you own Treasury bonds, bills, or savings bonds, or funds that hold them?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the whole fund dividend
- Subtracting interest on agency mortgage-backed securities

## Prompt
- Is Treasury interest taxed in Louisiana?
