# Louisiana Fortify Homes Program Grants (Code 31E)
## Description
Grants from the Louisiana Fortify Homes Program, which helps homeowners install FORTIFIED roofs, are exempt from Louisiana tax to the extent included in federal AGI.

## Rules
- Full grant amount excluded; no cap
- Applies to grants received on or after January 1, 2025 (Act 32 of the 2025 Regular Session)

Example: a $10,000 roof grant included in federal AGI → $10,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a) and R.S. 22:1483.1. Unchanged for 2026
- A roof paid by this grant does NOT qualify for the fortified roof credit (code 466), and grant-funded costs don't count toward the retrofit deduction (16E)

## Required Information
- Grant amount and date
- Whether it was included in federal AGI

## Questions
- Did you receive a Fortify Homes grant?
- Was it reported as income federally?

## Common Errors
- Excluding a grant not included in federal AGI
- Also claiming the fortified roof credit for the same roof

## Prompt
- I got a state grant for a new roof. Is it taxable in Louisiana?
