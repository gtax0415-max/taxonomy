# Voluntary Retrofit of a Residential Structure (Code 16E)
## Description
Deduction for voluntarily upgrading your home to the state uniform construction code or to the IBHS FORTIFIED Home standards.

## Amount (changed for 2026)
- 50% of qualifying costs, up to $10,000 per structure (was $5,000)
- FORTIFIED Home improvements now qualify (Act 473 of the 2025 Regular Session, for tax periods beginning January 1, 2026)
- Costs paid with state, local, or federal incentives (such as a Fortify Homes grant) do not count
- Rental property does not qualify

Example: a $14,000 FORTIFIED retrofit with no grant → $7,000 deduction.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(2), as amended by Act 473 of 2025
- Whether the homestead requirement in the 2025 instructions continues for 2026 was not confirmed
- The same roof can also qualify for the fortified roof CREDIT (code 466); confirm interaction before using the same costs for both

## Required Information
- Retrofit costs
- Proof of standard met
- Incentives received

## Questions
- Did you retrofit your home to FORTIFIED or building-code standards in 2026?
- Did a grant pay for any of it?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $5,000 limit
- Including grant-funded costs

## Prompt
- I fortified my house against hurricanes. Is there a Louisiana deduction?
