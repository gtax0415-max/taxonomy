# Elementary and Secondary School Tuition (Code 17E)
## Description
Deduction for tuition and fees paid to a qualifying nonpublic school or public university laboratory school for kindergarten through grade 12.

## Amount
- 100% of tuition and fees (including required uniforms, textbooks, and supplies), up to $6,000 per child
- Cannot exceed taxable income

Example: tuition of $8,000 → $6,000 deduction.

## Eligibility
- Nonpublic school meeting the statute's criteria, or a public university lab school
- Child claimed as a dependent; Act 349 of the 2025 Regular Session tightened the dependency test from 2025 (sources differ on federal vs. Louisiana return; confirm on the 2026 instructions)

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.10. Unchanged amount for 2026
- Tuition paid from a START K12 account (28E) doesn't qualify
- LDR's school expense worksheet is required; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Tuition and fees paid by child
- School name

## Questions
- Does your child attend a private or lab school?
- How much tuition did you pay in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding $6,000 per child
- Including tuition paid from START K12
