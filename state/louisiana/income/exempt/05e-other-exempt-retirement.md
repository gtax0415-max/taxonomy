# Other Retirement Benefits Exempt by Law (Code 05E)
## Description
Benefits from Louisiana public retirement systems other than LASERS (02E) and TRSL (03E) that state law fully exempts.

## Rules
Fully exempt, including DROP disbursements; enter the system name or statute and the month and year retired. Systems include:
- Louisiana School Employees' Retirement System (R.S. 11:1003)
- State Police (R.S. 11:1331)
- LSU system plan (R.S. 11:952.3)
- Judges' plans (R.S. 11:570, 11:1378)
- Assessors (R.S. 11:1403), Clerks of Court (R.S. 11:1526), District Attorneys (R.S. 11:1583), Registrars of Voters (R.S. 11:2033), Sheriffs (R.S. 11:2182)
- Municipal Employees, including Baton Rouge and Shreveport plans (R.S. 11:1735)
- Parochial Employees (R.S. 11:1905)
- Municipal Police (R.S. 11:2228) and Firefighters (R.S. 11:2263)
- Local police and fire funds of Alexandria, Bossier City, Lafayette, Monroe, and New Orleans
- New Orleans city and Sewerage & Water Board plans (R.S. 11:3823)

Example: a retired sheriff's deputy's $24,000 pension → $24,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026
- Subtracted before the 65+ retirement exemption (06E)
- Private pensions, IRAs, and out-of-state government pensions are not 05E (06E may apply)

## Required Information
- 1099-R, system name, retirement date

## Questions
- Which Louisiana public retirement system pays your pension?

## Common Errors
- Coding an out-of-state government pension as 05E
- Coding LASERS or TRSL here instead of 02E or 03E
