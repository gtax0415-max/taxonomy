# Road Home and Restore Louisiana Benefits (Code 11E)
## Description
Disaster recovery grants, loans, and benefits from Louisiana's recovery programs are exempt from Louisiana tax to the extent included in federal AGI.

## Rules
- Exempt: gratuitous grants, loans, or benefits from the Road Home Corporation, the Louisiana Recovery Authority, the Office of Community Development Disaster Recovery Unit, or the Louisiana Family Recovery Corps
- Includes Restore Louisiana payments
- Only amounts included in federal AGI are subtracted

Example: a taxable Restore Louisiana payment of $8,000 → $8,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a)(i). Unchanged for 2026
- Fortify Homes Program grants have their own code (31E)

## Required Information
- Program, amount, and whether included in federal AGI

## Questions
- Did you receive a Road Home or Restore Louisiana payment in 2026?
- Was it included in your federal income?

## Common Errors
- Excluding amounts not included in federal AGI
- Using 11E for FEMA or insurance payments
