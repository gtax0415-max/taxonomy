# Digital Nomad (Code 29E)
## Description
Former exclusion of half the wages of a certified remote worker who moved to Louisiana.

## Status for 2026 — EXPIRED
- No exemption for wages earned after December 31, 2025 (R.S. 47:297.18)
- A 2025 bill (SB 159) to extend it to 2026–2027 did not become law
- Do not use for 2026

## How it worked (2022–2025)
50% of wages, up to $150,000, for up to two years, for a certified remote worker who became a resident after 2021 and worked full time for an out-of-state employer.

## Required Information
- Year the wages were earned (only 2022–2025 wages ever qualified)

## Questions
- Were you certified as a digital nomad? (2026 wages no longer qualify)

## Common Errors
- Claiming the exemption on 2026 wages

## Prompt
- I moved to Louisiana as a remote worker. Can I still exclude half my wages?
