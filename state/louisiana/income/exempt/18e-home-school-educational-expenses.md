# Educational Expenses for Home-Schooled Children (Code 18E)
## Description
Deduction for part of the cost of educating a child in a BESE-approved home school.

## Amount
- 50% of qualified expenses (textbooks, curricula), up to $6,000 per child
- Cannot exceed taxable income

Example: home school costs of $1,600 → $800 deduction.

## 2025–2026 change
Act 349 of the 2025 Regular Session tightened the dependency test from 2025: the child must be claimed as your dependent for the current or prior year. Sources differ on whether this means the federal or the Louisiana return; confirm on the 2026 instructions.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.11 (separate section, not repealed in 2024)
- Expenses paid with START K12 funds don't qualify

## Required Information
- Expenses by child
- Home school approval

## Questions
- Do you home-school your child in an approved program?
- What did you spend on books and curricula?

## Common Errors
- Deducting 100% of costs
- Including START K12-funded expenses

## Prompt
- I home-school my kids. Is there a Louisiana deduction?
