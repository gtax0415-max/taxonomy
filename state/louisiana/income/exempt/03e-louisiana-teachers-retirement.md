# Louisiana Teachers' Retirement Benefits (Code 03E)
## Description
Benefits from the Teachers' Retirement System of Louisiana are fully exempt.

## Rules
- Entire taxable TRSL benefit exempt, including DROP account disbursements; no cap
- Also covers the Orleans Parish School Employees system
- Enter the month and year retired

Example: a retired teacher's $28,000 TRSL pension → $28,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 11:704. Unchanged for 2026
- Subtracted before the 65+ retirement exemption (06E)

## Required Information
- TRSL 1099-R taxable amount
- Retirement month and year

## Questions
- Do you receive a TRSL pension?

## Common Errors
- Coding another system as 03E (school employees' system is 05E)
- Also counting TRSL income toward 06E

- Is my teacher pension taxed in Louisiana?
