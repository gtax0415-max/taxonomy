# ABLE Account Deposits (New for 2026)
## Description
Deposits into a Louisiana ABLE account (a savings account for a person with a disability) are exempt up to an annual limit per beneficiary.

## Amount
- Up to $2,400 per beneficiary ($4,800 per beneficiary on a joint return), plus accrued interest
- Unused room under the cap carries forward; deposits above the cap do not
- Deposits plus interest withdrawn for anything other than qualified disability expenses are included in taxable income

Example: a single parent deposits $3,000 for a disabled child → $2,400 exempt; the extra $600 does not carry forward.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a) and R.S. 17:3088, as amended by Act 110 of the 2025 Regular Session, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026
- The ABLE program reports deposits to LDR each January
- No Schedule E code or LDR guidance yet; use the code on the 2026 form

## Required Information
- Deposits by beneficiary
- Filing status

- Carrying forward deposits above the cap
