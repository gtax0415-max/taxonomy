# Educational Expenses for a Quality Public Education (Code 19E)
## Description
Deduction for part of what parents pay for items a public K–12 school requires.

## Amount
- 50% of qualifying expenses, up to $6,000 per child
- Shared among everyone claiming the same child
- Cannot exceed taxable income

Example: required uniforms and supplies of $700 → $350 deduction.

## Eligibility
- Child attends a Louisiana public elementary or secondary school
- Items are required by the school: uniforms, textbooks or instructional materials, supplies
- Child claimed as a dependent; Act 349 of the 2025 Regular Session tightened the dependency test from 2025 (sources differ on whether it refers to the federal or Louisiana return; confirm on the 2026 instructions)

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.12. Unchanged amount for 2026
- Expenses paid from a START K12 account (28E) don't qualify

## Required Information
- Expenses by child
- School's required-items list

## Questions
- Does your child attend a public school that requires uniforms or supplies?
- How much did you spend?

## Common Errors
- Deducting 100% of costs
- Including optional items
- Including START K12-funded purchases
