# Recreation Volunteer (Code 13E)
## Description
Flat exemption for registered volunteers at a public recreation department.

## Amount
$500 per qualifying volunteer.

## Eligibility
- Registered as a volunteer with a state or local recreation department
- 30 or more uncompensated hours during 2026

Example: a youth sports coach volunteering 40 hours with the parish recreation department → $500.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a)(xii). Unchanged for 2026
- Each spouse who qualifies may claim it

## Required Information
- Department certification and hours

## Questions
- Did you volunteer 30+ unpaid hours with a public recreation department?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for volunteering at private leagues or charities
- Claiming without the department's certification

## Prompt
- I coach youth sports for the parish. Is there a Louisiana deduction?
