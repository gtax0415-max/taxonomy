# START K12 Savings Program Contributions (Code 28E)
## Description
Deposits into a START K12 account, Louisiana's savings program for elementary and secondary education costs, are exempt up to an annual limit per beneficiary.

## Amount
- $1,200 per beneficiary ($2,400 per beneficiary on a joint return)
- Unused room under the cap carries forward per account; deposits above the cap do not
- Amounts withdrawn in the same year don't qualify
- Expenses paid from the account can't also be claimed under 17E, 18E, or 19E

Example: joint filers deposit $2,000 for one child → $2,000 exempt.

## Status for 2026
Unchanged. A 2025 bill (SB 13) to double the limits to $2,400 / $4,800 died in committee.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a) and R.S. 17:3100.5; LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 22-016
- Non-qualified withdrawals are added back on Schedule E Line 2C

## Required Information
- Deposits and withdrawals by beneficiary
- Carryforward room

## Questions
- Did you deposit into a START K12 account in 2026?
- Did you withdraw any of it the same year?

## Common Errors
- Using the college START limit ($2,400 / $4,800)
- Also deducting tuition paid from the account under 17E
