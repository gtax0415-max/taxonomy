# Louisiana Bonus Depreciation and Amortization (Code 32E)
## Description
Elective Louisiana deduction of 100% of the cost of qualified property and research and experimental costs in the year placed in service or incurred, reduced by what was already deducted federally.

## Amount
- Deduction = 100% of the cost of qualified property (IRC 168(k)) or qualified improvement property, or of research and experimental costs (IRC 174), MINUS the depreciation or amortization claimed federally for the same year
- Covers property placed in service, or costs incurred, on or after January 1, 2025
- Elected by claiming it on a timely original or amended Louisiana return
- In later years, federal depreciation on the same assets is added back (Schedule E Line 2E); recapture applies on sale

Example: equipment costing $20,000 with $4,000 of federal depreciation in 2026 → $16,000 deduction.

## 2026 interaction with federal law
Louisiana uses the Internal Revenue Code as of January 1, 2024, and was not updated for the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Federal law now allows 100% bonus depreciation (property acquired after January 19, 2025) and immediate expensing of domestic research costs, so for many 2026 assets federal and Louisiana deductions match and 32E is zero.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.25 (Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session). Unchanged for 2026
- LDR requires its bonus depreciation form attached; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Cost and placed-in-service date of each asset
- Federal depreciation claimed

## Questions
- Did your business buy equipment or incur research costs in 2026?
- Did you already take 100% bonus depreciation federally?

## Common Errors
- Deducting the full cost on top of federal depreciation
- Forgetting the add-back in later years
