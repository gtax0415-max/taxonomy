# Military Pay Exclusion (Code 10E)
## Description
Louisiana residents on extended active duty outside Louisiana may exclude part of their military pay.

## Amount
- Up to $50,000 of active-duty pay for service outside Louisiana
- Requires full-time active duty for 120 or more consecutive days

Example: a reservist deployed out of state for six months earns $40,000 for that service → up to $40,000 excluded.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(e); LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 22-015. Unchanged for 2026
- Military retired pay is fully exempt under code 04E, not here
- Keep copies of orders; e-filers give a copy to the preparer
- Part-year residents use the nonresident worksheet

## Required Information
- Orders and dates of active duty
- Pay earned outside Louisiana

## Questions
- Were you on active duty outside Louisiana for 120+ consecutive days?
- How much pay was for that service?

## Common Errors
- Excluding pay for service inside Louisiana
- Using the old $30,000 limit
- Coding military retirement as 10E

## Prompt
- I'm in the military and was deployed out of state. Is my pay taxed in Louisiana?
