# Federal Retirement Benefits (Code 04E)
## Description
Retirement benefits from the U.S. government, civilian and military, are fully exempt in Louisiana.

## Rules
- Fully exempt, no cap: federal civil service annuities, military retired pay, and military Survivor Benefit Plan payments (LDR also lists Railroad Retirement)
- Enter the month and year retired

Example: a federal retiree's $28,000 annuity → $28,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:44.2. Unchanged for 2026
- Active-duty pay is a different code (10E)
- Thrift Savings Plan distributions are generally treated as other retirement income (06E if 65+), not 04E; confirm against LDR guidance

## Required Information
- 1099-R taxable amount and payer
- Retirement month and year

## Questions
- Do you receive a federal civilian or military pension?

## Common Errors
- Coding TSP withdrawals as 04E
- Also counting the pension toward 06E

## Prompt
- Is my military retirement taxed in Louisiana?
