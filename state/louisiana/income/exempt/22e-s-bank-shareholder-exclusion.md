# S Bank Shareholder Income Exclusion (Code 22E)
## Description
Shareholders of a bank taxed as an S corporation exclude the part of their K-1 income that was already used to compute Louisiana's bank shares tax.

## Rules
- Exclude the share of income tied to net earnings used in the bank shares tax
- Adjust if the K-1 is amended or changed by a federal audit
- Per LDR's instructions, allowed only if the bank did NOT make the entity-level income tax election

Example: a shareholder excludes $15,000 of K-1 income taxed through the bank shares tax.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.3. Unchanged for 2026
- How the 2026 S corporation changes (Act 382 of 2025) affect S banks was not addressed by LDR

## Required Information
- K-1 income and the shares-tax computation

## Questions
- Do you own shares in an S corporation bank?
- Did the bank elect entity-level tax?

## Common Errors
- Excluding all K-1 income instead of the shares-tax portion
- Claiming when the bank made the entity-level election (use 24E)

## Prompt
- I own stock in a Subchapter S bank.
