# Employment of Certain Qualified Disabled Individuals (Code 21E)
## Description
Deduction for an employer (including a sole proprietor or pass-through owner) who provides continuous employment to a qualified individual with a disability.

## Amount
- 50% of the employee's gross wages for the first four continuous months
- 30% of gross wages for each later continuous month
- No dollar cap; the program is limited to 100 employees statewide

## Eligibility
- Employee works at least four continuous months, averaging 20+ hours a week, at comparable pay and setting
- Employee has a severe, chronic disability that began before age 22, or a VA service-connected disability rating of at least 50%
- Application approved by LDR and the Department of Health

Example: a sole proprietor pays a qualifying employee $3,000 a month for six months → 50% × $12,000 + 30% × $6,000 = $7,800.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.13. Unchanged for 2026
- LDR requires its application form attached; that state form is not a source document here

## Required Information
- Wages by month
- Approval and eligibility documentation

## Questions
- Does your business employ someone with a qualifying disability?
- Was the employment approved under the program?

## Common Errors
- Using 50% for all months
- Claiming without program approval

## Prompt
- I hired a person with a disability. Is there a Louisiana deduction?
