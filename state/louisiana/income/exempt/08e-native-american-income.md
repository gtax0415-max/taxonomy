# Native American Income (Code 08E)
## Description
Income from reservation sources is exempt for an enrolled tribal member who lives on that tribe's reservation.

## Rules
- Exempt: on-reservation income of an enrolled member living on the reservation
- Taxable: off-reservation income; all income of members living off the reservation
- Part-year: taxed based on where the member lived when the income was earned

Example: a tribal member living on the reservation exempts wages from a tribal employer on the reservation.

## Notes
- Authority: LAC 61:I.1303. Unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- Enrollment and residence
- Source of each income item

## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member of a tribe, living on its reservation?
- Was the income earned on the reservation?

## Common Errors
- Exempting off-reservation income
- Claiming while living off the reservation

## Prompt
- I live on a reservation in Louisiana. Is my income taxed?
