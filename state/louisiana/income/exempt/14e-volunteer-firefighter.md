# Volunteer Firefighter (Code 14E)
## Description
Flat exemption for active volunteer firefighters who keep up their training.

## Amount
$500 per qualifying volunteer.

## Eligibility
- Active member of the Louisiana State Firemen's Association, or on the State Fire Marshal's volunteer firefighter insurance roster
- Completed 24 hours of continuing education during 2026

Example: a volunteer firefighter with 30 training hours → $500.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:293(9)(a). Unchanged for 2026
- Each spouse who qualifies may claim it

## Required Information
- Membership or roster proof
- Training hours

## Questions
- Are you an active volunteer firefighter?
- Did you complete 24 hours of training in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the 24 training hours
- Claiming as a paid firefighter

## Prompt
- I'm a volunteer firefighter. Is there a Louisiana deduction?
