# Other Exempt Income (Code 49E)
## Description
Catch-all code for income Louisiana does not tax that has no specific code. Each item must be identified and documented on an attached schedule.

## Common items
- Disability income: up to $6,000 a year of payments for permanent total disability (R.S. 47:44.1(B)); not combinable with the blind/deaf/amputee exemption
- Home adaptations for a permanently disabled person: up to $5,000, only for families with gross income of $50,000 or less; requires a physician's certificate and a notarized statement (R.S. 47:59.1). Note this is a deduction for costs, not exempt income

Example: a permanently and totally disabled worker exempts $6,000 of disability income.

## New items for 2026 (codes not yet confirmed)
- ABLE account deposits (Act 110 of the 2025 Regular Session)
- Hardship retirement distributions when the taxpayer or spouse is terminally ill (Act 251 of the 2025 Regular Session)
Use specific codes if the 2026 form assigns them; otherwise 49E with a schedule.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026 apart from the new items above

## Required Information
- Description, source, and amount of each item
- Supporting documents

## Questions
- Do you have income you believe Louisiana does not tax that has no other code?

## Common Errors
- Using 49E for income with its own code
- Omitting the explanatory schedule
- Taking more than $6,000 of disability income

## Prompt
- I receive permanent disability payments. Are they taxed in Louisiana?
