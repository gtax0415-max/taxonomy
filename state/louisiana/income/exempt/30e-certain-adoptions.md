# Certain Adoptions (Code 30E)
## Description
$5,000 deduction per child for adopting a child from foster care, or an unrelated infant under one year old at placement through a private agency or attorney.

## Amount
- $5,000 per child, in the year the adoption becomes final
- One deduction per child; cannot exceed taxable income
- Cannot be combined with the $5,000 refundable credit for adopting an unrelated infant (code 78F) for the same child

## 2026 rules
- Foster care adoption (R.S. 47:297.20) also covers youth in extended foster care. Act 349 of the 2025 Regular Session lets LDR set an alternative process if getting the eligibility letter is an undue burden
- Private infant adoption (R.S. 47:297.21) unchanged

## Notes
- For an unrelated infant, compare: the 78F credit ($5,000 refundable, child under three) is worth far more than this $5,000 deduction (about $150 at the 3% rate)
- Federal adoption credit is separate

Example: a foster care adoption final in 2026 → $5,000 deduction.

## Required Information
- Finalization date
- Type of adoption and placement date

## Questions
- Did you finalize an adoption in 2026?
- Was it from foster care or a private placement of an infant?

## Common Errors
- Taking both 30E and 78F for the same child
- Claiming in the placement year instead of the finalization year

## Prompt
- We adopted a child from foster care.
