# Louisiana State Employees' Retirement Benefits (Code 02E)
## Description
Benefits paid by the Louisiana State Employees' Retirement System (LASERS) are fully exempt from Louisiana tax.

## Rules
- Entire taxable LASERS benefit exempt; no dollar cap
- Enter the month and year each spouse retired (informational, not an eligibility cutoff)
- Other Louisiana public retirement systems have their own exemption codes

Example: a retired state employee's $30,000 LASERS pension → $30,000 exempt.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 11:405. Unchanged for 2026
- Exempt amounts here are subtracted before the 65+ retirement exemption (06E)

## Required Information
- LASERS 1099-R taxable amount
- Retirement month and year

## Questions
- Do you receive a LASERS pension?
- When did you retire?

## Common Errors
- Coding another retirement system as 02E
- Also counting LASERS income toward 06E

## Prompt
- Is my state retirement taxed in Louisiana?
