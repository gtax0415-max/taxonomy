# Louisiana Adjusted Gross Income (Schedule E)
## Description
Louisiana AGI is federal AGI plus Louisiana additions, minus income Louisiana exempts.

## Structure
- Line 1: federal AGI
- Lines 2A–2E: additions (other-state bond interest, START and START K12 recapture, pass-through entity loss add-back, bonus depreciation add-back)
- Line 3: Line 1 plus additions, not less than zero
- Lines 4A–4G: exempt income by code; Line 4H total
- Line 5: Louisiana AGI, carried to IT-540 Line 7

Example: federal AGI $60,000 including $7,020 of taxable Social Security (code 07E, fully exempt) → Louisiana AGI $52,980.

## New for 2026
- Two 2025 Acts create new Schedule E items for 2026 not yet covered by a file: an ABLE account deposit exclusion (Act 110 of the 2025 Regular Session) and a deduction for hardship retirement distributions when the taxpayer or spouse is terminally ill (Act 251 of the 2025 Regular Session). Codes not yet assigned
- The voluntary retrofit deduction (16E) and retirement income exemption (06E) amounts changed for 2026

## Notes
- Codes are not interchangeable between schedules
- Line numbers are from the 2025 form

## Required Information
- Federal AGI
- Each addition and exemption with code and amount

## Questions
- Do you have income Louisiana exempts, such as Social Security or government pensions?
- Do you have tax-exempt interest from other states' bonds?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to mark the Schedule E box on Line 7
- Letting Line 3 go below zero

## Prompt
- How is Louisiana AGI figured?
