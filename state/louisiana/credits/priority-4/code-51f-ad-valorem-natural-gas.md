# Ad Valorem Natural Gas Credit (Code 51F)
## Description
Credit for local property tax paid on natural gas held, used, or consumed in providing natural gas storage services. The current-year credit goes here; any part not refunded carries forward under code 502.

## Amount
100% of the tax paid, with the same refund and 10-year carryforward rules as the inventory tax credit (code 50F).

## Status for 2026
Available to individuals and pass-through owners; the C corporation bar for taxes paid on or after July 1, 2026 applies here too.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6006(A)(2)
- Rare for individual filers

## Required Information
- Tax paid and payment dates
- Ownership share

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a natural gas storage business that paid parish property tax?

## Common Errors
- Putting a carryforward on 51F instead of code 502
