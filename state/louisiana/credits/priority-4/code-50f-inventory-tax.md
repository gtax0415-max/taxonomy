# Inventory Tax Credit (Code 50F)
## Description
Credit for local property tax paid on business inventory held by manufacturers, distributors, and retailers. The current-year credit goes here; any part that is not refunded carries forward under code 500.

## Amount
- 100% of parish ad valorem tax paid on inventory
- Excess over tax: fully refunded if eligible tax paid was $500,000 or less; above $500,000, 75% refunded and the rest carried forward up to 10 years (with an extra rule over $1 million)
- Separate refund limits apply to newly formed businesses
- Manufacturers with an ITEP contract: nonrefundable, carryforward only (code 500)

## Status for 2026
- Still available to individuals and pass-through owners
- C corporations (and estates/trusts taxed under R.S. 47:300.1) are barred for taxes paid on or after July 1, 2026 (Act 412 of the 2025 Regular Session), with an extended carryforward of their existing credits
- From 2026 an S corporation's entire credit passes through to its shareholders

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6006(A)(1); LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 26-011
- Some parishes may exempt inventory from property tax under the 2024–2025 reforms; no tax paid means no credit
- Credit is for tax actually PAID; the payment date matters

## Required Information
- Inventory tax paid and payment dates
- Ownership share, if a pass-through
- Whether the business is an ITEP manufacturer

## Questions
- Does your business pay parish property tax on inventory?
- When was it paid?

## Common Errors
- Putting a carryforward on 50F instead of code 500
- Claiming tax assessed but not paid
