# Angel Investor (Code 262)
## Description
Transferable credit for an accredited investor's cash investment in an LED-certified Louisiana Entrepreneurial Business.

## 2026 rules
- 25% of the investment; 35% for businesses in Opportunity Zones or small parishes (Act 515 of the 2025 Regular Session added parishes under 50,000 people)
- Investment limits: $720,000 per business per year; $1.44 million per business in total
- Reservations: Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) ended them June 30, 2025; Act 515 extended the cutoff to June 30, 2026
- From 2026, unused annual program cap no longer carries forward
- The credit becomes usable in the income year 24 months after certification; credits expire starting in the 11th tax year after they were granted
- Not refundable

## Transfers
Allowed to other Louisiana taxpayers; LDR must receive written notice within 10 business days showing the certificate, parties, date, amount, and price.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6020
- The exact end of the reservation window under Act 515 was not fully confirmed

## Required Information
- Certification number and amount
- Year usable and expiration year
- Transfer details, if bought

## Questions
- Did you invest in an LED-certified startup, or buy an angel investor credit?
- When was the credit certified?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the 24-month waiting period ends
- Claiming a credit past its expiration
