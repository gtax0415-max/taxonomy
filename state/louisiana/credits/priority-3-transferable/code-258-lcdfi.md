# Louisiana Community Development Financial Institution (LCDFI) (Code 258)
## Description
Carryover-only code for the former credit for investing in a certified Louisiana Community Development Financial Institution.

## Status for 2026 — carryover only
- The program (R.S. 51:3081–3094) was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), effective January 1, 2025
- LDR allows remaining balances to be used for their carryover period

## Notes
- The original carryforward length and transfer terms were not confirmed (the repealed text is no longer published); rely on the holder's certification
- Rare

## Required Information
- Certified amount, year, and remaining balance

## Questions
- Do you hold an unused LCDFI credit from before 2025?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit
