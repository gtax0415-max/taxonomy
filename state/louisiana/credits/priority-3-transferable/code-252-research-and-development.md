# Research and Development — SBIR/STTR (Code 252)
## Description
The transferable part of Louisiana's research credit: a credit based on federal Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) or Small Business Technology Transfer (STTR) awards. The non-transferable research credit is code 458.

## 2026 rules
- Credit = 30% of the SBIR or STTR award received during the year
- Statewide claims limited to $12 million per fiscal year from July 1, 2025, first-come, first-served; a claim refused under the cap rolls to the next fiscal year with priority (Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2))
- No credit for grant funds received after December 31, 2029
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years from when it was earned (a transfer does not restart it)
- Not refundable

## Transfers
SBIR/STTR credits from tax year 2018 on may be sold to another Louisiana taxpayer. The transfer must be recorded in the registry and LDR notified within 10 business days; a $200 fee per transferee applies.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6015(D)

## Required Information
- Certification number, amount, year earned
- Purchase details, if bought

## Questions
- Did your business receive an SBIR or STTR grant, or did you buy such a credit?

## Common Errors
- Using code 458 for a transferred SBIR/STTR credit
- Restarting the carryforward at purchase
