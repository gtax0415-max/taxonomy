# Motion Picture Investment (Code 251)
## Description
Credit for investment in a state-certified film or TV production or qualified entertainment company. Claimed on Schedule J's transferable-credit lines with the state certification number.

## 2026 rules
- Applications on or after July 1, 2025: up to 40% for approved projects under LED program rules (Act 44 of the 2025 Regular Session); the old per-production and per-person payroll caps no longer apply to these productions
- Applications from July 1, 2017 to June 30, 2025: 25% base plus add-ons, up to 40%
- Statewide caps from July 1, 2025: $125 million of credits issued per fiscal year and $125 million of claims per fiscal year (Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2))
- No applications on or after July 1, 2031
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years
- Not refundable; the cash option is selling the credit back to LDR at 90% of face value (85% for older projects)

## Transfers
- Credits from projects certified after July 1, 2017 generally can be sold only back to the state, not to other taxpayers; a buyer in 2026 usually holds credits from earlier projects
- Transfers must be recorded in the state tax credit registry, and LDR notified within 10 business days; a transfer fee applies

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6007
- The transfer is registered on a state form whose certification number goes on Lines 12A–15A; that form is not a source document here
- Credits earned by an entity that elected the pass-through entity tax cannot be used on the individual return

## Required Information
- Certification number and credit amount
- Purchase date and amount, if bought
- Carryforward balance

## Questions
- Did you earn or buy a Louisiana motion picture credit?
- Do you have the state certification number?
- Was the credit already sold back to the state?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the certification number
- Claiming a credit already sold back to LDR
- Carrying forward more than five years
