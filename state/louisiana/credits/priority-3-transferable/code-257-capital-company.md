# Capital Company (Code 257)
## Description
Carryover-only code for the former credit for investing in a certified Louisiana capital company (CAPCO).

## Status for 2026 — carryover only
- No capital company could be certified after June 30, 2003, and no new capital certified after December 31, 2003
- Unused credit carries forward until used

## Amount (as originally certified)
35% of the cash investment.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 51:1924. A related section (51:1932, the capital company's own tax exemption) was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2); whether 51:1924 remains in force was not confirmed
- Transfers were limited to affiliates and sophisticated investors
- Extremely rare in 2026

## Required Information
- Certified amount and remaining balance

## Questions
- Do you still hold an unused capital company credit from before 2004?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a credit for a recent investment

- What is code 257?
