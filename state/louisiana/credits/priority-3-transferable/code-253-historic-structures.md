# Rehabilitation of Historic Structures (Code 253)
## Description
Transferable credit for rehabilitating an income-producing historic structure in a downtown development district, cultural district, or listed on the National Register.

## 2026 rules
- 25% of eligible rehabilitation costs incurred January 1, 2023 through December 31, 2028 (35% for qualifying rural structures)
- No credit for costs incurred on or after January 1, 2029
- $5 million per taxpayer (with affiliates) per year per district
- Statewide reservations limited to $85 million a year for Part 2 applications received on or after January 1, 2025 (was $125 million)
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years
- Can be used alongside the 20% federal historic rehabilitation credit
- Not refundable

## Transfers
May be resold any number of times; LDR must be notified within 10 business days and a processing fee applies. A transfer does not extend the carryforward.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6019
- Owner-occupied residential rehabilitation has a separate credit; this file is the commercial credit

## Required Information
- Certification number, credit amount, and year earned
- Purchase details, if bought

## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a historic building, or buy a historic rehabilitation credit?
- When were the costs incurred?

## Common Errors
- Using a 20% rate for 2023–2028 costs
- Assuming a purchase restarts the five-year carryforward
