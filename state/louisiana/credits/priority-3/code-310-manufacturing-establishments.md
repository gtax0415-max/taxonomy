# Manufacturing Establishments (Code 310)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit available to a manufacturing establishment under a contract with the State Board of Commerce and Industry.

## Status for 2026 — existing contracts only
- No new contracts may be entered into, and no existing contracts renewed, after June 30, 2025 (Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), amending R.S. 47:4302)
- A 2026 credit therefore requires a contract in force that was signed or last renewed on or before June 30, 2025

## Eligibility
- Contract in effect for 2026
- Individuals claim only as sole proprietors or through a pass-through entity

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:4301 et seq. Credit amount follows the contract

## Required Information
- Contract and its term
- K-1 share

## Questions
- Does your business have a manufacturing establishment contract with the Board of Commerce and Industry?
- When was it signed or last renewed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming under a contract renewed after June 30, 2025
- Claiming after the contract term ends

- My manufacturing company has a state incentive contract.
