# Nonrefundable Child Care Credit (Federal AGI over $25,000)
## Description
Louisiana gives a percentage of the federal child and dependent care credit. Above $25,000 of federal AGI the Louisiana credit is nonrefundable; at $25,000 or less it is refundable and handled in a separate file.

## Amount
Base is the federal credit CLAIMED on the federal return (Schedule 3, line 2, i.e. after the federal tax limit):
- Federal AGI $25,001–$35,000: 30%
- Federal AGI $35,001–$60,000: 10%
- Federal AGI over $60,000: the LESSER of $25 or 10%

Limited to Louisiana tax remaining; any excess carries forward up to five years. Carryforwards from 2021–2025 are used before the 2026 credit.

## 2026 federal interaction
The One Big Beautiful Bill Act enlarged the federal credit from 2026: the maximum rate rises to 50%, phasing to 35% between federal AGI of $15,000 and $43,000, and drops further toward 20% only above $75,000 ($150,000 joint). Expense limits stay $3,000 / $6,000. A bigger federal credit raises the Louisiana credit in the $25,001–$60,000 bands, but above $60,000 the $25 cap usually binds, so the federal increase changes nothing.

Example: joint filers, federal AGI $96,000, one child, $3,000 of expenses. Federal credit 35% × $3,000 = $1,050. Louisiana: lesser of $25 or $105 = $25.

## Eligibility
- Louisiana resident who claimed the federal child and dependent care credit for 2026
- Federal AGI over $25,000

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.4. Louisiana rule unchanged for 2026
- Louisiana uses FEDERAL AGI here, not Louisiana income
- No federal Form 2441 means no Louisiana credit
- This credit is the base for the Workforce Child Care Expense Credit (separate file)

## Required Information
- Federal AGI
- Federal credit claimed (Schedule 3, line 2)
- Child care credit carryforwards from 2021–2025

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal child care credit on Form 2441?
- What was your federal AGI?
- Do you have unused Louisiana child care credit from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Applying 30% above $35,000
- Ignoring the $25 cap above $60,000
- Using the federal credit before the tax limit instead of the amount claimed
- Forgetting carryforwards, or using ones older than five years (2020 and earlier have expired)

## Prompt
- I claimed the federal child care credit and my AGI is $48,000.
- How much is the Louisiana nonrefundable child care credit?
