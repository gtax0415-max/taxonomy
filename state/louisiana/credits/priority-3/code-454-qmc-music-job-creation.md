# Qualified Music Company (QMC) Music Job Creation (Code 454)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a qualified music company's Louisiana payroll, certified by Louisiana Economic Development. Use is limited to 50% of the tax.

## Status for 2026 — previously certified credits only
- Part of the sound recording / music credit program in R.S. 47:6023
- Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) moved the program's application deadline up to July 1, 2025; no credits are granted for applications received on or after that date
- A 2026 claim needs an LED certification from an earlier application

## Amount
The certified amount, limited to 50% of the Louisiana tax.

## Notes
- Statute mapping of code 454 to R.S. 47:6023 is NOT fully confirmed; verify against the 2026 code list

## Required Information
- LED certification and amount
- K-1 share
- Louisiana tax, to apply the 50% limit

## Questions
- Do you have an LED certification for the music job creation credit?
- When was the application submitted?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than 50% of the tax
- Claiming based on an application filed after July 1, 2025

## Prompt
- My music company has a Louisiana job creation credit.
- What is code 454?
