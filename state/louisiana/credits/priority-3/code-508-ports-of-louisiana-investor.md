# Ports of Louisiana Investor (Code 508)
## Description
Carryover-only code for credits earned by investing in qualified port facility projects.

## Status for 2026 — carryover only
- R.S. 47:6036 was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), effective January 1, 2025
- Previously certified credit may be used for its remaining carryforward period (LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-012)

## Amount (as previously certified)
- Up to 72% of a qualifying project's capital costs, generally taken at 5% a year
- Unused credit carries forward up to 10 years

## Required Information
- Certified amount and amount used since

## Questions
- Do you have a certified Ports of Louisiana investor credit with a remaining balance?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit under a repealed program
- Carrying forward more than 10 years

- What is code 508?
