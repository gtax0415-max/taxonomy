# Work-Based Learning Credit (Code 467) — NEW for 2026
## Description
Nonrefundable employer credit for hours worked by participants in eligible work-based learning programs. Replaces the apprenticeship credit (code 463), which stopped generating new credit after 2025.

## Amount
- The LESSER of $2.50 per hour worked or $2,500 per participant
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years
- Available for taxable periods beginning January 1, 2026 through 2031

## Eligibility
- Business employed an eligible participant in a qualifying work-based learning program in 2026
- Individuals claim it as sole proprietors or through a pass-through entity

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6003, enacted by Act 376 of the 2025 Regular Session (HB 533)
- Code 467 comes from LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-028; confirm it on the 2026 IT-540 code list
- LDR requires its own certification form with the return; that state form is not a source document here
- Participant eligibility rules and any statewide cap were not verified in this review

## Required Information
- Hours per participant
- Certification per participant
- K-1 share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business employ apprentices or work-based learning participants in 2026?
- How many hours did each work?

## Common Errors
- Using old code 463 for 2026 hours
- Applying the old $1.25 / $1,250 amounts
