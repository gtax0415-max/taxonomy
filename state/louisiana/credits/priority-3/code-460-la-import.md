# Louisiana Import Credit (Code 460)
## Description
Credit for importers that increased cargo moving through Louisiana ports. For 2026 this code is CARRYFORWARD ONLY.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- R.S. 47:6036.1 was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), effective January 1, 2025
- No awards for applications received after July 1, 2025
- Previously certified credit may be used for its remaining carryforward period (LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-012)

## Amount (as previously certified)
- Up to $50 per net new twenty-foot equivalent unit (TEU) above base volume, or up to $100 per TEU under a project agreement
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years

## Notes
- Rare for individual filers

## Required Information
- Certified amount, year claimable, and amount used since

## Questions
- Do you have an LED-certified Louisiana import credit with a remaining balance?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit under a repealed program
- Carrying forward more than five years
