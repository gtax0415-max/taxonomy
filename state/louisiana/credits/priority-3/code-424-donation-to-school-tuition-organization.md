# Donations to School Tuition Organizations (Code 424)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to a school tuition organization (STO) that funds scholarships for students to attend qualified nonpublic schools.

## Amount
- Credit = the part of the donation the STO actually used for scholarships (administrative costs, capped at 5%, are excluded)
- Limited to Louisiana tax; excess carries forward up to three years
- No per-taxpayer or statewide dollar cap in the statute
- No other Louisiana tax benefit (such as a deduction) for the same donation

## 2026 changes
- Act 282 of the 2025 Regular Session (HB 477): public lab schools run by colleges or universities count as qualified schools, for donations funding the 2026–27 school year onward
- Act 403 of the 2025 Regular Session (SB 27): changes scholarship rules for students (multiple STOs allowed; scholarship caps tied to the state per-pupil amount). Does not change the donor's credit formula

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6301
- The STO issues a state receipt form, which LDR requires attached; that form is not a source document here
- Interaction with the LA GATOR education savings account program was not verified

## Required Information
- STO name and donation amount
- Amount used for scholarships, per the STO's receipt
- Carryforwards from 2023–2025

## Questions
- Did you donate to a school tuition organization in 2026?
- How much did the STO report as used for scholarships?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the full donation instead of the scholarship portion
- Also deducting the donation on the Louisiana return
- Carrying forward more than three years

## Prompt
- I donated to a scholarship organization for private schools.
- What is code 424?
