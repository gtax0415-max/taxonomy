# Apprenticeship Credit (2022) (Code 463)
## Description
Nonrefundable employer credit for hours worked by registered apprentices. For 2026 this code is CARRYFORWARD ONLY; new 2026 hours go to the Work-Based Learning credit (code 467).

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- R.S. 47:6033(G): no credit may be earned after December 31, 2025
- Act 376 of the 2025 Regular Session (HB 533) created the replacement Work-Based Learning credit for 2026 through 2031 (see code-467 file)

## Amount (credits earned through 2025)
- $1.25 per hour worked by an apprentice, up to $1,250 per apprentice per year
- Apprentice must work at least 250 hours
- Statewide cap $2.5 million per year
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6033
- Individuals claim it as sole proprietors or through a pass-through entity

## Required Information
- Year each credit was earned and amount used since
- Certification for each apprentice

## Questions
- Do you have unused apprenticeship credit earned in 2022–2025?
- Did your business employ apprentices in 2026? (If so, see code 467)

## Common Errors
- Claiming new 2026 apprentice hours under code 463
- Carrying the credit forward more than five years

## Prompt
- I have apprenticeship credit left over from last year.
- What is code 463?
