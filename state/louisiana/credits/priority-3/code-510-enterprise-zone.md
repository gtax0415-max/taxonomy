# Enterprise Zone (Code 510)
## Description
Nonrefundable job credit for businesses that created net new jobs under an enterprise zone contract.

## Status for 2026 — existing contracts only
LED stopped accepting advance notifications on July 1, 2025 (R.S. 51:1787(L), as amended by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2)). Credits under existing contracts, and their carryforwards, continue.

## Amount
- $1,000 per net new job for projects with advance notification filed on or after April 1, 2016
- $3,500 per net new job if the hire received public assistance in the prior six months
- $2,500 per job for older contracts
- Unused credit carries forward up to 10 years
- Contracts run up to five years

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 51:1781 et seq. (credit in 51:1787)
- LDR requires the contract attached to the return
- The separate refundable investment credit is not reported here

## Required Information
- Contract, its term, and approved jobs
- Carryforward balance

## Questions
- Does your business have an enterprise zone contract?
- When was the advance notification filed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a project with advance notification on or after July 1, 2025
- Carrying forward more than 10 years
