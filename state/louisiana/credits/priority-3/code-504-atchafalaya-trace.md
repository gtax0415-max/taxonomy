# Atchafalaya Trace (Code 504)
## Description
Carryover-only code for credits earned under the former Atchafalaya Trace Heritage Area program.

## Status for 2026 — program repealed
- The statutes authorizing the credit (R.S. 25:1226–1226.6) were repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), effective for income tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- No new credit can be earned in 2026

## Carryover
Only an unused amount earned before 2025 can appear here, and only if still within its carryforward period. The repealing act has no savings clause, but LDR's Revenue Information Bulletin 25-012 says credits earned before the 2025 repeals may still be used for their remaining carryforward period. Confirm code 504 remains on the 2026 IT-540 code list.

## Required Information
- Year and amount of the original credit
- Amount used each year since

## Questions
- Do you have an Atchafalaya Trace credit carried over from before 2025?
- When was it earned and how much remains?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit under a repealed program
- Claiming a carryover past its expiration
