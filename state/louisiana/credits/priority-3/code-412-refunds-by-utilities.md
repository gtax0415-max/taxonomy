# Refunds by Utilities (Code 412)
## Description
Credit a utility may elect, instead of a deduction that would create a net loss, when it must refund to customers amounts collected under a rate increase that was later denied by a court or regulator.

## Amount
- 72% of the extra Louisiana income tax caused solely by including the refunded amounts in income
- Excess carries over to the next two taxable years

## Status for 2026
Available; no change found in 2024–2025 legislation.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:287.664
- Almost never used by individuals; only an owner of a pass-through utility would see it

## Required Information
- Refund order and amounts refunded
- Tax computation with and without the refunded income

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a utility ordered to refund customers?

## Common Errors
- Taking both the deduction and the credit
- Carrying over more than two years
