# Recycling Credit (Code 550)
## Description
Credit for buying qualified new recycling manufacturing or process equipment, or qualified service contracts, used exclusively in Louisiana and certified by DEQ.

## Amount
- 14% of the cost, less any other tax credits received for the same purchase
- Each year's claim limited to 20% of the total credit and to 50% of that year's tax
- Unused credit carries forward until used
- DEQ certifies no more than $3.6 million per year statewide

## Status for 2026
Available; no sunset. Act 6 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session removed the franchise tax from the credit effective 2026.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6005
- LDR requires the DEQ certification attached to the return

## Required Information
- DEQ certification and certified cost
- Total credit and amounts used each year

## Questions
- Did your business buy DEQ-certified recycling equipment?
- How much of the credit has been used in earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Taking more than 20% of the total credit in one year
- Exceeding 50% of the year's tax
