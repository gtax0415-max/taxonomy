# Neighborhood Assistance (Code 457)
## Description
Credit for a business contribution to an approved neighborhood organization program in an impoverished area (assistance, job training, education, crime prevention, community services).

## Amount
- Up to 50% of the approved contribution
- Maximum $180,000 per year (the statute says per corporation; application to individual filers not confirmed)
- Statewide credits each fiscal year limited to 1% of the prior year's corporate income tax collections
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years

## Status for 2026
Available; no change found in 2024–2025 legislation (Act 5 repealed neighboring sections but not this credit).

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:35 and R.S. 47:287.753
- Requires advance written approval of the proposal

## Required Information
- Approval letter and approved amount
- Contribution receipt
- Carryforward balance

## Questions
- Did your business contribute to an approved neighborhood assistance program?
- Do you have written approval from the Commissioner of Administration?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without advance approval
- Taking more than 50% of the contribution or more than $180,000

## Prompt
- My business funded a neighborhood assistance program.
