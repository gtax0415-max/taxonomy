# Firearm Safety Devices (Code 465)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for buying a firearm safety device, such as a gun safe, lockbox, or trigger lock.

## Amount
- The LESSER of the device cost (including sales tax, excluding shipping or installation) or $500
- One qualifying purchase per year; separate purchases cannot be combined
- Statewide cap $500,000 per year, first-come, first-served
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years
- No credit for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2027

## 2026 rules
- Purchases from any dealer that collects Louisiana sales tax qualify (Act 160 of the 2025 Regular Session); the dealer no longer needs a federal firearms license
- LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-019 replaces the earlier bulletin

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.24 (not repealed with the rest of R.S. 47:297)
- LDR requires the itemized receipt attached to the return

## Required Information
- Receipt with device, price, tax, seller, and date

## Questions
- Did you buy a gun safe, lockbox, or trigger lock in 2026?
- Was it one purchase, and did the seller charge Louisiana sales tax?

## Common Errors
- Combining several purchases
- Including shipping or installation
- Claiming more than $500

## Prompt
- I bought a gun safe this year.
- What is code 465?
