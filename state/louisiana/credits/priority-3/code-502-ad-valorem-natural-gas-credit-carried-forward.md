# Ad Valorem Natural Gas Credit Carried Forward (Code 502)
## Description
Carryforward of the unrefunded part of the credit for local property tax paid on natural gas held, used, or consumed in natural gas storage. The CURRENT-year credit is a refundable credit reported elsewhere (Schedule F, code 51F); code 502 is only the amount carried forward.

## Amount
Uses the same refund rules as the inventory tax credit (see code 500):
- Excess over tax is refunded in full if eligible tax paid was $500,000 or less
- Above that, part is refunded and the rest carries forward up to 10 years

## Status for 2026
- Still available to individuals and pass-through owners
- C corporations (and estates/trusts taxed under R.S. 47:300.1) are barred for taxes paid on or after July 1, 2026; not relevant to most individual filers

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6006(A)(2)
- Rare for individual filers

## Required Information
- Year each carryforward arose and amount used since
- K-1 share

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a natural gas storage business that paid parish property tax?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from a prior year?

## Common Errors
- Putting the current-year credit on code 502 instead of the refundable schedule
- Carrying forward more than 10 years

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a natural gas property tax credit carryforward.
- What is code 502?
