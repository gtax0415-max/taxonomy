# Research and Development (Code 458)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for qualified research in Louisiana, tied to the federal research credit (IRC §41) and certified by Louisiana Economic Development (LED).

## 2026 rules
- Claims are limited to a statewide total of $12 million per fiscal year, beginning July 1, 2025, on a first-come, first-served basis (Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2))
- A claim denied because the cap is reached may be used on an original return filed in the next fiscal year, with priority
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years
- Individuals claim it only as sole proprietors or through a pass-through entity

## Eligibility
- The business claimed the federal research credit (or qualifies through SBIR/STTR participation)
- LED certified the credit

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6015
- The fiscal-year cap means a valid credit can be deferred, not just reduced; check whether the claim was accepted under the cap

## Required Information
- LED certification amount
- Federal Form 6765
- K-1 share and any carryforward

## Questions
- Did your business claim the federal research credit?
- Did LED certify a Louisiana credit?
- Was the claim deferred by the annual cap?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without LED certification
- Ignoring the $12 million annual cap deferral
- Carrying the credit forward more than five years
