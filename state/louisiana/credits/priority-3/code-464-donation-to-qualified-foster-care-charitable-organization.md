# Donations to Qualified Foster Care Charitable Organizations (Code 464)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to organizations that serve foster children and families.

## Amount
- Credit = the part of the donation the organization actually used for services, up to $50,000
- Statewide cap $500,000 per calendar year, first-come, first-served, prorated if exceeded
- Limited to Louisiana tax; excess carries forward up to five years

## 2026 rules
Act 349 of the 2025 Regular Session (HB 238) rewrote the receipt and definition provisions, applying to periods beginning on or after January 1, 2025. The credit formula is unchanged; no sunset.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6042
- The organization issues a state receipt form, which LDR requires attached; that form is not a source document here
- DCFS sends LDR an annual list of qualifying organizations

## Required Information
- Organization name and confirmation it qualifies
- Amount used for services, per the receipt
- Carryforward balance

## Questions
- Did you donate to a foster care organization in 2026?
- Is it on the state's list of qualifying organizations?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the whole donation instead of the portion used for services
- Exceeding $50,000
