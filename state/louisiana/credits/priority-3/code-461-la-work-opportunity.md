# Louisiana Work Opportunity Credit (Code 461)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an employer that hires participants in a work-release program.

## Status for 2026 — still available
- Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) had cut off new certifications after June 30, 2025
- Act 2025 Regular Session (HB 567) repealed that sunset for income tax periods beginning on or after January 1, 2026, so new 2026 hires can generate the credit again

## Amount
Per the statute and certification. The amount formula was NOT re-verified in this review.

## Eligibility
- The business hired an eligible work-release participant
- Certification obtained for the hire
- Individuals claim it as sole proprietors or through a pass-through entity

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:287.750

## Required Information
- Certification per participant
- Wages paid
- K-1 share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business hire someone from a work-release program in 2026?
- Did you obtain certification for the hire?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the credit expired in 2025
- Claiming without a certification
