# Inventory Tax Credit Carried Forward and ITEP (Code 500)
## Description
Two pieces of the credit for local property tax paid on business inventory: (1) the unrefunded part carried forward from earlier years and (2) the nonrefundable credit of manufacturers with an Industrial Tax Exemption Program (ITEP) contract. The CURRENT-year refundable inventory credit is reported elsewhere (Schedule F, code 50F), not here.

## Amount
- Credit = 100% of parish ad valorem tax paid on inventory
- Excess over tax: fully refunded if eligible tax paid was $500,000 or less; above $500,000, 75% is refunded and the rest carries forward up to 10 years (with an extra rule over $1 million)
- ITEP manufacturers: nonrefundable; carries forward up to 10 years

## Status for 2026
- Still available to individuals and pass-through owners
- C corporations (and estates/trusts taxed under R.S. 47:300.1) are barred for taxes paid on or after July 1, 2026, with an extended carryforward for their existing credits
- Starting in 2026, an S corporation's entire credit flows through to its shareholders (Act 382 of the 2025 Regular Session)

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6006(A)(1)
- Several parishes may exempt inventory from property tax under the 2024–2025 reforms; no tax paid means no credit

## Required Information
- Inventory tax paid, by year
- Carryforward balance and year each arose
- ITEP contract status, if a manufacturer

## Questions
- Does your business pay parish property tax on inventory?
- Do you have inventory credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Is the business a manufacturer with an ITEP contract?

## Common Errors
- Putting the current-year refundable credit on code 500
- Carrying forward more than 10 years
- Claiming credit for tax not yet paid

## Prompt
- My S corporation pays inventory tax.
- What is code 500?
