# Tax Equalization (Code 305)
## Description
Credit for a business that located in Louisiana instead of a competing state, sized to bring its Louisiana state taxes down to what it would have paid in that other state.

## Status for 2026 — existing contracts only
No new contracts and no renewals after June 30, 2025 (R.S. 47:3204(M), added by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2)).

## Amount
The state tax exemption set in the contract each year. State taxes only. Contracts run five years.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:3201 et seq. (operative section 47:3204)
- LDR requires a copy of the contract attached to the return
- Rare for individual filers

## Required Information
- Contract and its term
- Credit granted for 2026
- K-1 share

## Questions
- Does your business have a tax equalization contract with Louisiana?
- When was it signed or last renewed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming under a contract signed or renewed after June 30, 2025
- Claiming after the contract term
