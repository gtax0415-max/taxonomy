# Louisiana Fortified Roof Credit (Code 466)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for installing a roof that earns an IBHS FORTIFIED Roof designation on your owner-occupied primary residence with a homestead exemption. Created by Act 404 of the 2025 Regular Session.

## Amount
- Credit = 100% of qualified purchase and installation costs, up to $10,000 (statute says per resident taxpayer; LDR says per residence; one credit per property)
- Evaluator, permit, and inspection fees do NOT count
- Costs must be incurred on or after July 1, 2025
- Limited to Louisiana tax; excess carries forward up to three years
- Statewide cap $10 million per fiscal year, first-come, first-served, prorated if exceeded
- No credit earned after December 31, 2031

## Eligibility
- Primary residence with a homestead exemption, owner-occupied
- Not new construction, a condominium, or a mobile home
- Installed by a program-approved contractor and designated FORTIFIED
- No Louisiana Fortify Homes Program grant for the same roof

## 2026 rules
- LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 26-012 (June 1, 2026) dropped the preliminary (pre-construction) evaluation requirement for all installations
- Application window: for a roof certified in 2026, apply to LDR between January 1 and June 30, 2027; the credit is claimed using LDR's approval

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6044 (some LDR material cites 47:6028 in error; 6028 is the youth jobs credit)
- Separate from the fortified-roof retrofit DEDUCTION (Schedule E), which a filer should not double-count with the same costs

## Required Information
- Contract, invoice, and FORTIFIED certificate
- Homestead exemption confirmation
- LDR approval and amount

## Questions
- Did you install a FORTIFIED roof on your homestead after July 1, 2025?
- Did you receive a Fortify Homes Program grant?
- Did you apply to LDR during the application window?

## Common Errors
- Including evaluator, permit, or inspection fees
- Claiming for a rental, second home, or new construction
- Claiming without LDR approval
- Claiming both the grant and the credit
