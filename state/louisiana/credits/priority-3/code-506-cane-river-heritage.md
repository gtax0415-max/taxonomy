# Cane River Heritage (Code 506)
## Description
Carryover-only code for credits awarded to small heritage-based businesses in the Cane River Heritage area.

## Status for 2026 — carryover only
- No new approvals after December 31, 2017
- R.S. 47:6026 was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2), effective January 1, 2025
- Remaining balances may be used within their 10-year carryforward from the award date (LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-012), so they expire by about 2027

## Amount (as originally awarded)
$1,080 for a qualifying cottage industry with 20 or fewer employees, plus $1,080 per net new hire who lived in the zone.

## Required Information
- Award date and amount
- Amount used since

## Questions
- Do you have a Cane River Heritage credit from before 2018 with a remaining balance?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a balance more than 10 years past the award date
