# Nonrefundable Workforce Child Care Expense Credit (formerly School Readiness Credit; Federal AGI over $25,000)
## Description
An additional credit for each child under age six who attended a child care facility rated two stars or higher in Louisiana's Quality Start rating system. Act 454 of the 2025 Regular Session RENAMED the School Readiness Tax Credits the Workforce Child Care Tax Credits for tax years beginning January 1, 2026; the 2026 form will likely use the new name.

## Amount
- Credit per child = the Louisiana child care credit (R.S. 47:297.4) × the facility's star percentage (see routing)
- Each child is calculated separately; if a child attended more than one facility, use the highest-rated one
- Limited to Louisiana tax remaining after the child care credit; excess carries forward up to five years, with older carryforwards used first

Example: Louisiana child care credit of $25, one child at a five-star center: $25 × 200% = $50.

## Eligibility
- Qualifying dependent under age six during the year
- Facility participates in Quality Start with at least two stars
- Taxpayer has a Louisiana child care credit under R.S. 47:297.4
- Federal AGI over $25,000 (at $25,000 or less the credit is refundable; separate file)

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6104. Percentages unchanged for 2026; only the name changed for this credit
- Act 454 changed amounts for the BUSINESS-supported child care credit (R.S. 47:6107), not this one
- The facility issues the rating certificate on a state form; it is the taxpayer's proof but not a source document for this taxonomy

## Required Information
- Each qualifying child's age and facility
- Facility star rating and license number
- Louisiana child care credit amount
- Carryforwards from 2021–2025

## Questions
- Was your child under six and in a rated child care center in 2026?
- What star rating did the center have?
- Did your child attend more than one center?

## Common Errors
- Claiming with no Louisiana child care credit
- Claiming for a child age six or older
- Claiming for a one-star or unrated facility
- Using the old "School Readiness" name when matching to the 2026 form

## Prompt
- My child goes to a four-star daycare. Is there an extra Louisiana credit?
- What is the workforce child care expense credit?
