# Louisiana Youth Jobs Credit (Code 462)
## Description
Nonrefundable employer credit for hiring eligible youth. For 2026 this code is CARRYFORWARD ONLY.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
R.S. 47:6028(H): no credit may be earned after December 31, 2025.

## Amount (credits earned through 2025)
- $1,250 per eligible youth working full time (32+ hours a week)
- $750 per eligible youth working part time (20 to under 32 hours a week)
- Youth age 16–23, previously unemployed, meeting a hardship test, hired on or after July 1, 2021, and employed at least three consecutive months
- Statewide cap $5 million per calendar year
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6028

## Required Information
- Year each credit was earned and amount used since
- Certification of the original credit

## Questions
- Do you have unused youth jobs credit earned in 2021–2025?

## Common Errors
- Claiming new credit for youth hired or employed in 2026
- Carrying forward more than five years
