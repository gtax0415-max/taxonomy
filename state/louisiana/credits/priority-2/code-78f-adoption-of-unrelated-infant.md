# Adoption of an Unrelated Infant (Code 78F)
## Description
Refundable Louisiana credit for adopting an unrelated child who was under age three at placement. Separate from, and in addition to, the federal adoption credit.

## Amount
- $5,000 per child
- Claimed for the tax year the adoption becomes final
- Fully refundable

## Eligibility
- Child not related to the adoptive parent
- Child age two or younger at placement
- Not an adoption from state foster care
- Not combined with the Louisiana adoption deduction (Schedule E code 30E) for the same child

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.23 (its own section). Unchanged for 2026
- SUNSET: no credit for tax years beginning after December 31, 2028
- The federal adoption credit (Form 8839) is separate; claiming one does not bar the other

## Required Information
- Finalization date
- Placement date and child's age
- Relationship to child

## Questions
- Did you finalize the adoption of an unrelated infant in 2026?
- How old was the child at placement?
- Was it a foster care adoption?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in the placement year instead of the finalization year
- Taking both this credit and deduction 30E for the same child
- Claiming for a foster care adoption
