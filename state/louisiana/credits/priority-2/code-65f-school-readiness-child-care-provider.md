# Workforce Child Care Provider Credit (formerly School Readiness) (Code 65F)
## Description
Refundable credit for child care providers serving children in the Child Care Assistance Program or foster children in state custody, based on the facility's quality rating.

## Amount
Average monthly number of eligible children × rate by star rating:
- Five star: $1,500
- Four star: $1,250
- Three star: $1,000
- Two star: $750
- One star or not participating: $0

## Eligibility
- Owns and operates a licensed facility rated two stars or higher
- Serves Child Care Assistance Program or foster children
- Sole proprietors and pass-through owners claim on the IT-540

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6105. Amounts unchanged for 2026; Act 454 of 2025 renamed the program only
- LDR requires the Department of Education certification attached

## Required Information
- Certified average eligible children
- Star rating
- Ownership share, if a pass-through

## Questions
- Do you own a child care center that serves CCAP or foster children?
- What is its star rating?

## Common Errors
- Counting all enrolled children instead of eligible children
- Using total rather than average monthly count

## Prompt
- I own a daycare that takes CCAP children.
