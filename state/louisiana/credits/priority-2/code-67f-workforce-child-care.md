# Workforce Child Care — Business-Supported (formerly School Readiness) (Code 67F)
## Description
Refundable credit for a business that supports quality child care for its employees. Act 454 of the 2025 Regular Session renamed it and substantially increased the rates and expense limits for 2026, while adding a statewide cap and an application step.

## Amount (2026)
Eligible expenses × rate by the facility's star rating (see routing). Eligible expenses per year:
- Building, renovating, equipping, or operating a facility: up to $100,000 (was $50,000)
- Payments for employees' child care: up to $10,000 per child (was $5,000)
- Buying child care slots for employees: up to $100,000 (was $50,000)

Rates were 20/15/10/5% before 2026; two-star facilities no longer qualify.

## 2026 cap and application
- Statewide cap $1 million for calendar 2026; rises $1 million a year (to $5 million) if at least 80% is used
- Apply to LDR between January 1 and February 28, 2027 for 2026 expenses; first-come, first-served, prorated for same-day requests
- Claim only the amount in LDR's approval letter

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6107(A)(1), with expense limits in R.S. 47:6102(7)
- LDR's 2025 legislative summary misstates the two-star rate; the statute and LDR's guide give 0%

## Required Information
- Expenses by type and facility
- Facility rating and license number
- LDR approval amount

## Questions
- Did your business pay for employees' child care or support a child care center in 2026?
- What star rating does the facility have?
- Did you apply to LDR by February 28, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Using the old 20/15/10/5% rates
- Claiming for a two-star facility
- Claiming without applying under the cap
