# Digital Interactive Media and Software (Code 73F)
## Description
Refundable credit for Louisiana spending on digital interactive media and software development, certified by Louisiana Economic Development.

## Amount (applications on or after July 1, 2017)
- 18% of Louisiana base investment, plus
- 7% of Louisiana resident payroll
- A company may instead elect, once at final certification, an 85% rebate of the credit

## Status for 2026
Available; no sunset or application cutoff found. Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) removed only an old legislative-review requirement. Act 432 of the 2025 Regular Session repealed one definition in the statute; its effect was not confirmed.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6022
- Credits for spending before 2012 were transferable with a 10-year carryforward; later credits are refundable

## Required Information
- Final certification amount
- K-1 share

## Questions
- Does your business develop software or games in Louisiana with an LED certification?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the initial certification amount instead of the final one
- Claiming both the credit and the rebate
