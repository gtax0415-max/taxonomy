# Milk Producers (Code 58F)
## Description
Refundable credit for Louisiana resident dairy farmers, triggered only in years when the federal milk price falls below the state's production cost.

## Amount
- Earned only if the USDA Federal Order 7 uniform price drops below the production price announced by the Louisiana Department of Agriculture and Forestry at some point during the year
- Tiered by pounds of milk sold: $3,600 for up to 1,000,000 pounds, rising to $21,600 for over 3,000,000 pounds
- Prorated by quarter
- Caps: $21,600 per producer; $1.8 million statewide per calendar year

## Status for 2026
Available; no sunset. Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) removed only an old legislative-review requirement.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6032
- The Department of Health certifies eligible producers to LDR by January 31 of the following year

## Required Information
- Pounds sold by quarter
- Certification status

## Questions
- Are you a Louisiana dairy producer with a valid permit?
- Did the federal price fall below the state production price in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in a year the price trigger did not occur
- Not prorating by quarter
