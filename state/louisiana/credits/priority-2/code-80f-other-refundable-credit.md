# Other Refundable Credit (Code 80F)
## Description
Catch-all code for a refundable credit that has no code of its own. LDR describes it only as reserved for future credits.

## Notes
- No new refundable individual credit was enacted in the 2025 or 2026 Regular Sessions, so nothing has been identified that uses 80F for 2026
- The 2025 credits (public school donations, fortified roof, work-based learning) are nonrefundable and have their own codes
- Recheck when the 2026 IT-540 is released

## Required Information
- Name and legal authority of the credit
- Certificate or approval amount

## Questions
- Do you have a refundable Louisiana credit not on the Schedule F code list?

## Common Errors
- Putting a nonrefundable credit here
