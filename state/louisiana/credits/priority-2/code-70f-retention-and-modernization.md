# Retention and Modernization (Code 70F)
## Description
Refundable credit for manufacturers that modernize Louisiana facilities, certified by Louisiana Economic Development.

## Status for 2026 — existing certifications only
No credits for applications received after June 30, 2025 (R.S. 51:2399.3(C), added by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2)). Remaining installments of approved credits continue.

## Amount
- 4% of qualified modernization spending (approvals on or after July 1, 2017), taken in five equal annual installments
- Statewide $7.2 million per year
- Not claimable until LED signs the project completion form; expires beginning in the 11th tax year after it was granted

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 51:2399.1 et seq.

## Required Information
- Certified amount and installment schedule
- Completion form date

## Questions
- Does your business have an LED-approved modernization credit?
- Which installment year is 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the full credit in one year instead of the annual installment
- Claiming before the project completion form is signed
