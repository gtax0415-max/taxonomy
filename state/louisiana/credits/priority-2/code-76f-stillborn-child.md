# Stillborn Child (Code 76F)
## Description
One-time refundable credit for a parent who delivered a stillborn child.

## Amount
- $2,000, claimed for the tax year of the stillbirth
- Fully refundable

## Eligibility
- The taxpayer delivered the child (the mother named on the fetal death certificate)
- Spontaneous fetal death at 20 or more weeks of gestation, or 350 grams or more
- Not an induced termination of pregnancy
- A certificate of fetal death was issued

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.19 (its own section, so not affected by the 2024 repeal of R.S. 47:297). Unchanged for 2026
- LDR requires the certified certificate attached to the return

## Required Information
- Date of the stillbirth
- Certified fetal death certificate

## Questions
- Did you deliver a stillborn child in 2026?
- Do you have a certified certificate of fetal death?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in a year other than the year of the stillbirth
- Claiming by someone other than the parent who delivered

## Prompt
- We lost our baby to stillbirth this year. Is there anything on my taxes?
