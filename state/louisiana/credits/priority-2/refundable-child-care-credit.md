# Refundable Child Care Credit (Federal AGI $25,000 or Less)
## Description
Refundable Louisiana credit equal to 50% of the federal child and dependent care credit, figured BEFORE the federal credit is limited by federal tax. Because low-income filers often owe no federal tax, Louisiana lets them benefit even when the federal credit is worth nothing to them.

## Amount
Louisiana credit = qualified expenses × federal percentage × 50%
- Expenses up to $3,000 (one qualifying person) or $6,000 (two or more), and no more than earned income (the lower-earning spouse's, if married)
- 2026 federal percentage (One Big Beautiful Bill Act): 50% at federal AGI of $15,000 or less, reduced one point for each $2,000 or part of $2,000 over $15,000. Within this band it runs from 50% down to 45% at $25,000
- Maximum Louisiana credit for 2026: $750 (one person) or $1,500 (two or more)

Example: AGI $18,000, one child, $3,000 of expenses. Federal percentage 48% → $3,000 × 48% × 50% = $720.

## Eligibility
- Louisiana resident with federal AGI of $25,000 or less
- Expenses would qualify for the federal credit (child under 13 or disabled dependent/spouse; care so you could work)
- Allowed even if no federal credit was actually claimed

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.4 (50% rate since 2007; unchanged for 2026)
- The 2025 worksheet printed lower federal percentages (35% falling to 30%); those are now wrong
- Above $25,000 the credit becomes nonrefundable (separate file)
- This credit is the base for the refundable Workforce Child Care Expense Credit (separate file)

## Required Information
- Federal AGI and earned income
- Qualifying persons and expenses paid
- Provider identification

## Questions
- Did you pay for child care so you could work or look for work?
- What was your federal AGI?
- How many children or dependents were in care?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 federal percentages
- Using the federal credit after the tax limit (often $0) instead of before it
- Not limiting expenses to earned income

## Prompt
- I earn about $20,000 and pay for daycare. Is there a Louisiana credit?
- How much is the Louisiana refundable child care credit?
