# Workforce Child Care — Fees and Grants to Resource and Referral Agencies (formerly School Readiness) (Code 68F)
## Description
Refundable credit for a business that pays fees or grants to child care resource and referral agencies (private agencies under contract with the Department of Education that help parents and providers).

## Amount
- Equal to the fees and grants paid, up to $5,000 per tax year
- Not subject to the $1 million cap that applies to code 67F

## Eligibility
- Business (including a sole proprietor or pass-through owner) paid an eligible agency in 2026

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6107(A)(2). Unchanged for 2026 apart from the program's new name
- LDR requires the agency receipt and any K-1 attached

## Required Information
- Agency name and amount paid
- K-1 share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business donate to a child care resource and referral agency?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $5,000
- Claiming a payment to a child care center (that is code 67F)
