# Funeral and Burial Expenses for a Pregnancy-Related Death (Code 77F)
## Description
Refundable credit for funeral and burial costs of a woman who died while pregnant or within one year after childbirth.

## Amount
- The LESSER of actual reasonable funeral and burial expenses or $5,000
- Claimed for the tax year of death
- Flowers, vaults, and urns do not count

## Eligibility
- Death occurred while pregnant or within one year of giving birth
- Claimed by the estate; if the estate does not claim it, by the person who paid the expenses

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.22 (its own section). Unchanged for 2026
- LDR requires the death certificate, expense schedule, and receipts attached

## Required Information
- Date of death and relationship to pregnancy
- Expenses paid and payer

## Questions
- Did a family member die during pregnancy or within a year after giving birth?
- Who paid the funeral and burial expenses?

## Common Errors
- Including flowers, vaults, or urns
- Both the estate and a family member claiming

## Prompt
- My daughter died after childbirth and we paid for the funeral.
- What is code 77F?
