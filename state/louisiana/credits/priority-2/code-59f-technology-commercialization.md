# Technology Commercialization (Code 59F)
## Description
Carryover-only code for the former credit for commercializing Louisiana technology.

## Status for 2026 — carryover only
- The program (R.S. 51:2351–2360) was repealed by Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2); LDR says the credit ended December 31, 2024
- A taxpayer with an eligible carryover may use this code for the rest of its 20-year carryover period

## Notes
- Whether any part is refundable is NOT confirmed; the 20-year carryover suggests unused amounts carry forward rather than refund
- Rare

## Required Information
- Original certified amount and year
- Amount used since

## Questions
- Do you have a certified technology commercialization credit with a remaining balance?

- Claiming a new 2026 credit
