# Musical and Theatrical Production (Code 62F)
## Description
Refundable, transferable credit for investment in qualified musical or theatrical productions and infrastructure in Louisiana.

## Status for 2026 — existing certifications only
No credits for applications received on or after July 1, 2025 (R.S. 47:6034(K)).

## Amount (certifications on or after July 1, 2017)
- Base investment: 7% ($100,000–$300,000), 14% ($300,000–$1 million), or 18% (over $1 million)
- Plus 7% of Louisiana resident payroll (pay above $1 million per person excluded)
- Limits: $1 million per project; $10 million statewide per fiscal year

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6034
- Transferable once; LDR must be notified in writing within 10 business days of a transfer
- Reported on Schedule F's transferable-credit lines with the state certification number, not as an ordinary code line

## Required Information
- Certification number and amount
- Transfer documents, if bought

## Questions
- Do you hold an LED-certified musical or theatrical production credit, or did you buy one?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the certification number
- Claiming a credit applied for after June 30, 2025
