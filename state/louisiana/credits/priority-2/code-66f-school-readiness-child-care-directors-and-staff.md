# Workforce Child Care Credit for Directors and Staff (formerly School Readiness) (Code 66F)
## Description
Refundable credit for child care directors and teaching staff at licensed, quality-rated facilities, based on their Louisiana Pathways career level. Renamed by Act 454 of the 2025 Regular Session; amounts are set by a separate CPI formula.

## Amount
Base amounts indexed each year by CPI-U, set by the Secretary of Revenue on December 1 of the prior year.

| Level | 2025 amount | 2026 estimate (× 1.027) |
|---|---|---|
| Director I / Teacher I | $2,192 | $2,251 |
| Director II / Teacher II | $2,921 | $3,000 |
| Director III / Teacher III | $3,653 | $3,752 |
| Director IV / Teacher IV | $4,381 | $4,499 |

2026 amounts: NOT YET PUBLISHED by LDR. Use the 2026 column only as a working estimate (2025 amounts increased by 2.7%, the inflation factor behind the 2026 retirement exemption and standard deduction). Replace with LDR's posted 2026 figures before filing.

## Eligibility
- Worked at least six months during 2026 at a licensed facility that participates in Quality Start
- Enrolled in the Louisiana Pathways Child Care Career Development System at a qualifying level

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6106 (Act 454 did not change the amounts or formula)
- The employer completes a state form for each person; LDR requires it attached, and the facility license number goes on Schedule F Line 5A

## Required Information
- Pathways level
- Facility license number
- Months worked

## Questions
- Do you work as a director or teacher at a licensed, rated child care center?
- What is your Louisiana Pathways level?
- Did you work there at least six months in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Using 2025 amounts for 2026
- Omitting the facility license number
- Claiming with less than six months of work
