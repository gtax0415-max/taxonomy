# Refundable Workforce Child Care Expense Credit (formerly School Readiness Credit; Federal AGI $25,000 or Less)
## Description
An additional refundable credit for each child under age six who attended a child care facility rated two stars or higher in Louisiana's Quality Start system. Act 454 of the 2025 Regular Session renamed the School Readiness Tax Credits the Workforce Child Care Tax Credits for 2026; the multipliers did not change.

## Amount
- Credit per child = the refundable child care credit × the facility's star percentage
- Each child is calculated separately; if a child attended more than one facility, use the highest-rated one
- Fully refundable at federal AGI of $25,000 or less

Example: refundable child care credit of $720, one child at a four-star center → $720 × 150% = $1,080 added.

## 2026 federal interaction
The base credit grows in 2026 because the federal child care percentage rose to 50% (see refundable-child-care-credit). That increase is multiplied here, so a five-star center can now add up to $1,500 per child.

## Eligibility
- Child under six during the year
- Facility participates in Quality Start with at least two stars
- Taxpayer has a refundable Louisiana child care credit

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6104
- The facility completes a state certificate; it is the taxpayer's proof but not a source document for this taxonomy

## Required Information
- Each child's age and facility
- Facility star rating and license number
- Refundable child care credit amount

## Questions
- Was your child under six and in a rated child care center in 2026?
- What star rating did the center have?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the facility's rating certificate
- Claiming for a child age six or older
- Using the 2025 base credit amount

## Prompt
- My child attends a four-star daycare and my income is low. Is there an extra credit?
- What is the workforce child care expense credit?
