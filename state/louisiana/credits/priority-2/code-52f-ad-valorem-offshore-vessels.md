# Ad Valorem Taxes on Offshore Vessels (Code 52F)
## Description
Refundable credit for local property tax paid on vessels operating in Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act waters.

## Amount
100% of the ad valorem tax paid; no cap. Any excess over tax is refunded.

## Status for 2026
Available; no sunset. Act 6 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session removed the franchise tax from the credit effective 2026, with no change for individuals.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6006.1
- Rare for individual filers; usually reached through a pass-through vessel owner

## Required Information
- Tax paid and certification
- Ownership share

## Questions
- Do you own (directly or through a business) a vessel taxed while in offshore federal waters?

## Common Errors
- Claiming tax on vessels not certified as in OCS waters
- Claiming the entity's full credit instead of your share
