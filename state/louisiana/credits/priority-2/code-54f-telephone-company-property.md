# Telephone Company Property (Code 54F)
## Description
Refundable credit for property tax a telephone company pays on its Louisiana public service property.

## Amount
- 40% of the ad valorem tax paid
- Paid from a dedicated state fund; if the fund falls short, the next year's credits are reduced pro rata

## Status for 2026
Available; no sunset. Act 5 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session (HB 2) removed only an old legislative-review requirement; Act 6 dropped the franchise tax effective 2026.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6014
- Individuals reach it only through an S corporation, partnership, or LLC that is a telephone company

## Required Information
- Tax paid on public service property
- Ownership share

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a telephone company that pays Louisiana property tax?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 100% instead of 40%
- Claiming tax on property other than public service property

- What is code 54F?
