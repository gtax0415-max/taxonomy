# Louisiana Earned Income Credit
## Description
Refundable credit equal to a percentage of the federal earned income credit (EIC). If you qualify for the federal EIC, you qualify for Louisiana's; Louisiana adds no tests of its own.

## Amount
- 2026: 5% of the federal EIC, rounded to the whole dollar
- The rate drops to 3.5% for tax years beginning in 2031
- Fully refundable

Example: federal EIC of $3,000 → Louisiana EIC of $150.

## Status for 2026 — still available
Two 2025 bills (SB 149 and HB 642) proposed repealing the credit for 2026. Neither passed, and the 2026 Regular Session made no change.

## Eligibility
- Louisiana resident (part-year residents prorate on the nonresident return)
- Eligible for the federal EIC for 2026

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:297.8 (a separate section, so not affected by the 2024 repeal of R.S. 47:297)
- A federal EIC denied or reduced by the IRS reduces the Louisiana credit too

## Required Information
- Federal EIC amount
- Residency status for the year

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal earned income credit?
- Were you a Louisiana resident all year?

## Common Errors
- Using 3.5% (that rate starts in 2031)
- Assuming the 2025 repeal bills passed
- Claiming without qualifying for the federal EIC

## Prompt
- I get the federal earned income credit. Does Louisiana have one?
- How much is the Louisiana EIC?
