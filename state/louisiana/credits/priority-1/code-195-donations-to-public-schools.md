# Donations to Public Schools (Code 195) — NEW for 2026
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to public schools graded D or F, for specified academic uses. Created by Act 423 of the 2025 Regular Session (SB 123) for 2026 and 2027.

## Amount
- Credit = 95% of the donation
- Statewide cap $1 million per calendar year, first-come, first-served by the date LDR receives a complete application; same-day requests over the cap are prorated, and the excess rolls to the next year
- No per-taxpayer or per-school cap
- Nonrefundable; no carryforward in the statute or LDR guidance
- Available for taxable periods beginning January 1, 2026; none earned for periods beginning on or after January 1, 2028

## Eligibility
- Recipient is a public school graded "D" or "F" in the most recent accountability year
- Donation is for instructional materials and classroom supplies, tutoring programs, in-school child care for student parents, school-based health clinics, or requirements for academically unacceptable schools
- Donor applied to LDR and received approval

## 2026 filing steps
- The school gives the donor a receipt when the donation is made
- Apply to LDR online between January 1 and February 28, 2027 for 2026 donations
- Claim the approved amount with code 195 and attach the approval letter

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6302; LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 25-031 (revised December 2025)
- LDR's 2025 legislative summary says 90%; the enacted law and the bulletin say 95%
- Code 195 is not on the 2025 form; confirm on the 2026 IT-540

## Required Information
- School name and letter grade
- Donation amount and use
- LDR approval amount

## Questions
- Did you donate to a public school in 2026?
- Was the school graded D or F?
- Did you apply to LDR by February 28, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Donating to an A, B, or C school
- Missing the application window
- Reporting it under code 399

## Prompt
- I donated to my local public school.
