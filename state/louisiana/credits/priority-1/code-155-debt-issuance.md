# Debt Issuance (Code 155)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for the State Bond Commission filing fee paid by an economic development corporation when it prepares and issues bonds.

## Amount
- Credit = 72% of the Bond Commission filing fee
- For 2026 the 72% rate is written into the statute itself (Act 6 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session), so it is no longer a temporary reduction from 100%

## Eligibility
- The fee was incurred by an economic development corporation issuing bonds under Title 33, Chapter 27
- An individual claims only a share passed through from such an entity

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6017. Amount confirmed for 2026
- Rare for individual filers

## Required Information
- Bond Commission fee receipt
- Owner's share from the K-1

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in an economic development corporation that issued bonds in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 100% of the fee
- Claiming bond issuance costs other than the Bond Commission filing fee

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a Bond Commission fee credit.
- What is credit code 155?
