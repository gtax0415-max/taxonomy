# Donations to Eligible Maternal Wellness Centers (Code 190)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to an eligible maternal wellness center, an organization that assists mothers with essential services and material support. Created in 2023 and first available for 2025; available for donations through December 31, 2030.

## Amount
- Credit = 50% of the donation
- Total credit in a year may not exceed 50% of the taxpayer's Louisiana tax liability
- Claimed only in the year the donation is made; no carryforward
- Statewide cap: $5 million of credits per calendar year, granted first-come, first-served through a Department of Revenue reservation
- No single center may account for more than 20% of the statewide credits

## Eligibility
- Donation made in 2026 to a center on the Louisiana Department of Health registry of eligible maternal wellness centers
- Credit reserved under the annual cap

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6111–6116 (credit in 47:6113). Unchanged for 2026
- The center completes the state's donation receipt; that state form is not a source document here, but the claim fails without it
- Once the $5 million cap is used, excess requests roll to the next year's cap

## Required Information
- Center name and confirmation it is registered
- Date and amount of each donation
- Reservation approval
- Louisiana tax liability, to apply the 50% limit

## Questions
- Did you donate to a maternal wellness center in 2026?
- Is the center on the state registry?
- Was the credit reserved under the annual cap?

## Common Errors
- Taking 50% of the donation without the 50%-of-tax limit
- Trying to carry an unused amount forward
- Claiming a donation to an unregistered center
- Also deducting the same donation as a charitable contribution without checking the interaction

## Prompt
- I donated to a pregnancy support center.
- What is the maternal wellness center credit?
