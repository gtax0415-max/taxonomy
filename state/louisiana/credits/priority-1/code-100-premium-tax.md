# Premium Tax (Code 100)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for Louisiana insurance premium tax paid by an insurer organized as a pass-through entity, allocated to its owners and claimed against their individual income tax.

## Amount
The owner's share of the premium tax paid, as allocated by the entity, limited to Louisiana tax remaining.

## Eligibility
- You own an interest in a pass-through insurer that paid Louisiana premium tax for 2026
- The entity allocated a share of that tax to you

## Notes
- Statutory authority and any 2026 change NOT confirmed in this review; verify before relying on this file
- Rare for individual filers; most will never use this code

## Required Information
- K-1 and the entity's premium tax statement
- Your ownership percentage

## Questions
- Do you own part of an insurance company taxed as a partnership or S corporation?
- Did it pay Louisiana premium tax and allocate a share to you?

## Common Errors
- Claiming premium taxes paid on your own insurance policies
- Claiming the entity's full premium tax instead of your share

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows Louisiana premium tax.
- What is credit code 100?
