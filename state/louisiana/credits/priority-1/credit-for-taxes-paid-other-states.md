# Credit for Net Income Taxes Paid to Other States
## Description
Nonrefundable priority 1 credit for a Louisiana resident who paid NET income tax to another state on income that Louisiana also taxes. Prevents the same income from being taxed twice. Not relevant to full-year residents whose income is all Louisiana-source.

## Amount
The credit is the SMALLER of:
- The net income tax actually imposed by and paid to the other state on that income, or
- Louisiana tax before credits × (Louisiana tax-table income attributable to the other state ÷ total Louisiana tax-table income)

At Louisiana's flat 3% rate for 2026, the Louisiana-side limit is roughly 3% of the double-taxed income, so a filer who paid a higher-rate state usually gets only part of that state's tax back.

## Eligibility
- Full-year or part-year Louisiana resident
- The income is taxed by BOTH Louisiana and the other state
- The tax was paid and reported to the other state on income computed under that state's own law
- No credit for tax paid to a state that itself gives nonresidents a credit for tax paid to their home state (a "reverse credit" state); there the other state, not Louisiana, gives relief

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:33. No change for 2026 identified in 2024–2025 legislation
- An owner may claim a share of an other-state entity-level tax based solely on net income (a PTET-type tax), if the related income is included in Louisiana income
- Tax WITHHELD by the other state is not the measure; use the final liability on that state's return after its own credits
- Line references follow the 2025 IT-540; the 2026 form is not yet released

## Required Information
- Other state's name and the income it taxed
- Net tax liability on the other state's return
- Louisiana tax-table income, total and attributable to that state
- Any entity-level tax passed through on a K-1

## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to another state on income that is also on your Louisiana return?
- Was that state's figure your final liability or just withholding?
- Did a partnership or S corporation pay tax to another state on your behalf?

## Common Errors
- Claiming tax withheld instead of the net tax liability
- Claiming the full other-state tax without the Louisiana ratio limit
- Claiming tax paid on income Louisiana does not tax
- Claiming credit for tax paid to a reverse-credit state

## Prompt
- I live in Louisiana but worked part of the year in Mississippi.
- I paid income tax to another state. How do I claim it on my Louisiana return?
