# Donations to Qualified Playgrounds (Code 150)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations of cash, equipment, goods, or services to a qualified playground in an economically depressed area.

## Amount
- Credit per donation = the LESSER of $720 or 36% of the value donated
- Total playground credits per taxpayer per year may not exceed $1,000
- Claimed only in the year the donation is made; no carryforward
- For 2026 the $720 and 36% figures are written into the statute (Act 6 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session), not temporary reductions

## Eligibility
- Donation made in 2026
- Recipient is a qualified playground under the statute

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:6008. Amounts confirmed for 2026
- A $2,000 donation gives $720 (36% would be $720); a $3,000 donation still gives $720

## Required Information
- Date, recipient, and value of each donation
- Proof the playground qualifies

## Questions
- Did you donate to a playground in 2026?
- Was it cash or goods/services, and what was the value?
- Is the playground in a designated economically depressed area?

## Common Errors
- Taking 36% with no $720 per-donation limit
- Missing the $1,000 yearly total limit
- Trying to carry an unused credit to 2027

## Prompt
- I donated playground equipment to a neighborhood park.
- What is credit code 150?
