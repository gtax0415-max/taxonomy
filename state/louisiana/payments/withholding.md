# Louisiana Tax Withheld
## Description
Total Louisiana income tax withheld from wages, pensions, and other payments during 2026.

## Rules
- Enter only LOUISIANA tax withheld; federal and other states' withholding do not belong here
- Attach copies of every W-2 and 1099 that shows Louisiana tax withheld (paper filers: paperclip, don't staple)
- 2026 employer withholding tables reflect the 3% flat rate and the inflation-adjusted standard deduction ($12,875 single or married filing separately; $25,750 joint, qualifying surviving spouse, or head of household), per LDR Revenue Information Bulletin 26-005

## Notes
- Because 2026 tables were updated in January 2026, withholding may differ from 2025 even at the same pay
- Line number is from the 2025 IT-540; confirm on the 2026 form

## Required Information
- Louisiana withholding from each W-2 and 1099

## Questions
- Do you have W-2s or 1099s showing Louisiana tax withheld?
- Did any employer withhold for another state by mistake?

## Common Errors
- Entering federal withholding
- Entering another state's withholding
- Forgetting to attach the W-2s and 1099s

## Prompt
- Where do I put the Louisiana tax withheld from my paycheck?
