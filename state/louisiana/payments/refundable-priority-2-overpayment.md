# Refundable Credits Larger Than the Tax
## Description
When refundable (priority 2) credits are more than the tax remaining after nonrefundable priority 1 credits, the excess is carried down to the payments section and refunded.

## Rules
- Refundable credits first reduce tax remaining after priority 1 credits
- Any excess becomes a payment on Line 25 and is refunded or credited forward like any overpayment
- Nonrefundable priority 3 credits cannot create an excess; they stop at zero tax

## Notes
- Line numbers are from the 2025 IT-540; confirm on the 2026 form
- 2026 changes in the refundable credits themselves (for example, the larger refundable child care credit) increase how often this line is used

## Required Information
- Tax after priority 1 credits
- Total refundable credits

## Questions
- Are your refundable credits larger than your Louisiana tax?

## Common Errors
- Letting nonrefundable credits produce a refund
- Counting the excess twice (as both a credit and a payment)

## Prompt
- My credits are more than my tax. Do I get the difference back?
