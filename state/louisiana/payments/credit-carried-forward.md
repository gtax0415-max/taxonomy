# Credit Carried Forward from 2025
## Description
The part of your 2025 overpayment you chose to apply to 2026 tax instead of receiving as a refund.

## Rules
- Use the amount LDR actually applied, which can differ from the 2025 return if LDR adjusted it (for example, to pay a debt)
- The carried-forward amount is treated as a 2026 payment

## Notes
- Line number is from the 2025 IT-540; confirm on the 2026 form

## Required Information
- 2025 IT-540 Line 37
- Any LDR adjustment notice

## Questions
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?
- Did LDR change that amount?

## Common Errors
- Entering the 2025 refund instead of the amount credited forward
- Ignoring an LDR adjustment

## Prompt
- I applied last year's refund to this year. Where does it go?
