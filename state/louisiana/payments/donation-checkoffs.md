# Donation Checkoffs (Schedule D) and Additional Donations
## Description
Taxpayers can donate part of a refund to listed funds on Schedule D, or pay extra donations to three funds along with the return.

## Schedule D funds (from the overpayment)
2025 list, carried into 2026:
- Military Family Assistance Fund
- Coastal Protection and Restoration Fund
- START Program
- Wildlife Habitat and Natural Heritage Trust Fund
- Louisiana Cancer Advisory Board
- Louisiana Food Bank Association
- Make-A-Wish Foundation of the Texas Gulf Coast and Louisiana
- American Red Cross
- Louisiana National Guard Honor Guard for Military Funerals
- Dreams Come True, Inc.
- Sexual Trauma Awareness and Response (STAR)
- Maddie's Footprints

## 2026 changes
- Restored for 2026 (Act 404 of the 2026 Regular Session): Louisiana Coalition Against Domestic Violence and Holden's Hope. A fund is now dropped only if it receives under $5,000 for two consecutive years
- Added from 2027, NOT 2026 (Act 400 of the 2026 Regular Session): Grambling University and Louisiana Tech University alumni associations

## Additional donations (paid with the return)
Lines 40 to 42: Military Family Assistance Fund, Coastal Protection and Restoration Fund, Louisiana Food Bank Association. Payment must be included with the return and cannot be refunded later.

Example: $25 of a refund to the Louisiana Food Bank Association.

## Notes
- Schedule D line numbers will shift on the 2026 form with the two restored funds; line numbers here are from the 2025 form

## Required Information
- Fund and amount for each donation

## Questions
- Do you want to donate part of your refund?
- Do you want to make an additional donation with your payment?

## Common Errors
- Donating more than the overpayment
- Listing the 2027-only alumni funds on a 2026 return

## Prompt
- Which funds can I donate to on my Louisiana return?
