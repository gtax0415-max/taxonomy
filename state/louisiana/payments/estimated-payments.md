# Estimated Payments for 2026
## Description
Louisiana estimated tax paid during 2026, and the rules for who must pay and the underpayment penalty.

## Who must pay
Anyone whose expected 2026 Louisiana tax, after credits and withholding, is more than $1,000 (single) or $2,000 (joint).

## 2026 due dates
- April 15, 2026
- June 15, 2026
- September 15, 2026
- January 15, 2027 (not needed if the return is filed and fully paid by January 31, 2027)
- Farmers and fishermen: one payment by January 15, or none if the return is filed and paid by March 1

## NEW for 2026 — lower safe harbor (Act 307 of the 2026 Regular Session)
- Required annual payment is the LESSER of 70% of 2026 tax (was 90%) or 100% of 2025 tax
- Each installment is 25% of that amount
- The underpayment period now runs to the 15th day of the fifth month after year-end, matching the May 15 due date
- Applies to taxable periods beginning on or after January 1, 2026

## Penalty
12% per year on each installment shortfall for the time it stays unpaid, figured on LDR's underpayment form; waivers may be requested within one year of the return due date.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:116 and 47:118
- Per the Act 307 digest, the annualized-income method is repealed for 2026; confirm on the 2026 underpayment form
- Line number is from the 2025 IT-540

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each payment
- 2025 total Louisiana tax

## Questions
- Did you make Louisiana estimated payments for 2026?
- When was each made?
- Did you owe more than $1,000 ($2,000 joint) after withholding?

## Common Errors
- Using the old 90% safe harbor
- Including a payment made with the extension instead of the extension line
- Including federal estimated payments

## Prompt
- I made quarterly Louisiana tax payments this year.
- Do I owe a penalty for underpaying estimated tax?
