# Interest and Penalties (Not Figured: Billed)
## Description
Interest and penalties that can apply to a 2026 return: the estimated-tax underpayment penalty, interest on late tax, and late-filing and late-payment penalties.

## Firm policy
These lines are left blank and the Department of Revenue bills them (penalty exclusion policy, October 6, 2026). LDR's instructions tell filers to compute them with worksheets; whether LDR always computes and bills amounts left blank is NOT confirmed in LDR's published guidance.

## 2026 rules
- Underpayment penalty: 12% per year on each installment shortfall (R.S. 47:118). For 2026, Act 307 of the 2026 Regular Session lowers the safe harbor to 70% of current-year tax (or 100% of 2025 tax) and, per the Act's digest, repeals the annualized-income method. LDR's underpayment form for 2026 had not been updated at review
- Interest: judicial rate plus 3 points, set each January (R.S. 47:1601). 10.5% for 2026; the 2027 rate is not yet published
- Late filing: 5% of the tax for each 30 days or part, up to 25% (R.S. 47:1602)
- Late payment: 0.5% per 30 days, up to 25%
- Combined late penalties capped at 25% of the tax
- The automatic extension to November 15, 2027 protects against the late-filing penalty only if the return is filed by then; it never extends time to pay
