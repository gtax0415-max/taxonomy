# Amount You Owe and Balance Due
## Description
When tax is more than payments and refundable credits, the difference is the amount owed. Donations from Schedule D, interest, late penalties, and the estimated-tax underpayment penalty are added to reach the balance due.

## How to pay
- Online through LaTAP by bank debit
- Credit or debit card through LDR's card processor (fee charged)
- Check or money order to the Louisiana Department of Revenue, with the last four digits of the SSN; e-filers use LDR's payment voucher
- Due by the original due date (May 15, 2027; likely May 17, 2027 because May 15 is a Saturday) even with an extension

## Interest and penalties (see penalties-and-interest file)
- Interest: judicial rate plus 3 points, reset each January. 2026 rate is 10.5%; the 2027 rate is not yet published
- Late filing: 5% per 30 days or part, up to 25%
- Late payment: 0.5% per 30 days, up to 25%
- Combined late penalties capped at 25% of the tax

## Notes
- 2026 donation checkoffs on Schedule D include two restored by Act 404 of the 2026 Regular Session (Louisiana Coalition Against Domestic Violence and Holden's Hope); donations increase the amount owed
- Line numbers are from the 2025 IT-540

## Required Information
- Total tax, payments, and any donations

## Questions
- Will you pay the balance by the due date?
- How will you pay?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the extension extends time to pay
- Forgetting donations add to the amount owed

## Prompt
- I owe Louisiana tax. How do I pay?
