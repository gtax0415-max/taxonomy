# Overpayment, Credit to 2027, and Refund
## Description
When payments and refundable credits exceed the tax, the overpayment is reduced by any estimated-tax underpayment penalty and any donations, and the rest is credited to 2027 tax, refunded, or split between the two.

## Order of the lines (2025 form numbering)
1. Line 32 — overpayment
2. Line 33 — underpayment penalty, if any
3. Line 34 — adjusted overpayment
4. Line 35 — donations from Schedule D (cannot exceed Line 34)
5. Line 36 — subtotal
6. Line 37 — amount credited to 2027 estimated tax
7. Line 38 — refund by paper check or direct deposit

## 2026 changes
- Act 404 of the 2026 Regular Session restored two Schedule D checkoffs for 2026: the Louisiana Coalition Against Domestic Violence and Holden's Hope. A checkoff is now removed only if it raises under $5,000 two years in a row
- Act 400 of the 2026 Regular Session adds Grambling and Louisiana Tech alumni checkoffs, but not until tax year 2027
- Underpayment penalty rules changed for 2026 by Act 307 (see estimated-payments)

## Notes
- First-time filers receive a paper check; others may choose direct deposit
- Line numbers are from the 2025 IT-540; confirm on the 2026 form

## Required Information
- Overpayment amount
- Donation choices
- Amount to credit to 2027

## Questions
- Do you want to apply any of your refund to 2027?
- Do you want to donate any of your refund?

## Common Errors
- Donations larger than the adjusted overpayment
- Forgetting the underpayment penalty reduces the refund
- Listing 2027-only checkoffs on the 2026 return
