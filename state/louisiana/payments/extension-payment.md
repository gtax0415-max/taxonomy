# Extension Payment
## Description
Payment made by the original due date toward 2026 tax for a return filed later under the extension.

## 2026 rules
- Original due date: May 15, 2027. May 15, 2027 is a Saturday, so the deadline should move to Monday, May 17, 2027 (confirm with LDR)
- Louisiana grants an AUTOMATIC six-month extension to file; no extension form is needed. For 2026 returns that runs to November 15, 2027
- The extension counts only if the return is filed within the extension period; otherwise the late-filing penalty runs from the original due date
- The extension does NOT extend time to pay; pay by the original due date online (LaTAP), with LDR's extension payment voucher, through software, or by card

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:103(D)
- Line number is from the 2025 IT-540

## Required Information
- Date and amount of the payment

## Questions
- Did you pay anything with your extension?
- Are you filing after the original due date?

## Common Errors
- Thinking the extension extends time to pay
- Reporting the extension payment as an estimated payment
