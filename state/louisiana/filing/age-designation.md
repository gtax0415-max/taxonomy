# Age Designation (65 or Older)
## Description
Boxes marking a taxpayer or spouse who is 65 or older. They no longer give an exemption amount; they identify who may take the 65+ retirement income exemption (code 06E).

## Rules
- Mark the box for each person age 65 or older by December 31, 2026 (or by the date of death)
- For 2026, that means born on or before January 1, 1962 (federal convention: you turn 65 the day before your 65th birthday)
- The 65, blind, and dependent exemptions were repealed by Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session

Example: a couple born in 1958 and 1960 marks both boxes.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- Each person's date of birth

## Questions
- What are your and your spouse's dates of birth?

## Common Errors
- Marking the box for a spouse who turns 65 in 2027
- Expecting an exemption amount for age

## Prompt
- What does the 65 or older box do now?
