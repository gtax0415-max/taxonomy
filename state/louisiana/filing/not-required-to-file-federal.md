# Not Required to File a Federal Return
## Description
How someone with Louisiana withholding but no federal filing requirement claims a refund.

## Rules
- Enter total wages and other income in the boxes above Line 7
- Mark the box for not required to file a federal return
- Enter 0 on the tax line (Line 13 on the 2025 form)
- Claim the Louisiana withholding as a payment and receive the refund

Example: a teenager with $3,000 of wages and $40 of Louisiana tax withheld enters the wages, marks the box, and gets $40 back.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026; line number from the 2025 form
- A first-time filer receives the refund as a paper check

## Required Information
- Wages and income
- Louisiana tax withheld

## Questions
- Are you required to file a federal return for 2026?
- Was Louisiana tax withheld?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the wages boxes blank
- Not attaching the W-2s

## Prompt
- How do I get my withholding back without a federal return?
