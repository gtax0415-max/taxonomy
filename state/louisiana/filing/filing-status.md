# Filing Status
## Description
Louisiana filing status must match the federal return. It sets the Louisiana standard deduction.

## Statuses and 2026 standard deduction
- 1 Single: $12,838
- 2 Married filing jointly: $25,676
- 3 Married filing separately: $12,838
- 4 Head of household: $25,676
- 5 Qualifying surviving spouse: $25,676

(2025: $12,500 / $25,000.) For status 4 or 5, enter the qualifying person's name if that person is not a dependent.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:294, indexed by CPI-U from 2026. Figures reflect the 2.7% adjustment also used for the 2026 retirement exemption; LDR's January 2026 withholding tables used preliminary amounts of $12,875 / $25,750. Confirm the final figures with LDR (the "RIB 26-019" cited earlier could not be located)

## Required Information
- Federal filing status

## Questions
- What filing status did you use on the federal return?

## Common Errors
- Using a status different from the federal return
- Using the 2025 standard deduction
