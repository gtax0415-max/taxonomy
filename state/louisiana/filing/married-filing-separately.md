# Married Filing Separately
## Description
Filing status 3, used when the federal return is married filing separately.

## Rules
- 2026 standard deduction: $12,838 (2025: $12,500)
- Enter the spouse's name and SSN
- Child care credits: a separate-return filer qualifies only if they lived apart from the spouse for the last six months of 2026, the child lived with them more than half the year, and they paid more than half the cost of the home (follows the federal Form 2441 rule)

Example: a spouse filing separately takes the $12,838 standard deduction.

## Notes
- $12,838 reflects the 2.7% CPI-U adjustment (R.S. 47:294); LDR's 2026 withholding tables used a preliminary $12,875. Confirm the final figure with LDR (the "RIB 26-019" cited earlier could not be located)
- Louisiana is a community property state; separate returns may need to split community income (see federal Pub. 555)

## Required Information
- Spouse identification
- Living arrangements, if claiming child care credits

## Questions
- Did you file separately on your federal return?
- Did you live apart from your spouse for the last six months of 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the child care credit without meeting the lived-apart test
- Ignoring community property allocation
