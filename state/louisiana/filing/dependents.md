# Dependents
## Description
List of dependents claimed on the federal return. Since 2025 they carry no Louisiana exemption amount, but they still matter for credits and deductions.

## Rules
- List up to eight dependents: first and last name, SSN, relationship, and date of birth; attach a statement for more
- Enter the total number claimed
- List only dependents claimed on the federal return
- No dependency exemption: Act 11 of the 2024 Third Extraordinary Session replaced personal and dependency exemptions with the standard deduction beginning 2025

## Why it still matters
- Child care credits, the workforce child care expense credit, and school expense deductions (17E, 18E, 19E) depend on the child being a dependent

Example: two children listed with SSNs and birth dates; the count is 2.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN, relationship, birth date

## Questions
- Whom did you claim as dependents on the federal return?

## Common Errors
- Listing a dependent not claimed federally
- Expecting a dependency exemption amount

## Prompt
- How many dependents can I list?
