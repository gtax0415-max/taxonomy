# Head of Household
## Description
Filing status 4, used when the federal return uses head of household. Louisiana gives it the same standard deduction as a joint return.

## Rules
- Must match the federal return
- Enter the qualifying person's name if that person is not a dependent
- 2026 standard deduction: $25,676 (200% of the single amount; 2025: $25,000)

Example: a single parent with a son files status 4 and takes $25,676.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:294. $25,676 reflects the 2.7% CPI-U adjustment; LDR's 2026 withholding tables used a preliminary $25,750. Confirm the final figure with LDR (the "RIB 26-019" cited earlier could not be located)
- Unlike federal law, Louisiana's head of household deduction equals the joint amount
- Louisiana is a community property state: a married filer who qualifies as head of household (considered unmarried) may still have community income to split on the federal return unless the community ended (for example, retroactive to the filing of a divorce petition, La. Civil Code art. 159) or the spouses lived apart all year (federal Pub. 555)

## Required Information
- Qualifying person

## Questions
- Who is your qualifying person?
- Did you file head of household federally?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the qualifying person's name when that person is not a dependent
- Using the single standard deduction

## Prompt
- What standard deduction does head of household get?
