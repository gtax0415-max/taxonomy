# Qualifying Surviving Spouse
## Description
Filing status 5, used when the federal return uses qualifying surviving spouse. It gets the joint standard deduction.

## Rules
- Must match the federal return: generally a spouse who died in 2024 or 2025, a dependent child living at home, and no remarriage
- Enter the qualifying person's name if that person is not a dependent
- 2026 standard deduction: $25,676 (2025: $25,000)

Example: a widow whose spouse died in 2025, with a child at home, files status 5.

## Notes
- The 2026 standard deduction is indexed by CPI-U (R.S. 47:294). $25,676 reflects the 2.7% adjustment also used for the 2026 retirement exemption; LDR's January 2026 withholding tables used a preliminary $25,750. The "RIB 26-019" cited in the earlier draft could not be located; confirm the final figure with LDR

## Required Information
- Year of spouse's death
- Qualifying child

## Questions
- In what year did your spouse die?
- Does a dependent child live with you?

## Common Errors
- Filing single when the federal return used qualifying surviving spouse
- Using status 5 in the year of death (file jointly instead)
