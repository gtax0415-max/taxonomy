# Federal Conformity and 2026 Law
## Description
Louisiana starts from federal adjusted gross income, so federal changes that affect AGI flow through automatically. Federal deductions taken after AGI do not reach the Louisiana return.

## 2026 federal changes and their Louisiana effect
- Flow through (affect AGI): changes to income items and above-the-line adjustments
- Do NOT reach Louisiana (taken after AGI): no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, the senior deduction, car loan interest, the non-itemizer charitable deduction (new for 2026), and the qualified business income deduction
- Louisiana reads only two below-AGI federal figures: the Schedule A medical deduction and the federal standard deduction (Lines 9A to 9C, per the 2025 form)
- Louisiana bonus depreciation (32E) is tied to federal law as of January 1, 2024, not the 2025 federal changes

## 2026 Louisiana changes (see the individual files)
- Standard deduction and 65+ retirement exemption indexed for inflation ($12,838 / $25,676 and $12,324; confirm final figures)
- New: ABLE deposit exclusion (Act 110 of 2025); terminal-illness hardship distribution deduction (Act 251 of 2025); public school donation credit, code 195 (Act 423 of 2025); work-based learning credit, code 467 (Act 376 of 2025)
- Changed: retrofit deduction raised to $10,000 (Act 473 of 2025); workforce child care credits renamed and business credit rates raised (Act 454 of 2025); S corporations pass-through by default (Act 382 of 2025); estimated tax safe harbor lowered to 70% (Act 307 of 2026); two refund checkoffs restored (Act 404 of 2026)
- Ended: digital nomad exemption (29E), youth jobs and apprenticeship credits (462, 463) for new activity
- Earned income credit stays at 5% (repeal bills failed)

Example: federal AGI $96,000 with no Schedule E items → $96,000 on Line 7, even if the filer took the federal overtime deduction.

## Required Information
- Federal AGI as filed
- Any new federal below-AGI deductions taken (to confirm they are not carried to Louisiana)

## Questions
- What is your federal AGI?
- Did you take any of the new federal deductions? (They don't carry to Louisiana)

## Common Errors
- Taking the federal tips or overtime deduction on the Louisiana return
- Assuming Louisiana follows 2025 federal depreciation changes

## Prompt
- Do the new federal deductions reduce Louisiana tax?
