# Full-Year Resident
## Description
Form IT-540 is the return for people who were Louisiana residents all year. Residents are taxed on all income, wherever earned.

## Rules
- Full-year residents file IT-540
- Nonresidents and part-year residents file Form IT-540B (outside this taxonomy)
- Income taxed by another state may qualify for the credit for taxes paid to other states

Example: a family living in Lafayette all year files IT-540.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026
- Military members keep their home-state residency under federal law regardless of where they are stationed

## Required Information
- Domicile during 2026

## Questions
- Did you live in Louisiana all of 2026?
- Did you earn income in another state?

## Common Errors
- Filing IT-540 as a part-year resident
- Leaving out income earned in another state

## Prompt
- Which Louisiana return do I file?
