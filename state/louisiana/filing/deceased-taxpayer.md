# Deceased Taxpayer or Spouse
## Description
How the return is marked when the taxpayer or spouse died during 2026.

## Rules
- Mark the Decedent Filing box (taxpayer) or Spouse Decedent box and enter the date of death
- Attach a copy of the death certificate
- When a refund is due to the estate, LDR's refund-claim form for a deceased taxpayer is also required (a state form, not a source document here)
- A surviving spouse may file jointly for the year of death, as on the federal return
- Age tests (such as 65 for code 06E) are applied as of the date of death

Example: a spouse died September 14, 2026 → mark Spouse Decedent with that date and file jointly.

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026
- A pregnancy-related death may qualify for funeral expense credit 77F

## Required Information
- Date of death
- Who is filing and in what capacity

## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die during 2026?
- Who is signing for the decedent?

## Common Errors
- Testing age 65 at December 31 for someone who died before the birthday
- Omitting the death certificate

## Prompt
- How do I file for a spouse who died?
