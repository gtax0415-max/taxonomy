# Consumer Use Tax
## Description
Use tax owed on items bought from sellers that did not charge Louisiana sales tax, such as some online or out-of-state purchases.

## Amount
- 9% of taxable purchases not taxed by the seller (5% state + 4% local, regardless of the actual parish rate)
- One of two boxes must be marked: no use tax due, or the amount from the worksheet

Example: $800 of untaxed online purchases → $72.

## 2026 rules
State sales and use tax rate is 5% (raised from 4.45% beginning January 1, 2025), so the 9% worksheet rate continues for 2026.

## Notes
- Authority: R.S. 47:302(K) (combined state and local rate for individuals)
- Purchases already reported on LDR's separate consumer use tax return are left out
- LDR's consumer use tax web page still shows an older 8.45% rate; the 2025 instructions and the 2026 consumer use tax form use 9%
- Whether credit is allowed for sales tax paid to another state on the same item was not confirmed

## Required Information
- Total untaxed purchases

## Questions
- Did you buy goods online or out of state without paying Louisiana sales tax?

## Common Errors
- Leaving both boxes blank
- Using 8.45% or the actual parish rate

## Prompt
- What is Louisiana consumer use tax?
