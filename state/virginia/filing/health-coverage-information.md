# Health Coverage Information Consent and Schedule HCI (Form 760 header)
## Description
Form 760 page 1 carries an oval reading "I/we authorize the sharing of certain information from Form 760 and Schedule HCI with the Dept. of Medical Assistance Services (DMAS), the Dept. of Social Services (VDSS), and the Virginia Health Benefit Exchange (VHBE)." Filling it in is voluntary. The agencies use the information to contact you about eligibility for medical assistance services or to newly enroll in a qualified health plan through the exchange. It has no effect on the tax computed.
## What is shared
If the individual (or spouse on a joint return) consents, the Department may disclose the name and date of birth of listed dependents and the individual's or spouse's:
- name, address and Social Security number
- email address and date of birth
- number and type of personal exemptions
- tax-filing status
- adjusted gross income
The Department discloses only under a written agreement with DMAS, VDSS and VHBE.
## Schedule HCI (Health Care Information Schedule)
Complete Schedule HCI and enclose it with the return (Form 760 or 760PY) when you fill in the consent oval. It asks for:
- Name(s) as shown on the Virginia return and your SSN
- Preferred method of contact, by oval: Email (with your email address), Spouse's Email, Phone (daytime number), or Mail (give an address only if different from page 1 of the return)
- Dependents: first name, last name and birth date, with space for 10
## Questions
- Would you like Virginia to share your return information with Medicaid, Social Services or the health insurance exchange so they can contact you about coverage?
- Do you, your spouse or your dependents lack health coverage?
- How do you prefer to be contacted: email, phone or mail?
- What are your dependents' names and birth dates?
## Common Errors
- Filling in the consent oval but not enclosing Schedule HCI
- Enclosing Schedule HCI without filling in the oval on Form 760
- Assuming consent affects the tax or refund; it does not
- Leaving the contact method blank
