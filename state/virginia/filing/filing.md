# Filing (Virginia Form 760 filing rules and header)
## Description
This folder covers the filing attributes of a Virginia resident return that decide whether, how and when it is filed and which amounts apply:
- who must file, and which Virginia form to use (residency)
- filing status, including a deceased spouse
- the dependent oval and its limit on the standard deduction
- due dates and extensions, and amended returns

Form 760 is the return for full-year residents, and for part-year residents whose income for the whole year was from Virginia sources. Other part-year residents use Form 760PY and nonresidents Form 763.
## How the filing items flow
- Residency decides the form: Form 760, Form 760PY or Form 763 (and Form 763-S for a refund of tax withheld from exempt income)
- Form 760, Lines 1-9 produce Virginia adjusted gross income (VAGI); Line 9 is compared with the filing threshold ($11,950 for Filing Status 1 or 3, $23,900 for Filing Status 2)
- Filing status (1, 2 or 3) sets the filing threshold, the standard deduction (Line 11) and the Spouse Tax Adjustment (Line 17)
- The dependent oval limits the standard deduction to earned income
- The amended return oval and reason code, and the overseas on due date oval, change the filing and payment deadlines
## General rules for every return
- Round every amount to the nearest dollar: under 50 cents down, 50 to 99 cents up
- Paper returns: use black ink, not pencil, and do not use staples. Enclose the original Form 760, Schedule ADJ, Schedule VAC, Schedule OSC and Schedule CR (no photocopies of these); photocopies of other supporting documents are acceptable
- Frequent enclosures:
  - Forms W-2, 1099 and VK-1 showing Virginia withholding
  - Virginia Schedule A, Schedule ADJ, Schedule ADJS
  - Schedule VAC, Schedule OSC, Schedule CR
  - Form 760C or Form 760F, and Virginia credit schedules
- Before starting, have the completed federal return, W-2, 1099 and VK-1 forms, and any other state returns if claiming the credit for tax paid to another state
- If you e-file, do not send a paper copy; Virginia Tax does not acknowledge receipt of paper returns or payments
- Keep copies of the return and all supporting documents for 3 years
## 2026 dates
The 2026 return is due May 1, 2027; because that is a Saturday, filing and paying by Monday, May 3, 2027 is timely. The overseas deadline is July 1, 2027, and the automatic extension runs to November 1, 2027.
