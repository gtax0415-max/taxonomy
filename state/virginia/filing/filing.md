# Filing (Virginia Form 760 header, footer and filing rules)
## Description
This folder covers everything about a Virginia resident return that is not a dollar line: who must file, which Virginia form to use, filing status, due dates and extensions, amended returns, the header ovals and identification boxes, the locality code, the dependent oval, the health coverage consent and Schedule HCI, and the page 2 footer (signatures, preparer authorization, Form 1099-G election, ID Theft PIN, filing election code and PTIN). Form 760 is the return for full-year residents (and for part-year residents whose income for the whole year was from Virginia sources); part-year residents use Form 760PY and nonresidents Form 763.
## How the filing items flow
- Residency decides the form: Form 760, Form 760PY or Form 763 (and Form 763-S for a refund of tax withheld from exempt income)
- Form 760, Lines 1-9 produce Virginia adjusted gross income (VAGI); Line 9 is compared with the filing threshold ($11,950 for Filing Status 1 or 3, $23,900 for Filing Status 2)
- Page 1 header: names, SSNs, first four letters of last name, birth dates, Deceased oval, locality code, address change oval, driver's license, health coverage consent oval, the "fill in all ovals that apply" block, filing status box, federal head of household oval, amended return reason code
- Page 2 footer: direct deposit, preparer authorization oval, Form 1099-G oval, signatures and phones, ID Theft PIN, preparer name, firm, phone, filing election code and PTIN
## General rules for every return
- Round every amount to the nearest dollar: under 50 cents down, 50 to 99 cents up
- Paper returns: use black ink, not pencil; do not use staples; enclose the original Form 760, Schedule ADJ, Schedule VAC, Schedule OSC and Schedule CR (no photocopies of these); photocopies of other supporting documents are acceptable
- Frequent enclosures: Forms W-2, 1099 and VK-1 showing Virginia withholding, Virginia Schedule A, Schedule ADJ, Schedule ADJS, Schedule VAC, Schedule OSC, Schedule CR, Form 760C or Form 760F, Virginia credit schedules
- Before starting, have the completed federal return, W-2, 1099 and VK-1 forms, and any other state returns if claiming the credit for tax paid to another state
- If you e-file, do not send a paper copy; Virginia Tax does not acknowledge receipt of paper returns or payments
- Keep copies of the return and all supporting documents for 3 years
