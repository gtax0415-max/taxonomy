# Due Dates, Extensions and the Overseas Oval (Form 760 header)
## Description
A calendar year 2026 Form 760 is due May 1, 2027. Because May 1, 2027 falls on a Saturday, a return filed and paid by Monday, May 3, 2027 is timely. Confirm the date the 2026 instructions print.

Virginia grants every taxpayer an automatic 6-month extension to file with no application. The extension is for filing only: at least 90% of the tax must be paid by the due date to avoid penalty. Taxpayers living or traveling outside the United States and Puerto Rico on the due date get until July 1, 2027 and fill in the "Overseas on due date" oval.
## Due dates
- Calendar year: May 1, 2027. If a due date falls on a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday, file and pay by the next business day without penalty or interest; for 2026 returns that is May 3, 2027.
- Fiscal year: postmarked by the 15th day of the 4th month after the fiscal year ends. Write "Fiscal Year Filer" across the top of page 1 and enclose a statement giving the beginning and ending months.
- Outside the U.S. or Puerto Rico (including military or naval service on duty outside them): July 1, 2027. Fill in the overseas on due date oval and enclose a statement explaining your situation.
- Mail: the envelope must be postmarked by the due date with correct postage; a return sent back for insufficient postage and remailed late is liable for penalties and interest. Commercial delivery counts as timely if the envelope bears a confirmation of shipment on or before midnight of the due date.
## Automatic extension
- 6 months, no application. The automatic extension provisions apply to returns filed by November 1, 2027.
- Pay at least 90% of the tax by the due date to avoid penalty; pay tentative tax with Form 760IP or online. It is reported on Form 760, Line 22.
- If the tax due with an extended return is more than 10% of total liability, an extension penalty applies; a return filed more than 6 months late gets no extension and owes the late filing penalty (see payments/penalties-and-interest.md).
- Interest runs on tax due from the due date to the date filed even with an extension.
## Special extensions
- Foreign income exclusion: if you qualify for the federal exclusion and requested a federal extension, apply by letter on or before the 1st day of the 7th month after the taxable year ends; the extension runs 30 days after the date you expect to qualify. Enclose the approved federal extension with the return.
- Combat zone: the same filing and payment extensions the IRS grants plus 15 days, or a 1-year extension, whichever is later. Spouses get the same extensions. Write "Combat Zone" across the top of the return, the envelope, and any correspondence answering a notice (Tax Bulletin 05-5).
- Deployed outside the U.S. (armed services): extension expires 90 days after deployment ends; write "Overseas Noncombat" on top of the return.
## Required Information
- Tax year type (calendar or fiscal) and the planned filing date
- Payments made by the due date
- Whether the taxpayer was outside the U.S. on the due date, in a combat zone, or deployed
## Questions
- Will you file by the May 2027 due date, or use the automatic extension?
- Have you paid at least 90% of your 2026 tax by the due date?
- Were you outside the United States on the due date?
- Are you a service member in a combat zone or deployed overseas?
- Is your tax year a calendar year?
## Common Errors
- Treating the automatic extension as extra time to pay
- Filing after November 1 and expecting extension treatment
- Filling in the overseas oval without enclosing the explanatory statement
- Omitting "Combat Zone" or "Overseas Noncombat" on the return and envelope

- When is my Virginia return due this year?
- Do I need to file anything to get an extension in Virginia?
