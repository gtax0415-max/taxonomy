# Signatures, Preparer Section and Footer Elections (Form 760 page 2)
## Description
The bottom of Form 760 page 2 holds two ovals (preparer authorization and electronic Form 1099-G), the declaration and signatures, phone numbers, the ID Theft PIN box, and the paid preparer section with the Filing Election code and PTIN.
## Ovals
- "I (We) authorize the Dept. of Taxation to discuss this return with my (our) preparer." The Department may then answer your preparer's inquiries without contacting you separately for authorization.
- "I agree to obtain my Form 1099-G at www.tax.virginia.gov." Mark it to get Form 1099-G/1099-INT electronically instead of by mail. To switch back after choosing electronic delivery, send a written request to the Department. The statement, issued each January, reports payments made or credited in the prior calendar year; itemizers need it for their federal return, and it also goes to those receiving interest payments of $10 or more.
## Signatures
Sign and date the return; on a joint return both spouses sign. Filing jointly makes you jointly and severally liable for the tax, and refunds are paid jointly. Enter your phone numbers.
## ID Theft PIN
If the Department learns your personal information was compromised it may assign a 7-character PIN. New PINs are mailed automatically each November or early December. A PIN is active only for its calendar year: use the calendar year 2027 PIN on the 2026 return filed in 2027, and the same PIN on a 2025 return filed in 2027. Enter all letters and numbers. Do not use an expired PIN or an IRS PIN. Lost PIN: call 804.367.8031. Identity theft line: 804.404.4185.
## Paid preparer
- Name, firm name, phone number and PTIN. A preparer with primary responsibility for the substantive accuracy of the return must include the PTIN (taxable years on or after January 1, 2019).
- E-file mandate: anyone who prepares, or employs people to prepare, 50 or more individual returns for compensation must e-file them all. Volunteers preparing returns for the elderly or poor in a nonprofit program are not preparers for this rule. A hardship waiver is requested on Form EFW.
- Filing Election code, required of paid preparers:
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 2 | Taxpayer opted out of electronic filing |
| 3 | Preparer prepares less than 50 returns annually |
| 4 | Preparer capable of electronic filing, but return cannot be accepted electronically |
| 5 | Preparer has a hardship waiver |
| 6 | Preparer capable of electronic filing, but not yet approved as electronic return originator by IRS |
## Questions
- May the Department talk to your preparer about this return?
- Do you want your Form 1099-G online instead of by mail?
- Did Virginia Tax send you an ID Theft PIN?
- (Preparer) Why is this return being filed on paper?
## Common Errors
- One spouse not signing a joint return
- Using an IRS Identity Protection PIN or last year's Virginia PIN
- Paid paper return with no Filing Election code or no PTIN
- A preparer over the 50-return threshold filing on paper without a waiver
