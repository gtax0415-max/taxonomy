# Return Header Ovals and Identification (Form 760 header)
## Description
The top of Form 760 page 1 collects identity details and a block of ovals to "fill in all that apply." This file covers the name or filing status changed oval, the Virginia return not filed last year oval, the federal Schedule C oval, the federal Earned Income Credit oval and amount, the Deceased oval, the address change oval, birth dates, SSNs and the driver's license boxes. The other header ovals (dependent, qualifying farmer, amended return, overseas on due date, health coverage consent) have their own files.
## Identification boxes
- Name and mailing address; Filing Status 3 filers put the spouse's name beside the Filing Status box, not in the spouse name field
- SSN and the first four letters of the last name, for you and (Filing Status 2) your spouse. Disclosure of the SSN is mandatory under Va. Code 58.1-209
- Birth dates, in the same order as names and SSNs; used to verify identity (and needed for the age deduction)
- Address change: fill in the oval in the street address area if the address changed since you last filed
- Virginia driver's license: ID number and issue date, or from a Virginia ID card if no license; both spouses on a joint return. Optional, requested to combat identity theft and fraud
## The ovals
- Name or Filing Status changed: your name, your spouse's name or filing status differs from last year's Virginia return
- Virginia return not filed last year
- Federal Schedule C filed with the federal return
- Earned Income Credit on federal return: fill in the oval and enter the amount of the federal credit claimed. The Virginia refundable Earned Income Credit on Schedule ADJ is computed from the federal credit
## Deceased taxpayers
- Surviving spouse filing jointly: the survivor is the primary taxpayer. List your name, SSN and birth date first, your spouse's in the Spouse fields, and fill in the Deceased oval next to the spouse's birth date. The refund is payable to the surviving spouse and may be direct deposited; no other documentation is required.
- Single filer: the court-appointed or certified personal representative (executor or administrator) encloses a copy of the court certificate. The refund is payable to the estate, issued as a check, and cashed or deposited with the personal representative's endorsement.
- Joint filers, both deceased: follow the single filer rules.
- A refund is issued to the surviving spouse or the estate unless a properly completed federal Form 1310 is provided; when e-filing, Form 1310 must be included with the federal filing.
## Questions
- Did your name, your spouse's name, your filing status or your address change since last year?
- Did you file a Virginia return last year?
- Did you file a federal Schedule C, or claim the federal Earned Income Credit (how much)?
- Did your spouse, or the person whose return this is, die during or after the year?
- Do you have a Virginia driver's license or ID card handy?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the federal EIC amount blank after filling in the oval
- Listing a deceased spouse first on a joint return filed by the survivor
- Birth dates entered in a different order than the names
- Personal representative omitting the court certificate
## Prompt
- My husband passed away in October; how do I file our Virginia return?
- What is the Earned Income Credit oval on the Virginia form?
- I got married and changed my name this year.
