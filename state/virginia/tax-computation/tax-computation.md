# Tax Computation (Form 760, Lines 16-18, plus Lines 32-33)
## Description
This folder turns Virginia taxable income (Form 760, Line 15) into the net amount of tax on Line 18, and covers the amounts added to what is owed after payments: the addition to tax for underpaid estimated tax, the late filing, extension and late payment penalties and interest (Schedule ADJ, Lines 18-21 → Form 760, Line 32), and consumer's use tax (Line 33). Virginia uses a graduated rate schedule (2%, 3%, 5% and 5.75%) with a Spouse Tax Adjustment of up to $259 for joint filers.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 15  Virginia taxable income (Line 9 - Line 14)
Line 16  Tax from tax table or Tax Rate Schedule (whole dollars; $0 if VAGI is below the filing threshold)
Line 17  Spouse Tax Adjustment (Filing Status 2 only; spouse's VAGI entered in the box)
Line 18  Net amount of tax = Line 16 - Line 17
         → nonrefundable credits (Lines 23-25) are limited to Line 18
Line 32  Addition to tax, penalty and interest (Schedule ADJ, Line 21; oval if Form 760C or 760F enclosed)
Line 33  Consumer's use tax (oval if none due)
Line 34  Lines 29 + 30 + 31 + 32 + 33 → increases Line 35 or reduces Line 36
```
Schedule ADJ side: Line 18 addition to tax (Form 760C or 760F) + Line 19 penalty (late filing or extension oval) + Line 20 interest = Line 21 → Form 760, Line 32.
