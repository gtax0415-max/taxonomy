# Spouse Tax Adjustment (Form 760, Line 17)
## Description
Couples filing jointly (Filing Status 2) may reduce their tax by up to $259 with the Spouse Tax Adjustment (STA) if both spouses have taxable income and their combined taxable income on Line 15 is more than $3,000. Because Virginia uses the same brackets for all filers, the STA lets both incomes on a joint return benefit from the lower 2%, 3% and 5% brackets, so a joint return does not pay more than separate returns would. Enter the STA on Line 17 and the spouse's VAGI in the box on Line 17. The rate brackets (Va. Code § 58.1-320) and the $930 and $800 exemption amounts are unchanged for 2026, so the $259 cap is the same as in prior years.
## Step A: separate VAGI (Worksheet for Determining Separate VAGI)
- Step 1: split federal AGI by spouse (wages; interest and dividends; state refunds; business; gains; pensions and IRAs; rents, partnerships, estates, trusts; other income; less adjustments). Columns must total the joint federal AGI.
- Step 2: add each spouse's additions (Form 760, Line 2); subtract each spouse's age deduction (Line 4), Social Security and Tier 1 (Line 5), state refund (Line 6) and other subtractions (Line 7). The columns must total Line 9.
## Step B: Spouse Tax Adjustment Worksheet
1. Each spouse's separate VAGI.
2. Each spouse's personal exemptions: (65 or over + blind) x $800, plus $930.
3. Line 1 minus Line 2 for each. If either is 0 or less, stop; no STA.
4. Taxable income from Form 760, Line 15.
5. Smaller of the two Line 3 amounts. If it is over $17,000 and Line 4 is over $34,000, skip to Line 12 and enter $259.
6. Line 4 minus Line 5 (not below $0).
7. Line 4 divided by 2.
8. Tax on the smaller of Line 5 or Line 7.
9. Tax on the larger of Line 6 or Line 7.
10. Line 8 + Line 9.
11. Tax from Form 760, Line 16.
12. STA = Line 11 minus Line 10, not more than $259. Enter on Line 17.
The STA Calculator at www.tax.virginia.gov gives the same result.
## Example from the instructions
The Smiths have combined taxable income of $47,000: $35,000 and $12,000. Without the STA their tax is $2,445. The STA is $220, reducing their tax to $2,225.
## Required Information
- Each spouse's share of federal AGI, item by item
- Each spouse's share of Virginia additions, subtractions and the age deduction
- Whether each spouse is 65 or over or blind
- Joint Virginia taxable income (Line 15) and tax (Line 16)
## Questions
- Are you filing a joint Virginia return?
- How much of your joint income belongs to each of you?
- Does each spouse have income above his or her own exemptions?
- Is either of you 65 or over or blind?
## Common Errors
- Leaving Line 17 blank on a two-earner joint return and overpaying
- Omitting the spouse's VAGI from the box on Line 17
- Separate VAGI columns that do not add up to joint federal AGI or Line 9
- Claiming the STA when one spouse has no income above exemptions
- Claiming more than $259
## Prompt
- We both work; is there a break for filing jointly in Virginia?
- What goes in the spouse's VAGI box on line 17?
- Should we file separately in Virginia to save tax?
