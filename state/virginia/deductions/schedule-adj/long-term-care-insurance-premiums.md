# Long-Term Health Care Premiums (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 106)
## Description
A deduction for premiums paid for long-term health care insurance, available only if no federal deduction was claimed for long-term health care insurance premiums. It is all or nothing: if a federal deduction of ANY amount was claimed for these premiums during the year, the Virginia deduction is completely disallowed.
## Amount
The amount of long-term health care insurance premiums paid. No dollar cap is stated.
## Eligibility
- Premiums are for long-term health care insurance.
- No federal income tax deduction of any amount was claimed for long-term health care insurance premiums paid during the taxable year (for example, as a medical expense on federal Schedule A or through another federal deduction).
## Interaction with other lines
A federal itemizer who includes the premiums in federal medical expenses also carries them to Virginia Schedule A, Line 1; that taxpayer cannot use code 106. Compare the two treatments before filing federally.
## Questions
- Do you pay premiums for a long-term care insurance policy? How much in 2026?
- Did you include any of those premiums in medical expenses or any other deduction on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the premiums here after including any portion on the federal return
- Claiming only the part not deducted federally (the whole Virginia deduction is lost)
- Including ordinary health insurance premiums
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my long-term care insurance on my Virginia return?
- I didn't itemize federally. Can I still deduct long-term care premiums in Virginia?
