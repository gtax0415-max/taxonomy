# Schedule ADJ Deductions (Schedule ADJ, Lines 8a-9; Form 760, Line 13)
## Description
Virginia-only deductions taken from Virginia adjusted gross income, whether the taxpayer itemizes or takes the standard deduction. Each deduction is entered on Schedule ADJ, Lines 8a-8c as a 3-digit code followed by the amount. Line 9 totals them and carries to Form 760, Line 13. These are different from the Schedule ADJ subtractions (Lines 4-7, codes 20-99), which reduce income before VAGI.
## How the lines flow
- Schedule ADJ, Lines 8a, 8b, 8c = code + amount for up to 3 deductions
- More than 3: Schedule ADJS, Lines 23-32 (code and amount for each additional deduction), total on ADJS Line 33, included in Schedule ADJ Line 9; fill in the oval at the bottom of Schedule ADJ and enclose Schedule ADJS
- Schedule ADJ, Line 9 = total deductions → Form 760, Line 13 → Line 14 → Line 15
- Enclose Schedule ADJ with the return
## The loss box
Do not fill in the loss box unless claiming a negative bank franchise tax deduction (code 112) or a business interest deduction recovery (code 117).

## New or changed for 2026
- Code 118 (eligible educators) is reinstated for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026; it was not available for 2025.
- Code 119 (utility interconnect triggering project) applies for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026.
- Code 116 is 20% of business interest disallowed federally.
## Questions
- Did you pay child care that qualified for the federal credit?
- Did you contribute to a Commonwealth Savers (Invest529, Prepaid529, CollegeAmerica, CollegeWealth) or ABLEnow account?
- Do you pay long-term care insurance premiums?
- Are you a teacher or educator with unreimbursed expenses?
- Do you have more than three deductions (Schedule ADJS needed)?
