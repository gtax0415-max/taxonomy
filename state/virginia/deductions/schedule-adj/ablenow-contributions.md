# ABLEnow Account Contributions (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 115)
## Description
A deduction for amounts contributed during the year to an ABLEnow account (a tax-advantaged account for qualified disability expenses of eligible individuals with certain disabilities). Unlike the Commonwealth Savers education deduction, the contributor does not have to be the account owner of record.

2026 federal change: starting January 1, 2026, federal law (the ABLE Age Adjustment Act) lets people whose disability began before age 46 open ABLE accounts; the earlier cutoff was age 26. More taxpayers may contribute to ABLEnow accounts for 2026 as a result. The $2,000 Virginia limit is unchanged.
## Amount
- Up to $2,000 per ABLEnow account per year. Contributions over $2,000 are carried forward and deducted in later years until fully deducted, but never more than $2,000 per account in any year.
- A contributor who has attained age 70 may deduct the full amount contributed, less any amounts previously deducted.

Example: a parent under 70 contributes $5,000 to a child's ABLEnow account → $2,000 deduction in 2026, $2,000 in 2027 and $1,000 in 2028.
## Conditions
- No deduction if the contributions are deducted on the contributor's federal income tax return.
- Recapture: deductions are recaptured in the year distributions or refunds are made for any reason other than (i) paying qualified disability expenses or (ii) the beneficiary's death.
- Refund contributions directed to an ABLEnow account on Schedule VAC, Section I (program type code 5) are contributions; the contributor is eligible for the Virginia deduction associated with the account.
## Required Information
- Contributions to each ABLEnow account in 2026, including refund contributions through Schedule VAC
- Contributor's age at year end
- Undeducted contributions carried forward from earlier years
- Any distribution not used for qualified disability expenses
## Questions
- Did you contribute to an ABLEnow account in 2026? How much, to how many accounts?
- How old were you at the end of the year?
- Do you have unused contributions from earlier years?
- Were there any distributions not used for disability expenses?
## Common Errors
- Using code 104 for ABLEnow contributions
- Deducting more than $2,000 per account in one year when under 70
- Forgetting recapture for non-qualified distributions or refunds
## Prompt
- I put money into my daughter's ABLEnow account. Can I deduct it in Virginia?
