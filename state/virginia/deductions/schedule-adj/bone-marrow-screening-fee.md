# Bone Marrow Screening Fee (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 103)
## Description
A deduction for the fee paid for an initial screening to become a possible bone marrow donor. It is allowed only if the fee was not reimbursed and was not deducted on the federal return (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 6). The statute joins the two conditions with "or"; the instructions apply both, which is the safer reading.
## Amount
The amount of the fee paid for the initial screening. No dollar limit is stated.
## Eligibility
- The fee is for an INITIAL screening to become a possible bone marrow donor.
- Not reimbursed.
- Not claimed as a deduction on the federal return (for example, as a medical expense on federal Schedule A).
## Required Information
- Amount and date of the screening fee
- Any reimbursement
- Whether the fee was included in federal medical expenses
## Questions
- Did you pay to be screened as a bone marrow donor?
- Were you reimbursed by a registry, employer or insurer?
- Did you include the fee in medical expenses on your federal Schedule A?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a reimbursed fee
- Deducting the fee both here and in federal (and Virginia Schedule A) medical expenses
- Using code 103 for costs of an actual donation (living organ and tissue donor expenses are code 110)
## Prompt
- I paid to join the bone marrow registry. Can I deduct that in Virginia?
