# Organ and Tissue Donor Expenses (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 110)
## Description
A deduction for unreimbursed out-of-pocket expenses paid by a living organ or tissue donor that were directly related to the donation and arose within 12 months of it, and were not taken as a medical deduction on the federal return (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 13). The deduction may be taken in the year of the donation or in the year the 12-month period ends.
## Amount
| Return | Deduction |
|---|---|
| Single or separate return | lesser of $5,000 or the actual amount paid |
| Joint return | lesser of $10,000 or the actual amount paid (the statute sets $5,000 per donor; $10,000 applies when both spouses donated. Confirm this line against the 2026 instructions) |

Example: a single donor paid $6,200 of unreimbursed costs → $5,000 deduction with code 110.
## Eligibility
- The taxpayer is a LIVING organ and tissue donor.
- The expenses were paid by the donor and not reimbursed.
- The expenses were not taken as a medical deduction on the federal return.
## Required Information
- Date of the donation
- Unreimbursed expenses within 12 months of the donation, and which year they will be deducted
- Whether any expense was included in federal medical expenses
## Questions
- Did you donate an organ or tissue as a living donor in 2026?
- What costs did you pay yourself, and were any reimbursed?
- Did you include these costs in federal medical expenses?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the $5,000 ($10,000 joint) limit
- Deducting expenses already included in federal (and Virginia Schedule A) medical expenses
- Including reimbursed costs
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother. Can I deduct my costs in Virginia?
