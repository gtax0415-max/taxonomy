# Commonwealth Savers Account Contributions (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 104)
## Description
A Virginia deduction for contributions to Commonwealth Savers (formerly Virginia529) education savings accounts: Prepaid529, Invest529, CollegeAmerica and CollegeWealth. There is no federal deduction for these contributions. The deduction is per account, and only the account owner of record may claim it. ABLEnow contributions are a separate deduction (code 115).
## Amount
- Under age 70 on or before December 31, 2026: the lesser of $4,000 or the amount contributed during the year, for EACH account. Contributions over $4,000 per account are carried forward and deducted in later years until fully deducted.
- Age 70 or older on or before December 31, 2026: the entire amount contributed during the year.

Example: a parent under 70 who owns two Invest529 accounts and puts $6,000 into one and $2,500 into the other deducts $4,000 + $2,500 = $6,500 in 2026 and carries $2,000 forward for the first account.
## Who can claim
- Only the owner of record for an account may claim the deduction.
- A refund contribution made through Schedule VAC, Section I (Form 760, Line 30) is deemed a contribution to the account for the 2026 taxable year, and only the owner of record as of December 31 is eligible for the deduction associated with it.
## Interaction with other lines
- Distributions or refunds from these accounts may produce a Schedule ADJ subtraction (code 34); see income/subtractions/commonwealth-savers-distributions.md.
- CollegeWealth and Prepaid529 no longer accept new accounts; existing owners may continue contributing.
## Questions
- Which Commonwealth Savers accounts do you own, and how much went into each in 2026?
- Are you the owner of record (not only the beneficiary or a gift giver)?
- How old were you on December 31, 2026?
- Do you have undeducted contributions carried forward from earlier years?
- Did you direct part of your refund into an account on Schedule VAC?
## Common Errors
- Applying the $4,000 limit per taxpayer instead of per account
- A grandparent or other non-owner claiming the deduction
- Forgetting the carryforward of prior excess contributions
- Putting ABLEnow contributions under code 104 (use code 115)
