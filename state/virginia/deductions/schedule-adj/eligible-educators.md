# Deduction for Eligible Educators (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 118)
## Description
A deduction of up to $500 for eligible educator qualifying expenses. It applied for taxable years 2022 through 2024, was not available for 2025, and was reinstated for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026. Expenses already deducted on the federal return cannot be claimed.
## Amount
Up to $500 of qualifying expenses actually paid or incurred. Qualifying expenses are 100 percent of the amount paid or incurred during the year for:
- participation in professional development courses; and
- books, supplies, computer equipment (including related software and services), other educational equipment, and supplementary materials used directly in the individual's service to students as an eligible educator.
## Who is an eligible educator
An individual who, for at least 900 hours during the taxable year, served as a Virginia licensed teacher, instructor, student counselor, special needs personnel, principal or student aide for public or private primary and secondary school students in Virginia.
## Conditions
The purchases were neither reimbursed nor claimed as a deduction on the eligible educator's federal income tax return for the year. The booklet does not state whether the $500 is per individual or per return; on a joint return with two eligible educators, confirm before claiming more than $500.
## Questions
- Are you a Virginia-licensed K-12 teacher, counselor, aide, principal or similar, with at least 900 hours in 2026?
- What did you spend on classroom supplies, equipment or professional development?
- Were any purchases reimbursed, or deducted on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Claiming expenses already taken as the federal educator expense deduction
- Claiming with fewer than 900 hours of service, or for service outside Virginia
- Claiming more than $500
## Prompt
- I'm a teacher who buys my own classroom supplies. Is there a Virginia deduction this year?
- Did Virginia bring back the teacher expense deduction?
