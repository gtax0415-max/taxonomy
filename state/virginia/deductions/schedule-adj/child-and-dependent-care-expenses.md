# Child and Dependent Care Expenses (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 101)
## Description
Virginia has no child care credit; instead it allows a deduction equal to the child and dependent care expenses on which the federal credit is based. The amount is taken from federal Form 2441: the expenses that are multiplied by the federal credit's decimal amount, up to $3,000 for one qualifying dependent and $6,000 for two or more. It is not the federal credit amount.
## Amount
| Qualifying dependents | Maximum deduction |
|---|---|
| One | $3,000 |
| Two or more | $6,000 |

Enter the amount on federal Form 2441 that is multiplied by the decimal amount. Example: a family with two children in day care who paid $11,000 and had $6,000 of expenses used on Form 2441 deducts $6,000, not the federal credit of a fraction of that.
## Eligibility
- A federal child and dependent care credit computation (Form 2441) is the base; the Virginia figure follows the expenses that federal law allows for the credit.
- Enter code 101 and the amount on Schedule ADJ, Line 8a, 8b or 8c.
## Questions
- Did you file Form 2441 with your federal return?
- How many qualifying children or dependents were in care?
- What expense amount appears on Form 2441 before the decimal is applied?
## Common Errors
- Entering the federal credit instead of the expense amount (the booklet says DO NOT ENTER THE FEDERAL CREDIT AMOUNT)
- Entering total care costs above $3,000 or $6,000
- Using code 101 without a matching federal Form 2441 computation
## Prompt
- Does Virginia give anything for daycare costs?
- I got the federal child care credit. What goes on my Virginia return?
