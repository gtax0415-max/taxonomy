# Tobacco Quota Buyout (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 108)
## Description
A deduction for payments received in the PRECEDING year under the Tobacco Quota Buyout Program of the American Jobs Creation Act of 2004, to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income. The deduction runs one year behind: the 2026 return deducts qualifying 2025 payments that were in 2025 federal AGI, and 2026 payments may generate a deduction on the 2027 return (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 11).

2026 status: the federal buyout program paid its ten annual installments for fiscal years 2005 through 2014. Under that schedule there are no 2025 installments, and a lump sum received in 2014 or earlier finished its ten years of deductions by 2024. A 2026 deduction should therefore be rare or impossible. Keep the file in case the code still appears on the 2026 form, and confirm when any payment was actually received.
## Amount
- Installment payments: the gain from the installment received in the preceding year.
- Single payment: 10% of the gain recognized for federal purposes in the year the payment was received, deducted in the following year and in each of the 9 succeeding taxable years.
## Eligibility
- The payment was included in federal AGI.
- Individuals cannot claim a deduction for a payment that has been, or will be, subtracted by a corporation, unless the subtraction is shown on a Schedule VK-1 from an S corporation.
## Required Information
- Year and amount of each buyout payment, and whether it was a lump sum or installment
- For a lump sum, the federal gain recognized and the years already deducted
## Questions
- Did you receive Tobacco Quota Buyout payments in 2025? In installments or one lump sum?
- If a lump sum, in which year was it received, and how many of the ten years of deductions remain?
- Was the payment received through a corporation?
## Common Errors
- Deducting 2026 payments on the 2026 return (they belong on the 2027 return)
- Deducting the full lump-sum gain in one year instead of 10% a year
- Claiming a payment a corporation subtracted without a Schedule VK-1
