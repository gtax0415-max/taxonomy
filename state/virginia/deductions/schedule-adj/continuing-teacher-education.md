# Continuing Teacher Education (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 105)
## Description
A licensed primary or secondary school teacher may deduct 20% of unreimbursed tuition costs for continuing teacher education courses that are required as a condition of employment. The expenses must not have been deducted from federal adjusted gross income (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 9).
## Amount
20% of the unreimbursed tuition. Example: $3,000 of required, unreimbursed course tuition → $600 deduction with code 105. No dollar cap is stated.
## Eligibility
- Licensed primary or secondary school teacher.
- The courses are required as a condition of employment.
- Tuition is unreimbursed.
- The expenses were not deducted from federal adjusted gross income.
## Interaction with other lines
Code 118 (eligible educators, up to $500) separately covers professional development courses and classroom purchases. Neither code may include amounts deducted on the federal return, and code 118 excludes reimbursed amounts.
## Required Information
- Tuition paid for required courses in 2026
- Any reimbursement
- Whether any of the tuition was deducted federally
## Questions
- Are you a licensed K-12 teacher?
- Were the courses required by your employer to keep your position?
- How much tuition did you pay, and was any reimbursed?
- Did you deduct any of it on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Deducting 100% of tuition instead of 20%
- Including reimbursed tuition or elective courses not required for employment
- Claiming tuition already deducted in federal AGI
## Prompt
- I'm a teacher taking required recertification classes. Is there a Virginia deduction?
