# Business Interest Deduction Recovery (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 117)
## Description
Virginia allowed a deduction of 20% of business interest disallowed federally (code 116). If that interest is allowed in a later year for federal purposes as it is carried forward, the increased amount that was already allowed for Virginia must be recovered on the Virginia return for the year in which it is later allowed federally. The recovery is a NEGATIVE deduction.
## How to enter
Enter the amount being recovered as a negative number on Schedule ADJ, Line 8 with code 117 and fill in the loss box. It reduces total deductions on Line 9.

Example: a taxpayer deducted $10,000 under code 116 in an earlier year (20% of $50,000 disallowed). In 2026 the full $50,000 carryforward is allowed federally. The Virginia portion already deducted, $10,000, is entered as -$10,000 with code 117.
## Questions
- Was any previously disallowed business interest allowed on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you claim the code 116 deduction for that interest in an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Entering the recovery as a positive deduction or without the loss box
- Recovering more than the amount previously deducted for Virginia
- Not recovering at all, which double-deducts the interest
## Prompt
- My carried-forward business interest was finally allowed federally. What happens in Virginia?
