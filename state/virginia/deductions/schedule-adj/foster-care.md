# Foster Care Deduction (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 102)
## Description
Foster parents may deduct $1,000 for each child residing in their home under permanent foster care, as defined in the Code of Virginia. There is no federal equivalent. The child must be claimed as a dependent on both the federal and the Virginia return.
## Amount
$1,000 per child in permanent foster care. Example: two children in permanent foster care → $2,000 with code 102.
## Eligibility
- The child resides in the taxpayer's home under PERMANENT foster care as defined in the Code of Virginia.
- The foster parents claim the child as a dependent on their federal AND Virginia income tax returns (the child is also counted in Exemption Section A for $930).
## Questions
- Is the foster placement a permanent foster care placement?
- Do you claim the child as a dependent on your federal return?
- How many foster children lived with you?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for a temporary or short-term placement rather than permanent foster care
- Claiming the deduction without claiming the child as a dependent on both returns
## Prompt
- We're foster parents. Is there a Virginia deduction?
- Do I get anything for my permanent foster child on my state return?
