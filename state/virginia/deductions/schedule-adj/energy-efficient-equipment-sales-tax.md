# Sales Tax Paid on Certain Energy Efficient Equipment or Appliances (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 109)
## Description
A deduction of 20% of the sales tax paid on certain energy efficient equipment or appliances. It is a deduction, not a refund of the sales tax, and is capped per year.
## Amount
| Return | Maximum deduction |
|---|---|
| Single or separate return | $500 per year |
| Joint return | $1,000 per year (the statute sets $500 per individual; $1,000 applies when both spouses made qualifying purchases) |

Example: $150 of sales tax paid on qualifying equipment → a $30 deduction with code 109.
## Eligibility
- The purchase is of qualifying energy efficient equipment or appliances.
- The deduction is based on the SALES TAX paid, not the purchase price.
- The items are listed in Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 12, and must be bought for the taxpayer's own use: ENERGY STAR clothes washers, room air conditioners, dishwashers and standard-size refrigerators; qualifying fuel cells; gas heat pumps; electric heat pump water heaters; electric heat pumps; central air conditioners; advanced gas or oil water heaters; advanced oil-fired boilers and furnaces; and programmable thermostats. Each has an efficiency standard in the statute.
## Required Information
- Each qualifying item, purchase date and Virginia sales tax paid
- Which spouse made each purchase, on a joint return
## Questions
- Did you buy energy efficient equipment or appliances in 2026?
- How much sales tax did you pay on them?
## Common Errors
- Deducting 20% of the purchase price instead of 20% of the sales tax
- Exceeding the $500 ($1,000 joint) annual limit

- I bought an energy efficient appliance. Is there a Virginia tax break?
