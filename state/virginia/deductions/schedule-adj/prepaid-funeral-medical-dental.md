# Prepaid Funeral, Medical, Dental (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 114)
## Description
A deduction for older taxpayers who still work and have modest income. It covers payments for (i) a prepaid funeral insurance policy that covers the taxpayer, or (ii) medical or dental insurance premiums for any person for whom the taxpayer may claim a deduction for such premiums under federal income tax law.
## Eligibility (all three required)
| Test | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Age | 66 or older |
| Earned income | at least $20,000 for the year |
| Federal adjusted gross income | not more than $30,000 for the year |
## Not allowed for
Any portion of premiums for which the taxpayer has:
- been reimbursed;
- claimed a federal income tax deduction;
- claimed another Virginia income tax deduction or subtraction; or
- claimed a federal income tax credit or any Virginia income tax credit.
## Amount
The qualifying premiums paid, after removing the portions listed above. No separate dollar cap is stated.
## Questions
- Are you 66 or older?
- Did you earn at least $20,000 from work, and is your federal AGI $30,000 or less?
- Do you pay for a prepaid funeral policy or medical or dental insurance?
- Were any of those premiums reimbursed, deducted federally or used for a credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming at age 65 (the test is 66 or older)
- Claiming with federal AGI over $30,000 or earned income under $20,000
- Double-counting premiums already deducted federally, used for a federal or Virginia credit, or claimed under another Virginia deduction such as code 106

- I'm 67 and still working part time. Can I deduct my dental insurance in Virginia?
