# Business Interest Deduction (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 116)
## Description
Virginia conforms to the federal business interest limitation under IRC section 163(j), including the 2025 federal change affecting how much is disallowed. Virginia then lets individuals deduct 20% of the business interest disallowed for federal purposes (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 15). The percentage was 50% for 2024 and was cut to 20% for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2025 by the 2026 Amendments to the 2025 Appropriation Act (HB 29, Chapter 7).
## Amount
20% of the business interest disallowed on the federal return. Example: $50,000 disallowed on Form 8990 → $10,000 deduction with code 116.
## What to enclose
A copy of federal Form 8990.
## Interaction with other lines
When interest disallowed federally is later allowed federally as it is carried forward, the part already deducted for Virginia must be recovered using code 117 (a negative amount with the loss box). The statute does not spell out this recovery; it comes from the instructions, so confirm it on the 2026 form.
## Required Information
- Business interest disallowed on the 2026 Form 8990, including amounts passed through on K-1s
- Virginia deductions taken in earlier years on interest now allowed federally
## Questions
- Did you (or a pass-through entity you own) have business interest disallowed under section 163(j)?
- Do you have Form 8990?
- Are you tracking the Virginia portion already deducted, for later recovery?
## Common Errors
- Deducting 100% of the disallowed interest instead of 20%
- Not enclosing Form 8990
- Failing to recover the amount under code 117 when the federal carryforward is later allowed
## Prompt
- My business interest was limited federally. Does Virginia allow any of it?
