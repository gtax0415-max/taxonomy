# Income from Dealer Disposition of Property (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 113)
## Description
Federal law does not let a dealer use installment reporting for certain sales, so the full gain is in federal AGI in the year of sale. For dispositions made on or after January 1, 2009, Virginia lets the dealer elect installment treatment instead. In the year of disposition the dealer deducts (code 113) the gain attributable to installment payments to be made in future years; in later years the dealer adds back (addition code 14) the gain attributable to payments received that year, the amount that would have been reported under the installment method.
## Conditions
- The gain arises from an installment sale for which federal law does not permit the dealer to elect installment reporting.
- The dealer elects installment treatment for Virginia purposes on or before the due date for filing the return.
- Each disposition must be tracked separately.
## Amount
Year of disposition: gain attributable to installment payments to be made in future taxable years.
## Questions
- Did you sell property as a dealer on an installment basis in 2026?
- Did you make the Virginia installment election by the return due date?
- Do you have earlier dispositions for which payments came in during 2026 (code 14 addition)?
## Common Errors
- Missing the election deadline
- Forgetting the code 14 addition in later years
- Combining dispositions instead of tracking each one separately
## Prompt
- I'm a dealer who sold property on installments. Does Virginia let me spread the gain?
