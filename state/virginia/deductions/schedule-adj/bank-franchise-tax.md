# Virginia Bank Franchise Tax (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 112)
## Description
A shareholder of a bank may need to adjust federal adjusted gross income when the bank (1) is subject to the Virginia Bank Franchise Tax (Va. Code section 58.1-1207) and (2) has elected to be taxed as an S corporation for federal purposes. The adjustment removes the shareholder's share of the bank's income and adds back its losses and excluded distributions. It can be positive or negative, and it is one of only two codes that may use the loss box.
## Computation of Virginia Bank Franchise Tax Deduction
- a. Allocable share of the bank's income or gain included in federal AGI
- b. Allocable share of the bank's losses or deductions included in federal AGI
- c. Value of distributions paid by the bank to the extent excluded from federal AGI
- d. Line b + Line c
- e. Line a - Line d = net deduction. If negative, enter it on Schedule ADJ, Line 8a and fill in the box marked "LOSS"

Example: K-1 income $12,000, no losses, excluded distributions $3,000 → e = $9,000 deduction.
## What to enclose
A copy of the Schedule K-1 the bank provided.
## Questions
- Do you own shares in a bank that is an S corporation?
- Is the bank subject to the Virginia Bank Franchise Tax?
- What does your K-1 show for income, losses and distributions?
## Common Errors
- Entering a negative result without filling in the LOSS box
- Leaving out excluded distributions on Line c
- Not enclosing the K-1
## Prompt
- I own stock in a Virginia S corporation bank. What adjustment do I make?
