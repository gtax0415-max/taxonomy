# Virginia Public School Construction Grants Program and Fund (Schedule ADJ, Line 8a-8c, code 107)
## Description
A deduction for total contributions made to the Virginia Public School Construction Grants Program and Fund, provided the amount was not deducted on the federal income tax return (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 8). It is separate from the Public School Foundations checkoff on Schedule VAC.
## Amount
Total contributions to the Program and Fund. No dollar cap is stated.
## Eligibility
- The contribution was made to the Virginia Public School Construction Grants Program and Fund.
- The amount was not claimed as a deduction on the federal return (for example, as a charitable gift on federal Schedule A).
## Required Information
- Amount contributed to the Fund in 2026
- Whether it was deducted on the federal return
## Questions
- Did you contribute to the Virginia Public School Construction Grants Program and Fund?
- Did you deduct that gift on your federal Schedule A?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the same contribution federally (and on Virginia Schedule A) and again under code 107
- Confusing this fund with a donation to a local public school foundation through Schedule VAC
## Prompt
- I donated to the state school construction fund. Is that a Virginia deduction?
