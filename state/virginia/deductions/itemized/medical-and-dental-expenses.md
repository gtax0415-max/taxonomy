# Medical and Dental Expenses (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 1-4)
## Description
Unreimbursed medical and dental expenses are deductible for Virginia only to the extent they exceed 10 percent of federal adjusted gross income. Virginia deconformed (for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2019) from the federal change to the medical expense deduction floor, so the Virginia figure is recomputed on Schedule A rather than copied from the federal Schedule A. When the taxpayer has conformity additions or subtractions, the floor uses Conformity FAGI from the Conformity Worksheet.
## How to compute
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Medical and dental expenses (do not include expenses reimbursed or paid by others) |
| 2 | Federal adjusted gross income from Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet; part-year residents use only income attributable to Virginia residency; if negative enter 0 |
| 3 | Line 2 x 10% (0.10) |
| 4 | Line 1 - Line 3; if Line 3 is more than Line 1, enter 0 |

Example: expenses of $9,000 and Conformity FAGI of $70,000. Line 3 = $7,000; Line 4 = $2,000.
## Conformity Worksheet (Schedule A, page 2)
Complete it BEFORE Schedule A.
1. Federal adjusted gross income from the federal return
2. Conformity additions to FAGI (Schedule ADJ, Line 2a)
3. Line 1 + Line 2
4. Conformity subtractions from FAGI (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a)
5. Conformity FAGI = Line 3 - Line 4

Use Line 5 instead of federal AGI wherever a federal AGI-based limit applies: the medical floor (Line 4), gifts to charity (Line 14), casualty and theft loss (Line 15), and the Line 17 limitation test (in place of Form 760, Line 1). With no conformity additions or subtractions, Line 5 equals federal AGI.
## Interaction with other lines
- Line 4 is included in Line 1 and Line 2 of the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, so medical expenses are NOT reduced by the overall limitation.
- Part-year residents use only the portion of federal AGI adjusted for conformity and earned while a Virginia resident.
- Long-term care premiums deducted on Schedule ADJ (code 106) and organ donor expenses (code 110) cannot also be counted here, and vice versa.
## Questions
- What medical and dental expenses did you pay that insurance or others did not reimburse?
- Do you have any conformity addition or subtraction on Schedule ADJ?
- Were you a Virginia resident all year?
## Common Errors
- Copying federal Schedule A medical expenses without recomputing at 10%
- Using federal AGI when there are conformity adjustments (use Conformity FAGI)
- Including reimbursed amounts
- Claiming the same long-term care premiums or organ donor costs here and on Schedule ADJ
