# Itemized Deduction Limitation and Income Tax Reduction (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 17-18)
## Description
Virginia kept the overall limitation on itemized deductions (the Pease limitation) that is suspended for federal purposes, and deconforms from the new federal overall limitation. Higher-income taxpayers reduce their itemized deductions by the smaller of 3% of income over a threshold or 80% of the deductions subject to the limit. Line 18 then removes state, local and foreign income taxes, in proportion when the limitation applies. The federal SALT cap amount is used inside this worksheet.
## Thresholds for 2026 (use federal filing status)
| Federal filing status | Form 760, Line 1 over |
|---|---|
| Married filing jointly or qualifying surviving spouse | $408,900 |
| Head of household | $374,800 |
| Single | $340,750 |
| Married filing separately | $204,450 |
Use Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet instead of Form 760, Line 1 if there are conformity adjustments.
## Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Part A
Complete after Schedule A Lines 1-16. Lines 1-11 are completed as though a full-year Virginia resident.
1. Total of Schedule A Lines 4, 5a, 5b, 5c, 6, 10, 14, 15 and 16c (Line 5a no more than the SALT cap amount)
2. Total of Lines 4, 9 and 15, plus gambling losses on Line 16a
3. Line 1 - Line 2 (if zero or less, the limitation does not apply)
4. Line 3 x 80%
5. Form 760, Line 1 (or Conformity Worksheet Line 5)
6. Threshold from the table
7. Line 5 - Line 6 (if zero or less, the limitation does not apply)
8. Line 7 x 3%
9. Smaller of Line 4 or Line 8
10. Line 3
11. Line 9 divided by Line 10, to 3 decimal places
12a. Residents and nonresidents: Line 1 - Line 9 → Schedule A, Line 17
12b. Part-year residents (Form 760PY): resident-period deductions only: (1) total of Lines 4, 5a, 5b, 5c, 6, 10, 14, 15, 16c; (2) Lines 5a, 5b, 5c, 6, 8e, 14 and 16c (minus gambling losses) x Line 11; (3) (1) - (2) → Line 17
## Part B: income tax modification
13. State and local income tax from Schedule A, Line 5a (no more than the SALT cap amount; part-year residents only the amount paid while a resident; include foreign income tax per the instructions)
14. Line 13 x Line 11
15. Line 13 - Line 14 → Schedule A, Line 18
## SALT cap amount inside the worksheet
For 2026 the cap is $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), reduced under the federal MAGI rule by 30% of MAGI over $505,000 ($252,500 married filing separately), but not below $10,000 ($5,000). Lines 1, 12(b) and 13 use the lesser of Line 5a or the cap.
## Different federal and Virginia filing status
Complete Lines 1-11 using the federal filing status as a full-year resident, skip 12a, and complete 12b with the itemized deductions that may be claimed on the Virginia Schedule A; then complete Lines 14-15.
## Example (single, no conformity adjustments)
Form 760, Line 1 = $500,000 (MAGI below $505,000, so the full $40,400 cap applies). Schedule A: Line 5a income tax $30,000, Line 5b $10,000, Line 10 $20,000 (no investment interest), Line 14 $6,000; total $66,000. Line 3 = $66,000; Line 4 = $52,800; Line 7 = $159,250; Line 8 = $4,777.50; Line 9 = $4,777.50; Line 11 = 0.072. Line 12a = $61,222.50 → Line 17. Part B: $30,000 x 0.072 = $2,160; Line 15 = $27,840 → Line 18. Line 19 = $33,382.50, rounded to whole dollars on the return.
## Questions
- What is your federal filing status and federal AGI?
- Do you have conformity adjustments?
- How much of your Schedule A is medical, investment interest, casualty or gambling losses?
- What is your MAGI for the SALT cap reduction?
## Common Errors
- Using Virginia filing status instead of federal filing status for the threshold
- Leaving Line 5a above the SALT cap amount inside the worksheet
- Reducing medical, investment interest, casualty or gambling losses
- Entering full Line 5a on Line 18 when the worksheet applies (use Part B, Line 15)
- Rounding Line 11 to other than 3 decimal places
