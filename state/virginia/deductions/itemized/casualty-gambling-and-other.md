# Casualty and Theft Losses, Gambling Losses and Other Itemized Deductions (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 15-16c)
## Description
Casualty and theft losses from a declared disaster, gambling losses and other federal itemized deductions carry from the federal return to Virginia Schedule A. They are defined by federal law. The casualty loss may need to be recomputed with Conformity FAGI when the taxpayer has conformity adjustments. Casualty losses and gambling losses are excluded from the reduction under the Virginia overall limitation.
## 2026 federal changes that carry to Virginia
Virginia conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as of December 31, 2025, including federal changes to itemized deductions (HB 29, Chapter 7; Tax Bulletin 26-1). For 2026, P.L. 119-21 makes three changes:
- **Casualty losses:** personal casualty losses are deductible when caused by a federally declared disaster or, beginning in 2026, a state-declared disaster. A Virginia emergency declared by the Governor can qualify. The limit to declared disasters is now permanent.
- **Gambling losses:** only 90% of wagering losses are deductible, and still no more than gambling winnings. A taxpayer can owe tax on gambling activity that broke even.
- **Educator expenses:** unreimbursed educator expenses above the $300 adjustment on federal Schedule 1 can now be itemized. Virginia's own $500 educator deduction (Schedule ADJ, code 118) cannot be claimed on the same expenses.

The 2026 federal Schedule A draft renumbers these lines: casualty to Line 16, and other deductions to Lines 17a-17z, with gambling on 17a. The 2026 Virginia Schedule A has not been released, so its line numbers may change from the 2025 form used below.
## The lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 15 | Casualty and theft loss(es) from a federally declared or, for 2026, state-declared disaster (other than net qualified disaster losses), from federal Form 4684 |
| 16a | Gambling losses (deductible amount: 90% of losses, no more than winnings) |
| 16b | Other: list type and amount |
| 16c | Add Lines 16a and 16b |
## Virginia adjustments
- Conformity: if there is a conformity addition or subtraction, recompute the casualty and theft loss by substituting Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet for the federal AGI used in the federal 10% limit.
- Overall limitation: the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Line 2 removes Line 15 and the gambling losses on Line 16a from the reduction base, so those amounts are never reduced. Line 16b other deductions remain in the base.
- Part-year residents (Line 12b of the worksheet) multiply Line 16c minus gambling losses by the Line 11 ratio.
- Do not list on Line 16b anything deducted elsewhere, such as on Schedule ADJ.
## Required Information
- Disaster declaration (federal or state) and the date and type of the loss
- Cost or basis of the property, fair market value before and after, and insurance or other reimbursement
- Total gambling winnings (Forms W-2G and unreported winnings) and documented losses
- Type and amount of any other itemized deduction, and confirmation it is not also claimed on Schedule ADJ
## Questions
- Did you have property damaged or stolen in a federally or state-declared disaster? Do you have Form 4684?
- Did you have gambling winnings and losses? Do you keep a wagering log?
- Are you a K-12 educator with classroom expenses over $300?
- Are there other itemized deductions on your federal Schedule A?
## Common Errors
- Entering a loss not from a declared disaster, or a net qualified disaster loss, on Line 15
- Deducting 100% of gambling losses instead of 90% for 2026
- Not recomputing the casualty loss with Conformity FAGI when conformity adjustments exist
- Leaving gambling losses in the limitation base
- Listing a Schedule ADJ deduction on Line 16b, or claiming the same educator expenses on both
## Prompt
- My house was damaged in a declared disaster. Does Virginia allow the loss?
- Can I deduct gambling losses on my Virginia return?
- Why can I only deduct 90% of my gambling losses?
