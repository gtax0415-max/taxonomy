# Casualty and Theft Losses, Gambling Losses and Other Itemized Deductions (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 15-16c)
## Description
Casualty and theft losses from a federally declared disaster, gambling losses and other federal itemized deductions carry from the federal return to Virginia Schedule A. They are defined by federal law. The casualty loss may need to be recomputed with Conformity FAGI when the taxpayer has conformity adjustments. Casualty losses and gambling losses are excluded from the reduction under the Virginia overall limitation.
## The lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 15 | Casualty and theft loss(es) from a federally declared disaster (other than net qualified disaster losses): amount from Line 18 of federal Form 4684 |
| 16a | Gambling losses |
| 16b | Other: list type and amount |
| 16c | Add Lines 16a and 16b |
## Virginia adjustments
- Conformity: if there is a conformity addition or subtraction, recompute the casualty and theft loss by substituting Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet for the federal AGI used in the federal limitation.
- Overall limitation: the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Line 2 removes Line 15 and any gambling losses on Line 16a from the reduction base, so those amounts are never reduced. Line 16b other deductions remain in the base.
- Part-year residents (Line 12b of the worksheet) multiply Line 16c minus gambling losses by the Line 11 ratio.
- Do not list on Line 16b anything deducted elsewhere, such as on Schedule ADJ.
## Questions
- Did you have property damaged or stolen in a federally declared disaster? Do you have Form 4684?
- Did you have gambling winnings and losses?
- Are there other itemized deductions on your federal Schedule A (Line 16)?
## Common Errors
- Entering a loss not from a federally declared disaster, or a net qualified disaster loss, on Line 15
- Not recomputing the casualty loss with Conformity FAGI when conformity adjustments exist
- Leaving gambling losses in the limitation base
- Listing a Schedule ADJ deduction on Line 16b
## Prompt
- My house was damaged in a declared disaster. Does Virginia allow the loss?
- Can I deduct gambling losses on my Virginia return?
