# Taxes Paid (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 5a-7)
## Description
Property and other taxes deducted on the federal return are also allowed on the Virginia return, but Virginia treats the three state and local tax lines differently from federal law. Real estate and personal property taxes are deductible in full, with no SALT cap. State and local income taxes are entered on Line 5a but then removed on Line 18, so they give no net Virginia deduction. The federal SALT cap amount matters only in two cases: the sales tax election, and the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet.
## The lines
| Line | Entry | Virginia rule |
|---|---|---|
| 5a | State and local income taxes OR general sales taxes (fill in the oval for sales tax) | Sales tax may not exceed the SALT cap amount. Income tax is entered as paid and reported federally, but capped at the SALT cap amount inside the limitation worksheet |
| 5b | State and local real estate taxes | Actual amount paid, imposed by Virginia or any other taxing jurisdiction; SALT cap does not apply |
| 5c | State and local personal property taxes | Actual amount paid; SALT cap does not apply; all other federal restrictions still apply |
| 6 | Other deductible taxes (list type and amount); fill in the oval for foreign income taxes | Foreign income tax is removed on Line 18 |
| 7 | Add Lines 5a, 5b, 5c and 6 | |
## The SALT cap amount for 2026
The 2026 Virginia General Assembly conformed to the federal SALT cap amount, including the reduction based on modified adjusted gross income (MAGI). The applicable cap for 2026 is $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately) unless the MAGI-based reduction applies. Under the federal rule the cap is reduced by 30% of MAGI over $505,000 ($252,500 married filing separately), but not below $10,000 ($5,000 married filing separately).

The cap affects the Virginia computation in only two situations:
1. Sales tax election: the Line 5a deduction may not exceed the SALT cap amount.
2. Income over the Line 17 threshold and income tax on Line 5a: Lines 1, 12(b) and 13 of the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet use the lesser of Line 5a or the SALT cap amount.

Most taxpayers use the federal Schedule A amounts without any SALT cap adjustment.
## Line 18: removing income taxes
- Taxpayers not using the limitation worksheet: Line 18 = Line 5a (income tax) + Line 6 foreign income tax.
- Taxpayers using the worksheet: Line 18 = Part B, Line 15.
- Sales tax elected on Line 5a: Line 18 is zero (plus any foreign income tax on Line 6, which is always included even with no state income tax deduction).

Example: a Virginia itemizer (below the Line 17 threshold) paid $14,000 of Virginia income tax, $9,000 real estate tax and $1,200 car tax. Line 7 = $24,200; Line 18 = $14,000; net taxes deduction = $10,200.
## Questions
- How much state and local income tax did you pay (withholding, estimates, prior-year balance)?
- Did you deduct sales tax instead of income tax on your federal return?
- What real estate and personal property (car) taxes did you pay, in Virginia or elsewhere?
- Did you deduct any foreign income tax?
- What is your modified adjusted gross income, if over $505,000?
## Common Errors
- Leaving state income tax in the Virginia deduction (it must be removed on Line 18)
- Applying the SALT cap to real estate and personal property taxes on Lines 5b and 5c
- Capping income tax on Line 5a itself when the limitation worksheet does not apply
- Forgetting the Line 6 oval and Line 18 for foreign income tax
- Not capping a sales tax election at the SALT cap amount
