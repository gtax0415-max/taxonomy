# Taxes Paid (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 5a-7)
## Description
Taxes deducted on the federal return are also allowed on the Virginia return, but Virginia treats the state and local tax lines differently from federal law. Real estate and personal property taxes are deductible in full, with no SALT cap. State and local income taxes are entered on Line 5a and then removed on Line 18, so they give no net Virginia deduction (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 1.a). The federal SALT cap amount matters in only two cases: the sales tax election, and taxpayers subject to the Virginia overall limitation.
## The lines
| Line | Entry | Virginia rule |
|---|---|---|
| 5a | State and local income taxes OR general sales taxes (fill in the oval for sales tax) | Sales tax may not exceed the SALT cap amount. Income tax is entered as paid, unless the taxpayer is subject to the overall limitation; then enter no more than the SALT cap amount on Line 5a itself |
| 5b | State and local real estate taxes | Actual amount paid, imposed by Virginia or any other taxing jurisdiction; SALT cap does not apply |
| 5c | State and local personal property taxes | Actual amount paid; SALT cap does not apply; all other federal restrictions still apply |
| 6 | Other deductible taxes (list type and amount); fill in the oval for foreign income taxes | Foreign income tax is removed on Line 18 |
| 7 | Add Lines 5a, 5b, 5c and 6 | |

The 2026 Virginia Schedule A has not been released. Line numbers follow the 2025 form. The 2026 federal Schedule A draft keeps Lines 5a-5e and 6, with the cap on Line 5e.
## The SALT cap amount for 2026
The 2026 General Assembly advanced Virginia's conformity date to December 31, 2025 (HB 29, Chapter 7; Tax Bulletin 26-1), which picks up the federal SALT cap increase. Virginia Tax confirmed on May 28, 2026 that the higher cap applies on Virginia Schedule A. The cap is still used only where Virginia applies it (below).

For 2026 the federal cap is $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately). It is reduced by 30% of modified adjusted gross income (MAGI) over $505,000 ($252,500 married filing separately), but not below $10,000 ($5,000 married filing separately). Virginia Tax's 2025 notice describes the phase-down threshold as "$250,000 for single filers". Under federal law the lower threshold applies only to married filing separately, so use $505,000 for single filers in 2026.

The cap affects the Virginia computation in only two situations:
1. Sales tax election: the Line 5a deduction may not exceed the SALT cap amount.
2. Income over the Line 17 threshold: the income tax on Line 5a may not exceed the SALT cap amount. Virginia Tax says to apply the cap to the amount entered on Line 5a, not later on the Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, or the return may not process correctly.

Most taxpayers enter the income tax actually paid, with no SALT cap adjustment.
## Line 18: removing income taxes
- Taxpayers not subject to the limitation: Line 18 = Line 5a income tax + Line 6 foreign income tax.
- Taxpayers subject to the limitation: Line 18 = Part B, Line 15 of the worksheet.
- Sales tax elected on Line 5a: Line 18 is zero, except for any foreign income tax on Line 6, which is always included even with no state income tax deduction.

Example: a Virginia itemizer below the Line 17 threshold paid $14,000 of Virginia income tax, $9,000 of real estate tax and $1,200 of car tax. Line 7 = $24,200; Line 18 = $14,000; net taxes deduction = $10,200.
## Required Information
- State and local income tax paid in 2026: withholding (W-2 Box 17 and 19, 1099 state withholding), estimated payments and prior-year balance due paid in 2026
- Whether the sales tax election was made federally, and the amount
- Real estate taxes paid (tax bills or Form 1098 Box 10), Virginia or elsewhere
- Personal property (car) taxes paid, from the locality's tax bill
- Other deductible taxes and any foreign income tax
- Federal filing status, federal adjusted gross income and MAGI, to test the Line 17 threshold and the SALT cap phase-down
## Questions
- How much state and local income tax did you pay (withholding, estimates, prior-year balance)?
- Did you deduct sales tax instead of income tax on your federal return?
- What real estate and personal property (car) taxes did you pay, in Virginia or elsewhere?
- Did you deduct any foreign income tax?
- Is your federal adjusted gross income over the Virginia limitation threshold, and is your MAGI over $505,000?
## Common Errors
- Leaving state income tax in the Virginia deduction (it must be removed on Line 18)
- Applying the SALT cap to real estate and personal property taxes on Lines 5b and 5c
- Capping income tax on Line 5a when neither the limitation nor the sales tax election applies
- Applying the cap only inside the limitation worksheet instead of on Line 5a
- Using the 2025 cap ($40,000) or threshold ($500,000) instead of the 2026 amounts
- Forgetting the Line 6 oval and Line 18 for foreign income tax
- Not capping a sales tax election at the SALT cap amount
