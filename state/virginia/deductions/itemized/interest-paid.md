# Interest Paid (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 8a-10)
## Description
Home mortgage interest, points, mortgage insurance premiums and investment interest are defined by federal law and carried from the federal Schedule A to Virginia Schedule A. Virginia does not change these amounts. In the Virginia overall limitation, investment interest is excluded from the reduction, while mortgage interest is subject to it.
## 2026 federal rules that carry to Virginia
Virginia conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as of December 31, 2025, including federal changes to itemized deductions (HB 29, Chapter 7; Tax Bulletin 26-1). That brings in two P.L. 119-21 changes:
- **Mortgage insurance premiums:** deductible again as qualified residence interest for 2026 and later. The deduction phases out between $100,000 and $110,000 of adjusted gross income ($50,000-$55,000 married filing separately). The 2026 federal Schedule A draft relabels Line 8d, previously reserved, as "Mortgage insurance premiums". The 2026 Virginia Schedule A has not been released; it is expected to follow.
- **Acquisition debt limit:** the $750,000 limit ($375,000 married filing separately) is now permanent. Interest on home equity debt is deductible only if the loan was used to buy, build or substantially improve the home.
## The lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 8 | Fill in the oval if you did not use all of your home mortgage loan(s) to buy, build or improve your home |
| 8a | Home mortgage interest and points reported to you on federal Form 1098 |
| 8b | Home mortgage interest not reported on Form 1098; if paid to the person you bought the home from, show that person's name, identifying number and address |
| 8c | Points not reported to you on Form 1098 |
| 8d | Mortgage insurance premiums, expected for 2026 (on the 2025 form, "Reserved for future use") |
| 8e | Add Lines 8a through 8d |
| 9 | Investment interest |
| 10 | Add Lines 8e and 9 |
## Interaction with other lines
- Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Line 2 subtracts Line 9 (investment interest), so investment interest is not reduced by the overall limitation. Line 8e mortgage interest, including mortgage insurance premiums, remains in the reduction base.
- For part-year residents, the Line 12b computation multiplies Line 8e (with other lines) by the Line 11 ratio.
- Married filing separately in Virginia after a joint federal return: mortgage interest that cannot be traced to one spouse is allocated by each spouse's share of combined federal AGI.
## Required Information
- Every Form 1098, with interest, points, mortgage insurance premiums and outstanding principal
- Seller-financed interest paid, with the seller's name, identifying number and address
- Points paid that are not on Form 1098
- How each loan's proceeds were used
- Investment interest expense and the federal Form 4952 limit
- Federal adjusted gross income, for the mortgage insurance phase-out
## Questions
- Do you have Forms 1098 for every mortgage?
- Did you pay private mortgage insurance or FHA/VA mortgage insurance premiums?
- Did you pay interest to an individual (seller financing)?
- Did you pay points that are not on Form 1098?
- Was any loan used for something other than buying, building or improving the home?
- Do you have investment interest expense?
## Common Errors
- Leaving 2026 mortgage insurance premiums off, or claiming them above the phase-out
- Omitting the seller's name, identifying number and address on Line 8b
- Not filling in the Line 8 oval for loan proceeds used for other purposes
- Deducting interest on debt over $750,000 or on home equity debt not used for the home
- Treating investment interest as subject to the overall limitation
