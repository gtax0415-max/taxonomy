# Gifts to Charity (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 11-14)
## Description
Charitable contributions are defined by federal law and reported on Virginia Schedule A at the limited amount allowed under federal law. When the taxpayer has conformity additions or subtractions, the AGI-based percentage limits are recomputed using Conformity FAGI from the Conformity Worksheet instead of federal AGI. Virginia adds a separate Schedule ADJ deduction (code 111) for the difference between 18 cents per mile and the charitable mileage rate used here.
## The lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 11 | Gifts by cash or check |
| 12 | Other than by cash or check |
| 13 | Carryover from prior year |
| 14 | Add Lines 11 through 13 |

Schedule A caution: report the limited amount as defined under federal law.
## Conformity recomputation
If there is a conformity addition (Schedule ADJ, Line 2a) or subtraction (Line 6a), recompute the federal percentage-of-AGI limits by substituting Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet for the federal AGI used federally. Otherwise, use the federal amount.
## Interaction with other lines
- Line 14 is in the base that the overall limitation reduces (Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Line 1, and not removed on Line 2).
- Charitable mileage: the mileage deduction claimed here stays on Schedule A; Schedule ADJ code 111 allows the difference between 18 cents per mile and the per-mile deduction allowed on Schedule A (or actual expenses, if less than 18 cents per mile).
## Questions
- What did you give to charity by cash or check, and do you have acknowledgments?
- Did you donate property, clothing or stock? What was it worth?
- Do you have a charitable carryover from last year?
- Did you drive for a charity? How many miles?
- Do you have any conformity addition or subtraction?
## Common Errors
- Entering gifts above the federal percentage limits
- Using federal AGI for the limit when there are conformity adjustments
- Forgetting the prior-year carryover on Line 13
- Missing the Schedule ADJ code 111 mileage difference
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my church donations on my Virginia return?
- I drove for a food bank. Is there a Virginia deduction for mileage?
