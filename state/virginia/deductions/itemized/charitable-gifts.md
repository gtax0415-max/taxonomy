# Gifts to Charity (Virginia Schedule A, Lines 11-14)
## Description
Charitable contributions are defined by federal law and reported on Virginia Schedule A at the amount allowed under federal law. When the taxpayer has conformity additions or subtractions, the AGI-based limits are recomputed using Conformity FAGI from the Conformity Worksheet. Virginia adds a separate Schedule ADJ deduction (code 111) for the difference between 18 cents per mile and the federal charitable mileage rate.
## 2026 federal changes that carry to Virginia
Virginia conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as of December 31, 2025, including federal changes to itemized deductions (HB 29, Chapter 7; Tax Bulletin 26-1). For 2026 that brings in the P.L. 119-21 rules:
- **0.5% floor:** itemized charitable contributions count only above 0.5% of the contribution base (generally AGI). The 2026 federal Schedule A draft adds a Charitable Contribution Limitation Worksheet and a new line for it, and renumbers the carryover and total lines. The 2026 Virginia Schedule A has not been released, so its line numbers may change from those below.
- **60% limit:** the 60%-of-AGI limit for cash gifts to public charities is permanent.
- **Federal 35% benefit cap (the 2/37 reduction under the new IRC § 68):** Virginia does not apply it. Virginia keeps its own overall limitation instead (Tax Bulletin 26-1), so start from the charitable amount before any federal 2/37 reduction.
- **Non-itemizer deduction ($1,000; $2,000 joint) for cash gifts:** this is not an itemized deduction. Virginia law has no equivalent, so it gives no Virginia deduction, and a federal non-itemizer takes the Virginia standard deduction.

Schedule A caution: report the limited amount as defined under federal law.
## Conformity recomputation
If there is a conformity addition (Schedule ADJ, Line 2a) or subtraction (Line 6a), recompute the federal percentage-of-AGI limits and the 0.5% floor by substituting Line 5 of the Conformity Worksheet for federal AGI. Otherwise, use the federal amount.
## Interaction with other lines
- Line 14 is in the base that the overall limitation reduces (Limited Itemized Deduction Worksheet, Line 1, and not removed on Line 2).
- Charitable mileage: the federal rate is fixed by statute at 14 cents per mile, and that amount stays here. Schedule ADJ code 111 adds the difference up to 18 cents per mile, or the difference to actual expenses if those are less than 18 cents.
## Required Information
- Cash and check gifts, with bank records or written acknowledgments (required for a single gift of $250 or more)
- Noncash gifts: description, fair market value, Form 8283, and a qualified appraisal over $5,000
- Charitable carryover from the 2025 federal return
- Volunteer miles driven for a charity
- Federal adjusted gross income, and any conformity adjustments
## Questions
- What did you give to charity by cash or check, and do you have acknowledgments?
- Did you donate property, clothing or stock? What was it worth?
- Do you have a charitable carryover from last year?
- Did you drive for a charity? How many miles?
- Do you have any conformity addition or subtraction?
## Common Errors
- Entering gifts above the federal percentage limits, or without the 2026 0.5% floor
- Using the federal amount after the 2/37 reduction instead of the amount before it
- Claiming the federal non-itemizer charitable deduction on the Virginia return
- Using federal AGI for the limit when there are conformity adjustments
- Forgetting the prior-year carryover on Line 13
- Missing the Schedule ADJ code 111 mileage difference
