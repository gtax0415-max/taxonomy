# Deductions and Exemptions (Form 760, Lines 10-15)
## Description
After Virginia adjusted gross income (VAGI) is figured on Form 760, Line 9, four items are subtracted to reach Virginia taxable income: itemized deductions OR the standard deduction, the personal and additional exemptions, and the Schedule ADJ deductions. Form 760, Line 14 adds Lines 10, 11, 12 and 13, and Line 15 (Virginia Taxable Income) is Line 9 minus Line 14. Line 15 is the amount the tax on Line 16 is figured on.
## How the lines flow
- Line 10 = Itemized deductions from Virginia Schedule A, Line 19 (leave blank if claiming the standard deduction)
- Line 11 = Standard deduction: $8,750 (Filing Status 1 or 3) or $17,500 (Filing Status 2), limited to earned income for a dependent
- Line 12 = Exemptions: Section A ($930 each for you, spouse on a joint return and dependents) plus Section B ($800 each for 65 or older and blind)
- Line 13 = Deductions from Schedule ADJ, Line 9 (codes 101-119 and 199)
- Line 14 = Lines 10 + 11 + 12 + 13
- Line 15 = Line 9 - Line 14 = Virginia Taxable Income → Form 760, Line 16 (tax)
## Key rules
- Standard or itemized must match the federal return: a federal itemizer must itemize for Virginia; a taxpayer who took the federal standard deduction must take the Virginia standard deduction.
- If one spouse claims itemized deductions, the other spouse must also claim itemized deductions.
- Schedule ADJ deductions are allowed whether the taxpayer itemizes or not. Do not put an item on Schedule A that is deducted on Schedule ADJ.
- If VAGI on Line 9 is less than $11,950 (Filing Status 1 or 3) or $23,900 (Filing Status 2), the tax is $0; complete Lines 10 through 15 and enter "0" on Lines 16 and 18.
