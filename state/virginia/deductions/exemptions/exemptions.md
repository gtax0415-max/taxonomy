# Exemptions (Form 760, Line 12)
## Description
Virginia still allows personal exemptions. They are entered in the Exemptions box at the top of Form 760 in two sections, and the sum of the two section totals goes on Form 760, Line 12, where it reduces Virginia adjusted gross income along with the standard or itemized deduction and the Schedule ADJ deductions.
## How the lines flow
- Section A: You + Spouse (Filing Status 2 only) + Dependents = count x $930 = Total Section A
- Section B: You 65 or over + Spouse 65 or over + You Blind + Spouse Blind = count x $800 = Total Section B
- Form 760, Line 12 = Total Section A + Total Section B → Line 14 → Line 15
## Rules that cut across both sections
- A taxpayer may never claim less than a whole exemption, and the same dependent may not be claimed on separate returns.
- Filing Status 3: each spouse determines exemptions as if separate federal returns had been filed; dependents that cannot be accounted for separately are allocated proportionately by each spouse's income.
- A taxpayer who claims the Credit for Low-Income Individuals (Form 760, Line 23) cannot claim the 65 or older or Blind exemptions.
- The exemption counts are also used on the Spouse Tax Adjustment Worksheet (Part 1, Line 2) and on Schedule ADJ, Line 12 for the Credit for Low-Income Individuals (personal and dependent exemptions only, $300 each).
