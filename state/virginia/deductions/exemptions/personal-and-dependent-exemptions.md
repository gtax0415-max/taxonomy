# Personal and Dependent Exemptions (Form 760, Line 12, Section A)
## Description
Each taxpayer, a spouse on a joint return, and each dependent is worth a $930 exemption for 2026. The count is entered in Exemption Section A at the top of Form 760, multiplied by $930, and added to Section B on Line 12. Virginia generally allows the same number of dependent exemptions as the federal return claims (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 2 a). The federal personal exemption amount is $0 (made permanent by P.L. 119-21), but exemptions remain "allowable" under IRC 151, so Virginia still counts them.
## Section A boxes
| Box | Count |
|---|---|
| You | 1 |
| Spouse if Filing Status 2 | 1 on a joint return |
| Dependents | number of dependents |
| Total Section A | sum x $930 |

Example: a married couple filing jointly with two children counts 1 + 1 + 2 = 4; 4 x $930 = $3,720.
## Who can be claimed
- Dependents: generally the same number of dependent exemptions allowed on your federal return.
- A taxpayer may never claim less than a whole exemption. The same dependent may not be claimed on separate returns.
- Filing Status 3 (married filing separately): each spouse determines exemptions as if separate federal returns had been filed, using the federal rules for separate reporting. If dependent exemptions cannot be accounted for separately, they are allocated proportionately between the spouses based on each spouse's income. One spouse may never claim less than a whole personal exemption.
- One spouse a resident, the other a nonresident, filing separately: the spouses allocate dependent exemptions as if they had filed separate federal returns. As a general rule, the spouse claiming a dependent must be reporting at least half of the total federal adjusted gross income.
## Interaction with other lines
- Schedule ADJ, Line 12 (Credit for Low-Income Individuals) uses the number of personal and dependent exemptions reported on Form 760 times $300; exemptions for 65 or older and blind are not included there.
- The Spouse Tax Adjustment Worksheet, Part 1, Line 2 gives each spouse $930 plus $800 for each 65 or older or blind box.
## Required Information
- Filing status
- Number of dependents on the federal return, with names and SSNs
- For Filing Status 3, which spouse claims each dependent
## Questions
- How many dependents are on your federal return?
- Are you filing jointly or separately? If separately, who claims each child?
- If separate, can the dependents be accounted for separately, or must they be allocated by income?
## Common Errors
- Counting the spouse in Section A on a Filing Status 3 return
- Claiming the same dependent on both spouses' separate returns
- Claiming a fraction of an exemption
- Adding the $800 Section B amounts into Section A
## Prompt
- How much is the Virginia exemption for my kids?
- We file separately in Virginia. Who gets the children?
