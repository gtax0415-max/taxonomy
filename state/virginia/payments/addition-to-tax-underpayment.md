# Addition to Tax for Underpaid Estimated Tax (Form 760C; Schedule ADJ, Line 18; Form 760, Line 32)
## Description
Virginia expects tax to be paid through the year by withholding or estimated payments. If it is not, an "addition to tax" (an interest-like charge) is computed on Form 760C and reported on Schedule ADJ, Line 18. Schedule ADJ, Line 21 adds Line 18, the penalty (Line 19) and interest (Line 20) and carries the total to Form 760, Line 32. For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, no addition to tax applies when the total underpayment is $1,000 or less. The threshold was $150 through 2025. The change was enacted by HB 1600 (2025 Acts, Chapter 725, fifth enactment clause; Va. Code § 58.1-492).
## No addition to tax if each payment is on time and
- you owe $1,000 or less with the 2026 return; or
- withholding plus timely estimated payments were at least 90% of 2026 tax after nonrefundable credits, or 100% of 2025 tax after nonrefundable credits; or
- you meet an exception computed on Form 760C (enclose it).
## Form 760C (2025 form; confirm against the 2026 form)
The 2025 form prints the old $150 threshold; for 2026 it is $1,000 by statute.
- Part I, underpayment: Line 1 current tax after Spouse Tax Adjustment and credits (withholding, estimated and extension payments are not credits); Line 2 90% of Line 1; Line 3 prior year tax; Line 4 smaller of Lines 2 and 3; Line 5 number of installments required; Lines 6-14 by installment. The 2026 installment dates are May 1, 2026, June 15, 2026, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027. Withholding may be divided by four or entered as actually withheld with a schedule. An overpayment in a later period cannot cancel an earlier underpayment.
- Installments required (2025 instructions): income first received on or before April 15, 4; after April 15 but before June 2, 3; after June 1 but before September 2, 2; after September 1, 1. File Form 760C to show fewer than four, or the Department computes as if four were due.
- Part II, exceptions (each period separately; only timely payments count): Exception 1, payments at least 25/50/75/100% of prior year tax (prior return must cover 12 months and show a liability); Exception 2, prior year income at current rates and exemptions; Exception 3, 90% of tax on annualized income (income through April 30 x 3, May 31 x 2.4, August 31 x 1.5; tax x 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%); Exception 4, 90% of tax on actual income for 4, 5 and 8 months. Exceptions 3 and 4 do not apply to the fourth installment.
- Part III, addition: underpayment x days late x daily rate. The 2025 form uses .00025 (9% per annum) through May 1. The 2026 Virginia rates are confirmed by Tax Bulletins 26-2, 26-3 and 26-4: 9% (first quarter), 8% (second), 9% (third) and 9% (fourth). The fourth installment runs into 2027, so the 2026 form also needs the first-quarter 2027 rate, which had not been announced as of October 2026.
## Who files Form 760C
Anyone underpaid as of any installment date, even if due a refund, and anyone liable for fewer than four installments. Not required if not required to file a return. Estates and trusts use it with different period end dates.
## Required Information
- Virginia withholding for 2026, and whether it will be spread evenly or as actually withheld
- Date and amount of each estimated payment
- 2025 Virginia tax after credits
- Income by period, if using an annualization exception
## Questions
- How much Virginia tax was withheld, and did you make estimated payments? On what dates?
- What was your 2025 Virginia tax after credits?
- Did your income arrive unevenly during the year?
- When did you first receive income not covered by withholding?
## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 form's $150 threshold instead of $1,000 for 2026
- Letting a later overpayment offset an earlier installment underpayment
- Counting late payments toward an exception
- Completing Form 760C but not enclosing it or filling in the Line 32 oval
- Treating estimated or extension payments as tax credits on Line 1
