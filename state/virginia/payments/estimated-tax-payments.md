# Estimated Tax Payments (Form 760, Line 20)
## Description
Line 20 reports estimated tax payments made for taxable year 2026 with Form 760ES or online. Estimated payments are required on income from any source other than income on which sufficient Virginia tax is withheld (2025 Form 760C instructions). Timely installments also decide whether an addition to tax is owed on Form 760C.
## 2026 installment dates (calendar year)
| Installment | Due |
|---|---|
| 1 | May 1, 2026 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 |
Fiscal year filers use the dates in the Form 760ES instructions. Farmers, fishermen and merchant seamen follow the Form 760F rules.
## Who should pay estimates
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2027, estimated payments are generally required if estimated Virginia tax exceeds Virginia withholding and other credits by more than $1,000. For 2026, no addition to tax is owed if the tax owed with the return is $1,000 or less, or timely payments reached 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax (after nonrefundable credits).
## How to pay
- File Form 760ES or pay online at www.tax.virginia.gov (Individual Online Services or eForms); payments can be scheduled for a future date.
- Electronic payment is mandatory if any installment exceeds or can reasonably be expected to exceed $1,500, any payment with a return or extension exceeds $1,500, or estimated total tax liability exceeds $6,000. Once a threshold applies, all future payments must be electronic.
- Individual Online Services shows total estimated payments made for the current year and any overpayment carried forward.
## What to enter
The total of 2026 estimated payments actually made (from vouchers, bank records or the online account). Do not include the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 (Line 21) or extension payments (Line 22).
## Questions
- Did you make 2026 Virginia estimated payments? How much and on what dates?
- Was any of last year's refund applied to 2026 (that goes on Line 21)?
- Did you pay anything with your extension (that goes on Line 22)?
- Do you expect to owe more than $1,000 beyond withholding for 2027?
## Common Errors
- Including the prior year overpayment or an extension payment on Line 20
- Reporting payments as made on time when they were late (affects Form 760C)
- Paying by check after crossing an electronic payment threshold
- Entering federal estimated payments
## Prompt
- Where do I enter the quarterly payments I made to Virginia?
- When are Virginia estimated taxes due?
- Do I need to make estimated payments to Virginia next year?
