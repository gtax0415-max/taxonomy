# Estimated Tax Payments (Form 760, Line 20)
## Description
Line 20 reports estimated tax payments made for taxable year 2026 with Form 760ES or online. Estimated payments are required on income from any source other than income on which sufficient Virginia tax is withheld. Timely installments also decide whether an addition to tax is owed on Form 760C.
## 2026 installment dates (calendar year)
| Installment | Due |
|---|---|
| 1 | May 1, 2026 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 |
Fiscal year filers use the dates in the Form 760ES instructions. Farmers, fishermen and merchant seamen (at least two-thirds of gross income from those sources) may make one payment by January 15, 2027 and follow the Form 760F rules. A return filed and fully paid by March 1, 2027 takes the place of the January installment.
## Who should pay estimates
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, estimated payments are required when Virginia tax, after withholding and credits, is expected to be more than $1,000. The threshold was $150 through 2025. The increase was enacted by the fifth enactment clause of HB 1600 (2025 Acts, Chapter 725), amending Va. Code § 58.1-492.

Separately, no addition to tax is owed if timely payments reached 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax (after nonrefundable credits). Virginia has no 110% rule for higher incomes.
## How to pay
- File Form 760ES or pay online at www.tax.virginia.gov (Individual Online Services or eForms); payments can be scheduled for a future date.
- Electronic payment is mandatory if any installment exceeds or can reasonably be expected to exceed $1,500, any payment with a return or extension exceeds $1,500, or total income tax liability exceeds $6,000. Once a threshold applies, all future payments must be electronic.
- Individual Online Services shows total estimated payments made for the current year and any overpayment carried forward.
## What to enter
The total of 2026 estimated payments actually made (from bank records, payment confirmations or the online account). Do not include the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 (Line 21) or extension payments (Line 22).
## Required Information
- Date and amount of each 2026 Virginia estimated payment, for each spouse if joint estimates were made
## Questions
- Did you make 2026 Virginia estimated payments? How much and on what dates?
- Was any of last year's refund applied to 2026 (that goes on Line 21)?
- Did you pay anything with your extension (that goes on Line 22)?
- Do you expect to owe more than $1,000 beyond withholding for 2027?
## Common Errors
- Including the prior year overpayment or an extension payment on Line 20
- Reporting payments as made on time when they were late (affects Form 760C)
- Paying by check after crossing an electronic payment threshold
- Entering federal estimated payments
## Prompt
- Where do I enter the quarterly payments I made to Virginia?
- When are Virginia estimated taxes due?
- Do I need to make estimated payments to Virginia next year?
