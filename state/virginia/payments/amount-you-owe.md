# Amount You Owe and Payment Options (Form 760, Line 35)
## Description
Line 35 is the balance due. If Line 27 shows tax you owe, add Lines 27 and 34 (next-year credit, contributions, addition to tax and penalties, use tax). If you have an overpayment on Line 28 but it is less than Line 34, subtract Line 28 from Line 34. Pay by the due date: May 1, 2027, which falls on a Saturday, so payment by Monday, May 3, 2027 is timely. A filed return that is not paid by the due date will be billed, and unpaid tax draws the late payment penalty and interest.
## Payment options
- Online (fastest): VATAX Online Services for Individuals or eForms, or with the return through e-File. Payments come from your checking or savings account, can be scheduled for a future date, and the Department charges no fee (www.tax.virginia.gov/payments).
- Credit or debit card: if you e-file or mail the return to the Department, pay at www.tax.virginia.gov/payments; the processing company adds a fee, shown before you confirm, and you may cancel then at no charge. After paying, fill in the oval on Line 35. If you file locally, call your Commissioner of the Revenue's office for card instructions.
- Check: if you file locally, make it payable to the Treasurer or Director of Finance of your city or county; otherwise to the Department of Taxation. Write your SSN and "2026 Virginia income tax payment" on it.
- Paying separately from the return but by the due date: use the payment voucher (Form 760PMT). If paying by check after the return was filed, do not send a copy of the return with the payment.
## Mandatory electronic payment
You must pay electronically if any estimated installment exceeded $1,500, any payment with a return or extension exceeded $1,500, or total income tax liability for any taxable year exceeded $6,000 (thresholds in effect since taxable year 2022). Once any threshold applies, all future individual income tax payments must be electronic. Otherwise a check is allowed.
## Returned payments
If your bank does not honor a payment, the Department may impose a $35 penalty (Va. Code 2.2-614.1) in addition to other penalties such as late payment.
## Interaction with other lines
- Line 34 includes Line 32 (addition to tax, penalty and interest) and Line 33 (use tax), so compute those first.
- Contributions on Lines 30-31 and a credit forward on Line 29 come out of an overpayment; if they exceed it, the excess is owed here.
## Payment plans
A taxpayer who cannot pay the full balance should file on time and pay what they can. Virginia Tax then sends a bill for the rest, and a payment plan can be set up only after that bill arrives.
- **How:** Teleplan, the automated line at 804.440.5100, using the SSN and the 5-digit bill number; or a representative at 804.367.8045. Online self-service plans are offered only for certain business taxes, not individual income tax.
- **Self-service eligibility:** less than $25,000 owed in tax, penalties and interest, and no levy, bankruptcy, collection agency referral or similar action on the account.
- **Term:** up to 5 years for individual income tax. A down payment is not required, but Virginia Tax suggests 10%.
- **Cost:** penalties and interest keep accruing for the life of the plan, so the shortest affordable plan costs least.
## Required Information
- If the balance cannot be paid in full, how much can be paid at filing
- How the taxpayer will pay (bank debit, card or check), and the bank details for a debit
- Whether any electronic payment threshold has ever applied
- Whether the return is filed locally or with the Department
## Questions
- Do you owe tax on Line 27, or is your overpayment smaller than Line 34?
- How do you want to pay: bank account online, card, or check?
- Have any of your payments exceeded $1,500, or is your tax over $6,000?
- Are you filing locally or with the Department?
## Common Errors
- Mailing a check when electronic payment is mandatory
- Paying by card without filling in the Line 35 oval
- Making a check payable to the wrong office for how the return is filed
- Sending a copy of an already filed return with a later check
- Leaving contributions on Lines 30-31 that turn a small refund into a balance due
