# Penalties and Interest (Schedule ADJ, Lines 19-20)
## Description
A return filed after the due date with tax due (May 1, 2027 is a Saturday, so a return filed by May 3, 2027 is timely) may owe a penalty and interest, computed by the taxpayer on Schedule ADJ, Lines 19 and 20. Line 21 adds them to any addition to tax (Line 18) and carries the total to Form 760, Line 32. The automatic extension applies to returns filed by November 1, 2027; which penalty applies depends on when the return is filed and how much is unpaid.
## Line 19 penalty
- Extension penalty: return filed within 6 months after the due date and the tax due with it is more than 10% of total tax liability. Rate: 2% per month or part of a month, from the due date to the filing date, maximum 12% of the tax due. Fill in the Extension Penalty oval.
- Late filing penalty: return filed more than 6 months after the due date. No extension applies; the penalty is 30% of the tax due with the return. Fill in the Late Filing Penalty oval.
- Late payment penalty (assessed by the Department, not on Line 19): tax not paid in full when the return is filed accrues 6% per month or part of a month from the filing date to the payment date, maximum 30%. During the extension period both the extension penalty (due date to filing) and the late payment penalty (filing to payment) can apply. To avoid the late payment penalty, pay any tax owed when you file.
## Line 20 interest
A tax due return filed after the due date, even with an extension, owes interest on the Form 760, Line 27 amount from the due date to the date filed or postmarked. For the daily interest factor call 804.367.8031 or contact your locality. Unpaid balances can draw further penalties and interest. The Virginia rates for 2026, confirmed by Tax Bulletins 26-2, 26-3 and 26-4, are 9% (first quarter), 8% (second), 9% (third) and 9% (fourth). Interest on a 2026 return filed late runs in 2027, so it will use the 2027 rates, which had not been announced as of October 2026.
Additional tax on an amended return carries interest from the original due date to the date the amended return is filed or postmarked.
## Other penalties in the instructions
- Returned payment: if your bank does not honor a payment, a $35 penalty may be imposed (Va. Code 2.2-614.1), on top of other penalties.
- Fraud: a false or fraudulent return, or failing or refusing to file with intent to evade, carries an additional civil penalty of 100% of the correct tax. Willful failure to file is a Class 1 misdemeanor; false statements with intent to defraud are a Class 6 felony.
## Required Information
- Date the return was filed or postmarked
- Tax due with the return and total tax liability for the year
- Date and amount of each payment

## Common Errors
- Assuming the automatic extension removes interest on unpaid tax
- Missing the extension penalty when more than 10% of the tax is still due
- Applying the extension penalty to a return filed after November 1 instead of the 30% late filing penalty
- Not filling in the Late Filing or Extension oval on Line 19
