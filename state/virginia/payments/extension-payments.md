# Extension Payments (Form 760, Line 22)
## Description
Virginia grants an automatic 6-month extension to file, with no application, but the extension does not extend the time to pay. Any tentative tax due must be paid by the original due date with the extension voucher (Form 760IP) or online. For 2026 returns the due date is May 1, 2027, a Saturday, so payment by Monday, May 3, 2027 is timely. Line 22 reports the tentative tax paid with Form 760IP or the amount paid as an extension payment on the Department's website.
## Why pay with the extension
- At least 90% of the tax must be paid by the due date to avoid penalty.
- If the tax still due when the extended return is filed is more than 10% of total liability, an extension penalty of 2% per month or part of a month applies from the due date to the filing date, maximum 12% (Schedule ADJ, Line 19).
- Interest runs on any unpaid tax from the due date to the filing date even with an extension (Schedule ADJ, Line 20).
- The automatic extension provisions apply only to returns filed by November 1, 2027; after that the 30% late filing penalty applies.
## How to pay
- Form 760IP, or online through the Department's eForms, Individual Online Services or e-File software; payments can be scheduled for a future date.
- Electronic payment is mandatory if any payment made with a return or extension exceeds $1,500, any estimated installment exceeds $1,500, or total income tax liability exceeds $6,000.
## What to enter
The total paid as extension payments for 2026. Keep extension payments separate from 2026 estimated payments (Line 20) and from the 2025 overpayment applied (Line 21).
## Required Information
- Date and amount of each extension payment for 2026
## Questions
- Did you pay anything to Virginia when you took the extension? How much and when?
- Did you use the voucher or pay online?
- Was the payment at least 90% of your 2026 tax?
## Common Errors
- Believing the automatic extension also extends the time to pay
- Entering an extension payment on Line 20 with estimated payments
- Paying less than 90% and then not computing the extension penalty
- Mailing a check for an extension payment over $1,500 when electronic payment is required
## Prompt
- I paid Virginia with my extension in April; where does that go on the return?
- Do I need to send a form to get a Virginia extension?
- How much should I pay with my Virginia extension?
