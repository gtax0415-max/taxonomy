# Refund and Direct Deposit (Form 760, Line 36)
## Description
If Line 28 (overpayment) is greater than Line 34 (next-year credit, contributions, addition to tax and penalties, use tax), subtract Line 34 from Line 28 and enter the difference on Line 36. This is your refund. Fill in the Direct Bank Deposit section to have it deposited; otherwise the Department issues a check.
## Direct deposit
- Domestic accounts only; no international deposits. The Department will not deposit to or through foreign financial institutions, and trying to will significantly delay the refund.
- Bank routing transit number: 9 digits, printed at the bottom of a check. The first 2 digits must be 01 through 12 or 21 through 32. Do not use a deposit slip to verify it; it may carry internal routing numbers.
- Bank account number: up to 17 digits, no hyphens, spaces or symbols, entered left to right with unused boxes blank. Do not include the check number.
- Mark Checking or Savings.
- The Department is not responsible for a lost refund caused by wrong account information; confirm the numbers with your financial institution.
## Refund timing and status
- e-File refunds are generally processed within 1 week but could take up to 4 weeks; paper returns generally within 8 weeks; Certified Mail can add 3 weeks. e-File with direct deposit is fastest.
- Fraud prevention reviews may delay a refund; answer any request to verify identity immediately.
- Check status at www.tax.virginia.gov or 804.367.2486.
## Refund offsets
Before issuing a refund, the Department checks for debts owed to Virginia agencies, Virginia local governments and the Virginia courts; all or part of the refund may be withheld and processing delayed. Eligible state tax debts may also be sent to the U.S. Treasury Offset Program to reduce federal refunds. If the same debt is collected twice, the excess is released automatically.
## Deceased taxpayers
A surviving spouse on a joint return may receive the refund by direct deposit. A refund payable to a decedent's estate is issued as a check, endorsed by the court-appointed personal representative. A refund is issued to the surviving spouse or the estate unless a properly completed federal Form 1310 is provided. See filing/filing-status.md.
## Reducing the refund on purpose
Line 29 credits part of the overpayment to 2027 estimated tax, and Lines 30-31 donate it through Schedule VAC; both reduce Line 36.
## Required Information
- Routing and account numbers and the account type, if direct deposit is wanted
- Any amount to credit to 2027 or to donate
## Questions
- Do you want direct deposit? Is the account at a U.S. bank?
- What are the routing and account numbers, and is it checking or savings?
- Do you owe any Virginia state agency, locality or court?
- Do you want to donate part of your refund or apply it to 2027?
## Common Errors
- Using the routing number from a deposit slip
- Including the check number in the account number
- Entering a foreign or international account
- Expecting direct deposit for a refund payable to an estate
