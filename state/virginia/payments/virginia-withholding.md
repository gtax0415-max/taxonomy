# Virginia Income Tax Withheld (Form 760, Lines 19a and 19b)
## Description
Lines 19a and 19b report the Virginia income tax withheld during 2026. Line 19a is your Virginia withholding from your W-2, 1099 and VK-1 forms; Line 19b, used only on a joint return (Filing Status 2), is your spouse's. Enclose copies of the Forms W-2, W-2G, 1099 and/or VK-1. Withholding is a payment, not a credit, so it is refunded in full if it exceeds the tax.
## What counts
- Virginia income tax shown on Forms W-2, W-2G, 1099 and Schedule VK-1.
- Only Virginia tax. Other states' withholding is not entered here; tax paid to another state may support the credit on Line 24 (Schedule OSC).
- Split by spouse on a joint return: each spouse's forms go on that spouse's line.
## Interaction with other lines
- Lines 19a and 19b go into Line 26 with estimated, prior year and extension payments and the credits on Lines 23-25.
- A resident below the filing threshold must file to get the withholding back (see filing/filing-requirements.md).
- For the estimated tax test on Form 760C, total withholding may be divided equally among the four installments, or entered as actually withheld with a schedule.
- Withholding is not a tax credit for the 760C or 760F liability line; it is subtracted as a payment.
## Withholding that does not belong on Form 760
- Tax withheld in error by an employer: file Form 763-S to obtain a refund.
- Military spouses and servicemembers who elected another state under the SCRA: tax withheld from SCRA-protected income is refunded on Form 763-S; Form VA-4 can stop future withholding.
- Nonresidents of Kentucky, DC, Maryland, Pennsylvania or West Virginia who meet the wage exceptions use Form 763-S.
## Required Information
- Virginia tax withheld on each W-2, W-2G, 1099 and VK-1, and whose form it is
- Any withholding taken in error or from SCRA-protected income
## Questions
- Do you have all your W-2s, W-2Gs, 1099s and VK-1s showing Virginia tax withheld?
- Which forms belong to you and which to your spouse?
- Did any employer withhold Virginia tax by mistake?
- Did you receive a Schedule VK-1 showing Virginia tax withheld?
## Common Errors
- Entering another state's withholding on Line 19a
- Putting both spouses' withholding on Line 19a, or using Line 19b on a separate return
- Forgetting VK-1 or 1099 withholding
- Not enclosing copies of the withholding statements
## Prompt
- Where do I put the Virginia tax from my W-2?
- My VK-1 shows Virginia tax withheld for me.
- My employer took out Virginia tax but I live in Maryland.
