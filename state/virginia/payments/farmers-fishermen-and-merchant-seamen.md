# Farmers, Fishermen and Merchant Seamen (Form 760 header oval; Form 760F)
## Description
Fill in the "Qualifying farmer, fisherman or merchant seaman" oval on page 1 if at least 2/3 of your gross income is from self-employment as a farmer, fisherman or merchant seaman. These taxpayers have a lighter estimated tax rule: the required payment is 66 2/3% of current year tax (not 90%), measured by withholding and estimated tax paid or credited by January 15, and no addition to tax at all if the return is filed and the tax paid in full by March 1. The addition to tax, if any, is computed on Form 760F instead of Form 760C.
## Tests (2025 Form 760F; confirm against the 2026 form)
- Test 1: gross income from self-employment as a farmer, fisherman and/or merchant seaman is at least two-thirds of annual gross income. For estimating purposes, the definition uses estimated Virginia gross income from those activities of at least 66 2/3% of total estimated Virginia gross income.
- Test 2: you filed Form 760 (or 760PY, 763 or 770) and paid the entire tax due by March 1 (the 2025 form says March 1, 2026).
- Both tests met: do not file Form 760F; no addition to tax. Test 1 only: use Form 760F. Test 1 not met: use Form 760C.
- No Form 760F is needed if you are not required to file a return. The 2025 form excuses tax of $150 or less. For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, the threshold is $1,000 (HB 1600, 2025 Acts, Chapter 725; Va. Code § 58.1-492). Confirm how the 2026 Form 760F prints it.
## Form 760F computation (2025 form; confirm against the 2026 form)
- Part I, exceptions: Exception 1, withholding and timely estimated payments at least equal the prior year tax after the Spouse Tax Adjustment and credits (Credit for Low-Income Individuals, credit for tax paid to another state, Schedule CR credits), with a 12-month prior return showing a liability. Exception 2, payments at least equal the prior year's tax recomputed at current rates and exemptions.
- Part II, underpayment: Line 3 current tax after the Spouse Tax Adjustment and credits (net tax less Form 760, Lines 23 through 25); Line 4 66 2/3% (.667) of Line 3; Line 5 prior year tax after STA and credits; Line 6 smaller of Lines 4 and 5; Line 7 withholding and estimated tax paid or credited by January 15; Line 8 underpayment.
- Part III, addition: Line 9 underpayment x Line 10 days after January 15 through the payment date or the due date, whichever is earlier x Line 11 daily rate. The 2025 form counted 106 days to May 1. For 2026 returns the period runs from January 15, 2027; May 1, 2027 is a Saturday, so confirm on the 2026 form whether the count ends May 1 or May 3. The 2025 form used .00025 (9% per annum). The rate for January to May 2027 will be the first-quarter 2027 Virginia rate, which had not been announced as of October 2026. Line 12 goes on the addition to tax line (Schedule ADJ, Line 18).
- Fiscal year filers adjust the dates and rate.
## Required Information
- Gross income from self-employed farming, fishing or merchant seaman work, and total gross income
- Withholding and estimated tax paid or credited by January 15, 2027
- Whether the return will be filed and paid in full by March 1, 2027
- Prior-year Virginia tax after credits
## Questions
- What share of your gross income came from farming, fishing or work as a merchant seaman?
- Were you self-employed in that work, or a wage employee?
- Will you file and pay in full by March 1?
- What Virginia tax was withheld or paid as estimates by January 15?
## Common Errors
- Filling in the oval for wage employees on farms or vessels rather than the self-employed
- Filing Form 760C with the 90% test when Form 760F applies
- Filing Form 760F when both tests are met (not needed)
- Completing Form 760F but not filling in the Line 32 oval and enclosing it

- I'm a commercial fisherman; do I have to pay quarterly estimates to Virginia?
- Most of my income is from my farm; what is Form 760F?
