# Voluntary Contributions and Commonwealth Savers Contributions (Schedule VAC; Form 760, Lines 30-31)
## Description
Schedule VAC has two parts. Section I sends all or part of the refund to existing Commonwealth Savers (formerly Virginia529) education accounts and ABLE accounts, and the total goes to Form 760, Line 30. Section II sends money to the voluntary contribution organizations, library foundations and public school foundations listed in the booklet, and the total goes to Form 760, Line 31. Both amounts are added on Form 760, Line 34, reducing the refund (Line 36) or adding to the amount owed (Line 35). A contribution is not a credit and does not reduce tax.

New for 2026: the Wildlife Corridor Grant Fund, added by HB 597 (2026 Acts, Chapter 636, effective July 1, 2026). The law allows both refund contributions and contributions paid with the return, so it belongs in Section C. Its Schedule VAC code had not been published as of October 2026; code 21 below is unconfirmed and must be checked against the 2026 Schedule VAC.
## Section I: Commonwealth Savers contributions (Form 760, Line 30)
- Section A: overpayment available = the overpayment on the return less the amount credited to next year's estimated tax (Form 760, Line 29). Section B contributions may not exceed it.
- Section B, Lines 1-5: for each account, the program type code, beneficiary's last name, account number and amount. CollegeAmerica and ABLEAmerica also need a routing number and the proper account number from the financial advisor. Invest529, Prepaid529 and CollegeWealth use the program account number; ABLEnow uses the ABLEnow account number.
- Line 6: total for all accounts → Form 760, Line 30. More than 5 accounts: use Schedule VACS and fill in the oval.

| Program type code | Program |
|---|---|
| 1 | Invest529 |
| 2 | Prepaid529 |
| 3 | CollegeWealth |
| 4 | CollegeAmerica or ABLEAmerica |
| 5 | ABLEnow |

Rules:
- Contributions are deemed made to the account for the 2026 taxable year.
- Accounts must already exist. CollegeWealth and Prepaid529 accept no new accounts, but existing owners may keep contributing.
- Anyone may direct a refund to an account. For education accounts, only the owner of record as of December 31 may take the Virginia deduction (Schedule ADJ code 104). For ABLEnow or ABLEAmerica, the contributor may take the deduction (code 115 for ABLEnow).
- By entering an amount, the filer authorizes the Department to send Commonwealth Savers the amounts, program information, and each spouse's name, SSN or tax ID, address and phone number. No interest accrues on the refund after the transfer. If Commonwealth Savers cannot match the account, it contacts the taxpayer and, failing that, returns the money to the address on the return.

### Schedule VACS
Supplemental schedule for more than five accounts. Lines 1-5 have the same fields as Section I. Line 6 totals them, and that total is included in Schedule VAC, Section I, Line 6. Use additional Schedules VACS if needed.
## Section II: other voluntary contributions (Form 760, Line 31)
- Section A, Line 1: overpayment available = overpayment less the amount credited to estimated tax and less the Section I contributions. Lines 2-7 may not exceed it.
- Section B, Lines 2-4 (organizations) and Lines 5-7 (library foundations): from the refund only.
- Section C, Lines 8-10 (organizations) and Lines 11-13 (public school foundations): from the refund OR as a payment, even if tax is owed or the gift is larger than the expected refund.
- Section D, Line 14: total of Lines 2-13 → Form 760, Line 31.
- More than 3 in a group: enter "00" (organizations) or "999999" (foundations) with the group total on the first line, and enclose a schedule of each code, name and amount.

## Required Information
- Expected overpayment and the amount to be credited to next year's estimated tax
- For each savings account: program, beneficiary's last name, account number and amount
- Routing number and advisor-supplied account number for CollegeAmerica or ABLEAmerica
- Owner of record of each education account as of December 31 (controls who may take the code 104 deduction)
- Each organization or foundation chosen, its code and amount
- Whether the return is an amended return
## Questions
- Is there an overpayment on Form 760, Line 28, and how much is going to next year's estimated tax (Line 29)?
- Do you want part of the refund in a Commonwealth Savers or ABLE account? Which program, beneficiary and account number?
- For CollegeAmerica or ABLEAmerica, do you have the account and routing numbers from your advisor?
- Do you want to give to any listed fund, library foundation or public school foundation? Which codes and amounts?
- If you owe tax, do you still want to give to a Section C organization or public school foundation?
- Is this an amended return?
## Common Errors
- Using a Section B organization or library foundation when there is no refund (only Section C and public school foundations can be paid when tax is owed)
- Contributions in Section I, or in Section II Lines 2-7, larger than the overpayment available
- Using a 2-digit code for a foundation (foundations use 6-digit codes), or using the wrong "more than 3" code ("00" for organizations, "999999" for foundations)
- Giving more than $25 to a political party, or to both parties
- Leaving out the routing number for CollegeAmerica or ABLEAmerica
- Directing a refund to a new CollegeWealth or Prepaid529 account (those programs accept no new accounts)
- Expecting a non-owner's education account refund contribution to give that person the code 104 deduction
- Filing Schedule VAC with an amended return
- Treating the contribution as a credit that lowers tax
