# Income (Form 760, Lines 1-9)
## Description
Virginia starts from federal adjusted gross income (Form 760, Line 1), adds items federal AGI leaves out that Virginia taxes (Line 2, from Schedule ADJ), and subtracts items federal AGI includes that Virginia does not tax (Lines 4-7). The result on Line 9 is Virginia adjusted gross income (VAGI), which decides whether a return must be filed and is the base for deductions, exemptions and the tax. Because Virginia starts from federal AGI, anything already in federal AGI is not restated; only the differences are reported.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1   Federal adjusted gross income (NOT federal taxable income)
Line 2   Additions from Schedule ADJ, Line 3
Line 3   Line 1 + Line 2
Line 4   Age deduction (you + spouse)
Line 5   Taxable Social Security and equivalent Tier 1 Railroad Retirement benefits
Line 6   State income tax refund or overpayment credit reported federally
Line 7   Subtractions from Schedule ADJ, Line 7
Line 8   Lines 4 + 5 + 6 + 7
Line 9   VAGI = Line 3 - Line 8
```
Schedule ADJ side:
- Lines 1-2c: additions (other state obligation interest, conformity additions, coded additions 10-99) → Line 3 → Form 760, Line 2
- Lines 4-6d: subtractions (U.S. obligation interest, disability income for you and spouse, conformity subtractions, coded subtractions 20-99) → Line 7 → Form 760, Line 7
- Schedule ADJS holds additional coded additions, subtractions or deductions when Schedule ADJ runs out of lines; its totals are included in Schedule ADJ Lines 3, 7 and 9
## Conformity for 2026
Virginia replaced rolling conformity with a fixed conformity date of December 31, 2025, and conforms to most of 2025 H.R. 1 (P.L. 119-21). It continues to deconform from bonus depreciation and from certain H.R. 1 business provisions, and for 2026 a section 179 conformity addition is required above the Virginia limits. New for 2026: a subtraction for IRC 280E-disallowed expenses of a Virginia-licensed cannabis business (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a). See Tax Bulletin 26-1.

## Key interactions
- The age deduction bars the disability income subtraction, the Credit for Low-Income Individuals and the refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit; married spouses each choose an age deduction or a disability subtraction
- The same income may not be used for more than one subtraction (for example military basic pay and National Guard)
- Line 9 below $11,950 (Filing Status 1 or 3) or $23,900 (Filing Status 2) means the tax is $0 (see filing/filing-requirements.md)
## Questions
- What is the adjusted gross income on your federal return?
- Did you have interest from other states' bonds, or U.S. Treasury interest?
- Were you (or your spouse) born on or before January 1, 1962?
- Did you receive Social Security, a state tax refund, unemployment, military or railroad retirement pay?
- Did you claim bonus depreciation or a large section 179 deduction federally?
