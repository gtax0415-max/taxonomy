# Certain Long-Term Capital Gains (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 52)
## Description
Long-term capital gain or investment services partnership income attributable to an investment in a "qualified business" as defined in Va. Code 58.1-339.4, or in any other technology business approved by the appropriate Secretariat, may be subtracted using code 52. Under current law the Secretary of Administration is the Secretariat charged with this approval.
## Requirements
- The business must have its principal facility in Virginia
- The business must have had less than $3 million in annual revenues for the fiscal year preceding the investment
- The investment must have been made between April 1, 2010 and June 30, 2020
- No investment is qualified if the business performs research in Virginia on human embryonic stem cells
## Mutually exclusive items
Taxpayers claiming the Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Credit cannot claim this subtraction for investments in the same business. (That credit expired January 1, 2026; unused credits may be carried over up to 15 taxable years.)
## What to enter
- The qualifying long-term capital gain or investment services partnership income
- Enter code 52 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Questions
- Did you sell stock or an interest in a small Virginia technology business in 2026 at a long-term gain?
- When did you make the investment?
- Did you (or do you carry over) the Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Credit for the same business?
## Common Errors
- Claiming gains on investments made outside the April 1, 2010 to June 30, 2020 window
- Claiming both this subtraction and the Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Credit for the same business
- Claiming short-term gains
## Prompt
- I sold my shares in a Virginia tech startup I invested in back in 2015.
