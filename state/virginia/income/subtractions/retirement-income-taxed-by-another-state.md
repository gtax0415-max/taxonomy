# Retirement Plan Income Previously Taxed by Another State (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 31)
## Description
Some taxpayers made retirement contributions that were deductible federally but were taxed by another state at the time. When the retirement income is received, Virginia allows a subtraction so the same contributions are not taxed twice, using code 31 (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 11). It applies to distributions from qualified plans under IRC 401, IRAs under IRC 408, deferred compensation plans under IRC 457, and federal government retirement programs.
## What to enter
- Retirement income received during the taxable year on which the contributions were taxed in another state, but were deductible from federal AGI during the same period
- The total subtraction cannot exceed the amount of the contributions previously taxed by another state (usually in a previous year)
- Enter code 31 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## What does not qualify
Virginia Supplemental Retirement (VRS) distributions do not qualify as previously taxed distributions for taxes paid to another state. Virginia does not previously tax contributions on the Virginia retirement plan.
## Required Information
- Total contributions taxed by the other state, and the years
- Amount of that basis subtracted in earlier Virginia years
- Taxable retirement distributions received in 2026
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a retirement plan while living or working in another state that taxed those contributions?
- Do you have records of the contributions taxed there?
- How much of that basis have you already subtracted in earlier years?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting more than the total contributions previously taxed by the other state
- Claiming the subtraction for VRS distributions
## Prompt
- I worked in a state that taxed my 401(k) contributions, and now I'm retired in Virginia.
