# Commonwealth Savers Income Distribution or Refund (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 34)
## Description
Virginia allows a subtraction for income included in federal AGI that is attributable to a distribution of benefits or a refund from a Commonwealth Savers prepaid tuition contract or savings trust account, using code 34 (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 12). For a refund, the subtraction is limited to refunds made because of the beneficiary's death, disability or receipt of a scholarship. These refunds can have taxable earnings federally, because the federal 10% additional tax is waived but the income tax is not.
## What to enter
- Income included in federal AGI that is attributable to a distribution of benefits or a refund from Commonwealth Savers
- For a refund, only in the event of the beneficiary's death, disability or receipt of a scholarship
- Enter code 34 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Related recapture
A refund for any other reason (not for qualified higher education expenses, death, disability or scholarship) triggers recapture of Virginia contribution deductions taken earlier (Schedule ADJ deduction code 104). See deductions/schedule-adj/commonwealth-savers-contributions.md.
## Required Information
- Earnings included in 2026 federal AGI from each Commonwealth Savers payment
- Whether each payment was a distribution of benefits or a refund, and the reason for any refund
## Questions
- Did you receive a distribution or refund from a Commonwealth Savers account in 2026?
- If it was a refund, was it because the beneficiary died, became disabled or received a scholarship?
- How much of it was taxable on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting refunds made for other reasons
- Subtracting the whole distribution rather than the income included in federal AGI
- Missing the recapture of earlier contribution deductions on a non-qualifying refund
## Prompt
- My child got a full scholarship and I took money out of the Commonwealth Savers plan.
