# Federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit Wages (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 21)
## Description
A business that claims the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit has wages eligible for that credit. Virginia allows the taxpayer to subtract the wages or salaries eligible for the federal credit that are included in federal adjusted gross income, using code 21.
## What to enter
- The amount of wages or salaries eligible for the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit included in federal AGI
- Do NOT enter the federal credit amount
- Enter code 21 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Questions
- Do you have a business (sole proprietorship or pass-through) that claimed the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit?
- What were the qualified wages behind the credit?
## Common Errors
- Entering the credit amount instead of the eligible wages
## Prompt
- My business took the federal Work Opportunity Credit for new hires.
