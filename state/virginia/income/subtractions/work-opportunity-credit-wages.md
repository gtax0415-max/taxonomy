# Federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit Wages (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 21)
## Description
A business that claims the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit must reduce its federal wage deduction by the amount of the credit (IRC 280C(a)). Virginia does not follow that reduction. It allows the taxpayer to subtract the wages eligible for the federal credit that were not deducted federally because of IRC 280C(a), using code 21 (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 6).
## 2026 status of the federal credit
The federal credit lapsed for individuals who begin work after December 31, 2025 and had not been reauthorized as of October 2026. For 2026 the subtraction can arise only from first-year wages of qualified employees who began work on or before December 31, 2025, or second-year wages of long-term TANF recipients. Congress has extended the credit retroactively in the past. If it does so again, 2026 hires would also qualify.
## What to enter
- The amount of wages or salaries eligible for the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit that was not deducted on the federal return because of IRC 280C(a)
- Because 280C(a) reduces the wage deduction by the credit, this usually equals the federal credit figured on Form 5884, not the total qualified wages
- Enter code 21 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Required Information
- Federal Work Opportunity Credit figured for 2026 and the wage deduction disallowed under 280C(a)
- Start date of each qualified employee (on or before December 31, 2025, unless the credit is extended)
- Share of the disallowed wages if from a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have a business (sole proprietorship or pass-through) that claimed the federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit for 2026?
- When did each qualified employee start work?
- By how much was the federal wage deduction reduced because of the credit?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the total qualified wages instead of the amount not deducted federally under 280C(a)
- Claiming it for employees who began work after December 31, 2025 while the federal credit has lapsed
## Prompt
- My business took the federal Work Opportunity Credit for new hires.
