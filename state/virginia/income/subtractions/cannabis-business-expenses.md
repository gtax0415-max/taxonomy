# Cannabis Business Expenses Disallowed by IRC 280E (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a)
## Description
New for 2026. For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, Virginia allows a deduction for ordinary and necessary expenditures made in connection with carrying on a trade or business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act (Va. Code 4.1-600 et seq.) that were disallowed under IRC 280E. The deduction is allowed even though the expenses are disallowed under federal law. It was enacted in the 2026 Appropriation Act (HB 30, Special Session I, Chapter 1, Enactment 5), which amended Virginia's conformity statute (§ 58.1-301). It removes from Virginia income expenses that the federal return did not allow.
## Who qualifies
- The trade or business must be licensed in Virginia pursuant to the Cannabis Control Act
- The expenditures must be ordinary and necessary expenses of carrying on that business
- The expenditures must have been disallowed federally under IRC 280E
- Taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026
Retail cannabis sales are authorized only from July 1, 2027, so in 2026 this mainly reaches businesses already licensed under the Act.
## Where it goes
Because the deduction is implemented through the conformity statute, it is treated as a conformity subtraction on Schedule ADJ, Line 6a. The Line 6a worksheet has two parts: A, bonus depreciation recomputation, and B, other conformity subtractions, which requires an enclosed schedule and explanation. The total (C) goes on Line 6a. Confirm the line placement against the final 2026 Schedule ADJ.
## Required Information
- License type, number and dates
- Expenses disallowed under 280E on the 2026 federal return, by category
- Ownership share if operated through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you own or operate a cannabis business, alone or through a pass-through entity?
- Is that business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act?
- Which expenses were disallowed on your federal return under IRC 280E?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the deduction for a business that is not licensed in Virginia
- Claiming expenses that were deducted federally (only 280E-disallowed amounts qualify)
- Entering the deduction as a coded subtraction (6b-6d) or a Schedule ADJ deduction code instead of Line 6a
- Claiming it for a taxable year beginning before January 1, 2026
## Prompt
- I run a licensed cannabis business in Virginia and couldn't deduct my expenses federally.
- Does Virginia let me deduct the costs that 280E disallows?
