# Cannabis Business Expenses Disallowed by IRC 280E (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a)
## Description
New for 2026. For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026, Virginia allows a deduction for ordinary and necessary expenditures made in connection with carrying on a trade or business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act (Va. Code 4.1-600 et seq.) that were disallowed under IRC 280E. It is claimed on Schedule ADJ, Line 6a, and removes from Virginia income expenses that the federal return did not allow.
## Who qualifies
- The trade or business must be licensed in Virginia pursuant to the Cannabis Control Act
- The expenditures must be ordinary and necessary expenses of carrying on that business
- The expenditures must have been disallowed federally under IRC 280E
- Taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026
## Where it goes
The 2026 Appropriation Act places the deduction on Line 6a of Schedule ADJ (conformity subtractions). The Line 6a worksheet has two parts: A, bonus depreciation recomputation, and B, other conformity subtractions, which requires an enclosed schedule and explanation. The total (C) goes on Line 6a.
## Questions
- Do you own or operate a cannabis business, alone or through a pass-through entity?
- Is that business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act?
- Which expenses were disallowed on your federal return under IRC 280E?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the deduction for a business that is not licensed in Virginia
- Claiming expenses that were deducted federally (only 280E-disallowed amounts qualify)
- Entering the deduction as a coded subtraction (6b-6d) or a Schedule ADJ deduction code instead of Line 6a
- Claiming it for a taxable year beginning before January 1, 2026
## Prompt
- I run a licensed cannabis business in Virginia and couldn't deduct my expenses federally.
- Does Virginia let me deduct the costs that 280E disallows?
