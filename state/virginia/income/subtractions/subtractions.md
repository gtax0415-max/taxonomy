# Subtractions from Federal Adjusted Gross Income (Form 760, Lines 4-9; Schedule ADJ, Lines 4-7)
## Description
Virginia removes from federal adjusted gross income items that federal AGI includes but Virginia does not tax. Three subtractions go directly on Form 760 (Line 4 age deduction, Line 5 taxable Social Security and Tier 1 railroad benefits, Line 6 state income tax refund). All others are reported on Schedule ADJ, Lines 4 through 6d, totaled on Schedule ADJ, Line 7, and carried to Form 760, Line 7. Form 760, Line 8 adds Lines 4 through 7, and Line 9 (Virginia adjusted gross income, VAGI) is Line 3 minus Line 8.
## How the lines flow
- Form 760, Line 4 = age deduction (You + Spouse)
- Form 760, Line 5 = taxable Social Security and equivalent Tier 1 Railroad Retirement benefits
- Form 760, Line 6 = state income tax refund or overpayment credit reported as income federally
- Schedule ADJ, Line 4 = U.S. obligation income; Lines 5a/5b = disability income (you/spouse); Line 6a = conformity subtractions; Lines 6b-6d = coded subtractions
- Schedule ADJ, Line 7 = Lines 4, 5a, 5b and 6a-6d → Form 760, Line 7
- Form 760, Line 8 = Lines 4 + 5 + 6 + 7
- Form 760, Line 9 = Line 3 - Line 8 = VAGI. If VAGI is less than $11,950 (Filing Status 1 or 3) or $23,900 (Filing Status 2), Virginia tax is $0.00; file to get a refund of any withholding or estimated tax.
## General rules
- A subtraction is allowed only to the extent the income is included in federal AGI. No amount previously excluded from FAGI can be subtracted.
- The same income may not be included in more than one subtraction.
- Schedule ADJ has three coded lines (6b-6d). With more than three coded subtractions, use Schedule ADJS (Lines 12-21, total on ADJS Line 22), include the ADJS total in Schedule ADJ, Line 7, fill in the oval at the bottom of Schedule ADJ, and enclose Schedule ADJS.
- Subtraction code 20 (Virginia obligations) is not addition code 20 (PTET add back).
## Military servicemembers
Virginia provides three military subtractions: combat zone pay (code 30), military basic pay (code 38) and Virginia National Guard pay (code 28). A servicemember may qualify for more than one, but the same income may not go in more than one; for example, the same pay cannot be deducted under both code 38 and code 28. Military retirement and related benefits are code 60 (and code 44 for Medal of Honor recipients).
## Items that block the income-based credits
The age deduction, code 28, code 38 and code 39 each bar the Credit for Low-Income Individuals and the Refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit. The age deduction and the disability subtraction cannot both be claimed by the same person.

## Questions
- Were you (or your spouse) born on or before January 1, 1962?
- Did you receive Social Security, railroad retirement, unemployment or a state tax refund?
- Do you own U.S. Treasury securities or Virginia bonds?
- Are you or your spouse in the military, the Virginia National Guard, or retired from the military?
- Are you permanently and totally disabled and receiving disability pay?
- Do you have depreciable assets that took bonus depreciation, or a licensed Virginia cannabis business?
## Prompt
- What income does Virginia not tax that the federal return does?
- I have more than three Virginia subtractions.
