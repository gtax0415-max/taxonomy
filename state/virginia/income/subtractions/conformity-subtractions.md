# Conformity Subtractions (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a)
## Description
Where Virginia's departures from the Internal Revenue Code make Virginia income lower than federal income, the difference is subtracted on Schedule ADJ, Line 6a. The main source is depreciation: assets that took federal bonus depreciation, or section 179 amounts that Virginia disallowed, have larger Virginia depreciation in later years. For 2026 the Line 6a subtraction also includes the new deduction for IRC 280E expenses of a Virginia-licensed cannabis business.
## Worksheet
- A. Bonus depreciation: if any asset received the 30% or 50% bonus deduction under IRC 168(k) in any year from 2001 through 2026, or bonus depreciation under IRC 168(l), 168(m), 1400L or 1400N, recompute depreciation as if no bonus was taken. If total 2026 Virginia depreciation is MORE than 2026 federal depreciation, the difference is the subtraction.
- B. Other conformity subtractions: the total of other conformity subtractions. Enclose a schedule and explanation.
- C. A + B → Schedule ADJ, Line 6a.
## Section 179 recovery (2026)
Virginia did not adopt the P.L. 119-21 increases to the IRC 179 limits. A federal section 179 deduction above $1,280,000 (reduced by property placed in service above $3,200,000) is added back on Line 2a. That addition increases the Virginia depreciable basis of the section 179 property, which increases the conformity depreciation subtraction for the remaining life of the property.
## Cannabis expenses (new for 2026)
The 2026 Appropriation Act allows a deduction on Line 6a for ordinary and necessary expenditures of a trade or business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act that were disallowed by IRC 280E. See cannabis-business-expenses.md.
## 2026 conformity context
Fixed IRC conformity date of December 31, 2025; conformity to most of P.L. 119-21, with the exceptions listed in conformity-additions.md. See Tax Bulletin 26-1 and www.tax.virginia.gov for any changes from the 2027 General Assembly Session.
## Interaction
The Age 65 and Older Deduction Worksheet subtracts conformity subtractions (worksheet Line 5) in computing AFAGI.
## Questions
- Did you take bonus depreciation on assets in earlier years that are still being depreciated?
- Did you have a Virginia section 179 addition in 2026 or earlier that created extra Virginia basis?
- Do you operate a Virginia-licensed cannabis business?
## Common Errors
- Stopping Virginia depreciation schedules after the bonus year, so the later-year subtraction is lost
- Netting additions and subtractions instead of reporting Line 2a and Line 6a separately
- Not enclosing a schedule and explanation for Part B
