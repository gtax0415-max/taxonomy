# Conformity Subtractions (Schedule ADJ, Line 6a)
## Description
Where Virginia's departures from the Internal Revenue Code make Virginia income lower than federal income, the difference is subtracted on Schedule ADJ, Line 6a. The main sources are depreciation and amortization timing differences. Assets that took federal bonus depreciation, qualified production property expensed under IRC 168(n), section 179 amounts above Virginia's limits, and R&E costs expensed federally under IRC 174A all produce larger Virginia deductions in later years. For 2026, Line 6a also includes the new deduction for IRC 280E expenses of a Virginia-licensed cannabis business.
## Worksheet
- A. Bonus depreciation: if any asset received bonus depreciation under IRC 168(k) in any year (at any rate, including the 100% rate restored by P.L. 119-21), or bonus depreciation under IRC 168(l), 168(m), 1400L or 1400N, recompute depreciation as if no bonus was taken. If total 2026 Virginia depreciation is MORE than 2026 federal depreciation, the difference is the subtraction.
- B. Other conformity subtractions: the total of other conformity subtractions. Enclose a schedule and explanation.
- C. A + B → Schedule ADJ, Line 6a.
## Recovery of 2025 H.R. 1 deconformity items (Part B)
Tax Bulletin 26-1 requires separate Virginia records for the P.L. 119-21 provisions Virginia does not follow:
- immediate expensing of qualified production property (IRC 168(n))
- immediate expensing of domestic R&E expenditures, including retroactive and catch-up deductions (IRC 174A); for Virginia these stay on the amortization schedule
- the increased section 179 limits

In any later year where the Virginia deduction exceeds the federal deduction, the difference is a "fixed date conformity subtraction." This happens, for example, when Virginia depreciation or amortization continues after federal full expensing.
## Section 179 recovery (2026)
Virginia did not adopt the P.L. 119-21 increases to the IRC 179 limits and applies the limits as they would be without that Act. A federal section 179 deduction above the Virginia limit is added back on Line 2a. That addition increases the Virginia depreciable basis of the section 179 property, which increases the conformity depreciation subtraction for the remaining life of the property. The Virginia dollar limit and phase-out threshold for 2026 (draft figures: $1,280,000, reduced by property placed in service above $3,200,000) have not been confirmed in published Virginia guidance; verify them against the final 2026 instructions.
## Cannabis expenses (new for 2026)
The 2026 Appropriation Act allows a deduction for ordinary and necessary expenditures of a trade or business licensed in Virginia under the Cannabis Control Act that were disallowed by IRC 280E. See cannabis-business-expenses.md.
## Not on this line: disallowed business interest
The Virginia deduction for 20% of business interest disallowed under IRC 163(j) is a Schedule ADJ deduction (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 15), not a conformity subtraction. For Taxable Year 2025 and later it is 20%, down from 50% for 2024.
## 2026 conformity context
Fixed IRC conformity date of December 31, 2025; conformity to most of P.L. 119-21, with the exceptions listed in income/additions/conformity-additions.md. Virginia continues to deconform from IRC 168(k) bonus depreciation. See Tax Bulletin 26-1 and www.tax.virginia.gov for any changes from the 2027 General Assembly Session.
## Interaction
The Age 65 and Older Deduction Worksheet subtracts conformity subtractions (worksheet Line 5) in computing AFAGI.
## Required Information
- Federal and Virginia depreciation for each asset that took bonus, 168(n) or excess section 179 expensing
- Federal and Virginia amortization of R&E costs expensed federally under 174A
- Licensed cannabis business expenses disallowed under 280E, if any
## Questions
- Did you take bonus depreciation on assets in earlier years that are still being depreciated?
- Did you have a Virginia section 179, 168(n) or 174A addition in 2025 or 2026 that created extra Virginia basis or amortization?
- Do you operate a Virginia-licensed cannabis business?
## Common Errors
- Stopping Virginia depreciation schedules after the bonus year, so the later-year subtraction is lost
- Netting additions and subtractions instead of reporting Line 2a and Line 6a separately
- Not enclosing a schedule and explanation for Part B
- Putting the 163(j) business interest amount here instead of as a deduction
