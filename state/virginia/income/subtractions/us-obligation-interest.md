# Income from Obligations of the U.S. (Schedule ADJ, Line 4)
## Description
Income from certain U.S. government obligations is taxable federally but exempt from state tax. Virginia subtracts it on Schedule ADJ, Line 4. The subtraction covers interest, dividends and gain from these obligations, to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income.
## Obligations whose income is NOT taxable in Virginia (subtract)
Tennessee Valley Authority; Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation; Federal Home Loan Bank; Federal Intermediate Credit Bank; Governments of Guam, Puerto Rico and Virgin Islands; U.S. Treasury bills, notes, bonds and savings bonds; Federal Land Bank; Federal Reserve Stock; Farm Credit Bank; Export-Import Bank of the U.S.; U.S. Postal Service; and Resolution Trust Corporation.
## Obligations whose income IS taxable in Virginia (do not subtract)
Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation, Federal National Mortgage Association, Government National Mortgage Association, Inter-American Development Bank, and International Bank for Reconstruction and Development.
## Questions
- Do you own U.S. Treasury bills, notes, bonds or savings bonds, or cashed savings bonds this year?
- Do you hold mutual funds that invest in U.S. government securities, and does the fund report the government share?
- Did you sell any of these obligations at a gain?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting Ginnie Mae, Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac income, which Virginia taxes
- Subtracting income that was not included in federal AGI
- Forgetting gains from sales of qualifying obligations
## Prompt
- I have Treasury bill interest. Does Virginia tax it?
- I cashed in old savings bonds.
