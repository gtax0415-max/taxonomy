# Gain from Eminent Domain (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 58)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2019, taxpayers may subtract any gain recognized from the taking of real property by condemnation proceedings, using code 58 (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 29).
## What to enter
- The gain recognized (included in federal AGI) from the taking of real property by condemnation
- Enter code 58 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Required Information
- Date of the taking and the condemning authority
- Gain recognized on the 2026 federal return (not gain deferred under IRC 1033)
## Questions
- Did a government take any of your real property through condemnation (eminent domain) in 2026?
- Did you recognize a gain on it on your federal return, or did you defer it?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting gain that was not recognized federally
- Applying it to sales that were not condemnation proceedings, or to property other than real property
## Prompt
- VDOT took part of my land for a road widening and paid me more than my basis.
