# First-Time Home Buyer Savings Account Income (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 54)
## Description
An individual may subtract income attributable to a designated first-time home buyer savings account that was taxed as interest, capital gains or other income for federal purposes, to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income, using code 54. Losses on the account deducted federally are added back under addition code 17.
## Use of the funds and recapture
- Distributions may only be used to pay or reimburse the down payment and allowable closing costs for the purchase of a single-family residence in Virginia by a qualified beneficiary
- Subtractions claimed by the account holder in all prior years are recaptured in the year account funds are withdrawn for any other purpose, even if the withdrawal occurs after Virginia's 3-year statute of limitations
## Designating the account
Designate the account by submitting documentation with the Virginia return for the first year you claim the subtraction, for each account designated:
- Name and address of the financial institution
- Names of any other individuals with an ownership interest
- Account number or other identifier
- Type of principal (cash or marketable securities) contributed as of the last day of the year
- Principal and interest in the account as of the last day of the year
- Withdrawals during the year
- The account beneficiary or beneficiaries
Updated information must be included for every later year in which a Virginia return is required, for each designated account.

## Questions
- Have you designated a savings account for buying a first home in Virginia?
- What interest, dividends or gains did the account earn in 2026?
- Did you withdraw funds for anything other than a down payment or closing costs?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the subtraction without filing the designation documentation
- Not updating account information in later years
- Forgetting recapture after a non-qualifying withdrawal
- Subtracting income but not adding back losses (code 17)
## Prompt
- I'm saving for my first house in a special Virginia savings account.
