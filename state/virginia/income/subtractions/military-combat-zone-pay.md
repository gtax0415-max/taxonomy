# Military Pay and Allowances in a Combat Zone or Qualified Hazardous Duty Area (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 30)
## Description
Virginia allows a subtraction for military pay and allowances earned while serving, by order of the President of the United States with the consent of Congress, in a combat zone or a qualified hazardous duty area treated as a combat zone for federal tax purposes under IRC 112. It is one of Virginia's three military subtractions (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 13). Enlisted combat pay is usually already excluded from federal AGI, so this subtraction mostly affects officer pay above the federal exclusion cap.
## What to enter
- Military pay and allowances earned while serving in a combat zone or qualified hazardous duty area
- Only to the extent included in federal AGI and not otherwise subtracted, deducted or exempted
- Enter code 30 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Interaction with other military subtractions
A servicemember may qualify for more than one military subtraction, but the same income may not be included in more than one.
## Required Information
- Dates of service in the combat zone or qualified hazardous duty area
- Pay and allowances for that period included in federal AGI (W-2 Box 1, not the Box 12 code Q excluded amount)
## Questions
- Did you serve in a designated combat zone or qualified hazardous duty area in 2026?
- How much of that pay is included in your federal AGI?
- Are you also claiming military basic pay (code 38) or National Guard pay (code 28)?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting combat pay that was already excluded from federal AGI
- Counting the same pay under code 30 and another military subtraction
## Prompt
- I was deployed to a combat zone for part of the year.
