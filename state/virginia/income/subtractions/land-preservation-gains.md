# Gains from Land Preservation (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 51)
## Description
Taxpayers who sell or transfer Virginia Land Preservation Tax Credits may recognize a gain or loss federally. Virginia does not count it: to the extent federal gain includes gain or loss recognized on the sale or transfer of a Land Preservation Tax Credit, the taxpayer subtracts the gain, or adds back the loss, on the Virginia return. Gain or income recognized on applying the credit against Virginia tax is also subtracted, to the extent it is in federal AGI. The subtraction uses code 51.
## What to enter
- Gain recognized federally on the sale or transfer of a Land Preservation Tax Credit
- Enter code 51 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## If there was a loss
The instructions require a recognized loss on the sale or transfer of the credit to be added back on the Virginia return. They do not say which addition line or code to use for it.
## Required Information
- Date, price and number of credits sold or transferred in 2026
- Federal gain or loss reported on the sale
## Questions
- Did you sell or transfer Land Preservation Tax Credits in 2026?
- Did the sale produce a gain or a loss on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the federal gain in Virginia income
- Subtracting a loss (it must be added back instead)
## Prompt
- I sold my land preservation credits to another taxpayer at a gain.
