# Income Received by Holocaust Victims (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 40)
## Description
To the extent included in federal adjusted gross income, Virginia allows a subtraction for income resulting from the return or replacement of assets stolen during the Holocaust and throughout the period leading up to, during and directly after World War II, and for compensation for forced labor performed under threat of death (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 19), using code 40.
## Qualifying causes
Assets stolen as a result of:
- Nazi persecution
- An individual being forced into labor against their will
- Transactions with or actions of the Nazi regime
- Treatment of refugees fleeing Nazi persecution
- Holding of such assets by entities or persons in the Swiss Confederation
## Who may claim
Only the first recipient of the income who was a victim or target of Nazi persecution, or that person's spouse, surviving spouse, child or stepchild. The subtraction does not extend to assets later bought with the proceeds.
## What to enter
- The income from the return or replacement of those assets, to the extent in federal AGI
- Enter code 40 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Required Information
- Nature and amount of the payment or returned asset, and the amount in 2026 federal AGI
- Relationship of the recipient to the victim
## Questions
- Did you receive a payment, settlement or returned property related to assets taken during the Holocaust or World War II era?
- Was any of it reported as income on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting amounts that were not included in federal AGI
- Subtracting income from assets bought with the proceeds
## Prompt
- My family received a restitution payment for property seized by the Nazis.
