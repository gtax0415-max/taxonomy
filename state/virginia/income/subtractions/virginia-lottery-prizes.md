# Virginia Lottery Prizes (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 24)
## Description
Virginia does not tax small Virginia Lottery prizes (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 7). The sum of all prizes under $600 awarded by the Virginia Lottery is subtracted, to the extent included in federal adjusted gross income, using code 24.
## What to enter
- The sum of all prizes under $600 awarded to you by the Virginia Lottery
- Only to the extent you included them in federal AGI
- Enter code 24 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## What does not qualify
The code covers only prizes under $600 awarded by the Virginia Lottery. Prizes of $600 or more, other states' lotteries, casino and sports wagering winnings are not included.
## Required Information
- Each Virginia Lottery prize under $600 won in 2026 and the total
- Whether those prizes were reported on the federal return
## Questions
- Did you win Virginia Lottery prizes of less than $600 each and report them on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Including prizes of $600 or more
- Subtracting prizes that were never reported as federal income
- Including casino, sports betting or other states' lottery winnings
## Prompt
- I won a few hundred dollars on Virginia Lottery scratchers.
