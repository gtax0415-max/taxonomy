# Certain Death Benefit Payments (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 49)
## Description
A beneficiary may subtract death benefit payments received from an annuity contract that are subject to federal income tax, using code 49. The subtraction applies for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2007.
## Requirements (all three)
1. The death benefit payment is made under an annuity contract with an insurance company
2. The payment is awarded to the beneficiary in a lump sum
3. The payment is subject to taxation at the federal level
## What does not qualify
Payments from a retirement plan. Payments taken other than in a lump sum.
## What to enter
- The federally taxable lump-sum death benefit from the annuity contract
- Enter code 49 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Questions
- Did you inherit an annuity from an insurance company after the owner died?
- Was the death benefit paid to you in one lump sum?
- Was any of it taxable on your federal return?
- Is the payment actually from a retirement plan (401(k), IRA, pension)?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting inherited retirement plan distributions
- Subtracting annuity death benefits paid in installments rather than as a lump sum
## Prompt
- My mother's annuity paid me a lump-sum death benefit and part of it was taxable.
