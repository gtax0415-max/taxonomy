# Congressional Medal of Honor Recipients (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 44)
## Description
An individual awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor may subtract all of the military retirement income they received (no dollar limit), using code 44.
## What to enter
- The amount of military retirement income received as an individual awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor
- Enter code 44 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Interaction with the military benefits subtraction
The same income may not be included in more than one subtraction. The military benefits subtraction (code 60) is not allowed for income on which another credit, exemption, subtraction or deduction is claimed, so military retirement already subtracted under code 44 cannot also be claimed under code 60.
## Required Information
- Military retirement income included in federal AGI for 2026
- Proof of the Medal of Honor award
## Questions
- Were you awarded the Congressional Medal of Honor?
- How much military retirement income did you receive in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the same retirement pay under code 44 and code 60
## Prompt
- I'm a Medal of Honor recipient receiving military retirement pay.
