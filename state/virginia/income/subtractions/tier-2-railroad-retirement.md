# Tier 2 and Other Railroad Retirement and Railroad Unemployment Benefits (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 22)
## Description
Railroad retirement benefits beyond the Tier 1 social security equivalent portion are taxed federally as a pension or annuity, but federal law (45 U.S.C. 231m and 352) bars states from taxing them. They are subtracted with code 22. Tier 1 benefits go instead on Form 760, Line 5.
## What to enter
- Tier 2 benefits, vested dual benefits and other Railroad Retirement Act benefits, and Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act benefits
- Only amounts included in federal AGI and reported on the federal return as a taxable pension or annuity
- Enter code 22 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Required Information
- Taxable Tier 2, vested dual benefit and supplemental annuity amounts for 2026
- Railroad unemployment or sickness benefits included in federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you receive railroad retirement benefits reported on an RRB-1099-R?
- Did you receive railroad unemployment benefits?
## Common Errors
- Putting Tier 2 benefits on Form 760, Line 5 (Tier 1 only)
- Subtracting the same Tier 1 amount on both Line 5 and code 22
## Prompt
- I'm a retired railroad worker with Tier 2 benefits.
