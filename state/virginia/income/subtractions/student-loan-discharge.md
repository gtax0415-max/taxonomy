# Discharge of Student Loans (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 55)
## Description
Effective for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2015, Virginia allows a subtraction, using code 55, for income attributable to the discharge of a student loan solely due to the student's death (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 26).
## 2026 federal interaction
P.L. 119-21 made the federal exclusion permanent for student loans discharged after December 31, 2025 because of the student's death or total and permanent disability (IRC 108(f)(5)). The exclusion requires the student's Social Security number on the return, and Virginia conforms to it. In most 2026 death discharges the amount is therefore already outside federal AGI and there is nothing to subtract. Code 55 matters for 2026 mainly if:
- the federal exclusion is lost, for example because the required SSN was not provided, or
- a qualifying death discharge is otherwise included in federal AGI.
## Which loans qualify
"Student loan" has the same meaning as under IRC 108(f): a loan to an individual to help them attend an educational organization, made by:
- The United States, or an instrumentality or agency of it
- A state, territory or possession of the United States, the District of Columbia, or any political subdivision of these
- Certain tax-exempt public benefit corporations that have assumed control over a state, county or municipal hospital and whose employees are deemed public employees under state law
- Educational organizations, if the loan was made under an agreement with one of the entities above, or under a program designed to encourage its students to serve in occupations or areas with unmet needs, where the services are for or under the direction of a governmental unit or certain tax-exempt organizations
## What does not qualify
- Private loans that do not meet the IRC 108(f) definition
- Discharges already excluded from federal income
- Discharges for reasons other than the student's death
## What to enter
- Income from the qualifying discharge included in federal AGI
- Enter code 55 on Line 6b, 6c or 6d, followed by the amount
## Required Information
- Discharge date and amount, lender type, and reason (death of the student)
- Whether the discharge was included in 2026 federal AGI, and why
## Questions
- Was a student loan forgiven because the student died?
- Who made the loan (government, qualifying hospital corporation, school program, or private lender)?
- Was the discharged amount included in your federal income, or excluded under IRC 108(f)(5)?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a discharged loan that does not meet the student loan definition
- Subtracting a discharge already excluded from federal income
- Subtracting a disability or other non-death discharge
## Prompt
- My son passed away and his student loan was forgiven; I got a 1099-C.
