# Social Security and Tier 1 Railroad Retirement Benefits (Form 760, Line 5)
## Description
Virginia does not tax Social Security benefits or the equivalent Tier 1 Railroad Retirement Act benefits (Va. Code § 58.1-322.02, subdivision 3). Whatever portion was taxable on the federal return, and so is in federal adjusted gross income, is subtracted in full on Form 760, Line 5.
## What to enter
- The amount of taxable Social Security and/or Tier 1 Railroad Retirement Act benefits included in federal AGI
- Only the TAXABLE amount from the federal return, not the gross benefits received
## What does not go here
Tier 2 Railroad Retirement benefits and other Railroad Retirement and Railroad Unemployment benefits are not entered on Line 5. They may qualify as subtraction code 22 on Schedule ADJ.
## Interaction with other lines
- Disability benefits paid by Social Security: the taxable portion is already subtracted on Line 5, so do not also subtract it as disability income on Schedule ADJ, Line 5.
- The Age 65 and Older Deduction Worksheet removes taxable Social Security and Tier 1 benefits from FAGI in computing AFAGI (worksheet Line 7).
## Required Information
- Taxable Social Security and Tier 1 amount included in 2026 federal AGI, for each spouse
## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security retirement, survivor or disability benefits in 2026?
- Did you receive railroad retirement benefits, and does your RRB-1099 show Tier 1 amounts?
- How much of those benefits was taxable on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Entering gross benefits rather than the federally taxable amount
- Including Tier 2 railroad benefits on Line 5 instead of code 22
- Subtracting Social Security disability benefits twice (Line 5 and Schedule ADJ, Line 5)
## Prompt
- Does Virginia tax my Social Security?
- Part of my railroad retirement is Tier 1.
