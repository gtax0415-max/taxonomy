# Virginia National Guard Income (Schedule ADJ, Lines 6b-6d, code 28)
## Description
Members of the Virginia National Guard may subtract wages or salaries for active and inactive service that are included in federal adjusted gross income, using code 28. It is one of three military subtractions Virginia provides.
## Limit
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2023, for persons of rank O6 and below, the subtraction may not exceed income received for 39 days or $5,500, whichever is less.
## Who does not qualify
Members of the active or reserve units of the Army, Navy, Air Force or Marines, or the National Guard of other states or the District of Columbia.
## Mutually exclusive items
- If you claim this subtraction, you cannot claim the Credit for Low-Income Individuals or the Refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit.
- The same income may not be included in more than one subtraction; for example, the same pay cannot be deducted under both this subtraction and military basic pay (code 38).
## Questions
- Are you in the Virginia National Guard (not another state's Guard or a federal reserve unit)?
- What is your rank, and how many days of pay did you receive?
- Are you also claiming military basic pay or combat zone subtractions?
- Are you claiming the Virginia low-income credit or earned income credit?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the lesser of 39 days of pay or $5,500 (rank O6 and below)
- Claiming it for federal reserve or other-state Guard service
- Subtracting the same pay under code 28 and code 38
- Claiming the low-income credit or refundable Virginia EIC along with this subtraction
## Prompt
- I drill with the Virginia National Guard.
