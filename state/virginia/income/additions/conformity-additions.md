# Conformity Additions (Schedule ADJ, Line 2a)
## Description
Virginia follows the Internal Revenue Code only as of a fixed date and with listed exceptions. Where a federal deduction that Virginia does not allow reduced federal AGI, the difference is added back on Schedule ADJ, Line 2a. For 2026 the main items are recomputed depreciation for assets that took bonus depreciation, and the new section 179 addition.
## 2026 conformity
- The 2026 Amendments to the 2025 Appropriation Act replaced Virginia's suspended rolling conformity with a fixed conformity date of December 31, 2025, with automatic conformity to later federal amendments that only extend the expiration date of a provision Virginia already follows.
- Virginia conforms to most of the 2025 federal Budget Reconciliation Act (H.R. 1, P.L. 119-21), except as noted. See Tax Bulletin 26-1.
- Virginia deconforms from: immediate expensing of qualified production property; immediate expensing of domestic research and experimental expenditures (including retroactive and catch-up provisions); and the increases to the expensing limits of certain depreciable assets.
- Virginia continues to deconform from: bonus depreciation; the five-year carryback of certain federal NOLs generated in 2008 or 2009; applicable high yield discount obligations; cancellation of debt income from certain business debts; certain business provisions of the federal CARES Act; and deduction of business expenses through certain COVID-related small business assistance programs before Taxable Year 2021.
## Worksheet
- A. Bonus depreciation: if any asset received the 30% or 50% bonus deduction under IRC 168(k) in any year from 2001 through 2026, or bonus depreciation under IRC 168(l), 168(m), 1400L or 1400N, recompute depreciation as if no bonus was taken. If total 2026 Virginia depreciation is LESS than 2026 federal depreciation, the difference is the addition.
- B. Other conformity additions: the total of any other conformity additions listed in the Conformity Update for 2026. Enclose a schedule and explanation.
- C. A + B → Schedule ADJ, Line 2a.
## Section 179 (new for 2026)
Virginia has not conformed to the P.L. 119-21 changes to IRC 179 (the higher dollar limit and the higher phase-out threshold). A section 179 conformity addition is required to the extent a federal section 179 deduction greater than $1,280,000 was claimed, with that limit reduced (not below zero) by the amount of section 179 property placed in service during 2026 over $3,200,000. The addition increases the Virginia depreciable basis of the property, which increases the conformity depreciation subtraction (Line 6a) over the property's remaining life.
## Interaction
- The Age 65 and Older Deduction Worksheet adds conformity additions to FAGI (worksheet Line 3) when computing AFAGI.
- The business interest difference is not a Line 2a item: Virginia allows a deduction of 20% of federally disallowed business interest as deduction code 116.
## Questions
- Have you ever claimed bonus depreciation on business or rental property?
- Did you elect section 179 expensing for 2026, and how much property did you place in service?
- Did you expense qualified production property or research and experimental costs federally?
## Common Errors
- Using federal depreciation on the Virginia return without recomputing
- Forgetting that the addition in early years reverses as a subtraction later (Line 6a)
- Not enclosing a schedule and explanation for Part B items
## Prompt
- I took bonus depreciation on equipment. What does Virginia do?
- I expensed $1.5 million under section 179.
