# Conformity Additions (Schedule ADJ, Line 2a)
## Description
Virginia follows the Internal Revenue Code only as of a fixed date and with listed exceptions. Where a federal deduction that Virginia does not allow reduced federal AGI, the difference is added back on Schedule ADJ, Line 2a. For 2026 the main items are:
- recomputed depreciation for assets that took bonus depreciation
- the additions for the P.L. 119-21 provisions Virginia does not follow: section 179 above the Virginia limit, qualified production property expensing, and domestic R&E expensing
## 2026 conformity
- The 2026 Amendments to the 2025 Appropriation Act (HB 29, Chapter 7) replaced Virginia's rolling conformity with a fixed conformity date of December 31, 2025. Virginia still conforms automatically to later federal amendments that only extend the expiration date of a provision it already follows.
- Virginia conforms to most of the 2025 federal Budget Reconciliation Act (H.R. 1, P.L. 119-21), except as noted. See Tax Bulletin 26-1.
- Virginia deconforms from: immediate expensing of qualified production property (IRC 168(n)); immediate expensing of domestic research and experimental expenditures (IRC 174A, including retroactive and catch-up provisions); and the increases to the IRC 179 expensing limits.
- Virginia continues to deconform from:
  - bonus depreciation
  - the five-year carryback of certain federal NOLs generated in 2008 or 2009
  - applicable high yield discount obligations
  - cancellation of debt income from certain business debts
  - certain business provisions of the federal CARES Act
  - deduction of business expenses through certain COVID-related small business assistance programs before Taxable Year 2021
- The itemized-deduction exceptions (the Pease limitation and the medical expense floor) are handled in the deduction computation, not on Line 2a.
## Worksheet
- A. Bonus depreciation: if any asset received bonus depreciation under IRC 168(k) in any year (at any rate, including the 100% rate restored by P.L. 119-21), or bonus depreciation under IRC 168(l), 168(m), 1400L or 1400N, recompute depreciation as if no bonus was taken. If total 2026 Virginia depreciation is LESS than 2026 federal depreciation, the difference is the addition.
- B. Other conformity additions: the total of any other conformity additions, including the "fixed date conformity additions" described in Tax Bulletin 26-1 for 168(n), 174A and section 179. Enclose a schedule and explanation.
- C. A + B → Schedule ADJ, Line 2a.
## Fixed date conformity additions (P.L. 119-21 items)
Tax Bulletin 26-1 requires separate Virginia records for these provisions, computed as if P.L. 119-21 had not been enacted. In any year the federal deduction exceeds the Virginia deduction (for example, federal immediate expensing), the excess is added back. The Virginia amount later becomes larger than the federal amount, and the difference is then subtracted on Line 6a. This applies to:
- qualified production property expensed under 168(n), which Virginia depreciates instead
- domestic R&E costs expensed under 174A, including 2025 catch-up deductions of previously capitalized costs, which Virginia keeps on the amortization schedule
## Section 179
Virginia has not conformed to the P.L. 119-21 changes to IRC 179 (the higher dollar limit and the higher phase-out threshold). A section 179 conformity addition is required to the extent the federal section 179 deduction exceeds the amount allowed under the limits as they would apply without P.L. 119-21. The draft 2026 instructions state that limit as $1,280,000, reduced (not below zero) by the cost of section 179 property placed in service during 2026 over $3,200,000. Those dollar figures had not been confirmed in published Virginia guidance as of October 2026; verify them against the final 2026 instructions. The addition increases the Virginia depreciable basis of the property, which increases the conformity depreciation subtraction (Line 6a) over the property's remaining life.
## Interaction
- The Age 65 and Older Deduction Worksheet adds conformity additions to FAGI (worksheet Line 3) when computing AFAGI.
- The business interest difference is not a Line 2a item: Virginia allows a deduction of 20% of federally disallowed business interest for 2025 and later (Va. Code § 58.1-322.03, subdivision 15), as a Schedule ADJ deduction (code 116 in this taxonomy; confirm on the final 2026 Schedule ADJ).
## Required Information
- Federal and Virginia depreciation for every asset that took bonus or 168(n) expensing
- Federal section 179 deduction and the cost of section 179 property placed in service in 2026
- R&E expenditures deducted federally under 174A in 2026, and any catch-up deductions
- Any other Part B item, with an explanation
## Questions
- Have you ever claimed bonus depreciation on business or rental property?
- Did you elect section 179 expensing for 2026, and how much property did you place in service?
- Did you expense qualified production property or research and experimental costs federally?
## Common Errors
- Using federal depreciation on the Virginia return without recomputing
- Forgetting that the addition in early years reverses as a subtraction later (Line 6a)
- Not enclosing a schedule and explanation for Part B items
- Following the federal 174A catch-up deduction for Virginia
## Prompt
- I took bonus depreciation on equipment. What does Virginia do?
- I expensed $1.5 million under section 179.
