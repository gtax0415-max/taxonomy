# First-Time Home Buyer Savings Account Loss (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 17)
## Description
Virginia lets an account holder subtract income earned in a designated first-time home buyer savings account (subtraction code 54). The mirror rule: to the extent excluded from federal adjusted gross income, an account holder must add back any loss attributable to the account that was deducted as a capital loss for federal income tax purposes, using addition code 17.
## What to enter
- Any loss attributable to the first-time home buyer savings account that was deducted as a capital loss on the federal return
- Enter code 17 on Line 2b or 2c, followed by the amount
## More information
The First-Time Home Buyer Savings Account Guidelines are in the Laws, Rules, and Decisions section of www.tax.virginia.gov. Account designation, annual reporting and recapture rules are summarized in first-time-home-buyer-account-income.md.
## Questions
- Have you designated an account as a Virginia first-time home buyer savings account?
- Did investments in that account produce a capital loss on your federal return this year?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the account's income (code 54) but not adding back its losses
- Adding back losses from ordinary brokerage accounts that were never designated
## Prompt
- My first-time home buyer savings account lost money in the market this year.
