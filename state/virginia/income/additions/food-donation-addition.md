# Food Donation Addition (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 18)
## Description
Virginia gives a credit for growing food crops or producing wholesome foods in the Commonwealth and donating them to a nonprofit food bank (available for taxable years 2023 through 2027). A taxpayer may not also keep the federal deduction for the same donation in Virginia income. To the extent the credit is allowed, any amount claimed as a federal income tax deduction for the donation must be added back using addition code 18.
## What to enter
- The amount claimed as a federal income tax deduction for the donation, to the extent a Virginia food donation credit is allowed
- Enter code 18 on Line 2b or 2c, followed by the amount
## Who has it
Only taxpayers who claim the Food Donation credit on Schedule CR AND deducted the same donation on the federal return. If no Virginia credit is claimed, no addition is required.
## Required Information
- Donations for which the Virginia credit was certified
- Federal deduction claimed for those same donations
## Questions
- Did you donate crops you grew or foods you produced in Virginia to a nonprofit food bank?
- Are you claiming the Virginia Food Donation credit on Schedule CR?
- Did you deduct the donation on your federal return (itemized charitable deduction or a business deduction)?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit while leaving the federal deduction in Virginia income
- Making the addition when no Virginia credit was claimed
## Prompt
- I donated produce from my farm to the food bank and got a Virginia credit.
