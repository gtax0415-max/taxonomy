# Pass-Through Entity Tax Add Back (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 20)
## Description
When a qualifying pass-through entity pays the Virginia elective tax, the owners receive a Schedule CR credit, and any deduction the entity took for state and local income taxes has already reduced their federal AGI. An owner who claims the Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment Credit on Schedule CR must add back their pro-rata share of any deduction for state and local income taxes paid by the entity, using addition code 20.
## What to enter
- Your pro-rata share of any deduction for state and local income taxes paid by the qualifying pass-through entity
- Required if you claim the Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment Credit on Schedule CR
- Enter code 20 on Line 2b or 2c, followed by the amount
## Code 20 appears twice
Addition code 20 (this item, Lines 2b-2c) is different from subtraction code 20, Income from Virginia Obligations (Lines 6b-6d). Enter this add-back in the additions section only.
## 2026 note
The 2026 Amendments to the 2025 Appropriation Act (HB 29, Chapter 7) made the Virginia elective pass-through entity tax and its credits permanent. Under prior law they would have expired January 1, 2027. The add-back therefore continues for 2026 and later years.
## Required Information
- PTET credit on your Form 502 Schedule VK-1
- Your share of the entity's deduction for state and local income taxes in 2026
## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a partnership, S corporation or LLC that elected to pay the Virginia pass-through entity tax?
- Are you claiming the PTET credit on Schedule CR?
- What is your share of the state and local income tax the entity deducted?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the PTET credit without adding back the entity's tax deduction
- Entering the add-back with code 20 in the subtraction section
- Adding back the full entity deduction instead of your pro-rata share
## Prompt
- My S corporation paid the Virginia PTET and I got a credit on my K-1.
