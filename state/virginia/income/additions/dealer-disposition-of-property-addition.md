# Income from Dealer Disposition of Property (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 14)
## Description
Federal law does not let dealers use installment reporting for certain sales, so the full gain is in federal income in the year of sale. Virginia lets the dealer elect installment treatment for Virginia purposes for dispositions made on or after January 1, 2009. The gain removed in the year of sale is then added back, year by year, as payments are received, using addition code 14.
## What to enter
- The amount that would be reported under the installment method from certain dispositions of property
- Applies in the years following the year of disposition, when in a prior year the taxpayer was allowed the Virginia adjustment for income from a dealer disposition made on or after January 1, 2009
- Each disposition must be tracked separately
## The two halves
| Year | Adjustment | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Year of disposition | Gain attributable to installment payments to be made in future years is removed | Schedule ADJ, Lines 8a-8c, deduction code 113 |
| Later years | Gain attributable to payments made during the year is added back | Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, addition code 14 |
The year-of-disposition adjustment requires that (i) federal law does not permit the dealer to elect installment reporting for the sale, and (ii) the dealer elects installment treatment for Virginia on or before the return due date.
## Required Information
- For each disposition: date, contract price, gross profit percentage, and the amount removed in the year of sale
- Principal received on each note in 2026
## Questions
- Did you take the dealer disposition adjustment (code 113) on an earlier Virginia return?
- What installment payments did you receive in 2026 on each of those dispositions?
## Common Errors
- Tracking several dispositions together instead of separately
- Forgetting the add-back in later years after taking the year-of-sale adjustment
- Entering the add-back as a deduction code instead of addition code 14
## Prompt
- I sold lots as a dealer and elected installment treatment for Virginia last year; now payments are coming in.
