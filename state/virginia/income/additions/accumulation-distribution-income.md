# Accumulation Distribution Income (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 11)
## Description
A trust beneficiary who receives an accumulation distribution computes a separate partial federal tax on it on federal Form 4970. Because that income is taxed outside the regular federal computation, Virginia requires the beneficiary to add it to federal adjusted gross income using addition code 11. Accumulation distributions arise mainly from foreign trusts and certain older domestic trusts.
## What to enter
- The taxable income used to compute the partial tax on an accumulated distribution, as reported on federal Form 4970
- Enter code 11 on Line 2b or 2c, followed by the amount
## Related item
Schedule CR includes a separate Trust Beneficiary Accumulation Distribution Tax Credit. It is a credit, not part of this addition; see the linked file for how it is claimed.
## Required Information
- Form 4970 taxable income used to compute the partial tax for 2026
## Questions
- Are you the beneficiary of a trust that distributed income it had accumulated in earlier years?
- Did you file federal Form 4970 for 2026?
## Common Errors
- Omitting the addition because the income does not appear in federal AGI
- Entering the Form 4970 tax rather than the taxable income used to compute it
## Prompt
- A family trust distributed several years of accumulated income to me and I filed Form 4970.
