# Communities of Opportunity Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 19, Line 19D)
## Description
A credit for participating landlords renting qualified housing units to tenants in the housing choice voucher program, equal to 10% of the fair market rent for the period rented (Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:04, administered by DHCD). The credit was available for taxable years beginning before January 1, 2026; 2024 legislation had extended the sunset by one year to that date, and no further extension was enacted. Taxpayers cannot earn it for 2026. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use (no current-year credit)
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 19D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification
No new credit can be authorized for 2026; the carryover must come from a credit DHCD certified for an earlier year. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit.
## Required Information
- DHCD-certified credit amount and the year it was earned
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Ownership share if allocated through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have unused communities of opportunity credit from 2025 or earlier, and from which year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a 2026 credit on Line A (it is reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 years
## Prompt
- I had a communities of opportunity credit last year that I could not fully use.
