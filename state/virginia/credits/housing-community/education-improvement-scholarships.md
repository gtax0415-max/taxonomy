# Education Improvement Scholarships Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 26, Line 26D)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2014, but before January 1, 2028, a credit may be claimed for monetary or marketable securities donations to scholarship foundations on an approved list published by the Virginia Department of Education. Credits may be earned in taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2013, but before January 1, 2028, and must be claimed beginning with the year earned.
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 65% of the monetary or marketable securities donation
- For individuals, the minimum eligible donation is $500 in a taxable year and the maximum is the first $125,000 of donations in a taxable year (these limits do not apply to business entities, including sole proprietorships)
- Total credits available in any fiscal year are capped at $25 million, awarded first-come, first-served under Department of Education procedures
- Unused credit carries over for the next 5 taxable years
- Credits are not transferable
## How to compute
- Line A: total eligible credit authorized by the Department of Education
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 26D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
The donor files a preauthorization with the Department of Education; after the foundation certifies the donation, the Department issues a tax credit certificate within 30 days. The amount on Line A is the certificate amount. Attach the certificate to the return. Only donations to foundations on the approved list qualify.
## Required Information
- Foundation name, donation date, and amount or securities value
- Department of Education tax credit certificate amount
- Carryover from prior years and its year of origin
## Questions
- Did you donate money or securities to a scholarship foundation on the Department of Education's approved list?
- How much did you give, and was it at least $500?
- What credit amount did the Department of Education certify?
## Common Errors
- Claiming 65% of donations over $125,000 as an individual
- Claiming a donation under $500 as an individual
- Waiting to claim the credit in a later year instead of starting with the year earned
- Omitting the certificate
## Prompt
- I donated $10,000 to a Virginia scholarship foundation.
