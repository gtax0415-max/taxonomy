# Livable Home Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 16, Line 16D)
## Description
Formerly the Home Accessibility Features for the Disabled credit (Va. Code § 58.1-339.7). For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2023, individuals or licensed contractors may be eligible for a credit of up to $6,500 for the purchase or construction of a new accessible residence, or up to 50% of the cost of retrofitting activities on an existing residence, not to exceed $6,500. The work must improve accessibility and provide universal visitability under DHCD guidelines. No credit is allowed for residential rental property. Credit exceeding the eligible individual's or contractor's tax liability may be carried forward for up to 7 years.
## Program caps
For fiscal years beginning on and after July 1, 2023, total credits granted for any fiscal year cannot exceed $2 million. DHCD allocates $1,000,000 for the purchase or construction of new residences and $1,000,000 for retrofitting or renovating existing residences or units, shifting any unused balance between the two. If approved requests exceed the allocation, DHCD prorates them among eligible applicants.
## How to compute
- Line A: credit authorized by DHCD
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 16D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 7-year carryover period
## Pre-approval (required)
Applications are filed with DHCD by January 31 of the year after the year the purchase, construction or retrofitting was completed, with documentation. DHCD issues a certification of the approved amount, which is attached to the return.
## Required Information
- Whether the project is a new residence or a retrofit, and the completion date
- Purchase price or retrofit cost
- DHCD-certified credit amount
- Carryover from prior years and its year of origin
## Questions
- Did you buy or build a new accessible home, or retrofit an existing one, and when was the work completed?
- What did the retrofit cost?
- Did you apply to DHCD by January 31, and what amount was certified?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without DHCD certification
- Claiming 50% of retrofit costs above the $6,500 cap
- Claiming the cost-based amount instead of the prorated amount DHCD certified
- Claiming for a rental property
## Prompt
- We installed a roll-in shower and widened doorways for my mother.
