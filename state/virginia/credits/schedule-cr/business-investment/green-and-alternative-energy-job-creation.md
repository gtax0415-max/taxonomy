# Green and Alternative Energy Job Creation Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 20, Line 20D)
## Description
The Green and Alternative Energy Job Creation Tax Credit expired January 1, 2025: for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2025, the credit can no longer be earned. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use (no current-year credit)
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 20D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification and pass-through
No new certification applies in 2026; the carryover must trace to a credit earned before the expiration. Owners of a partnership, S corporation or LLC claim their share as shown on the Schedule VK-1. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit.
## Questions
- Do you have unused green job creation credit from a year before 2025?
- In what year was the credit earned, so the 5-year limit can be checked?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 taxable years
- Omitting the carryover computation schedule
## Prompt
- I have leftover Virginia green jobs credit from my solar company.
