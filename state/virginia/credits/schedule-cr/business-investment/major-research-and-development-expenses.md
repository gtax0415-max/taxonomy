# Major Research and Development Expenses Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 27, Line 27D)
## Description
The Major Research and Development Tax Credit expired January 1, 2025: for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2025, taxpayers can no longer earn it. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 10 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. The booklet lists it as carryover only for 2026.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 27D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit; this amount cannot be more than 75% of the tax on your return
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 10-year carryover period
## Limits
Two caps apply to Line 27D: the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit (Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A less other credits claimed) and 75% of the tax on the return. Whatever is not allowed carries to Line E.
## Certification and pass-through
No new credit is authorized for 2026. Owners of pass-through entities use the share on their Schedule VK-1. Enclose a computation schedule for the carried-forward credit.
## Questions
- Do you have unused major research and development credit from a year before 2025?
- In which year was it earned, so the 10-year limit can be checked?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the 75%-of-tax cap on Line 27D
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
