# Motion Picture Production Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 2, Line 2A)
## Description
Qualifying motion picture production companies may receive a series of refundable individual and corporate income tax credits for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, but prior to January 1, 2031. For 2026, the sunset date was extended from January 1, 2027, to January 1, 2031. It is one of the four refundable Schedule CR credits, so it is not limited by the maximum nonrefundable credit and has no carryover line.
## Credit components
- Base credit: for a production with qualifying expenses of at least $250,000 filmed in Virginia, 15% of qualifying expenses, or 20% if filmed in an economically distressed area designated by the Virginia Economic Development Partnership Authority.
- Additional Virginia resident credit: 10% of total aggregate Virginia resident payroll if total Virginia production costs are at least $250,000 but not more than $1 million; 20% if over $1 million.
- Additional first-time industry employee credit: 10% of total aggregate payroll for Virginia residents employed as first-time actors or first-time production crew members.
The aggregate of all motion picture credits issued is capped at $6.5 million per fiscal year.
## Certification
The production company must submit an initial application to the Virginia Film Office at least 30 days before production and enter into a Memorandum of Understanding. After production, it submits documentation to the Film Office and receives a certification letter. The credit may be claimed only after receiving that letter. Enter the amount authorized by the Virginia Film Office on Line 2A.
## Questions
- Did your company (or an entity you own) produce a film in Virginia with a Film Office Memorandum of Understanding?
- Have you received the certification letter, and what amount does it authorize?
- Did a pass-through entity issue you a Schedule VK-1 for this credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming before the certification letter is issued
- Reporting it with the nonrefundable credits in Section 2 instead of Section 3
- Treating it as limited by tax liability
