# Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Investments Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 11)
## Description
The Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Investments Tax Credit expired January 1, 2026: for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026, the credit can no longer be earned. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 15 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. The booklet lists it as carryover only.
## How to compute (SPOUSE and YOU columns)
- Line A: reserved for future use (no current-year credit)
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years, in each column
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 11D: your credit, lesser of Line C (YOU) or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit, not to exceed $50,000 per taxpayer
- Line 11E: spouse's credit, the same test for the SPOUSE column, not to exceed $50,000 per taxpayer
- Line F: Line C less Line D and/or Line E, carried forward only within the 15-year carryover period
## Interaction with Schedule ADJ
Taxpayers claiming the Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Credit cannot claim the Certain Long-Term Capital Gains subtraction (Schedule ADJ, code 52) for investments in the same business.
## Questions
- Do you or your spouse have unused qualified equity or subordinated debt credit from earlier years?
- In which year was each credit earned?
- Did you also claim the code 52 capital gains subtraction for the same business?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit on Line A (it is reserved)
- Combining spouses' carryovers in one column or exceeding $50,000 per taxpayer
- Claiming both this credit and the code 52 subtraction for the same business
## Prompt
- I invested in a Virginia startup years ago and still have credit to use.
