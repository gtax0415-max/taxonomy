# Business and Investment Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Credits earned by businesses, their owners or investors, reaching an individual either directly (sole proprietor) or through a Schedule VK-1 from a partnership, S corporation or LLC. Most of the credits in this folder have expired and can be claimed in 2026 only as carryovers from prior years; the schedule marks their current-year lines "RESERVED FOR FUTURE USE". The Motion Picture Production Tax Credit is current and refundable. Nonrefundable credits flow to Schedule CR, Section 2, Line 1A; the refundable credit flows to Section 4, Line 1A; both reach Form 760, Line 25 through Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A.
## How the lines flow
- Section 1 Part (credit allowable line) → Section 2, Part 1, Line 1A → Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25
- Section 3, Part 2, Line 2A → Section 4, Line 1A → Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25

## Carryover-only credits
For each expired credit, enter the prior-year carryover on Line B, add it on Line C, claim the lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit, and carry the rest forward only within the stated period. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit.
## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a business that earned a Virginia credit, and did you receive a Schedule VK-1?
- Do you have a prior-year Schedule CR showing carryover of any of these credits, and from which year was the credit earned?
- Were you involved in a motion picture production certified by the Virginia Film Office?
