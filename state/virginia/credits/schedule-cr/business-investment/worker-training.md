# Worker Training Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 30, Line 30D)
## Description
The Worker Training Tax Credit expired July 1, 2025: for taxable years beginning on or after July 1, 2025, taxpayers can no longer earn it. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 3 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 30D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 3-year carryover period
## Certification and pass-through
No new credit can be earned in 2026. Owners of a partnership, S corporation or LLC use the share on their Schedule VK-1. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit. Because the carryover period is only 3 years, the claiming order (prior-year carryovers in expiration order) matters when the maximum nonrefundable credit is tight.

## Common Errors
- Carrying forward beyond 3 years
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
