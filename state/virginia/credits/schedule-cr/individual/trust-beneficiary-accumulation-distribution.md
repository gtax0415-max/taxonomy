# Trust Beneficiary Accumulation Distribution Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 2, Line 1A)
## Description
A credit under Va. Code § 58.1-370 for a trust beneficiary who receives an accumulation distribution. The Schedule CR instructions give only the claiming mechanics; Schedule CR has no numbered Part for this credit. It is listed first among the credits claimed on Schedule CR in the booklet.
## How to claim
- If it is the only credit you claim on Schedule CR, enter it on Form 760, Line 25 and write "Trust Beneficiary Accumulation Distribution Credit" to the left of the entry box.
- If you claim other Schedule CR credits, add this credit to the total on Schedule CR, Section 2, Line 1A.
- Enclose a schedule showing the credit computation.
## Refundability and limits
Because it is added to the Section 2 total of nonrefundable credits, it is treated as nonrefundable and falls within the maximum nonrefundable credit (Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A). The instructions give no carryover period; under the claiming order, credits without a carryforward provision are claimed first when the maximum is limited.
## Related Schedule ADJ addition (code 11)
Accumulation Distribution Income (Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 11) adds the taxable income used to compute the partial tax on an accumulated distribution as reported on federal Form 4970. The code 11 addition and this credit both relate to the same accumulation distribution; review both.
## Questions
- Did you receive an accumulation distribution from a trust and file federal Form 4970?
- Are you claiming any other Schedule CR credit this year?
- Do you have the computation of the credit to enclose?
## Common Errors
- Looking for a Part for this credit on Schedule CR (there is none)
- Entering it on Form 760, Line 25 without the written label, or entering it there when other Schedule CR credits are claimed
- Omitting the computation schedule
## Prompt
- I got a distribution from an old family trust that accumulated income for years.
