# Individual Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Three nonrefundable Schedule CR credits that arise from an individual's own circumstances rather than a business: the Trust Beneficiary Accumulation Distribution Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-370), the Foreign Source Retirement Income Tax Credit for foreign tax on a foreign pension, and the Firearm Safety Device Tax Credit (up to $300 per individual). The trust beneficiary credit has no Part of its own on Schedule CR; it is added directly to the Section 2 total or, if it is the only Schedule CR credit, entered on Form 760, Line 25.
## How the lines flow
- Trust beneficiary credit → Schedule CR, Section 2, Line 1A (or directly Form 760, Line 25 if it is the only credit)
- Section 1, Part 9, Line 9G / Part 33, Lines 33D and 33E → Section 2, Part 1, Line 1A → Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25
