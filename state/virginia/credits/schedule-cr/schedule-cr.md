# Schedule CR Credits (Schedule CR, Section 5; Form 760, Line 25)
## Description
Schedule CR (Credit Computation Schedule for Individuals, used with Forms 760, 760PY, 763 and 765) is where every Virginia credit that does not appear on Form 760 or Schedule ADJ is claimed. Nonrefundable credits are computed part by part in Section 1 and totaled in Section 2; the four refundable credits are in Section 3 and totaled in Section 4; Section 5 adds the two totals and the result goes to Form 760, Line 25. Most Schedule CR credits need a certificate, authorization or allocation from the Department of Taxation's Tax Credit Unit or another agency before they can be claimed.
## How the schedule flows
- Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A = maximum nonrefundable credits: total tax on the return less the Spouse Tax Adjustment, the Credit for Low-Income Individuals (or the nonrefundable Virginia Earned Income Credit on Form 760PY or Form 763) and the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State
- Section 1, Parts 2-33 = each nonrefundable credit, each limited to "the balance of maximum credit available"
- Section 2, Part 1, Line 1A = total nonrefundable credits (Lines 2A, 3D, 4F, 7G, 8D, 9G, 10E, 11D, 11E, 13B, 15F, 16D, 17D, 18F, 18G, 19D, 20D, 21D, 22F, 23F, 24D, 26D, 27D, 28D, 30D, 31D, 32D, 33D and 33E)
- Section 3, Parts 2, 3, 5 and 6 = refundable credits (Lines 2A, 3A, 5A, 6A)
- Section 4, Line 1A = total refundable credits
- Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A = Section 2, Line 1A + Section 4, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25
## Maximum nonrefundable credit and claiming order
The maximum nonrefundable credit is Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A reduced by the other credits claimed, regardless of their order on the schedule. If nonrefundable credits exceed the balance:
1. First claim nonrefundable credits with no carryforward provision;
2. Next claim carryover credits from prior years, in expiration order;
3. Then use current-year credits in the order of their carryover provision;
4. Finally report unused credits as carryovers to later years to the extent the law allows.
## Refundable credits (Section 3)
Only four Schedule CR credits are refundable: Motion Picture Production (Part 2), Agricultural Best Management Practices (Part 3), Conservation Tillage and Precision Agriculture Equipment (Part 5) and Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment (Part 6).
## Pass-through credits (Schedule VK-1)
Partners, S corporation shareholders and LLC members may claim credits passed through by the entity, generally in proportion to ownership. An entity distributing a credit must issue each owner a Schedule VK-1.

## What to enclose
Enclosures listed on the schedule are required when claiming the original credit, and computation schedules are required when claiming a carried-forward credit. Missing enclosures may cause a credit to be disallowed.
## Questions
- Did you receive a Schedule VK-1 from a partnership, S corporation or LLC showing a Virginia credit?
- Did the Tax Credit Unit or another state agency send you a credit certification or authorization letter?
- Do you have unused Virginia credits carried over from prior years, and from which year?
- Did a pass-through entity you own make the Virginia elective PTE tax election?
