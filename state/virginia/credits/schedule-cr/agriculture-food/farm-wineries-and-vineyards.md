# Farm Wineries and Vineyards Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 21, Line 21D)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, an individual and corporate income tax credit is available for Virginia farm wineries and vineyards equal to 25% of the cost of all qualified capital expenditures made in establishing new Virginia farm wineries and vineyards and for capital improvements to existing ones.
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 25% of qualified capital expenditures
- Total credits for a calendar year cannot exceed $250,000; if applications exceed $250,000, the Department of Taxation allocates credits pro rata
- Credit exceeding the taxpayer's liability carries forward 10 years
- You cannot claim both this credit and a federal IRC § 179 deduction for the same expenses
## How to compute
- Line A: total eligible credit authorized by the Department of Taxation
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 21D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 10-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
The business must apply by April 1 on Form FWV to the Department of Taxation, Tax Credit Unit. A late application disqualifies the credit. The credit requires Tax Credit Unit certification; a letter certifying the credit is sent by June 30.
## Questions
- What capital expenditures did the winery or vineyard make in the year?
- Was Form FWV filed by April 1, and what amount was certified?
- Did you take a federal section 179 deduction for the same expenses?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and a section 179 deduction on the same costs
- Using the full 25% when the Department prorated the credit
- Missing the April 1 application date
## Prompt
- Our winery added a new tasting room and bottling line this year.
