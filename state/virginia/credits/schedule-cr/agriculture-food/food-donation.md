# Food Donation Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 28, Line 28D)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2023, but before January 1, 2028, a person engaged in the business of farming (as defined under 26 C.F.R. § 1.175-3) who donates food crops or wholesome food produced in Virginia to a nonprofit food bank may claim a credit for the year of the donation. "Wholesome food" means food that meets all federal, state and local quality standards, including food that may not be readily marketable due to appearance, age, freshness, grade, surplus or other condition.
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 50% of the fair market value of the crops or wholesome food donated during the year
- No taxpayer may claim more than $10,000 in credits for a taxable year
- No more than $250,000 in credits can be issued in any fiscal year
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
## Conditions
The credit is allowed only if (i) the food bank's use of the donation is related to providing food to the needy, (ii) the food is not transferred for use outside Virginia or used by the food bank as consideration for services or property, and (iii) if sold by the food bank, it is sold to the needy, other nonprofit food banks, or organizations that intend to use it to feed the needy.
## How to compute
- Line A: total eligible credit authorized by the Department of Taxation
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 28D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
The nonprofit food bank must prepare and furnish Form FCD-2 to the donor within 30 days of the donation. The business must apply for the credit by February 1 on Form FCD-1 to the Tax Credit Unit; a late application disqualifies the credit. The credit requires Tax Credit Unit certification, and a letter is sent to certify it.
## Related Schedule ADJ addition (code 18)
To the extent this credit is allowed, any amount claimed as a federal income tax deduction for the same donation must be added back on Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 18.
## Questions
- Is your farm a farming business, and did it donate Virginia-grown crops or food to a nonprofit food bank?
- Did the food bank give you Form FCD-2 within 30 days?
- Did you file Form FCD-1 by February 1?
- Did you deduct the donation on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $10,000
- Missing the February 1 application deadline
- Forgetting the code 18 addition for the federal deduction
## Prompt
- I donated several tons of apples from my orchard to the regional food bank.
