# Pass-Through Entity Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Credits received as an owner of a partnership, S corporation or LLC. Generally, an entity distributes a credit to its owners in proportion to their ownership and must issue each owner a Schedule VK-1; the owner then claims the credit on the matching Part of Schedule CR (for example Historic Rehabilitation or Biodiesel). This folder holds the one credit that exists only because of the entity: the refundable Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment Credit, which passes the entity-level elective tax paid to the owners.
## How the lines flow
- Form 502 Schedule VK-1 → Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 6, Line 6A → Section 4, Line 1A → Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25
- Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 20 (Pass-Through Entity Tax Add Back) → Schedule ADJ, Line 3 → Form 760, Line 2
