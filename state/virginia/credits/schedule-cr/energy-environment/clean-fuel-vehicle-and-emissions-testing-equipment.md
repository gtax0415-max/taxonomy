# Clean-Fuel Vehicle and Vehicle Emissions Testing Equipment Tax Credits (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 7)
## Description
Part 7 of Schedule CR is titled "Clean-Fuel Vehicle and Vehicle Emissions Testing Equipment Tax Credits" and has two sub-parts. The Clean-Fuel Vehicle and Qualified Electric Vehicle Credit sub-part (Lines A, B and C) is entirely "RESERVED FOR FUTURE USE" on the 2026 draft: there is no current-year line and no carryover line, and the 2026 Schedule CR instructions and booklet do not list the credit. The Vehicle Emissions Testing Equipment Tax Credit sub-part (Lines D-H) is current: it can be earned in 2026 and carried over.
## Clean-Fuel Vehicle and Qualified Electric Vehicle Credit: status
- Current credit: none (Line A reserved)
- Carryover: no line on the 2026 draft (Lines B and C reserved)
- Nothing from this sub-part flows to Line 7G. Do not claim a vehicle purchase here.
## Vehicle Emissions Testing Equipment Tax Credit: amount
20% of the purchase or lease price paid during the year for equipment certified by DEQ for vehicle emissions testing in a locality required by law to implement an enhanced vehicle emissions inspection program, or in a locality adjacent to one. Only expenses listed in the agreement, or dynamometers purchased or leased separately, are eligible.
## How to compute
- Line D: 20% of the purchase or lease price paid in 2026
- Line E: carryover from prior years (enclose computation)
- Line F: D + E
- Line 7G: lesser of F or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line H: F minus G, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification and enclosures
No separate Department of Taxation form is required. Enclose with Schedule CR a copy of the Air Check Virginia Station Participation and Services Agreement (from Opus Inspection, 703.822.7587) or the Northern Virginia Analyzer Equipment Certification (from Air Check Virginia, 703.583.3800). Keep documentation, since an audit may verify the claim.
## Questions
- Do you operate an emissions inspection station, and did you buy or lease testing equipment in 2026?
- Do you have the station participation agreement or the analyzer equipment certification?
- Did you buy an electric or clean-fuel vehicle expecting a Virginia credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a Virginia credit for a clean-fuel or electric vehicle on Part 7 (the lines are reserved)
- Claiming equipment not listed in the agreement
- Omitting the agreement or certification enclosure
## Prompt
- I bought an electric car, is there a Virginia credit?
- My garage bought new emissions testing equipment for Air Check Virginia.
