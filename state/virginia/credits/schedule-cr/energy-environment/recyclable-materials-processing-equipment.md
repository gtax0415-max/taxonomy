# Recyclable Materials Processing Equipment Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 4, Line 4F)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1999, and before January 1, 2027, a credit may be claimed for purchases during the year of (i) machinery and equipment used predominantly in or on the premises of Virginia manufacturing facilities that make items for sale from recyclable materials, or (ii) machinery and equipment used predominantly at facilities predominantly engaged in advanced recycling (single-stream or multi-stream plants that break waste down into raw constituents for resale, including materials recovery or reclamation facilities).
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 20% of the purchase price paid; purchase price may be the original total capitalized cost less capitalized interest
- Cannot exceed 40% of Virginia income tax liability for the year, computed before the credit
- Total credit approved for any year may not exceed $2 million; if approvals exceed $2 million, the Department apportions credits by the ratio of $2 million to total approved
- Unused credit carries forward 10 taxable years
## How to compute
- Line A: 20% of qualifying equipment cost
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line D: 40% of tax per return
- Line E: lesser of C or D
- Line 4F: lesser of E or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line G: C minus F, within the 10-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
DEQ certifies all recycling machinery and equipment: submit DEQ Form 50-11S with support to DEQ by March 1. Then submit Form RMC, with the DEQ certification, to the Department of Taxation by June 1. A late application disqualifies the credit. Timely applicants are notified of the allowable credit by September 1. Because the return is due May 1, most claimants must file on extension or amend to claim it. The credit requires certification from the Tax Credit Unit.
## Questions
- What recycling equipment was bought in 2026, and at what capitalized cost?
- Was DEQ certification applied for by March 1 and Form RMC filed by June 1?
- Has the Tax Credit Unit notified you of the allowed amount?
## Common Errors
- Filing the return by May 1 without the certified amount instead of extending or amending
- Ignoring the 40%-of-tax cap
- Missing the March 1 or June 1 deadline
## Prompt
- We bought a baler for our recycling plant in Virginia.
