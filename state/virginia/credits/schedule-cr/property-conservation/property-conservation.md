# Property and Conservation Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Credits for rehabilitating historic buildings, conveying land for conservation, keeping riparian forest buffers, and farm conservation practices and equipment. Historic Rehabilitation, Land Preservation and Riparian Waterway Buffer are nonrefundable Section 1 credits with carryovers; Agricultural Best Management Practices and Conservation Tillage and Precision Agriculture Equipment are refundable Section 3 credits. Each requires certification from a state agency (Department of Historic Resources, Department of Taxation, Department of Forestry, local Soil and Water Conservation District). Two have matching Schedule ADJ subtractions for gain or income tied to the credit (codes 51 and 53).
