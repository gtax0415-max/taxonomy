# Conservation Tillage and Precision Agriculture Equipment Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 5, Line 5A)
## Description
A refundable credit, for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2021, but before January 1, 2030, for individual and corporate taxpayers engaged in agricultural production for market who have a soil conservation plan approved by the local soil and water conservation district, are implementing a nutrient management plan developed by a certified nutrient management planner, and buy qualifying conservation tillage or precision agricultural application equipment. It is one of the four refundable Schedule CR credits.
## Amount
- 25% of all expenditures for equipment certified by the Virginia Soil and Water Conservation Board as reducing soil compaction (such as a no-till planter or drill), or providing more precise pesticide and fertilizer application or injection
- Soil compaction equipment includes guidance systems that control traffic patterns to minimize soil disturbance in planting, including planters, drills or attachments to equipment already owned
- Cannot exceed $17,500 in the year of purchase (Line 5A: "not to exceed $17,500")
## Certification (required)
Apply on Form AEC to the Department of Taxation, Tax Credit Unit. The credit requires Tax Credit Unit certification; a letter is sent to certify the credit. Enter the authorized amount on Line 5A.
## Not the same as the expired credits
The older Conservation Tillage Equipment Tax Credit and the Precision Fertilizer and Pesticide Application Equipment Tax Credit expired January 1, 2021, and their 5-year carryover periods expired January 1, 2026. No carryover of those credits is claimable in 2026.
## Questions
- Do you farm for market with an approved soil conservation plan and a nutrient management plan?
- What certified no-till or precision application equipment did you buy, and at what cost?
- Have you filed Form AEC and received the certification letter?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $17,500
- Claiming equipment not certified by the Soil and Water Conservation Board
- Trying to claim carryover of the expired older equipment credits
## Prompt
- I bought a no-till drill for my farm this year.
