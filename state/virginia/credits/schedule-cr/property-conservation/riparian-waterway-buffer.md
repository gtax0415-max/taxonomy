# Riparian Waterway Buffer Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 17, Line 17D)
## Description
Individuals and corporations may qualify for a credit of 25% of the value of the timber on land designated as a riparian buffer for a waterway. A riparian buffer is a wooded area adjacent to a waterway on which timber may be harvested. The credit may not exceed $17,500 or the total amount of tax, whichever is less.
## Requirements
- The owner must refrain from harvesting more than 50% of the timber from tracts designated as riparian buffers
- The buffer must be at least 35 feet wide and no more than 300 feet
- There must be a Forest Stewardship Plan and annual certification of compliance for each tract
- The buffer must remain in place for at least 15 years
- The same land cannot be the subject of this credit again for 15 years after it was first taken
## How to compute
- Line A: credit authorized by the Virginia Department of Forestry
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 17D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
Apply by filing Department of Forestry (DOF) Form 18.8 with DOF or online at www.dof.virginia.gov. The amount entered on Line A is the amount DOF authorizes. Unused credit may be carried over for the succeeding 5 taxable years.
## Questions
- Do you own wooded land along a stream or river designated as a riparian buffer?
- Has DOF authorized a credit, and for how much?
- Do you have a Forest Stewardship Plan and the annual compliance certification?
- Was this land used for the credit in the last 15 years?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $17,500
- Harvesting more than 50% of the buffer timber
- Reclaiming the same land within 15 years
## Prompt
- I agreed not to cut the trees along the creek on my property.
