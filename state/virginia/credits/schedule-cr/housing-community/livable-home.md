# Livable Home Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 16, Line 16D)
## Description
Formerly the Home Accessibility Features for the Disabled credit. For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2023, individuals or licensed contractors may be eligible for a credit of up to $6,500 for the purchase or construction of a new accessible residence, or up to 50% of the cost of retrofitting activities on an existing residence, not to exceed $6,500. Credit exceeding the eligible individual's or contractor's tax liability may be carried forward for up to 7 years.
## Program caps
For fiscal years beginning on and after July 1, 2023, total credits granted for any fiscal year cannot exceed $2 million. DHCD allocates $1,000,000 for the purchase or construction of new residences and $1,000,000 for retrofitting or renovating existing residences or units. If credits issued exceed the fiscal year cap, DHCD prorates them among eligible applicants.
## How to compute
- Line A: credit authorized by DHCD
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 16D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 7-year carryover period
## Pre-approval (required)
Individuals or licensed contractors must obtain pre-approval before claiming the credit. Applications are filed with DHCD by January 31 of the year after the year the purchase, construction or retrofitting was completed, with documentation.
## Questions
- Did you buy or build a new accessible home, or retrofit an existing one, and when was the work completed?
- What did the retrofit cost?
- Did you apply to DHCD by January 31, and what amount was authorized?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without DHCD pre-approval
- Claiming 50% of retrofit costs above the $6,500 cap
- Claiming the cost-based amount instead of the prorated amount DHCD authorized
## Prompt
- We installed a roll-in shower and widened doorways for my mother.
