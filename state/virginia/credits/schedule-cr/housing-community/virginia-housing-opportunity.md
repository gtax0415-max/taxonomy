# Virginia Housing Opportunity Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 31, Line 31D)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2021, but before January 1, 2031, a housing opportunity tax credit (HOTC) is available for certain low-income building projects in an amount up to the federal low-income housing tax credit allocated or allowed by VHDA. The project must be a qualified low-income building under federal law that is located in Virginia, placed in service on or after January 1, 2021, and allocated HOTC by VHDA. A qualified taxpayer claims the HOTC against Virginia tax before reduction by any other credits.
## Amount and timing
- For calendar years 2026 through 2030, total HOTC authorized cannot exceed $64 million per calendar year, with $20 million first allocated to projects in the Balance of State Pool defined in VHDA's Qualified Allocation Plan
- Credits are allowed ratably, one-tenth annually over a 10-year credit period, with a first-year reduction under a federal calculation that becomes allowable in the first year after the credit period
## How to compute
- Line A: total eligible credit authorized by VHDA
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 31D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period (per the form; the instructions do not separately state a period)
## Allocation among owners
A pass-through entity may allocate the HOTC to some or all partners, members or shareholders in any agreed manner, regardless of federal LIHTC allocation, substantial economic effect under IRC § 704(b), or federal partner status, as long as the owner is admitted on or before the return due date (including amendments) for the HOTC year. Interests, including credits, may be assigned to other pass-through entities or qualified taxpayers if acquired before the return claiming the HOTC is filed.
## Questions
- Do you hold an interest in a VHDA-allocated low-income housing project placed in service in 2021 or later?
- What HOTC amount is allocated to you for 2026, and which year of the 10-year period is it?
- When were you admitted as a partner or member?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the whole 10-year credit in one year instead of the annual one-tenth
- Claiming an allocation received after the return was filed
## Prompt
- My K-1 from an affordable housing fund shows a Virginia housing opportunity credit.
