# Communities of Opportunity Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 19, Line 19D)
## Description
The Communities of Opportunity Tax Credit expired January 1, 2026: for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2026, taxpayers can no longer earn it. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use (no current-year credit)
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 19D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification
No new credit can be authorized for 2026; the carryover must come from a credit earned in an earlier year. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit.
## Questions
- Do you have unused communities of opportunity credit from 2025 or earlier, and from which year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a 2026 credit on Line A (it is reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 years
## Prompt
- I had a communities of opportunity credit last year that I could not fully use.
