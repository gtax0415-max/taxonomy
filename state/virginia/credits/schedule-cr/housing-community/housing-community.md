# Housing and Community Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Nonrefundable Schedule CR credits for donations to community and scholarship organizations and for housing: Neighborhood Assistance Act, Education Improvement Scholarships, Livable Home, Virginia Housing Opportunity, and the expired Communities of Opportunity credit (carryover only). Each current credit is authorized by an agency outside the tax return: the Department of Social Services or Department of Education (Neighborhood Assistance), the Department of Education (scholarships), the Department of Housing and Community Development (Livable Home), and the Virginia Housing Development Authority (Housing Opportunity).

## Questions
- Did you donate money or securities to a Neighborhood Assistance Program organization or an approved scholarship foundation?
- Did you build, buy or retrofit an accessible home, with DHCD pre-approval?
- Do you invest in a low-income housing project allocated a Virginia housing opportunity credit?
