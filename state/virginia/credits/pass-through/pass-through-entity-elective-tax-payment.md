# Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment Credit (Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 6, Line 6A)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2021, a qualifying pass-through entity (PTE) may make an annual election to pay a 5.75% tax at the entity level. 2023 legislation expanded eligibility by removing the requirement that the PTE be 100% owned by natural persons or persons eligible to be S corporation shareholders. A corresponding refundable credit is available to the owners for the income tax paid by a qualifying PTE that makes the election and pays the tax. The effect is to shift the income tax burden from the owners to the entity.

The election and its credits were set to expire January 1, 2027. The 2026 Amendments to the 2025 Appropriation Act (HB 29, Chapter 7, effective February 20, 2026) made them permanent, so the election is available for 2026 and later years.
## How to claim
- Line 6A: credit allowable this year, the amount from Form 502 Schedule VK-1
- Line B: reserved for future use
- The PTE reports the PTET on Form 502 Schedule VK-1; each owner claims their share on Schedule CR
- Enclose Form 502 Schedule VK-1 with the return
It is one of the four refundable Schedule CR credits (Section 3), so it is not limited by the maximum nonrefundable credit and has no carryover.
## Related Schedule ADJ addition (code 20)
If you claim this credit, you must add back your pro-rata share of any deduction for state and local income taxes paid by the qualifying PTE: Schedule ADJ, Lines 2b-2c, code 20 (Pass-Through Entity Tax Add Back). Claiming the credit without the add-back overstates the benefit.
## Other states and amended returns
- Elective PTE tax paid to another state under a law substantially similar to Virginia's is handled through the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (Schedule OSC), with a statement from the PTE documenting your share of the income, tax liability and tax paid. That out-of-state credit was also made permanent by HB 29, Chapter 7.
- Amended return reason code 05 is "Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment Credit."
## Required Information
- PTET credit amount on Form 502 Schedule VK-1
- Your share of the entity's federal deduction for state and local income taxes (for the code 20 add-back)
- Any elective tax the entity paid to other states on your behalf
## Questions
- Did your partnership, S corporation or LLC elect to pay the Virginia PTET for 2026?
- What PTET credit amount appears on your Form 502 Schedule VK-1?
- What was your share of the entity's deduction for state and local income taxes on the federal return?
## Common Errors
- Forgetting the code 20 add-back of the entity's state income tax deduction
- Reporting the credit with nonrefundable credits in Section 2
- Omitting the Form 502 Schedule VK-1
- Claiming another state's PTE tax here instead of on Schedule OSC
## Prompt
- My partnership paid Virginia pass-through entity tax on my behalf.
- My VK-1 shows a PTET credit, where does it go?
