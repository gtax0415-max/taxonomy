# Foreign Source Retirement Income Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 9, Line 9G)
## Description
A credit for Virginia residents who paid income tax to a foreign country on pension or retirement income derived from past employment in that country, provided the income is included in Virginia taxable income for the year. United States territories are treated as foreign countries. A country that does not qualify for the federal foreign tax credit (IRC § 901(j)) does not qualify for this credit. Tax paid to other states goes on Schedule OSC instead.
## How to compute
- Line A: qualifying taxable income on which the foreign country's tax is based
- Line B: Virginia taxable income (if you filed separately in the foreign country but jointly in Virginia, enter only the Virginia taxable income of the filer taxed by the foreign country)
- Line C: qualifying tax paid to the foreign country, and the country's name
- Line D: Virginia income tax
- Line E: Line A divided by Line B, to one decimal place, not over 100% (for example, 0.3163 becomes 31.6%)
- Line F: Line D times Line E
- Line 9G: lesser of Line C or Line F, not to exceed the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
## Currency and documents
Convert the foreign currency to U.S. dollars using the prevailing exchange rate that most nearly reflects its value when the taxes were actually paid. Enclose a copy of the foreign return or other proof of tax paid and a schedule showing the currency conversion. No agency certification is needed.
## Carryover
Part 9 has no carryover line and the instructions state no carryover period.
## Required Information
- Country (or U.S. territory) that taxed the pension, and whether it is an IRC § 901(j) country
- Pension income taxed there and the foreign tax actually paid, with payment dates
- Exchange rates used to convert the tax to U.S. dollars
- Which spouse was taxed abroad, if filing jointly in Virginia
## Questions
- Do you receive a pension from past employment in a foreign country or a U.S. territory?
- How much tax did you pay to that country on it, and when?
- Is the pension included in your Virginia taxable income?
## Common Errors
- Claiming tax on foreign income other than pension or retirement income from past employment there
- Converting currency at the wrong date's rate
- Using joint Virginia taxable income when only one spouse was taxed abroad
- Putting foreign tax on Schedule OSC
## Prompt
- I retired from a job in Germany and Germany taxes my pension.
- I get a Puerto Rico government pension and pay tax there.
