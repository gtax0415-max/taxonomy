# Virginia Earned Income Credit (Schedule ADJ, Lines 14, 16b and 17)
## Description
A refundable Virginia credit equal to 20% of the earned income credit claimed on the federal return. For Taxable Years 2025 and 2026, legislation increased the refundable portion from 15% to 20%. You are eligible if you claimed the earned income credit on your federal return and are not barred by the eligibility rules below. It is an alternative to the nonrefundable Credit for Low-Income Individuals; you can claim only one.
## How to compute
- Line 14: enter the earned income credit claimed on your federal return (enter $0 if none). Also fill in the Form 760 oval for the federal earned income credit and enter the amount claimed.
- Line 13: if you do not qualify for the Credit for Low-Income Individuals but claimed the federal credit, enter $0 there and continue.
- Line 15 and Line 16a: reserved for future use.
- Line 16b: Line 14 times 20% (.20). This is a refundable credit.
- Line 17: choose the option that benefits you most, the refundable Line 16b amount OR the nonrefundable low-income credit (lesser of Line 13 or Form 760, Line 18 reduced by the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State). Enter it on Line 17 and on Form 760, Line 23.
## Married filing separately
A spouse filing separately first determines their proportion of the earned income used to qualify for the federal credit, then multiplies that proportion by the total Virginia Earned Income Credit (20% of the federal credit). Each spouse claims the proportional share on their own return.
## Eligibility bars (mutually exclusive items)
The credit may NOT be claimed if you, your spouse, or any dependent claimed on your return or your spouse's return claims the age deduction, the exemption for taxpayers who are blind or age 65 and over, the Virginia National Guard subtraction (code 28), the basic military pay subtraction (code 38), or the federal and state employee subtraction (code 39), or if you are claimed as a dependent on another taxpayer's return. Neither spouse may claim the age deduction if one spouse claims this credit, even on separate returns.
## Federal reminder
The booklet notes that many workers with earned income of $70,200 or less may qualify for up to $8,231 in federal earned income credit; a taxpayer who has not claimed it federally cannot claim the Virginia credit.
## Questions
- Did you claim the earned income credit on your federal return, and how much?
- Is anyone on your return claiming the age deduction, the 65 or blind exemption, or subtraction code 28, 38 or 39?
- If married filing separately, how much of the earned income used for the federal credit was yours?
## Common Errors
- Using 15% instead of the 20% rate that applies for 2026
- Claiming both this credit and the Credit for Low-Income Individuals
- Claiming it with the age deduction, the 65 or blind exemption, or subtraction code 28, 38 or 39
- Each separately filing spouse claiming the full 20% instead of a proportional share
- Forgetting the Form 760 federal earned income credit oval and amount
## Prompt
- I got the earned income credit on my federal return, does Virginia match it?
- Is the Virginia earned income credit refundable?
