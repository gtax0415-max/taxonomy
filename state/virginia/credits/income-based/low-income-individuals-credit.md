# Credit for Low-Income Individuals (Schedule ADJ, Lines 10-13 and 17)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit of $300 per personal and dependent exemption for taxpayers whose family Virginia adjusted gross income (family VAGI) is equal to or less than the federal poverty guidelines for the family size (Va. Code § 58.1-339.8). It has no federal counterpart. It is an alternative to the refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit computed in the same section; you can claim only one, the one that benefits you most.
## Amount
- $300 per personal and dependent exemption, not including the age 65 or blind exemptions
- Limited to Form 760, Line 18 reduced by any Credit for Tax Paid to Another State; no refund and no carryforward
## 2026 poverty guidelines
The statute uses the HHS guidelines for the 48 contiguous states published during the taxable year. For 2026 these are the guidelines published January 15, 2026:

| Family size | Poverty guideline |
|---|---|
| 1 | $15,960 |
| 2 | $21,640 |
| 3 | $27,320 |
| 4 | $33,000 |
| 5 | $38,680 |
| 6 | $44,360 |
| 7 | $50,040 |
| 8 | $55,720 |

For each additional person, add $5,680.
## How to compute
- Line 10: enter the VAGI (Form 760, Line 9) of you, your spouse and each dependent, and total them. This is family VAGI. Attach a schedule if more than 4 exemptions.
  - Married filing jointly: if you entered the joint VAGI exactly as on Form 760, Line 9, no separate spouse figure is needed; otherwise the two amounts must add up to the joint VAGI.
  - Married filing separately: you must provide your spouse's VAGI (from the spouse's Form 760, or computed as if the spouse had to file a resident Form 760, for example a nonresident spouse). List dependents not claimed on your return too. Only one spouse may claim the credit.
- Line 11: number of family members. You qualify if family VAGI is equal to or less than the poverty guideline for that size (table above).
- Line 12: number of personal and dependent exemptions on Form 760. Do not include the age 65 or blind exemptions.
- Line 13: Line 12 times $300. If you do not qualify but claimed the federal earned income credit, enter $0 and go to Line 14.
- Line 15: reserved for future use.
- Line 17: nonrefundable credit = lesser of Line 13 or Form 760, Line 18 reduced by any Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (Schedule OSC). Compare it with the refundable Line 16b amount and enter the better one here and on Form 760, Line 23.
## Eligibility bars (mutually exclusive items)
The credit may NOT be claimed if you, your spouse, or any dependent claimed on your return or your spouse's return claims:
- the age deduction (and neither spouse may take the age deduction if one spouse claims this credit, even filing separately)
- the exemption for taxpayers who are blind or age 65 and over
- the Virginia National Guard subtraction (code 28)
- the basic military pay subtraction (code 38)
- the federal and state employee subtraction (code 39)
It is also barred if you are claimed as a dependent on another taxpayer's return.
## Required Information
- Federal AGI of you, your spouse and each dependent, and the Virginia additions and subtractions that convert each to VAGI
- Number of people in the family and number of personal and dependent exemptions
- Whether anyone in the household is 65 or older or blind, or has National Guard, basic military or low-paid federal/state employee pay
- Spouse's income if married filing separately, and whether the spouse is also claiming the credit
## Questions
- What is the Virginia AGI of each person in your family, including dependents with income?
- How many personal and dependent exemptions are on your return?
- Is anyone on the return taking the age deduction, the 65 or blind exemption, or subtraction code 28, 38 or 39?
- If married filing separately, what is your spouse's VAGI, and is your spouse also claiming this credit?
## Common Errors
- Using the prior year's poverty guidelines instead of those published in 2026
- Claiming the 65 or blind exemption together with this credit
- Counting the age 65 and blind exemptions on Line 12
- Leaving out a dependent's or spouse's VAGI from family VAGI
- Claiming both this credit and the refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit
- Taking the full $300 per exemption when it exceeds Form 760, Line 18 less the other-state credit (the credit is not refundable)
## Prompt
- Our family income is under the poverty line, is there a Virginia credit?
