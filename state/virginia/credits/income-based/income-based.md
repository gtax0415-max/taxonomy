# Income-Based Credits (Schedule ADJ, Lines 10-17)
## Description
Schedule ADJ, page 2, holds two alternative credits: the nonrefundable Credit for Low-Income Individuals ($300 per personal and dependent exemption, for families at or below the federal poverty guidelines) and the refundable Virginia Earned Income Credit (20% of the federal earned income credit for 2026). A taxpayer can claim only one of them. Both are computed in the same section, and the chosen amount on Schedule ADJ, Line 17 goes to Form 760, Line 23.
## How the lines flow
- Line 10 = family VAGI (you, spouse, and each dependent)
- Line 11 = number of family members, compared with the Poverty Guideline Table
- Line 12 = personal and dependent exemptions (not age 65 or blind)
- Line 13 = Line 12 times $300 (or $0 if you do not qualify for the low-income credit)
- Line 14 = federal earned income credit claimed
- Lines 15 and 16a = reserved for future use
- Line 16b = Line 14 times 20% (refundable)
- Line 17 = the better of Line 16b (refundable) OR the lesser of Line 13 and Form 760, Line 18 reduced by any Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (nonrefundable) → Form 760, Line 23
## Shared eligibility bars
Neither credit may be claimed if you, your spouse, or any dependent claimed on your return or your spouse's return claims the age deduction, the exemption for taxpayers who are blind or age 65 and over, the Virginia National Guard subtraction (code 28), the basic military pay subtraction (code 38), or the federal and state employee subtraction (code 39), or if you are claimed as a dependent on another taxpayer's return. Neither spouse may claim the age deduction if one spouse claims either credit, even on separate returns.
