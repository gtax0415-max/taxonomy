# Farm Wineries and Vineyards Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 21, Line 21D)
## Description
For taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011 (Va. Code § 58.1-339.12, no sunset date), an individual and corporate income tax credit is available for Virginia farm wineries and vineyards equal to 25% of the cost of all qualified capital expenditures made in establishing new Virginia farm wineries and vineyards and for capital improvements to existing ones. A Virginia vineyard must have at least one contiguous acre of grapes used or intended for a Virginia farm winery.
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 25% of qualified capital expenditures (listed items such as barrels, bottling equipment, presses, tanks, grape plants, irrigation equipment, tractors and trellis materials)
- Total credits for a calendar year cannot exceed $250,000; if applications exceed $250,000, the Department of Taxation allocates credits pro rata
- Credit exceeding the taxpayer's liability carries forward 10 years
- The credit cannot be claimed to the extent a federal IRC § 179 deduction was taken for the same expenses
## How to compute
- Line A: total eligible credit authorized by the Department of Taxation
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line 21D: lesser of C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: C minus D, within the 10-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
The business must apply to the Department of Taxation, Tax Credit Unit, by April 1 (Form FWV). A late application disqualifies the credit. A letter certifying the credit is sent by June 30.
## Required Information
- Qualified capital expenditures for the year, by item
- Department of Taxation certified (possibly prorated) credit amount
- Any federal section 179 deduction on the same assets
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
- Carryover from prior years and its year of origin
## Questions
- What capital expenditures did the winery or vineyard make in the year?
- Was the application filed by April 1, and what amount was certified?
- Did you take a federal section 179 deduction for the same expenses?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and a section 179 deduction on the same costs
- Using the full 25% when the Department prorated the credit
- Missing the April 1 application date
## Prompt
- Our winery added a new tasting room and bottling line this year.
