# Agriculture and Food Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Two nonrefundable Schedule CR credits for Virginia farm businesses: the Farm Wineries and Vineyards Tax Credit for capital expenditures, and the Food Donation Tax Credit for farmers who donate Virginia-grown food to nonprofit food banks. Both require an application to the Department of Taxation's Tax Credit Unit by a fixed date and a certification letter before the credit is claimed. The food donation credit has a matching Schedule ADJ addition (code 18). Farm conservation credits (best management practices, conservation tillage equipment) are in the property-conservation folder.
