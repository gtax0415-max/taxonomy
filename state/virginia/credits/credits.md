# Credits (Form 760, Lines 23-25)
## Description
Virginia claims its income tax credits in three places on Form 760, all inside the payments and credits block that ends at Line 26. Line 23 carries the Credit for Low-Income Individuals or the Virginia Earned Income Credit from Schedule ADJ, Line 17. Line 24 carries the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State from Schedule OSC, Line 21. Line 25 carries every other credit, computed on Schedule CR and taken from Schedule CR, Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A. Line 26 adds Lines 19a through 25 (withholding, estimated and extension payments, and the three credit lines), and Line 26 is compared with Line 18 (net amount of tax) to get the tax owed (Line 27) or the overpayment (Line 28).
## How the lines flow
- Schedule ADJ, Lines 10-17 → Schedule ADJ, Line 17 → Form 760, Line 23
- Schedule OSC, Lines 1-21 → Schedule OSC, Line 21 → Form 760, Line 24
- Schedule CR, Sections 1-5 → Schedule CR, Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25
- Form 760, Lines 19a through 25 → Form 760, Line 26 → compared with Line 18 → Line 27 (tax you owe) or Line 28 (overpayment)
## Refundable and nonrefundable
- The Form 760, Line 25 instructions note that the Credit for Low-Income Individuals, the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State and most Schedule CR credits are nonrefundable, and the total of all nonrefundable credits cannot exceed the tax liability on Form 760, Line 18.
- Refundable: the Virginia Earned Income Credit (20% of the federal earned income credit, Schedule ADJ, Line 16b) and the four Schedule CR, Section 3 credits (Motion Picture Production, Agricultural Best Management Practices, Conservation Tillage and Precision Agriculture Equipment, Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Payment).
## Ordering and the maximum nonrefundable credit
The credits are applied in a fixed sequence, and each later group is limited by what the earlier groups used:
1. Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (Schedule OSC) is limited on its own computation, and the nonrefundable Credit for Low-Income Individuals is limited to Form 760, Line 18 reduced by any Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (Schedule ADJ, Line 17).
2. Schedule CR nonrefundable credits are limited to the maximum on Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A: the total tax on the return less the Spouse Tax Adjustment, the Credit for Low-Income Individuals (or the nonrefundable Virginia Earned Income Credit available on Form 760PY or Form 763) and the Credit for Tax Paid to Another State.
3. Within Schedule CR, when nonrefundable credits exceed the balance of the maximum, claim first the credits with no carryforward, next prior-year carryovers in expiration order, then current-year credits in the order of their carryover provisions, and carry the unused balance forward where the law allows.
4. The Schedule CR, Section 3 refundable credits are not limited by the maximum.
