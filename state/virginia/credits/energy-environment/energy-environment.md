# Energy and Environment Credits (Schedule CR)
## Description
Nonrefundable Schedule CR credits for recycling equipment, vehicle emissions testing equipment, equipment that burns waste motor oil, and biodiesel and green diesel fuel production. Each needs certification from the Department of Environmental Quality (DEQ), the Department of Energy or the Department of Taxation's Tax Credit Unit, or the enclosure of a specific agreement. The Clean-Fuel Vehicle and Qualified Electric Vehicle Credit lines in Part 7 are all reserved for future use in 2026.
## How the lines flow
- Section 1, Part 4, Line 4F / Part 7, Line 7G / Part 13, Line 13B / Part 15, Line 15F → Section 2, Part 1, Line 1A → Section 5, Part 1, Line 1A → Form 760, Line 25

## Related expired credits
The What's New section of the booklet notes that the former Conservation Tillage Equipment Tax Credit and the Precision Fertilizer and Pesticide Application Equipment Tax Credit expired January 1, 2021, and their 5-year carryover periods expired January 1, 2026. No carryover of either can be claimed in 2026. They are different from the current refundable Conservation Tillage and Precision Agriculture Equipment Tax Credit (property-conservation folder).
