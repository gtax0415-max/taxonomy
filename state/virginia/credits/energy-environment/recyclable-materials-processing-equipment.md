# Recyclable Materials Processing Equipment Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 4, Line 4F)
## Description
A credit may be claimed for purchases during the year of (i) machinery and equipment used predominantly in or on the premises of Virginia manufacturing facilities that make items for sale from recyclable materials, or (ii) machinery and equipment used predominantly at facilities predominantly engaged in advanced recycling (single-stream or multi-stream plants that break waste down into raw constituents for resale, including materials recovery or reclamation facilities).

The Code of Virginia (§ 58.1-439.7) still shows the credit ending for taxable years beginning before January 1, 2025. The Appropriation Act overrides that: Item 3-5.26 of the 2025 Act (continued as Item 3-5.23 of the 2026 Appropriation Act) keeps the credit in effect for taxable years beginning before January 1, 2027. 2026 is therefore the last year purchases can earn the credit unless it is extended again.
## Amount and limits
- Credit = 20% of the purchase price paid; purchase price may be the original total capitalized cost less capitalized interest
- Cannot exceed 40% of Virginia income tax liability for the year, computed before the credit
- Total credit approved for any year may not exceed $2 million; if approvals exceed $2 million, the Department apportions credits by the ratio of $2 million to total approved
- Unused credit carries forward 10 taxable years
## How to compute
- Line A: 20% of qualifying equipment cost
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line D: 40% of tax per return
- Line E: lesser of C or D
- Line 4F: lesser of E or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line G: C minus F, within the 10-year carryover period
## Certification (required)
DEQ certifies all recycling machinery and equipment (application to DEQ by March 1). The taxpayer then applies to the Department of Taxation with the DEQ certification by June 1; a late application disqualifies the credit. Timely applicants are notified of the allowable credit by September 1. Because the return is due May 1, most claimants must file on extension or amend to claim it. Submit purchase receipts and invoices with the return.
## Required Information
- Description, purchase date and capitalized cost of each item bought in 2026
- DEQ certification and the Department's notice of allowable credit
- Virginia tax before the credit (for the 40% limit)
- Ownership share if through a pass-through entity
- Carryover from prior years and its year of origin
## Questions
- What recycling equipment was bought in 2026, and at what capitalized cost?
- Was DEQ certification applied for by March 1 and the Department of Taxation application filed by June 1?
- Has the Tax Credit Unit notified you of the allowed amount?
## Common Errors
- Filing the return by May 1 without the certified amount instead of extending or amending
- Ignoring the 40%-of-tax cap
- Missing the March 1 or June 1 deadline
- Assuming the credit continues for 2027 purchases
## Prompt
- We bought a baler for our recycling plant in Virginia.
