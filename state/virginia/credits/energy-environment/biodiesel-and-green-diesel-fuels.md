# Biodiesel and Green Diesel Fuels Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 15, Line 15F)
## Description
Beginning January 1, 2008, a credit is available for Virginia biodiesel and green diesel fuel producers who produce up to 2 million gallons of fuel per year. It is available only during the first 3 years of production. The credit is $0.01 per gallon, not to exceed $5,000 annually, and cannot exceed the tax liability for the year claimed. Unused credit carries over to the next 3 taxable years.
## How to compute
- Line A: credit authorized by the Department of Taxation, or the amount transferred to you in the current year
- Line B: carryover from prior years
- Line C: A + B
- Line D: total credit transferred to others in the current year
- Line E: C minus D
- Line 15F: lesser of E or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line G: E minus F, within the 3-year carryover period
## Certification, allocation and transfer
- After the Virginia Department of Energy certifies that the requirements of Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:02 are met, the producer applies to the Department of Taxation (Form BFC).
- A partnership, S corporation or LLC must allocate the credit to owners in proportion to ownership (Form TCA) within 30 days of certification, but at least 90 days before the owners file.
- The credit may be transferred to another taxpayer; the transfer must be completed before the end of a tax year to use the credit for that year.
- Enclose the Department of Taxation letter of certification authorizing the credit.
## Required Information
- Year of first production and gallons produced in 2026
- Certified credit amount, or the amount transferred to you
- Credit sold or transferred to others in 2026
- Ownership share if allocated through a pass-through entity
- Carryover from prior years and its year of origin
## Questions
- Do you produce biodiesel or green diesel in Virginia, and is this within the first 3 years of production?
- How many gallons did you produce, and what amount did the Department of Taxation certify?
- Did you buy or sell any of this credit during the year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $5,000 in a year
- Forgetting to subtract credit transferred to others on Line D
- Using a transfer completed after year-end for the current year
## Prompt
- My farm makes biodiesel and got a certification letter from Virginia Tax.
