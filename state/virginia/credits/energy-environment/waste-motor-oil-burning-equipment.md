# Waste Motor Oil Burning Equipment Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 13, Line 13B)
## Description
A business that operates a facility in Virginia that accepts waste motor oil from the public may claim a credit equal to 50% of the purchase price paid during the year for equipment used exclusively to burn waste motor oil at that facility. The credit is limited to $5,000 per taxpayer per taxable year.
## How to compute
- Line A: 50% of the purchase price paid in 2026 for the equipment
- Line 13B: the Line A amount, up to $5,000, not to exceed the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
## Carryover
Part 13 has no carryover line, and the instructions do not provide a carryover period for this credit. Under the general claiming order, a credit with no carryforward provision is claimed first when the maximum nonrefundable credit is limited.
## Certification (required)
Apply for equipment certification with the Department of Environmental Quality (Form DEQ 50-12). DEQ issues a statement certifying that the equipment is used for burning waste motor oil. To claim the credit, enclose the DEQ certification and receipts, invoices or other documentation confirming the purchase price.
## Required Information
- Facility location and confirmation it accepts waste motor oil from the public
- Equipment description and purchase price paid in 2026
- DEQ certification statement
## Questions
- Does your Virginia business accept waste motor oil from the public?
- Is the equipment used only to burn waste motor oil at that facility?
- Did DEQ certify the equipment, and what did it cost?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $5,000 in a year
- Claiming equipment also used for other fuels
- Omitting the DEQ certification and purchase documents
## Prompt
- Our auto shop takes used oil from customers and bought a waste oil heater.
