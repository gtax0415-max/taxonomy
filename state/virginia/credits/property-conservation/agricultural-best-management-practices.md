# Agricultural Best Management Practices Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 3, Line 3A)
## Description
A refundable credit, effective for taxable years on and after January 1, 1998, but before January 1, 2030, for individual and corporate taxpayers engaged in agricultural production for market, or who have equines that create needs for agricultural best management practices (BMPs) to reduce nonpoint source pollutants. It is one of the four refundable Schedule CR credits, so it is not limited by tax liability and there is no carryover.
## Amount
- With a soil conservation plan approved by the local Soil and Water Conservation District (SWCD): 25% of the first $100,000 spent on SWCD-approved BMPs
- For taxable years on and after January 1, 2021, with a resource management plan approved by the local SWCD: 50% of the first $100,000 spent on BMPs implemented on the acreage in the plan; if a cost-share payment is received, only 25% of the first $100,000
- Maximum aggregate credit per taxpayer per taxable year: $75,000
- Maximum credits issued per fiscal year: $2 million
## Restrictions
A taxpayer claiming this credit may not claim any other Virginia tax credit for costs related to the same practices. A partnership or S corporation must allocate the credit to partners and shareholders in proportion to ownership.
## Certification (two steps)
1. Receive certification from the local SWCD.
2. Apply to the Department of Taxation, Tax Credit Unit (Form ABM). The Department sends a letter certifying the credit; that letter is the amount entered on Line 3A.
## Required Information
- Type of approved plan (soil conservation or resource management) and the acreage covered
- BMP expenditures for the year and any cost-share payments received
- SWCD certification and the Department's certification letter
- Ownership percentage if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you farm for market or keep horses, and do you have an approved soil conservation or resource management plan?
- How much did you spend on SWCD-approved practices, and did you receive cost-share payments?
- Have you received the SWCD certification and the Department of Taxation letter?
## Common Errors
- Taking 50% when a cost-share payment was received
- Exceeding $75,000
- Claiming another Virginia credit for the same practices
- Reporting it in Section 2 as nonrefundable
## Prompt
- I fenced cattle out of the stream under my conservation district plan.
