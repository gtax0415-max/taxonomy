# Credit for Tax Paid to Other States (Schedule OSC; Form 760, Line 24)
## Description
Virginia taxes residents on all income wherever earned. When a resident also pays income tax to another state on the same income, Schedule OSC allows a nonrefundable credit for that tax, limited to the Virginia tax on that income. One computation is made per state (two per schedule), and the total from Schedule OSC, Line 21 goes to Form 760, Line 24. Tax paid to a foreign country is not covered here; a foreign pension's tax is handled by the Foreign Source Retirement Income credit on Schedule CR.
## How the lines flow
- Schedule OSC, Lines 1-10 (first state) and Lines 11-20 (second state) → Schedule OSC, Line 21 → Form 760, Line 24
- Nonrefundable: together with the other nonrefundable credits, limited to the net tax on Form 760, Line 18
