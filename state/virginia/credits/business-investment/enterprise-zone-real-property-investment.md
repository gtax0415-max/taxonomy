# Enterprise Zone Real Property Investment Tax Credit Carryover (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 2, Line 2A)
## Description
The Enterprise Zone Act Tax Credit has expired, and only Enterprise Zone Real Property Investment Tax Credit carryover amounts from prior years are allowed in 2026. No new credit can be earned, and no current-year certificate is involved; the credit was originally administered by the Virginia Department of Housing and Community Development (DHCD). The booklet lists it as "Enterprise Zone Act Tax Credit (carryover only)".
## Carryover worksheet
- A. Credit carried over from prior years
- B. Allowable credit: the amount from Line A or the maximum credit allowed
- C. Amount to be carried over to next year: Line A minus Line B
Enter Line B on Schedule CR, Section 1, Line 2A. The allowable amount is also limited by the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit (Section 1, Part 1, Line 1A less other credits claimed).
## Expiration date note
The draft Schedule CR instructions and the draft Schedule CR form state the expiration differently: the instructions say the credit expired June 30, 2019, while the form says it expired for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2020. Either way, only carryover remains. The instructions do not state the remaining carryover period; use the period that applied when the credit was earned. Confirm both points against the final 2026 Schedule CR.
## Required Information
- The original credit year and amount, and the carryover used in each later year
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have an unused enterprise zone real property investment credit from a prior year?
- How much of it have you already used, and in which years?
## Common Errors
- Trying to claim a new 2026 enterprise zone credit (only carryover is allowed)
- Claiming more than the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Omitting the carryover computation schedule
## Prompt
- I rehabbed a building in an enterprise zone years ago and still have credit left.
