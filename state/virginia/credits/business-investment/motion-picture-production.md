# Motion Picture Production Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 3, Part 2, Line 2A)
## Description
Qualifying motion picture production companies may receive a series of refundable individual and corporate income tax credits for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, but prior to January 1, 2031 (Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:03). HB 400 and SB 612 (2026 Acts, Chapters 795 and 827, effective July 1, 2026) extended the sunset from January 1, 2027, to January 1, 2031. It is one of the four refundable Schedule CR credits, so it is not limited by the maximum nonrefundable credit and has no carryover line.
## Credit components
- Base credit: for a production with qualifying expenses of at least $250,000 filmed in Virginia, 15% of qualifying expenses, or 20% if filmed in an economically distressed area designated by the Virginia Economic Development Partnership Authority.
- Additional Virginia resident credit: 10% of total aggregate Virginia resident payroll if total Virginia production costs are at least $250,000 but not more than $1 million; 20% if over $1 million.
- Additional first-time industry employee credit: 10% of total aggregate payroll for Virginia residents employed as first-time actors or first-time production crew members.

The aggregate of all motion picture credits issued is capped at $6.5 million per fiscal year. Political advertising, news programs, live sporting events, reality television and obscene material do not qualify. An episodic television season counts as one production. No interest is paid on the refund.
## Certification
The production company must apply to the Virginia Film Office before production begins in Virginia and enter into a Memorandum of Understanding. After production, the Film Office certifies the final credit amount and the fiscal year in which it may be refunded. The credit may be claimed only after that certification. Enter the certified amount on Line 2A. The return for the year production is completed must state the production name and description and include a detailed accounting of the qualifying expenses.
## Required Information
- Film Office certificate: credit amount and refund fiscal year
- Production name, description and completion date
- Qualifying expenses, Virginia resident payroll and first-time employee payroll
- Ownership share if allocated through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Did your company (or an entity you own) produce a film in Virginia with a Film Office Memorandum of Understanding?
- Have you received the certification, and what amount does it authorize?
- Did a pass-through entity issue you a Schedule VK-1 for this credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming before the certification is issued
- Reporting it with the nonrefundable credits in Section 2 instead of Section 3
- Treating it as limited by tax liability
