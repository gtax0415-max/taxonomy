# International Trade Facility Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 22, Line 22F)
## Description
The International Trade Facility Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:06) was available for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, but before January 1, 2025. It equaled either $3,500 per qualified full-time employee or 2% of capital investment tied to increased trade activities. It can no longer be earned, and the Department issued no credits after the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 10 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line D: 50% of tax owed this taxable year
- Line E: maximum credit, lesser of Line C or Line D
- Line 22F: lesser of Line E or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line G: Line C minus Line F, carried forward only within the 10-year carryover period
## Recapture
The credit is subject to recapture if the number of qualified full-time employees in any of the 5 years after the credit year falls below the average number employed during the credit year. If it falls below the number employed before any credit was claimed, all credits for that facility are recaptured. Recapture first reduces credits carried forward before it increases tax. Credits earned in 2021 through 2024 are still inside the 5-year recapture window in 2026.
## Required Information
- Year the credit was earned and the amount the Department certified
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Qualified full-time employee count in the credit year and in 2026
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have unused international trade facility credit from before 2025?
- Has the business's full-time employee count dropped below its credit-year average?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the 50%-of-tax cap on Line D
- Overlooking recapture when employment falls
- Carrying forward beyond 10 years
## Prompt
- Our port logistics company still has international trade facility credit carryover.
