# Hardwood Initiative Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 32, Line 32D)
## Description
The Hardwood Initiative Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-339.13, the reforestation and afforestation tax credit) was available for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2022, but before January 1, 2025. It equaled the lesser of out-of-pocket expenditures for Department of Forestry hardwood best practices or $1,000 per taxpayer per year. Taxpayers can no longer earn it. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 32D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification and pass-through
No new credit is authorized for 2026. Owners of pass-through entities use the share on their Schedule VK-1. A computation schedule is required for a carried-forward credit.
## Required Information
- Year the credit was earned (2022, 2023 or 2024) and the amount approved
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have hardwood initiative credit left from a year before 2025?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 years
## Prompt
- Our sawmill still has some Virginia hardwood credit carryover.
