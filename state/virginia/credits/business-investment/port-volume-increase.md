# Port Volume Increase Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 23, Line 23F)
## Description
The Virginia Port Volume Increase Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:10) was available for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, but before January 1, 2025, to agricultural, manufacturing-related and mineral and gas entities that increased their cargo volume through Virginia ports. Taxpayers can no longer earn it. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line D: reserved for future use
- Line E: Line C less Line D
- Line 23F: lesser of Line E or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line G: Line E minus Line F, carried forward only within the 5-year carryover period
## Transfers
Credits issued for taxable years 2018 through 2024 were transferable, but only within one calendar year of the holder earning the credit. No transfer can occur in 2026. A transferee claims the credit it bought as carryover, within the original 5-year period.
## Certification and pass-through
No new credit is authorized for 2026. Owners of pass-through entities use the amount shown on their Schedule VK-1. Enclose a computation schedule for the carried-forward credit.
## Required Information
- Year the credit was earned and the amount the Virginia Port Authority certified
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Credit bought by transfer, if any, and its original issue year
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have port volume increase credit left from before 2025, and from which year?
## Common Errors
- Entering a current-year credit (Lines A and D are reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 years
## Prompt
- We still have Virginia port volume credit on our books.
