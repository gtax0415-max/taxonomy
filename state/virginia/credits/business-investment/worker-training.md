# Worker Training Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 30, Line 30D)
## Description
The Worker Training Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-439.6:1) had two parts:
- 35% of eligible worker training expenses (up to $500 per qualified employee or $1,000 per non-highly compensated worker), for taxable years beginning before July 1, 2025. A calendar-year 2025 return could still earn it.
- 35% of a manufacturer's costs for orientation and training for students in grades 6-12 (up to $2,000 a year), for taxable years beginning before January 1, 2025.

Neither part can be earned for 2026. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 3 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 30D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 3-year carryover period
## Certification and pass-through
No new credit can be earned in 2026. Owners of a partnership, S corporation or LLC use the share on their Schedule VK-1. A computation schedule is required when claiming a carried-forward credit. Because the carryover period is only 3 years, the claiming order (prior-year carryovers in expiration order) matters when the maximum nonrefundable credit is tight. Credit earned in 2022 expires after 2025, so the oldest usable carryover in 2026 is from 2023.
## Required Information
- Year each credit was earned and the amount approved
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have unused worker training credit, and in which year was it earned?
## Common Errors
- Carrying forward beyond 3 years
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
## Prompt
- I have a Virginia worker training credit left over from 2024.
