# Major Business Facility Job Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 8, Line 8D)
## Description
The Major Business Facility Job Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-439) applies to taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 1995, but before July 1, 2025. It is $1,000 per qualified full-time employee above the threshold (50 jobs, or 25 in an economically distressed area or enterprise zone). The credit year is the taxable year after the facility opens or expands, and one-half of the credit is allowed in each of two years. A calendar-year taxpayer's 2025 taxable year began before July 1, 2025, so it could still be a credit year. A business approved to receive the first half in 2025 may obtain the remaining 50% for Taxable Year 2026. Unused credits can be carried forward for 10 taxable years or until fully taken, whichever is sooner.
## How to compute
- Line A: current credit amount authorized by the Department of Taxation (include all expansions); for 2026 this is the remaining 50% installment for businesses approved in 2025
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years (include all expansions)
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line D (8D): lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried to next year only within the 10-year carryover period
## Recapture
If the number of qualified full-time employees drops below the credit-year average in any of the 5 years after the credit year, the credit is recomputed and the difference recaptured. If the number drops below the threshold, all credits for that facility are recaptured. Recapture first reduces credits not yet allowed and carryovers.
## Certification
The Line A amount is the amount authorized by the Department of Taxation; a taxpayer cannot compute its own current-year amount. An owner of a partnership, S corporation or LLC receives a share on a Schedule VK-1, generally in proportion to ownership.
## Status for 2026
Current-year: only the second 50% installment for businesses approved in 2025. Otherwise carryover only.
## Required Information
- Department authorization letter, including the second-installment amount
- Carryover from prior years, by facility and expansion, with year of origin
- Qualified full-time employee count in the credit year and in 2026
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Was your business approved for the credit with the first half received in 2025?
- Do you have unused carryover from earlier years, including expansions?
- Did you receive a Schedule VK-1 showing this credit?
- Has employment at the facility fallen since the credit year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a new credit for a credit year beginning on or after July 1, 2025
- Leaving out expansion amounts on Lines A and B
- Carrying the credit beyond 10 years
- Overlooking recapture when employment falls
## Prompt
- My company is getting the second half of its major business facility job credit this year.
