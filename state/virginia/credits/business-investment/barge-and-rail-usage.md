# Barge and Rail Usage Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 24, Line 24D)
## Description
The Barge and Rail Usage Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-439.12:09) was available for taxable years beginning on and after January 1, 2011, but before January 1, 2025. It went to international trade facilities moving cargo by barge or rail instead of trucks. It can no longer be earned, and the Department issued no credits after the fiscal year ending June 30, 2025. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 5 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. For 2026 it is carryover only.
## How to compute
- Line A: reserved for future use
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 24D: lesser of Line C or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit
- Line E: Line C minus Line D, carried forward only within the 5-year carryover period
## Certification and pass-through
No new authorization applies in 2026. Owners of a partnership, S corporation or LLC use the share shown on the Schedule VK-1. A computation schedule is required for a carried-forward credit.
## Required Information
- Year the credit was earned and the amount the Department certified
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Ownership share if received through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Do you have barge and rail usage credit left from a year before 2025?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a current-year amount (Line A is reserved)
- Carrying forward beyond 5 years
