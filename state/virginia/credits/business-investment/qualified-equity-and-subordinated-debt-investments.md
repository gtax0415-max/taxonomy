# Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Investments Tax Credit (Schedule CR, Section 1, Part 11)
## Description
The Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Investments Tax Credit (Va. Code § 58.1-339.4) equaled 50% of qualified investments in small Virginia technology businesses, for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1999, but before January 1, 2026. 2025 was the last year it could be earned. Credits earned in prior taxable years can be carried forward for 15 taxable years or until the total credit has been taken, whichever is sooner. HB 2653 (2025 Acts, Chapter 306, effective July 1, 2025) added a sunset that repeals the credit for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026. The 2025 booklet lists it as carryover only. Confirm on the 2026 Schedule CR that Part 11 still accepts carryovers after the repeal before claiming one.
## How to compute (SPOUSE and YOU columns)
- Line A: reserved for future use (no current-year credit)
- Line B: carryover credit from prior years, in each column
- Line C: Line A + Line B
- Line 11D: your credit, lesser of Line C (YOU) or the balance of the maximum nonrefundable credit, not to exceed $50,000 per taxpayer
- Line 11E: spouse's credit, the same test for the SPOUSE column, not to exceed $50,000 per taxpayer
- Line F: Line C less Line D and/or Line E, carried forward only within the 15-year carryover period
## Holding period and forfeiture
The investor must hold the equity for at least three full calendar years after the year the credit was allocated. Exceptions apply for liquidation of the business, a merger or acquisition by an unaffiliated party, or the investor's death. Otherwise, selling early forfeits both used and unused credits, plus interest of 1% per month, compounded monthly, from the allocation date. Credits allocated for 2023, 2024 and 2025 are still inside this window during 2026.
## Interaction with Schedule ADJ
Taxpayers claiming the Qualified Equity and Subordinated Debt Credit cannot claim the Certain Long-Term Capital Gains subtraction (Schedule ADJ, code 52) for investments in the same business.
## Required Information
- Year each credit was allocated and the amount, for each spouse
- Amount of the credit used in each later year
- Whether any of the investment was sold or redeemed in 2026, and why
- Any code 52 subtraction claimed for the same business
## Questions
- Do you or your spouse have unused qualified equity or subordinated debt credit from earlier years?
- In which year was each credit earned?
- Did you sell any of the investment before holding it three full calendar years?
- Did you also claim the code 52 capital gains subtraction for the same business?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit on Line A (it is reserved)
- Combining spouses' carryovers in one column or exceeding $50,000 per taxpayer
- Claiming both this credit and the code 52 subtraction for the same business
- Missing the forfeiture when the investment is sold early
## Prompt
- I invested in a Virginia startup years ago and still have credit to use.
