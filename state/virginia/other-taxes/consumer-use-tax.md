# Consumer's Use Tax (Form 760, Line 33)
## Description
When a retailer does not collect Virginia sales tax, the purchaser owes it directly as consumer's use tax. It applies to purchases, leases or rentals of tangible personal property shipped or brought into Virginia: websites, shopping networks, mail order catalogs and out-of-state retailers. Report the tax on Line 33; if none is owed, fill in the oval on Line 33.
## What is taxable
Taxable items include food, furniture, carpets, clothing, linens, appliances, computers, books, CDs, DVDs, artwork, antiques and jewelry. Separately stated shipping and delivery charges are not taxable, but "handling" and combined "shipping and handling" charges are. Nonprescription drugs and proprietary medicines for the cure, mitigation, treatment or prevention of disease in humans are exempt.
## Rates (by delivery location)
- 7%: Historic Triangle (James City County, York County, Williamsburg City)
- 6.3%: localities with the additional 1% local option tax (City of Danville; Charlotte, Gloucester, Halifax, Henry, Northampton, Patrick and Pittsylvania Counties)
- 6%: Hampton Roads (Chesapeake, Franklin, Hampton, Newport News, Norfolk, Poquoson, Portsmouth, Suffolk and Virginia Beach Cities; Isle of Wight and Southampton Counties), Northern Virginia (Alexandria, Fairfax, Falls Church, Manassas and Manassas Park Cities; Arlington, Fairfax, Loudoun and Prince William Counties), Central Virginia (Charles City, Chesterfield, Goochland, Hanover, Henrico, New Kent and Powhatan Counties; Richmond City)
- 5.3%: all other cities and counties
- 1%: eligible food for home consumption and personal hygiene products (exempt from state tax, 1% local tax everywhere)

Mecklenburg County is authorized to levy the additional 1% but does not collect it, so it uses 5.3%.

2026 change: the 2026 Appropriation Act (HB 30, Special Session I) lets every county and city adopt the additional 1% school-construction tax by referendum, effective July 1, 2026. Referendums are on the November 3, 2026 ballot, and in Northern Virginia the revenue may also fund transit. Any new rate will start after the vote, so the 2026 rates above should still apply to most 2026 purchases. Check the rate in effect for the purchase date and delivery locality.

## Sales and Use Tax Worksheet (required for purchases of $1,000 or more)
1. Taxable purchases: Column A general merchandise; Column B food and personal hygiene products
2. Multiply by the rate: Column A at 5.3%, 6.0%, 6.3% or 7.0% for your city or county; Column B at 1.0%
3. Add Line 2, Columns A and B; enter on Form 760, Line 33
The Department may review your account and bill you if it finds the tax was underestimated.
## Required Information
- Each purchase without Virginia sales tax: date, amount and delivery locality
- Whether any single purchase was $1,000 or more
- Split between general merchandise and food or personal hygiene items
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online, by phone, by mail or out of state without being charged Virginia sales tax?
- Did any single purchase cost $1,000 or more?
- Where were the items delivered?
- Were any purchases food for home use or personal hygiene products?
## Common Errors
- Leaving Line 33 blank instead of entering the tax or filling in the no tax due oval
- Using the table for purchases of $1,000 or more
- Using the 5.3% rate in a 6%, 6.3% or 7% locality
- Excluding "handling" charges, or including separately stated shipping
## Prompt
- I bought a couch online and wasn't charged sales tax; do I owe Virginia anything?
- What is the use tax line on the Virginia 760?
- I live in Fairfax County; what rate do I use?
