# Other Taxes (Form 760, Line 33)
## Description
Virginia's resident return collects one tax other than income tax: consumer's use tax on purchases where no Virginia sales tax was charged. It is reported on Form 760, Line 33 and added on Line 34, so it increases the amount owed (Line 35) or reduces the refund (Line 36). It does not change Virginia taxable income or the income tax on Line 18. Virginia has no separate household employment tax or surtax on the individual income tax return.
## How the lines flow
- Sales Tax Estimation Table (purchases under $1,000) or Sales and Use Tax Worksheet → Form 760, Line 33
- Form 760, Line 33 → Line 34 (with Lines 29-32) → Line 35 (amount you owe) or Line 36 (refund)
- No use tax owed → fill in the oval on Line 33
