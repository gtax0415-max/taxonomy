# Contributions and Checkoffs (Schedule VAC; Form 760, Lines 30-31)
## Description
Schedule VAC lets a Virginia filer send part or all of a refund to Commonwealth Savers education or ABLE accounts (Section I → Form 760, Line 30) and to listed voluntary contribution organizations, library foundations and public school foundations (Section II → Form 760, Line 31). Both lines are added into Form 760, Line 34, so they reduce the refund on Line 36 or, for Section II, Section C, can be paid with a balance due on Line 35. They are not tax credits and do not reduce tax.
## How the lines flow
- Schedule VAC, Section I, Line 6 (with Schedule VACS if more than 5 accounts) → Form 760, Line 30
- Schedule VAC, Section II, Line 14 → Form 760, Line 31
- Form 760, Line 34 = Lines 29 through 33 → Line 35 (amount you owe) or Line 36 (refund)
