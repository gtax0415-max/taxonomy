# Virginia Individual Income Tax (Form 760, Tax Year 2026)
## Description
Virginia taxes full-year residents on Form 760. The return starts from federal adjusted gross income, applies Virginia additions and subtractions to reach Virginia adjusted gross income (VAGI), subtracts deductions and exemptions, and taxes the result at 2%, 3%, 5% and 5.75%. Credits, payments, contributions, the addition to tax and use tax then produce the balance due or refund. Virginia has no local individual income tax.
## How the return flows
- Lines 1-9: federal adjusted gross income, additions and subtractions → VAGI (income/)
- Lines 10-15: itemized or standard deduction, exemptions and Schedule ADJ deductions → Virginia taxable income (deductions/)
- Lines 16-18: tax, Spouse Tax Adjustment, net tax (tax-computation/)
- Lines 19a-26: withholding, estimated and extension payments, and credits (payments/, credits/)
- Lines 27-36: tax owed or overpayment, contributions, addition to tax, use tax, amount owed or refund (payments/, other-taxes/)
## Key 2026 figures
- Filing threshold: VAGI of $11,950 (Filing Status 1 or 3) or $23,900 (Filing Status 2)
- Standard deduction: $8,750 or $17,500 (joint)
- Exemptions: $930 each, plus $800 for 65 or older or blind
- Virginia Earned Income Credit: 20% of the federal credit, refundable
- Estimated tax threshold: $1,000
- Fixed federal conformity date: December 31, 2025
- Due date: May 1, 2027 (a Saturday, so May 3, 2027 is timely)
