# Federal Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
Line 1 of the Ohio IT 1040 is federal AGI from Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 11a. Ohio AGI is this number plus Schedule of Adjustments additions minus Schedule of Adjustments deductions. Line 1 must match the federal return.
## Amount
The amount on federal Form 1040 / 1040-SR / 1040-NR, line 11a.
## Notes
- If federal AGI is zero or negative, include a copy of page 1 of the federal return
- A foreign trust that files federal Form 1040-NR does not use the IT 1040. It files Ohio IT 1041
- Ohio does not start from federal taxable income. Below-the-line federal deductions never touch this line
## Required Information
- Federal Form 1040, 1040-SR, or 1040-NR, line 11a
- Page 1 of the federal return if line 1 is zero or negative
## Questions
- Does Ohio line 1 equal federal line 11a?
- Is federal AGI zero or negative?
## Common Errors
- Entering federal taxable income instead of federal AGI
- Using Form 1040 line 11 when the form shows 11a as the AGI box Ohio wants
- Filing an IT 1040 for a foreign trust
## Prompt
- Where does my federal AGI go on the Ohio return?
- My federal AGI is negative. Do I still file Ohio?
