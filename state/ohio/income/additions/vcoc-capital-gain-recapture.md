# Ohio VCOC Capital Gain Recapture
## Description
Addition that reverses a deduction taken under R.C. 5747.01(A)(35)(a) when the Director of Development certifies to the tax commissioner that the requirements for the deduction were not met. R.C. 5747.01(A)(35)(b). This is an income addition, not a reduced deduction.

The certification runs to the tax commissioner, not to you. The Director reviews the VCOC's annual reporting under R.C. 122.851(D), so a company-level failure can trigger recapture for every investor who deducted.
## Amount
Amounts previously deducted under (A)(35)(a) that the certification covers. The add-back is not limited to the current year's deduction — it reaches back to whatever was deducted in earlier years under that division.
## Eligibility
- You deducted Ohio VCOC capital gain in this year or a prior year
- The Director of Development has certified noncompliance
## Required Information
- Prior-year Ohio returns showing the (A)(35)(a) deduction and the amount
- The noncompliance certification or notice
- The R.C. 122.851 certificate from the VCOC, which you must keep for four years from the later of the return's final filing date or the date the return claiming the deduction was filed
## Questions
- Did you take the VCOC capital-gain deduction in 2026 or any later year?
- Has Development certified that the company failed the requirements?
- How much did you deduct, and in which years?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the recapture off after a certification
- Putting the recapture in the deduction column
- Adding back only the current year when earlier years were also deducted
- Assuming a company-level failure does not reach an individual investor
