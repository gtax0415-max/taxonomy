# Pass-Through Entity Add-Back
## Description
Add Ohio pass-through entity tax from Form IT 1140 and/or IT 4738 to the extent it was not included in federal AGI. This is the counterpart to the refundable pass-through entity credit on Schedule of Credits line 43.
## Amount
Amounts reported on Ohio IT K-1, lines 3a or 4a, that did not increase federal AGI.
## Eligibility
You are an investor in a PTE that paid Ohio tax on IT 1140 and/or IT 4738.
## Required Information
- Ohio IT K-1, lines 3a and 4a
- Matching PTE credit on Schedule of Credits line 43
## Questions
- Did your K-1 report Ohio entity tax on line 3a or 4a?
- Did you also claim the line 43 credit?
## Common Errors
- Taking the PTE credit without this add-back
- Putting K-1 tax paid on IT 1040 withholding line 14
## Prompt
- My S corporation paid Ohio tax for me. Do I add it back?
