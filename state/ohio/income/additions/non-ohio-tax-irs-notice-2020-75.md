# Non-Ohio Tax Related to IRS Notice 2020-75
## Description
Add your share of taxes a pass-through entity paid to another state or the District of Columbia if the tax was enacted to comply with IRS Notice 2020-75 and was deducted in computing federal or Ohio AGI.
## Amount
Proportionate share reported on Ohio IT K-1, lines 3b or 4b.
## Eligibility
Both of the following:
- The tax was enacted for IRS Notice 2020-75
- The tax was deducted in computing federal or Ohio AGI
## Required Information
- Ohio IT K-1 lines 3b or 4b
- The other state's PTE tax return, if the K-1 is missing
## Questions
- Did a partnership deduct another state's PTE tax on your federal K-1?
## Common Errors
- Adding ordinary state income tax instead of a 2020-75 entity tax
- Missing the matching business-income piece on Schedule of Business Income line 8
## Prompt
- My partnership paid New York PTE tax. Do I add it back in Ohio?
