# Non-Qualified College Tuition Expenses
## Description
Add 1099-Q distributions from a 529 plan that were not used for qualified higher-education expenses (and were not paid because of death, disability, or a scholarship) if you deducted the contribution or a tuition-credit purchase on an earlier Ohio Schedule of Adjustments.
## Amount
The 1099-Q amount that meets all three tests: not already in federal AGI; not a qualified or exception distribution; previously deducted in Ohio.
## Eligibility
You took an Ohio 529 deduction or tuition-credit deduction in a prior year and later took a nonqualified distribution.
## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q
- Prior-year Ohio 529 deductions
## Questions
- Was this 529 withdrawal for qualified higher-education expenses?
- Did you deduct those contributions on a prior Ohio return?
## Common Errors
- Adding a qualified 529 withdrawal
- Adding a distribution that was already in federal AGI
- Adding a contribution made by someone else that you never deducted
## Prompt
- I took money out of an Ohio 529 for a car. Do I add it back?
