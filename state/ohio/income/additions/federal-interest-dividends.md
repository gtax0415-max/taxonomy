# Federal Interest and Dividends
## Description
Add interest or dividends on U.S. government obligations that are exempt from federal tax but not exempt from state tax. Most ordinary Treasury interest is the opposite case and is deducted on line 26.
## Amount
Only the federally exempt slice that Ohio is allowed to tax.
## Required Information
- 1099-INT or statement identifying the obligation
## Questions
- Is this interest federally exempt but still taxable to Ohio?
## Common Errors
- Putting Series EE bond interest here instead of on line 26
- Adding Fannie Mae or Ginnie Mae interest that is already in federal AGI and is not deductible on line 26 either
## Prompt
- I have federally tax-exempt federal interest. Does Ohio add it back?
