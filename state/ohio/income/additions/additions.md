# Ohio Additions
## Description
Income Ohio taxes that was not in federal AGI, or that Ohio requires you to put back after a federal deduction. The total is Schedule of Adjustments line 12, which flows to IT 1040 line 2a. Leave line 2a blank if there are no additions. Include the Schedule of Adjustments whenever line 2a has an amount.

## Matching deductions
Most additions in this folder reverse or pair with a Schedule of Adjustments deduction:

- Line 5 loss on Ohio public obligations ↔ line 18 income from Ohio public obligations
- Line 6 nonmedical MSA withdrawal ↔ line 45 MSA deposits
- Line 4 nonqualified 529 distribution ↔ line 37 529 contributions
- Line 8 ineligible Homebuyer Plus withdrawal ↔ line 24 Homebuyer Plus contributions
- Line 9 168(k) / 179 add-back ↔ line 27 Ohio depreciation deduction (1/5, 1/2, or 1/6 in later years)
- VCOC recapture ↔ the R.C. 5747.01(A)(35)(a) VCOC capital-gain deduction
- Line 2 PTE add-back ↔ the refundable PTE credit on Schedule of Credits line 43
