# Ineligible Withdrawals From an Ohio Homebuyer Plus Account
## Description
Add withdrawals or transfers from an Ohio Homebuyer Plus account that were not used for eligible expenses, plus leftover amounts when the account is closed. Eligible expenses are a down payment or closing costs on the owner's Ohio primary residence, account fees, a transfer to another account you own at a different institution, or amounts withdrawn and redeposited into an account you own within 90 days.
## Amount
Limited to amounts you previously deducted for an account you own, plus amounts deposited into your account by anyone other than you.
## Required Information
- Homebuyer Plus account statements
- Prior-year line 24 deductions
- Closing or purchase records if you bought a home
## Questions
- Was the withdrawal for an Ohio primary-residence down payment or closing costs?
- Did you redeposit within 90 days?
## Common Errors
- Adding an eligible down-payment withdrawal
- Adding more than you previously deducted plus third-party deposits
## Prompt
- I closed my Ohio Homebuyer Plus account and kept the cash.
