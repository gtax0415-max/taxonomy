# Reimbursement of Expenses
## Description
Add reimbursements received in 2026 for expenses you deducted on a previously filed Ohio income tax return, if the reimbursement is not included in your 2026 federal AGI. R.C. 5747.01(A)(11)(b).

The deduction being reversed is an Ohio Schedule of Adjustments deduction from an earlier year — unreimbursed medical and health care expenses is the common one. If you never took an Ohio deduction for the expense, there is nothing to add back.
## Amount
The 2026 reimbursement that was not included in federal AGI, limited to the amount you previously deducted on an Ohio return.
## Required Information
- Prior Ohio return showing the deduction
- Reimbursement records for 2026
- Confirmation the reimbursement is not already in 2026 federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you deduct this expense on an earlier Ohio return?
- Is the reimbursement already in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Adding a reimbursement that is already in federal AGI
- Confusing this line with line 28, which deducts federal Schedule A refunds that ARE in federal AGI
- Adding a reimbursement of an expense that was only deducted federally, never on the Ohio schedule
## Prompt
- I got repaid for medical expenses I deducted on last year's Ohio return.
