# Non-Ohio State or Local Government Interest and Dividends
## Description
Add interest and dividends paid on obligations or securities of a non-Ohio state, city, county, or other local government. Ohio does not exempt other states' municipal bonds.
## Amount
Interest and/or dividends paid on those non-Ohio obligations, but only the portion that is not already in federal AGI.
## Do not include
- Amounts already in federal AGI
- Obligations of Ohio or an Ohio city, county, school district, or other Ohio local government
- Obligations of a U.S. territory or the federal government
## Eligibility
You received interest or dividends from a non-Ohio state or local issuer.
## Required Information
- 1099-INT / 1099-DIV showing the payer and state
- Confirmation the amount is not already in federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you own municipal bonds issued by another state or city?
- Is that interest already in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Adding Ohio municipal-bond interest
- Adding Treasury interest here instead of checking line 10 or line 26
- Double-counting interest already in federal AGI
## Prompt
- I own California municipal bonds.
- Does Ohio tax out-of-state muni interest?
