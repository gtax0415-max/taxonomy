# Medical Savings Account Add-Back
## Description
Add amounts withdrawn from a medical savings account for nonmedical purposes, but only if those amounts were deducted on the Ohio Schedule of Adjustments in some tax year. R.C. 5747.01(A)(14). Use the MSA worksheet, page 42 of the 2025 booklet; confirm the page when the 2026 booklet ships.

The same worksheet computes the matching MSA deposit deduction on Schedule of Adjustments line 45.
## Amount
The worksheet nets the year's nonmedical withdrawals against the year's deposits and account earnings. Complete the worksheet and carry its result across — do not enter gross withdrawals on line 6.
## Required Information
- MSA contribution and withdrawal records
- Prior-year Ohio returns showing the MSA deduction
- Completed MSA worksheet (include a copy with the return)
## Questions
- Did you take a nonmedical withdrawal from an Ohio MSA?
- Did you deduct those deposits in a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Adding a withdrawal used for qualifying medical expenses
- Adding HSA amounts that were deducted federally, not on the Ohio schedule
- Entering gross withdrawals instead of the worksheet result
## Prompt
- I pulled money from my Ohio medical savings account to pay rent.
