# Ohio Public Obligations Loss
## Description
Add any loss from the sale or disposition of Ohio public obligations to the extent that loss reduced federal AGI. Ohio also exempts the matching gain on Schedule of Adjustments line 18.
## Amount
The federally deducted loss on Ohio public obligations.
## Required Information
- Sale records for the Ohio obligation
- The federal return showing the loss
## Questions
- Did you sell Ohio public obligations at a loss that hit federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Adding a loss on a non-Ohio bond
- Forgetting the matching income deduction on line 18 when you have a gain
## Prompt
- I sold Ohio municipal bonds at a loss.
