# IRC 168(k) and 179 Depreciation
## Description
Add 5/6 of federal bonus depreciation under IRC 168(k), and 5/6 of IRC 179 expense above the December 31, 2002 limit. The add-back is deducted in later years on line 27.
## Amount
- Default: 5/6 of 168(k) and excess 179
- Employers who increased Ohio withholding by at least 10% over the prior year: 2/3
- Taxpayers with a federal NOL caused by the 168(k) and/or 179 expense: 6/6 (the entire amount)
## No add-back
- Employers who increased Ohio withholding by at least their total 168(k) and 179 expense
- 168(k) or 179 from a pass-through entity you own less than 5%
## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 and K-1 depreciation detail
- Ohio withholding comparison if claiming the 2/3 or no-add-back employer rule
- Worksheet at tax.ohio.gov/worksheets
## Questions
- How much 168(k) and 179 did you take federally?
- Do you own at least 5% of the pass-through that generated it?
## Common Errors
- Adding 100% when 5/6 is required
- Adding depreciation from a <5% PTE interest
- Forgetting the later line 27 recovery
## Prompt
- I took federal bonus depreciation. What do I add back in Ohio?
