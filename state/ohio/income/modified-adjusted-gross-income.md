# Modified Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
MAGI is Ohio AGI (IT 1040 line 3) plus the business income deduction from Schedule of Adjustments line 13. If you did not take the business income deduction, MAGI equals Ohio AGI. R.C. 5747.01(II).

MAGI is not an IT 1040 line. It is the test number for exemptions and for several credits.
## What MAGI controls
- Exemption amount on IT 1040 line 4 ($2,400 / $2,150 / $1,900 / $0)
- Retirement income credit
- Lump sum retirement credit
- Senior citizen credit
- Lump sum distribution credit
- Child care and dependent care credit
- Exemption credit
- Joint filing credit
- Taxable income in an earned-income-base school district
## MAGI vs. MAGI less exemptions
Two different tests, and the files that use them differ:

| Test | Threshold | Applies to |
|---|---|---|
| MAGI | $500,000 (2026; was $750,000 in 2025) | Personal/dependent exemption, joint filing credit eligibility |
| MAGI | less than $40,000 | Child care and dependent care credit |
| MAGI less exemptions | less than $100,000 | Retirement income, lump sum retirement, senior citizen, lump sum distribution credits |
| MAGI less exemptions | less than $30,000 | Exemption credit ($20 per exemption) |
| MAGI less exemptions | tiered 0 / $25,000 / $50,000 / $75,000 | Joint filing credit percentage (20 / 15 / 10 / 5%) |

"MAGI less exemptions" subtracts the IT 1040 line 4 exemption amount from MAGI, not from Ohio AGI.
## The trap
The business income deduction does NOT help you qualify for anything that tests MAGI. Claiming it lowers Ohio AGI by the deducted amount and MAGI adds that same amount straight back, so MAGI is identical whether or not you take the deduction.

Example: a filer with $260,000 of business income and no other income has Ohio AGI of $10,000 after the $250,000 deduction, but MAGI of $260,000. They are over the $100,000 MAGI-less-exemptions cliff for the retirement and senior credits, and would have been over it either way. The deduction cuts the tax base. It does not buy credits.

The related trap runs the other direction: a taxpayer who looks at Ohio AGI alone will think they qualify for credits they cannot claim.
## Required Information
- Ohio AGI, IT 1040 line 3
- Business income deduction, Schedule of Adjustments line 13
- Worksheet on booklet page 40
## Questions
- Did you take the business income deduction?
- Are you testing a credit that uses MAGI, or MAGI less exemptions?
## Common Errors
- Using federal AGI as MAGI
- Using Ohio AGI as MAGI after taking the business income deduction
- Expecting the business income deduction to bring you under a MAGI threshold
- Applying a "MAGI less exemptions" threshold to a test that uses plain MAGI, or the reverse
- Treating MAGI as a phase-out when most MAGI tests are cliffs
## Prompt
- What is Ohio MAGI?
- Why didn't the business income deduction get me under the retirement credit limit?
