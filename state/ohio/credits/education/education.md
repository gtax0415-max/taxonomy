# Education Credits
## Description
Credits for home education spending, private-school tuition, donations to scholarship-granting organizations, and certified work-based learning. None of these is refundable.
## Credits in this folder
- Home School Expenses Credit (home-school-expenses-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 14. $250 per qualifying student
- Scholarship Donation Credit (scholarship-donation-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 15. $1,500 on a joint return, $750 otherwise
- Nonchartered, Nonpublic School Tuition Credit (nonchartered-nonpublic-school-tuition-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 16. $1,000 or $1,500 per return
- Credit for Work-Based Learning Experiences (work-based-learning-experiences-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 17. ODE certificate required
## Changes still worth flagging in 2026
- Home school expenses moved from $250 per RETURN to $250 per QUALIFYING STUDENT effective 2025. A household educating three qualifying students reaches $750
- The scholarship donation credit's joint cap has been a flat $1,500 since April 3, 2023, regardless of which spouse donated. The old "two separate $750 donations" rule is dead but still circulates on SGO fundraising pages
