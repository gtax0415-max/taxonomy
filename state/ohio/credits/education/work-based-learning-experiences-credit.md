# Credit for Work-Based Learning Experiences
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Education and Workforce (DEW, formerly the Ohio Department of Education) for approved work-based learning. You cannot compute this from expenses. The certificate states the amount and the tax year.

## Amount
The amount printed on the ODE certificate.

## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODE for this tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach the ownership split showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return

Details and applications are at the Department of Education and Workforce. Booklet text and older certificates may still say ODE; it is the same program.

## Required Information
- ODE certificate
- Tax year shown on the certificate
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity

## Questions
- Do you have an ODE certificate for this year?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?

## Common Errors
- Entering estimated training expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
