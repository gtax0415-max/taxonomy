# Nonchartered, Nonpublic School Tuition Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for tuition paid for a compulsory-age dependent to an eligible nonchartered, nonpublic K-12 school approved by the Ohio Department of Education. One credit per return.

## Amount
Lesser of total tuition paid for all dependents or:
- $1,000 if federal AGI is less than $50,000
- $1,500 if federal AGI is $50,000 or more

The income test uses FEDERAL AGI (IT 1040 line 1), not Ohio MAGI. Higher federal AGI produces the larger cap.

## Eligibility
- Tuition paid for a compulsory-age dependent
- School is on the approved nonchartered, nonpublic list maintained by the Department of Education and Workforce (formerly ODE); ODT links it at tax.ohio.gov/credits
- One credit per return regardless of filing status or number of children

Chartered nonpublic schools, public schools, and unapproved schools do not qualify.

## Required Information
- Tuition receipts for the tax year
- Proof of payment
- Confirmation the school is on the approved nonchartered, nonpublic list
- Federal AGI

## Questions
- Is the school nonchartered and on the ODE list?
- What is your federal AGI relative to $50,000?
- Are you paying tuition, or only fees and books?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a chartered Catholic or other chartered private school
- Using Ohio AGI for the $50,000 breakpoint
- Multiplying the cap by the number of children
- Including homeschool expenses here instead of line 14

## Prompt
- I pay tuition to a small private school that is not chartered.
- Does Ohio have a private school tuition credit?
