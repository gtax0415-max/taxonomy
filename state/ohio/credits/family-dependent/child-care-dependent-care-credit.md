# Child Care and Dependent Care Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for filers who claimed the federal credit for child and dependent care expenses on Form 2441 and whose Ohio MAGI is under $40,000. Ohio copies the federal credit at low income and then cuts it to 25% of a different federal line in the upper band.

## Amount
Use the worksheet on booklet page 45:
- If MAGI is under $20,000: the credit equals Form 2441, line 9c
- If MAGI is $20,000 through $39,999: the credit equals 25% of Form 2441, line 11
- If MAGI is $40,000 or more: $0

MAGI here is Ohio AGI plus the business income deduction. The $40,000 test is a cliff.

## Eligibility
- You claimed the federal child and dependent care credit on Form 2441 for the same year
- Ohio MAGI is less than $40,000
- Include the worksheet with the return

## Notes
- Ohio does not create its own qualifying-person or qualifying-expense test. No federal 2441 means no Ohio credit
- The two federal line references are different on purpose. Line 9c is the credit before limitation; line 11 is the credit after the federal tax-liability limit. Using the wrong line in the $20,000–$39,999 band overstates or understates the Ohio figure
- Nonrefundable, so it only helps if tax remains after the retirement and senior credits that print above it

## Required Information
- MAGI from the worksheet on booklet page 40
- Federal Form 2441, lines 9c and 11
- Proof the federal credit was claimed

## Questions
- Did you file federal Form 2441?
- Is your Ohio MAGI under $20,000 or under $40,000?
- Did you add the business income deduction back into MAGI?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it with MAGI of $40,000 or more
- Using federal AGI instead of Ohio MAGI
- Copying Form 2441 line 9c into the upper band instead of 25% of line 11
- Forgetting to attach the worksheet

## Prompt
- I claimed the federal child care credit and my Ohio income is about $28,000.
- Does Ohio match the federal dependent care credit?
