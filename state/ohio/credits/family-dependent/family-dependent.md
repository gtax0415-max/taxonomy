# Family and Dependent Credits
## Description
Credits that turn on household makeup: exemptions, a joint return, child or dependent care, and leftover adoption credit. None of these is refundable.
## Credits in this folder
- Exemption Credit (exemption-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 9. $20 per exemption if MAGI less exemptions is under $30,000. Nonrefundable
- Joint Filing Credit (joint-filing-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 12. 5%–20% of remaining tax, max $650. Nonrefundable
- Child Care and Dependent Care Credit (child-care-dependent-care-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 6. Requires federal Form 2441 and MAGI under $40,000. Nonrefundable
- Ohio Adoption Credit Carryforward (adoption-credit-carryforward.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 18. Carryforward only; the credit is repealed
