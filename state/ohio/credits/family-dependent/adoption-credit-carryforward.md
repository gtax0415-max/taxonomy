# Ohio Adoption Credit Carryforward
## Description
The Ohio adoption credit is repealed and cannot be newly generated. What replaced it is the Ohio Department of Children & Youth adoption GRANT, which is a Schedule of Adjustments deduction on line 23, not a credit. Send anyone with a recent adoption there. Unused amounts from years when the credit existed may be used for the remainder of the original five-year carryforward period.

## Amount
Whatever unused balance remains from a pre-repeal credit, until the five-year window closes or the balance is used.

## Eligibility
- You generated the credit in a year when former R.C. 5747.37 was in force
- The five-year carryforward has not expired
- You have remaining tax to absorb a nonrefundable credit

## Required Information
- Prior-year returns showing the original credit and amounts used
- Years remaining in the five-year window

## Questions
- Did you claim the Ohio adoption credit before it was repealed?
- How much is left, and which year did the clock start?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new adoption credit for a current-year adoption
- Losing track of the five-year limit
- Expecting a refund of unused carryforward

## Prompt
- We adopted last year and used to get an Ohio adoption credit.
- Can I still claim an Ohio adoption credit in 2026?
