# Ohio Individual Income Tax Credits — Index
## Description
Master index for the Ohio credit taxonomy. Ohio computes credits on the Ohio Schedule of Credits, then parks two totals on Form IT 1040: nonrefundable credits on line 9 and refundable credits on line 16. There is no city earned-income add-on. The only second-layer credit most people see is the $50 school-district senior citizen credit on Form SD 100.

Modified adjusted gross income is the gate for the household credits. MAGI is Ohio AGI (IT 1040, line 3) plus the business income deduction from Schedule of Adjustments, line 13. If you took that deduction, MAGI is HIGHER than Ohio AGI.

## The two landing lines
| IT 1040 line | What lands there | Refundable |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Ohio Schedule of Credits, line 40 — all nonrefundable credits | No |
| 16 | Ohio Schedule of Credits, line 47 — all refundable credits | Yes |

Credits apply in printed order on the Schedule of Credits. Lines 2 through 9 come off tax first. Line 12 (joint filing credit) is a percentage of the remaining tax on line 11, capped at $650. Lines 13 through 35 come off next. Residency credits on lines 38 and 39 come last among the nonrefundable credits. Refundable credits on lines 41 through 46 pay out on IT 1040 line 16.

## Folders
- income-based/ — Earned Income Credit (Schedule of Credits, line 13). Nonrefundable
- family-dependent/ — Exemption Credit (line 9), Joint Filing Credit (line 12), Child Care and Dependent Care Credit (line 6), Adoption Credit carryforward (line 18)
- education/ — Home School Expenses (line 14), Scholarship Donation (line 15), Nonchartered Nonpublic School Tuition (line 16), Work-Based Learning (line 17)
- retirement-senior/ — Retirement Income (line 2), Lump Sum Retirement (line 3), Senior Citizen (line 4), Lump Sum Distribution (line 5)
- civic-volunteer/ — Displaced Worker Training (line 7). Campaign Contribution is repealed for 2026
- other-jurisdiction/ — Nonresident Credit (line 38, Form IT NRC), Resident Credit (line 39, Form IT RC)
- business-investment/ — Certificate credits and the pass-through entity credit (lines 19–35 and 41–46)
- school-district/ — Senior Citizen Credit on Form SD 100, lines 26 and 36

## The MAGI cliff family
These die entirely once the test number reaches the ceiling. They are cliffs, not phase-outs. Note that the tests use TWO different numbers — read the middle column before applying a threshold:

| Credit | Test number | Must be |
|---|---|---|
| Retirement income, lump-sum retirement, senior citizen, lump-sum distribution | MAGI less exemptions | less than $100,000 |
| Exemption credit | MAGI less exemptions | less than $30,000 |
| Child care and dependent care | MAGI | less than $40,000 |
| Joint filing credit (eligibility) | MAGI | less than $500,000 |
| Personal and dependent exemptions | MAGI | less than $500,000 |

Each threshold is "less than," not "at or below." The joint filing credit uses MAGI for eligibility but MAGI LESS EXEMPTIONS for its 20/15/10/5% rate tier.

## 2026 tax parameters
| Item | 2026 amount |
|---|---|
| Nonbusiness tax | $332 + 2.75% of taxable nonbusiness income over $26,050 |
| Business income tax | 3% after the $250,000 / $125,000 MFS deduction |
| Personal exemption | $2,400 / $2,150 / $1,900; $0 if MAGI ≥ $500,000 |
| Exemption credit | $20 per exemption if MAGI less exemptions is less than $30,000 |
| Joint filing credit | Up to $650; MAGI must be under $500,000 |
| Educator expense deduction | $300 per qualifying educator |
| 529 / STABLE | $4,000 per beneficiary |
| Pregnancy resource center | $750 per taxpayer |
| VCOC capital gain deduction | New; 100% Ohio-business slice / 50% other. R.C. 5747.01(A)(35) |
| Qualifying capital gain, sale of a business interest | New; lesser of the gain or the entity's deductible payroll. R.C. 5747.01(A)(34), 5747.79 |
| Campaign contribution credit | Repealed |
| Calendar 2026 interest rate | 7% |
| Sales tax holiday | Aug 7–9, 2026; clothing ≤ $75, school items ≤ $20 |

## What changed for 2026
- Nonbusiness rate flattens to 2.75% over $26,050 (the 2025 3.125% bracket is gone)
- MAGI cliff for exemptions and the joint filing credit drops from $750,000 to $500,000
- Campaign contribution credit is repealed
- TWO new capital-gain deductions first available in 2026: the VCOC deduction, R.C. 5747.01(A)(35), and the qualifying capital gain on the sale of a business interest, R.C. 5747.01(A)(34) and 5747.79. Both come off before the $250,000 business income deduction when the gain is business income
- Inflation indexing of the brackets and the personal exemption is suspended for tax years 2025 AND 2026 by H.B. 96, Section 757.120(A). The exemption tiers are therefore locked at $2,400 / $2,150 / $1,900 for 2026 — they are not pending an indexed update
- School district income tax on estates repealed effective January 1, 2026
- 2026 IT 1040 booklet not published as of September 2026; Schedule of Credits line numbers below are from the 2025 schedule
