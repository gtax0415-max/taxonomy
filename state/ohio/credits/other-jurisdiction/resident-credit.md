# Resident Credit
## Description
Credit for Ohio residents (and part-year residents, for the resident portion) who paid income tax to another state or the District of Columbia on income that Ohio also taxes. Full-year nonresidents cannot take it.

## Amount
The LESSER of:
- Income taxes paid to another state or D.C., or
- The portion of your Ohio tax liability attributable to the income taxed by the other state

Form IT RC is required. Attach it.

## Eligibility
- Ohio resident or part-year resident
- Income taxed by Ohio and by another state or D.C.
- You actually paid the other jurisdiction's income tax
- Full-year nonresidents are barred

Local income taxes (city, county, occupational) are not "another state." They do not generate this credit.

## Required Information
- The other state's return and tax paid
- Description of the income taxed by both jurisdictions
- Completed Form IT RC

## Questions
- Which state also taxed this income?
- Was that a state income tax, or a city/occupational tax?
- Are you a full-year nonresident trying to use this line instead of IT NRC?

## Common Errors
- Claiming tax paid to a city as a resident credit
- Using this line as a nonresident instead of the nonresident credit
- Entering the other state's entire tax without limiting it to the Ohio-taxed slice
- Omitting Form IT RC

## Prompt
- I live in Ohio and pay tax to Indiana on a rental property.
- Can I credit my Kentucky tax on my Ohio return?
