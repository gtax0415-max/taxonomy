# Senior Citizen Credit
## Description
Flat $50 nonrefundable credit for a return on which the taxpayer (or, on a joint return, a qualifying spouse) was 65 or older at year-end, MAGI less exemptions is under $100,000, and the lump sum distribution credit has never been taken.

## Amount
- $50 per return, not per person
- A joint return with two spouses 65 or older still gets $50 on the IT 1040

## Eligibility
All of the following must be true:
- MAGI less exemptions is less than $100,000
- You were 65 or older at the end of the tax year
- You have not previously taken the Ohio lump sum distribution credit

You do not need retirement income to claim this credit. Age plus the MAGI test is enough.

## Interaction with the school district credit
The school-district senior citizen credit on Form SD 100 is a separate $50 per district. You can claim the SD 100 credit even if you were ineligible for this IT 1040 credit. See school-district-senior-citizen-credit.md.

## Required Information
- Date of birth showing age 65 or older on December 31
- MAGI less exemptions
- Confirmation the lump sum distribution credit was never claimed

## Questions
- Were you 65 or older on December 31?
- Is MAGI less exemptions under $100,000?
- Did you ever take the lump sum distribution credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming $50 per spouse
- Claiming it after a prior lump sum distribution credit
- Assuming the $100,000 test uses federal AGI
- Skipping the SD 100 $50 credit because this IT 1040 credit was denied

## Prompt
- I turned 65 this year.
- Is there an Ohio senior citizen tax credit?
