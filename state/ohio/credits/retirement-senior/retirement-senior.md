# Retirement and Senior Credits
## Description
Four related nonrefundable credits on the Ohio Schedule of Credits. All four share a $100,000 MAGI-less-exemptions cliff. Two of them are one-time elections that permanently retire a smaller annual credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Retirement Income Credit (retirement-income-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 2. Up to $200
- Lump Sum Retirement Credit (lump-sum-retirement-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 3. One-time election
- Senior Citizen Credit (senior-citizen-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 4. $50
- Lump Sum Distribution Credit (lump-sum-distribution-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 5. One-time election that retires the $50 senior credit
## The two elections
| If you elect | You give up forever |
|---|---|
| Lump sum retirement credit (line 3) | Retirement income credit (line 2), this year and every later year |
| Lump sum distribution credit (line 5) | Senior citizen credit (line 4), this year and every later year |

These are independent elections. Taking line 3 does not, by itself, kill line 4.
## Shared MAGI cliff
MAGI less exemptions must be under $100,000. MAGI is Ohio AGI plus the business income deduction. Crossing $100,000 by one dollar zeros all four IT 1040 credits.
## Social Security and military retirement
Those amounts are deducted on the Schedule of Adjustments, so they are not in Ohio AGI and do not count as retirement income for line 2 or line 3.
