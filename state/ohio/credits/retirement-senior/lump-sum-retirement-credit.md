# Lump Sum Retirement Credit
## Description
One-time alternative to the annual retirement income credit. You convert a total lump-sum distribution into a multi-year credit using the recipient's age multiple from Table 1. Taking it bars the line 2 retirement income credit this year and every later year.

## Amount
Worksheet, booklet page 43:
1. Enter the recipient's retirement income included in Ohio AGI
2. Divide by the Table 1 age multiple (page 44)
3. Look up that quotient on Table 2
4. Multiply the Table 2 credit by the same age multiple

If the computed credit exceeds remaining tax, the worksheet spreads the unused amount over future years: unused credit divided by (the age multiple minus one) gives a future annual retirement credit, rounded to the dollar. Example from ODT's own instructions: a $1,250 credit against $800 of tax leaves $450, divided by 24, or about $19 a year going forward. That carryforward is still the lump-sum credit, not a revival of line 2.

## Careful with the table names
The booklet and the standalone LS WKS form number their two tables in OPPOSITE order. In the booklet, Table 2 (page 44) holds the retirement income credit amounts and Table 1 holds the age multiples. On the LS WKS form the numbering flips. This file uses the booklet's numbering.

## Eligibility
All of the following must be true:
- MAGI less exemptions is less than $100,000
- You received a TOTAL lump-sum distribution on account of retirement
- The income is included in Ohio AGI
- The plan is a qualified pension, retirement, or profit-sharing plan
- You have not previously claimed this credit

A "total distribution" is generally checked in box 2b of Form 1099-R.

## The permanent trade
If you take this credit, you cannot take the retirement income credit on this year's return or any future return. Each person may take the lump-sum retirement credit only once. On a joint return, use column A and column B if both spouses qualify.

## Required Information
- 1099-R showing a total distribution
- Recipient's age on the last day of the tax year
- Worksheet from booklet page 43
- MAGI less exemptions

## Questions
- Is box 2b marked as a total distribution?
- Have you ever claimed this credit before?
- Is the computed multi-year credit large enough to justify giving up the $200 annual credit for life?

## Common Errors
- Electing this credit for a partial IRA withdrawal that is not a total distribution
- Claiming line 2 and line 3 on the same return
- Forgetting the future-year carryforward when the credit exceeds current tax
- Using Social Security or deducted military retirement as the line 1 input

## Prompt
- I rolled my entire 401(k) out as a lump sum this year.
- Should I take the Ohio lump sum retirement credit instead of the $200 credit?
