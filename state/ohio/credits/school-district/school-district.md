# School District Credits
## Description
Ohio school district income tax is a separate return, Form SD 100, not a credit on the IT 1040. The only credit on that return is the senior citizen credit.
## Credits in this folder
- School District Senior Citizen Credit (school-district-senior-citizen-credit.md) — Form SD 100, line 26 (traditional base) or line 36 (earned-income base). $50 per taxing district
## Notes
You can claim the SD 100 credit even if you were ineligible for the $50 senior credit on the Ohio IT 1040. The IT 1040 senior credit dies at $100,000 of MAGI less exemptions and dies forever after a lump sum distribution credit. The SD 100 credit does not use those tests.
