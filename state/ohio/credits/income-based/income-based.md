# Income-Based Credits
## Description
Credits that scale with earned income. Ohio's earned income credit is 30% of the federal EIC — the same headline percentage New York used for 2025 — but Ohio's version is NONREFUNDABLE. That single difference is why a zero-tax Ohio return often collects nothing even when the federal EIC is large.
## Credits in this folder
- Earned Income Credit (earned-income-credit.md) — Ohio Schedule of Credits, line 13 → line 40 → Form IT 1040, line 9. Nonrefundable
## Comparison
| Credit | Rate | Line | Refundable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ohio EIC | 30% of federal EIC (Form 1040 line 27a) | Schedule of Credits 13 | No |
## What New York and Illinois filers miss
New York's EIC is refundable and is reduced by the household credit. Illinois's EIC is refundable at 20% of federal and can expand beyond federal law. Ohio's EIC is 30% of federal, not refundable, and is not offset by a household credit. Ohio has no noncustodial-parent EIC and no city EIC.
