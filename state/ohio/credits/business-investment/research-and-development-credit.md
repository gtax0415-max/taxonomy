# Research and Development Loan Repayment Credit
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Development for payments made on a loan from the state's Research and Development Loan Fund. R.C. 166.21 and 5747.331. R.C. 5747.98 lists it by the shorter name "research and development credit," which is where the confusion starts.

This is NOT a credit for research spending. It is tied to repaying a specific state loan. You cannot compute it from expenses — the certificate states the amount and the tax year.

## Not the same as the 7% R&D credit
Ohio's better-known research and development investment credit, 7% of the excess of current-year qualified research expenses over the three-year average, is R.C. 5751.51 and runs against the COMMERCIAL ACTIVITY TAX. It is not an individual income tax credit and does not belong on the Schedule of Credits. A client asking about "the Ohio R&D credit" almost always means that one.

## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate, which reflects qualified research and development loan payments made during the preceding calendar year. Unused amounts carry forward until fully utilized.

The credit claimable against the income tax and the CAT combined, for any one loan attributable to a calendar year, cannot exceed $150,000. R.C. 5751.52(E).

## Eligibility
- The taxpayer is a borrower from the Research and Development Loan Fund
- ODOD issued a certificate for this tax year, including its determination that the borrower was not in default as of December 31 of the certificate year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.

## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
- Record of any amount of the same loan's credit claimed against the CAT, for the $150,000 test

## Questions
- Do you have an ODOD certificate for tax year 2026?
- Are you thinking of the 7% CAT research credit instead?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?

## Common Errors
- Claiming this line for ordinary research expenses with no state loan
- Confusing it with the 7% R.C. 5751.51 CAT credit
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation

## Prompt
- I received an ODOD certificate for research and development loan payments.
- Does Ohio have an R&D tax credit on the individual return?
