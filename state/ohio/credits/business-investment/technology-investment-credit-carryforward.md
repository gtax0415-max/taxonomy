# Technology Investment Credit Carryforward
## Description
Carryforward-only credit. The technology investment credit was granted by the Ohio Department of Development and was REPEALED effective September 29, 2013. No new certificates are issued. Only the unused balance from a pre-repeal certificate can still be claimed, for the remaining years of the original 15-year carryforward window.

Because certificates stopped in 2013, the last windows close around 2028. Expect this line to be empty on almost every return.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. Unused nonrefundable amounts carry forward remaining years of the original 15-year window.
## Eligibility
- A pre-repeal ODOD certificate, issued before September 29, 2013
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 122.152 and former R.C. 5747.33

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have a pre-2013 certificate with carryforward left, and prior returns showing how much was used?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a newly generated credit; none exist after September 29, 2013
- Losing track of how much of the carryforward was already used
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting a refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line, or the reverse
## Prompt
- I have leftover Ohio technology investment credit from years ago.
- Where does the Technology Investment Credit Carryforward go on the Ohio IT 1040?
