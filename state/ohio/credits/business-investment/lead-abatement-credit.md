# Lead Abatement Credit
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Health (ODH). You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year. Granted by the Ohio Department of Health.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. Unused nonrefundable amounts carry forward 7 years.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODH for this tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 5747.26

For program rules, visit odh.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an ODH certificate for tax year 2026?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting a refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line, or the reverse
