# Credit for Film and Theater Capital Improvement Projects
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Development (ODOD). You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year. Listed on the 2025 Schedule of Credits.

## Repeal status — do not treat this as repealed without checking
A repeal of this credit (R.C. 122.852, 5726.59, 5747.67, 5751.55) moved through versions of H.B. 96, and LSC analyses of the bill describe it as repealing the film and Broadway theater capital improvement credit. But R.C. 5747.98, as amended by H.B. 96 effective September 30, 2025, STILL lists the refundable credit for film and theater capital improvement projects under section 5747.67 in its credit-ordering list. Separately, S.B. 159 of the 136th General Assembly proposes to repeal 122.852, 5726.59, 5747.67, and 5751.55.

Read together, that suggests 5747.67 survived H.B. 96 and the repeal is being pursued in S.B. 159. Confirm the current status of 5747.67 and of S.B. 159 before telling anyone the credit is gone, and before dropping the line from a 2026 return. If it was repealed, check whether previously issued certificates remain claimable.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. This credit is REFUNDABLE, so there is no carryforward — any excess is paid out on IT 1040 line 16.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODOD for this tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 122.852 and 5747.67, and check both for repeal before relying on them

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an ODOD certificate for tax year 2026?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting this REFUNDABLE certificate on a nonrefundable line. It belongs on Schedule of Credits line 45, flowing to IT 1040 line 16 — not line 40/line 9
