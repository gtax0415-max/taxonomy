# Business and Investment Credits
## Description
Most remaining Ohio credits are granted by another agency and claimed with a certificate, an Ohio IT K-1, or both. Individuals rarely self-compute these. If there is no certificate (or no IT K-1 for the pass-through entity credit), there is no credit.

## Status flags for 2026
- InvestOhio (line 27): no new qualifying investments after November 3, 2025; existing certificates still claimable
- Film and theater capital improvement (line 45): repeal was pursued in H.B. 96 and again in S.B. 159; R.C. 5747.67 still appeared in the credit-ordering statute after H.B. 96. Confirm before dropping the line
- Technology investment (line 30): carryforward only; no certificates since 2013

## Shared claiming rules
- Attach the certificate that lists the amount and the tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach the ownership split and the Ohio IT K-1 when one was issued
- Do not claim commercial-vehicle training on estimated expenses approved before the credit application
- Low-income housing and affordable single-family housing cannot be claimed unless you appear on the designated reporter's allocation report
## Questions
- Which agency issued the certificate, and for which tax year?
- Is the credit refundable or nonrefundable on the Schedule of Credits?
