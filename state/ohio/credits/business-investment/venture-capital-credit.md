# Venture Capital Credit
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Venture Capital Authority. You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year. Attach the certificate from the Ohio Venture Capital Authority.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. This credit is REFUNDABLE, so there is no carryforward — any excess is paid out on IT 1040 line 16.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by the Ohio Venture Capital Authority for this tax year
- Confirm the program is still issuing certificates before collecting documents; the authority has been dormant for long stretches
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 5747.80

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an Ohio Venture Capital Authority certificate for tax year 2026?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting this refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line — it belongs on line 46, not line 40

- I received an Ohio Venture Capital Authority certificate for Venture Capital Credit.
