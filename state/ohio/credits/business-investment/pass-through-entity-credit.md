# Pass-Through Entity Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for Ohio income tax a pass-through entity already paid on your behalf on Form IT 4708, IT 4738, and/or IT 1140. This is the individual-side counterpart to the addback on Ohio Schedule of Adjustments, line 2 — but the two do not cover the same forms.

| Entity form | Credit on line 43 | Addback on Schedule of Adjustments line 2 |
|---|---|---|
| IT 1140 | Yes | Yes |
| IT 4738 | Yes | Yes |
| IT 4708 | Yes | No |

The booklet's line 2 addback reaches only IT 1140 and IT 4738 tax. Composite tax paid on an IT 4708 is creditable without a matching addback, so "credit without addback" is not automatically an error — check which form the entity filed before assuming the return is out of balance.

## Amount
Sum of:
- Income taxes paid by the PTE (Ohio IT K-1, line 3)
- Indirect PTE credits from taxes paid by a PTE you own through another entity (Ohio IT K-1, line 4)

The credit can refund tax down to a negative net, because it is in the refundable section.

## Eligibility
- You are an investor in a PTE that filed IT 4708, IT 4738, and/or IT 1140 and paid Ohio tax on your share
- You have the Ohio IT K-1, or you can reconstruct the ownership chain

## If you do not have an IT K-1
Provide a narrative and/or diagram that includes ownership percentages and FEINs for every entity in the chain. Processing will not accept a lone dollar amount.

## Related adjustment
Schedule of Adjustments, line 2 adds back the Ohio entity tax that reduced federal or Ohio income. See booklet page 19. The credit on line 43 then hands that tax back so it is not paid twice.

## Required Information
- Every Ohio IT K-1 for the year
- Line 3 and line 4 amounts
- Ownership diagram if any K-1 is missing
- Matching addback on Schedule of Adjustments, line 2

## Questions
- Did any partnership, S corporation, or LLC send you an Ohio IT K-1?
- Did you also add the tax back on Schedule of Adjustments line 2?
- Is part of the credit indirect, from a lower-tier entity on line 4?

## Common Errors
- Reporting K-1 withholding as IT 1040 line 14 Ohio withholding. Line 14 is only W-2 / 1099 Ohio withholding. PTE tax belongs on Schedule of Credits line 43
- Taking an IT 1140 or IT 4738 credit without the line 2 addback
- Dropping indirect credits on line 4
- Attaching a federal K-1 instead of the Ohio IT K-1

## Prompt
- My S corporation paid Ohio tax for me.
- Where does the Ohio IT K-1 tax-paid amount go?
