# Grape Production Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a taxpayer in the business of producing grapes who buys qualifying property used to produce grapes in Ohio. R.C. 5747.28. This is one of the few business credits individuals can compute without an agency certificate — it is the only file in this folder with no issuing agency.

## Amount
- 10% of the cost of purchasing and installing or constructing the qualifying property
- Pass-through investors claim a proportionate share
- Unused amounts carry forward up to seven years

## Qualifying property
Any property, plant, or equipment used to produce grapes in Ohio.

## Recapture — verify before relying on it
This file has carried a rule that disposing of the property, or ceasing to use it as qualifying property, within seven years of placing it in service triggers recapture. R.C. 5747.28 is short and the recapture language has not been confirmed against it. Treat the seven-year holding period as a planning caution, not a settled rule, until it is checked against the statute and the 2026 booklet.

## Eligibility
- You are engaged in producing grapes
- The property is used to produce grapes in Ohio
- You purchased and installed or constructed it during the tax year

## Required Information
- Invoices showing cost of purchase, installation, or construction
- Description of the property and Ohio location
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Prior-year carryforward, if any

## Questions
- Is the property used to produce grapes in Ohio?
- What did you pay to buy and install it this year?
- Has the property been sold or converted within seven years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming land or a tasting room that does not produce grapes
- Claiming property used outside Ohio
- Assuming no consequence from an early sale without checking R.C. 5747.28
- Expecting a refund of the unused 10%

## Prompt
- I planted a vineyard and bought equipment in Ohio.
- Is there an Ohio grape or wine tax credit?
