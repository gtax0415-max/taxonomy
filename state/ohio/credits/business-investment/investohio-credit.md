# InvestOhio Credit
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Development (ODOD). You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year. Certificate from ODOD required.

## The program is sunsetting — this matters for 2026
InvestOhio is the small business investment credit. Under H.B. 96, the award of NEW small business investment tax credits sunsets January 2, 2026, and November 3, 2025 was the last date on which an investment could qualify. Certificates already issued, and the required two-year holding period running out from earlier investments, still produce claims on 2026 and later returns.

So: no new qualifying investments, but existing certificates are live. Do not tell a client with a 2024 or 2025 investment that the credit is gone, and do not let one plan a new investment expecting it.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. Unused nonrefundable amounts carry forward 7 years.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODOD for this tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 5747.81

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an ODOD certificate for tax year 2026 from an investment made on or before November 3, 2025?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated investment amounts without a certificate
- Planning a new qualifying investment after the November 3, 2025 cutoff
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting a refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line, or the reverse

- I received an ODOD certificate for InvestOhio Credit.
