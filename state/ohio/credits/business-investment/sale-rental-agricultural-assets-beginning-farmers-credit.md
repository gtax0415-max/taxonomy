# Credit for Sale/Rental of Agricultural Assets to Beginning Farmers
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Agriculture. You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year. Pass-through owners claim their portion and must attach the ownership split.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. Unused nonrefundable amounts carry forward 7 years.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODA for this tax year
- Note there is a SEPARATE beginning-farmer credit for the farmer's own financial management program costs; this file covers only the asset owner's credit
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 901.61(D) and 5747.77(A)

For program rules, visit agri.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an ODA certificate for tax year 2026?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting a refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line, or the reverse
