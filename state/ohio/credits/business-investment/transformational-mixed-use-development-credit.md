# Credit for Transformational Mixed-Use Development
## Description
Certificate credit granted by the Ohio Department of Development. You cannot compute this from expenses or investment dollars on your own. The certificate states the amount and the tax year.

First available against the individual income tax for tax year 2025 and continuing in 2026. Certificates issued before September 30, 2025 cannot be claimed here. The TMUD program itself is older than that date — the September 30, 2025 line is about when the credit reached the income tax, not when the program started.
## Amount
The amount printed on the certificate. Unused nonrefundable amounts carry forward 5 years.
## Eligibility
- Certificate issued by ODOD for this tax year
- If the certificate is held by a pass-through entity, attach documentation showing your portion
- Attach the certificate to the return
- See R.C. 122.09 and 5747.87

For program rules, visit development.ohio.gov.
## Required Information
- Certificate showing the credit amount and tax year
- Ownership percentage if claimed through a pass-through entity
- Ohio IT K-1 when the entity issued one
## Questions
- Do you have an ODOD certificate for tax year 2026, issued on or after September 30, 2025?
- Is the certificate in your name or the entity's name?
## Common Errors
- Entering estimated expenses without a certificate
- Using a certificate issued for a different tax year
- Omitting the pass-through allocation
- Putting a refundable certificate on a nonrefundable line, or the reverse
