# Ohio Historic Preservation Credit
## Description
Certificate credit for certified historic rehabilitation. The same program can be nonrefundable (line 33) or refundable (line 41) depending on when the certificate was issued and whether an enhanced-credit amendment applies. Putting it on the wrong line is the processing error.

## Which line
Use refundable line 41 if:
- You received an amended certificate for a project approved between June 30, 2020 and September 13, 2022 that qualifies for the enhanced credit, or
- You received an original certificate issued after September 13, 2022 and before July 1, 2024

That refundable amount is fully refundable up to $10,000,000 claimed per project in any tax year.

Use nonrefundable line 33 if those date tests fail.

Line 33 unused amounts carry forward up to five years. Line 41 excess is refunded.

## Unresolved: the $3,000,000 rule
An earlier version of this file added a second line-33 condition — certificate issued on or before September 13, 2022 or after June 30, 2024, AND the credit exceeds $3,000,000. That cannot be read alongside the sentence above it. If failing the date tests already sends the credit to line 33, a further $3,000,000 condition on the same certificates does nothing; read literally it implies a pre-September 2022 certificate UNDER $3,000,000 goes to refundable line 41, which contradicts the date rule.

The $3,000,000 figure is probably a real feature of R.C. 5747.76 and the enhanced-credit amendments, but the way it interacts with the date windows is not settled here. Verify against R.C. 5747.76, R.C. 149.311, and the 2026 booklet before applying it, and do not rely on the version above.

## Eligibility
- ODOD certificate stating the amount and the tax year
- Pass-through owners attach the ownership split
- Certificate attached to the return

## Required Information
- Certificate issue date and, if amended, the original approval window
- Amount on the certificate
- Project identifier for the $10,000,000 per-project cap
- Ownership percentage if claimed through an entity

## Questions
- What is the issue date on the certificate?
- Is this an amended certificate for a mid-2020 to mid-2022 approval?
- Is the credit over $3,000,000?

## Common Errors
- Defaulting every historic certificate to line 33
- Defaulting every historic certificate to line 41
- Applying the $3,000,000 rule without first resolving how it interacts with the date windows (see above)
- Forgetting the five-year carryforward on the nonrefundable side

## Prompt
- I received an Ohio historic preservation tax credit certificate.
- Is the Ohio historic credit refundable?
