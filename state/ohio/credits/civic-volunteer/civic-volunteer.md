# Civic and Volunteer Credits
## Description
Two nonrefundable credits that are not income-tested the way the retirement stack is. Only one of them is live in 2026: the campaign contribution credit died after tax year 2025.
## Credits in this folder
- Displaced Worker Training Credit (displaced-worker-training-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 7. $500 combined cap across the 12-month training window
- Campaign Contribution Credit (campaign-contribution-credit.md) — Schedule of Credits, line 8. REPEALED; tax year 2025 was the last year. The file is retained for prior-year returns and to answer the question
## Related
- Work-based learning certificate credit — see state/ohio/credits/education/work-based-learning-experiences-credit.md
## Questions
- Did you lose a job because the location closed or your position was abolished?
- Are you asking about campaign contributions? There is no 2026 credit; R.C. 5747.29 was repealed effective January 1, 2026
## Prompt
- My plant closed and I paid for a training program.
- I donated to an Ohio House candidate.
