# Campaign Contribution Credit
## Description
REPEALED for tax year 2026. Do not claim this credit on a 2026 return. H.B. 96 repealed R.C. 5747.29 effective January 1, 2026.

Tax year 2025 was the last year. That year the credit was $50 ($100 joint) for cash contributions to campaign committees of candidates for governor, lieutenant governor, secretary of state, auditor, treasurer, attorney general, Ohio Supreme Court, State Board of Education, or the General Assembly.
## Amount
$0 for 2026.
## Eligibility
None for 2026.
## Common Errors
- Carrying a 2025 contribution onto a 2026 return
- Treating a local or federal contribution as if the old credit still existed
