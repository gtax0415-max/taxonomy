# Displaced Worker Training Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a displaced worker who pays for job training after losing a job to a closing, a move, or the abolishment of a position or shift. The credit is 50% of unreimbursed training costs, capped at $500 for the entire 12-month window, even if that window straddles two tax years.

## Amount
- 50% of amounts you paid, minus reimbursements
- Cap $500 for the 12-month period after the job loss
- If the 12 months span two tax years, the two years together cannot exceed $500
- The worksheet subtracts any amount already claimed last year

## Who is a displaced worker
All of the following must be true:
- You lost or left the job because the location closed or moved, or your position or shift was abolished
- You paid for job training to improve your ability to get a job
- You worked no more than 20 hours per week at a new job

A voluntary quit for a better offer is not displacement. A layoff from a still-open shift may not be either.

## Qualifying expenses
College tuition and fees, vocational training, and required course materials such as books and software.

## Non-qualifying expenses
Computers and upgrades, professional dues, job-placement or career-planning services (resume workshops, interview critiques, skills assessments), and certification or licensure tests and fees.

## Required Information
- Date the job was lost
- Proof the loss was a closing, move, or abolished position or shift
- Receipts for training paid with personal funds or loans during the following 12 months
- Amount claimed on last year's return, if the window crossed January 1
- Worksheet from booklet page 45

## Questions
- Was the job eliminated, or did you leave for another reason?
- When did the 12-month clock start?
- Did you already take part of the $500 last year?
- Are you working more than 20 hours a week at the new job?

## Common Errors
- Claiming certification exam fees
- Claiming a new laptop
- Taking $500 in year one and another $500 in year two of the same 12-month window
- Missing the 20-hour new-job limit

## Prompt
- My plant closed and I paid for a training program.
- Is there an Ohio credit for job-retraining after a layoff?
