# Ohio Unpaid Use (Sales) Tax — Line 12
## Description
The sales tax you owe as the buyer when the seller did not collect Ohio tax — most often internet, catalog, television, or out-of-state purchases brought back for use in Ohio. It is reported on the income tax return for convenience. R.C. 5747.083.

You owe Ohio use tax if both are true:
- You made purchases where sales tax was not collected; AND
- The purchases were not qualifying purchases during Ohio's sales tax holiday
## Rate
Ohio use tax is the combined state-plus-county (and transit) rate for the county where you used the item. The 2025 booklet table on page 46 is the rate in effect on October 1, 2025. Rates change during the year; use the rate on the purchase date at tax.ohio.gov.

Examples from that October 1, 2025 table:
- Cuyahoga 8.00%
- Franklin 8.00%
- Hamilton 7.80%
- Licking (COTA) 8.25%
- Butler, Lorain, Stark, Wayne 6.50%

There is no Illinois-style AGI estimate table and no separate 1% grocery line. One combined rate × the purchase total.
## Worksheet
1. Total purchases during the tax year on which you owe Ohio use tax
2. Your county's use tax rate
3. Line 1 × line 2, rounded to the nearest dollar → IT 1040 line 12

If you owe use tax to more than one county, use the Ohio Use Tax Voluntary Payment Form at tax.ohio.gov/forms.
## 2026 sales tax holiday
August 7, 12:00 a.m. through August 9, 11:59 p.m. Qualifying items bought in that window do not belong on line 12:
- Clothing $75 or less per item
- School supplies $20 or less per item
- School instructional materials $20 or less per item

The 2025 expanded holiday (most tangible personal property $500 or less) does not repeat in 2026. Electronics, business purchases, vehicles, alcohol, tobacco, and items over those price caps still generate use tax if the seller collected nothing.
## Vehicles, boats, and titled property
Do not report a vehicle, boat, or aircraft bought out of state on line 12. Those are paid at titling, not on the IT 1040.
## Notes
- Line 12 sits after the credits. Nonrefundable credits cannot absorb use tax
- Most large online retailers now collect Ohio destination tax, so line 12 is usually small private-party, foreign, and small-seller purchases
- Foreign VAT is not a credit against Ohio use tax
- Paper filers still complete the line; enter 0 if nothing is owed
## Required Information
- Untaxed purchase totals and dates
- County of use and the rate in effect on the purchase date
- Whether any item was bought during the August 7–9, 2026 holiday
## Questions
- Did a seller collect Ohio tax, or only another state's tax?
- Which county did you use the item in?
- Was the purchase during the August 7–9 holiday, and did it fit the clothing / supply caps?
- Do you owe tax to more than one county?
## Common Errors
- Using last year's expanded-holiday rules on a 2026 laptop or appliance
- Applying the state-only 5.75% rate and skipping the county/transit add-on
- Putting a titled vehicle on line 12
- Treating foreign VAT as tax already paid
- Leaving the line blank when the amount is zero
## Prompt
- Do I have to fill in the use tax line on my Ohio return?
- I bought a laptop online and was not charged tax.
- Does the Ohio sales tax holiday cover what I bought in August?
