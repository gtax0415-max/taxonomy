# Ohio Other Taxes — Index
## Description
Taxes and charges that sit next to, or on top of, the regular Ohio income tax. None of these is a credit or a Schedule of Adjustments subtraction. School district income tax is a separate return (SD 100), not a line on the IT 1040.
## Files in this folder
- use-tax.md — IT 1040 line 12 unpaid use (sales) tax
- interest-penalty.md — IT 1040 line 11 and SD 100 line 9, Form IT/SD 2210
- interest-due.md — IT 1040 line 21 and SD 100 line 17; calendar-year 2026 rate 7%
- business-income-tax.md — IT 1040 line 8b, flat 3% on leftover business income
- school-district-income-tax.md — Form SD 100
## What Ohio does not have
No medical-cannabis or gaming-licensee asset-sale surcharge like Illinois IL-1040 line 22. No separate AMT. No city income tax on the state return — municipal tax is filed with the city.
## Order on the IT 1040
```
Line 8a   Tax on nonbusiness income
Line 8b   Tax on business income (3%)     ← business-income-tax.md
Line 8c   Total income tax
Lines 9-10 Credits
Line 11   Interest penalty                ← interest-penalty.md
Line 12   Unpaid use (sales) tax          ← use-tax.md
Line 13   Total Ohio tax
Line 21   Interest due                    ← interest-due.md
```
School district tax is computed on the SD 100 and paid with a separate OUPC.
