# School District Income Tax
## Description
A separate Ohio tax on residents of districts that voted an income tax. It is not a line on the IT 1040. File Form SD 100. About 210 districts levy it as of January 2026. Working in a taxing district without living there is not enough.

You may owe school district tax even when Ohio income tax is zero. In that case you still file both the SD 100 and the IT 1040. Full-year nonresidents enter school district number 9999 on the IT 1040 and do not file an SD 100 unless they were Ohio residents for part of the year.
## Two bases
| Base | What is taxed | SD 100 lines |
|---|---|---|
| Traditional (T) | MAGI less exemptions received while a resident of the district | 22-28; liability to line 6 |
| Earned income (E) | Wages, salaries, tips, and net self-employment included in MAGI while a resident. Not retirement, interest, dividends, capital gains, or unemployment | 29-38; liability to line 7 |
## Who must file
All three:
- You lived in a taxing school district for any part of the year
- You received income while living there
- That income produces tax on SD 100 line 25 or 35

One SD 100 covers every district you lived in. Page 3 holds two traditional and two earned-income columns; add extra page 3 copies if needed. Use The Finder at tax.ohio.gov/finder for the four-digit district number and rate.
## Senior citizen credit
$50 per taxing district if you were 65 or older at year-end. Available on the SD 100 even if you could not take the $50 senior credit on the IT 1040. See state/ohio/credits/school-district/ and state/ohio/credits/retirement-senior/senior-citizen-credit.md.
## Part-year residents
Tax only the income received while you lived in the district. Identify dollars by date if you can; otherwise multiply the base by the residency factor on page 1. Joint filers with different move dates cannot use the simple factor — see tax.ohio.gov/schooldistrict.
## Withholding
School district withholding is the four-digit code in W-2 box 20 or 14. Report it on the Schedule of School District Withholding, not as Ohio state withholding and not as city tax. Erroneous withholding is refunded only by filing the SD 100.
## Interest penalty and interest
Same mechanics as the IT 1040: IT/SD 2210 on SD 100 line 9; 7% interest on line 17 for 2026.
## Notes
- Military residents can owe school district tax even when stationed outside Ohio
- Pay with a School District Income Tax OUPC, never combined with the IT 1040 payment
- Estimated SD payments if expected liability minus withholding exceeds the usual threshold; 2026 SD estimated worksheet allows a $50 senior reduction
## Required Information
- Address history and four-digit district numbers
- Whether each district is T or E
- MAGI and exemptions from the IT 1040
- W-2 school district withholding
- Days in each district if part-year
## Questions
- Did you live in a taxing district, or only work there?
- Is the district traditional or earned-income?
- Did your employer withhold using the wrong four-digit code?
## Common Errors
- Skipping the SD 100 because the IT 1040 shows no tax
- Using city tax withheld as school district withholding
- Taxing retirement income in an earned-income district
- Filing a separate SD 100 per district
- Combining the SD payment with the IT 1040 check
## Prompt
- How do I know if I owe Ohio school district income tax?
- I moved mid-year. How do I split the SD 100?
- My W-2 has a school district number that is not where I live.
