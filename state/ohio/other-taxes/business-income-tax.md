# Tax on Business Income
## Description
Ohio taxes business income that remains after the $250,000 ($125,000 MFS) business income deduction at a flat 3%. That tax is IT 1040 line 8b. It is separate from the nonbusiness tax on line 8a.

For 2026, leftover nonbusiness income is $332 plus 2.75% of the amount over $26,050. Business income does not use that schedule.
## Amount
Schedule of Business Income Part 3 × 0.03, not more than Ohio taxable income after exemptions.
## Required Information
- Completed Ohio Schedule of Business Income
- Business income deduction from Schedule of Adjustments line 13
## Questions
- After the $250,000 deduction, is any business income left?
## Common Errors
- Running leftover business income through the 2.75% nonbusiness bracket
- Taking line 8b without attaching the Schedule of Business Income
