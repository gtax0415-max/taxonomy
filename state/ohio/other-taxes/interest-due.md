# Interest Due
## Description
Interest on tax not paid by the unextended due date of the 2026 return (April 15, 2027). Calendar year 2026 statutory rate is 7%; confirm the 2027 calendar-year rate at tax.ohio.gov/interestcalculator when paying a 2026 balance in 2027. An extension to file does not stop interest. Generally no interest if you are due a refund.

Certain military servicemembers covered by R.C. 5747.026 do not owe this interest during their extension period. Use tax.ohio.gov/interestcalculator.
## Required Information
- Balance due
- Date paid
- Whether a military filing/payment extension applies
## Questions
- Did you pay the tax by April 15, or only file by the October 15 extension?
## Common Errors
- Treating a filing extension as a payment extension
- Using the federal underpayment rate instead of Ohio's 7%
## Prompt
- I filed in October. Do I owe Ohio interest?
