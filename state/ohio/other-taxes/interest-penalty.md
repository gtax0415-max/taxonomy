# Interest Penalty on Underpayment of Estimated Tax
## Description
A charge for not paying enough Ohio or school district tax during the year through withholding and timely estimates. Computed on Form IT/SD 2210. It is not the same as the interest on line 21, which runs only after the April due date.
## When it usually does not apply
Wage earners with Ohio (or school district) withholding generally owe nothing. Farmers and fishers have a special rule under Ohio Adm. Code 5703-7-04.
## When to run the form
Estimated Ohio tax liability (total tax minus credits) less Ohio withholding is more than $500, or you have non-wage income and little or no withholding. Same idea on the SD 100 if school district withholding is short.
## 2026 estimate calendar (payments toward tax year 2026)
| Quarter | Due |
|---|---|
| 1 | April 15, 2026 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 |

The 2026 IT 1040 / SD 100 themselves are due April 15, 2027. An extension to file does not extend the time to pay estimates or the April 15 tax.
## Required Information
- Completed IT/SD 2210
- Withholding and estimate dates and amounts
## Questions
- Is tax minus credits minus withholding more than $500?
- Did you have large non-wage income with no Ohio withholding?
## Common Errors
- Skipping IT/SD 2210 because you got a federal extension
- Combining Ohio and school district estimates on one coupon
## Prompt
- Do I owe an Ohio underpayment penalty?
- I sold stock and had no extra withholding.
