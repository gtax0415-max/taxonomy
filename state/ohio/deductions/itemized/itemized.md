# Ohio Itemized Deductions
## Description
EMPTY BY DESIGN. Ohio has no itemized-deduction schedule. Unreimbursed employee expenses, charitable gifts, mortgage interest, and property taxes do not come off Ohio AGI. Federal AGI arrives on IT 1040 line 1 before the federal standard or itemized deduction is applied, so the federal itemizing choice does not carry into Ohio at all.

Two Schedule of Adjustments lines are easy to confuse with itemizing:
- Line 28 subtracts a REFUND of a prior federal Schedule A expense that is in this year's federal AGI
- Line 44 subtracts unreimbursed medical expenses on Ohio's own worksheet, not federal Schedule A. You do not have to itemize federally to claim it
## The SALT cap
There is no itemized-deduction workaround on the IT 1040, but Ohio does have a SALT-cap workaround at the entity level: the elective pass-through entity tax on form IT 4738. The PTE pays Ohio tax and deducts it federally, and the owner claims a refundable credit on Schedule of Credits line 43 with a matching add-back on Schedule of Adjustments line 2. That is an entity election, not a deduction an individual takes here.
