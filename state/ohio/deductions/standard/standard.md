# Ohio Standard Deduction
## Description
EMPTY BY DESIGN. Ohio has no standard deduction. Federal AGI arrives on IT 1040 line 1 already determined before the federal standard or itemized deduction, and Ohio does not layer a standard deduction on top.

What does the work instead is the $26,050 zero bracket. For 2026, taxable nonbusiness income at or below $26,050 is taxed at 0%, and the schedule starts at $332 plus 2.75% above that. The threshold is not indexed.

Filers looking for a "standard deduction" in Ohio are usually looking for one of these instead:
- Personal and dependent exemption on IT 1040 line 4 — see ../exemptions/exemptions.md
- Schedule of Adjustments subtractions — see ../subtractions/
## Questions
- Are you trying to take the federal standard deduction again on the Ohio return? You cannot
- Are you thinking of the $26,050 zero bracket, or of the personal exemption?
