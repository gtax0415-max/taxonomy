# Ohio National Guard Reimbursements and Benefits
## Description
Deduct Ohio Adjutant General reimbursements of Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance premiums if the reimbursements are in federal AGI, you bought the coverage under the SGLI Act, and you were on active duty in the Ohio National Guard for the months the premiums were paid. Also deduct death benefits paid by the Adjutant General on the death of an active-duty Ohio National Guard member, to the extent in federal AGI.
## Required Information
Reimbursement or death-benefit statements; active-duty months.
## Questions
- Were you on Ohio National Guard active duty when the SGLI premiums were paid?
## Common Errors
- Deducting SGLI reimbursements for months you were not on ONG active duty
## Prompt
- The Ohio Adjutant General reimbursed my SGLI premiums.
