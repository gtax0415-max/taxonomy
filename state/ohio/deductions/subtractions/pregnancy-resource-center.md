# Contributions to a Pregnancy Resource Center
## Description
Deduct contributions to a qualifying pregnancy resource center. Cap $750 per taxpayer. Joint filers: each spouse who donates may claim up to $750, so $1,500 combined.

The 2025 booklet limited first-year gifts to September 30, 2025 through December 31, 2025 because that is when H.B. 96 took effect. Current R.C. 5747.01(A)(44) has no sunset. Calendar-year 2026 gifts qualify.
## Amount
Lesser of $750 or the amount donated during the tax year, per taxpayer.
## Eligibility
The center must meet R.C. 5180.71(B): private nonprofit; primary purpose is promoting childbirth; free services to pregnant women and parents of children under 12 months; not associated with abortion-related activities; no listed discrimination.
## Required Information
Receipt showing the organization, amount, and date.
## Questions
- Is the organization a qualifying pregnancy resource center under R.C. 5180.71(B)?
- Did each spouse donate, or only one?
## Common Errors
- Treating the 2025 first-year date window as if 2026 gifts do not count
- Claiming $1,500 when only one spouse donated
## Prompt
- I donated to a pregnancy resource center in 2026.
