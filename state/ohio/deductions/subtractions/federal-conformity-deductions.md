# Federal Conformity Deductions
## Description
Only for federal conformity adjustments — cases where Ohio has not adopted a federal change and allows a subtraction to reach the Ohio result. R.C. 5701.11. Do not enter federal Schedule A items here. See tax.ohio.gov/conformity for the current list; the 2026 booklet is not published.

This is the deduction side of a pair. The addition side is Schedule of Adjustments line 11.
## Before using this line
Most "Ohio should let me deduct this" questions are not conformity questions. A federal break that reduces federal AGI already flowed into Ohio on IT 1040 line 1 and needs no entry here. A federal break taken below the line never reaches Ohio and this line does not rescue it. Line 31 is only for items the current conformity guidance names.
## Required Information
Current Ohio conformity guidance and the federal schedule supporting the amount.
## Questions
- Does this year's conformity release actually list a deduction, or are you trying to import a below-the-line federal deduction?
## Common Errors
- Putting itemized deductions here
- Using this line for a federal deduction Ohio never decoupled from
