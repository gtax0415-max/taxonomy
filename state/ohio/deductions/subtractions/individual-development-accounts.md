# Individual Development Accounts
## Description
Deduct contributions you made to an Ohio county individual development account program for the purpose of matching funds. You cannot deduct amounts you deposited into your own IDA.
## Required Information
County JFS records of matching contributions.
## Questions
- Was this a matching contribution to a county IDA program, or a deposit to your own account?
## Common Errors
- Deducting deposits into your own IDA
## Prompt
- I contributed matching funds to a county IDA.
