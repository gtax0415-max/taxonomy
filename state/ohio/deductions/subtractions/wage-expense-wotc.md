# Wage Expense Not Deducted Due to the Federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit
## Description
Deduct the amount you reported as your federal work opportunity tax credit. Ohio lets you recover the wage expense the federal credit disallowed.
## Amount
The federal WOTC amount from the federal return.
## Required Information
Federal Form 5884 or the credit amount on the federal return.
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal work opportunity tax credit?
## Common Errors
- Deducting wages that were already deducted federally
## Prompt
- I claimed the federal work opportunity credit. Is there an Ohio deduction?
