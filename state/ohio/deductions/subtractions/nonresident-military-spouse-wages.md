# Nonresident Military and Civilian Spouse Wages
## Description
Ohio residents cannot use this line. Deduct military pay and allowances in federal AGI earned by a nonresident servicemember. Also deduct Ohio compensation of a nonresident civilian spouse who is in Ohio solely because of the servicemember's orders.
## Required Information
Servicemember residency; spouse residency and reason for presence in Ohio; W-2s.
## Questions
- Is the servicemember a nonresident of Ohio?
- Is the spouse in Ohio only because of military orders?
## Common Errors
- Ohio-resident servicemembers using this line
- A civilian spouse who has an independent Ohio domicile claiming it
## Prompt
- My spouse is in the Army and we are stationed in Ohio but claim another state.
