# Ohio Educator Expenses
## Description
Deduct education expenses incurred by a qualifying Ohio educator during the tax year, to the extent not otherwise deducted or excluded in computing Ohio AGI. R.C. 5747.01(A)(31) and (JJ).

Cap $300 per qualifying educator. The cap rose from $250 to $300 effective for tax year 2025 and is unchanged for 2026.

A qualifying Ohio educator is a kindergarten through grade 12 Ohio public or private school teacher, instructor, counselor, principal, or aide who worked at least 900 hours during the school year. Education expenses are those described in IRC 62(a)(2)(D).
## Amount
Lesser of qualifying expenses or $300 per qualifying educator. Two qualifying educators filing jointly can each claim up to $300.
## Required Information
Role and 900-hour test; receipts; confirmation the expenses were not deducted federally or elsewhere on the Ohio schedule.
## Questions
- Did you work at least 900 hours in an Ohio K-12 school?
- Did you already take the federal educator-expense deduction for the same dollars?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a college instructor, or for a K-12 educator under 900 hours
- Deducting the same dollars that already came off through the federal educator-expense deduction, which reduces federal AGI and therefore Ohio AGI
- Claiming it for a school outside Ohio
## Prompt
- I am an Ohio teacher and I bought classroom supplies.
