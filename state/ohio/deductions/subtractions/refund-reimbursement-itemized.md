# Refund or Reimbursement for Itemized Deductions
## Description
Deduct amounts in federal AGI that reimburse expenses you previously deducted on federal Schedule A, if you have not already deducted them on the Ohio Schedule of Adjustments. R.C. 5747.01(A)(11)(a).

Example: Sue claimed an itemized deduction of $500 of attorney fees on her 2024 federal Schedule A, got $200 back in 2026, reported the $200 as other income federally, and deducts $200 here.
## Required Information
Prior federal Schedule A; 2026 federal other-income reporting.
## Questions
- Was this expense deducted on federal Schedule A in an earlier year?
- Is the reimbursement in 2026 federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a reimbursement already removed on another Ohio line
- Confusing this with line 7, which ADDS reimbursements that are NOT in federal AGI
## Direction check
| | In federal AGI? | Ohio treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Reimbursement of a prior federal Schedule A expense | Yes | Deduct here, line 28 |
| Reimbursement of a prior Ohio Schedule of Adjustments deduction | No | Add on line 7 |
## Prompt
- I got a refund of medical expenses I itemized federally two years ago.
