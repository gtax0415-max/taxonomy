# Ohio Depreciation Deduction
## Description
Deduct the scheduled recovery of the IRC 168(k) and 179 amounts you added back on line 9 in a prior year. R.C. 5747.01(A)(18). Take it in equal increments in consecutive tax years. You may take it even if you no longer directly or indirectly own the asset. Verify any deduction reported on an IT K-1 against your own prior add-backs before claiming it.
## Amount
- 1/5 of each prior 5/6 add-back
- 1/2 of each prior 2/3 add-back
- 1/6 of each prior 6/6 add-back
## Two different rules that sound alike
Do not confuse these:

- No carryforward of an unused portion. If an increment exceeds what you can use in its year, the excess is gone. It does not move to a later year
- NOL years are skipped, not lost. You cannot claim the deduction in a tax year with an NOL, an NOL carryback, or an NOL carryforward. In that case you carry the increment forward to the next tax year in which you have none of the three. The schedule pauses and resumes; it does not forfeit the increment

So an increment can legitimately land in a non-consecutive year, but only because an NOL year intervened.
## Required Information
Prior-year line 9 add-backs and the fraction used in each source year; NOL status for the current year; worksheet at tax.ohio.gov/worksheets.
## Questions
- What fraction did you add back in the source year?
- Do you have an NOL, carryback, or carryforward this year?
## Common Errors
- Deducting an amount you never added back
- Taking the deduction in an NOL year instead of pausing it
- Treating a paused NOL-year increment as forfeited
- Taking uneven increments instead of the fixed fraction
