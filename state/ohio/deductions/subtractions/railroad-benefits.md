# Certain Railroad Benefits
## Description
Deduct railroad retirement, supplemental railroad retirement, dual railroad retirement, railroad disability, and railroad unemployment benefits to the extent they are in federal AGI and exempt from state tax. Do not repeat amounts already deducted as taxable Social Security on line 16.
## Required Information
Form RRB-1099 or RRB-1099-R.
## Questions
- Are these RRB benefits already on federal line 6b?
## Common Errors
- Double-counting line 16 Social Security
- Deducting a regular pension that is not an RRB benefit
## Prompt
- I get railroad retirement. Does Ohio tax it?
