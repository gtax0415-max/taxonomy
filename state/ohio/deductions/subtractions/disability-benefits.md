# Disability Benefits
## Description
Deduct disability benefits included in federal AGI. Use the terms of the plan that paid them. You may not deduct retirement benefits that converted from disability at a minimum retirement age, temporary wage-continuation payments, or payments for temporary illness or injury such as employer or third-party sick pay.
## Required Information
1099-R or payer statement; plan terms showing the payment is disability.
## Questions
- Has the plan converted the benefit to retirement?
- Is this sick pay or wage continuation?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a pension that used to be disability
- Deducting short-term sick pay
## Prompt
- I receive long-term disability. Does Ohio tax it?
