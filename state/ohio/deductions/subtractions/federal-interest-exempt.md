# Federal Interest and Dividends Exempt From State Taxation
## Description
Deduct interest and dividends from U.S. government or U.S. possession obligations that federal law exempts from Ohio tax, to the extent included in federal AGI. Examples: Series E, EE, H, or I savings bonds; Treasury notes, bills, and bonds; Sallie Maes. Not deductible: interest on a federal tax refund; Fannie Maes; Ginnie Maes.
## Required Information
1099-INT; fund year-end statement breaking out U.S. government interest.
## Questions
- Is this Treasury interest, or Fannie/Ginnie Mae interest?
## Common Errors
- Deducting Fannie Mae or Ginnie Mae interest
- Deducting interest on a federal refund
## Prompt
- I earned Treasury bond interest. Does Ohio tax it?
