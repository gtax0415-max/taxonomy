# Qualified Organ Donor Expenses
## Description
Deduct qualified organ-donation expenses incurred during the year to the extent not otherwise deducted or excluded federally. One-time cap $10,000 per taxpayer. Qualifying expenses are travel, lodging, and wages or salary forgone in connection with donating a qualifying organ (all or part of a liver, pancreas, kidney, intestine, lung, or human bone marrow) to another human being.
## Required Information
Medical records of the donation; travel and lodging receipts; proof of forgone wages.
## Questions
- Was this your donation of a qualifying organ?
- Have you used this deduction in a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it more than once
- Deducting expenses already excluded federally
- Claiming someone else's donation
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney and lost wages during recovery.
