# Unreimbursed Medical and Health Care Expenses
## Description
Deduct unreimbursed health care costs to the extent not otherwise deducted or excluded federally. R.C. 5747.01(A)(10). Ohio's deduction is available whether or not you itemized federally, and it runs on Ohio's own Unreimbursed Health Care Expenses Worksheet (2025 booklet page 41), not on federal Schedule A.
## Two buckets, only one of which has a floor
NOT subject to the 7.5% floor — deductible in full:
- Dental, vision, and health insurance premiums paid during any portion of the year in which you were NOT eligible for Medicare or an employer-paid health care plan. ACA marketplace premiums usually land here
- Qualified long-term care insurance premiums

Subject to the 7.5% floor — deductible only above 7.5% of federal AGI:
- Dental, vision, and health insurance premiums paid while you WERE eligible for Medicare or an employer-paid plan, including Medicare Part B, Part D, and Medigap premiums
- All other "medical care" under IRC 213: prescriptions, copays, doctor visits, hospital and nursing care, eyeglasses, hearing aids, braces, crutches, wheelchairs

The self-employed health insurance deduction already taken on federal Schedule 1 reduces the unsubsidized-premium bucket, so the same dollars are not deducted twice.
## Amount
Complete the worksheet and carry its total to Schedule of Adjustments line 44. Include a copy of the worksheet with the return. Confirm the worksheet's page and line numbering when the 2026 booklet ships.
## Do not deduct
- Amounts paid with pre-tax dollars, including HSA and FSA money and your payroll-deducted share of employer premiums
- Amounts later reimbursed or refunded
- Amounts paid by insurance or by another person
- Amounts paid for anyone other than you, your spouse, and your dependents

Premium entries must be net of any related premium refunds or reimbursements.
## Required Information
Completed worksheet; premium statements; federal AGI; the federal self-employed health insurance deduction amount.
## Questions
- Were you eligible for Medicare or an employer plan when you paid the premium?
- Did you already deduct self-employed health insurance federally?
- Were any premiums paid with pre-tax dollars?
## Common Errors
- Putting Medicare or employer-plan premiums in the floor-free bucket
- Running long-term care premiums through the 7.5% floor
- Deducting pre-tax HSA or FSA premiums
- Assuming Ohio follows federal Schedule A, or that you must itemize federally to qualify
- Skipping the worksheet
## Prompt
- I paid Medicare premiums and doctor copays. Is there an Ohio deduction?
