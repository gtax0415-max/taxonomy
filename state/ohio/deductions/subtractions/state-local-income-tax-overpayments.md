# State and Local Income Tax Overpayments
## Description
Deduct the taxable state and local income tax refund included in federal AGI. Enter the amount from 2026 federal Schedule 1, line 1. R.C. 5747.01(A)(11)(a).

Only the portion that federal law made taxable reaches Schedule 1 line 1. If you took the federal standard deduction in the year of the overpayment, the refund is not in federal AGI at all and there is nothing to subtract here.
## Amount
Federal Schedule 1, line 1.
## Required Information
Federal Schedule 1.
## Questions
- Did you report a state tax refund on federal Schedule 1?
## Common Errors
- Entering the refund received instead of the taxable portion on Schedule 1 line 1
- Subtracting a refund that never entered federal AGI
## Prompt
- I got a state tax refund that showed up on my federal return.
