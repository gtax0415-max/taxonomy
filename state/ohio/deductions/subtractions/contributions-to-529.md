# Contributions to a 529 Plan
## Description
Deduct contributions you made to a 529 savings plan, limited to $4,000 per beneficiary per year. Excess carries forward under the same annual cap. Married taxpayers share one $4,000 combined cap per beneficiary, joint or separate. Also deduct 529 income included in federal AGI and negative amounts in box 2 of Form 1099-Q that were not deducted federally. You may not deduct 529 income that is not in federal AGI, or amounts contributed by another person.
## Required Information
529 statements; beneficiary list; prior-year unused contributions. Worksheet at tax.ohio.gov/faq-529.
## Questions
- How much did you contribute per beneficiary?
- Did someone else fund the account?
## Common Errors
- Deducting another person's contributions
- Taking $4,000 per spouse per beneficiary on top of the combined cap
