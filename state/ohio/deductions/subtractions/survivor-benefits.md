# Survivor Benefits
## Description
Deduct survivor benefits included in federal AGI. Most life-insurance proceeds are not in federal AGI and therefore are not deducted here. Generally the amount must be received because of another person's death. Age, relationship, and the decedent's prior tax treatment do not matter. Amounts payable without death as a precondition are not survivor benefits.
## Required Information
Plan terms; 1099-R or payer statement; confirmation the amount is in federal AGI.
## Questions
- Would this amount have been paid if the covered person were still alive?
## Common Errors
- Deducting life insurance that was never in federal AGI
- Deducting a payment that does not require a death
## Prompt
- I receive survivor benefits from my spouse's pension.
