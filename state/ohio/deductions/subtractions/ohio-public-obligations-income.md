# Income From Ohio Public Obligations
## Description
Deduct, to the extent included in federal AGI: interest income earned from Ohio public obligations and Ohio purchase obligations; gains from the sale or disposition of those obligations; and income from a certain transfer agreement or an enterprise transferred under that agreement. R.C. 5747.01(A)(7), (8), and (26).
## The mirror image
Gains on Ohio public obligations come off here. Losses on the same obligations go the other way: they are an ADDITION on Schedule of Adjustments line 5, to the extent the loss reduced federal AGI. Ohio removes these obligations from its base in both directions, so a taxpayer with a loss does not get to keep the federal benefit.

Out-of-state municipal interest is the opposite case again: it is an addition on line 1, not a deduction here.
## Required Information
1099-INT, 1099-OID, or sale records identifying the Ohio obligation.
## Questions
- Is this interest from an Ohio public obligation already in federal AGI?
- Did you have gains or losses on the sale?
## Common Errors
- Deducting out-of-state muni interest here
- Taking the gain deduction but skipping the matching loss addition on line 5
