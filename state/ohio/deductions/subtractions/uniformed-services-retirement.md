# Uniformed Services Retirement Income
## Description
Deduct retirement income in federal AGI related to service in the uniformed services (armed forces, NOAA commissioned corps, Public Health Service), reserve components, or the National Guard. A mixed civil-service pension is deductible only for the military-service fraction. Example: 10 years Army + 20 years National Parks, $90,000 in federal AGI → deduct $30,000. Also deduct survivor-benefit-plan payments collected by a surviving or former spouse. Amounts deducted here cannot be used for the retirement income credit or lump-sum retirement credit.
## Required Information
1099-R; years of military vs total federal service if the pension is civil service.
## Questions
- Is the entire pension military, or a mixed civil-service pension?
- Are you a surviving spouse collecting SBP?
## Common Errors
- Using the deducted amount in the retirement-credit table
- Deducting 100% of a mixed civil-service pension
