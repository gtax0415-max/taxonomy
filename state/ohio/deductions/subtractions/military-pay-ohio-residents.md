# Military Pay for Ohio Residents
## Description
An Ohio resident on active duty in the Army, Air Force, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Space Force, another designated armed-forces branch, a reserve component, or the National Guard may deduct military pay and allowances included in federal AGI and received while stationed outside Ohio. Nonresidents and resident servicemembers stationed in Ohio cannot use this line.
## Required Information
LES or W-2; duty station outside Ohio; Ohio residency.
## Questions
- Are you an Ohio resident?
- Were you stationed outside Ohio when you earned the pay?
## Common Errors
- Nonresidents using this line instead of line 33
- Residents stationed in Ohio claiming it
## Prompt
- I am an Ohio resident stationed in Texas. Does Ohio tax my military pay?
