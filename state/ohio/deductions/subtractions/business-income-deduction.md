# Business Income Deduction
## Description
Deduct business income computed on the Ohio Schedule of Business Income. You must attach that schedule. Leftover business income after the deduction is taxed at a flat 3%.
## Amount
Schedule of Business Income line 13. Cap $250,000, or $125,000 if married filing separately.
## Two things that come first in 2026
When a capital gain is business income, it comes off under R.C. 5747.01(A)(34) or (A)(35) BEFORE this deduction. The statute says so expressly in both divisions. Only what survives those enters the $250,000 cap, and only what survives the cap is taxed at 3%:

- (A)(34) and R.C. 5747.79 — qualifying capital gain on the sale of an ownership interest in a business
- (A)(35) — Ohio VCOC capital gain

## This deduction does not lower MAGI
MAGI is Ohio AGI PLUS the business income deduction, so claiming it leaves MAGI exactly where it was. It cannot move a taxpayer under the $100,000 retirement-credit cliff, the $40,000 child care cliff, or the $500,000 exemption cliff. It cuts the tax base and nothing else. See state/ohio/income/modified-adjusted-gross-income.md.

## What is business income
Income from the regular course of a trade or business; property integral to that business; a liquidation including goodwill; certain sales of an ownership interest; and compensation or guaranteed payments from a PTE you own at least 20% of. See R.C. 5747.01(B) and (C) and the examples on booklet pages 11-12.
## Required Information
Completed Ohio Schedule of Business Income; federal Schedules B, C, D, E, F; ownership percentages.
## Questions
- Do you have Schedule C, E, or F income that Ohio treats as business income?
- Do you own at least 20% of a PTE that pays you wages or guaranteed payments?
## Common Errors
- Copying every federal Schedule C/E number without applying Ohio's business-income definition
- Treating a spouse's wages from your S corporation as business income when the spouse owns 0%
- Taking the deduction without attaching the schedule
- Applying the $250,000 cap before the 2026 capital-gain deductions
- Expecting the deduction to restore a MAGI-tested credit
## Prompt
- I have a side business. What is the Ohio business income deduction?
