# Portion of Certain College Grants Used to Pay Room and Board
## Description
Deduct the portion of a federal Pell grant and/or Ohio College Opportunity Grant included in federal AGI that was received by you, your spouse, or your dependent and used to pay qualifying room and board, including meal plans furnished by the educational institution.
## Required Information
Grant statements; school billing for room and board; proof the grant is in federal AGI.
## Questions
- Was the grant used for institution-furnished room and board, not off-campus rent?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the entire Pell grant
- Deducting off-campus rent
## Prompt
- Part of my Pell grant paid dorm and meal plan.
