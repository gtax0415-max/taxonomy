# Contributions to and Interest Earned on Ohio Homebuyer Plus Accounts
## Description
Deduct contributions you made to an Ohio Homebuyer Plus account owned by you, your spouse, a sibling, a child or stepchild, or a grandchild. Cap $5,000 per account per year, or $10,000 if both spouses contribute on a joint return. Excess does not carry forward. Lifetime cap $25,000 per contributor to accounts of a single owner. Also deduct interest on an account you or your spouse own, and employer contributions included in federal AGI. Only available for five years after the account is opened; a transfer does not reset the clock. The owner cannot deduct someone else's contribution or a financial-institution opening gift.
## Required Information
Account opening date; contribution records; interest reported in federal AGI; employer W-2 detail if applicable.
## Questions
- Whose account did you fund?
- When was the account opened?
- Have you already used the $25,000 lifetime cap?
## Common Errors
- Carrying unused contributions to next year
- Deducting a contribution another person made to your account
- Claiming the deduction more than five years after opening
## Prompt
- I put money in an Ohio Homebuyer Plus account.
