# Nonresident Income From Ohio Disaster Work
## Description
A nonresident may deduct compensation or business receipts for disaster work in Ohio during a disaster response period if the nonresident had no other Ohio-sourced income during the year. Disaster work is repair, renovation, installation, or construction of property and equipment destroyed by the declared disaster and owned by a public utility, commercial mobile radio service provider, cable provider, or video provider, plus preparation for that work. The response period starts 10 days before the presidential or gubernatorial declaration and ends 60 days after the declaration expires or is rescinded.
## Required Information
Residency; declaration dates; proof the only Ohio income was disaster work.
## Questions
- Are you a nonresident?
- Did you have any other Ohio-source income this year?
## Common Errors
- Residents claiming this line
- Claiming it when you also had Ohio wages or rental income
## Prompt
- I came into Ohio after a storm to repair utility lines.
