# Payments Related to the East Palestine Train Derailment
## Description
Deduct amounts in federal AGI received because of the February 3, 2023 train derailment near East Palestine, Ohio, for personal or funeral expenses, residence repair or contents, common-carrier death or injury payments, general-welfare government payments, or lost-business payments from government, a railroad, or a related insurer or agent. Only the uninsured slice qualifies. Do not deduct amounts already excluded federally under IRC 139, IRS Notice 2024-46, or the Federal Disaster Tax Relief Act of 2023.
## Required Information
Payer statements; proof the amount is in federal AGI and not already excluded.
## Questions
- Is this payment already excluded from federal AGI?
- Did insurance cover the same expense?
## Common Errors
- Deducting an amount already excluded federally
- Deducting insurance-covered costs
## Prompt
- I received a payment after the East Palestine derailment.
