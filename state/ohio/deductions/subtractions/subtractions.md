# Ohio Subtractions
## Description
Income the federal return taxed that Ohio does not, plus Ohio-only contributions and recoveries. Every amount in this folder flows to Schedule of Adjustments line 47, then IT 1040 line 2b. Include the schedule whenever line 2b has an amount.

Line numbers are from the 2025 schedule. The 2026 booklet is not published. TWO new capital-gain subtractions are required by statute for taxable years beginning in or after 2026 even before Ohio prints line numbers for them: the qualifying capital gain on the sale of an ownership interest, R.C. 5747.01(A)(34) and 5747.79, and the Ohio VCOC capital gain, R.C. 5747.01(A)(35). Both come off BEFORE the $250,000 business income deduction in R.C. 5747.01(A)(28) when the gain is business income.

## High-traffic lines
Social Security, U.S. government interest, 529 contributions, business income, military retirement, and the medical worksheet.
## Ordering
When a capital gain is business income, deduct it under (A)(34) or (A)(35) first, then run what survives through the line 13 business income deduction, then tax the remainder at 3%. Reversing that order overstates the deduction.
