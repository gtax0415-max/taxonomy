# Repayment of Income Reported
## Description
Deduct an IRC 1341(a)(2) repayment made this year if you included the amount in federal income in a prior year, have not deducted it on any other Ohio line, it did not qualify for the Ohio resident or nonresident credit in the year recognized, and this year you claimed either a federal Schedule A deduction or a federal tax credit for the repayment.
## Required Information
Proof of the prior-year inclusion; proof of this year's repayment and federal 1341 treatment.
## Questions
- Did the original income get an Ohio resident or nonresident credit?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a repayment that already received an Ohio residency credit in the income year
## Prompt
- I had to pay back income I reported two years ago.
