# Rural Practice Incentive Program Repayments
## Description
Deduct, to the extent in federal AGI, loan principal and interest the Ohio Department of Higher Education repaid on your behalf under the rural practice incentive program for qualifying law-school expenses. Service attorneys in underserved communities can receive up to $30,000 for a three-year obligation and up to an additional $20,000 for a fourth or fifth year.
## Required Information
ODHE repayment statements; confirmation the amount is in federal AGI.
## Questions
- Did ODHE repay a law-school loan under this program, and is that amount in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Deducting repayments you made yourself
- Deducting an amount already excluded federally
## Prompt
- ODHE repaid part of my law school loan for rural practice.
