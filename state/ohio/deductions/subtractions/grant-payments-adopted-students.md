# Grant Payments for Students Who Are Adopted
## Description
Deduct, to the extent in federal AGI, grant payments the Ohio Department of Higher Education paid to a state institution or private college for you, your spouse, or your dependents. The program is a one-time $2,500 grant for adopted students in Ohio who apply with ODHE.
## Required Information
ODHE grant notice; school name; confirmation the amount is in federal AGI.
## Questions
- Was this the ODHE adopted-student grant, and is it in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Confusing this with the ODCY adoption grant on line 23
## Prompt
- My adopted child received a $2,500 Ohio college grant.
