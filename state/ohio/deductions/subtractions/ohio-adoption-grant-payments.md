# Ohio Adoption Grant Payments
## Description
Deduct the one-time Ohio Department of Children & Youth adoption grant included in federal AGI. Grants for adoptions finalized on or after January 1, 2023: $10,000 conventional, $15,000 if you were the child's foster caregiver, $20,000 if the child has a diagnosed special need.
## Required Information
ODCY grant notice; confirmation the amount is in federal AGI.
## Questions
- Did you receive the Ohio adoption grant, and is it in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Claiming this as a credit instead of a deduction
- Confusing it with the repealed adoption credit carryforward
## Prompt
- I received an Ohio adoption grant. Is it taxable?
