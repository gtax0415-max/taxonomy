# Reciprocity Wages
## Description
Deduct compensation earned in Ohio during the part of the year you were a resident of Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Pennsylvania, and/or West Virginia.
## Do not include
Ohio-sourced business income, lottery, casino or sports-gaming winnings, rental or royalty income, capital gains, or non-employee wages. Compensation from a PTE you own at least 20% of is reclassified as distributive share and does not qualify.
## Required Information
Residency dates; W-2s showing Ohio wages; the reciprocal state's two-letter code.
## Questions
- Did you live in IN, KY, MI, PA, or WV while earning Ohio wages?
## Common Errors
- Deducting lottery winnings or rental income
- Deducting wages from a PTE you own 20% or more
## Prompt
- I live in Kentucky and work in Cincinnati.
