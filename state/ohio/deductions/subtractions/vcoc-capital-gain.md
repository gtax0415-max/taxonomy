# Ohio VCOC Capital Gain Deduction
## Description
NEW for taxable years beginning in or after 2026. Deduct capital gain from a qualifying interest in an Ohio venture capital operating company, to the extent the gain is in federal or Ohio AGI and is not deducted elsewhere.

If the gain is business income, take this deduction BEFORE the $250,000 business income deduction in R.C. 5747.01(A)(28).
## Amount
- 100% of the capital gain attributable to the company's investments in Ohio businesses during the period it was an Ohio VCOC
- 50% of the capital gain attributable to the company's investments in all other businesses during that period

Terms follow R.C. 122.851.
## Recapture
If the Director of Development certifies that the company did not comply, add back amounts previously deducted. That add-back is an income addition, not a deduction. See state/ohio/income/additions/vcoc-capital-gain-recapture.md.
## Eligibility
- Taxable year beginning in or after 2026
- The gain comes from a qualifying interest in an Ohio venture capital operating company
- The gain is in federal or Ohio AGI
## Required Information
- Evidence the issuer was an Ohio VCOC under R.C. 122.851 for the investment period
- Split of the gain between Ohio-business investments and other investments
- Confirmation the gain is in federal AGI
## Questions
- Is this a 2026 gain, or a 2025 gain that does not qualify?
- How much of the VCOC's underlying investments were in Ohio businesses?
- Is the gain business income that should come off before the $250,000 deduction?
## Common Errors
- Claiming this on a 2025 return
- Deducting 100% of a gain that is only partly attributable to Ohio-business investments
- Taking the business income deduction first and then this one
- Missing the later add-back if Development certifies noncompliance
