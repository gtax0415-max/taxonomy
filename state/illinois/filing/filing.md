# Filing — Step 1, Step 5 (nonresidents), Step 12, and return mechanics
## Description
Everything on the IL-1040 that is not a number: who must file, residency, filing status, the dependent and resident checkboxes, Schedule NR for nonresidents and part-year residents, the health insurance checkbox, signature, due dates, extensions, amendments, and penalties. Get these wrong and every number that follows is computed on the wrong base.
