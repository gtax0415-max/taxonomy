# Return Mechanics — Due Dates, Extensions, Amendments, Penalties, Line 42, Signature
## Description
The procedural rules from the IL-1040 general information and Step 12, restated for tax year 2026. Most are ordinary, but three are Illinois-specific enough to matter: the extension is automatic and does not need to be requested, the use-tax line cannot be amended, and an unsigned return is treated as not filed.
## Dates for tax year 2026
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| Return and payment due | April 15, 2027 |
| Automatic six-month extension to FILE | October 15, 2027 — no request needed; a federal extension longer than six months carries over |
| Extension payment to avoid penalty and interest | Form IL-505-I by April 15, 2027 |
| Estimated payments for 2026 | April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027 |
| Overpayment forfeited if the return is never signed | three years after the extended due date |
The filing period follows the federal period; fiscal-year filers note the year end at the top of the form.
## Step 1, Line A — identification
Full name, date of birth (required since 2025), SSN or ITIN (do not redact), address, email, and the Illinois county of residence at December 31 (the county lived in while a resident, for part-year filers; blank for nonresidents). Foreign addresses: street, apartment, city/province/postal code in that order, and the unabbreviated country on the Foreign Nation line.
## Decedents
- Surviving spouse filing jointly: write "deceased" and the date of death above the spouse's name, sign as "filing as surviving spouse"; the refund issues to the survivor without Form IL-1310
- Single decedent: the personal representative signs with title and phone, writes "deceased" and "in care of" with the executor's address, and attaches Form IL-1310 if a refund is due
## Rounding
Whole dollars: drop under 50 cents, round 50 cents and up. Add cents first and round only the total.
## Amended returns — Form IL-1040-X
Required when you find an error, or when the IRS or a federal 1040-X changes something that affects Illinois income, additions, subtractions, exemptions, or credits and the change is FINAL. For a federal refund, wait for IRS acceptance before amending Illinois. Never file a second IL-1040. Two things an IL-1040-X cannot change: the use tax on Line 21 (use Form ST-44) and Schedule G donations.
## Penalties and interest (Publication 103)
- Late-filing penalty if no processable return by the extended due date
- Late-payment penalty on tax unpaid after the ORIGINAL due date, even with an extension
- Late-payment penalty for underpaid estimates — see ../payments/underpayment-penalty.md
- Bad check penalty; cost-of-collection fee if a Final Notice (IDOR-2-BILL) goes unpaid 30 days; frivolous return penalty
- Interest from the day after the original due date until paid
If you cannot pay, FILE anyway by the due date to avoid the late-filing penalty; only the late-payment penalty and interest accrue. IDOR bills penalties and interest; you need not compute them.
## Reportable transactions
A federal reportable or listed transaction must also be disclosed to Illinois: attach one copy of the federal disclosure form and mail a second to IDOR, P.O. Box 19029, Springfield, IL 62794-9029. Illinois subtractions on Lines 5-7 are not reportable transactions.
## Address changes
Report through the website or by phone after filing, with the move date.
## Step 12, Line 42 — health insurance checkbox
Optional. Checking it, and providing an email in Step 1, lets IDOR share income and contact information with the Department of Insurance so Get Covered Illinois can assess Marketplace eligibility. Boxes for Self, Spouse, and Dependent(s) identify who is uninsured. No tax effect; information is confidential and used only for coverage eligibility.
## Signature
Both spouses sign a joint return; a parent or guardian signs for a minor. Unsigned returns are not filed — nonfiler penalty applies and, after three years past the extended due date, any overpayment is forfeited. Paid preparers sign with PTIN, phone, and firm FEIN/address if employed by a firm. The optional third-party designee may discuss this and prior related returns with IDOR until revoked.
## Attachments and mailing
Staple W-2s and 1099s, all required schedules, powers of attorney, and letters of office to the return. Paper returns: no payment → P.O. Box 19041, Springfield IL 62794-9041; with payment → P.O. Box 19027, Springfield IL 62794-9027, with Form IL-1040-V and the check stapled to the front. Electronic filing through MyTax Illinois is free.
## Common Errors
- Requesting an extension that is already automatic, then missing the April payment
- Filing a second original IL-1040 instead of IL-1040-X
- Amending Line 21 use tax on an IL-1040-X
- Forgetting the second signature on a joint return
- Omitting date of birth (required since 2025)
