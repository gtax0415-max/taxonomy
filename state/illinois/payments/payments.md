# Payments, Penalty, Donations, and Refund — Steps 8 through 11
## Description
The back page of the IL-1040. Total tax (Line 24) is settled against six items on Lines 25-30 — four PAYMENTS and two REFUNDABLE CREDITS — then adjusted for the estimated-tax penalty and any Schedule G donations, and the result is a refund, a credit forward, or a balance due.

## Why the order matters
Lines 25-30 are all applied AFTER total tax, so unlike the Line 15-17 credits they are never capped at the tax: an excess produces a refund. That is what makes the EITC and Child Tax Credit refundable and lets pass-through withholding come back to a nonresident whose Illinois tax is smaller than what the entity withheld.
## 2026 parameters
- Estimated-payment trigger: expected liability over $1,000 after withholding, pass-through withholding, and credits
- Safe harbor: timely installments totaling at least 90% of the current year's tax or 100% of the prior year's tax
- 2026 installment due dates: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027
- Minimum refund $1; minimum payment $1
- Withholding rate 4.95% with the $2,925 allowance (Booklet IL-700-T)
