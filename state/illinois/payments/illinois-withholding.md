# Illinois Income Tax Withheld — Line 25
## Description
Tax your employer, pension payer, or other payer already sent to Illinois on your behalf, claimed as a payment. Since 2018 every withholding form must be itemized on Schedule IL-WIT; IDOR matches the schedule against payer filings, and unmatched withholding is the most common cause of a delayed refund.
## Amount
The total Illinois income tax withheld shown on all your forms for the year, as listed on Schedule IL-WIT. Include:
- W-2 Box 17 (state income tax) where Box 15 shows IL
- W-2G Illinois withholding on gambling and lottery winnings
- 1099-R Illinois withholding on retirement distributions (common even though the income is subtracted on Line 5 — the withholding is still a payment and is refunded)
- 1099-G, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, 1099-K Illinois withholding
- Household employee withholding is the EMPLOYER's Line 20; the employee claims it on their own Line 25
## Schedule IL-WIT
One line per form: payer name, FEIN, form type, Illinois wages or income, and Illinois withholding. The total carries to Line 25. Withholding not listed on IL-WIT is not credited.
## Notes
- Do NOT include tax withheld by Iowa, Kentucky, Michigan, or Wisconsin employers; recover it from that state
- Do NOT include federal withholding or another state's withholding
- Tax withheld IN ERROR from a nonresident (e.g., a Wisconsin resident's Illinois wages) is refundable on a Schedule NR return with an employer letter
- Over-withholding on a retirement distribution is refunded once the income is subtracted on Line 5 — retirees often stop Illinois withholding on 1099-R payments entirely, because Illinois will not tax the income
- Withholding is treated as paid evenly through the year for the underpayment penalty, so increasing withholding late in the year cures an estimated-tax shortfall in a way a late estimate cannot
- Adjust withholding with Form IL-W-4; the 2026 allowance is $2,925 per exemption
## Required Information
- Every form showing Illinois withholding, with payer FEIN
- Illinois wages or income on each
## Questions
- Which W-2s, 1099s, and W-2Gs show Illinois tax withheld?
- Is any of it actually withholding for another state?
- Was Illinois tax withheld from retirement income that Illinois will not tax?
## Common Errors
- Entering Line 25 without Schedule IL-WIT or with forms missing from it
- Claiming Wisconsin or other-state withholding as Illinois
- Not attaching the W-2s and 1099s
- Retirees paying Illinois withholding on pensions all year for a refund the following spring
## Prompt
- Where do I put my Illinois withholding on the IL-1040?
- What is Schedule IL-WIT?
- Illinois tax was withheld from my pension, do I get it back?
