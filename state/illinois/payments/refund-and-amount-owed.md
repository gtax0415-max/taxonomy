# Refund, Credit Forward, and Amount You Owe — Step 11
## Description
How the return settles. An overpayment (Line 37) can be refunded (Line 38), credited to next year's estimates (Line 40), or split between the two. A balance (Line 41) is due April 15 regardless of any extension. The credit-forward dating rules are the part people get wrong: a late-filed return can make the credit arrive too late to cover the first estimate.
## Line 37 — overpayment
Line 32 minus Line 36 (penalty and donations). If Line 36 exceeds Line 32, leave Line 37 blank; the shortfall goes to Line 41.
## Lines 38-39 — refund and method
- No refund under $1
- IDOR offsets the refund against outstanding Illinois tax, penalties, and interest, and against debts certified by other agencies and governments (child support, student loans, other states)
- Check ONE Line 39 box: direct deposit (routing, account, checking or savings) or paper check
- First-year filers get a paper check regardless
- No international ACH: a foreign bank produces a check instead
- A refund can be sent straight into a Bright Start or Bright Directions 529 account using the plan's routing number and a prefixed account number (see source_doc) — and the deposit counts as a 2027 contribution for next year's Line 13 subtraction
- Mismatched names on the account cause the bank to reject the deposit; IDOR then mails a check
## Line 40 — credit forward, and when it counts as paid
Lines 38 + 40 must equal Line 37. The credit is applied to the estimated-payment period currently due on the date you FILE:
- File a 2026 return by January 15, 2028 (last 2027 installment) → applied to 2027 estimates; file after that → applied to 2028
Deemed payment date:
- Return filed by the extended due date (October 15, 2027): credit treated as paid on the ORIGINAL due date, April 15, 2027 — except any portion arising from payments made after April 15, which is dated when paid
- Return filed AFTER the extended due date: credit dated the day the return was filed, so the first and possibly later 2027 installments are late
IDOR reduces the credit by any outstanding tax, penalty, or interest, which can itself trigger an underpayment penalty. To apply the credit to a different period, send a written request (name, SSN, source period, target period) to P.O. Box 19023, Springfield IL 62794-9023 WITH the return; the election is irrevocable and only later periods are allowed.
## Line 41 — amount you owe
Line 33 plus Line 36 (or Line 36 minus Line 32 when donations exceed the overpayment). Under $1 need not be paid, but the return must still be filed. Due April 15, 2027. Payment options:
- Electronic debit through MyTax Illinois or a preparer, with bank details plus your IL-PIN (many credit unions block savings-account debits; no foreign banks)
- Credit card through ACI Payments, payILtax, or Link2Gov/FIS, with a convenience fee, or in person at a regional office
- Check or money order payable to "Illinois Department of Revenue" (not IRS), SSNs and tax year in the lower left, U.S. funds on a U.S. bank, stapled with Form IL-1040-V to the front of a paper return
- Cash at the Springfield or Chicago office
Late payment brings a late-payment penalty and interest, billed by IDOR; compute them yourself on IL-2210 only if you prefer.
## Notes
- Splitting the overpayment is allowed; put the refund portion on Line 38 and the rest on Line 40
- A refund applied to a spouse's certified debt on a joint return is the injured-spouse scenario — see ../filing/filing-status.md
- Refund status is available online; 1099-G amounts for the following year's federal return are on MyTax Illinois, not mailed
## Required Information
- Bank account details or 529 account details
- Whether to credit forward and how much
- IL-PIN for electronic payment
## Questions
- Do you want the overpayment refunded, applied to 2027 estimates, or split?
- Direct deposit, paper check, or a deposit into Bright Start or Bright Directions?
- Are you filing after October 15, 2027 with a credit forward?
- Does either spouse owe a debt that could be offset?
## Common Errors
- Taking account numbers from a deposit slip
- Checking both Line 39 boxes, or neither
- Crediting forward on a late-filed return and missing the first installment
- Assuming the filing extension extends the April 15 payment date
- Lines 38 and 40 not adding to Line 37
## Prompt
- How do I get my Illinois refund by direct deposit?
- Can I apply my Illinois refund to next year's estimated tax?
- How do I pay the Illinois tax I owe?
- Can I deposit my Illinois refund into my Bright Start account?
