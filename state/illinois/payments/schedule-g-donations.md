# Voluntary Charitable Donations — Schedule G, Line 35
## Description
Check-off contributions to state-administered charitable funds, made through the return. They COST money — reducing the refund or increasing the balance due — and are not a credit or a subtraction. The confusion with the Illinois Gives credit (which REDUCES tax by 25% of a community-foundation gift) is common enough to state plainly: Schedule G is giving, Illinois Gives is a credit.
## Amount
Any whole-dollar amount of $1 or more per fund, listed on Schedule G. Line 35 is the total.
## The funds
Schedule G lists the funds available each year — historically including Wildlife Preservation, Child Abuse Prevention, Alzheimer's Disease Research, Assistance to the Homeless, Diabetes Research, Hunger Relief, Special Olympics, Military Family Relief, and others; the 2026 list is whatever the 2026 Schedule G prints. Funds are added and removed by the legislature.
## Notes
- IRREVOCABLE: a Schedule G donation cannot be changed or removed on Form IL-1040-X
- Federally, a Schedule G contribution is a charitable gift to a governmental fund, deductible on federal Schedule A if you itemize; Illinois gives no state benefit because it has no itemized deductions
- The donation is paid out of the overpayment first; if there is no overpayment it is added to the amount you owe on Line 41
- Not the same as the Illinois Gives credit (Schedule 1299-C, code 5670) — see ../credits/civic-charitable/illinois-gives-credit.md
## Required Information
- Fund names and amounts
## Questions
- Do you want to donate part of your refund to one of the state charitable funds?
- Are you looking for a charitable CREDIT instead? That is Illinois Gives, not Schedule G
## Common Errors
- Expecting Line 35 to reduce tax
- Trying to reverse a donation on an amended return
- Confusing Schedule G with the Illinois Gives credit
## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my Illinois refund to charity on the return?
- What is Schedule G on the Illinois return?
- I put $50 on Schedule G by mistake, can I amend?
