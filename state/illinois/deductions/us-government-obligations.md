# U.S. Government Obligation Interest
## Description
Federal law (31 U.S.C. 3124) bars states from taxing interest on U.S. Treasury obligations. Illinois implements that on Schedule M Line 22: Treasury bills, notes, bonds, savings bonds, and qualifying federal agency interest that was taxed federally come off. The same subtraction reaches the U.S.-obligation share of a mutual fund's dividends, which is where most of the work is.
## What is subtracted
- Interest on U.S. Treasury bills, notes, bonds, and TIPS
- Series EE and I savings bond interest INCLUDED in federal AGI (interest excluded federally under the education exclusion is not subtractable)
- Interest on obligations of U.S. agencies and instrumentalities that Publication 101 lists as exempt — Federal Farm Credit Banks, Federal Home Loan Banks, TVA, and others. NOT Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac, or GNMA, whose securities are not direct U.S. obligations
- Income exempt from state tax by other federal statutes, per Publication 101
## Mutual fund distributions
- A fund investing EXCLUSIVELY in U.S. government obligations: subtract the entire federally taxed distribution
- A mixed fund: subtract the portion attributable to U.S. obligations as reported by the fund. If the fund does not report a percentage, multiply the distribution by (fund's investment in exempt U.S. obligations ÷ fund's total investment)
- Money market funds follow the same rule; the fund's year-end "percentage of income from U.S. government securities" letter is the attachment
## Notes
- The subtraction is NET of related bond premium amortization
- Capital gains on the SALE of Treasuries are not interest and are fully taxable
- Repurchase agreements backed by Treasuries do not qualify
- Pass-through U.S. obligation interest from a partnership or trust goes on Line 14, not here
- Attach Schedule B (if filed), the fund statement, and any worksheet — Line 22 without support is routinely disallowed
## Required Information
- 1099-INT Box 3 amounts
- Fund percentage statements for every fund with government holdings
- Bond premium amortization allocated to the obligations
## Questions
- Do you hold Treasuries, savings bonds, or a government money market or bond fund?
- Did the fund report the percentage of its income from U.S. obligations?
- Was any savings bond interest excluded federally for education?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting GNMA, Fannie Mae, or Freddie Mac interest
- Subtracting 100% of a mixed fund's dividends
- Subtracting savings bond interest that was never taxed federally
- Subtracting gains on Treasury sales
- Not attaching the fund statement
## Prompt
- Is Treasury interest taxable in Illinois?
- My money market fund holds Treasuries, can I subtract the dividends on my Illinois return?
- Are I bonds taxed by Illinois?
