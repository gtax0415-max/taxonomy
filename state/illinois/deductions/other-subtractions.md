# Other Subtractions — the remaining Schedule M lines
## Description
Seventeen narrow subtractions. Each applies to a small population, but each is the ONLY way its item comes out of Illinois income. Every one requires the amount to have been included in IL-1040 Line 1 (or Lines 2-3) first.
## Line 15 — Restoration of amounts held under claim of right
The IRC Section 1341 amount: either the deduction used to compute the federal Section 1341 credit, or the itemized deduction taken for the repayment. Because Illinois has no itemized deductions, this is the only route for a repaid bonus, disability overpayment, or commission clawback to reduce Illinois income.
## Line 16 — Contributions to a job training project
Contributions under the Tax Increment Allocation Redevelopment Act to a TIF-district job training project (Informational Bulletin FY 90-40). Pass-through contributions go on Line 14.
## Line 17 — Expenses related to federal credits or federally tax-exempt income
Expenses disallowed federally under IRC Sections 45G(e)(3), 171(a)(2), 265, or 280C because they related to income exempt federally but added back on IL-1040 Line 2, or because a federal credit was claimed for them (e.g., wages disallowed for the federal R&D or WOTC credit), or amounts included in AGI under IRC Section 87 (alcohol fuels credit). Since Illinois taxes the interest and does not give the credit, it allows the expense.
## Line 23 — August 1, 1969 valuation limitation
From Illinois Schedule F, Line 17: the portion of a capital gain attributable to appreciation BEFORE August 1, 1969, the date the Illinois income tax took effect, on property held since then. Attach Schedule F and the federal forms. Still relevant for inherited farmland and long-held real estate.
## Line 24 — River edge redevelopment zone and high impact business dividends
From Schedule 1299-C Step 1, Line 7: dividends from a corporation conducting substantially all its business in a river edge redevelopment zone, or from a DCEO-designated high impact business in a federal foreign trade zone in Illinois. Attach Schedule 1299-C. Pass-through versions also come here, not Line 14.
## Line 25 — Recovery of items previously deducted on federal Schedule A
Refunds of OTHER states' income taxes and other recoveries of prior federal itemized deductions that are in federal Schedule 1 and IL-1040 Line 1 this year. Illinois never allowed the deduction, so it does not tax the recovery. Attach federal page 1, Schedule 1, and the schedule showing the source. (Illinois's own refund goes on IL-1040 Line 6.)
## Line 26 — Ridesharing money and benefits
Amounts, other than salary, received by a DRIVER in a genuine commuter ridesharing (carpool/vanpool) arrangement, if included in Line 1. NOT income from Uber, Lyft, or other ridesharing companies.
## Line 27 — Accelerated life insurance, endowment, or annuity benefits
Payments received before they would ordinarily be paid, as an indemnity for a TERMINAL ILLNESS, to the extent included in Line 1.
## Line 28 — Lloyd's plan of operation income
Income from a Lloyd's plan of operation included in AGI, if reported on your behalf on Form IL-1065 (the mirror of the Line 3 addition).
## Line 29 — Illinois pre-need funeral, burial, and cemetery trust income
Income earned by trusts under the Illinois Pre-Need Cemetery Sales Act or the Illinois Funeral or Burial Funds Act, if included in Line 1 or Line 3.
## Line 30 — Health care provider education loan repayments
Loan repayments made for eligible health care providers who agree to practice in designated shortage areas under the Underserved Health Care Provider Workforce Act, if included in Line 1 or Line 3. Re-worded in the 2025 instructions to track the Act.
## Line 31 — Nazi persecution reparations
Reparations or other amounts received as a victim of racial or religious persecution by Nazi Germany or any Axis regime, or as the heir of such a victim, to the extent in AGI.
## Line 37 — Railroad sick pay and railroad unemployment compensation
Amounts included in Line 1 (Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act). Attach the 1099-G or W-2 plus federal page 1 and Schedule 1. Not railroad retirement (Line 5) and not third-party sick pay.
## Line 38 — Unjust imprisonment compensation
Compensation awarded by the Illinois Court of Claims for time unjustly served in an Illinois prison, if in Line 1.
## Line 39 — 529 distributions taxed because of a federal education credit
Distributions from Bright Start, Bright Directions, or College Illinois that you had to include in federal AGI because you claimed the American Opportunity or Lifetime Learning Credit on the same expenses. Illinois lets the distribution stay tax-free.
## Line 40 — Cannabis establishment Section 280E deductions
Deductions disallowed federally under IRC 280E for a cannabis establishment licensed under the Cannabis Regulation and Tax Act, or a cultivation center or medical dispensary under the Compassionate Use of Medical Cannabis Program Act, operating in Illinois, and not added back under IITA 203. Illinois allows ordinary business expenses the federal code denies to cannabis businesses.
## Line 41 — Fertility fraud awards
Amounts received from a judgment or settlement for fertility fraud or donor fertility fraud under the Illinois Fertility Fraud Act or a similar action in another state.
## Notes
- Lines 32-33 and 42 are subtotal and total lines; Line 42 → IL-1040 Line 7
- Any subtraction on Schedule M is disallowed if the underlying income is not in Line 1, 2, or 3; several lines say so explicitly and IDOR applies the rule to all
- Nothing here covers unemployment compensation, gambling losses, or itemized deductions — see ../income/not-allowed.md
