# Military Pay Subtraction
## Description
Illinois exempts pay for service in the U.S. Armed Forces, the National Guard of any state, and dual-status military technicians, to the extent it was TAXED federally and included in IL-1040 Line 1. Combined with the retirement subtraction (which exempts military retirement pay on Line 5), an Illinois-domiciled service member typically owes Illinois tax only on civilian side income.
## What is subtracted
- Active duty, reserve, and National Guard pay from the U.S. Armed Forces or the National Guard of any state, included in federal AGI
- Pay received as a dual-status military technician (federal civilian employee required to hold Guard membership) — attach both the W-2 and SF-50
## What is not subtracted
- Combat pay already excluded from federal AGI — nothing to subtract
- Pay as a civilian employee of the military
- Voluntary Separation Incentive payments
- Ready Reserve Mobilization Income Insurance Program payments
- Pay as an officer in the Public Health Service (not Armed Forces for this purpose)
- Military RETIREMENT pay — subtractable, but on IL-1040 Line 5, not here
## Notes
- Residency does not change: an Illinois domiciliary stationed elsewhere remains an Illinois resident and files as one, then subtracts the military pay. A service member domiciled elsewhere stationed in Illinois is a nonresident and Illinois does not tax military pay at all (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act)
- Military spouses: under the Military Spouses Residency Relief Act and the 2023 Veterans Auto and Education Improvement Act, a spouse may elect the service member's domicile for state tax purposes; that is a residency question, not a Schedule M subtraction
- The W-2 attachment is mandatory; IDOR rejects Line 21 without it
- If the military pay is also in Column B of Schedule CR (taxed by another state), it must be subtracted again on Schedule CR Line 39
## Required Information
- Military W-2(s) with Box 1 wages
- SF-50 for dual-status technicians
- Whether any pay is civilian, VSI, or Public Health Service
## Questions
- Were you on active duty, in the reserves, or in the National Guard during the year?
- Is the pay on a W-2 from DFAS or the Guard, and is it in your federal AGI?
- Are you a dual-status technician?
- Is any of the income military retirement rather than pay?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting federally excluded combat pay
- Subtracting civilian DoD wages
- Omitting the W-2 or, for technicians, the SF-50
- Putting military retirement on Line 21 instead of Line 5
## Prompt
- I am in the Illinois National Guard, is my drill pay taxable in Illinois?
- I am active duty Army stationed in Texas but my home of record is Illinois, do I owe Illinois tax?
- Is military retirement taxed in Illinois?
