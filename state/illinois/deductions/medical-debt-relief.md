# Medical Debt Relief Subtraction
## Description
New for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025 (Public Act 103-0647) and continuing for 2026. Illinois funds the purchase and cancellation of medical debt for eligible residents through the Medical Debt Relief Act. Cancelled debt is ordinarily income under IRC Section 61(a)(11); to the extent it lands in federal AGI, Illinois subtracts it so the relief is not clawed back as state tax.
## Amount
The amount included in an ELIGIBLE RESIDENT's federal AGI that is attributable to medical debt relief received under the Medical Debt Relief Act.
## Eligibility
- You must be an eligible resident under the Act — generally household income at or below 400% of the federal poverty level, or medical debt equal to 5% or more of annual income, as determined by the program
- The debt must have been relieved under the Act's program, not by a hospital's own charity policy, a bankruptcy discharge, or a private settlement
- The amount must actually be in federal AGI. Most relief under such programs is structured so no Form 1099-C is issued; if nothing was included federally, there is nothing to subtract
## Notes
- Debt cancelled under the federal insolvency or bankruptcy exclusions is already out of federal AGI and gets no Illinois subtraction (none needed)
- A 1099-C for medical debt settled privately at a discount is taxable in Illinois; this line does not cover it
- New enough that IDOR has not issued detailed guidance; keep the program notice
## Required Information
- Documentation the relief came through the Medical Debt Relief Act program
- The amount reported in federal AGI
## Questions
- Was your medical debt cancelled through the Illinois Medical Debt Relief Act program?
- Did you receive a 1099-C, and is the amount on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting privately negotiated medical debt forgiveness
- Subtracting relief that was never included in federal AGI
## Prompt
- Illinois cancelled my medical debt, do I owe tax on it?
- What is the medical debt relief subtraction on Schedule M?
