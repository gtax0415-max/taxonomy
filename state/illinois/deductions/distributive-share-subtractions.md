# Distributive Share of Subtractions
## Description
Subtractions earned inside a partnership, S corporation, trust, or estate and passed through on an ILLINOIS Schedule K-1-P or K-1-T. You may claim them only if the entity notifies you on the Illinois schedule. Almost every subtraction with its own Schedule M line has a pass-through version that goes HERE instead.
## Amount
The total of Column A subtractions on all Illinois K-1-Ps and K-1-Ts received, where the entity's tax year ends within your tax year.
## Routing rules — where pass-through items go
| Pass-through item | Goes on |
|---|---|
| Job training project contributions | Line 14 (not Line 16) |
| Special depreciation subtraction | Line 14 (not Line 19) |
| U.S. government obligation interest | Line 14 (not Line 22) |
| Most other subtractions | Line 14 |
| River edge redevelopment zone and high impact business dividends | Line 24, via Schedule 1299-C Step 1 |
| Retirement payments to retired partners (K-1-P) and beneficiary share of retirement plan payments (K-1-T) | IL-1040 Line 5, NOT Schedule M |
## Notes
- Attach the Illinois K-1-P or K-1-T showing your name and SSN. A federal K-1 or a preparer worksheet is rejected and the subtraction disallowed
- If the entity is a grantor trust or disregarded entity and the notice does not show you as the reporting person, attach a statement explaining who reports the income
- Illinois replacement tax paid by the entity is a subtraction to the entity, not to you
- Pass-through withholding and PTE tax credits from the same schedule go to IL-1040 Lines 27 and 28
## Required Information
- Every Illinois K-1-P and K-1-T with Column A subtraction totals
- Any retired-partner or beneficiary retirement amounts, for Line 5
## Questions
- Did you receive an Illinois K-1-P or K-1-T with subtractions in Column A?
- Does it include retirement payments to you as a retired partner or beneficiary?
- Does the entity's year end fall within your 2026 tax year?
## Common Errors
- Attaching the federal K-1
- Putting pass-through U.S. obligation interest on Line 22, or pass-through depreciation on Line 19
- Putting retired-partner retirement payments on Line 14 instead of IL-1040 Line 5

- I am a retired partner receiving payments on a K-1-P, is that taxable in Illinois?
