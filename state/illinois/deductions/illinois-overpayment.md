# Illinois Income Tax Overpayment
## Description
If you itemized federally in the prior year and deducted Illinois income tax, the refund of that tax is taxable federal income the following year (Schedule 1 Line 1). Illinois will not tax its own refund, so the amount comes off on Line 6. With the SALT cap at $40,400 for 2026 and more filers itemizing again, this line is live for more people than it was under the $10,000 cap.
## Amount
The total Illinois income tax overpayment for the prior year that was included in federal AGI — including any part applied to another liability or credited forward rather than refunded in cash. Only the portion the federal return actually taxed (Schedule 1 Line 1 can be less than the refund under the tax-benefit rule).
## What does not go here
- OTHER states' refunds. They stay in Illinois income, unless they qualify as a recovery of a prior federal Schedule A deduction on Schedule M Line 25 — see other-subtractions.md
- An Illinois refund that was NOT included in federal AGI (you took the federal standard deduction in the prior year). Nothing to subtract
- Illinois property tax refunds, sales tax refunds
## Notes
- The 1099-G is not mailed; get the amount from MyTax Illinois
- Interest IDOR paid on a late refund is separate interest income and is taxable in Illinois
- If the prior-year overpayment was applied to estimated tax, it is still an "overpayment" for this line to the extent federally taxed
## Required Information
- Prior-year Illinois refund and credit-forward amount
- Federal Schedule 1 Line 1 and the taxable-refund worksheet
## Questions
- Did you itemize on your federal return last year and deduct Illinois income tax?
- Did you receive or apply an Illinois overpayment this year, and is it on federal Schedule 1 Line 1?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a refund that was never taxed federally
- Subtracting another state's refund on this line
- Subtracting the full refund when the federal tax-benefit rule taxed only part
## Prompt
- My Illinois refund from last year is taxable on my federal return, does Illinois tax it too?
- Where do I enter my state tax refund on the IL-1040?
