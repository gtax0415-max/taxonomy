# Illinois Special Depreciation Subtraction
## Description
The other half of Illinois's bonus depreciation decoupling. After the federal bonus depreciation is added back on Schedule M Line 5 in the year an asset is placed in service, Form IL-4562 gives back the depreciation Illinois allows each year — the amount that would have been deductible without bonus — as a subtraction on Line 19. Over the asset's life, and on its disposition, the additions and subtractions net to zero; the state simply refuses to let the deduction be front-loaded.
## Amount
Form IL-4562, Step 3, Line 19. For property placed in service in tax years ending on or after December 31, 2021, the subtraction each year equals the regular MACRS depreciation that would have been allowed on the bonus-depreciated basis had bonus not been claimed; in the year of sale or disposition, any remaining unrecovered addition is subtracted in full.
## Notes
- Requires a Form IL-4562 EVERY year an asset with an Illinois addition is on the books; the subtraction cannot be reconstructed without the chain
- On disposition, the catch-up subtraction also affects Illinois gain: the Illinois basis is higher than federal basis by the unrecovered addition, and IL-4562 handles it through the final-year subtraction rather than a separate basis adjustment
- Pass-through depreciation subtractions from a K-1-P or K-1-T go on Line 14, not here
- Bonus depreciation rules changed federally three times in a decade (100%, phase-down to 40% for 2025 under TCJA, then 100% restored by OBBBA for property acquired after January 19, 2025); IL-4562 has separate lines for each regime, and the 2026 form will reflect the restored 100% rate
- Nonresidents apportion through Schedule NR
## Required Information
- All prior IL-4562 forms
- Current-year federal depreciation by asset
- Disposition details for any asset sold
## Questions
- Do you have assets on which Illinois bonus depreciation was added back in a prior year?
- Did you sell or dispose of any of them this year?
- Do you have every prior year's IL-4562?
## Common Errors
- Skipping Line 19 in years after the addition, permanently losing the deduction
- Missing the final-year catch-up on disposition
- Claiming the subtraction with no prior addition on record
- Reporting pass-through amounts here instead of Line 14
