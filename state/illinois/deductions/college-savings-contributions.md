# College Savings Plan Contributions — Bright Start, Bright Directions, College Illinois
## Description
Subtraction for contributions to Illinois's own 529 plans, worth up to $495 ($990 joint) of Illinois tax at the caps. It is the most valuable "deduction" available to a working family in Illinois and one of the few state incentives that rewards saving for college. Only the three Illinois programs qualify; contributions to any other state's plan are worth nothing here.
## Amount for 2026
- Contributions made during the tax year to Bright Start, Bright Directions, or College Illinois, including contributions your EMPLOYER made on your behalf
- Capped at $10,000 per year ($20,000 married filing jointly), across all accounts combined
- Gift contributions to someone else's account count; check the gift box in Column C
## What does not count
- Contributions to another state's 529 or to a private prepaid plan
- Rollovers or transfers between 529 accounts, in any direction
- Earnings rolled in from another program (only the contribution portion of an inbound rollover ever qualified, and IDOR treats 529-to-529 transfers as excluded entirely)
- Contributions above the cap; there is no carryforward of the excess
## Mechanics
- Table 13: ONE account per row, account number in Column A (nothing else in that column), contribution in Column B, gift box in Column C. Attach a page in the same format for more accounts
- Line 13a is the total; Line 13 is the lesser of 13a or the cap
- Gift contribution without an account number: attach the cancelled check and the account holder's name and address
## Notes
- The subtraction is per RETURN, not per beneficiary. Two children do not double the cap; two spouses filing jointly do
- Contribution deadline is December 31; there is no April 15 look-back
- The subtractions are RECAPTURED (Schedule M Lines 7 and 9) if the account is later rolled to another state's plan or withdrawn for non-qualified use — see ../../income/additions/college-savings-recapture.md
- Refunds directed into Bright Start or Bright Directions through the IL-1040 direct-deposit routing count as contributions in the year deposited
- Employer contributions may also give the employer the Student-Assistance Contributions credit (1299-C code 5420)
## Required Information
- Account number and contribution amount per account
- Whether each contribution was a gift
- Employer contributions shown separately
## Questions
- Did you contribute to Bright Start, Bright Directions, or College Illinois this year, and how much per account?
- Did your employer contribute on your behalf?
- Was any of it a rollover from another plan?
- Are you filing jointly?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting contributions to another state's plan
- Exceeding $10,000 / $20,000, or claiming the cap per child
- Including a rollover
- Putting a name instead of the account number in Column A
- Missing employer contributions
