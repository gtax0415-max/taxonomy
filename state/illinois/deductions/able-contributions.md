# Illinois ABLE Account Contributions
## Description
Subtraction for contributions to a qualified ILLINOIS ABLE account — the tax-advantaged savings account for a person whose disability began before age 26 (age 46 under federal law beginning in 2026). Same structure and same caps as the 529 subtraction, and the two caps are SEPARATE: a family can subtract $10,000 for Bright Start and another $10,000 for Illinois ABLE in the same year.
## Amount for 2026
- Contributions made during the tax year to a qualified Illinois ABLE program account
- Capped at $10,000 per year ($20,000 married filing jointly)
- Gift contributions count; check Column C
## What does not count
- Contributions to another state's ABLE program
- Rollovers or transfers between accounts (including 529-to-ABLE rollovers)
- Amounts above the federal annual ABLE contribution limit are not permitted by the plan in the first place
## Mechanics
Table 20 works exactly like Table 13: one account per row, account number only in Column A, amount in Column B, gift box in Column C. Line 20 is the lesser of Line 20a or the cap.
## Notes
- 2026 federal change: the ABLE Age Adjustment Act raises the onset-of-disability age from 26 to 46 for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025, expanding who can open an account; the Illinois subtraction follows the account, so newly eligible savers get it
- Recapture applies (Schedule M Lines 7 and 9) if the account is rolled to another state's ABLE plan or funds are withdrawn for non-qualified expenses, other than on the beneficiary's death or disability
- Earnings from an Illinois ABLE account are exempt from the Line 4 addition
## Required Information
- Account number and contributions per account
- Whether any contribution was a gift
## Questions
- Did you contribute to an Illinois ABLE account this year?
- Was it the Illinois program, or another state's?
- Was any of it a rollover?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting contributions to another state's ABLE plan
- Combining ABLE and 529 contributions under one $10,000 cap — they are separate
- Including a 529-to-ABLE rollover
## Prompt
- Can I subtract Illinois ABLE contributions on my state return?
- I contribute to both Bright Start and an ABLE account, is the limit $10,000 total?
