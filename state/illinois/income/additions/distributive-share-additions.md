# Distributive Share of Additions
## Description
Additions that originate inside a partnership, S corporation, trust, or estate and are passed to you on an ILLINOIS Schedule K-1-P or K-1-T. The entity is required to notify you; you report only what the Illinois schedule shows. Every kind of addition that would otherwise have its own Schedule M line — special depreciation, tax-exempt interest, 529 earnings — goes on Line 2 when it arrives through an entity, not on the item's own line.
## Amount
The total of Column A additions on all Schedules K-1-P and K-1-T received, where the entity's tax year ends within your tax year.
## What typically comes through
- Federally tax-exempt interest earned by the entity
- The entity's Illinois special depreciation addition (Form IL-4562 at the entity level)
- Illinois replacement tax deducted federally
- Guaranteed payments treated as compensation for replacement tax purposes are NOT an addition to you — check the K-1-P instructions for the current list
## Notes
- The Illinois K-1-P or K-1-T is the only acceptable attachment. Federal Schedule K-1s and preparer worksheets are rejected, and the addition may be disallowed
- If the issuing entity is a grantor trust or disregarded entity and the K-1 does not show you as the person reporting the income, attach a statement explaining who does
- The mirror-image subtractions come through on Schedule M Line 14, except river edge and high impact dividend subtractions, which go on Line 24
- Pass-through withholding and PTE tax credits shown on the same K-1-P go to IL-1040 Lines 27 and 28, not here
## Required Information
- Every Illinois K-1-P and K-1-T, with Column A totals
- Entity FEIN and tax year end
## Questions
- Did you receive an ILLINOIS Schedule K-1-P or K-1-T, as opposed to only a federal K-1?
- Does Column A show additions?
- Does the entity's tax year end within your 2026 tax year?
## Common Errors
- Attaching the federal K-1 instead of the Illinois schedule
- Reporting the entity's special depreciation on Schedule M Line 5 instead of Line 2
- Missing a K-1-T from a trust that also passes through PTE credits
## Prompt
- My Illinois K-1-P has an amount in Column A additions, where does it go?
- I am a partner in an Illinois partnership, what do I add back?
