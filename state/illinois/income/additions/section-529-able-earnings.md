# IRC Section 529 and ABLE Program Earnings
## Description
Earnings distributed from a college savings, prepaid tuition, or ABLE program that were EXCLUDED from federal AGI (because the distribution paid qualified expenses) are added back in Illinois — UNLESS the program is an Illinois plan or an out-of-state 529 that meets two disclosure conditions. In practice this hits families who saved in another state's plan and never realized Illinois taxes the growth.
## Amount
The earnings portion of distributions not included in federal AGI (Form 1099-Q Box 2), for every non-exempt program.
## Exempt programs — no addition
- Bright Start College Savings Pool
- Bright Directions College Savings Pool
- College Illinois Prepaid Tuition Program
- Qualified Illinois ABLE program accounts
- Any other state's qualified 529 program that (1) complies with the College Savings Plans Network disclosure principles AND (2) annually informs Illinois residents and distributors that Illinois in-state programs exist. Most major state plans now meet both; confirm with the plan
## Not an addition
- Earnings rolled over tax-free into another program
- Rollovers or transfers between 529 accounts in either direction
- Earnings already included in federal AGI (a non-qualified distribution) — those are in Line 1 already
## Notes
- The addition is on EARNINGS only, never on the return of contributions
- Non-qualified withdrawals trigger a different, separate addition: recapture of prior Illinois contribution SUBTRACTIONS on Schedule M Line 9 — see college-savings-recapture.md. Both can apply in the same year
- Distributive share of this addition from a trust goes on Line 2
## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q for each program, with earnings and whether the distribution was qualified
- Whether the plan is Illinois-sponsored or a compliant out-of-state plan
## Questions
- Did you take money out of a 529 or ABLE account this year?
- Which state's plan is it?
- Were the earnings excluded from your federal AGI?
- Was any of it a rollover?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Bright Start or Bright Directions earnings
- Adding back the full distribution rather than the earnings
- Missing the addition for a non-compliant out-of-state plan
- Treating a 529-to-529 rollover as a distribution
## Prompt
- I used a Utah 529 plan to pay tuition, does Illinois tax the earnings?
- Are Bright Start withdrawals taxable in Illinois?
