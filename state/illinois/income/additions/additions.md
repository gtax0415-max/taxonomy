# Additions to Federal AGI
## Description
Amounts Illinois taxes that the federal return did not. Line 2 (federally tax-exempt interest and dividends) sits directly on the IL-1040; everything else runs through Schedule M Step 2 and lands on Line 3. Additions are entered as positive numbers. The theme is consistent: Illinois does not honor federal exemptions for municipal bond interest, out-of-state 529 earnings, bonus depreciation, or the federal QSBS exclusion.

## What changed for 2026
- Federally excluded gain on Qualified Small Business Stock under IRC Section 1202 becomes an Illinois addition for tax years ending on or after December 31, 2026 (PA 104-0468). Illinois had conformed to the federal exclusion since 1993; this is the first decoupling
- The PTE tax add-back's original sunset (tax years beginning before January 1, 2026) is superseded by the continuation of the PTE election; expect the add-back on the 2026 Schedule M
## Notes
- Schedule M must be attached whenever Line 3 has an amount, with K-1-P/K-1-T or Form IL-4562 as applicable
- Distributive-share versions of every addition go on Line 2, never on the item's own line
