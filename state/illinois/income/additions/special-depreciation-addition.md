# Illinois Special Depreciation Addition
## Description
Illinois decouples from federal BONUS depreciation. Bonus depreciation claimed federally under IRC Section 168(k) is added back on Schedule M Line 5 in the year claimed, and Form IL-4562 then computes an Illinois depreciation subtraction (Schedule M Line 19) over the property's life so the total deduction eventually matches. For a sole proprietor, landlord, or farmer who took 100% bonus depreciation, this addition can be the largest number on the Illinois return.
## Amount
Form IL-4562, Step 2, Line 4 — the federal bonus depreciation for property placed in service this year. For property placed in service in tax years ending on or after December 31, 2021, IL-4562 adds back the full bonus amount and allows an Illinois subtraction equal to the depreciation that would have been allowed without bonus; the addition and the later subtractions net to zero over the asset's life.
## Who this hits
- Schedule C, E, and F filers who elected bonus depreciation
- Anyone with federal bonus depreciation on a directly owned asset. Bonus depreciation taken INSIDE a partnership or S corporation is added back at the entity level and reaches you on Schedule K-1-P → Schedule M Line 2, not Line 5
## Notes
- Section 179 expensing is NOT bonus depreciation and is not added back; Illinois conforms to Section 179
- The 2026 federal environment matters: the One Big Beautiful Bill Act restored 100% bonus depreciation for property acquired after January 19, 2025, so 2026 federal returns will again show large bonus amounts — and Illinois will add every dollar back
- Keep Form IL-4562 for every year the asset is depreciated; each year's subtraction depends on the prior additions
- A nonresident's addition flows through Schedule NR in proportion to Illinois business activity
## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 and the bonus depreciation amount by asset
- Placed-in-service dates
- Prior-year Forms IL-4562 for the subtraction side
## Questions
- Did you claim bonus depreciation on your federal return this year?
- Is the asset owned directly or through a partnership or S corporation?
- Do you have prior-year IL-4562 forms for assets still being depreciated?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Section 179 expensing
- Omitting Form IL-4562, which makes both the addition and the later subtractions unverifiable
- Reporting pass-through bonus depreciation on Line 5 instead of Line 2
- Forgetting the Line 19 subtraction in later years and permanently overpaying
