# Recapture of College Savings and ABLE Contribution Subtractions
## Description
Illinois lets you subtract up to $10,000 ($20,000 joint) a year of contributions to its own 529 and ABLE plans. If the money then leaves an Illinois plan for another state's plan, or is pulled out for non-qualified use, the subtractions come back as an addition. The recapture is capped at what you actually subtracted, so it can never exceed the benefit you received.
## Line 7 — rollover to an out-of-state plan
Add the SMALLER of:
- Total contribution subtractions you claimed for the Illinois plan, minus any amounts already recaptured in prior years, or
- The amount transferred during the year from the Illinois plan to the out-of-state plan
## Line 9 — non-qualified withdrawal or refund of contributions
Applies when funds were withdrawn or contributions refunded, NOT used for qualified expenses at an eligible institution, and the withdrawal did NOT result from the death or disability of the beneficiary. Add the SMALLER of:
- Total contribution subtractions claimed for the plan, minus any Line 7 recapture this year and any prior-year recapture for that plan, or
- The non-qualified withdrawal or refund amount
## Notes
- Both lines are about CONTRIBUTIONS previously subtracted. The EARNINGS on a non-qualified withdrawal are separately taxable — they are already in federal AGI (Line 1) with a federal penalty, and Illinois taxes them there
- A qualified withdrawal for tuition, or a 529-to-529 rollover WITHIN Illinois plans, triggers nothing
- Since 2024 federal law allows 529-to-Roth IRA rollovers; Illinois has not addressed whether that is a "transfer to an out-of-state plan" — treat conservatively and document
- Track cumulative subtractions and recaptures per account; the caps are lifetime figures
## Required Information
- Every year's Line 13 or Line 20 subtraction for the account
- The rollover, withdrawal, or refund amount and date
- Whether the beneficiary died or became disabled
## Questions
- Did you move a Bright Start, Bright Directions, College Illinois, or Illinois ABLE account to another state's plan?
- Did you withdraw money for something other than qualified expenses?
- How much have you subtracted for contributions to that account over the years?
## Common Errors
- Recapturing more than was ever subtracted
- Missing the recapture on a rollover to another state's plan because "no tax was due federally"
- Recapturing on a death-or-disability withdrawal
- Double-recapturing the same contributions on both Line 7 and Line 9
## Prompt
- I moved my Bright Start account to Nevada's plan, does Illinois care?
- I withdrew 529 money for a non-school expense, what happens in Illinois?
