# Federally Tax-Exempt Interest and Dividend Income — Line 2
## Description
The second line of Illinois income and the addition that applies to the most filers. Interest on state and local government bonds is exempt from FEDERAL tax but fully taxable in Illinois — including Illinois's own municipal bonds, with a short statutory list of exceptions. The entire Form 1040 Line 2a figure is added on IL-1040 Line 2, and the exempt Illinois issuers are then backed out on Schedule M Line 34.
## Amount
- Your own: Form 1040 Line 2a, in full, on IL-1040 Line 2
- Your child's, if you elected Form 8814: the tax-exempt interest and dividends from that form, on Schedule M Line 1
- Pass-through: the Column A addition on Schedule K-1-P or K-1-T, on Schedule M Line 2
## The two-step for Illinois bonds
Illinois does NOT exempt Illinois municipal bond interest as a rule. Add ALL of Line 2a first, then subtract on Schedule M Line 34 only the interest from issuers the legislature specifically exempted (Illinois Housing Development Authority, Illinois Sports Facilities Authority, Illinois Finance Authority under specified sections, the regional development authorities, and the others listed in ../deductions/illinois-and-territory-bond-interest.md). Interest from a City of Chicago or Illinois general obligation bond is taxable. Interest from Guam, Puerto Rico, Virgin Islands, American Samoa, and Northern Mariana Islands bonds comes back out on Line 35. Interest on U.S. Treasury obligations is not on Line 2a at all — it is federally taxable — and is subtracted on Line 22.
## Mutual funds
Exempt-interest dividends from a municipal bond fund are added in full. A fund's Illinois-exempt share can NOT be subtracted on Line 34 — the exemption is lost when the bonds are held indirectly through a fund. This is the trap: a national municipal bond fund and an "Illinois municipal" fund are taxed identically in Illinois.
## Notes
- Bond premium amortization that reduced Line 2a already reduces the addition
- Private activity bond interest that is taxable for federal AMT purposes is still tax-exempt interest on Line 2a and is added the same way
- A child's Form 8814 income later gets the same Line 34/35 treatment through Schedule M Line 36
## Required Information
- Form 1040 Line 2a
- Issuer-level detail for any Illinois bonds held directly, to support a Line 34 subtraction
- Form 8814 if filed
- K-1-P or K-1-T additions
## Questions
- Do you have an amount on Form 1040 Line 2a?
- Is any of it from bonds you hold directly, and from which Illinois issuer?
- Is any of it from a mutual fund?
- Did you report a child's interest and dividends on Form 8814?
## Common Errors
- Omitting Line 2 because "municipal bonds are tax-free"
- Netting Illinois-exempt issuers against Line 2a instead of adding the full amount and subtracting on Line 34
- Subtracting the Illinois portion of a municipal bond fund's dividends
- Entering a K-1-P addition on Line 2 of the IL-1040 instead of Schedule M Line 2

- I own Illinois muni bonds, are they exempt from Illinois tax?
