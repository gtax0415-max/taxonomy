# What Cannot Be Subtracted in Illinois
## Description
Schedule M is a closed list. Anything not on it, or not on the face of the IL-1040 at Lines 5 and 6, stays in Illinois income. This file exists because the items below are the ones people try to subtract every year, and the answer for each is no.
## Not subtractable, with the reason
| Item people try to subtract | Why not, and what applies instead |
|---|---|
| Out-of-state wages or income | Illinois taxes residents on everything. Relief is the Schedule CR CREDIT (IL-1040 Line 15), not a subtraction — see credits/other-state-tax/ |
| Unemployment compensation | Fully taxable in Illinois. Only RAILROAD unemployment is subtracted (Schedule M Line 37) |
| Gambling losses | No Illinois deduction, even against winnings. Illinois lottery and casino winnings are fully taxable and, for nonresidents, Illinois-sourced |
| Federal itemized deductions (Schedule A) | Illinois has no itemized deductions. Mortgage interest, charitable gifts, medical expenses, and SALT produce nothing |
| Federal standard deduction | Illinois substitutes the $2,925 exemption allowance |
| Wages you think are wrong | Only a corrected W-2 or an employer letter on letterhead can change wages. A letter from you or your preparer is not accepted |
| Social Security and pensions on Schedule M | They ARE subtractable, but on IL-1040 LINE 5, not Schedule M. Putting them on Line 7 double-counts or gets rejected |
| Other states' income tax refunds | Only ILLINOIS refunds come off (Line 6). Other states' refunds included in federal AGI stay in, except through Schedule M Line 25 when they are a recovery of a prior federal Schedule A deduction |
| Savings bond interest not in federal AGI | Not in federal AGI means nothing to subtract; only federally TAXED U.S. obligation interest comes off on Line 22 |
| Combat pay excluded federally | Same logic — Line 21 covers military pay that IS in federal AGI |
| An NOL carryforward not used federally this year | Illinois follows the federal NOL actually used; carrying the unused portion into Line 1 deducts it twice |
| Refigured passive activity losses | Illinois uses the federal passive loss figures as filed |
| Contributions to a non-Illinois 529 plan | Only Bright Start, Bright Directions, and College Illinois qualify on Line 13 |
| Rollovers between 529 accounts | Neither an addition nor a subtraction; ignored both ways |
| Third-party sick pay, retirement sick pay | Only RAILROAD sick pay is subtracted (Line 37) |
| Income from ridesharing COMPANIES (Uber, Lyft) | Line 26 is for true carpool cost-sharing among commuters, not gig driving |
