# Affordable Housing Donations Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit equal to 50% of a donation made under Section 7.28 of the Illinois Housing Development Act to a qualified affordable housing project. The 50% rate is the highest of any Illinois credit, and the credit is TRANSFERABLE, which makes it a market: donors who cannot use it sell it to taxpayers who can. Available for donations in tax years ending on or after December 31, 2001 through tax years ending on or before December 31, 2026.
## Amount
- 50% of the donation, or the amount distributed or transferred to you
- Nonrefundable with a FIVE-YEAR carryforward
- The credit must be claimed in the year the donation is made, the reservation letter is issued, or the credit is transferred to you
## Eligibility — three ways in
1. You made the donation and hold a certificate from IHDA or the City of Chicago
2. You received the credit on Schedule K-1-P from a partnership or S corporation
3. The credit was TRANSFERRED to you, which is allowed only to (a) the purchaser of land designated solely for affordable housing under the Act, or (b) another donor who has also made a Section 7.28 donation. Transfers follow 47 Ill. Adm. Code 355.309, and any portion already used against the transferor's tax cannot be transferred
## Notes
- Tax-exempt donors who cannot use the credit may still earn and transfer it — that is where most purchased credits originate
- Enter the transferor's FEIN in Column D of Schedule 1299-C when claiming a transferred credit
- The donation may ALSO be a federal charitable deduction; unlike Illinois Gives there is no requirement to forgo it
- Sunset: no new credits for donations in tax years ending after December 31, 2026 unless extended
## Required Information
- The IHDA or City of Chicago certificate or reservation letter
- Donation amount and date
- For transfers: the written notice of transfer and the transferor's FEIN
- Any Schedule K-1-P
## Questions
- Did you donate to an affordable housing project and receive an IHDA or City of Chicago certificate?
- Did you buy or receive a transferred credit, and do you have the transfer documentation?
- Did the credit come through a partnership or S corporation?
## Common Errors
- Claiming 50% of a donation to a housing charity that has no IHDA or Chicago certificate
- Claiming in the wrong year — the credit is tied to the certificate or transfer year, not necessarily the cash payment
- Omitting the transfer documentation or the transferor's FEIN
- Missing the five-year carryforward when the credit exceeds tax
