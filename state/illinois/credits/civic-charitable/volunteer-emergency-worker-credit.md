# Volunteer Emergency Worker Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable $500 credit for volunteer firefighters and volunteer emergency services members. Unusual in two ways: it must be APPLIED FOR in advance and awarded by certificate, and it is the only Schedule ICR credit with NO INCOME LIMIT. First available for tax year 2023.
## Amount
- $500 per certificate holder
- $1,000 on a joint return where both spouses hold certificates
- Nonrefundable, no carryforward. Combined with the property tax and education expense credits, cannot exceed tax due
- The $500,000 / $250,000 AGI cliff that applies to the other two Schedule ICR credits does NOT apply here
## The certificate gate
No certificate, no credit. IDOR opens an online application each year and awards certificates in FEBRUARY to the first 10,000 qualified applicants, subject to a statewide cap of $5,000,000 per calendar year. Applications are first come, first served. A volunteer who qualifies in every respect but did not apply, or applied after the cap was hit, gets nothing for that year.
## Who is a volunteer emergency worker
Either:
- A member, other than on a full-time career basis, of a fire department, fire protection district, or fire protection association with a Fire Department Identification Number from the Office of the State Fire Marshal, who does not serve full-time for any other fire department or government entity, OR
- A volunteer member of a county or municipal emergency services agency who volunteered at least 100 hours in the preceding calendar year and is registered with IEMA and the Office of Homeland Security
## Eligibility
- Served as a volunteer emergency worker for at least NINE MONTHS of the taxable year
- Received no more than $5,000 in compensation for the volunteer service during the year
- Listed by the fire chief (to OSFM) or volunteer coordinator (to IEMA/OHS) as meeting the above
- Submitted a complete online application to IDOR
- Among the first 10,000 qualified applicants
## Notes
- The certificate number, not the certificate itself, is entered on Schedule ICR; keep the certificate in case IDOR asks
- A stipend or per-call payment is "compensation" for the $5,000 test
- Career firefighters who also volunteer elsewhere are excluded by the definition
## Required Information
- Certificate number for each spouse
- Confirmation of nine months of service and compensation under $5,000
- Whether a spouse also holds a certificate
## Questions
- Did you apply to IDOR and receive a 2026 Volunteer Emergency Worker Credit Certificate?
- Did you serve at least nine months of the year?
- Were you paid more than $5,000 for the volunteer work?
- Does your spouse also hold a certificate?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit without a certificate because you clearly qualify
- Missing the application window and the 10,000-applicant cap
- Applying the AGI cliff to this credit — it does not apply
- Expecting a refund or carryforward from a nonrefundable, no-carryforward credit
- Counting service by a career firefighter who also volunteers
## Prompt
- I am a volunteer firefighter in Illinois, is there a tax credit?
- How do I get the Illinois volunteer emergency worker certificate?
- My spouse and I both volunteer for the fire district.
