# Educator Credits
## Description
Credits for people who WORK in Illinois K-12 schools, as opposed to parents who pay them (family-dependent/k-12-education-expense-credit.md). Illinois has one, and it is more generous than the federal educator expense deduction it resembles.
## Credits in this folder
- K-12 Instructional Materials and Supplies Credit (k-12-instructional-materials-credit.md) — Schedule 1299-C, Step 2 worksheet, code 5740 → Form IL-1040, Line 17. Nonrefundable, five-year carryforward
## Federal comparison
The federal educator expense DEDUCTION is $350 for 2026 ($700 joint, both educators; $300 in 2025) and reduces taxable income. The Illinois CREDIT is up to $500 ($1,000 joint, both educators) and reduces tax dollar for dollar. At Illinois's 4.95% rate the state credit is worth far more than the state's share of the federal deduction would be. Both can be claimed on the same expenses; there is no coordination rule.
