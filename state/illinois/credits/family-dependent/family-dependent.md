# Family and Dependent Credits
## Description
Credits based on having children, paying for their schooling, or adopting them. The shape of this category is DIFFERENT from New York's: Illinois has no child care credit at all, its child tax credit is a percentage of the earned income credit rather than a per-child dollar amount, and it DOES have a state adoption credit, which New York lacks.
## Credits in this folder
- Illinois Child Tax Credit (child-tax-credit.md) — Schedule IL-E/EITC, Step 5 → Form IL-1040, Line 30. Refundable
- K-12 Education Expense Credit (k-12-education-expense-credit.md) — Schedule ICR, Section B → Form IL-1040, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Adoption Credit (adoption-credit.md) — Schedule 1299-C, Step 2 worksheet, code 5780 → Form IL-1040, Line 17. Nonrefundable, five-year carryforward
## What Illinois does NOT have
- No child and dependent care credit. Daycare and preschool expenses produce nothing on the Illinois return. The only dependent-care credit in the Illinois code (code 2200) is for MANUFACTURERS running an on-site facility
- No per-child credit independent of earnings. A family with children but no earned income gets no Illinois child tax credit because it gets no EITC
## 2026 parameters
- Child Tax Credit: 40% of the Illinois EITC, maximum about $658 (three or more children)
- K-12 Education Expense Credit: 25% of expenses over $250, cap $750, AGI cliff $500,000 / $250,000
- Adoption Credit: $2,000, or $5,000 for an Illinois child at least one year old
- Nothing changed from 2025 except the federal EITC inflation adjustments flowing into the child credit
## What changed for 2025 (prior year)
- The Child Tax Credit DOUBLED from 20% to 40% of the Illinois EITC and was made permanent (35 ILCS 5/244, exempt from the sunset rule)
- The Adoption Credit and Student-Assistance Contribution credit were extended through tax years ending on or before December 31, 2029
## The federal linkage
The Child Tax Credit is 40% of the Illinois EITC, which is 20% of the FEDERAL EITC. So the Illinois child credit is effectively 8% of the federal EITC, and every federal EITC inflation adjustment flows through with no change to Illinois law. The Adoption Credit starts from the federal adoption credit actually received on Form 8839, then applies Illinois caps.

## Questions
- Do you qualify for the Illinois earned income credit, and is any of your children under 12?
- Did you pay tuition, book fees, or lab fees to an Illinois K-12 school last year?
- Did you adopt, or pay adoption expenses for, a child under 18?
- Is your federal AGI above $500,000 joint or $250,000 otherwise?
