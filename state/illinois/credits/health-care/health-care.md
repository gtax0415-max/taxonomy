# Health and Care Credits
## Description
THIS CATEGORY IS EMPTY FOR ILLINOIS, and the emptiness is the information. Illinois has no long-term care insurance credit, no nursing home credit, no health insurance or premium credit, no caregiver credit, and no medical expense credit. The folder exists so that the taxonomy stays parallel with federal/ and state/new-york/, and so that a query about any of these topics lands on a definitive answer rather than a missing file.
## What a New York filer would look for and not find
| New York credit | Illinois equivalent |
|---|---|
| Long-term care insurance credit (IT-249) | None |
| Nursing home assessment credit (IT-258) | None |
| Automated external defibrillator credit | None |
| Organ donation credit | None on the income tax; Illinois offers paid organ donation leave, not a credit |
## The only health-adjacent items on an Illinois return
- Medical expenses are NOT deductible on the Illinois return at all — Illinois has no itemized deductions
- The Health Insurance Checkbox at Step 12, Line 42 of Form IL-1040 lets an uninsured filer ask to be contacted about Marketplace coverage; it carries no dollar value
- Medical debt relief received is subtracted from income at Step 3, Line 18 (added for tax year 2025, continues for 2026) — a subtraction, not a credit
- Marketplace coverage through Get Covered Illinois is subsidized by the FEDERAL premium tax credit only
## Related

- Business-side health credits on Schedule 1299-C: the Hospital credit (code 5620, for hospital OWNERS) and the Recovery and Mental Health credit (code 0180, for EMPLOYERS hiring people in recovery) — see business-investment/business-investment.md. Neither is available to an individual patient or caregiver
## Questions
