# Property-Based Credits
## Description
Credits tied to owning a home in Illinois. Illinois has ONE, the property tax credit, but it is the most widely claimed credit on the Illinois return and the one every homeowner should check. There is no New York state analogue on the income tax return — New York handles property tax relief through STAR and a separate real property tax credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Illinois Property Tax Credit (property-tax-credit.md) — Schedule ICR, Section A → Form IL-1040, Line 16. Nonrefundable
## What Illinois does NOT have
- No renter's credit. Renters get nothing on the income tax return for rent paid
- No senior freeze or homestead exemption on the INCOME tax — those are property tax bill exemptions administered by the county assessor and do not appear on Form IL-1040
## Related
- The credit is computed on the same Schedule ICR as the K-12 education expense credit and the volunteer emergency worker credit, and the three together cannot exceed tax due
- Property tax paid is also a federal itemized deduction subject to the SALT cap — see federal/deductions/
## Questions
- Did you own and live in an Illinois home during 2025 and pay its property tax bill during 2026?
- Is your federal AGI under $500,000 joint or $250,000 otherwise?

- I own a house in Illinois, is there a property tax credit?
- Can I get a credit for the rent I pay in Illinois?
