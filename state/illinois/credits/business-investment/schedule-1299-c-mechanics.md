# Schedule 1299-C Mechanics
## Description
Schedule 1299-C is not a credit; it is the LEDGER on which every line-17 credit is listed, ordered, applied, and carried. It must be filed in any year you EARN or CARRY a credit, even if none of it can be used against current tax. Getting the ordering wrong does not just misstate this year's credit — it corrupts the carryforward for every future year.
## The three steps
- Step 1: dividend subtractions (River Edge Redevelopment Zone and High Impact Business foreign trade zone dividends) → Schedule M, Line 24. Not credits; listed here because they share the form
- Step 2: worksheets for the three credits computed on the form itself — Research and Development (5340), K-12 Instructional Materials (5740), and Adoption (5780). Every other credit arrives as a certificate or K-1-P amount
- Step 3: the ledger, Lines 21 through 41, one line per credit per year earned
## Column A — Years left to carry, and the ordering rule
Enter how many years the credit can still be carried: 0, 2, 5, or 10 in the first year (the first digit of the credit code tells you), and one less each subsequent year. Lines MUST be listed in ascending Column A order:
1. All credits with 0 years left (no-carryforward credits and any credit in its final year), in ascending credit-code order
2. Then 1 year left, then 2, and so on up to 10
This forces credits to be applied in order of EARLIEST EXPIRATION, so nothing expires unused while a longer-lived credit is consumed. Within the same Column A value, order by credit code.
## Columns B through G — identifying and totaling
- B: four-digit credit code
- C: Illinois tax year ending when the credit was FIRST earned, as YYYY-MM. For a carried credit this is the ORIGINAL year, not the current one. For a K-1-P amount, use YOUR tax year end, not the entity's
- D: identifying number, FIRST YEAR ONLY — the COR certificate number for Illinois Gives, or the FEIN of the original earner for a transferred credit. Blank otherwise and blank in carryforward years
- E: credit earned this year (from Step 2 or a certificate) or carried in from Column J of last year's schedule
- F: distributive share from Schedule K-1-P or credit transferred to you THIS year only. Never populated in a carryforward year
- G: E + F
## Columns H through J — applying and carrying
- Line 20 is the tax available for credits (IL-1040 tax after the Schedule CR and Schedule ICR credits)
- H: running remainder. Line 21 H = Line 20 minus Line 21 G; each subsequent H = prior H minus this line's G. STOP writing when H hits zero or goes negative; leave later H cells blank
- I: credit applied. If H is zero or positive, I = G. If H is negative, I = G + H (the partial amount that fit). If H is blank, I = 0
- J: carryforward. If A is 0, J = 0 — the remainder is LOST. Otherwise J = G minus I
- Line 42: sum of Column I. Line 43: the lesser of Line 20 or Line 42 → IL-1040 Line 17
## Same credit, multiple years — the worked pattern from the instructions
A manufacturer with a Dependent Care Assistance credit (2200) earned $125 in 2024, $750 in 2025, and $400 in 2026 plus $175 distributed from a partnership in 2026 lists three lines: A=0, C=2024-12, E=125; A=1, C=2025-12, E=750; A=2, C=2026-12, E=400, F=175, G=575. The 2024 credit is applied first because it expires first. (The IDOR instructions illustrate the same pattern one year earlier.)
## Attachments
- Every DCEO, DNR, DHS, IDOR, IHDA, or City of Chicago certificate for credits earned this year
- Every Schedule K-1-P and K-1-T listing a credit — the entity's tax year ending must fall within your tax year
- Transfer notices for purchased credits (the new DCEO certificate, or the seller's written notice to IDOR)
- Federal Form 8839 if claiming Adoption
- Do NOT attach the Illinois Gives COR; retain it
## Recapture
If property behind an investment credit is disposed of, converted to personal use, or moved out of the zone within 48 months, or an agreement-based credit hits a recapture event, the credit actually USED in prior years is added back on Schedule 4255 → IL-1040 Line 13. See schedule-4255-recapture.md.
## Notes
- Round to whole dollars
- If you run out of lines, attach additional Schedules 1299-C but total only on the original
- If you receive several K-1-Ps for the SAME credit code, add them and enter one Column F total; if you hold several IDOR Returning Citizens certificates or Illinois Gives CORs, each goes on its OWN line
- Keep Schedules 1299-C and 1299-I permanently; next year's Column E and Column A come directly from this year's Column J and Column A
- Attach Schedule 1299-C to Form IL-1040-X when amending, checking the box at the top
## Required Information
- Prior-year Schedule 1299-C
- Every certificate, K-1-P, K-1-T, and transfer notice for the year
- Tax available for credits from IL-1040 after Lines 15 and 16
## Questions
- Do you have last year's Schedule 1299-C showing Column J carryforwards?
- Which credits did you earn this year, and from which certificates or K-1s?
- Are any credits in their final carryforward year?
## Common Errors
- Listing credits in the order received rather than by years-left-to-carry, so a credit expires unused
- Putting the entity's K-1-P year in Column C instead of your own tax year ending
- Populating Column F in a carryforward year
- Writing Column H entries after the remainder goes negative
- Carrying forward a Column A = 0 credit — it is gone
- Not filing the schedule in a year with no usable credit, which breaks the carryforward chain
