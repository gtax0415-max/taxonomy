# Schedule 4255 — Recapture of Investment and Additional Income Tax Credits
## Description
Not a credit but its reversal. When property behind an investment credit stops qualifying within 48 months, or an agreement-based credit hits a recapture trigger, the credit that was actually USED in prior years is added back to this year's tax on IL-1040 Line 13. Recapture is reported in the year the disqualification happens; you may NOT amend the earlier return to remove the property. For an individual the most likely paths in are an Angel Investment sold within three years and a Schedule K-1-P showing a partnership's recapture.
## Which credits recapture, and what triggers it
### Investment credits — Steps 1 through 5 (property becomes disqualified within 48 months of being placed in service)
| Credit | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Enterprise Zone / River Edge Redevelopment Zone Investment (5080) | Disposed of, converted to personal use, or moved outside the zone |
| High Impact Business Investment (5220) | Since 1997: relocating the entire facility in violation of a Section 18-183 property tax abatement agreement |
| REV Illinois Investment (5230) | Disposed of, converted to personal use, or moved off the REV project site |
| Replacement Tax Investment (Form IL-477) | Disposed of or moved out of Illinois; also converted to personal use or no longer primarily in manufacturing, retailing, coal, or fluorite mining |
| MICRO Investment (5830) | Disposed of, converted to personal use, or moved off the MICRO project site |
"Disposed of" includes sold, exchanged, traded in, abandoned, retired, destroyed by casualty, stolen, or GIFTED. Mortgaging or pledging the property is not a disposition unless it is converted to a non-qualifying use.
### Agreement-based credits — Step 7
| Credit | Trigger |
|---|---|
| Angel Investment (5460) | Disposed of the investment within THREE YEARS of making it (other than a permitted sale to a non-related member), or the business failed the Illinois employment threshold through three years after the last certificate |
| EDGE (5300) and New Construction EDGE (5320) | Ceased operations at the project location intending to leave Illinois |
| Historic Preservation (1030) | Failed to place or keep the rehabilitated structure in service, demolished or altered it against the plan, disposed of it, or used it inconsistently with the program |
| New Markets Development (5500) | Received a final recapture order from IDOR |
| REV Illinois (5240), REV Construction Jobs (5250), MICRO Illinois (5840), MICRO Construction Jobs (5850) | Ceased operations at the project location intending to leave Illinois |
## Amount
Only the credit ACTUALLY USED in prior years is recaptured — unused carryforward simply disappears. For investment credits Step 6 does this by (a) recomputing the credit on the disqualified property's basis at the original rate, (b) comparing it with the total credit originally computed on ALL property that year and the tax it was applied against (including a carryforward year), and (c) limiting the recapture to the used portion. Step 7 credits are recaptured at the full amount claimed.
## Mechanics
- One Schedule 4255 PER TAX YEAR in which the now-disqualified property was placed in service, earliest year first; each schedule reports only that year's property. Line 22 of each carries to Line 18 of the next
- Column A is the placed-in-service date (all within the schedule's year); Column B the disqualification date (all within the current year); Columns C and E must match the description and basis used when the credit was claimed
- Rates: Enterprise Zone .005; River Edge the percentage used on the original Schedule 1299; HIB, REV, and MICRO printed on the form; Replacement Tax .005 plus any employment-increase add-on from the original IL-477
- Step 7 is completed only on the LAST Schedule 4255 if several are attached; all agreement-based recapture from all years goes there
- Partnerships and S corporations report the partners' and shareholders' shares on Schedule K-1-P Lines 54a-54e; the partner enters K-1-P amounts on Line 19 (investment credits) and Line 32 (agreement credits) and pays the recapture on their own return
- Total recapture is included in IL-1040 Line 13. Because it is added to tax BEFORE Lines 15-17, the current year's credits, including new Schedule 1299-C credits, can offset it
- Round to whole dollars; attach every Schedule 4255 and any K-1-P
## Notes
- The 48-month clock runs from the placed-in-service date, not the tax year end
- A casualty loss or theft is a disposition and triggers recapture even though the taxpayer received nothing
- Recapture of Replacement Tax Investment credit (Column D) lands on the entity's replacement tax lines, not on IL-1040; only Columns A, B, C, and E flow to Line 13
- If both Step 6 and Step 7 apply, the two totals are added before entry on Line 13
## Required Information
- Original Schedule 1299 and return for the placed-in-service year: description, basis, rate, total credit, tax it offset
- Disqualification date and reason
- For Angel Investment: investment date, certificate dates, sale date and buyer relationship
- K-1-P Lines 54a-54e received
## Questions
- Did you sell, gift, scrap, or move any property you previously claimed an Illinois investment credit on, and was it within four years of putting it in service?
- Did you sell an angel investment within three years of making it?
- Did a partnership or S corporation send you a K-1-P with amounts on Lines 54a through 54e?
- How much of the original credit was actually applied against tax, and in which years?
## Common Errors
- Amending the prior-year return to drop the property instead of filing Schedule 4255 in the disqualification year
- Recapturing the full credit computed rather than the portion actually used
- Combining property from different placed-in-service years on one Schedule 4255
- Forgetting that a gift or a casualty loss is a disposition
- Missing recapture reported on a K-1-P
- Completing Step 7 on every attached schedule instead of only the last
