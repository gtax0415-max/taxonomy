# Business and Investment Credits (Schedule 1299-C)
## Description
Every nonrefundable credit that is not on Schedule ICR or Schedule CR flows through Schedule 1299-C to Form IL-1040, Line 17. Most are earned by a business and reach an individual either directly (sole proprietors, investors, donors) or as a DISTRIBUTIVE SHARE on Schedule K-1-P from a partnership or S corporation. Nearly all require a CERTIFICATE from a state agency before they can be claimed. The three individual-only credits on the same schedule — Adoption (5780), K-12 Instructional Materials (5740), and Illinois Gives (5670) — are filed in family-dependent/, educator/, and civic-charitable/ respectively.

## Mutual exclusions worth knowing
- REV Investment, MICRO Investment, and AIM each bar the Replacement Tax Investment, Enterprise Zone Investment, and High Impact Business Investment credits on the same property
- Dependent Care Assistance Program (2200) and the corporate Employee Child Care 5% credit cannot both be claimed
- High Impact Business Investment cannot be claimed on property eligible for Enterprise Zone Investment
