# Other Taxes — IL-1040 Step 7
## Description
Three amounts that are ADDED to the income tax after the nonrefundable credits, on Lines 20-22, and folded into total tax on Line 24. None of them is an income tax on income. One is money you withheld from someone else (household employees), one is a sales tax you owe as a buyer (use tax), and one is a surcharge that only applies to a handful of asset sales by cannabis and gaming licensees. Line 21 is the one that matters to everyone: it may NOT be left blank.

## Where they land
IL-1040 Line 14 (tax after recapture) − Lines 15-17 (credits) = Line 19 (tax after nonrefundable credits). Then Line 23 = Lines 20 + 21 + 22, and Line 24 = Line 19 + Line 23 = TOTAL TAX. Because they come after the credits, Schedule CR, ICR, and 1299-C credits can NEVER offset use tax, household withholding, or a surcharge. Refundable credits (EITC, Child Tax Credit) and payments on Lines 25-30 can.
## 2026 parameters
- Use tax rates 6.25% general merchandise / 1% qualifying food, non-prescription drugs, medical appliances — unchanged
- ST-44 threshold $600 ($1,200 joint) — unchanged
- UT Table by AGI — same brackets and amounts as 2025; 0.05% of AGI above $100,000
- Household withholding rate follows the 4.95% rate and the $2,925 allowance in Booklet IL-700-T for 2026
- Gaming licensee surcharge: tax years beginning 2019 through 2027 — active for 2026, sunsets after 2027
- Medical cannabis surcharge: for each tax year the Compassionate Use of Medical Cannabis Program is in effect — the program was made permanent in 2019, so no sunset
