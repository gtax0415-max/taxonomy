# Child Deduction
## Description
North Carolina's replacement for a child tax credit. A deduction per qualifying child, sliding down by federal AGI and filing status. Only children for whom you were ALLOWED the federal child tax credit under IRC Section 24 count.
## Amounts per child (2026 — unchanged; table verified against G.S. 105-153.5(a1))
| Per child | MFJ / Qualifying surviving spouse | Head of household | Single / MFS |
|---|---|---|---|
| $3,000 | Up to $40,000 | Up to $30,000 | Up to $20,000 |
| $2,500 | Over $40,000 - $60,000 | Over $30,000 - $45,000 | Over $20,000 - $30,000 |
| $2,000 | Over $60,000 - $80,000 | Over $45,000 - $60,000 | Over $30,000 - $40,000 |
| $1,500 | Over $80,000 - $100,000 | Over $60,000 - $75,000 | Over $40,000 - $50,000 |
| $1,000 | Over $100,000 - $120,000 | Over $75,000 - $90,000 | Over $50,000 - $60,000 |
| $500 | Over $120,000 - $140,000 | Over $90,000 - $105,000 | Over $60,000 - $70,000 |
| $0 | Over $140,000 | Over $105,000 | Over $70,000 |
## No child tax credit
Bills to restore a refundable North Carolina child tax credit (Senate Bill 641, House Bill 628) were introduced in 2025 but not enacted. For 2026 the only child benefit is this deduction.
## What it is worth
At 3.99%, the $3,000 maximum saves about $120 per child. A family with no North Carolina tax gets nothing — it is not refundable.
## The cliff structure
The table has no phase-in: one dollar over a threshold drops the deduction by $500 per child. For a married couple with three children at $80,000 versus $80,001 of AGI, North Carolina taxable income rises $1,500.
## The federal linkage
- The child must have been allowed the federal child tax credit — under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, that requires an SSN valid for work for the child and at least one parent
- Children who get only the federal $500 credit for other dependents do NOT count
- With conformity updated to July 5, 2025, the post-OBBBA Section 24 rules govern
## Required Information
- Number of children on federal Schedule 8812 allowed the child tax credit
- Federal AGI and filing status
## Questions
- How many children received the federal child tax credit?
- What is your federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Counting dependents who got only the credit for other dependents
- Using the wrong filing status row
- Treating it as a credit
