# North Carolina Deductions
## Description
After Schedule S adjustments, two deductions reduce income: the CHILD DEDUCTION (Line 10b) and EITHER the NC standard deduction OR NC itemized deductions (Line 11). There are no personal exemptions and no extra standard deduction for age 65 or blindness.
THE HEADLINE FOR 2026: North Carolina did NOT adopt any of the new federal below-the-line deductions (tips, overtime, car loan interest, seniors) and did NOT raise its standard deduction, even after updating its IRC conformity date to July 5, 2025. A new gambling loss itemized deduction does apply.
