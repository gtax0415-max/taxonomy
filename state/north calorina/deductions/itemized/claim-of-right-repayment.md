# Repayment of Claim of Right Income
## Description
Deduction for repaying income taxed in an earlier year when it appeared you had an unrestricted right to it, to the extent not already deducted in arriving at federal AGI.
## Amount for 2026 (rewritten by S.L. 2026-31, effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026)
- Repayment OVER $3,000: deduct the full repayment
- Repayment of $3,000 OR LESS: deduct the repayment LESS (i) the IRC 67(a) floor (2% of AGI) MINUS (ii) all other items deductible under IRC 67(b), with the reduction never more than the 2% floor. In other words, other miscellaneous itemized items absorb the 2% floor first. (The 2025 worksheet simply subtracted 2% of AGI.)
- NO North Carolina deduction if you computed federal tax for the repayment year under the IRC 1341(a)(5) credit method
- The repaid amount is measured by income as modified by North Carolina (G.S. 105-153.5 and 105-153.6), not just federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you repay wages, benefits, or other income you reported in an earlier year?
- Was the repayment over $3,000?
## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 flat 2%-of-AGI worksheet on a 2026 return
- Claiming the North Carolina deduction after using the federal IRC 1341 credit method
## Prompt
- I had to pay back an overpayment of unemployment benefits.
