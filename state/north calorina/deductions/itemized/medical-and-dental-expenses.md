# Medical and Dental Expenses
## Description
Medical and dental expenses allowable under IRC 213, deductible to the extent they exceed 7.5% of federal AGI.
## Computation
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 7a | Medical and dental expenses allowable under IRC 213 |
| 7b | Federal AGI from D-400, Line 6 (may be negative) |
| 7c | Line 7b × 7.5% (zero if negative) |
| 7d | Line 7a − Line 7c, not below zero |
## Notes
- Exclude items not included in federal AGI, such as employer-paid premiums or pre-tax premiums
- The floor uses FEDERAL AGI, not North Carolina income
## Questions
- What did you pay out of pocket for medical care, premiums, and long-term care?
## Common Errors
- Including pre-tax or employer-paid premiums
- Using North Carolina taxable income for the floor

- Can I deduct medical expenses on my North Carolina return?
