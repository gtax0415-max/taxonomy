# Charitable Contributions
## Description
The amount allowed as a charitable deduction under IRC 170. No separate North Carolina cap beyond the federal percentage limits.
## 2026 federal changes that may flow through
With conformity at July 5, 2025, the post-OBBBA IRC 170 applies, including the new floor for itemizers beginning in 2026 (contributions deductible only above 0.5% of the contribution base). Confirm on the 2026 Schedule A instructions how NCDOR applies the floor. The federal NON-ITEMIZER deduction ($1,000 / $2,000 MFJ) is not an itemized deduction and does NOT carry to North Carolina.
## Notes
- A donation that is the basis for an allocated NC Conservation Tax Credit (credits/property-conservation/) may NOT also be deducted here
- You can itemize for North Carolina even if you took the federal standard deduction — compute the IRC 170 amount as if itemizing federally
- Carryovers of excess contributions follow federal rules
## Questions
- How much did you give, in cash and property?
- Do you have written acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more?
## Common Errors
- Omitting charity because you did not itemize federally
- Claiming the federal non-itemizer charitable deduction on the North Carolina standard deduction
