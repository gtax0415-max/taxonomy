# North Carolina Itemized Deductions (Schedule A)
## Description
North Carolina itemized deductions are a SHORT LIST, not the federal Schedule A. You may itemize for North Carolina even if you took the federal standard deduction, and vice versa. State and local income and sales taxes are NOT deductible.
## Items in this folder
- Mortgage Interest and Real Estate Taxes (mortgage-interest-and-property-tax.md) — Lines 1-5, $20,000 combined cap
- Charitable Contributions (charitable-contributions.md) — Line 6
- Medical and Dental Expenses (medical-and-dental-expenses.md) — Lines 7a-7d
- Repayment of Claim of Right Income (claim-of-right-repayment.md) — Line 8

## Not deductible in North Carolina
State and local income taxes, sales taxes, personal property taxes, investment interest, casualty losses (other than the special timber loss for 2023/2024), and miscellaneous deductions.
## When to itemize
Itemize only if Schedule A, Line 10 exceeds the standard deduction ($12,750 single / $19,125 HOH / $25,500 MFJ). With mortgage interest plus property tax capped at $20,000, a joint filer needs meaningful charity or medical expense on top to beat $25,500.
## Married filing separately
If one spouse itemizes, the other's standard deduction is zero. The $20,000 mortgage/property tax cap applies to BOTH spouses combined.
