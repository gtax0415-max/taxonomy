# Estimated Tax and Underpayment Interest
## Who must pay
Estimated tax is required if the tax on the return, minus North Carolina withholding and credits, is $1,000 OR MORE. Pay with Form NC-40 or online.
## 2026 installment dates
| Installment | Due |
|---|---|
| 1 | April 15, 2026 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 |
## Line 26e — interest on underpayment
- Compute on Form D-422; do NOT attach it (keep it)
- Interest is computed per installment, so it can be owed even when a refund is due
- No interest if there was no 2025 tax liability, or if 2026 tax minus credits and withholding is under $1,000
- On a refund return, the interest reduces the overpayment (Line 28)
## Exceptions — enter code "E" in the box
- Farmers and fishermen (at least two-thirds of gross income from farming or fishing) who file and pay in full by March 1, 2027
- Annualized income computed on Form D-422A
## Line 29 — applying the 2026 overpayment to 2027
Elect on Line 29. The election cannot be changed after filing. Because the last 2027 estimated payment is due January 15, 2028, the 2026 return must be filed by then to apply the overpayment.
## Common Errors
- Attaching D-422
- Assuming a refund means no underpayment interest
