# Due Dates and Extensions
## 2026 return
| Item | Date |
|---|---|
| Calendar-year 2026 return and payment | April 15, 2027 |
| Fiscal year | 15th day of the 4th month after year end |
| Weekend or holiday | Next business day |
| Extended due date | October 15, 2027 |
## Automatic extension with a federal extension
If you received an automatic federal extension, fill in the Federal Extension circle — that IS the North Carolina extension. Without the circle, the return is treated as late.
## Form D-410
Without a federal extension, file Form D-410 by the original due date.
## An extension to file is NOT an extension to pay
Interest runs from April 15. The failure-to-pay penalty is avoided only if at least 90% of the tax was paid by the original due date and the rest (with interest) is paid by the extended date.
## Out of the Country
Fill in the Out of Country circle if you (or a joint-filing spouse) were out of the country on the due date and a U.S. citizen or resident. This gives an AUTOMATIC FOUR-MONTH extension to file AND pay, but interest still accrues from the original due date. "Out of the country" means living and working outside the U.S. and Puerto Rico, or military service outside them.
## Nonresident aliens
File when the federal return is due.
## Common Errors
- Getting a federal extension but not filling in the Federal Extension circle
- Treating the extension as an extension to pay
