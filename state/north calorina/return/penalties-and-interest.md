# Tax Due, Penalties, and Interest
## Line 26a — tax due
If Line 25 is less than Line 19, subtract Line 25 from Line 19. If Line 25 is negative (amended returns), add Lines 19 and 25.
## Line 26b — penalties
| Penalty | Amount |
|---|---|
| Failure to file (no valid extension) | 5% of net tax per month or part month, maximum 25% |
| Failure to pay | 5% of net tax due, regardless of how late |
| Extension relief | No automatic late-payment penalty if 90% was paid by the due date and the balance is paid by the extended date |
| Amended return | No late-payment penalty if the amount shown is paid with the return |
| Proposed assessment | Late-payment penalty if not paid within 45 days of the notice |
| Frivolous return | Up to $2,000 (raised from $500 by S.L. 2026-31) |
| Payment in wrong form | 5% of the tax, minimum $1, maximum $1,000 |
| Other | Negligence, fraud; criminal penalties for willful failure or evasion |
Enter the total of all penalties on Line 26b. Waivers are available under NCDOR's penalty waiver policy.
## Line 26c — interest
Charged from the original due date to payment, even with an extension. Set every six months by the Secretary of Revenue (same rate for underpayments and overpayments). 7% per year for ALL of 2026 (January 1 - June 30 and July 1 - December 31, 2026). The 7% rate has applied since January 1, 2025; check ncdor.gov/interest for 2027 periods.
## Line 26d
Line 26b + Line 26c.
## Line 27 — amount due
Lines 26a + 26d + 26e. Pay online by bank draft (free) or Visa/Mastercard ($2 per $100 fee), or by check or money order drawn on a U.S. bank in U.S. dollars with a personalized Form D-400V. Write "2026 D-400", name, address, and last four SSN digits on the check. Mail to PO Box 25000, Raleigh, NC 27640-0640.
## Collection assistance fee
20% of tax, penalty, and interest unpaid 60 days after a Notice of Collection, unless paid in full or under an installment agreement first.
