# Tax Withheld and Other Payments
## Lines 20a and 20b — North Carolina withholding
- 20a: your North Carolina withholding; 20b: spouse's on a joint return
- Include ONLY North Carolina withholding — never another state's
- Attach every W-2, 1099, and statement showing North Carolina withholding. Software-generated W-2s/1099s cannot verify withholding
## Line 21 — other payments
| Line | Payment |
|---|---|
| 21a | 2026 estimated tax, including any 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 |
| 21b | Paid with Form D-410 extension |
| 21c | Nonresident partner's share of North Carolina tax paid by the partnership — attach NC K-1 (D-403) |
| 21d | Nonresident shareholder's share of North Carolina tax paid by the S corporation — attach NC K-1 (CD-401S) |
Tax paid by a TAXED PTE under the pass-through entity election is not a Line 21c/21d payment — that income is instead deducted on Schedule S, Line 38a.
## Line 23
Lines 20a through 22 (Line 22 is for amended returns).
## Common Errors
- Including other states' withholding
- Claiming 21c/21d without the NC K-1
- Claiming a taxed-PTE entity payment as a credit
