# Amended Returns (Schedule AM)
## When you must amend
- You made an error or received new information
- You need to report an NC net operating loss
- The IRS changed your federal return — amend within SIX MONTHS of receiving the IRS report
- You voluntarily amended federally and it INCREASES North Carolina tax — amend within six months
- A federal amendment that DECREASES North Carolina tax — amend within the refund statute of limitations
## Consequences of not reporting federal changes
NCDOR may assess within three years of receiving the IRS report (or of the federal amended filing), and you forfeit any refund the change would have produced.
## How to amend
1. Fill in the Amended Return circle on the D-400
2. Attach Schedule AM to the FRONT, with the year, reason circles, and a detailed explanation
3. Show corrected amounts on Lines 1-21
4. Line 22: tax paid with the original return plus later payments (not penalties or interest)
5. Line 24: overpayment on the original return (as adjusted), including amounts applied to estimates or donated; not refund interest
6. Line 25: Line 23 − Line 24; if negative, see penalties-and-interest.md (Line 26a)
7. Re-file every schedule filed originally (S, A, PN, PN-1, D-400TC) even if unchanged
8. Pay with Form D-400V Amended or online
## Lines 29-32 on an amended return
Do not repeat designations already taken on the original. A new overpayment can go to the next year's estimates only if the amendment is filed by January 15 of the following year.
## Statute of limitations
- Refund: later of three years from the due date (or extended due date) or two years from payment. Longer periods apply to federal determinations, worthless debts or securities, capital loss and NOL carrybacks, and contingent events
- Additional tax: generally within three years of the later of filing or the due date
## 2026 legislation note
Session Laws 2026-31 and 2026-41 changed 2022-2025 law retroactively (R&E add-back, gambling loss itemized deduction, Helene timber loss, property tax limit). Taxpayers affected should amend those years; 2026 returns follow the 2026 instructions.
