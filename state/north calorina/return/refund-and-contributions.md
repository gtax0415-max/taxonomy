# Overpayment, Donations, and Refund
## Line 28 — overpayment
If Line 25 exceeds Line 19, subtract Line 19 (and any Line 26e interest) from Line 25.
## Designations from the overpayment (irrevocable after filing)
| Line | Fund |
|---|---|
| 29 | Applied to 2027 estimated tax (see estimated-tax.md) |
| 30 | N.C. Nongame and Endangered Wildlife Fund |
| 31 | N.C. Education Endowment Fund (K-12 public schools) |
| 32 | N.C. Breast and Cervical Cancer Control Program |
Line 33 = Lines 29 through 32.
## Contributing without a refund
- Education Endowment Fund: enclose Form NC-EDU with a payment (one check may cover tax and contribution with D-400V and NC-EDU), or mail to NC DPI
- Wildlife Fund: donate online or mail to the N.C. Wildlife Resources Commission
- BCCCP: mail to the NC Cancer Prevention and Control Branch, payable to NC DHHS
## Line 34 — refund
Line 28 minus Line 33. Refunds under $1.00 are issued only on request. Direct deposit requires e-filing. Paper refund returns go to PO Box R, Raleigh, NC 27634-0001.
## Refund setoff
Refunds can be applied to debts owed to state or local agencies, or claimed by the IRS for federal tax debts. Setoffs are not subject to Departmental review.
