# Return Header, Deceased Taxpayers, and Signature
## Header items
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Tax period | Calendar year 2026, or the fiscal year beginning in 2026 |
| SSNs | Full nine digits for you and a joint-filing spouse — required by 17 NCAC 06B .0104 |
| Name and address | Capital letters; include apartment number |
| County | First five letters of the county where you lived on December 31, 2026 (may differ from mailing county) |
| Veteran Information | Voluntary; used only for statistics on veterans filing in North Carolina |
| Amended Return circle | See amended-returns.md |
| Out of Country / Federal Extension | See due-dates-and-extensions.md |
## Deceased taxpayer
| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| Personal representative filing for an unmarried decedent, or a separate return | Decedent's name with your address; fill in the circle; enter date of death; sign; attach the certificate of appointment |
| Surviving spouse filing jointly | Both names; enter date of death; sign |
| Surviving spouse filing separately for the decedent | Decedent's name with your address; enter date of death; sign |
A refund on a return filed by someone other than a surviving spouse or personal representative goes to the Clerk of Superior Court in the decedent's county. An estate with North Carolina income may also need Form D-407.
## Signature
- The return is not valid unless signed; both spouses sign a joint return
- Include a contact phone number, which can speed processing
- Joint and several liability applies to joint returns; relief follows federal innocent spouse relief under IRC 6015
## Paid preparer authorization
Checking the box lets NCDOR contact the preparer to supply missing information, check processing or refund status, receive notices on request, and respond to proposed notices. It does NOT let the preparer receive the refund, bind you, or represent you in audits or Departmental review — that needs a power of attorney. The preparer signs and enters an FEIN, SSN, or PTIN.
## Paper return rules
- Do not send photocopies or prior-year forms
- Do not mix form versions (for example, a software D-400 with a web-fill D-400TC)
- Whole dollars, no commas or dollar signs; negative amounts shown with the circle (web) or a minus sign (web-fill); leave unused lines blank
