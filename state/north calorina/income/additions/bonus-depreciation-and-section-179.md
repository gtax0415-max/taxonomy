# Bonus Depreciation and Section 179 Expense
## Description
North Carolina does NOT follow federal bonus depreciation or the federal Section 179 limits. The difference is added back now and recovered over the next five years. NCDOR confirmed in September 2026 that the bonus depreciation decoupling is unchanged by the 2026 conformity update — so the federal 100% bonus depreciation restored by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act is still mostly added back.
## Amount — bonus depreciation
- ADD BACK 85% of the bonus depreciation deducted federally (Line 3)
- DEDUCT 20% of that add-back in each of the FIVE taxable years following the add-back year
## Amount — Section 179
- North Carolina limits: $25,000 dollar limit and $200,000 investment limit
- ADD BACK 85% of the difference between the federal Section 179 deduction and the deduction computed with North Carolina limits (Line 4)
- DEDUCT 20% of the add-back in each of the next five years
Federal limits for 2026 (Rev. Proc. 2025-32) are $2,560,000, phasing out above $4,090,000 of property placed in service, so the gap is enormous for any business that uses Section 179 above $25,000. North Carolina's $25,000 / $200,000 limits are not indexed.
## 2026 recovery lines
On the 2026 return, the recovery lines cover add-backs made on the 2021 through 2025 returns (on the 2025 form these were Lines 23a-23e for 2020-2024). Enter each year's 20% separately.
## Example
A sole proprietor takes $100,000 of federal bonus depreciation in 2026. North Carolina add-back: $85,000. North Carolina deductions: $17,000 a year on the 2027 through 2031 returns.
## Notes
- Only the taxpayer who added back may take the recovery
- On disposal, the basis difference is handled through Lines 6 and 28 (see basis-and-other-additions.md)
- Line 22 (bonus asset basis) applies when an asset with remaining bonus deductions transfers with carryover basis
## Required Information
- Federal bonus depreciation and Section 179 claimed, including amounts from K-1s
- Section 179 recomputed with North Carolina limits
- Add-back history for the prior five years
## Questions
- Did you or any pass-through entity claim bonus depreciation or Section 179 in 2026?
- Do you have add-backs from 2021-2025 still being recovered?
## Common Errors
- Adding back 100% instead of 85%
- Forgetting the 20% recoveries in later years
- Missing pass-through bonus depreciation reported on K-1s
- Assuming the 2026 conformity update adopted federal bonus depreciation
## Prompt
- I took bonus depreciation on my rental, what do I do in North Carolina?
- What is the North Carolina Section 179 limit?
