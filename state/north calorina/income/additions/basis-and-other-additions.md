# Basis Differences and Other Additions
## Opportunity Zone gains (Lines 2 and 25)
North Carolina does not follow the IRC 1400Z-2 deferral. Gain reinvested in a qualified Opportunity Fund is taxed by North Carolina in the year of sale (Line 2). When the deferred gain is later recognized federally, deduct it to the extent North Carolina already taxed it (Line 25). Opportunity Zone rules changed federally under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act; the North Carolina add-back applies to any new deferral as well unless the 2026 instructions say otherwise.
## Basis differences on disposition (Lines 6 and 28)
Because North Carolina depreciation differs (85% bonus and Section 179 add-backs), North Carolina basis is usually HIGHER. On sale:
- If federal basis exceeds state basis, add the difference (Line 6)
- If state basis exceeds federal basis, deduct the difference (Line 28) — the common case
## Expenses allocable to exempt income (Line 12)
Add back expenses deducted in arriving at AGI that relate to income wholly excluded federally or wholly exempt from North Carolina tax.
