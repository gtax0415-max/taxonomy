# Education-Related Additions
## 529 recapture (Line 9)
North Carolina allowed a deduction for NC 529 contributions from 2006 through 2013. If you took that deduction and withdraw funds in 2026 for a purpose not allowed under IRC 529, add back the amount previously deducted. North Carolina has NO current 529 deduction.
## Employer student loan payments (Line 11)
North Carolina has not followed the IRC 127(c) expansion letting employers pay up to $5,250 of an employee's student loans tax-free. The excluded amount is added back. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act made the federal rule permanent; check the 2026 Schedule S to confirm the add-back continues after the July 5, 2025 conformity update.
## Questions
- Did you withdraw from an older NC 529 account for a non-education purpose?
- Did your employer pay your student loans?
## Common Errors
- Expecting a North Carolina deduction for 529 contributions
- Missing employer loan payments excluded on the W-2
## Prompt
- Does North Carolina give a deduction for 529 contributions?
- My employer pays my student loans.
