# Additions to Federal AGI (Schedule S, Part A)
## Description
Items NOT in federal AGI that North Carolina taxes. Most exist because North Carolina decouples from a federal provision. If any addition applies, BOTH PAGES of Schedule S must be attached.
## Items in this folder
- Interest on Other States' Obligations (other-state-bond-interest.md) — Line 1
- Bonus Depreciation and Section 179 (bonus-depreciation-and-section-179.md) — Lines 3 and 4, with recoveries on Lines 23 and 24
- Domestic Research and Experimental Expenditures (domestic-research-expenditures.md) — NEW, 80% add-back; reported on the reserved line (Line 15 on the 2025 form)
- Net Operating Losses (../net-operating-loss/net-operating-loss.md) — federal NOL add-back Line 7, NC NOL deduction Line 39; full Form NC-NOL treatment in its own folder

## The 85% / 20% pattern
North Carolina's decoupling adjustments for depreciation follow one pattern: add back 85% of the federal deduction now, then deduct 20% of that add-back in each of the next five years. The new R&E adjustment uses a different pattern: 80% add-back, recovered 25% a year over four years. Track every add-back by year, because the recovery is only allowed to the taxpayer who made it.
