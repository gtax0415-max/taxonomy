# Domestic Research and Experimental Expenditures (NEW)
## Description
NEW decoupling created by Session Law 2026-31 (G.S. 105-153.6A). The One Big Beautiful Bill Act restored immediate federal expensing of domestic R&E costs under IRC 174A(a). North Carolina did NOT adopt it. Individuals who expensed domestic R&E federally must add most of it back.
## Amount
- ADD BACK 80% of the domestic R&E expenditures deducted under IRC 174A(a) for the year
- DEDUCT 25% of the add-back in each of the FOUR following taxable years
## Example
$100,000 deducted federally in 2026 → $80,000 added back in 2026 → $20,000 deducted on each of the 2027-2030 returns.
## Effective dates
- Tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, generally
- Tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2022, for taxpayers who elected federal retroactive application of 174A for 2022-2024 — those years must be amended
## Notes
- Foreign (non-domestic) R&E is NOT subject to this adjustment
- Deductions of previously unamortized domestic R&E costs under the OBBBA transition rule ARE subject to it
- Partnerships and S corporations do not make the adjustment themselves; they pass through the information and each owner adjusts on their own return
- NCDOR says the 2026 instructions will show where to report; for 2023-2025 the add-back goes on the reserved Schedule S line with "Domestic R&E" written in
## Required Information
- Domestic R&E deducted under 174A(a), by year
- Pass-through R&E information
- Prior add-backs and recoveries taken
## Questions
- Did your business deduct domestic research costs federally in 2026?
- Did you make the small-business retroactive 174A election for 2022-2024?
- Did a K-1 report R&E information?
## Common Errors
- Assuming the July 2025 conformity update adopted 174A
- Adding back foreign R&E
- Missing the four-year recovery
## Prompt
- My company expensed R&D costs, does North Carolina allow that?
