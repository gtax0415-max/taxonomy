# Taxable Social Security and Railroad Retirement Benefits
## Description
North Carolina does not tax Social Security or railroad retirement benefits. Whatever portion was taxable federally is subtracted in full.
## Amount
- Title II Social Security benefits included in federal AGI — the taxable amount on Form 1040, Line 6b
- Tier 1 and Tier 2 railroad retirement benefits included in federal AGI, including railroad unemployment and sickness benefits
## Notes
- Subtract only what was INCLUDED in federal AGI, not the gross benefit
- Supplemental Security Income is not taxable federally, so there is nothing to subtract
- The federal senior deduction under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act is a separate, below-the-line federal deduction and does NOT carry to North Carolina
## Required Information
- Taxable Social Security from Form 1040
- Railroad retirement amounts included in AGI
## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security or railroad retirement benefits?
- How much was taxable on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the gross benefit rather than the taxable portion
- Forgetting the subtraction and paying North Carolina tax on benefits
## Prompt
- Does North Carolina tax Social Security?
