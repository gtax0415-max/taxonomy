# Other Subtractions
## Gain on exempt North Carolina obligations issued before July 1, 1995 (Line 26)
Deduct the gain from selling North Carolina obligations issued before July 1, 1995, if the law authorizing them exempted the gain.
## Business expense reduced by claiming a federal credit (Line 29)
If a federal credit (or the Employee Retention Credit against payroll tax) required you to reduce a deductible business expense, deduct that expense for North Carolina — but only to the extent North Carolina does not allow a similar credit. Common with the federal work opportunity credit and employer credits.
## Personal Education Student Account deposits (Line 30)
Deduct PESA deposits under Article 41 of Chapter 115C to the extent included in federal AGI.
## State Emergency Response and Disaster Relief Reserve Fund payments (Line 31)
Deduct hurricane relief assistance paid from the fund, excluding payments for goods or services you provided.
## Economic incentive payments (Line 32)
Deduct economic incentive payments under G.S. 143B-437.012 or Parts 2G or 2H of Article 10 of G.S. 143B.
## Certain North Carolina grant payments (Line 33)
Deduct COVID-19 relief grants from the Business Recovery Grant program, ReTOOLNC, and specified rent and utility assistance programs, to the extent in federal AGI. Most were paid in 2021-2023 and rarely appear on 2026 returns.
## Notes
- Line numbers are those of the 2025 Schedule S; confirm against the 2026 form when it is published
- The Hurricane Helene timber casualty loss deduction created in 2026 applies only to 2023 or 2024 returns, not 2026
