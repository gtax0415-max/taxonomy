# Interest From U.S. Obligations
## Description
States may not tax interest on U.S. government obligations. Subtract interest from Treasury bills, notes, bonds, and savings bonds, and from obligations of U.S. possessions, that is included in federal AGI.
## Notes
- Mutual fund dividends qualify only to the extent the fund reports U.S. obligation interest
- Interest from agencies only "backed" by the U.S. (e.g., GNMA, FNMA) generally does not qualify
- Interest on federal tax refunds does not qualify
## Questions
- Did you hold Treasury securities or U.S. savings bonds?
## Common Errors
- Missing Box 3 interest
- Subtracting GNMA or FNMA interest
## Prompt
- Is Treasury bill interest taxable in North Carolina?
