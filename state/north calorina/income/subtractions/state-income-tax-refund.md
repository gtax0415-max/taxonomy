# State or Local Income Tax Refund
## Description
A state or local income tax refund that is included in federal AGI (because it was deducted federally in the prior year) is subtracted. North Carolina does not allow a deduction for state income taxes, so the refund is not North Carolina income.
## Notes
- Only subtract the amount actually included in federal AGI (federal Schedule 1)
- With the higher federal SALT cap from 2025 on, more refunds become federally taxable, and more filers need this line
## Questions
- Did you report a state tax refund as income on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a refund that was not in federal AGI
## Prompt
- My state tax refund is on my federal return, do I pay North Carolina tax on it?
