# Bailey Settlement Retirement Benefits
## Description
Under Bailey v. State of North Carolina, the state cannot tax certain retirement benefits of North Carolina state and local government and federal (including military) retirees who were VESTED by August 12, 1989. Qualifying benefits are subtracted in full.
## Who qualifies
- Retirees (or their beneficiaries) with FIVE OR MORE YEARS of creditable service as of August 12, 1989, receiving benefits from:
  - NC Teachers' and State Employees' Retirement System
  - NC Local Governmental Employees' Retirement System
  - NC Consolidated Judicial Retirement System
  - Federal Employees' Retirement System or Civil Service Retirement System
  - Military retirement
- The state's 401(k) and 457 plans, if the retiree contributed or contracted to contribute before August 12, 1989
## What does NOT qualify
- Local government 457 plans
- 403(b) annuities
- Pensions from other states' governments
- Service vested after August 12, 1989
## Notes
- Attach Form 1099-R or W-2 to support the deduction
- A Bailey retiree must still file if gross income exceeds the filing threshold
- Military retirees use Line 20 for Bailey-eligible pay; they cannot deduct the same income again on Line 21
## Required Information
- 1099-R, plan name, and vesting date
## Questions
- Which retirement system pays your pension?
- Did you have five years of service by August 12, 1989?
## Common Errors
- Claiming Bailey for a 403(b) or local 457 plan
- Claiming for service vested after 1989
- Double-deducting military retirement on Lines 20 and 21
## Prompt
- I'm a retired North Carolina teacher, is my pension taxable?
- What is the Bailey exclusion?
