# Deductions From Federal AGI (Schedule S, Part B)
## Description
Items INCLUDED in federal AGI that North Carolina does not tax. Retirement income is the big category: North Carolina exempts Social Security entirely but has NO general pension or IRA exclusion — only the Bailey settlement and uniformed services retirement rules.

## Retirement income at a glance
| Income | North Carolina treatment |
|---|---|
| Social Security, Tier 1 and Tier 2 railroad retirement | Fully exempt |
| NC state or local, or federal, pension with 5+ years of service by August 12, 1989 | Exempt under Bailey |
| Military retirement, 20+ years or medically retired, and qualifying SBP | Exempt |
| Other IRA, 401(k), 403(b), private pension | Taxable at 3.99% |
| Other states' government pensions | Taxable |
## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security or railroad retirement?
- Did you retire from North Carolina, local, or federal government service, and were you vested by August 12, 1989?
- Are you a military retiree with 20 years of service or a medical retirement?
- Did you receive U.S. Treasury interest?
