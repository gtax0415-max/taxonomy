# Uniformed Services Retirement Benefits
## Description
Full subtraction for military and other uniformed services retirement pay for those with 20 YEARS OF SERVICE or a MEDICAL RETIREMENT, and for qualifying Survivor Benefit Plan payments. Covers retirees who do not qualify under Bailey.
## Who qualifies
- Retired members with at least 20 years in the uniformed services, or medically retired
- Beneficiaries receiving Survivor Benefit Plan payments from such a member
## What does NOT qualify
- Severance pay for separation under 10 U.S.C. Chapter 61
- Retirement pay from fewer than 20 years without medical retirement
- Amounts already deducted under Bailey (Line 20)
## Required Information
- 1099-R and proof of service or medical retirement
## Questions
- Did you serve 20 years or retire medically?
- Did you already use Bailey for this income?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for severance pay
- Deducting the same pay on Lines 20 and 21
## Prompt
- Is my military retirement taxed in North Carolina?
