# Income of Members of a Federally Recognized Indian Tribe
## Description
Income earned or received by an ENROLLED MEMBER of a federally recognized tribe is subtracted if it is derived from activities on a federally recognized reservation WHILE THE MEMBER RESIDES ON THAT RESERVATION. In North Carolina this chiefly affects members of the Eastern Band of Cherokee Indians living on the Qualla Boundary.
## Both tests must be met
1. Enrolled member of a federally recognized tribe
2. Income derived from reservation activities while residing on the reservation
Income from intangibles with a situs on the reservation and retirement income tied to reservation activity qualify.
## Questions
- Are you an enrolled tribal member?
- Do you live on the reservation, and was the income earned there?
## Common Errors
- Claiming while living off the reservation
- Claiming for a tribe that is state-recognized but not federally recognized
## Prompt
- I'm an Eastern Band Cherokee member working on the Qualla Boundary.
