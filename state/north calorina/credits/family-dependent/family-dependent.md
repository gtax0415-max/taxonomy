# Family and Dependent Credits
## Description
NORTH CAROLINA HAS NO FAMILY OR DEPENDENT CREDITS. There is no state child tax credit, no child and dependent care credit, no earned income credit, and no adoption credit. North Carolina delivers family relief through the CHILD DEDUCTION, which reduces taxable income on Form D-400, Line 10b.
This file exists so a question about family credits routes correctly instead of returning nothing. It is the sharpest contrast with New York, where the Empire State Child Credit and the dependent care credit are refundable.
## Credits in this folder
None. The family benefit is a deduction — see below.
## The child deduction (a deduction, not a credit)
Full detail: see deductions/child-deduction.md. The table below is unchanged for 2026.
- Line 10a: number of qualifying children for whom you were ALLOWED a federal child tax credit
- Line 10b: number of children × the per-child amount from the Child Deduction Table
Per-child amounts for 2026, by federal AGI:
| Per child | Married filing jointly / Qualifying surviving spouse | Head of household | Single / Married filing separately |
|---|---|---|---|
| $3,000 | Up to $40,000 | Up to $30,000 | Up to $20,000 |
| $2,500 | Over $40,000 - $60,000 | Over $30,000 - $45,000 | Over $20,000 - $30,000 |
| $2,000 | Over $60,000 - $80,000 | Over $45,000 - $60,000 | Over $30,000 - $40,000 |
| $1,500 | Over $80,000 - $100,000 | Over $60,000 - $75,000 | Over $40,000 - $50,000 |
| $1,000 | Over $100,000 - $120,000 | Over $75,000 - $90,000 | Over $50,000 - $60,000 |
| $500 | Over $120,000 - $140,000 | Over $90,000 - $105,000 | Over $60,000 - $70,000 |
| $0 | Over $140,000 | Over $105,000 | Over $70,000 |
## What the deduction is worth in tax
At the 2026 rate of 3.99%, the $3,000 maximum saves about $120 per child. Because it is a deduction, a family with no North Carolina tax gets NOTHING from it — there is no refundable equivalent.
## The federal linkage
Eligibility follows the federal child tax credit under IRC Section 24: only children for whom a federal credit was ALLOWED count. North Carolina now conforms to the IRC as of July 5, 2025, so the post-OBBBA Section 24 rules (including the work-eligible SSN requirement) decide which children count.
