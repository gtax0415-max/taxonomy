# Article 3L Income-Producing Historic Structure Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rehabilitating a certified historic structure that is INCOME-PRODUCING — commercial, office, or rental property. It PIGGYBACKS ON THE FEDERAL CREDIT: you must be allowed the federal Section 47 rehabilitation credit for the same expenditures. This is the current North Carolina historic credit for commercial projects.
## Amount
The credit is the SUM of a base amount and any bonuses, computed on qualified rehabilitation expenditures (QREs):
| Component | Rate |
|---|---|
| Base, QREs up to $10 million | 15% |
| Base, QREs from $10 million to $20 million | 10% |
| Bonus — property in a development tier 1 or 2 county | +5% on QREs up to $20 million |
| Bonus — targeted former manufacturing or agricultural property, at least 65% vacant for two years | +5% on QREs up to $20 million |
| Bonus — building originally used for education and still used for education | +5% |
Maximum credit per project: $4.5 million.
## Timing and carryforward
- Taken IN FULL in the year the property is placed in service — not in installments
- Unused credit carries forward for up to NINE succeeding years
- The carryforward stays on Form NC-Rehab, not D-400TC, Line 14
## Eligibility
- Must be allowed the federal Section 47 credit for the same QREs — federal certification by the National Park Service is required
- Property must be in North Carolina
- Expenditures must be incurred before the Article 3L sunset (January 1, 2030 per current NCDOR reporting)
## Pass-through entities
A partnership or S corporation may allocate the credit among owners in its discretion, but an owner's adjusted basis in the entity at year end of the placed-in-service year must be at least 40% of the credit allocated to that owner. Owners claim their share through their own Form NC-Rehab.
## Notes
- Because the credit depends on federal Section 47 allowance, any federal disallowance or recapture flows through
- North Carolina conforms to the IRC as of July 5, 2025 (Session Law 2026-31), so post-OBBBA Section 47 governs eligibility
- Attach Form NC-Rehab to the FRONT of Form D-400
- Also allowed against franchise tax and gross premiums tax, but individual claimants use D-400
## Required Information
- Total QREs and the placed-in-service date
- County development tier designation for the year
- Whether the targeted-property or education bonus applies
- Federal Section 47 approval
- For owners of a pass-through, the allocated credit and adjusted basis
## Questions
- Did the project qualify for the federal historic rehabilitation credit?
- What were the total qualified rehabilitation expenditures?
- What county is the building in, and what is its development tier?
- Was it a vacant mill, factory, or farm building?
- When was it placed in service?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a personal residence, which belongs on Line 13 instead
- Claiming without federal Section 47 approval
- Spreading the credit over five years as under old Article 3D, when 3L is taken in full when placed in service
- Putting Article 3L carryovers on D-400TC, Line 14
- Failing to attach Form NC-Rehab to the front of the return
