# Article 3H Income-Producing Mill Credit
## Description
Credit for rehabilitating an income-producing HISTORIC MILL FACILITY — a former manufacturing or agricultural building that was largely vacant. For ordinary mill projects it is a LEGACY credit (Article 3L absorbed the mill concept into its targeted-property bonus). BUT for 2026 Session Law 2026-31 added a NEW Article 3H credit for an eligible CORPORATE CAMPUS (G.S. 105-129.71(a2)) — see below.
## Amount
- 30% or 40% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures, depending on the development tier of the county where the mill is located
- Enter total qualified expenditures on Line 10a ONLY IN THE FIRST YEAR the credit is taken
- Enter the TOTAL credit on Line 10b (contrast with Line 11b, which is an installment)
## NEW for 2026 — corporate campus credit (G.S. 105-129.71(a2))
- 40% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures for a certified rehabilitation of an eligible corporate campus
- QREs must be at least $10,000,000, qualify for the federal Section 47 credit, and be incurred on or after January 1, 2026; the credit cannot be claimed for a tax year beginning before January 1, 2026
- Phased projects claim the credit for the QREs of each phase in the year that phase is placed in service
- Must provide the eligibility certification and cost certification to the Secretary
- An eligible corporate campus must: be a certified or State-certified historic structure; have at one time served as a corporate headquarters; sit on a parcel or commonly owned parcels of at least 20 acres; be subject to a preservation agreement (G.S. 121-35); be National Register listed and certified as a local landmark; and have been at least 80% vacant for two years before the eligibility certification
- Reported on the income-producing mill line (Line 10b on the 2025 form); confirm on the 2026 D-400TC
## Eligibility (legacy mill projects)
- An application for an eligibility certification must have been submitted to the State Historic Preservation Officer
- Must qualify for the federal Section 47 credit
- The project's certification must still be valid for the placed-in-service year — confirm the applicable sunset in G.S. 105-129.75
## Notes
- Line 10a is informational in the first year only; leave it blank in later years
- Carried-over amounts go to Line 14
## Required Information
- SHPO eligibility certification
- Qualified rehabilitation expenditures and placed-in-service date
- County development tier
## Questions
- Did the project receive a mill eligibility certification under the old program?
- When was it placed in service?
- Is this the first year the credit is being taken?
## Common Errors
- Entering expenditures on Line 10a in years after the first
- Claiming Article 3H for a new project that should go under Article 3L
## Prompt
- We converted a historic textile mill into apartments.
