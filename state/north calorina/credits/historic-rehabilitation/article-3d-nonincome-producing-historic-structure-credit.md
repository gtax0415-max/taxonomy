# Article 3D Nonincome-Producing Historic Structure Credit
## Description
LEGACY credit for homeowners who rehabilitated a historic residence under the old Article 3D program. Closed to new projects; used only for remaining INSTALLMENTS. The current homeowner credit is Article 3L (D-400TC, Line 13).
## Amount
- 30% of rehabilitation expenses for a nonincome-producing certified historic structure, subject to the Article 3D per-residence cap
- Taken in FIVE EQUAL ANNUAL INSTALLMENTS beginning in the year placed in service
- Enter only the current-year INSTALLMENT on Line 9
## Carryforward
Unused installments carry forward and are reported on D-400TC, Line 14 in later years.
## Notes
- The 30% rate is double the current Article 3L homeowner rate of 15%, which surprises people comparing old and new projects
- No new expenses may be added
## Required Information
- Original credit, placed-in-service year, and installments already claimed
- Any carryforward
## Questions
- When was the old project placed in service?
- How many installments remain?
## Common Errors
- Entering the full credit instead of one installment
- Using Line 9 for a new homeowner project, which belongs on Line 13
## Prompt
- I restored my historic house years ago and still get a credit.
