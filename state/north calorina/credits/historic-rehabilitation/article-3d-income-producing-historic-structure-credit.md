# Article 3D Income-Producing Historic Structure Credit
## Description
LEGACY credit. Article 3D was North Carolina's historic rehabilitation credit before Article 3L. Per the D-401 instructions it expired for qualified rehabilitation expenditures and expenses incurred by January 1, 2023, the end of the grandfather period. Nothing new can qualify; the line exists only for taxpayers still receiving INSTALLMENTS from earlier projects.
## Amount
- 20% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures for an income-producing certified historic structure, tied to the federal Section 47 credit
- Taken in FIVE EQUAL ANNUAL INSTALLMENTS beginning in the year the property was placed in service
- Enter only the INSTALLMENT for the current year on Line 8, not the total credit
## Carryforward
Any installment not used because of the tax limit carries forward. Report carried-over 3D amounts on D-400TC, Line 14, not on Line 8.
## Eligibility
- Project must have qualified under Article 3D, including any grandfathered project
- Must be the same taxpayer, or an allocated owner of the pass-through entity, that earned the credit
## Notes
- Five installments from a project placed in service in 2022 end with the 2026 return — 2026 is the last year a new 3D installment can appear; after that only Line 14 carryovers remain
- No new expenditures may be added
- Take the credit amount and percentage from the project's records rather than recomputing
## Required Information
- Original credit amount and placed-in-service year
- Installments already taken
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- When was the 3D project placed in service?
- How many of the five installments have you taken?
- Do you have any unused installment carried forward?
## Common Errors
- Entering the full credit instead of the annual installment
- Treating a new project as Article 3D when it belongs under Article 3L
- Putting a carryover on Line 8 instead of Line 14

- I'm still getting installments from an old North Carolina historic credit.
