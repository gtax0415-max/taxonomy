# Article 3H Nonincome-Producing Mill Credit
## Description
LEGACY credit for rehabilitating a historic mill facility that is NOT income-producing, such as a mill converted to the owner's own residence. Available only to projects certified under the old Article 3H program.
## Amount
- 30% of rehabilitation expenses
- Taken in INSTALLMENTS — Line 11b is the current-year installment, not the total
- Enter rehabilitation expenses on Line 11a ONLY IN THE FIRST YEAR
## Eligibility
- Eligibility certification application must have been submitted to the State Historic Preservation Officer
- Property must be a certified historic mill facility in North Carolina
## Notes
- Contrast with the income-producing mill credit on Line 10b, which is the total credit rather than an installment
- Carryovers go to Line 14
## Required Information
- SHPO certification, expenses, placed-in-service date, installments taken
## Questions
- Is the mill your residence rather than rental or commercial property?
- How many installments have been taken?
## Common Errors
- Entering the full credit on Line 11b
- Repeating Line 11a expenses after the first year
## Prompt
- I live in a converted historic mill and got a North Carolina credit.
