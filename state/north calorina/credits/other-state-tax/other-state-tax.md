# Other-State Tax Credits
## Description
Relief from double taxation when income earned while a North Carolina resident is also taxed by another state or country. For most North Carolina filers who claim any credit at all, THIS IS THE CREDIT — it is the only broadly applicable individual credit left on the North Carolina return.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Income Tax Paid to Another State or Country (credit-for-income-tax-paid-to-another-state.md) — Form D-400TC, Part 1 → Line 7a → Form D-400, Line 16. Nonrefundable, residents only
## Who this affects most
- Residents who commute to Virginia or South Carolina for work
- Remote workers taxed by an employer's state under a convenience-of-employer rule
- Residents with rental property or pass-through business income in another state
- S corporation shareholders whose corporation pays another state's corporate-level tax
- Part-year residents, for the resident portion of the year only
## What is NOT covered
- City, county, or other local income taxes
- Federal income tax
- Tax paid by a nonresident of North Carolina — nonresidents do not get this credit at all
- Income earned in a state with no wage tax (Tennessee, Florida), because no tax was paid
## Questions
- Were you a North Carolina resident for all or part of the year?
- Did you file a return with another state or country?
- Was the income taxed by the other state also taxed by North Carolina?
- How many states or countries taxed you?
## Prompt
- I live in North Carolina but work in Virginia.
- I paid tax to South Carolina, can I get credit in North Carolina?
- I have rental property in another state.
