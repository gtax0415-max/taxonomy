# Property Conservation Credits
## Description
Credits for permanently protecting land. THE HEADLINE FOR 2026: the reenacted NC Conservation Tax Credit covers donations made from January 1, 2025, and the credit is claimed the year AFTER the donation. So 2026 is the FIRST RETURN on which anyone can claim it.
## Credits in this folder
- NC Conservation Tax Credit (nc-conservation-tax-credit.md) — Form NC-74 application → allocation notice from NCDOR → Form D-400TC → Form D-400, Line 16. Nonrefundable
## Related
- Charitable contributions (deductions/itemized/charitable-contributions.md) — the same donation cannot also be deducted for North Carolina
- Federal conservation easement deduction under IRC 170(h) is separate and still available federally
## Questions
- Did you donate land or a conservation easement in North Carolina in 2025 or 2026?
- Did you file Form NC-74 by April 15 of the year after the donation?
- Did NCDOR send you an allocation notice?
