# Tax Credit Carryovers (D-400TC Line 14)
## Description
Unused North Carolina credits from prior years that are still within their carryforward period and are NOT tracked on Form NC-478 or Form NC-Rehab. In practice this is mostly unused Article 3D and Article 3H historic credit installments.
## What goes here
- Unused installments of Article 3D income-producing and nonincome-producing historic credits
- Unused Article 3H mill credits
- Other D-400TC credits that carry forward under their own article
## What does NOT go here
- Article 3L carryovers — stay on Form NC-Rehab
- Business incentive and energy credit carryovers — stay on Form NC-478, Line 19
- Credit for tax paid to another state — it has NO carryforward at all
## How the limit works
Line 14 is added into Line 16 with the current-year credits, and Line 18 limits the total to the North Carolina tax. Whatever is not used rolls forward again until its carryforward period ends.
## Required Information
- Credit type, origination year, original amount, amounts used each year, and remaining balance
## Questions
- Which credit is being carried over, and from which year?
- Has the carryforward period expired?
- Is it tracked on NC-478 or NC-Rehab instead?
## Common Errors
- Carrying forward unused out-of-state tax credit, which is not allowed
- Double-counting an Article 3L or NC-478 carryover here
- Continuing to claim after the carryforward period ends
## Prompt
- I couldn't use all of my North Carolina credit last year.
- What goes on D-400TC Line 14?
