# Business Incentive and Carryover Credits
## Description
Credits that reach the individual return from business activity or from earlier years. Almost everything here is a LEGACY or CARRYFORWARD item — most North Carolina business incentive credits have been repealed or sunset, and what remains on individual returns is mainly unused credit working through its carryforward period.
## Credits in this folder
- Business Incentive and Energy Tax Credits (business-incentive-and-energy-tax-credits.md) — Form NC-478 series → D-400TC, Line 19. Limited to 50% of tax
- Tax Credit Carryovers (tax-credit-carryovers.md) — D-400TC, Line 14. Carryovers not tracked on NC-478 or NC-Rehab
## Three separate carryover tracks
| Credit | Where the carryover lives |
|---|---|
| Articles 3D and 3H historic credits, other D-400TC credits | D-400TC, Line 14 |
| Business incentive and energy credits (Articles 3A, 3B, 3F, 3J, etc.) | Form NC-478 series → D-400TC, Line 19 |
| Article 3L historic credits | Form NC-Rehab → D-400TC, Lines 12 and 13 |
Putting a carryover on the wrong track is the most common error in this category — the same dollar can be claimed twice or lost.
