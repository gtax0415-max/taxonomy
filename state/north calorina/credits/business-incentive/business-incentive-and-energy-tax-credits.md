# Business Incentive and Energy Tax Credits
## Description
Nonrefundable credits under North Carolina's business incentive articles, summarized on Form NC-478, "Summary of Tax Credits Limited to 50% of Tax." On an individual return these are almost always CARRYFORWARDS or REMAINING INSTALLMENTS from credits whose programs have expired — the Department notes that many are repealed or designated for sunset.
## The 50% limit
The total of these credits is limited to 50% of the tax, reduced by the sum of all other credits claimed. So ordering matters: out-of-state and historic credits on D-400TC Lines 7a through 15 apply first, and the NC-478 credits can use at most half of what is left. D-400TC Line 20 still cannot exceed the tax on D-400, Line 15.
## What reaches this line
Examples of articles that fed Form NC-478 and may still produce carryforwards:
- Article 3A — older jobs and investment credits, some with 20-year carryforwards for large investments
- Article 3B — renewable energy property credit, sunset for property placed in service after 2015 (limited later exceptions)
- Article 3F — research and development credit, sunset January 1, 2016
- Article 3J — creating jobs, investing in business property, investing in real property, sunset January 1, 2014
- Article 3K — railroad intermodal facility credit
Check the carryforward period for each specific article; many have run out by 2025.
## Eligibility
A taxpayer that qualified for an expired or sunset credit may continue to take remaining installments or carryovers IF IT CONTINUES TO MEET THE STATUTORY ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS that originally applied.
## Notes
- Attach Form NC-478 and required supporting schedules to the FRONT of Form D-400
- Pass-through owners receive their share on the NC K-1
- A residential solar system placed in service in 2015 or earlier had a five-year carryforward — anything left from it has expired
## Required Information
- Form NC-478 and the specific supporting form for each credit
- Carryforward balances and origination years
- Evidence of continued eligibility where required
## Questions
- Which article is the credit under, and in what year did it originate?
- Is the carryforward period still open?
- Do you still meet the original eligibility requirements?
- Did a pass-through entity allocate the credit to you on an NC K-1?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the 50% of tax limit
- Claiming an expired carryforward
- Reporting NC-478 carryovers on D-400TC, Line 14 instead of Line 19
- Omitting the supporting NC-478 schedules
## Prompt
- I have an old North Carolina renewable energy credit carryforward.
- My K-1 shows a North Carolina business credit.
- What goes on D-400TC Line 19?
