# Tax Computation
## Description
Tax at 4.4% (line 13), alternative minimum tax (line 14), credit recapture (line 15), subtotal (line 16), net income tax after nonrefundable credits (line 21), dealer / e-bike repayment (line 22), net tax (line 23), and the late payment penalty, interest and estimated tax penalty (lines 44 to 46).

## 2026 figures
- Tax rate 4.4% (no TABOR rate cut)
- Colorado AMT rate 3.47%
- Interest: 2026 discounted 8% / regular 11%; 2027 discounted 7% / regular 10%
- Late payment penalty: greater of $5 or 5% + 0.5% per month, up to 12%
- Estimated tax: $1,000 exemption; 70% of current tax or 100% / 110% of prior tax

## How the lines flow
- Line 12 → line 13 → line 16 (with 14 and 15) → line 21 after lines 17 to 20 → line 23 with line 22
- Estimated tax penalty → line 46
