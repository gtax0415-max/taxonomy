# Annualized Installment Method
## Description
Taxpayers whose income was bunched later in the year can lower the required early installments by annualizing income through each period, reducing or eliminating the estimated tax penalty.

## Amount
| Period ending | Annualization factor | Applicable percentage |
|---|---|---|
| March 31 | 4 | 17.5% |
| May 31 | 2.4 | 35% |
| August 31 | 1.5 | 52.5% |
| December 31 | 1 | 70% |

- Installment = annualized income × 4.4% × applicable percentage − earlier installments

## Notes
- Example: Colorado taxable income $10,000 through March 31 → annualized $40,000 → tax $1,760 × 17.5% = $308 due April 15, 2026

## Required Information
- Colorado taxable income through each period end

## Questions
- Was your 2026 income received unevenly, mostly late in the year?
- Did you use the annualized method on your federal Form 2210?

## Common Errors
- Using the method without the federal election
- Forgetting to subtract earlier installments
