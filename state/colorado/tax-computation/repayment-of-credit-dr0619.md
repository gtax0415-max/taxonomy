# Repayment of Dealer or E-Bike Retailer Credit
## Description
Vehicle dealers, financing entities and e-bike retailers that claimed a credit for discounts given at the point of sale must repay it when the sale or lease no longer qualifies.

## Amount
- The repayment amount figured on the dealer / e-bike credit schedule

## Notes
- Report the repayment separately; do not net it against the credit on line 32
- Example: dealer must repay $1,200 for a lease cancelled within the required term → line 22 $1,200

## Required Information
- Credits to be repaid and the reason

## Questions
- Are you a vehicle dealer, financing entity or e-bike retailer that must repay a credit claimed earlier?

## Common Errors
- Netting the repayment against line 32

## Prompt
- What is line 22 on the Colorado return?
