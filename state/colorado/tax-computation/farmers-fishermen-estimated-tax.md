# Farmers and Fishermen Estimated Tax Exception
## Description
Farmers and fishermen get relaxed estimated tax rules: no quarterly payments, and no penalty if they file and pay by March 1.

## Amount
- File and pay in full by March 1, 2027 → no penalty
- Otherwise: required payment is 50% of 2026 tax (instead of 70%) or the prior-year tax test, as one installment due January 15, 2027

## Notes
- Example: farm couple with 80% of gross income from Schedule F files and pays on March 20, 2027. 2026 tax $8,000 → 50% = $4,000; 2025 tax $7,000 → required payment $4,000, all due January 15, 2027

## Required Information
- Gross income from farming or fishing and total gross income (2025 and 2026)
- Filing and payment date

## Questions
- Was at least two-thirds of your gross income from farming or fishing?
- Will you file and pay by March 1, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Using 70% when the farmer rule applies
- Spreading the farmer installment over four quarters

## Prompt
- Do Colorado farmers have to make quarterly estimated payments?
