# Recapture of Prior-Year Credits
## Description
Some Colorado credits must be paid back if the project or condition that earned them later fails (for example, a housing project falls out of compliance or an employee forfeits home savings contributions).

## Credits that may be recaptured (not a complete list)
- Affordable Housing Credit
- Affordable Housing in Transit-Oriented Communities Credit
- Middle-Income Housing Credit
- Employer Assistance for Home Purchase Credit
- Historic Property Preservation Credit
- Industrial Clean Energy Credit

## Notes
- Report recapture as a separate tax, not by reducing this year's credits
- Example: investor must recapture $4,000 of a middle-income housing credit after the project fails compliance; tax $6,200 → line 16 $10,200

## Required Information
- Recapture notice and amount

## Questions
- Did you receive notice that a prior-year Colorado credit must be paid back?

## Common Errors
- Netting recapture against this year's credits

- Where do I report recapture of a Colorado credit?
