# Colorado Film Incentive Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for production companies that make qualified film, television or media productions in Colorado (HB23-1309, extended by HB24-1358). The credit is the amount on the certificate.

## Amount
- Up to 22% of qualified Colorado expenditures
- Statewide limit of $5 million a year
- Reservations through 2029; certificates issued through December 31, 2031

## Eligibility
- Production company with a certificate from the Colorado Office of Film, Television and Media

## Notes
- Example: certificate of $20,000 passed through to an owner → $20,000 credit

## Required Information
- Film office certificate
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did you receive a Colorado film incentive certificate for 2026?
- Did it come to you through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the certificate
- Claiming more than the certified amount
