# Disability Assistance Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for low-income Colorado residents who were disabled and received full disability benefits for the entire year (HB24-1268, tax years beginning 2025).

## Amounts
2025 table, carried for 2026 (amounts and thresholds are indexed, but DOR has not yet published the 2026 figures):

| Credit | Single AGI | Joint AGI |
|---|---|---|
| $1,200 | $10,000 or less | $16,000 or less |
| $1,000 | $10,001 – $12,500 | $16,001 – $20,000 |
| $800 | $12,501 – $15,000 | $20,001 – $24,000 |
| $600 | $15,001 – $17,500 | $24,001 – $28,000 |
| $400 | $17,501 – $20,000 | $28,001 – $32,000 |

## Eligibility
- Colorado resident all year
- Disabled and unable to engage in substantial gainful activity all year
- Full benefits from January 1 to December 31 from a plan based solely on disability: Social Security or SSI, Colorado Department of Human Services, or another bona fide public or private plan (VA letters must show 50% or more)
- Not claiming the Property Tax/Rent/Heat Credit rebate for the same year

## Notes
- Check the box for each benefit source and attach proof; DOR asks for a benefit letter dated within 90 days
- Example: single, SSDI all year, AGI $13,400 → $800

## Required Information
- Benefit letters for the full year
- Federal AGI and filing status
- Whether a PTC rebate was claimed

## Questions
- Were you disabled and receiving full disability benefits all of 2026? From which source?
- What is your federal AGI?
- Did you apply for the Property Tax/Rent/Heat Credit rebate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming both this credit and the PTC rebate
- Claiming without proof or with an old benefit letter
- Using the 2025 table when the 2026 indexed table is published

## Prompt
- Does Colorado have a credit for people with disabilities?
- I've been on SSDI all year with low income.
