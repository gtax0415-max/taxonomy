# Low-Income Child Care Expenses Credit
## Description
Separate Colorado credit for low-income filers who could not use the federal child care credit. Repealed for 2026 by HB24-1134, which folded these filers into the main credit.

## Status for 2026 — the credit is gone
- Part IV of DR 0347 computes $0 for 2026
- For 2026, the main Colorado child care credit is 70% of the full federal credit the filer was eligible for, figured without the federal tax-liability limit. AGI must be $60,000 or less (indexed starting 2027)
- Because the main credit is a percentage of the federal credit, the larger 2026 federal Form 2441 credit under OBBBA flows through automatically

## What it covered while available (2025)
- Federal credit $0 and AGI $25,000 or less
- 25% of expenses for a child under 13
- Capped at $500 for one child, $1,000 for two or more

## Required Information
- None for this credit in 2026; for the main credit, federal Form 2441 and provider receipts

## Questions
- Not asked for 2026: route child care expenses to the main credit

## Common Errors
- Claiming Part IV on a 2026 return
- Missing the main credit because federal tax was zero

## Prompt
- What happened to the Colorado low-income child care credit?
- I had no federal tax but paid for day care.
