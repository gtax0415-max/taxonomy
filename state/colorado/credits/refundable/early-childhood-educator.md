# Early Childhood Educator Income Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for credentialed early childhood educators (C.R.S. 39-22-547). The statute covered tax years 2022 through 2025 only, and no extension was enacted.

## Status for 2026 — the credit is gone
- Expired for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026
- No 2026 credit can be claimed, regardless of credential or income
- The Qualified Care Worker Credit (HB24-1312) continues for 2026 and covers many of the same workers: $1,200 per qualifying person, AGI limit $75,000 single / $100,000 joint, at least 720 hours of qualifying care

## What it covered while available (2025)
- Credit by credential level: Level I $872; Level II $1,162; Levels III – VI $1,743
- AGI limit $75,000 single, $150,000 joint (not indexed)
- At least six months working at a licensed program rated Colorado Shines level 1 or higher

## Required Information
- None for 2026; for an amended 2025 return, the CDEC credential and employment records

## Questions
- Did you work in child care in 2026? (route to the Qualified Care Worker Credit)
- Are you amending a 2025 or earlier return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the early childhood educator credit on a 2026 return
- Missing the Qualified Care Worker Credit for 2026

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for preschool teachers?
- Can I still claim the early childhood educator credit?
