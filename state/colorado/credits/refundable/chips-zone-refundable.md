# Refundable CHIPS Zone Credit
## Description
Refundable portion of the Creating Hope and Investing in the Production of Semiconductors (CHIPS) Zone credit (HB23-1260). Businesses in a CHIPS Zone that receive a refund certificate from the Economic Development Commission may take part of their certified credit as a refund instead of carrying it forward.

## Amount
- Refundable amount is 80% of the credit covered by the refund certificate
- Annual limit of $750,000 per taxpayer on the refundable amount
- The remaining 20% is forfeited as the price of refundability, not carried forward on this line

## Eligibility
- Business located in a designated CHIPS Zone
- Holds an EDC-approved refund certificate for the tax year
- The refund mechanism runs through FY 2028-29, so it is available for 2026

## Notes
- Electronic filing is required, with the certificates attached
- The nonrefundable CHIPS Zone credit is claimed separately
- Example: refund certificate covers $9,375 of credit → 80% = $7,500 → DR 0104CR line 20 $7,500

## Required Information
- EDC refund certificate
- CHIPS Zone credit certificates from the EZ Tax Credit system
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Do you operate a business in a Colorado CHIPS Zone?
- Did the Economic Development Commission approve a refund certificate for 2026?
- Did the credit come to you through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Entering the full certified credit instead of 80%
- Entering the nonrefundable CHIPS Zone credit here
- Claiming without an EDC refund certificate
- Filing on paper

## Prompt
- Is the CHIPS Zone credit refundable?
- My company received a CHIPS Zone refund certificate.
