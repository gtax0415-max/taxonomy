# Alternative Transportation Options Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for employers that pay for employees' alternative commuting options (HB22-1026, extended by HB24-1036). Tax year 2026 is the LAST year; the credit ends for tax years beginning January 1, 2027.

## Amount
- 50% of qualifying expenditures
- Limited to $2,000 per employee
- Maximum $125,000 credit per year

## Eligibility
- At least 3 employees in Colorado
- Filed the annual employer plan report (DR 1323) with DOR before claiming; the report is not attached to the return

## Notes
- Example: 4 employees, $10,000 spent on transit passes → 50% = $5,000 (under the $8,000 per-employee limit)

## Required Information
- Expense invoices and receipts
- Employee count
- Confirmation that the annual plan report was filed
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Does your business pay for transit, vanpool or other alternative commuting for employees?
- How many employees do you have in Colorado?
- Did you file the annual employer plan report?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without filing the annual plan report
- Exceeding the $2,000-per-employee limit
- Assuming the credit continues after 2026

## Prompt
- What is the alternative transportation options credit?
- We buy RTD passes for our employees.
