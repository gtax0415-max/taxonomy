# Business Personal Property Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for business personal property tax paid on the first $18,000 of actual value (C.R.S. 39-22-537.5). HB25-1296 limited it to tax years beginning before January 1, 2026.

## Status for 2026 — the credit is gone
- Not available for tax year 2026; business personal property tax paid in 2026 does not qualify
- Tax year 2025 was the last year (tax for property tax year 2024, paid in 2025)

## What it covered while available (2025)
- Credit = the tax on up to $18,000 of actual value (not indexed)
- Actual value = assessed value ÷ assessment rate (27.9% nonresidential; 26.4% agricultural or renewable energy property, for property tax year 2024)
- If actual value is over $18,000: credit = ($18,000 ÷ actual value) × tax
- Partners and shareholders included their share of the entity's value and tax
- Real property tax never qualified
- Example (2025): actual value $25,000, tax $2,000 → ($18,000 ÷ $25,000) × $2,000 = $1,440

## Required Information
- None for 2026; for an amended 2025 return, the county property tax statement

## Questions
- Are you amending a 2025 or earlier return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit on a 2026 return
- Including real property tax
- Using assessed value as actual value
