# Aircraft Modification for Unleaded Fuel Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for owners who modify a piston aircraft designed for leaded aviation gasoline so it can run on unleaded fuel (HB24-1235, C.R.S. 39-22-560, tax years 2025 – 2029).

## Amount
- 50% of out-of-pocket modification costs
- Maximum $500 per aircraft

## Eligibility
- Aircraft registered with the FAA in Colorado
- Modification approved by an STC or other FAA authorization
- Costs paid in 2026

## Notes
- Enter the STC number on the return
- Example: $1,400 of modification costs → 50% = $700, limited to $500

## Required Information
- STC or FAA approval number
- Modification receipts
- FAA registration

## Questions
- Did you modify an aircraft to use unleaded aviation gasoline in 2026?
- Is the aircraft registered with the FAA in Colorado?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $500 per aircraft
- Omitting the STC number

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for converting a plane to unleaded fuel?
