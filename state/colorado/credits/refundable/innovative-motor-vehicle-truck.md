# Innovative Motor Vehicle and Innovative Truck Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for buying or leasing a new electric or other innovative vehicle or truck registered in Colorado (HB23-1272). Most buyers assign the credit to the dealer at the point of sale; those who keep it claim it on the return.

## Amounts
LIGHT-DUTY VEHICLES, purchased or leased in 2026:
- Base credit: $750 (the $1,500 statutory amount, halved by the HB23-1272 revenue-forecast trigger; confirmed on DOR's 2026 assignment form)
- Additional $2,500 if MSRP is under $35,000 (2024 – 2028)
- MSRP cap: $80,000
- The $600 bonus for assigning the credit ended after 2025

TRUCKS, 2026:
- Light-duty truck $750; medium-duty $4,000; heavy-duty $8,000

## Eligibility
- New qualifying vehicle purchased, or leased for at least 2 years, in 2026
- Permanently registered in Colorado
- Credit not assigned to a dealer or financing entity

## Notes
- HB26-1289 raises the base to $2,000 and extends the $2,500 bonus to MSRP under $40,000 for 2027, not 2026
- Example: buyer keeps the credit on a $32,000 EV → $750 + $2,500 = $3,250

## Required Information
- Purchase invoice or lease agreement
- Colorado registration
- Whether the credit was assigned

## Questions
- Did you buy or lease an electric or other innovative vehicle in 2026?
- What was the MSRP?
- Did the dealer take the credit at the point of sale?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a credit already assigned to the dealer
- Using the 2025 amounts ($3,500 base, $600 assignment bonus)
- Claiming for a vehicle over the MSRP cap

## Prompt
- How do I claim the Colorado EV credit?
- I bought an electric car in Colorado this year.
