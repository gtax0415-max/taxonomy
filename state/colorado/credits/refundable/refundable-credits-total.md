# Total Refundable Credits
## Description
DR 0104CR line 26 adds the refundable credits in Part I and carries the total to Form DR 0104 line 31, where it is treated like a payment. It can produce a refund even with no tax.

## Amount
- Sum of DR 0104CR lines 1, 2, 5 (or 6) and 7 through 25
- Lines 3 and 4 are EITC inputs and are not added

## 2026 changes inside the total
- Line 1: Family Affordability part is $0; only the regular Child Tax Credit applies
- Line 2: child care credit is 70% of the federal credit (was 50%)
- Line 5: Colorado EITC is 25% (was 50%)
- Line 7: business personal property credit is repealed ($0)
- Line 10: early childhood educator credit expired ($0)

## Notes
- Example: child tax credit $600 + EITC $410 → line 26 $1,010 → Form DR 0104, line 31 $1,010

## Required Information
- Each refundable credit's amount and supporting documents

## Questions
- Which refundable credits apply to you?

## Common Errors
- Adding lines 3 and 4 into the total
- Adding both line 5 and line 6
- Including lines 7 or 10 on a 2026 return
