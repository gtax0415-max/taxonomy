# Food Accessibility Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for small food retailers and producers that buy equipment to improve access to fresh food in low-access communities (HB23-1008, C.R.S. 39-22-549, tax years 2024 – 2030).

## Amount
- 75% of qualifying equipment costs for 2025 – 2030 (85% for 2024)
- Statewide limit of $10 million a year
- The credit is the amount on the certificate

## Eligibility
- Certified by the Colorado Department of Agriculture for 2026
- HB26-1289 broadens eligibility starting 2027, not 2026

## Notes
- Example: $5,333 of qualifying refrigeration equipment → certificate for $4,000

## Required Information
- Department of Agriculture certificate
- Equipment invoices
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did the Colorado Department of Agriculture certify a food accessibility credit for you?
- What equipment did you buy, and when?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the certificate
- Using the 2024 85% rate

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado food accessibility credit?
- My small grocery bought new coolers to sell fresh produce.
