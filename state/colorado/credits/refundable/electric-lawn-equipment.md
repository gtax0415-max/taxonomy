# Electric-Powered Lawn Equipment Credit for Retailers
## Description
Refundable credit for registered retailers that sell qualifying electric lawn and garden equipment at a point-of-sale discount (SB23-016).

## Amount
- Retailer credit: 33% of the purchase price of qualifying equipment
- Customer discount: 30% of the price, shown separately on the receipt
- The retailer keeps the 3-point difference as an administrative fee
- Same rates for 2024, 2025 and 2026; HB26-1289 extends the credit through 2029

## Eligibility
- Retailer registered with DOR
- Quarterly reports filed
- Discount given at the point of sale

## Notes
- Example: qualifying sales of $3,000 → customer discounts $900; retailer credit $990

## Required Information
- Receipts with the discount line
- Total qualifying sales
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Are you a registered retailer that sold qualifying electric lawn equipment in 2026?
- What were your total qualifying sales?

## Common Errors
- A buyer claiming the retailer credit
- Using the 30% discount rate instead of the 33% credit rate

## Prompt
- Can I get a Colorado credit for buying an electric lawn mower?
- My hardware store sells electric mowers at a discount.
