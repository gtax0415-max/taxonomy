# Apprenticeship Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for employers that hire apprentices through a Registered Apprenticeship Program in a "New and Emerging" occupation (HB24-1439). The credit is the amount on the certificate from Apprenticeship Colorado.

## Amounts
- $6,300 per apprentice for the first 6 months of employment
- Plus $1,050 for each additional month
- Maximum $12,600 per apprentice and 10 apprentices per employer ($126,000)
- Statewide cap of $15 million a year; available tax years 2025 – 2034

## Eligibility
- Apprentice works at least 1,040 hours in a registered program in a New and Emerging occupation
- Employer completed the reservation and then the certificate application with Apprenticeship Colorado

## Notes
- Example: one apprentice employed 8 months → $6,300 + (2 × $1,050) = $8,400

## Required Information
- Certificate from Apprenticeship Colorado
- Apprentice employment dates and hours
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Does your business employ registered apprentices in a New and Emerging occupation?
- Did you receive a tax credit certificate from Apprenticeship Colorado?
- How many months was each apprentice employed?

## Common Errors
- An apprentice claiming the employer credit
- Claiming without a certificate
- Claiming more than 10 apprentices

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado apprenticeship credit?
- My business hired two registered apprentices.
