# Refundable Renewable Energy Investment Credit
## Description
Refundable enterprise zone investment credit for renewable energy property placed in service from 2015 through 2020 where the taxpayer elected refundability. No new property qualifies; only remaining refundable balances from those years carry into 2026.

## Amount
- Remaining refundable balance carried forward from prior years
- Use is limited to $750,000 a year

## Eligibility
- Renewable energy investment in an enterprise zone placed in service after January 1, 2015 and before January 1, 2021
- Refund election made for that investment

## Notes
- HB25-1296 adds a $2 million per-taxpayer cap on enterprise zone credits beginning in 2026; whether it limits these older refundable balances is not confirmed
- HB26-1289 creates a new refund election for projects completed after January 1, 2027, which does not affect 2026
- Example: remaining refundable balance $3,200 → $3,200 credit

## Required Information
- EZ credit certificates for the original investment
- Placed-in-service date
- Prior-year use of the refundable balance
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did you elect a refund for an enterprise zone renewable energy investment placed in service 2015–2020?
- How much of the refundable balance remains?

## Common Errors
- Claiming an investment placed in service after 2020
- Claiming more than the remaining balance

## Prompt
- Is the renewable energy credit refundable?
- I still have a refundable enterprise zone renewable energy credit balance.
