# Refundable Credits
## Description
Colorado refundable credits for 2026. Most are totaled on DR 0104CR line 26 and carried to Form DR 0104 line 31. Three are entered directly among the payments on Form DR 0104: gross conservation easement (line 29), innovative motor vehicle and truck (line 30), and the DR 0619 dealer and e-bike retailer credit (line 32).

## 2026 status changes
- Not available for 2026: business personal property (line 7), early childhood educator (line 10), family affordability (part of line 1), low-income child care (DR 0347 Part IV)
- Rate changes for 2026: Colorado EITC 25% (was 50%); child care credit 70% of the federal credit (was 50%); innovative vehicle $750 base (was $3,500 with assignment bonus); e-bike $250 (was $500)
- Last year in 2026: alternative transportation options, residential energy storage

## How the lines flow
- DR 0104CN → CR line 1; DR 0347 → CR line 2; EITC → CR line 5 or 6; DR 0104TN → CR line 9
- CR line 26 → Form DR 0104 line 31 → line 33
