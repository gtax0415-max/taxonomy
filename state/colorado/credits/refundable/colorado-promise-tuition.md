# Colorado Promise Tuition and Fee Credit
## Description
Refundable credit that reimburses eligible Colorado students for out-of-pocket tuition and fees at Colorado public colleges and universities (HB24-1340, tax years 2025 – 2032). The institution determines eligibility and the amount; the student claims it.

## Amount
- Out-of-pocket tuition and fees (after scholarships and grants) for the first 65 credit hours
- The amount is stated on the institution's certificate

## Eligibility
- Student at a Colorado public institution of higher education
- Household AGI on the FAFSA or CASFA of $90,000 or less
- Holds a certificate from the institution

## Notes
- The credit belongs to the student even if a parent paid the bill
- Example: certificate shows $2,300 of out-of-pocket tuition and fees → $2,300 credit on the student's return

## Required Information
- Institution's Colorado Promise certificate
- Student's identifying information

## Questions
- Are you a student at a Colorado public college or university?
- Did your school send you a Colorado Promise certificate?

## Common Errors
- A parent claiming the student's credit
- Claiming without a certificate
- Claiming tuition already covered by grants or scholarships

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado Promise tuition credit?
- My school said I qualify for Colorado Promise.
