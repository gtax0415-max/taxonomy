# Family Affordability Tax Credit
## Description
Per-child refundable credit for children under 17 (HB24-1311) that is turned on only when the state's TABOR revenue forecast clears a growth trigger. It was available for 2024 and 2025.

## Status for 2026 — not available
- The trigger uses the December forecast for the fiscal year beginning in the tax year. The December 2025 forecast put the FY 2026-27 growth factor below 3.00%, so the credit is $0 for 2026
- The same trigger holds the Colorado EITC at its 25% floor for 2026
- Legislative Council's September 2026 forecast also projects it off for 2027
- HB26-1223 (signed June 4, 2026) creates a separate family credit, but it begins with tax year 2027, not 2026

## What it covered while available (2025)
- Per child: up to $3,273 (under 6) and $2,455 (ages 6–16) at AGI $15,000 or less ($26,000 joint)
- Phased down in $5,000 AGI bands to $0 above $85,000 single ($96,000 joint)

## Required Information
- None for 2026

## Questions
- Not asked for 2026: the credit is not available

## Common Errors
- Claiming 2025 table amounts on a 2026 return
- Confusing it with the regular Colorado Child Tax Credit, which still applies to children under 6

## Prompt
- Can I claim the Colorado Family Affordability Tax Credit for 2026?
- I got a big Colorado child credit last year. Will I get it again?
