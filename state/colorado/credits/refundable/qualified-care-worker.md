# Qualified Care Worker Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for child care workers and direct care workers with modest income (HB24-1312, tax years 2025 – 2028). For 2026 it is the main credit for early childhood workers, since the Early Childhood Educator credit expired after 2025.

## Amount
- $1,200 per qualifying person
- Up to $2,400 on a joint return when both spouses qualify

## Eligibility
- At least 720 hours of qualifying care work in Colorado in 2026
- Federal AGI $75,000 or less (single) or $100,000 or less (joint)
- Child care workers registered in Colorado Shines PDIS with a CDEC attestation
- HB25-1296 lets informal family, friend and neighbor caregivers qualify when caring for children outside their own home

## Notes
- Example: home care aide, single, AGI $38,000, 1,500 hours → $1,200

## Required Information
- Attestation number (child care workers)
- Hours worked and employer
- Federal AGI and filing status

## Questions
- Did you work in child care or direct care in Colorado in 2026?
- How many hours did you work?
- What is your federal AGI and filing status?
- Do you have a CDEC attestation number?

## Common Errors
- Claiming with fewer than 720 hours
- Claiming above the AGI limit
- Omitting the attestation number for a child care worker

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for care workers?
- I'm a preschool teacher. Is there a Colorado credit for 2026?
