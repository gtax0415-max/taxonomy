# Additional Credit for Dealers and Electric Bicycle Retailers
## Description
Refundable credit for businesses that front a state credit to customers at the point of sale: vehicle dealers and financing entities that accept assigned innovative vehicle credits, and retailers that discount qualified electric bicycles (C.R.S. 39-22-555, tax years 2024 – 2032).

## Amounts
- ASSIGNED VEHICLE CREDITS: the innovative vehicle or truck credits assigned to the dealer (2026 light-duty $750, plus $2,500 under $35,000 MSRP); the dealer may keep up to $250 per vehicle
- ELECTRIC BICYCLES, 2026: $250 credit per bike, with a $225 discount to the customer (2024 – 2025 were $500 and $450; halved by the HB23-1272 revenue-forecast trigger)

## Eligibility
- Dealer or financing entity with signed assignments for 2026 sales or leases
- Registered retailer that gave the e-bike discount at the point of sale

## Notes
- Any repayment of a previously claimed amount is not entered here
- Example: bike shop sells 18 qualifying e-bikes in 2026 → 18 × $250 = $4,500

## Required Information
- Assignment records, invoices and leases
- E-bike sales receipts with the discount line

## Questions
- Are you a vehicle dealer or financing entity that accepted assigned credits?
- Are you a registered retailer that sold qualified e-bikes with the discount?
- How many sales qualified in 2026?

## Common Errors
- A vehicle buyer using this line instead of Line 30
- Using the 2025 $500 e-bike amount for 2026

## Prompt
- Where does a dealer claim assigned EV credits?
- My bike shop gave the Colorado e-bike discount.
