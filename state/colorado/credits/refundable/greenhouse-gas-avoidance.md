# Certified Greenhouse Gas Avoidance Credits
## Description
Five refundable credits certified by the Colorado Energy Office for projects that cut greenhouse gas emissions. The credit claimed is the amount on the CEO certificate.

## Amounts
- INDUSTRIAL CLEAN ENERGY (HB23-1272, 2024–2032): 30% of emissions study costs, or 30–50% of emissions-reduction project costs, for industrial facility owners
- GEOTHERMAL ENERGY EXPENDITURE (HB23-1272, 2024–2032): up to 30% of project costs (up to 50% for select projects), $5 million per project, competitive awards
- GEOTHERMAL ENERGY PRODUCTION (HB23-1272, 2024–2032): per kWh of geothermal electricity produced, as certified by CEO
- SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL PRODUCTION: 30% of facility construction cost, up to $2 million for 2026. Repealed for 2027 by HB26-1289 and replaced by a per-gallon purchase credit
- CLEAN HYDROGEN (HB23-1281): $1.00/kg (tier one) or $0.33/kg (tier two), up to $500,000 per taxpayer for 2026–2028. CEO cannot issue certificates until the PUC finalizes emissions accounting rules, so expect few or no 2026 claims

## Eligibility
- Holds a CEO certificate for the 2026 tax year
- Geothermal credits go to building owners, campuses, developers, utilities, governments and similar project owners, NOT individual homeowners

## Notes
- Example: a developer's geothermal district heating project receives a $5,000 Geothermal Energy Expenditure certificate → $5,000 credit

## Required Information
- CEO certificate and credit type
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did the Colorado Energy Office certify a clean energy, geothermal, aviation fuel or hydrogen credit for you?
- Which credit type is on the certificate?

## Common Errors
- A homeowner claiming a geothermal credit for a home heat pump
- Claiming without the certificate
- Missing Industrial Clean Energy recapture

## Prompt
- Where do I claim the Colorado geothermal credit?
- Our plant received an Industrial Clean Energy credit certificate.
