# Gross Conservation Easement Credit
## Description
Credit for donating a perpetual conservation easement on Colorado land (C.R.S. 39-22-522). It reduces tax first; a limited refund is allowed only in years when state revenue exceeds certain thresholds. The credit can be sold to other taxpayers.

## Amounts
- 90% of the easement's fair market value for donations before January 1, 2027 (80% for donations from 2027)
- Cap of $5 million per donation, usable up to $1.5 million a year
- Statewide cap of $50 million a year (2025 – 2031)
- Unused credit carries forward up to 20 years
- REFUND: up to $50,000 per donation, only in years the State Controller certifies revenue above thresholds. Whether a refund is allowed for 2026 is not yet known; DOR posts it in November or December

## Eligibility
- Perpetual conservation easement donated to a qualified holder
- Division of Conservation certificate issued

## Notes
- Example: easement appraised at $500,000 → credit $450,000, usable up to $1.5 million a year, limited by tax unless the 2026 refund year is certified

## Required Information
- Division of Conservation certificate
- Appraisal and Form 8283
- Federal deduction amount for the addback
- Prior-year use of the credit

## Questions
- Did you donate a conservation easement, or buy a conservation easement credit?
- Did you receive the Division of Conservation certificate?
- Did you take a federal charitable deduction for the easement?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the addback of the federal deduction
- Claiming a refund in a year the Controller has not certified
- Using the 80% rate for a 2026 donation

## Prompt
- Where do I claim the Colorado conservation easement credit?
- I donated a conservation easement on my ranch.
