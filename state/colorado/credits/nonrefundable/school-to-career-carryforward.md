# Carryforward of School-to-Career Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for businesses that invested in qualified school-to-career programs (C.R.S. 39-22-520). Repealed by HB24-1036 for tax years after 2024; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years, earliest year first; a 2024 credit can be used through 2029

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have unused school-to-career credit from 2024 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a 2026 credit

## Prompt
- Can I still use my school-to-career credit?
