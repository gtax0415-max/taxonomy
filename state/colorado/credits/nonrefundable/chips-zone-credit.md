# Nonrefundable CHIPS Zone Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credits for semiconductor and related businesses in a Creating Hope and Investing in the Production of Semiconductors (CHIPS) Zone (HB23-1260). Line 19 is used only by taxpayers who also hold a refund certificate; others claim CHIPS Zone credits with enterprise zone credits on line 18.

## Amounts
2026 rates (OEDIT):
- Investment: 3%
- New employee: $1,100 per employee
- Research and development: 3%
- Commercial vehicle: 1.5%
- Job training: 12%
- No vacant building credit in CHIPS Zones
- Unused credit carries forward up to 12 years

## Eligibility
- Business in a designated CHIPS Zone with certified credits for 2026
- Credits available for tax years 2024 – 2035

## Notes
- Electronic filing strongly recommended; attach each certificate
- Example: $5,000 of certified CHIPS credit used against tax → line 19 $5,000, limited with lines 17, 18 and 20 to line 16

## Required Information
- CHIPS Zone credit certificates and any refund certificate
- Prior-year carryforwards
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Do you have a CHIPS Zone credit certificate?
- Do you also hold a refund certificate?

## Common Errors
- Putting the refundable 80% on line 19
- Using line 19 without a refund certificate

## Prompt
- What is the CHIPS Zone credit?
