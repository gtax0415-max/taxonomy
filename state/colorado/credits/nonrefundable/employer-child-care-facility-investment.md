# Employer Child Care Facility Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that provide licensed child care facilities for their employees and invest in equipment for them (C.R.S. 39-22-517(2)).

## Amount
- 10% of qualified investment in tangible personal property used in the facility
- Unused credit carries forward up to 3 years

## Eligibility
- Facility licensed by the Colorado Department of Early Childhood and provided for employees
- Available for tax years before January 1, 2029 (HB24-1036 had ended it after 2025; HB25-1296 extended it)

## Notes
- Real property does not qualify
- Attach the license and the property list
- Example: employer furnishes an on-site center with $30,000 of equipment → credit $3,000

## Required Information
- License
- Property list with cost and date placed in service
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Does your business provide a licensed child care facility for employees?
- What equipment did you buy for it in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Using the 20% rate for operators
- Assuming the credit ended after 2025
- Including real property
