# Nonrefundable Credits
## Description
Colorado nonrefundable credits for 2026: one file per DR 0104CR Part III line (35 to 63; the certificate-number lines 52 and 62 are covered in their credit's file), the Part II credit for tax paid to another state, the column A / column B totals (lines 64 and 65), and the credits entered directly on Form DR 0104 lines 18 to 20. Lines 17 to 20 together cannot exceed the tax on line 16. Carryforward-only lines are marked.

## How the lines flow
- Each credit: column A (available) → column B (used) → line 64 → line 65 (+ line 34) → Form DR 0104, line 17
