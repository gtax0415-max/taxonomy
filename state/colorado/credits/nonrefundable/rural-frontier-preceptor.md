# Rural and Frontier Health Care Preceptor Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for health care professionals who serve as unpaid preceptors for students in rural or frontier areas of Colorado (HB16-1142, extended by HB22-1005 through tax year 2032; amount raised by HB24-1036).

## Amount
- $2,000 per preceptorship, up to 3 a year ($6,000 maximum) — confirmed on the 2026 certification form
- Before 2025 the credit was $1,000 total
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years

## Eligibility
- Preceptor in a rural or frontier area, certified by the teaching institution or AHEC
- Certification emailed to dor_preceptor@state.co.us and confirmed by DOR before filing
- Statewide limit of 300 preceptors a year, first come by email timestamp

## Notes
- Attach the certification to the return
- Example: rural physician completes two preceptorships → $4,000

## Required Information
- Certification and DOR confirmation
- Number of preceptorships

## Questions
- Were you an unpaid preceptor in a rural or frontier area in 2026?
- Did DOR confirm your credit?
- How many preceptorships did you complete?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before DOR confirmation
- Using the old $1,000 amount
