# Employer Assistance for Home Purchase Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for Colorado employers that contribute to employees' home savings accounts to help them buy a home (HB23-1189, C.R.S. 39-22-558). Tax year 2026 is the LAST year.

## Amount
- 5% of the employer's contributions
- Contributions count up to $5,000 per employee
- Maximum $500,000 of contributions per employer per year
- Unused credit carries forward up to 5 years

## Eligibility
- Contributions made in tax years 2024 – 2026 to qualifying employee home savings accounts

## Notes
- The matching employee subtraction also ends after 2026
- Example: employer contributes $20,000 across four employees ($5,000 each) → credit $1,000

## Required Information
- Contributions by employee
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did your business contribute to employee home savings accounts in 2026?
- How much per employee?

## Common Errors
- An employee claiming the employer credit
- Counting more than $5,000 per employee
- Assuming the credit continues after 2026

## Prompt
- What is the employer home purchase credit?
