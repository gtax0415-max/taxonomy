# Rural Jump-Start Zone Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit that eliminates Colorado income tax on income from an approved new business in a Rural Jump-Start Zone, and on new hires' wages from that business (C.R.S. 39-30.5, extended by HB24-1001 through tax years beginning before January 1, 2031).

## Amount
- New business: 100% of the Colorado income tax on income from the zone
- New hire: 100% of the Colorado income tax on wages paid for work in the zone
- Up to 4 consecutive years
- No carryforward

## Eligibility
- Business approved by OEDIT for the Rural Jump-Start program
- New hire holds a certificate from the business

## Notes
- DOR's page describes the new-hire credit as 100% of wages; the statute measures it by the income tax on those wages
- Example: new hire whose Colorado tax on zone wages is $1,200 → $1,200 credit

## Required Information
- OEDIT certificate
- Zone income or wages
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Do you own or work for an approved Rural Jump-Start Zone business?
- Which year of the 4-year benefit is 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the certificate
- Carrying forward unused credit

## Prompt
- What is the Rural Jump-Start credit?
