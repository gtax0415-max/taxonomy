# Homeless Contribution Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to homelessness prevention projects approved by the Colorado Division of Housing (HB22-1083, C.R.S. 39-22-548). HB26-1015 extended it through tax year 2030.

## Amount
- 25% of the contribution (30% for projects in underserved rural areas)
- Monetary gifts of at least $100; in-kind gifts over $5,000
- Maximum $100,000 per taxpayer per year
- Unused credit carries forward up to 5 years

## Eligibility
- Contribution to a project approved by the Division of Housing, directly or through an approved intermediary
- Certificate issued by the nonprofit

## Notes
- The certificate number goes on line 62 and is not added into the totals
- Example: $4,000 to an approved urban project → credit $1,000; certificate HCTC-55120 on line 62

## Required Information
- Certificate and number
- Contribution amount and type
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did you give to an approved homelessness project in 2026?
- Did you receive a tax credit certificate? What is its number?
- Is the project in a rural area?

## Common Errors
- Adding the certificate number into the totals
- Claiming without a certificate

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for donating to homeless programs?
