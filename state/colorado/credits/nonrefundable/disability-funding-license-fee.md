# Carryforward of Disability Funding Committee License Fee Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for buying a uniquely valuable license plate registration number from the Colorado Disability Funding Committee (C.R.S. 39-22-535). Repealed by HB24-1036; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit for tax years 2025 and later
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years, earliest year first

## What it covered while available
- 20% of the purchase price of the registration number

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have unused disability license plate credit from 2024 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit

## Prompt
- What is the disability license plate fee credit carryforward?
