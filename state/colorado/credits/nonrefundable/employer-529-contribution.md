# Employer Contributions to Employee 529 Plan Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that contribute to employees' CollegeInvest (Colorado 529) accounts (contributions in tax years 2019 through those beginning before January 1, 2032).

## Amount
- 20% of the employer's contributions
- Maximum $500 per employee
- Unused credit carries forward up to 3 years; not transferable

## Eligibility
- Contributions made to a CollegeInvest qualified state tuition account owned by an employee

## Notes
- Example: $2,500 contributed to one employee's account → 20% = $500

## Required Information
- Contributions by employee
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did your business contribute to employees' CollegeInvest accounts in 2026?
- How much per employee?

## Common Errors
- An employee claiming this credit
- Exceeding $500 per employee

## Prompt
- Is there a credit for employer 529 contributions?
