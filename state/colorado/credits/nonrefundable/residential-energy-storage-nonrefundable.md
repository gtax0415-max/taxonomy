# Residential Energy Storage Systems Credit (Owner)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an owner who buys and installs a qualifying battery storage system at a Colorado residence and does not assign the credit to the seller (SB22-051, extended by SB25-026).

## Amount
- 10% of the purchase price of the system
- 2026 is the LAST year
- Any amount above the tax is lost: no refund and no carryforward

## Eligibility
- Residential energy storage system installed at a Colorado residence in 2026
- Credit not assigned to the seller

## Notes
- Assigning the credit to the seller usually gets the owner the full value as a discount, because the seller's credit is refundable
- Example: $15,000 system → credit $1,500; tax $1,100 → $1,100 used, $400 lost

## Required Information
- Purchase and installation invoice
- Whether the credit was assigned

## Questions
- Did you install a home battery system in Colorado in 2026?
- Did you assign the credit to the seller?

## Common Errors
- Carrying the unused part forward
- Claiming here after assigning the credit to the seller
