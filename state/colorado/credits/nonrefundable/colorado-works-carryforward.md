# Carryforward of Colorado Works Program Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that hired Colorado Works (TANF) recipients (C.R.S. 39-22-521). Repealed by HB24-1036 for tax years after 2024; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit
- Unused credit carries forward only 3 years, earliest year first; a 2024 credit can be used through 2027, and 2022 or earlier credits have expired

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have unused Colorado Works credit from 2023 or 2024?

## Common Errors
- Carrying it past three years
- Claiming a 2026 credit

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado Works credit carryforward?
