# Enterprise Zone Contribution Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for monetary or in-kind contributions to an enterprise zone administrator or a certified enterprise zone project (C.R.S. 39-30-103.5).

## Amount
- 25% of the value of the contribution
- In-kind contributions can make up no more than 50% of the credit (OEDIT describes this as 12.5% for in-kind gifts)
- Maximum $100,000 per taxpayer per year
- Unused credit carries forward up to 5 years
- Not subject to the new $2 million enterprise zone cap, which applies to the investment-type zone credits

## Eligibility
- Contribution to a project certified by the enterprise zone administrator
- Certification of the contribution issued to the donor

## Notes
- Example: $2,000 cash to a certified enterprise zone project → credit $500

## Required Information
- EZ contribution certification
- Value and type of contribution
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a certified enterprise zone project in 2026?
- Was it cash or in-kind?
- Did you receive the certification from the zone administrator?

## Common Errors
- Reporting it on Form DR 0104, line 18
- Claiming the full in-kind value at 25% without the 50% in-kind limit

## Prompt
- What is the enterprise zone contribution credit?
