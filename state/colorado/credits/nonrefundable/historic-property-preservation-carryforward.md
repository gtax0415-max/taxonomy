# Carryforward of Historic Property Preservation Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rehabilitating historic property under the older law (C.R.S. 39-22-514), which applied to tax years 1991 through 2019. Only carryforwards remain; current credits use the separate Preservation of Historic Structures credit.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit
- Unused credit carries forward up to 10 years; a 2019 credit can be used through 2029

## What it covered while available (1991 – 2019)
- 20% of qualified rehabilitation costs, maximum $50,000 per property

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have historic property preservation credit from 2019 or earlier still unused?

## Common Errors
- Putting a section 39-22-514.5 credit on this line
- Using a credit more than 10 years old

## Prompt
- Where do I claim my old historic preservation credit?
