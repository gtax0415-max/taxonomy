# Carryforward of Plastic Recycling Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for investing in plastic recycling equipment (C.R.S. 39-22-114.5). It ended after tax year 2022; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years, earliest year first; a 2022 credit can be used through 2027

## What it covered while available (through 2022)
- 20% of up to $10,000 of investment, maximum $2,000

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have plastic recycling investment credit left from 2022 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new credit for 2026
- Using a credit from 2020 or earlier, which has expired

## Prompt
- Can I still use my plastic recycling credit?
