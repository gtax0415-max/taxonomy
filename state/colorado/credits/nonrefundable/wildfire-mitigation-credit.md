# Wildfire Mitigation Expenses Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for landowners who pay for wildfire mitigation on their Colorado property (HB22-1007, C.R.S. 39-22-543). HB26-1289 extends it through 2030 and makes it larger and refundable starting in 2027, not 2026.

## Amount
- 2026: 100% of qualified costs, up to $1,000
- No refund and no carryforward for 2026; any credit above tax is lost
- From 2027 (HB26-1289): up to $2,000, refundable, AGI limit $300,000

## Eligibility
- Wildfire mitigation measures on real property in Colorado
- Costs paid to a third-party provider (HB24-1036)
- Federal taxable income at or below the indexed limit ($120,000 base; $129,200 for 2025; DOR will set the 2026 figure after year end)
- HB26-1289 limits the credit to individual landowners starting in 2026 (State Auditor, June 2026); businesses and other entities no longer qualify

## Notes
- Example: federal taxable income $84,000; $1,350 paid to a contractor to clear brush → credit $1,000

## Required Information
- Provider invoices
- Property location
- Federal taxable income

## Questions
- Did you pay a contractor for wildfire mitigation on your Colorado property in 2026?
- What is your federal taxable income?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $1,000
- Carrying the unused amount forward
- Claiming costs for work you did yourself
- Using the 2027 $2,000 amount for 2026

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for wildfire mitigation?
