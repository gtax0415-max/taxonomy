# Carryforward of Live Organ Donation Paid Leave Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that paid employees during leave to donate an organ (HB18-1202, C.R.S. 39-22-540). It expired after tax year 2024; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit for leave paid in 2025 or later
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years, earliest year first; a 2024 credit can be used through 2029

## What it covered while available (2020 – 2024)
- 35% of wages paid during the leave plus the cost of temporary replacement help
- Not available for an employee earning $80,000 or more

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have unused live organ donation leave credit from 2024 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit
- Using a carryforward more than five years old

## Prompt
- Can I use my organ donation leave credit carryforward?
