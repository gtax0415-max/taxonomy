# Child Care Contribution Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for monetary contributions to a qualifying child care facility or program in Colorado (C.R.S. 39-22-121). HB26-1004 extended it through tax years beginning before January 1, 2038.

## Amount
- 50% of qualifying monetary contributions
- Maximum credit $100,000 a year
- Unused credit carries forward up to 5 years

## Eligibility
- Contribution of money to a facility or program licensed by the Department of Early Childhood or Department of Human Services, or registered with DOR
- Certification issued by the recipient

## Notes
- The same gift may also be a federal itemized deduction; Colorado does not require an addback
- Example: $1,000 to a licensed nonprofit preschool → credit $500

## Required Information
- Recipient's certification for each contribution
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did you give money to a licensed child care facility or program in 2026?
- Did the recipient give you a contribution certification?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the recipient's certification
- Claiming non-cash gifts

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for donating to child care?
