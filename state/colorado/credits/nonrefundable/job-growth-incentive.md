# Colorado Job Growth Incentive Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for businesses that create net new jobs under a project approved by the Economic Development Commission. HB26-1014 extended it through tax years beginning before January 1, 2035.

## Amount
- 50% of employer FICA paid on net new jobs, for up to 96 months
- Amount stated on the OEDIT certificate
- Unused credit carries forward up to 10 years

## Eligibility
- EDC-approved project
- Net new jobs maintained as required

## Notes
- Example: OEDIT certifies $12,000 for an approved project passed through a partnership → $12,000 available

## Required Information
- OEDIT certificate
- Prior-year carryforwards
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did your business receive a Job Growth Incentive credit certificate for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without OEDIT certification
- Assuming the credit ended after 2026
