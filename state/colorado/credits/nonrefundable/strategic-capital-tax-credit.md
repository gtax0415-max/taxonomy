# Strategic Capital Tax Credit
## Description
Lets a business that made a strategic capital investment of $100 million or more treat its Job Growth Incentive and certain Enterprise Zone credits as transferable or as 5-year carryforwards (HB17-1356, C.R.S. 24-46-104.3).

## Amount
- The certified amount, limited to $10 million a year during a 10-year benefit period
- Lines 17 – 20 of Form DR 0104 together cannot exceed the tax on line 16
- A transferee gets up to 3 additional years to use the credit

## Eligibility
- Precertified by the Economic Development Commission between July 1, 2017 and June 30, 2020 (no new precertifications)
- Or a transferee holding OEDIT transfer certification

## Notes
- Attach a copy of each certificate
- Example: certificate available $3,000, tax $10,000, no other credits → $3,000

## Required Information
- OEDIT certificates or transfer certification
- Prior use and expiration years
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Do you hold a Strategic Capital Tax Credit certificate or a transferred credit?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the line 16 tax together with lines 17 – 19
- Claiming without the certificates
