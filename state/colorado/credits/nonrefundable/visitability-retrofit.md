# Retrofitting a Residence to Increase Visitability Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for retrofitting a home to make it accessible for a person with an illness, impairment or disability (C.R.S. 39-22-541, extended by SB23-196 through tax year 2028).

## Amount
- The lesser of retrofit costs or $5,000 per residence
- When retrofits are needed for more than one qualifying person (the filer and dependents), up to $5,000 per person
- Unused credit carries forward up to 8 years
- Statewide limit of $1 million of certificates a year, first come, first served

## Eligibility
- Family income of $191,456 or less for modifications completed in 2026 ($150,000 base, adjusted for inflation; $187,109 for 2025)
- Certificate from the Division of Housing

## Notes
- Enter the certificate number on the return and attach a copy
- Example: certificate for $4,200 of ramp and doorway work → $4,200 available

## Required Information
- Division of Housing certificate and number
- Retrofit invoices
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did you retrofit your home for accessibility in 2026?
- Did you receive a Division of Housing certificate?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the certificate number
- Claiming without a certificate

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado credit for home accessibility improvements?
