# Colorado Minimum Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for prior-year Colorado alternative minimum tax, tied to the federal minimum tax credit (C.R.S. 39-22-105). Colorado's AMT (3.47% of Colorado AMT income) continues for 2026.

## Amount
- 12% of the federal minimum tax credit ALLOWED for 2026 on Form 8801
- Limited to the Colorado tax
- No carryforward of the Colorado credit

## Eligibility
- Federal prior-year minimum tax credit allowed for 2026

## Notes
- HB26-1221 would have ended this credit after 2025, but it died in committee; the credit continues for 2026
- Enter both the federal credit and the Colorado credit on the line
- Example: federal credit allowed $5,000 → Colorado credit $600

## Required Information
- Federal Form 8801 (credit allowed)
- Residency status

## Questions
- Did you use a federal prior-year minimum tax credit on Form 8801 for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Taking 12% of the federal carryforward instead of the credit allowed
- Leaving the federal credit field blank

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado AMT credit?
