# CHFA Housing Credits
## Description
Three nonrefundable credits for owners of an interest in a qualified housing development. CHFA allocates each credit and determines the amount.

## Amounts
AFFORDABLE HOUSING CREDIT (state low-income housing credit):
- Tax years 2015 – 2036; claimed over a 6-year credit period
- 2026 allocation caps: $10 million standard plus $12 million accelerated (70% in the first year)
- Unused credit carries forward up to 11 years

TRANSIT-ORIENTED COMMUNITIES CREDIT (HB24-1434):
- Tax years 2025 – 2034; 5-year credit period
- 2026 allocation cap $2 million
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years

MIDDLE-INCOME HOUSING CREDIT (HB24-1316):
- Allocations 2025 – 2029; tax years 2025 – 2033; 5-year credit period
- 2026 allocation cap $5 million
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years; recapture applies

## Eligibility
- Direct or indirect ownership interest in a CHFA-certified development
- Amount as shown on the CHFA certificate and the owner's allocation

## Notes
- Attach the CHFA certification
- Example: a partner's share of a CHFA-certified affordable housing credit is $8,000 → available $8,000, used up to the tax

## Required Information
- CHFA certificate and credit type
- Owner's share from the K-1 or allocation statement
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Do you hold an interest in a CHFA-certified housing development?
- Which housing credit is it?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the CHFA certification
- Missing recapture when the development falls out of compliance
