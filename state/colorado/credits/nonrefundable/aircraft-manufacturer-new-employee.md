# Carryforward of Aircraft Manufacturer New Employee Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for aircraft manufacturers that hired new employees in an aviation development zone (C.R.S. 39-35-104). It ended after tax year 2022; only carryforwards remain.

## Status for 2026 — carryforward only
- No new credit
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years, earliest year first; a 2022 credit can be used through 2027

## What it covered while available (2006 – 2022)
- $1,200 per new employee

## Required Information
- Original credit amount and year
- Amounts used in prior years

## Questions
- Do you have aircraft manufacturer new employee credit left from 2022 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit
- Using a credit from 2020 or earlier, which has expired

## Prompt
- Can I still use the aircraft manufacturer credit?
