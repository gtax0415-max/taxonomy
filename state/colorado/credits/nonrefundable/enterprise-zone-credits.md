# Enterprise Zone Credits
## Description
Nonrefundable credits for businesses that invest, hire, train or do research in a designated Colorado enterprise zone. Each credit must be certified through the EZ Tax Credit system before it can be claimed.

## Amounts
2026 rates (DOR Enterprise Zone Tax Guide, April 2026):

| Credit | 2026 rate | Carryforward |
|---|---|---|
| Investment | 3% of qualified investment | 14 years |
| Commercial vehicle | 1.5% | per guide |
| Job training | 12% of training costs | per guide |
| Research and development | 3% of increased R&D, taken 25% a year over 4 years | unlimited |
| Vacant building rehabilitation | 25%, up to $50,000 | 5 years |
| New employee | $1,100 each; enhanced rural $2,000; agricultural processing $500 | 5 / 7 years |
| Employer health insurance | $1,000 per covered new employee | 5 years |

NEW FOR 2026 (HB25-1296):
- A taxpayer's total enterprise zone credit is capped at $2 million a year unless the Economic Development Commission grants a waiver
- No credit for property used in retail gasoline or diesel sales or in wireless telecommunications facilities

Unchanged: the $750,000 annual usage limit on credits used against tax.

## Eligibility
- Business activity located in an enterprise zone
- Pre-certified before the activity and certified after year end

## Notes
- HB26-1289 changes several of these credits starting in 2027 (commercial vehicle credit ends, new employee and R&D limits), not 2026
- Electronic filing strongly recommended; attach each certificate
- Example: $80,000 of qualified equipment → investment credit $2,400 (3%)

## Required Information
- Final certifications
- Supporting cost and payroll records
- Prior-year carryforwards
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Does your business operate in a Colorado enterprise zone?
- Did you pre-certify and certify your 2026 credits?
- Do you have enterprise zone credits carried forward?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the $2 million cap without an EDC waiver
- Claiming the enterprise zone contribution credit here
- Claiming without certification

## Prompt
- Where do enterprise zone credits go on the Colorado return?
- My business bought equipment in an enterprise zone.
