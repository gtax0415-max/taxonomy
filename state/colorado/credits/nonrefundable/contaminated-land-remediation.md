# Remediation of Contaminated Land Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable, transferable credit for approved environmental cleanup of contaminated property in Colorado (extended by HB24-1116 through tax years beginning before January 1, 2030).

## Amount
- 40% of the first $750,000 of certified costs plus 30% of the next $750,000 — maximum $525,000
- Rural sites: 50% and 40% — maximum $675,000
- Costs above $1.5 million per project earn nothing
- Statewide limit of $5 million a year, $2 million reserved for rural sites
- Unused credit carries forward up to 5 years

## Eligibility
- Owner of the property, or transferee of the credit
- Costs and any transfer certified by CDPHE

## Notes
- A transfer agreement must be filed with CDPHE within 30 days
- Example: $400,000 of certified non-rural costs → 40% = $160,000

## Required Information
- CDPHE certificate
- Transfer agreement, if any
- Prior-year carryforwards
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Did you clean up contaminated land under a CDPHE-approved plan, or buy a remediation credit?
- Is the site rural?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without CDPHE certification
- Using old rates (50% of the first $100,000)

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado brownfield credit?
