# Advanced Industry Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for investing in a qualified small business in an advanced industry (C.R.S. 39-22-532 and 24-48.5-112). HB25-1157 extended it through 2031.

## Amount
- 25% of the qualified investment, or 35% in a rural or economically distressed area
- Maximum $100,000 per investment; minimum investment $10,000
- Statewide pool of $4 million for 2026 ($2.5 million a year for 2027 – 2031)
- Unused credit carries forward up to five years; not transferable

## Eligibility
- Investment certified by OEDIT
- The credit equals the amount on the certificate

## Notes
- Example: $100,000 investment in a non-rural advanced industry company → 25% = $25,000

## Required Information
- OEDIT certificate
- Investment records
- Colorado K-1 for any allocated share

## Questions
- Did you invest in an OEDIT-certified advanced industry company in 2026?
- Is the company in a rural or economically distressed area?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the certificate
- Using the old 25%/30% rates and $50,000 cap

## Prompt
- What is the Colorado advanced industry investment credit?
