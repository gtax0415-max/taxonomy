# Long-Term Care Insurance Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for Colorado residents who pay long-term care insurance premiums (C.R.S. 39-22-122). No end date in law; available for 2026.

## Amount
- 25% of premiums paid during the year
- Maximum $150 per policy
- No carryforward

## Eligibility
- Paid premiums on a long-term care insurance policy in 2026
- Federal TAXABLE income (not AGI) under the limit for the filing status and number of policies

## Notes
- Example: joint filers, federal taxable income $78,000, two policies with premiums $1,400 and $900 → $150 + $150 = $300

## Required Information
- Premium statements
- Number of policies and who is covered
- Federal taxable income

## Questions
- Did you pay long-term care insurance premiums in 2026?
- How many policies, and who do they cover?
- What is your federal taxable income?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $150 per policy
- Using AGI instead of federal taxable income for the limit

## Prompt
- Does Colorado give a credit for long-term care insurance?
