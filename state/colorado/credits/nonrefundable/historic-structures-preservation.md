# Preservation of Historic Structures Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rehabilitating a designated historic building in Colorado (C.R.S. 39-22-514.5, extended by HB24-1314 through tax years beginning before January 1, 2037, with reservations through 2032). Commercial credits are transferable; residential credits are not.

## Amounts
COMMERCIAL (OEDIT), 2026:

| Location | First $2 million of costs | Costs above $2 million |
|---|---|---|
| Urban | 25% | 20% |
| Rural | 35% | 30% |

- Plus 5% for deed-restricted affordable housing; the former 5% disaster-area increase no longer applies
- Caps: $1 million per property per year ($1.5 million in the housing pool); $15 million statewide a year

RESIDENTIAL (History Colorado or Certified Local Government), 2026:
- 20% of qualified costs (35% rural)
- Cap $100,000 per residence for applications from 2025

BOTH:
- Unused credit carries forward up to 10 years
- Residential credits become refundable starting 2027, not 2026

## Eligibility
- Certified historic structure
- Rehabilitation approved and completed; certificate issued

## Notes
- The certificate number is entered on line 52 and is not added into the totals
- Example: urban commercial rehabilitation, $600,000 of qualified costs → 25% = $150,000; certificate HS-2026-0142 on line 52

## Required Information
- Certificate and number
- Transfer documents, if any
- Prior-year carryforwards
- Colorado K-1 for any pass-through share

## Questions
- Do you have a historic structures credit certificate? What is the certificate number?
- Is the property commercial or residential, urban or rural?
- Do you have credit carried forward?

## Common Errors
- Adding the certificate number into the totals
- Claiming a section 39-22-514 credit here
- Applying the old disaster-area increase

- How do I claim the historic structures credit?
