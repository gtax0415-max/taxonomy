# Child Care Center Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for operators of a licensed child care center, family child care home or foster care home who invest in tangible personal property used in the business (C.R.S. 39-22-517(1)).

## Amount
- 20% of qualified investment in tangible personal property
- Unused credit carries forward up to 3 years

## Eligibility
- Licensed by the Colorado Department of Early Childhood
- Property is depreciable tangible personal property used in the center or home
- Available for tax years before January 1, 2029 (HB24-1036 had ended it after 2025; HB25-1296 extended it)

## Notes
- Real property does not qualify
- Attach the license and the property list
- Example: family child care home buys $5,000 of qualifying equipment → credit $1,000

## Required Information
- License
- Property list with cost and date placed in service

## Questions
- Do you operate a licensed child care center or home?
- What equipment or furnishings did you buy for it in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Including real property
- Assuming the credit ended after 2025
- Omitting the license copy
