# Credits
## Description
Colorado credits for 2026 in two groups.

NONREFUNDABLE (subfolder nonrefundable/): reduce tax on Form DR 0104 lines 17 to 20 and together cannot exceed line 16. They include DR 0104CR Part III (lines 35 to 65), the Part II credit for tax paid to another state (lines 27 to 34), and the enterprise zone, CHIPS Zone and Strategic Capital credits on lines 18 to 20.

REFUNDABLE (subfolder refundable/): treated like payments. DR 0104CR Part I (lines 1 to 26) totals on Form DR 0104 line 31; the conservation easement (line 29), innovative vehicle (line 30) and dealer / e-bike retailer (line 32) credits go directly on Form DR 0104.

## 2026 highlights
- Not available: business personal property, early childhood educator, family affordability, low-income child care Part IV
- Changed: Colorado EITC 25%; child care credit 70% of federal; innovative vehicle $750; e-bike $250; heat pump credit halved for existing buildings; enterprise zone $2 million cap; wildfire credit limited to individuals
- Last year: alternative transportation, residential energy storage, employer home purchase assistance
- Extended: Job Growth Incentive (2034), child care contribution (2037), homeless contribution (2030), child care facility credits (2028)

## How the lines flow
- DR 0104CR line 65 → Form DR 0104 line 17; enterprise zone → line 18; CHIPS Zone → line 19; Strategic Capital → line 20
- DR 0104CR line 26 → Form DR 0104 line 31; conservation easement → line 29; innovative vehicle → line 30; dealer / e-bike → line 32
