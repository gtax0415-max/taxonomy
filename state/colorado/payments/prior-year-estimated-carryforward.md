# Prior-Year Estimated Tax Carryforward
## Description
The part of the 2025 overpayment that the taxpayer chose to apply to 2026 estimated tax instead of receiving as a refund.

## Amount
- The amount on the 2025 return's estimated tax credit carryforward line (line 41), as accepted by DOR

## Notes
- The 2025 instructions point to "line 38" of the prior-year return; that is the TABOR refund line. The carryforward is line 41
- Example: the 2025 return applied $500 to 2026 → line 25 $500

## Required Information
- 2025 carryforward amount
- Any DOR adjustment

## Questions
- Did you apply part of your 2025 Colorado refund to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Entering the full 2025 refund instead of the amount applied
- Following the instructions' line 38 reference

## Prompt
- Where do I enter last year's refund applied to this year?
