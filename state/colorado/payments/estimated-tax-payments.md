# Estimated Tax Payments
## Description
Quarterly Colorado estimated tax payments made for 2026.

## Amount
- Sum of 2026 estimated payments
- 2026 due dates: April 15, June 15 and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027
- 2026 tax rate: 4.4%

## Notes
- Safe harbor to avoid the penalty: pay the lesser of 70% of 2026 tax, or 100% of 2025 tax (110% if 2025 AGI was over $150,000)
- The dates of each payment matter for the penalty
- Check the total in Revenue Online before filing
- Example: $800 paid on April 14, June 12 and September 15, 2026 and January 14, 2027 → line 26 $3,200

## Required Information
- Amount and date of each payment

## Questions
- What estimated payments did you make for 2026, and on what dates?
- Are you a farmer or fisherman?

## Common Errors
- Including the extension payment on line 26
- Omitting the January 2027 payment
- Including the prior-year overpayment applied (line 25)
