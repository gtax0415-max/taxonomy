# Extension Payment
## Description
Payment made by the original due date when the return will be filed later. Colorado gives an automatic 6-month extension to FILE, but not to PAY.

## Amount
- Amount paid with the extension voucher or online as an extension payment
- To avoid the late-payment penalty, at least 90% of the 2026 tax must be paid by April 15, 2027

## Notes
- The extended filing deadline for 2026 returns is October 15, 2027
- Example: $500 paid online on April 13, 2027 → line 27 $500

## Required Information
- Amount and date of the extension payment

## Questions
- Did you make an extension payment for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Entering the extension payment as an estimated payment
- Assuming the extension also extends the time to pay
