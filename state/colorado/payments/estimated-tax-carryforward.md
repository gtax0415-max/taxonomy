# Estimated Tax Credit Carryforward to 2027
## Description
Part or all of the 2026 overpayment can be applied to 2027 estimated tax instead of being refunded.

## Amount
- Any amount up to the overpayment on line 40

## Notes
- The amount becomes the prior-year carryforward on the 2027 return
- Example: line 40 $1,500; $1,000 applied to 2027 → line 41 $1,000; line 42 $500

## Required Information
- Amount to apply

## Questions
- Do you want to apply any of your 2026 overpayment to 2027 estimated tax?

## Common Errors
- Carrying forward more than line 40

## Prompt
- Can I apply my Colorado refund to next year?
