# Other Prepayments (Real Estate and Estate or Trust Withholding)
## Description
Colorado tax paid on the taxpayer's behalf by a closing agent on a real estate sale, or by an estate or trust for a nonresident beneficiary.

## Amount
- REAL ESTATE: the lesser of 2% of the sales price or the net proceeds, withheld from nonresident sellers on sales over $100,000 (unless the seller signed an exemption affirmation)
- ESTATE OR TRUST: 4.4% of the nonresident beneficiary's Colorado share
- Enter the total actually paid in 2026 for the 2026 tax year

## Notes
- Mark each box that applies
- Example: trust paid $350 of Colorado tax for a nonresident beneficiary → line 28 $350, estate/trust box marked

## Required Information
- Closing statement and withholding copy
- Estate or trust statement or K-1

## Questions
- Did you sell Colorado real estate in 2026 as a nonresident, and was tax withheld at closing?
- Did an estate or trust pay Colorado tax on your share?

## Common Errors
- Not marking the box
- Entering real estate withholding on line 24

## Prompt
- Where do I claim Colorado real estate withholding?
