# Deceased Taxpayer
## Description
A surviving spouse or personal representative files the final Colorado return for a person who died during the year or before filing.

## Notes
- Use the same filing status as the federal return
- Claim every credit, subtraction and deduction the decedent was entitled to
- Age tests for a person who died in 2026 use the age reached at death
- There is no TABOR refund for 2026, so TABOR eligibility at death does not matter this year
- Example: Paul died August 3, 2026; his wife files jointly, marks Deceased for Paul, signs "Filing as Surviving Spouse," and attaches the death certificate with the refund claim

## Required Information
- Date of death
- Death certificate
- Who is filing and their authority

## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die during 2026 or before filing?
- Who is filing: the surviving spouse or a personal representative?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the death certificate with a refund claim
- Using a different filing status than the federal return

## Prompt
- How do I file a Colorado return for my husband who died this year?
