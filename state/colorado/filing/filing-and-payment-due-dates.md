# Due Dates, Extension and Payment
## Description
Key dates for 2026 Colorado individual returns.

## Dates for tax year 2026
- Return and payment due: April 15, 2027
- Automatic extension to file: October 15, 2027 (no extension to pay)
- Abroad on April 15: filing due June 15, 2027
- 2027 estimated tax: required when expected 2027 tax after credits and withholding is over $1,000

## Notes
- Revenue Online accepts returns as timely until midnight; mailed returns must be postmarked by the due date
- The extension payment is figured as expected tax minus withholding, estimates and other payments
- Example: expected 2026 tax $3,400, withholding $2,900 → pay $500 by April 15, 2027; enter it on line 27 when filing in September 2027

## Required Information
- Expected tax and payments to date

## Questions
- Will you file by April 15, 2027?
- Do you expect to owe tax when you file?

## Common Errors
- Treating the filing extension as an extension to pay
- Sending a paper voucher when the payment was made online
