# Third Party Designee, Signatures, Paid Preparer and Mailing
## Description
The return must be signed under penalty of perjury. The filer can also let a named person (often the preparer) talk to DOR about this return.

## Notes
- The designee can discuss return and refund status, request copies of notices and transcripts, and respond to questions; the authority ends four years after signing and can be revoked
- File through Revenue Online or approved software, or mail all required pages; the ZIP codes are exclusive to DOR, so no street address is needed
- Example: couple uses a paid preparer and authorizes her to answer DOR questions → Designee Yes with her name and phone; both spouses sign; preparer completes the preparer block

## Required Information
- Signatures and dates
- Designee details
- Preparer details

## Questions
- Do you want to let someone else discuss this return with DOR?
- Did a paid preparer prepare your return?

## Common Errors
- Only one spouse signing a joint return
- Mailing without all required pages

## Prompt
- Who can talk to the Colorado Department of Revenue about my return?
