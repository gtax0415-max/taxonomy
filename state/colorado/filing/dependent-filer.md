# Filer Claimed as a Dependent on Another Return
## Description
A person who can be claimed as someone else's dependent files a Colorado return like any other filer. The federal dependent standard deduction limit is already built into federal taxable income.

## Notes
- The TABOR refund age test (18 or older on January 1) does not matter for 2026 because there is no TABOR refund
- Example: 17-year-old earns $6,000 of W-2 wages with $120 Colorado withholding → federal taxable income $0 → Colorado tax $0 → $120 refunded

## Required Information
- Federal taxable income
- W-2 Colorado withholding

## Questions
- Can someone claim you as a dependent for 2026?
- Did you have Colorado tax withheld?

## Common Errors
- Not filing to recover Colorado withholding
- Claiming a TABOR amount for 2026

## Prompt
- My teenager had a summer job; does she file a Colorado return?
