# Filing Status
## Description
Colorado uses the same filing status as the federal return. There are no status boxes on the Colorado form; the status shows through whether spouse information is entered.

## Where status matters in 2026
- High-earner deduction addback: $1,000 single / separate / head of household; $2,000 joint (AGI over $300,000)
- QBI addback threshold: $500,000 / $1,000,000 joint
- Social Security subtraction ages 55 – 64: $75,000 single / $95,000 joint
- Child tax credit and disability assistance credit tables
- Child care credit: married filing separately generally cannot claim it (follows the federal Form 2441 rule)
- DOR instructions do not say which column a qualifying surviving spouse uses

## Notes
- Example: spouses file a joint 2026 Form 1040 with Maya listed first → Maya in "Your" fields and Leo in the spouse fields; the joint $2,000 deduction cap applies

## Required Information
- Federal filing status
- Order of spouses on the federal return

## Questions
- What filing status did you use on your 2026 federal return?
- If joint, who is listed first?

## Common Errors
- Filing jointly in Colorado after filing separately federally
- Listing the spouse on a separate return
- Reversing the order of spouses

## Prompt
- Can I file single in Colorado if I filed jointly federally?
