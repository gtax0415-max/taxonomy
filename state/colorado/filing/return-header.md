# Taxpayer, Spouse and Contact Information
## Description
The top of the return identifies the filer and spouse. Dates of birth matter because they drive the age tests for the Social Security, pension and military retirement subtractions.

## Notes
- Age tests for 2026: 65 or older on December 31, 2026 = born 1961 or earlier; 55 – 64 = born 1962 – 1971; under 55 = born 1972 or later
- Example: Ana Ruiz, born 04/22/1988, files single with an ITIN from Pueblo; page 1 shows her name, date of birth and ITIN, page 2 her address and phone

## Required Information
- Names, dates of birth, SSN or ITIN, mailing address, phone

## Questions
- Do your names and numbers match your Social Security cards or ITIN letters?
- What is your current mailing address and phone number?

## Common Errors
- A different SSN on later pages than on page 1
- Leaving the date of birth blank
