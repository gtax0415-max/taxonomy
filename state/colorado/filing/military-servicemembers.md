# Military Servicemembers and Spouses
## Description
A servicemember's state of legal residence does not change just because of a duty assignment. Colorado residents in the military remain Colorado residents unless they formally change their legal residence.

## Notes
- No 2025 or 2026 change found
- Military retirement subtraction for under-55 retirees: $15,000 (confirmed for 2026)
- Example: Colorado-domiciled Army sergeant stationed in Texas all of 2026 → full-year Colorado return, all military pay included

## Required Information
- State of legal residence
- Days stationed outside the U.S.
- Spouse's residency and employment

## Questions
- Is Colorado your state of legal residence on DD Form 2058?
- Were you stationed outside the United States 305 days or more in 2026?
- Are you a nonresident military spouse working in Colorado?

## Common Errors
- Assuming pay is exempt for a Colorado-domiciled member stationed elsewhere
- Subtracting active duty pay on Other Subtractions

## Prompt
- I'm in the military and stationed out of state; do I file in Colorado?
