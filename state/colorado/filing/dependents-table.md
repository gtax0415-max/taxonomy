# Dependents Table
## Description
List every dependent claimed on the federal return. The table drives the Colorado child tax credit, the EITC and the child care credit.

## Notes
- Each dependent's SSN or ITIN must be issued by the due date including extensions, unless the Deceased box is marked
- The Child Tax Credit box goes on dependents under 17; the Colorado child tax credit itself is paid only for children under 6 (born 2021 or later)
- The Family Affordability Tax Credit for older children is $0 for 2026
- Example: Lily (born 2019) and Max (born 2022) both get the Child Tax Credit box; only Max counts toward the 2026 Colorado child tax credit

## Required Information
- Name, SSN or ITIN, and year of birth of each dependent
- Whether each meets the child tax credit tests

## Questions
- Who did you claim as dependents on your federal return?
- What are their SSNs or ITINs and years of birth?
- Did each child live with you more than half of 2026?

## Common Errors
- Marking the Child Tax Credit box for a 17-year-old
- Leaving off a released child when claiming the Colorado EITC for that child
- Listing six dependents on a paper return

## Prompt
- Which dependents do I list on my Colorado return?
