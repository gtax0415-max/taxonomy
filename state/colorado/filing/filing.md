# Filing
## Description
Everything on the return that is not an income, tax, credit or payment amount: who must file, filing status, residency, taxpayer and spouse information, ID fields, deceased boxes, the dependents table, the health coverage Easy Enrollment box, dependent filers, military servicemembers, the third party designee and signatures, due dates and payments, amended returns, rounding, and the 2026 law changes.

## How the pages flow
- Page 1 → residency, names, dates of birth, SSN or ITIN, Deceased box, ID fields
- Page 2 → contact information and dependents table → child tax credit, EITC, child care credit
- Page 3 → health coverage box; line 1 begins
- Pages 7 and 8 → direct deposit, third party designee, signatures, paid preparer, mailing
