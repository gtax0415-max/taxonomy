# Health Coverage Easy Enrollment Box
## Description
Colorado residents can use the return to ask the State to check whether uninsured household members qualify for free or reduced-cost health coverage. The box does not change any tax amount.

## Notes
- The form asks for household contact information, household size, federal AGI and each member's name, date of birth and (optionally) SSN or ITIN
- No change found for 2026
- Example: single parent whose 10-year-old has no insurance checks the box; household size 2, AGI $41,200, both members listed

## Required Information
- Household members and federal AGI

## Questions
- Does anyone in your household lack health coverage?
- Do you want the State to check if you qualify for free or reduced-cost coverage?

## Common Errors
- Checking the box without including the Easy Enrollment form
- Expecting the box to affect the refund

## Prompt
- What is the health coverage box on the Colorado return?
