# Whole Dollars and Negative Amounts
## Description
Colorado returns use whole dollars. Federal taxable income and federal AGI can be negative; subtractions cannot.

## Notes
- Round each amount to the nearest dollar; amount boxes print ".00"
- Percentages (for example the credit for tax paid to another state) are figured to four decimals (xxx.xxxx)
- Payment amounts are also rounded to the nearest dollar
- Example: federal taxable income −$1,234 (net operating loss year) → line 1 ($1,234); tax cannot go below $0

## Required Information
- None

## Questions
- Is your federal taxable income or AGI negative?

## Common Errors
- Entering cents
- Entering a negative subtraction

## Prompt
- Do I round to whole dollars on the Colorado return?
