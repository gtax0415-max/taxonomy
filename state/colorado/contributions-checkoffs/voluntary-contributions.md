# Voluntary Contributions
## Description
Filers can donate all or part of their refund to Colorado charitable funds. A donation only comes out of a refund; it never adds to tax owed.

## Notes
- This is the 2025 list. No 2026 check-off bill was found and DOR reportedly plans no new funds for 2026, but the 2026 schedule is not published, so the list is not confirmed
- Gifts made from a 2026 refund are made in 2027 for federal charitable deduction purposes
- Example: refund $660; $25 to the Pet Overpopulation Fund and $50 to Feeding Colorado → total $75; net refund $585

## Required Information
- Funds and amounts chosen

## Questions
- Would you like to donate part of your refund to any Colorado fund? How much to each?

## Common Errors
- Donating more than the refund
- Leaving out the registration number for "Donate to a Colorado Nonprofit"

## Prompt
- Can I donate my Colorado refund to charity?
