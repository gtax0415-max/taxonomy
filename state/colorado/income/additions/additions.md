# Additions to Federal Taxable Income
## Description
Colorado additions on Form DR 0104 lines 2 to 9: state income tax (2), QBI deduction (3), standard or itemized deduction for AGI over $300,000 (4), business meals (5), nonqualified CollegeInvest distributions (6), nonqualified ABLE distributions (7), reserved (8), and other additions with an explanation (9). Line 10 is the subtotal.

## 2026 changes
- Standard or itemized deduction addback: caps cut to $1,000 / $2,000 (Proposition MM)
- Qualified overtime deduction added back (HB25-1296); placed on Other Additions until the 2026 form assigns a line
- QBI addback made permanent (HB25B-1001)
- Business meals addback continues through 2030 (HB23-1008)
- No addback for the federal tips, car loan interest or senior deductions

## How the lines flow
- Lines 1 to 7 and 9 → line 10
- Line 9 bond interest → line 36 (modified AGI)
