# Forfeited Employer Home Savings Account Contributions
## Description
Employees who subtracted an employer's home savings account contribution in an earlier year must add it back when the contribution is forfeited (for example, on leaving the job early).

## Amount
- The full forfeited contribution that was previously subtracted

## Notes
- The subtraction covers 2024 – 2026, so forfeitures of those contributions can still occur in later years
- Example: employee subtracted $3,000 in 2025 and forfeited it on leaving the job in 2026 → add $3,000

## Required Information
- Forfeited amount and year originally subtracted

## Questions
- Did you forfeit any employer home savings contributions you subtracted before?

## Common Errors
- Adding back only the earnings instead of the full contribution

## Prompt
- What if I forfeit an employer home savings contribution?
