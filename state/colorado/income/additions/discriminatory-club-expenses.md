# Expenses at Clubs That Restrict Membership
## Description
Colorado disallows business deductions for spending at, or payments to, clubs that discriminate in membership.

## Amount
- The amount deducted federally for expenditures at or payments to such a club

## Notes
- Example: business owner deducted $1,400 of client entertainment at a men-only club → add $1,400

## Required Information
- Club expenses deducted federally

## Questions
- Did you deduct any expenses at a club that restricts membership?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the addback because the expense was a business deduction

## Prompt
- Are club expenses deductible in Colorado?
