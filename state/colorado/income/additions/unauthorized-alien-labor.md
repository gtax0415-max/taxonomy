# Unauthorized Alien Labor Expenses
## Description
Colorado disallows business deductions for wages and other pay for labor services of workers not authorized to work in the United States. The amount deducted federally is added back.

## Amount
- The disallowed labor expense deducted on the federal return, directly or through a pass-through entity

## Notes
- DOR's Individual Income Tax Guide explains which expenses are disallowed
- Example: landscaping sole proprietor deducted $12,000 for labor the Guide treats as unauthorized alien labor → add $12,000

## Required Information
- Labor costs deducted and worker authorization records

## Questions
- Did your business deduct pay for workers not authorized to work in the United States?

## Common Errors
- Leaving it out because the worker was paid as a contractor

- What is the unauthorized alien labor addition in Colorado?
