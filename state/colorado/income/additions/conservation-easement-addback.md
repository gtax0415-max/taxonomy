# Conservation Easement Deduction Addback
## Description
A donor who claims the Colorado gross conservation easement credit cannot also keep the federal charitable deduction for the same easement in Colorado income. The federal deduction is added back.

## Amount
- The federal charitable deduction for the easement taken in 2026 (including amounts carried forward federally and used in 2026)

## Notes
- Example: rancher deducts $60,000 for an easement on Schedule A and claims the Colorado credit for the same easement → add $60,000

## Required Information
- Federal deduction for the easement in 2026
- Colorado credit certificate

## Questions
- Did you donate a conservation easement and claim the Colorado credit for it?
- How much of the federal deduction did you use in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Keeping both the federal deduction and the Colorado credit without the addback

## Prompt
- Do I add back my conservation easement deduction in Colorado?
