# Business Meals Addback
## Description
Colorado disallows the federal deduction for business meals under IRC 274(k) for tax years 2024 through 2030 (HB23-1008). The whole deductible amount is added back, not just a restaurant-meal portion.

## Amount
- The full business meals deduction claimed federally (Schedule C line 24b), plus any amount on a Colorado K-1

## Notes
- An earlier law (HB21-1311) added back only the extra 50% restaurant-meal deduction, and only for 2022; that rule no longer applies
- Example: consultant deducts $1,150 of business meals on Schedule C → add $1,150

## Required Information
- Meals deducted federally
- K-1 meals additions

## Questions
- Did you deduct business meals on Schedule C?
- Does any Colorado K-1 show a business meals addition?

## Common Errors
- Entering total meals spent instead of the amount deducted (usually 50%)
- Adding back only half the deduction

## Prompt
- Do I add back business meals on my Colorado return?
