# Non-Colorado State and Local Bond Interest
## Description
Interest on municipal bonds is tax-exempt federally, but Colorado taxes interest from other states' bonds.

## Amount
- Interest from non-Colorado state and local bonds, minus bond premium amortization and expenses allocable to the interest
- Exempt-interest dividends from a fund count to the extent they come from non-Colorado bonds

## Notes
- Also enter the bond interest in the modified AGI section (line 36)
- Example: Form 1099-INT box 8 $3,000: $1,100 from 2015 Denver Water bonds and $1,900 from California bonds with $100 premium amortization → add $1,800

## Required Information
- Tax-exempt interest by issuer state
- Premium amortization

## Questions
- Did you receive tax-exempt interest?
- From which states' bonds, and when were any Colorado bonds issued?

## Common Errors
- Adding back Colorado bond interest
- Leaving the amount out of line 36

## Prompt
- Is municipal bond interest taxable in Colorado?
