# Nonqualified CollegeInvest Distributions
## Description
When CollegeInvest money is withdrawn for non-qualified purposes, Colorado recaptures the contributions that were subtracted in earlier years.

## Amount
- The nonqualified distribution, refund or withdrawal, figured under DOR's CollegeInvest guidance

## Notes
- Example: parent withdraws $4,000 to buy a car after subtracting contributions in earlier years → add the nonqualified amount figured under DOR's guidance

## Required Information
- 1099-Q
- Use of the funds
- Prior subtractions

## Questions
- Did you take money out of a CollegeInvest account for something other than college costs?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out a nonqualified withdrawal because federal tax was paid on the earnings

## Prompt
- What happens in Colorado if I withdraw from a 529 for non-college costs?
