# Other Additions
## Description
Catch-all line for Colorado additions that have no line of their own. Each item must be named in the explanation.

## Amount
- Sum of the items listed in routing

## Notes
- First-time home buyer savings account withdrawals not used for a home are recaptured; DOR says to use "the applicable line," which is not confirmed
- Example: joint filers with $1,800 of Texas municipal bond interest and a $6,400 overtime deduction → $8,200, explained "Other state bond interest $1,800; qualified overtime deduction $6,400"

## Required Information
- Amount and description of each item

## Questions
- Do you have any of the additions listed?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the explanation blank
- Including Colorado municipal bond interest

## Prompt
- What goes on Colorado's Other Additions line?
