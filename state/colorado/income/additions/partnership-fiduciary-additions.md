# Partnership, S Corporation and Fiduciary Additions
## Description
Owners of partnerships and S corporations, and beneficiaries of estates and trusts, pick up the Colorado additions passed through to them.

## Amount
- Other additions shown on the Colorado K-1
- Fiduciary adjustments that increase income

## Notes
- Keep the K-1s with the return records
- Example: Colorado K-1 shows $2,300 of other additions → add $2,300, explained "Partnership/Fiduciary"

## Required Information
- Colorado K-1s and fiduciary statements

## Questions
- Did you receive a Colorado K-1 or fiduciary statement showing Colorado additions?

## Common Errors
- Putting the K-1 state income tax share here instead of on line 2
- Putting K-1 business meals here instead of on line 5

## Prompt
- Where do I report Colorado K-1 additions?
