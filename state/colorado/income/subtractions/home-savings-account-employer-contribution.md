# Employer Contribution to Home Savings Account
## Description
Employees can subtract an employer's contribution to their home savings account when the contribution qualified for the employer assistance for home purchase credit (HB23-1189). Tax year 2026 is the LAST year.

## Amount
- The employer contribution included in federal taxable income

## Eligibility
- Contribution made in tax years 2024 – 2026 to a qualifying home savings account
- Contribution qualified for the employer's credit

## Notes
- Example: employer deposits $2,500 into the employee's home savings account and includes it in W-2 wages → subtract $2,500

## Required Information
- W-2 and account statement

## Questions
- Did your employer contribute to a home savings account for you in 2026?
- Was it included in your W-2 wages?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a contribution that was not taxable federally
- Assuming the subtraction continues after 2026

## Prompt
- Is my employer's home savings contribution taxable in Colorado?
