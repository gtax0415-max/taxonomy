# Qualified Reservation Income
## Description
Income earned on a reservation by an enrolled member of that tribe who lives there is not subject to Colorado tax.

## Amount
- Income derived wholly from reservation sources, to the extent included in federal taxable income

## Eligibility
- Enrolled tribal member
- Lives on the reservation
- Income wholly from reservation sources

## Notes
- Proof of membership, residence and income source is submitted every three years
- Example: enrolled Southern Ute member living on the reservation earns $48,000 from a tribal enterprise on the reservation → subtract $48,000

## Required Information
- Enrollment proof
- Residence proof
- Income source documents

## Questions
- Are you an enrolled tribal member living on the reservation?
- Is your income from sources on the reservation?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting off-reservation wages

## Prompt
- Is reservation income taxed in Colorado?
