# HB21-1002 CARES Act Subtraction Carryforward
## Description
HB21-1002 let taxpayers subtract, starting in 2021, amounts they had to add back in 2020 because Colorado decoupled from certain retroactive CARES Act provisions (NOL, business interest and excess business loss rules). Any part not used carries forward until used up.

## Amount
- 2026: the remaining balance, limited to Colorado taxable income
- The $150,000 annual cap that applied for 2022 – 2025 no longer applies from 2026 (2021 cap was $300,000)
- No expiration; applied to the earliest year possible and after all other subtractions

## Notes
- Example: $40,000 of unused balance remains after 2025, and Colorado taxable income before this subtraction is $90,000 → subtract $40,000

## Required Information
- Original 2021 subtraction amount
- Amounts used in each year since

## Questions
- Do you have an HB21-1002 subtraction balance left from 2021?

## Common Errors
- Entering a new CARES Act amount
- Still applying the $150,000 cap in 2026

## Prompt
- What is the HB21-1002 subtraction?
