# Reacquisition of Colorado Residency During Active Duty
## Description
Servicemembers with Colorado as their home of record who moved their legal residency to another state and later reacquired Colorado residency may subtract active duty pay received after reacquiring Colorado residency.

## Amount
- Active duty pay included in federal taxable income, received after the date Colorado residency was reacquired

## Eligibility
- Colorado is the military home of record
- Acquired legal residency in another state after enlisting
- Reacquired Colorado residency on or after January 1, 2016

## Notes
- Attach the home-of-record form and the residency evidence for the year
- Example: Navy officer with a Colorado home of record became a Florida resident in 2018 and re-registered to vote in Colorado on March 1, 2026 → subtract active duty pay from March 1 to December 31, 2026 ($58,000)

## Required Information
- Home-of-record document
- Residency evidence and date
- Active duty pay after that date

## Questions
- Is Colorado your military home of record?
- Did you reacquire Colorado residency in 2026, and on what date?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the required evidence
- Subtracting pay received before residency was reacquired

## Prompt
- I re-established Colorado residency while on active duty; is my pay exempt?
