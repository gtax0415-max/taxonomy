# Other Subtractions
## Description
Catch-all line for allowable Colorado subtractions that have no line of their own on the subtractions schedule. Each amount must be explained.

## Amount
- Sum of allowable subtractions not listed elsewhere, each included in federal taxable income

## Notes
- DOR's Income Tax Subtractions page lists what qualifies
- The 2026 subtractions schedule has not been published; the two new 2026 subtractions are placed here until it is
- Example: a former AmeriCorps member has a $3,700 education award in federal taxable income → $3,700, explained as "Segal AmeriCorps Education Award"

## Required Information
- Amount and description of each subtraction
- Document showing the amount was taxed federally

## Questions
- Do you have any Colorado subtraction not listed on its own line?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the explanation blank
- Subtracting out-of-state income or an NOL
- Subtracting an amount that was not in federal taxable income

## Prompt
- What can go on Colorado's other subtractions line?
