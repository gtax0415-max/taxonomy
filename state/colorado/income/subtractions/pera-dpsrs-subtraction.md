# PERA / DPSRS Subtraction
## Description
PERA and Denver Public Schools retirement contributions made in 1984 – 1986 were taxed by Colorado when made. Retirees subtract those previously taxed contributions as they are recovered through pension payments, so they are not taxed twice.

## Amount
- The previously taxed contributions recovered in 2026, per the PERA statement

## Notes
- Attach the PERA statement
- Example: retired teacher's PERA statement shows $1,320 of previously taxed contributions recovered in 2026 → subtract $1,320

## Required Information
- PERA statement of previously taxed contributions

## Questions
- Did you contribute to PERA between mid-1984 and 1986, or to DPSRS in 1986?

## Common Errors
- Entering current-year contributions

## Prompt
- What is the PERA subtraction?
