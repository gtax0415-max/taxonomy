# Segal AmeriCorps Education Award Subtraction
## Description
New Colorado subtraction for Segal AmeriCorps Education Awards received for AmeriCorps national service and used during the year (HB24-1240, C.R.S. 39-22-104(4)(cc)). Available for tax years 2026 through 2033.

## Amount
- The amount of the award used during 2026

## Eligibility
- Award earned through AmeriCorps national service
- Award used in 2026 (paid toward tuition or qualified student loans)

## Notes
- The statute does not expressly limit the subtraction to amounts in federal taxable income; DOR guidance is pending. To avoid a double benefit, subtract only amounts reported as income federally unless DOR says otherwise
- The 2026 subtractions schedule is not published; report under Other Subtractions with an explanation until it is
- Example: $3,700 of award paid toward student loans and reported as income federally → subtract $3,700

## Required Information
- AmeriCorps Form 1099 and payment history

## Questions
- Did you use a Segal AmeriCorps Education Award in 2026?
- Was it reported as income on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for an award used before 2026

## Prompt
- Is my AmeriCorps education award taxed in Colorado?
