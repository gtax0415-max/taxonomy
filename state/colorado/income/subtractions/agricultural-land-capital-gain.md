# Colorado Agricultural Land Capital Gain Subtraction
## Description
Farmers who sell Colorado agricultural land can subtract part of the capital gain.

## Amount
- Capital gain from the sale included in federal taxable income, up to $100,000

## Eligibility
- Required to file federal Schedule F
- Land is agricultural land in Colorado

## Notes
- Complete DR 1316 and attach it with the property tax statement, Schedule F and Schedule D or Form 4797
- Example: farmer sells 80 acres of Colorado farmland for a $140,000 long-term gain → subtract $100,000

## Required Information
- Gain on the sale
- Schedule F
- Property tax statement and closing statement

## Questions
- Do you file Schedule F?
- Did you sell Colorado agricultural land in 2026, and what was the gain?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the required attachments
- Claiming without a Schedule F

## Prompt
- Is there a Colorado capital gain subtraction for farmland?
