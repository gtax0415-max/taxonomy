# CollegeInvest Contribution Subtraction
## Description
Subtraction for contributions to Colorado's CollegeInvest 529 college savings program.

## Amount
- Contributions up to the per-beneficiary limit
- 2026 limit: $26,200 single / $39,200 joint per beneficiary (published by CollegeInvest; DOR has not yet published the 2026 figure). 2025 was $25,400 / $38,100
- Married filing separately and head of household use the single limit
- The limit is shared with Colorado ABLE contributions for the same beneficiary

## Notes
- Complete the owner fields even if you or your spouse own the account
- Example: joint filers contribute $6,000 to a CollegeInvest account for their daughter → subtract $6,000

## Required Information
- Owner name and SSN, beneficiary, contribution totals

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a CollegeInvest account in 2026? For which beneficiaries and how much?
- Did you also contribute to a Colorado ABLE account for the same person?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting contributions to another state's 529 plan
- Subtracting an employer's contribution
- Using the 2025 limit for 2026

## Prompt
- Can I deduct my 529 contributions in Colorado?
