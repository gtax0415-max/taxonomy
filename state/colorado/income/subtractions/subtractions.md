# Subtractions From Income
## Description
Colorado subtractions are figured on the DR 0104AD schedule and the total goes to Form DR 0104 line 11. They include the state tax refund, U.S. government interest, Social Security, pension and annuity, military retirement under 55, agricultural land capital gain, CollegeInvest and ABLE contributions, the non-itemizer charitable subtraction, reservation income, PERA/DPSRS, railroad benefits, the marijuana and natural medicine business deduction, military residency reacquisition, employer home savings contributions, FAMLI benefits, other subtractions and the HB21-1002 carryforward. No negative amounts.

## 2026 changes
- New: Segal AmeriCorps Education Award (HB24-1240) and first responder death benefit (SB25-310), on Other Subtractions until the 2026 schedule assigns lines
- Limits: CollegeInvest / ABLE $26,200 single / $39,200 joint per beneficiary (CollegeInvest figure; DOR not yet published)
- HB21-1002 carryforward: the $150,000 annual cap ends; limited only by Colorado taxable income
- Last year: employer home savings account contribution
- Marijuana: medical marijuana moved to Schedule III in April 2026, which may shrink the 280E subtraction for medical businesses
- Open: how the non-itemizer charitable subtraction interacts with the new federal non-itemizer deduction
- Related 2026 additions to cross-check: overtime (HB25-1296) and the QBI addback (HB25B-1001)

## How the lines flow
- DR 0104AD lines 1 to 21 → subtotal → Form DR 0104 line 11
- Each person's Social Security subtraction reduces that person's pension limit
