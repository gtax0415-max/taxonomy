# First Responder Death Benefit Subtraction
## Description
New Colorado subtraction starting with tax year 2026 for the state death benefit paid to survivors of a first responder killed in the line of duty (SB25-310, implementing Proposition 130; C.R.S. 39-22-104(4)(ee)).

## Amount
- The amount received under C.R.S. 24-33.5-122(4) (a $1 million benefit) to the extent included in federal taxable income
- No separate cap

## Eligibility
- Received the state first responder death benefit in 2026
- The amount was not excluded federally under IRC 101(h)

## Notes
- DOR has not yet published the 2026 subtractions schedule, so the line is not confirmed; report under Other Subtractions with an explanation until it is
- Example: surviving spouse receives the state death benefit and $X of it is included in federal taxable income → subtract $X; if the whole amount was excluded federally, subtract nothing

## Required Information
- Benefit amount and payer statement
- Amount included in federal taxable income

## Questions
- Did you receive a death benefit because a first responder died in the line of duty?
- Was any of it taxed on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a benefit that was not in federal taxable income
- Subtracting other survivor payments not paid under the state benefit

## Prompt
- Are first responder survivor benefits taxed in Colorado?
