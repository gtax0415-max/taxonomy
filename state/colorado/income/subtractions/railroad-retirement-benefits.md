# Railroad Retirement Benefit Subtraction
## Description
Railroad retirement benefits are exempt from state tax under federal law, so Colorado subtracts the full taxable amount.

## Amount
- All railroad retirement benefits included in federal taxable income, with no age or dollar limit

## Notes
- Attach all RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R statements
- Example: retired conductor with $21,000 of taxable railroad retirement benefits → subtract $21,000

## Required Information
- RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R

## Questions
- Did you receive railroad retirement benefits in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Also claiming the same benefits on the Social Security or pension lines

## Prompt
- Are railroad retirement benefits taxed in Colorado?
