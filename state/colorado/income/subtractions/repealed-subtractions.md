# Repealed Subtractions
## Description
HB24-1036 removed several old subtractions that were claimed on the Other Subtractions line. None of them can be claimed for 2026.

## Status for 2026 — not available
- Nonresident disaster relief worker subtraction: repealed
- Medical savings account deduction: repealed
- Catastrophic health insurance deduction: repealed
- First-time home buyer savings account subtraction: no subtraction for 2025 or later; non-qualified withdrawals are still added back as an Other Addition
- Wildfire mitigation subtraction: last year was 2024. The separate wildfire mitigation credit continues (up to $1,000 for 2026)

## Notes
- Example: a landowner who paid $3,000 for wildfire mitigation in 2026 cannot subtract it; the wildfire mitigation credit may apply instead

## Required Information
- None

## Questions
- Did you plan to claim a medical savings account, first-time home buyer savings, or wildfire mitigation deduction?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a repealed subtraction on Other Subtractions
- Forgetting the addback for a non-qualified first-time home buyer account withdrawal
