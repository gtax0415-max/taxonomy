# FAMLI Benefits Subtraction
## Description
Colorado does not tax Family and Medical Leave Insurance (FAMLI) paid leave benefits. Any amount included in federal taxable income is subtracted.

## Amount
- FAMLI benefits included in federal taxable income, limited to the amount on Schedule 1 line 7

## Notes
- Example: Schedule 1 line 7 shows $9,600 — $6,000 FAMLI and $3,600 unemployment → subtract $6,000

## Required Information
- FAMLI 1099-G
- Schedule 1 line 7 breakdown

## Questions
- Did you receive FAMLI paid leave benefits in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting unemployment benefits

## Prompt
- Are FAMLI benefits taxed in Colorado?
