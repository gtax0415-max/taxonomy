# Military Retirement Subtraction, Under Age 55
## Description
Retired servicemembers under 55 can subtract part of their military retirement pay (HB18-1060, extended by HB23-1084 through 2028).

## Amount
- Lesser of $15,000 or taxable military retirement pay, per person (confirmed for 2026)

## Eligibility
- Age 54 or younger on December 31, 2026
- Each spouse qualifies separately

## Notes
- Attach the 1099-R
- Example: retired Air Force member age 46 with $31,000 of taxable military retirement → subtract $15,000

## Required Information
- 1099-R and age of each recipient

## Questions
- Do you receive military retirement pay?
- How old were you on December 31, 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming both this and the pension subtraction on the same pay
- Claiming at age 55 or older

## Prompt
- Is military retirement taxed in Colorado?
