# U.S. Government Interest
## Description
States cannot tax interest on U.S. government obligations. Colorado removes that interest from federal taxable income.

## Amount
- Interest from U.S. obligations included in federal taxable income

## Notes
- Example: Form 1099-INT box 3 shows $2,150 of Series EE savings bond and Treasury interest → subtract $2,150

## Required Information
- Forms 1099-INT and 1099-DIV
- Fund percentage of U.S. obligation income

## Questions
- Did you receive interest from U.S. savings bonds or Treasury securities?
- Do your mutual funds report a U.S. government obligation percentage?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting Fannie Mae or Ginnie Mae interest
- Subtracting savings bond interest already excluded federally for education
- Subtracting 100% of mutual fund dividends

## Prompt
- Is Treasury interest taxed in Colorado?
