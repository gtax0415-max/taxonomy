# Income: Federal Taxable Income, Additions, Subtractions and Colorado Taxable Income
## Description
Colorado starts from federal taxable income (line 1), adds the Colorado additions on lines 2 to 9 (subfolder additions/), subtracts the subtractions from the DR 0104AD schedule on line 11 (subfolder subtractions/) and arrives at Colorado taxable income on line 12. Federal deductions taken below AGI flow through line 1 unless Colorado requires an addback. The 2026 tax rate is 4.4%.

## How the lines flow
- Form 1040, line 15 → line 1
- Lines 2 to 7 and 9 → line 10 (line 8 reserved)
- DR 0104AD subtotal → line 11
- Line 10 − line 11 → line 12 → tax

## 2026 notes
- New subtractions for 2026: first responder death benefits (SB25-310) and Segal AmeriCorps Education Awards (HB24-1240); until the 2026 schedule assigns lines, they go on Other Subtractions
- The QBI deduction addback (line 3) for AGI over $500,000 ($1,000,000 joint) is permanent from 2026 (HB25B-1001); a SALT Parity election still requires the full addback
