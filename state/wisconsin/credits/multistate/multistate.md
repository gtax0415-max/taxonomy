# Multistate Credits
## Description
Credits that prevent double taxation when Wisconsin residents also pay income tax to another state.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Net Income Tax Paid to Another State (credit-for-tax-paid-to-other-states-os.md) — Schedule OS → Form 1, Line 20. Nonrefundable, no carryforward. 79,592 claims, $369.7 million used for tax year 2024 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234)
## Related
- Reciprocity (Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan wages) — handled by refund from the other state, not by credit (Wisconsin Publication 121)
- Manufacturing and agriculture credits cannot be based on the same income — see ../manufacturing-agriculture/
## Prompt
- I live in Wisconsin and paid tax to Minnesota.
