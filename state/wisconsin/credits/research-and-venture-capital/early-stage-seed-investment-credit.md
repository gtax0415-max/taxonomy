# Early Stage Seed Investment Credit (Schedule VC, Part II)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of 25% of investments paid to a WEDC-certified FUND MANAGER that the manager invests in qualified new business ventures. Available to individuals, corporations, and pass-through owners (unlike the angel credit, which is individuals only).
## How it works
- Line 7d qualified investment × 25% (or specially allocated amount) = Line 8
- + pass-through (9d) + purchased credits (10) = Line 11
- + carryforward (12) − credits sold (14) = Line 15
## 2026 parameters
Rate 25%; 15-year carryforward; 3-year holding period with the same recapture exceptions as the angel credit; transfers allowed only with prior fund manager authorization, notice to WEDC and DOR, once per 12 months, fee up to 5%. No 2026 change identified.
## Notes
- The fund manager's initial investment in the QNBV must follow the manager's certification
- BASIS REDUCTION applies
- Capital gain rules for sellers and purchasers of transferred credits match the angel credit
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- WEDC certifications for the business and fund manager; fund manager statement; K-1 copies if passed through; Schedule CF
## Questions
- Did you invest through a WEDC-certified fund manager in 2026?
- Have you bought or sold seed credits?
## Common Errors
- Treating a direct investment as a seed credit (it is the angel credit)
- Missing the fund manager statement
## Prompt
- I invested in a Wisconsin venture fund.
