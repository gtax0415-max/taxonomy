# Wisconsin Research Credits (Schedule R)
## Description
Credit for increasing qualified research expenses (QREs) incurred IN Wisconsin. Statutory formula (not the federal one), but QREs are defined by IRC section 41 as adopted by Wisconsin. Up to 25% of the current-year credit is refundable; the rest carries forward 50 years (2025 Wis. Act 220).
## How it works
| Lines | Step |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | Wisconsin research wages, supplies, computer rental, 65% of contract research (75% to a qualified consortium), orphan drug QREs |
| 7-8 | Remove wages used for the development zones credit = Wisconsin research expenses |
| 9-11 | Subtract 50% of the average of the prior 3 years' Wisconsin QREs |
| 12-14 | × rate |
| 15-16 | + pass-through credits = total credit |
| 17 | 25% of Line 16 = maximum refundable |
| 18-21 | Credit used against tax first; refundable = lesser of Line 17 or the unused remainder (Line 19) |
| 22-23 | + carryforward = total nonrefundable |
## 2026 parameters
| Research type | Rate | Rate if no QREs in any of the prior 3 years |
|---|---|---|
| General qualified research | 5.75% | 2.875% |
| Internal combustion engines | 11.5% | 5.75% |
| Certain energy efficient products | 11.5% | 5.75% |
| Refundable share | up to 25% of current-year credit (carryforwards are not refundable) | |
| Carryforward | 50 YEARS — extended from 15 by 2025 Wis. Act 220 (effective April 10, 2026) for unused research credits not expired as of that date | |
Rates unchanged for 2026; the carryforward change is the 2026 difference.
## Wisconsin vs. federal definitions
- Wisconsin uses IRC section 41 as of December 31, 2022, excluding 41(f)(1), (2), (5), (6), and (h); the 100% contract-research rate for small businesses/universities/federal labs (41(b)(3)(D)(i)) is NOT adopted
- Wisconsin did not adopt TCJA section 13206 or 2025 federal section 70302 changes to sections 41, 174, and 280C — pre-2022 expensing rules continue for Wisconsin, so research expense treatment differs from federal and may need a Wisconsin modification
- Expenses must be incurred IN Wisconsin; out-of-state spending cannot be allocated in, though mixed expenses may be reasonably allocated
## Notes
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- Short years are annualized; acquisitions/dispositions of a major portion of a business require QRE adjustments
- Combined groups may share nonrefundable research credits (Form 6CS)
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Wisconsin QRE detail by category; prior 3 years' Wisconsin QREs
- Separate Schedule R per credit type; K-1 copies for pass-throughs; Schedule CF
## Questions
- What qualified research did you perform in Wisconsin in 2026, and what did it cost?
- Did you have Wisconsin QREs in each of 2023, 2024, and 2025?
- Is any research related to internal combustion engines or energy efficient products?
## Common Errors
- Using federal QREs (including out-of-state) instead of Wisconsin-only QREs
- Applying federal section 174/174A treatment for Wisconsin
- Treating carryforwards as refundable
- Combining credit types on one Schedule R
## Prompt
- We have engineers doing product development in Wisconsin.
- My K-1 shows a Wisconsin research credit.
