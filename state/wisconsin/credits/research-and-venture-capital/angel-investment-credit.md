# Angel Investment Credit — Individuals Only (Schedule VC, Part I)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of 25% of a cash investment made directly by an accredited "angel investor" (or through an angel investment network) in a WEDC-certified qualified new business venture (QNBV).
## How it works
- Line 1d qualified investments verified by WEDC as FIRST eligible for a credit in the tax year × 25% = Line 2
- + network pass-through (3d) + credits bought (3e) = Line 4 current credit
- + carryforward (5) − credits sold (5a) = Line 6 available
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rate | 25% of qualified investment |
| Carryforward | 15 years (a purchaser keeps the seller's remaining period) |
| Holding period | 3 years or the credit is recaptured |
| Transfer fee | WEDC may charge up to 5%; one transfer per credit per 12 months |
## Who qualifies
- ACCREDITED INVESTOR investing own money; owns or controls less than 20% of the QNBV, and is not a close relative of a 20% owner; meets a wealth/income/insider/sophistication test at the time of investment
- Investment must be cash for equity (stock, partnership/membership interest) acceptable to WEDC
- QNBV (WEDC certified, annually): Wisconsin headquarters; fewer than 100 employees with at least 51% in Wisconsin; innovation or differentiating technology; in operation in Wisconsin no more than 10 consecutive years; not more than $12 million of seed-credit investment or $10 million of prior private equity; excludes real estate, finance, professional services, retail/wholesale, hospitality, transportation, construction, and similar
## Notes
- BASIS REDUCTION: the credit reduces the investment's basis even if not usable
- RECAPTURE (reported on Form 1, line 26): repay the credit if the investment is held under 3 years — unless WEDC finds it became worthless, or it was held at least 12 months and a bona fide liquidity event occurred
- Transfers: seller recognizes capital gain (usually price received, basis zero); purchaser recognizes gain when the credit offsets tax; purchased credits cannot be used in years before purchase
- Not the same as a CREDIT IS INCOME credit — instead it reduces basis
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- WEDC QNBV certification and tax credit verification form (or K-1 copy)
- Investment dates and amounts; transfer documents for bought/sold credits
## Questions
- Did WEDC verify your investment as first eligible for a credit in 2026?
- Do you own 20% or more of the company, or are you related to someone who does?
- Have you sold the investment within 3 years?
## Common Errors
- Claiming before WEDC verification or for a non-certified company
- Not reducing basis
- Missing recapture after an early sale
## Prompt
- I invested in a Wisconsin startup through an angel group.
