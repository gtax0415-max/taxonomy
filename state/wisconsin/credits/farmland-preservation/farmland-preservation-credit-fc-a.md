# Farmland Preservation Credit — Schedule FC-A (post-2009 / zoning)
## Description
Refundable per-acre credit for farmland in a certified farmland preservation zoning district, or under a farmland preservation agreement entered into (or modified) on or after July 1, 2009. Unlike Schedule FC, it does NOT depend on household income or property tax amount.
## How it works
| Acre category | Rate per qualifying acre | Line |
|---|---|---|
| In a farmland preservation zoning district AND under a post-July 1, 2009 agreement | $12.50 | Lines 7-8 |
| In a farmland preservation zoning district, NOT under a post-2009 agreement | $10.00 | Lines 9-10 |
| In a farmland preservation area under a purchased agricultural easement (sec. 93.73) or a post-2009 agreement, NOT in a zoning district | $10.00 | Lines 11-12 |
Acres per category are multiplied by your ownership percentage (four decimals) and rounded to whole acres on the Qualifying Acres Schedule (one per farm, page 4 if more than 7 parcels).
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rates | $12.50 / $10 / $10 per acre (statutory, not indexed) |
| Property taxes | 2026 taxes paid, or legally owed, on the qualifying acres |
| Gross farm revenues test (each farm) | $6,000 in 2026, or $18,000 total for 2024-2026 |
| Filing deadline (calendar year) | April 15, 2031 |
## Eligibility
- Full-year Wisconsin resident (corporations organized in Wisconsin)
- A DATCP certificate of compliance for EACH farm (attach copies unless the instructions' exception applies)
- Paid, or legally responsible for, the 2026 property taxes on the qualifying acres
- Gross farm revenues test met by each farm (a renter's revenues count; name them in Question 6)
## Notes
- Cannot be claimed on the same acres as Schedule FC
- The FC-A credit received is income and is reported in FEDERAL AGI for the year received, so no separate Wisconsin addition is needed (Schedule AD instructions, line 13) — unlike Schedule FC
- Not available if you or your spouse claim the homestead credit or the veterans and surviving spouses property tax credit for the same year
- Ownership is tested at the END of the taxable year
- Household members who each qualify may either each claim their own ownership share or designate one member to claim the household's share; disputes go to the Secretary of Revenue
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Certificate of compliance number(s) per county
- Parcel numbers and acres by category for each farm
- Ownership percentage of each farm
## Questions
- Is your land in a farmland preservation zoning district?
- Do you have a post-2009 agreement or a purchased agricultural easement?
- Do you have a current certificate of compliance?
- How many qualifying acres per category, and what share do you own?
## Common Errors
- Using total acres instead of ownership-adjusted acres
- Applying $12.50 to zoned acres that have no post-2009 agreement
- Missing the certificate of compliance
- Claiming acres also used on Schedule FC
## Prompt
- My farm is in a farmland preservation zoning district.
- I signed a farmland preservation agreement in 2015.
