# Farmland Preservation Credits
## Description
Refundable Wisconsin credits for owners of farmland that is protected by a farmland preservation agreement or located in a certified farmland preservation zoning district. There are two separate schedules with completely different formulas.
## Credits in this folder
- Farmland Preservation Credit — Schedule FC (farmland-preservation-credit-fc.md) — agreements entered into BEFORE July 1, 2009. Income- and property-tax-based formula → Form 1, Line 31a. Refundable
- Farmland Preservation Credit — Schedule FC-A (farmland-preservation-credit-fc-a.md) — agreements entered into on or after July 1, 2009, or land in a farmland preservation zoning district or area. Per-acre formula → Form 1, Line 31b. Refundable
## Which schedule
- A claimant may file FC, FC-A, or BOTH — but never on the same acreage under both
- A pre-July 1, 2009 agreement can be MODIFIED so the land moves to FC-A (contact DATCP)
- An agreement whose application reached the county clerk between January 1, 2008 and June 30, 2009 counts as "pre-July 1, 2009" even if signed later
## What changed for 2026
No statutory change to either formula was identified for 2026. The FC tables and the FC-A per-acre rates are fixed in statute and are not indexed. Year references roll forward: 2026 residency, 2026 property taxes, 2025 taxes paid in full (FC), and a 2024-2026 gross farm profits lookback.
## Related
- Homestead credit and the veterans and surviving spouses property tax credit — claiming either blocks Schedule FC for the same year (see ../property-tax-relief/homestead-credit.md)
- The credit received is INCOME: Schedule FC credits are added on Schedule AD, line 13 to the extent not in federal AGI; Schedule FC-A credits are reported in federal AGI
## Questions
- Do you own at least 35 acres of Wisconsin farmland?
- Is it under a farmland preservation agreement, and when was the agreement signed?
- Is it in a farmland preservation zoning district?
- Did the land produce at least $6,000 of gross farm profits?
## Prompt
- I own a farm in Wisconsin under a farmland preservation agreement.
- Can I get a credit for my farmland property taxes?
