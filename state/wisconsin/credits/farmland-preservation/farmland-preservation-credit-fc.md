# Farmland Preservation Credit — Schedule FC (pre-July 1, 2009 agreements)
## Description
Refundable credit computed from household income and property taxes on farmland subject to a farmland preservation agreement entered into before July 1, 2009. The "old law" credit: smaller household income produces a larger credit.
## How it works
1. Line 11a: net 2026 property taxes on the claim farmland (your household's ownership share only)
2. Line 11b: smaller of Line 11a or $6,000
3. Line 10: household income (Wisconsin income plus add-backs on Lines 9a-9z)
4. Line 12: Table 1 amount from household income
5. Line 13: Line 11b minus Line 12 (not below zero)
6. Line 14: Table 2 amount from Line 13
7. Line 15a: 80% of Line 14 (or Line 15b for mixed percentages using Worksheet 1)
8. Line 16: 10% special minimum credit = 10% of Line 11b
9. Line 17: the LARGEST of Lines 15a, 15b, and 16
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Property tax cap (Line 11b) | $6,000 |
| Table 1: household income producing $0 on Line 12 | under $5,000 |
| Table 1: household income at which Line 12 reaches the full $6,000 | $36,622 or more |
| Table 2: maximum Line 14 | $4,200 (Line 13 of $6,000 or more) |
| Maximum regular credit at 80% | $3,360 |
| 10% special minimum credit | up to $600, available REGARDLESS of income |
| Minimum acreage | 35 acres |
| Gross farm profits test | $6,000 in 2026, or $18,000 total for 2024-2026 |
| Prior-year taxes paid in full | 2025 property taxes |
| Agreement in effect on | July 1, 2026 |
| Filing deadline (calendar year) | April 15, 2031 (4 years after unextended due date) |
Table values come from the 2025 instructions and are statutory (not indexed); confirm against the 2026 Table 1 and Table 2. Because Table 1 exhausts the $6,000 cap at about $36,622 of household income, most claimants above that level receive only the 10% special minimum credit.
## Credit percentage
- 100% — county with a certified agricultural preservation plan AND exclusive agricultural zoning, both certified before July 1, 2009 and in effect December 31, 2026
- 80% — subject to a pre-July 1, 2009 agreement in effect July 1, 2026 (the usual case, Line 15a)
- 70% — exclusive agricultural zoning certified before July 1, 2009 in a county with no agricultural preservation plan as of July 1, 2009
- Mixed parcels → Worksheet 1, Line 15b
## Eligibility
- Owner of the farmland (or household member) during the 2026 taxable year
- Full-year Wisconsin resident; corporations must be organized in Wisconsin
- Neither you nor your spouse claimed the HOMESTEAD credit or the VETERANS AND SURVIVING SPOUSES PROPERTY TAX credit for 2026
- 2025 property taxes on all claim farmland paid in full
- At least 35 acres (before rounding)
- Gross farm profits test met — OR at least 35 acres enrolled in the Conservation Reserve Program during 2026
- No notice of noncompliance with a soil and water conservation plan (unless cancelled before the filing deadline)
- Only ONE household member may claim
## Household income — add-backs that surprise people
Household income starts from Wisconsin Form 1, Line 7 and adds back items excluded from taxable income, including: depreciation (farm depreciation over $25,000 plus ALL nonfarm depreciation, with 33¢ per mile standard mileage treated as depreciation), nonfarm business losses beyond their depreciation, nontaxable capital gains, capital loss carryforwards, 401(k)/457 deferrals (W-2 Box 12 codes D, E, F, G, H, S, Y), IRA/SEP/SIMPLE deductions, nontaxable pension and IRA distributions (except rollovers), tax-exempt and U.S. bond interest, nontaxable Social Security and SSI (including Medicare premiums withheld), child support, public assistance, unemployment, workers' compensation, and farm income of dependents under 18.
## Notes
- The credit received is INCOME for the year received; any part not already in federal AGI is added on Schedule AD, line 13 (Schedule M, line 13) — see ../../income/additions/credits-reported-as-income.md. The prior-year credit received also reduces property taxes used for the school property tax credit
- Co-owned land: Question 4 reports TOTAL acres, but Line 11a reports only your ownership share of taxes
- Line 18 certification: if the agreement was filed with a prior claim and is unchanged, you must notify the County Land Conservation Committee before filing and check Line 18
- Contract number: the 5-digit DATCP agreement number is required (new requirement on the 2025 form)
- Wisconsin e-file no longer supports Schedule FC; use modernized e-file (MeF) software or paper
- Nonfilers can claim by filing a Form 1 with only name, address, and the Line 31a credit, plus an income schedule
- Reckless or fraudulent claims: up to 10 years ineligible for ANY refundable credit
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- 2026 property tax bills (signed treasurer printouts acceptable; not stubs or checks)
- Copy of the executed farmland preservation agreement (and extension, transfer, Exhibit A as applicable)
- Ownership verification if co-owned or bought/sold during the year
- Treasurer statement if 2025 taxes show as unpaid on the bills
- All household income items for Lines 8-9
- Worksheets 1-3 as applicable
## Questions
- When was your farmland preservation agreement entered into, and what is its 5-digit number?
- How many acres, and what share do you own?
- What were the net 2026 property taxes on those acres?
- Were 2025 property taxes paid in full?
- Did you or your spouse claim homestead or veterans property tax credit?
- What was total household income, including nontaxable items?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than your ownership share of property taxes
- Leaving out nontaxable income (Social Security, pension, deferrals) from household income
- Not adding back nonfarm depreciation or mileage depreciation
- Claiming the same acres on both FC and FC-A
- Forgetting to report last year's credit as income
