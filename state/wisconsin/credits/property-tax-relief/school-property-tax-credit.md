# Renter's and Homeowner's School Property Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rent or property taxes paid on your principal residence. The most-claimed Wisconsin credit: 2,056,906 claims, $466.4 million for tax year 2024 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234). Available whether or not you claim homestead credit.
## Amounts (statutory tables; same for 2026)
| Credit | Formula built into the table | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Renter — heat included | about 2.4% of rent (20% of rent treated as tax × 12%) | $300 at rent of $12,500+ |
| Renter — heat not included | about 3% of rent (25% × 12%) | $300 at rent of $10,000+ |
| Homeowner | 12% of property taxes | $300 at taxes of $2,500+ |
| Combined renter + homeowner | | $300 ($150 married filing separately or married HOH, each spouse using own rent/taxes) |
## Rules
- Rent: only for your principal residence; exclude rent for tax-exempt housing (dorms, nonprofit senior housing, public housing that pays no PILOT), business-use rent, and the value of food or services; share of rent only if living with others
- Property taxes: those PAID in 2026 (date received by the municipality) on your principal home, excluding special assessments, service charges, delinquent interest, business/rental/farm portions, second homes; mobile home permit fees count; reduce by any refund of taxes such as a farmland preservation credit received
- Joint owners use their ownership share; bought/sold homes follow the closing statement (else months of ownership)
- Not allowed if you or your spouse claim the veterans and surviving spouses property tax credit
