# Property Tax Relief Credits
## Description
Refundable Wisconsin credits that offset property taxes (or the share of rent treated as property tax) for lower-income households.
## Credits in this folder
- Homestead Credit (homestead-credit.md) — Schedule H or H-EZ, with Rent Certificate for renters → Form 1, Line 33 (Form 1NPR, Line 63). Refundable, maximum $1,168 (2025 tables)
- School Property Tax Credit (school-property-tax-credit.md) — Form 1, Lines 16a-16b. Up to $300. Nonrefundable
- Veterans and Surviving Spouses Property Tax Credit (veterans-and-surviving-spouses-property-tax-credit.md) — Form 1, Line 34. Refundable
## Interactions
- Claiming homestead or veterans property tax credit for the year BLOCKS Schedule FC farmland preservation credit (see ../farmland-preservation/farmland-preservation-credit-fc.md)
- The retirement income subtraction (age 67+) blocks homestead credit for that year

- Do you rent or own your Wisconsin home?
- What is your total household income, including nontaxable income?
## Prompt
- I rent an apartment in Wisconsin and have low income.
- Can I get money back for my property taxes?
