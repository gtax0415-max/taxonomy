# Eligible Veterans and Surviving Spouses Property Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit equal to the property taxes PAID during the year on the principal dwelling of a 100%-disabled Wisconsin veteran or an eligible unremarried surviving spouse. 17,474 claims, $68.1 million for tax year 2024 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234).
## Eligibility (certified by the Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs)
- Eligible veteran: honorable active-duty service; Wisconsin resident at entry or for any consecutive 5 years after entry; current Wisconsin resident for ch. 45 benefits; 100% service-connected disability rating (38 USC 1114 or 1134) or 100% individual unemployability
- Eligible unremarried surviving spouse: of a service member meeting the residency test who died on active duty as a resident, or died as a resident with a 100% rating, or (Guard/reserve) died in the line of duty during training, or whose spouse receives dependency and indemnity compensation
## Computation
- Property taxes paid in 2026 (date received by the municipality — e.g., a December 2026 installment counts for 2026; a 2027 installment counts for 2027) on the principal dwelling and up to ONE acre (Worksheets 1-2 prorate larger or mixed-use parcels)
- Excludes special assessments, delinquent interest, service charges, and business-deductible taxes
- Joint owners use their share (married couples filing jointly use 100% of the combined share); mobile home permit fees count; renters required by written agreement to pay the taxes directly to the municipality qualify
- Claim within 4 years of the unextended due date
## Certification and attachments
- First-time claimants obtain WDVA certification before claiming: Form WDVA 2097 (Brochure B0106) with the DD Form 214, the VA disability award letter, and, if applicable, the death certificate, marriage certificate, and Form WDVA 0001 — through the county veterans service officer or WDVA, Attn: BRC-PTC Application, PO Box 7843, Madison WI 53707-7843
- Prior-year certification carries forward if still eligible (no need to attach it again)
- Attach the property tax bill and proof of 2026 payment (and the certification if newly issued); renters attach the landlord agreement and proof of direct payment
- If the 4-year claim deadline approaches while WDVA reviews eligibility, an extension may be requested (Fact Sheet 1122)
## Interactions
Claiming it bars the school property tax credit, homestead credit, and farmland preservation credit for you and your spouse; it is also barred by the age-67 retirement income subtraction.
## Prompt
- I'm a 100% disabled veteran in Wisconsin.
