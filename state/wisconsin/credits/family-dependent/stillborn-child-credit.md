# Credit for Stillborn Child (NEW 2026)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for parents who experience a stillbirth during the year that required a fetal death report. Effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.
## Amount (lesser of tax or)
| Situation | Credit |
|---|---|
| Married couple filing jointly | $2,000 |
| Married couple filing separately | $1,000 each |
| Unmarried parents | $1,000 each |
## Rules
- Parents must be FULL-YEAR Wisconsin residents
- A copy of the fetal death report must be submitted with the return
- Not allowed on a return covering less than 12 months, unless due to the taxpayer's death
- Nonrefundable; no carryforward stated
- Barred in a year the age-67 retirement income subtraction is claimed (it is a sec. 71.10(4) credit)
