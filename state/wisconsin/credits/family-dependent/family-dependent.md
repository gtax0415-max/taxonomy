# Family and Dependent Care Credits
## Description
Wisconsin credits tied to family expenses, built on federal definitions but with Wisconsin-specific limits.
## Credits in this folder
- Additional Child and Dependent Care Credit (additional-child-dependent-care-credit-wi-2441.md) — Schedule WI-2441 → Form 1, Line 14. Nonrefundable, no carryforward. 102,114 claims, $131.5 million used for tax year 2024 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234)
- Credit for Stillborn Child (stillborn-child-credit.md) — NEW 2026. Nonrefundable, $2,000 joint / $1,000 per parent
