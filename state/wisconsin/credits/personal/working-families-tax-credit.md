# Working Families Tax Credit (Form 1, line 17)
## Status
DOR instructs filers NOT to enter any amount: no credit is allowable for any individual or couple filing Form 1 because anyone who would qualify has no tax to offset. The line remains on the form for statutory reasons only. Treat as zero for 2026.
