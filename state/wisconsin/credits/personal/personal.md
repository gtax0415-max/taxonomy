# Personal Credits on Form 1
## Credits in this folder
- Itemized Deduction Credit (itemized-deduction-credit.md) — Schedule 1 → Form 1, Line 13. 5% of itemized deductions above the Wisconsin standard deduction. Nonrefundable
- Blind Worker Transportation Services Credit (blind-worker-transportation-credit.md) — Form 1, Line 15. 50% of expenses, $1,500 maximum per person. Nonrefundable
- Working Families Tax Credit (working-families-tax-credit.md) — Form 1, Line 17. No amount allowable
- Married Couple Credit (married-couple-credit.md) — Schedule 2 → Form 1, Line 18. 3% of the lower-earning spouse's qualified earned income, $480 maximum. Nonrefundable
- Repayment Credit (repayment-credit.md) — Form 1, Line 32. Repayments over $3,000. Refundable
## Common rule
All are barred for a year the age-67 retirement income subtraction is claimed. None carry forward.
