# Repayment Credit (claim of right)
## Rules
- You repaid during 2026 an amount included in income in an earlier year because you thought you had an unrestricted right to it
- Repayment over $3,000, and NOT subtracted on Schedule SB line 28
- Credit = tax for the earlier year as filed − tax recomputed without the repaid amount
- Alternative: the Schedule SB line 28 subtraction, which for 2026 may again be available under Wisconsin's IRC (see ../../income/exclusions/federal-income-exempt-in-wisconsin.md)
## Prompt
- I had to pay back a bonus I received last year.
