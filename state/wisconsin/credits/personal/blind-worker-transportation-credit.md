# Blind Worker Transportation Services Credit
## Rules (same for 2026)
- Taxpayer (or spouse on a joint return) is BLIND under IRC 63(f)(4), employed, and paid for transportation between home and work
- Qualifying services: mass transit, paratransit, taxicab, or transportation network company (rideshare)
- Credit = 50% of expenses, up to $3,000 of expenses ($1,500 credit) per qualifying person
- Not for reimbursed costs or costs paid from an ABLE account whose contributions were subtracted
## Prompt
- I'm blind and take rideshares to work.
