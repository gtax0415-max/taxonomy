# State Historic Rehabilitation Credit — Individuals Only (Schedule HR, Part II)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of 25% of qualified costs to preserve or rehabilitate a historic home the taxpayer OWNS AND LIVES IN. There is no federal equivalent (the federal credit requires income-producing property). Cannot be transferred.
## 2026 parameters
| Item | Most filers | Married filing separately |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | 25% | 25% |
| Minimum qualified costs per project | more than $10,000 | more than $5,000 |
| Maximum costs counted per project | $40,000 | $20,000 |
| Maximum credit per project | $10,000 | $5,000 |
| Carryforward | 15 years | 15 years |
The minimum is measured over the whole 2-year (or 5-year phased) rehabilitation period, not one tax year. No 2026 change identified.
## Eligibility
- Own and occupy the property as a personal residence; no sale in the first year after completion; not acquired under an agreement to sell it back within 5 years
- Not used in a trade or business or held for income — prorate if partly business use
- Wisconsin Historical Society certifies the property is listed or eligible (or contributing in a historic district), and the plan and completed work meet standards
- Completed within 2 years after physical work begins (5 years if planned in phases)
## Qualifying costs
Work done AFTER the Historical Society approves the plan on: the exterior; interior of a window sash when the exterior is also worked on; structural elements; heating or ventilating systems; electrical or plumbing systems (NOT fixtures). Architectural fees for a National or State Register nomination within 5 years before submission also count. Purchase and enlargement costs do not.
## Timing election
Line 12a: claim when the work is completed; Line 12b: claim as costs are paid (Line 12c total to date).
## Recapture (Form 1, line 26)
If the property is sold, conveyed, or found noncompliant within 5 years after the work is completed, repay a percentage of the credit USED: 100% (within year 1), 80% (year 2), 60% (year 3), 40% (year 4), 20% (year 5), based on full years from the approval date. Unused carryover is reduced by the same percentage. Report on Form 1, line 26 with a federal Form 4255 marked "Wisconsin" (sec. 71.07(9r)(k)).
## Required Information
- Approved Part 3 after completion (approved Part 2 while in progress)
- Cost records per project (Lines 13a-13f; attach detail for more than six projects)
- Schedule CF for carryforward
## Questions
- Is your home listed on, or eligible for, the National or State Register?
- Did the Wisconsin Historical Society approve your plan before work began?
- What did you spend on qualifying work, and over what period?
- Is any part of the home used for business or rental?
## Common Errors
- Starting work before plan approval
- Including fixtures, interior finish work, or additions
- Exceeding $40,000 of costs or $10,000 of credit per project
- Trying to sell or transfer the credit
