# Wisconsin Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (Schedule LI)
## Description
Nonrefundable state credit for owners of a qualified low-income housing development in Wisconsin that receive an allocation certificate from the Wisconsin Housing and Economic Development Authority (WHEDA). It parallels the federal section 42 credit.
## How it works
- WHEDA issues an allocation certificate specifying the credit amount; the claimant reports it on Line 1
- Line 2d pass-through credits + Line 1 = Line 3 current-year credit; Line 4 carryforward; Line 5 available credit
- CREDIT PERIOD: 6 taxable years beginning with the year the development is placed in service (the year the LAST building is placed in service, for multi-building developments)
- COMPLIANCE PERIOD: 15 years beginning with the first year of the credit period
## 2026 parameters
The amount is set by the WHEDA allocation certificate — no taxpayer-side rate. Carryforward 15 years. No 2026 change identified.
## Qualifications (WHEDA)
- Certificate issued to a person with an ownership interest in the qualified development
- Credit is necessary for the development's financial feasibility
- Recorded restrictive covenant requiring operation as a qualified development, in compliance with the federal Fair Housing Act (Title VIII, Civil Rights Act of 1968), for the compliance period or longer
- Issued under WHEDA's qualified allocation plan (IRC 42(m))
- "Qualified development" = a section 42(g) qualified low-income housing project located in Wisconsin. The former requirement that it be FINANCED WITH TAX-EXEMPT BONDS (IRC 42(i)(2)) was REMOVED by 2025 Wis. Act 236 for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2024
## 2025 Wis. Act 236 changes (apply to 2026)
- No tax-exempt bond financing requirement
- WHEDA must allocate 35% of credits to RURAL areas (city, village, or town under 10,000 population at least 10 miles from any municipality of 50,000+), unless rural applications are insufficient
- An insurer that is a member of a pass-through entity may claim the credit
- If DOR assesses at the entity level, the pass-through entity (rather than its members) may be liable for the resulting tax
## Pass-through allocation — unusual rule
Partnerships and LLCs may allocate the credit EITHER by ownership interest OR under a written agreement among partners/members entered into no later than the last day of the entity's taxable year. A partner claiming under such an agreement must attach a copy. S corporations allocate by ownership only.
## Notes
- Fiduciaries use Lines 3a/3b
- Eligibility questions go to WHEDA (wheda.com, 1-800-334-6873); tax questions to DOR
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Repayment (Form 1, line 26)
If the development's qualified basis falls below the prior year-end qualified basis, repay the same amount you must repay to the IRS for the federal low-income housing credit.
## Required Information
- WHEDA allocation certificate (or K-1 copy if passed through)
- Written allocation agreement, if used
- Schedule CF for carryforward
## Questions
- Did WHEDA issue an allocation certificate, and for which credit-period year is 2026?
- Is the credit allocated to you by ownership or by written agreement?
## Common Errors
- Claiming beyond the 6-year credit period
- Special allocation without a timely written agreement attached
- Missing the WHEDA certificate
## Prompt
- I invested in an affordable housing partnership in Wisconsin.
