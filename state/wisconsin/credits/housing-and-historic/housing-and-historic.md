# Housing and Historic Rehabilitation Credits
## Description
Nonrefundable Wisconsin credits for affordable housing developments and for rehabilitating historic buildings — both income-producing buildings and owner-occupied historic homes.
## Credits in this folder
- Wisconsin Low-Income Housing Tax Credit (low-income-housing-tax-credit-li.md) — Schedule LI → Schedule CR, Line 12. Allocated by WHEDA. Nonrefundable, 15-year carryforward
- Supplement to the Federal Historic Rehabilitation Credit (supplement-federal-historic-rehabilitation-credit.md) — Schedule HR Part I (and HR-5 / Part III for transfers) → Schedule CR, Line 13. Certified by WEDC. Transferable
- State Historic Rehabilitation Credit — individuals only (state-historic-rehabilitation-credit.md) — Schedule HR Part II → Schedule CR, Line 16. Owner-occupied historic homes. Not transferable
## What changed for 2026
- Low-income housing: tax-exempt bond financing requirement removed; 35% rural allocation; insurers in pass-throughs may claim (2025 Wis. Act 236)
- Historic supplement: $50,000 QRE test for substantial rehabilitation (tax years after 2025); SHPO-only approval when no federal credit; $3.5 million parcel cap now a 15-year limit (2025 Wis. Act 238)
- State historic (homes): no change identified
- Year-dependent items rolled forward: carryforward window 2011-2026, claim deadlines April 15, 2031
## Related
- The federal counterparts are IRC section 42 (low-income housing) and IRC section 47 (rehabilitation); the Wisconsin historic supplement follows the federal timing
- Carryforward ledger: ../credit-administration/schedule-cf-carryforward.md (codes 39, 29, 40)
## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a WHEDA-allocated housing development?
- Are you rehabilitating a historic building used for business or rental?
- Are you restoring a historic home you live in?
