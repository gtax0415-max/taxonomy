# Supplement to the Federal Historic Rehabilitation Credit (Schedule HR, Part I)
## Description
Nonrefundable, TRANSFERABLE credit equal to 20% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures (QREs) on a certified historic building used for the production of income. It supplements the federal 20% rehabilitation credit under IRC section 47. Available to individuals, C corporations, and on a limited basis tax-exempt entities.
## How it works
- Line 2f QREs for the year × 20% = Line 3 total credit
- Line 4 = 20% of Line 3 if the credit must be spread over 5 years; otherwise Line 3
- + pass-through (5d) + purchased credits (6) = Line 7; + carryforward (8) = Line 9; − credits transferred out (10) = Line 11 available
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rate | 20% of QREs |
| Minimum QREs | more than $50,000 |
| Maximum credit | $3,500,000 per parcel ($17,500,000 of QREs) — now a 15-YEAR limit for all projects on the parcel, no longer a lifetime limit (2025 Wis. Act 238, effective April 10, 2026) |
| Substantial rehabilitation | For taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025, a building is treated as substantially rehabilitated if QREs during the 24-month period are at least $50,000 (2025 Wis. Act 238). Earlier years: QREs exceeding the greater of $5,000 or adjusted basis |
| Spread | QREs after December 31, 2017 (no transition rule): 20% per year over 5 years |
| Carryforward | 15 years (purchasers inherit the seller's remaining period) |
## Timing
- Default: computed in the year the building is placed in service. Election: computed on progress expenditures in the year paid
- Must be claimed at the same time as the federal credit — Act 238 clarifies this applies whether or not the federal credit is actually claimed (purchased credits follow the transfer rules)
- Transition rule (100% in one year) applied only to owners since December 31, 2017 whose measuring period began by June 20, 2018 — of little relevance by 2026
## Qualifications
- WEDC certification and contract (attach)
- Project approved by the National Park Service or Wisconsin Historical Society; meets the Secretary of the Interior's Standards. Under Act 238, if the federal credit is NOT claimed, only State Historic Preservation Officer approval is required (no recommendation to the Secretary of the Interior)
- Written State Historic Preservation Officer certification: listed or eligible property, or contributing in a historic district; plan followed; costs not for acquisition or enlargement; costs not incurred before plan approval
- Depreciable, income-producing building; QREs exclude acquisition and enlargement costs
## Transfers (Part III and Schedule HR-5)
- Any person, including a 501(c)(3), may sell or transfer the credit in whole or part to a Wisconsin taxpayer; DOR must be notified with transfer documents and must certify ownership for each transfer
- Purchaser may first use it in the taxable year of purchase — no amending earlier years
- Schedule HR-5: when a 5-year-spread credit is transferred before the 5 years end, the unclaimed balance is reported on HR-5, ending the seller's annual Schedule HR filing
- If DOR later disallows a transferred credit, only the ORIGINAL transferor is liable
- Seller recognizes capital gain = consideration − basis (usually zero); holding period runs from the placed-in-service date
- Purchaser recognizes capital gain when the credit offsets Wisconsin tax = tax offset − price paid (plus costs)
## Form HR-T — transfer certification process
- The transferor emails Form HR-T with the WEDC certification agreement, the proposed transfer documents, any K-1s and the partnership allocation document, and Schedule HR-5 (5-year credits) or Schedule HR (current-year credits) to dorhistoriccredit@wisconsin.gov — it is NOT filed with a tax return
- The credit must be claimed before or at the time it is transferred
- DOR issues a Notice of Certification to the TRANSFEROR only, who must deliver it to the transferee; both attach the Notice to their returns, and the transferee attaches Schedule HR or HR-5 to claim it
- Ownership transfers no earlier than receipt of funds and the Notice date; the transfer must occur before the end of the transferee's taxable year to be used that year; requests after November 1 may not be approved by December 31
## Repayment (Form 1, line 26)
If federal rehabilitation credit on the same QREs is recaptured, repay a proportionate share of the Wisconsin supplement using the federal recapture percentage (federal Form 4255 marked "Wisconsin"); if no federal credit was claimed, the state-credit 5-year schedule (100%/80%/60%/40%/20%) applies.
## Notes
- Basis: QREs are added to basis and depreciated straight-line; the credit reduces basis
- Recapture: DOR can recover the credit for noncompliance; if the federal credit on the same QREs is recaptured, the Wisconsin credit must be repaid too
- Not available for buildings listed as contributing buildings in the State or National Register, or for converting non-historic nonresidential property previously used for housing into housing (per instructions)
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- WEDC contract; approved Parts 2 and 3 (or submitted Part 3); occupancy certificate
- Adjusted basis and measuring-period dates
- K-1 and operating agreement / special allocation documentation if passed through
- DOR transfer certification for purchased credits
- Schedule CF for carryforward
## Questions
- Is the building income-producing and certified historic?
- Do you have a WEDC contract?
- Which year of the 5-year spread is 2026 for this project?
- Did you buy or sell any credit during 2026?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the full credit in one year when the 5-year spread applies
- Claiming in a different year than the federal credit
- Not reducing basis by the credit
- Using a purchased credit on an amended prior-year return

- We are rehabbing a historic warehouse into apartments.
