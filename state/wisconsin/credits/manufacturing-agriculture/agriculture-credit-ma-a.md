# Agriculture Credit (Schedule MA-A)
## Description
Nonrefundable 7.5% credit on qualified production activities income from tangible personal property produced, grown, or extracted on Wisconsin land assessed as AGRICULTURAL by the local assessor. Same line structure as the manufacturing credit (see manufacturing-credit-ma-m.md).
## How it works
Identical to MA-M Lines 1-22, with these differences:
- Production gross receipts also include crop insurance proceeds for destroyed or damaged crops, to the extent in federal AGI
- Property factor (Lines 12b/13) uses REAL PROPERTY AND IMPROVEMENTS only (no equipment), at original cost; rented land = 8 × net annual rent
- Classification is by the LOCAL ASSESSOR (agricultural class), not the DOR manufacturing roll

## Eligibility and key questions
- Question A: produce, grow, or extract on land assessed as agricultural — required
- Question B: a landowner who ONLY RENTS farmland to others has rental receipts, which are not production gross receipts — no credit
- The same excluded-receipts list as MA-M applies (including dispositions of land and government grants)
## Notes
- Business income limitation (Part II) applies to individuals and fiduciaries, exactly as on MA-M
- Interacts with the farmland preservation credits only through income: both are separate credits on different land tests
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Schedule MA-A (only attachment needed unless pass-through: then K-1 copies)
- Farm receipts, COGS, direct and indirect costs
- Original cost of farm real property and improvements, beginning and end of year
- Part II recomputed tax for each farm business
## Questions
- Is your land classified as agricultural on the local assessment?
- Do you farm it yourself, or only cash-rent it out?
- Did you receive crop insurance proceeds?
- Do you have farming activity in other states?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit on cash rent received
- Including equipment in the agricultural property factor
- Using assessed or market value instead of original cost
- Omitting the Part II limitation
## Prompt
- I run a dairy farm in Wisconsin.
- I grow corn and soybeans on my own land.
