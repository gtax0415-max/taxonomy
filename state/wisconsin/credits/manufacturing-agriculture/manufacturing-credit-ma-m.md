# Manufacturing Credit (Schedule MA-M)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of 7.5% of qualified production activities income from tangible personal property manufactured, in whole or in part, on Wisconsin property assessed as manufacturing (sec. 70.995) or classified as manufacturing personal property (sec. 70.995(5n)). The single largest Wisconsin individual business credit.
## How it works
| Lines | Step |
|---|---|
| 1 | Production gross receipts (sale, lease, rental, license of qualified production property) |
| 2-5 | Subtract COGS and direct costs allocable to those receipts |
| 6-10 | Subtract indirect costs × (production gross receipts ÷ all gross receipts) |
| 11 | QPAI. Zero or less → no credit |
| 12a-14 | Manufacturing property factor: Wisconsin manufacturing property ÷ all property used to manufacture qualified production property (100% if all manufacturing is in Wisconsin — check 12a) |
| 15a | Line 11 × factor |
| 15b-15d | Corporations: smaller of 15a or Wisconsin net income |
| 15e-15g | Individuals/fiduciaries: 15a minus QPAI used for the other-state tax credit |
| 16 | × 7.5% |
| 17-18 | + pass-through credit = current credit |
| 19-20 | + carryforward (Schedule CF) = available |
| 21-22 | Individuals/fiduciaries: limited to tax on the business's income (Part II) |
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rate | 7.5% of eligible QPAI |
| Property values (Lines 12b/13) | ORIGINAL COST, averaged beginning/end of year; rented property = 8 × net annual rent. NOT assessed value |
| Carryforward | 15 years |
| Claim deadline | 4 years after unextended due date (April 15, 2031 for 2026) |
| Credit is income | 2025 credit → income on 2026 return; 2026 credit → income on 2027 return |
| Questions A-B | Use the 2026 Business Activity Roll account and 2026 Notice of Real Property Assessment parcel numbers |
| Question D | First approved by DOR in 2026 for classification on the 2027 roll (credit may be claimed in the approval year) |
## Manufacturing classification
- 2023 Wis. Act 12 repealed personal property tax beginning assessment year 2024 and created sec. 70.995(5n) to classify manufacturing personal property
- Establishments not classified as manufacturing before January 1, 2024 must apply to DOR State and Local Finance by JULY 1 of the taxable year (July 1, 2026)
- Real property: generally request certification by March 1 of the prior year; if DOR approves classification but listing waits until the next January 1, the credit may still be claimed in the approval year
## Excluded receipts
Film production; producing, transmitting, or distributing electricity, natural gas, or potable water; constructing real property and related engineering/architectural services; food and beverages prepared at a retail establishment; dispositions of land; forgiven PPP loans, EIDL grants, and other government grants or loans.
## Multistate examples
- Products made entirely in another state and never transferred to Wisconsin: excluded from receipts and from the property factor
- Products started in another state and FINISHED in Wisconsin: receipts count in full, but the other state's property goes in the factor denominator, reducing the percentage
## Business income limitation (individuals and fiduciaries — Part II)
For each business: column (b) actual Wisconsin tax (Form 1, Line 12 in 2025 numbering) − column (c) tax recomputed without that business = column (d); credit allowed = smaller of (d) and (e) credit from that business. A loss business cannot use credit against another business's tax. Partners, LLC members, and S shareholders may offset only tax on their distributive share. The excess carries forward.
## Notes
- Individuals: Line 8 gross receipts excludes non-business income (spouse's wages, unrelated Schedule E rentals)
- Line 9 and Line 14 percentages carry to four decimals
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Schedule MA-M; K-1 copies for pass-through credits plus a schedule of each entity's name, FEIN, and amount
- Account/parcel numbers, or copies of DOR assessment notices
- Cost accounting split between COGS, direct, and indirect costs
- Part II recomputed returns for each business
- Schedule CF for carryforward
## Questions
- Is the property on the DOR manufacturing roll or classified as manufacturing personal property?
- Is any manufacturing done outside Wisconsin?
- Do you claim the credit for tax paid to another state on this income?
- What is your Wisconsin tax with and without this business's income?
- What 2025 M&A credit must be added to 2026 income?
## Common Errors
- Using assessed value instead of original cost in the property factor
- Including non-business gross receipts for an individual
- Skipping the Part II business income limitation
- Forgetting to add the prior-year credit to Wisconsin income
- Missing the July 1 classification application for newly classified personal property
