# Manufacturer's Sales Tax Credit Carryforward (Schedule MS)
**Status:** LEGACY — carryforward only; expected fully expired for 2026 (see below)
## Description
Carryforward of the old credit for sales tax paid on fuel and electricity consumed in manufacturing. The credit could not be COMPUTED for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2006; Schedule MS only releases remaining carryforward.
## 2026 status — expected fully expired
- Last year a credit could be computed: taxable years beginning in 2005
- Carryforward for such credits: 20 years → last usable taxable year begins in 2025
- Therefore NO manufacturer's sales tax credit should remain usable on a 2026 return. Confirm whether DOR issues a 2026 Schedule MS; if it does not, treat this credit as closed and delete any remaining balance from records
## Rules (for amended 2025 or earlier returns)
- Only taxpayers whose total unused carryforward was $25,000 or less at the start of their 2006 taxable year can claim it
- Credits for years beginning before 1998 (non-corporations) already expired under the 15-year rule
- The credit is MANDATORY in amount: it must be used as determined on Schedule MS (not optional like other Schedule CR credits)
- Pass-through and sole proprietorship credits may only offset tax on that business's income, computed business by business (Part II, columns b-g); a loss business gets zero. Corporations use 7.9% of net income as the column (e) proxy
- Partnerships and LLCs do not file Schedule MS; their partners/members do
- Not tracked on Schedule CF
## Questions
- Do you still carry an unused manufacturer's sales tax credit from before 2006?
- Was your unused balance at the start of 2006 $25,000 or less?
## Common Errors
- Carrying a balance into 2026 after the 20-year period has run
- Treating it as optional
## Prompt
- My records show an old manufacturer's sales tax credit carryforward.
