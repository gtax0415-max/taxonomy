# Manufacturing and Agriculture Credits
## Description
Wisconsin's largest business credits. The manufacturing and agriculture credit (often called the "M&A credit") equals 7.5% of qualified production activities income from property assessed as manufacturing or agricultural in Wisconsin, which in practice nearly eliminates Wisconsin tax on that income. In tax year 2023, 8,873 individuals used about $332 million of manufacturing credit and 12,424 used about $31 million of agriculture credit (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 230).
## Credits in this folder
- Manufacturing Credit (manufacturing-credit-ma-m.md) — Schedule MA-M → Schedule CR, Line 14 → Form 1, Line 19. Nonrefundable, 15-year carryforward
- Agriculture Credit (agriculture-credit-ma-a.md) — Schedule MA-A → Schedule CR, Line 15 → Form 1, Line 19. Nonrefundable, 15-year carryforward
- Manufacturer's Sales Tax Credit Carryforward (manufacturers-sales-tax-credit-carryforward-ms.md) — Schedule MS → Schedule CR, Line 19. Legacy carryforward only; likely fully expired by 2026
## What changed for 2026
- No enacted change to the 7.5% rate or the formula was found. Senate Bill 177 (2025-26 session), which would modify the credit for linen supply and industrial laundry businesses, was referred to committee in April 2025 and is NOT law as of this writing
- "Credit is income" timing is DIFFERENT for M&A: the credit computed for 2025 is income on the 2026 return (see below)
## Shared mechanics (MA-M and MA-A are mirror images)
- Qualified production activities income (QPAI) = production gross receipts − COGS − direct costs − a share of indirect costs (Lines 1-11)
- × Wisconsin manufacturing/agricultural property factor (Lines 12a-14)
- × 7.5% (Line 16)
- Corporations are capped at Wisconsin net income (Lines 15b-15d); individuals are capped by the BUSINESS INCOME LIMITATION (Part II) — the credit can only offset tax on that business's income
- Income taxed by another state and used for the other-state tax credit is removed (Line 15f) — no double benefit
- If a taxpayer has both manufacturing and agricultural QPAI, file BOTH schedules separately
## Credit is income — the year after
Unlike most Wisconsin credits (income in the year computed), M&A credits are income on the return for the year AFTER computation, to the extent not already in federal income. So a 2026 return must include the 2025 M&A credit computed; the 2026 credit becomes income on the 2027 return.
## Questions
- Do you manufacture on Wisconsin property assessed or classified as manufacturing?
- Do you grow or produce on Wisconsin land assessed as agricultural?
- Did you compute an M&A credit for 2025 (income on the 2026 return)?
