# Wisconsin Credit Administration
## Description
The two schedules that route and track Wisconsin credits. They are not credits themselves, but almost every business credit passes through them.
## Schedules in this folder
- Schedule CR, Other Credits (schedule-cr-other-credits.md) — collects nonrefundable credits (total → Form 1, Line 19) and refundable business credits (total → Form 1, Line 35)
- Schedule CF, Carryforward of Unused Credits (schedule-cf-carryforward.md) — tracks unused nonrefundable credits by year for 15 years
- Schedule I, Federal-to-Wisconsin Conformity (../../income/schedule-i-federal-adjustments.md) — recomputes federal AGI, itemized deductions, and federal credits (notably the EIC) under Wisconsin's IRC
- Wisconsin NOL moved to ../../deductions/subtractions/wisconsin-net-operating-loss.md (NOL add-backs affect homestead, farmland preservation, and business-income-limited credits)
