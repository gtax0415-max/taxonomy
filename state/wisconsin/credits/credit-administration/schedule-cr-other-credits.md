# Schedule CR — Other Credits (routing hub)
## Description
The Wisconsin schedule that gathers credits computed on their own schedules and sends the totals to the return. Column A = credit AVAILABLE for 2026; Column B = credit actually USED in 2026.

### Part II — Corporations only

## Key rules
- NO INTEREST is paid on refunds from the enterprise zone jobs, business development, or electronics and IT manufacturing zone credits (or the homestead credit)
- RETIREMENT INCOME SUBTRACTION BAR: an individual who claims the retirement income subtraction for 2026 must NOT file Schedule CR — no new credits and no carryforwards may offset tax for that year. (The 2025 instructions point to Schedule SB Line 16 or Schedule M Line 50; confirm the 2026 line.) The unused carryforward is not lost, but the year still counts toward the 15-year window
- ORDERING IS ELECTIVE: the taxpayer chooses how much of each nonrefundable credit to use (Column B), except the manufacturer's sales tax credit, which must be used as determined on Schedule MS. Example: with $2,000 of tax, a $2,000 research credit, and a $4,000 development zones credit, you may use either one — use the credit closest to expiring first
- CARRYFORWARD-ONLY credits: when only a carryforward remains, Columns A and B equal Schedule CF Columns (d) and (e)
- Attach Schedule CR, Schedule CF for any carryforward, each underlying schedule, and any WEDC certification
## Questions
- Which Wisconsin credit schedules are you filing?
- Do you have carryforwards from prior years, and from which years?
- Are you claiming the retirement income subtraction?
## Common Errors
- Filing Schedule CR in a year the retirement income subtraction is claimed
- Putting individual farmland preservation credit on Schedule CR instead of Form 1, Lines 31a/31b
- Using later-expiring credits before credits about to expire
- Column B exceeding Column A, or exceeding tax
