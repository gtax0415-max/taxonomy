# Schedule CF — Carryforward of Unused Credits
## Description
Year-by-year ledger for nonrefundable Wisconsin credits not fully used. Unused credit carries forward up to 15 years. File one Schedule CF PER CREDIT, with a row for each year the credit was computed.
## 2026 window
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rows on the 2026 Schedule CF | 2011 through 2026 |
| Expiring after 2026 | Credits computed in 2011 — usable on the 2026 return, NOT carried to 2027 (2011 Column (f) shaded) — EXCEPT research credits, now carried 50 years |
| Already expired | Credits computed in 2010 or earlier |
(The 2025 form ran 2010-2025 with 2010 shaded; each year drops the oldest row.)
## Columns
- (a) Year computed
- (b) Credit computed — include own credits AND pass-through credits; for transferred supplement to federal historic rehabilitation, angel investment, or early stage seed credits use the ORIGINAL computation year; for transferred economic development credit use the TRANSFER year
- (c) Credit used through 2025 — include amounts transferred to others
- (d) = (b) − (c), available for 2026 → Schedule CR Column A
- (e) Used in 2026 = Schedule CR Column B + any amount transferred in 2026
- (f) = (d) − (e), available for 2027

## Key rules
- EXCEPTION — RESEARCH CREDITS: 2025 Wis. Act 220 (effective April 10, 2026) extends the carryover of unused research credits from 15 to 50 years for credits not expired on that date (research expense credits, codes 22-24, and, per the Act's cross-references, the research facilities carryforwards — confirm on the 2026 Schedule CF). A 2011 research credit therefore does NOT expire after 2026
- 2025 Wis. Act 118 repealed many obsolete credits but delays each repeal until its remaining carryforwards can no longer be used, so listed carryforward-only credits remain claimable
- Use is OPTIONAL each year ("may" in the statute), so a taxpayer can save credits — but the 15-year clock runs regardless
- RETIREMENT INCOME SUBTRACTION: for a year the individual claims it, enter nothing in the current-year row or Column (e); no credits offset tax that year
- Do NOT file Schedule CF if a credit was computed and fully used in the same year
- File a new Schedule CF every year until the carryforward is gone or expires
## Required Information
- Prior-year Schedule CF for each credit
- Credit amounts computed each year, including K-1 pass-throughs
- Any credit transfers in or out
## Questions
- Which nonrefundable Wisconsin credits do you have left over, and from which years?
- Do you have any 2011 credits that will expire after 2026?
- Did you buy, sell, or transfer any credits?
## Common Errors
- Combining two different credits on one Schedule CF
- Using newer credits while 2011 credits expire unused
- Wrong year for transferred credits
- Carrying forward during a retirement-subtraction year as if it were used
