# Enterprise Zone Jobs Credit (Schedule EC)
## Description
Refundable credit for businesses certified by WEDC that operate in a WEDC-designated enterprise zone. Among the largest Wisconsin corporate credits by dollars: 15 corporate claimants used about $65.4 million in tax year 2022 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 230).
## How it works
- WEDC computes the credit; the taxpayer simply reports the certified amount. The computation is based on job creation, job retention, capital investment, training, and supply chain purchases
- Schedule EC: Line 1 WEDC-certified credit + Line 2d pass-through credits = Line 3 total credit
- Fully refundable — it is not limited to tax and needs no carryforward
## 2026 parameters
No 2026 rate table exists on the taxpayer side: the amount is whatever WEDC certifies under the taxpayer's contract. Use the 2026 WEDC certification/verification.
## 2026 change
2025 Wis. Act 164 (taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025) lets WEDC certify ONE business that manufactures biofuel in Wisconsin, mostly aviation biofuel, for up to $120,000,000 of enterprise zone credit.
## Eligibility
- Compute: any individual, estate, trust, partnership, LLC, corporation, or tax-exempt organization conducting business in an enterprise zone and certified by WEDC
- Claim: individuals and corporations. Pass-through entities pass it to owners
- Not allowed for expenditures already included in the basis of another Wisconsin credit (e.g. the research expense credit) — no double dipping
## Notes
- CREDIT IS INCOME: report it on the Wisconsin return for the year computed
- Fiduciaries complete Lines 3a (beneficiaries' share, shown on 2K-1) and 3b (entity's share)
- A pass-through entity that elects entity-level tax and claims the credit itself cannot also pass it through
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+ (see ../credits.md)
## Required Information
- WEDC certification to claim tax benefits AND WEDC verification of expenses
- For pass-through recipients: copy of Schedule 5K-1, 3K-1, or 2K-1 instead of the certification
- Entity name and FEIN for each pass-through amount
## Questions
- Is your business certified by WEDC in an enterprise zone?
- What amount did WEDC certify for 2026?
- Did you receive this credit on a K-1?
- Are any of the same expenses used for another Wisconsin credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without attaching WEDC certification and verification
- Forgetting to include the credit in Wisconsin income
- Using expenses already counted for the research credit
- Passing the credit through after making the entity-level tax election
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a Wisconsin enterprise zone jobs credit.
- Our company is certified by WEDC in an enterprise zone.
