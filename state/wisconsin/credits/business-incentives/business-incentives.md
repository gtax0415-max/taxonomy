# Wisconsin Business Incentive Credits
## Description
Credits for businesses that create jobs, invest, or contract for work in Wisconsin. Most require advance certification from the Wisconsin Economic Development Corporation (WEDC) or, new for 2026, the State Film Office. All flow through Schedule CR to the individual Form 1 / 1NPR (or Form 2, 4, 4T for fiduciaries and corporations).
## Credits in this folder
- Enterprise Zone Jobs Credit (enterprise-zone-jobs-credit.md) — Schedule EC → Schedule CR, Line 35 → Form 1, Line 35. Refundable
- Business Development Credit (business-development-credit.md) — Schedule BD → Schedule CR, Line 37 → Form 1, Line 35. Refundable
- Electronics and Information Technology Manufacturing Zone Credit (electronics-it-manufacturing-zone-credit.md) — Schedule EIT → Schedule CR, Line 38 → Form 1, Line 35. Refundable
- Community Rehabilitation Program Credit (community-rehabilitation-program-credit.md) — Schedule CM → Schedule CR, Line 8 → Form 1, Line 19. Nonrefundable
- Employee College Savings Account Contribution Credit (employee-college-savings-account-credit-es.md) — Schedule ES → Schedule CR, Line 6 → Form 1, Line 19. Nonrefundable; 2026 maximum $840 per employee
- Employer-Provided Child Care Credit (employer-provided-child-care-credit.md) — NEW 2026. Nonrefundable
- Rail Infrastructure Modernization and Maintenance Credits (rail-infrastructure-credits.md) — NEW 2026-2030. Nonrefundable, transferable
- Film Production Services Credit (film-production-services-credit.md) — NEW 2026, schedule not yet released. Mixed refundable / nonrefundable
- Film Production Company Investment Credit (film-production-company-investment-credit.md) — NEW 2026, schedule not yet released. Nonrefundable
## What changed for 2026
- Two NEW film credits created by 2025 Wis. Act 15 take effect for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025, i.e. first claimable on 2026 returns
- The schedules EC, BD, EIT, and CM themselves had no 2026 rate change identified; their percentages and caps are statutory or set by WEDC contract
- The retirement income subtraction bar (see ../credits.md) applies to these credits for individuals who are 67+
## Common features
- CERTIFICATION REQUIRED: no WEDC (or Film Office) certification, no credit. Attach the certification/verification, or the K-1 if the credit passed through
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- PASS-THROUGH: partnerships, LLCs treated as partnerships, and tax-option (S) corporations cannot claim; the credit passes to owners by ownership interest (3K-1 / 5K-1). Exception: an entity that elects to pay tax at the entity level may claim certain credits itself and may NOT also pass them through
- FIDUCIARIES split the credit with beneficiaries in proportion to income (2K-1) using the "a/b" lines of each schedule
- COMBINED GROUP members report on Form 6 rather than Schedule CR
- RECKLESS OR FRAUDULENT CLAIMS: up to 10 years of ineligibility plus penalties (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin #180)
