# Rail Infrastructure Modernization and Maintenance Credits (NEW 2026)
**Status:** NEW for 2026 (taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025 and before January 1, 2031)
## Modernization credit
- WEDC-certified class II or III railroads in Wisconsin, owners/lessees of a rail siding, spur, or industry track, and local governments
- 50% of qualified new rail infrastructure capital expenditures in Wisconsin placed in service after December 31, 2025 (right of way, engineering, new track, reactivating inactive track, loading docks, transload structures)
- Maximum $2,000,000 per credit application; WEDC may allocate up to $10,000,000 per calendar year
## Maintenance credit
- WEDC-certified class II or III railroads only
- 50% of qualified short line maintenance expenditures in Wisconsin (track materials, third-party labor, wages of maintenance employees)
- Capped at $5,000 × miles of track owned or leased in Wisconsin on December 31; no expenditures used for a federal credit or funded by federal/state grants
## Both credits
- The same expenditure cannot be used for both credits
- Pass-through entities allocate to owners by ownership
- CREDIT IS INCOME; 5-year carryforward; claim within 4 years; attach WEDC verification
- TRANSFERABLE with DOR approval (transferee uses it only in years still available to the original claimant); revoked certification is added back to tax
## Prompt
- We operate a short line railroad in Wisconsin.
