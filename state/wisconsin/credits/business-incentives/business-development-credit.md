# Business Development Credit (Schedule BD)
## Description
Refundable credit for businesses that contract with WEDC to create or retain jobs and invest in Wisconsin. Corporations used about $15.3 million across 31 claimants in tax year 2022 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 230).
## How it works
WEDC determines and certifies the credit, which can include up to:
| Component | Maximum rate |
|---|---|
| Wages paid to an eligible employee | 10% |
| Wages paid to an eligible employee in an economically distressed area (as determined by WEDC) | 5% (as printed on the 2025 schedule) |
| Training costs for eligible employees | 50% |
| Personal property investment in a qualifying capital project | 3% |
| Real property investment in a qualifying capital project | 5% |
| Wages for corporate headquarters positions created or retained in Wisconsin | A WEDC-set percentage |
| Investment in workforce housing | 15% |
| Investment in an employee child care program | 15% |
| Contributions to third parties that build or rehabilitate workforce housing or establish a child care program, including local revolving loan funds | within the workforce housing / child care components (law change reported in Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 232, January 2026) |
A qualifying capital project involves total investment of at least $250,000, or at least $10,000 per eligible employee on the project if under $250,000.
## 2026 parameters
Rates above are statutory maximums carried from the 2025 schedule; the actual amount is what WEDC certifies for 2026. No 2026 change identified.
## Qualifications (WEDC)
- Operating or intending to operate a business in Wisconsin, and under contract with WEDC
- Certification lasts no more than 10 cumulative years
- Makes a capital investment and creates or retains full-time jobs
- Does not reduce Wisconsin net employment below the year before certification
- WEDC can require REPAYMENT of benefits for a year the business failed to employ required eligible employees
## Notes
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- Fiduciaries use Lines 3a / 3b
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- WEDC certification/verification (or K-1 copy for pass-through recipients)
- Entity name, FEIN, and amount for each pass-through credit
## Questions
- Do you have a WEDC business development contract, and what did WEDC certify for 2026?
- Did net Wisconsin employment fall below the pre-certification level?
- Did you receive the credit through a K-1?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than WEDC certified
- Omitting the credit from Wisconsin income
- Missing a WEDC repayment obligation after falling short on employment
## Prompt
- We signed a business development agreement with WEDC.
- My S corporation passed through a Wisconsin business development credit.
