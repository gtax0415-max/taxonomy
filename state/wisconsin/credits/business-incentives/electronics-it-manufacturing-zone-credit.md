# Electronics and Information Technology Manufacturing Zone Credit (Schedule EIT)
## Description
Refundable credit for a business certified by WEDC that begins operations in the designated electronics and information technology manufacturing (EITM) zone. In practice this is a very narrow credit tied to a single large project in southeastern Wisconsin; most individual filers will only see it as a K-1 pass-through, if at all.
## How it works
Two components, both certified by WEDC:
| Component | Rate | Aggregate cap |
|---|---|---|
| Zone payroll credit (Line 1) | 17% of eligible zone payroll | $1,500,000,000 |
| Capital expenditure credit (Line 2) | 15% of significant capital expenditures in the zone | $1,350,000,000 |
- Eligible payroll per employee is capped at $100,000 and must be wages to FULL-TIME employees for work in the zone, or elsewhere in Wisconsin for the benefit of the zone operations, as determined by WEDC
- Schedule EIT: Line 1 + Line 2 = Line 3; plus pass-through Line 4d = Line 5
## 2026 parameters
Rates and caps are statutory and unchanged as far as identified. Amount claimed = 2026 WEDC certification.
## Notes
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- Fiduciaries use Lines 5a / 5b
- Eligibility questions go to WEDC; tax questions go to DOR (DORFranchise@wisconsin.gov)
## Required Information
- WEDC certification/verification for each component (or K-1 copy for pass-through recipients)
- Entity name, FEIN, and amount for each pass-through
## Questions
- Are you certified by WEDC for the EITM zone?
- Did you receive this credit on a K-1?
## Common Errors
- Entering an amount not matching the WEDC certification
- Including part-time wages or wages above $100,000 per employee in the payroll base
- Omitting the credit from Wisconsin income
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows an electronics and information technology manufacturing zone credit.
