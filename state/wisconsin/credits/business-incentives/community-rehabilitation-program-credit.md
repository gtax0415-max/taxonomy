# Community Rehabilitation Program Credit (Schedule CM)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a business that contracts with a community rehabilitation program — a nonprofit, county, municipality, or state or federal agency that provides vocational rehabilitation to people with disabilities — to perform work for the business.
## How it works
- Credit = 5% of the amount PAID in the taxable year to the program for work performed
- Payments counted are capped at $500,000 per program, so the maximum credit is $25,000 per program
- If you paid more than one program, complete a separate Schedule CM for each (Part I Lines 1-2 and Part II only) and carry each Line 2 to Line 3 of the first schedule
- Line 5 (2026 credit) + Line 6 (carryover from Schedule CF) = Line 7 available credit
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Credit rate | 5% |
| Payment cap per program | $500,000 |
| Maximum credit per program | $25,000 |
| Carryforward | 15 years |
| Claim deadline | within 4 years of the unextended due date of the return (April 15, 2031 for a calendar-year 2026 return) |
Rate and cap are statutory; no 2026 change identified.
## Qualifying "work"
Production, packaging, assembly, food service, custodial service, clerical service, and other commercial activities that improve employment opportunities for people with disabilities. Vocational rehabilitation services include education, training, employment, counseling, therapy, placement, and case management.
## Eligibility
- Any individual, estate, trust, partnership, LLC, corporation, S corporation, or tax-exempt organization with a contract with a community rehabilitation program
- Pass-through entities cannot claim; credit flows to owners by ownership interest. Estates and trusts share with beneficiaries in proportion to income
## Notes
- PART II FIRST: the program must complete and sign Part II before you complete Part I. Fill in your entity name (Part II Line 2) and taxable year (Line 3) before handing it to the program. No Part II, no credit
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed — even the portion carried forward
- Corporate reorganizations: IRC section 383 limitations may restrict the carryforward
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Signed Part II from each program (contract date, total payments received, payments for work performed)
- Amount actually paid in the taxable year
- Pass-through K-1s with entity name and FEIN
- Prior Schedule CF if carrying an unused balance
## Questions
- Did your business contract with a nonprofit or agency that employs people with disabilities?
- How much did you pay them during 2026 for work performed?
- Has the program signed Part II?
- Do you have unused credit from prior years?
## Common Errors
- Filing without the program's signed Part II
- Using amounts invoiced but not paid during the year
- Exceeding the $500,000 per-program cap
- Not filing Schedule CF for the unused balance
## Prompt
- We hire a sheltered workshop to do our packaging.
- Our cleaning contractor is a nonprofit that employs people with disabilities.
