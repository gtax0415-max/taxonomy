# Wisconsin Employer-Provided Child Care Credit (NEW 2026)
**Status:** NEW for 2026 — schedule not yet published
## Description
Nonrefundable Wisconsin credit equal to the federal employer-provided child care credit (IRC 45F, as in effect for federal purposes on April 5, 2026) claimed on the federal return, for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.
## Rules
- Claimants: individuals, estates, trusts, C corporations, insurers that claim the federal 45F credit; partnerships and S corporations pass it through by ownership
- Excludes amounts for child care facilities located outside Wisconsin or services provided outside Wisconsin
- Federal 45F(d) recapture (tax increase) must be added to Wisconsin tax in the same amount
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- 15-year carryforward; IRC 383 limits after corporate ownership changes; claim within 4 years of the unextended due date
- Barred in a year the age-67 retirement income subtraction is claimed
## Related
Business development credit also covers investment in an employee child care program (and, after WTB 232, contributions to third parties establishing one).
## Prompt
- My business provides child care for employees in Wisconsin.
