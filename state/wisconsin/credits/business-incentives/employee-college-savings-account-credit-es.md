# Employee College Savings Account Contribution Credit (Schedule ES)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an employer that contributes to an employee's Wisconsin college savings (529) account.
## How it works
Per-employee worksheet: contributions made in the year × 50%, capped at the annual maximum. Total of all employees → Line 2; + pass-through (Line 3d) = Line 4 current credit; + carryforward (Line 5) = Line 6 available.
## Parameters
| Item | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Rate | 50% of employer contributions | 50% |
| Maximum credit per employee | $820 | $840 (DFI, Wisconsin 529 College Savings Program) |
| Carryforward | 15 years | 15 years |
| Claim deadline | 4 years after unextended due date | April 15, 2031 |
The $800 base set by 2023 Wis. Act 148 is indexed annually for inflation.
## Eligibility
- Sole proprietors, partners, LLC members, S corporation shareholders, and corporations that are employers
- ESTATES AND TRUSTS are not eligible to compute the credit
- The employee's pay must be reported (or required to be reported) on a W-2 issued by the claimant — no credit for contractors
- Account must be a Wisconsin college savings account (sec. 224.50)
## Notes
- CREDIT IS INCOME in the year computed
- Pass-through entities allocate by ownership interest only
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- Contributions per employee during 2026
- Schedule ES (plus 3K-1/5K-1 copies if passed through); Schedule CF for carryforward
## Questions
- Did your business contribute to employees' Wisconsin 529 accounts in 2026?
- How much per employee, and are they W-2 employees?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the per-employee cap
- Claiming for 1099 contractors or non-Wisconsin 529 plans
- Omitting the credit from Wisconsin income
## Prompt
- My company contributes to employees' Edvest accounts.
