# Film Production Services Credit (NEW 2026)
## Description
New credit created by 2025 Wis. Act 15 for film production companies producing an accredited film, video, broadcast advertisement, or television production in Wisconsin. Effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025, so 2026 is the first year it can be claimed.
## How it works
Three components, each 30% or a sales-tax reimbursement:
| Component | Amount | Refundable? |
|---|---|---|
| Wages paid to Wisconsin-resident employees for services in Wisconsin on the production | 30% | No — 15-year carryforward |
| Production expenditures incurred in Wisconsin | 30% | YES |
| Wisconsin sales and use tax paid on property and services used directly in the production | 100% of tax paid | No — 15-year carryforward |
## 2026 parameters
- Wages must be for services rendered after December 31, 2025
- "Accredited production" threshold: aggregate wages over $100,000 (production 30 minutes or longer) or over $50,000 (under 30 minutes), measured over the 12 months after principal filming begins
- If production expenditures are $1,000,000 or more, total credits are limited to the first $250,000 of wages per employee, and the wages of the claimant's two highest-paid employees cannot be used. 2025 Wis. Act 174 (effective April 4, 2026) bases this on ACTUAL production expenditures for the year (not budgeted)
- State Film Office may allocate no more than $5,000,000 per fiscal year in total (shared with the investment credit) and $1,000,000 per applicant
- Claim within 4 years of the unextended due date
## Eligibility
- Production company that owns the copyright (or contracts directly with the owner) and has a viable commercial distribution plan
- Application must be filed with and approved by the State Film Office each year; attach the approved application
- EXCLUDED productions: news and current events, weather or market reports, talk shows, sports events, galas and awards shows, fundraising productions, productions subject to 18 USC 2257 record-keeping, and productions primarily for industrial, corporate, or institutional purposes
## Notes
- 2025 Wis. Act 174 clarified that salaries and wages ARE production expenditures except those related to marketing and distribution (WTB 230 had described production expenditures as excluding wages). How wages split between the wage and expenditure components should be confirmed on the 2026 film credit schedule
- Marketing/distribution costs are excluded; Wisconsin-domiciled music, air travel, and insurance are included
- Applications are made to the State Film Office for EACH taxable year (applications open — FilmWisconsin.com)
- A transferee of the credit may use it against the transferee's own tax
- The same wages or sales tax cannot support more than one credit
- Nonrefundable portions are TRANSFERABLE (sale allowed with DOR notification and certification of ownership)
- No interest is paid on refunds
- Pass-through entities compute and allocate to owners by ownership interest
- Retirement income subtraction bar applies to individuals 67+
## Required Information
- State Film Office approved application / accreditation
- Wisconsin-resident wage detail, production expenditure detail, sales and use tax paid
- Confirmation a Wisconsin CPA was retained for compliance audits
## Questions
- Is the production accredited by the State Film Office for 2026?
- How much was paid in Wisconsin-resident wages, Wisconsin production expenditures, and Wisconsin sales tax?
- Is the budget $1,000,000 or more (triggering the per-employee wage limit)?
## Common Errors
- Including pre-2026 wages
- Treating the whole credit as refundable — only the production-expenditure portion is
- Claiming without State Film Office approval

- We are shooting a TV series in Wisconsin in 2026.
