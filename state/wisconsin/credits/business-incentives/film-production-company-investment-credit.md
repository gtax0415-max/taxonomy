# Film Production Company Investment Credit (NEW 2026)
**Status:** NEW for 2026 — schedule and line not yet published by DOR
## Description
New nonrefundable credit from 2025 Wis. Act 15 for establishing a film production company in Wisconsin. Available for the first THREE taxable years the claimant does business in Wisconsin as a film production company. Effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025.
## How it works
Credit = 30% of amounts paid in the year to establish the company for:
- Depreciable tangible personal property used EXCLUSIVELY (at least 95%) in the film business, purchased after December 31, 2025
- Acquiring, constructing, rehabilitating, remodeling, or repairing real property, where physical work began after December 31, 2025 or the project is placed in service after that date. Acquired real property cannot be "previously owned property" (owned by the claimant or a related person in the prior two years)
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Rate | 30% |
| Window | First 3 taxable years in business in Wisconsin |
| Carryforward | 15 years |
| Program cap | $5,000,000 per fiscal year combined with the services credit; $1,000,000 per applicant |
| Claim deadline | 4 years after unextended due date |
## Notes
- 2025 Wis. Act 174: certification is applied for each taxable year; a transferee may use a transferred credit against its own tax
- Expenses used for the film production services credit cannot also be used here
- Transferable with DOR notification and certification
- IRC section 383 limits carryforward after a corporate ownership change
- Pass-through entities allocate to owners
## Required Information
- State Film Office written certification (attach to return)
- Purchase invoices, construction contracts, placed-in-service dates
## Questions
- When did the company start doing business in Wisconsin as a film production company?
- What equipment or real property was purchased or built after December 31, 2025?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for property with more than 5% non-film use
- Claiming after the 3-year window
- Double-counting expenses with the services credit
## Prompt
- We are opening a production studio in Wisconsin.
