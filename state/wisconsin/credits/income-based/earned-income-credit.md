# Wisconsin Earned Income Credit
## Description
Refundable credit equal to a percentage of the federal earned income credit. 196,296 claimants used $94.2 million for tax year 2024 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234).
## Rates (statutory; same for 2026)
| Qualifying children | Wisconsin % of federal EIC | 2026 maximum (computed from federal 2026 maximums) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No Wisconsin credit | — |
| 1 | 4% | 4% × $4,427 = $177 |
| 2 | 11% | 11% × $7,316 = $805 |
| 3 or more | 34% | 34% × $8,231 = $2,799 |
Federal 2026 investment income limit: $12,200 (a cliff). Computed maximums assume the federal EIC under Wisconsin's IRC equals the federal amount; if Schedule I changes FAGI or earned income, use the recomputed federal credit.
## Eligibility
- Qualify for the federal EIC with at least one qualifying child
- FULL-YEAR Wisconsin resident
- Married filing separately: ineligible unless you meet IRC 7703(b) (then file head of household, married, for Wisconsin)
- "Federal earned income credit" means the credit computed under the IRC as adopted by Wisconsin — complete Schedule I Part III when Part I adjustments exist
## Notes
- Barred in a year the age-67 retirement income subtraction is claimed
- Reckless claims: no refundable credits for 2 years; fraudulent: 10 years, plus penalties
- If the IRS computes your federal EIC, fill in the number of children, write "EIC," and DOR will compute the Wisconsin credit
## Required Information
Federal Schedule EIC; Form 8867 if a paid preparer prepared the federal return.
## Questions
- Do you qualify for the federal EIC, and how many qualifying children?
- Were you a full-year Wisconsin resident?
## Common Errors
- Claiming with no qualifying child
- Using the federal EIC when Schedule I changed FAGI
## Prompt
- Does Wisconsin have an earned income credit?
