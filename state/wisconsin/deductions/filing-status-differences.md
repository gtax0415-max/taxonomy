# How Wisconsin Deductions and Credits Change by Filing Status (2026)
## Standard deduction and exemptions
| Status | Maximum standard deduction | Zero above Wisconsin income of | Personal exemptions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single | $13,960 | $136,453 | $700 (+$250 at 65+) |
| Head of household | $18,030 | $136,453 | $700 + $700 per dependent (+$250 at 65+) |
| Married filing jointly | $25,840 | $159,690 | $700 each spouse + dependents (+$250 per spouse 65+) |
| Married filing separately | $12,280 | $75,869 | $700 + dependents claimed (+$250 at 65+) |
## Status-dependent subtractions and limits
| Item | Single / HOH | Married filing jointly | Married filing separately |
|---|---|---|---|
| Unemployment partial-exclusion base | $12,000 | $18,000 | $0 if lived with spouse; $12,000 if not |
| Capital loss deduction limit | $3,000 | $3,000 | $1,500 |
| Edvest/529 contribution subtraction (per beneficiary) | $5,280 | $5,280 | half (2025: $2,560) |
| Retirement income subtraction, age 67+ | $24,000 | $48,000 if both 67+, else $24,000 | $24,000 |
| $5,000 retirement subtraction FAGI limit | under $15,000 | under $30,000 | combined spouses' FAGI under $30,000 |
| Disability income exclusion | allowed | allowed ($25,400 limit if both eligible) | NOT allowed |
| Tuition and fee subtraction phase-out (2025) | $69,480-$83,370 | $111,160-$138,960 | $55,580-$69,480 |
## Status-dependent credits
| Credit | Effect of status |
|---|---|
| Married couple credit | Married filing jointly only |
| Wisconsin earned income credit | Not for married filing separately unless IRC 7703(b) is met (then head of household, married) |
| School property tax credit | $300 cap; $150 per spouse for married filing separately and married HOH |
| Additional child and dependent care credit | Married filing separately only if the federal living-apart rules are met |
| Stillborn child credit | $2,000 joint; $1,000 each for separate returns or unmarried parents |
