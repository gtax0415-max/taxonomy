# Retirement Income in Wisconsin
## Files in this folder
- Retirement Income Subtractions (retirement-income-subtractions.md) — military, pre-1964 public systems, railroad, the age-67 $24,000/$48,000 subtraction (bars all credits), and the age-65 $5,000 low-income subtraction
## Quick rules
- Social Security: not taxable in Wisconsin
- Military retirement and railroad retirement: not taxable
- Other pensions and IRA/401(k) distributions: taxable unless a subtraction applies
- Early-distribution penalty: Wisconsin adds 33% of the federal additional tax (Form 1, line 25)
