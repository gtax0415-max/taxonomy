# Wisconsin Net Operating Loss (Schedules NOL1, NOL2, NOL3)
## Description
Wisconsin computes its own NOL. The federal NOL deduction is added back and replaced by the Wisconsin NOL deduction. Individuals, estates, and trusts.
## Carryback and carryforward
- NOLs from taxable years beginning in 2014 or later: carry back 2 years (Form X-NOL), then forward 20 years — or elect to waive the carryback and only carry forward
- NOLs from years beginning before January 1, 2014: 15-year carryforward, tracked on Publication 120 Worksheet 3 (not NOL3); none from 2010 or earlier remain usable in 2026; 2011 losses can be used through 2026
- A carryforward may be used only if the loss-year return was filed within 4 years of its unextended due date
## NOL1 — computing the loss (loss year)
- Start with Wisconsin income (Form 1, line 7; 1NPR: line 16 col. B less line 29 col. B) minus the ALLOWABLE FEDERAL standard deduction (2025: $15,750 single/MFS, $31,500 MFJ, $23,625 HOH; 2026 federal amounts $16,100 / $32,200 / $24,150 — confirm on 2026 NOL1 instructions)
- Add back: Wisconsin capital gain exclusion, and gains deferred/excluded for qualified Wisconsin businesses and Wisconsin QOFs
- Nonbusiness deductions only to the extent of nonbusiness income; capital losses only to the extent of capital gains; add back NOL deductions from other years
- A FEDERAL NOL is required (modifications cannot create one), except Wisconsin tax-option (S) corporation shareholder losses
- Only losses SOURCED to Wisconsin are Wisconsin NOLs: a nonresident's securities losses source to the state of residence and cannot be carried back against prior Wisconsin income (Tax Appeals Commission, Panahi v. DOR, July 2, 2026)
## NOL2 — using the loss (use year)
- Part I: deduction cannot exceed Wisconsin taxable income before the NOL (income − Wisconsin standard deduction − exemptions; 1NPR prorates)
- Part II: Wisconsin MODIFIED TAXABLE INCOME (no NOL, no capital gain exclusions, no net capital loss, not below zero) = the amount of loss "used" each year on NOL3, even when no deduction results
## NOL3 — ledger
Lines 1b-22b record loss used each year (negative), line 23 shows expired loss. Used only for NOLs used in years beginning on or after January 1, 2018.
## Interactions
- Homestead (Schedule H line 11e) and farmland preservation (Schedule FC line 9e) ADD BACK NOL carryforwards/carrybacks to household income
- An NOL lowers tax available for business-income-limited credits (M&A), increasing credit carryforwards
## Required Information
NOL1 in the loss year; NOL2 and NOL3 every year a carryback or carryforward is claimed (with Form 1, 1NPR, 2, or X-NOL).
## Questions
- Did you have a federal NOL in 2026 or earlier years, and was the loss Wisconsin-sourced?
- Do you want to waive the 2-year carryback?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the federal NOL on the Wisconsin return
- Not reducing the NOL by modified taxable income in years when no deduction was used
- Claiming a Wisconsin NOL for nonresident-period securities losses

- I had a business loss. Can I carry it forward in Wisconsin?
