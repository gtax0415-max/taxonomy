# Private School Tuition Subtraction (Schedule PS)
## Description
Subtract tuition paid for a dependent child to attend an eligible private school, grades K-12.
## Parameters (statutory — same for 2026)
| Pupil | Maximum per child |
|---|---|
| Elementary (K-8; not 3K/4K) | $4,000 |
| Secondary (9-12) | $10,000 |
| Grade 8 and 9 in the same year | elementary part up to $4,000 + secondary part; total not over $10,000 |
No income limit. Grade is determined as of January 1 of the year paid (or first enrollment if mid-year).
## Eligibility
- Child claimed as YOUR dependent on your Wisconsin return (a grandparent paying for a parent's dependent cannot claim it)
- Full-year residents, part-year residents, and nonresidents may claim it; the school need not be in Wisconsin
- Eligible institution: private/religious school, privately controlled, at least 875 hours of instruction, sequential core curriculum, not for avoiding compulsory attendance; pupils return home at least 2 months each summer, or school licensed as a child welfare agency
## What counts as tuition
Tuition and mandatory book fees paid to the school. NOT: voucher-paid amounts, 529-paid amounts, room/board, registration, building, cap-and-gown fees, supplies, equipment rentals, meals, transportation, uniforms, before/after-school care, activities, non-credit classes.
## Required Information
School name, address, FEIN; each child's name, SSN, grade, and tuition (Schedule PS attached).
## Prompt
- We pay Catholic high school tuition. Is it deductible in Wisconsin?
