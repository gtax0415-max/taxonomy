# Farm Loss Carryover
## Description
Non-active farmers whose farm losses were limited on 2007-2013 Wisconsin returns may subtract the disallowed losses later, for up to 15 years, against net profit or gain from the SAME farming business.
## 2026 window
15-year carryforward: losses disallowed in 2011, 2012, and 2013 can still be used in 2026; 2011 losses expire after 2026. Losses from 2007-2010 have expired.
## Computation
Lesser of the remaining carryover or the current-year net profits/net gains from sale of capital or business assets of the same farming business (to which the loss limit applied).
## Prompt
- I have old Wisconsin farm loss carryovers.
