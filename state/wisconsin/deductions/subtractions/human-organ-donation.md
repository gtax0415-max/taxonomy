# Human Organ Donation Subtraction
## Rules
- FULL-YEAR Wisconsin resident; the donor is you, your spouse, or your dependent
- Organ: all or part of a liver, pancreas, kidney, intestine, lung, or bone marrow, donated to another person for transplantation
- Unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages
- Maximum $10,000; claimed only once and only in the year of the transplant
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother.
