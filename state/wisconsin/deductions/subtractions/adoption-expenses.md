# Adoption Expenses Subtraction
## Description
Subtract adoption fees, court costs, and legal fees for a child whose final adoption order (any state) or foreign adoption registration is entered during the year.
## 2026 parameters
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Maximum per child | $15,000 (raised from $5,000 by 2025 Wis. Act 15, effective 2025) |
| Expenses that count | paid in 2024, 2025, and 2026 for an adoption finalized in 2026 |
| Residency | FULL-YEAR Wisconsin resident |
| Multiple children | $15,000 per child |
## Notes
- Exclude amounts reimbursed by any adoption assistance program
- Separate from the federal adoption credit; both may apply
## Prompt
- We finalized an adoption this year.
