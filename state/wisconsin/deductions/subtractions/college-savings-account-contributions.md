# Wisconsin College Savings Account (529) Contributions
## Description
Owners of, or persons authorized to contribute to, an Edvest or Tomorrow's Scholar account may subtract contributions, per beneficiary, per year. Rollovers from another state's 529 into a Wisconsin account also qualify.
## Parameters
| Item | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Maximum subtraction per beneficiary | $5,130 | $5,280 (DFI, Wisconsin 529 College Savings Program) |
| Married filing separately | $2,560 | half the full amount — confirm on 2026 Schedule CS |
Indexed annually (2023 Wis. Act 148 base $5,000 for 2024).
## Rules
- Contributions above the limit may be carried forward to later years (Schedule CS tracks the carryover)
- Withdrawals within 365 days of a subtracted contribution are added back
- Contributions made by the tax filing deadline count for the prior year (DFI), so contributions through April 15, 2027 may be subtracted on the 2026 return — confirm the election mechanics on the 2026 Schedule CS
- Tuition paid from these accounts cannot also be used for the tuition or private school subtractions
## Line 11 — Edvest tuition-unit earnings
Earnings included in FAGI from a Wisconsin tuition-unit account refunded because the beneficiary finished without using all units, or received a non-cash scholarship/waiver, may be subtracted.
