# Long-Term Care Insurance Subtraction
## Description
Subtract premiums for a long-term care insurance policy covering you or your spouse (not dependents) — coverage primarily for convalescent or custodial care, chronic or terminal illness, at home or in an institutional/community setting.
## Computation
Premiums paid − portion deducted as self-employed health insurance − portion deducted on Schedule C/F for an employee-spouse; limited to Wisconsin income (and prorated for 1NPR).
## NOT eligible
Medicare supplement or replacement policies; continuing care contracts (entrance fee over $10,000 or transfer of at least $10,000 or 50% of estate); premiums paid with untaxed HSA or tax-free retirement-plan distributions.
## 2026 parameters
No dollar cap; no change identified. Amounts subtracted are removed from medical expenses for the itemized deduction credit.
