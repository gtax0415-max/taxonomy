# Wisconsin Subtraction Modifications (Expense-Based)
## Description
Wisconsin-only "deductions" taken as subtractions from income. Most are limited to the Wisconsin share of income for 1NPR filers (prorated by Wisconsin income ÷ federal AGI) and cannot exceed Wisconsin income.
