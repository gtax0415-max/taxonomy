# ABLE Account Contributions Subtraction
## Description
Subtract contributions to a qualified ABLE (Achieving a Better Life Experience) account for a disabled beneficiary.
## Parameters
| Item | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Combined maximum for ALL contributors to one account | $19,000 | $19,000 — Wisconsin keeps the annual gift-tax amount; the federal 2026 limit of $20,000 does NOT apply (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234) |
| Extra for an employed beneficiary with no employer-plan contributions | lesser of compensation or prior-year one-person poverty line ($15,060) | lesser of compensation or 2025 one-person federal poverty guideline ($15,650 — confirm on 2026 worksheet) |
## Rules
- Subtraction = contributions less refunds of excess contributions (excluding earnings); rollovers/transfers do not count
- An account accepting more than $19,000 of 2026 contributions is NOT a qualified ABLE account for Wisconsin
- Nonqualified withdrawals are added back (../../income/additions/college-savings-and-able-nonqualified-distributions.md)
- ABLE-paid transportation costs cannot be used for the blind worker transportation credit
## Prompt
- I contributed to my daughter's ABLE account.
