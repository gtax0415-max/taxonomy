# Wisconsin Deductions — Index (Tax Year 2026)
## Description
Wisconsin has no itemized deductions. Taxable income = Wisconsin income − sliding-scale STANDARD DEDUCTION − personal EXEMPTIONS. Most other "deductions" are SUBTRACTION MODIFICATIONS on Schedule SB (Form 1) or Schedule M Part II (Form 1NPR). Federal itemized deductions only matter through the nonrefundable ITEMIZED DEDUCTION CREDIT (Form 1, line 13 — ../credits/personal/itemized-deduction-credit.md).

## Federal deductions affected by Wisconsin's IRC (via Schedule I, not SB)
For 2026 Wisconsin follows the IRC as of December 31, 2022 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234). TCJA items suspended through 2025 return for Wisconsin — e.g., the moving expense deduction (sec. 217) for all taxpayers — while 2025 federal-law items do not apply. Recompute on Schedule I: ../income/schedule-i-federal-adjustments.md
## Not deductions in Wisconsin for 2026
- Federal deductions for qualified tips, overtime, the additional senior deduction, and car loan interest are below federal AGI and have no Wisconsin effect
- A Wisconsin tips/overtime subtraction (May 2026 special session) was NOT enacted
