# Wisconsin Personal Exemptions
## 2026 amounts (OFFICIAL — unchanged from 2025)
| Exemption | Amount |
|---|---|
| Taxpayer, spouse (joint return), each dependent | $700 |
| Additional — taxpayer 65 or older on December 31, 2026 | $250 |
| Additional — spouse 65 or older (joint return) | $250 |
## Rules
- No $700 exemption for anyone who can be claimed as a dependent on another return; a single filer claimable as a dependent gets 0
- Dependents = those listed as dependents on federal Form 1040 (Wisconsin keeps exemptions even though federal personal exemptions are $0)
- The $250 age exemption is allowed only to someone who also receives the $700 exemption
- A person born January 1, 1962 is treated as 65 on December 31, 2026
- Form 1NPR: prorated by Wisconsin income ÷ federal AGI
