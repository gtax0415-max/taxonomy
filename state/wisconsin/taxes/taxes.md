# Taxes on Form 1 Other Than Income Tax
## Files
- Sales and Use Tax (use-tax.md) — Form 1, Line 23
- Penalty on IRAs, Retirement Plans, MSAs, HSAs (retirement-account-penalties.md) — Form 1, Line 25 (33% of federal)
- Other Penalties and Credit Repayments (other-penalties-and-credit-repayments.md) — Form 1, Line 26
## Not on the Wisconsin individual return
Wisconsin has no household employment tax line and no individual surcharge on Form 1 (household employers handle state unemployment insurance with the Department of Workforce Development; the economic development surcharge applies only to businesses).
