# Sales and Use Tax on Out-of-State Purchases (Form 1, Line 23)
## Description
Wisconsin use tax is owed by the PURCHASER on taxable items used, stored, or consumed in Wisconsin when the seller did not charge Wisconsin sales tax — typically internet, mail-order, or out-of-state purchases, and purchases from foreign retailers (even if foreign tax or customs duty was paid). Individuals report it on Form 1, line 23. Filers with no use tax due must check the certification box; only a certified "no use tax due" counts as a filed use tax return.
## What is taxable (examples from the instructions)
Furniture, carpet, clothing, computers, books, CDs, DVDs, certain digital goods (e-books, music, video games, e-greeting cards), artwork, antiques, jewelry, and most coins bought for more than face value — plus other tangible personal property and taxable services.
## Computation (worksheet)
1. Total purchases subject to Wisconsin sales and use tax on which no tax was charged
2. Rate = 5% state + county tax for where the item is used + 2.0% city tax (City of Milwaukee only)
3. Use tax = line 1 × rate, rounded to whole dollars → Form 1, line 23
## 2026 county rates
| Location of use, storage, or consumption | County rate | Combined |
|---|---|---|
| Milwaukee County (outside the city) | 0.9% | 5.9% |
| City of Milwaukee | 0.9% county + 2.0% city | 7.9% |
| Waukesha County and Winnebago County | none | 5.0% |
| All other counties (70 of 72 now levy the county tax, including Manitowoc since January 1, 2025 and Racine since April 1, 2025) | 0.5% | 5.5% |
Confirm against the 2026 rate chart in the 2026 Form 1 instructions. Premier resort area taxes and other local excise taxes are not reported on line 23.
## Rules
- Applies only to purchases made in 2026 and used in Wisconsin; the county of use determines the county tax
- Sales tax properly paid to another U.S. state on the same item may be credited against the Wisconsin use tax (Publication 201)
- Businesses registered for sales/use tax report business purchases on their sales and use tax return, not Form 1
- Example (instructions): $300 of clothing delivered where the combined rate is 5% → $15 use tax; at 5.5% it would be $16.50, rounded to $17
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online, by mail, or out of state in 2026 without being charged Wisconsin sales tax?
- In which county (and is it the City of Milwaukee) was the item used?
## Common Errors
- Leaving line 23 blank instead of entering tax or checking the "no use tax due" box
- Using the seller's location rather than the Wisconsin county of use
- Ignoring purchases from foreign retailers
## Prompt
- Do I owe Wisconsin use tax on my online purchases?
