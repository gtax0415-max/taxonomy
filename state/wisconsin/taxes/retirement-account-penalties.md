# Wisconsin Penalty on IRAs, Retirement Plans, MSAs, and HSAs (Form 1, Line 25)
## Description
Wisconsin adds a penalty equal to 33% of certain federal additional taxes. Anyone who owes it must file a Wisconsin return.
## Federal taxes included (× 33%)
- Additional tax on IRAs and other qualified retirement plans (federal Schedule 2, line 8) — EXCEPT any amount based on Form 5329 line 8 (Coverdell and ABLE distributions)
- Form 5329 lines 4, 17, 25, 33, 41, 49, 51, and 55 when paid separately (excess contributions, required minimum distribution shortfall, etc.)
- Form 5330 excess contribution tax (line 2) and prohibited transaction taxes (lines 3a, 3b)
- Section 72(m)(5) excess benefits tax (Schedule 2, line 17j)
- Archer MSA distribution tax (Form 8853, line 9b) and HSA distribution tax (Form 8889, line 17b)
## Exceptions
No Wisconsin penalty on payments exempt from Wisconsin tax or excluded from Wisconsin income under Schedule SB lines 13, 14, 16, and 17 (pre-1964 public systems, federal CSRS, the retirement income subtractions). No penalty on Coverdell or ABLE account distributions.
## Example
Early 401(k) withdrawal of $15,000 (code 1): federal 10% tax $1,500 → Wisconsin penalty $495.
## Required Information
Copy of federal Form 5329 and/or 5330 attached to Form 1.
## Prompt
- I took money out of my 401(k) early. Does Wisconsin charge a penalty too?
