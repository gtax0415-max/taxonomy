# Other Penalties and Credit Repayments (Form 1, Line 26)
## Credit repayments (reported for the year of the recapture event)
| Credit | Repayment trigger |
|---|---|
| State historic rehabilitation (personal residence) | Sale, conveyance, or noncompliance within 5 years after completion: 100% / 80% / 60% / 40% / 20% of the credit used, by full years from approval (federal Form 4255 marked "Wisconsin") |
| Supplement to federal historic rehabilitation | Proportionate share when the federal credit on the same QREs is recaptured; the 5-year state schedule if no federal credit was claimed |
| Angel investment and early stage seed investment | Investment held less than 3 years (not if WEDC finds it worthless, or after 12 months if a bona fide liquidity event occurs) |
| Low-income housing | Qualified basis falls below the prior year-end basis — same amount repaid to the IRS |
Related credit files: ../credits/housing-and-historic/, ../credits/research-and-venture-capital/.
## Penalties
- Related-person asset sale: a buyer who disposes, within 2 years, of business or farm assets bought from a related person whose gain was excluded (not for involuntary conversions) — ../income/capital-gains/sale-of-assets-to-related-person.md
- Inconsistent estate basis reporting: 20% of the underpayment caused by claiming a basis above the federal estate tax basis
