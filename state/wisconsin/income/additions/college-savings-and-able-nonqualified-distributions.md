# Nonqualified College Savings (529) and ABLE Distributions (Addition)
## 529 — Edvest and Tomorrow's Scholar (AD line 3)
Add back, via Schedule CS:
- Distributions not used for qualified higher education expenses, to the extent previously subtracted
- Rollovers from a Wisconsin plan to ANOTHER state's plan
- Any withdrawal within 365 days after a contribution that was subtracted
NOT added back:
- Rollovers to the beneficiary's (or family member's) ABLE account, up to that year's ABLE annual limit ($19,000 Wisconsin limit for 2026 per Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234; 2025 also $19,000)
- 529-to-Roth IRA rollovers meeting the federal rules (annual Roth limit, $35,000 lifetime)
## ABLE (AD line 4)
The beneficiary adds back amounts withdrawn for anything other than qualified disability expenses, and amounts returned to the owner's estate at termination.
## 2026 notes
- Wisconsin does not recognize the new federal TRUMP ACCOUNTS (sec. 530A) — no exclusion for Wisconsin (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234)
- An ABLE account that accepts more than $19,000 of 2026 contributions does not qualify as an ABLE account for Wisconsin
