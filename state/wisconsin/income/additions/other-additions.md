# Other Wisconsin Additions
## Lump-sum distributions (AD line 6)
If federal tax was figured on Form 4972 (special averaging), add the capital gain part (Form 4972, line 6) plus the taxable amount (line 10), less any federal estate tax (line 18). No part may be treated as capital gain on Schedule WD.
## Passive foreign investment company excess distributions (AD line 7)
Add the excess distribution not included in federal AGI (Form 8621 / 8621-A).
## Moving a business out of Wisconsin or the U.S. (AD line 9)
Add back federally deducted costs of moving a Wisconsin business operation out of state or out of the country: vehicle and storage rentals, moving company charges, consulting and surveys, brokerage fees, architecture/design/remodeling, costs and losses of selling Wisconsin property, lease cancellation and utility fees, employee wages and reimbursements, professional fees, meals, lodging, fuel, and mileage.
## Other additions (AD line 32)
Any federal deduction not allowed for Wisconsin, e.g., a passive activity loss incurred while a resident of another state and not allocable to Wisconsin.
## Form 1NPR only
Schedule M line 26: other income from federal Schedule 1, line 9, received while a resident or from Wisconsin sources (exclude any federal NOL shown as a negative).
## Note — federal-law differences go on Schedule I, not here
Bonus depreciation, OBBBA-only deductions, and other IRC-conformity differences are adjusted on Schedule I (see ../schedule-i-federal-adjustments.md).
