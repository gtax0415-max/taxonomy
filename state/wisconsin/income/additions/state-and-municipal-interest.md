# State and Municipal Bond Interest (Addition)
## Description
Interest that is tax-exempt federally (Form 1040, line 2a) is generally TAXABLE in Wisconsin, including most Wisconsin municipal bonds. It must be added back unless the bond is on Wisconsin's exempt list.
## How it works
- Start with federal tax-exempt interest (1040, line 2a), reduced by any expenses that had to be allocated to it federally (attach the computation)
- Exclude interest from the bonds on Wisconsin's statutory exempt list (applies to direct holdings AND mutual fund shareholders)
- Form 1NPR: only interest received while a Wisconsin resident
## Wisconsin-exempt bonds (not added back) — summary of the 18 listed categories
- Wisconsin public housing / community development authority bonds; Wisconsin municipal redevelopment authority bonds
- WHEDA bonds: multifamily affordable or elderly housing issued on or after January 1, 2004; most issued before January 29, 1987; public affairs network loans; certain economic development loans (sec. 234.65)
- Pre-January 29, 1987 out-of-state public housing agency bonds exempt federally for reasons beyond IRC sec. 103
- Local exposition district bonds and sponsoring-municipality borrowings for them; professional baseball park district; professional football stadium; local cultural arts district; Wisconsin Aerospace Authority
- Bonds of Puerto Rico, Guam, U.S. Virgin Islands, Northern Mariana Islands, and American Samoa (issued after October 16, 2004)
- Wisconsin Health and Educational Facilities Authority: IT hardware/software bonds issued on or after October 27, 2007; certain refinancing bonds; issues of $35,000,000 or less
- Certain sec. 66.0304 conduit revenue bonds (DOR maintains the list)
## 2026 parameters
No dollar thresholds; list is statutory. Check DOR's conduit revenue bond list for 2026 issues.
## Notes
- Interest on U.S. government obligations works the opposite way — taxable federally, exempt in Wisconsin (see ../exclusions/federal-income-exempt-in-wisconsin.md)
- Municipal interest is also part of homestead and farmland preservation household income
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds or a tax-exempt bond fund? Which states issued them?
## Common Errors
- Assuming Wisconsin municipal bonds are exempt in Wisconsin (most are not)
- Adding back interest from Puerto Rico or the other listed territories
## Prompt
- Is my municipal bond interest taxable in Wisconsin?
