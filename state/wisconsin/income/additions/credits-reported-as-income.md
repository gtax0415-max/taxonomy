# Credits That Must Be Reported as Income (Addition)
## Description
Many Wisconsin business credits are taxable income. The credit is added on Schedule AD even if it is refundable or carried forward unused. This is the income side of the "CREDIT IS INCOME" rule noted in each credit file.
## Which credits, and which year
| Credit | Added on the 2026 return |
|---|---|
| Enterprise zone jobs | credit computed for 2026 |
| Community rehabilitation program | credit computed for 2026 |
| Research credits | credit computed for 2026 |
| Business development | credit computed for 2026 |
| Electronics & IT manufacturing zone | credit computed for 2026 |
| Employee college savings account contribution | credit computed for 2026 |
| NEW: Employer-provided child care; rail infrastructure modernization/maintenance | credit computed for 2026 (statute says the credit is included in Wisconsin income; addition line per 2026 Schedule AD) |
| MANUFACTURING AND AGRICULTURE | credit computed for 2025 (Line 16 of the 2025 Schedule MA-M / MA-A) — one year lag |
| Farmland preservation — Schedule FC | credit RECEIVED during 2026, to the extent not already in federal AGI (AD line 13) |
| Farmland preservation — Schedule FC-A | received in 2026 and reported in federal AGI — no Wisconsin addition needed |
## NOT on this list
Low-income housing, historic rehabilitation, angel and early stage seed (those reduce investment basis instead), film credits, and personal credits (homestead, EIC, school property tax, etc.) are not added back here.
## Pass-through credits
Credits from S corporations, partnerships, and LLCs are NOT entered on lines 14-25; they are reflected through the pass-through adjustments (AD lines 28-31; for 1NPR, in the Wisconsin-source amount on line 11).
## Questions
- Which Wisconsin credits did you compute for 2026, and what M&A credit did you compute for 2025?
- Did you receive a Schedule FC farmland preservation credit in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Adding only the portion of a credit actually used
- Adding the 2026 M&A credit instead of the 2025 one
- Double counting K-1 credits
