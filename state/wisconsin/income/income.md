# Wisconsin Income — Index (Tax Year 2026)
## Description
How Wisconsin turns federal adjusted gross income into Wisconsin taxable income, and where each income item lives in this taxonomy. Wisconsin starts from federal AGI and then ADDS items Wisconsin taxes but federal law does not (Schedule AD) and SUBTRACTS items Wisconsin exempts (Schedule SB). Wisconsin has no itemized deductions; it uses a sliding-scale standard deduction plus personal exemptions (see ../deductions/).

## 2026 conformity — read first
For 2026 Wisconsin still follows the IRC as amended to December 31, 2022 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234, July 2026). Most 2025 federal law changes do NOT apply, and TCJA provisions suspended through 2025 are RESTORED for Wisconsin in 2026. Any resulting difference in FAGI goes on Schedule I, not on Schedule AD/SB.
