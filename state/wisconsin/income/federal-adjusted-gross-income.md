# Federal AGI and Wisconsin Income (Form 1, Lines 1-7)
## Line by line
| Line | Item | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Federal adjusted gross income | Federal Form 1040, line 11 (from a pro forma federal return if the Wisconsin filing status differs, e.g., an amended change to joint) |
| 2 | Schedule I adjustments | Differences from Wisconsin's IRC (December 31, 2022 for 2026) — schedule-i-federal-adjustments.md |
| 3 | FAGI for Wisconsin | Line 1 + line 2; total Form W-2 wages included in line 3 are entered on the line below it |
| 4 | Additions (Schedule AD, line 33) | additions/ |
| 6 | Subtractions (Schedule SB, line 50) | exclusions/, ../deductions/subtractions/, ../deductions/retirement/ |
| 7 | Wisconsin income | Basis for the standard deduction, homestead and farmland household income, the NOL, and Schedule OS |
## Notes
- Below-the-line federal deductions (qualified tips, overtime, senior deduction, car loan interest, QBI, standard or itemized deductions) never enter the Wisconsin computation
- If no federal return is required, list income and deductions on a separate sheet with Form 1
