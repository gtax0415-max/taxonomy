# Veterinary Loan Repayment Grant Subtraction
Amounts received from Wisconsin's veterinary loan repayment grant program (sec. 39.389) are subtracted to the extent included in federal AGI. Effective for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2024, so it applies to 2026. Parallels the existing subtraction for primary care and psychiatry shortage grants.
## Prompt
- I received a Wisconsin veterinary loan repayment grant.
