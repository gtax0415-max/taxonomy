# Federal Income Exempt from Wisconsin Tax
## Description
Items included in federal AGI that Wisconsin does not tax. They are subtracted on Schedule SB (Form 1) or Schedule M Part II (Form 1NPR). Expense-type subtractions (insurance, tuition, 529, etc.) are in ../../deductions/subtractions/; retirement income subtractions are in ../../deductions/retirement/.
