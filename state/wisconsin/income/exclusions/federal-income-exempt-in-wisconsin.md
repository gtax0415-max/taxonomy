## Repayment of income previously taxed — 2026 change to watch
- Through 2025 this subtraction was effectively unavailable because the federal 2%-floor miscellaneous itemized deductions were suspended (TCJA) and Wisconsin followed that suspension
- Wisconsin follows the IRC as of December 31, 2022 and has restored TCJA-suspended provisions for 2026 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234). The 2%-floor deductions — and therefore this subtraction — are expected to be available again for 2026 under Wisconsin's IRC. Confirm on the 2026 Schedule SB instructions
- Repayments over $3,000 may instead use the repayment CREDIT (Form 1, line 32) if not subtracted
