# U.S. Armed Forces Active Duty Pay and Combat Zone Deaths
## Active duty pay (SB line 18)
- Subtract basic, special, and incentive pay (37 USC ch. 3 and 5) for ACTIVE duty included in W-2 wages
- Covers all regular and reserve components under the Secretaries of Defense, Army, Navy, Air Force (incl. Coast Guard); Reserve/Guard called to active federal service under 10 USC 12302(a), 12304, 12304(b), or state service under 32 USC 502(f)
- NOT included here: inactive duty training pay (see the 2026 Guard/Reserve subtraction below); housing allowance and other nontaxable pay
- Subtracted pay cannot be used as earned income for the married couple credit
## NEW 2026 — National Guard and Reserve pay
2025 Wis. Act 205 creates a subtraction, first available on 2026 returns, for National Guard and Reserve members' federal pay under 37 USC 204(a)(2), 206, or 206a (including inactive-duty training pay, which the active duty subtraction excludes) that is not subtracted under any other provision. Line number per the 2026 Schedule SB.
## Combat zone related death (SB line 19)
All income of a service member who died on active duty in a combat zone or from wounds/disease/injury incurred there is subtracted for the year of death (and the prior year if no return was filed). Attach DD Form 1300.
## 2026 conformity
Wisconsin does NOT treat the federal "qualified hazardous duty areas" (Sinai, Kenya, Mali, Burkina Faso, Chad) as combat zones except for filing extensions; income excluded federally for those areas must be added back for Wisconsin via Schedule I (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234). The Sinai is specifically excluded from "combat zone" for the death subtraction.
