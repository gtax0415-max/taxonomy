# Wisconsin Capital Gains
## Description
Wisconsin recomputes capital gains and losses on its own Schedule WD and offers several exclusions not available federally.
## Files in this folder
- wisconsin-capital-gains-wd.md — 30% long-term exclusion (60% for farm assets), capital loss limit, Schedule WD addition/subtraction
- qualified-wisconsin-business-gain-qi.md — Schedule QI exclusion for 5-year investments in a qualified Wisconsin business; deferral (Schedule CG)
- sale-of-assets-to-related-person.md — exclusion for selling business or farm assets to relatives (and the buyer's 2-year penalty)
- wisconsin-qualified-opportunity-fund.md — 10%/15% subtraction for Wisconsin QOF investments
## Questions
- Did you sell stock, real estate, business, or farm assets in 2026?
- Was any of it an investment in a qualified Wisconsin business or Wisconsin QOF held 5+ years?
