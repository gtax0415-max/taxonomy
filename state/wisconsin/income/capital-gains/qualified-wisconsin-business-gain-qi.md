# Gain on Investment in a Qualified Wisconsin Business (Schedule QI)
## Description
Long-term capital gain from selling an investment in a QUALIFIED WISCONSIN BUSINESS can be fully excluded if held at least 5 years. File one Schedule QI per sale (one QI even if a sale covers several qualifying lots).
## Qualified Wisconsin investment
- Amounts paid for stock or another ownership interest in a corporation, S corporation, partnership, or LLC
- Business was a qualified Wisconsin business for the year of investment AND at least 2 of the next 4 years
- Investment made after December 31, 2010 and held at least 5 uninterrupted years
- Qualified business = certified by WEDC (earlier years) or REGISTERED with DOR (2014 and later); DOR publishes the registration list but has not verified eligibility
- Single-member LLC: only if it elected C or S corporation status (Form 8832/2553) on or before January 1, 2011
## Computation
- Line 5 sales price; Line 6 WISCONSIN basis (reduced by deferred gain from Schedule CG and adjusted for 3K-1/5K-1 basis differences per Schedule T); Line 7 deferred gain
- Line 10 excluded gain → Schedule WD, line 15a. Deferred gain is TAXABLE on sale and cannot be excluded
- Excludable gain cannot exceed FMV at sale minus FMV when acquired
- C corporation stock: exclusion follows the specific qualifying lots (can exist even if the overall sale is a loss)
- Partnership/S corporation interests: allocate by FMV of qualifying investments ÷ FMV of all investments in the entity
## 2026 note
For a 2026 sale, qualifying investments must have been made by 2021 (5-year hold) in a business qualified that year and 2 of the following 4 years.
## Required Information
- Complete copy of the federal return, Schedule QI, and the allocation computation
## Questions
- When did you invest, and was the business on DOR's qualified Wisconsin business list that year and later?
- Did any of the investment come from a deferred gain (Schedule CG)?
## Common Errors
- Counting lots bought in years the business was not qualified, or held under 5 years
- Excluding deferred gain
- Using federal basis
## Prompt
- I sold stock in a Wisconsin startup I held for six years.
