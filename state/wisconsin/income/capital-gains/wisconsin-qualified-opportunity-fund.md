# Wisconsin Qualified Opportunity Fund (QOF) Subtraction
## Requirements
- In a prior year you deferred a capital gain by investing in a Wisconsin QOF
- The fund filed Wisconsin Form WQOF for the investment year and gave you a copy (not required for years before 2020)
- Held at least 5 years
- For the current year you qualify for the federal opportunity zone exclusion
- You are not also excluding/deferring the gain under the qualified Wisconsin business program (Schedules QI / CG)
## Computation
Deferred gain × 10% (held 5 to under 7 years) or × 15% (held 7 years or more).
## 2026 note
Under the IRC Wisconsin follows (as of December 31, 2022), deferred QOF gains are recognized by December 31, 2026, so 2026 is the key recognition year for most pre-2022 deferrals. Confirm the 2026 instructions' treatment before claiming.
## Notes
- Do not include QOF subtractions already passed through on Schedules 3K-1 (Part V, line 15) or 5K-1 (Part IV, line 19)
## Prompt
- I invested capital gains in a Wisconsin opportunity fund.
