# Sale of Business or Farm Assets to a Related Person
## Description
A seller may exclude the remaining taxable long-term capital gain from selling business assets or assets used in farming to close relatives.
## Requirements
- Buyer is the seller's child, grandchild, great-grandchild, parent, sibling, niece/nephew, grandparent, great-grandparent, aunt, or uncle (blood, marriage, or adoption)
- Asset held more than 12 months; gain is capital gain federally (ordinary recapture does not qualify)
- Stock in a corporation/trust: 15 or fewer shareholders/beneficiaries (family counted as one), no more than two share classes, all natural persons (other than an estate), engaged in FARMING
- Partnership/LLC interest: 15 or fewer members, all natural persons, engaged in FARMING
- "Business assets" exclude investment and residential rental property unless subject to self-employment tax; farm rental property may qualify as "used in farming"
## Computation
After netting on Schedule WD: related-party share of net long-term gain × 40% (farm assets — the other 60% was already excluded) or × 70% (business assets and qualifying shares/interests).
## Buyer's penalty
A related buyer who sells or disposes of the assets within TWO years owes a penalty on Form 1, line 26 (not for involuntary conversions).
## Prompt
- I'm selling my farm equipment to my son.
