# Related Entity Expenses (Schedules RT and RT-1)
## Description
Interest, rent, management fees, and intangible expenses paid, accrued, or incurred to a RELATED ENTITY must be added back. They may then be subtracted if one of three conditions is met. Applies to individuals, corporations, and pass-throughs.
## Addback and disclosure
- ALL such related-entity expenses are added back, regardless of amount
- Schedule RT disclosure is required only if the total exceeds $100,000 (after apportionment for multistate taxpayers); it must be filed by the EXTENDED due date of the return or the deduction can be disallowed — DOR will not accept a late RT
- Pass-through entities file Schedule RT; owners do not file it for passed-through expenses (and do not enter K-1-reported amounts on these lines)
## Definitions
- Related entity: related under IRC 267(b), 318(a), 707(b) (>50%), or 1563 controlled group; also non-qualified (captive) REITs
- Interest: sec. 163 interest; Rent: real property, including affixed personal property and related services, capital leases included; Management fees: purchased services for accounts receivable, benefits, insurance, legal, payroll, data processing, purchasing, taxation, finance, securities, accounting, compliance; Intangible expenses: royalties, patent/technical/copyright fees, licensing, factoring/discounting losses, amortization, other intangible-property costs
## Conditions for deduction (any one)
- A — Business purpose other than state tax avoidance; meaningful change in economic position; arm's-length terms
- B — The related payee included the income in a tax base AND its aggregate effective tax rate is at least 80% of the payer's (computed without loss/credit carryforwards; non-qualified REITs never qualify)
- C — Conduit: the payee paid the same expense to an unrelated party in the same taxable year (or before the payer's unextended due date), or is a bank/savings/S&L holding company or subsidiary (not for debt used to acquire own stock or assets under sec. 368)
## Schedule RT-1 (payee relief)
If the payer's deduction is disallowed, the payer completes Part I and gives RT-1 to the payee, who subtracts the corresponding income and files RT-1. Not available if the expense was actually deductible under Condition A. Pass-through payees report RT-1 at the entity level and on the K-1 "other items."
## Questions
- Do you pay rent, interest, management fees, or royalties to a related person or entity?
- Do the total expenses exceed $100,000?
## Common Errors
- Netting the addback and subtraction without making both entries
- Missing the extended-due-date deadline for Schedule RT
## Prompt
- I rent my business building from my own LLC.
