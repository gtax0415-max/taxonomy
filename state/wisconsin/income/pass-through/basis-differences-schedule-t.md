# Federal vs. Wisconsin Basis Differences (Schedule T — Transitional Adjustments)
## When to file
- You sell or dispose of an asset with a different Wisconsin and federal basis, or
- You depreciate/amortize an asset whose Wisconsin and federal basis differed when acquired
NOT for differences caused by Wisconsin's IRC definition (e.g., bonus depreciation) or different elections (e.g., Section 179) — those go on SCHEDULE I or a pro forma federal return.
## Constant basis assets (land, stocks, bonds)
Adjust only when sold (Part I). Common causes:
- Assets owned December 31, 1964
- Inheritances from deaths before 1992 valued differently for Wisconsin inheritance tax
- Wisconsin tax-option (S) corporation stock
- Qualified new business venture or qualified Wisconsin business investments whose Wisconsin basis was reduced by deferred gain (2011 or later)
- Out-of-state like-kind exchange gain deferred while a nonresident (not reportable to Wisconsin)
Nonresidents adjust only for assets located in Wisconsin.
## Changing basis assets (depreciable/amortizable property, inventories, capitalized intangibles, accruals and reserves)
- Acquired in a tax year beginning in 2014 or later with a basis difference: write the difference off over the asset's life (Part III) — or within 5 years or less with DOR consent (Office of Technical Services, PO Box 8933, Madison WI 53708-8933)
- On disposition before fully written off, take the remainder in the year of sale (Part II, using a recomputed "Wisconsin" Form 4797, and Forms 4684/6252/8824 as needed)
- Assets owned at the start of the first tax year beginning in 2014 have the same federal and Wisconsin basis — no adjustment
## Part I signs
Column C = federal basis − Wisconsin basis; a higher Wisconsin basis produces a negative (loss) adjustment.
## Examples from the instructions
- Stock bought with a $1,000 federal deferred gain (federal $3,000 / Wisconsin $4,000) and qualified Wisconsin business stock bought with a $5,000 Wisconsin deferred gain (federal $20,000 / Wisconsin $15,000) → net +$4,000 on WD line 15
- Ohio land exchanged for Wisconsin land while an Ohio resident: Wisconsin basis $75,000 vs. federal $25,000 → −$50,000
## Prompt
- My Wisconsin basis is different from my federal basis.
