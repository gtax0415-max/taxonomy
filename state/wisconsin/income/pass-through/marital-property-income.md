# Marital Property (Community) Income Differences
## Description
Wisconsin is a marital property (community property) state. Spouses filing separately — including married filing as head of household — or divorced/separated during the year may have to report a different amount of income for Wisconsin than federally.
## Rules
- Applies only while BOTH spouses are domiciled in Wisconsin; otherwise income follows title/ownership (common law)
- Additions: income taxable to you rather than your spouse; subtractions: income taxable to your spouse rather than you
- Earned income for the married couple credit ignores marital property rules
## References
Publication 109 (married filing separately and divorced in the year) and Publication 113 (marital property reporting).
## Prompt
- We're married but filing separate Wisconsin returns.
