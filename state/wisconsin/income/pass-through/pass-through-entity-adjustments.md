# Pass-Through Entity Adjustments and the Entity-Level Tax Election
## Ordinary adjustments (no entity-level election)
- Wisconsin Schedules 5K-1 / 3K-1 / 2K-1 report owner-level Wisconsin adjustments → AD 28/30 or SB 43/47
- Owner-level limitations (basis, at-risk, passive, Section 179 business-income limit) applied under Wisconsin's IRC → additions; previously suspended losses/Section 179 now allowed → subtractions
- Federal S corporation that elected NOT to be a Wisconsin tax-option corporation: reverse its items and add distributions of earnings and profits (Schedule D items excluded — handled on WD)
- Differences caused by IRC conformity (e.g., bonus depreciation) go on SCHEDULE I, not here
- Name and FEIN of each entity required; attach the K-1s
## ENTITY-LEVEL TAX ELECTION (secs. 71.21(6), 71.365(4m))
When the partnership or S corporation elects to pay Wisconsin tax itself, its income is REMOVED from the owner's Wisconsin income:
1. Run Schedule I, AD, and SB as if no election
2. Net all of that entity's items in Wisconsin income
3. Net income → SUBTRACT (SB 46 / SB 48); net loss → ADD BACK (AD 29 / AD 31)
- Lower-tier elections (box 4 checked) require the same removal for the lower tier's share; trusts/estates (2K-1 Box D) too
- Owner consequences: no credit for tax paid to other states on that income; S corporation charitable contributions not usable for the subtraction or itemized deduction credit; M&A credit cannot offset tax on entity-taxed income; withholding claimed only if not refunded/applied at entity level
- The election is made yearly by the EXTENDED due date of the entity return (for a calendar 2026 Form 3: September 15, 2027; Form 5S: October 15, 2027)
## S corporation charitable contributions (SB 42)
Owners may treat Schedule 5K-1 line 12a contributions as a subtraction instead of using them for the itemized deduction credit (not if entity-level tax elected). Other Schedule A items from the S corporation may likewise be subtracted (SB 43).
