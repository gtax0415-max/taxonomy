## Key 2026 facts
- Wisconsin follows the IRC as amended to December 31, 2022 for 2026; most 2025 federal (OBBBA) changes do not apply and TCJA-suspended items are restored for Wisconsin
- Brackets 3.50% / 4.40% / 5.30% / 7.65%; single brackets top out at $332,720 (MFJ $443,630)
- Standard deduction maximums: $13,960 single, $18,030 HOH, $25,840 MFJ, $12,280 MFS (phasing to zero)
- Exemptions $700 per person, $250 extra at 65+
- Age-67 retirement income subtraction up to $24,000 ($48,000 joint) BARS ALL CREDITS for the year
- New for 2026 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletins 232-234): stillborn child credit, employer-provided child care credit, rail infrastructure credits, film credits first claimable, National Guard/Reserve pay subtraction, 50-year research credit carryforward, low-income housing and historic credit changes; financial-exploitation loss and veterinary loan grant subtractions (2024/2025+) apply
- NOT enacted: a Wisconsin tips/overtime subtraction (May 2026 special session failed)
