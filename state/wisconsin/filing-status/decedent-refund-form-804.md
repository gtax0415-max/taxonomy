# Claim for Decedent's Wisconsin Refund (Form 804)
## Use
Claim a refund for a deceased taxpayer. File with the decedent's Form 1 or 1NPR (place on top; e-file as a PDF; check the box below the special conditions box), or mail with an uncashable refund check to DOR, PO Box 8903, Madison WI 53708-8903.
## Priority of claimants
Court-appointed or certified personal representative (attach domiciliary letter — a will or Transfer by Affidavit is not enough), then: surviving spouse, child, parent, brother or sister, creditor. Relatives must have proof of death available.
## Related final-return rules
- Report the decedent's income only to date of death
- Joint return allowed for the year of death if the survivor did not remarry; write "Filing as surviving spouse"
- Special condition codes: 06 (single/primary decedent), 07 (spouse deceased), 08 (both)
- Homestead credit cannot be claimed on behalf of a deceased person
## Prompt
- My mother died and was due a Wisconsin refund.
