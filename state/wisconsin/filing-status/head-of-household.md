# Head of Household (NOT Married or Married)
## Who qualifies
- Anyone who qualifies as head of household on the federal return may file Wisconsin head of household
- A federal QUALIFYING SURVIVING SPOUSE files Wisconsin as HEAD OF HOUSEHOLD (Wisconsin has no QSS status)
- Filers not required to file federally ask DOR whether they qualify
## Two boxes on Form 1
| Box | Use |
|---|---|
| Head of household, NOT married | Unmarried individual who paid over half the cost of keeping up a home for a qualifying person |
| Head of household, MARRIED | Married person who lived apart from the spouse for the last 6 months of 2026 and paid over half the cost of a home that was the main home of a child, stepchild, or foster child for more than half the year — enter the spouse's name and SSN |
## Wisconsin effects
- 2026 maximum standard deduction $18,030 (separate phase-down schedule; zero at $136,453); single rate brackets
- Married HOH filers: $150 school property tax credit cap; marital property income differences reported; the married HOH route preserves the Wisconsin EIC for separated spouses who meet IRC 7703(b)

- I'm separated with my child living with me. Can I file head of household in Wisconsin?
- I was a qualifying surviving spouse federally. What do I use in Wisconsin?
