# Married Filing Joint Return
## Who qualifies
- Married on December 31, 2026
- Spouse died during 2026 and the survivor did not remarry in 2026
- Married at the end of 2026 and the spouse died in 2027 before the 2026 return was filed
A joint return may be filed even if only one spouse had income or the spouses lived apart. A joint Form 1 requires both spouses to be full-year Wisconsin residents (or to elect Wisconsin residency under 50 U.S.C. 4001); otherwise use Form 1NPR.
## Rules
- Both spouses sign and are jointly and severally liable
- Cannot change from joint to separate after the original due date
- Not allowed if the spouses have different tax years
- Year of death: write "Filing as surviving spouse"; use special condition code 06, 07, or 08 (filing-extensions-and-special-conditions.md)
## Wisconsin effects
- Married couple credit available when both spouses have earned income (../credits/personal/married-couple-credit.md)
- 2026 maximum standard deduction $25,840, phasing to zero at $159,690
- Two $700 exemptions plus dependents; $250 more for each spouse 65+
