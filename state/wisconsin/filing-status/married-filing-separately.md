# Married Filing Separate Return
## Requirements
- Enter the spouse's full name and SSN
- Report income under Wisconsin MARITAL PROPERTY law while both spouses are Wisconsin domiciliaries (Publication 109); a marital property agreement can classify income as individual property (../income/pass-through/marital-property-income.md)
## Consequences in Wisconsin
See ../deductions/filing-status-differences.md for the full list. Key items:
- No married couple credit, no disability income exclusion, no Wisconsin EIC (unless IRC 7703(b) is met — then use head of household, married)
- $1,500 capital loss limit; $0 unemployment base if the spouses lived together at any time
- School property tax credit capped at $150 per spouse
- Separate → joint may be changed on an amended return; joint → separate may not after the due date
