# Who Must File, Which Form, and Residency
## Which form
- FORM 1: full-year Wisconsin residents. A joint Form 1 requires BOTH spouses to be full-year residents (or to elect Wisconsin as state of residence under 50 U.S.C. 4001)
- FORM 1NPR: domiciled in another state or country at any time during 2026, or filing jointly with a spouse who was
## Domicile
Your true, fixed, permanent home to which you intend to return. Once established it changes only when you (1) intend to abandon it and act on that intent, (2) intend to acquire a new domicile and act on that intent, AND (3) are physically present in the new domicile.
## Armed forces
A Wisconsin resident when entering military service stays a Wisconsin resident for the whole military career unless residency is changed or an election under 50 U.S.C. 4001 (Servicemembers Civil Relief Act / Military Spouses Residency Relief) is made (Publication 128).
## Who must file — 2026 gross income thresholds
| Status | Under 65 | 65 or older |
|---|---|---|
| Single | $14,660 | $14,910 |
| Married filing jointly | $27,240 | $27,490 one 65+ / $27,740 both |
| Married filing separately (each spouse) | $12,980 | $13,230 |
| Head of household | $18,730 | $18,980 |
2026 thresholds are DERIVED as maximum standard deduction + exemptions (the method reproduces every published 2025 amount: $14,260 / $26,510 / $12,630 / $18,220); confirm on the 2026 Form 1 instructions. "Gross income" excludes items exempt from Wisconsin tax (e.g., Social Security, U.S. government interest). Age: a January 1, 2027 birthday counts as a year older on December 31, 2026.
## Other filing requirements
- DEPENDENTS (claimable by another): file if gross income exceeds $1,350 and includes at least $451 of unearned income, or if gross income exceeds the maximum standard deduction (2026: $13,960 single, $18,030 HOH, $25,840 MFJ, $12,280 MFS). The $1,350 / $451 figures are 2025; confirm for 2026
- Anyone owing the Wisconsin penalty on an IRA, retirement plan, Coverdell excess contribution, ABLE account, HSA, or Archer MSA
- Nonresidents and part-year residents with gross income of $2,000 or more (married: combined), except nonresidents whose only income is exempt disaster-relief work during a Governor-declared emergency (Publication 411)
## Who should file even if not required
To get a refund of Wisconsin withholding or estimated tax, or to claim the earned income credit or the veterans and surviving spouses property tax credit (both refundable).
## Location fields on page 1
- Tax district: city, village, or town and county where you lived on December 31, 2026
- School district number: from the Form 1 instructions list (Section I districts operating high schools; Section II elementary-only districts — e.g., City of Milwaukee 3619, Hartford Jt. No. 1 elementary 2443). Missing numbers delay processing
