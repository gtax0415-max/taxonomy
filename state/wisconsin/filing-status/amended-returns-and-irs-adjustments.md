# Amended Returns and Federal Adjustments
## Deadlines
- Refund claims: within 4 years after the unextended due date (April 15, 2031 for 2026 returns); refunds of tax paid from an office or field audit within 4 years after the assessment if paid and not appealed. A protective amended return preserves later claims; an IRS Advance Pricing Agreement does not extend the period
- Federal or other-state amended return, or a final IRS adjustment, that affects Wisconsin income, a credit, an NOL/capital loss carryforward, or tax: file an amended Wisconsin return (or send the IRS report to the Audit Bureau, PO Box 8906, Madison WI 53708-8906) within 180 days
## How
Use Form 1 with the amended box checked and attach Schedule AR plus all changed schedules (not a copy of the original). NOL carrybacks use Form X-NOL instead. Enter corrected amounts on changed lines and the original (or adjusted) amounts elsewhere.
## Amended-only lines
- Line 36: tax paid with the original and afterward (not interest or penalties)
- Line 38: refund from the original (before underpayment interest/penalty reductions; as adjusted by DOR)
- Line 42: the amount applied to 2027 estimates generally must match the original unless the amended return is filed by January 15, 2028 (for the 2026 return)
- Line 44: enter only the CHANGE in underpayment interest (negative if reduced) with a corrected Schedule U
## Other rules
- Filing status: separate → joint allowed; joint → separate not allowed after the due date
- Donations: may be increased anytime; decreased only on an amended return filed by October 15, 2028 (or within 18 months of a late original filing) for the 2026 return
- Credit repayments (historic, angel/seed, low-income housing) are reported by amending/reporting on line 26 for the year of the recapture event
## Copies of prior returns
Request through My Tax Account (Request Tax Record Copy) with the fee.
