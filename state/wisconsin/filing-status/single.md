# Single
## Who qualifies (status on December 31, 2026)
- Never married
- Legally separated under a final decree of divorce or separate maintenance (in Wisconsin, a judgment of legal separation under sec. 767.35)
- Widowed before January 1, 2026 and not remarried (unless head of household applies)
## Wisconsin effects
- 2026 maximum standard deduction $13,960, phasing to zero at Wisconsin income of $136,453
- One $700 personal exemption (none if claimable as someone else's dependent); $250 more at 65+
- School property tax credit up to $300
