# Signatures, Third Party Designee, and Wisconsin Identity Protection PIN
## Signatures
Form 1 is not valid unless signed; both spouses sign a joint return; include daytime phone numbers. Survivors or personal representatives signing for a decedent note their relationship (see decedent-refund-form-804.md).
## Third party designee
Checking "Yes" and giving the designee's name, phone, and a 5-digit PIN lets DOR discuss processing questions with that person, who may supply missing information, ask about refund/payment status, and respond to math-error, offset, and preparation notices. The designee cannot receive refund checks, bind the taxpayer, or represent the taxpayer (that needs Form A-222, Power of Attorney). The authorization ends by the unextended due date of the NEXT year's return (for the 2026 return: April 15, 2028).
## Wisconsin Identity Protection PIN
Enter the 7-character PIN DOR issued (each spouse's if both received one); leave blank otherwise. It is different from the IRS IP PIN. Sign up through My Tax Account.
