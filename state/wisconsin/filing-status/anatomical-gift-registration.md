# Anatomical Gift (Organ and Tissue Donor) Registration — Schedule 3, Part II
## Description
Filers may add themselves (and a spouse on a joint return) to the Wisconsin Donor Registry by checking the box and giving sex and date of birth. DOR passes the authorization to the Department of Transportation for the Department of Health Services registry. No effect on tax.
## Rules
- Wisconsin residents at least 15 years old, or emancipated minors
- Do not complete if already registered, or if a nonresident or part-year resident who left Wisconsin
- Optional — not required to file, pay, or get a refund
