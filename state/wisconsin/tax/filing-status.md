# Wisconsin Filing Status
## The five Form 1 statuses
| Status | Rules |
|---|---|
| Single | Never married; legally separated under a final decree of divorce or separate maintenance (Wisconsin: judgment under sec. 767.35) on December 31, 2026; or widowed before January 1, 2026 and not remarried |
| Married filing joint return | Married on December 31, 2026; spouse died in 2026 (no remarriage); or spouse died in 2027 before the 2026 return was filed. Both spouses sign and are jointly liable. Cannot switch from joint to separate after the due date. Not allowed if spouses have different tax years |
| Married filing separate return | Spouse's name and SSN required. Usually more tax |
| Head of household, NOT married | Qualifies as head of household federally |
| Head of household, MARRIED | Married but lived apart from the spouse for the last 6 months of 2026 and paid over half the cost of a home for a child, stepchild, or foster child for more than half the year; enter the spouse's name and SSN |
A federal QUALIFYING SURVIVING SPOUSE files Wisconsin as HEAD OF HOUSEHOLD. Filers not required to file federally ask DOR whether they qualify for head of household. If more than one status applies, choose the one with the lowest tax.
## What married filing separately costs in Wisconsin
- No married couple credit; no disability income exclusion
- More unemployment compensation taxable if the spouses lived together at any time
- Capital loss limit $1,500 instead of $3,000
- Smaller standard deduction ($12,280 maximum, phasing out by $75,869) and half-size bracket thresholds; $150 school property tax credit cap per spouse (also for married head of household)
- No Wisconsin EIC unless IRC 7703(b) is met (then use married HOH); child and dependent care credit only if the federal living-apart rules are met
- Marital (community) property income must be split under Wisconsin marital property law (Publication 109)
- Head of household married filers also report marital property income differences
## Changing status on an amended return
Separate → joint is allowed (both sign; tax due must be paid in full); joint → separate is not allowed after the original due date. Federal AGI on line 1 must come from a federal (or pro forma) return using the same status.
