# Wisconsin Tax Rates, Brackets, and Filing Thresholds (2026)
## 2026 tax rate schedules — full-year residents (OFFICIAL, 2026 Form 1-ES)
| Rate | Single / Head of household / Estates and trusts | Married filing jointly | Married filing separately |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.50% | $0 – $15,110 | $0 – $20,150 | $0 – $10,080 |
| 4.40% | $15,110 – $51,950 | $20,150 – $69,260 | $10,080 – $34,630 |
| 5.30% | $51,950 – $332,720 | $69,260 – $443,630 | $34,630 – $221,820 |
| 7.65% | over $332,720 | over $443,630 | over $221,820 |

The expanded 4.40% bracket comes from 2025 Wis. Act 15 (effective 2025); 2026 thresholds are the indexed amounts (August 2024 is now the base year for indexing).

## Head of household uses the SINGLE brackets
Head of household gets a larger standard deduction but the same rate schedule as single filers.
## Nonresidents and part-year residents
Brackets are prorated by Wisconsin income ÷ federal AGI. Example from DOR: a single filer with a 20% ratio pays 3.5% on the first $3,022 ($15,110 × 20%), 4.4% on the next $7,368, 5.3% on the next $56,154, and 7.65% above $66,544.
## Who must file — 2026 (DERIVED, confirm on 2026 Form 1 instructions)
The gross income filing threshold equals the maximum standard deduction plus exemptions (this reproduces every 2025 published threshold exactly).
| Filing status | Under 65 | 65 or older |
|---|---|---|
| Single | $14,660 | $14,910 |
| Married filing jointly | $27,240 | $27,490 (one 65+) / $27,740 (both 65+) |
| Married filing separately | $12,980 | $13,230 |
| Head of household | $18,730 | $18,980 |
Also must file: nonresident/part-year resident with $2,000+ gross income; anyone owing a penalty on an IRA, retirement plan, HSA, Archer MSA, or Coverdell/ABLE excess; dependents above the dependent thresholds. "Gross income" excludes items exempt from Wisconsin tax (e.g., Social Security, U.S. government interest).
## Other 2026 rate items
- Penalty on IRAs, retirement plans, MSAs, HSAs: 33% of the related federal additional taxes (Form 1, line 25) — not on Coverdell/ABLE distributions or on exempt retirement payments
- Estimated tax: required if 2026 tax after withholding and credits is $500 or more; installments April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027; safe harbors 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax
- Interest: 12% per year on tax not paid by the due date (1% per month during an extension); 18% per year on delinquent bills; refund interest 3% (see ../administration/refunds-payments-interest-and-penalties.md)
