# Other Taxes, Penalties, Credit Repayments, and Donations (Form 1 lines 23-26)
## Line 23 — Sales and use tax on out-of-state purchases
Report Wisconsin use tax on taxable purchases (internet, mail order, out-of-state, foreign) stored, used, or consumed in Wisconsin where no Wisconsin tax was charged. Rate = 5% state + county tax (2025: 0.5% in most counties, 0.9% Milwaukee County, none in Waukesha or Winnebago; Racine County from April 1, 2025) + 2.0% City of Milwaukee. Use the 2026 rate chart in the 2026 instructions. Check the certification box if none is due — only certified returns count as a filed use tax return.
## Line 24 — Donations (Schedule 3, Part I)
Endangered resources, cancer research, veterans trust fund, multiple sclerosis, military family relief fund, Second Harvest/Feeding America, Red Cross Wisconsin disaster relief, Special Olympics Wisconsin. Reduces the refund or increases the balance due.
## Line 25 — Penalties on IRAs, retirement plans, MSAs, HSAs
33% of the federal additional taxes (federal Schedule 2 line 8, Form 5329 when paid separately, Form 5330 excise taxes, sec. 72(m)(5), Archer MSA and HSA distribution taxes). No penalty on Coverdell or ABLE distributions or on distributions exempt from Wisconsin tax.
## Line 26 — Other penalties and credit repayments
- Buyer's penalty for disposing within 2 years of business/farm assets bought from a related person whose gain was excluded (../income/capital-gains/sale-of-assets-to-related-person.md)
- Inconsistent estate basis reporting: 20% of the resulting underpayment
- Credit repayments: state historic rehabilitation, supplement to federal historic rehabilitation, angel and early stage seed investment, low-income housing (see the credit files)
