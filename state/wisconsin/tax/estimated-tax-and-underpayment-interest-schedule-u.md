# Estimated Tax and Underpayment Interest (Form 1-ES / Schedule U)
## Who must pay estimated tax for 2026
Anyone expecting at least $500 of 2026 tax after withholding and credits whose withholding will be less than the smallest of: 90% of 2026 tax, 100% of 2025 tax (12-month return), or 90% of annualized 2026 tax. Exempt: full-year 2025 residents with zero 2025 liability. The prior-year safe harbor is not available to estates or trusts with $20,000+ of taxable income.
## 2026 installment dates (calendar year)
April 15, 2026 · June 15, 2026 · September 15, 2026 · January 15, 2027 (fiscal filers: 15th day of months 4, 6, 9, and 1 of the next year).
- Filing the 2026 return and paying in full by January 31, 2027 counts as the January installment if no earlier installment was required
- Farmers and fishers (two-thirds of gross income from farming/fishing in 2025 or 2026): pay all by January 15, 2027, or file and pay by March 1, 2027 with no estimates
## Underpayment interest (Schedule U)
- Required annual payment = smaller of 90% of current-year net tax (less refundable credits) or 100% of prior-year tax
- No interest if current-year tax minus withholding is under $500
- Rate: 12% per year, computed per installment from each due date to the payment date or April 15 of the following year, whichever is earlier
- Short method (no estimates, or four equal timely payments) uses a single multiplier (2025 form: .0798, with a daily factor of .0003287 for early payments); the regular method computes each installment; the annualized method (Part IV) uses factors 4, 2.4, 1.5, 1 and percentages 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%, 90%
- Withholding is treated as paid evenly unless you show actual dates (code 08)
- Overpayments carry back and forward between installments in the prescribed order
## Form 1 line 44 quick tests
You owe underpayment interest if line 43 (amount owed) is at least $500 AND more than 10% of the tax shown (line 22 minus lines 30-35), or if any installment was short. No interest if the prior year was a 12-month return and either (1) you were a full-year resident with zero prior-year tax, or (2) timely withholding plus estimates (lines 28-29) at least equal the prior-year tax shown. Failure to enter an applicable exception code can trigger an assessment.

## Overpayment application
To apply a 2025 overpayment to 2026 estimates after filing, the election must be made by January 15, 2027 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234).
