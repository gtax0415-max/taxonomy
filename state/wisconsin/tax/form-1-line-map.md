- Schedule 1 — Itemized deduction credit; Schedule 2 — Married couple credit
- Schedule 3 — Part I donations; Part II anatomical gift (organ donor) registration (residents 15+ or emancipated minors)
## Retirement subtraction bar
Claiming the age-67 retirement income subtraction (Schedule SB line 16) bars Schedule CR and Form 1 lines 13-20 and 30-35.
## Filing logistics
Due April 15, 2027 for calendar 2026 (federal extension provisions apply to Wisconsin, but interest runs at 1% per month during the extension). Mail: payment enclosed PO Box 268, Madison WI 53790-0001; refund/no payment PO Box 59, 53785-0001; homestead claimed PO Box 34, 53786-0001.
