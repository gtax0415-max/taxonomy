# Wisconsin Information Returns — Form 9b
## Who files
Individuals, fiduciaries, partnerships, LLCs, and corporations doing business in Wisconsin that pay individuals:
- Box 1: rents and royalties of $600+ to Wisconsin residents (any property location)
- Box 2: rents and royalties of $600+ to nonresidents on Wisconsin property
- Box 3: annuities, pensions, and other nonwage compensation of $600+ to Wisconsin residents, and $600+ to nonresidents for services performed in Wisconsin
Payers other than corporations report rents and royalties only if they deduct them for Wisconsin. If the federal 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, 1099-R, or W-2 is required, file that instead of Form 9b; if Wisconsin tax was withheld, the federal form is required.
## 2026 — Wisconsin keeps the $600 threshold
The federal threshold for many payments rises to $2,000 for 2026, but Wisconsin still requires reporting of non-wage payments for services in the state over $600 (and whenever Wisconsin tax is withheld). Deductions for payments over $600 not reported to Wisconsin can be DISALLOWED (e.g., sec. 71.26(3)(e)) (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234).
## Deadlines
For 2026 payments: file with DOR and furnish recipients by January 31, 2027 — which falls on a Sunday, so the due date is expected to be February 1, 2027 (the 2025 form was due February 2, 2026). The recipient deadline cannot be extended; a 30-day DOR extension may be requested (Publication 117).
## Other rules
- 10 or more returns of one type → must file electronically
- Penalty $10 per violation for late, incorrect, or incomplete returns due to willful neglect
- Sales taxes imposed on the service provider and paid by the buyer are part of the reportable payment
- Mail Copy A to Wisconsin Department of Revenue, PO Box 8920, Madison WI 53708-8920
