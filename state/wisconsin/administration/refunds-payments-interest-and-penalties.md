# Refunds, Payments, Interest, and Penalties
## Refunds (lines 40-42)
- Overpayment (line 40) is reduced by any underpayment interest (line 44); choose the refund (line 41) and/or amount applied to 2027 estimated tax (line 42; joint returns apply to joint estimates)
- No refund before March 1 unless both the individual and the employer have filed all required returns and forms
- Refund interest: 3% per year from the due date, EXCEPT refunds issued within 90 days of the later of the due date or filing date; refunds from homestead, enterprise zone jobs, business development, and electronics and IT manufacturing zone credits; and amounts applied to estimates
- Refund status: wait 12 weeks; (608) 266-8100, (414) 227-4907, 1-866-947-7363, or online
- Divorced filers: attach the decree (code 04) to direct refunds/liabilities; injured spouse: Form 8379 (code 05)
## Paying a balance (lines 43-46)
- Free direct debit at tap.revenue.wi.gov/pay; check or money order to the Wisconsin Department of Revenue (Form PV if e-filed); credit card, Apple Pay, PayPal with a $1 fee plus 2.25%
- Interest on tax not paid by the due date: 12% per year from the due date (line 46, separate from line 45)
- Underpayment interest on estimates: line 44 (see ../tax/estimated-tax-and-underpayment-interest-schedule-u.md)
- Can't pay: installment agreement (Form A-771) with a $20 fee; unpaid bills accrue delinquent interest of 18% per year and a collection fee of the greater of $35 or 6.5%; unpaid debts may be offset against federal refunds (Treasury Offset Program)
## Penalties for not filing or incorrect returns
- Delinquent tax interest 18% per year; civil penalties up to 100% of unreported tax; criminal: fine up to $10,000 and imprisonment
- Refundable credit claims (EIC, homestead, veterans property tax, etc.): RECKLESS claim → no refundable credits for 2 years (and up to a 25% penalty); FRAUDULENT claim → no refundable credits for 10 years (and up to a 100% penalty)
