# Filing Deadlines, Extensions, Special Conditions, and How to File
## Due date
Calendar-year 2026 Form 1 is due April 15, 2027. Fiscal or short-period filers enter the period and special condition code 11.
## Extensions
- No advance request to Wisconsin: any federal extension provision applies (e.g., the automatic 6-month federal extension) — attach a copy of federal Form 4868 or a statement naming the federal provision
- Interest of 1% per month runs on tax unpaid after April 15, 2027 even with an extension; pay by April 15 using a 2026 Form 1-ES (not Form PV)
- No extension-period interest for combat zone/contingency operation (code 02) or federally declared disaster (code 03) extensions
- Farmers and fishers without estimates: file and pay by March 1, 2027
## Special condition codes (page 1 box; if several apply, enter 99 and list them)
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|

A separate box marks that Form 804 (decedent's refund) is filed with the return.
## E-filing
Fastest refunds. WisTax (DOR's free system through My Tax Account) is limited to full-year residents with a Wisconsin driver's license/ID who filed a Wisconsin return or homestead claim in the past five years, have only limited Schedule AD/SB items, withholding only from W-2, 1099-R, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, W-2G, and only these credits: school property tax, itemized deduction, additional child and dependent care, blind worker transportation, married couple, earned income, and veterans and surviving spouses property tax. Homestead claims can be filed separately in WisTax. Attachments not sent electronically go with Form W-RA within 48 hours of acknowledgment.
## Paper filing
Original (not photocopied) pages, black ink, whole dollars (round 50 cents up), no commas or dollar signs, minus sign for negatives, no staples. Assemble: payment (paper clipped to front), Form 804 on top if filed, the five Form 1 pages, Wisconsin schedules, Schedule AR if amended, W-2/1099 withholding statements, complete federal return, extension copy, divorce decree, Form 8379. Mail to: payment enclosed PO Box 268, Madison WI 53790-0001; refund or no payment PO Box 59, Madison WI 53785-0001; homestead claimed PO Box 34, Madison WI 53786-0001. Private delivery: 2135 Rimrock Rd., Madison WI 53713.
