# Wisconsin Allocation and Separate Accounting Data (Form C) — business entities
## Scope
Not an individual form. Partnerships, corporations, S corporations, and exempt organizations that ALLOCATE (rather than apportion) income use Form C to split book income and the reconciliation to Wisconsin net income between Wisconsin and outside Wisconsin.
## Key points
- Part I: allocation of income and expenses; book income must agree with federal Schedule M-1 line 1 (or M-3 separate-company net income)
- Part II: reconcile to federal taxable income, add Wisconsin additions/subtractions, then separate the portion apportioned vs. allocated → allocable net income to Form N, line 6
- Requires written DOR permission to use separate accounting; disclose inter-branch transfers (sales, purchases, management fees, interest, royalties) and transfer-pricing method
## Relevance to individuals
Owners see the result through Wisconsin-source income on Schedules 3K-1 / 5K-1.
