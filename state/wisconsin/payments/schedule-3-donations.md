# Schedule 3 Donations (Form 1, Line 24)
## Description
Voluntary gifts made on the return. They increase the balance due or reduce the refund. Any combination of funds and amounts may be chosen.
## Funds
| Line | Fund | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1a | Endangered Resources | DNR program protecting native species and natural communities; matched up to a set amount by state funds |
| 1b | Cancer Research | Split equally between the Medical College of Wisconsin and the UW Carbone Cancer Center |
| 1c | Veterans Trust Fund | Wisconsin Department of Veterans Affairs programs for veterans and dependents |
| 1d | Multiple Sclerosis | National MS Society, for Wisconsin programs |
| 1e | Military Family Relief Fund | Department of Military Affairs aid to families of Wisconsin service members on active duty |
| 1f | Second Harvest / Feeding America | 65% Feeding America Eastern Wisconsin, 20% Second Harvest Southern Wisconsin, 15% Feed My People |
| 1g | Red Cross Wisconsin Disaster Relief | American Red Cross Wisconsin Disaster Relief Fund |
| 1h | Special Olympics Wisconsin | Special Olympics Wisconsin, Inc. |
## Amended returns
Donations may be added or increased on an amended return at any time; a decrease is allowed only if the amended return is filed by October 15, 2028 for the 2026 return (or within 18 months of a late-filed original).
