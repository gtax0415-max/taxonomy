# Wisconsin Pass-Through Entity Withholding
## Description
Partnerships, LLCs, tax-option (S) corporations, estates, and trusts pay Wisconsin withholding on the Wisconsin income of nonresident owners (Form PW-1). The owner claims it as a payment. A full-year resident may claim Wisconsin pass-through withholding allocated from a TIERED entity in which they are a member.
## Rules
- Claim only amounts reported on the Wisconsin K-1 / PW-1 statement for the 2026 tax year
- If the entity elected entity-level tax (secs. 71.21(6), 71.365(4m)) and requested a refund or application of the withholding at the entity level, the owner may not claim it
- 2025 Wis. Act 137 (March 29, 2026) clarifies that pass-through withholding and nonresident entertainer withholding reduce a nonresident's liability
