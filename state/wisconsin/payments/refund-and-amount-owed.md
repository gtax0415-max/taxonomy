# Refund and Amount Owed (Form 1, Lines 40-46)
## Overpayment (lines 40-42)
- Line 40 is reduced by any underpayment interest on line 44
- Line 41 refund; line 42 amount applied to 2027 estimated tax (joint returns apply it to joint estimates)
- No refund before March 1 unless the individual and the employer filed all required returns
- Refund interest 3% per year from the due date, except refunds issued within 90 days of the later of the due date or filing date, refunds of homestead, enterprise zone, business development, and electronics and IT manufacturing zone credits, and amounts applied to estimates
- Refund status after 12 weeks: (608) 266-8100, (414) 227-4907, 1-866-947-7363, or online
- Divorce decree apportioning a refund or liability: attach it (special condition 04); injured spouse Form 8379 (code 05)
## Balance due (lines 43-46)
- Pay by April 15, 2027: free direct debit (tap.revenue.wi.gov/pay); check or money order to the Wisconsin Department of Revenue (Form PV if e-filed); card, Apple Pay, or PayPal ($1 + 2.25%)
- Interest on tax not paid by the due date: 12% per year (line 46, not included in lines 43 or 45)
- Cannot pay in full: installment agreement (Form A-771), $20 fee; unpaid bills accrue 18% delinquent interest and a collection fee of the greater of $35 or 6.5%; unpaid liabilities may be offset against federal refunds (Treasury Offset Program)
## Penalties for not filing or incorrect returns
Civil penalties up to 100% of unreported tax; criminal fines up to $10,000 and imprisonment. Reckless refundable-credit claims bar refundable credits for 2 years; fraudulent claims for 10 years.
