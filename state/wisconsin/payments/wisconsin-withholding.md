# Wisconsin Income Tax Withheld (Form 1, Line 28)
## What counts
- Form W-2 Box 17 and Form 1099-R Box 14 — only where Box 15 shows Wisconsin
- Forms W-2G, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC (and other information returns) showing Wisconsin withholding
- Wisconsin pass-through entity withholding allocated from a tiered entity (see pass-through-withholding.md)
Attach readable copies of every withholding statement (paper returns) or report them with the e-filed return.
## Do NOT include
- Withholding for other states, Social Security or Medicare tax, federal withholding
- Statements from other tax years
- Withholding from an S corporation or partnership that elected entity-level tax where the entity requested a refund of, or application of, that withholding at the entity level
Do not alter withholding statements; ask the employer or payer for a corrected form.
## Related
- Form WT-4 / WT-4A control employee withholding; WT-4P for pensions
- No refund before March 1 unless the individual and the employer have filed all required returns (refund-and-amount-owed.md)
