# Underpayment Interest (Schedule U; Form 1, Line 44)
## When it applies
- Form 1 line 43 (amount owed) is at least $500 AND more than 10% of the tax shown (line 22 minus lines 30-35), or
- Any installment was short — even if a refund is due
No interest if the prior year was a 12-month return and either (1) a full-year resident had zero prior-year tax, or (2) timely withholding plus estimates at least equal the prior-year tax.
## Computation
- Required annual payment = smaller of 90% of current-year net tax (less refundable credits) or 100% of prior-year tax
- Rate 12% per year per installment, from each due date to the payment date or April 15 of the following year, whichever is earlier
- Short method (no estimates, or four equal timely payments); regular method by installment with overpayment carrybacks/carryforwards; annualized income method (Part IV: factors 4, 2.4, 1.5, 1; percentages 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%, 90%)
- Withholding is treated as paid evenly unless actual dates are shown (code 08)
## Exception and waiver codes (lowest applicable code)
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 02 | Full-year resident with zero prior-year tax |
| 03 | Estate or grantor trust within 2 years of death |
| 04 | Farmer or fisher filing and paying by March 1, 2027 |
| 05 | Waiver — casualty, disaster, unusual circumstance |
| 06 | Waiver — retired after 62 or disabled, reasonable cause |
| 07 | Waiver — active duty stationed outside the continental U.S. |
| 08 | Annualized income method or actual withholding dates |
| 09 | Net operating loss in annualization |
| 10 | Federally declared disaster or terroristic/military action |
Failing to enter an applicable code can trigger an assessment.
