# Wisconsin Estimated Tax Payments (Form 1-ES; Form 1, Line 29)
## Who must pay
Anyone expecting at least $500 of 2026 Wisconsin tax after withholding and credits whose withholding will be less than the smallest of 90% of 2026 tax, 100% of 2025 tax (12-month return), or 90% of annualized 2026 tax. Full-year 2025 residents with zero 2025 tax need not pay. The prior-year rule is not available to estates or trusts with $20,000+ of taxable income.
## 2026 due dates (calendar year)
April 15, 2026 · June 15, 2026 · September 15, 2026 · January 15, 2027 (fiscal filers: 15th day of months 4, 6, 9 and month 1 of the next year).
- Later first-payment dates when the requirement arises later: June 15 (pay 1/2), September 15 (3/4), January 15, 2027 (all)
- Filing the 2026 return and paying in full by January 31, 2027 counts as the last installment if no earlier installment was required
- Farmers and fishers (two-thirds of gross income from farming/fishing in 2025 or 2026): pay all by January 15, 2027, or file and pay by March 1, 2027 with no estimates
## Line 29 entries
- All 2026 Form 1-ES payments, including any Form 1-ES extension-type payment, plus the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026
- Joint returns: both spouses' separate and joint payments; separate returns may split joint payments as the spouses choose (DOR splits by liability if they disagree)
- Name changes: attach a statement listing payments made under the prior name
- An election to apply a 2025 overpayment to 2026 estimates must be made by January 15, 2027 (Wisconsin Tax Bulletin 234)
## Payment methods
Free online payment (tap.revenue.wi.gov/pay), personalized Form 1-ES vouchers by check, or card/Apple Pay/PayPal through the processor ($1 plus 2.25%).
