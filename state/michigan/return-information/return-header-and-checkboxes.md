# Return Header, Checkboxes, and Relief Requests
## Lines 1-4 — identification
- Names, address, full nine-digit SSNs (partial SSNs delay processing). Foreign addresses: province/state on the City line, postal code, and country code
- Line 4 School District Code (5 digits): the district where the filer lived on December 31, 2026 (code list in the booklet). Nonresidents enter 10000
## Line 5 — State Campaign Fund
Check to direct $3 of tax (per filer/spouse) to the fund for gubernatorial candidates who accept spending limits. Does NOT change tax or refund.
## Line 6 — Farmers, fishermen, seafarers
Check if at least two-thirds of gross income is from farming, fishing, or seafaring. Unlocks the special estimated-tax options (one voucher by January 15, 2027, or file and pay by March 1, 2027). Farm wages or corporate farm employment do not qualify; seafarers are those whose wages are exempt from withholding under 46 USC 11108.
## Lines 35a-35d — Penalty and/or interest relief request
- 35a: filer or spouse eligible for a federal extension due to COMBAT ZONE service (do not complete 35c-35d)
- 35b: affected by a DECLARED DISASTER in Michigan for which Treasury issued a relief notice (extensions of filing and payment deadlines with penalty and interest waived). 35c: affected address. 35d: notice date, disaster date, and how affected. Treasury may request documentation
- 2026 note: Treasury issued 2026 notices for severe weather (March–April 2026), extreme winter weather, and rising water levels; check the notice list for affected counties and dates
## Line 40 — Digital asset question
Check if, during 2026, the filer or spouse received a digital asset as a reward, award, or payment for property or services, or sold, exchanged, or otherwise disposed of a digital asset. Mirrors the federal Form 1040 digital asset question; gains flow through federal AGI.
## Deceased Taxpayer box
Enter the date of death (MM-DD-YYYY) for a filer or spouse who died after December 31, 2025. See ../payments-and-filing/taxpayer-status/deceased-taxpayer-refund.md.
## Amended Return box
Check at the top of page 1 and include Schedule AMD (see ../payments-and-filing/amended-and-carryback/amended-return.md).
## Questions
- Which school district did you live in on December 31?
- Is at least two-thirds of your income from farming or fishing?
- Were you affected by a declared Michigan disaster or serving in a combat zone?
- Did you buy, sell, or receive cryptocurrency or other digital assets?
## Prompt
- What is the school district code on the Michigan return?
