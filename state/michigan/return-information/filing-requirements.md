# Who Must File a Michigan Return (2026)
## Must file
- Tax is owed, OR a refund is due, OR federal AGI exceeds the exemption allowance (2026: $5,900 per exemption, plus special exemptions)
- Recommended: file whenever a federal return is filed, to avoid Treasury correspondence
- Withholding refund: file if Line 14 income is less than the exemption allowance but Michigan tax was withheld
- Nonresidents with Michigan-source income (Michigan wages unless from a reciprocal state, Michigan rental or business income, Michigan lottery and casino winnings); reciprocal-state residents claiming a refund of Michigan withholding file an MI-1040 with Schedule NR
- A personal representative files for a taxpayer who died in 2026 (or in 2027 before filing) if tax is owed or a refund is due; a full-year exemption is allowed for the decedent
## Dependent filers
Someone who can be claimed as a dependent need not file if AGI is $1,500 or less (single or MFS) or $3,000 or less (joint), unless claiming a refund of withholding (2025 booklet amounts; confirm 2026).
## Deadlines (2026 return)
- Due: April 15, 2027 (postmark); payment due the same day
- Refund claims: within four years (April 15, 2031)
- Keep returns and schedules six years
## Filing rules
- Complete the federal return first
- Use the 2026 forms; all pages of MI-1040, MI-1040CR, CR-2, and CR-7 must be filed
- Only the MI-1040, MI-1040CR, MI-1040CR-2, and MI-1040CR-7 may be filed alone; all other schedules must accompany an MI-1040
- Do not file without an SSN or ITIN for each person
- Paid preparers doing 11 or more Michigan returns must e-file eligible returns
- Home Heating Credit claim and City of Detroit return go loose in the envelope, not stapled
- Identity theft victims: paper file with copies of photo ID, federal return, W-2s/1099s, and affidavits
## Questions
- What is your federal AGI and how many exemptions do you have?
- Was Michigan tax withheld?
- Can someone else claim you as a dependent?
