# Refund, Balance Due, Direct Deposit, and Signatures
## Line 36 — You owe
Line 25 minus Line 34 (if Line 34 is negative, add it to Line 25). Add penalty and interest in the boxes provided. Balances under $1 need not be paid (return still required). Late payment: interest at 1% above prime (2026: 8.48% then 7.85%) and penalty 5% for the first two months, then 5% more per month or part, to 25% (e.g., $500 unpaid six months → $125 penalty). If annualizing income, MI-2210 must be attached; otherwise Treasury may bill.
## Lines 37-39 — Overpayment, credit forward, refund
- Line 38 credit forward to 2027 estimated tax — ORIGINAL returns only
- Refunds under $1 are not issued
- Refund returns mail to Lansing, MI 48956; balance-due returns to Lansing, MI 48929 (confirm 2026 addresses)
## Direct deposit
Nine-digit routing number (first two digits 01-12 or 21-32), account number up to 17 characters, checking or savings. Deposits forwarded to a foreign bank (IAT) are returned and reissued as a check.
## Refund offsets
Refunds and credits are first applied to debts: past-due state taxes, student loans, Friend of the Court child support, IRS levies, amounts owed to state agencies, garnishments. Joint filers may receive Form 743 (Income Allocation for Non-Obligated Spouse) to protect the non-obligated spouse's share. A letter explains any offset.
## Michigan Form 1099-G
Issued if the taxpayer itemized federally and received a Michigan income tax refund; includes amounts credited forward, intercepted, or applied to use tax or contributions. Excludes homestead, EITC, and other refundable credits. May be federally taxable; the refund is subtracted on Michigan Schedule 1 Line 16 if included in AGI.
## Signatures
- Both spouses sign a joint return; a child too young to sign: parent signs "by (name) parent (or guardian) for minor child"
- Paid preparers must sign and give their PTIN or FEIN (Taxpayer Protection Act); check the box to let Treasury discuss the return with the preparer (for broader authority use Form 151)
- Preparer fraud may be reported to Treasury's Fraud Unit, P.O. Box 30140, Lansing, MI 48909
## Appeals
Refund denials or adjustments may be appealed by requesting an informal conference within 60 days.
## Questions
- Do you want your refund by direct deposit, and to which account?
- Would you like to apply any overpayment to 2027 estimated tax?
- Do you owe any state debts or child support?

- Why was my Michigan refund smaller than expected?
