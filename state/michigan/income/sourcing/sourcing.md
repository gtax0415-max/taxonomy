# Income Sourcing and Gain Adjustments
## Description
Forms that decide which income belongs to Michigan and adjust gains Michigan cannot tax. Their results flow to Schedule 1 (residents) or Schedule NR (nonresidents and part-year residents).
## Files in this folder
| File | Form(s) | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| nonresident-part-year.md | Schedule NR | Allocating all income between Michigan and other states; prorating exemptions |
| business-income-apportionment.md | MI-1040H | Apportioning business income taxable in Michigan and another state (sales factor) |
| excess-business-loss.md | MI-461, Form 5606 | Splitting the federal § 461(l) limit between Michigan and other states |
| capital-gains-adjustments.md | MI-1040D, MI-8949, MI-4797 | Gains on out-of-state property, U.S. obligations, and pre-October 1, 1967 holdings |
## Allocate vs apportion
- Allocate: assign an item wholly to one state (wages, nonbusiness interest, rent from real property)
- Apportion: divide business income between states using the sales factor on MI-1040H
- Michigan business income includes a flow-through owner's share of portfolio income (interest, dividends, capital gains). Guaranteed payments are NOT business income
## Order of completion
MI-461 (if a federal Form 461 limit applies) → MI-1040H → MI-1040D / MI-4797 → Schedule NR (nonresidents) → Schedule 1
## Prompt
- I have income from Michigan and another state.
