# Additions to Income (Schedule 1, Lines 1-9)
## Description
Amounts added to federal AGI because Michigan taxes income the federal return excludes, or disallows a deduction the federal return allowed.

## Notes
- Nonresidents and part-year residents complete Schedule NR first
- Include the federal schedules and K-1s that support any out-of-state item; Treasury's "Business, Rental & Royalty Activity Worksheet" is the recommended way to show type, source, and location
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds from states other than Michigan?
- Are you self-employed?
- Did you claim a federal NOL deduction?
- Did you withdraw from a Michigan 529, MiABLE, or First-Time Home Buyer account for a nonqualified purpose?
## Prompt
- What does Michigan add back to federal AGI?
