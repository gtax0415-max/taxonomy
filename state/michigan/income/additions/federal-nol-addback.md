# Federal Net Operating Loss Deduction Add-back
## Description
Michigan computes its own NOL. The federal NOL deduction included in AGI is added back, and any MICHIGAN NOL is then deducted separately on Schedule 1, Line 30 (Form 5674).
## Who reports it here
- Residents: enter the federal NOL deduction to the extent included in AGI
- Nonresidents and part-year residents: do NOT use this line. Put the federal NOL deduction in Schedule NR, Line 11, Column C

- Michigan NOL deduction: deductions/subtractions/michigan-nol-deduction.md
## Questions
- Did you deduct a net operating loss carryforward on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the federal NOL and the Michigan NOL (double benefit)
- Nonresidents entering the federal NOL here instead of on Schedule NR
