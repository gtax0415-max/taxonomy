# Social Security, Military, and Railroad Retirement Subtractions
## Description
Michigan fully exempts these, at any age, to the extent included in federal AGI.
## What goes where
| Line | Item |
|---|---|
| 11 | Military retirement for service in the U.S. Armed Forces or Michigan National Guard; taxable Tier 1 and Tier 2 railroad retirement. Report on Schedule W Table 2 even if no Michigan tax was withheld |
| 14 | Compensation for active duty in the U.S. Armed Forces included in AGI (report on Schedule W Table 1); the TAXABLE portion of Social Security from Form 1040 |
## Not military pay
U.S. Public Health Service compensation, contractor pay, and civilian pay for the military.
## Interactions
- Line 11 amounts reduce the retirement subtraction maximum, the standard deduction, and the senior investment income cap
- Military pay reduces the standard deduction
- 2026-2028: taxable Social Security no longer reduces the Tier 3 standard deduction
- Nonresidents and part-year residents: do not include amounts allocated to another state on Schedule NR Line 10
- Social Security is still counted in total household resources for the Homestead Property Tax Credit
## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security, military retirement, or railroad retirement?
- Were you on active duty?
## Common Errors
- Entering total Social Security instead of the taxable portion
- Listing military or railroad retirement on Form 4884
- Omitting military pay from Schedule W

- Is my military pension taxed in Michigan?
