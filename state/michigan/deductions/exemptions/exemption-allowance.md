# Exemption Allowance
## Description
A fixed dollar amount per person that reduces Michigan income before the 4.25% rate applies. It is Michigan's substitute for the old federal personal exemption.
## 2026 figures
| MI-1040 line | Exemption | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9a | Personal exemption — filer, spouse, each dependent | $5,800 | $5,900 (confirmed, Form 446) |
| 9b | Special exemption — deaf, blind, hemiplegic, paraplegic, quadriplegic, or totally and permanently disabled (per qualifying individual) | $3,400 | Pending — PROJECTED $3,500 (2.6% index, rounded to $100) |
| 9c | Qualified disabled veteran (per veteran) | $500 (statutory; not indexed) |
| 9d | Certificate of Stillbirth from MDHHS (per certificate) | $5,800 | $5,900 (equals the personal exemption) |
| 9e | Filer who can be claimed as a dependent by someone else: 0 on 9a and this fixed amount | $1,500 (statutory; not indexed) |
| 9f | Total → Line 15 | | |
## How it works
- Dependents follow federal law (qualifying child or qualifying relative)
- One person can get both a personal exemption (9a) and a special exemption (9b)
- A person who CAN be claimed on another return (even if not actually claimed) uses Line 9e instead of 9a
- Nonresidents and part-year residents prorate by Michigan income ÷ total income on Schedule NR Lines 15-19; if one spouse was a full-year resident and the other was not, use Worksheet 6
## Interaction with the senior standard deduction
The Tier 3 standard deduction is reduced by the exemptions on Lines 9a and 9d. See ../retirement/michigan-standard-deduction.md.
## Required Information
- Number of people in the household and who is a federal dependent
- Disability status; VA disability rating; stillbirth certificates
## Questions
- How many dependents do you claim federally?
- Is anyone in the household deaf, blind, paraplegic, or totally and permanently disabled?
- Is anyone a qualified disabled veteran?
- Can someone else claim you as a dependent?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a 9a exemption when someone else can claim you
- Missing the special exemption for a disabled household member
- Not prorating for part-year residency
## Prompt
- What is the Michigan personal exemption for 2026?
