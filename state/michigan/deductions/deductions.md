# Michigan Deductions — Index
## Description
Michigan has no itemized deductions and (for most people) no standard deduction. It reduces income in two ways: subtractions on Schedule 1 (to MI-1040 Line 13) and the exemption allowance (MI-1040 Line 15).
## Folders
- exemptions/ — personal, dependent, special, disabled veteran, and stillbirth exemptions (MI-1040 Line 9)
- retirement/ — retirement and pension subtraction (Form 4884), Michigan standard deduction for seniors, senior investment income deduction, and Social Security / military / railroad
- subtractions/ — every other Schedule 1 subtraction, including the NEW 2026 tips and overtime deductions

## Not deductible in Michigan
Federal itemized deductions (except certain wagering losses), the federal standard deduction, sick pay and employer disability benefits, most unemployment benefits, political contributions, Michigan lottery winnings, and deferred compensation distributions received while a resident.
