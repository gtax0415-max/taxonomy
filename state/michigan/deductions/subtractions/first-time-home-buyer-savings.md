# First-Time Home Buyer Savings Program (Form 5792)
## Description
A Michigan-only savings program for buying a first home in Michigan. Contributions and interest are deductible; qualified withdrawals are tax-free; nonqualified withdrawals are recaptured with a 10% penalty.
## 2026 — FINAL YEAR for contributions
| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Accounts may be opened | January 1, 2022 through December 31, 2026 |
| Contributions deductible | Tax years 2022 through 2026 only; 2026 is the last year |
| Contribution deduction cap | $5,000 single / $10,000 joint (net of qualified withdrawals) |
| Interest deduction | Tax years 2022 and after, to the extent in AGI (continues after 2026) |
| Maximum account balance | $50,000 (may keep earning interest) |
| Qualified withdrawal | At least one year after opening; for eligible costs (down payment and settlement-statement disbursements) of a single-family Michigan residence by the qualified beneficiary |
| Penalty on nonqualified withdrawal | 10% (MI-1040 Line 23), unless an exception code applies |
## Definitions
- Account holder: opens the account; may also be the beneficiary. One account per beneficiary per holder; a beneficiary may be on several accounts
- Qualified beneficiary / first-time home buyer: Michigan resident who has not owned or purchased a single-family residence in the 3 years before the purchase
## Net contribution when several people contribute and there is a qualified withdrawal
Net = own contribution − (withdrawal × own contributions ÷ all contributions). Example: Sarah $6,000 of $8,000 total; $5,000 withdrawal; $6,000 − (75% × $5,000) = $2,250.
## Nonqualified withdrawal codes (Form 5792)
10 death or disability of beneficiary; 20 bankruptcy; 30 transfer to an account for another beneficiary; 40 hardship; 50 beneficiary or spouse transferred or deployed out of Michigan on active duty; 60 other (penalty applies). Add-back = 0 if the withdrawal is less than never-deducted contributions; otherwise the smaller of the withdrawal or total contributions and interest deducted.
## Required Information
- Account holder, beneficiary, institution, account number, open date, balances
- Contributions, interest, and withdrawals for the year; 1099-INT; settlement statement for purchases
## Questions
- Do you have a Michigan first-time home buyer savings account, and when was it opened?
- Will you contribute before December 31, 2026 (the last deductible year)?
- Did you withdraw funds, and were they used for a Michigan home purchase by the beneficiary?
## Common Errors
- Claiming contributions made after 2026
- Withdrawing within one year of opening
- Using funds for a non-Michigan home or for a buyer who owned a home in the prior 3 years
- Reporting the add-back but not the 10% penalty
