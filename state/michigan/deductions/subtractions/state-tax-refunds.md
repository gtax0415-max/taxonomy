# Michigan and City Income Tax Refunds, Homestead Credit Refunds, and FTE Tax Refunds
## Description
Refunds of Michigan or Michigan-city income tax and homestead property tax credits that were included in federal AGI (because the taxpayer itemized in the prior year) are subtracted, since Michigan never allowed the deduction.
## How it works
- Only subtract refunds actually included in federal AGI. If you took the federal standard deduction for 2025, your 2025 refunds received in 2026 are not in AGI and are not subtracted
- Farmers: subtract (to the extent in AGI) the amount by which the state/city refund and homestead credit exceed the business portion of the homestead credit
- Flow-through entity tax refunds: an owner's share of a Michigan FTE tax refund included in the distributive share → Form 6072 Line 4. Apply the MI-1040H Line 8 percentage if apportioned. Composite (Form 807) participants report it on the composite return instead
## Questions
- Did you itemize on your 2025 federal return?
- Did you receive a 1099-G for a Michigan or city refund in 2026?
- Did a flow-through entity you own receive a Michigan FTE tax refund?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a refund that was never included in AGI
- Subtracting another state's refund (only Michigan and Michigan cities qualify)
## Prompt
- My Michigan refund was on a 1099-G. Do I subtract it?
