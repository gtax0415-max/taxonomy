# Other Subtractions
## Line 15 — Renaissance Zone deduction
Residents of a designated Renaissance Zone may subtract income earned or received while living in the zone. Requirements: permanent zone resident, approved by the local assessor, not delinquent on abated state or local taxes, file an MI-1040 every year, gross income of $1 million or less. Part-year (at least 183 consecutive days) residents use Schedule NR. Unearned income such as capital gains may have to be prorated. Zones phasing out reduce the deduction by 25%, 50%, and 75% in their final three years.
## Line 19 — Michigan oil, gas, and nonferrous metallic minerals
Subtract gross income subject to Michigan severance tax, to the extent in AGI, and add back the related expenses on Schedule 1 Line 6. Include Form 5889 and federal schedules.
## Line 20 — Resident tribal members
Members of a federally recognized tribe with a Michigan tax agreement may subtract income exempt under the agreement (wages, interest, pensions earned within the Agreement Area). Non-agreement tribes: see RAB 1988-47. Income earned while living outside the Agreement Area or Indian Country does not qualify.
## Line 22 — Recreational marihuana businesses
Ordinary and necessary expenses of a business licensed under the Michigan Regulation and Taxation of Marihuana Act (MRTMA) that would have been deductible but for IRC § 280E. Medical marihuana (MMFLA) expenses do not qualify. Include the license and itemized expenses; dual licensees must separate adult-use and medical.
## Line 23 — Miscellaneous subtractions (only these)
- Qualified MESP, MAP, or MiABLE withdrawals to the extent included in AGI
- Benefits from a discriminatory self-insured medical expense reimbursement plan included in AGI
- Losses from the Michigan column of MI-1040D Line 13 or MI-4797 Line 18b
- IRC decoupling adjustments (2025 PA 24) when the worksheet total is positive
- The amount used for the federal credit for the elderly or disabled (Schedule R Line 19)
- Holocaust victim payments
- Wagering losses deducted on federal Schedule A (itemizers only). Nonresidents: only losses on wagers at Michigan casinos or race tracks, limited to Michigan wagering gains
## NOT miscellaneous subtractions
Retirement benefits (Form 4884), other itemized deductions, sick pay and employer disability benefits, most unemployment benefits, political contributions, Michigan bingo/raffle/charity game prizes, deferred compensation received while a resident, lottery winnings (except installments on prizes won on or before December 30, 1988), and military spouse income (use Schedule NR).
## Questions
- Do you live in a Renaissance Zone?
- Are you a member of a tribe with a Michigan tax agreement?
- Do you own a licensed recreational marihuana business?
- Did you itemize and deduct gambling losses federally?
## Common Errors
- Listing items on Line 23 that are not on the permitted list
- Claiming medical marihuana expenses on Line 22
- Nonresidents deducting non-Michigan gambling losses
