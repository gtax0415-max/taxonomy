# Michigan 529, MiABLE, and Michigan Education Trust Deductions
## Description
Michigan allows a deduction for contributions to ITS OWN plans only: the Michigan Education Savings Program (MESP), the MI 529 Advisor Plan (MAP), Michigan Achieving a Better Life Experience (MiABLE), and Michigan Education Trust (MET) prepaid tuition. Other states' 529 plans do not qualify.
## Line 17 limits (statutory)
| Account type | Single | Joint |
|---|---|---|
| MESP and MAP combined | $5,000 | $10,000 |
| MiABLE | $5,000 | $10,000 |
| Total Line 17 | $10,000 | $20,000 |
Deduct contributions LESS qualified withdrawals and rollovers made in the same year (net), computed separately for each account, to the extent not deducted in AGI.
## Line 18 — MET
- Purchase of a MET prepaid tuition contract in 2026: total price including processing fee (no dollar cap)
- Payroll deduction, monthly, or pay-as-you-go contracts: amounts paid in 2026 (not late or NSF fees)
- Charitable contributions to the MET Charitable Tuition Program (scholarships for former foster youth)
- Terminated contract: amount included in AGI as income to the purchaser
## Recapture
Nonqualified MESP/MAP/MiABLE withdrawals and MET refunds of previously deducted amounts are ADDED BACK on Schedule 1 Line 8. See ../../income/additions/miscellaneous-additions.md.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to MESP, the MI 529 Advisor Plan, MiABLE, or MET in 2026?
- Did you withdraw or roll over from those accounts in the same year?
## Common Errors
- Deducting contributions to another state's 529
- Deducting gross instead of net contributions
- Exceeding the $5,000 / $10,000 per-category limits
