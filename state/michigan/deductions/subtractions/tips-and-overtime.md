# Qualified Tips and Qualified Overtime Deductions (NEW for 2026)
## Description
2025 PA 24 (signed October 7, 2025) created Michigan deductions equal to the FEDERAL deductions for qualified tips (IRC § 224) and qualified overtime compensation (IRC § 225). Because the federal deductions are taken BELOW AGI, they would otherwise not reach the Michigan return, which starts from AGI.
## Years available
Tax years 2026, 2027, and 2028 only. NOT available on 2025 Michigan returns, even though the federal deductions began in 2025.
## How it works
- Deduct, to the extent not deducted in AGI, an amount equal to the tips and overtime deductions allowed on the FEDERAL return for the same year
- Eligibility, definitions, caps, and phase-outs all come from the federal rules. If the federal return allows nothing (for example, married filing separately), Michigan allows nothing
- Residents: the full federal amounts
- Nonresidents: only tips and overtime attributable to services performed in Michigan
## Federal parameters that flow through (2025-2028)
| Deduction | Federal cap | Federal phase-out |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified tips (occupations that customarily and regularly receive tips) | $25,000 per return | Reduced above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint) |
| Qualified overtime compensation (the FLSA premium portion only) | $12,500 ($25,000 joint) | Reduced above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint) |
Confirm federal amounts on federal Schedule 1-A. Treasury has said it will issue more implementation guidance for 2026; the 2026 Schedule 1 line number is not yet published.
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A tips and overtime deductions
- For nonresidents: the portion earned for services performed in Michigan
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal deduction for tips or overtime on your 2026 federal return?
- Were you a Michigan resident all year, and if not, where did you perform the work?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the deduction on a 2025 Michigan return
- Claiming more than the federal deduction
- Nonresidents deducting tips or overtime earned outside Michigan
- Deducting the full overtime pay instead of only the premium portion the federal rules allow
## Prompt
- Does Michigan have no tax on tips or overtime?
- I am a server in Michigan. Can I deduct my tips on my state return?
