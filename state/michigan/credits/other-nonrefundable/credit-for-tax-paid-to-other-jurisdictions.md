# Credit for Income Tax Imposed by Government Units Outside Michigan
## Description
Michigan RESIDENTS are taxed on income from everywhere. When income is also taxed by another jurisdiction, this credit prevents double taxation. It is for residents (and for the resident portion of a part-year return) only.
## Jurisdictions that qualify
- A NONreciprocal state (not Illinois, Indiana, Kentucky, Minnesota, Ohio, or Wisconsin — Michigan residents owe no wage tax to those states)
- A local government outside Michigan, INCLUDING cities in reciprocal states (e.g., Ohio municipal income tax)
- The District of Columbia
- A Canadian province (file Form 777)
- Not available for tax paid to foreign countries other than Canadian provinces
## How it works
1. Line 18a: total income tax paid to the other unit(s) on income also taxed by Michigan
2. For each unit separately: (out-of-state income taxed by both) ÷ (MI-1040 Line 14 income subject to tax) × MI-1040 Line 17 tax
3. Credit for each unit = the smaller of the other unit's tax or the Step 2 amount; add all units → Line 18b
## Example (2025 booklet; mechanics unchanged for 2026)
Resident with $40,000 Michigan wages, $10,000 wages taxed by another state ($700 tax there), $3,000 interest. Line 14 $53,000; taxable $47,200; tax $2,006. Ratio $10,000 ÷ $53,000 = 19%; $2,006 × 19% = $381. Line 18a $700; Line 18b $381. For 2026, use the $5,900 exemption and the 2026 figures.
## Exclusions
- Tax paid on income already SUBTRACTED on Schedule 1 (e.g., out-of-state business or rental income subtracted on Line 13, wages allocated away on Schedule NR while a nonresident) — no double benefit
- Tax paid while a nonresident of Michigan
## Canadian provinces (Form 777)
Credit limited to the provincial tax NOT used as a credit on the federal return (Form 1116). Attach T-1, T-4, federal Form 1116, and Form 1040.
## Required Information
- Copy of each other-jurisdiction return (the credit may be denied without it)
- Income taxed by both Michigan and the other unit
## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to another state, a city outside Michigan, D.C., or a Canadian province?
- Were you a Michigan resident when you earned that income?
## Common Errors
- Claiming credit for tax paid to a reciprocal STATE on wages (not taxable there)
- Missing Ohio or other city taxes, which DO qualify
- Claiming credit on income subtracted on Schedule 1
- Not attaching the other return
## Prompt
- I live in Michigan and work in New York.
- I pay Toledo city tax. Can I get credit in Michigan?
