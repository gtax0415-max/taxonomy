# Other Nonrefundable Credits (Michigan)
## Description
Nonrefundable credits on MI-1040 Lines 18-20 that reduce Line 17 tax to no less than zero. Any unused amount is lost (no carryforward).
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Income Tax Imposed by Government Units Outside Michigan (credit-for-tax-paid-to-other-jurisdictions.md) — MI-1040 Lines 18a / 18b; Form 777 for Canadian provinces
- Credit for Organ Donation Expenses (credit-for-organ-donation-expenses.md) — MI-1040 Lines 20a / 20b
## Related
- Historic Preservation Tax Credit (Line 19) — see ../historic-preservation/
## Ordering
Line 21 = Line 17 tax − (18b + 19b + 20b), not less than zero.
## Prompt
- I paid tax to another state. Does Michigan give me credit?
- I donated a kidney. Is there a Michigan tax credit?
