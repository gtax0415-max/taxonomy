# Historic Preservation Tax Credit — Plans Approved after 2020 (Form 5803)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of 25% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures on a certified historic resource in Michigan, under PA 343 of 2020. Available to owners, long-term lessees, assignees, and members of flow-through entities that did the rehabilitation.
## How it works
1. Qualified expenditures from the Certificate of Completed Rehabilitation (Line 3)
2. Basic credit = the LESSER of 25% of expenditures or the SHPO credit reservation from Part 2 (Lines 4a-4c)
3. Add any assigned credit or flow-through share (Line 5) and the carryforward from the prior year (Line 6)
4. Total (Line 7) → MI-1040 Line 19a
5. Tax liability limit: MI-1040 Line 17 tax less other nonrefundable credits on Line 18b (Lines 8-10)
6. Credit used = lesser of the liability or the available credit (Line 12) → MI-1040 Line 19b
7. Excess (Line 13) carries forward
## 2026 figures
| Parameter | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Credit rate | 25% of qualified expenditures, capped at the SHPO reservation |
| Statewide program cap | $5 million per calendar year, allocated first-come, first-served by category |
| Carryforward | Up to 10 years or until used |
| Refundable | No |
| Initial claim window | Within 5 years after the Certificate of Completed Rehabilitation is issued |
| Recapture period | Sale, disposal, or certificate revocation within 5 years of the certificate |
The annual cap has historically been split $2 million for commercial projects of $2 million or more, $2 million for smaller commercial projects, and $1 million for owner-occupied residential projects. Confirm the 2026 allocation with SHPO.
## Pending legislation
S.B. 633 (introduced October 30, 2025) would change the program's sunset from January 1, 2031 to January 1, 2026. H.B. 4503-4504 propose updates and expansions. Status must be checked before advising on NEW 2026 credits.
## Qualified expenditures
Only expenditures paid or incurred during the periods prescribed under FEDERAL IRC § 47(a)(2) (the federal rehabilitation credit timing rules) qualify.
## Assignment
- A qualified taxpayer may assign all or part of the credit; an assignee may reassign
- Assignments cannot be revoked and must be done and claimed in the tax year the Certificate is issued
- SHPO provides and approves the assignment form
- Recapture of an assigned credit remains the responsibility of the qualified taxpayer who received the Certificate
## Eligibility
- Property is listed on the National Register, the State Register, or in a local historic district, and individually listed or contributing
- Rehabilitation certified by SHPO under a plan approved after December 31, 2020
- Filer is the owner, a long-term lessee, an assignee, or a member of the flow-through entity that received the Certificate
## Required Information
- SHPO project number (one Form 5803 per project)
- State equalized value (SEV) of the resource
- Qualified expenditures per the Certificate; SHPO credit reservation
- Certificate of Completed Rehabilitation, Parts 1-3 of the SHPO application
- Assignment certificate or K-1 notes if applicable
- Prior-year Form 5803 Line 13 carryforward
## Questions
- What is your SHPO project number, and when was the Certificate of Completed Rehabilitation issued?
- What were your qualified expenditures and your SHPO credit reservation?
- Were you assigned the credit, or did it come through a partnership or S corporation?
- Do you have a carryforward from a prior Form 5803?
- Do you plan to sell the property within five years of the Certificate?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than the SHPO reservation
- Entering a Form 3581 carryforward on Form 5803 Line 6
- Missing the 5-year initial claim window
- Treating the credit as refundable
- Not reporting recapture after an early sale
