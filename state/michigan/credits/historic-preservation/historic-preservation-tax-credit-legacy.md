# Historic Preservation Tax Credit — Legacy Plans Certified before 2012 (Form 3581)
## Description
Credit under Michigan's original historic preservation program. Rehabilitation plans stopped being certified after December 31, 2011, so for 2026 this form is used almost entirely to use up CARRYFORWARDS. Individual filers complete Part 1 and Part 3 only; Part 2 is not used on an individual return.
## How it works (individual filers)
1. Part 1: basic credit, generally 25% of qualified expenditures LESS the federal rehabilitation credit available under IRC § 47(a)(2) (e.g., 25% − 20% federal = 5% state), limited to the SHPO ceiling
2. Part 3, Line 7: assigned credit from the assignor's approval letter
3. Lines 10-14: limit the credit to the tax liability; Line 14 → MI-1040 Line 19b
4. Line 15: excess carries forward (10-year limit from the original credit year)
5. Line 16: refund election (see below) → MI-1040 Line 29
## Refund election
For certificates issued for tax years after 2008 with a credit under $250,000, a taxpayer could elect to forgo the carryforward and receive 90% of the excess over tax liability as a refund. The election must be made in the year the certificate is issued, is irrevocable, and does not apply to carryforwards. In 2026 this is relevant only if a certificate is issued in 2026 under a pre-2012 approval, which is rare.
## 2026 notes
- Because the carryforward period is 10 years, most legacy carryforwards have expired or are near expiration by 2026. Track the original credit year for each carryforward
- If the filer assigned part of the credit to someone else, leave Lines 1-6 blank
## Recapture
If the resource is sold or the certification is revoked less than five years after it was placed in service, a percentage may be recaptured. For assigned credits, recapture falls on the assignor.
## Required Information
- SHPO project number
- Part 1 and Part 3 certification application, signed and dated
- Detailed list of qualified expenditures and dates paid
- Federal rehabilitation credit amount available (FEDERAL Form 3468) for the Michigan reduction
- Original credit year and prior-year carryforward
## Questions
- Do you have a Michigan historic credit carryforward from a plan certified before 2012?
- What year was the credit first generated?
## Common Errors
- Carrying a Form 3581 balance onto Form 5803
- Claiming a carryforward past its 10-year limit
- Electing the refund on a carryforward
