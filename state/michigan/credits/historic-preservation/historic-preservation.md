# Historic Preservation Credits (Michigan)
## Description
Credits for rehabilitating certified historic resources in Michigan, administered with the State Historic Preservation Office (SHPO). Two separate programs exist, each with its own form.
## Credits in this folder
- Historic Preservation Tax Credit, plans approved after 2020 (historic-preservation-tax-credit.md) — Form 5803 → MI-1040, Lines 19a / 19b. NONREFUNDABLE, 10-year carryforward. The current program
- Historic Preservation Tax Credit, legacy plans certified before 2012 (historic-preservation-tax-credit-legacy.md) — Form 3581 → MI-1040, Line 19b (nonrefundable) and Line 29 (refundable election). Mostly carryforwards today
## Which form
| Plan approved / certified | Form |
|---|---|
| After December 31, 2020 | Form 5803 |
| Before January 1, 2012 | Form 3581 |
| 2012-2020 | No state credit program existed |
Never carry a Form 3581 carryforward onto Form 5803 Line 6. If a filer has both, add the totals on MI-1040.
## Watch in 2026
Two opposing legislative efforts are pending: S.B. 633 (introduced October 30, 2025) would move the program's sunset from January 1, 2031 to January 1, 2026, effectively ending new credits; H.B. 4503-4504 would update and expand the program. Check SHPO and the Legislature before relying on availability of NEW 2026 credits.
## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a historic property in Michigan?
