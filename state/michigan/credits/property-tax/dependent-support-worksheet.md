# Worksheet for Determining Support (Form 4622)
## Description
Sent by Treasury when a filer claiming a Homestead Property Tax Credit or Home Heating Credit was claimed as a DEPENDENT on someone else's federal return but reported no support in total household resources. Because a dependent receives more than half of their support from another person, that support must be counted as income (gifts or expenses paid on their behalf) for these credits.
## How it works
1. The filer checks either "I AM a dependent" or "I am NOT a dependent"
2. A dependent reports the support provided by the person who claimed them, plus support from state, local, and other welfare agencies (Line 17)
3. That amount is added to total household resources, usually reducing or eliminating the credit
4. Return the worksheet with the Request for Information and documents (tuition statements, property tax statements, lease)
## Related rules (2026)
- On the credit claims, the value over $300 of gifts and expenses paid on the filer's behalf, including support received as someone's dependent, belongs on MI-1040CR Line 24 (Other nontaxable support) or the equivalent line on MI-1040CR-2 / CR-7
- A filer claimed as a dependent enters $1,500 on MI-1040 Line 9e instead of a personal exemption
- Property tax credits cannot be filed on behalf of minor children
## Questions
- Did a parent or anyone else claim you as a dependent on their 2026 federal return?
- How much did they pay toward your rent, tuition, food, or other costs?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the homestead credit as a dependent without reporting the support received
## Prompt
- Treasury sent me a Worksheet for Determining Support.
