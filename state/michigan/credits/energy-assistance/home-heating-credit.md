# Home Heating Credit (MI-1040CR-7)
## Description
Refundable credit for low-income Michigan homeowners and renters to help pay heating costs, funded by the federal LIHEAP block grant. Can be filed with or without an MI-1040. The payment may be issued as an energy draft to the heat provider.
## How it works
- STANDARD credit: a standard allowance from Table A based on exemptions (household size), reduced by a percentage of total household resources, and subject to an income ceiling
- ALTERNATE credit: based on actual heating costs up to a Table B maximum, reduced by a percentage of total household resources. Claimants receive the larger computed credit; a personal representative for a deceased claimant may claim only the standard credit
- The computed credit has been multiplied by a funding percentage reflecting available federal LIHEAP money for the year
## 2026 — status of figures
Table A and Table B for 2026 depend on the federal LIHEAP grant and are published with the 2026 MI-1040CR-7 booklet (early 2027). Nothing has been published for 2026. Use the 2025 figures only as placeholders.
| Parameter | 2025 (published / reported) | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Standard allowance — 2 exemptions | $815 (official booklet) | Pending |
| Standard allowance — 3 exemptions | $1,027 (official booklet) | Pending |
| Income ceiling — 0 or 1 exemption | $17,243 (secondary source; confirm Table A) | Pending |
| Income ceiling — each additional exemption (above 6) | About $6,057 (secondary source) | Pending |
| Filing deadline | September 30, 2026 | September 30, 2027 (postmark) |
## Deadline
The 2026 claim must be postmarked by September 30, 2027. An income tax extension does NOT extend it, late claims are denied, and an amended claim asking for more money is not accepted after that date.
## Household members and Form 4976
MI-1040CR-7 Line 16 lists the filer, spouse, and household members with name, SSN, age, dependent status, citizenship or qualified-alien status, and LIHEAP-required ethnicity, race, and gender codes. With more than five members, list the rest on Form 4976 (Attachment 08A). Codes — Ethnicity: H, N, U; Race: A, S, B, P, W, R (multi-race), O, U; Gender: M, F, O, U.
## Eligibility (general)
- Owned or rented and occupied a Michigan homestead; residents only (part-year residents prorate by days; nonresidents are not eligible)
- Total household resources within the ceiling for the number of exemptions
- Special rules for shared housing (divide the allowance), adult care facilities, and people whose heat is included in rent
- Total household resources are defined as for the Homestead Property Tax Credit (see property-tax/homestead-property-tax-credit.md)
## Required Information
- Everyone in the household with SSN, age, and LIHEAP demographic codes
- All household income, taxable and nontaxable
- Heat provider, account number, and heating costs (for the alternate credit)
- Months of occupancy
## Questions
- How many people live in your home, and what is your total household income?
- Who is your heat provider, and is heat included in your rent?
- Did you live in the home all year?
## Common Errors
- Missing the September 30 postmark deadline
- Omitting household members or their SSNs
- Using an income tax extension as an extension for this claim
## Prompt
- Can I get help with my heating bill?
