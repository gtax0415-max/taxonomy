# Energy Assistance Credits (Michigan)
## Description
Credits funded by the federal Low Income Home Energy Assistance Program (LIHEAP) block grant.
## Credits in this folder
- Home Heating Credit (home-heating-credit.md) — MI-1040CR-7, with Form 4976 for households over five people. Refundable, paid as a separate check, direct deposit, or energy draft; not a line on the MI-1040
## Questions
- Do you pay for heat in a Michigan home you own or rent?
- Is your household income low relative to its size?
## Prompt
- Can I get help with my heating bill through my taxes?
