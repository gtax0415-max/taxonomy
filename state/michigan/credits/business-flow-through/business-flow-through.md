# Flow-Through Entity Credits (Michigan)
## Description
Credits that pass to individual owners from Michigan business entities. Michigan's elective Flow-Through Entity (FTE) tax lets partnerships and S corporations pay Michigan income tax at the entity level; each owner then claims a credit for their share.
## Credits in this folder
- Flow-Through Entity Tax Credit (flow-through-entity-tax-credit.md) — Form 6072 (Schedule FTE) and Form 6074 → MI-1040, Line 30. Refundable. Also drives a Schedule 1 addition and subtraction
