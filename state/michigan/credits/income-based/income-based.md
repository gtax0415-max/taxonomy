# Income-Based Credits (Michigan)
## Description
Michigan credits whose amount depends on earned income and family size rather than a specific expense.
## Credits in this folder
- Michigan Earned Income Tax Credit (michigan-earned-income-tax-credit.md) — FEDERAL Schedule EIC → Form 1040 Line 27 → MI-1040, Lines 28a / 28b. Refundable. 30% of the federal credit
## Notes
- The Michigan EITC piggybacks entirely on the federal Earned Income Credit (federal/credits/income-based/earned-income-credit.md). If the federal credit is zero, the Michigan credit is zero
- The Homestead Property Tax Credit is also income-tested but is filed under property-tax/
