# Michigan Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC)
## Description
Refundable credit equal to 30% of the federal earned income credit. Michigan performs no separate eligibility test: whatever the federal return allows, Michigan pays 30% of it.
## How it works
Enter the federal earned income credit on MI-1040 Line 28a, multiply by 30%, and enter the result on Line 28b. Line 28b is added to refundable credits and payments on Line 34.
## 2026 figures
The Michigan rate is 30% (unchanged). The federal 2026 maximum credits come from Rev. Proc. 2025-32 (see federal/credits/income-based/earned-income-credit.md).
| Qualifying children | Federal maximum (2026) | Michigan 30% maximum (2026) |
|---|---|---|
| None | $664 | $199 |
| One | $4,427 | $1,328 |
| Two | $7,316 | $2,195 |
| Three or more | $8,231 | $2,469 |
Michigan amounts are 30% of the federal maximum, rounded to the dollar. The actual credit is 30% of the federal credit actually allowed, not of the maximum.
## Inherited federal rules
Every federal limit flows through, including:
- Earned income and AGI limits by filing status (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)
- The investment income CLIFF: $12,200 for 2026. One dollar over and both the federal AND Michigan credits are lost
- The federal bar on married filing separately (with the narrow separated-spouse exception)
- The SSN, age, and qualifying child tests
- The phase-out, which means a raise in the phase-out range reduces both credits at once
## Michigan-specific notes
- Part-year residents and nonresidents: the credit must be prorated to the Michigan portion of income; confirm the method in the 2026 MI-1040 instructions and Schedule NR
- If the IRS reduces or disallows the federal credit, the Michigan credit must be reduced by amending the MI-1040 (Schedule AMD)
## Required Information
- Federal earned income credit from FEDERAL Form 1040, Line 27
- Residency status for the full year
## Questions
- Did you claim the earned income credit on your federal return, and how much was it?
- Were you a full-year Michigan resident?
- Did the IRS change your federal EIC after filing?
## Common Errors
- Entering the Michigan credit on Line 28a instead of the federal credit
- Claiming the Michigan credit when the federal credit was disallowed
- Not prorating for part-year residency
- Missing the investment income cliff, which wipes out both credits
## Prompt
- Does Michigan match the earned income credit?
- I got $4,000 of EIC on my federal return. What does Michigan give me?
