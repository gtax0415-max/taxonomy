# Paying a Balance Due (MI-1040-V)
## Description
Voucher used ONLY to mail a payment AFTER the MI-1040 has been filed (typically e-filers paying by check). Not for estimates (MI-1040ES), extensions (Form 4), or City of Detroit tax (CITY-V).
## 2026 use
Pay the 2026 MI-1040 Line 36 balance by April 15, 2027. Use the 2026 revision of the voucher (the 2025 voucher is labeled "2025 MI-1040-V").
## Rules
- Paying electronically (eCheck, card, or e-file direct debit) — no voucher needed
- Paper return with check enclosed — no voucher needed
- Check to "State of Michigan" with "2026 MI-1040-V" and the last four SSN digits; mail loose (not attached) to Michigan Department of Treasury, P.O. Box 30774, Lansing, MI 48909
- Do not attach the return, write notes, or send the voucher without payment
- Full SSNs required
- Late payment: interest at 1% above prime (7.85% annual from July 1, 2026; rate after January 1, 2027 pending) plus penalty of 5% for the first two months, then 5% per month, to 25%
## Questions
- Did you already file your return, and how will you pay the balance?
## Common Errors
- Using MI-1040-V for an estimated payment
- Stapling the check or return to the voucher
