# Extension of Time to File (Form 4)
## Description
An extension of time to FILE, never to PAY. Interest and late-payment penalty run from April 15, 2027 on any unpaid 2026 tax.
## Individual income tax rules
- A federal extension automatically extends the Michigan return to the new federal due date. No Michigan form is needed unless a payment is due
- Without a federal extension, Treasury generally grants six months when Form 4 is filed with the required payment
- Do NOT file Form 4 without a payment; requests without the required payment are denied
- Not needed when a refund is expected (refunds can be claimed for four years)
- Paying the extension payment electronically replaces mailing Form 4
- Does NOT extend: the Home Heating Credit (MI-1040CR-7) or City of Detroit returns (use Form 5209)
## Completing Form 4 (individual)
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Check "Income Tax" |
| 2 | Tax year end: 12-2026 |
| 5 / 7 | Filer and spouse SSNs |
| 6 | Check if a federal extension was granted (keep a copy; do not send it) |
| 4 | Good cause, if no federal extension (initial or final return, change in accounting period, books unavailable). Inability to pay is not good cause |
| 9 | Tentative annual tax (worksheet A − B) |
| 11 | Payments to date: withholding, estimates, 2025 credit forward |
| 13 | Payment amount |
Postmark by April 15, 2027. Mail to Michigan Department of Treasury, P.O. Box 30774, Lansing, MI 48909; check to "State of Michigan" with "Michigan Extension" and the last four SSN digits. Individuals receive no response.
## Penalty and interest
Interest at 1% above the adjusted prime rate from the original due date. Late payment penalty 5% per month after the second month to 25%; negligence 10%; intentional disregard 25%.
## Questions

- Will you owe Michigan tax for 2026 after withholding and estimates?
## Common Errors
- Assuming the extension extends time to pay
- Filing Form 4 with no payment
- Expecting it to extend the Home Heating Credit
