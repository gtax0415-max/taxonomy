# Use Tax on Out-of-State Purchases (MI-1040 Line 24)
## Description
Michigan's 6% use tax is owed on items bought from sellers that did not charge Michigan sales tax (internet, catalog, telephone, out-of-state trips, and foreign purchases brought into Michigan). It is paid on the MI-1040 and increases the balance due.
## Worksheet 1
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Purchases under $1,000 each × 6% — or the Table 1 amount if receipts are incomplete |
| 2 | Each single purchase of $1,000 or more × 6% (always required when no tax was collected) |
| 3 | Total → MI-1040 Line 24 (enter 0 if none) |
Tax applies to the total price including shipping and handling.
## Table 1 — estimate by AGI (2025 booklet; confirm 2026 table)
| AGI (MI-1040 Line 10) | Use tax |
|---|---|
| $0 – $10,000 | $2 |
| $10,001 – $20,000 | $6 |
| $20,001 – $30,000 | $10 |
| $30,001 – $40,000 | $14 |
| $40,001 – $50,000 | $18 |
| $50,001 – $75,000 | $25 |
| $75,001 – $100,000 | $35 |
| Above $100,000 | AGI × 0.04% |
Using the table does not prevent an audit.
## Credit for tax paid elsewhere
If another state's sales tax of at least 6% was paid, nothing is owed; if less, pay the difference. Purchases in foreign countries owe the full 6%.
## Amending
Use tax cannot be changed on an amended MI-1040. Write to Treasury, Business Taxes Division, P.O. Box 30427, Lansing, MI 48909.
## Questions
- Did you buy items online or out of state without paying Michigan sales tax?
- Any single purchase of $1,000 or more?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the line blank instead of entering the tax or 0
- Using the table for purchases of $1,000 or more
