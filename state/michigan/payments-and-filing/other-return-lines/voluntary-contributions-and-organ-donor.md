# Voluntary Contributions and Organ Donor Registry (Form 4642)
## Description
Optional donations made through the return. They INCREASE tax liability (reduce a refund or increase the balance due). The same schedule lets filers join the Michigan Organ Donor Registry.
## Funds (2025 form; confirm the 2026 list)
| Line | Fund |
|---|---|
| 1 | American Red Cross Michigan Fund |
| 2 | Animal Welfare Fund (MDARD shelter grants) |
| 3 | Children Trust Michigan – Prevent Child Abuse Michigan |
| 4 | Military Family Relief Fund |
| 5 | United Way Fund |
Choose $5 or $10, or any amount greater than $10. Line 6 total → MI-1040 Line 22. Form 4642 must accompany the MI-1040 for the money to reach the funds.
## Organ Donor Registry
Check box a (filer) and/or b (spouse) and give dates of birth. People already registered (heart symbol on license or ID) need not register again. Treasury keeps no record of registry status; removal is through the Secretary of State. Information: Gift of Life Michigan, 1-866-500-5801.
