# Payments, Penalties, and Filing Administration
## Description
Everything on the MI-1040 that is not income, deductions, or credits: tax withheld, estimated payments, extensions, balance-due payments, voluntary contributions, penalties and interest, and the administrative forms that control who files, where, and who may speak for the taxpayer.
## Key 2026 dates
| Event | Date |
|---|---|
| 2026 estimated payments (MI-1040ES) | April 15, 2026; June 15, 2026; September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027 |
| 2026 MI-1040 due; extension (Form 4) and payment due | April 15, 2027 |
| Farmers/fishermen/seafarers alternative (no estimates) | File and pay by March 1, 2027 |
| Skip the 4th estimate by filing early | File the 2026 MI-1040 before February 1, 2027 |
| 2026 Home Heating Credit claim | September 30, 2027 (no extension) |
| Last day to claim a 2026 refund | April 15, 2031 (four years) |
Dates follow the 2025 pattern rolled forward; confirm against the 2026 MI-1040 booklet. April 15, 2027 falls on a Thursday.

## Penalties (Revenue Act)
| Situation | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Failure to file or pay the annual return | 5% of tax due for the first two months, plus 5% per additional month or part, up to 25% |
| Negligence | 10% |
| Intentional disregard | 25% |
| No estimated payment for a quarter | 25% of the underpayment (minimum $25) |
| Estimated payment short or late | 10% of the underpayment (minimum $10) |
