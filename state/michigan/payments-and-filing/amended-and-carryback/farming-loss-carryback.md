# Farming Loss Carryback Refund Request (Form 5603)
## Description
The only way to carry a Michigan NOL BACK. Available for the farming portion of a Group 2 TCJA NOL (loss years 2021 and later; form header says loss year 2022 and later). Non-farming Group 2 NOLs cannot be carried back. Filed separately, not with the MI-1040, and mailed to a different address.
## Rules
- Carryback period: generally the two years before the loss year. A 2026 farming loss goes to 2024, then 2025
- The carryback deduction in each year is the lesser of the remaining farming loss or 80% of that year's Michigan income subject to tax computed WITHOUT exemptions or other NOL deductions
- Farming loss = lesser of the NOL computed using only farming income and deductions, or the total NOL (IRC § 172); "farming business" per IRC § 263A
- A Michigan excess business loss from MI-461 cannot be carried back even if it contains farm loss
- Election to waive the carryback is made on Schedule MI-1045 and is irrevocable; if made, do not file Form 5603
- Deadline: within four years after the due date of the LOSS-year return. A 2026 loss (return due April 15, 2027): file Form 5603 by April 15, 2031
- After carryback, reduce the carryover for any Michigan excess capital loss and other § 172 modifications in the carryback year
## How it works
Part 1 — farming loss available (lesser of the MI-1045 NOL and the farming loss). Part 2 — recompute each carryback year's tax at that year's rate (4.25% for 2024 and 2025) after the carryback deduction, less credits, compare with tax paid → refund (Line 29). Part 3 — unused farming loss and non-farming NOL carry forward as Group 2 NOL (Line 36).
## Filing
Mail to Michigan Department of Treasury, P.O. Box 30058, Lansing, MI 48909. Include Schedule MI-1045 and support; if marital or filing status changed, check Line 1 and attach the recomputation (IRS Publication 536 method). Include MI-1040CR-5 and Schedule CR-5 if requesting a revised Farmland credit.
## Questions
- Is your loss from a farming business?
- Did you elect on Schedule MI-1045 to forgo the carryback?
- Did you have Michigan taxable income in the two prior years?
## Common Errors
- Filing an amended MI-1040 instead of Form 5603
- Carrying back non-farming NOL or an MI-461 excess business loss
- Ignoring the 80% limit
- Mailing Form 5603 with the loss-year MI-1040
## Prompt
- I had a farm loss this year. Can I get a refund of Michigan tax from earlier years?
