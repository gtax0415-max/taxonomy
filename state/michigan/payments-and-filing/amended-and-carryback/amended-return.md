# Amended Returns (Schedule AMD)
## Description
To amend a Michigan return, file a complete new MI-1040 with the Amended Return box checked, plus Schedule AMD explaining every change and all supporting documents. File only after the original has finished processing.
## When NOT to use Schedule AMD
Amending ONLY a Homestead Property Tax Credit (MI-1040CR or CR-2) or Home Heating Credit (MI-1040CR-7) with no MI-1040 changes: file a new claim with its own Amended box; no new MI-1040 or AMD. An amended MI-1040CR-7 requesting more money is not accepted after September 30 of the year following the claim year (for 2026 claims: September 30, 2027).
## Credits that DO require an amended MI-1040 and AMD
Historic Preservation (Form 3581 / 5803), Farmland Preservation (MI-1040CR-5 and Schedule CR-5), FTE credit (Form 6072 and Form 6074).
## Reason checkboxes (line 6)
a federal audit/adjustment · b filing status · c AGI · d additions · e subtractions · f retirement (Form 4884) · g dependents/exemptions (complete the Line 7 dependent chart) · h credit for tax paid to other states · i Michigan withholding (Schedule W) · j NOL carryforward · k homestead credit · l veterans/blind credit · m farmland credit · n other
## Deadlines
- Refund claims: four years from the original due date. A 2026 return (due April 15, 2027) can be amended for a refund until April 15, 2031
- Federal changes: if a change to the federal return affects Michigan taxable income, file an amended MI-1040 within 120 days of the change, with the amended federal return, supporting schedules, and payment of any tax and interest
- Once a joint return is filed, separate returns cannot be substituted after the due date
- Farming NOL carrybacks use Form 5603, NOT an amended MI-1040
## Lines that cannot be amended on the MI-1040
- Line 22 voluntary contributions: enter the amount from the original return
- Line 24 use tax: enter the original amount; to change it, write to Michigan Department of Treasury, Business Taxes Division, P.O. Box 30427, Lansing, MI 48909
- Line 38 credit forward: available only on an original return
## Examples (from the form)
- Corrected W-2 after filing: new MI-1040, Schedule W, AMD box 6i, copy of corrected W-2
- Under-reported property taxes only: new MI-1040CR with Amended box and tax bills; no MI-1040 or AMD
## Questions
- What changed since you filed, and did the original finish processing?
- Is the change only to a property tax or home heating credit claim?
- Did the IRS change your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Filing AMD for a credit-only change to MI-1040CR/CR-2/CR-7
- Missing the Line 33 reconciliation of the original refund or payment
- Amending the MI-1040 for a farming carryback
## Prompt
- I need to amend my Michigan return.
