# Authorized Representative Declaration (Form 151)
## Description
Authorizes Treasury to communicate with a named person or firm, revokes prior authority, or directs copies of dispute notices to a representative. It is separate from the "Treasury may discuss my return with my preparer" checkbox on the MI-1040, which covers only the preparer named on that return.
## Parts
| Part | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1 | Taxpayer information (spouse too if joint) |
| 2 | Revoke all prior authorizations (1), or for specific taxes/years (2), or stop copies of dispute notices (3) |
| 3 | Appoint a representative. Naming an ENTITY authorizes anyone at the entity; naming an INDIVIDUAL authorizes only that person. Start and end dates optional (default: signing date, until revoked) |
| 4 | Authority: 1 receive confidential information on request; 2 make presentations of fact or argument; 3 sign returns; 4 enter into agreements (may be limited to specific taxes and years) |
| 5 | Copies of all future letters and notices for a specific tax DISPUTE (specific tax and period required; "all" is rejected; not for city tax; only one representative per dispute) |
| 6 | Signatures |
Part 5 alone gives no authority to act; check Part 4 boxes too. Missing "Required" items invalidate the form.
## Special cases
- Minor child: parent signs the child's name "by (name) parent (or guardian) for minor child"
- Deceased taxpayer: do NOT use Form 151; use MI-1310 with a death certificate or letters of authority

## Questions
- Do you want someone else to talk to Treasury for you, and with what authority?
- Is this for a specific dispute?
## Common Errors
- Using Form 151 for a deceased taxpayer
- Completing Part 5 without Part 4
- Writing "all taxes" or "all periods" in Part 5
