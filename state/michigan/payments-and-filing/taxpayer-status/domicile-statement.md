# Statement to Determine State of Domicile (Form 3799)
## Description
Voluntary form to show Treasury where the taxpayer's domicile (permanent home) is. Without the information, Treasury assumes Michigan residency. Residents are taxed on ALL income from any source; nonresidents only on Michigan income.
## The legal test
A person may have several residences but only ONE domicile, which continues until a new one is established. Changing domicile requires proof of ALL THREE:
1. Specific intent to abandon the old domicile
2. Specific intent to acquire a new domicile
3. Physical presence in the new domicile
## Evidence the form collects
- Abandoning Michigan (Part B): employment transfer (temporary vs permanent), Michigan license or ID dates, voter registration, car registration, where a will is probated, what happens to the Michigan home
- Acquiring a new domicile (Part C): residential address outside Michigan, formal declaration of domicile elsewhere, other state's license or ID, other state income tax returns
- Physical presence (Part D): spouse and dependents moved; location of primary bank or safe deposit box, registered boats or snowmobiles, clubs, primary doctor
- Part F conclusion: (a) domicile left Michigan on a stated date → part-year return for the year of change; (b) domicile remains Michigan → resident returns; (c) never domiciled in Michigan → file only if Michigan-source income
## Consequences
- Part-year residents file with Schedule NR for the year of change
- Homestead Property Tax Credit requires Michigan domicile and at least six months of residency
- College students and others whose permanent home is elsewhere are not Michigan residents
## Questions
- When did you move, and do you still own or rent a Michigan home?
- Where are you registered to vote, licensed to drive, and registered for vehicles?
- Did your spouse and dependents move with you?
## Common Errors
- Assuming a temporary job assignment changes domicile
- Keeping a Michigan driver's license and voter registration after "moving"
## Prompt
- I moved to Florida. How do I prove I am no longer a Michigan resident?
