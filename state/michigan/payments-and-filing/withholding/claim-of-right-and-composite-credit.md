# Repayment (Claim of Right) Credit and Composite Filer Credit
## Claim of right / repayment of income taxed in a prior year
- If income reported in a prior year was repaid in 2026 (e.g., unemployment benefits), a credit may be available
- If the repayment was SUBTRACTED in arriving at 2026 AGI, no Michigan credit (income already reduced)
- If the repayment was deducted on federal Schedule A or claimed as a federal credit, Michigan allows a credit = amount repaid × Michigan rate in effect in the year the tax was paid (4.25% for 2024-2026 years; 4.05% for 2023)
- Add the credit to Line 31 and write "Claim of Right/Repayment"; attach the computation, proof of repayment, and federal Form 1040 and schedules
## Composite filer participants
A nonresident owner included on a flow-through entity's Michigan Composite Return (Form 807) may file an MI-1040 and claim the Michigan tax paid on the owner's behalf: add it to Line 31 and write "Composite Filing". The composite return itself is an entity filing and outside this taxonomy. Composite participants do not also claim the FTE credit, FTE add-back, or FTE refund subtraction for amounts reported on the composite return.
## Questions
- Did you repay income that was taxed in an earlier year?
- Were you included on a partnership's or S corporation's Michigan composite return?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a Michigan repayment credit when the repayment already reduced AGI
- Double-claiming composite tax as both withholding and an FTE credit
## Prompt
- I had to pay back unemployment benefits from last year.
