# Employee's Michigan Withholding Exemption Certificate (MI-W4)
## Description
The state counterpart to the federal W-4. It tells an employer how many personal and dependent exemptions to allow (each worth $5,900 of annual wages in 2026) and whether the employee is exempt from Michigan withholding. The federal W-4 cannot be used instead. Retirees use the MI W-4P (Withholding Certificate for Michigan Pension or Annuity Payments) to direct pension withholding; without one, payers withhold 4.25% on all taxable distributions.
## Key rules
- File on or before the first day of work. Without one, the employer withholds with ZERO exemptions
- Line 6 exemptions may not exceed those claimable on the MI-1040. Dependents are federal qualifying children and qualifying relatives
- Do not double-count: no personal exemption if someone else claims you; claim it with only one employer; do not claim a spouse's or dependents' exemptions that the spouse claims
- Line 7: optional additional flat amount per pay
- File a new MI-W4 within 10 days if residency changes or exemptions decrease (divorce or separation, spouse claims own exemption, dependent no longer qualifies)
## Exemption from withholding
- Line 8a — no expected liability: ALL of: employment intermittent, temporary, or part-time; exemptions exceed annual taxable pay; exempt from federal withholding; no Michigan liability last year
- Line 8b — wages exempt: nonresident military spouse; resident of a reciprocal state (IL, IN, KY, MN, OH, WI) working in Michigan; tribal member with a tax agreement living in the agreement area; enrolled member of a non-agreement tribe living and working in its Indian Country
- Line 8c — domicile in a Renaissance Zone
## Employer obligations
Keep the certificate; report new hires (www.mi-newhire.com); send Treasury a copy when an employee claims 10 or more exemptions or claims exemption from withholding (Tax Technical Section, P.O. Box 30477, Lansing, MI 48909). Any alteration to the form makes it invalid.
## 2026 tips and overtime note
Michigan's 2026-2028 tips and overtime deductions are claimed on the annual return; the MI-W4 does not change withholding for them. Employees expecting a large deduction reduce over-withholding only through the number of exemptions they are entitled to, not by an extra "deduction" line.
## Questions
- Are you a resident of Michigan or a reciprocal state?
- How many exemptions will you claim on your MI-1040?
- Does your spouse claim exemptions with their employer?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the same exemptions with two employers
- Claiming exempt status while expecting a Michigan liability
- Using the federal W-4
