# Michigan Withholding (Schedule W)
## Description
Michigan tax withheld is claimed only through Schedule W. Do NOT send W-2s or 1099s with the return; keep them six years.
## 2026 withholding rules (Form 446, 2026)
| Item | 2026 |
|---|---|
| Withholding rate | 4.25% of compensation after the exemption allowance |
| Per-exemption allowance on MI-W4 | $5,900 |
| Pension withholding with no MI W-4P | 4.25% of all taxable distributions |
| Bonuses and supplemental pay paid separately | Flat 4.25%, no exemption adjustment |
| Nonresident gambling winnings at Michigan casinos/tracks | 4.25% of reportable winnings |
## Table 1 — W-2, W-2G, corrected W-2
Filer/spouse flag, employer EIN, employer name, Box 1 wages, Box 17 Michigan tax withheld. Report MILITARY PAY here even if no Michigan tax was withheld (ties to the Schedule 1 Line 14 subtraction).
## Table 2 — 1099 forms
Filer/spouse flag, payer EIN, payer name, taxable amount, Michigan tax withheld. Report MILITARY RETIREMENT and RAILROAD RETIREMENT (Tier 1 and Tier 2) here even if no Michigan tax was withheld (ties to Schedule 1 Line 11).
## Notes
- Use additional Schedule W forms for more rows and carry the subtotal
- A corrected W-2 received after filing requires an amended MI-1040 with a new Schedule W and Schedule AMD box 6i
- If an employer over-withheld and did not refund within the year, the employee claims the full amount withheld on the return
- City income tax withholding (Detroit, etc.) is NOT Michigan withholding
- Michigan residents who paid tax to another state claim the MI-1040 Line 18 credit, not withholding
## Required Information
- Every W-2, W-2G, and 1099 showing Michigan withholding, military pay, military retirement, or railroad retirement
## Questions
- Did your employer or pension payer withhold Michigan income tax?
- Do you have military pay, military retirement, or railroad retirement?
## Common Errors
- Including city tax or another state's tax as Michigan withholding
- Omitting military pay or retirement because nothing was withheld
- Attaching W-2s instead of completing Schedule W
