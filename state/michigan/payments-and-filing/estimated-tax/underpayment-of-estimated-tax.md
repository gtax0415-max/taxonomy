# Underpayment of Estimated Tax (MI-2210)
## Description
Computes interest and penalty when estimated payments or withholding were late or short in any quarter — even if the return shows a refund. Optional: a taxpayer may leave the interest line blank, omit MI-2210, and let Treasury bill.
## Skip the form when
- 2026 tax after credits minus withholding is $500 or less (on the 2025 form: MI-1040 Line 21 minus Lines 26, 27, 28b, 29, 30, and 31)
- No 2025 liability (if a return was required), or no 2025 filing requirement with a full 12-month federal return (no penalty; interest may still apply)
- Timely equal installments of at least 100% of 2025 tax or 90% of 2026 tax
- Farmers/fishermen/seafarers with two-thirds of income from those sources who file and pay by March 1, 2027
## Required annual payment (Part 1)
Smaller of 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax — 110% of 2025 tax if 2025 AGI exceeded $150,000 ($75,000 MFS). Divide by four unless annualizing.
## 2026 installment columns
A: April 15, 2026 · B: June 15, 2026 · C: September 15, 2026 · D: January 15, 2027. One-fourth of withholding is treated as paid on each date unless actual dates are documented. A 2025 overpayment credited forward applies to the first installment. Extension payments and payments after January 15, 2027 are not estimates.
## 2026 interest (Part 2)
| Rate period | Annual | Daily factor |
|---|---|---|
| January 1 – June 30, 2026 | 8.48% | 0.0002324 |
| July 1 – December 31, 2026 | 7.85% | 0.0002150 |
| January 1 – April 15, 2027 | Pending (set in an autumn 2026 bulletin) | |
Interest runs from each installment date to the payment date or April 15, 2027. Payments apply to the oldest underpayment first.
## Penalty (Part 3)
25% of the underpayment for a quarter with NO estimate (minimum $25); 10% for an underpaid or late quarter (minimum $10). For the penalty, payments apply in the quarter received and are not carried back; overpayments carry forward.
## Annualized income method
For uneven income (seasonal business, capital gain, bonus, severance): complete all four worksheet columns with year-to-date income through 3/31, 5/31, 8/31, and 12/31/2026, annualization factors 4, 2.4, 1.5, 1; tax at 4.25%; required percentages 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%, 90%.
## Questions
- Did you make 2026 estimated payments, and on what dates?
- What was your 2025 tax and AGI?
- Was your income concentrated late in the year?
## Common Errors
- Counting an extension payment as an estimate
- Forgetting the 110% rule above $150,000 AGI
- Using the 2025 interest rates for 2026 periods
## Prompt
- Will I owe a penalty for not paying Michigan estimates?
