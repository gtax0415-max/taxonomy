# Estimated Income Tax (MI-1040ES, 2026)
## Description
Quarterly prepayments of Michigan income tax for income without enough withholding (self-employment, investments, pensions without withholding, gains). Required for residents AND nonresidents.
## Who must pay (2026)
Anyone expecting to owe MORE than $500 on the 2026 MI-1040, unless 2026 withholding will be at least the smallest of:
| Safe harbor | Amount |
|---|---|
| Current year | 90% of 2026 tax (qualified farmers, fishermen, seafarers: 66⅔%) |
| Prior year | 100% of 2025 tax |
| Prior year, higher income | 110% of 2025 tax if 2025 AGI exceeded $150,000 ($75,000 married filing separately) |
Not required if two-thirds of gross income is from farming, fishing, or seafaring and the taxpayer either pays the full estimate with a voucher by January 15, 2027, or files and pays by March 1, 2027.
## 2026 due dates (calendar year)
| Installment | Due | Income change during |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | April 15, 2026 | January 1 – March 31 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 | April 1 – May 31 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 | June 1 – August 31 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 | September 1 – December 31 |
Filing the 2026 MI-1040 (and paying) before February 1, 2027 replaces the fourth installment. Fiscal-year filers: first installment is the 15th day of the fourth month (form instruction as printed).
## Worksheet (2026)
1. Estimated 2026 income subject to tax (federal AGI ± Schedule 1)
2. Exemption allowance in DOLLARS ($5,900 per personal/dependent exemption plus special exemptions)
3. Line 1 − Line 2
4. × 4.25%
5-7. Less estimated credits and expected withholding
8. Estimated tax due; 9. ÷ 4 (or remaining vouchers)
Apply the 2025 overpayment to the first quarter, then later quarters until used.
## Paying
e-Payments (eCheck, credit or debit card) at www.michigan.gov/iit — no voucher mailed. By mail: check to "State of Michigan", last four SSN digits and "2026 MI-1040ES" on the check, with the voucher, unstapled, to Michigan Department of Treasury, P.O. Box 30774, Lansing, MI 48909. Married couples filing separately must use separate vouchers and payments.
## 2026 factors that change the estimate
- Retirement subtraction rises to $67,610 / $135,220 (full phase-in)
- Tips and overtime deductions begin
- Tier 3 standard deduction no longer reduced by Social Security
- Exemption rises to $5,900
## Questions
- Do you expect to owe more than $500 after withholding and credits?
- What was your 2025 Michigan tax and 2025 AGI?
- Is at least two-thirds of your income from farming or fishing?
## Common Errors
- Sending a voucher with no payment
- Combining the estimate with another payment
- Using the pre-2026 retirement limits to project tax
