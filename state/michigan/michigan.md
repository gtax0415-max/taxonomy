# Michigan Individual Income Tax — Taxonomy Index
## How a Michigan return is built
| MI-1040 line | Step | Folder |
|---|---|---|
| 10 | Start with FEDERAL AGI (Form 1040, Line 11) | income/ |
| 11 | + Additions (Schedule 1, Lines 1-9) | income/additions/ |
| 13 | − Subtractions (Schedule 1, Lines 10-31) | deductions/subtractions/, deductions/retirement/ |
| 14 | = Income subject to tax | |
| 15 | − Exemption allowance (Line 9f, or Schedule NR Line 19) | deductions/exemptions/ |
| 16 | = Taxable income | |
| 17 | × 4.25% = Tax | |
| 18-20 | − Nonrefundable credits (other-jurisdiction tax, historic preservation, organ donation) | credits/ |
| 22-24 | + Voluntary contributions, First-Time Home Buyer penalty, use tax | payments-and-filing/other-return-lines/ |
| 31-32 | Withholding, estimates, extension payments | payments-and-filing/ |
| 35-40 | Relief requests, refund or balance due, digital asset question | return-information/ |
| 26-30 | Refundable credits | credits/ |
Line numbers are from the 2025 MI-1040 and Schedule 1. Confirm them against the 2026 forms when Treasury releases them (typically December). The 2026 Schedule 1 will need at least one NEW line for the tips and overtime deductions.

## Folders
- credits/ — Michigan credits (built earlier)
- income/ — federal AGI starting point, additions, and the sourcing forms (Schedule NR, MI-1040H, MI-461, MI-1040D, MI-8949, MI-4797)
- deductions/ — exemptions, retirement and senior deductions, and all other subtractions
- return-information/ — who must file, filing status and residency, header checkboxes (school district, campaign fund, farmer box, disaster/combat relief, digital assets), refund, balance due, direct deposit, signatures
- payments-and-filing/ — withholding (Schedule W, MI-W4), 2026 estimates (MI-1040ES, MI-2210), extension (Form 4), payment voucher (MI-1040-V), voluntary contributions (Form 4642), amended returns (Schedule AMD), farming loss carryback (Form 5603), domicile (Form 3799), power of attorney (Form 151), deceased taxpayer refund (MI-1310)

## Scope — individual income tax only
Included: the MI-1040 and every schedule, credit claim, worksheet, payment form, and administrative form an individual (resident, part-year resident, or nonresident) uses with it, as listed on Treasury's Individual Income Tax forms page.
Excluded as not part of the individual return: Fiduciary Income Tax (MI-1041 and its schedules); the Composite Return itself (Form 807 — only the participating owner's credit on MI-1040 Line 31 is covered); Michigan Business Tax and Corporate Income Tax forms (including Form 4594 business farmland claims); Flow-Through Entity Tax returns (Forms 5772, 5774 — referenced only as the source of the owner's credit); Partnership Adjustments Payment Voucher (Form 5839); City of Detroit and other city income tax returns; sales and use tax returns (individual use tax on MI-1040 Line 24 is covered).
## 2026 parameter labels used in every file
- Confirmed: published by Treasury or set by statute for 2026
- PROJECTED: indexed amount not yet published; estimated with the 2.6% factor Treasury applied to the official 2026 retirement maximum and rounded as the statute requires. Replace when Treasury publishes the 2026 booklet (typically December 2026)
- Pending: not indexable (depends on appropriations or federal funding); the 2025 value is shown as a placeholder
