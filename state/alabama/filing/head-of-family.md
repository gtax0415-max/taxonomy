# Head of Family (Schedule HOF)
## Description
Filing status for an unmarried person who maintains a home for a qualifying person. Carries a $3,000 personal exemption and the head-of-family standard deduction column. Code of Alabama §40-18-1 (same meaning as federal head of household) and §40-18-19.

## Requirements
- Unmarried or legally separated on December 31
- NOT a surviving spouse (federal definition, IRC §2(a))
- The qualifying person is not a foster child
- One of two tests:
  1. Paid more than half the cost of keeping up a home where you and a qualifying person lived for more than six months, OR
  2. Paid more than half the cost of keeping up your PARENT's main home for the whole year (the parent must be your dependent but need not live with you)

## Qualifying persons
- Your unmarried child, stepchild, adopted child, grandchild or great-grandchild (need not be your dependent)
- A married child in those groups, if your dependent
- Any relative you can claim as a dependent

## Schedule HOF asks for
- Qualifying person's name, SSN and relationship
- Whether you rent or own the home
- Marital status (married, divorced or legally separated) and the date
- Whether the qualifying person lived with you
- Whether you paid more than half the support

## Notes
- ALDOR requires that you actually be unmarried; a married person living apart generally files married filing separately (ALDOR guidance; the federal "considered unmarried" rule may not apply)
- Temporary absences count as time in the home
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A single mother whose son lived with her all year, and who pays the household costs, files head of family with Schedule HOF.

## Required Information
- Qualifying person details
- Household costs and who paid them
- Marital status and dates

## Questions
- Who lived with you, and for how long?
- Did you pay more than half the cost of the home?
- Were you unmarried on December 31?
- Did your spouse die in 2024 or 2025?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out Schedule HOF
- Using a foster child as the qualifying person
- Claiming it while married and living apart
- Claiming it as a surviving spouse
