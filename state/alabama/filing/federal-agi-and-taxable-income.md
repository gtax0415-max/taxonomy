# Federal AGI and Federal Taxable Income
## Description
Part IV, line 4 asks for federal adjusted gross income and federal taxable income exactly as reported on the 2026 federal return.

## Rules
- Enter FEDERAL amounts, not Alabama AGI or taxable income
- Federal AGI: Form 1040, line 11; federal taxable income: Form 1040, line 15
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Federal AGI of $136,000 and taxable income of $102,180 are entered as on Form 1040.

## Required Information
- 2026 federal Form 1040

## Questions
- What were your federal AGI and taxable income?
- Will you amend your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Entering Alabama AGI
- Using amounts from a draft federal return that changed before filing

## Prompt
- Where do I report federal AGI on the Alabama return?
