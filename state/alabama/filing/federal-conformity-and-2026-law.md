# Federal Conformity and 2026 Law
## Description
Alabama adopts specific federal provisions by reference rather than federal taxable income. ALDOR's analysis of the 2025 federal act (One Big Beautiful Bill Act), dated October 31, 2025 and updated November 10, 2025, sets out which provisions flow to Alabama.

## Federal items Alabama does NOT follow
- No tax on tips (IRC §224)
- Federal overtime deduction (IRC §225); Alabama has its own $1,000 deduction instead
- Federal $6,000 senior deduction
- Qualified business income deduction (IRC §199A)
- Federal standard deduction and the itemized deduction limit (IRC §68)
- Non-itemizer charitable deduction
- Federal dependent care and adoption credits

## Federal items Alabama DOES follow
- Qualified vehicle loan interest, up to $10,000, phase-out figured on Alabama AGI (Schedule A)
- Federal cap on deductible taxes ($40,400 for 2026), figured on Alabama AGI; in practice it limits property taxes
- 0.5% of income floor on charitable gifts (itemizers, from 2026)
- Mortgage insurance premiums treated as mortgage interest (from 2026)
- 529 K-12 expansion, dependent care assistance exclusion ($7,500), HSA changes
- Section 179 and bonus depreciation

## 2026 Alabama acts
- Act 2026-604 (HB 527): overtime premium deduction up to $1,000 per taxpayer, 2026–2028 (also suspended the state food sales tax May 1–June 30, 2026, which does not affect Form 40)
- Act 2026-551 (HB 250): employer Trump Account contributions and employer student loan payments excluded, from 2026
- Rural Hospital Investment credit (Act 2025-404, amended by Act 2026-34): 100% of donations up to $15,000 ($30,000 joint), limited to tax, 3-year carryforward, tax years 2026–2028; $20 million statewide cap for 2026 (reached within the first month); a new credit on the 2026 return
- Act 2025-119 (Portable Benefits Act): adjustment for independent contractors' portable benefit account contributions, from 2026
- Act 2026-603 (HB 341): National Guard drill pay exclusion, 2027–2029 (not 2026)
- Act 2026-100 (HB 27): catastrophe savings account changes, from 2027 (not 2026)

## Example
A server with $22,000 of tips reports all of it in Alabama wages; the federal tips deduction does not carry over.

## Required Information
- Federal return and any federal deductions new for 2025 or 2026

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal tips, overtime or senior deduction?
- Did you buy a new car with a loan in 2025 or 2026?
- Did you donate to a rural hospital through the Alabama program?

## Common Errors
- Carrying the federal tips, overtime or senior deduction to Alabama
- Applying the federal tax cap or vehicle interest phase-out using federal AGI instead of Alabama AGI
- Applying 2027 law (Guard pay, catastrophe savings) to 2026

## Prompt
- Does Alabama follow the new federal deductions?
