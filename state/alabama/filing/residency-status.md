# Residency (Full Year or Part Year)
## Description
Part IV, line 1 asks whether the filer was a full-year or part-year Alabama resident.

## Rules
- FULL YEAR: report income from all sources
- PART YEAR: check Part Year and enter the dates of Alabama residence; report income received while a resident (and Alabama-source income otherwise); itemized deductions only from the resident period; full personal exemption and standard deduction allowed
- Nonresidents file Form 40NR (out of scope)
- A resident who enters military service generally stays an Alabama resident; military spouses follow the federal Military Spouses Residency Relief Act
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A family living in Huntsville all year checks Full Year.

## Required Information
- Residence dates
- Income earned before and after a move

## Questions
- Did you live in Alabama all of 2026?
- If not, when did you move in or out?

## Common Errors
- Checking Part Year without dates
- Reporting all-year income on a part-year return
- Deducting itemized expenses paid while a nonresident

## Prompt
- Am I a full-year Alabama resident?
- I moved to Alabama in July.
