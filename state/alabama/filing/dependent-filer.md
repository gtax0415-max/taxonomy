# Filer Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
A person claimed as a dependent on someone else's return (for example, a student) still files on their own and still gets the FULL Alabama personal exemption and the regular standard deduction. Alabama has no reduced dependent standard deduction.

## Rules
- Files Single (line 1) unless married
- $1,500 personal exemption allowed (line 13)
- Standard deduction from the regular chart (line 11), based on the filer's own AGI
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A teenager with $11,648 of wages, claimed by a parent: files Single, $1,500 exemption, $3,000 standard deduction from the chart.

## Required Information
- The filer's own income
- Whether someone else claims them

## Questions
- Can someone else claim you as a dependent?
- What was your total income?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the personal exemption
- Applying the federal limited dependent standard deduction

## Prompt
- Does a dependent get the personal exemption in Alabama?
