# Married Filing Separate
## Description
Each spouse files a separate Form 40. The personal exemption is $1,500 and the married-filing-separately standard deduction column applies.

## Rules
- Spouse's SSN goes on line 3. If the spouse is not required to file, attach a statement explaining why
- If the spouse is a nonresident alien with no income and no SSN, check the NRA box
- BOTH spouses must take the standard deduction or BOTH must itemize, unless they lived apart all year
- Neither spouse may deduct expenses the other paid
- Federal income tax deduction (line 12): if the spouses filed a JOINT federal return, each deducts the joint federal liability times their share of joint federal AGI
- Alabama is not a community property state
- A joint Alabama return cannot be changed to separate returns after the due date
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Spouses who filed jointly federally (federal tax $10,000; AGI split 60/40) file separately in Alabama: one deducts $6,000 and the other $4,000.

## Required Information
- Spouse's SSN
- Whether the spouse itemizes
- Each spouse's share of federal AGI and the federal liability

## Questions
- Did you and your spouse file a joint federal return?
- Is your spouse itemizing on a separate Alabama return?
- Did you live apart all year?

## Common Errors
- Taking the standard deduction when the spouse itemizes
- Deducting the full joint federal tax on each return
- Deducting expenses the other spouse paid
