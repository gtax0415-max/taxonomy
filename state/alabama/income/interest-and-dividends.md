# Interest and Dividends (Schedule B)
## Description
Taxable interest and dividends. Alabama taxes all dividends as ordinary income and taxes interest on other states' municipal bonds, but exempts interest on U.S. and Alabama obligations.

## Rules
- Schedule B required when total interest and dividends (taxable and exempt) are $1,500 or more
- Schedule B, Part A: EXEMPT interest (U.S. obligations, §40-18-14(a)(3)(d); Alabama and its political subdivisions, §40-18-14(a)(3)(f))
- Schedule B, Part B: taxable interest and dividends; line 3 → Form 40, line 6
- Interest on OTHER states' and their localities' bonds is taxable
- Qualified dividends get no special rate
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
$612 of bank interest and no other interest or dividends: line 6 is $612, no Schedule B needed.

## Required Information
- Each payer and amount
- Issuer of any tax-exempt bond interest (Alabama or another state)
- U.S. Treasury interest (1099-INT box 3)

## Questions
- Did you receive interest or dividends?
- Do you hold municipal bonds, and from which states?
- Do you hold U.S. Treasuries or savings bonds?

## Common Errors
- Exempting all of 1099-INT box 8, including other states' bonds
- Taxing U.S. Treasury interest
- Skipping Schedule B at $1,500 or more

## Prompt
- Is municipal bond interest taxable in Alabama?
