# Alimony Received
## Description
Alimony and separate maintenance received are Alabama income ONLY to the extent they are federally taxable. Code of Alabama §40-18-14(a)(1) adopts IRC §71. Alimony under an agreement executed after December 31, 2018 is NOT Alabama income.

## Rules
- Agreement executed on or before December 31, 2018, and not modified to adopt the post-2018 rules: taxable
- Agreement executed (or so modified) after 2018: not taxable
- Child support: never taxable
- Mirrors the alimony-paid deduction (Part II, line 4)
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
$18,000 received under a 2015 divorce decree: Part I, line 1 is $18,000. The same payments under a 2020 decree: not reported.

## Required Information
- Execution and modification dates of the agreement
- Alimony received in 2026, separate from child support

## Questions
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed?
- Was it modified after 2018 to adopt the new rules?
- How much of what you received is child support?

## Common Errors
- Reporting alimony under a post-2018 agreement
- Including child support

## Prompt
- Is alimony taxable in Alabama?
