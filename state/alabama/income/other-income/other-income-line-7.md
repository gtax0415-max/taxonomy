# Other Income
## Description
Taxable income not reported elsewhere, with its nature and source.

## What goes here
- Gambling winnings, lottery and contest prizes and awards
- Cancelled debt, to the extent taxable under IRC §108 (Code of Alabama §40-18-14(a)(3)h)
- W-2 items not included in box 16 (unreported tips, taxable dependent care benefits)
- Non-qualified withdrawals from an Alabama 529 or ABLE account, plus 10%
- Non-qualified withdrawals from a catastrophe savings or home buyer savings account
- Foreign earned income exclusion, entered as a negative amount with Form 2555

## Notes
- Gambling losses are not subtracted here; they are an itemized deduction, limited to winnings. Alabama does not follow the federal 2026 rule limiting losses to 90%
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Casino winnings of $12,400 (W-2G): Part I, line 7, with the source described.

## Required Information
- Type, source and amount of each item

## Questions
- Did you have gambling winnings or prizes?
- Was any debt cancelled?
- Did you take money out of a 529, ABLE or home buyer account for a non-qualified purpose?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out cancelled debt
- Netting gambling losses against winnings here
- Forgetting the 10% addition on a non-qualified 529 or ABLE withdrawal

## Prompt
- Where does gambling income go in Alabama?
