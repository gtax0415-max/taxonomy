# Business Income or Loss
## Description
Net profit or loss from federal Schedule C, attached to the Alabama return, adjusted for Alabama differences shown as "Other Expenses" with an explanation.

## Alabama differences
- No qualified business income (QBI) deduction
- No home office income limit
- No passive loss or excess business loss limit
- Section 179 and 100% bonus depreciation follow federal law: 2026 section 179 limit $2,560,000, phase-out beginning at $4,090,000 of property placed in service (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)
- Self-employment tax: no "half of SE tax" adjustment. The FULL SE tax is an itemized deduction on Alabama Schedule A (only if itemizing)
- Gas and oil percentage depletion is 12%

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A freelancer's Schedule C profit of $21,245: Part I, line 2.

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule C
- Any Alabama adjustments

## Questions
- Did you have self-employment income?
- Did you take a home office deduction limited federally?
- Do you plan to itemize on Alabama Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting half of SE tax here
- Claiming the QBI deduction
- Applying the federal home office or passive limits
- Claiming SE tax on Schedule A while taking the standard deduction

## Prompt
- Where does Schedule C income go in Alabama?
