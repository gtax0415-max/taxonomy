# Rents, Royalties, Partnerships, Estates and Trusts (Schedule E)
## Description
Income and losses from rental property, royalties and pass-through entities. Alabama has NO passive activity loss limit, so rental and passive losses are allowed in full.

## How the schedule works
- Part I: rental and royalty income and expenses by property
- Part II, line 24: partnerships, S corporations, estates and trusts (name, EIN, type, amount)
- Line 25: total → Form 40, page 2, Part I, line 5

## Rules
- No passive loss limit (IRC §469 not adopted)
- The federal excess business loss limit does not apply
- Use the ALABAMA K-1 amounts, which already reflect Alabama adjustments
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A rental with a $9,000 loss suspended federally: the full $9,000 is deducted in Alabama.

## Required Information
- Income and expenses for each property
- Each K-1's Alabama amount

## Questions
- Do you own rental property or receive royalties?
- Do you receive K-1s from partnerships, S corporations, estates or trusts?
- Do you have federally suspended passive losses?

## Common Errors
- Carrying the federal passive loss limit into Alabama
- Using federal K-1 amounts instead of Alabama K-1 amounts
- Later deducting a prior-year federally suspended loss that Alabama already allowed

## Prompt
- Can I deduct my rental loss in Alabama?
