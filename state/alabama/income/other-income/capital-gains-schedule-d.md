# Capital Gains and Losses (Schedule D)
## Description
Gains and losses on sales of property, each listed on Alabama Schedule D. Code of Alabama §40-18-8: the entire gain or loss is recognized and taxed at ordinary rates.

## Rules
- No preferential rate for long-term gains
- No $3,000 capital loss limit and no capital loss carryover: net losses are deducted in full in the year they occur (practitioner consensus; ALDOR's Schedule D instructions should be checked)
- Excess capital losses cannot create a net operating loss
- Loss on a personal residence is not deductible
- Section 121 home sale exclusion follows federal law
- Like-kind exchanges and involuntary conversions follow federal law
- Gains on precious-metal bullion are excluded (Act 2024-447)
- Capital gain distributions and business asset sales are included
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Stock sold for a $6,300 gain: listed on Schedule D; $6,300 on Part I, line 3.

## Required Information
- For each sale: property, dates acquired and sold, sale price, basis, depreciation allowed

## Questions
- Did you sell stock, crypto, real estate or business assets?
- Did you sell your home?
- Do you have a federal capital loss carryover?

## Common Errors
- Applying the federal $3,000 loss limit
- Bringing a federal capital loss carryover into Alabama
- Deducting a loss on a personal residence
- Using federal basis where Alabama depreciation differed

## Prompt
- Are capital gains taxed differently in Alabama?
