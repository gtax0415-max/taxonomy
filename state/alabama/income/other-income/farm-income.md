# Farm Income or Loss
## Description
Net farm profit or loss from federal Schedule F, attached to the Alabama return, adjusted for Alabama differences.

## Rules
- Attach federal Schedule F; show any Alabama adjustment as "Other Expenses" with an explanation
- Alabama follows federal section 179 and bonus depreciation
- The federal excess business loss limit does NOT apply in Alabama
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Farmers with at least two-thirds of gross income from farming may skip estimated payments by filing and paying by March 1, 2027
- Farm losses are allowed in full

## Example
A family farm with $34,935 net profit: Part I, line 6.

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule F
- Any Alabama adjustments

## Questions
- Do you farm?
- Was your federal farm loss limited?
- Did you receive patronage dividends or farm program payments?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out patronage dividends or program payments
- Carrying a federal excess business loss limit into Alabama

## Prompt
- Where does farm income go on Alabama Form 40?
