# Defined Benefit Plans, Public and Military Retirement
## Description
These retirement payments are EXEMPT from Alabama tax (Code of Alabama §40-18-19(a)) and are listed on Schedule RS, Part I with the reason, not added to income.

## Exempt retirement income
- Defined benefit plans under IRC §414(j), to the extent federally taxable
- Teachers', Employees' and Judicial Retirement Systems of Alabama
- Federal civil service and other U.S. government retirement
- Military retirement
- Railroad Retirement
- TVA pension
- Alabama police and firefighter retirement

## Notes
- Alabama tax withheld from an exempt pension is still claimed (Schedule RS, Part V)
- Lump-sum or defined contribution payouts (401(k), 403(b), IRA) are NOT exempt here; they may qualify for the $6,000 age-65 exclusion
- Confirm defined benefit status with the plan administrator if unclear
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A $28,000 company pension from a defined benefit plan: listed in Schedule RS Part I and not taxed.

## Required Information
- Payer and plan type for each 1099-R

## Questions
- Is your pension a defined benefit plan?
- Is it from an Alabama public system, the federal government or the military?

## Common Errors
- Taxing a defined benefit pension
- Treating a 401(k) or IRA as exempt
- Not claiming withholding on the exempt pension

## Prompt
- Are pensions taxed in Alabama?
