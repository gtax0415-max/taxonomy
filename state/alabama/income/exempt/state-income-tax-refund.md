# State Income Tax Refund
## Description
State income tax refunds are not Alabama income and are not entered. Because Alabama does not allow a deduction for state income tax, a refund of it is not taxable.

## Notes
- The ALDOR list of nontaxable items says "state income tax refunds" without limiting it to Alabama, so refunds from other states are also excluded
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A 2025 Alabama refund of $310 (1099-G) is not entered on the 2026 return.

## Required Information
- 1099-G amount (to confirm it is a state income tax refund, not unemployment or another payment)

## Questions
- Did you receive a state income tax refund in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Entering the refund as other income
- Confusing a 1099-G for unemployment with a tax refund

## Prompt
- Is my state tax refund taxable in Alabama?
