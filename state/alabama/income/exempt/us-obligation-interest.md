# Interest on U.S. and Alabama Obligations
## Description
Interest on U.S. government obligations and on obligations of Alabama and its counties, cities and other political subdivisions is exempt from Alabama tax (Code of Alabama §40-18-14(a)(3)(d) and (f)). It is listed in Schedule B, Part A.

## Exempt
- U.S. Treasury bills, notes and bonds; U.S. savings bonds (1099-INT box 3)
- Bonds of Alabama and its political subdivisions

## NOT exempt
- Bonds of other states and their localities, even if federally tax-exempt
- Interest from Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac and similar agencies not backed as U.S. obligations

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
$4,600 of savings bond interest: Schedule B, Part A; not in line 6.

## Required Information
- Treasury and savings bond interest
- Issuer of each municipal bond

## Questions
- Do you hold U.S. Treasuries or savings bonds?
- Are your municipal bonds issued in Alabama?

## Common Errors
- Taxing Treasury or savings bond interest
- Exempting interest on another state's bonds

## Prompt
- Is Treasury interest taxable in Alabama?
