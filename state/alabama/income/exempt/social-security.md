# Social Security and Railroad Retirement
## Description
Social Security benefits and Tier I and Tier II Railroad Retirement benefits are NOT taxed by Alabama and are not entered anywhere on Form 40.

## Notes
- Applies regardless of income or the federal taxable amount
- Railroad Retirement Tier II paid on Form RRB-1099-R is also exempt
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A retiree with $17,900 of federally taxable Social Security reports nothing in Alabama.

## Required Information
- SSA-1099 and RRB-1099 amounts (to confirm the source)

## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security or Railroad Retirement?

## Common Errors
- Carrying the federal taxable amount into Alabama income
- Reporting RRB-1099-R Tier II benefits as a taxable pension

## Prompt
- Is Social Security taxed in Alabama?
