# Employer Trump Account Contributions and Education Loan Payments
## Description
NEW for 2026. Two employer-provided benefits are excluded from Alabama income by Act 2026-551 (HB 250), which adds Code of Alabama §40-18-14(a)(3)o and p, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.

## What is excluded
- Employer payments of principal or interest on an employee's qualified education loan, paid to the employee or the lender (the federal rule in IRC §127(c)(1)(B)); permanent
- Employer contributions to an employee's or dependent's Trump Account (federal One Big Beautiful Bill Act §70204)

## Notes
- Covers employer contributions only. Trump Account earnings and later distributions follow the federal rules
- Reverses ALDOR's October 2025 position that these payments were taxable Alabama wages
- Check W-2 box 16: if the employer already left these out of Alabama wages, do not subtract them again

## Example
An employer's $2,500 Trump Account contribution: not Alabama income. If W-2 box 16 already excludes it, no adjustment is needed.

## Required Information
- Employer contributions and loan payments for 2026
- Whether W-2 box 16 already excludes them

## Questions
- Did your employer contribute to a Trump Account for you or your child?
- Did your employer pay any of your student loans?

## Common Errors
- Adding the contribution to wages
- Excluding the amount twice when box 16 already leaves it out

## Prompt
- Is an employer Trump Account contribution taxable in Alabama?
