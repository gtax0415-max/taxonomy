# Unemployment Compensation
## Description
Unemployment compensation is not taxed by Alabama and is not entered. ALDOR's booklet and its list of exempt income include unemployment compensation.

## Notes
- Separately, up to $50,000 of severance, unemployment or termination pay from an administrative downsizing is excluded (Code of Alabama §40-18-19.1)
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Unemployment of $5,600 (1099-G box 1): not entered.

## Required Information
- 1099-G box 1 amount

## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment compensation?

## Common Errors
- Including it in other income
- Carrying the federal taxable amount into Alabama

## Prompt
- Is unemployment taxable in Alabama?
