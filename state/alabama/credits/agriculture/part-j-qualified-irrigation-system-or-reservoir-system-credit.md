# Qualified Irrigation System or Reservoir System Credit
## Description
One-time nonrefundable credit for a farmer who installs irrigation equipment or builds an off-stream reservoir. Code of Alabama §§40-18-340 to -345 (Act 2012-391, amended through Act 2018-468).

## Amount
- 20% of the cost of equipment, installation, fuel-to-electric conversion or reservoir construction
- Capped at $10,000 (for tax years 2023 and later)
- No program cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Agricultural business (NAICS Sector 11)
- One equipment purchase OR one reservoir per taxpayer, never both
- Reservoir must be off-stream and upland: no dam, not in a stream bed, not in wetlands
- A system drawing surface water must use a qualified reservoir, unless it draws from a river averaging more than 8,000 cubic feet per second
- Annual informational report filed with the Department of Agriculture & Industries, and pre-certification in MAT

## 2026 status
- Repealed December 31, 2028 (Act 2023-546), so available for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years after the year placed in service
- Owners of pass-through entities claim their share

## Required Information
- Cost and placed-in-service date
- Type of system and water source
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Did your farm install irrigation equipment or build a reservoir?
- Where does the system get its water?
- Have you claimed this credit before?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $10,000
- Claiming a second system or both equipment and a reservoir
- A reservoir built with a dam or in a stream
- Missing the Agriculture & Industries report

## Prompt
- I installed an irrigation system on my farm.
