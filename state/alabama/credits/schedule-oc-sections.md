# Schedule OC: Other Available Credits (Overview)
## Description
Schedule OC is where an Alabama Form 40 filer claims every income tax credit other than withholding and estimated payments. Each Part (A through AD) figures one credit. The credits are then applied against the tax on Form 40, line 17 in a fixed order, each limited to the tax that remains. Five credits can produce a refund through Section F.

## How the schedule works
- Section A: the starting tax, carried from Form 40, line 17
- Parts A–AD: each figures the credit available for the year
- Section C: applies current-year credits in order, each limited to the remaining tax; any excess goes to the carryforward column if that credit allows a carryforward
- Section D: applies carryforwards from earlier years
- Section E: line E5 is the net tax, carried to Form 40, line 18
- Section F: the REFUNDABLE portion of Parts K, M, S, AB and AC (the part not absorbed by tax), line F6 carried to Form 40, line 25

## 2026 status
- Alabama credit amounts are set by statute and are NOT indexed for inflation, so most 2026 amounts equal 2025
- Changes that matter for 2026: Growing Alabama program cap rises to $29 million; Storm Shelter credit extended through tax year 2028 (Act 2024-223); Veterans Employment employer credit unavailable for unemployed veterans hired after December 31, 2023
- Several credits end soon: 2017 Historic Rehabilitation and Railroad Modernization after 2027; Enterprise Zone, Coal and Veterans Employment repealed in 2028

## Notes
- Most individual credits must be PRE-CERTIFIED in My Alabama Taxes (MAT) before they can be claimed. Credits passed through from a partnership or S corporation are reported from the Schedule K-1 instead
- Form 40A filers cannot use Schedule OC; a taxpayer with any credit must file Form 40
- Only five Parts are refundable through Section F. Every other credit is limited to tax and is lost or carried forward

## Example
Tax on line 17 is $1,200 and the filer has a $2,000 Alabama Adoption Tax Credit (Part M). Section C applies $1,200, so line 18 is $0. The unused $800 goes through Section F to Form 40, line 25 and is refunded.

## Required Information
- Each credit certificate, letter or MAT approval
- Schedule K-1 credit amounts from any pass-through entity
- Carryforward amounts and the year each arose

## Questions
- Do you hold any Alabama tax credit certificates or approval letters?
- Did a partnership or S corporation pass any Alabama credits to you?
- Do you have unused Alabama credits from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a credit that was never pre-certified in MAT
- Filing Form 40A when a credit requires Form 40
- Treating a nonrefundable credit as refundable
- Forgetting prior-year carryforwards in Section D
- Applying a carryforward after its carryforward period has expired
