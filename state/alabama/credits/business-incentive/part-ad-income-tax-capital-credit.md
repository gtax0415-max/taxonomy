# Income Tax Capital Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for capital investment in a qualifying Alabama project. Code of Alabama §§40-18-190 to -203 (Act 95-187). CLOSED to new projects since January 2, 2016 (replaced by the Alabama Jobs Act); only grandfathered projects still claim it.

## Amount
- Up to 5% of the project's capital costs each year for 20 years
- Limited to income tax on income from the project
- No program cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Project filed Form INT on or before January 2, 2016
- Project meets its job and wage requirements each year
- Individuals receive it through an S corporation, partnership or LLC on Form K-RCC

## Notes
- No carryforward (except certain projects under §40-18-194(b)(10)b)
- Not transferable
- A project may miss requirements in up to 3 of its 20 years with no credit those years; a later failure can trigger forfeiture of prior credit
- Attach Form K-RCC and Schedule KRCC-I to the return

## Required Information
- K-RCC amount from each entity
- Project's year in its 20-year period
- Income from the project

## Questions
- Did you receive a Form K-RCC from a business you own?
- Is the project still within its 20-year period?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a project started after 2015
- Claiming more than the tax on project income
- Missing Schedule KRCC-I

## Prompt
- I received a Form K-RCC from my S corporation.
