# Investment Credit – Alabama Jobs Act
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for capital investment in a qualifying project approved by the Alabama Department of Commerce. Code of Alabama §40-18-376, amended by Act 2023-34 (Game Plan).

## Amounts
- Up to 1.5% of qualified capital investment each year
- For up to 10 years, or 15 years in a targeted or jumpstart county
- Amount set by the project agreement and certified annually by Commerce
- No per-taxpayer statutory change for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
- If the project agreement allows it, the first 5 years of credit can be transferred at no less than 85% of face value
- New projects can be approved through July 31, 2028
- Sole proprietors attach the certification; partners and S corporation shareholders report their share on line P2
- The entity must file an allocation schedule in MAT

## Required Information
- Project agreement and annual certification
- Credit amount for this year
- K-1 allocation, if from a pass-through
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Does your business have a Jobs Act project agreement with Commerce?
- Did you receive this year's investment credit certification?
- Did you buy a transferred investment credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without an executed project agreement or MAT allocation
- Claiming more than the certified amount
- Using a carryforward older than 5 years

## Prompt
- My company has an Alabama Jobs Act investment credit.
