# Port Credit – Alabama Renewal Act
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for businesses that increase cargo shipped through Alabama ports. Code of Alabama §§40-18-400 to -403 (Act 2016-102, amended by Acts 2021-2 and 2023-419).

## Amounts
Per unit of increase over the base period:
| Increase | Per TEU | Per net ton | Per kg air cargo | Per VEU |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Under 5% | $50 | $3.00 | $0.04 | $2.91 |
| 5%–14.99% | $75 | $4.50 | $0.06 | $4.37 |
| 15%–24.99% | $100 | $6.00 | $0.08 | $5.82 |
| 25% or more | $125 | $7.50 | $0.10 | $7.28 |

- Shipments must exceed 105% of the prior 12 months and meet minimum volumes (10 TEU, 75 net tons, 15,000 kg or 400 VEU)
- Program cap: $5 million a year
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
- Commerce must approve the 12-month incentive period in advance; claim in the tax year that includes its last day
- Transferable only with the sale of substantially all company assets
- Related expense deductions are disallowed
- Attach the certification to the return

## Required Information
- Commerce certification and amount
- Incentive period dates
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Does your business ship cargo through an Alabama port?
- Did Commerce approve an incentive period?
- When did the incentive period end?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without advance Commerce approval
- Claiming in the wrong tax year
- Using a carryforward older than 5 years

## Prompt
- My company increased shipments through the Port of Mobile.
