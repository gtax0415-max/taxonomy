# Alabama Enterprise Zone Credit or Exemption
## Description
Nonrefundable credit (or, alternatively, a tax exemption) for businesses that locate or expand in an Alabama enterprise zone and hire new permanent employees. Code of Alabama §§41-23-20 to -32. The program is effectively DORMANT for 2026: ADECA reported no participation in calendar year 2025.

## Amounts
CREDIT option (§41-23-24):
- Income tax credit of 80%, 60%, 40%, then 20% (years 4–5) of tax on zone operations
- Training credit up to $1,000 per employee
- Investment credit of 10% of the first $10,000, 5% of the next $90,000, then 2%
- Overall cap: $2,500 per new permanent employee
- At least 30% of new permanent employees must have been unemployed for 90 days or more

EXEMPTION option: up to 5 years of exemption from income, business privilege and sales/use tax under a contract with the Governor. The exemption is not a credit amount.

## 2026 status
- Repealed by Act 2023-546 effective December 2028 (the Act and ALDOR cite slightly different dates). Credits already awarded are preserved
- Zones are any "targeted or jumpstart county" (Act 2019-392)
- Realistically only a 2024–2025 credit carried forward, or an existing exemption contract, will appear on a 2026 return

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 2 years
- Business must apply to ADECA before claiming
- Partners and S corporation shareholders claim their share by ownership percentage

## Required Information
- ADECA approval and the zone location
- Count of new permanent employees and how many were previously unemployed
- Training costs and capital investment
- Carryforward amounts and year generated

## Questions
- Is your business located in an Alabama enterprise zone (targeted or jumpstart county)?
- Did ADECA approve the business before the expansion?
- Do you have an enterprise zone carryforward from 2024 or 2025?
- Did you elect the exemption instead of the credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without ADECA approval
- Exceeding the $2,500 per-employee cap
- Using a carryforward older than 2 years
- Claiming both the exemption and the credit

## Prompt
- My business expanded in a jumpstart county.
- I have an enterprise zone credit carryforward.
