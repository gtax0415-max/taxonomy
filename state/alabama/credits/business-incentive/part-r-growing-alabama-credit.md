# Growing Alabama Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for cash contributions to an approved economic development organization (EDO) for a Commerce-approved project. Code of Alabama §§40-18-417 to -417.7 (Act 2021-2, amended by Acts 2023-34 and 2023-512).

## Amounts
- 100% of the cash contribution, up to the amount reserved
- Limited to 50% of the taxpayer's Alabama tax liability
- Program cap for calendar 2026: $29 million (2025 was $26 million; 2027 rises to $32 million)
- At least 25% of the cap is reserved for targeted or jumpstart counties

## 2026 changes
- Program cap increased from $26 million to $29 million
- 2026 SB289 (effective June 1, 2026) lets sites owned by more than one EDO qualify through joint applications. It does not change the credit amount or the 50% limit
- No new applications approved after July 31, 2028

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
- Reserve in MAT within 30 days of the opening; pay the EDO within 21 days; the EDO confirms within 60 days
- Cannot also take an itemized deduction for the same contribution
- Not transferable except pro rata to pass-through owners

## Required Information
- Reservation confirmation and amount
- Contribution date and amount
- EDO confirmation
- Alabama tax liability (for the 50% limit)

## Questions
- Did you reserve a Growing Alabama credit in MAT?
- Did you pay the EDO within 21 days?
- Did you also deduct the contribution?

## Common Errors
- Contributing without a reservation
- Claiming more than 50% of tax liability
- Deducting the same contribution on Schedule A
- Using a carryforward older than 5 years

## Prompt
- I contributed to an Alabama economic development organization.
- How does the Growing Alabama credit work?
