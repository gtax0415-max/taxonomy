# Rural Hospital Investment Tax Credit
## Description
NEW for 2026. Nonrefundable credit for donations to eligible rural Alabama hospitals. Code of Alabama §§40-18-550 to -562 (Act 2025-404, amended by Act 2026-34). The 2026 Schedule OC is not yet published, so the Part letter is not known.

## Amounts
- 100% of the donation
- Individual cap: $15,000 (single, head of family, married filing separately); $30,000 married filing jointly
- Pass-through entities: up to $450,000
- Limited to the tax owed; no percentage-of-tax limit for individuals
- Statewide cap: $20 million for 2026 ($25 million for 2027, $30 million after); the 2026 cap was reserved within about the first month
- Per-hospital cap: $750,000 for 2026
- Tax years 2026–2028

## Notes
- Must RESERVE in My Alabama Taxes first, donate within 30 days, and the hospital certifies receipt within 60 days
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years
- Not transferable
- Cannot also deduct the donation as a charitable gift

## Required Information
- Reservation confirmation
- Donation amount and date
- Hospital name

## Questions
- Did you reserve a Rural Hospital Investment credit for 2026?
- Did you donate within 30 days of the reservation?
- Did you also deduct the gift on Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Donating without a reservation (the 2026 cap is already reached)
- Claiming more than $15,000 / $30,000
- Deducting the same donation on Schedule A

## Prompt
- I donated to a rural hospital in Alabama. Is there a credit?
