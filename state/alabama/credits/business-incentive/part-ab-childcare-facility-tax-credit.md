# Childcare Facility Tax Credit
## Description
REFUNDABLE credit for for-profit licensed childcare providers that serve children in Alabama's Childcare Subsidy Program. Code of Alabama §§40-18-540 to -546 (Act 2024-303). Not the same as a credit for parents' childcare costs; Alabama has no such individual credit.

## Amounts
Per eligible child (average monthly count), by Quality STARS rating:
- 5 stars: $2,000
- 4 stars: $1,750
- 3 stars: $1,500
- 2 stars: $1,250
- 1 star: $1,000

Cap: $25,000 per facility. Program cap: $5 million a year, 25% reserved for rural areas.

## Eligibility
- For-profit provider licensed by DHR and participating in Quality STARS
- Children age 5 or under enrolled in the Childcare Subsidy Program

## 2026 status
- Available for tax years 2025–2027; unchanged for 2026
- 2026 reservations opened January 5, 2026, first come, first served
- Year-end claim due to ALDOR by March 1

## Notes
- Any amount not used against tax is refunded through Section F
- No carryforward; not transferable except pro rata to pass-through owners
- Must reserve in MAT and get ALDOR approval before claiming on the return

## Required Information
- Certificate amount
- Star rating and average monthly count of eligible children
- Reservation confirmation

## Questions
- Do you own a for-profit licensed childcare center in Alabama?
- What is its Quality STARS rating?
- How many subsidized children age 5 or under do you serve?
- Did you reserve the credit in MAT?

## Common Errors
- Parents trying to claim it for their own childcare costs
- Claiming without a reservation or certificate
- Exceeding the $25,000 per-facility cap
- Claiming as a nonprofit provider

## Prompt
- I own a childcare center in Alabama.
- Is there an Alabama credit for childcare?
