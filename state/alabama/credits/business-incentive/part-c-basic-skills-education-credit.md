# Basic Skills Education Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that pay for an approved basic skills education program for their Alabama employees. Code of Alabama §§40-18-135 to -139 (Act 93-907).

## Amount
- 20% of actual education costs: instructors, materials, equipment, textbooks, and employee wages paid while attending
- Limited to the employer's Alabama income tax
- No dollar cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Program APPROVED IN WRITING IN ADVANCE by the State Department of Education
- Teaches basic skills through a 12th-grade level, including English as a second language
- Employees are Alabama residents, work at least 24 hours a week, and have been continuously employed at least 16 weeks

## Notes
- No carryforward and no carryback. Credit above the tax is lost
- No credit for any cost that was reimbursed
- The certificate number is entered on line C1

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount
- Program costs by category
- Employee eligibility (residency, hours, length of employment)

## Questions
- Did you pay for a basic skills or GED program for your employees?
- Was the program approved before it started?
- Do you have the Workplace Education Tax Credit Certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming costs for a program that wasn't pre-approved
- Including reimbursed costs
- Including employees who don't meet the hours or tenure test
- Expecting to carry the unused credit forward

## Prompt
- I paid for GED classes for my employees.
- My business ran an English-language program for workers.
