# Innovating Alabama Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for cash contributions to an economic development organization (EDO) for a project approved by Innovate Alabama, such as technology accelerators and support for small innovative companies. Code of Alabama §§41-10-840 to -847 (Act 2023-33, amended by Act 2024-441). NOT a research and development credit.

## Amounts
- 100% of the contribution, up to the amount reserved
- Limited to 50% of the tax owed
- Program cap: $25 million a year, at least $10 million for innovative-company projects
- No per-taxpayer cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
- Reserve with ALDOR within 30 days of the window opening; contribute within 21 days; the EDO confirms receipt to ALDOR
- No credits for projects not approved by July 31, 2028
- Not transferable; passes through to S corporation and partnership owners
- Cannot also deduct the contribution

## Required Information
- Reservation confirmation and amount
- Contribution date and amount
- Alabama tax liability (for the 50% limit)
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Did you reserve an Innovating Alabama credit before contributing?
- Did you contribute within 21 days?
- Did you also deduct the contribution?

## Common Errors
- Treating it as an R&D credit
- Contributing before reserving or after the 21-day window
- Exceeding 50% of tax liability
- Deducting the same contribution

## Prompt
- I contributed to an Innovate Alabama project.
