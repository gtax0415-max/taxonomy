# Railroad Modernization Act of 2019
## Description
Credit for Class II and Class III railroads that rehabilitate track in Alabama. Code of Alabama §§37-11C-1 to -6 (Act 2019-459, renewed by Act 2022-341). For an individual on Form 40 this almost always arises as a PURCHASED (transferred) credit.

## Amounts
- 50% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures
- Cap: $4,100 per mile of track for 2023–2027
- Program cap: $4.5 million a year
- Unchanged for 2026

## 2026 status
- The credit ends after tax year 2027. 2026 HB190, which would have extended it to 2032, did not pass

## Notes
- Refundable only to the railroad itself. A transferee gets no refund and no carryforward, so for an individual it is nonrefundable and any excess is lost
- Part T is NOT among the Section F refundable Parts
- Transferable once at no less than 85% of present value
- Pass-through railroads claim at the entity level; the credit does not pass to owners

## Required Information
- Certificate or transfer certificate and amount
- Purchase price, if transferred

## Questions
- Did you purchase an Alabama railroad modernization credit?
- Do you own a Class II or III railroad?

## Common Errors
- Claiming as an owner of a pass-through railroad
- Expecting a refund or carryforward on a transferred credit
- Using the old $3,500 per-mile cap

## Prompt
- I bought an Alabama railroad tax credit.
