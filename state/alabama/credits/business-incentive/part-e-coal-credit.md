# Coal Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for corporations that produce Alabama-mined coal above a base-year level. Code of Alabama §§40-18-220 to -222, amended by Act 2023-546. An INDIVIDUAL gets it only as an allocation from an S corporation or other producing entity.

## Amount
- $1 per ton of Alabama-mined coal produced above the base year
- Base year: 1994, or the first 12 consecutive months of production for a producer that wasn't mining in 1994
- No dollar cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years, for credits arising in tax year 2023 and later
- A pass-through entity may allocate the credit among owners in any proportion, not only by ownership
- Not transferable or saleable
- The producer must be precertified and file the allocation schedule in MAT; ALDOR then issues the letter to each allocatee
- Repealed December 31, 2028 unless extended; credits awarded before then are preserved

## Required Information
- ALDOR allocation letter and amount
- K-1 showing the allocated share
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Are you an owner of a company that mines coal in Alabama?
- Did the company allocate a coal credit to you?
- Do you have a coal credit carryforward from 2023 or later?

## Common Errors
- An individual claiming the credit directly rather than through an allocation
- Claiming before the entity files its allocation in MAT
- Carrying forward a pre-2023 credit, which had no carryforward

## Prompt
- My S corporation mines coal in Alabama.
- My K-1 shows an Alabama coal credit.
