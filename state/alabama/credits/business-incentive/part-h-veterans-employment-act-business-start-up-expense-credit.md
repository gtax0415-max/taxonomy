# Veterans Employment Act – Business Start-up Expense Credit
## Description
One-time nonrefundable credit for a recently discharged, unemployed veteran who starts an Alabama business. Code of Alabama §40-18-323.

## Amount
- Up to $2,000 of start-up expenses
- One business per veteran, one time

## Eligibility
- Honorable or general discharge within 2 years before the business start date
- Unemployment status certified by the Alabama Department of Workforce
- Business started after April 2, 2012, located in Alabama, and at least 50% owned by the veteran
- Business has at least $3,000 of net profit in the year the credit is claimed

## 2026 status
- §40-18-324(c) bars credits under Act 2018-194 for any unemployed veteran hired after December 31, 2023. Its application to the start-up credit is unclear; confirm with ALDOR before claiming for a business started after 2023
- The article is repealed December 31, 2028 (Act 2023-546)

## Notes
- No carryforward
- Cannot also be used with the Part G employer credit for the same veteran

## Required Information
- Discharge date and type
- Business start date and ownership percentage
- Start-up expenses
- Business net profit for the year

## Questions
- When were you discharged, and when did you start the business?
- Do you own at least half of the business?
- Did the business make at least $3,000 net profit this year?
- Were you certified as unemployed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming with a discharge more than 2 years before the start date
- Claiming in a year the business made less than $3,000
- Claiming more than once

## Prompt
- I'm a veteran and started a small business in Alabama.
