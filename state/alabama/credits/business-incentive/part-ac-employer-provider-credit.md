# Employer Provider Credit
## Description
REFUNDABLE version of the employer childcare credit for an employer that is ITSELF a qualifying childcare provider. Covers costs to operate, maintain or upgrade its own facility, or to pay for employees' childcare. Code of Alabama §40-18-541 (Act 2024-303).

## Amounts
- 100% of eligible expenses for an employer with fewer than 25 employees; 75% for others
- Cap: $600,000 per employer per year
- Shares the employer credit program cap: $17.5 million for 2026

## Eligibility
- For-profit childcare provider licensed by DHR and in Quality STARS
- Same employee and child rules as Part AA

## 2026 status
- Available for tax years 2025–2027
- Reservation in MAT required; claim due by March 1

## Notes
- Any amount not used against tax is refunded through Section F
- No carryforward
- Passes through pro rata to owners only
- Different from Part AB, which pays $1,000–$2,000 per subsidized child

## Required Information
- Certificate amount
- Expenses by category
- Reservation confirmation

## Questions
- Do you own a licensed childcare center that is also an employer?
- Did you reserve the credit in MAT?
- Are you also claiming Part AB for the same facility?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the same expenses on both Part AA and Part AC
- Confusing Part AC with the per-child Part AB credit
- Claiming without a reservation

## Prompt
- I run a childcare center and pay for my employees' childcare.
