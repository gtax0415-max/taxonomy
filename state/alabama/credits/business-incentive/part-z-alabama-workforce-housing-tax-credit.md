# Alabama Workforce Housing Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for owners of affordable rental housing projects that also receive the federal low-income housing tax credit. Code of Alabama §§40-18-530 to -535 (Act 2024-302). Individuals usually receive it through a partnership.

## Amounts
- Up to $2 million per project per year, for a credit period of up to 10 years set by the AHFA certificate
- Program cap: $5 million per award cycle, plus unawarded or recaptured amounts
- 20–25% of each cycle reserved for rural areas
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Federal LIHTC project, mainly 4% bond-financed deals (9% projects only for the rural set-aside)
- Final AHFA certificate issued before any allocation to owners

## 2026 status
- Available for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- AHFA can award credits through September 30, 2027 unless extended; awarded credits continue for their full period

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years
- Not transferable; a pass-through entity allocates to owners through MAT with ALDOR approval
- Lines Z1 and Z2 need the project name and number

## Required Information
- Project name and number
- Annual credit and allocated share
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in an Alabama affordable housing project?
- Did the partnership allocate a Workforce Housing credit to you?
- Has AHFA issued the final eligibility certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the final AHFA certificate
- Missing the project name and number
- Using a carryforward older than 5 years

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows an Alabama workforce housing credit.
