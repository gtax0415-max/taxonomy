# Veterans Employment Act – Employer Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a small employer that hires an unemployed or combat veteran into a full-time Alabama job. Code of Alabama §§40-18-320 to -325 (formerly "Heroes for Hire"). For 2026 it is LARGELY CLOSED: the statute bars the credit for any unemployed veteran hired after December 31, 2023, and no extension has been enacted.

## Amount
- $2,000 per qualifying veteran hired after March 12, 2018
- Claimed in the year the veteran completes 12 consecutive months of employment

## Eligibility
- Employer qualifies under the Full Employment Act (50 or fewer employees, net job increase); the hire is also counted on Part F
- Job is full-time, pays at least $14 per hour, and is mostly performed in Alabama
- Veteran's status verified by the Alabama Department of Workforce before the credit is claimed

## 2026 status
- §40-18-324(c): no credit for an unemployed veteran HIRED AFTER DECEMBER 31, 2023 unless extended by the Legislature. No extension found through the 2026 session
- A veteran hired in late 2023 who completes 12 months in 2024 or 2025 was claimed then, so a NEW 2026 claim for an unemployed veteran is unlikely to qualify
- Whether the cutoff also applies to combat-veteran hires is unclear
- The whole article is repealed December 31, 2028 (Act 2023-546)

## Notes
- No carryforward
- Cannot be combined with the Part H start-up credit for the same veteran

## Required Information
- Veteran's hire date and discharge documentation
- Hourly wage and hours
- Department of Workforce verification
- Date 12 months of employment were completed

## Questions
- When was the veteran hired?
- Is the job full-time at $14 an hour or more?
- Did the veteran complete 12 months of employment this year?
- Does the business qualify under the Full Employment Act?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a veteran hired after December 31, 2023
- Claiming before 12 months of employment are completed
- Claiming for a part-time job or a wage under $14
- Claiming both Part G and Part H for the same veteran

## Prompt
- I hired a veteran at my business.
- Can I still get the Alabama Heroes for Hire credit?
