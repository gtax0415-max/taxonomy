# Employer Tax Credit (Childcare)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for for-profit employers that pay for their employees' childcare. Code of Alabama §§40-18-540 to -546 (Act 2024-303). A childcare provider claiming as an employer uses Part AC instead, which is refundable.

## Amounts
- 100% of eligible childcare expenses for an employer with fewer than 25 employees; 75% for others
- Cap: $600,000 per employer per year
- Program cap: $17.5 million for 2026 (up from $15 million for 2025; $20 million for 2027)
- 25% of the cap reserved for small or rural employers until July 1

## Eligibility
- For-profit business operating in Alabama
- Prioritizes employees who qualify for the earned income tax credit
- Covered employees are Alabama residents earning $80,000 or less (excluding overtime and bonuses), with children age 5 or under
- Facility licensed by DHR and in Quality STARS, or run by a public college or university

## 2026 status
- Available for tax years 2025–2027
- 2026 reservations opened January 5, 2026
- Year-end claim due in MAT by March 1

## Notes
- No carryforward
- Claimed in the year the expenses are incurred
- Passes through pro rata to owners; otherwise not transferable
- A reservation does not guarantee the credit

## Required Information
- Reservation confirmation
- Eligible expenses by employee
- Employee count (for the 100% rate)
- Certificate amount

## Questions
- Does your business pay for employees' childcare?
- Do you have fewer than 25 employees?
- Did you reserve the credit in MAT?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a reservation
- Covering employees earning over $80,000
- Using a facility that isn't licensed or in Quality STARS
- Exceeding $600,000

## Prompt
- My business pays for employees' daycare.
