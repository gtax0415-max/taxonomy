# Full Employment Act of 2011 Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a small Alabama employer that creates a new full-time job. Code of Alabama §§40-18-290 to -294 (Act 2011-551). Sole proprietors claim it directly; partners and S corporation shareholders claim their share.

## Amount
- $1,000, one time, for each new full-time employee
- No program cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Business existed on June 9, 2011 with 50 or FEWER employees on that date
- Employee hired after June 9, 2011 at MORE than $10 per hour
- Claimed in the year the employee completes 12 consecutive months of employment
- Full-time Alabama employees at year-end must exceed last year-end's count by the number of new hires claimed this year plus one for each employee credited in earlier years
- Pre-certified by ALDOR in My Alabama Taxes (MAT)

## 2026 status
- Repealed December 31, 2028 (Act 2023-546), so available for 2026

## Notes
- No carryforward; credit above the tax is lost
- An employer claiming the Part G veterans credit must also qualify here; the veteran is counted on Part F too

## Required Information
- Hire date and hourly wage of each new employee
- Date each completed 12 months
- Year-end headcounts
- Employee count on June 9, 2011

## Questions
- Did your business have 50 or fewer employees on June 9, 2011?
- Did a new full-time employee paid more than $10 an hour complete 12 months this year?
- Did your total full-time headcount go up?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a business started after June 9, 2011
- Claiming before the employee completes 12 months
- Headcount falling below the required increase because of earlier credited hires
- Skipping MAT pre-certification

## Prompt
- My small business hired new full-time employees.
