# Preceptor Tax Incentive Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for community-based health professionals who supervise students, without pay, in medically underserved rural Alabama areas. Code of Alabama §§40-18-520 to -527 (Act 2023-519).

## Amounts
| Preceptor | Per rotation | Annual maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Physician (MD/DO), dentist, optometrist | $500 | $6,000 |
| Physician assistant | $425 | $5,100 |
| Advanced practice nurse | $425 | $5,100 |

- Up to 12 rotations a year
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Preceptorship at an Alabama facility not owned or operated by the school, under an agreement with a qualifying Alabama school
- UNCOMPENSATED: preceptor and facility certify no payment from any source
- Rotation in a medically underserved rural area
- AHEC certificate pre-certified in MAT

## 2026 status
- Program repealed September 29, 2031 unless extended
- ALDOR and the Act start the credit in tax year 2024; the 2025 Schedule OC instructions say 2025

## Notes
- No carryforward; cannot reduce tax below zero
- Not transferable

## Required Information
- Profession and number of rotations
- AHEC certificate amount
- School and facility names

## Questions
- Did you supervise health professions students this year?
- Were you paid for precepting?
- Was the rotation in a rural underserved area?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a paid preceptorship
- Claiming more than 12 rotations
- Using the physician rate for a PA or nurse practitioner
- Precepting at a school-owned facility

## Prompt
- I precept medical students at my rural clinic.
