# Rural Physician Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a physician who lives and practices in a small rural Alabama community with a qualifying hospital. Code of Alabama §§40-18-130 to -133 (Act 93-313). No certificate is issued; the physician documents eligibility directly.

## Amounts
- $5,000 per year, for up to 5 consecutive tax years
- $10,000 on a joint return if BOTH spouses qualify
- Limited to the tax owed; no program cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Physician lives AND practices in a community of under 25,000 people (latest decennial census) that has a hospital with an emergency room
- Admitting privileges at a small or rural hospital: an acute-care hospital with fewer than 105 beds more than 20 miles from another Alabama acute-care hospital, or one receiving Medicare rural reimbursement
- Physicians already practicing in a rural community on May 4, 1993 are excluded. A physician who practiced rurally before can qualify again only after 3 years practicing in an urban community

## 2026 status
- Repealed December 31, 2028 (Act 2023-546) unless extended, so available for 2026
- Bills to raise the credit to $10,000 (2025 HB46, 2026 HB115) did not pass

## Notes
- No carryforward; credit above the tax is lost
- Residence is tested on its own: a home outside the city limits of a qualifying community has been denied (Tax Tribunal No. 1724)
- Populations of nearby places cannot be combined
- Outpatient clinics do not meet the hospital test; a partial year of rural residence has been denied

## Required Information
- Community of residence and practice, and its population
- Hospital name, bed count and distance to the nearest acute-care hospital
- Years the credit has already been claimed
- Whether the spouse also qualifies

## Questions
- Are you a physician living and practicing in an Alabama town of under 25,000?
- Do you have admitting privileges at a small rural hospital with an emergency room?
- How many years have you already claimed this credit?
- Were you practicing in a rural community on May 4, 1993?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for more than 5 years
- Living outside the qualifying community's limits
- Claiming when the hospital has 105 or more beds
- Claiming $10,000 when only one spouse qualifies

## Prompt
- I'm a doctor practicing in a small Alabama town.
