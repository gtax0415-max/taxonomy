# Adoption Credit Worksheet (Schedule AAC)
## Description
Schedule AAC lists each adoption that became final during the year and figures the Alabama Adoption Tax Credit carried to Schedule OC, Part M. The credit is REFUNDABLE and one-time per child. Code of Alabama §§40-18-360 and -361 (amended by 2022 HB487). See part-m-alabama-adoption-tax-credit.md for the credit rules.

## How the schedule works
- Part I: adoptees who are Alabama residents (private adoptions, or qualified foster children in Alabama DHR custody)
- Part II: out-of-state or foreign adoptions, or children who are not Alabama residents
- Each row: child's name, effective date of adoption, SSN, birth mother's name, agency name and agency address
- Part III, line 1: number of Part I children × $2,000
- Part III, line 2: number of Part II children × $1,000
- Part III, line 3: line 1 + line 2, carried to Schedule OC, Part M

## Amounts (tax years 2023–2027, so 2026)
- $2,000 per Part I child
- $1,000 per Part II child
- From 2028 the credit reverts to $1,000

## Notes
- Only adoptions that became FINAL during the tax year
- Adoptive parents must be Alabama residents
- Adopting a spouse's child does not qualify
- Separate from the federal adoption credit (Form 8839); the Alabama amount is flat, not based on expenses

## Example
A child adopted from Alabama foster care, final in 2026: Part I, line 1 = $2,000. Against $1,200 of tax, $1,200 is applied and $800 is refunded.

## Required Information
- Each child's name, SSN and adoption date
- Birth mother's name
- Agency name and address
- Whether the child was an Alabama resident or in DHR custody

## Questions
- Did you finalize an adoption in 2026?
- Was the child an Alabama resident or in Alabama foster care?
- Was the child your spouse's child?

## Common Errors
- Entering the federal credit amount instead of the flat Alabama amount
- Listing a non-Alabama adoption in Part I
- Listing an adoption that is not yet final

## Prompt
- How much is the Alabama adoption credit?
- We adopted a child from Alabama foster care this year.
