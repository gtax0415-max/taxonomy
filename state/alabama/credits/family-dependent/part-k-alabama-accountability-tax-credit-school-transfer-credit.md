# Alabama Accountability Act – School Transfer Credit
## Description
REFUNDABLE credit for parents who move a child out of an assigned priority (formerly "failing") public school to a nonpriority public school or a private school. Code of Alabama §16-6D-8 (Act 2013-64, amended through Act 2023-418).

## Amount
The LESSER of:
1. 80% of the average annual state cost of a public K-12 student
2. Actual tuition and required fees

The 2025 figure was $5,198 per student. The 2026 figure will appear on the 2026 Schedule AATC; it is recalculated each year.

## Eligibility
- Student attended, was assigned to, or was notified of assignment to a priority school
- Only grades the child would have attended at the priority school count
- Parent must first seek a transfer within the same local school system
- Students in the Department of Youth Services school district are excluded

## 2026 status
- Still available for 2026. The CHOOSE Act (Act 2024-21) did not repeal it
- A student cannot receive both this credit and a CHOOSE Act education savings account

## Notes
- Any amount not used against tax is refunded through Section F
- Not transferable
- Application must be filed within the refund-claim period

## Required Information
- Priority school name and assignment notice
- New school, tuition and required fees
- Student's grade and enrollment dates

## Questions
- Was your child assigned to a priority school?
- Did you move your child to another school?
- Did your child also receive CHOOSE Act funds?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than tuition and fees, or more than the per-student limit
- Claiming grades not offered at the priority school
- Claiming alongside a CHOOSE Act account for the same student
- Using last year's per-student amount

## Prompt
- We moved our child out of a failing school.
