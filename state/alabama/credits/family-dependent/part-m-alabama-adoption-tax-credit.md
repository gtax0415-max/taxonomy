# Alabama Adoption Tax Credit
## Description
Fully REFUNDABLE, one-time credit per child for Alabama residents who finalize an adoption. Code of Alabama §§40-18-360 and -361; made refundable by Act 2022-299. Unlike the federal adoption credit, it is a flat amount, not a percentage of expenses.

## Amounts (tax years 2023–2027, so 2026)
- $2,000 per child: private adoption or adoption of a qualified foster child, where the child is an Alabama resident (Schedule AAC, Part I)
- $1,000 per child: out-of-state or foreign adoption, or a child who is not an Alabama resident (Schedule AAC, Part II)
- From 2028 the amount reverts to $1,000

## Eligibility
- Adoptive parents are Alabama residents
- Claimed in the year the adoption becomes FINAL
- Qualified foster child: in the permanent legal custody of the Alabama Department of Human Resources, or placed by DHR in an approved foster home for adoption

## What does NOT qualify
- Adopting your spouse's child
- Adopting a child of either parent

## Notes
- Any amount not used against tax is refunded through Section F to Form 40, line 25
- No carryforward needed, since the excess is refunded
- Separate from, and in addition to, the federal adoption credit on Form 8839

## Required Information
- Date the adoption became final
- Child's name and SSN
- Birth mother's name
- Agency name and address
- Whether the child was an Alabama resident or in DHR custody

## Questions
- Did your adoption become final this year?
- Was the child an Alabama resident, or adopted from foster care in Alabama?
- Is the child your spouse's child?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in the year the process started instead of the year it became final
- Claiming $2,000 for a non-Alabama or foreign adoption
- Claiming a stepchild adoption
- Treating the credit as nonrefundable and losing the excess

## Prompt
- We finalized an adoption in Alabama this year.
- We adopted a child from foster care.
- We adopted a child from another country.
