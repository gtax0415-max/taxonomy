# Storm Shelter Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for building or installing a storm shelter at your primary residence. Code of Alabama §40-18-111 (Act 2021-540), extended by Act 2024-223.

## Amounts
- The LESSER of $3,000 or 50% of the cost, net of any reimbursement or grant
- Program cap: $2 million a year statewide (first come, first served through AEMA)
- Unchanged for 2026

## 2026 status
- Extended through tax year 2028 by Act 2024-223, so it is available for 2026
- Claimed in the year the AEMA certificate is RECEIVED, not necessarily the year built

## Eligibility
- Shelter at the taxpayer's primary residence
- Rated for an EF5 tornado and meets FEMA safe-room criteria
- Only one shelter per lot

## Notes
- Not refundable, not transferable, no carryforward. It cannot reduce tax below zero
- Claim under the name shown on the certificate
- Pre-certify in MAT

## Required Information
- AEMA certificate and amount
- Total cost and any reimbursement or grant
- Date the certificate was received

## Questions
- Did you install a storm shelter at your home?
- Do you have the AEMA certificate, and when did you receive it?
- Did you receive any grant or reimbursement?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 50% of cost above $3,000
- Not reducing cost by a grant or reimbursement
- Claiming in the build year instead of the certificate year
- Claiming for a second home or rental

## Prompt
- I installed a tornado shelter at my house.
