# 2013 Alabama Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rehabilitating historic buildings under the original 2013 program. Code of Alabama §§40-9F-1 to -8 (Act 2013-241, amended by Act 2014-452). The program CLOSED to new projects in 2016; on a 2026 return this is almost always a CARRYFORWARD.

## Amounts
- 25% of qualified rehabilitation expenditures for a certified historic structure; 10% for a pre-1936 non-historic structure
- Caps: $5,000,000 per project; $50,000 for a certified historic residence
- Full credit taken in the placed-in-service year

## 2026 status
- Only projects with an AHC reservation from 2013–2015 qualify
- Unused credit carries forward 10 years, so the last carryforwards can still be used in 2026
- No new credits

## Notes
- Not refundable
- Transferable once with ALDOR approval
- The certificate must be attached; pass-through owners attach the Recipient certificate
- Projects from 2018 onward use the 2017 program (Part S), which is refundable

## Required Information
- Certificate amount and year placed in service
- Credit used in each prior year
- Remaining carryforward

## Questions
- Do you have an Alabama Historical Commission certificate from the 2013 program?
- When was the project placed in service?
- How much of the credit is left?

## Common Errors
- Confusing the 2013 program (Part N) with the 2017 program (Part S)
- Using a carryforward more than 10 years old
- Not attaching the certificate
- Treating it as refundable

## Prompt
- I still have a historic rehabilitation credit carryforward.
