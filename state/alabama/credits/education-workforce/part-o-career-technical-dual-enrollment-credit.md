# Career-Technical Dual Enrollment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to the Alabama Community College System to fund career-technical dual enrollment for high school students. Code of Alabama §§16-60-350 to -354 (Act 2014-147, Alabama Future Workforce Initiative).

## Amounts
- 50% of the contribution
- Limited to 50% of Alabama tax liability and $500,000 per year
- Program cap: $5 million a year statewide
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years
- The contribution must be made (postmarked) by the last day of the tax year
- Cannot also deduct the same contribution as a charitable gift
- A person claimed as a dependent cannot claim it
- 2026 HB517 (TRAIN Act) creates a separate credit and does not change this one

## Required Information
- Certificate or receipt amount and date
- Alabama tax liability (for the 50% limit)
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Did you contribute to the Alabama Community College System for dual enrollment?
- When was the contribution made?
- Did you also deduct it on Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the full contribution instead of 50%
- Exceeding 50% of tax liability
- Double-dipping with an itemized deduction
- Using a carryforward older than 3 years

## Prompt
- I donated to the Alabama dual enrollment program.
