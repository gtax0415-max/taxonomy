# Alabama Accountability Act – Scholarship Granting Organization Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to an approved scholarship granting organization (SGO). Code of Alabama §16-6D-9 (amended through Act 2023-418).

## Amounts
- 100% of the contribution
- Limited to 100% of the tax owed (raised from 50% by Act 2022-390)
- Maximum $100,000 per individual or married couple filing jointly
- Program cap: $40 million a year (raised from $30 million by Act 2023-418), rising automatically if demand stays high
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years
- Must RESERVE the credit in MAT before contributing
- SGO must be on ALDOR's approved list
- Cannot also deduct the contribution as a charitable gift
- Not transferable; passes through to S corporation and partnership owners
- Separate from the new federal SGO credit, which starts with 2027 contributions

## Required Information
- Reservation approval and amount
- Contribution date and amount
- SGO name
- Carryforward amounts by year

## Questions
- Did you reserve the credit before contributing?
- Is the SGO approved by ALDOR?
- Did you also deduct the gift on Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Contributing without a reservation
- Using the old 50%-of-tax limit
- Exceeding $100,000 on a joint return
- Deducting the same contribution
- Using a carryforward older than 3 years

## Prompt
- I donated to an Alabama scholarship granting organization.
