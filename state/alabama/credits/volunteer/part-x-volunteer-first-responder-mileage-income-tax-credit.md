# Volunteer First Responder Mileage Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for volunteer firefighters and rescue squad members for miles driven in a personal vehicle on fire, emergency or rescue calls. Act 2023-510, Code of Alabama §40-18-19.3.

## Amount
- Unreimbursed miles driven on calls × the mileage rate
- The Act uses the Alabama state-employee mileage rate (§36-7-22), which follows the IRS business rate. For 2026 that rate is 72.5 cents for January–June and 76 cents for July–December (the IRS raised it mid-year). ALDOR has not yet said whether it will split the year or use one rate; confirm when the 2026 instructions are released
- No dollar cap and no program cap

## Eligibility
- Member of a volunteer fire department certified by the Alabama Forestry Commission, or of an Alabama volunteer rescue squad
- Miles driven in a personal vehicle and not reimbursed
- Certificate and log pre-certified in MAT before filing

## Notes
- No carryforward; limited to the tax owed
- Miles used for this credit cannot also be deducted
- One certificate and log per department
- Filed under the SSN of the certificate holder, even on a joint return
- Different from the Part V training credit; a volunteer may qualify for both

## Required Information
- Total unreimbursed miles to calls
- Department name and supervisor
- Mileage rate for the year

## Questions
- Do you volunteer with a fire department or rescue squad?
- Did you drive your own vehicle to calls?
- Were you reimbursed for any miles?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 rate (70 cents) or one rate for all of 2026 without checking ALDOR's instructions
- Including reimbursed miles
- Deducting the same miles elsewhere
- Missing the department-signed certificate or log

## Prompt
- I drive my own car to fire calls as a volunteer.
