# Volunteer Emergency Responders Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for certified volunteer firefighters and EMTs who complete required training. Code of Alabama §§40-18-480 and -481 (Act 2022-298).

## Amounts
- $300: certified volunteer firefighter or licensed EMT-Basic
- $600: Firefighter II, Advanced EMT or EMT-Paramedic
- Certifications cannot be combined; take the higher one
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Active member of a volunteer or combination fire department, or a qualifying rescue squad
- At least 30 hours of approved training during the year
- Pre-certified in MAT each year with the signed certificate

## 2026 status
- Available for tax years 2023–2027
- 2026 HB278, which would have made it permanent and changed the amounts from 2027, was not enacted

## Notes
- No carryforward
- Each spouse files separately for pre-certification
- A responder who signs as their own supervisor must show proof of heading the department
- Different from the Part X mileage credit; a volunteer may qualify for both

## Required Information
- Certification level
- Training hours
- Department name and supervisor

## Questions
- Are you a volunteer firefighter or EMT?
- What is your certification level?
- Did you complete 30 hours of training this year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming $600 at the basic certification level
- Claiming without 30 training hours
- Claiming without the signed certificate pre-certified in MAT

## Prompt
- I'm a volunteer firefighter in Alabama.
