# Estimated Tax and Extension Payments
## Description
Total of 2026 estimated tax payments, any 2025 overpayment applied to 2026, and any payment made with the automatic extension.

## Who must pay estimates
- Expected tax of $500 or more after withholding and credits, AND
- Withholding plus credits will be less than the smaller of 90% of the 2026 tax or 100% of the 2025 tax (110% if 2025 Alabama AGI was over $150,000, or $75,000 married filing separately)

## Due dates for 2026
- April 15, 2026
- June 15, 2026
- September 15, 2026
- January 15, 2027

## Farmers
- At least two-thirds of gross income from farming: may pay once by January 15, 2027, or skip estimates by filing and paying in full by March 1, 2027

## Extension
- The 6-month extension to October 15, 2027 is automatic, but it does NOT extend the time to pay
- Pay the balance by April 15, 2027 with Form 40V ("Automatic Extension Payment") or in My Alabama Taxes; it goes on line 23

## Notes
- ALDOR does not confirm estimated payments, so the taxpayer's records are the only proof
- Underpayment penalty is figured on Form 2210AL and reported on Schedule ATP and line 31

## Example
Four payments of $900 plus $500 applied from 2025: line 23 is $4,100.

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each 2026 estimate
- 2025 overpayment applied
- Extension payment

## Questions
- What estimated payments did you make for 2026, and when?
- Did you apply any 2025 refund to 2026?
- Did you pay with an extension?

## Common Errors
- Including the balance paid with the 2025 return
- Including a 2025 overpayment that was refunded rather than applied
- Missing a January payment made for 2026

## Prompt
- Where do Alabama estimated payments go?
