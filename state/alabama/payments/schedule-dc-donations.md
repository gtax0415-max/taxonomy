# Donation Check-offs (Schedule DC)
## Description
Taxpayers may donate all or part of an overpayment to state funds. Donations reduce the refund and cannot be reversed.

## Funds (2025 list; confirm on the 2026 form)
- 1a Senior Services Trust Fund
- 1b Alabama Arts Development Fund
- 1c Alabama Nongame Wildlife Fund
- 1d Child Abuse Trust Fund
- 1e Alabama Veterans Program
- 1f Foster Care Trust Fund
- 1g Mental Health
- 1h Alabama Breast and Cervical Cancer Program
- 1i Alabama Military Support Foundation
- 1j Alabama Veterinary Medical Foundation Spay-Neuter Program
- 1k Cancer Research Institute
- 1l Children First Trust Fund
- 1m State Parks Division, Department of Conservation and Natural Resources
- 1n Department of Mental Health – 2023
- 1o Alabama Medicaid Agency

## Notes
- Line 2 total → Form 40, line 34
- Donations come only from an overpayment; they cannot be made when tax is owed
- If ALDOR later corrects the return, the donation cannot be used to pay additional tax
- Donations can be deducted as charitable gifts on the next year's Alabama Schedule A
- 2026 HB 438 (a VOCAL check-off) died in the Senate, so the 2026 list should match 2025
- Separate from the $1/$2 Alabama Election Campaign Fund on Form 40, lines 20a/20b, which adds to tax

## Example
$25 to the Alabama Nongame Wildlife Fund from a $500 overpayment: refund is $475.

## Required Information
- Funds and amounts chosen
- Overpayment amount

## Questions
- Do you want to donate part of your refund?
- To which funds?

## Common Errors
- Donating more than the overpayment
- Trying to donate when tax is owed
- Forgetting to deduct last year's donations

## Prompt
- Which Alabama funds can I donate my refund to?
