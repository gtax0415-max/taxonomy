# Payments from Schedule CP
## Description
Refundable credit for Alabama income tax that a partnership, S corporation, estate or trust paid on the owner's behalf. For a full-year resident this is mainly the ELECTING PASS-THROUGH ENTITY (PTE) tax credit. Code of Alabama §40-18-24.5 (Act 2021-423).

## How it works
- An electing PTE pays Alabama tax at the entity level (5% of each owner's share); the owner gets a refundable credit for their share
- Composite payments made on Form PTE-C for nonresident owners also go on Schedule CP; they mainly affect Form 40NR filers
- Schedule CP, Section A lists each entity: owner's SSN as shown on the K-1, entity name and FEIN, and amount paid (column G)
- Section B, line 1 totals the payments, carried to Form 40, line 26

## 2026 status
- Unchanged for 2026; no sunset
- The entity must check the "Electing PTE" box on a timely filed Form 65 or 20S each year (since tax year 2025)

## Notes
- The owner must still report their share of the entity's income
- It is a payment line, not a Schedule OC credit

## Required Information
- Each entity's name and FEIN
- Amount of tax paid on the owner's behalf

## Questions
- Did a partnership or S corporation you own elect to pay Alabama tax at the entity level?
- Does your K-1 show Alabama tax paid on your behalf?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the PTE tax on Schedule OC instead of Schedule CP
- Omitting the share of income while claiming the credit
- Using the wrong SSN or FEIN
