# Alabama Tax Withheld
## Description
Alabama income tax withheld from wages, retirement distributions, gambling winnings and other payments. It is a payment toward the tax and is refundable if it exceeds the tax.

## How it flows
- W-2 withholding: Schedule W-2, column G, line 16
- 1099 and W-2G withholding: Schedule W-2, line 17
- 1099-R withholding is also listed on Schedule RS, Part V (exempt and taxable distributions alike), then included on Schedule W-2, line 17
- Schedule W-2, line 18, column G → Form 40, line 5a → line 22

## Notes
- Only ALABAMA income tax; never federal, Social Security or Medicare tax, or another state's tax
- Withholding on an EXEMPT pension (Schedule RS Part I) is still claimed
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A W-2 with $2,140 in box 17: Schedule W-2 column G, then lines 5a and 22.

## Required Information
- Alabama withholding from every W-2, 1099-R, W-2G and other 1099

## Questions
- How much Alabama tax was withheld from each W-2, 1099 or W-2G?
- Was Alabama tax withheld from a pension?

## Common Errors
- Including federal or FICA withholding
- Omitting withholding on an exempt pension
- Counting 1099-R withholding twice (Schedule RS and Schedule W-2)

## Prompt
- Where does Alabama withholding go?
