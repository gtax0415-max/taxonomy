# Other Miscellaneous Deductions
## Description
Deductions from the list in the Schedule A instructions that are not subject to the 2% floor, chiefly GAMBLING LOSSES up to gambling winnings. Line 26 is for qualified long-term care insurance premiums.

## Gambling losses
- Deductible only up to gambling winnings reported as income (Code of Alabama §40-18-15, IRC §165(d); ALDOR Rule 810-3-17-.01)
- Alabama does NOT follow the federal 2026 rule limiting losses to 90%
- In Price v. ALDOR (Tax Tribunal, June 9, 2026), losses were allowed up to winnings; the older method of netting casino losses against gross winnings was rejected
- Records are required

## Long-term care premiums (line 26)
- Qualified long-term care insurance premiums (ALDOR Rule 810-3-15-.26), entered here rather than with medical expenses

## Notes
- Medical premiums do not go on line 25

## Example
Gambling losses of $5,000 against $12,400 of winnings: $5,000 on line 25.

## Required Information
- Gambling winnings and losses with records
- Long-term care premiums

## Questions
- Did you have gambling winnings and losses?
- Do you keep records of losses?
- Did you pay long-term care insurance premiums?

## Common Errors
- Deducting losses above winnings
- Applying the federal 90% limit
- Putting long-term care premiums on the medical line
