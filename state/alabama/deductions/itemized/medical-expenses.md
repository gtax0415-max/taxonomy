# Medical and Dental Expenses
## Description
Unreimbursed medical and dental expenses above 4% of Alabama AGI (Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(13)). The federal floor is 7.5%.

## Rules
- Line 1: expenses; line 2: Alabama AGI; line 3: 4% of line 2; line 4: deductible amount
- After-tax health insurance premiums qualify
- Lodging for medical care: up to $50 per night per person
- Unchanged for 2026

## What does NOT qualify
- Premiums paid pre-tax through a cafeteria plan
- Medicare Part A (basic) and the 1.45% Medicare tax withheld (that goes on line 6)
- Nonprescription drugs
- Long-term care premiums (go on line 26, not line 1)
- Reimbursed expenses

## Example
Medical bills of $37,008 with Alabama AGI of $66,560: $37,008 − $2,662 = $34,346.

## Required Information
- Medical expenses paid and reimbursements
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- What medical and dental expenses did you pay in 2026?
- Were any reimbursed?
- Were your premiums paid pre-tax?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal 7.5% floor
- Including pre-tax premiums
- Putting long-term care premiums on line 1

## Prompt
- How much medical can I deduct in Alabama?
