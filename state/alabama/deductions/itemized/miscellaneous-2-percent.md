# Job Expenses and Miscellaneous (2% Floor)
## Description
Unreimbursed employee expenses and other miscellaneous expenses above 2% of Alabama AGI. Still ALLOWED in Alabama (Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(23)), even though the federal return has suspended them permanently.

## Lines
- Line 20: unreimbursed employee expenses (job travel, union dues, job education, supplies)
- Line 21: other expenses (investment fees, tax preparation, safe deposit box)
- Line 22: total
- Line 23: 2% of Alabama AGI
- Line 24: line 22 minus line 23

## Notes
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
A teacher's $1,500 of unreimbursed supplies with Alabama AGI of $60,850: $1,500 − $1,217 = $283.

## Required Information
- Unreimbursed job expenses
- Other miscellaneous expenses
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- Did you pay job expenses your employer did not reimburse?
- Did you pay for tax preparation or investment advice?

## Common Errors
- Assuming Alabama follows the federal suspension
- Including reimbursed expenses
- Using federal AGI for the 2% floor
