# Interest You Paid
## Description
Home mortgage interest, points, qualified vehicle loan interest and investment interest. Personal interest (credit cards, personal loans) is not deductible.

## Lines
- Line 10a: mortgage interest and points reported on Form 1098
- Line 10b: mortgage interest not on a 1098 (give payee's name and address)
- Line 11: qualified vehicle loan interest (see vehicle-loan-interest.md)
- Line 12: points not reported on a 1098
- Line 13: investment interest (Form 4952A)
- Line 14: total

## 2026 changes
- Mortgage insurance premiums are again treated as mortgage interest; Alabama follows the federal rule (Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(2))
- Federal mortgage debt limits apply: $750,000 ($375,000 married filing separately)
- Mortgage insurance premiums phase out federally between $100,000 and $109,000 of AGI ($50,000–$54,500 married filing separately); Alabama figures limits on Alabama AGI

## Example
Mortgage interest of $12,600 on Form 1098: line 10a.

## Required Information
- Form 1098 amounts
- Mortgage insurance premiums
- Investment interest

## Questions
- Do you have a mortgage?
- Did you pay mortgage insurance premiums?
- Did you pay margin interest?

## Common Errors
- Deducting personal loan or credit card interest
- Leaving out mortgage insurance premiums for 2026

## Prompt
- Is mortgage interest deductible in Alabama?
