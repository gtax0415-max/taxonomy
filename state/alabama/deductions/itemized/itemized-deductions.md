# Itemized Deductions (Schedule A)
## Description
Alabama Schedule A totals itemized deductions. Alabama filers itemize far more often than federal filers because Social Security and Medicare (FICA) withheld are deductible, along with job expenses and federal-suspended items.

## Schedule A lines (2025 form)
- Lines 1–4: medical and dental (over 4% of Alabama AGI)
- Lines 5–9: taxes (real estate, FICA and SE tax, Railroad Retirement Tier 1, other)
- Lines 10a–14: interest (mortgage, vehicle loan, points, investment)
- Lines 15–18: gifts to charity
- Lines 19a–19c: casualty and theft losses
- Lines 20–24: job expenses and miscellaneous (over 2% of Alabama AGI)
- Line 25: other miscellaneous deductions
- Line 26: qualified long-term care insurance premiums
- Line 27: total → Form 40, line 11, box a

## Rules
- All limits use ALABAMA AGI (Form 40, line 10)
- Alabama does not follow the federal itemized deduction limit (IRC §68) that starts in 2026
- Married filing separately: both spouses use the same method unless they lived apart all year
- Part-year residents: only expenses paid while an Alabama resident
- A filer can switch from standard to itemized on an amended return

## Example
Joint wage earners with $10,404 of FICA and $12,600 of mortgage interest itemize ($23,004 exceeds the $5,000–$8,500 standard deduction).

## Required Information
- Expenses in each category

## Questions
- Do you want to itemize?
- How much FICA was withheld from your pay?

## Common Errors
- Taking the standard deduction when FICA alone exceeds it
- Using federal AGI for the floors
- Mixed methods on separate returns

- Should I itemize in Alabama?
