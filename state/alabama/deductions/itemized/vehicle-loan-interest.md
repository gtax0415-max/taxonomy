# Qualified Vehicle Loan Interest
## Description
Interest on a loan to buy a NEW personal vehicle assembled in the U.S. Alabama follows the federal deduction (IRC §163(h)(4)) but claims it as an ITEMIZED deduction, with the phase-out figured on Alabama AGI.

## Amount
- Up to $10,000 a year
- Reduced by $200 for each $1,000 (or part) of Alabama AGI over $100,000 ($200,000 married filing jointly)
- Loans for vehicles bought from January 1, 2025; tax years 2025–2028
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- New (original-use) vehicle for personal use; final assembly in the U.S.
- Loan secured by the vehicle
- A refinance qualifies if the balance does not increase

## Notes
- Line 11a/11b: purchase date and VIN; more than two vehicles needs the Vehicle Loan Interest Spreadsheet
- Used and business vehicles don't qualify

## Example
Car loan interest of $3,543 with joint Alabama AGI of $80,080: deducted in full.

## Required Information
- Interest paid, purchase date and VIN for each vehicle
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- Did you buy a new car with a loan in 2025 or 2026?
- Was it assembled in the U.S.?
- What was the VIN and purchase date?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a used vehicle
- Leaving out the VIN or purchase date
- Using federal AGI for the phase-out
