# Investment Interest (Form 4952A)
## Description
Interest on money borrowed to buy investments, limited to net investment income on Alabama Form 4952A. The unused part carries forward.

## Rules
- Deduction is the smaller of investment interest or net investment income; the excess carries to next year
- Figured as if the federal passive loss limit did not exist
- Net capital gain counts as investment income
- Investment expenses are those allowed after the 2% floor (Schedule A, line 21 or 24, whichever is smaller)
- Form 4952A is not needed if the only investment income is interest and dividends, there are no other investment expenses, the interest does not exceed that income, and there is no carryover
- Unchanged for 2026

## Example
Margin interest of $7,100 with $12,600 of investment income: $7,100 deducted.

## Required Information
- Investment interest paid
- Investment income and expenses
- Prior-year Alabama carryforward

## Questions
- Did you pay margin or other investment interest?
- Do you have an Alabama carryforward from 2025?

## Common Errors
- Skipping Form 4952A when it is required
- Using the federal carryforward amount
