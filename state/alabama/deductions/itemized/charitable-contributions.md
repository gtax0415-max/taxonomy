# Gifts to Charity
## Description
Cash and noncash gifts to qualified charities, deductible to the federal limits (Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(10)), with the percentage limits figured on Alabama AGI. Only for itemizers.

## Lines
- Line 15: cash or check (written acknowledgment for $250 or more)
- Line 16: other than cash (attach federal Form 8283 if over $500)
- Line 17: carryover from the prior year
- Line 18: total

## 2026 changes
- The federal 0.5% floor applies: gifts are deductible only above 0.5% of the contribution base (Alabama follows; ALDOR OBBBA summary). Where the 2026 form subtracts it is not yet known
- The federal deduction for non-itemizers does NOT apply in Alabama

## Notes
- Donations made through Schedule DC on the 2025 Alabama return can be deducted on the 2026 return

## Example
Cash gifts of $10,000 with Alabama AGI of $120,000: 0.5% is $600, so $9,400 is deductible.

## Required Information
- Cash and noncash gifts, with receipts
- Carryover from 2025
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- What did you give to charity in 2026?
- Did you give property?
- Do you have a carryover?

## Common Errors
- Omitting Form 8283 for noncash gifts over $500
- Ignoring the 2026 0.5% floor
- Claiming gifts while taking the standard deduction
