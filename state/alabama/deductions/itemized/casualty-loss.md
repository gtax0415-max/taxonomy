# Casualty and Theft Losses
## Description
Personal casualty and theft losses, following the federal rules (Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(6)) but with the 10% floor figured on ALABAMA AGI.

## Rules
- Line 19a: loss from federal Form 4684, box A (line 15) or box B (line 16)
- Line 19b: if box B is checked, 10% of Alabama AGI; otherwise 0
- Line 19c: line 19a minus line 19b
- Personal losses are limited to federally declared disasters; from 2026, state-declared disasters also qualify (Alabama follows)
- Reduce by insurance reimbursement received or expected; $100 per event
- No deduction if claimed on a federal estate tax return

## Example
A hurricane loss of $36,700 after insurance and the $100 reduction: line 19a.

## Required Information
- Disaster declaration
- Loss amount, basis and insurance
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- Was your property damaged in a declared disaster?
- What did insurance pay?

## Common Errors
- Using federal AGI for the 10% floor
- Claiming a loss outside a declared disaster
- Not subtracting insurance
