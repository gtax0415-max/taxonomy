# Dependent Exemption (Schedule DS)
## Description
An exemption for each dependent, with the amount set by Alabama AGI (Code of Alabama §40-18-19(a)(9)). Alabama uses its OWN dependent test, not the federal qualifying child or relative rules.

## Amounts (unchanged for 2026)
| Alabama AGI | Per dependent |
|---|---|
| $50,000 or less | $1,000 |
| $50,001 – $100,000 | $500 |
| Over $100,000 | $300 |

## Who qualifies
- You provided MORE than half of the person's support for the year, AND
- The person is your child or descendant, stepchild, parent or ancestor, stepparent, sibling or half-sibling, in-law, or aunt, uncle, niece or nephew by blood (an adopted child counts as a child)

## Who does NOT qualify
- Foster children, cousins, friends and other unrelated household members
- Yourself or your spouse

## Schedule DS
- Line 1a: each dependent's name, SSN, relationship, and Yes/No "Did you provide more than one-half of the dependent's support?"
- Line 1b: number of dependents → Form 40, Part III, line 1

## Notes
- Scholarships don't count as the dependent's own support; housing is valued at fair rental value
- A child born or who died during the year qualifies
- Married filing separately: claim only dependents you separately supported over 50%

## Example
Two children, joint Alabama AGI of $79,872: 2 × $500 = $1,000.

## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN and relationship
- Support provided
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- Who did you support in 2026?
- Did you pay more than half of their support?
- How are they related to you?

## Common Errors
- Using $1,000 when AGI is over $50,000
- Claiming a foster child or cousin
- Using federal AGI for the tier

## Prompt
- How much is the Alabama dependent exemption?
