# Firefighter's Insurance Premium
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for premiums a firefighter personally pays for the supplemental CANCER insurance policy established for Alabama firefighters. Code of Alabama §36-30-50 (Act 2019-361).

## Amount
- 100% of premiums paid by the individual with after-tax dollars
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Career firefighters
- Volunteer firefighters after 12 months of service

## What does NOT qualify
- Premiums paid by the fire department
- Premiums paid pre-tax or already deducted federally
- Other insurance (life, disability, health) not under §36-30-50

## Required Information
- Premiums paid personally
- Firefighter status

## Questions
- Are you a career or volunteer firefighter?
- Did you pay the cancer insurance premium yourself, after tax?

## Common Errors
- Deducting premiums the department paid
- Deducting other kinds of insurance
- Assuming only volunteers qualify

## Prompt
- What is the Alabama firefighter insurance deduction?
