# Small Employer Employee Health Insurance
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for health insurance premiums an employee of a small business pays under the employer's plan. Code of Alabama §40-18-15.3.

## Amount
- 100% of the premiums the employee paid
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Employer has FEWER than 25 employees
- Employee's wages are $50,000 or less
- Alabama AGI of $75,000 or less ($150,000 married filing jointly)
- Plan is provided by the employer

## Notes
- Premiums paid pre-tax through a cafeteria plan are already excluded from wages and should not be deducted again
- Attach or keep Form 4020

## Required Information
- Premiums paid by the employee
- Wages from the small employer
- Employer headcount
- Alabama AGI

## Questions
- Does your employer have fewer than 25 employees?
- Were your wages $50,000 or less?
- Did you pay part of your health insurance premium?
- Were the premiums taken out pre-tax?

## Common Errors
- Deducting only 50% (the employee deducts 100%)
- Claiming with wages over $50,000 or AGI over the limit
- Deducting pre-tax premiums
- Missing Form 4020

## Prompt
- What is the small employer health insurance deduction?
