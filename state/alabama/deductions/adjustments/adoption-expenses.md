# Adoption Expenses
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for reasonable LEGAL and MEDICAL expenses of adopting a minor child. Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(24); ALDOR Rule 810-3-15-.24. Separate from the Alabama Adoption Tax Credit (Schedule OC, Part M) and from the federal adoption credit.

## Amount
- Actual qualifying expenses paid or incurred in 2026, not reimbursed
- No dollar cap
- Unchanged for 2026

## Qualified expenses
- Attorney fees and court costs
- Medical and hospital costs of the birth mother related to the birth
- The adoptee's medical costs paid or incurred before the adoption petition is granted

## What does NOT qualify
- Adoption agency fees
- Any expense if the adoption petition is not granted
- Expenses reimbursed by an employer or anyone else
- Travel, meals and lodging (deductible federally for the credit, but not here)

## Notes
- Alabama does not follow the federal adoption credit; there is no Alabama rule reducing this deduction for expenses used for the federal credit
- The statute does not expressly exclude a stepchild adoption from this deduction (the Part M credit does exclude it); confirm with ALDOR for a stepchild adoption
- The same family can also claim the $2,000/$1,000 Alabama Adoption Tax Credit

## Required Information
- Legal and medical expenses by type and date
- Reimbursements received
- Whether the petition was granted

## Questions
- Did you pay legal or medical costs to adopt a child?
- Was the adoption petition granted?
- Did your employer reimburse any of the costs?

## Common Errors
- Deducting agency fees
- Deducting reimbursed costs
- Forgetting the separate Alabama adoption credit

## Prompt
- Are adoption expenses deductible in Alabama?
