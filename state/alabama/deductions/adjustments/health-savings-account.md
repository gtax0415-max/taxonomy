# Health Savings Account
## Description
Deduction for HSA contributions made OUTSIDE payroll, matching the federal deduction. Code of Alabama §40-18-15.6.

## Amounts (2026 federal limits, which flow through)
- Self-only coverage: $4,400
- Family coverage: $8,750
- Catch-up at age 55 or older: $1,000
- HDHP minimum deductible: $1,700 self-only / $3,400 family
- HDHP out-of-pocket maximum: $8,500 self-only / $17,000 family

## 2026 changes
ALDOR says Alabama follows the One Big Beautiful Bill Act changes:
- Bronze and catastrophic marketplace plans are HSA-eligible
- Direct primary care arrangements up to $150 a month ($300 family) don't disqualify the account
- Telehealth safe harbor made permanent

## Notes
- Employer contributions and pre-tax payroll contributions (W-2 code W) are already excluded from wages and are NOT deducted here
- The total of all contributions, including the employer's, must stay within the limit

## Required Information
- Personal (after-tax) contributions
- W-2 code W amount
- Coverage type and months covered

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an HSA outside of payroll?
- Was your coverage self-only or family?
- Are you 55 or older?

## Common Errors
- Deducting payroll contributions a second time
- Exceeding the limit once employer contributions are counted
- Using 2025 limits

## Prompt
- Is my HSA contribution deductible in Alabama?
