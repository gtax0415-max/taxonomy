# Alabama CollegeCounts 529 or PACT Contributions
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for contributions to Alabama's own 529 plans: the CollegeCounts 529 Fund or the PACT program. Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(28).

## Amounts
- Up to $5,000 per taxpayer per year
- Up to $10,000 on a joint return ONLY if BOTH spouses contribute (each limited to $5,000)
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Only Alabama plans qualify; contributions to another state's 529 plan are not deductible
- Contributions postmarked by December 31 count for that year
- A non-qualified withdrawal is added back to income, plus 10%, on line 7 (Other Income)
- Rolling CollegeCounts money to another state's plan triggers the same recapture; rolling another state's plan into CollegeCounts does not
- For 2026, ALDOR says Alabama follows the federal expansion of K-12 qualified expenses (up to $20,000)
- 529-to-Roth IRA rollovers: ALDOR guidance conflicts on whether they are recaptured; confirm before claiming

## Example
A married couple where only one spouse contributes $6,000: deduction is $5,000. If each spouse contributes $3,000: deduction is $6,000.

## Required Information
- Contributions by each spouse, with dates
- Plan name
- Any withdrawals or rollovers

## Questions
- Did you or your spouse contribute to CollegeCounts or PACT in 2026?
- Who made each contribution?
- Did you withdraw or roll over any 529 money?

## Common Errors
- Deducting another state's 529 plan
- Claiming $10,000 when only one spouse contributed
- Missing the recapture on a non-qualified withdrawal or out-of-state rollover

## Prompt
- Is my 529 contribution deductible in Alabama?
