# Moving Expenses
## Description
Deduction for moving expenses, limited to the same people the federal law allows AND to a new job location in ALABAMA. Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(18) adopts IRC §217; ALDOR Rule 810-3-15-.12. Civilians who move for a new job get NO deduction.

## Eligibility
- Active-duty member of the Armed Forces moving under a permanent change of station
- Starting 2026: federal law (One Big Beautiful Bill Act) also allows certain intelligence-community employees and new appointees; ALDOR says Alabama follows this
- New principal place of work must be in Alabama

## Amount
- Unreimbursed costs of moving household goods and travel to the new home, figured on Form 3903
- No dollar cap

## Notes
- Attach Form 3903 and enter the new job's city, state and ZIP on line 6
- Reimbursements excluded from W-2 wages (code P) cannot also be deducted

## Required Information
- Orders or proof of eligible status
- New duty station or job location
- Moving costs and reimbursements

## Questions
- Are you on active military duty, and did you move under orders?
- Is your new duty station in Alabama?
- Were any costs reimbursed?

## Common Errors
- A civilian claiming the deduction
- Claiming a move to a duty station outside Alabama
- Including reimbursed costs
- Leaving out the new location

## Prompt
- Can I deduct moving expenses in Alabama?
- I'm in the military and was transferred to Maxwell Air Force Base.
