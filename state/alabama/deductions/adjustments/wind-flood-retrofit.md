# Wind or Flood Retrofit
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for costs to retrofit a home to resist wind or flood damage. Code of Alabama §§40-18-15.4 (coastal Alabama Insurance Underwriting Association area) and 40-18-15.5 (statewide).

## Amount
- The LESSER of 50% of the cost or $3,000 (combined under both sections)
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Retrofit of the taxpayer's legal residence
- Meets IBHS FORTIFIED, ICC 500/600 or a standard approved by the Alabama Residential and Energy Codes Board
- Certified by an approved evaluator

## Notes
- Costs paid with a grant (such as Strengthen Alabama Homes) do not qualify unless the grant was included in income
- Separate from the Catastrophe Savings Account deduction

## Example
A $4,000 FORTIFIED roof upgrade, no grant: deduction is $2,000. A $10,000 upgrade: deduction is capped at $3,000.

## Required Information
- Retrofit cost and certification
- Any grant received

## Questions
- Did you install a FORTIFIED roof or other wind or flood upgrade?
- Do you have the certification?
- Did a grant pay for part of it?

## Common Errors
- Deducting the full cost
- Deducting grant-funded costs
- Claiming without certification

- Can I deduct a FORTIFIED roof in Alabama?
