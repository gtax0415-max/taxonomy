# Alimony Paid
## Description
Deduction for alimony paid to a former spouse, allowed ONLY to the same extent as federal law. Code of Alabama §40-18-15(a)(17) adopts IRC §215; ALDOR Rule 810-3-15-.13. Because Alabama follows the federal rule, alimony under a divorce or separation agreement executed after December 31, 2018 is NOT deductible.

## Amount
- Payments under an agreement executed on or before December 31, 2018 (and not later modified to adopt the new rule): deductible in full
- Agreements executed after 2018: $0
- Unchanged for 2026

## What does NOT qualify
- Child support
- Property settlements or lump-sum divisions
- Voluntary payments not required by the agreement

## Notes
- Recipient's last name and SSN must be entered on line 4
- The recipient reports pre-2019 alimony as income on their Alabama return

## Required Information
- Date the divorce or separation agreement was executed or modified
- Amount paid during 2026
- Recipient's name and SSN

## Questions
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed?
- Was it modified after 2018?
- How much of the payments was child support?

## Common Errors
- Deducting alimony under a post-2018 divorce
- Including child support
- Leaving the recipient's SSN blank

## Prompt
- Can I deduct alimony in Alabama?
- I divorced in 2021 and pay alimony.
