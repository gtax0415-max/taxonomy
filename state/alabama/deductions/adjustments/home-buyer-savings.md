# First-Time and Second Chance Home Buyer Savings Account
## Description
Alabama-only deduction for deposits to a savings account designated to buy a home in Alabama. Code of Alabama §§24-11-1 et seq. (Act 2018-467, amended by Act 2022-223). Figured on Schedule HBC.

## Amounts
- Deposits up to $5,000 a year (single or head of family); $10,000 married filing jointly
- Interest earned in the account is excluded from income
- Deduction available for up to 10 years from the first year claimed
- Account cap: $25,000 individual / $50,000 joint, principal plus earnings; no deduction once the cap is reached
- Unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- First-time buyer, or a "second chance" buyer who has not owned a home recently
- Home must be in Alabama
- Accounts may be opened through December 31, 2028

## Notes
- Schedule HBC must be attached, or the deduction is denied
- Non-qualified withdrawals are taxable plus a 10% penalty, with exceptions

## Required Information
- Account opening date and first year deducted
- 2026 deposits and year-end balance
- Interest earned
- Any withdrawals and home purchase details

## Questions
- Do you have a designated home buyer savings account?
- When did you first claim the deduction?
- Did you buy a home or withdraw funds this year?

## Common Errors
- Omitting Schedule HBC
- Deducting more than $5,000 / $10,000
- Deducting after the 10-year period or above the account cap

## Prompt
- How does the Alabama home buyer savings account work?
