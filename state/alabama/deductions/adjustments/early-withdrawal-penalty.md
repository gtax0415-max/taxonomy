# Penalty on Early Withdrawal of Savings
## Description
Deduction for the penalty a bank charges when a certificate of deposit or other time deposit is cashed before maturity. Same as the federal deduction.

## Amount
- The full penalty shown on Form 1099-INT box 2 (or 1099-OID box 3)
- No limit
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- The related interest is still reported in full as income (Schedule B); do not net the penalty against it
- Penalties for early withdrawals from IRAs or retirement plans are NOT deductible here

## Required Information
- 1099-INT box 2 or 1099-OID box 3 amount

## Questions
- Did you cash a CD or time deposit before it matured?
- Does your 1099-INT show an amount in box 2?

## Common Errors
- Netting the penalty against interest income
- Deducting an IRA early-withdrawal penalty

## Prompt
- Where does the early withdrawal penalty go?
