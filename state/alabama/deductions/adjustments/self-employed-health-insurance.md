# Self-Employed Health Insurance
## Description
Deduction for health insurance premiums paid by a self-employed person for themselves, their spouse and dependents. Same as the federal deduction under IRC §162(l).

## Amount
- Premiums paid, limited to net self-employment profit from the business that established the plan
- Includes qualified long-term care premiums up to the age-based federal limits
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Not allowed for any month the taxpayer (or spouse) was eligible for an employer-subsidized plan
- Premium tax credit recipients must reconcile on Form 8962 first
- Premiums deducted here cannot also be listed on Schedule A

## Required Information
- Premiums paid by month
- Net self-employment profit
- Months eligible for any employer plan

## Questions
- Are you self-employed and paying your own health insurance?
- Were you or your spouse eligible for an employer plan any month this year?

## Common Errors
- Also listing the premiums on Schedule A
- Claiming months of employer-plan eligibility
- Deducting more than the business's net profit

- Where does self-employed health insurance go?
