# Recapture of Certain Indiana Offset Credits (Schedule 4, Line 3; Schedule IN-CR)
## Description
Indiana requires repayment of certain previously claimed offset (non-refundable) credits when conditions are not met. Complete Schedule IN-CR and carry its line 9 total to Schedule 4, line 3. If filing electronically, keep a copy of Schedule IN-CR; if filing on paper, enclose it. Recapture counts as tax for the estimated tax computation (IT-2210, line 1).
## The five credits
| Credit | Code | Trigger | Column E amount |
|---|---|---|---|
| ABLE 529A Account Credit | 872 | Non-qualified withdrawal (not for disability-related expenses; account closed within 12 months; rollover to another ABLE account or a 529 plan other than Indiana529) | Non-qualified withdrawal x 20% |
| Employer Child Care Expenditure Credit | 876 | Facility stops operating as a qualified facility; change in ownership unless the buyer assumes recapture; enrolling children of non-employees | Credits previously claimed: 100% in the first year after the credit was first allowed, reduced 20% each later year, none in the sixth year |
| Indiana529 Education Savings Plan Credit | 837 | Non-qualified withdrawal (not for qualified higher education expenses, death or disability, scholarship, or custodian transfer; account closed within 12 months; rollover to another 529 plan or ABLE account) | Non-qualified withdrawal x 20%; withdrawals of 2018 K-12 contributions x 10% (check Column B) |
| Physician Practice Ownership Tax Credit | 880 | Sold or relinquished ownership in a physician-owned practice and employed by a health system or non-physician-owned practice, within five years of receiving the credit | Credits previously claimed |
| Residential Historic Rehabilitation Credit | 831 | Property transferred, or non-conforming modifications made, less than five years after completing the certified work | Total credit previously claimed |
## Schedule IN-CR columns
A: credit name. B: X if the Indiana529 withdrawal was based on a K-12 contribution. C: account number (ABLE, Indiana529), "1" (child care), license number with leading zeros (physician), or account or certification number (historic). D: 3-digit code (printed with an "R" prefix box). E: amount recaptured. Line 9: total → Schedule 4, line 3. Attach sheets for more than eight lines.
## 2026 note on the Indiana529 credit
From 2026 the Indiana529 credit is 20% of all contributions, with no split between higher education and K-12 (SEA 453). The K-12 box and the 10% rate on IN-CR relate to 2018 K-12 contributions; confirm how the 2026 Schedule IN-CR handles them.
## Examples (Schedule IN-CR)
- John made two $300 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) non-qualified withdrawals from his son's Indiana529 account: $600 x 20% = $120 in Column E.
- Anna claimed a $1,200 Residential Historic Rehabilitation Credit and sold the property less than five years after the work was completed: $1,200 in Column E.
## Booklet and schedule wording
The booklet calls credit 880 the "Physicians Practice Ownership Credit" in its 2025 changes list and "Physician Practice Ownership Tax Credit" on page 29; Schedule IN-CR uses "Physician Practice Ownership Tax Credit". The booklet says child care recapture may be "some or all" of the credits; Schedule IN-CR's Column E instruction says to enter the credits previously claimed, while its narrative gives the 20%-per-year phase-down.
## Questions
- Did you take money out of an Indiana529 or ABLE account for a non-qualified purpose, or close an account within 12 months of opening it?
- Did you claim the employer child care, physician practice ownership or residential historic rehabilitation credit in earlier years, and has anything changed?
## Common Errors
- Recapturing the whole withdrawal instead of 20% of it
- Missing recapture on a rollover from Indiana529 to another state's 529 plan
- Reporting IEDC credit recapture on Schedule IN-CR
## Prompt
- I withdrew from my Indiana529 for a non-qualified expense. Do I owe anything to Indiana?
