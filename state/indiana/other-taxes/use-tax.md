# Use Tax on Internet, Mail Order and Out-of-State Purchases (Schedule 4, Line 1)
## Description
Purchases made outside Indiana, by mail or catalog, through radio or television advertising and/or over the internet may be subject to Indiana sales and use tax if sales tax was not paid at the time of purchase. This "use tax" is 7% (.07). Effective July 1, 2025, use tax also applies to the purchase price of services and items other than tangible personal property that are subject to Indiana sales tax but on which sales tax was not paid in whole or in part.
## Who pays
Indiana sellers collect the sales tax. When you buy from an out-of-state company, you must make sure use tax is paid: either the seller collects it, or you pay it directly to Indiana on Schedule 4, line 1.
## Computation
Multiply the total purchase price of all taxable purchases on which no Indiana tax was paid by 7%. If you paid sales tax to the state where the item was purchased, you get a credit against Indiana use tax for the amount paid, up to 7%.
## Example
In 2026 you bought furniture for $2,000 in a state with no sales tax and a $500 laptop online from a seller that charged no tax (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Use tax = $2,500 x .07 = $175. If you had paid 4% sales tax ($80) to the furniture state, use tax = $175 - $80 = $95.
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online, by mail or out of state in 2026 without being charged Indiana sales tax?
- Did you pay sales tax to another state on any of those purchases?
- Did you buy taxable services without paying sales tax?
## Common Errors
- Omitting use tax on untaxed online purchases
- Taking credit for more than 7% of another state's tax
- Reporting innkeeper's or food and beverage tax here instead of on Form ST-115
## Prompt
- Do I owe Indiana use tax on things I bought online?
