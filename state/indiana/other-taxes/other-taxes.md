# Other Taxes (Schedule 4, Lines 1-4; Form IT-40, Line 10)
## Description
Schedule 4 collects the Indiana taxes that are not figured on Indiana adjusted gross income: use tax on untaxed purchases, state and county tax withheld from household employees, and recapture of offset credits. The Schedule 4, line 4 total goes to Form IT-40, line 10 and is added to the state and county income taxes on line 11. Credit recapture counts as tax for the estimated tax computation (IT-2210, line 1); use tax and household employment taxes do not.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1  Use tax on internet, mail order and out-of-state purchases (7%)
Line 2  Household employment taxes (Schedule IN-H)
Line 3  Recapture of certain Indiana offset credits (Schedule IN-CR, line 9)
Line 4  Lines 1 + 2 + 3 → Form IT-40, line 10
```
