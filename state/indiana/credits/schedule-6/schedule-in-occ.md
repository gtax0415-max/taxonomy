# Schedule IN-OCC, Other Offset Credits (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7)
## Description
Schedule IN-OCC is where many business and certified credits are reported instead of on Schedule 6, line 6. Part A lists the current-year credits claimed (code, certification or project number, amount); the total on Schedule IN-OCC, line 8 is entered on Schedule 6, line 7 and the schedule is enclosed. Part B reports carryforwards from prior years and every current-year credit that was not fully claimed, even one that cannot be carried over.

## Form not reviewed
The Schedule IN-OCC form did not download with the 2025 set used for this taxonomy (it is to be fetched again if a scenario reaches it). Its line layout beyond "Part A," "Part B" and "line 8" is not described here.
## Combined Limitation
Line 7 counts with lines 4 through 6; together they cannot exceed IT-40, line 8.
## Questions
- Which certified credits do you hold this year, and what are their codes and certification numbers?
- Do you have carryforwards from prior years?
- Will any current-year credit go unused because of the tax limit?
## Common Errors
- Listing an IN-OCC credit directly on line 6 as well
- Leaving out the certification number, PIN or project number
- Not reporting unused current-year credits on Part B
- Not enclosing the certificate or approval letter where the credit requires it
