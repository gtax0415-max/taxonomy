# School Scholarship Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 849)
## Description
A credit for donations to certain scholarship-granting organizations (SGOs). The credit equals 50% of the contribution made to the SGO for a school scholarship program. There is no limit on how much a donor can give, but the whole program cannot award more than $18.5 million (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) in credits per state fiscal year. The SGO must be certified by the Indiana Department of Education. In some cases DOR may round the credit down to the nearest dollar if it receives information that the credit should be the rounded-down amount.
## Later change
HEA 1266 (2026 session), effective January 1, 2027, adds certain IRC 501(c)(3) organizations on the list submitted to the U.S. Treasury under IC 20-53-1 to the definition of SGO. It does not apply to 2026.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 849 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
A $2,000 contribution to a certified SGO → $1,000 credit, subject to IT-40 line 8 and the Combined Limitation.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a scholarship granting organization certified by the Indiana Department of Education?
- Did the SGO give you a credit certification, and for how much?
## Common Errors
- Listing it directly on line 6 instead of through Schedule IN-OCC
- Claiming the full donation instead of 50%
- Giving to an uncertified organization
