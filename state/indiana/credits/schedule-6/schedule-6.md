# Schedule 6: Offset Credits (Form IT-40, Schedule 6, Lines 1-8)
## Description
Schedule 6 credits cannot be refunded; they only reduce Indiana state and/or county tax. Lines 1 through 3 offset county tax and together cannot exceed IT-40, line 9. Lines 4 through 7 offset state adjusted gross income tax and together cannot exceed IT-40, line 8. Line 8 totals lines 1 through 7 and is carried to IT-40, line 13. If you claim a credit from a pass through entity, include Schedule IN K-1 or IT-41 Schedule IN K-1.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1   Credit for local taxes paid outside Indiana             ┐ not more than county tax
Line 2   Community revitalization enhancement district credit   │ on IT-40, line 9
Line 3   Other local credits, 3a-3b (name and code; none now)    ┘ (Combined Limitation)
Line 4   College credit (Schedule CC-40, line 6)                 ┐
Line 5   Credit for taxes paid to other states                   │ not more than state AGI tax
Line 6   Other credits, 6a-6d (name, 3-digit code, amount)        │ on IT-40, line 8
Line 7   Schedule IN-OCC, line 8                                 ┘ (Combined Limitation)
Line 8   Add lines 1 through 7 → IT-40, line 13 (Total Offset Credits)
```
## Line 6 or Schedule IN-OCC
Credits the booklet says to report on Schedule IN-OCC (Part A) must not be listed directly on Schedule 6; the Part A total goes to line 7. Prior-year carryforwards go on Schedule IN-OCC, Part B, and current-year credits not fully claimed are also reported on Part B, even if they cannot be carried over. All other coded credits are entered on line 6 with the credit name, code and amount (enclose additional sheets if more than four).

## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to a non-Indiana city or county, or to another state or country?
- Did you give to an Indiana college or university?
- Did you contribute to Indiana529 or an Indiana ABLE account?
- Are you an Indiana public school teacher, counselor, librarian, principal or superintendent who bought classroom supplies?
- Do you hold a Schedule IN K-1 or a certificate from IEDC, IHCDA or DOR for a credit, or a carryforward from last year?
## Common Errors
- Listing an IN-OCC credit both on line 6 and through line 7
- Letting lines 1-3 exceed county tax or lines 4-7 exceed state tax
- Putting another state's withholding on Schedule 5 instead of claiming the credit on line 5
- Missing the three-digit code on line 6
