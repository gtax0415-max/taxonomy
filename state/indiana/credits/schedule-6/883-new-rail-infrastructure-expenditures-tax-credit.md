# New Rail Infrastructure Expenditures Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 883)
## Description
Effective Jan. 1, 2025, a credit for new railroad infrastructure investments. The credit is 50% of the qualified expenditures, but may not exceed the lesser of 50% of the qualified new rail expenditures for each served customer project completed in the year or $500,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per rail served customer project. New on the 2025 form.
## Certification
A credit application may only be filed with DOR after the project is completed. DOR evaluates eligibility from the miles of track owned or leased in Indiana and a description and certification of the qualified new rail infrastructure expenditures.
## Assignment
The credit may be assigned in writing, once; the assignee may not assign it again. A tax exempt transferor (government agency or IRC 501(c)(3) organization) must complete the transfer within one year after the close of the tax year for which the credit was certified.
## Law (HEA 1461, 2025)
Unused credit may be carried forward up to five years; no carryback or refund. Aggregate limit $5,000,000 per state fiscal year beginning after June 30, 2025. The credit expires after December 31, 2027.
## How to claim
Enter code 883 on Part A of Schedule IN-OCC for the year you claim the credit, with the certification number, and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7. If necessary, complete Part B to report credits claimed from a carryover or credits to be carried over.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
A completed rail served customer project with $1,400,000 of qualified new rail expenditures → 50% = $700,000, limited to $500,000.
## Questions
- Did you complete a new rail served customer project, and what were the qualified expenditures?
- Has DOR certified the credit?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding $500,000 per project
- Reporting under code 882

- What is the Indiana new rail infrastructure credit?
