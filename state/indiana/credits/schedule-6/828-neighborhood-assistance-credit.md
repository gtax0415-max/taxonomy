# Neighborhood Assistance Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 828)
## Description
If you made a contribution or engaged in activities to upgrade areas in Indiana, you may be able to claim a credit for this assistance. The program is run by the Indiana Housing & Community Development Authority (IHCDA), Neighborhood Assistance Program. Owners of pass-through entities such as S corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies are eligible.
## Not eligible
Fees paid to your neighborhood association are not eligible and must not be reported on this line.
## Amount
The 2025 booklet does not state the credit percentage or any cap; the amount comes from the IHCDA approval. Contact IHCDA's Neighborhood Assistance Program for the rules.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 828 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to an organization approved under Indiana's Neighborhood Assistance Program?
- Do you have the IHCDA approval or the Schedule IN K-1 showing the credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming neighborhood or homeowner association dues
- Claiming a donation to an organization not approved under the program
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit for donating to a neighborhood program?
