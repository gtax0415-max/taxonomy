# Other Local Credits (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 3)
## Description
Schedule 6, line 3 has two rows (3a and 3b) for other credits against county tax, each with a credit name, code number and amount, and the form allows additional sheets. The 2025 booklet says: "Currently, there are no other local credits available to be reported in this space." Leave line 3 blank unless the 2026 booklet names a credit for it.
## Limit
Lines 1 through 3 together cannot be greater than the county tax on IT-40, line 9 (Combined Limitation).
## Booklet inconsistency
The booklet's Combined Limitation example puts a community revitalization enhancement district amount "on line 3a." The form prints CRED on line 2, so that example does not change the rule that line 3 is currently unused.
