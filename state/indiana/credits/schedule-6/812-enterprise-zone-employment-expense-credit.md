# Enterprise Zone Employment Expense Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 812)
## Description
Certain areas in Indiana are designated enterprise zones to encourage investment and job growth in distressed urban areas. This credit is the lesser of 10% of qualifying wages or $1,500 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per qualified employee, up to the amount of tax liability on income derived from the enterprise zone. Sole proprietors who operate and/or invest in a business in a zone and owners of pass-through entities may claim it. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #66.
## Records
Schedule EZ must be enclosed if claiming this credit.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 812 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
- Also limited to the tax on income derived from the enterprise zone.
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Three qualified employees earning $12,000 each: 10% of each is $1,200 (less than $1,500) → $3,600, limited to the tax on zone income.
## Questions
- Does your business operate in an Indiana enterprise zone?
- How many qualified employees and how much in qualifying wages?
- What was your tax on income from the zone?
## Common Errors
- Omitting Schedule EZ
- Exceeding $1,500 per employee
- Using the tax on all income instead of zone income
