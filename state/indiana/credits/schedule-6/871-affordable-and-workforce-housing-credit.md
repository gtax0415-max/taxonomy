# Affordable and Workforce Housing Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 871)
## Description
A credit for the construction of a qualified low income building located in Indiana. The building must have been awarded a federal affordable housing tax credit using a 30% present value of the qualified basis and be financed by tax exempt bonds subject to the federal private activity bond volume cap. The Indiana Housing and Community Development Authority (IHCDA) determines the amount. The credit is new on the 2025 form.
## Timing
The credit is a five-year credit, with 20% of the credit first allowable each year for five years. Booklet example: a $1,000,000 credit for a building placed in service in 2025 is $200,000 first claimable in each year from 2025 to 2029. Any unused credit may be carried forward for nine years following the first year of the credit period, so the credits for that project all expire in 2034.
## Assignment
The credit may be assigned in writing. Additional guidance is to be provided for submission and handling of assigned credits.
## 2026 change (SEA 163)
Effective July 1, 2026, the expiration of the credit chapter is extended from July 1, 2028, to July 1, 2033.
## How to claim
Enter code 871 on Part A of Schedule IN-OCC for the year in which you are claiming the credit, with the certification/project number provided by IHCDA for the project (or relevant portion of it), and carry the total to Schedule 6, line 7. If necessary, complete Part B to report credits claimed from a carryover or credits to be carried over.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IHCDA determined an affordable and workforce housing credit for your project, and what is the certification/project number?
- Which year of the five-year period is this?
- Was the credit assigned to you?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the full credit in the first year instead of 20% a year
- Omitting the IHCDA certification/project number
- Carrying forward past the nine-year limit
