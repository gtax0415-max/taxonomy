# Employer Child Care Expenditure Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Code 876)
## Description
A credit for employers who incur certain costs for Indiana licensed child care for their employees' children. The credit is 50% of the employer's qualified expenditures, up to a maximum credit of $100,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per employer. Allowable credits are $2,500,000 per state fiscal year, awarded in the order of returns claiming the credit. The credit is not available to employers who were in the business of operating a child care facility before making the expenditures.
## Qualified expenditures (2025 booklet)
- Acquisition, construction, rehabilitation or expansion of property used as part of an Indiana qualified child care facility of the taxpayer operated for the taxpayer's employees;
- Costs under a contract between the taxpayer and an Indiana qualified child care facility for such acquisition, construction, rehabilitation or expansion; or
- Costs of complying with the licensure requirements of IC 12-17.2 as part of acquiring or constructing an Indiana qualified child care facility.
## 2026 changes (HEA 1177, retroactive to January 1, 2026)
- Taxpayer threshold raised from 100 or fewer employees to 500 or fewer.
- The facility no longer has to be operated by the taxpayer, jointly with the taxpayer, or under a Section 45F(c)(1)(A)(iii) contract with the taxpayer.
- Qualified expenditures also include operating costs of a facility under a contract with the taxpayer; operating costs of the taxpayer's own facility for its employees (including training, scholarship programs and higher pay for employees with more child care training); contracts with a facility, or with an intermediate entity that contracts with facilities, to provide child care to employees; and contracts for child care resources and referral services for employees.
- The definition of a recapture event and the recapture procedure are repealed.
- The credit chapter expires July 1, 2027 (SEA 463, 2025).
## Recapture (2025 rules)
Under the 2025 rules the credit is recaptured if, within five years after it was allowed, the facility stops operating as an Indiana qualified child care facility, ownership changes (unless the acquirer assumes the recapture liability), or the facility enrolls a child of a non-employee; casualty losses excepted. Recapture is 100% in the year after the credit is allowed, reduced by 20% each year for the next four years, and none in the sixth year. Report on Schedule IN-CR, code 876 (Column C enter "1"). HEA 1177 repeals the recapture provisions effective January 1, 2026; confirm how the 2026 Schedule IN-CR treats credits allowed in earlier years.
## How to claim
The 2025 booklet does not say whether this credit is entered under Schedule 6, line 6 or on Schedule IN-OCC. Confirm against the 2026 booklet and the Schedule IN-OCC instructions before entry.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- How many employees did you have during the year?
- What child care expenditures did you make, and for which facility?
- Were you already in the child care business?
- Did any recapture event occur for a credit claimed in earlier years?
## Common Errors
- Applying the 100-employee limit for 2026
- Claiming more than $100,000
- Claiming as an existing child care operator
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit for an employer that pays for employee child care?
