# Railroad Expenditures Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 882)
## Description
Effective Jan. 1, 2025, a credit for qualified railroad expenditures. The credit is 50% of the qualified railroad expenditures, but not more than the number of miles of Class II or Class III railroad track owned or leased by the taxpayer in Indiana at the close of the year multiplied by $3,500 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). New on the 2025 form.
## Certification
A credit application may only be filed with DOR after the project is completed. DOR certifies the credit based on the miles of track owned or leased in Indiana and a description and certification of the qualified railroad expenditures.
## Assignment
The credit may be assigned in writing. A taxpayer may make only one assignment and the assignee may not assign it again. If the transferor is a tax exempt entity (a government agency or an IRC 501(c)(3) organization), the transfer must be completed within one year after the close of the tax year for which the credit was certified.
## Law (HEA 1461, 2025)
Unused credit may be carried forward up to five years; no carryback or refund. Aggregate limit $9,500,000 per state fiscal year beginning after June 30, 2025. A pass-through entity and its owners may not claim more than one credit for the same expenditure. The credit expires after December 31, 2027.
## How to claim
Enter code 882 on Part A of Schedule IN-OCC for the year you claim the credit, with the certification number, and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7. If necessary, complete Part B to report credits claimed from a carryover or credits to be carried over.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
$300,000 of qualified expenditures; 40 miles of Class III track → 50% = $150,000; cap 40 x $3,500 = $140,000 → $140,000 credit.
## Questions
- How many miles of Class II or III track do you own or lease in Indiana at year end?
- What were the qualified railroad expenditures, and has DOR certified the credit?
- Was the credit assigned?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the per-mile cap
- Filing the application before the project is complete
- Assigning a credit received by assignment
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit for short line railroad track work?
