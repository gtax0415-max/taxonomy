# ABLE 529A Account Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 872)
## Description
A credit for contributions made to the Indiana ABLE 529A savings plan (InvestABLE Indiana), administered through the Indiana ABLE Authority. Only contributions to this specific Indiana plan qualify. The credit is 20% of the contributions, up to $500 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), and not more than the state tax on IT-40, line 8. Schedule IN-ABLE must be completed and enclosed.
## Schedule IN-ABLE
- Column A: mark if you or your spouse do not own the account.
- Column B: calendar year of the contribution (YYYY). A "2027" entry on the 2026 return would mean a contribution between January 1 and April 15, 2027, made on or before the date the return was filed (the 2025 form applies this to 2026 contributions for 2025).
- Column C: the tax year for which you claim the credit.
- Column D: account number (list an account once per contribution year).
- Column E: contributions for which you claim the credit; list each account and each year on its own line.
- Line 10: total of Column E; line 11: line 10 x .20; line 12: lesser of line 11 or 500; line 13: IT-40, line 8; line 14: lesser of line 12 or line 13 → under Schedule 6, line 6.
## Contributions after year end
A contribution made after the year ends and by the regular due date may be claimed for the earlier year. The election must be made by April 15 (or the extended due date) and cannot be made on a late or amended return filed after the due date. The election is irrevocable; not claiming it is an irrevocable election not to. A contribution can be split between two years, but the part designated for one year cannot be claimed again in the next. For 2026 this means a contribution made by the 2026 return's regular due date in 2027; confirm the dates on the 2026 Schedule IN-ABLE.
## Not eligible
- Money credited because of bonus points or other consideration earned by the taxpayer
- Money transferred from any qualified ABLE program under IRC 529A or a similar plan
- Money transferred from any qualified tuition program under IRC 529 or a similar plan
- 2026 change (SEA 243, effective January 1, 2026): money transferred in a qualified ABLE rollover contribution described in IRC Section 530A(d)(4)(B) is not a "contribution"
## Recapture
An account owner who makes a non-qualified withdrawal (one not related to the eligible individual's disability or blindness, a withdrawal from an account closed within 12 months of opening, or, in Schedule IN-CR's words, "a rollover distribution or transfer from the ABLE 529A or any other ABLE account or a Section 529 education savings plan other than an Indiana529 education savings plan") must repay all or part of the credit: 20% of the non-qualified withdrawal on Schedule IN-CR (code 872, account number in Column C), carried to Schedule 4, line 3.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 872 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
- No carryback or carryforward is described for this credit. Schedule IN-ABLE's instructions warn that if you have other Schedule 6 state credits allowable only in the current year, you may have to report a lower amount.
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Contributions of $2,000 during 2026 to a relative's Indiana ABLE account: line 11 $400; line 12 $400; IT-40 line 8 $1,800 → credit $400. With $3,000 of contributions, line 11 is $600 and the credit is limited to $500.
## Questions
- Did you or your spouse contribute to an Indiana ABLE (InvestABLE Indiana) account, and how much to each account?
- Do you own the account?
- Did you contribute between January 1 and the due date of the return and want it counted for the earlier year?
- Was any amount a rollover or transfer from another plan?
- Did the account owner take a non-qualified withdrawal?
## Common Errors
- Claiming contributions to a non-Indiana ABLE plan or to Indiana529 on this schedule
- Claiming more than $500
- Claiming the same early-year contribution on two returns
- Including rollovers or transfers
- Missing Schedule IN-ABLE
