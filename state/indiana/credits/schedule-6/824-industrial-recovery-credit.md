# Industrial Recovery Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 824)
## Description
A credit based on a taxpayer's qualified investment in a vacant industrial facility located in a designated industrial recovery site. If the Indiana Economic Development Corporation approves the application and the rehabilitation plan, you are entitled to a credit based on the "qualified investment." The minimum age for an eligible facility has been reduced from 20 years to 15 years. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible.
## Investment dates
Except as follows, the credit is only for a qualified investment made before Jan. 1, 2020. A credit is also allowed for a qualified investment made after Dec. 31, 2019, and before Jan. 1, 2030, if the credit is awarded under an application approved by IEDC before Jan. 1, 2020, or an agreement between the taxpayer and IEDC entered into before Jan. 1, 2021. Any unused credit existing before Jan. 1, 2020, is still eligible for carryforward for an unlimited number of years.
## 2026 note (HEA 1210)
IEDC may not enforce repayment provisions in certain agreements executed on or before December 31, 2020, that award both a redevelopment tax credit and an industrial recovery credit (a credit of up to $10,000,000, capital investment of at least $250,000,000, in a county of 350,000 to 400,000 population); the agreements are to be amended by June 30, 2026.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 824 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
- Restriction for Certain Tax Credits - Limited to One per Project applies (apply first).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Booklet: Matthew has a $500 Indiana529 credit and a $600 industrial recovery credit; state tax $700. Line 6a $500 (Indiana529), line 6b $200 (industrial recovery, code 824); $400 carries to next year.
## Questions
- Has IEDC approved an industrial recovery credit for your investment?
- When was the investment made, and under what approval or agreement date?
- Do you have unused credit from before 2020?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for an investment after 2019 without a qualifying pre-2020 approval or pre-2021 agreement
- Not carrying forward the unused amount
- Claiming with another listed project credit for the same project
