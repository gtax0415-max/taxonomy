# Small Modular Nuclear Reactor Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 884)
## Description
A credit for expenditures incurred in the manufacture of a small modular nuclear reactor (in Indiana, under HEA 1007, 2025). The credit is 20% of these expenditures incurred during the taxable year. The credit must be approved by DOR before any claim. New on the 2025 form.
## Carryforward
Any unused credit above the current year tax liability can be carried forward indefinitely. It may not be carried back or refunded.
## How to claim
List the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A using code 884 and the PIN provided by DOR, and carry the total to Schedule 6, line 7. If you carry a credit forward from a prior year or to next year, list it on Schedule IN-OCC, Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
$2,000,000 of qualified expenditures → $400,000 credit; with state tax of $150,000 in the year, $250,000 carries forward.
## Questions
- Did you incur expenditures manufacturing a small modular nuclear reactor in Indiana?
- Has DOR approved the credit and issued a PIN?
## Common Errors
- Claiming before DOR approval
- Omitting the PIN
