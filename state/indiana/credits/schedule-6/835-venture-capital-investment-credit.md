# Venture Capital Investment Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 835)
## Description
A taxpayer that provides qualified investment capital to a qualified Indiana business may be eligible for this credit. Certification must be obtained from the Indiana Economic Development Corporation Development Finance Office, VCI Credit Program.
## 2026 changes (HEA 1406, effective July 1, 2026)
IEDC may certify a business that makes retail sales as a qualified Indiana business when the retail sales are a method of selling a unique product the business developed, holds patents on or otherwise owns. For assignments, nothing prevents combining individual credits of less than $10,000 for assignment.
## Records
If you claim as an owner of a pass-through entity, keep Schedule IN K-1 with your records; DOR can require it.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 835 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- Restriction for Certain Tax Credits - Limited to One per Project applies (apply first).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Did you invest in an IEDC-certified qualified Indiana business, and do you have the certification?
- Was another project credit claimed for the same project?
## Common Errors
- Using code 835 for an investment fund (868)
- Listing on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
## Prompt
- I invested in an Indiana startup. Is there a venture capital credit?
