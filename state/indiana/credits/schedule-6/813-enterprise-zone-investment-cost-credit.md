# Enterprise Zone Investment Cost Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 813)
## Description
A credit based on qualified investments made within an Indiana enterprise zone. It can be up to a maximum of 30% of the investment, depending on the number of employees, the type of business and the amount of investment in the zone. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #66.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 813 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
- Restriction for Certain Tax Credits - Limited to One per Project applies (apply first).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Did you invest in a business in an Indiana enterprise zone?
- Was any other project credit claimed for the same project?
## Common Errors
- Claiming this and another listed project credit for the same project
- Assuming 30% without IEDC documentation
