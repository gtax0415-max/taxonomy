# Indiana's Research Expense Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 822)
## Description
Indiana has a research expense credit similar to the federal credit for research and experimental expenses paid in carrying on your trade or business in Indiana. Owners of pass-through entities such as S corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies may claim it; enclose Schedule IN K-1 to support the claim.
## Sole proprietors and disregarded entities
If you claim the credit as a sole proprietor or as the owner of a disregarded entity such as a single-member LLC, keep a completed Form IT-20REC with your records (DOR can request it) and also complete Schedule IN-OCC, Part B with the information for the credit.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 822 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6. Sole proprietors and owners of disregarded entities also complete Schedule IN-OCC, Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Do you have Indiana qualified research expenses, or a Schedule IN K-1 showing the credit?
- Are you a sole proprietor or single-member LLC owner who must keep Form IT-20REC?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal credit amount instead of the Indiana computation
- Not completing Schedule IN-OCC, Part B as a sole proprietor
