# Hoosier Business Investment Credit - Logistics (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 860)
## Description
A credit for qualified expenditures for certain logistics investments. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible. The credit is administered by the IEDC. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #95.
## Records
If you claim as an owner of a pass-through entity, keep Schedule IN K-1 with your records; DOR can require it.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 860 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- The booklet refers to the Restriction for Certain Tax Credits - Limited to One per Project for this credit, although the restriction's list names only the Hoosier business investment credit generally.
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IEDC approved a logistics investment credit for you or your entity?
## Common Errors
- Reporting under code 820 instead of 860
- Listing on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
