# Health Reimbursement Arrangement Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 878)
## Description
A credit for employers with no more than 49 employees at any time during the year that contribute to health reimbursement arrangements for their employees. The credit, computed employee by employee, is the lesser of the amount contributed to the HRA during the year or $400 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for the first year the employer qualifies, $200 for the second year and $0 for the third and later years. It is available for the first two years that the employer provides an HRA amount at least equal to the previous benefit year's level, or equal to the amount contributed per covered individual toward the employer-provided health insurance plan in the previous benefit year. Total credits are capped at $10,000,000.
## 2026 changes (HEA 1210, retroactive to January 1, 2026)
- The per-employee limit is the lesser of the employer's HRA contribution for the year or $400 for the year the HRA is established, $200 for the year immediately after, and $0 thereafter.
- No credit for an HRA established in a taxable year beginning before January 1, 2024.
- For taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025, if a pass through entity is entitled to the credit but has no state tax liability, each shareholder, partner or member receives the credit times their percentage of distributive income. (The 2025 booklet says the credit cannot be passed through.)
- The $10,000,000 cap is per calendar year (the 2025 booklet says per fiscal year). A claim from a pass-through entity is treated as filed when the entity files; a taxpayer that must carry over part of the credit is treated as claiming the full amount.
## Reporting
Report on Schedule IN-OCC. There is no certification/project number for this credit; if one is required, enter a numeric value. The credit cannot be used to reduce or eliminate nonresident withholding tax due.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 878 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Ten employees, $500 contributed for each in the year the HRA is established → 10 x lesser of $500 or $400 = $4,000. Next year, $500 each again → 10 x $200 = $2,000.
## Questions
- How many employees did you have at any time during the year?
- When was the HRA established, and how much did you contribute per employee?
- Are you claiming through a pass-through entity?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a third year
- Exceeding the per-employee amount
- Claiming for an HRA established before 2024
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit for a small employer that funds an HRA?
