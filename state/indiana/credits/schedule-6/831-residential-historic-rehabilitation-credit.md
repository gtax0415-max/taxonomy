# Residential Historic Rehabilitation Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 831)
## Description
A credit for the repair and rehabilitation of residential property that is listed on the Indiana Register of Historic Sites and Structures, is at least 50 years old and will be used as your primary residence. All work must meet the Secretary of the Interior's Standards for Rehabilitation of Historic Properties. The credit is 20% of expenditures or $10,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is less. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #87A; contact the Indiana Department of Natural Resources, Division of Historic Preservation and Archaeology.
## Recent law (HEA 1616, 2025)
- Effective July 1, 2025, a $10,000 limit per taxpayer.
- State fiscal year maximum of $250,000 of credits approved; once reached, no further applications are approved that year, and a credit that would exceed it is reduced to the amount remaining.
- A credit granted before March 10, 2025, for an expenditure claimed in a year other than the year the work was performed may be claimed in the first taxable year beginning after December 31, 2024, and carried forward under IC 6-3.1-22-14.
## Recapture
The credit must be repaid if the property is transferred less than five years after completion of the certified preservation or rehabilitation work, or if within five years additional modifications are made that do not meet the standards of the Office of Community and Rural Affairs. Report on Schedule IN-CR, code 831, with the account or certification number. Booklet example: Anna claimed $1,200 and sold within five years → $1,200 recaptured.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 831 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Approved expenditures of $30,000 → 20% = $6,000, under the $10,000 limit → $6,000 credit, subject to IT-40 line 8 and the Combined Limitation. Expenditures of $60,000 → $12,000, limited to $10,000.
## Questions
- Is your home on the Indiana Register of Historic Sites and Structures and at least 50 years old?
- Is it your primary residence?
- Was the credit approved, and for how much?
- Have you sold the home or made non-conforming changes within five years of finishing the work?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without state approval
- Exceeding $10,000
- Claiming for rental or non-primary residences
- Not recapturing after a sale within five years
