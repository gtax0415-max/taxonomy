# Attainable Homeownership Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 875)
## Description
A credit for contributions of cash and other property made to Habitat for Humanity of Indiana. The credit is 50% of allowable contributions up to a maximum credit of $10,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per taxable year. The total of credits per state fiscal year is limited to $4,000,000. Certification must be obtained from Habitat for Humanity of Indiana.
## Reporting
The approved credit is reported on Schedule IN-OCC (the booklet points to the corporate/partnership form page for it). Enclose Schedule IN-OCC.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 875 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
A certified $6,000 contribution → $3,000 credit.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to Habitat for Humanity of Indiana (the state organization)?
- Did Habitat for Humanity of Indiana certify the credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a gift to a local Habitat affiliate or other charity without certification
- Claiming the full gift instead of 50%
- Exceeding $10,000
