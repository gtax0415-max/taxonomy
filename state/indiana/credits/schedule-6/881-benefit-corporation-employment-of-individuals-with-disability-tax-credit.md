# Benefit Corporation Employment of Individuals with Disability Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Code 881)
## Description
A credit for employers that hire individuals referred through a vocational rehabilitation services program for individuals with a disability, where the employer is a benefit corporation (IC 23-1.3-2-3) with no more than 50 employees at any time during the year. For an individual the credit is only available as a shareholder in an S corporation and is reported on Schedule IN K-1.
## Criteria (all)
- The employer is a benefit corporation as defined in IC 23-1.3-2-3.
- No more than 50 employees at any time during the taxable year.
- Hires an individual referred through a vocational rehabilitation services program for individuals with a disability (the credit-eligible employee), first hired after Dec. 31, 2023.
- The employee works an average of at least 20 hours per week at a compensation rate similar to other employees doing the same or similar tasks.
- The employee is not hired to replace an employee who was terminated, unless for misconduct or the terminated employee voluntarily left.
## Amount
30% of the credit-eligible employee's wages for the first year the wages are allowable for the credit, 40% for the second year, and 50% for the third and later years.
## How to claim
The 2025 booklet says only that the credit will be reported to you on Schedule IN K-1; it does not say whether the shareholder enters it under Schedule 6, line 6 or on Schedule IN-OCC. Confirm against the 2026 booklet.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Are you a shareholder in an S corporation that is a benefit corporation?
- Does your Schedule IN K-1 report this credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming as a sole proprietor or partner
- Using code 877 for a benefit corporation credit, or 881 for an ordinary employer
