# Headquarters Relocation Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 818)
## Description
A business may be eligible for a credit of as much as 50% of the cost of relocating its headquarters to Indiana if it meets one of two sets of criteria. Some or all of the credit may be refundable; the refundable part is on Schedule 5, line 10, and the offset part is reported here through Schedule IN-OCC. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #97. The credit is administered by the IEDC.
## First test (all)
- Annual worldwide revenue of $50 million (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form);
- At least 75 Indiana employees (for credits awarded before July 1, 2022); and
- Relocates its corporate headquarters to Indiana.
## Second test - (1) or (2), and (3), and (4) or (5)
1. Received at least $4 million in venture capital in the six months immediately before applying for the credit.
2. Closes on at least $4,000,000 in venture capital not more than six months after applying.
3. Has at least 10 Indiana employees (for credits awarded before July 1, 2022).
4. Relocates its corporate headquarters to Indiana.
5. Relocates jobs equal to 80% of the business's total payroll in the preceding quarter to an Indiana location.
## Records
Submit a copy of the IEDC certificate verifying the credit for the year; otherwise the credit is denied. Enclose proof of the relocation costs and of employment of the minimum number of Indiana employees and, if applicable, payroll in Indiana and everywhere. If IEDC granted a refundable credit under the second test, keep the supporting documentation and see Schedule 5, line 10.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 818 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- From July 1, 2025, the credit is subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Which test did the business meet, and what did IEDC certify?
- Was any part ruled refundable?
- How much is being used against state tax this year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the same dollars here and on Schedule 5, line 10
- Not submitting the IEDC certificate
