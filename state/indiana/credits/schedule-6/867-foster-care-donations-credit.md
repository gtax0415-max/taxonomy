# Foster Care Donations Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 867)
## Description
A credit for donations to qualifying foster care organizations and, from July 1, 2023, for qualifying contributions to the Insuring Foster Youth Trust Fund. The credit is 50% of the donation, up to $10,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per taxable year. No more than $2,000,000 in credits can be awarded during a calendar year. Credits go through a DOR application and approval process.
## Records
Enclose the approval letter from the Department of Revenue with the return; otherwise the credit will be denied. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 867 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
A $3,000 approved donation → $1,500 credit. A $25,000 donation → $12,500, limited to $10,000.
## Questions
- Did you donate to a qualifying foster care organization or the Insuring Foster Youth Trust Fund?
- Did DOR approve the credit, and do you have the approval letter?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without the DOR approval letter
- Claiming more than $10,000
- Listing it on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit for donating to foster care?
