# Redevelopment Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 863)
## Description
A credit for a qualified investment for the redevelopment or rehabilitation of real property located within a qualified redevelopment site. The credit is administered by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation.
## 2026 changes (HEA 1406 and HEA 1210)
- From July 1, 2026, IEDC may commit redevelopment credits to a regional development authority under an approved development plan and award them to taxpayers proposing qualified investment in a site in that plan; $35,000,000 a fiscal year is to be awarded to development authorities.
- From July 1, 2026, a small town opportunity initiative: a for-profit taxpayer undertaking a qualified community project receives a redevelopment credit of 20% of the project cost, a nonprofit 30% (with certain pre-approval acquisition costs counted); these projects carry no repayment obligation.
- HEA 1210: IEDC may not enforce repayment provisions in certain agreements executed on or before December 31, 2020, that award both a redevelopment and an industrial recovery credit.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 863 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- From July 1, 2025, the credit is subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IEDC approved a redevelopment tax credit for your investment?
- Is the project part of a regional development plan or the small town opportunity initiative?
## Common Errors
- Listing on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
