# Physician Practice Ownership Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 880)
## Description
A credit of $20,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) against state income tax for a physician who owns part of a newly established physician owned practice, with a $20,000 credit for each of the two years following establishment of the practice (each award is $60,000 over three years). The requirements changed effective Jan. 1, 2025 (HEA 1427): the credit is no longer limited to primary care physicians and covers physicians engaged in the practice of medicine.
## Physician requirements
- A physician engaged in the practice of medicine;
- Has an ownership interest in a corporation, LLC, partnership or other legal entity organized to provide health care services as a physician owned entity;
- Is not employed by a health system (as defined in IC 16-18-2-168.5); and
- Has state income tax liability.
- Ownership interest in the practice's income of at least 5% if the practice has not more than 10 owners, or equal to 50% of the practice's income divided by the number of physician owners (more than 10 owners).
## Practice requirements
The practice must be established as a legal entity under Indiana law after Dec. 31, 2023; open and begin providing health care services in a calendar year beginning after Dec. 31, 2023; and have billed for health care services for at least six months of that calendar year.
## Approval and cap
The practice applies to DOR for approval of the credit for its owners for a calendar year after June 30 of that year and not later than June 30 of the following year, with a certified list of physician owners. Credits are approved in chronological order of applications, and total awards are limited to $10,000,000 per year (the booklet says per calendar year; HEA 1427 says per state fiscal year beginning July 1, 2025). Attach proof of DOR's approval.
## Recapture
If you sell, transfer or otherwise give up your ownership interest and are employed by a health system or another non-physician owned practice, you must recapture previously claimed credits within five years of receiving the credit: Schedule IN-CR, code 880, with your license number (leading zeros, no letters after the number) in Column C.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC with code 880; for the PIN use your physician license number without the last letter. Carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7. Use the same code and reporting for a credit based on the 2024 version of the credit.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Are you a physician owner of a practice established after 2023, and what is your ownership share?
- Are you employed by a health system?
- Did DOR approve the credit, and for which year of the three?
- Have you given up your ownership interest?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without DOR approval
- Claiming while employed by a health system
- Entering the license number with the trailing letter as the PIN
- Missing recapture after leaving the practice
