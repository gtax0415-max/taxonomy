# Mine Reclamation Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Code 874)
## Description
A credit for a qualified investment at a mine reclamation site. The credit must be approved by the IEDC. The credit for a taxable year cannot exceed 30% of the IEDC-approved qualified investment for the year or $5,000,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is less.
## Records
Provide a copy of the IEDC certification. If you claim as an owner of a pass-through entity, you must have a Schedule IN K-1 reporting the claimed credit; the entity provides and retains the IEDC certification.
## Assignment
The credit can be assigned to a lessee, who remains subject to the same requirements. The assignment must be in writing, any consideration may not exceed the value of the part assigned, and both parties report the assignment on their state returns for the year of assignment.
## How to claim
The 2025 booklet does not say whether this credit is entered under Schedule 6, line 6 or on Schedule IN-OCC. Confirm against the 2026 booklet and the Schedule IN-OCC instructions before entry.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IEDC certified a mine reclamation credit for your investment?
- Was the credit assigned to or by you?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding 30% of the approved investment or $5,000,000
- Claiming without IEDC certification or a Schedule IN K-1
## Prompt
- What is the Indiana mine reclamation credit?
