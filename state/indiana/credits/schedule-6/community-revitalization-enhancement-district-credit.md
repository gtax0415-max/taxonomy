# Community Revitalization Enhancement District Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 2)
## Description
A state and local income tax liability credit for a qualified investment made within a community revitalization enhancement district (CRED). The expenditure must be made under a plan adopted by an advisory commission on industrial development and approved by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation before it is made. The credit equals 25% of the qualified investment made by the taxpayer during the taxable year. It is available to owners of pass-through entities such as S corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies. It is nonrefundable and cannot be carried back; excess credit may be carried forward to the next tax year.
## Amount on line 2
The allowable credit on line 2 is the lesser of the available credit or the county tax on IT-40, line 9. Claim any unused amount (within certain limitations) under Schedule 6, line 6 with code 808 against state tax.
## Limits
- Restriction - Limited to One per Project: CRED is one of the credits that cannot be combined with another listed credit for the same project. Apply the restriction first.
- Combined Limitation: lines 1 through 3 together cannot exceed IT-40, line 9. Apply the credit for local taxes paid outside Indiana first, then CRED.
- From July 1, 2025, CRED credits are subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
## Booklet line references
The 2025 Schedule 6 prints CRED on line 2. The booklet's Combined Limitation example puts the limited CRED amount "on line 3a," and the code 808 instructions refer to CRED not used "on Schedule 6, line 3." These references do not match the form; enter CRED on line 2 and confirm against the 2026 booklet.
## Example
Qualified investment $4,000 → credit $1,000 (25%). County tax $700, no other local credits: line 2 $700; the remaining $300 goes on line 6 as code 808 against state tax, limited with lines 4-7 to IT-40, line 8; anything still unused carries forward.
## Questions
- Did you (or an entity you own) make a qualified investment in a CRED under an IEDC-approved plan?
- How much was invested during the year?
- Do you also claim a credit for local taxes paid outside Indiana?
## Common Errors
- Using the whole credit against county tax above IT-40, line 9
- Forgetting the remainder can go on line 6, code 808
- Claiming CRED and another listed project credit for the same project
