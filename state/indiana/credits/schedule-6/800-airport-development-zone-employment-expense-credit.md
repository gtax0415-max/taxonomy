# Airport Development Zone Employment Expense Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 800)
## Description
This credit has been repealed. However, any previously approved yet unused credit is available to be claimed.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 800 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Do you have a previously approved credit that has not been fully used?
- How much was used in earlier years, and how much remains?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a new credit for the current year
- Claiming more than the unused balance
- Omitting the code
