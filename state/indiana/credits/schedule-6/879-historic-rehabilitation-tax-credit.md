# Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 879)
## Description
A credit for certain expenses to rehabilitate a qualified historic structure: a certified historic structure under IRC section 47(c)(3), a building individually listed on the register of Indiana historic sites and historic structures, or a building in and contributing to a district listed on that register. If the restoration and preservation costs are qualified rehabilitation expenditures under IRC 47(c)(2) and total at least $5,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), the credit is 25% of the qualified rehabilitation expenses, or 30% for non-income producing property. The credit is administered by the IEDC.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 879 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- From July 1, 2025, the credit is an "applicable tax credit" within the IEDC $300 million aggregate limit per state fiscal year (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
$80,000 of qualified rehabilitation expenditures on an income-producing listed building → $20,000; if non-income producing, $24,000.
## Questions
- Is the building a certified historic structure or on the Indiana register (individually or in a district)?
- Are the costs qualified rehabilitation expenditures, and at least $5,000?
- Is the property income producing?
## Common Errors
- Mixing this credit with code 819 or 831
- Using 30% for income-producing property
