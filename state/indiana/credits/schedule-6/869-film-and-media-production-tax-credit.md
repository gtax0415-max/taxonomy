# Film and Media Production Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 869)
## Description
Effective July 1, 2022, a credit for qualified film and media production expenses. The credit equals the expenses multiplied by a percentage set by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation, but not more than 30% of the expenses. Beginning July 1, 2025, any single credit may not exceed $250,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), and the aggregate of credits may not exceed $2,000,000 (SEA 306, 2025). Certification must be obtained from IEDC.
## Assignment from January 1, 2026 (SEA 306)
A taxpayer may assign any part of the credit. The assignment must be in writing and IEDC must be notified as it prescribes. A taxpayer may make only one assignment, and the assignee may not assign it again. Both the taxpayer and the assignee report the assignment on their adjusted gross income tax returns for the year of assignment. The taxpayer may not receive value greater than the part of the credit assigned.
## Records
Enclose the certification letter from IEDC with the return, otherwise the credit will be denied.
## Expiration
The credit chapter is extended to July 1, 2031 (SEA 306, 2025).
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 869 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IEDC certified a film and media production credit for you, and at what percentage?
- Did you assign or receive any part of the credit?
## Common Errors
- Not enclosing the IEDC certification letter
- Exceeding $250,000 for a single credit
- Assigning more than once
