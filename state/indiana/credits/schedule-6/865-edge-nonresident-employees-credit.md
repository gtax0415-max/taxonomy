# Economic Development for a Growing Economy - Nonresident Employees (EDGE-NR) (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 865)
## Description
A credit for incremental state income tax amounts that would have been withheld on employees from reciprocal states if those employees had been subject to Indiana state tax withholding. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible. Unlike the EDGE and EDGE-R credits, EDGE-NR is non-refundable. It is administered by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation.
## Records
If you claim as an owner of a pass-through entity, keep Schedule IN K-1 with your records; DOR can require it.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 865 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Does your business have an IEDC agreement for EDGE-NR covering employees from reciprocal states?
- Do you have the Schedule IN K-1 showing your share?
## Common Errors
- Putting EDGE-NR on Schedule 5 as a refundable credit
- Listing it on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
## Prompt
- What is the Indiana EDGE-NR credit?
