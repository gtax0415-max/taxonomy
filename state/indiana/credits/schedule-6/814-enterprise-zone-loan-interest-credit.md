# Enterprise Zone Loan Interest Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 814)
## Description
A credit of up to 5% of the interest received from all qualified loans made before Jan. 1, 2018, for use in an Indiana enterprise zone. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #66 and Schedule LIC for how to calculate it. Sole proprietors and owners of pass-through entities may claim it.
## Records
Schedule LIC must be enclosed if claiming this credit.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 814 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
$20,000 of interest received on qualified pre-2018 zone loans → up to $1,000.
## Questions
- Did you make loans for use in an Indiana enterprise zone before 2018, and how much interest did you receive?
## Common Errors
- Claiming on loans made in 2018 or later
- Omitting Schedule LIC
## Prompt
- Is there an Indiana credit on interest from enterprise zone loans?
