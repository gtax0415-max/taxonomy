# Community Revitalization Enhancement District (CRED) Credit - State Tax (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 808)
## Description
The community revitalization enhancement district credit (25% of a qualified investment in a CRED, see Schedule 6, line 2) offsets both state and local tax, and any unused remainder carries forward. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible. Claim on Schedule 6, line 2 first against county tax; the amount not used there is claimed under line 6 with code 808 against state tax.
## Booklet line reference
The 2025 booklet says the remaining credit is what was not used "on Schedule 6, line 3"; the form prints CRED on line 2. Read it as line 2 and confirm against the 2026 booklet.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 808 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
- Restriction - Limited to One per Project applies (apply first).
- From July 1, 2025, CRED awards are subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
Booklet: Megan's $200 CRED credit is cut to $60 on the county tax lines; the remaining $140 is entered under line 6 as code 808 against state tax.
## Questions
- How much CRED credit was left after Schedule 6, line 2?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the same CRED dollars on line 2 and under code 808
- Not carrying forward the remainder
