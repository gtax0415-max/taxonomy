# Individual Development Account Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 6, Code 823)
## Description
A credit for qualified contributions made to a community development corporation participating in an Individual Development Account (IDA) program. The organization must have an approved program number from the Indiana Housing and Community Development Authority (IHCDA) before a contribution qualifies for pre-approval; applications for the credit are filed through IHCDA. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible, and S corporations and partnerships may take the credit and pass the unused portion through to shareholders and partners.
## Records
Keep the approval certification or letter of assignment with your records; DOR can ask for it. (The 2025 booklet says "approval certification from IEDC" in this paragraph, although the credit is administered by IHCDA.)
## Amount
The booklet does not state the credit rate or cap; use the IHCDA approval.
## How to claim
Enter the credit name, code 823 and the amount under Schedule 6, line 6 (rows 6a-6d; enclose additional sheets if needed).
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a community development corporation's IDA program?
- Was the contribution pre-approved by IHCDA?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without IHCDA pre-approval
- Claiming a contribution to an organization without an approved program number
## Prompt
- What is the Indiana Individual Development Account credit?
