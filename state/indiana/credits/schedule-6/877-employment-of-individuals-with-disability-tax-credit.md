# Employment of Individuals with Disability Tax Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Code 877)
## Description
A credit for employers that hire individuals referred through a vocational rehabilitation services program for individuals with a disability. The credit is 20% of the credit-eligible employee's wages for the first year the wages are allowable for the credit, 30% for the second year and 40% for the third and later years.
## Criteria (all)
- No more than 500 employees at any time during the taxable year.
- Hires an individual referred through a vocational rehabilitation services program for individuals with a disability (the credit-eligible employee), first hired after Dec. 31, 2023.
- The employee works an average of at least 20 hours per week at a compensation rate similar to other employees doing the same or similar tasks.
- The employee is not hired to replace an employee who was terminated, unless for misconduct or the terminated employee voluntarily left.
- For a partnership interest, eligibility is determined at the partnership level.
## Form EDTC W-1 and notices
If you claim the credit from your own activities (not from a Schedule IN K-1 or IT-41 Schedule IN K-1), DOR may ask you to complete Form EDTC W-1, Employment of Individuals with Disability Tax Credit Worksheet. Sole proprietors and disregarded entities may receive a Notice of Proposed Assessment; submit the EDTC W-1. If the notice is not resolved within 60 days of its date, a protest can be filed with DOR's Legal Division; failure to protest timely may lead to collection.
## How to claim
The 2025 booklet does not say whether this credit is entered under Schedule 6, line 6 or on Schedule IN-OCC. Confirm against the 2026 booklet and the Schedule IN-OCC instructions before entry.
## Limits
Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Example
A credit-eligible employee hired in 2025 earns $24,000 in 2026, the second year the wages are allowable: credit $24,000 x 30% = $7,200, subject to IT-40 line 8.
## Questions
- Did you hire someone referred through a vocational rehabilitation program after 2023?
- How many employees did you have, and is the business a benefit corporation?
- What were the employee's wages and hours, and which year of employment is this?
## Common Errors
- Using the benefit corporation rates (30/40/50%) for an ordinary employer
- Claiming for an employee hired before 2024 or working under 20 hours a week
- Claiming for a replacement of an employee terminated without misconduct
## Prompt
- Does Indiana give a credit for hiring people with disabilities?
