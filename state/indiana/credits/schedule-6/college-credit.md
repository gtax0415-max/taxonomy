# College Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 4; Schedule CC-40)
## Description
A credit for money or property donated to an eligible Indiana college or university, or to a corporation or foundation organized and operated exclusively for the benefit of an eligible college or university. An individual's credit is 50% of the contributions or $100 on a single return ($200 on a joint return) (2025 amounts; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is less, and not more than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8. Complete and enclose Schedule CC-40 (or a statement with the same information).
## Schedule CC-40
- Part 1: each contribution with the institution's name, its 4-digit code from the list on the back of the schedule (for example 5301 Indiana University, 7907 Purdue University, 4905 IvyTech Community College of Indiana, 7103 University of Notre Dame), date (MM/DD/YYYY), amount and the type of return filed by the contributor (individual).
- Part 2, Individual and Fiduciary Computation (Column A single or married filing separately; Column B married filing jointly):
  1. Total contributions listed in Part 1
  2. 50% of line 1
  3. Limitation: $100 (Column A) or $200 (Column B)
  4. Lesser of line 2 or line 3
  5. Indiana adjusted gross income tax from IT-40, line 8
  6. Allowable College Credit: lesser of line 4 or line 5 → Schedule 6, line 4
- Part 3 is the corporation computation and does not apply to the IT-40.
## What does not qualify
Tuition, fees, or room and board paid to a college or university are not contributions.
## Combined Limitation
Lines 4 through 7 together cannot exceed IT-40, line 8. The college credit cannot be carried over.
## Records
Keep documentation of the contributions; DOR can ask for it later. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #14.
## Example
Married filing jointly, gave $300 to Ball State University (code 1801). Line 2 $150; line 3 $200; line 4 $150; IT-40 line 8 $2,950; line 6 = $150 → Schedule 6, line 4.
## Questions
- Which Indiana colleges or college foundations did you give to, on what dates and how much?
- Are you filing jointly?
- Were any payments for tuition, fees or room and board?
## Common Errors
- Using $200 on a single or married filing separately return
- Claiming tuition payments
- Giving to an out-of-state college or an institution not on the CC-40 list
- Exceeding IT-40, line 8 or the Combined Limitation
