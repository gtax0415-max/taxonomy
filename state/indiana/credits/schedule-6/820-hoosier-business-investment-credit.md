# Hoosier Business Investment Credit (IT-40 Schedule 6, Line 7 via Schedule IN-OCC, Code 820)
## Description
A credit for qualified investments, which include the purchase of new telecommunications, production, manufacturing, fabrication, processing, refining or finishing equipment. Owners of pass-through entities are eligible. The credit is administered by the Indiana Economic Development Corporation (IEDC). See Income Tax Information Bulletin #95.
## Records
If you claim as an owner of a pass-through entity, keep Schedule IN K-1 with your records; DOR can require it.
## How to claim
Report the credit on Schedule IN-OCC, Part A with code 820 and carry the Schedule IN-OCC total to Schedule 6, line 7; do not also list it on line 6. Enclose Schedule IN-OCC. Report carryforwards and any unused current-year amount on Part B.
## Limits
- Restriction for Certain Tax Credits - Limited to One per Project applies (apply first).
- From July 1, 2025, the credit is subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Combined Limitation: Schedule 6, lines 4 through 7 together cannot be greater than the state adjusted gross income tax on IT-40, line 8 (2.95% of Indiana AGI for 2026). The credit does not reduce county tax.
## Questions
- Has IEDC approved a Hoosier Business Investment credit for you or your entity?
- Was another project credit claimed for the same project?
## Common Errors
- Listing on line 6 instead of Schedule IN-OCC
- Claiming with another listed credit for the same project
