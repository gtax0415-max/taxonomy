# Economic Development for a Growing Economy Credit - EDGE (IT-40 Schedule 5, Line 8)
## Description
The EDGE credit is available to businesses that conduct certain activities designed to foster job creation in Indiana under an agreement with the Indiana Economic Development Corporation (IEDC). An individual with business income (including partnership or S corporation income) may be eligible, including owners of pass-through entities such as S corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies. The credit is refundable: the booklet contrasts it with EDGE-NR, which is "a non-refundable credit."
## How to claim
- Complete and enclose Schedule IN-EDGE and enter its line 19 on Schedule 5, line 8.
- The figures for Schedule IN-EDGE come from Schedule IN K-1 or from the approved IEDC credit agreement letter. Include the completed Schedule IN K-1.
- If all or part of your share was claimed by the pass-through entity, you may not claim that previously claimed credit on your own behalf.
- Contact IEDC for eligibility requirements.
## Limits and changes
- From July 1, 2025, EDGE credits awarded under IC 6-3.1-13 are subject to the limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (the IEDC aggregate limit on applicable tax credits for a state fiscal year) (HEA 1001, 2025).
- From January 1, 2025, IEDC may no longer pay an EDGE credit from the statewide innovation development district fund (HEA 1001, 2025).
- Schedule IN-EDGE itself was not among the 2025 form texts used for this taxonomy; its lines 1-18 are not described here.
## Questions
- Do you have a Schedule IN K-1 or IEDC agreement letter showing an EDGE credit?
- Did the entity already claim any part of your share?
## Common Errors
- Entering the credit without Schedule IN-EDGE and the Schedule IN K-1
- Claiming a share the pass-through entity already claimed
- Putting EDGE-NR (code 865) on this line
