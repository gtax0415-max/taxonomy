# Schedule 5: Credits (Form IT-40, Schedule 5, Lines 1-13)
## Description
Schedule 5 lists Indiana tax payments and the credits that can be refunded. Every line is added on line 13 (Total Credits), which is carried to IT-40, line 12. Because Schedule 5 amounts are treated like payments, a credit on lines 5 through 11 is not limited to the tax: any amount above the total Indiana tax (IT-40, line 15) becomes an overpayment on IT-40, line 16. Schedule 5 is printed on the same page as Schedule IN-DONATE.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1   Indiana state tax withheld (Schedule IN-W, line 26)
Line 2   Indiana county tax withheld (Schedule IN-W, line 27)
Line 3   Pass Through Entity Tax Credit (from Schedule IN K-1 / IT-41 Schedule IN K-1)
Line 4   Estimated tax paid for the year, including any Form IT-9 extension payment
Line 5   Unified tax credit for the elderly (Table A or Table B)
Line 6   Earned income credit: Schedule IN-EIC, line A-3 (10% of the federal EIC)
Line 7   Lake County residential income tax credit (Worksheets A-D)
Line 8   Economic development for a growing economy credit: Schedule IN-EDGE, line 19
Line 9   EDGE retention credit: Schedule IN-EDGE-R, line 19
Line 10  Headquarters relocation credit (refundable portion)
Line 11  Adoption credit: Adoption Credit Worksheet, line 47 (total for all children)
Line 12  Reserved for future use
Line 13  Add lines 1 through 12 → IT-40, line 12 (Total Credits)
```
## Key rules
- Lines 1 through 4 are payments, written under payments/. Do not claim tax withheld for other states or for localities outside Indiana on lines 1 and 2; that is a Schedule 6 offset credit (lines 1 and 5). Do not report PTET as withholding or estimates, and do not report another state's PTET on line 3.
- Line 5 can alternatively be claimed on Form SC-40 by a filer who is not required to file the IT-40. File one or the other, once.
- Line 6 requires Schedule IN-EIC; the credit is 10% of the federal EIC.
- Line 7 cannot be claimed with the homeowner's residential property tax deduction (Schedule 2, line 2) in the same year.
- Lines 8 to 10 come from IEDC-approved credits, usually through Schedule IN K-1. The headquarters relocation credit is split: the refundable portion here, the offset portion on Schedule IN-OCC (code 818).
- Line 11 is 20% of the federal adoption credit per child, up to $2,500 per child (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), figured on the Adoption Credit Worksheet.

## Questions
- How much Indiana state and county tax was withheld, and did you make estimated or extension payments?
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older, and is your federal AGI below $10,000?
- Did you claim the federal earned income credit or adoption credit?
- Do you own a home in Lake County, Indiana, and pay its property tax?
- Do you have a Schedule IN K-1 or IEDC letter showing an EDGE, EDGE-R or headquarters relocation credit?
## Common Errors
- Entering other-state or non-Indiana local withholding on lines 1 and 2
- Claiming the elderly credit on both Form SC-40 and Form IT-40
- Taking the Lake County credit and keeping the homeowner's property tax deduction
- Entering the federal EIC or the federal adoption credit instead of the Indiana amount
- Entering anything on reserved line 12
## Prompt
- What goes on Indiana Schedule 5?
