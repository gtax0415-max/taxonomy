# Indiana Earned Income Credit (IT-40 Schedule 5, Line 6; Schedule IN-EIC)
## Description
Indiana's earned income credit is 10% of the federal earned income credit. Indiana follows all requirements and allowances for the federal EIC, so a filer must be eligible for and have claimed the federal EIC; otherwise stop. The credit is refundable. Schedule IN-EIC must be completed and enclosed by every filer claiming it.
## Income limit - booklet and schedule disagree
- 2025 IT-40 booklet (page 31): income on IT-40, line 1 must be less than $68,650 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). If it is the same amount or more, you are not eligible.
- 2025 Schedule IN-EIC instructions: income on IT-40, line 1 must be less than $68,675 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form).

The two 2025 documents print different limits. $68,675 matches the 2025 federal limit for married filing jointly with three or more children. Resolve against the 2026 booklet and Schedule IN-EIC; for a return with IT-40, line 1 between $68,650 and $68,674 the result depends on which figure governs.
## Schedule IN-EIC
Section A - Figure your Indiana EIC
- A-1: the earned income credit from your federal return
- A-2: your earned income used to figure the federal credit
- A-3: line A-1 x 10% (.10) → Schedule 5, line 6

Section B - Complete if you claimed one or more children on federal Schedule EIC (up to three children; use the same children as on the federal schedule wherever possible)
- Child's first and last name and SSN. A child born and died in the year without an SSN: enter "Died" in the third SSN box and keep the birth certificate, death certificate and/or hospital records showing the child was born alive.
- B-1 Relationship: A your child; B grandchild; C stepchild; D foster child (not related; placed by an authorized placement agency); E other (related foster child, or a brother, sister, stepbrother, stepsister or their descendant, for example a niece or nephew, cared for as your own child).
- B-2 Age: A under 18; B age 18; C age 19-24 and full-time student; D age 19 or older and totally disabled.
- B-3 Location: A lived with you at least half the year; B born or died in the year and lived with you while alive.
## Rules
- Federal EIC unknown by Indiana's due date: file without the credit, then file an amended Indiana return when the federal amount is known.
- Indiana's Publication EIC is retired (from tax year 2023).
- Schedule IN-EIC must be filed with the return for the credit to be allowed.
## Example
Federal EIC $2,400, federal AGI $31,000. Schedule IN-EIC line A-1 $2,400; A-3 = $2,400 x 10% = $240 → Schedule 5, line 6 $240, refundable.
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal earned income credit, and how much?
- What is your federal AGI (IT-40, line 1)?
- Which children did you list on federal Schedule EIC?
- What is each child's relationship, age and time living with you?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the Indiana EIC without a federal EIC
- Entering the federal EIC on Schedule 5, line 6 instead of 10% of it
- Omitting Schedule IN-EIC or Section B when children were claimed federally
- Listing different children than on the federal Schedule EIC
- Ignoring the income limit, or using the wrong one of the two printed limits without noting the conflict
