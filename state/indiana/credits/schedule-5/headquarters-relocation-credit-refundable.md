# Headquarters Relocation Credit - Refundable Portion (IT-40 Schedule 5, Line 10)
## Description
A business with annual worldwide revenue of $50 million (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), at least 75 employees (for credits awarded before July 1, 2022), that relocates its corporate headquarters to Indiana may be eligible for a credit of up to 50% of the cost incurred in relocating the headquarters. A second test covers venture-backed businesses (see code 818). The credit is reported on Schedule IN-OCC. Some or all of it may be refundable if IEDC has so ruled; only that refundable part goes on Schedule 5, line 10.
## Amount on line 10
Enter the refundable amount of the credit less the portion of the credit used to offset your tax liability. The offset portion is claimed as code 818 through Schedule IN-OCC and Schedule 6, line 7.

Caution (booklet): the offset amount and the refundable amount together may not exceed the total credit available. The 2025 booklet says the combination of the credit "claimed here (offset amount) and on lines 29 through 31 (refundable amount)" may not exceed the total; IT-40 has no lines 29 through 31, so read this as the offset portion (Schedule 6 through IN-OCC) plus the refundable portion (Schedule 5, line 10). Confirm against the 2026 booklet.
## Records
- Enclose Schedule IN-OCC with the return.
- Keep the documentation IEDC provided that supports the refundable portion; DOR may request it.
- For the credit in general (code 818): submit the IEDC certificate verifying the credit for the year, proof of relocation costs and proof of minimum Indiana employment and, if applicable, payroll in Indiana and everywhere. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #97.
## Limits and changes
From July 1, 2025, headquarters relocation credits awarded under IC 6-3.1-30 are subject to the IEDC aggregate limits in IC 5-28-6-9 (HEA 1001, 2025).
## Example
IEDC certifies a $40,000 credit, of which $25,000 is ruled refundable. The owner's state tax after other credits is $6,000 and $6,000 of the credit is used through IN-OCC (code 818) to offset it. Schedule 5, line 10 = $25,000 - $6,000 = $19,000 under the booklet's wording; the total used ($6,000 + $19,000) stays within the $40,000 credit.
## Questions
- Has IEDC certified a headquarters relocation credit for you or your entity for this year?
- Did IEDC rule any part of it refundable, and how much?
- How much of the credit are you using to offset tax on Schedule IN-OCC?
## Common Errors
- Entering the whole credit on Schedule 5, line 10 when IEDC ruled no part refundable
- Claiming the same dollars on both Schedule IN-OCC and Schedule 5
- Not keeping the IEDC documentation for the refundable portion
