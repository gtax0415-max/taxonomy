# Adoption Credit (IT-40 Schedule 5, Line 11)
## Description
If you claimed an adoption credit on your federal return, you may claim an Indiana credit equal to 20% of the federal credit allowed per child, or $2,500 per child (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is less. When the claim rests on a federal carryover, the total Indiana credit allowable for the child over all years is still limited to $2,500. The credit is figured per child (up to three columns) on the Adoption Credit Worksheet in the booklet, and the total of line 47 goes on Schedule 5, line 11. It is refundable.

The 2025 booklet was revised (12-25) for the adoption credit; its cover notes "revised adoption credit instructions and worksheet on pages 32 through 34."
## Federal refundable portion
Indiana has incorporated the federal refundable credit limitation of IRC section 23(a)(4). Any portion of the federal credit above your federal tax liability (after credits applied before the adoption credit) is allowed in determining the Indiana credit; worksheet line 34 adds the refundable part (Form 8839, line 13) to the nonrefundable part used.
## Carryforward years
A carryforward credit claimed on federal Form 8839 may be used if it is from the preceding five tax years. The 2025 worksheet covers 2020, 2021, 2022, 2023 and 2024. For 2026 the window would be 2021 through 2025; confirm the years and the Form 8839 line references on the 2026 worksheet.
## Adoption Credit Worksheet (2025 lines, one column per child)
| Lines | Content |
|---|---|
| 1-4 | Child's first name, last name, year of birth, identification number |
| 5 | Check if this child is NOT claimed as a dependent |
| 6-10 | 2020 carryforward: 6 = 2020 Form 8839 line 11 (this child); 7 = 2020 Form 8839 line 12; 8 = line 6 / line 7 to four decimals; 9 = 2020 carryforward used this year (Carryforward Worksheet line 2 minus line 10); 10 = line 8 x line 9, rounded → line 36 |
| 11-15 | 2021 carryforward, same pattern (line 13 = 11 / 12; line 14 = Carryforward Worksheet line 3 minus line 12) → line 37 |
| 16-20 | 2022 carryforward (line 18 = 16 / 17; line 19 = Carryforward Worksheet line 4 minus line 14) → line 38 |
| 21-25 | 2023 carryforward (line 23 = 21 / 22; line 24 = Carryforward Worksheet line 5 minus line 16) → line 39 |
| 26-30 | 2024 carryforward (line 28 = 26 / 27; line 29 = Carryforward Worksheet line 6 minus line 18) → line 40 |
| 31-35 | Current year: 31 = current Form 8839 line 11a; 32 = Form 8839 line 12; 33 = line 31 / line 32 to four decimals; 34 = (Form 8839 line 18 minus line 15, not less than zero) plus Form 8839 line 13; 35 = line 33 x line 34, rounded → line 41 |
| 36-41 | Enter lines 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 and 35 |
| 42 | Sum of lines 36 through 41 |
| 43 | Line 42 x 20% (0.20) |
| 44 | $2,500 |
| 45 | Sum of any previous Indiana adoption credits claimed for the child |
| 46 | Line 44 minus line 45, not less than zero |
| 47 | Lesser of line 43 and line 46 → Schedule 5, line 11 (IT-40PNR Schedule F, line 11) |

Lines 6-30 are used only for a carryforward; complete only the years you are carrying forward from.
## 2026 change (SEA 243, retroactive to January 1, 2022)
Only individuals who are residents of Indiana during the taxable year are eligible. For a part-year resident the federal credit is multiplied by days of Indiana residence over days of residence in all states (combined days for a joint return); that proration belongs to IT-40PNR and is out of scope here.
## Examples
- One child, federal credit allowed this year (worksheet line 34) $10,000, line 33 ratio 1.0000: line 35 $10,000; line 43 $2,000; line 46 $2,500; line 47 = $2,000.
- One child, line 42 $15,000: line 43 $3,000; line 47 limited to $2,500.
- Carryforward year, $1,000 of Indiana credit already claimed for the child in earlier years, line 43 $2,200: line 46 = $2,500 - $1,000 = $1,500; line 47 = $1,500.
## Records
Keep a copy of federal Form 8839, the federal Adoption Credit Carryforward Worksheets (if any) and federal Form 1040; DOR can request them. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #111.
## Questions
- Did you claim a federal adoption credit this year or carry one forward, and from which years?
- For each child: name, year of birth, identification number, and whether claimed as a dependent?
- How much Indiana adoption credit have you claimed for each child in earlier years?
- Were you an Indiana resident for the whole year?
## Common Errors
- Entering 20% of the whole family's federal credit instead of figuring each child separately
- Exceeding $2,500 per child across all years by ignoring earlier Indiana claims (line 45)
- Leaving out the federal refundable part on line 34
- Using a carryforward older than five years
- Confusing this credit with the Schedule 3 additional exemption for an adopted child (Schedule IN-DEP-A)
## Prompt
- We adopted a child and got the federal adoption credit. What does Indiana allow?
- I am carrying forward a federal adoption credit. Can I still get the Indiana credit?
