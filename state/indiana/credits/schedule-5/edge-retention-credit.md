# Economic Development for a Growing Economy Retention Credit - EDGE-R (IT-40 Schedule 5, Line 9)
## Description
The EDGE-R credit is the job-retention counterpart of the EDGE credit: it is available to businesses that conduct certain activities designed to foster job retention in Indiana under an agreement with the Indiana Economic Development Corporation. Individuals with business income, including owners of S corporations, partnerships and limited liability companies, may be eligible. Like EDGE, it is refundable.
## How to claim
- Complete and enclose Schedule IN-EDGE-R and enter its line 19 on Schedule 5, line 9.
- The figures come from Schedule IN K-1 or the approved IEDC credit agreement letter. Include the completed Schedule IN K-1.
- If all or part of your share was claimed by the pass-through entity, you may not claim the previously claimed credit yourself.
- Schedule IN-EDGE-R was not among the 2025 form texts used for this taxonomy; its lines 1-18 are not described here.
## Questions
- Do you have a Schedule IN K-1 or IEDC agreement letter showing an EDGE-R (retention) credit?
- Did the entity already claim any part of it?
## Common Errors
- Combining EDGE and EDGE-R on one line; each has its own schedule and line
- Claiming without Schedule IN-EDGE-R and Schedule IN K-1
