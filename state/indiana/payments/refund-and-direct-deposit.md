# Refund and Direct Deposit (Form IT-40, Lines 21-22)
## Description
You have a refund if line 18 (overpayment after donations) is greater than the total of line 19d (applied to next year's estimates) and line 20 (estimated tax penalty). Line 21 = line 18 minus lines 19d and 20. If the result is less than zero, you have an amount due (see amount-due-and-payment.md); line 19d cannot be greater than line 18 minus line 20.
## Direct deposit (line 22)
- 22a Routing number: nine digits, beginning 01 through 12 or 21 through 32. Do not use a deposit slip to verify it.
- 22b Account number: up to 17 digits, left to right, no hyphens or symbols; leave unused boxes blank.
- 22c Type: Checking, Savings, or Hoosier Works MC.
- 22d: place an "X" if the refund will go to an account outside the United States; DOR will then mail a paper check.
## Hoosier Works MasterCard
If you have a Hoosier Works MasterCard, enter its 12-digit account number (upper right of the monthly statement) on line 22b, leave line 22a blank, and check "Hoosier Works MC" on line 22c. Do not use the 16-digit card number. The deposit appears on the monthly statement.
## Refund offsets
Indiana law requires money owed to the state, its agencies and certain federal agencies (past-due taxes, student loans, child support, SNAP benefits, IRS levies) to be deducted before a refund is issued. DOR sends a letter for state or federal tax debts; other agencies notify you of their offsets.
## Timing and status
E-filed returns average 10 to 14 business days; paper returns can take up to 12 weeks. Late withholding statements and Schedule C losses commonly delay refunds. Check status with the exact refund amount and an SSN from the return through DOR's online refund status, INTIME, or 317-232-2240.
## Statute of limitations
For 2025 returns, refund claims must be made by April 17, 2029 (Nov. 15, 2029 if filing under extension); no refund is issued after it expires. The 2026 booklet will give the 2026 dates.
## Example
Line 18 $640; line 19d $200; line 20 $0 → line 21 = $440, deposited to checking: 22a routing, 22b account, 22c Checking.
## Questions
- Do you want your refund by direct deposit? To checking, savings or a Hoosier Works MasterCard?
- Is the account outside the United States?
- Do you owe any state agency, child support, or federal debts that could offset the refund?
## Common Errors
- Using the routing number from a deposit slip
- Entering the 16-digit Hoosier Works card number or filling line 22a for the card
- Not reducing the refund for line 19d and line 20
- Leaving line 22d blank for a foreign account
## Prompt
- How do I get my Indiana refund deposited on my Hoosier Works card?
- Why is my Indiana refund smaller than line 18?
