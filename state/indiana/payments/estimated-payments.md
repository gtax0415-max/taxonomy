# Estimated Tax Paid (Schedule 5, Line 4)
## Description
Enter the total of estimated tax payments made for 2026, including any extension payment made with Form IT-9 (or an online Indiana extension payment). Do not include estimated tax paid for 2027. The 2025 return's line 19d amount applied to 2026 estimates is a 2026 estimated payment.
## 2026 installment due dates
Per the 2025 booklet, 2026 installments are due April 15, 2026 (1st), June 15, 2026 (2nd), Sept. 15, 2026 (3rd) and Jan. 15, 2027 (4th). An amount applied from the 2025 return is treated as paid on the day that return was filed (postmarked): filed April 15, 2026 counts as the 1st installment; June 3, 2026 as the 2nd; July 22, 2026 as the 3rd.
## Who should pay estimated tax
You should pay estimated tax if you have income not subject to Indiana withholding, or expect withholding to be too low, and you expect to owe more than $1,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) when you file. Payments can be made through INTIME (electronic bank payment or Visa, MasterCard or Discover cards; up to four payments can be scheduled a year in advance), by Form ES-40 with a check or money order, or by direct debit of up to four payments when e-filing.
## Example
Morgan paid $500 on each 2026 due date and paid $300 with Form IT-9 on April 15, 2027. The 2025 return applied $120 to 2026. Line 4 = $2,000 + $120 + $300 = $2,420.
## Questions
- What estimated payments did you make for 2026, and on what dates?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?
- Did you pay with an Indiana extension?
## Common Errors
- Including a payment intended for 2027
- Omitting the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026
- Reporting the extension payment separately or not at all
- Reporting PTET as estimated tax
