# Donation Check-Offs (Schedule IN-DONATE; Form IT-40, Line 17)
## Description
You may contribute all or a portion of your Form IT-40, line 16 overpayment to three funds. On Schedule IN-DONATE, line 1, enter the fund name, its 3-digit code and the amount. Line 2 is the total of lines 1a through 1c and is entered on Form IT-40, line 17. Donations reduce your refund; they do not reduce tax and are not credits. Enclose Schedule IN-DONATE (it is the bottom of Schedule 5, enclosure sequence 04).
## Funds
| Code | Fund | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 200 | Indiana Nongame Wildlife Fund | Funds the Indiana Wildlife Diversity Program: protection and management of more than 750 wildlife species in Indiana, from songbirds and salamanders to state-endangered Trumpeter swans and spotted turtles |
| 201 | Military Family Relief Fund | Indiana Department of Veterans Affairs emergency grants to military and veteran families for food, housing, utilities, medical services, transportation and other essential needs; has helped more than 2000 families since 2007 |
| 202 | Public K through 12 Education Fund | Helps fund public education for kindergarten through grade 12 in Indiana |
## Limitation (booklet page 35)
- The total of the donations cannot exceed the overpayment on Form IT-40, line 16.
- If the total designated is more than the available overpayment, the donations are reduced on a pro rata basis. Example: Sam designates $20 to each fund ($60 total) but his overpayment is $51; each donation is reduced evenly to $17.
- If you donate and also apply part of the overpayment to next year's estimated tax, the overpayment is applied first to the selected funds, then to the estimated tax account, and any remainder is refunded. Example: Aaron donates $100 to the Nongame Wildlife Fund and applies $50 to next year's estimates, but his overpayment is only $110. The full $100 goes to the fund and $10 is applied to estimates.
## Without an overpayment
If you do not have an overpayment, do not change your return. You can give directly: online donation for the Nongame Wildlife Fund through the DNR, or a check payable to the Military Family Relief Fund sent to the Indiana Department of Veterans Affairs, 302 W. Washington Street, Suite E-120, Indianapolis, IN 46204.
## Example
Line 16 overpayment $300. Donations: 200 Nongame Wildlife $25, 202 Public K through 12 Education $25. Schedule IN-DONATE line 2 = $50; Form IT-40, line 17 = $50; line 18 = $250.
## Questions
- Do you want to donate part of your overpayment? To which funds and how much?
- Do you also want to apply part of the overpayment to next year's estimated tax?
## Common Errors
- Leaving off the 3-digit code
- Donating more than line 16 (donations are cut pro rata)
- Expecting a donation when the return shows an amount due
- Treating the donation as a credit or deduction
## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my Indiana refund to wildlife?
- I owe tax. Can I still give to the Military Family Relief Fund on my return?
