# Late Penalty and Interest (Form IT-40, Lines 24-25)
## Description
If the return is filed after the original due date (April 15, 2027 for the 2026 return; confirm against the 2026 form) and you have an amount due, you may owe a penalty and you will owe interest.
## Line 24 - Penalty
Penalty is 10% of the amount due (line 23 minus line 20) or $5 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is greater.

Exception: no penalty is due if you have an extension of time to file, file and pay the remaining tax by the extended due date, and prepaid at least 90% of the amount due by the original due date.
## Line 25 - Interest
Interest is owed, even with an extension, if the return is filed after the original due date and there is an amount due. Figure interest on line 23 minus line 20 using the rate in Departmental Notice #3. The Departmental Notice #3 published September 2026 lists 7% for calendar year 2026 and 6% for calendar year 2027. A 2026 return balance paid after April 15, 2027 accrues interest during 2027 at 6%; any portion of the delay falling in 2028 uses the 2028 rate when published.
## Example
2026 return filed June 15, 2027 without an extension; line 23 $1,200; line 20 $0.
- Line 24: 10% x $1,200 = $120 (greater than $5).
- Line 25: $1,200 x 6% x 61/365 = about $12 (illustrative day count; follow DOR's method).
- Line 26 = $1,200 + $120 + $12 = $1,332.
## Questions
- When did you (or will you) file and pay?
- Did you have a federal or Indiana extension, and how much had you paid by April 15, 2027?
## Common Errors
- Using the 2026 interest rate for a 2027 late payment
- Computing penalty and interest on line 23 without subtracting line 20
- Assuming an extension removes interest
- Entering less than the $5 minimum penalty
