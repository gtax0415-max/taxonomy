# Amount Due and Payment (Form IT-40, Lines 23 and 26)
## Description
Line 23 is the tax still owed. If line 21 is less than zero, enter it on line 23 as a positive number. Or, if line 15 is greater than line 14: subtract line 14 from line 15 (A), add any line 20 estimated tax penalty (B), and enter A + B on line 23. Line 26 (Amount You Owe) = lines 23 + 24 + 25.
## Payment options
- Electronic: DOR's INTIME portal accepts electronic bank payments (ACH/e-check) with no fee, and Visa, MasterCard and Discover debit or credit cards (fees apply). No login is needed; use "Make a Payment."
- Mail: check or money order payable to Indiana Department of Revenue, sent with the return to Indiana Department of Revenue, P.O. Box 7224, Indianapolis, IN 46207-7224. Do not send cash. Returns without payment go to P.O. Box 40, Indianapolis, IN 46206-0040.
- In person at a DOR district office (most are appointment-only): exact cash, money order, cashier's check or personal check. All payments in U.S. funds.
## Payment plan
If you cannot pay the full amount, you may set up a payment plan in INTIME after your return finishes processing. Penalty and interest are due on all amounts paid after the original due date.
## Returned payments
A payment that cannot be collected in full (check, card, electronic transfer) is assessed a $35 penalty (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), due immediately by certified check, bank draft or money order.
## Example
Line 15 $3,980; line 14 $3,600; line 20 $25. A = $380; B = $25; line 23 = $405. Filed on time: line 26 = $405.
## Questions
- How will you pay the balance due: INTIME bank payment, card, check or in person?
- Can you pay in full by the due date, or do you need a payment plan?
## Common Errors
- Forgetting to add the line 20 penalty on line 23
- Mailing a payment to the P.O. Box 40 address
- Assuming a payment plan stops interest
