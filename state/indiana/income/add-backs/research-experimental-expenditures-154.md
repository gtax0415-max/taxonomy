# Specified Research and Experimental Expenses Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line 6, Code 154)
## Description
If you claimed a federal deduction for specified research and experimental expenses that must be amortized for federal purposes under IRC Section 174, add back the amount actually deducted federally with code 154. The Indiana-allowable amount is claimed on Schedule 2 with code 641 (outside this folder).
## 2025 booklet rules
- If you claim a full federal deduction for domestic research expenses for 2025, do not enter a code 154 add-back for those expenses.
- If you elect a full federal deduction for domestic research expenses for 2022 through 2024 and amend the 2022-2024 federal returns, amend the Indiana returns to reverse any code 154 add-backs based on those expenses, and claim no further modifications after 2024.
- Example: $100,000 of specified research expenses incurred in 2025, $10,000 amortized federally. Code 154 adds back $10,000; code 641 deducts $100,000. For 2026-2030, Individual A continues to use code 154 to report timing differences.
## SEA 243 (2026 legislation)
- IC 6-3-2-29 now defines specified research or experimental expenditures, for taxable years beginning after Dec. 31, 2024, as foreign research or experimental expenditures (IRC 174(b)) and domestic research or experimental expenditures (IRC 174A(b)).
- For taxable years beginning after Dec. 31, 2021, add back the amount deducted under P.L. 119-21, Section 70302(f)(2) for the year.
- A taxpayer eligible for the P.L. 119-21, Section 70302(f)(1) retroactive deduction who does not make the election is treated as required to capitalize. A taxpayer who makes the election follows the federal treatment, must amend all Indiana returns that reported modifications under this section (or for which an amended federal return was filed), and the amended federal return is treated as a final IRS adjustment on the later of its filing date or Oct. 31, 2025.
- Confirm against the 2026 form and instructions how code 154 and code 641 apply to 2026 domestic research expenditures and to amounts deducted under Section 70302(f)(2).
## Example
2026: a sole proprietor amortizes $40,000 of foreign research expenses federally, deducting $4,000. Code 154 = $4,000; the Indiana-allowable amount is claimed with code 641 according to the 2026 instructions.
## Questions
- Did you have research or experimental expenditures in 2022 through 2026? Domestic or foreign?
- Did you make any P.L. 119-21 retroactive election or deduct unamortized amounts under Section 70302(f)(2)?
- Did you report codes 154 and 641 on prior Indiana returns?
## Common Errors
- Adding back fully deducted 2025 or later domestic research expenses
- Not amending prior Indiana returns after a federal retroactive election
- Dropping the multi-year code 154 timing differences
## Prompt
- Do I add back research expenses on my Indiana return for 2026?
