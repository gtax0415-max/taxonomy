# Out-of-State Municipal Obligation Interest Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line 3)
## Description
Interest from a direct obligation of a state or political subdivision other than Indiana (out of state, or OOS) is excluded from federal AGI but is taxable by Indiana if the obligation was acquired after Dec. 31, 2011. Add that interest back on line 3.
## What is not added back
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Interest on obligations held or acquired before Jan. 1, 2012 | Not subject to Indiana income tax |
| Interest on Indiana state or local obligations | Indiana obligation |
| Interest on obligations of Puerto Rico, Guam, Virgin Islands, American Samoa or Northern Mariana | Exempt under federal law; no add-back |
| Taxable municipal bond interest already in federal AGI | Already included on line 1 |
## Funds
For mutual funds, add back only the portion of exempt-interest dividends from non-Indiana state and local obligations acquired after Dec. 31, 2011, using the fund's state breakdown. (Income Tax Information Bulletin #19 has more detail.)
## Example
Federal tax-exempt interest of $2,400: $900 from Indiana bonds, $1,100 from an Ohio bond bought in 2018, $400 from an Illinois bond bought in 2010. Line 3 = $1,100.
## Questions
- Did you receive tax-exempt interest? From which states' bonds?
- When did you acquire each out-of-state bond?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Indiana municipal interest
- Adding back interest on pre-2012 holdings
- Adding back U.S. territory bond interest
## Prompt
- Is interest on my Ohio municipal bond taxable in Indiana?
