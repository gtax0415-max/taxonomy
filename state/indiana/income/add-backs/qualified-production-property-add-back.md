# Qualified Production Property Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line or Code to Be Assigned on the 2026 Form)
## Description
New for 2026 (IC 6-3-2-30, SEA 243, effective retroactively to July 4, 2025). Federal law (P.L. 119-21) allows a special depreciation allowance under IRC Section 168(n) for "qualified production property" as defined in IRC Section 168(n)(2). Indiana does not follow it. If you elect to claim the 168(n) allowance for qualified production property used by you and placed in service during the current or an earlier taxable year, add or subtract the amount needed to make your Indiana adjusted gross income equal to what it would be if no 168(n) election had been made.

The 2025 Schedule 1 has no line or code for this modification. Code to be assigned on the 2026 form; confirm where it is reported before filing.
## Rules
- Placed-in-service year: typically a positive add-back (the 168(n) allowance minus the depreciation otherwise allowable for Indiana).
- Later years: Indiana depreciation exceeds federal, so the entry is negative, as with bonus depreciation.
- Election out of other allowances: if a 168(n) election causes you to be treated as electing not to claim other IRC 168 special depreciation allowances, you are treated as having made that election for Indiana too.
- Recapture under IRC 168(n)(5): you are treated as having made the 168(n) election and as having disposed of the property on the 168(n)(5) date; report any income from the property for that year subject to this modification, and report later depreciation, gain or loss as otherwise provided by the Code.
## Example
In 2026 a sole proprietor places a $400,000 qualified production facility in service and elects the 168(n) allowance, deducting $400,000 federally. Without the election, assume $10,256 of depreciation would be allowable. The 2026 Indiana modification is $389,744 positive; in later years the Indiana depreciation is entered as negative amounts.
## Questions
- Did you or a pass-through entity elect the IRC 168(n) special depreciation allowance for qualified production property?
- What depreciation would have been allowed without the election?
- Did any 168(n)(5) recapture occur?
## Common Errors
- Treating the 168(n) allowance as ordinary bonus depreciation on line 4 without confirming the 2026 form's placement
- Not keeping a separate Indiana depreciation schedule for the property
- Ignoring recapture under 168(n)(5)
