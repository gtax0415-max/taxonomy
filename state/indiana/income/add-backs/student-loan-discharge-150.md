# Student Loan Discharge Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line 6, Code 150)
## Description
For 2025, if a student loan was discharged during the year and you excluded the discharge from federal gross income, you added back the excluded amount with code 150. SEA 243 amended IC 6-3-1-3.5 so the add-back for amounts excluded under IRC Section 108(f)(5) applies to taxable years ending after Dec. 31, 2020 and before Jan. 1, 2026. A calendar-year 2026 return therefore has no code 150 add-back. Keep this code for amended returns for 2021 through 2025 and confirm on the 2026 form that the code has been removed.
## Exceptions (2025 rules)
No add-back was required for amounts discharged or repaid through:
- the Public Service Loan Forgiveness Program;
- the Teacher Loan Forgiveness Program;
- the National Health Service Corps Loan Repayment Program;
- other programs that qualify under IRC 108(f)(4);
- a Closed School or Defense to Repayment discharge, if the IRS permits exclusion;
- death or total and permanent disability of the student;
- bankruptcy;
- insolvency, limited to the amount of insolvency (other exceptions apply first).
Loans forgiven in 2025 under income-based repayment plan corrections were subject to the add-back (and the insolvency exception).
## Example
Jo's $22,000 income-driven repayment balance was forgiven in November 2025 and excluded federally: 2025 code 150 = $22,000. A forgiveness in 2026 has no Indiana code 150 add-back; whatever federal AGI includes simply flows through Form IT-40, line 1.
