# Employer Student Loan Payment Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line 6, Code 148)
## Description
If your employer paid any amount toward your student loans and you excluded the payment from federal gross income, add back the amount you excluded. This applies whether the employer paid you or paid the loan on your behalf. Indiana provides a related Schedule 2 deduction for the interest portion (Employer Student Loan Payment Interest Deduction, booklet page 20), which is outside this folder.
## Example
Employer paid $4,800 of Sam's student loan in 2026 and excluded it from W-2 box 1. Code 148 = $4,800. If $900 of that payment was interest, Sam may claim the Schedule 2 interest deduction for that portion.
## Questions
- Did your employer pay any of your student loans in 2026? Was it excluded from your W-2 wages?
- How much of the payment was interest?
## Common Errors
- Not adding back because the employer paid the lender directly
- Adding back an amount already included in W-2 wages
## Prompt
- My employer pays my student loans tax-free. Does Indiana tax it?
