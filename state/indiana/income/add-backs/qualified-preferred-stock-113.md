# Qualified Preferred Stock (Schedule 1, Line 6, Code 113)
## Description
Code 113 applies if you:
- had losses from the sale or exchange of preferred stock in the Federal National Mortgage Association (Fannie Mae) or Federal Home Loan Mortgage Corporation (Freddie Mac);
- treated the loss as ordinary for federal income tax purposes in the year incurred; and
- previously added back an amount that has not been allowed as a deduction.
You may continue deducting the loss not previously allowed as a capital loss, computed under the federal capital loss limits (IRC 1211 and 1212). Enter the deduction with code 113.
## Example
Remaining unallowed Indiana capital loss from a 2008 Freddie Mac preferred stock add-back: $9,000. Assume that, with no other capital gains, the IRC 1211 and 1212 limits allow $3,000 of capital loss this year: code 113 = -3000 in 2026, leaving $6,000.
## Questions
- Did you add back losses on Fannie Mae or Freddie Mac preferred stock in earlier years? What balance remains?
- Do you have other capital gains or losses in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the whole remaining balance in one year without the capital loss limits
- Entering the amount as positive
