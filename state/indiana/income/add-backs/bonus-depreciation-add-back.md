# Bonus Depreciation Add-Back (Schedule 1, Line 4)
## Description
Indiana does not allow the additional first-year special depreciation deduction under IRC Section 168(k) (bonus depreciation) for property placed in service after Sept. 11, 2001. Figure the net income or loss that would have been in federal AGI without bonus depreciation, and enter the difference, which may be positive or negative, on line 4. The add-back continues for 2026 under the January 1, 2026 conformity date (SEA 243).
## Later years
After the initial adjustment, keep a separate Indiana depreciation schedule. In later years Indiana depreciation exceeds federal depreciation on the same asset; enter that excess as a negative number on line 4. On disposition, Indiana gain or loss uses the Indiana basis.
## Special situations
- Property acquired in a like-kind exchange, or in a year with an excess business loss: special rules apply (Income Tax Information Bulletin #118).
- Excess business loss under IRC 461(l): report the full add-back here and the offsetting code 151 deduction on line 6 (see excess-business-loss-modification-151.md).
- Excess inclusion income: see code 153.
- Qualified production property (IRC 168(n)): handled by the separate 2026 modification; an election under 168(n) can also be treated as an election out of other special depreciation allowances (see qualified-production-property-add-back.md).
## Examples
- Mack took bonus depreciation; without it he would have deducted $500 of depreciation. After refiguring, he adds back $1,500 (his bonus deduction was $2,000).
- Ann made an initial bonus adjustment last year. This year she is entitled to $150 of additional Indiana depreciation: line 4 = -150.
## Questions
- Did you or a pass-through entity claim bonus depreciation in 2026 or earlier years?
- Do you keep a separate Indiana depreciation schedule?
- Did you dispose of assets on which bonus depreciation was taken?
- Were assets acquired in a like-kind exchange, or is there an excess business loss?
## Common Errors
- Adding back the full bonus amount without allowing regular Indiana depreciation
- Forgetting the negative entries in later years
- Using federal basis to compute Indiana gain on sale
- Missing bonus depreciation passed through on K-1s
## Prompt
- I took 100% bonus depreciation federally. What does Indiana require?
- How do I recover the depreciation I added back in prior years?
