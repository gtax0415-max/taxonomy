# Taxable Railroad Retirement Benefits (Schedule 2, Line 6)
## Description
Indiana does not tax railroad retirement benefits issued by the U.S. Railroad Retirement Board. If you included such benefits on federal Form 1040/1040-SR, line 5b (2025 form line; confirm on the 2026 federal form), enter that amount on Schedule 2, line 6.
## What does not go here
| Item | Where it goes |
|---|---|
| Unemployment or sickness benefits from the Railroad Retirement Board | Schedule 2, line 11, code 624 |
| Other pension or retirement income | no deduction on this line |
| Railroad benefits already included in the Form 1040, line 6b amount | Schedule 2, line 5 |
## Example
Form RRB-1099-R shows $18,000 of tier 2 benefits, all taxable on Form 1040, line 5b (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Schedule 2, line 6 = $18,000.
## Questions
- Did you receive benefits from the U.S. Railroad Retirement Board? Were they reported on Form 1040, line 5b or line 6b?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the same railroad benefits on both line 5 and line 6
- Putting railroad unemployment or sickness benefits here instead of code 624
## Prompt
- Are my railroad retirement benefits taxed in Indiana?
