# Qualified Passenger Vehicle Loan Interest Deduction, 2026 Only (Schedule 2, Line 11)
## Description
For the 2026 tax year only, Indiana allows a deduction equal to the qualified passenger vehicle loan interest deducted federally under IRC section 163 and attributable to the exception in IRC 163(h)(4) (IC 6-3-2-33, SEA 243, effective January 1, 2026). "Qualified passenger vehicle loan interest" has the meaning in IRC 163(h)(4)(B). The deduction starts from the federal amount on federal Schedule 1-A, line 30 (line per Income Tax Information Bulletin #128, which refers to the 2025 version of the federal form), including federal phaseouts, and cannot exceed it. It is allowed only for interest paid or accrued while an Indiana resident and is not allowed to estates or trusts. The Schedule 2 code number is not yet published: code to be assigned on the 2026 form.
## Full-year residents
The deduction equals the federal deduction. The location and registration of the vehicle do not matter: an Indiana resident who registers and keeps a vehicle in Florida still qualifies.
## Married filing jointly
The allowable interest is determined separately for each spouse (the spouse who would be treated as paying it if unmarried). Spouses may allocate the deduction between themselves but not more to one spouse than would have been allowable to that spouse filing separately. Bulletin #128, Example 10: Jack (Marion County) and Diane (Hamilton County) each paid $11,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) of interest and claimed a $10,000 federal deduction. The state deduction is $10,000, and they must allocate the $10,000 between themselves for county tax on Schedule CT-40.
## Bulletin wording
The bulletin's vehicle loan section says the Indiana deduction "begins with the amount of qualified overtime compensation allowable as a deduction for federal income tax purposes", which appears to be a drafting slip; the same paragraph identifies the amount as the line 30 vehicle loan interest deduction, and the statute refers to the IRC 163(h)(4) interest deduction.
## Example
A full-year Hamilton County resident claimed a $3,100 federal deduction for interest on a new car loan. Schedule 2, line 11: qualified passenger vehicle loan interest, code to be assigned on the 2026 form, $3,100.

## Common Errors
- Deducting all interest paid instead of the federal deduction allowed
- Allocating to one spouse more than that spouse's own interest
- Assuming an out-of-state registration disqualifies the deduction
