# Taxable Social Security Benefits (Schedule 2, Line 5)
## Description
Indiana does not tax Social Security income. Enter the amount from federal Form 1040/1040-SR, line 6b (2025 form line; confirm on the 2026 federal form) on Schedule 2, line 5. This removes all Social Security included in federal AGI.
## What does not go here
- Other pension or retirement income. The booklet says: do not enter any other types of pension or retirement income on lines 5 and 6.
- Social Security disability income is also untaxed by Indiana and is covered by this line through Form 1040, line 6b; it does not qualify for the disability retirement deduction (code 602).
## Example
Form 1040, line 6a shows $24,000 of benefits and line 6b shows $9,500 taxable (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Schedule 2, line 5 = $9,500.
## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security benefits, and what is the taxable amount on Form 1040, line 6b?
## Common Errors
- Entering gross benefits (line 6a) instead of the taxable amount (line 6b)
- Adding pension or IRA income to this line
## Prompt
- Does Indiana tax Social Security?
