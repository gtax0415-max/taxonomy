# Indiana Enrichment Scholarship Account Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 638)
## Description
A deduction is available if you received an annual grant amount distributed to your Indiana enrichment scholarship account that is used to pay for qualified expenses. Qualified expenses are enrichment materials, activities or programs approved by the Indiana Department of Education to improve student proficiency in math or reading.
## What does not qualify
- Grants not included in federal gross income.
- Grant amounts used for items other than qualified expenses.
## 2026 status
The 2025 Legislative Synopsis (HEA 1002-2025) reports that student enrichment grants under IC 20-52 are repealed effective July 1, 2025, and that the related amounts are removed from the adjusted gross income definition. The 2025 booklet still lists code 638. Confirm against the 2026 form whether code 638 remains and for which grants.
## Example
An enrichment account received a $500 grant used for an approved reading tutoring program and included in federal gross income (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 638 = $500, if the code remains for 2026.
## Questions
- Did you receive an Indiana enrichment scholarship account grant, and when?
- Was it included in federal gross income and spent on approved math or reading enrichment?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a grant that was not in federal gross income
- Claiming the code without checking whether it remains on the 2026 form

- Is the enrichment scholarship grant for my child taxable in Indiana?
