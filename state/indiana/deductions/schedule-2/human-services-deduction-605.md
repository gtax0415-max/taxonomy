# Human Services Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 605)
## Description
The human services deduction is intended to eliminate any individual income tax imposed on Medicaid recipients who are living in a:
- Hospital,
- Skilled nursing facility,
- Intermediate care facility,
- Licensed county home,
- Licensed boarding or residential home, or
- Certified Christian Science facility (listed with and certified by the Commission for Accreditation of Christian Science Nursing Organizations/Facilities, Inc.).
The goal is to reduce the individual's adjusted gross income tax liability to zero.
## How to determine eligibility
1. Prepare the return without the human services deduction.
2. If a refund is due, you are generally not eligible: file without the deduction and the refund will be issued.
3. If an amount is due, you are eligible to use the deduction.
Generally the deduction should not be used together with most tax credits to create a refund.
## Example
A Medicaid recipient in a skilled nursing facility has Indiana AGI of $6,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) before this deduction and no withholding, so tax is due. The deduction is the amount that brings the Indiana tax to zero, here $6,000 of income, entered with code 605.
## Questions
- Are you a Medicaid recipient living in one of the listed facilities?
- Without this deduction, does your return show a refund or an amount due?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the deduction when the return already shows a refund
- Using it to generate a refund of credits
## Prompt
- My mother is on Medicaid in a nursing home. Does she owe Indiana income tax?
