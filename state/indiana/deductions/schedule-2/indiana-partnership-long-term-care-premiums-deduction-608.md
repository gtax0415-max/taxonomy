# Indiana Partnership Long-Term Care Policy Premiums Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 608)
## Description
You may deduct premiums paid during the year for Indiana partnership long-term care insurance for yourself and/or your spouse.
## Qualifying policy
The policy shows this statement in a box on the outline of coverage, the application or the front page of the policy:
"This policy qualifies under the Indiana Long-Term Care program for Medicaid Asset Protection. This policy may provide benefits in excess of the asset protection provided in the Indiana Long-Term Care program."
If the statement is not in a box on your policy, it is not a qualifying policy.
## No double benefit
Certain self-employed individuals deduct these premiums on federal Form 1040/1040-SR. The Indiana deduction is the premiums paid minus any amount already deducted on the federal return.
## Married filing separately
You may not deduct amounts paid by or on behalf of your spouse.
## Example
A couple paid $3,600 of partnership policy premiums (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form); $1,000 was deducted federally as self-employed health insurance. Code 608 = $2,600.
## Questions
- Does your long-term care policy carry the Indiana Long-Term Care program statement in a box?
- How much did you pay in premiums, and was any deducted on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Deducting premiums for a non-partnership long-term care policy
- Double-deducting premiums already taken federally
- On separate returns, deducting premiums for the spouse's policy
## Prompt
- Are my long-term care insurance premiums deductible in Indiana?
