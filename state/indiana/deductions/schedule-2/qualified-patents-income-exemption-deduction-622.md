# Qualified Patents Income Exemption Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 622)
## Description
Some income from qualified patents included in federal taxable income may be exempt from Indiana adjusted gross income tax. A qualified patent is a utility patent or a plant patent issued after December 31, 2007, for an invention resulting from a development process conducted in Indiana. A design patent does not qualify.
## How to claim
Complete Schedule IN-PAT and keep it with your records; DOR can request it. Enter code 622 under Schedule 2, line 11. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #104.
## Example
An inventor received royalties from a utility patent issued in 2015 for an invention developed in Indiana; Schedule IN-PAT shows $20,000 of exempt income (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 622 = $20,000.
## Questions
- Do you receive income from a utility or plant patent issued after 2007 for an invention developed in Indiana?
- Have you completed Schedule IN-PAT?
## Common Errors
- Claiming income from design patents
- Claiming the full patent income without Schedule IN-PAT
## Prompt
- I earn royalties on a patent. Is any of it exempt in Indiana?
