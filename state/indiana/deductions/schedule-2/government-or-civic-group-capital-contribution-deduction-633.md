# Government or Civic Group Capital Contribution Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 633)
## Description
A deduction for certain capital contributions made by a government or civic group. Deduct any eligible contributions listed on a Schedule K-1 you received from an S corporation, or from an estate or trust that owns a portion of an S corporation and through which you receive a distribution. Keep the Schedule K-1(s); DOR can request them.
## Example
An S corporation K-1 reports your $2,500 share of an eligible capital contribution from a city (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 633 = $2,500.
## Questions
- Did an S corporation K-1 (directly or through an estate or trust) report a government or civic group capital contribution?
## Common Errors
- Claiming contributions not reported on a K-1
- Claiming it through a partnership K-1 (the booklet names S corporations, and estates or trusts owning S corporation interests)
