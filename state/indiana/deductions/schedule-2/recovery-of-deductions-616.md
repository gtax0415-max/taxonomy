# Recovery of Deductions (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 616)
## Description
Indiana generally does not allow itemized deductions. If you reported recovered itemized deductions as "other income" on federal Schedule 1, line 8 (2025 form line; confirm on the 2026 federal form), enter that amount with code 616. A recovery is a return of an amount you deducted in an earlier year; the most common are refunds, reimbursements and rebates of previously itemized deductions.
## Eligibility
You are not eligible if you did not complete the "other income" line on federal Schedule 1. State tax refunds go on Schedule 2, line 3, not here.
## Booklet wording
The booklet describes the itemized deductions as those "from IRS Form 172, Part 1, or the equivalent worksheet from IRS Publication 536", which appears to carry over NOL wording; the operative rule is the recovered amount reported as other income on federal Schedule 1.
## Example
In 2026 a taxpayer received a $700 reimbursement of medical expenses itemized federally in 2025 and reported $700 as other income on federal Schedule 1 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 616 = $700.
## Questions
- Did you report a recovery of a previously itemized deduction (other than a state tax refund) as other income on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Putting a state tax refund under code 616 instead of line 3
- Claiming code 616 for a recovery not included in federal income
## Prompt
- I got reimbursed for medical bills I itemized last year. Does Indiana tax that?
