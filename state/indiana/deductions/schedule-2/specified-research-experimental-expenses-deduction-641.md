# Specified Research and Experimental Expenses Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 641)
## Description
If you claimed a federal deduction for specified research and experimental expenses that must be amortized for federal purposes under IRC section 174, deduct the amount of expenses paid or incurred in the current year. The federal amortization is added back with Code 154 on Schedule 1, which also handles later-year timing differences. Do not claim code 641 for research expenses whose deduction is disallowed under IRC 280C(c).
## 2026 law (SEA 243)
- For taxable years beginning after December 31, 2024, "specified research or experimental expenditures" means foreign research or experimental expenditures (IRC 174(b)) and domestic research or experimental expenditures (IRC 174A(b)).
- Amounts deducted federally under P.L. 119-21, Section 70302(f)(2) are added back for years after 2021.
- A taxpayer who elects the retroactive federal deduction (P.L. 119-21, Section 70302(f)(1)) treats the expenditures the same way for Indiana and must amend Indiana returns that reported modifications.
## Booklet guidance
- If you claim a full federal deduction for domestic research expenses for the year, do not enter code 641 for those expenses (the federal amount already reduces AGI).
- Example (booklet): Individual A incurred $100,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) of specified research expenses in 2025 and reported $10,000 of amortized expenses federally. Code 641 = $100,000; Code 154 adds back $10,000. For 2026-2030 Individual A continues to use Code 154 for timing differences.
## Example for 2026
Foreign research expenditures of $40,000 paid in 2026 must be amortized federally (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 641 = $40,000 and the federal amortization taken for 2026 is added back with Code 154. Domestic research expenses deducted in full federally get no code 641 entry.
## Questions
- Did you incur research or experimental expenditures this year? Domestic or foreign?
- Are they deducted in full federally (IRC 174A) or amortized (IRC 174)?
- Did you make the retroactive federal election for 2022 through 2024?
## Common Errors
- Claiming code 641 for domestic expenses already deducted in full federally
- Forgetting the Code 154 add-back of federal amortization
- Not amending prior Indiana returns after a retroactive federal election
