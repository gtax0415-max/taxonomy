# Indiana-only Tax-exempt Bonds Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 636)
## Description
If you had interest from a bond issued by or in the name of certain Indiana government subdivisions or entities, or amounts received upon redemption or maturity of the bond, deduct any interest or other income included in federal gross income. See IC 6-8-5-1.
## What does not qualify
- Bond interest excluded from federal gross income.
- If you sell the bond, any amount for which it is sold in excess of your purchase price.
## Example
A taxable Indiana county bond paid $800 of interest included in federal AGI (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 636 = $800. A gain on selling that bond above purchase price is not deductible.
## Questions
- Do you hold taxable bonds issued by an Indiana government unit? How much interest was included in federal income?
## Common Errors
- Deducting federally tax-exempt municipal interest (already excluded)
- Deducting gain on sale above purchase price
## Prompt
- I own taxable Indiana municipal bonds. Is the interest taxed by Indiana?
