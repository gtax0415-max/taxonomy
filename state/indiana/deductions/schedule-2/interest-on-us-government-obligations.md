# Interest on U.S. Government Obligations (Schedule 2, Line 4)
## Description
If federal AGI on Form IT-40, line 1 includes interest from a direct obligation of the U.S. government, you can deduct that interest. Examples are U.S. savings bonds, U.S. Treasury bills and U.S. government certificates. This interest is usually reported on federal Schedule B.
## What qualifies
- Interest from direct U.S. government obligations that is included in federal AGI.
- Interest income from U.S. government obligations reported to you from a trust, estate, partnership or S corporation.
## What does not qualify
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| U.S. savings bond interest excluded for higher education expenses (federal Schedule B, line 3) | already excluded from federal AGI |
| Interest on obligations that are not direct U.S. government obligations | not covered by this line |
## Example
Form 1099-INT shows $350 of interest in box 3 from Treasury bills, and a partnership K-1 reports $50 of U.S. obligation interest (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Line 4 = $400.
## Questions
- Do you have interest from U.S. savings bonds, Treasury bills, notes or bonds?
- Did any K-1 report U.S. government interest?
- Did you exclude savings bond interest for education expenses?
## Common Errors
- Deducting bond interest already excluded on federal Schedule B, line 3
- Missing U.S. obligation interest passed through on a K-1
