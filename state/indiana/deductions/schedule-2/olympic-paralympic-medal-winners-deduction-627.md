# Olympic/Paralympic Medal Winners Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 627)
## Description
If you won a gold, silver and/or bronze medal at the Olympic or Paralympic games, you may deduct the value of the medal(s) plus prize money received during the year from the United States Olympic Committee for winning the medal(s). Amounts already deducted or excluded in determining federal AGI are not deductible. The deduction may be claimed only in the tax year the medal was won.
## Example
A medalist received $37,500 of USOC prize money and a medal valued at $1,000, all included in federal AGI (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 627 = $38,500. If the prize money was excluded federally, only the amount still in federal AGI may be deducted.
## Questions
- Did you win an Olympic or Paralympic medal this year? What prize money did you receive, and was any excluded federally?
## Common Errors
- Deducting amounts already excluded from federal AGI
- Claiming the deduction in a year after the medal was won
## Prompt
- I won an Olympic medal. Is the prize money taxed in Indiana?
