# Schedule 2: Deductions (Schedule 2, Lines 1-12)
## Description
Schedule 2 lists every Indiana deduction from income. Lines 1 through 10 each have their own line; every other deduction goes under line 11 ("Other Deductions") with its name, three-digit code number and amount (lines 11a, 11b, 11c, with additional sheets if needed). Line 12 adds lines 1 through 11 and carries to Form IT-40, line 4. Enclose Schedule 2 with the return. All entries are rounded to whole dollars.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1    Renter's deduction: lesser of rent paid or $3,000 ($1,500 MFS)
Line 2    Homeowner's residential property tax deduction: lesser of tax paid or $2,500 ($1,250 MFS)
Line 3    State tax refund reported on federal return (federal Schedule 1, line 1)
Line 4    Interest on U.S. government obligations
Line 5    Taxable Social Security benefits (Form 1040, line 6b)
Line 6    Taxable railroad retirement benefits (Form 1040, line 5b portion)
Line 7    Active military service deduction
Line 8    Private school/homeschool deduction: $1,000 per qualifying child
Line 9    Indiana net operating loss deduction (Schedule IT-40NOL)
Line 10   Nontaxable portion of unemployment compensation (worksheet line 7)
Line 11   Other deductions, name + 3-digit code + amount (11a, 11b, 11c)
Line 12   Total deductions = lines 1-11 → Form IT-40, line 4
```
Dollar amounts are 2025 amounts (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form).
## 2026 changes
- Three new deductions for 2026 only, each equal to the federal Schedule 1-A amount: qualified tips (IC 6-3-2-31), qualified overtime compensation (IC 6-3-2-32) and qualified passenger vehicle loan interest (IC 6-3-2-33). Their Schedule 2 code numbers are not yet published: code to be assigned on the 2026 form.
- Code reference date January 1, 2026 (SEA 243). For specified research and experimental expenses (code 641) the 2026 law changes which expenditures are covered.
