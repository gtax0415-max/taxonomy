# Disability Retirement Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 602)
## Description
A deduction of up to $5,200 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) per qualifying individual for disability retirement income. To qualify you must have been:
- Permanently and totally disabled at the time of retirement,
- Retired on disability before the end of the tax year, and
- Received disability retirement income during the tax year.
"Permanently and totally disabled" means unable to engage in any substantial gainful activity because of a physical or mental condition that a physician determines has lasted or can be expected to last at least a year, or to result in death.
## Schedule IT-2440 computation
```
1A/1B  Total disability payments received (you / spouse)
2      Line 1A + 1B
3A/3B  Excess of disability payments over $100 per week (worksheet: weekly pay minus $100, times full weeks)
4      Excess of federal AGI over $15,000 ($7,500 if married filing separately)
5      Lines 3A + 3B + 4
6      Line 2 - line 5, not below zero → Schedule 2, line 11, code 602
```
The deduction is limited to the lesser of disability pay received or $100 a week ($5,200 a year), reduced by federal AGI over $15,000. The joint-return total cannot exceed $10,400. (All 2025 amounts.) Table A converts biweekly, semimonthly and monthly pay to weekly pay.
## Enclose or keep?
The booklet (page 20) says to keep the completed, doctor-signed Schedule IT-2440 with your records. The 2025 Schedule IT-2440 itself says to complete lines 1 through 6 and enclose the schedule with the Indiana return. The two disagree; enclosing it with a paper return satisfies both.
## What does not qualify
- Social Security disability income (Indiana does not tax it).
## Example
Single, received $90 a week for 52 weeks ($4,680) of disability retirement pay, federal AGI $14,000. Line 3 = $0 (under $100 a week); line 4 = $0 (AGI under $15,000). Line 6 = $4,680 → code 602. With federal AGI of $17,000 instead, line 4 = $2,000 and the deduction = $2,680.
## Questions
- Did you retire on disability, and were you permanently and totally disabled when you retired?
- How much disability pay did you receive each week, and what is your federal AGI?
- Do you have the Physician's Statement signed and dated?
## Common Errors
- Claiming Social Security disability benefits under code 602
- Ignoring the $100-per-week limit or the federal AGI reduction
- Missing the signed Physician's Statement
## Prompt
- I receive a disability pension. Is there an Indiana deduction?
