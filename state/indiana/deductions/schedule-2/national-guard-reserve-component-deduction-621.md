# National Guard and Reserve Component Members Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 621)
## Description
If you are eligible, your deduction is your military income received for service in the reserve component of the armed forces or in the Indiana National Guard. For 2023 and later, deployment, mobilization or federalization is no longer required, and regular military income of Guard and reserve members is fully deductible under this code. Unlike line 7, this deduction has no dollar limit. Do not deduct this income on any other line or code.
## Who is eligible
A member of the reserve components of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, Marine Corps, Merchant Marine or Space Force; or a member of the Indiana Army National Guard or Indiana Air National Guard.
## Do not deduct income received
- As a civilian member of the armed forces or National Guard.
- As a member of an active duty component of the armed forces.
- From employment or any other source outside the National Guard or reserve components.
Combat zone pay is not taxable and so is not deductible. You cannot claim both this deduction and the Military Service Deduction (line 7) on the same income. See Income Tax Information Bulletin #27.
## Example (booklet)
Brooklynn earned $2,640 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) from the Indiana National Guard. She claims $2,640 with code 621 (her husband's active Army pay goes on line 7).
## Questions
- Are you a member of a reserve component or the Indiana National Guard? How much military pay did you receive for that service?
- Do you also have civilian pay from the Guard or another employer?
## Common Errors
- Reporting Guard or reserve pay on line 7
- Including civilian technician pay
