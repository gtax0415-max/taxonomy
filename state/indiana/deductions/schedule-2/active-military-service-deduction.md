# Active Military Service Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 7)
## Description
If federal AGI includes military pay from active duty in the armed forces, you (and your spouse, if filing jointly and both qualify) may deduct it. Beginning in 2024 the deduction is the full amount of military pay included in federal AGI. Beginning in 2025, members of the United States Space Force, the United States Public Health Service Commissioned Corps and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps are also eligible (HEA 1280-2025).
## Three military deductions
| Income | Where |
|---|---|
| Active duty military pay | Schedule 2, line 7 |
| Military retirement income and/or survivor's benefits | Schedule 2, line 11, code 632 |
| Indiana National Guard and reserve component pay | Schedule 2, line 11, code 621 |
The same income cannot be deducted under both line 7 and code 621.
## What does not qualify
- Combat zone pay: it is not taxable federally or by Indiana, so there is nothing to deduct.
- National Guard and armed forces reserve pay (use code 621).
## Examples (booklet)
- Louis earned $25,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) from active service in the Army; his wife Brooklynn earned $2,640 from the Indiana National Guard. Line 7 = $25,000 for Louis. Brooklynn claims $2,640 with code 621 on line 11.
- Jim was on active duty the first month and in a combat zone the rest of the year. His W-2, box 1 shows $1,250. He deducts $1,250.
## Filing requirement
Enclose your military W-2 form(s). See Income Tax Information Bulletin #27.
## Questions
- Were you (or your spouse) on active duty, and how much military pay is in W-2 box 1?
- Were you in the National Guard or reserves instead, or retired from the military?
- Were you in a combat zone?
## Common Errors
- Reporting National Guard or reserve pay on line 7
- Deducting combat zone pay that was never in federal AGI
- Not enclosing the military W-2
## Prompt
- Is my active duty military pay taxed by Indiana?
- I am in the Space Force. Can I take the military service deduction?
