# Health Care Sharing Ministry Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 643)
## Description
If you paid for membership in a health care sharing ministry as defined by IC 27-1-2.1-1, you may deduct the amount paid for membership. Deduct only membership amounts.
## What does not qualify
- Unreimbursed health care expenses.
## Example
Monthly membership shares of $450 for 12 months = $5,400 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 643 = $5,400. Medical bills the ministry did not share are not deductible.
## Questions
- Are you a member of a health care sharing ministry, and how much did you pay in membership amounts?
## Common Errors
- Including unshared medical bills
- Deducting the ministry's own payments of your bills instead of your membership amounts
