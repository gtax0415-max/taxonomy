# Repayment of Previously Taxed Income Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 630)
## Description
A deduction for repaying income previously taxed ("claim of right") if:
- You reported the income to Indiana in a previous year,
- You repaid some or all of it this year, and
- For federal purposes you are eligible to claim the repayment as an itemized deduction or claim a credit based on the repayment.
You need not actually itemize federally. Indiana has no matching credit, but allows this deduction.
## Unemployment adjustment
If an unemployment compensation deduction was claimed in the year the income was reported, reduce the income previously reported by the amount repaid, refigure that deduction, and subtract the difference from the repayment.
## Examples (booklet, with years shifted for 2026 use)
- Ryan received $1,700 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) of unemployment, reported it in full, and later repaid $345. He had claimed a $73 unemployment deduction; refigured on $1,355 it falls by $15. Code 630 = $330 ($345 - $15).
- Cynthia repaid $3,400 of income reported in an earlier year and claimed a federal credit for it. Code 630 = $3,400.
## What does not qualify
- Repaid Social Security income (Indiana never taxed it).
## Questions
- Did you repay income this year that you reported on an earlier Indiana return? What kind of income?
- Did you claim an Indiana unemployment deduction in the year the income was reported?
## Common Errors
- Deducting repaid Social Security
- Skipping the unemployment refiguring step
## Prompt
- I had to pay back unemployment benefits from last year. Can I deduct that in Indiana?
