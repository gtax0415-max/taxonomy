# Small Employer Health Insurance Premium Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 639)
## Description
If you claimed a federal credit for small employer health insurance premiums under IRC section 45R, and would have been permitted a deduction for those premiums except for the disallowance under IRC section 280C(h), you may deduct the portion of the premiums disallowed for federal purposes.
## Example
A sole proprietor paid $20,000 of employee health premiums and claimed a $5,000 IRC 45R credit, so $5,000 of the premium deduction was disallowed federally (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 639 = $5,000.
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal small employer health insurance credit (Form 8941)? How much of the premium deduction was disallowed?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the full premium instead of only the disallowed portion
## Prompt
- I took the small business health care credit. Does Indiana let me deduct the disallowed premiums?
