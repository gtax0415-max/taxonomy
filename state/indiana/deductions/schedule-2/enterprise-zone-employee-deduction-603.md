# Enterprise Zone Employee Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 603)
## Description
Certain areas within Indiana are designated enterprise zones to encourage investment and job growth in distressed urban areas. If you are eligible, your employer gives you Form IT-40QEC. The deduction is one-half of the earned income shown on Form IT-40QEC or $7,500 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), whichever is less.
## Married couples
If both spouses received Form IT-40QEC, each may take the deduction, up to $7,500 each and $15,000 combined.
## Example
Form IT-40QEC shows $12,000 of qualifying earned income. Half is $6,000. Code 603 = $6,000.
## Questions
- Did your employer give you Form IT-40QEC? What earned income does it show?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the full IT-40QEC amount instead of half
- Claiming without Form IT-40QEC
