# Railroad Unemployment and Sickness Benefits Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 624)
## Description
Benefits issued by the U.S. Railroad Retirement Board are not taxable by Indiana. Deduct unemployment and/or sick pay benefits issued by the Board if you included them as taxable income on your federal return and did not already deduct them on Schedule 2, lines 5 and/or 6. Keep the statements (such as Form 1099G) issued by the Board.
## What does not qualify
- Supplemental sick pay benefits.
- Railroad unemployment on the Unemployment Compensation Worksheet (it is excluded from worksheet line 1 and deducted here instead).
## Example
A railroad worker received $4,200 of Railroad Retirement Board unemployment benefits, taxable federally (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Code 624 = $4,200; none of it goes on the line 10 worksheet.
## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment or sickness benefits from the Railroad Retirement Board? Were they taxable federally?
## Common Errors
- Running railroad unemployment through the line 10 worksheet
- Including supplemental sick pay
## Prompt
- Are railroad unemployment benefits taxable in Indiana?
