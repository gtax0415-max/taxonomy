# Indiana Lottery Winnings Annuity Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 629)
## Description
You may deduct annuity payments received from a winning Hoosier Lottery ticket for a lottery held before July 1, 2002. It applies only to prizes won from the Hoosier Lottery Commission.
## What does not qualify
| Item | Reason |
|---|---|
| Hoosier Lottery tickets for lotteries held after June 30, 2002 | outside the date limit |
| Other state lotteries, casinos and other gambling | not Hoosier Lottery |
| Prizes bought from a winning ticket holder for valuable consideration | purchaser is not eligible |
## Example (booklet)
Jennifer won $2,000,000 with a ticket bought in June 2002 and receives annual payments of $100,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), reported on her federal return. Code 629 = $100,000.
