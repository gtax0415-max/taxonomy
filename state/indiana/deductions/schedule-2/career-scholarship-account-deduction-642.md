# Career Scholarship Account Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 642)
## Description
If you received a distribution from a career scholarship account and that distribution is included in your federal adjusted gross income, you may deduct the portion of the distribution included in federal AGI. Enter "Career Scholarship Account Deduction", code 642 and the amount under Schedule 2, line 11.
## What does not qualify
- A distribution that was not included in federal AGI.
## Example
A $4,000 career scholarship account distribution was received and $4,000 is included in federal AGI (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Line 11 entry: code 642, $4,000.
## Questions
- Did you receive a career scholarship account distribution, and was it included in your federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Deducting a distribution that was excluded federally
