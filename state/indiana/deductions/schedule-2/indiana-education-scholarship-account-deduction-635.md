# Indiana Education Scholarship Account Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 635)
## Description
A deduction is available if you received an annual grant amount distributed to your Indiana education scholarship account that is used to pay for qualified expenses. IC 20-51.4-2-9 lists qualified expenses.
## What does not qualify
- Grants not included in federal gross income.
- Grant amounts used to pay for items other than qualified expenses.
## Example
An education scholarship account received a $6,500 grant included in federal gross income (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form); $6,000 paid qualified tuition and $500 paid for a non-qualified item. Code 635 = $6,000.
## Questions
- Did you receive an Indiana education scholarship account grant? Was it included in federal gross income?
- How much was spent on qualified expenses?
## Common Errors
- Deducting grants excluded from federal income
- Deducting amounts spent on non-qualified items
## Prompt
- Is my Indiana education scholarship account grant taxable for Indiana?
