# Military Retirement Income and/or Survivor's Benefits Deduction (Schedule 2, Line 11, Code 632)
## Description
If federal AGI includes military retirement income and/or survivor's benefits, you (and/or your spouse, if filing jointly and both qualify) may deduct it. For 2022 and later, the deduction is the entire amount of military retirement income and/or survivor's benefits. From 2025 the deduction also covers the United States Space Force, the United States Public Health Service Commissioned Corps and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration Commissioned Officer Corps (HEA 1280-2025).
## Filing requirement
Enclose your military retirement income statement(s) and/or survivor's benefit statement(s). See Income Tax Information Bulletin #6.
## Example
A retired Air Force veteran's Form 1099-R shows $28,000 of taxable military retirement (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form); the spouse receives no military income. Code 632 = $28,000.
## Questions
- Do you or your spouse receive military retirement pay or a military survivor benefit? How much is taxable?
## Common Errors
- Reporting military retirement on line 7 (active duty) instead of code 632
- Not enclosing the retirement statement
