# Exemptions (Schedule 3, Lines 1-7)
## Description
Indiana allows fixed-dollar exemptions instead of the federal standard deduction. Schedule 3 adds them up on line 7, which goes to Form IT-40, line 6 and is subtracted to reach Indiana adjusted gross income (line 7). Schedule IN-DEP must be enclosed when claiming dependents (lines 2 and 3) and Schedule IN-DEP-A when claiming adopted child exemptions (line 6).
## How the lines flow
```
Line 1  $2,000 if married filing jointly; otherwise $1,000 (even if claimed on someone else's return)
Line 2  Number of dependents (Schedule IN-DEP, Box 5) x $1,000
Line 3  Additional dependent child exemptions (Schedule IN-DEP, Box 6: count of Box E and Box F checks) x $1,500
Line 4  Number of age 65 or older and blind boxes x $1,000
Line 5  Age 65 or older with IT-40 line 1 under $40,000 ($20,000 MFS): boxes x $500
Line 6  Additional adopted child exemptions (Schedule IN-DEP-A, Box 6) x $3,000
Line 7  Total exemptions = lines 1-6 → Form IT-40, line 6
```
Dollar amounts and the $40,000/$20,000 thresholds are 2025 amounts (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Age tests are as of December 31 of the tax year.
## Key rules
- A first-year additional dependent child gets both Box E and Box F checked on Schedule IN-DEP, so line 3 counts that child twice: $3,000 instead of $1,500 for one year.
- An adopted child must also be listed on Schedule IN-DEP with Box E checked, or the adopted child exemption is disallowed.
- If you (or your spouse on a joint return) can be claimed as a dependent by someone else, you cannot claim any dependents; you still enter $1,000 on line 1.
- County tax: spouses in different counties each use their own personal, age 65 and additional age 65 exemptions; dependent exemptions are assigned in whole to either spouse. See tax-computation/county-tax.md.
