# Additional Dependent Child Exemption (Schedule 3, Line 3)
## Description
An additional $1,500 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for each dependent child listed on Schedule IN-DEP who meets the dependent child definition. In the first taxable year the additional exemption is allowable for a child, the amount is $3,000 instead of $1,500 for that one year.
## Dependent child definition
A son, stepson, daughter, stepdaughter, foster child, child for whom you are a legal guardian, and/or your spouse's child if filing jointly, who is either under age 19 by the end of the tax year, or a full-time student under age 24 by the end of the tax year, and whom you can claim as a dependent on line 2. A foster child must have lived with you the entire year.
## Boxes E, F and 6 on Schedule IN-DEP
- Box E: check for each listed dependent who meets the definition.
- Box F: check if the dependent is eligible for the additional child exemption for the first time. Not allowed if the child could have been claimed for the $1,500 exemption in any previous year, regardless of whether the child was claimed.
- Box 6: count all checked E and F boxes; enter in Box 6 and on Schedule 3, line 3. Line 3 = Box 6 x $1,500.
## Examples
- Booklet: the Does check Box 1E for their daughter Tatum (born 2012). Box 6 = 1; line 3 = $1,500.
- A child born in 2026: Box E and Box F are both checked; Box 6 = 2; line 3 = $3,000 for 2026, then $1,500 in later years.
- A grandson listed in Box 5 who is not a foster child or ward: no Box E.
## Questions
- Which dependents are your children, stepchildren, foster children (living with you all year) or wards?
- How old was each at year end, and is anyone 19 to 23 and a full-time student?
- Is this the first year any child could be claimed for this exemption (for example, a child born in 2026)?
## Common Errors
- Checking Box F for a child who could have been claimed in an earlier year
- Checking Box E for a grandchild or other relative who is not a foster child or ward
- Counting only Box E checks in Box 6 and missing Box F
