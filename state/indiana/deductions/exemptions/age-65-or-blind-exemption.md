# Age 65 or Older or Blind Exemption (Schedule 3, Line 4)
## Description
If you and/or your spouse (if filing jointly) are age 65 or older by December 31 of the tax year, each can take an additional $1,000 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). If you and/or your spouse are legally blind, each can take another $1,000. Place an "X" in each box that applies, count the boxes and multiply by $1,000. Up to four boxes on a joint return.
## County tax
Spouses in different counties on January 1 each use their own age 65 exemption on Schedule CT-40.
## Relation to the federal senior deduction
The federal senior deduction (P.L. 119-21) is below federal AGI and has no Indiana effect; this exemption and the line 5 additional exemption are the Indiana equivalents.
## Example
A married couple filing jointly: husband 67, wife 63 and legally blind. Boxes: you 65 or older, spouse blind = 2. Line 4 = $2,000.
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older by December 31? Is either of you legally blind?
## Common Errors
- Checking a spouse box on a separate return
- Missing the blindness box for someone who is also 65
