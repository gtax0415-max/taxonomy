# Additional Exemption for Age 65 or Older (Schedule 3, Line 5)
## Description
An additional $500 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for you and/or your spouse (if filing jointly) if you are age 65 or older and the amount on Form IT-40, line 1 (federal AGI) is less than $40,000, or less than $20,000 if married filing separately. Enter the line 1 amount on Schedule 3, line 5, mark the boxes that apply and multiply the count by $500.
## Key points
- The test uses federal AGI (IT-40, line 1), the joint figure on a joint return, not Indiana AGI.
- On a married filing separately return only the "You were age 65 or older" box applies.
- On Schedule CT-40, each spouse in a different county uses his or her own additional age 65 exemption.
## Examples
- Married filing jointly, both 70, federal AGI $38,500: 2 boxes, line 5 = $1,000.
- Same couple with federal AGI $40,000: not less than $40,000, so line 5 = $0.
- Single, 66, federal AGI $25,000: line 5 = $500.
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older by December 31?
- What is your federal AGI (Form IT-40, line 1)?
## Common Errors
- Testing Indiana AGI instead of federal AGI
- Claiming it when AGI equals exactly $40,000
- Using the $40,000 threshold on a separate return
## Prompt
- Is there an extra Indiana exemption for low-income seniors?
