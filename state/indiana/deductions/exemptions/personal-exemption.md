# Personal Exemption (Schedule 3, Line 1)
## Description
Enter $2,000 if married filing jointly; all other filers enter $1,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form). Enter $1,000 even if you are claimed as a dependent on someone else's return, such as a parent's or guardian's.
## County tax
On a joint return where spouses lived in different counties on January 1, each spouse uses his or her own $1,000 personal exemption on Schedule CT-40. Exemptions are used in whole and cannot be split.
## Example
A college student claimed as a dependent by her parents files her own IT-40: line 1 = $1,000. A married couple filing jointly: line 1 = $2,000.

## Common Errors
- Entering $0 because someone else claims you as a dependent
- Entering $2,000 on a married filing separately return
