# Additional Exemption for Adopted Child (Schedule 3, Line 6)
## Description
An additional $3,000 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for each qualifying adopted child. The adoption must be finalized before the end of the taxable year. The child must also be listed on Schedule IN-DEP and must qualify for the additional dependent child exemption there (Box E checked); otherwise this exemption is disallowed. Complete and enclose Schedule IN-DEP-A.
## Schedule IN-DEP-A, lines 1 through 5
For each adopted dependent enter first and last name (Boxes A and B), SSN (Box C) and date of birth (Box D). Check Box E if the first listed taxpayer is an adoptive parent; check Box F if the spouse is an adoptive parent.
Box 6: the number of adopted dependents listed. Enter it in Box 6 and on Schedule 3, line 6. Do not count the parent boxes (E and F) marked; count children.
## Rules
- If both parents are adoptive parents, only one additional adopted child exemption is allowed for the child.
- A non-adoptive parent (for example a biological parent of a child adopted by a stepparent) may not claim it unless the adoptive parent files jointly with the non-adoptive parent.
- An adopted child is always treated as your own child; a child lawfully placed with you for adoption counts as an adopted child for dependency, but this exemption requires a finalized adoption.
## Example
A married couple filing jointly finalized the adoption of a 4-year-old in 2026; both are adoptive parents. Schedule IN-DEP: Box 5 counts the child ($1,000), Box E and Box F checked if first year (2 x $1,500 = $3,000). Schedule IN-DEP-A: child listed, Boxes E and F checked, Box 6 = 1. Schedule 3, line 6 = $3,000. Total for the child: $1,000 + $3,000 + $3,000 = $7,000 (assuming 2026 is the first year the additional child exemption is allowable).
## Questions
- When was the adoption finalized? Is the child listed on Schedule IN-DEP with Box E checked?
- Which of you is an adoptive parent?
## Common Errors
- Entering the number of parent boxes checked in Box 6 instead of the number of children
- Claiming before the adoption is final
- Not checking Box E on Schedule IN-DEP for the adopted child
