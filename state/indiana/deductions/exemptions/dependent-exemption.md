# Dependent Exemption (Schedule 3, Line 2)
## Description
A $1,000 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for each dependent: a qualifying child (Steps 1 and 2) or a qualifying relative (Step 3) under the rules in dependent-qualification-rules.md. Complete and enclose Schedule IN-DEP.
## Schedule IN-DEP, lines 1 through 5
For each dependent enter:
- First and last name (Boxes A and B),
- Nine-digit SSN (Box C), matching the Social Security card,
- Date of birth (Box D, mm dd yyyy).
Box 5: add the dependents listed and enter the number in Box 5 and on Schedule 3, line 2. Line 2 = Box 5 x $1,000.
## Example (booklet)
Cooper and Grace Doe claim their daughter Tatum (line 1: Tatum Doe, 123 45 6789, 06 01 2012) and Grace's grandmother Irene Smith, who lives with them (line 2: Irene Smith, 987 65 4321, 10 15 1940). Box 5 = 2; Schedule 3, line 2 = $2,000.
## More than four dependents
Use an additional Schedule IN-DEP and add its dependents to Box 5 (and Box 6) of the first schedule (booklet: June lists her fifth dependent on a second IN-DEP and enters 5 in Box 5 of the first).
## SSN rules
Name and SSN must agree with the Social Security card or the exemption may be disallowed. Use an ATIN for a child placed for adoption whose SSN you do not know (federal Form W-7A), or an ITIN for a non-citizen, non-resident alien dependent (Form W-7). For a child born and died in the year without an SSN, enter "Died" in Box C and keep the birth certificate, death certificate or hospital records showing a live birth.
## Questions
- Who lived with you and whom did you support? Does each meet the qualifying child or qualifying relative test?
- Do you have each dependent's SSN and date of birth?
- Could anyone claim you as a dependent?
## Common Errors
- Name or SSN not matching the Social Security card
- Claiming dependents when you can be claimed by someone else
- Forgetting to enclose Schedule IN-DEP
