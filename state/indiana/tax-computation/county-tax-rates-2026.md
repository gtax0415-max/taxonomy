# County Tax Rates and Codes for 2026 (Schedule CT-40, Lines 2A and 2B)
## Description
The county tax rate is the rate of the Indiana county where you lived on January 1, 2026. The table below compares the 2025 Schedule CT-40 chart with Departmental Notice #1 effective October 1, 2026, which marks with an asterisk any county whose rate changed after the January 1, 2026 notice. Departmental Notice #1 rates are published for withholding; the 2026 Schedule CT-40 chart (not yet issued) governs the return.
## What changed for 2026
- Six counties changed effective January 1, 2026 (not starred in the October notice, so the change came with the January 1, 2026 notice): Carroll .022733 → .024733; Grant .0255 → .0275; Greene .0215 → .0235; Howard .0195 → .0235; Shelby .016 → .017; Union .02 → .0275.
- Boone changed from .017 to .0171 effective October 1, 2026 (starred in the October notice). The October change is a withholding rate for periods beginning on or after October 1, 2026. How the 2026 Schedule CT-40 chart will show Boone for the full year (.017, .0171, or another figure) is not yet published; confirm against the 2026 form.
- The 2025 chart starred Monroe, Decatur, Fayette, Floyd, Fulton, Gibson, Henry, Jay, Rush and Switzerland as 2025 changes; their rates are unchanged for 2026.

## Example
A Howard County resident on January 1, 2026 with Indiana AGI of $50,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form): county tax = $50,000 x .0235 = $1,175 (the 2025 rate .0195 would give $975).

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 chart rate for Carroll, Grant, Greene, Howard, Shelby or Union
- Using the county where you work instead of where you lived on January 1
- Applying Boone's October withholding rate without checking the 2026 chart
