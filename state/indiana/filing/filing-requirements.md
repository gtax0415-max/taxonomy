# Filing Requirements (Form IT-40)
## Description
A full-year Indiana resident must file Form IT-40 if gross income (the total of all income before deductions) was greater than the total of the filing exemptions. For filing requirement purposes Indiana allows a $1,000 exemption (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form) for you, $1,000 for your spouse if married filing jointly, and $1,000 for each dependent you are eligible to claim. If gross income is less than the total, you are not required to file.
## Who should file
- You lived in Indiana and received income, or
- You lived outside Indiana and had any income from Indiana (Form IT-40PNR or IT-40RNR; out of scope here).
## Filing to get money back
Even when not required, file to recover Indiana state or county tax withheld by an employer, estimated tax payments, or refundable credits such as the Indiana earned income credit.
## Decedents
A return must be filed for an individual who died if the deceased was under age 65 with gross income more than $1,000, or age 65 or older with gross income more than $2,000 (2025 amounts; confirm against the 2026 form). See deceased-taxpayers.md.
## Example
- Married couple filing jointly with two dependents: filing exemptions = 4 x $1,000 = $4,000. Gross income $3,600 → not required to file; if $250 of Indiana tax was withheld, they file to get it back.
- Single filer, no dependents, gross income $12,000 → must file.
## Unified tax credit for the elderly (Form SC-40)
Some low-income taxpayers age 65 or older who are not required to file Form IT-40 can claim the unified tax credit for the elderly on the simplified Form SC-40 instead. File only one of Form SC-40 or Form IT-40. That credit is outside this folder.
## Questions
- What was your total gross income for 2026 (before any deductions)?
- How many dependents can you claim?
- Was any Indiana state or county tax withheld, or did you make estimated payments?
## Common Errors
- Comparing taxable income (instead of gross income) with the exemption total
- Not filing when below the threshold and leaving withholding unrefunded
- Using the $1,500 additional dependent amount (a Schedule 3 exemption) in the filing-requirement test; the booklet test uses $1,000 per person
