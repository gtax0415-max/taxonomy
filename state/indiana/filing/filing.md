# Filing (Form IT-40 Header, Line 1 and Schedule 7)
## Description
This folder covers who must file a full-year resident return, which Indiana return to use, filing status, the federal adjusted gross income starting point (Form IT-40, line 1), the January 1 county codes, due dates and extensions, Schedule 7 (Additional Required Information), deceased taxpayers, and amended returns. Schedule IN-W (Indiana Withholding Statements) is in payments/.

Scope is Form IT-40, the full-year resident return. Part-year residents, full-year nonresidents (Form IT-40PNR) and reciprocal-state nonresidents (Form IT-40RNR) are out of scope.
## How the return starts
```
Header   Married filing separately box; Amended box
         County codes: you lived / you worked / spouse lived / spouse worked on Jan. 1
Line 1   Federal adjusted gross income (Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11)
Line 2   Indiana add-backs (Schedule 1, line 7) → income/add-backs/add-backs.md
Line 3   Line 1 + line 2
...
Schedule 7  Federal filing (line 1), out-of-state wage income (line 2), extensions (line 3),
            farm/fishing (line 4), Schedule IN-40PA (line 5), date of death (line 6)
```
## Key rules
- Complete the federal return first; Indiana starts from federal AGI and Indiana filing status follows the federal filing status.
- For 2026 Indiana uses the Internal Revenue Code as amended and in effect on January 1, 2026 (SEA 243); the 2025 booklet used January 1, 2023.
- The 2026 state adjusted gross income tax rate is 2.95% (the 2025 Form IT-40, line 8 prints 3%).
- The 2026 return is due April 15, 2027 (the 2025 return was due April 15, 2026; confirm against the 2026 form).
