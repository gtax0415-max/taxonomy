# Due Dates and Extensions (Form IT-40; Schedule 7, Line 3)
## Description
The 2025 return was due April 15, 2026; the 2026 return is due April 15, 2027 (confirm against the 2026 form). Fiscal year returns are due by the 15th day of the 4th month after the close of the fiscal year. An extension extends the time to file, not the time to pay: interest is owed on any tax paid after the original due date, even with an extension.
## Extended due date
For the 2025 return, both Indiana Form IT-9 and federal Form 4868 extended the Indiana filing date to Nov. 16, 2026. The 2026 booklet will print the extended date for 2026 returns; confirm it against the 2026 form.
## How to get an extension
- Federal extension: if you have a federal extension (Form 4868, Form 2350, or an online federal extension payment), you automatically have an Indiana extension and do not file Form IT-9. Check Schedule 7, line 3a.
- Indiana Form IT-9: if you have no federal extension, file Form IT-9 by the original due date for the request to be valid. You may file IT-9 online if you make a payment with it by the due date. Check Schedule 7, line 3b.
- You need an extension to file late whether you owe, expect a refund, or break even.
## Penalty relief with an extension
No late penalty is owed if you (1) paid at least 90% of the tax expected to be owed by the original due date, (2) file by the extended due date, and (3) pay the remaining balance (including interest) with the return. Interest is still owed on amounts paid after the original due date.
## Reporting extension payments
Add any Indiana extension payment to estimated tax paid and report the total on Schedule 5, line 4.
## Military
- On duty outside the United States and Puerto Rico on the due date: automatic 60-day extension; enclose a statement verifying you were outside the U.S. or Puerto Rico on the due date.
- In a presidentially declared combat zone: automatic extension of 180 days after leaving the combat zone, or after release from a hospital outside the U.S. for service-related hospitalization. The spouse must use the same method of filing for federal and Indiana. Write "Combat Zone" across the top of the form above the SSN.
## Statute of limitations for refunds
For 2025 returns, a refund claim must be made by April 17, 2029 (Nov. 15, 2029 if filing under extension). The 2026 booklet will print the 2026 dates.
## Example
Lee expects to owe $2,000 for 2026, files Form 4868 federally and pays $1,900 to Indiana with Form IT-9 by April 15, 2027. Lee files in October and pays the $100 balance plus interest. Lee checks Schedule 7, lines 3a and 3b, includes the $1,900 on Schedule 5, line 4, and owes no late penalty because more than 90% was paid on time.
## Questions
- Did you file a federal extension (Form 4868 or 2350) or make a federal extension payment?
- Did you file Form IT-9 or make an Indiana extension payment, and how much?
- Were you outside the U.S. in the military or in a combat zone on the due date?
- Do you file on a fiscal year?
## Common Errors
- Assuming an extension also extends the time to pay
- Filing IT-9 when a federal extension already covers Indiana (not wrong, but unnecessary)
- Leaving the extension payment off Schedule 5, line 4
- Not checking Schedule 7, line 3a or 3b
## Prompt
- Do I need a separate Indiana extension if I filed one with the IRS?
- I filed an extension. Will I owe a penalty?
