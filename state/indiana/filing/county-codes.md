# County Codes (Form IT-40, Header)
## Description
Enter the two-digit county code numbers (from the chart on the back of Schedule CT-40) for the county where you, and your spouse if filing jointly, lived and worked on Jan. 1 of the tax year. For the 2026 return the date is Jan. 1, 2026. These codes drive the county tax on Schedule CT-40 and Form IT-40, line 9, and the county codes on line 19a and 19b.
## County where you lived
The county where you maintained your home on Jan. 1. With more than one Indiana home on that date, use in order: where you were registered to vote; where your personal automobile was registered; where you spent the majority of your time in Indiana during the year. A move to another county after Jan. 1 does not change the county until the next year.
## County where you worked
The county of principal employment: where your main place of business was located or your main work activity was performed on Jan. 1. With more than one job on Jan. 1, use the county where you worked the most hours and earned the most income. A job change after Jan. 1 does not change the code until next year.
## Special codes
| Situation on Jan. 1 | Code |
|---|---|
| Principal employment outside Indiana | 00 (out-of-state) in the worked box |
| Worked in Illinois / Kentucky / Michigan / Ohio / Pennsylvania / Wisconsin | 94 / 95 / 96 / 97 / 98 / 99 in the worked box |
| Retired, homemaker or unemployed | County of residence code in both the lived and worked boxes (do not write "Retired" etc.) |
| Military stationed outside Indiana, single or with family along | 00 in all county boxes (no county tax) |
| Military stationed outside Indiana but home or spouse and family still in an Indiana county | That county (subject to county tax) |
| Military stationed in Indiana | County where you lived on Jan. 1 of the year you entered service |
## County rates
Rates on the 2025 CT-40 chart are 2025 rates. Departmental Notice #1 (2026) lists county rates effective October 1, 2026; for example Boone County changed from .017 to .0171 on October 1, 2026. Use the rate chart printed with the 2026 Schedule CT-40.
## Example
Jessie worked in Marion County on Jan. 1, 2026, and moved to a Johnson County job on Feb. 10, 2026. She enters 49 (Marion) as the county where she worked.
## Questions
- Where did you (and your spouse) live on Jan. 1, 2026?
- Where was your main job on Jan. 1, 2026? Was it outside Indiana, and in which state?
- Were you retired, a homemaker or unemployed on Jan. 1?
- Are you in the military, and where were you stationed on Jan. 1?
## Common Errors
- Using the county you lived in on Dec. 31 or after a mid-year move
- Writing "Retired" in the worked box instead of the residence code
- Entering 00 instead of 94-99 for work in a neighboring state
- Leaving the spouse boxes blank when spouses lived in different counties
## Prompt
- I moved counties in March. Which county code do I use?
- I'm retired. What goes in the county where you worked box?
