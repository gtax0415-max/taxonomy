# Deceased Taxpayers (Schedule 7, Line 6)
## Description
If an individual died during the tax year, or died after the year ended but before filing, the executor, administrator or surviving spouse must file a return for the individual if the deceased:
- was under age 65 and had gross income more than $1,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form),
- was age 65 or older and had gross income more than $2,000 (2025 amount; confirm against the 2026 form), or
- was a nonresident with gross income from Indiana (out of scope).
Only one return should be filed for the deceased.
## Date of death (Schedule 7, line 6)
If the taxpayer or spouse died during 2026 and the return is filed with that person's name on it, enter the date of death in the taxpayer's or spouse's box. The booklet example writes Jan. 9 as 01/09 with the year; the form box takes MM/DD with the year preprinted. Do not enter a date of death if the person died before 2026, or after Dec. 31, 2026 but before filing (that date goes on the 2027 return).
## Signing
- Joint return by the surviving spouse: the survivor signs his or her own name and writes "Filing as Surviving Spouse" after the signature.
- Executor or administrator appointed: must file and sign (even if it is not the final return), showing the relationship after the signature.
- No executor or administrator: the person filing signs and gives the relationship (for example, "John Doe, nephew").
DOR may ask for a copy of the death certificate; keep a copy.
## Refund check for a deceased individual
If a refund check cannot be cashed, the surviving spouse, administrator, executor or other person obtains from the State Comptroller a widow's and distributee's affidavit (POA-20) or a reissuance of refund (IN-1310), and sends it with a copy of the death certificate to the State Comptroller.
## Filing status and credits
A surviving spouse may file jointly for the year of death, following the federal return. Indiana has no qualifying surviving spouse status (see filing-status.md).
## Example
Ray died March 3, 2026, age 72, with $18,000 of pension income. His wife files a joint 2026 Form IT-40, enters 03/03 in the spouse's date of death box on Schedule 7, line 6, and signs "Filing as Surviving Spouse."
## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die during 2026? On what date?
- Who is filing: the surviving spouse, an appointed executor or administrator, or someone else?
- Was the decedent's gross income above the filing threshold?
## Common Errors
- Entering a date of death for someone who died in early 2027 on the 2026 return
- Filing more than one return for the decedent
- Signing without stating the relationship
