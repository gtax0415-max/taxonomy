# Filing Status (Form IT-40, Header)
## Description
Indiana filing status follows the federal return. If you and your spouse file a joint federal return, you must file a joint Indiana return. If you file separate federal returns, you must file separate Indiana returns. Form IT-40 has no filing status checkboxes other than the "married filing separately" box at the top of the form; status is otherwise shown by whether a spouse's name and Social Security number are entered.
## Statuses
| Federal status | How it appears on Form IT-40 |
|---|---|
| Married filing jointly | Both names and both SSNs; spouse county codes completed when applicable |
| Married filing separately | Enter both SSNs in the boxes at the top and place an "X" in the box directly to the right of those boxes. Enter only the filer's name on the top line; do not enter the spouse's name on the second name line |
| Head of household (married persons living apart) | If you were not divorced or legally separated and filed federally as head of household, do not check the married filing separately box and do not enter your spouse's name or SSN |
| Single or head of household (unmarried) | Your name and SSN only |
| Qualifying surviving spouse | Indiana has no qualifying surviving spouse status or box. File with your own name and SSN only; the status does not change Indiana tax |
## Effects of status
Filing status changes dollar caps on some Indiana lines (for example, the renter's deduction is $3,000, or $1,500 if married filing separately (2025 amounts; confirm against the 2026 form)). On a joint return where spouses lived in different counties on Jan. 1, county tax is figured separately for each (Schedule CT-40). The state tax rate (2.95% for 2026) does not depend on filing status.
## Example
Dana and Chris are married and file separate federal returns. Dana's IT-40 shows Dana's name, both SSNs and an "X" in the married filing separately box; Chris files a separate IT-40 the same way.
## Questions
- What filing status did you use on your 2026 federal return?
- If married, were you divorced or legally separated during the year, or did you file as head of household?
## Common Errors
- Filing jointly in Indiana after filing separately federally (or the reverse)
- Entering the spouse's name on a married filing separately return
- Checking the married filing separately box after filing federally as head of household
- Looking for a qualifying surviving spouse box
## Prompt
- Can I file jointly in Indiana if we filed separately federally?
- I was head of household federally but still married. What do I put on Form IT-40?
