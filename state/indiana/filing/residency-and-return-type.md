# Residency and Return Type (Form IT-40)
## Description
Indiana has three individual income tax returns. Form IT-40 is for full-year residents: you (and your spouse, if married filing jointly) maintained legal residence in Indiana from Jan. 1 through Dec. 31 of the tax year. This taxonomy covers Form IT-40 only. Form IT-40PNR (part-year residents and full-year nonresidents) and Form IT-40RNR (full-year residents of Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania or Wisconsin whose only Indiana income was wages, salaries, tips or commissions) are out of scope.
## Full-year resident
- Physical presence all year is not required. Residents, including military personnel, who leave Indiana for a temporary stay are residents during the absence.
- Retired persons spending part of the year in another state may still be full-year residents if they maintain their legal residence in Indiana and intend to return during part of the year, keep their Indiana driver's license, keep their Indiana voting rights, and claim a homestead deduction on their Indiana home.
## Military personnel
- An Indiana resident at enlistment remains an Indiana resident wherever stationed and reports all income on Form IT-40.
- A member who changed legal residence (military home of record) during the year is a part-year resident (Form IT-40PNR, out of scope).
- A nonresident stationed in Indiana files only if there is non-military Indiana-source income (Form IT-40PNR, out of scope).
- A full-year resident member whose spouse is a legal resident of another state and who files jointly federally files Form IT-40PNR (out of scope).
- County codes for military members follow special rules; see county-codes.md.
## Proof of residency
Nonresidents may be asked for proof of non-residency such as a driver's license or proof of home ownership.
## Example
Pat and Lee lived in Indianapolis all of 2026 and file jointly → Form IT-40. If Lee had moved to Indiana in June 2026, the couple would file Form IT-40PNR (out of scope).
## Questions
- Did you (and your spouse) maintain your legal home in Indiana all of 2026?
- Did either of you move into or out of Indiana during 2026?
- Are you in the military, and is your home of record Indiana?
## Common Errors
- Filing Form IT-40 jointly when one spouse was not a full-year resident
- Treating a temporary absence (school, military, winter home) as a change of residence
## Prompt
- I spend winters in Florida. Am I still an Indiana resident?
- My spouse lives in another state. Which Indiana form do we file?
