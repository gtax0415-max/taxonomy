# Federal Adjusted Gross Income (Form IT-40, Line 1)
## Description
Indiana's computation starts from federal adjusted gross income. Enter the adjusted gross income from federal Form 1040 or 1040-SR, line 11 (the 2025 line; confirm the line on the 2026 federal form). Indiana then adds back items on Schedule 1 (line 2), subtracts Schedule 2 deductions (line 4) and Schedule 3 exemptions (line 6) to reach Indiana adjusted gross income (line 7), which is taxed at 2.95% for 2026 (line 8).
## Conformity for 2026
For 2026 the Internal Revenue Code used for Indiana is the Code as amended and in effect on January 1, 2026 (SEA 243). The 2025 booklet used January 1, 2023 and did not follow P.L. 119-21 (the One Big Beautiful Bill Act). Federal changes enacted after January 1, 2026 that change federal AGI are handled with the conformity add-back codes (see income/add-backs/conformity-add-back-120.md and income/add-backs/conformity-add-back-negative-147.md).
## No federal return
If you were not required to file a federal return, complete a "sample" federal return and enter the amount you would have shown. Answer Schedule 7, line 1 ("Are you filing a federal income tax return?") accordingly.
## Entry rules
Round to whole dollars. Report a loss with a negative sign (write a $125 loss as -125). Do not use commas.
## Example
Federal Form 1040, line 11 shows $68,412.60 → Form IT-40, line 1 = 68413. A federal AGI loss of $2,310 → line 1 = -2310.
## Questions
- What is the adjusted gross income on your 2026 federal Form 1040, line 11?
- Were you required to file a federal return?
## Common Errors
- Entering federal taxable income or total income instead of AGI
- Dropping the negative sign on a loss
- Using an AGI from a federal return that was later amended without updating Indiana
