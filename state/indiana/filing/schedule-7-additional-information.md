# Schedule 7: Additional Required Information (Lines 1-5)
## Description
Schedule 7 collects information Indiana needs to process Form IT-40. Its entries do not change the tax, but the schedule must be enclosed with every Form IT-40 because it also carries the Authorization statement the filers sign under. Line 6 (date of death) is covered in deceased-taxpayers.md.
## Line 1 - Federal filing information
Place an "X" in Yes or No: "Are you filing a federal income tax return for 2026?"
## Line 2 - Out-of-state income
If you and/or your spouse received salary, wage, tip or other compensation income from Illinois, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania or Wisconsin, enter the 2-digit state code and the income for each of you.

| State | Code | State | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Illinois | 94 | Ohio | 97 |
| Kentucky | 95 | Pennsylvania | 98 |
| Michigan | 96 | Wisconsin | 99 |

This entry is for information only and does not change the refund or amount owed. Note that Illinois is listed on Schedule 7 even though the reciprocal-state nonresident return (Form IT-40RNR) covers only Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania and Wisconsin residents. Credit for tax paid to another state is not claimed here.
## Line 3 - Extension of time to file
- 3a: "X" if you filed federal Form 4868 (the booklet also names Form 2350) or made an online federal extension payment.
- 3b: "X" if you filed Indiana Form IT-9 or made an Indiana extension payment online.
## Line 4 - Farm/fishing income
"X" if at least two-thirds of your gross income was from farming or fishing. You must then attach Schedule IT-2210 and enter "F" on Form IT-40, line 20a. No underpayment penalty is assessed if you either paid all estimated tax by Jan. 15 and filed by April 15, or filed and paid all tax due by March 2 (2025-return dates: Jan. 15, 2026, April 15, 2026 and March 2, 2026; confirm the 2026-return dates).
## Line 5 - Schedule IN-40PA filers
"X" if you are a spouse who claims not to be liable for all or part of a tax liability because it belongs to the other spouse (eligible for federal Form 8857, Request for Innocent Spouse Relief). This applies if a joint-return liability is not yours, only part is yours, or you received a DOR assessment arising from tax attributable to your spouse. Complete and submit Schedule IN-40PA with supporting documentation.
## Example
Rae lives in Lafayette and commuted to an Illinois employer, earning $41,000. Line 1 Yes; line 2 state code 94 and $41,000; Rae's county-where-worked code on the IT-40 header is also 94.
## Questions
- Are you filing a 2026 federal return?
- Did you or your spouse earn wages in Illinois, Kentucky, Michigan, Ohio, Pennsylvania or Wisconsin? How much each?
- Did you get a federal or Indiana extension?
- Was at least two-thirds of your gross income from farming or fishing?
- Are you seeking relief from tax owed on a joint return because it belongs to your spouse?
## Common Errors
- Omitting Schedule 7 (it carries the Authorization statement)
- Expecting line 2 to give a credit or reduce tax
- Checking line 4 without attaching Schedule IT-2210 and entering F on IT-40 line 20a
- Checking line 5 without enclosing Schedule IN-40PA
## Prompt
- I work in Ohio but live in Indiana. Where do I report that?
- What is Schedule 7 for?
