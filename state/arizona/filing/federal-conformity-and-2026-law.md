# Federal Conformity and 2026 Law Changes (Laws 2026, Chapter 140)
## Description
Arizona starts from federal adjusted gross income, so it must decide each year which federal changes to adopt. Laws 2026, Chapter 140 (HB 4168 / SB 1861), signed June 13, 2026, sets the Internal Revenue Code conformity date at January 1, 2026 and makes the changes below for tax year 2026. ADOR has not published the 2026 forms; the 2025 forms and instructions are the basis for every line reference.
## 2026 changes
| Item | 2026 rule | Where |
|---|---|---|
| IRC conformity | January 1, 2026 | line 12 |
| Qualified tips subtraction | as deducted federally (from tax year 2025, continuing) | page 6, V (2025 routing) |
| Qualified overtime compensation subtraction | as deducted federally (from 2025, continuing) | page 6, V |
| Federal enhanced deduction for seniors | subtracted (from 2025, continuing) | page 6, V |
| Qualified passenger vehicle loan interest | tax year 2025 only; not available for 2026 | none for 2026 |
| Trump account distributions | new subtraction from 2026 | line not yet assigned |
| Child and dependent care expenses exceeding the federal child and dependent care credit | new subtraction from 2026 | line not yet assigned |
| Standard deduction | base $15,750 single and married filing separate, $23,625 head of household, $31,500 married filing joint, adjusted for inflation as the federal basic standard deduction (A.R.S. 43-1041(H)); $16,100 / $24,150 / $32,200 (federal 2026 amounts; Arizona 2026 figures not yet published) | line 43 |
| Charitable increase to the standard deduction | full amount of charitable contributions, capped at $1,000 (single, married filing separate) and $2,000 (married filing joint); no head of household cap is named (2025: 34% with no cap) | line 44, page 3 |
| Itemized state and local taxes | limited to $10,000 | line 43, Schedule A |
| Dependent Tax Credit | $125 per dependent under 17 (was $100); $25 for 17 and over; phase-out unchanged | line 49 |
| Qualified production property (IRC 168(n)) | addition for the special depreciation allowance to the extent not previously added | page 5 (line not yet assigned) |
| Credit for Increased Research Activities | refundable portion (Form 308-I) repealed from 2026 | line 58, box 581 |
| Credit for New Employment | Form 345 repealed from 2026 | Form 301, lines 17 and 50 |
## How ADOR routed the 2025 federal deductions
On the 2025 forms the tips, overtime, vehicle loan interest and senior deductions were entered on the MCTCP (Middle Class Tax Cuts Package) worksheet and carried to Form 140, page 6, line V (Other Adjustments). Until the 2026 form assigns its own lines, the same route is the reference for 2026.
## Example
A single filer has federal AGI of $64,000, which includes $4,000 of qualified overtime. The federal overtime deduction is taken on Schedule 1-A below AGI, so it does not reduce federal AGI. Arizona starts from $64,000 on line 12 and subtracts the $4,000 on page 6, line V, so the overtime is excluded from Arizona taxable income as well.
## Notes
- Other 2026 changes from earlier laws: the adoption expense subtraction rises to $5,000 single or head of household and $10,000 married (HB 2155, 2025); the Military Family Relief Fund credit ends after 2026
- Chapter 140 also cuts the corporate low-income STO credit cap to $110,000,000 from FY 2027 and repeals the corporate pollution control equipment credit
- Not repealed: Solar Energy Devices (Form 310) and Renewable Energy Production (Form 343); HB 4152, which would have repealed them, was vetoed
- A.R.S. 43-1041 adjusts Arizona's standard deduction for inflation in the same manner as the federal basic standard deduction, so the 2026 amounts are expected to equal the federal $16,100 / $24,150 / $32,200; ADOR publishes the official figures with the 2026 form
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- 2026 federal return, including Schedule 1-A and Form 2441
- Trump account distribution statements
- Federal Form 4562 for IRC 168(n) property
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal tips, overtime or senior deduction on Schedule 1-A?
- Did you receive a distribution from a Trump account?
- Did you pay child and dependent care expenses above the federal credit amount?
- Did you claim special depreciation for qualified production property?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the vehicle loan interest subtraction for 2026
- Claiming the refundable research credit for 2026
- Assuming the 34% charitable increase still applies
## Prompt
- What changed for Arizona taxes in 2026?
