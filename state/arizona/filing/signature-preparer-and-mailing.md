# Signatures, Paid Preparer and Mailing (Form 140, Page 2 Footer)
## Description
Form 140 is not a valid return unless signed. On a joint return both spouses sign even if only one had income; the department cannot mail a refund check without proper signatures.
## Signature block
| Field | Entry |
|---|---|
| Your signature, date, occupation | required |
| Spouse's signature, date, occupation | joint returns |
| Paid preparer's signature, date, firm's name (preparer's if self-employed) | paid preparers |
| Paid preparer's street address, city, state, ZIP, phone, TIN | paid preparers; TIN is the PTIN, SSN or business EIN |

A paid preparer who omits the identification number may be subject to a penalty. The declaration: under penalties of perjury the return is true, correct and complete; a preparer's declaration is based on all information the preparer has.
## Mailing
- With a payment: Arizona Department of Revenue, PO Box 52016, Phoenix, AZ 85072-2016.
- Refund, no tax, or tax owed without a payment: PO Box 52138, Phoenix, AZ 85072-2138.
- With Form 140-SBI: mail both in the same envelope; the department applies an overpayment on one return to the other; net tax due with payment → PO Box 52016; net refund → PO Box 52138.
- Timely mailing: postmarked by midnight April 15 (designated private delivery services count; a private meter postmark counts if received within five business days, GTR 16-1). One tax year per envelope. Do not staple; do not include correspondence.
## Assembly order (mailed returns)
From the top: Form 203 (placed on top, if filing); Form 140-SBI; Form 131-SBI; Form 301-SBI with its credit forms; Form 140 pages 1 and 2; pages 3 and 4; pages 5 and 6; Schedule DFE; Form 131; Form 140PTC; Form 301 with credit forms; credit Forms 308-I, 334, 349; copy of federal Schedule A (if itemizing); Arizona Schedule A (if adjusting); AZ Claim of Right-Individual; copy of the other state or country return (Form 309 or 309-SBI); Forms W-2, W-2G and 1099 showing Arizona withholding (last).
## Other
- Form 285 authorizes the department to release confidential information to an appointee.
- Records: keep them until the period of limitations ends (generally four years).
## Example
A married couple owes $350 and pays by check: both sign, the check payable to Arizona Department of Revenue shows the SSN, "Form 140" and "2026", and the return goes to PO Box 52016.
## Notes
- Mailing addresses follow the 2025 instructions; confirm them in the 2026 instructions
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Signatures of both spouses on a joint return
- Paid preparer's PTIN, SSN or EIN
- Payment check or money order, if mailing a payment
## Questions
- Will both spouses sign?
- Are you mailing a payment with the return?
- Do you want the department to be able to discuss the return with someone else?
## Common Errors
- One spouse not signing a joint return
- Mailing a return with a payment to the refund address
- Mailing Form 140-SBI separately
## Prompt
- Where do I mail my Arizona return?
