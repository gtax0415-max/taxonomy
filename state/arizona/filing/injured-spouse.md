# Injured Spouse Protection of Joint Overpayment (Form 140, Box 4a)
## Description
Check box 4a only if you and your spouse file a joint return and one of you is an injured spouse requesting protection from having the joint overpayment applied against the other spouse's delinquencies or debts for back child support, court fees, and fees to counties, cities or educational institutions.
## Requirements
- The spouse requesting protection must have Arizona income with Arizona tax withheld and reported on that spouse's own Form W-2 or Form 1099.
- Complete Arizona Form 203, Request for Injured Spouse Protection from Application of Joint Overpayment Against Spouse's Delinquencies and Debts, and include it with the return.
- Form 203 cannot be used for protection from offset for past-due federal taxes; contact the IRS.
## Example
A couple files jointly; the husband owes back child support. The wife had $1,800 of Arizona tax withheld on her W-2. She checks box 4a and includes Form 203 so her share of the overpayment is protected.
## Notes
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form W-2 or 1099 showing the requesting spouse's own Arizona withholding
- Notice of the other spouse's debts
- Arizona Form 203
## Questions
- Does either spouse owe child support, court fees or fees to a county, city or school?
- Did the requesting spouse have Arizona withholding on his or her own W-2 or 1099?
## Common Errors
- Checking box 4a on a separate return
- Checking box 4a without including Form 203
- Using Form 203 for a federal tax debt
## Prompt
- My spouse owes child support. Can I keep my part of our Arizona refund?
