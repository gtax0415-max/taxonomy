# Filing Requirements (Who Must File Form 140)
## Description
An Arizona resident must file if gross income is more than the threshold for the filing status. Figure gross income the same way as for federal purposes, then exclude income Arizona does not tax. A resident reports income from all sources, including out-of-state income. The thresholds track the standard deduction.
## Thresholds
| Filing status | 2025 threshold (gross income more than) | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Single | $15,750 | $16,100 (federal 2026 amounts; Arizona 2026 figures not yet published) |
| Married filing joint | $31,500 | $32,200 (federal 2026 amounts; Arizona 2026 figures not yet published) |
| Married filing separate | $15,750 | $16,100 (federal 2026 amounts; Arizona 2026 figures not yet published) |
| Head of household | $23,625 | $24,150 (federal 2026 amounts; Arizona 2026 figures not yet published) |

The 2025 instructions print the 2025 standard deduction amounts as the thresholds. The 2026 thresholds are shown on the assumption that they again equal the standard deduction. A.R.S. 43-1041 adjusts Arizona's standard deduction for inflation in the same manner as the federal basic standard deduction, so the 2026 amounts are expected to equal the federal $16,100 / $24,150 / $32,200; ADOR publishes the official figures with the 2026 form.
## Income Arizona does not tax (excluded when testing the threshold)
- Interest from U.S. Government obligations
- Social Security retirement benefits under Title II of the Social Security Act
- Railroad Retirement Act benefits: tier 1 and tier 2, railroad disability benefits (RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R), railroad unemployment and sickness payments
- Pay for active service as a member of the Reserves, National Guard or the U.S. Armed Forces
- Benefits, annuities and pensions as retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services (a surviving spouse of a deceased military veteran may exclude 100% of uniformed services payments)
## Special filers
- Refund only: a person who is not required to file must still file to get a refund of Arizona tax withheld.
- American Indians: must file if the requirements are met unless all apply: enrolled member of an Indian tribe, lives on that tribe's reservation, and earned all income on that reservation (ITR 96-4). The spouse of an American Indian who is not an enrolled member must file if the requirements are met.
- Military: an active duty member need not file if the only income is active duty pay and no Arizona tax was withheld. Any other income, or Arizona withholding, requires a return. A member who was an Arizona resident on entering the service remains a resident until a new domicile is established, and subtracts active duty pay on Form 140, line 32. A nonresident stationed in Arizona is not taxed on military pay but reports other Arizona income on Form 140NR (Pub. 704).
- Child's unearned income: if the parent included the child's unearned income on the federal return, the child does not file an Arizona return and the parent includes that income.
- Fiduciary returns: an estate or trust may need to file Form 141AZ.
## Examples
- Single, wages $14,000 and taxable Social Security $3,000: gross income for the test is $14,000 because Social Security is excluded; $14,000 is below the single threshold, so no return is required unless Arizona tax was withheld.
- Married filing joint, wages $40,000: $40,000 is more than the joint threshold, so Form 140 (or 140A or 140EZ) is required.
## Notes
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal return (gross income and adjusted gross income)
- Forms W-2 and 1099-R showing Arizona withholding
- Tribal enrollment and residence records, or military pay records, if relevant
## Questions
- What was your total gross income for 2026, and how much was Social Security, U.S. bond interest or military pay?
- Was any Arizona tax withheld?
- Are you an enrolled tribal member living and working on your reservation?
- Are you on active duty, and is that pay your only income?
## Common Errors
- Testing the threshold with Social Security or military pay included
- Not filing when only a refund of withholding is due
- A child filing separately for income already on the parent's return
## Prompt
- Do I have to file an Arizona return if I only made $12,000?
- I am in the military and stationed out of state. Do I file in Arizona?
