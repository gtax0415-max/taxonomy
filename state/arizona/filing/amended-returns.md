# Amended Returns (Form 140X)
## Description
To change a return after filing, do not file a new Form 140; file Arizona Form 140X, Individual Amended Income Tax Return, after the original return has processed. Generally a refund claim must be made within four years.
## When to amend
- You amend your federal return for any year: file Form 140X for that year.
- The IRS changes your federal taxable income: report the change within 90 days of the final determination, using either:
  - Option 1: file Form 140X within 90 days, including a complete copy of the federal notice; or
  - Option 2: file a copy of the final federal notice within 90 days with a statement requesting the department recompute the tax and saying whether you agree or disagree; if you disagree, include supporting documents. Mail to Individual Income Audit, Arizona Department of Revenue, PO Box 29084, Phoenix, AZ 85038-9084.
- Arizona adopts or declines federal changes after you file (the 2025 booklet notice): an amended return may be needed.
- The other state or country refunds or credits tax for which you claimed the Form 309 credit: notify the department and amend.
## Items not changed on an amended return
- Voluntary gifts (lines 64 to 74) cannot be changed later.
- The property tax credit cannot be claimed on an amended return filed after the due date of the return; a claim that only corrects Form 140PTC is made on a corrected Form 140PTC.
## Period of limitations
Usually four years from the date the return was due or filed; six years if income was understated by more than 25%; no limit for a false or fraudulent return or no return.
## Form 140X line references used elsewhere
Tax (line 37), recapture (line 38), Family Income and Dependent Tax Credits (boxes 40a and 40b), nonrefundable credits (line 41), refundable research credit (line 45), Other Adjustments (page 7, line V); these are the 2025 Form 140X lines named in Forms 301 and 309 and the MCTCP worksheet.
## Example
The IRS finalizes a $4,000 increase to 2026 federal AGI on March 1, 2028. The taxpayer files Form 140X with the federal notice by May 30, 2028 (within 90 days), reporting Arizona tax of 2.5% x $4,000 = $100 more.
## Notes
- Because Laws 2026, Chapter 140 was enacted before the 2026 forms, a 2026 return is less likely to need amending for later conformity; a 2025 return filed before June 13, 2026 may need Form 140X to pick up retroactive 2025 changes (the tips, overtime, senior and vehicle loan interest subtractions)
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- The original Arizona return as filed
- The amended federal return or final IRS determination, with its date
- Documents supporting the change
## Questions
- Did you amend your federal return or receive a final IRS change?
- When did the IRS determination become final?
- Did the other state refund tax you used for the Form 309 credit?
## Common Errors
- Filing a second Form 140 instead of Form 140X
- Missing the 90-day deadline after an IRS change
- Trying to change voluntary gifts on Form 140X
## Prompt
- The IRS adjusted my return. What do I do for Arizona?
