# Deceased Taxpayer (Form 140; Form 131)
## Description
If a taxpayer died before filing a 2026 return, the spouse or personal representative (an executor, administrator, or anyone in charge of the decedent's property) may have to file and sign a return for the decedent. If the decedent was not required to file but had tax withheld, a return must be filed to get the refund. Use the form the decedent would have used.
## On the return
- Print "deceased" after the decedent's name and enter the date of death.
- Spouse died in 2026, survivor did not remarry in 2026: a joint return may be filed showing the decedent's income before death and the survivor's income for all of 2026.
- Spouse died in 2027 before the 2026 return is filed: the joint return shows all income of both spouses for 2026.
- Print "Filing as surviving spouse" where you sign. A personal representative other than the spouse also signs.
- Standard deduction: a surviving spouse is considered married at the close of the year if the spouse died during the year; enter the joint standard deduction if a joint return would have been filed, or the separate amount if separate returns would have been filed.
## Claiming a refund
Complete Arizona Form 131, Claim for Refund on Behalf of Deceased Taxpayer, and place it behind Form 140. For a Form 140-SBI refund, also complete Form 131-SBI and place it behind Form 140-SBI. A Form 140PTC or 140ET claim for a decedent also uses Form 131.
## Example
John died on August 3, 2026; his wife Ann did not remarry. Ann files a 2026 joint Form 140 with John's income through August 3 and her income for the year, writes "deceased" and 08/03/2026 after John's name, signs "Filing as surviving spouse", and includes Form 131 to claim the refund.
## Notes
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Death certificate (date of death)
- Letters of appointment for a personal representative
- The decedent's Forms W-2 and 1099
- Arizona Form 131 if claiming a refund
## Questions
- When did the taxpayer die, and did the surviving spouse remarry in the same year?
- Who is the personal representative?
- Is a refund due?
## Common Errors
- Leaving off Form 131 when claiming a refund for a decedent
- Not writing "deceased" and the date of death after the name
- Filing single for a spouse who died during the year

- My husband died this year. How do I file his Arizona return?
