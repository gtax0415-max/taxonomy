# Filing (Arizona Form 140 header, filing status, signature area and filing rules)
## Description
This folder covers everything about the Arizona resident return that is not an income, deduction, tax or payment amount: who must file, which Arizona form to use, residency, filing status (boxes 4 to 7), the page 1 header entries and boxes, dependent information (boxes 10a and 10b, lines 10c to 10p), injured spouse protection (box 4a), due dates and extensions, amended returns, deceased taxpayers, the 2026 federal conformity law, and the signature, paid preparer and mailing rules. Form 140 is the return for full-year residents only; part-year residents file Form 140PY and nonresidents file Form 140NR.
## How the filing items flow
- Residency decides the form: full-year resident → Form 140 (or Form 140A or 140EZ if eligible); part-year resident → Form 140PY; nonresident, including a nonresident alien → Form 140NR
- Gross income over the filing threshold for the filing status → a return is required (filing-requirements.md)
- Page 1 header: box 82F (filing under extension), fiscal year dates (66F), names and SSNs (line 1), address and daytime phone (lines 2, 94), city, state, ZIP and last names used in the last four prior years (lines 3, 97); revenue use boxes 80, 81 and 88
- Boxes 4 to 7: filing status (married filing joint, head of household, married filing separate, single); box 4a injured spouse
- Boxes 8, 9, 10a, 10b and 11a: numbers claimed for exemptions and the Dependent Tax Credit; lines 10c to 10e and 11b to 11c on page 1, continued on page 4, Parts 1 and 2
- Line 12 onward: income (income/), deductions (deductions/), tax (tax-computation/), credits (credits/), payments (payments/), voluntary gifts (payments/voluntary-gifts.md)
- Page 2 footer: signatures, occupations, paid preparer block, mailing addresses
## General rules for every return
- Complete the federal return first; Arizona starts from federal adjusted gross income (line 12)
- Round to whole dollars: 50 cents or more rounds up, less than 50 cents rounds down; do not enter cents
- Use black ink; enter SSNs in the same order every year, and the same order as the first names
- Enter the number claimed in boxes 8, 9, 10a, 10b, 11a and 40E; do not use a check mark or an "X"
- Include Forms W-2, W-2G, 1099-B, 1099-DIV, 1099-G, 1099-INT, 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC and 1099-R only if they show Arizona withholding
- Do not staple any items to the return
## What changed for 2026
- Federal conformity, new subtractions and credit changes: see federal-conformity-and-2026-law.md (HB 4168, Laws 2026, Chapter 140)
- Filing thresholds and the standard deduction are expected to follow the federal 2026 amounts under A.R.S. 43-1041
- Due dates: April 15, 2027 (return), October 15, 2027 (extended)
