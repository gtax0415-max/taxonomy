# Dependent Information (Form 140, Boxes 10a and 10b, Lines 10c to 10p)
## Description
Boxes 10a and 10b give the number of qualifying dependents under age 17 (box 10a) and age 17 and over (box 10b). They are used to compute the Dependent Tax Credit on line 49. Include only dependents used for that credit. You may claim only individuals who qualify as your dependent for federal purposes.
## Columns for each dependent (lines 10c to 10e, page 4 lines 10f to 10p)
| Column | Entry |
|---|---|
| (a) | First and last name (do not list yourself or spouse) |
| (b) | Social Security number |
| (c) | Relationship to taxpayer |
| (d) | Number of months the person lived in your home in 2026 (temporary absences for illness, education, business or vacation count; a child's required hospital stay after birth counts) |
| (e) | Box 1 if under age 17 (box 10a); box 2 if age 17 or over (box 10b) |
| (f) | Check if you did not claim this person on your federal return due to educational credits |
## Rules
- Lines 10c to 10e hold three dependents; for more, check the box on page 1 and complete page 4, Part 1 (lines 10f to 10p). With more than three dependents, Part 1 and the worksheet in the instructions are required for line 49.
- A dependent not claimed federally so the student could claim a federal education credit may still be claimed in Arizona (ITR 05-2).
- A qualifying parent or grandparent who is also your dependent may go in box 10b or box 11a, not both.
- An individual claimed as an Other Exemption on line 40 or as a qualifying parent or grandparent on line 41 cannot be included in box 10b.
- On married filing separate returns, a dependent supported from community income may be claimed by either spouse, not both.
- You may lose the Dependent Tax Credit if this information is not furnished.
## Example
A couple has children aged 12 and 16 and a 19-year-old full-time student, all federal dependents. Box 10a = 2, box 10b = 1, and three rows are completed on lines 10c to 10e.
## Notes
- For 2026, each dependent in box 10a is worth $125 on line 49 (up from $100; Laws 2026, Chapter 140), so the box 10a / 10b split matters more
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN, relationship, months in the home and age at the end of 2026
- Records of any education credit release
## Questions
- Who are your federal dependents, and how old is each on December 31, 2026?
- How many months did each live with you?
- Did you release a student so he or she could claim an education credit?
- Is any dependent also a parent or grandparent you support?
## Common Errors
- Putting a check mark instead of a number in boxes 10a and 10b
- Counting the same person in box 10b and box 11a or line 40
- Classifying a child who turned 17 during 2026 as under 17
## Prompt
- Where do I list my kids on the Arizona return?
