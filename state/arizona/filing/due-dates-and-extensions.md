# Due Dates and Extensions (Form 140, Box 82F; Form 204)
## Description
The 2026 calendar year return is due by midnight, April 15, 2027. A fiscal year return is due the 15th day of the fourth month after the close of the fiscal year. If a due date falls on a weekend or legal holiday, a return or payment is timely on the next business day.
## Extensions
- Automatic 6-month extension, to October 15, 2027, by either:
  - Arizona Form 204, filed (postmarked) by April 15, 2027, or
  - the federal extension (Form 4868), which Arizona accepts for the period it covers.
- Either way, check box 82F on page 1; a copy of the extension is not included with the return.
- An extension does not extend the time to pay. Pay at least 90% of the tax by the original due date to avoid the extension underpayment penalty; interest runs from the original due date.
- Form 204 lines 1 to 7: tax liability, Arizona withholding, estimated payments, credits, total, balance, payment enclosed. If filing under a federal extension but paying by credit card or electronic payment, do not mail Form 204.
- Form 204 also extends the small business income return (Form 140-SBI); an extension payment for that return uses Form 204-SBI.
- Nonresident aliens: the Arizona return is due April 15 even when the federal return is due in June.
- Property tax credit claims (Form 140PTC) must be filed by April 15 or the extended due date.

## Mailing Form 204
With a payment: Arizona Department of Revenue, PO Box 29085, Phoenix, AZ 85038-9085. Without a payment: PO Box 52138, Phoenix, AZ 85072-2138. Write "2026 Extension Request" on the envelope (2025 instructions use the 2025 year).
## Example
Tax for 2026 is expected to be $3,000 with $2,200 withheld. Paying $500 with Form 204 by April 15, 2027 brings payments to $2,700, which is 90% of $3,000, so no extension underpayment penalty applies; interest is charged on the remaining $300 from April 15, 2027 until paid.

## Required Information
- Form 204 or federal Form 4868
- Extension payment confirmation from AZTaxes.gov
## Questions
- Will you file by April 15, 2027? If not, did you file Form 204 or a federal extension?
- Did you pay at least 90% of the tax by April 15, 2027?
## Common Errors
- Filing under extension without checking box 82F
- Treating the extension as an extension of time to pay
- Mailing Form 204 when the payment was made electronically under a federal extension
