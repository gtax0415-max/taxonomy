# Residency Status (Form 140 vs Form 140PY vs Form 140NR)
## Description
Form 140 is for full-year residents only. You, and your spouse if filing jointly, may file Form 140 only if both of you were full-year Arizona residents. A resident is taxed on all income no matter where it is earned.
## Definitions
- Resident: domicile in Arizona. Domicile is the place of your permanent home, where you intend to return when living or working temporarily in another state or country. A temporary absence does not end residency.
- Part-year resident: during 2026 moved into Arizona intending to become a resident, or moved out intending to give up Arizona residency. File Form 140PY.
- Nonresident (including a nonresident alien): file Form 140NR. A nonresident alien electing small business income reporting files Form 140NR-SBI.
- Help determining residency: ITP 92-1, Procedure for Determining Residency Status.
## Joint returns with a spouse who is not a full-year resident
A joint Form 140 is not allowed if the spouse is a nonresident alien (a citizen of and living in another country), a resident of another state, or a part-year Arizona resident. A joint return with a part-year resident spouse is filed on Form 140PY, and with a nonresident spouse on Form 140NR (ITR 14-1). When one spouse is a resident and the other is not and they file separately, special rules apply (ITR 93-20, Pub. 200).
## Military
A member who was an Arizona resident when entering the service remains a resident wherever stationed until a new domicile is established. A nonresident stationed in Arizona files Form 140NR for non-military Arizona income.
## Dual residents
A full-year Arizona resident who is also a resident of another state under that state's law may claim a credit on Form 309 for tax paid to that state on income that would be sourced there if filed as a nonresident.
## Example
A couple lives in Phoenix all year; one spouse works in California for four months and returns. Both are full-year residents and file a joint Form 140 reporting all income.
## Notes
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Facts about your permanent home and intent to return
- Dates of any move into or out of Arizona in 2026
- Your spouse's residency
## Questions
- Where was your permanent home during 2026, and did you intend to return to Arizona while away?
- Did you move into or out of Arizona during 2026, and on what date?
- Was your spouse an Arizona resident for the whole year?
## Common Errors
- Filing a joint Form 140 when one spouse is a part-year resident or nonresident
- Treating a temporary work assignment out of state as a change of residency
- A nonresident alien filing Form 140
## Prompt
- I moved to Arizona in July. Which form do I file?
- My wife lives in Mexico. Can we file a joint Form 140?
