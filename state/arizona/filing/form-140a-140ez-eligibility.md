# Form 140A and Form 140EZ Eligibility (When Form 140 Is Required)
## Description
Full-year residents may file Form 140, 140A or 140EZ. Form 140 must be used instead of Form 140A or 140EZ if any of the following apply (2025 rules; confirm against the 2026 forms):
- Arizona taxable income is $50,000 or more, regardless of filing status
- You are making adjustments to income (any line 15 to 36 addition or subtraction)
- You itemize deductions
- You claim tax credits other than the family income tax credit, the credit for increased excise taxes or the property tax credit
- You are claiming estimated payments
- You received the Arizona Families Tax Rebate and are subtracting it (page 6, item U; a 2025 item, with no 2026 rebate)
- The booklet's list also names active duty military pay as a member of the U.S. Armed Forces and pay for active service as a reservist or National Guard member
## Related rules
- The tips, overtime and senior subtractions (and, for 2025 only, vehicle loan interest) are not available on Form 140A or 140EZ; a resident who wants them must file Form 140 (MCTCP worksheet).
- The property tax credit cannot be claimed on Form 140EZ; use Form 140 or 140A with Form 140PTC.
- Form 140PTC and the increased excise tax credit flow to Form 140A, lines 27 and 26 (2025 lines).
## Example
Single, wages $38,000, standard deduction, no other items: taxable income is under $50,000 and no adjustments apply, so Form 140A or 140EZ may be used. If the same person had $2,000 of qualified overtime deducted federally, Form 140 is required to subtract it.
## Notes
- The new 2026 subtractions (Trump account distributions, dependent care above the federal credit) are adjustments to income, so they also require Form 140
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- A draft Arizona return showing taxable income, adjustments, deduction method, credits and estimated payments
## Questions
- Will your Arizona taxable income be $50,000 or more?
- Do you have any additions or subtractions, such as U.S. bond interest, Social Security, 529 contributions, tips or overtime?
- Will you itemize or claim school, charity or other credits?
- Did you make estimated payments?
## Common Errors
- Using Form 140EZ to claim the property tax credit
- Using Form 140A or 140EZ and losing the tips, overtime or senior subtraction
