# Credits (Form 140, Lines 49 to 51 and 56 to 58)
## Description
Arizona credits are either nonrefundable (they reduce tax on lines 49 to 51 but not below zero) or refundable (they are added to payments on lines 56 to 58 and can produce a refund).
## How the lines flow
```
Nonrefundable (reduce line 48 → line 52)
Line 49  Dependent Tax Credit: $125 per dependent under 17, $25 per dependent 17 and over (2026), phased out above $200,000 / $400,000 federal AGI
Line 50  Family Income Tax Credit (worksheet; $40 per person, maximum $240 or $120)
Line 51  Form 301 nonrefundable credits (Form 301, Part 2, line 60)
Refundable (added to payments on line 59)
Line 56  Increased Excise Tax Credit ($25 per person, maximum $100 per household)
Line 57  Property Tax Credit (Form 140PTC, line 15)
Line 58  Other refundable credits: box 581 Form 308-I (repealed from 2026), 582 Form 334, 583 Form 349
```
## What changed for 2026
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026:
- DEPENDENT TAX CREDIT rises from $100 to $125 per dependent under 17 (line 49); the $25 amount and phase-out are unchanged
- REFUNDABLE R&D GONE: box 581 (Form 308-I) has no 2026 amount; the research credit stays as a nonrefundable credit on Form 301, line 1
- NEW EMPLOYMENT CREDIT (Form 345) repealed from 2026
- NOT repealed: Solar Energy Devices (Form 310) and Renewable Energy Production (Form 343); HB 4152, which would have repealed them, was vetoed

Unchanged because they are set by statute and not indexed: Family Income Tax Credit, Increased Excise Tax Credit, Property Tax Credit, Public School credit ($200 / $400) and Military Family Relief Fund credit ($200 / $400).

Last year under current law: the Military Family Relief Fund credit (Form 340) ends December 31, 2026, and Form 315 pollution control carryovers from 2021 expire after 2026.
## Form 301 not required for
The Dependent Tax Credit, Family Income Tax Credit, Property Tax Credit, Increased Excise Tax Credit, the refundable research credit (tax years before 2026 only), and the Form 334 and Form 349 refundable credits.

## Notes
- Form 140 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; confirm them against the 2026 Form 140 when ADOR releases it
- Nonrefundable credits apply in this order: line 49, then line 50, then Form 301 (line 51)
## Required Information
- Federal AGI, filing status and each dependent's age at the end of 2026
- Every credit form and its receipts or certifications
- Prior-year Form 301 Credit Carryover Worksheet
## Questions
- How many dependents do you have, and what is your federal AGI?
- Is your income low enough for the family income, excise or property tax credits?
- Did you give to QCOs, QFCOs, public schools, STOs or the Military Family Relief Fund?
- Did you pay tax to another state, install a solar device or receive pass-through credits?
## Prompt
- What tax credits does Arizona offer?
