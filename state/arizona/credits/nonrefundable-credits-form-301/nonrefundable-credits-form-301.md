# Nonrefundable Credits and Recapture (Form 301 → Form 140, Line 51)
## Description
Form 301 summarizes available nonrefundable credits (current year plus carryovers), applies them against tax, and totals recapture. Include Form 301 and each credit form with the return, or the credit may be denied. Total credits used (line 60) cannot exceed line 33 and go on Form 140, line 51. Form 301 is not needed to claim only the Dependent Tax Credit, Family Income Tax Credit, Property Tax Credit, Increased Excise Tax Credit, or the refundable research, motion picture or qualified facilities credits.
## What changed for 2026
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026:
- REPEALED for taxable years beginning January 1, 2026: the Credit for New Employment (Form 345, line 17); a savings clause covers credits already established
- REFUNDABLE R&D GONE: the 75% refund of excess Form 308-I credit is eliminated retroactive to January 1, 2026; the nonrefundable credit and its 15-year carryforward remain
- DEPENDENT TAX CREDIT rises from $100 to $125 per dependent under 17 (it reduces the line 33 limit through line 32)
- NOT REPEALED: Solar Energy Devices (Form 310) and Renewable Energy Production (Form 343). An earlier omnibus, HB 4152, would have repealed both but was vetoed

Inflation-adjusted 2026 caps (ADOR): QCO $506 / $1,009; QFCO $632 / $1,262; private STO (Form 323) $787 / $1,570; switcher STO (Form 348) $784 / $1,561 (single or head of household / married filing joint).

## Ordering with the other-state credit
Arizona allows the Form 309 credit only after all other credits: first credits that cannot be carried over or refunded, then credits with a limited carryover, then refundable credits; any remaining tax may then absorb the Form 309 credit.
## Credit Carryover Worksheet (kept with records)
For up to ten credit types: (a) credit form; (b) whether carryover is allowed; (c) credit available for 2026 (Form 301, lines 1 to 24); (d) credit used (lines 34 to 58); (e) carryover to 2027 = (c) - (d). A Form 308-I credit refunded is not carried over.
