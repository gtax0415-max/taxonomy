# Credit for Agricultural Pollution Control Equipment (Form 325; Form 301, Lines 9 and 42)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for taxpayers in commercial agriculture who incur expenses to purchase tangible personal property used primarily in the trade or business to control or prevent pollution. Unused credit carries forward five years.
## Form 301 entries
Column (a) Form 325, line 25; column (b) line 26; column (c) line 27. Line 42: credit used, not more than line 9, column (c).
## Example
A dairy buys qualifying manure control equipment and Form 325 shows a $2,500 credit; with tax of $4,000 the full $2,500 is used on line 42.
## Notes
- Not affected by the 2026 repeal of the corporate pollution control equipment credit; that repeal (Laws 2026, Chapter 140) applies to a different credit
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Purchase invoices for the equipment and a description of how it controls or prevents pollution
- Prior Form 325 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Did your farm buy pollution control equipment in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the basis and depreciation add-backs when the equipment is sold

- Is there an Arizona credit for farm pollution control equipment?
