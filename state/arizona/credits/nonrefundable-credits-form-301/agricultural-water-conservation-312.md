# Agricultural Water Conservation System Credit (Form 312; Form 301, Lines 4 and 37)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for expenses incurred to purchase and install an agricultural water conservation system in Arizona. The expenses disallowed because of the credit are added back on page 5, G(a), or G(b) for a credit passed through from an S corporation (Form 312-S, line 1). Unused credit carries forward five years.
## Form 301 entries
Column (a) Form 312, line 17; column (b) line 18; column (c) line 19. Line 37: credit used, not more than line 4, column (c).
## Example
A farmer's Form 312 shows a current credit of $5,000 and tax of $3,000: $3,000 is used on Form 301, line 37 and $2,000 carries forward.
## Notes
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- The S corporation's statement of your pro rata share, for a pass-through credit
- Invoices for purchasing and installing the system in 2026
- Amount of those expenses deducted federally (for the page 5, G add-back)
- Prior Form 312 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
