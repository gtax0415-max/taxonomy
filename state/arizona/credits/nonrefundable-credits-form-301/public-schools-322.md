# Credit for Contributions Made or Fees Paid to Public Schools (Form 322; Form 301, Lines 7 and 40)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit equal to cash contributions made or certain fees paid to an Arizona public school (a school district school, a career technical education district or a charter school providing kindergarten through grade 12), up to $200 single or head of household and $400 married filing joint (statutory amounts, not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026). Married filing separate: generally one-half of the joint credit. Only individuals may claim it; it carries forward five years.
## Qualifying purposes
- Standardized testing for college credit or readiness (SAT, PSAT, ACT, AP, IB and similar)
- Career and technical education industry certification assessments
- Preparation courses and materials for standardized testing
- Cardiopulmonary resuscitation training
- Extracurricular activities (school-sponsored, for example band uniforms, varsity athletic equipment, lab equipment, in-state or out-of-state trips solely for competitive events; not senior trips or recreational, amusement or tourist events)
- Character education programs (A.R.S. 15-719)
- From June 30, 2019 through June 30, 2029: capital items, community school meal programs (not a meal or meal card for the payer), student consumable health care supplies, and playground equipment and shade structures
## Form 322 lines
Lines 1 to 3 (2026): (a) date, (b) 9-digit CTDS code, (c) school name, (d) district or charter holder, (e) amount; 4 Continuation Sheet (4a to 4h); 5 total; 6 to 8 and 9 (9a to 9h) for January 1 to April 15, 2027 payments claimed on 2026; 10 total; 11 line 5 + line 10; 12 cap; 13 smaller (one-half if separate); 14 to 19 carryover from 2021 to 2025; 20 to 22 → Form 301, line 7.
## Example
Single parent pays $250 of extracurricular fees and gives $50 for CPR training at an Arizona public high school in 2026: line 11 $300, line 13 = $200.
## Notes
- A payment made January 1 to April 15, 2027 can go on the 2026 or the 2027 return, but not both
- The capital items, meal programs, health supplies and playground purposes are allowed through June 30, 2029
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Receipts showing school name, district or charter holder, amount, date and activity
- The 9-digit CTDS code for each school
- Filing status
- Prior Form 322 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Which public schools did you pay fees or give to, for what activity, and do you have the CTDS codes and receipts?
## Common Errors
- Claiming preschool or university payments
- Claiming senior trip fees
- Exceeding the cap on separate returns
