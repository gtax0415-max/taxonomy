# Credit for Donation of School Site (Form 331; Form 301, Lines 10 and 43)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for donating real property and improvements to a school district or a charter school for use as a school or as a site for constructing a school. If the donation was deducted on federal Schedule A, it is removed on Arizona Schedule A, line 6. Unused credit carries forward five years.
## Form 301 entries
Column (a) Form 331, line 19; column (b) line 20; column (c) line 21. Line 43: credit used, not more than line 10, column (c).
## Example
Form 331 shows a $15,000 credit and 2026 tax is $6,000: $6,000 is used and $9,000 carries forward.
## Notes
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Deed and appraisal or other valuation of the donated property and improvements
- The school district's or charter school's acceptance of the donation
- Prior Form 331 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Did you donate land or buildings to a school district or charter school?
## Common Errors
- Also deducting the donation on Arizona Schedule A
## Prompt
- Is there an Arizona credit for donating land for a school?
