# Credit for Business Contributions by an S Corporation to School Tuition Organizations for Displaced Students or Students with Disabilities - Individual (Form 341-I; Form 301, Lines 15 and 48)
## Description
An S corporation that makes qualifying contributions of $5,000 or more to a school tuition organization for displaced students or students with disabilities may pass the credit through to its individual shareholders pro rata; shareholders claim it on Form 341-I. Unused credit carries forward five years. Form 341-I itself is not among the sources.
## Form 301 entries
Column (a) Form 341-I, line 19; column (b) line 20; column (c) line 21. Line 48: credit used, not more than line 15, column (c).
## Example
A shareholder's pro rata Form 341-I credit is $2,000 and tax is $2,400: $2,000 is used on line 48.
## Notes
- Corporate donations for this credit (Form 341) must be pre-approved by ADOR through the STO; the statewide cap is $6,000,000 per fiscal year
- This is separate from the low-income S corporation credit on Form 335-I (Form 301, line 12)
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Form 341-I and the S corporation's statement of your pro rata share of the credit
- Your ownership percentage in the S corporation for 2026
- Prior Form 341-I carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Did your S corporation pass through a credit for gifts to an STO for displaced or disabled students?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the corporation's whole credit instead of the pro rata share
