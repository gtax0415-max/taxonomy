# Credit for Contributions to Private School Tuition Organizations (Form 323; Form 301, Lines 8 and 41)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for cash contributions to a certified private school tuition organization (STO) that gives scholarships or tuition grants to qualified schools. Only individuals may claim it; it carries forward five years.
## Maximum credit
| Filing status | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Single, head of household | $769 | $787 |
| Married filing joint | $1,535 | $1,570 |
| Married filing separate | one-half of joint, up to $769 | one-half of joint, up to $787 |
## STO and qualified school
An STO is exempt under IRC 501(c)(3), allocates at least 90% of annual contribution revenue to scholarships or tuition grants, and makes them available to students of more than one qualified school. A qualified school is an Arizona nongovernmental primary or secondary school (kindergarten through grade 12) or a preschool serving students with disabilities that does not discriminate and fingerprints staff with unsupervised student contact; charter schools are excluded. ADOR certifies STOs.
## Not allowed
A contribution designated for the direct benefit of your own dependent (IRC 152 meaning), a contribution conditioned on naming a student beneficiary, or "swapping" designations with another donor.
## Form 323 lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1-5 | 2026 contributions: date, STO name, street address, city and state, amount; 4 Continuation Sheet (4a to 4h); 5 total |
| 6-10 | January 1 to April 15, 2027 contributions claimed on 2026 (9a to 9h); 10 total |
| 11 | Line 5 + line 10 |
| 12 | Cap: $787 single or head of household; $1,570 married (2026) |
| 13 | Smaller of 11 or 12; one-half if married filing separate |
| 14-19 | Carryover from 2021 to 2025 |
| 20-22 | → Form 301, line 8, columns (a), (b), (c) |
| 23 | Part 4: total contributions (line 11; one-half for married filing separate) |
| 24 | Maximum Form 323 credit: $787 single or head of household or separate; $1,570 joint (2026) |
| 25 | Excess contributions = line 23 - line 24, not less than 0 → available for Form 348 |
## Example
Single, gave $1,400 to a certified STO in 2026, tax $400: Form 323 credit $787; uses $400, carries $387 forward; Part 4 excess $1,400 - $787 = $613 → Form 348.
## Notes
- Gifts made January 1 to April 15, 2027 can go on the 2026 return at the 2026 cap, or on the 2027 return at the higher 2027 cap ($797 single or head of household; $1,589 married filing joint)
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Receipts from certified STOs (name, address, amount, date, tax year claimed)
- Gifts made January 1 to April 15, 2027 that you want on the 2026 return
- Filing status
- Prior Form 323 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Which certified STOs did you give to and when? Were any gifts designated for your own child?
- Did you give more than the Form 323 cap?
## Common Errors
- Designating the gift for your own dependent
- Not completing Part 4 when contributions exceed the cap
