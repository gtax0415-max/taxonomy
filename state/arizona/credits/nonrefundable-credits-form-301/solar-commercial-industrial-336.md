# Credit for Solar Energy Devices - Commercial and Industrial Applications (Form 336, Expired)
## Description
The credit for installed solar energy devices for commercial, industrial or other nonresidential applications located in Arizona has expired (Form 140 instructions, line 51). The 2025 booklet version adds that properly established credits can be carried forward for the respective five-year period using Form 336, but the 2025 Form 301 has no line for this credit. Treatment of any remaining carryover is a point to confirm.
## Example
A business owner who installed commercial solar panels in 2026 has no Arizona credit for them; a home installation would use Form 310.
## Notes
- The credit is expired, not newly repealed; nothing in Laws 2026, Chapter 140 changes it
## Required Information
- Prior Form 336 filings and carryover records, if any
## Questions
- Do you have a Form 336 carryover from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the residential Form 310 credit for a commercial installation
## Prompt
- Does Arizona give a credit for solar on my business building?
