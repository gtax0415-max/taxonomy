# Credit for Taxes Paid to Another State or Country (Form 309; Form 301, Lines 2 and 35)
## Description
Arizona residents are allowed a credit for net income taxes imposed by and paid to another state or country on income also taxed by Arizona in the same year, if the income is sourced in the other state or country and that jurisdiction does not give Arizona residents a credit. Payroll withholding alone is not a net income tax; you must file a net income tax return there. Foreign taxes qualify if creditable under IRC 901 and 903 (complete Form 309 even if federal Form 1116 was not required). The credit is used only in the year incurred: no carryforward or carryback. It is applied after all other credits (A.R.S. 43-1071). Notify the department and amend if the other jurisdiction later refunds the tax.
## Pass-through entity taxes (2025 lists)
Nonresident returns filed with Alabama, Arkansas, Colorado, Connecticut, District of Columbia, Georgia, Idaho, Illinois, Kansas, Louisiana, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, Ohio, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, South Carolina, Utah and Wisconsin qualify for the credit for entity-level tax paid on your behalf; California, Oregon and Virginia do not. The credit for entity-level tax may not exceed what would have been allowed had the income been taxed at the individual level (A.R.S. 43-1071(G)). The lists are subject to change; confirm them against the 2026 Form 309 instructions.
## Form 309 lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| A / B | Other state (two-letter code) or other country ("Various" for multiple countries through a RIC) |
| 1 | Description of each income item taxed by both (columns a to c; no SBI items) |
| 2 | Income from the item reportable to both |
| 3 | Portion included in Arizona adjusted gross income |
| 4 | Portion included in the other jurisdiction's equivalent of Arizona AGI (source income, as adjusted under its law) |
| 5 | Smaller of line 3 or line 4 |
| 6 | Total of line 5 (not less than 0) |
| 7 | Arizona tax less all other credits (Form 140, line 46 less credits), plus your share of entity-level tax where applicable |
| 8 | Line 6 |
| 9 | Entire income on which Arizona tax is imposed: Form 140, line 42 + lines 38 to 41 |
| 10 | Line 8 / line 9 (four decimals, not more than 1.0000) |
| 11 | Line 7 x line 10 |
| 12a/12b | Other state or country name; income tax paid there (less credits; no federal, city or county tax, penalties or interest; entity-level tax limited to the lesser of tax paid or the individual-level tax) |
| 13 | Line 6 |
| 14 | Entire income on which the other jurisdiction's tax is imposed (for a nonresident there, only income sourced there) |
| 15 | Line 13 / line 14 (not more than 1.0000) |
| 16 | Line 12b x line 15 |
| 17 | Smaller of line 11 or line 16 → Form 301, line 2, column (a); total of all Forms 309 if more than one |

Page 2, Schedule of Income Allocation (dual residents only): lines 1 to 10 by income type, columns (a) federal, (b) Arizona, (c) statutory state of residence, (d) the part of (c) sourced there as for a nonresident; column (d) feeds Part 1, line 1.
## Example
From the instructions (figures illustrative): Arizona tax after other credits $730; farm income taxed by State XY $16,000; entire Arizona income $58,000; line 10 .2759; line 11 $201; State XY tax $486 with ratio 1.0000; line 17 = $201.
## Notes
- No carryover: Form 301, line 2 has no column (b) amount
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- The other state's or country's return as filed, and proof of payment
- Income items taxed by both jurisdictions and the amounts reported to each
- Schedule K-1s showing entity-level tax paid in other states
- Currency conversion for foreign tax
## Questions
- Did you file a resident or nonresident return with another state or country, and pay its tax?
- Which income did both tax? Did a partnership or S corporation pay tax for you elsewhere?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit for withholding without filing the other state's return
- Claiming for California, Oregon, Virginia and the other listed states' nonresident returns
- Applying the credit before other credits
## Prompt
- I worked in New Mexico and paid tax there. Do I get an Arizona credit?
