# Credit for Entity-Level Income Tax Paid on Your Behalf (Form 355; Form 301, Lines 23 and 56)
## Description
For taxable years beginning after December 31, 2021, a partnership or S corporation may elect to pay Arizona tax at the entity level, at the individual rate (2.5% for 2026, the same flat rate as 2025) on income attributable to resident owners and Arizona-source income attributable to nonresident owners. Each owner is notified and has at least 60 days to opt out. Individual owners included in the election claim a nonrefundable credit for their share on Form 355; unused credit carries forward five years.
## Form 355 lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Partnership payment: Form 165 Schedule K-1, Part 7, line 11, or K-1(NR), Part 8, line 23 |
| 2 | S corporation payment: Form 120S Schedule K-1, Part 5, line 8, or K-1(NR), Part 6, line 21 |
| 3 | Fiduciary distributed credit: Form 141AZ Schedule K-1, Part 5, line 12, or K-1(NR), Part 6, line 14 |
| 4 | Lines 1 + 2 + 3 (add all K-1s) |
| 5-9 | Carryover by year (2022, 2023, 2024 printed on the 2025 form; 2025 to be added for 2026) |
| 10 | Total carryover |
| 11 | Line 4 → Form 301, line 23, column (a) (or Form 301-SBI, line 16) |
| 12 | Line 10 → Form 301, line 23, column (b) |
| 13 | Line 11 + line 12 → Form 301, line 23, column (c) |

The 2025 instructions for line 12 said not to enter the carryover on Form 301, line 23, column (b), while the form said to enter it there; check whether the 2026 instructions resolve this.
## Example
K-1 from an S corporation shows $1,250 of Arizona entity-level tax paid for you: line 2 $1,250, line 4 $1,250 → Form 301, line 23 = $1,250; page 5, P addition of the amount deducted.
## Notes
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Every Arizona Schedule K-1 or K-1(NR) showing entity-level tax paid on your behalf
- The amount of the entity-level tax deducted at the entity level (for the page 5, P addition)
- Prior Form 355 carryovers from 2022 to 2025
## Questions
- Did any partnership or S corporation make the Arizona entity-level tax election for you?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit without the page 5, P addition
