# Credit for Increased Research Activities - Individuals (Form 308-I; Form 301, Lines 1 and 34)
## Description
A credit for qualified research expenses for research conducted in Arizona. The nonrefundable credit carries forward 15 years. Through 2025, a qualifying taxpayer with an ACA Certificate of Qualification could take a refund of 75% of the current year's excess credit (Form 308-I, Part 7, line 38 → Form 140, line 58, box 581), with no carryforward from the year of the refund. HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, eliminates the refundable portion and the related Arizona Commerce Authority certification, retroactive to January 1, 2026.
## Form 301 entries
- 2026: columns (a), (b), (c) from Form 308-I, lines 68, 69, 70 (2025 line numbers; confirm on the 2026 Form 308-I). No refund option.
- 2025 only (for reference): a taxpayer claiming the 75% refund entered Form 308-I, line 34(d) in column (a), 0 in column (b).
- Line 34: credit used, not more than line 1, column (c).
## Example
A 2026 research credit of $30,000 against $10,000 of tax: $10,000 is used on Form 301, line 34 and $20,000 carries forward; no refund for 2026.
## Notes
- A 2025 credit that was refunded does not carry over into 2026
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Records of qualified research expenses for research conducted in Arizona in 2026 and the base-period amounts
- Prior Form 308-I carryovers (15-year carryforward)
## Questions
- Did you conduct qualified research in Arizona? Do you have prior-year carryovers?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the refundable portion for 2026
## Prompt
- Is the Arizona research credit still refundable in 2026?
