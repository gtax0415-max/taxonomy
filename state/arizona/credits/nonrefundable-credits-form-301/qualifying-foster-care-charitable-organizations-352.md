# Credit for Contributions to Qualifying Foster Care Charitable Organizations (Form 352; Form 301, Lines 20 and 53)
## Description
A separate nonrefundable credit for cash contributions to qualifying foster care charitable organizations (QFCOs). Contributions to a QFCO after December 31, 2015 cannot be claimed on Form 321. Only individuals may claim it; it carries forward five years.
## Maximum credit
| Filing status | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Single, married filing separate, head of household | $618 | $632 |
| Married filing joint | $1,234 | $1,262 |

On married filing separate returns, the form computes one-half of the joint credit. A gift made January 1 to April 15, 2027 can be claimed on the 2026 return at the 2026 cap, or on the 2027 return at the higher 2027 cap ($640 single, married filing separate or head of household; $1,278 married filing joint).
## What is a QFCO
A 501(c)(3) charity or designated community action agency that each operating year provides services to at least 200 qualified individuals in Arizona and spends at least 50% of its budget on services to qualified individuals in Arizona. Qualified individuals include foster children, people in independent living, transitional independent living or extended foster care programs, and people under 27 who left foster care on reaching 18, through adoption or guardianship after 15, or through reunification after 14. Services include cash assistance, medical care, behavioral health, child care, food (including snacks at foster youth events), clothing, shelter, job placement and training, character education, workforce development, secondary education student retention, housing or financial literacy, and other assistance to meet basic needs or provide normalcy, used in Arizona.
## Form 352 lines
Same structure as Form 321: lines 1 to 3 and Continuation Sheet 4a to 4h (2026 gifts); lines 6 to 8 and 9a to 9h (January 1 to April 15, 2027 gifts); 5, 10, 11 totals; 12 cap; 13 current credit (one-half if separate); 14 to 19 carryover from 2021 to 2025; 20 to 22 → Form 301, line 20, columns (a) to (c).
## Example
Married filing joint, gave $1,500 to a certified QFCO in 2026 and $1,009 to a QCO: Form 352 credit $1,262 and Form 321 credit $1,009, both on Form 301.
## Notes
- Do not count January 1 to April 15, 2026 gifts already claimed on the 2025 return
- A gift used for this credit cannot also be an itemized deduction or count toward the charitable standard deduction increase
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Receipts from certified QFCOs showing the 5-digit QFCO code, date and amount
- Gifts made January 1 to April 15, 2027 that you want on the 2026 return
- Filing status
- Prior Form 352 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- Which foster care charities did you support, and are they on the certified QFCO list?
## Common Errors
- Putting QFCO gifts on Form 321
- Using the 2025 cap for 2026
## Prompt
- What is the Arizona foster care tax credit limit for 2026?
