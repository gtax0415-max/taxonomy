# Credit for Donations to the Military Family Relief Fund (Form 340; Form 301, Lines 14 and 47)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for cash donations to the Military Family Relief Fund administered by the Arizona Department of Veterans' Services. The credit was extended through December 31, 2026 (Laws 2018, HB 2192), so 2026 is the last year it is available unless the Legislature extends it again. Donations qualify only until total donations for the calendar year reach one million dollars, on a first come, first served basis decided by ADVS, which issues the receipt. The credit is the smallest of: the amount donated; $200 single or head of household; $400 married filing joint ($200 each on separate returns, one-half of the joint credit); and the tax liability. There is no carryover. Only individuals may claim it; no itemized deduction for the same donation.
## Subaccounts
Pre-9/11 and post-9/11 veterans subaccounts give financial assistance based on need, up to $20,000 per family, to families of service members who were deceased, wounded, injured or became seriously ill on or before (pre-9/11) or after (post-9/11) September 11, 2001, and who were deployed from an Arizona base, claimed Arizona as home of record, or were Arizona National Guard members at deployment; if discharged, the discharge was honorable.
## Form 340 lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Received an ADVS receipt showing the contribution qualifies? (No → stop) |
| 2 | Qualified donations to the pre-9/11 subaccount in 2026 |
| 3 | Qualified donations to the post-9/11 subaccount in 2026 |
| 4 | Line 2 + line 3 |
| 5 | $200 single or head of household; $400 married filing joint; $200 married filing separate |
| 6 | Smaller of line 4 or line 5 → Form 301, line 14, columns (a) and (c) |
## Form 301, line 47
Enter the smaller of Form 301, Part 1, line 14 or Part 2, line 31 (as printed on the 2025 form; confirm on the 2026 form).
## Example
Married filing joint, donated $500 to the post-9/11 subaccount with a qualifying receipt: line 4 $500, line 6 $400.
## Notes
- 2026 IS THE LAST YEAR under current law: the credit ends December 31, 2026. Confirm on the 2026 Form 340 whether a further extension was enacted
- Donations count only until total qualifying donations for the calendar year reach $1,000,000
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- The ADVS receipt stating the donation qualifies and showing the subaccount
- Amount and date of each 2026 donation
- Filing status
## Questions
- Do you have the ADVS receipt saying the donation qualifies? Which subaccount?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without a qualifying receipt
- Expecting a carryover
- Planning a 2027 donation for this credit (it expires after 2026 unless extended)
## Prompt
- Is there an Arizona credit for donating to military families?
