# Credit for Contributions to Certified School Tuition Organizations (Form 348, the "switcher" credit; Form 301, Lines 19 and 52)
## Description
A second, separate nonrefundable credit for STO contributions that exceed the maximum Form 323 credit. You must first claim the maximum current-year credit on Form 323. Only individuals may claim it; it carries forward five years. A taxpayer with a Form 348 carryover may use it without claiming a Form 323 credit.
## Maximum credit
| Filing status | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Single, head of household | $766 | $784 |
| Married filing joint | $1,527 | $1,561 |
| Married filing separate | one-half of joint | one-half of joint |
## Form 348 lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1a | Claiming a 2026 Form 323 credit? |
| 1b | Contributions in excess of the Form 323 credit (Form 323, line 25)? |
| 1c | Claiming only a Form 348 carryover? (Yes → skip Part 2) |
| 2-6 | 2026 contributions, listing all claimed on Form 323 (5 from Continuation Sheet 5a to 5h); 6 total |
| 7-11 | January 1 to April 15, 2027 contributions (10 from 10a to 10h); 11 total |
| 12 | Line 6 + line 11 |
| 13 | Form 323 maximum: $787 single or head of household; $1,570 married (2026) |
| 14 | Potential credit = line 12 - line 13 |
| 15 | Form 348 maximum: $784 single or head of household; $1,561 married (2026) |
| 16 | Smaller of 14 or 15; one-half if married filing separate |
| 17-22 | Carryover from 2021 to 2025 |
| 23-25 | → Form 301, line 19, columns (a), (b), (c) |
## Example
Single, gave $1,600 to certified STOs in 2026: Form 323 credit $787; line 14 = $1,600 - $787 = $813; Form 348 cap $784 → line 16 = $784. The $29 above both caps is not a credit.
## Notes
- Gifts made January 1 to April 15, 2027 can go on the 2026 return at the 2026 cap, or on the 2027 return at the higher 2027 cap ($794 single or head of household; $1,580 married filing joint)
- ADOR lists the 2026 switcher maximum for married filing separate as $784, while the form computes one-half of the joint figure; confirm on the 2026 Form 348
- Form 301 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 301; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 301 when this was revised, so confirm line numbers against it
## Required Information
- Receipts from certified STOs for every 2026 contribution (and any January 1 to April 15, 2027 contribution claimed on 2026)
- Form 323, Part 4, line 25 (excess contributions)
- Filing status
- Prior Form 348 carryovers from 2021 to 2025
## Questions
- How much did you give to STOs in total, and did you claim the full Form 323 credit?
- Do you have Form 348 carryovers?
## Common Errors
- Claiming Form 348 before using the full Form 323 cap
- Listing only the excess contributions instead of all contributions in Part 2

- What is the Arizona STO switcher credit?
