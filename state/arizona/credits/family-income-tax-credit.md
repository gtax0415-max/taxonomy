# Family Income Tax Credit (Form 140, Line 50)
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for low-income families; it can only reduce tax and cannot be refunded. The income tables and the $40 per person, $240 / $120 maximums are set by statute and are not indexed for inflation, so they are unchanged for 2026.
## Step 1 - Worksheet I
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Form 140, line 42 |
| 2-5 | Form 140, lines 38, 39, 40 and 41 |
| 6 | Lines 2 through 5 |
| 7 | Form 140, line 13 if you elected Form 140-SBI |
| 8 | Lines 1 + 6 + 7 |
## Steps 2 and 3 - maximum income
Count the dependents in boxes 10a and 10b plus qualifying parents or grandparents in box 11a who are federal dependents. You qualify if Worksheet I, line 8 is equal to or less than the maximum.
| Table I, married filing joint | Maximum | Table II, head of household | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 or 1 | $20,000 | 0 or 1 | $20,000 |
| 2 | $23,600 | 2 | $20,135 |
| 3 | $27,300 | 3 | $23,800 |
| 4 or more | $31,000 | 4 | $25,200 |
| | | 5 or more | $26,575 |

Table III, single or married filing separate: 0 or more dependents, $10,000.
## Step 4 - Worksheet II
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Number of dependents (same count as Step 3) |
| 2 | 2 if filing status 4 (joint); 1 if status 5, 6 or 7 |
| 3 | Line 1 + line 2 |
| 4 | Line 3 x $40 |
| 5 | $240 if status 4 or 5; $120 if status 6 or 7 |
| 6 | Smaller of line 4 or line 5 → Form 140, line 50 |
## Example
Married filing joint with 2 children; line 42 $21,000, no exemptions or SBI. Worksheet I, line 8 = $21,000, which is not more than the Table I maximum of $23,600 for 2 dependents. Worksheet II: 2 + 2 = 4 x $40 = $160; maximum $240 → line 50 = $160 (limited to the tax on line 48 after line 49).
## Notes
- Applied before Form 301 credits: it reduces the Form 301, line 33 limit through line 32
- Form 140 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; confirm them against the 2026 Form 140 when ADOR releases it
## Required Information
- Form 140, lines 13 and 38 to 42
- Number of federal dependents in boxes 10a and 10b and qualifying parents in box 11a
- Filing status
## Questions
- What are your Arizona AGI and exemptions?
- How many federal dependents, including parents in box 11a, do you claim?
## Common Errors
- Comparing line 42 alone instead of Worksheet I, line 8
- Expecting a refund of the unused credit
## Prompt
- Do I qualify for the Arizona family income tax credit?
