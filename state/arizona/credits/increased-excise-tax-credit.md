# Increased Excise Tax Credit (Form 140, Line 56; Form 140ET)
## Description
A refundable credit for increased excise taxes, $25 per person up to $100 per household. The $25 per person, $100 per household and the $25,000 / $12,500 income limits are set by statute and are not indexed for inflation, so they are unchanged for 2026.
## Eligibility
- You have an SSN valid for employment (a spouse claimed for must have a valid SSN or an ITIN; qualifying children must have a valid SSN or ITIN). If your spouse has a valid SSN but you do not, neither of you can claim it.
- Form 140, line 12 is $25,000 or less (married filing joint or head of household) or $12,500 or less (single or married filing separate).
- You are not claimed as a dependent by any other taxpayer.
- You were not sentenced for at least 60 days of 2026 to a county, state or federal prison. On a joint return, if your spouse was so sentenced, you may claim the credit for yourself but not your spouse (Pub. 709).
## Worksheet (Form 140 instructions)
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Dependents in boxes 10a and 10b plus qualifying parents or grandparents in box 11a who are federal dependents, excluding any dependent who is not an Arizona resident |
| 2 | 2 if filing status 4; 1 if status 5, 6 or 7 |
| 3 | Line 1 + line 2 |
| 4 | Line 3 x $25 |
| 5 | Maximum credit $100 |
| 6 | Smaller of line 4 or line 5 → Form 140, line 56 |

The credit cannot exceed $100 per household; if another member of the household claimed less than $100, you may claim the rest. Do not use this worksheet if you claim the property tax credit; Form 140PTC, Part 2 figures the excise credit, entered from Form 140PTC, line 17 on Form 140, line 56.
## Form 140ET (stand-alone claim)
For a person who qualifies but is not required to file an income tax return and does not qualify for the property tax credit. Lines: 1 to 3 names and address; 4 to 7 filing status; 8 certification of "Who Can File"; 9A1 to 9A3 dependents (up to 2 on a joint claim, 3 otherwise); 10 number of dependents; 11 2 or 1; 12 line 10 + line 11; 13 line 12 x $25; 14 smaller of line 13 or $100; box 14A foreign account; routing and account (98); lines 15 to 17 for an amended claim (box 95). Box 82F applies when filing under extension. A married person whose spouse was sentenced for at least 60 days must file a married filing separate claim. The 2025 Form 140ET instructions printed filing thresholds of $15,000, $30,000, $15,000 and $22,500, which differ from the Form 140 thresholds.
## Example
Head of household, federal AGI $19,000, two Arizona-resident children: (2 + 1) x $25 = $75 → line 56 = $75.
## Notes
- Form 140 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; confirm them against the 2026 Form 140 when ADOR releases it
## Required Information
- Federal AGI (Form 140, line 12) and filing status
- SSNs or ITINs for you, your spouse and each dependent
- Which dependents were Arizona residents
- Whether anyone was sentenced to 60 days or more in 2026
## Questions
- What is your federal AGI and filing status?
- Is anyone claiming you as a dependent? Were you incarcerated 60 days or more in 2026?
- Has anyone else in your household claimed this credit?
## Common Errors
- Counting non-resident dependents
- Claiming both on Form 140 and Form 140PTC
- Exceeding $100 per household
