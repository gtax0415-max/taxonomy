# Property Tax Credit (Form 140, Line 57; Form 140PTC)
## Description
A refundable credit for Arizona property taxes paid, directly or through rent, by low-income seniors and SSI recipients. Complete Form 140PTC and enter Form 140PTC, line 15 on Form 140, line 57; include Form 140PTC with the return. The income limits and credit schedules are set by statute and are not indexed for inflation, so they are unchanged for 2026.
## Eligibility (all required)
1. Arizona resident for the whole year.
2. You (or your spouse, if joint) were 65 or older by December 31, 2026, or, if under 65, received Title 16 Supplemental Security Income (not regular Social Security disability). Date of birth is required in box 79 (born January 1, 1962 counts as 65 at the end of 2026).
3. Total household income under $3,751 if you lived alone, or under $5,501 if others lived with you.
4. You paid property taxes or rent on your main Arizona home during 2026 (rent for the whole year), or were a nursing home resident (taxed home, paid with your own funds), a cooperative shareholder or a condominium member.
5. You are not filing Form 140EZ.
## Due date
Form 140PTC is due April 15, 2027 (or the extended due date). The credit is disallowed if not filed by then, and cannot be claimed on an amended return filed after the due date.

## Part 1 household income (columns: you, spouse, other persons, total)
A wages, tips; B dividends and interest (including U.S. and Arizona municipal interest); C business and farm income; D net gain or loss from sales (loss limited to $1,500 per person); E pensions and annuities (excluding Social Security, railroad retirement and veteran's disability pensions; including IRA distributions); F rent and royalty; G S corporation, partnership, estate and trust income; H alimony; I other income with a schedule; J total → line 10. Excluded: Social Security, railroad retirement, workers' compensation loss of time payments, Arizona unemployment, veteran's disability pensions, welfare and other governmental relief, non-governmental gifts, and prior-year Form 140PTC refunds.
## Schedules (statutory; same for 2026)
Schedule 1 (alone): $502 at $0 to 1,750, then one step down per income band ($1,751 to 1,850 → $479, $1,851 to 1,950 → $457, and so on) to $56 at $3,651 to 3,750, and $0 at $3,751 and up. Schedule 2 (with others): $502 at $0 to 2,500, then one step down per band ($2,501 to 2,650 → $479, $2,651 to 2,800 → $457, and so on) to $56 at $5,351 to 5,500, and $0 at $5,501 and up. Each schedule has 22 bands; use the printed table.
## Part 2 (excise credit)
Lines 1a to 1c Arizona-resident dependents; 2 count; 3 two for a joint claim, otherwise one; 4 line 2 + line 3; 5 line 4 x $25; 6 smaller of line 5 or $100 → line 17.
## Example
A 72-year-old renter living alone has household income of $3,000 (Schedule 1 band $2,951 to 3,050 → $212) and Form 201 shows $400 of property taxes on her rent. Line 15 = smaller of $212 or $400 = $212.
## Notes
- The 2026 Form 140PTC is due April 15, 2027 and cannot be filed late or on a late amended return
- Form 140 line numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; confirm them against the 2026 Form 140 when ADOR releases it
## Required Information
- Property tax statement or receipt for taxes actually paid in 2026, or Form 201 from the landlord or nursing home
- Income of every household member
- Date of birth, or the SSA statement showing Title 16 SSI
- Number of Arizona-resident dependents (for Part 2)
## Questions
- Are you 65 or older, or receiving SSI? Did you live in Arizona all year?
- What was everyone's income in the household?
- Did you own or rent, and how much property tax was paid?
## Common Errors
- Leaving out Form 201 or the property tax statement
- Counting Social Security as household income
- Filing more than one claim per household
