# Other Refundable Credits (Form 140, Line 58; Boxes 581 to 583)
## Description
Enter the total refundable credits from the credit forms below, checking the box for each one, and include the forms. Form 301 is not required for these refundable amounts.
| Box | Credit | Amount from |
|---|---|---|
| 581 | Credit for Increased Research Activities - Individuals (refundable portion) | Form 308-I, Part 7, line 38 (2025) |
| 582 | Credit for Motion Picture Production Costs | Form 334, Part 1, line 6 |
| 583 | Credit for Qualified Facilities | Form 349, Part 8, line 20 |
## 2026
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, eliminates the refundable portion of the research credit (Form 308-I) retroactive to January 1, 2026, so box 581 has no 2026 amount; the nonrefundable research credit remains on Form 301, line 1.
## Refundable Credit Worksheet
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Form 308-I, Part 7, line 38 |
| 2 | Form 334, Part 1, line 6 |
| 3 | Form 349, Part 8, line 20 |
| 4 | Lines 1 + 2 + 3 → Form 140, line 58 |
## Example
2026: Form 334 refundable amount $12,000 and Form 349 installment $8,000: worksheet line 4 = $20,000; line 58 = $20,000 with boxes 582 and 583 checked.
## Notes
- Box numbers and line 58 follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR may drop box 581 from the 2026 form
- Qualified facilities (Form 349) are authorized through December 31, 2030
## Required Information
- Form 334 and Form 349 with their ACA certificates
- The 2026 installment amount for Form 349
## Questions
- Do you have an ACA-approved motion picture or qualified facilities credit?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a refundable research credit for 2026
- Not checking the box for each credit
