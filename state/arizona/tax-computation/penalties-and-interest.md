# Penalties and Interest
## Description
If you file or pay late, the department charges penalties and interest on the amount owed.
| Penalty | Rate | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Late filing | 4.5% (.045) of the tax required to be shown for each month or fraction of a month late | 25% of the tax found remaining due |
| Late payment | 0.5% (.005) of the tax shown for each month or fraction of a month, from the original due date until paid | 10% of the unpaid tax |
| Extension underpayment | 0.5% (.005) of the tax not paid for each 30-day period or fraction, from the original due date, when less than 90% was paid by the original due date | 25% of the unpaid tax; when charged, no late payment penalty under A.R.S. 42-1125(D) |
| Estimated payment underpayment | interest-equivalent, figured on Form 221 (line 76) | 10% of each underpayment |

If two or more of the late filing, late payment and extension underpayment penalties apply, the total cannot exceed 25%. Interest is charged on tax not paid by the due date, from the original due date even with an extension, at the federal short-term rate plus 3 points: 7% for January to March 2026, 6% for April to June 2026 and 7% from July 1, 2026 (ADOR). Rates for 2027, when most 2026 balances accrue interest, are set quarterly. The department may charge $50 for a check returned unpaid, and a $50 NSF fee for an electronic payment with an incorrect routing or account number.
## Example
Tax due $2,000, return filed and paid 2 months and 10 days late with no extension: late filing 3 months x 4.5% = 13.5% ($270); late payment 3 months x 0.5% = 1.5% ($30); total $300 (15%, under 25%), plus interest.
## Notes
- Penalty rates are statutory (A.R.S. 42-1125) and unchanged for 2026; the interest rate follows the federal underpayment rate and changes quarterly
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
