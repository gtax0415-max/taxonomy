# Tax from Recapture of Credits (Form 140, Line 47; Form 301, Lines 27 to 30)
## Description
When a credit must be recaptured, the recapture tax is figured on the credit form, totaled on Form 301, Part 2, and added to the tax on Form 140, line 47.
| Form 301 line | Source |
|---|---|
| 27 | Recapture of the Credit for Motion Picture Production Costs, Form 334, line 15 |
| 28 | Recapture of the Credit for Qualified Facilities, Form 349, line 19 |
| 29 | Recapture of the Credit for Affordable Housing, Form 354, line 12 |
| 30 | Lines 27 + 28 + 29 → Form 140, line 47 (Form 140PY and 140NR line 57; Form 140X line 38) |
## Line reference
Form 140, line 47 and Form 301, line 30 agree that the recapture total is on Form 301, Part 2, line 30. The 2025 Form 140 instructions for line 47 say "Form 301, Part 2, line 31", which on the 2025 Form 301 is the subtotal of tax plus recapture; use line 30.
## Example
A partner's share of qualified facilities credit recapture is $2,000 (Form 349, line 19): Form 301, line 28 = $2,000; line 30 = $2,000; Form 140, line 47 = $2,000.
## Notes
- Qualified facilities (Form 349) and motion picture (Form 334) credits remain in force for 2026, so recapture can still arise
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 334, line 15; Form 349, line 19; Form 354, line 12 (recapture computations)
## Questions
- Did you claim the motion picture, qualified facilities or affordable housing credit in a prior year, and has a recapture event occurred?
## Common Errors
- Entering Form 301, line 31 (subtotal) on line 47
## Prompt
- What is the recapture line on the Arizona return?
