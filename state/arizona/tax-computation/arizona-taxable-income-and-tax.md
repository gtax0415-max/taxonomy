# Arizona Taxable Income and Tax (Form 140, Lines 45 and 46)
## Description
Line 45: subtract lines 43 and 44 from line 42; if less than zero, enter 0. Line 46: multiply line 45 by 2.5% (.025). The 2.5% rate applies to all income levels and filing statuses (flat rate since 2023 under A.R.S. 43-1011; the 2026 Form 140ES uses 2.5% for 2026, and Laws 2026, Chapter 140 did not change it). Line 46 is also entered on Form 301, line 26 and Form 221, line 2.
## Example
Married filing joint, line 42 $90,000, standard deduction $32,200, no charitable increase: line 45 = $57,800; line 46 = $57,800 x 2.5% = $1,445.
## Notes
- The example uses the expected 2026 joint standard deduction of $32,200
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
