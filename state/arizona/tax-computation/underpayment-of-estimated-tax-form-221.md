# Underpayment of Estimated Tax (Form 140, Lines 76 and 77; Form 221)
## Description
An individual who fails to make required estimated payments is subject to a penalty on any installment that is late or underpaid. Complete and include Form 221 to determine the penalty and enter the total from Form 221, box 30c on Form 140, line 76. The penalty equals the interest that would accrue on the underpayment, cannot exceed 10% of the underpayment, and compounds annually on January 1.
## Who must make estimated payments for 2026
Filing status single, married filing separate or head of household: Arizona gross income (federal AGI for a full-year resident) over $75,000 for 2025 and over $75,000 for 2026; married filing joint: over $150,000 for both years. Required payments plus withholding must total the smaller of 90% of the 2026 tax or 100% of the 2025 tax (the latter only if a 2025 return was filed).
## Form 140, line 77 boxes
- 771 Annualized/Other: you checked the Form 221 line 1 box; completed the annualized income worksheet; or elected nonresident alien treatment federally and made three installments with the first equal to 50%.
- 772 Farmer or Fisherman: you report as a farmer or fisherman federally (calendar year: file by March 1, 2027 and pay in full; fiscal year: file by the first day of the third month after year end and pay in full); do not complete Form 221.
- 773: you completed and are including Form 221.

## Form 221, Part B
Lines 16 to 27 apply the rate for each rate period to each column's underpayment for the number of days unpaid (lines 18, 21, 24, 27 = underpayment x days / 365 x rate; amounts are not rounded). Line 28 totals each column; line 29 limits each column to the smaller of line 15 x 10% or line 28; box 30a totals line 29; box 30b is any waiver requested; box 30c = 30a - 30b, rounded to whole dollars → Form 140, line 76 (Form 140PY line 85; Form 140NR line 83). 2026 rates (ADOR, federal short-term rate plus 3 points): 7% for January 1 to March 31, 2026; 6% for April 1 to June 30, 2026; 7% from July 1, 2026. The rate for January 1 to April 15, 2027 is not yet published. The 2026 Form 221 sets the rate periods and day counts; use those (the 2025 form's 7% rate and 76 / 92 / 92 / 105 day periods do not carry over).
## Annualized Income Installment Worksheet (lines 1 to 23)
Periods ending March 31, May 31, August 31 and December 31: Arizona AGI without exemptions (1) x annualization factors 4.0, 2.4, 1.5, 1.0 (2, 3); itemized deductions annualized (4 to 6) or the full standard deduction (7); larger of the two (8); line 3 - line 8 (9); exemptions (10); line 9 - line 10 (11); x 2.5% (12); credits including the Dependent Tax Credit (13); line 12 - line 13 (14); applicable percentages 22.5%, 45%, 67.5%, 90% (15, 16); prior installments (17); line 16 - line 17 (18); one-fourth of Form 221 line 9 (19); carryover of the regular installment (20, 21, 22); smaller of line 18 or line 21 (23) → Form 221, line 11. If used for any due date, use it for all.
## Example
2026 tax after credits $8,000, withholding $3,000, 2025 tax after credits $6,000. Line 6 = $5,000 (over $1,000). Line 7 = $7,200; line 9 = $6,000 (smaller). Line 11 = $1,500 per quarter. With $750 of withholding counted each quarter and no estimates, each quarter is $750 underpaid, and Part B computes interest on each $750 until paid, limited to $75 per column (10%).
## Notes
- The $75,000 / $150,000 thresholds and the 10% cap are statutory and unchanged for 2026
- Part B uses Arizona's 2026 rates (7%, 6%, 7% by quarter so far); take the exact periods and day counts from the 2026 Form 221
- The 2026 Form 140ES worksheet predates Laws 2026, Chapter 140, so 2026 tax figured early in the year may differ from the final return
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- 2026 and 2025 Arizona returns (tax after credits)
- Arizona withholding
- Dates and amounts of 2026 estimated payments
- Quarterly income records if using the annualized method
- Documentation for any waiver
## Questions
- What were your 2025 and 2026 Arizona gross income and tax?
- When and how much did you pay in estimates? Was income uneven during the year?
- Are you a farmer or fisherman, or did you retire after 62 or become disabled?
## Common Errors
- Filing Form 221 when line 6 is under $1,000
- Counting estimated payments on line 5
- Rounding the Part B line amounts
