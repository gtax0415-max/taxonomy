# 2026 Arizona Income Tax Withheld (Form 140, Line 53)
## Description
Enter the Arizona income tax withheld shown on Forms W-2 and on Forms 1099-R (pensions, annuities and similar). Do not include tax withheld for another state. Include Forms W-2, W-2G and the 1099 forms after the last page of the return only if they show Arizona withholding. Forms W-2 should arrive by January 31. If line 53 is more than line 52, do not complete Form 221.
## Example
W-2 Arizona withholding $1,840 and 1099-R Arizona withholding $300: line 53 = $2,140.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Every Form W-2 and Form 1099 showing Arizona withholding
- Any corrected W-2c or 1099
## Questions
- What Arizona withholding is on each W-2 and 1099?
## Common Errors
- Including another state's withholding
- Including estimated payments on line 53
