# 2026 Arizona Extension Payment (Form 140, Line 55; Form 204)
## Description
Enter the payment sent with the 2026 extension request (Arizona Form 204) or the electronic extension payment made on AZTaxes.gov. Form 204 lines: 1 tax liability (may be estimated); 2 Arizona withholding; 3 estimated payments; 4 credits (see Form 301); 5 lines 2 to 4; 6 balance (line 1 - line 5); 7 payment enclosed; box 95 filing status (95a joint, 95b separate, 95c head of household, 95d single). Pay at least 90% of the tax by April 15, 2027 to avoid the extension underpayment penalty.
## Example
Form 204: liability $4,000, withholding $2,500, estimates $800, credits $0 → balance $700; payment of $700 enclosed → Form 140, line 55 = $700.
## Notes
- The extension moves the 2026 filing deadline to October 15, 2027, but not the payment deadline
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 204 or the AZTaxes.gov confirmation
- Amount and date of the extension payment
