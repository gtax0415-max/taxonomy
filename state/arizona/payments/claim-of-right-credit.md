# Arizona Claim of Right (Form 140, Box 54b and Line 54c)
## Description
If you recomputed a prior year's tax under Arizona's claim of right provisions (repayment of income taxed in an earlier year, ITP 16-1), complete the Arizona Claim of Right-Individual form, include it with the return, and enter the amount from its line 8 in box 54b. With more than one form, add all line 8 amounts. Line 54c = box 54a + box 54b. The repayment deduction itself is added back on page 5, E.
## Example
Claim of Right-Individual, line 8 = $190; estimated payments $1,000: box 54a $1,000, box 54b $190, line 54c $1,190.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Arizona Claim of Right-Individual form(s), line 8
- Proof of the repayment and the year the income was originally taxed
## Questions
- Did you repay more than $3,000 of income taxed in a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Entering the claim of right amount without the form
## Prompt
- Where does the Arizona claim of right credit go?
