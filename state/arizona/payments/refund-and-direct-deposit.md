# Refund and Direct Deposit (Form 140, Line 79, Box 79A)
## Description
Line 79 = line 63 - line 78; if less than zero, enter the amount owed on line 80. A refund may be applied to debts owed to an Arizona state agency, court, county, city or town, or certain federal agencies; the department sends a letter. Check status at AZTaxes.gov ("Where's my refund?").
## Direct deposit
- Routing number: nine digits, first two 01 to 12 or 21 to 32 (sample 250250025); do not use a "payable through" number on the check.
- Account number: up to 17 characters, no hyphens, spaces or symbols, entered left to right (sample 20202086); not the check number.
- Mark checking (C) or savings (S) in box 98.
- Box 79A: check if the deposit will ultimately be placed in a foreign account; do not enter numbers; a check is mailed.
- The department is not responsible for a lost refund from wrong account information.
## Example
Line 63 $314, gifts $20 on line 65, no penalty: line 78 $20, line 79 $294 by direct deposit.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Bank routing number (nine digits) and account number
- Account type (checking or savings)
- Whether the deposit will end up in a foreign account
## Questions
- Do you want direct deposit? To checking or savings? Is the account foreign?
## Common Errors
- Entering a routing number that is not nine digits
- Entering bank numbers when checking box 79A
## Prompt
- How do I get my Arizona refund by direct deposit?
