# Overpayment Applied to 2027 Estimated Tax (Form 140, Line 62)
## Description
Enter the part of the line 61 overpayment you want applied to 2027 estimated tax. Once applied, it cannot be used to pay tax later found due for 2026, and it cannot be refunded until the 2027 return is filed. On the 2027 Form 221 it counts as paid with the first installment.
## Example
Overpayment $900; apply $500 to 2027: line 62 $500, line 63 $400.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 140, line 61 (overpayment)
- Amount the taxpayer wants applied to 2027 estimated tax
## Questions
- Do you want any of your refund applied to next year's estimated tax?
## Common Errors
- Applying more than line 61
## Prompt
- Can I apply my Arizona refund to next year?
