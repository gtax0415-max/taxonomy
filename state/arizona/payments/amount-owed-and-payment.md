# Amount Owed and Payment (Form 140, Lines 78 and 80)
## Description
Line 78 = lines 64 through 74 (voluntary gifts) + line 76 (estimated payment penalty). Line 80 = line 60 + line 78. If voluntary gifts exceed the overpayment, enter the difference on line 80.
## Paying
- Check or money order, payable to Arizona Department of Revenue, with SSN, "Form 140" and the tax year written on it; U.S. dollars only; include it with the return; do not send cash. A returned check may cost $50. Processing can take 2 to 3 weeks.
- Electronic payment from checking or savings at AZTaxes.gov ("Make a Payment", E-Check); no fee; keep the confirmation number; a $50 NSF fee applies for wrong routing or account numbers; not allowed if the money comes from a foreign account.
- Credit card through the third-party provider at AZTaxes.gov, with a convenience fee.
- Direct debit through your software vendor.
- Installments: request a payment plan at AZTaxes.gov; interest and the late payment penalty apply to tax not paid by April 15, 2027.
## Example
Line 60 $350, gifts $25 on line 64, no penalty: line 78 $25, line 80 $375.
## Notes
- Tax for 2026 is due April 15, 2027; an extension (Form 204) extends the time to file, not to pay
- The PO Box and payment methods follow the 2025 instructions; confirm them in the 2026 Form 140 instructions
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 140, lines 60, 64 to 74 and 76
- Payment method, and bank routing and account numbers for an e-check or direct debit
- Whether a payment plan is needed
## Questions
- How will you pay any balance due? Do you need a payment plan?
## Common Errors
- Leaving gifts or the penalty out of line 80
- Sending the Arizona payment to the IRS
## Prompt
- How do I pay what I owe Arizona?
