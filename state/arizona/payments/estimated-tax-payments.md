# 2026 Estimated Tax Payments (Form 140, Line 54a; Form 140ES)
## Description
Box 54a: the total of 2026 Arizona estimated payments plus any 2025 refund applied to 2026 estimated tax. Claiming estimated payments requires Form 140 (not 140A or 140EZ).
## Who must pay for 2026 (2026 Form 140ES)
| Filing status | Arizona gross income for 2025 more than | and for 2026 more than |
|---|---|---|
| Single | $75,000 | $75,000 |
| Married filing joint | $150,000 | $150,000 |
| Married filing separate | $75,000 | $75,000 |
| Head of household | $75,000 | $75,000 |

For a full-year resident, Arizona gross income is federal AGI. Payments plus withholding must total 90% of the 2026 tax or 100% of the 2025 tax (the latter only if you were required to file and filed a 2025 return). Anyone may make voluntary payments.
## Due dates (calendar year)
April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026 and January 15, 2027 (next business day if a weekend or holiday). Fiscal year: the 15th day of the fourth, sixth and ninth months of the fiscal year and the first month of the next. Exceptions: file and pay in full by February 1, 2027 and skip the fourth payment; farmers and fishermen make one installment by January 15, 2027 or none if filing and paying by March 1, 2027; electing nonresident aliens pay three installments (June 15, September 15, 2026, January 15, 2027) with the first equal to 50%.
## 2026 Form 140ES worksheet (summary)
Federal estimated income (1); additions (2 to 6); subtractions including SBI income (7 to 14); itemized or standard deduction with the charitable increase (15) and other exemptions (16); deductions and exemptions (17); income (18); x 2.5% (19); credits including the Dependent Tax Credit (20); tax (21); 90% (22); prior-year tax (23); smaller (24); expected withholding (25); balance (26); one-fourth less any overpayment applied (27). The 2026 worksheet, printed before Laws 2026, Chapter 140 was signed on June 13, 2026, tells filers to use the 2025 standard deduction and exemption amounts. Chapter 140 changes 2026 deductions (the charitable increase becomes 100% of contributions up to $1,000 / $2,000, and the SALT itemized deduction is capped at $10,000) and raises the Dependent Tax Credit to $125, so recheck later installments. Voluntary payment methods: 10%, 15% or 20% of the federal 1040-ES payment; installments; or a lump sum before January 15, 2027.
## Form 140ES voucher
Line 1 payment amount (whole dollars); line 2 quarter box (one per voucher); box 95 filing status; box 94 phone; composite 140NR box. Mail to Arizona Department of Revenue, PO Box 29085, Phoenix, AZ 85038-9085.
## Example
Single, 2025 federal AGI $95,000, expected 2026 tax $2,400 with $1,200 withheld, 2025 tax $2,000: 90% of 2026 tax $2,160 vs 2025 tax $2,000 → required $2,000; less withholding $1,200 = $800, or $200 per quarter. Line 54a = $800 if all four are paid.
## Notes
- The $75,000 / $150,000 thresholds are statutory, not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026
- Arizona's flat rate stays 2.5% for 2026
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- 2025 and expected 2026 Arizona gross income
- 2025 Arizona tax and expected 2026 tax
- Dates and amounts of each 2026 estimated payment, and any 2025 overpayment applied to 2026
- Expected 2026 Arizona withholding
## Questions
- What was your 2025 Arizona gross income, and what do you expect for 2026?
- What estimated payments did you make for 2026, and when? Did you apply a 2025 refund?
## Common Errors
- Leaving out the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026
- Including the extension payment on line 54a (it goes on line 55)
## Prompt
- Do I need to make Arizona estimated payments in 2026?
