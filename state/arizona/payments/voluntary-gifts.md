# Voluntary Gifts (Form 140, Lines 64 to 75)
## Description
You may give some or all of your refund, or more than your entire refund, to any of these funds. Amounts beyond the refund are sent with the return and included on line 80. A gift cannot be changed later on an amended return. Gifts are added on line 78 and do not change the tax.
## Funds
| Line | Fund | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 64 | Solutions Teams Assigned to Schools Fund | Gifts go to the Arizona Assistance for Education Fund; the Arizona Board of Education distributes money to the Arizona Department of Education to fund solutions teams assigned to schools |
| 65 | Arizona Wildlife Fund | Protects wildlife (bald eagles, Apache trout, black-footed ferrets and other species) and improves wildlife watching areas statewide |
| 66 | Child Abuse Prevention Fund | Financial aid to community agencies for child abuse prevention programs |
| 67 | Domestic Violence Services Fund | Financial aid to shelters for victims of domestic violence |
| 68 | Political Gift | Goes to the political party selected on line 75 |
| 69 | Neighbors Helping Neighbors Fund | Emergency aid to eligible recipients for utility bills, energy conservation and weatherization |
| 70 | Special Olympics Fund | Programs of the Arizona Special Olympics |
| 71 | Veterans' Donations Fund | May be used for veterans in Arizona |
| 72 | I Didn't Pay Enough Fund | Goes to the Arizona general fund |
| 73 | Sustainable State Parks and Roads Fund | Operating, maintaining and improving buildings, roads, parking lots, highway entrances and related structures used to operate state parks |
| 74 | Spaying and Neutering of Animals Fund | Reduces pet overpopulation by sterilizing dogs and cats in Arizona at minimal or no cost, including impounded animals |
## Line 75 - Political party
If an amount is entered on line 68, check one box: 751 Democratic, 752 Libertarian or 753 Republican. Select only one party; if none is selected, the department returns the line 68 amount.
## Line 78
Lines 64 through 74 plus the line 76 estimated payment penalty. Line 79 = line 63 - line 78; if negative, the difference is owed on line 80.
## Example
Overpayment (line 63) $300; gifts $20 to the Arizona Wildlife Fund (65), $10 to Special Olympics (70) and $15 political gift (68) to the Libertarian party (box 752); no penalty. Line 78 = $45; line 79 = $255.
## Notes
- The list of funds follows the 2025 Form 140; the Legislature can add or remove checkoff funds, so confirm the list and line numbers on the 2026 form
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- The amount for each fund
- The party chosen for a line 68 political gift
## Questions
- Do you want to give part of your refund to any of the eleven funds? How much to each?
- If giving a political gift, to which party?
## Common Errors
- Entering a political gift without checking one party on line 75
- Checking more than one party
- Expecting to change a gift on an amended return
## Prompt
- Can I donate to Arizona wildlife on my tax return?
- What happens if I don't pick a party for my political gift?
