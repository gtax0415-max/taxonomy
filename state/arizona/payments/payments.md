# Payments, Refund and Amount Owed (Form 140, Lines 53 to 63 and 78 to 80)
## Description
Withholding, estimated and extension payments and refundable credits are totaled on line 59 and compared with the balance of tax (line 52). An overpayment may be applied to 2027 estimated tax; voluntary gifts and any estimated payment penalty are then subtracted to give the refund or the amount owed.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 53  2026 Arizona income tax withheld (W-2, 1099-R)
Line 54a 2026 Arizona estimated payments (including 2025 overpayment applied)
Line 54b Arizona Claim of Right credit (Claim of Right-Individual form, line 8)
Line 54c 54a + 54b
Line 55  2026 extension payment (Form 204 or AZTaxes.gov)
Line 56  Increased Excise Tax Credit            (refundable)
Line 57  Property Tax Credit (Form 140PTC)      (refundable)
Line 58  Other refundable credits (582, 583)    (refundable)
Line 59  Total payments and refundable credits = lines 53 through 58
Line 60  Tax due: line 52 - line 59 if line 52 is larger (skip 61 to 63)
Line 61  Overpayment: line 59 - line 52 if line 59 is larger
Line 62  Amount of line 61 applied to 2027 estimated tax
Line 63  Balance of overpayment = line 61 - line 62
Lines 64-74  Voluntary gifts; line 75 political party
Line 76  Estimated payment penalty (Form 221); line 77 boxes 771 to 773
Line 78  Lines 64 through 74 + line 76
Line 79  Refund = line 63 - line 78 (if less than zero, enter the amount owed on line 80); 79A foreign account; routing, account, checking or savings (98)
Line 80  Amount owed = line 60 + line 78 (or gifts in excess of the overpayment)
```

## What changed for 2026
- Box 581 (refundable research credit) on line 58 has no 2026 amount: Laws 2026, Chapter 140 repealed the refundable portion retroactive to January 1, 2026
- The Dependent Tax Credit rises to $125 (line 49), which lowers line 52 and can raise a refund
- Payment, extension and estimated-tax rules are unchanged; the 2026 return and payment are due April 15, 2027
