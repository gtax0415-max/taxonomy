# Interest Paid on Mortgage Credit Certificates (Schedule A, Line 5)
## Description
If you claimed the federal credit for interest paid on mortgage credit certificates (Form 8396), the federal mortgage interest deduction was reduced by the credit. Arizona lets you deduct the mortgage interest paid in 2026 equal to the amount of that federal credit. Skip line 5 if you did not claim the federal credit.
## Example
Form 8396 credit $1,500: Schedule A, line 5 = $1,500.
## Notes
- Arizona Schedule A, Form 140 and page 3 line numbers follow the 2025 forms; ADOR had not released the 2026 forms when this was revised, so confirm them against them
## Required Information
- Federal Form 8396
- Form 1098
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal mortgage interest credit on Form 8396?
## Common Errors
- Missing the add-back after a federal MCC credit
## Prompt
- I have a mortgage credit certificate. Does Arizona let me deduct that interest?
