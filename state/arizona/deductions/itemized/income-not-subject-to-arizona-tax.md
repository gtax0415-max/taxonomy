# Deductions Related to Income Not Subject to Arizona Tax (Schedule A, Line 8)
## Description
You may not deduct any expense that relates to income not subject to Arizona tax. Enter the federal Schedule A deductions allocable to such income, including:
1. Interest or other expenses to purchase or carry U.S. obligations when the income is not subject to Arizona tax
2. State taxes paid to other states for prior years in which you were not an Arizona resident
3. Employee business expenses related to income not subject to Arizona tax

The list is not complete. (The 2025 instructions for this line say "complete line 7"; the form line is 8.)
## Example
Investment interest of $1,200 on a loan used to buy Treasury bonds whose interest is subtracted on line 28: Schedule A, line 8 = $1,200.
## Notes
- Arizona Schedule A, Form 140 and page 3 line numbers follow the 2025 forms; ADOR had not released the 2026 forms when this was revised, so confirm them against them
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A detail
- Records allocating expenses to income Arizona does not tax
## Questions
- Did you deduct expenses tied to U.S. bond interest, military pay or other income Arizona does not tax?
- Did you deduct another state's tax for a year before you lived in Arizona?
## Common Errors
- Leaving deductions for nontaxable income in Arizona itemized deductions
## Prompt
- Which federal itemized deductions does Arizona disallow?
