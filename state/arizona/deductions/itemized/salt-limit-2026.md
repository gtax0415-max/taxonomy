# Limit on State and Local Taxes (2026, $10,000)
## Description
For tax year 2026, HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, limits the Arizona itemized deduction for state and local taxes to $10,000. A.R.S. 43-1042: for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025, the taxpayer may deduct up to $10,000 of the federal itemized deduction for state and local taxes allowed under IRC 164(b)(7), in lieu of the federal amount. The 2025 Arizona Schedule A has no line for a state and local tax limit of its own (it carries the federal Schedule A amounts, with line 7 adjusting only for amounts used for Arizona credits), so the 2026 Schedule A is expected to add one. The statute does not state a separate amount for married filing separate returns; watch the 2026 instructions.
## Example
Federal Schedule A state and local taxes: Arizona income tax $7,000 and real estate taxes $6,500, total $13,500. For Arizona the deduction is limited to $10,000, reducing Arizona itemized deductions by $3,500.
## Notes
- The federal limit for 2026 is $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separate), phased down above $505,000 of modified AGI to no less than $10,000, so Arizona itemizers with large property or income taxes lose part of the federal amount; compare with the standard deduction and the new charitable increase
- Arizona Schedule A, Form 140 and page 3 line numbers follow the 2025 forms; ADOR had not released the 2026 forms when this was revised, so confirm them against them
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A state and local taxes (income or sales, real estate, personal property)
- Filing status
## Questions
- What state and local income (or sales), real estate and personal property taxes are on your federal Schedule A?
## Common Errors
- Carrying the higher federal limit into Arizona itemized deductions
## Prompt
- Is there a SALT cap on the Arizona return?
