# Itemized Deductions (Form 140, Line 43, Box 43I; Form 140 Schedule A)
## Description
You may itemize on the Arizona return even if you take the standard deduction federally. Start from a completed federal Form 1040 Schedule A (complete one even if you did not itemize federally). If none of the Arizona adjustments applies, enter the federal Schedule A total on line 43; otherwise complete Arizona Form 140 Schedule A, Itemized Deduction Adjustments, and enter its line 15. Check box 43I and include a copy of federal Schedule A.
## When Arizona Schedule A is required
1. You are deducting medical and dental expenses.
2. You claimed the federal mortgage credit certificate credit (Form 8396).
3. Your federal Schedule A includes expenses related to income not subject to Arizona tax.
4. You are claiming an Arizona credit for any amount allowed as a charitable contribution or a state income tax deduction.
## How the lines flow
```
Lines 1-4   Medical and dental: total expenses vs federal allowed; line 3 = increase, line 4 = decrease
Line 5      Mortgage interest equal to the federal Form 8396 credit (increase)
Line 6      Charitable contributions used for Arizona credits (decrease)
Line 7      State income taxes used for Arizona credits (decrease; page 2 worksheet 1A-7A)
Line 8      Deductions related to income not subject to Arizona tax (decrease)
Line 9      Lines 3 + 5 (increases)
Line 10     Lines 4 + 6 + 7 + 8 (decreases)
Line 11     Total federal itemized deductions allowed
Line 12-13  Line 11 + line 9
Line 14-15  Line 13 - line 10 = Arizona itemized deductions → Form 140, line 43 (not less than 0)
2026        State and local taxes limited to $10,000 (no 2025 line)
```
## What changed for 2026
- State and local taxes limited to $10,000 (A.R.S. 43-1042, added by Laws 2026, Chapter 140)
- Medical expenses still deductible in full
- The charitable increase for standard deduction filers changed to 100% up to $1,000 / $2,000, which may make itemizing less attractive
## Married filing separate (community property)
Both spouses must itemize or both take the standard deduction. Expenses paid with community funds are divided equally; expenses paid with separate funds are deducted by the spouse who paid them; never more than 100% in total. If one spouse claims 100% of the itemized deductions, the other enters 0 on line 43 (ITR 93-19).
