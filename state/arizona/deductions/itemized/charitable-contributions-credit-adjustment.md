# Charitable Contributions Used for Arizona Credits (Schedule A, Line 6)
## Description
You cannot claim both a deduction and a credit for the same charitable contribution. Enter the amount of charitable contributions deducted on federal Schedule A for which you are claiming an Arizona credit. Skip line 6 if none.
## Credits that require the adjustment
| Form | Credit |
|---|---|
| 321 | Contributions to Qualifying Charitable Organizations |
| 322 | Contributions Made or Fees Paid to Public Schools |
| 323 | Contributions to Private School Tuition Organizations |
| 331 | Donation of School Site |
| 335-I | Contributions made by an S Corporation to a School Tuition Organization - Individual |
| 340 | Donations to the Military Family Relief Fund |
| 341-I | S Corporation contributions to an STO for Displaced Students or Students with Disabilities - Individual |
| 348 | Contributions to Certified School Tuition Organizations - Individual |
| 352 | Contributions to Qualifying Foster Care Charitable Organizations |
## Timing
A credit contribution made January 1 to April 15 can be claimed on the prior year's return. Exclude the deduction in the year the contribution is deducted if the credit was taken on the other year's return.
## Example
Federal Schedule A charitable deduction $4,000 includes $1,009 to a QCO and $400 to a public school, both claimed for credits on the 2026 return: line 6 = $1,409.
## Notes
- The Military Family Relief Fund credit (Form 340) ends after 2026 unless extended, so this is the last year Form 340 gifts need this adjustment
- A.R.S. 43-1042 bars claiming both a deduction and a credit for the same charitable contribution
- Arizona Schedule A, Form 140 and page 3 line numbers follow the 2025 forms; ADOR had not released the 2026 forms when this was revised, so confirm them against them
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A charitable deduction detail
- Receipts for gifts claimed for Arizona credits on the 2026 return (and January to April 2026 gifts claimed on 2025)
## Questions
- Which of your federally deducted contributions are you also using for Arizona credits?
## Common Errors
- Taking both the credit and the itemized deduction for the same gift

- Can I deduct my QCO donation and also take the credit?
