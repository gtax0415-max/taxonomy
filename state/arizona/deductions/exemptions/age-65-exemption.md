# Age 65 or Over Exemption (Form 140, Box 8, Line 38)
## Description
Line 38 = number in box 8 x $2,100 (statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026).
## Box 8
- Single or head of household: enter 1 if you were 65 or older in 2026 and not claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer.
- Married filing joint: enter 1 if you or your spouse was 65 or older and not claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer; enter 2 if both were 65 or older and neither is claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer.
- Married filing separate: enter 1 if you were 65 or older and not claimed by another taxpayer; you cannot take the exemption for your spouse.
## Birthday rule
A person whose 65th birthday is January 1, 2027 (born January 1, 1962) is considered 65 at the end of 2026, as for federal purposes.
## Example
Married filing joint, one spouse born March 3, 1960 and the other born January 1, 1962: box 8 = 2, line 38 = 2 x $2,100 = $4,200.
## Notes
- Separate from the federal enhanced senior deduction, which Arizona subtracts through page 6 (MCTCP); both can apply
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Dates of birth of you and your spouse
- Whether either is claimed as a dependent by another taxpayer
## Questions
- What are your and your spouse's dates of birth?
- Can anyone claim either of you as a dependent?
## Common Errors
- Checking the box instead of entering a number
- Claiming a spouse's exemption on a married filing separate return
## Prompt
- Is there an Arizona exemption for people over 65?
