# Blind Exemption (Form 140, Box 9, Line 39)
## Description
Line 39 = number in box 9 x $1,500 (statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026). If you or your spouse were partially blind as of December 31, 2026, get a statement certified by your eye doctor or registered optometrist that you cannot see better than 20/200 in your better eye with glasses or contact lenses, or that your field of vision is 20 degrees or less. If the condition is not likely to improve, a certified statement to that effect may be used instead. Keep the statement with your records.
## Box 9
- Single or head of household: enter 1 if you are totally or partially blind.
- Married filing joint: enter 1 if you or your spouse is blind; 2 if both are.
- Married filing separate: enter 1 if you are blind, or your spouse is blind and your spouse (1) is totally or partially blind, (2) has no Arizona adjusted gross income for the year, and (3) is not the dependent of another taxpayer; enter 2 if both conditions are met.
## Example
Married filing joint, one spouse legally blind: box 9 = 1, line 39 = $1,500.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Certified statement from an eye doctor or registered optometrist (kept with records)
## Questions
- Is either of you totally or partially blind, and do you have the certified statement?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the exemption without the eye doctor's certified statement on file
