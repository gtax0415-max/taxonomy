# Qualifying Parents and Grandparents (Form 140, Box 11a, Lines 11b to 11i, Line 41)
## Description
Line 41 = number in box 11a x $10,000 (statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026). Complete lines 11b and 11c on page 1 (and page 4, Part 2, lines 11d to 11i, checking the page 1 box) before totaling box 11a. You may lose the exemption if the information is not furnished.
## Who qualifies
Your parent, grandparent or great-grandparent, or on a joint return your spouse's, if all apply (A.R.S. 43-1023(C)):
1. Was 65 or older during 2026.
2. Lived in your principal residence for the entire year; if the person died during the year, lived with you for the entire part of the year he or she was alive. Temporary absences for special circumstances, such as a hospital stay or hospice care, count as time lived in your home.
3. You paid more than one-half of the person's support and maintenance costs (ITP 14-1 worksheet).
4. Required assistance with activities of daily living, meaning two or more of: dressing, eating, ambulating, toileting, medicating and hygiene, shopping, housekeeping, managing personal finances, basic communication, food preparation and transportation (ITR 14-3; ITP 14-2 checklist).
## Columns for lines 11b to 11i
| Column | Entry |
|---|---|
| (a) | First and last name |
| (b) | Social Security number |
| (c) | Relationship |
| (d) | Number of months lived in your home in 2026 |
| (e) | Check if age 65 or over |
| (f) | Check if the person died in 2026 |
## Interaction with other items
A parent or grandparent who is also your dependent may be counted in box 10b (Dependent Tax Credit) or box 11a, not both, and cannot also be an Other Exemption on line 40. Federal dependents counted in box 11a also count toward the Family Income Tax Credit and Increased Excise Tax Credit worksheets.
## Example
Your 82-year-old mother lives with you all year, you pay 70% of her support, and she needs help with bathing, medicating and transportation: box 11a = 1, line 41 = $10,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Name, SSN and date of birth of each parent or grandparent
- Months lived in your home
- ITP 14-1 support worksheet and ITP 14-2 daily living checklist
## Questions
- Did a parent or grandparent 65 or older live with you all year?
- Did you pay more than half of the support, and does the person need help with daily activities?
## Common Errors
- Counting the same person in box 10b and box 11a
- Claiming a parent who lived elsewhere part of the year
