# Other Exemptions (Form 140, Line 40, Box 40E; Page 4, Part 3)
## Description
You may take a $2,300 exemption (statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026) for each of the following individuals; complete page 4, Part 3 and enter the number of individuals in box 40E. Line 40 = box 40E x $2,300.
## Who qualifies
- A person age 65 or over (related to you or not) if either applies:
  1. In 2026 you paid more than one-fourth of the cost of keeping this person in an Arizona nursing care institution, Arizona residential care institution or Arizona assisted living facility, and your cost was more than $800 (box C1); or
  2. In 2026 you paid more than $800 for Arizona home health care or other medical costs for the person (box C2).
  If both apply to the same person, only one exemption is allowed.
- A stillborn child, if the stillbirth occurred in 2026, you received a certificate of birth resulting in stillbirth from the Arizona Department of Health Services, and the child would otherwise have been a member of your household.
## Page 4, Part 3 columns (rows 1 to 10)
| Column | Entry |
|---|---|
| (a) | First and last name; for an unnamed stillborn child, "stillborn child" |
| (b) | SSN; for a stillborn child, the certificate number |
| (c) | Age 65 or over: box C1 (care facility cost) or C2 (home health or medical costs) |
| (d) | Check for a stillborn child in 2026 |
## Restrictions
An individual taken on line 40 cannot also be claimed on line 41 or for the Dependent Tax Credit (and is not included in box 10b).
## Example
You paid $6,000 of the $20,000 annual assisted living cost for an unrelated 80-year-old friend in Phoenix: more than one-fourth ($5,000) and more than $800, so box C1 is checked, box 40E = 1 and line 40 = $2,300.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Receipts for Arizona care facility, home health or medical costs, and the total cost
- ADHS certificate of birth resulting in stillbirth, if applicable
## Questions
- Did you pay care facility, home health or medical costs in Arizona for someone 65 or older? How much of the total?
- Did you have a stillbirth in 2026 and receive the ADHS certificate?
## Common Errors
- Claiming two exemptions when both C1 and C2 apply to one person
- Claiming the same person on line 40 and line 41 or box 10b
## Prompt
- I pay for my aunt's assisted living. Is there an Arizona exemption?
