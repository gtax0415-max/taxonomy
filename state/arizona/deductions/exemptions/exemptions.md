# Exemptions (Form 140, Boxes 8, 9, 11a and Lines 38 to 41)
## Description
Arizona allows exemptions for age 65 or over, blindness, qualifying parents and grandparents, and "other exemptions" for certain people 65 or over and stillborn children. Enter the number claimed in boxes 8, 9, 11a and 40E; do not use a check mark or "X", or the exemption may be lost. If you complete lines 8, 9 and 11a, also complete lines 38, 39 and 41.
## How the lines flow
```
Box 8   Number age 65 or over (you and/or spouse)  → Line 38 = box 8 x $2,100
Box 9   Number blind (you and/or spouse)           → Line 39 = box 9 x $1,500
Box 40E Number listed on page 4, Part 3            → Line 40 = box 40E x $2,300
Box 11a Number of qualifying parents/grandparents  → Line 41 = box 11a x $10,000
Line 42 Arizona adjusted gross income = line 37 - lines 38 through 41
```
All amounts are statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026.
## One benefit per person
A person may be counted only once among box 10b (Dependent Tax Credit), box 11a (line 41) and line 40 (other exemption).

## Required Information
- Dates of birth
- Eye doctor's certified statement for blindness
- Support and care cost records
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older on December 31, 2026, or blind?
- Did a parent or grandparent 65 or older live with you all year?
- Did you pay nursing, assisted living, home health or medical costs for someone 65 or older, or have a stillborn child in 2026?
