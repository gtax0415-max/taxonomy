# Increased Standard Deduction for Charitable Contributions (Form 140, Line 44; Page 3)
## Description
Taxpayers who take the standard deduction may increase it for charitable contributions that would have been allowed as itemized deductions (gifts allowed on federal Schedule A, Gifts to Charity). Complete Form 140, page 3 and include it with the return, check box 43S on line 43 and box 44C on line 44.
## 2026 rule
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, changes the increase (A.R.S. 43-1041) from a percentage of contributions (25% indexed to the Phoenix consumer price index for 2022 to 2025, 34% on the 2025 form, no cap) to the full amount of charitable contributions, capped at $1,000 for single and married filing separate filers and $2,000 for married filing joint. The statute names no head of household cap. The 2026 page 3 is not published; the 2025 lines 1C to 6C are expected to remain, with line 7C becoming the capped amount.
## Page 3 lines (2025 layout)
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1C | 2026 gifts by cash or check |
| 2C | 2026 gifts other than by cash or check |
| 3C | Carryover from prior year |
| 4C | Lines 1C through 3C |
| 5C | Contributions made in 2026 for which you claim an Arizona credit on the current (2026) or prior (2025) return |
| 6C | Line 4C - line 5C, not less than 0 |
| 7C | 2025: line 6C x 34%. 2026: the allowable amount (full amount, capped) → Form 140, line 44 |

Note 1: reduce contributions by those made January 1 through December 31 for which an Arizona credit is claimed on the current return or was claimed on the prior return (5C). Note 2: if you itemized federally and reduced federal Schedule A contributions for an Arizona credit, add that federal adjustment back on 1C and enter the Arizona credit amount on 5C.
## Examples
- Married filing joint, 2026 cash gifts $3,500, of which $1,009 went to a QCO and is claimed on Form 321: 4C = $3,500, 5C = $1,009, 6C = $2,491; line 44 = $2,000 (cap).
- Single, 2026 cash gifts $600, none used for credits: line 44 = $600 (under the $1,000 cap). Under the 2025 rule the same gifts gave $600 x 34% = $204.
## Notes
- The head of household cap is not named in the statute; check the 2026 page 3 instructions before applying a cap or no cap
- Arizona Schedule A, Form 140 and page 3 line numbers follow the 2025 forms; ADOR had not released the 2026 forms when this was revised, so confirm them against them
## Required Information
- Charitable contribution receipts (cash and noncash)
- Prior-year carryover
- List of contributions used for Arizona credits
## Questions
- What charitable gifts did you make in 2026, in cash and in property? Any carryover?
- Did you use any of those gifts for an Arizona tax credit?
- Are you taking the standard deduction?
## Common Errors
- Not checking box 44C or not including page 3
- Including gifts used for QCO, QFCO, school or other Arizona credits
- Applying 34% instead of the 2026 full-amount rule, or ignoring the cap
