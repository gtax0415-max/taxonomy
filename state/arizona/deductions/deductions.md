# Exemptions and Deductions (Form 140, Lines 38 to 45)
## Description
After additions and subtractions (line 37), Arizona subtracts exemptions (lines 38 to 41) to reach Arizona adjusted gross income (line 42), then the standard or itemized deduction (line 43) and, for standard deduction filers, the charitable increase (line 44), to reach Arizona taxable income (line 45). Arizona has no personal exemption; dependents are covered by the Dependent Tax Credit (line 49), not an exemption.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 37  Income after additions and subtractions
Line 38  Age 65 or over: box 8 x $2,100
Line 39  Blind: box 9 x $1,500
Line 40  Other exemptions: box 40E x $2,300 (page 4, Part 3)
Line 41  Qualifying parents and grandparents: box 11a x $10,000
Line 42  Arizona adjusted gross income = line 37 - lines 38 through 41, not less than 0
Line 43  Deductions: box 43I itemized (Schedule A) or box 43S standard
Line 44  Charitable increase to the standard deduction (box 44C, page 3)
Line 45  Arizona taxable income = line 42 - lines 43 and 44, not less than 0
```
Exemption amounts are statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026. Standard deduction: $16,100 / $24,150 / $32,200; A.R.S. 43-1041 adjusts Arizona's standard deduction for inflation in the same manner as the federal basic standard deduction, so the 2026 amounts are expected to equal the federal $16,100 / $24,150 / $32,200; ADOR publishes the official figures with the 2026 form.
## What changed for 2026
- Standard deduction adjusted for inflation in the same manner as the federal basic standard deduction (A.R.S. 43-1041(H)).
- Charitable increase: the full amount of charitable contributions, capped at $1,000 (single, married filing separate) and $2,000 (married filing joint); the statute names no head of household cap. 2025 used 34% with no cap.
- Itemized state and local taxes are limited to $10,000.
- Married filing separate: both spouses itemize or both take the standard deduction.

## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Filing status and dates of birth
- Exemption records (parents, care costs, blindness)
- Itemized deduction records and charitable gifts
## Questions
- Is anyone on the return 65 or older, or blind?
- Do you support a parent or grandparent who lives with you, or pay care costs for someone 65 or older?
- Will you itemize, and did you make charitable gifts?
## Prompt
- What is the Arizona standard deduction for 2026?
- Does Arizona have a personal exemption?
