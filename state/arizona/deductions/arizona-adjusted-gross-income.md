# Arizona Adjusted Gross Income (Form 140, Line 42)
## Description
Subtract lines 38 through 41 (the exemptions) from line 37. If less than zero, enter 0. Line 42 is also the starting point of the Family Income Tax Credit worksheet (Worksheet I, line 1, with the exemptions added back) and of the Form 309 resident worksheet for line 9.
## Example
Line 37 $58,000; age 65 exemptions for two (line 38 = 2 x $2,100 = $4,200): line 42 = $53,800.
## Notes
- Exemption amounts are statutory under A.R.S. 43-1023 and not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 140, lines 37 to 41
## Questions
- What exemptions apply (age 65, blind, other, parents and grandparents)?
## Common Errors
- Entering a negative amount
