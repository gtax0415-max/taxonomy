# Income (Form 140, Lines 12 to 37)
## Description
Arizona starts from federal adjusted gross income (line 12), which is Arizona gross income for a full-year resident. Small business income moved to Form 140-SBI is taken out on line 13. Arizona then adds income the federal return excludes (lines 15 to 18, with the page 5 schedule) and subtracts income Arizona does not tax (lines 24 to 36, with the page 6 schedule). The result on line 37 goes to the exemptions on lines 38 to 41.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 12   Federal adjusted gross income
Line 13   Small business income moved to Form 140-SBI, line 10 (box 13S)
Line 14   Modified federal AGI = line 12 - line 13
Line 15   Non-Arizona municipal interest                       (addition)
Line 16   Partnership income adjustment (Form 165 K-1, positive)(addition)
Line 17   Total federal depreciation                           (addition)
Line 18   Other additions, page 5 total (item T)               (addition)
Line 19   Subtotal = lines 14 through 18
Lines 20-23  Net capital gain, short-term, long-term, long-term from assets acquired after 2011 (information lines)
Line 24   25% of line 23                                       (subtraction)
Line 25   Net capital gain from investment in a qualified small business
Line 26   Recalculated Arizona depreciation
Line 27   Partnership income adjustment (Form 165 K-1, negative)
Line 28   Interest on U.S. obligations
Line 29a  Federal, Arizona state or local government pensions (up to $2,500 each)
Line 29b  Retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services
Line 30   Social Security and Railroad Retirement benefits (taxable amount)
Line 31   Certain wages of American Indians
Line 32   Active service pay: reserves, National Guard, U.S. armed forces
Line 33   Net operating loss adjustment
Line 34a/34b/34c  529 college savings plan and 529A (ABLE) contributions
Line 35   Line 19 - lines 24 through 34c
Line 36   Other subtractions, page 6 total (item W)
Line 37   Line 35 - line 36 → exemptions (lines 38 to 41)
```
Dollar caps in this section (the $2,500 government pension exclusion, the $2,000 / $4,000 529 and ABLE limits, the $500 fireplace conversion) are statutory, not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026.
## Small business income election
Each addition or subtraction directly related to small business income reported on Form 140-SBI is made on Form 140-SBI, not Form 140. Items not related to small business income stay on Form 140.
## 2026 conformity notes
- IRC conformity date January 1, 2026 (HB 4168, Laws 2026, Chapter 140, signed June 13, 2026; general effective date September 12, 2026).
- Subtractions for qualified tips, qualified overtime and the federal enhanced senior deduction continue for 2026; Trump account distributions and child and dependent care expenses above the federal credit are new; the vehicle loan interest subtraction was 2025 only. On the 2025 form these deductions route through the MCTCP worksheet to page 6, line V.
- New addition for special depreciation on qualified production property (IRC 168(n)).

## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
- The two new 2026 subtractions (Trump account distributions and dependent care expenses above the federal credit) have no line yet
## Required Information
- 2026 federal return (federal AGI and Schedule 1-A)
- Forms 1099-INT, 1099-R, SSA-1099 and W-2
- 529 and ABLE contribution statements
- Form 140-SBI if electing small business income treatment
## Questions
- What is your 2026 federal adjusted gross income?
- Are you electing to report small business income on Form 140-SBI?
- Did you receive interest from other states' municipal bonds, U.S. obligations, government pensions, Social Security or military pay?
- Did you contribute to a 529 or ABLE account?
- Did you claim the federal tips, overtime or senior deduction?
