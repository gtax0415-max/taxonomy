# Small Business Income Election (Form 140, Lines 13 and 14; Form 140-SBI)
## Description
Individuals may elect to report small business income included in federal AGI on Arizona Form 140-SBI, Small Business Income Tax Return, taxed separately from Form 140. Small business income is income reported on federal Schedules B, C, D, E and F and Form 4797 (for amounts not on Schedule D). The election is made by completing Form 140-SBI and including it with Form 140 when filed. See Form 140-SBI instructions and Publication 712. This taxonomy describes the election and its effect on Form 140; the Form 140-SBI computation itself is outside Form 140.
## On Form 140
- Line 13: check box 13S and enter the small business income from Form 140-SBI, line 10.
- Line 14: modified federal AGI = line 12 - line 13.
- A "zero" SBI return filed only to claim estimated or extension payments made for the SBI return: check the box and enter 0 on line 13; file both returns together by the due date including extension.
- Lines 15 to 36: each addition or subtraction directly related to SBI goes on Form 140-SBI. Capital gains moved to the SBI return are excluded from lines 20 to 24.
- Family Income Tax Credit worksheet I, line 7 adds back line 13.
- If Form 140-SBI is not included, processing may be delayed or the line 13 subtraction denied.
## Related forms
- Form 301-SBI for credits that must be claimed on the SBI return; Form 301, line 59 carries credits not used on Form 301-SBI.
- Form 309-SBI for tax paid to another state on SBI; Form 309 covers income not on the SBI return.
- Form 131-SBI for a deceased taxpayer's SBI refund; Form 204-SBI for an SBI extension payment.
- Mail Form 140-SBI with Form 140 in the same envelope.
## Example
Federal AGI $180,000 includes $100,000 of Schedule C income reported on Form 140-SBI, line 10. Line 13 = $100,000 (box 13S checked); line 14 = $80,000.

## Required Information
- Federal Schedules B, C, D, E, F and Form 4797
- Any SBI estimated or extension payments
## Questions
- Do you have Schedule C, E, F, B, D or 4797 income you want taxed on Form 140-SBI?
- Did you make estimated or extension payments for an SBI return?
## Common Errors
- Entering line 13 without including Form 140-SBI
- Making an SBI-related addition or subtraction on Form 140
- Mailing Form 140-SBI separately

- What is the Arizona small business income election?
