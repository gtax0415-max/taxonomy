# Other Additions and Total (Page 5, S and T; Line 18)
## Description
Page 5, S covers other adjustments that are needed. For example, you must add back expenses related to income that Arizona does not tax. If you have more than one other adjustment, add them and enter the total. Page 5, T is the total of all page 5 amounts; it is entered on page 5 and on Form 140, line 18. Complete and include page 5 only if you report a line 18 adjustment.
## Example
Page 5 shows A $1,000, I $4,000 and S $250: T = $5,250, entered on line 18.
## Notes
- The new 2026 qualified production property addition (IRC 168(n)) may be reported here or on its own line; confirm on the 2026 form
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Records of expenses related to income Arizona does not tax
- Every other page 5 item, to total on T
## Questions
- Did you deduct expenses that relate to income Arizona does not tax?
## Common Errors
- Entering page 5 items on line 18 without including page 5
- Omitting an item from the T total

- What goes on the Other Adjustments line of the Arizona additions page?
