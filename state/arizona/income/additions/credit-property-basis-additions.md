# Basis Additions for Credit Property (Page 5, H(a), H(b), H(c))
## Description
When property for which an Arizona credit was claimed is sold or otherwise disposed of, the Arizona basis is lower than the federal basis. Add the amount by which the adjusted basis computed under the Internal Revenue Code exceeds the Arizona adjusted basis.
| Item | Property | Arizona basis rule |
|---|---|---|
| H(a) | property for which the Credit for Investment in Qualified Small Businesses (Form 338) was claimed | A.R.S. 43-1074.02 |
| H(b) | agricultural pollution control equipment for which the Form 325 credit was taken before the cutoff year | A.R.S. 43-1081.01 |
| H(c) | pollution control equipment for which the Form 315 credit was taken before the cutoff year | A.R.S. 43-1081 |

The 2025 form says the Form 325 and Form 315 credits must have been taken "before taxable year 2025"; the 2025 instructions say "before taxable year 2022". Confirm the cutoff on the 2026 form.
## Example
Pollution control equipment has a federal adjusted basis of $40,000 and an Arizona adjusted basis of $30,000 when sold in 2026. Page 5, H(c) = $10,000.
## Notes
- Laws 2026, Chapter 140 repealed the corporate pollution control equipment credit for 2026; that does not change these individual basis rules
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal and Arizona adjusted basis records for the credit property
- Sale or disposal records
## Questions
- Did you sell property on which you claimed an Arizona credit?
- What are the federal and Arizona adjusted bases?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal gain on the sale without the basis addition
## Prompt
- I sold equipment I took an Arizona credit on. Is there an adjustment?
