# Entity-Level Income Tax Payment (Page 5, P)
## Description
If you claim the Form 355 Credit for Entity-Level Income Tax Paid on Your Behalf, add the amount the partnership or S corporation deducted for Arizona income tax it paid on your behalf in 2026. Enter the tax actually paid in 2026, shown on Form 165 Schedule K-1, Part 7, lines 12 to 15, or Form 120S Schedule K-1, Part 5, lines 9 to 12 (2025 line references in the Form 140 instructions). Tax paid in 2027 for tax year 2026, including a fourth-quarter estimate paid in January 2027 and payments made with the entity's return, is added back on the 2027 return.
## Line references
The 2025 Form 355 takes the credit from Form 165 Schedule K-1, Part 7, line 11 and Form 120S Schedule K-1, Part 5, line 8, while the Form 140 instructions for this addition cite Part 7, lines 12 to 15 and Part 5, lines 9 to 12. Use the K-1 lines that show the tax actually paid during 2026.
## Example
An S corporation paid $1,250 of Arizona entity-level tax for a shareholder in 2026, deducted in arriving at the shareholder's federal income, and $300 more in January 2027. Page 5, P = $1,250 on the 2026 return.
## Notes
- The entity pays Arizona tax at the 2.5% individual rate for 2026
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Arizona Form 165 or 120S Schedule K-1 showing entity-level tax paid
- Dates of each entity-level tax payment
## Questions
- Did a partnership or S corporation elect to pay Arizona tax at the entity level for you?
- When was each payment made?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the Form 355 credit without the page 5, P addition
- Including tax paid in 2027

- My S corp paid Arizona PTE tax. What do I add back?
