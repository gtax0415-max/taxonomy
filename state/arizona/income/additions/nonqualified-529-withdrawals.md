# Nonqualified Withdrawals from 529 College Savings Plans (Page 5, I)
## Description
Add a nonqualified 529 withdrawal if you received it and did not include it in federal AGI. The addition is the amount withdrawn, but not more than the contributions subtracted in prior years minus amounts added back in prior years.
## Not nonqualified
- A qualified withdrawal to pay the beneficiary's qualified higher education expenses
- A withdrawal because of the beneficiary's death or disability
- A withdrawal on account of a scholarship or an allowance or payment described in IRC 135(d)(1)(B) or (C) received by the beneficiary, to the extent of that amount
- A rollover or change of designated beneficiary
## Example
A parent subtracted $2,000 a year for three years ($6,000) and took a $4,000 nonqualified withdrawal not included in federal AGI, with no earlier add-backs. Page 5, I = $4,000.
## Notes
- The 529 contribution subtraction stays at $2,000 / $4,000 per beneficiary for 2026, which limits what can later be added back
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q
- Prior Arizona returns showing 529 subtractions (line 34a) and earlier add-backs
- Records of how the withdrawal was used
## Questions
- Did you take a 529 withdrawal that was not used for education?
- How much did you subtract for 529 contributions in prior years, and how much was already added back?
## Common Errors
- Adding more than the prior subtractions
- Adding a rollover between plans
## Prompt
- I took money out of my kid's 529 for other things. What happens on my Arizona return?
