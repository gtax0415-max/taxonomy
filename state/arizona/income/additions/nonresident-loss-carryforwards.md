# Nonresident Loss Carryforwards (Page 5, K and L)
## Description
A loss incurred from non-Arizona sources while a nonresident cannot reduce Arizona taxable income on a resident return.
- K: add the federal net operating loss carryforward deduction from non-Arizona sources incurred while a nonresident that is included in federal AGI (ITR 13-5). A prior-year resident return cannot be amended to carry back such a loss.
- L: add the federal capital loss carryforward deduction from non-Arizona sources incurred before Arizona residency that is included in federal AGI (ITR 13-6). This addition is not related to small business income and is always made on Form 140.
## Example
A taxpayer moved to Arizona in 2024 with a $3,000 federal capital loss carryover from stock losses incurred while living in Ohio, deducted on the 2026 federal return. Page 5, L = $3,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal NOL and capital loss carryover worksheets
- Records of where and when each loss arose and the date Arizona residency began
## Questions
- Did you have NOL or capital loss carryovers from before you moved to Arizona?
## Common Errors
- Letting a pre-residency loss carryover reduce Arizona income
## Prompt
- Can I use my capital loss from before I moved to Arizona?
