# Amortization or Depreciation for Child Care Facility before 1990 (Page 5, N)
## Description
If you elected to amortize the cost of a child care facility under Arizona law in effect before 1990 and are still deducting amortization or depreciation for that facility on the federal return, add that amount back.
## Example
Federal depreciation of $2,500 in 2026 on a facility already amortized for Arizona under the pre-1990 election: page 5, N = $2,500.
## Notes
- Applies only to pre-1990 elections, so few 2026 returns use it
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Records of the pre-1990 Arizona amortization election
- 2026 federal depreciation schedule for the facility
## Questions
- Did you make a pre-1990 Arizona amortization election for a child care facility?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the same facility cost again federally without the add-back
## Prompt
- What is the Arizona child care facility addition?
