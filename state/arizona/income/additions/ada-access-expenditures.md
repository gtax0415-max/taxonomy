# Americans with Disabilities Act Access Expenditures (Page 6, N and Page 5, M)
## Description
Arizona allows a subtraction for the full amount of eligible business access expenditures paid or incurred during the year to comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 or A.R.S. Title 41, chapter 9, article 8, by retrofitting developed real property originally placed in service at least ten years before the current year (A.R.S. 43-1022 and 43-1024). Any of those costs included in federal AGI for the year, plus federally amortized amounts, must then be added back on page 5, M so the cost is not deducted twice.
## Eligible business access expenditures
Reasonable and necessary amounts to:
- remove barriers that prevent a business from being accessible to or usable by individuals with disabilities
- provide qualified interpreters or other ways of making audio materials available to hearing-impaired individuals
- provide qualified readers, taped texts and other ways of making visually delivered materials available to individuals with visual impairments
- acquire or modify equipment or devices for individuals with disabilities
- provide other similar services, modifications, materials or equipment

A taxpayer cited for noncompliance by federal or state enforcement officials gets no subtraction for expenditures required to cure the cited violation.
## Example
A shop owner spends $15,000 in 2026 on ramps and doors for a building placed in service in 2001 and deducts $1,000 of depreciation on them federally. Page 6, N = $15,000 and page 5, M = $1,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Invoices for the 2026 access expenditures
- Date the property was originally placed in service
- Federal depreciation or amortization of those costs
- Any noncompliance citation
## Questions
- What access improvements did you make in 2026, and when was the building first placed in service?
- Were you cited for an ADA violation?
## Common Errors
- Taking the subtraction without adding back the federal depreciation
## Prompt
- Is there an Arizona deduction for ADA improvements to my business?
