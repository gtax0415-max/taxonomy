# Depreciation Adjustments (Form 140, Lines 17 and 26; Page 6, K)
## Description
Arizona requires the federal depreciation deduction to be added back in full (line 17) and replaced with depreciation recalculated under Arizona rules (line 26). When the property is sold, a subtraction on page 6, item K recovers the basis difference.
## Line 17 - Total federal depreciation
Enter the total depreciation deducted on the federal return. If you enter an amount here, also take the line 26 subtraction.
## Line 26 - Recalculated Arizona depreciation
Add the amounts for each group and enter the total:
| Placed in service in taxable years beginning | Arizona depreciation |
|---|---|
| Before December 31, 2012 | IRC 167(a) depreciation as if you elected not to claim bonus depreciation |
| From and after December 31, 2012 through December 31, 2013 | depends on the method used for those assets |
| From and after December 31, 2013 through December 31, 2015 | IRC 167(a) depreciation as if bonus were 10% of federal IRC 168(k) bonus |
| From and after December 31, 2015 through December 31, 2016 | as if bonus were 55% of federal bonus |
| From and after December 31, 2016 | as if bonus were the full (100%) federal bonus |

See ITP 16-2 for examples of Arizona bonus depreciation.
## Page 6, K - Basis adjustment on disposition
For property sold or otherwise disposed of during the year by a taxpayer who added back all depreciation for that property for all years beginning from and after December 31, 1999, subtract the IRC 167(a) depreciation allowed to the extent it has not already reduced Arizona taxable income. In practice this allows a subtraction for the basis difference on any asset for which federal bonus depreciation was claimed.
## 2026 addition for qualified production property
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, adds, for taxable years beginning January 1, 2026, the federal special depreciation allowance for qualified production property (IRC 168(n)) to the extent not previously added; see qualified-production-property-depreciation.md.
## Example
A sole proprietor deducted $12,000 of federal depreciation on equipment placed in service in 2024 with full federal bonus. Line 17 = $12,000. Because assets placed in service after 2016 use the full federal bonus, Arizona depreciation is also $12,000: line 26 = $12,000, and the net effect is zero.
## Notes
- Arizona's IRC conformity date for 2026 is January 1, 2026, so federal depreciation rules in effect on that date apply
- Form 140 line, box and page item references follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 and depreciation schedules by placed-in-service year
- Arizona depreciation records
- Records of assets sold or disposed of in 2026
## Questions
- How much depreciation did you deduct federally, and in which years were the assets placed in service?
- Did you sell any depreciated assets in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Entering line 17 without line 26
- Applying the 10% or 55% bonus rule to assets placed in service after 2016
