# Special Depreciation for Qualified Production Property (New 2026 Addition)
## Description
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, requires, for individual and corporate taxable years beginning January 1, 2026, an addition for the federal special depreciation allowance for qualified production property under IRC 168(n), to the extent not previously added. The 2026 form has not been published, so the line is not yet assigned; on the 2025 layout an addition without its own line is reported on page 5 (other additions) and carried to line 18.
## Points to settle when the 2026 form is published
- Whether the amount is reported on its own page 5 letter or within line 17 (total federal depreciation), which already adds back all federal depreciation
- How the related Arizona depreciation subtraction is figured
## Example
A manufacturer deducted $50,000 of IRC 168(n) special depreciation federally in 2026 and has not added it back. The addition is $50,000, reported where the 2026 form directs.
## Notes
- Confirmed in the enacted Senate fact sheet for HB 4168: the addition applies to the extent the amount was not previously added
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 showing the IRC 168(n) special depreciation allowance
- Prior Arizona returns showing amounts already added back (including line 17)
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal special depreciation allowance for qualified production property?
- Was any part already added back on line 17 or an earlier return?
## Common Errors
- Adding the same depreciation on line 17 and again as this addition
