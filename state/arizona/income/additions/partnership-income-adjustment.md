# Partnership Income Adjustment (Form 140, Lines 16 and 27)
## Description
If you received an Arizona Form 165 Schedule K-1 whose line 3 shows a difference between federal and state distributable income, enter a positive difference on line 16 as an addition, or a negative difference on line 27 as a subtraction (no minus sign or parentheses).
## Example
- K-1 line 3 shows 1,500 → line 16 = $1,500.
- K-1 line 3 shows (700) → line 27 = $700.
## Notes
- Arizona's IRC conformity date for 2026 is January 1, 2026 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), which can change the federal-state difference partnerships report
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Arizona Form 165 Schedule K-1, line 3, for each partnership
## Questions
- Did you receive an Arizona Form 165 Schedule K-1, and what does line 3 show?
## Common Errors
- Entering a negative amount on line 16 instead of line 27
- Using a minus sign on line 27
