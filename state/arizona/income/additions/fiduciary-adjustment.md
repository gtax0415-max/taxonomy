# Fiduciary Adjustment (Page 5, B and Page 6, B)
## Description
An estate or trust reports your share of its fiduciary adjustment on Arizona Form 141AZ Schedule K-1, line 3. A positive amount is an Other Addition (page 5, B); a negative amount is an Other Subtraction (page 6, B).
## Example
A trust K-1 line 3 shows 650: page 5, B = $650. A K-1 showing (650) instead goes on page 6, B as $650.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Arizona Form 141AZ Schedule K-1, line 3, for each estate or trust
## Questions
- Did you receive an Arizona Form 141AZ Schedule K-1 from an estate or trust?
## Common Errors
- Putting a negative fiduciary adjustment on page 5
## Prompt
- Where does the trust adjustment on my Arizona K-1 go?
