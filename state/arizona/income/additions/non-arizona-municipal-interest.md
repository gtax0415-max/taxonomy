# Non-Arizona Municipal Interest (Form 140, Line 15)
## Description
Enter interest from non-Arizona municipal bonds that was not included in federal income. You may reduce the interest by expenses to purchase or carry the obligation that could not be deducted on the federal return.
## Records
Keep a schedule of payors and amounts of tax-exempt interest, and supporting documents (bank or brokerage statements) for Arizona municipal interest that is exempt from Arizona tax.
## Example
A brokerage 1099-INT shows $1,200 of tax-exempt interest: $800 from California bonds and $400 from City of Tucson bonds. Line 15 = $800; the Tucson interest is not added.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Forms 1099-INT or 1099-DIV with tax-exempt interest
- Fund or brokerage statements showing the state of each issuer
- Expenses to purchase or carry the bonds not deductible federally
## Questions
- Did you receive tax-exempt interest, and from which states' bonds?
- Did you pay expenses to carry those bonds that were not deductible federally?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Arizona municipal bond interest
- Adding back the whole 1099 tax-exempt amount without splitting by state
## Prompt
- Is my municipal bond fund interest taxable in Arizona?
