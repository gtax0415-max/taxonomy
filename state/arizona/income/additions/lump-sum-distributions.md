# Ordinary Income Portion of Lump-Sum Distributions (Page 5, C)
## Description
If you used federal averaging for a lump-sum distribution from a pension or profit-sharing plan, the amount was excluded from federal AGI and taxed separately. Arizona has no averaging, so enter the amount treated as ordinary income on the federal return. If you chose to treat the capital gain portion as ordinary income, include it too (ITR 93-5).
## Example
A retiree used Form 4972 on a $90,000 lump-sum distribution, $80,000 ordinary income and $10,000 capital gain treated as ordinary. Page 5, C = $90,000.
