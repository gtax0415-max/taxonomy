# Additions to Income (Form 140, Lines 15 to 19 and Page 5)
## Description
Additions bring into Arizona gross income amounts that federal AGI leaves out or that Arizona does not allow as deductions. Lines 15, 16 and 17 are on page 1; every other addition is listed on page 5, Other Additions to Arizona Gross Income, whose total (item T) goes to line 18. Include page 5 only if you report a line 18 amount. If you are electing Form 140-SBI, an addition directly related to small business income goes on Form 140-SBI instead.
## How the lines flow
```
Line 15  Non-Arizona municipal interest
Line 16  Partnership income adjustment (Form 165 Schedule K-1, line 3, positive)
Line 17  Total federal depreciation
Line 18  Other additions = page 5, item T
  A     Married persons filing separate returns
  B     Form 141AZ Schedule K-1 fiduciary adjustment (positive)
  C     Ordinary income portion of lump-sum distributions excluded federally
  D     Items previously deducted for Arizona purposes
  E     Claim of right: amounts repaid in 2026
  F(a)  Claim of right: amounts repaid in prior years
  F(b)  Net operating loss due to claim of right
  G(a)  Agricultural water conservation expenses (Form 312)
  G(b)  S corporation pass-through of the Form 312 credit
  H(a)  Basis difference on property for which the Form 338 credit was claimed
  H(b)  Basis difference on agricultural pollution control equipment (Form 325)
  H(c)  Basis difference on pollution control equipment (Form 315)
  I     Nonqualified withdrawals from 529 plans
  J     Sole proprietorship loss of a nonprofit medical marijuana dispensary
  K     Federal NOL carryforward from non-Arizona sources accrued while a nonresident
  L     Federal capital loss carryforward from non-Arizona sources before residency
  M     ADA access expenditures (when subtracted on page 6, N)
  N     Amortization or depreciation for a child care facility before 1990
  O     Net capital loss from exchanging one kind of legal tender for another
  P     Entity-level income tax payment (Form 355)
  Q     Motion picture production costs (Form 334)
  R     Other adjustments related to tax credits
  S     Other adjustments
  T     Total → line 18
Line 19  Subtotal = lines 14 through 18
```
## What changed for 2026
- NEW: addition for the federal special depreciation allowance on qualified production property (IRC 168(n)), to the extent not previously added (HB 4168, Laws 2026, Chapter 140); the 2026 form has not assigned its line
- IRC conformity date moves to January 1, 2026
- The other page 5 additions are unchanged
