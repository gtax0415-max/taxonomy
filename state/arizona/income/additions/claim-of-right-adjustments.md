# Claim of Right Adjustments (Page 5, E, F(a), F(b); Page 6, F)
## Description
When income taxed in an earlier year is repaid under a claim of right, Arizona has its own procedure (ITP 16-1). The federal deduction for the repayment is added back and the benefit is taken instead through the Arizona Claim of Right-Individual form, whose line 8 amount is entered in box 54b.
## Items
| Item | Enter when |
|---|---|
| Page 5, E (repaid in 2026) | during 2026 you repaid amounts held under a claim of right; the repayment was more than $3,000; and you deducted it on the 2026 federal return. Enter the amount deducted federally. |
| Page 5, F(a) (repaid in prior years) | in a prior year you repaid amounts, computed that year's tax under Arizona's claim of right provisions, a net operating loss or capital loss resulted, you may use the carryover in 2026, and the carryover in federal AGI is more than the amount allowed for Arizona. Enter the excess. |
| Page 5, F(b) | add back any net operating loss previously claimed that relates to the claim of right reported |
| Page 6, F (subtraction) | same conditions as F(a), but the carryover allowed for Arizona is more than the amount in federal AGI. Enter the excess. |
## Small business income
If you made the Form 140-SBI election and the repaid income is the type reported on Form 140-SBI, lines 4 through 9, make the adjustment on the SBI return.
## Example
An employee repaid $5,000 of 2025 commissions in 2026 and deducted it federally. Page 5, E = $5,000; the Arizona Claim of Right-Individual form computes the credit entered in box 54b.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Records of the amount repaid and the year it was originally taxed
- The federal deduction for the repayment
## Questions
- Did you repay income in 2026 that was taxed in an earlier year? How much?
- Did you deduct it on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Using page 5, E for a repayment of $3,000 or less
- Not including the Arizona Claim of Right-Individual form
## Prompt
- I had to pay back income I was taxed on last year. How does Arizona handle that?
