# Agricultural Water Conservation Addition (Page 5, G(a) and G(b))
## Description
A taxpayer claiming the Agricultural Water Conservation System Credit (Form 312) must add back the expenses deducted federally that are disallowed because of the credit (G(a)). A shareholder claiming the credit passed through from an S corporation adds the pro rata share of the adjustment the corporation reports on Form 312-S, line 1 (G(b)).
## Example
A farmer deducted $8,000 of qualifying water conservation system expenses federally and claims the Form 312 credit on them: page 5, G(a) = $8,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 312, or Form 312-S, line 1 for an S corporation pass-through
- Federal deduction for the system expenses
## Questions
- Are you claiming the Form 312 credit directly or from an S corporation?
## Common Errors
- Taking the credit while keeping the federal deduction
