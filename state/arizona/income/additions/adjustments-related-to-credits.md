# Other Adjustments Related to Tax Credits (Page 5, R)
## Description
Some credits require an addition so the same cost is not both credited and deducted.
- Form 325: if you claimed the Agricultural Pollution Control Equipment Credit before tax year 2025 (instructions wording), add the amount by which IRC depreciation or amortization on the property exceeds the depreciation or amortization computed under the IRC on the property's Arizona adjusted basis.
- Form 315: if you previously claimed the Pollution Control Equipment Credit before tax year 2022, add the same kind of difference.
- Form 310: if you claim the solar energy device credit, no depreciation deduction is allowed for the device; any such depreciation in federal AGI must be added to Arizona gross income for each period it is deducted. The Form 310 instructions do not name the page 5 item; R, the item for adjustments related to tax credits, is the one that fits.

If you have more than one adjustment related to tax credits, add them together.
## Example
IRC depreciation on Form 315 property is $4,000 and depreciation on its Arizona adjusted basis is $3,200: page 5, R = $800.
## Notes
- The Form 310 solar credit remains available for 2026 (its repeal in HB 4152 was vetoed), so the solar depreciation add-back still applies
- Laws 2026, Chapter 140 repealed the separate corporate pollution control equipment credit; it does not change these individual add-backs
- The Form 325 cutoff wording differs between the 2025 form and instructions; confirm on the 2026 form
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Depreciation schedules under the IRC and on the Arizona adjusted basis for Form 325 or 315 property
- Form 310 credit records and any federal depreciation on the solar device
## Questions
- Have you claimed the Form 315 or 325 credit, and are you still depreciating that property?
- Did you depreciate a solar device on which you claimed the Form 310 credit?
## Common Errors
- Missing the depreciation difference in years after the credit year
## Prompt
- I got a pollution control credit years ago. Do I still have an add-back?
