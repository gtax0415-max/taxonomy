# Motion Picture Production Costs (Page 5, Q)
## Description
If you claim a credit on Form 334, Credit for Motion Picture Production Costs, add the motion picture production costs deducted under the Internal Revenue Code for which the credit is claimed. If you received a pass-through credit from a partnership, LLC or S corporation, also add your pro-rated expenses from Form 334-P, line 4b or Form 334-S, line 4b.
## Example
A production company owner claims the Form 334 credit on $200,000 of production costs deducted federally: page 5, Q = $200,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 334, or Form 334-P or 334-S, line 4b
- Production costs deducted federally
