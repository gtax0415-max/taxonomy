# Enhanced Deduction for Seniors (MCTCP Line 5 → Page 6, V)
## Description
Taxpayers who qualify for the federal enhanced deduction for seniors may take the same deduction on the Arizona return as a subtraction, from tax year 2025 and continuing for 2026 (HB 4168, Laws 2026, Chapter 140, signed June 13, 2026). On the 2025 form it is entered on MCTCP worksheet line 5 and carried to page 6, V. It is separate from the Arizona age 65 exemption on line 38. It is not available on Form 140A or 140EZ. Married couples who filed separately federally (and so could not claim it) but jointly in Arizona compute the subtraction as if they had filed jointly federally.
## Example
Both spouses are over 65 and claim $12,000 of federal enhanced senior deduction: MCTCP line 5 = $12,000, included in page 6, V; box 8 = 2 and line 38 also applies.
## Notes
- The federal enhanced senior deduction runs for 2025 through 2028
- ADOR may replace the MCTCP worksheet on the 2026 form; confirm the line
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A (enhanced deduction for seniors)
- Dates of birth
- Filing status federally and for Arizona
## Questions
- Did you or your spouse claim the federal senior deduction? How much?
- Did you file jointly federally?
## Common Errors
- Skipping the subtraction because the age 65 exemption was claimed
## Prompt
- Does Arizona give the new senior deduction?
