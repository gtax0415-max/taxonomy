# Net Capital Gain and the 25% Subtraction for Assets Acquired After 2011 (Form 140, Lines 20 to 24)
## Description
Arizona allows a subtraction of 25% of net long-term capital gain included in federal AGI from investments in assets acquired after December 31, 2011 (the 25% rate has applied since 2015 under A.R.S. 43-1022 and is unchanged for 2026). Lines 20 to 23 are information lines; only line 24 reduces income.
## Lines
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 20 | Total net capital gain or (loss) from the federal capital gain or (loss) line, included in federal AGI |
| 21 | Total net short-term capital gain or (loss) from federal Schedule D (exclude mutual fund short-term gain distributions not reported on Schedule D) |
| 22 | Total net long-term capital gain or (loss) from Schedule D; if limited to Form 1099-DIV amounts with no Schedule D required, the 1099-DIV amount |
| 23 | Net long-term capital gain from assets acquired after December 31, 2011: worksheet line 5 if positive; otherwise 0 |
| 24 | Line 23 x 25% |
## Worksheet for Net Long-Term Capital Gain Subtraction (keep with your records)
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Net long-term capital gain or (loss) from assets acquired after December 31, 2011, included in federal AGI (include qualifying K-1 amounts; no short-term items) |
| 2 | Net long-term capital loss from legal tender exchanges on post-2011 assets included on page 5, O |
| 3 | Net long-term capital gain from qualified small business investment on post-2011 assets included in line 25 |
| 4 | Net long-term capital gain from legal tender exchanges on post-2011 assets included on page 6, R |
| 5 | (Line 1 + line 2) - (line 3 + line 4); if positive, enter on line 23; if zero or negative, enter 0 |
## Acquisition dates
- Include only gain that can be shown to come from assets acquired after December 31, 2011. If the date cannot be determined, the asset is treated as acquired before January 1, 2012.
- 1099-DIV capital gain distributions from a fund that does not report the acquisition dates of the assets sold cannot be included unless the fund administrator provides the dates.
- 1099-B sales of your own fund shares generally show your purchase dates and may be included.
- Gift or inheritance: acquired on the date the donor or decedent acquired it.
## Example
Federal Schedule D: short-term loss ($1,000); long-term gain $11,000, of which $8,000 is from stock bought in 2015 and $3,000 from stock bought in 2009. Line 20 = $10,000, line 21 = ($1,000), line 22 = $11,000. Worksheet line 1 = $8,000, line 5 = $8,000 → line 23 = $8,000; line 24 = $8,000 x 25% = $2,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule D and Form 8949 with acquisition dates
- Forms 1099-B and 1099-DIV, and any K-1s
- Donor's or decedent's acquisition date for gifted or inherited assets
## Questions
- What are your net short-term and long-term gains on federal Schedule D?
- When did you acquire each asset sold? Were any received by gift or inheritance?
- Do your fund 1099-DIVs show acquisition dates?
## Common Errors
- Claiming line 24 without completing lines 20 to 23
- Including long-term gain from assets acquired before 2012 or with unknown dates
- Counting gain already subtracted on line 25 or page 6, R
