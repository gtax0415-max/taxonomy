# Marijuana Establishment Expenses (Page 6, P and Q)
## Description
Federal law disallows deductions for businesses selling marijuana; Arizona allows a subtraction for the ordinary and necessary expenses related to sales of recreational use products.
- P: a sole proprietorship of an Arizona marijuana establishment, marijuana testing facility, or dual licensee that operates on a for-profit basis enters the total from Arizona Schedule DFE, line 16; an LLC treated as a disregarded entity that elected for-profit operation does the same. Include Schedule DFE after page 6. Do not include cost of goods sold or expenses already deducted federally. A sole proprietorship or disregarded LLC dual licensee that takes the page 6, L subtraction cannot take P. Sole proprietors and disregarded LLCs contact the Arizona Department of Health Services to elect for-profit operation.
- Q: S corporation shareholders enter their pro rata share of those expenses from Form 120S Schedule K-1, line 7.
## Example
A licensed for-profit establishment run as a sole proprietorship reports $85,000 on Schedule DFE, line 16: page 6, P = $85,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Expense records for recreational sales not deducted federally (sole proprietors and disregarded LLCs)
- Form 120S Schedule K-1, line 7 (S corporation shareholders)
- License type and the for-profit election
## Questions
- Is the business a marijuana establishment, testing facility or for-profit dual licensee? How is it organized?
- Did you complete Schedule DFE or receive a 120S K-1 showing line 7?
## Common Errors
- Including cost of goods sold already allowed federally
- Taking both L and P
