# Trump Account Distributions (New 2026 Subtraction)
## Description
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, adds a subtraction for distributions from a Trump account (IRC 530(a)) beginning in tax year 2026, to the extent not already excluded from Arizona gross income. ADOR has not published the 2026 form, so the line and any limits are not yet known. The subtraction applies to amounts included in federal AGI; an amount already excluded federally cannot be subtracted again.
## Example
A beneficiary receives a $2,000 Trump account distribution that is included in 2026 federal AGI: the Arizona subtraction is $2,000, reported where the 2026 form directs.
## Notes
- New for 2026; the line will be assigned on the 2026 Form 140
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- The Trump account distribution statement for 2026
- The amount included in 2026 federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you receive a distribution from a Trump account in 2026? How much was included in federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a distribution that was not included in federal AGI
