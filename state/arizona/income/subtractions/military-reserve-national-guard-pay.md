# Pay for Active Service: Reserves, National Guard or U.S. Armed Forces (Form 140, Line 32)
## Description
Members of the U.S. armed forces subtract pay received for active duty military service, and members of the reserves or National Guard subtract pay for active service, including weekend and two-week training, to the extent included in federal AGI.
## Not subtracted
- Full-time civil service pay of a "military technician (dual status)," even if a uniform is worn (ITR 12-2)
- Active duty pay of the commissioned corps of the U.S. Public Health Service or NOAA (ITR 10-1)
## Example
A National Guard member earns $4,800 of drill pay and $55,000 of civilian wages: line 32 = $4,800.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form W-2 from DFAS for active duty, reserve or National Guard pay
- Whether you are a dual status technician
## Questions
- Did you receive active duty, reserve or National Guard pay? Are you a dual status technician?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting dual status technician wages
- Subtracting Public Health Service or NOAA pay

- Is my National Guard pay taxable in Arizona?
