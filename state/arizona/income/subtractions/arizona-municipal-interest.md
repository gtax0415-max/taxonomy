# Federally Taxable Arizona Municipal Interest (Page 6, C)
## Description
Subtract interest received on obligations of the State of Arizona or its political subdivisions, as evidenced by bonds, that is included in Arizona gross income. Do not enter Arizona municipal interest that is exempt from federal tax and not in federal AGI (ITR 15-1).
## Example
A taxable City of Mesa bond pays $900 of interest reported in federal AGI: page 6, C = $900.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-INT showing the federally taxable Arizona bond interest
- Issuer of each bond (to confirm it is Arizona or an Arizona political subdivision)
## Questions
- Do you hold Arizona municipal bonds whose interest is taxable federally?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting federally exempt Arizona bond interest
## Prompt
- My Arizona bond interest is taxable federally. Do I pay Arizona tax on it?
