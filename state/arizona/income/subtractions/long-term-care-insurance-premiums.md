# Long-Term Care Insurance Premiums (Page 6, M)
## Description
Subtract premium costs for long-term care insurance for qualified long-term care services, which are necessary diagnostic, preventive, therapeutic, curing, treating, mitigating and rehabilitative services, and maintenance and personal care services. The subtraction is allowed only if you do not claim itemized deductions for the year.
## Example
A single taxpayer taking the standard deduction pays $2,200 of long-term care premiums: page 6, M = $2,200. If she itemized instead, no page 6, M subtraction would be allowed.
## Notes
- Laws 2026, Chapter 140 caps the 2026 SALT itemized deduction at $10,000 and changes the charitable standard deduction increase, which can change whether itemizing is better, and so whether this subtraction is available
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
