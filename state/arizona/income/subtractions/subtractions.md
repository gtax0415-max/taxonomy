# Subtractions from Income (Form 140, Lines 20 to 37 and Page 6)
## Description
Subtractions remove from Arizona gross income amounts that federal AGI includes but Arizona does not tax. Lines 24 to 34c are on page 1; all others are listed on page 6, Other Subtractions from Arizona Gross Income, whose total (item W) goes to line 36. You may subtract only items with statutory authority, may not subtract amounts allocable to income excluded from Arizona taxable income, and may not subtract amounts already excluded from federal AGI. Include page 6 only if you report a line 36 amount. If you elect Form 140-SBI, a subtraction directly related to small business income goes on Form 140-SBI.
## How the lines flow
```
Lines 20-23 Net capital gain or (loss); short-term; long-term; long-term from assets acquired after December 31, 2011
Line 24  25% of line 23
Line 25  Net capital gain from investment in a qualified small business
Line 26  Recalculated Arizona depreciation
Line 27  Partnership income adjustment (negative Form 165 K-1, line 3)
Line 28  Interest on U.S. obligations
Line 29a Federal, Arizona state or local government pensions (up to $2,500 per taxpayer)
Line 29b Retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services
Line 30  Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits (taxable amount)
Line 31  Certain wages of American Indians
Line 32  Active service pay: reserves, National Guard, U.S. armed forces
Line 33  Net operating loss adjustment
Line 34a 529 college savings plan contributions; 34b 529A (ABLE) contributions; 34c = 34a + 34b
Line 35  Line 19 - lines 24 through 34c
Line 36  Other subtractions = page 6, item W
  A  Married persons filing separate returns
  B  Form 141AZ Schedule K-1 fiduciary adjustment (negative)
  C  Federally taxable Arizona municipal interest
  D  Adoption expense
  E  Qualified wood stove, wood fireplace or gas-fired fireplace
  F  Claim of right: amounts repaid in prior years
  G  Expenses not allowed federally because of federal credits
  H  Qualified state tuition distributions
  I  Installment sale income taxed by another state in a prior year
  J  Agricultural crops given to Arizona charitable organizations
  K  Basis adjustment for property sold or disposed of
  L  Sole proprietorship income of a nonprofit medical marijuana dispensary
  M  Long-term care insurance premiums
  N  ADA access expenditures
  O  Exploration expenses deferred before January 1, 1990
  P  Marijuana establishment expenses (sole proprietorship, Schedule DFE line 16)
  Q  Marijuana establishment expenses (S corporation shareholders, Form 120S K-1 line 7)
  R  Net capital gain from exchanging one kind of legal tender for another
  S  Virtual currency and NFTs received in an airdrop
  T  Gas fees not included in virtual currency or NFT basis
  U  Arizona Families Tax Rebate (2025 item; no 2026 rebate)
  V  Other adjustments (MCTCP worksheet: tips, overtime, senior deduction)
  W  Total → line 36
Line 37  Line 35 - line 36
```
## What changed for 2026
- ADOPTION (page 6, D): cap rises from $3,000 to $5,000 single or head of household and $10,000 married (HB 2155, 2025, A.R.S. 43-1022)
- NEW (lines not yet assigned): Trump account distributions; child and dependent care expenses above the federal credit (HB 4168, Laws 2026, Chapter 140)
- CONTINUES: qualified tips, qualified overtime and enhanced senior subtractions
- ENDED: passenger vehicle loan interest subtraction (2025 only)
- Unchanged statutory amounts: $2,500 government pension (29a), $2,000 / $4,000 529 and ABLE (34a, 34b), 25% capital gain rate (24), $500 fireplace conversion (E)

## Required Information
- 2026 federal return, including Schedule D and Schedule 1-A
- Forms 1099-R, SSA-1099, 1099-Q and W-2 (military)
- 529 and ABLE contribution statements, adoption records, long-term care premium statements
## Questions
- Do you have capital gains on assets bought after 2011?
- Did you receive Social Security, a government or military pension, U.S. bond interest or military pay?
- Did you contribute to a 529 or ABLE account, adopt a child, or pay long-term care insurance premiums?
