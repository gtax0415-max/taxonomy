# Child and Dependent Care Expenses Exceeding the Federal Credit (New 2026 Subtraction)
## Description
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, adds a subtraction, beginning in tax year 2026, for child and dependent care expenses that exceed the federal child and dependent care credit. The 2026 form is not published; the line, the expense measure (for example, whether the Form 2441 expense limits apply) and any cap are to be confirmed when ADOR releases it.
## Example
Federal Form 2441 shows $6,000 of qualifying expenses and a federal credit of $1,200. As described in the law, the subtraction is $6,000 - $1,200 = $4,800, subject to how the 2026 form measures expenses.
## Notes
- The law refers to expenses for a qualifying individual paid or incurred during the tax year, to the extent not already excluded from Arizona gross income
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Form 2441 (qualifying expenses and the credit)
- Provider receipts and provider identification
- Any employer dependent care benefits excluded from income
## Questions
- How much did you pay for child or dependent care in 2026, and what was your federal credit?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the full expenses without reducing them by the federal credit
