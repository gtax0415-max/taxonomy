# Net Capital Gain from Investment in an Arizona Qualified Small Business (Form 140, Line 25)
## Description
Subtract the net capital gain included in federal AGI from investment in a qualified small business as determined by the Arizona Commerce Authority (ACA) under A.R.S. 41-1518. Net all gains and losses from investments in Arizona qualified small businesses, including amounts on Forms 165, 120S and 141AZ Schedule K-1, and enter the allowable subtraction.
## Requirements
The investment must have been made after the ACA certified the company and before its certification expired. The ACA's Small Business Incentives: Angel Investment page lists certified businesses and dates.
## Example
A taxpayer sells shares of an ACA-certified company bought during its certification period for a $20,000 long-term gain (acquired 2019). Line 25 = $20,000, and the $20,000 goes on line 3 of the net long-term capital gain worksheet so it is not also counted on line 23.
## Notes
- The ACA angel investment program runs through June 30, 2031, so new certifications continue in 2026
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- ACA certification of the company and its certification dates
- Investment and sale records, and any Forms 165, 120S or 141AZ Schedule K-1
## Questions
- Was the company certified by the ACA when you invested?
- Did you have losses on other qualified small business investments to net?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting gain from an investment made before certification
- Also including the gain on line 23
## Prompt
- Is gain on my Arizona angel investment taxable?
