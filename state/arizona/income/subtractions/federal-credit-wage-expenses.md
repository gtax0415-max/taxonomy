# Certain Expenses Not Allowed for Federal Purposes (Page 6, G)
## Description
When certain federal credits are claimed, the related wages cannot be deducted federally. Arizona lets you subtract the portion of wages or salaries paid or incurred during the year equal to the amount of these federal credits received for 2026:
- Federal Work Opportunity Credit
- Empowerment Zone Employment Credit
- Credit for employer-paid social security taxes on employee cash tips
- Indian Employment Credit

If you make the small business election, take this subtraction on Form 140-SBI.
## Example
A restaurant owner's federal credit for employer social security on tips is $3,100, so $3,100 of wages was not deductible federally: page 6, G = $3,100.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Forms 5884, 8844, 8845 and 8846 for 2026
- Wage expense records showing the disallowed amount
## Questions
- Did you claim any of these federal employment credits on your business return?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the credit amount when no wage deduction was disallowed

- Can I deduct wages I lost federally because of the tip credit?
