# Other Adjustments (Page 6, V), Total (W) and the MCTCP Worksheet
## Description
Page 6, V holds allowable subtractions not listed elsewhere; with more than one, add them. On the 2025 forms ADOR also routes the federal deductions created in 2025 (qualified tips, qualified overtime, qualified passenger vehicle loan interest and the enhanced deduction for seniors) to V through the Middle Class Tax Cuts Package (MCTCP) worksheet. Page 6, W is the total of page 6, entered on line 36. Complete and include page 6 only if you report a line 36 amount; the MCTCP worksheet is kept with your records, not filed.
## MCTCP worksheet (2025 layout)
| Line | Entry |
|---|---|
| 1 | Other Adjustments not related to the MCTCP |
| 2 | IRS Schedule 1-A, line 13 (qualified tips deduction) |
| 3 | IRS Schedule 1-A, line 21 (qualified overtime compensation deduction) |
| 4 | IRS Schedule 1-A, line 30 (qualified passenger vehicle loan interest deduction) - 2025 only |
| 5 | IRS Schedule 1-A, line 37 (enhanced deduction for seniors) |
| 6 | Total of lines 1 to 5 → Form 140, page 6, line V (Form 140PY page 6, Y; Form 140NR page 6, P; Form 140X page 7, V) |

Schedule 1-A line numbers are from the 2025 federal schedule; confirm against the 2026 federal form.
## Filing status differences (MCTCP rules)
- Joint federal return, separate Arizona returns: reduce the federal amounts to the taxpayer's own amounts, excluding the spouse's.
- Separate federal returns, joint Arizona return, and the separate federal status made you ineligible for the senior, tips or overtime deduction: compute the Arizona subtraction as if you had filed jointly federally.
- Separate federal returns, joint Arizona return, vehicle loan interest claimed: combine both federal amounts (2025 only).
- Part-year and nonresident filers subtract only tips and overtime included in Arizona gross income.
## 2026
HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, continues the tips, overtime and senior subtractions for 2026, ends the vehicle loan interest subtraction after 2025, and adds subtractions for Trump account distributions and for child and dependent care expenses that exceed the federal credit. The 2026 form may give these their own lines; until then V is the route used for unlisted subtractions.
## Example
A married couple filing jointly: Schedule 1-A tips deduction $6,000, overtime $2,500, enhanced senior deduction $6,000. MCTCP line 6 = $6,000 + $2,500 + $6,000 = $14,500 → page 6, V = $14,500.
## Notes
- The federal tips, overtime and senior deductions run for 2025 through 2028
- ADOR may replace the MCTCP worksheet or give the new 2026 subtractions their own lines
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A
- Records of any other allowable subtraction
- Filing status federally and for Arizona
## Questions
- What deductions did you claim on federal Schedule 1-A?
- Do you have any other allowable subtraction not listed on page 6?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the federal tips, overtime or senior deduction off the Arizona return
- Including vehicle loan interest on a 2026 return
- Filing Form 140A or 140EZ and losing these subtractions
