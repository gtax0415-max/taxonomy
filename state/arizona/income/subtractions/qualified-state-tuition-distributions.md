# Qualified State Tuition Program Distributions (Page 6, H)
## Description
A beneficiary of a qualified state tuition program (a program meeting IRC 529) may subtract the amount distributed for qualified education expenses that had to be included in federal AGI.
## Example
A student received a $6,000 distribution for tuition, of which $700 of earnings was included in federal AGI: page 6, H = $700.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q
- Records of qualified education expenses
- Amount of the distribution included in federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you receive a 529 distribution, and was any of it taxable federally?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the whole distribution rather than the amount included in federal AGI
## Prompt
- Is my taxable 529 distribution taxed in Arizona?
