# Qualified Wood Stove, Wood Fireplace or Gas-Fired Fireplace (Page 6, E)
## Description
Subtract up to $500 (statutory, not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026) of the cost of converting an existing fireplace on your Arizona property to a qualified wood stove, a qualified wood fireplace, or a gas-fired fireplace and non-optional equipment directly related to its operation. Do not include taxes, interest or other finance charges.
## Definitions
- Qualified wood stove or wood fireplace: a residential wood heater manufactured on or after July 1, 1990, or sold at retail on or after July 1, 1992, that meets the U.S. EPA July 1990 particulate emissions standards.
- Gas-fired fireplace: a device burning natural or liquefied petroleum gas through a burner system permanently installed in the fireplace; converting a wood-burning fireplace to permanently installed noncombustible gas logs qualifies.
## Example
Converting a wood fireplace to gas logs costs $1,900 plus $140 sales tax: page 6, E = $500.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Invoice for the conversion, separating taxes, interest and finance charges
- Manufacturer's EPA certification for a wood stove or wood fireplace
