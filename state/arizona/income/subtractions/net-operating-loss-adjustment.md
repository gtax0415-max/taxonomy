# Net Operating Loss Adjustment (Form 140, Line 33)
## Description
Arizona did not adopt the special federal rules for net operating losses incurred during 2008 or 2009 (the IRC 172(b)(1)(H) election to carry a loss back 3, 4 or 5 years instead of 2, under the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act of 2009 or the Worker, Homeownership, and Business Assistance Act of 2009). Figure how much NOL carryforward would have been allowed on the federal return had the election not been made, and enter the amount that exceeds the NOL carryforward actually deducted in federal AGI.
## Example
Without the election, $7,000 of the 2009 NOL would carry forward to 2026; $2,000 was deducted federally. Line 33 = $5,000.
## Notes
- Only 2008 or 2009 losses are involved, so few 2026 returns use this line
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal NOL carryforward worksheets
- Records of the IRC 172(b)(1)(H) election
## Questions
- Did you elect the extended carryback for a 2008 or 2009 NOL?
## Common Errors
- Using line 33 for an ordinary NOL difference unrelated to the 2008 or 2009 election
## Prompt
- What is the Arizona NOL adjustment on line 33?
