# Arizona Families Tax Rebate (Page 6, U)
## Description
On the 2025 return, enter the total 2025 Arizona Families Tax Rebate received during 2025 that was required to be included in Arizona gross income under the IRC. A filer subtracting the rebate must use Form 140. The sources contain no 2026 rebate; confirm whether item U remains on the 2026 form.
## Example
A family received a $250 rebate in 2025 that was included in 2025 federal AGI: page 6, U = $250 on the 2025 return; nothing on the 2026 return unless a rebate is received in 2026.
## Notes
- Laws 2026, Chapter 140 creates no new rebate for 2026, so item U is expected to be 0 or removed on the 2026 form
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Arizona Families Tax Rebate payment record, if any rebate was received in 2026
## Questions
- Did you receive an Arizona Families Tax Rebate during 2026?
## Common Errors
- Carrying a 2025 rebate onto the 2026 return
## Prompt
- Do I subtract the Arizona family rebate?
