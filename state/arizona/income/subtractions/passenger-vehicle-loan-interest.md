# Qualified Passenger Vehicle Loan Interest (MCTCP Line 4) - Not Available for 2026
## Description
On the 2025 return, taxpayers who claimed the federal qualified passenger vehicle loan interest deduction could subtract it through MCTCP worksheet line 4 and page 6, V; spouses who filed separately federally and jointly in Arizona combined both federal amounts. HB 4168 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140), signed June 13, 2026, makes this subtraction tax year 2025 only. For 2026 there is no Arizona subtraction for this federal deduction.
## Example
A taxpayer deducts $1,800 of car loan interest federally for 2026: no Arizona subtraction; MCTCP line 4 is 0 for 2026.
## Notes
- The federal deduction continues through 2028, so the federal and Arizona treatment differ for 2026
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A (only to confirm the amount stays in Arizona income)
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal car loan interest deduction? (No Arizona effect for 2026.)
## Common Errors
- Carrying the 2025 vehicle loan interest subtraction into 2026
