# Installment Sale Income Taxed by Another State in a Prior Year (Page 6, I)
## Description
Subtract installment sale income that is subject to Arizona tax in 2026 if you paid income tax to another state on that income in a prior year. Enter the amount included in 2026 Arizona gross income. Income taxed by both Arizona and the other state in 2026 is not subtracted; a Form 309 credit may apply instead.
## Example
A taxpayer sold Colorado land in 2023 while a Colorado resident and paid Colorado tax on the whole gain then; after moving to Arizona, $10,000 of the gain is reported in 2026 federal AGI. Page 6, I = $10,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Form 6252 for 2026
- The other state's prior-year return showing tax paid on the gain
## Questions
- Did you pay another state's tax on this installment gain in an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using this subtraction when both states tax the income in 2026
## Prompt
- I already paid another state's tax on my installment sale. Does Arizona tax it again?
