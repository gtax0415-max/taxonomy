# Retired or Retainer Pay of the Uniformed Services (Form 140, Line 29b)
## Description
Subtract 100% of benefits, annuities and pensions received as retired or retainer pay of the uniformed services of the United States, to the extent included in federal income. If both spouses receive such income, each subtracts 100%. A surviving spouse of a deceased military veteran receiving payments from the uniformed services may exclude 100% of them. Do not enter this pay on line 29a.
## Example
A retired Air Force officer receives $36,000 of taxable retired pay: line 29b = $36,000.
## Notes
- The 100% subtraction is unchanged for 2026
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-R from DFAS or the uniformed service
- For survivors, proof that payments come from the uniformed services
## Questions
