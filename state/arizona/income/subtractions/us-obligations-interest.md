# Interest on U.S. Obligations (Form 140, Line 28)
## Description
Enter interest from U.S. Government obligations, such as savings bonds and Treasury bills, included in federal income. Interest or other expenses incurred to purchase or carry the obligations are not deductible: if they are in Arizona gross income, reduce the subtraction; if itemizing, exclude them from the itemized deduction.
## Not subtracted
- Interest on Fannie Mae (FNMA) or Ginnie Mae (GNMA) bonds, which Arizona taxes (ITR 06-1)
- Amounts from a qualified pension plan or an IRA that invests in U.S. obligations; these are not interest income (ITR 96-2, 96-3)
## Example
Form 1099-INT shows $600 in box 3 (Treasury interest) and $400 of bank interest. Line 28 = $600.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 5 / page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-INT (box 3)
- Fund statements giving the percentage of income from U.S. obligations
- Expenses to purchase or carry the obligations
## Questions
- Did you receive interest on U.S. savings bonds, Treasury bills, notes or bonds?
- Do your funds report a percentage of income from U.S. obligations?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting GNMA or FNMA interest
- Subtracting IRA distributions from Treasury funds
## Prompt
- Is my Treasury bill interest taxed by Arizona?
