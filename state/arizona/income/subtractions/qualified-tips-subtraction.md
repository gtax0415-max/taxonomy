# Qualified Tips Subtraction (MCTCP Line 2 → Page 6, V)
## Description
Taxpayers who qualify for the federal qualified tips deduction may take the same deduction on the Arizona return as a subtraction. It began in tax year 2025 and continues for 2026 under Laws 2026, Chapter 140. On the 2025 form it is entered on MCTCP worksheet line 2 from federal Schedule 1-A and carried to page 6, V. It is not available on Form 140A or 140EZ.
## Example
A server's federal Schedule 1-A tips deduction is $9,000: MCTCP line 2 = $9,000, included in page 6, V.
## Notes
- The federal deduction runs for tax years 2025 through 2028; Arizona follows it
- On the 2026 form the MCTCP worksheet may change; confirm the line when the form is released
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A (qualified tips deduction)
- Forms W-2 and 1099 reporting tips
- Filing status federally and for Arizona
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal qualified tips deduction? How much?
- If married, are you filing the same way federally and in Arizona?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting all tips received instead of the federal deduction amount
- Including the spouse's tips on a separate Arizona return
## Prompt
- Does Arizona exempt my tips?
