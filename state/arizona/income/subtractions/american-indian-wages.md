# Certain Wages of American Indians (Form 140, Line 31)
## Description
Enrolled members of federally recognized American Indian tribes may subtract wages earned while living and working on their tribe's reservation (ITR 96-4).
## Example
An enrolled member lives on her tribe's reservation and earns $42,000 working for the tribe there, plus $5,000 off-reservation: line 31 = $42,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form W-2 for the reservation wages
- Proof of tribal enrollment
- Proof of residence on your own tribe's reservation
## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member, and do you live and work on your own tribe's reservation?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting wages earned off the reservation or on another tribe's reservation
## Prompt
- Do tribal members pay Arizona tax on reservation wages?
