# Qualified Overtime Compensation Subtraction (MCTCP Line 3 → Page 6, V)
## Description
Taxpayers who qualify for the federal qualified overtime compensation deduction may subtract the same amount on the Arizona return, from tax year 2025 and continuing for 2026 (Laws 2026, Chapter 140). On the 2025 form it is entered on MCTCP worksheet line 3 and carried to page 6, V. It is not available on Form 140A or 140EZ.
## Example
A nurse's federal Schedule 1-A overtime deduction is $3,400: MCTCP line 3 = $3,400, included in page 6, V.
## Notes
- The federal deduction runs for tax years 2025 through 2028; Arizona follows it
- On the 2026 form the MCTCP worksheet may change; confirm the line when the form is released
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A (qualified overtime compensation deduction)
- Form W-2 overtime reporting
- Filing status federally and for Arizona
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal overtime deduction? How much?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting total overtime pay instead of the federal deduction
