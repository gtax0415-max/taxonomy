# Gas Fees Not Included in Virtual Currency or NFT Basis (Page 6, T)
## Description
Subtract the amount allowed by A.R.S. 43-1028 for gas fees not already included in the taxpayer's virtual currency or non-fungible token basis. A gas fee is a fee paid to the operator of a virtual network for using the network to facilitate the purchase, sale or exchange of virtual currency or an NFT.
## Example
A trader paid $180 of network fees in 2026, of which $120 was added to basis on purchases. Page 6, T = $60.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Wallet or exchange records of network fees paid in 2026
- Cost basis records showing which fees were already added to basis
## Questions
- What network fees did you pay, and were they added to basis or deducted from proceeds?
