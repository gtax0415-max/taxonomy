# Agricultural Crops Given to Arizona Charitable Organizations (Page 6, J)
## Description
Arizona allows a subtraction for qualified crop gifts made during 2026 to one or more charitable organizations if all apply: you are in the business of farming or processing agricultural crops; the crop was grown in Arizona; and the gift was to a charitable organization located in Arizona that is exempt from Arizona income tax. The subtraction is the larger of the wholesale market price or the most recent sale price of the crop, but it cannot include any amount deducted under IRC 170 for the contribution that exceeds the cost of producing the crop (ITP 12-1).
## Example
A grower gives citrus to an Arizona food bank: wholesale price $5,000, most recent sale price $4,600, no IRC 170 deduction above production cost. Page 6, J = $5,000.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Donation receipts from the Arizona charity
- Wholesale market price and most recent sale price of the crop
- Production cost and any IRC 170 deduction taken
## Questions
- Are you in the business of farming or processing crops, and were the crops grown in Arizona?
- Which charity received them, and what were the wholesale and recent sale prices?
## Common Errors
- Using production cost instead of the larger market or sale price

- Can I deduct crops I donated to a food bank in Arizona?
