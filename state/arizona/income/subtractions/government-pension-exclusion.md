# Exclusion for U.S. Government, Arizona State or Local Government Pensions (Form 140, Line 29a)
## Description
Subtract the smaller of the qualifying pension received or $2,500 (statutory, not indexed for inflation, so unchanged for 2026, A.R.S. 43-1022), counting only the amount reported as income on the federal return. If both spouses receive qualifying pensions, each may subtract up to $2,500.
## Qualifying sources
- United States Government Service Retirement and Disability Fund
- United States Foreign Service Retirement and Disability System
- Any other retirement system or plan established by federal law (plans authorized and enacted into the U.S. Code, not plans merely regulated by federal law)
- Arizona State Retirement System; Arizona State Retirement Plan
- Corrections Officer Retirement Plan; Public Safety Personnel Retirement System; Elected Officials' Retirement Plan
- Retirement plans for employees of an Arizona county, city or town
- Optional retirement programs of the Arizona Board of Regents and Arizona community college districts
## Not on this line
Uniformed services retired or retainer pay (line 29b); public pensions from states other than Arizona.
## Example
Married filing joint: one spouse receives a taxable ASRS pension of $18,000 and the other a federal civil service pension of $1,900. Line 29a = $2,500 + $1,900 = $4,400.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form 1099-R for each pension, showing the paying retirement system and taxable amount
- Which spouse receives each pension
## Questions
- Which retirement system pays each pension, and what is the taxable amount?
- Do both spouses receive qualifying pensions?
## Common Errors
- Combining spouses' pensions into one $2,500 limit, or exceeding $2,500 per person
- Subtracting a pension from another state's system or a private employer plan
## Prompt
- I get an Arizona state pension. How much can I exclude?
