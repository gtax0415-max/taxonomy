# U.S. Social Security or Railroad Retirement Benefits (Form 140, Line 30)
## Description
Arizona does not tax Social Security benefits under Title II of the Social Security Act or benefits paid by the Railroad Retirement Board under the Railroad Retirement Act (tier 1 or tier 2, railroad disability benefits reported on RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R, railroad unemployment benefits and railroad sickness payments) (ITR 16-1). Enter only the taxable amount included in federal income.
## Example
SSA-1099 shows $24,000 of benefits, of which $9,500 is taxable on the federal return: line 30 = $9,500.
## Notes
- Arizona continues to exempt these benefits fully in 2026
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Form SSA-1099 or RRB-1099 / RRB-1099-R
- Taxable amount from the 2026 federal return
## Questions
- How much of your Social Security or railroad retirement was taxable federally?
## Common Errors
- Entering the gross benefit instead of the taxable amount
## Prompt
- Does Arizona tax Social Security?
