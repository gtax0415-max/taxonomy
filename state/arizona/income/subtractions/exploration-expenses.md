# Exploration Expenses Deferred before January 1, 1990 (Page 6, O)
## Description
If you deferred exploration expenses determined under IRC 617 in a taxable year ending before January 1, 1990 and have not previously taken a subtraction for them, call the department at one of the numbers on page 1 of the instructions to determine the subtraction.
## Example
A mining investor deferred $12,000 of exploration costs in 1988 and has never subtracted them; the department advises the amount to enter on page 6, O.
## Notes
- Applies only to expenses deferred before 1990, so very few 2026 returns use it
- Form 140 line and box numbers follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Records of exploration expenses deferred under IRC 617 before January 1, 1990
- Prior Arizona returns showing whether any subtraction was taken
## Questions
- Did you defer mining exploration expenses before 1990 that were never subtracted for Arizona?
## Common Errors
- Using this item for exploration expenses from 1990 or later
## Prompt
- What is the Arizona exploration expense subtraction?
