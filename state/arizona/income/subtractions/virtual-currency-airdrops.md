# Virtual Currency and Non-Fungible Tokens Received in an Airdrop (Page 6, S)
## Description
To the extent not already excluded from Arizona gross income under the IRC, subtract the value of virtual currency and non-fungible tokens received in an airdrop, measured at the time of the airdrop. There is no subtraction for appreciation after receipt (A.R.S. 43-1028).
## Definitions (A.R.S. 43-1028)
- Airdrop: receipt of virtual currency through a distribution of virtual currency to the distributed ledger addresses of multiple taxpayers.
- Non-fungible token: a non-fungible cryptographic asset on a blockchain with unique identifiers or metadata that make it irreplaceable and non-exchangeable for a similar token or asset.
- Virtual currency: a digital representation of value that functions as a medium of exchange, a unit of account and a store of value, other than the U.S. dollar or a foreign currency.
- Foreign currency: the coin and paper money of another country designated as legal tender, that circulates and is customarily used as a medium of exchange there.
## Example
Tokens worth $1,300 when airdropped are reported as income federally and later sold for $2,000. Page 6, S = $1,300; the $700 gain on sale is not subtracted.
## Notes
- Form 140 line and page 6 item letters follow the 2025 Form 140; ADOR had not released the 2026 Form 140 when this was revised, so confirm them against it
## Required Information
- Wallet or exchange records showing each airdrop's value when received
- Federal return showing the airdrop income
