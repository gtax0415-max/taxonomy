# Filing for a Deceased Taxpayer
## When a return is required
A return is required for someone who died before filing if their 2026 income met the filing requirement OR they received advance payments of the 2026 Minnesota Child Tax Credit.
## How to file
- Use the same filing status as the decedent's federal return
- Enter the decedent's name and the filer's name; write "DECD" and the date of death after the decedent's last name
## Who gets the refund
- Surviving spouse filing jointly: refund goes to the spouse
- Personal representative: attach the court appointment; refund paid to the estate
- No spouse and no personal representative: complete Form M23, Claim for a Refund for a Deceased Taxpayer, and include it
## Advance Child Tax Credit
If one spouse on a joint return dies before filing, advance payments are attributed to the surviving spouse.
## Prompt
- My mother died this year. Do I file her Minnesota return?
