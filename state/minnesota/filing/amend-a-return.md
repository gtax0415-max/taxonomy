# Amend an Income Tax Return (Form M1X)
## When to amend
- To correct income, deductions, credits, or filing status on an original Form M1
- To claim a credit you missed (for example, the Renter's Credit, which now must be claimed on the income tax return)
- After amending the federal return, or after the IRS changes the federal return, if it affects Minnesota
- After a partnership audit pushes adjustments to partners
- RETROACTIVE FEDERAL CONFORMITY (2026 Tax Bill): Revenue will NOT adjust returns automatically for retroactive conformity items; affected taxpayers should amend once updated forms are available
## Deadlines for claiming a refund (2026 Tax Bill, for claims filed on or after May 28, 2026)
The LATER of:
- 3½ years from the original due date or extended due date, or
- 2 years from paying the tax, penalty, or interest
The bill removed other limitations, including the earlier one-year limit after an audit assessment, appeal determination, or commissioner-filed return.
## Federal changes
- File Form M1X within 180 DAYS of the IRS notice of a change, or of filing a federal amended return, if it affects Minnesota
- If the federal change does not affect Minnesota, send a letter of explanation within 180 days with the federal amended return or IRS notice
- Failing to report: 10% penalty on additional tax, and Revenue gets six more years to assess
## How to file
- Tax year 2026 amended returns may be filed ELECTRONICALLY
- Complete Form M1X plus a corrected Form M1 and every changed schedule, and explain the changes
- Paper: Minnesota Amended Individual Income Tax, Mail Station 1060, St. Paul
## Limits
- An amended refund cannot be applied to estimated tax
- Advance Child Tax Credit payments cannot be elected on an amended return; amending a return that changes the child tax credit ends future advance payments
- Minnesota generally allows only NOL carryforwards (no carrybacks)
- Property tax refunds are amended separately (Form M1PRX)
## Questions
- What changed from your original return, and why?
- Did the IRS change your federal return or did you file a federal amended return? When?
## Common Errors
- Missing the 180-day window for federal changes
- Filing Form M1X without the corrected schedules
- Expecting Revenue to adjust for retroactive conformity automatically
## Prompt
- I forgot to claim the Renter's Credit. How do I fix my Minnesota return?
- The IRS changed my federal return.
