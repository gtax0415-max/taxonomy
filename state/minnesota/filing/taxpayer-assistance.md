# Taxpayer Assistance and Rights
## Where's My Refund
On Revenue's website: confirm the return was received, track processing, and see the date sent. Requires SSN, date of birth, and the EXACT refund amount. Automated phone: 651-296-4444 or 1-800-657-3676 (also for Form 1099-G amounts).
## Free tax preparation
IRS-certified VITA volunteer sites across Minnesota help people 60 or older, people with disabilities, people with limited English, and filers under an annual income limit that the IRS resets each filing season (Revenue's site still shows $67,000; University of Minnesota Extension, reviewed in 2026, shows $69,000 — check the limit announced for the 2027 filing season). AARP Tax-Aide sites have no income limit. Search "Free Tax Preparation" on revenue.state.mn.us or call 651-297-3724 or 1-800-657-3989.
## Free electronic filing
For the 2026 filing season Revenue listed free e-file options for AGI of $89,000 or less; the 2027-season threshold will be announced in January 2027. The 2026 Tax Bill also requires Revenue to build a direct free-file system on its website.
## Questions and interpreters
651-296-3781 or 1-800-652-9094; individual.incometax@state.mn.us. Free interpreter services in any language.
## Taxpayer Rights Advocate
For problems not resolved through normal channels: 651-556-6013, dor.tra@state.mn.us, Mail Station 7102.
## Separation of Liability Program
For someone who filed a joint return, is no longer married, and still owes part of the joint liability (Mail Station 7701).
## Identity protection
Revenue never asks for personal information by unsolicited email or phone. Report suspicious contacts to 651-296-3781.
## Use of information
Return information is private under state law; Revenue may share it only as the law allows (for example, with certain government entities) or with your consent.
