# Filing the Return: Header, Filing Status, Attachments, Signatures, and Checkboxes
## What you need
Name and address; SSN or ITIN; date of birth (taxpayer and spouse); the completed federal return. Missing items delay refunds; a payment may not be processed.
## Header
- Legal names as on Social Security cards; one address (home or PO box); check NEW if the address changed, FOREIGN if foreign
- County of residence
- Married filing separately: enter the spouse's name and SSN in the filing status area, not in the name block
## Federal filing status (must match the federal return)
Single, Married Filing Jointly, Married Filing Separately, Head of Household, Qualifying Surviving Spouse. If federal Form 1040-NR was filed treating a nonresident or dual-status alien spouse as a U.S. resident, check Married Filing Separately on Form M1.
## State Elections Campaign Fund
Each spouse may designate $5 by party code (or 99, General Campaign Fund). It does not change tax or refund. 2026 codes (Form M1 near-final draft, 8/3/2026): Republican 11; Democratic/Farmer-Labor 12; Grassroots/Legalize Cannabis 14; Libertarian 16; Legal Marijuana Now 17; Forward Independence 18 (was Independence-Alliance in 2025); General Campaign Fund 99 (payments/nongame-wildlife-checkoff.md).
## Lines A–F (federal reference amounts)
A wages (Form 1040 line 1z); B taxable IRA, pensions, annuities (lines 4b + 5b); C unemployment (Schedule 1 line 7); D federal taxable income (line 15, negative if negative); E total Social Security (line 6a); F taxable Social Security (line 6b).
## Number formatting
- Round to whole dollars (0.50 and above rounds up)
- Leave lines blank if zero or not applicable
- Enter negatives for Line D, Lines 1, 3, or 13b when less than zero
- No cents, dashes, dollar signs, slashed zeros or sevens; explanations on a separate sheet
## Paper filing
- Include Form M1, all required Minnesota schedules, and a COMPLETE copy of the federal return and schedules; no staples or tape (paperclip only)
- Mail to Minnesota Individual Income Tax, Mail Station 0010, 600 N. Robert St., St. Paul, MN 55146-0010
- Electing advance Child Tax Credit payments on a paper return: use Mail Station 0015 (2025 instructions; confirm for 2026)
- Send payment vouchers and checks SEPARATELY from the return
## Signatures and preparer
- Paper returns must be signed (both spouses on a joint return); an unsigned paper return is invalid
- Paid preparers sign and provide a PTIN (or VITA/TCE number)
## Checkboxes at the bottom of Form M1
| Checkbox | Effect |
|---|---|
| I do not want my paid preparer to file my return electronically | Opts out of e-filing by a preparer |
| I am filing this return for Net Investment Income Tax requirements | Used by composite/PTE electing owners who file Form M1 only to pay NIIT (taxes/net-investment-income-tax.md) |
| I authorize the Minnesota Department of Revenue to discuss this tax return with the preparer or third-party designee | Lets Revenue discuss line items, tax due, notices, payments, and refunds, and lets the preparer cancel direct deposit or debit payments and request abatement. Valid for one year after the due date (current original returns) or one year after submission (amended or noncurrent returns). It does NOT authorize signing, audit or appeal representation, or discussing abatement progress — use Form REV184i, Power of Attorney, for those |
| I do not have health insurance and want to learn what choices I have | Authorizes sharing name(s), household size, dependents' birth dates, address, AGI, email, phone, and county with MNsure |
## Records to keep
Keep returns, worksheets, W-2s, 1099s, and receipts until the statute of limitations ends; keep original receipts for the Child and Dependent Care Credit, K-12 credit or subtraction, Student Loan Credit, and itemized deductions.
## Common errors to avoid
Misspelled names or wrong SSNs for dependents; wrong bank routing or account numbers; totals carried to the wrong lines; not filing because you cannot pay (file and pay what you can).
## Prompt
- What do I need to file a Minnesota return?
- What do the checkboxes at the bottom of Form M1 mean?
