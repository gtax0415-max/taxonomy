# Adjusted Gross Income Worksheet for PTE Deductions
## Why it exists
When a partnership or S corporation pays pass-through entity (PTE) tax, the entity deducts it, lowering the owner's federal AGI. Many Minnesota limits are based on AGI, so Minnesota requires owners to ADD BACK their share of the deducted PTE tax when measuring income for those limits. Otherwise an owner could qualify for income-tested benefits only because of the entity-level deduction.
## Worksheet
1. AGI from Form M1, Line 1
2. Your share of PTE taxes deducted by the partnership or S corporation (Minnesota entities report it on line 2 of Schedules KPI and KS)
3. Step 1 + Step 2 = AGI used for the limits listed below
## Where the adjusted AGI is used (2025 Form M1 instructions list)
- Schedule M1CD (Child and Dependent Care Credit) AGI
- Schedule M1ED (K-12 Education Credit) AGI
- Schedule M1CWFC (Child and Working Family Credits) AGI
- Schedule M1SA AGI-based floors and the charitable contribution limits
- Schedule M1CAT (casualty loss 10% floor)
- Schedule M1R (age 65 or disabled subtraction)
- Schedule M1SLC (Student Loan Credit)
- Schedules M1CR and M1RCR (credits for tax paid to other states)
- Schedule M1QPEN (public pension subtraction)
- Standard deduction limitation worksheet, dependent exemption worksheet, 529 credit worksheet, Past Military Service Credit worksheet, and the Social Security subtraction worksheet (where a step uses taxable income, increase it by Step 2)
The 2026 Form M1 instructions will restate this list with 2026 line numbers (several schedules were renumbered for 2026).
## Interaction
This is separate from the Schedule M1MB Line 2 ADDITION, which adds the deducted state income taxes back into Minnesota taxable income.
## Prompt
- My S corporation paid PTE tax. Does that affect my Minnesota credits?
