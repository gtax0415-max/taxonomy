# Minnesota Income (Tax Year 2026)
## Description
Minnesota does not have its own definition of gross income. It STARTS from federal adjusted gross income and then adjusts it:
1. Form M1, Line 1 — federal AGI (Form 1040, Line 11)
2. Form M1, Line 2 — ADDITIONS (Schedule M1M, Line 12 + Schedule M1MB, Line 12): income Minnesota taxes but the federal return excludes or deducts
3. Form M1, Line 3 — Line 1 + Line 2
4. Lines 4–8 — deductions, exemptions, state refund, and SUBTRACTIONS (see minnesota/deductions/)
5. Line 9 — Minnesota taxable income
## Federal conformity for 2026
- Minnesota conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as amended through MAY 1, 2026 (2026 Tax Bill, Laws 2026, Ch. 128), which brings in most of H.R. 1 (OBBBA). Schedule M1NC is discontinued for 2026
- Where Minnesota DECOUPLED from OBBBA, the difference now appears as a regular ADDITION on Schedule M1M or M1MB (for example, employer student loan payments, transportation fringe benefits above Minnesota's limit, Sinai/hazardous-duty pay, rural and agricultural loan interest, certain business meals, subpart F look-through)
- Because federal AGI is the starting point, OBBBA's new BELOW-the-line federal deductions on Schedule 1-A (tips, overtime, car loan interest, the senior deduction) never reach Minnesota — see minnesota/deductions/deductions.md

## Part-year residents and nonresidents
Additions and subtractions are computed on the full-year amounts, then Schedule M1NR allocates income to Minnesota. Nonresident partners who elected composite income tax do not make certain partnership additions.
