# Disallowed Educational Assistance Payments (NEW for 2026)
## Description
Federal law (IRC section 127, made permanent with student loan payments by OBBBA) lets employers pay up to an annual limit of education costs and student loan payments tax-free. Minnesota did NOT adopt the student loan exclusion and FREEZES its educational assistance exclusion at $5,250. The excess is added back.
## What to add back
- ALL employer payments toward the employee's student loan principal or interest (paid to the employee or directly to the lender)
- Employer-paid tuition, fees, books, supplies, and equipment ABOVE $5,250
- Employer-provided instructional courses (with books, supplies, equipment) ABOVE $5,250
## Related
- Critical access dental clinic employees: the first $5,250 is still added back here; only assistance ABOVE $5,250 is subtracted on Line 35 (minnesota/deductions/subtractions/education/critical-access-dental-student-loan.md)
- Student Loan Credit: see minnesota/credits/education/student-loan-credit.md for how employer-paid loan amounts interact
## Required Information
- Employer statement of educational assistance and student loan payments
## Questions
- Did your employer pay any of your student loans in 2026?
- Did your employer pay more than $5,250 for your education?
## Common Errors
- Assuming the federal exclusion carries to Minnesota
- Adding back the first $5,250 of tuition assistance (only the excess, but ALL student loan payments)
## Prompt
- My employer pays my student loans. Is that taxable in Minnesota?
