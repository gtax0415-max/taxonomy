# First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account — Addition and Additional Tax
## Description
Earnings on a designated Minnesota first-time homebuyer savings account are SUBTRACTED each year. If money is later used for nonqualified purposes (or the account is held too long), the earnings come back as an ADDITION plus a 10% additional tax.
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1HOME final draft)
ANNUAL CONTRIBUTION CAP: $14,000 ($28,000 MFJ)
LIFETIME CONTRIBUTION CAP: $50,000 ($100,000 MFJ)
ADDITION: nonqualified withdrawals + transfers to another first-time homebuyer account + balance remaining after 10 YEARS that exceeds total contributions, minus total contributions (Schedule M1HOME line 5 − line 3)
ADDITIONAL TAX: 10% of the addition
## Qualified withdrawals (no addition)
Down payment, closing costs, construction cost, or construction financing for a single-family residence in Minnesota (including a manufactured home, trailer, mobile home, condo, townhome, or cooperative).
## Not treated as nonqualified
Withdrawals because of the account holder's or beneficiary's death or disability; disbursements under federal bankruptcy law or Minnesota Statutes chapters 550 or 551.
## Required Information
- One Schedule M1HOME per account: bank, account number, dates, balances, beneficiary, every withdrawal and its purpose
## Questions
- Did you withdraw from a first-time homebuyer account for anything other than buying or building a Minnesota home?
- Has the account been open more than 10 years?
## Common Errors
- Forgetting the 10% additional tax on Form M1, Line 14a
- Counting a transfer to another first-time homebuyer account as qualified
## Prompt
- I took money out of my Minnesota first-time homebuyer account for something else.
