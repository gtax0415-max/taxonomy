# Interest from Bonds of Other States (State or Local Government Bond Interest)
## Description
Municipal bond interest is federally tax-exempt, but Minnesota taxes interest from bonds issued by OTHER states and their local governments. Interest from Minnesota and Minnesota local-government bonds stays exempt.
## How it works
Add back the interest included (or that should have been included) on Form 1040, Line 2a from bonds issued by:
- A state other than Minnesota
- A county, city, school district, or other local government in another state
## Not added
- Minnesota state and local bonds
- U.S. Treasury and other federal obligations (these are taxable federally and are SUBTRACTED for Minnesota — see minnesota/deductions/subtractions/other/net-interest-from-us-bonds.md)
- Bonds of U.S. territories when federal law bars state taxation (verify by issuer)
## Related
- Mutual fund exempt-interest dividends are Line 2, not Line 1 (mutual-fund-dividends.md)
- Expenses connected with exempt interest may need adjusting (expenses-for-nontaxed-income.md)
## Required Information
- Tax-exempt interest by issuing state (brokerage year-end detail)
## Questions
- Did you receive tax-exempt municipal bond interest? Which states issued the bonds?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Minnesota bond interest
- Adding back the whole Line 2a without splitting by state
- Putting mutual fund exempt-interest dividends on Line 1
## Prompt
- I have muni bonds from California. Does Minnesota tax them?
- My 1099-INT shows tax-exempt interest.
