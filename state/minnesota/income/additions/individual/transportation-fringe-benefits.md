# Qualified Transportation Fringe Benefits Above Minnesota's Limit (NEW for 2026)
## Description
Minnesota did not adopt OBBBA's change to the inflation indexing of the transportation fringe exclusion, so the Minnesota monthly limit is lower than the federal one. The difference is added back.
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1M instructions)
| Benefit | Federal monthly exclusion | Minnesota monthly exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Qualified parking | $340 | $335 |
| Commuter highway vehicle and transit pass | $340 | $335 |
MAXIMUM ADDITION: $60 per year for parking and $60 per year for transit/vanpool (up to $120 if both)
JOINT RETURNS: compute separately for each spouse and add
## Required Information
- Monthly parking and transit benefits received
## Questions
- Did your employer provide tax-free parking or transit benefits above $335 a month?
## Common Errors
- Adding back more than $60 per benefit type
- Combining spouses' benefits into one computation
## Prompt
- My employer pays for my parking. Does Minnesota tax any of it?
