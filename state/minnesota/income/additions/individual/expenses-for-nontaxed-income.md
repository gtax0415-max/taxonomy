# Expenses for Nontaxed Income
## Description
If an expense was deducted federally but relates to income Minnesota does NOT tax, it is added back so the taxpayer does not get a double benefit.
## Examples
- Expenses connected with income subtracted on Schedule M1M Lines 19–24 (Railroad Retirement Board benefits, reciprocity income, reservation income, military pay)
- Business expenses tied to exempt reservation income
## Exception
Expenses connected with U.S. bond interest or U.S. bond fund dividends are NOT added here; they reduce the U.S. bond subtraction instead (Schedule M1M, Line 16).
## Required Information
- Federally deducted expenses and the Minnesota-exempt income they relate to
## Questions
- Are you subtracting military, reservation, reciprocity, or railroad income, and did you deduct related expenses federally?
## Common Errors
- Adding back U.S.-bond-related expenses here (they belong on Line 16)
## Prompt
- I deducted expenses for income Minnesota does not tax.
