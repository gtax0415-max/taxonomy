# Phase-Out of Itemized Deductions (listed by Revenue as an addition)
## Description
Revenue's "Additions to Income" page still lists this item from the era when Minnesota started from federal TAXABLE income and added back part of federal itemized deductions. Since tax year 2019 Minnesota starts from federal AGI, uses its own Schedule M1SA, and applies the phase-out DIRECTLY as a reduction on Schedule M1SA. There is NO Schedule M1M addition line for it in 2026.
## 2026 figures
PHASE-OUT BEGINS: AGI over $244,400 ($122,200 married filing separately)
SECOND TIER: AGI over $337,800 ($168,900 MFS)
80% CAP: at AGI over $1,107,750 the reduction is 80% of the reducible deductions
