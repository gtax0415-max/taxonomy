# Armed Services Pay for the Sinai Peninsula and Other Areas (NEW for 2026)
## Description
OBBBA extended the federal combat-zone-style exclusion to service in certain hazardous duty areas. Minnesota did NOT adopt that provision, so the excluded pay is added back.
## Areas covered by the addition
- Sinai Peninsula of Egypt
- Kenya
- Mali
- Burkina Faso
- Chad
## Interaction with the military pay subtraction
The active-duty military pay subtraction (Schedule M1M, Line 22; nonresidents Line 24) applies only "to the extent the income is federally taxable." This pay is federally EXCLUDED, so on the face of the 2026 schedule it is not offset by Line 22 — the addition makes it taxable to Minnesota. Confirm against the final instructions before relying on this for a Minnesota resident. See minnesota/deductions/subtractions/military/active-duty-military-pay.md.
## Required Information
- Amount of federally excluded pay for service in the listed areas
## Questions
- Did you serve in the Sinai Peninsula, Kenya, Mali, Burkina Faso, or Chad in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Treating these areas like a Minnesota combat zone exclusion
## Prompt
- I was deployed to the Sinai. Does Minnesota tax that pay?
