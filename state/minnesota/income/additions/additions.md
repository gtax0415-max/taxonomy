# Additions to Income (Minnesota, Tax Year 2026)
## Description
Additions INCREASE Minnesota income. They capture income excluded federally, deductions Minnesota does not allow, and federal provisions Minnesota decoupled from.

## Questions

- Did your employer pay your student loans, or tuition over $5,250?
- Did you receive transit or parking benefits from your employer?
- Do you own business property that took bonus depreciation, or a share of a pass-through entity?
