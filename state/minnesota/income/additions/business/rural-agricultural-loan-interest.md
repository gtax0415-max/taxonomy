# Interest on Loans Secured by Rural or Agricultural Real Property (NEW for 2026)
## Description
OBBBA (IRC section 139L) lets qualified lenders exclude 25% of interest received on loans secured by rural or agricultural real property. Minnesota did NOT adopt it, so the excluded interest is added back. Effective for taxable years ending after July 4, 2025.
## Who is affected
Mainly banks, insurers, and similar lenders, and individuals who receive the exclusion through a pass-through (Schedule KPI/KS line 8).
## Prompt
- Our bank excluded interest on farm loans federally.
