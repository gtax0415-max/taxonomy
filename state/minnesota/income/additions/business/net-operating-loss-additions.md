# NOL Carryover Adjustment — WHBA of 2009 (Addition)
## Description
Minnesota did not adopt the Worker, Homeownership, and Business Assistance Act of 2009, which let 2008 or 2009 NOLs be carried back 3, 4, or 5 years. Any federal carryforward of those losses used on the 2026 federal return is added back. The Minnesota-only carryforward is subtracted instead (Schedule M1MB, Line 15).
## Notes
Federal carryforwards of 2008–2009 losses run 20 years (through 2028–2029), so a few returns still have this adjustment in 2026.
## Prompt
- I still have a 2009 NOL carryforward.
