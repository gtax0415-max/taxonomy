# Accelerated Recognition of Installment Sale Gains (Schedule M1AR)
## Description
When a Minnesota resident who sold a partnership or S corporation interest (or certain property) on the installment method becomes a NONRESIDENT, Minnesota can require the remaining deferred gain to be recognized ("accelerated") in the year of the move, so Minnesota does not lose the right to tax it.
## How it works
- Complete Schedule M1AR; the accelerated gain is added on Schedule M1MB, Line 6
- In later years, when the gain is recognized federally, it is SUBTRACTED (Schedule M1MB, Line 17) so it is not taxed twice
- Nonresident owners may elect composite income tax in a year with an accelerated installment sale (2026 Tax Bill, tax years after 2025); composite tax cannot be elected if the gain is elected to be deferred
## Questions
- Did you sell a business interest on installments and then leave Minnesota?
## Common Errors
- Missing the later-year subtraction
## Prompt
- I sold my share of an S corporation on installments and moved out of Minnesota.
