# Section 179 Expensing (Addition and Subtraction)
## Description
Revenue still lists Section 179 under both Additions and Subtractions, but there is NO 2026 adjustment and NO line for it on the 2026 Schedule M1MB.
## History
- Tax years 2006–2019: Minnesota added back 80% of the federal Section 179 deduction above the amount allowed in 2003, then subtracted 20% in each of the next five years
- Tax year 2020 onward: full conformity to federal Section 179; no addition required
- The last five-year subtractions came from 2019 additions and ended with tax year 2024. Disposing of an asset early does not accelerate the remaining subtractions
## For 2026
Federal Section 179 (OBBBA set base amounts of $2.5 million with a $4 million phase-out threshold, indexed for inflation after 2025 — use the 2026 indexed amounts from the federal taxonomy) flows into Minnesota through AGI with no adjustment.
