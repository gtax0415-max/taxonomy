# Certain Business Meals and Entertainment (NEW for 2026)
## Description
Minnesota did not adopt OBBBA provisions that allow larger deductions for certain meals and entertainment. Effective for taxable years beginning after December 31, 2025.
## Add back
1. Meals deducted at 100% under IRC 274(n)(2)(C) — food or beverages on commercial vessels, oil and gas platforms, and fishing vessels (and related processing facilities): add back the portion above the normal 50% limit
2. Expenses deducted under IRC 274(e)(8) for entertainment-related goods or services sold to customers in a bona fide transaction for full consideration (not those that are part of the taxpayer's own trade or business). If the entertainment includes meals, add back only 50% of the meals
## Prompt
- We deducted 100% of crew meals on our fishing boat.
