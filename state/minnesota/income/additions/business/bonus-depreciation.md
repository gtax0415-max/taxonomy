# Bonus Depreciation — 80% Addition and 20% Subtraction
## Description
Minnesota does not allow federal bonus depreciation in the year claimed. It adds back 80% and gives it back as a 20% subtraction in each of the next five years. Revenue lists this under both Additions and Subtractions; this file covers both.
## 2026 addition (Schedule M1MB Worksheet for Line 1)
1. Federal bonus depreciation (Form 4562 lines 14 + 25)
2. MINUS bonus depreciation on QUALIFIED PRODUCTION PROPERTY under IRC 168(n) — new for 2026, NOT added back
3. PLUS bonus depreciation passed through as a partner or shareholder (KPI/KS line 4)
4. × 80%
5. PLUS the 80% addition passed through as a trust beneficiary (KF line 5)
If bonus depreciation created a loss that cannot be used in 2026 (passive loss, excess business loss, basis limit), the addition can be reduced; the bonus is added back later when the suspended loss is allowed (Line 4).
## 2026 subtraction (Schedule M1MB Worksheet for Line 13)
20% of each prior addition from 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 (each reduced by any NOL generated that year), plus any subtraction passed through on Schedule KF line 18.
## Federal context for 2026
OBBBA restored 100% bonus depreciation for property acquired after January 19, 2025 and created 100% expensing for qualified production property. Minnesota conforms on the production property expensing but keeps the 80/20 rule for other bonus depreciation.
## Suspended losses (Line 4)
If a federal suspended loss from 2001–2005 or 2008–2025 that was generated by bonus depreciation is used in 2026, and 80% was not added back originally, add 80% of the bonus portion now.
## Disposition and amendments
Amending a federal return to recapture bonus depreciation requires amending the affected Minnesota returns (addition and later subtractions).
## Required Information
- Form 4562; KPI/KS/KF bonus amounts; prior five years of M1MB bonus additions and any NOLs
## Questions
- Did you or a pass-through you own take bonus depreciation in 2026 or 2021–2025?
- Was any of it qualified production property?
## Common Errors
- Adding back qualified production property bonus
- Forgetting the 20% subtractions from prior-year additions
- Ignoring KF pass-through amounts
