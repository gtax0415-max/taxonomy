# Federal Adjustments for Partnerships (BBA Partnership Audits)
## Description
Under the federal centralized partnership audit regime (Bipartisan Budget Act of 2015), IRS audit adjustments or administrative adjustment requests can change partners' income for a prior "reviewed year." Minnesota requires those changes to be reported. Revenue lists this under Additions, but it is handled by amended or federal-adjustments reporting, not a 2026 Schedule M1M line.
## How it works
- PARTNERSHIP PAYS ELECTION: the partnership files Schedule M3BBA (Partnership Audit Report) and pays at the entity level. Not available for adjustments from an administrative adjustment request
- OTHERWISE: the partnership AND each partner file amended Minnesota returns (Form M1X for individuals) for the applicable years reporting their share of the federal adjustments
- Federal changes must be reported within 180 days; failing to report carries a 10% penalty and extends the audit period
- Nonresident partners who elected composite income tax do not make the related addition
## Prompt
- My partnership went through an IRS audit and pushed out adjustments.
