# State Income Taxes Passed Through from a Pass-Through Entity
## Description
When a partnership, S corporation, or trust deducts state income taxes (including Minnesota or another state's elective pass-through entity tax) at the entity level, that deduction lowers the owner's federal AGI. Minnesota adds it back so state income tax is never deductible against Minnesota income.
## How it works
Add the state income taxes deducted in arriving at AGI, generally the total of line 2 on Schedules KPI, KS, and KF.
## Interaction
- Minnesota PTE tax paid by the entity comes back to the owner as a refundable credit on Schedule M1REF, Line 10
- Use the Adjusted Gross Income Worksheet for PTE Deductions (Form M1 instructions) for AGI-based limits on other schedules
- The Minnesota PTE tax is extended through tax year 2027
## Questions
- Does any partnership or S corporation you own pay state income tax or elect PTE tax?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the PTE credit without this addition
- Missing PTE taxes paid to OTHER states by a multistate entity
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows state taxes paid by the partnership.
