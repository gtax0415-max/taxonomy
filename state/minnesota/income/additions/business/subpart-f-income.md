# Certain Subpart F Income — Look-Through Rule Disregarded (NEW for 2026)
## Description
Minnesota does not adopt the federal "look-through" rule for related controlled foreign corporations (IRC 954(c)(6)), which OBBBA made permanent. For Minnesota, dividends, interest, rents, and royalties between related CFCs can be subpart F income. The addition is offset by any amount that would then come out of net CFC tested income (NCTI); neither can go below zero.
## Computation (Worksheet for Line 9)
1. Related-CFC dividends, interest, rents, and royalties (Form 5471 Schedule G line 14 worksheet item 12)
2. Net CFC tested income before the Minnesota QBAI deduction
3. Lesser of 1 or 2
4. Line 1 − Line 3 = addition
Attach a pro-forma federal Schedule K-2 Part VI reflecting the Minnesota look-through adjustment.
## Related subtraction
Minnesota allows a deduction of 10% of qualified business asset investment (QBAI) against NCTI (Schedule M1MB, Line 22), computed under the IRC as of May 1, 2023 — see minnesota/deductions/subtractions/business/qbai-deduction.md.
## Prompt
- I am a U.S. shareholder of controlled foreign corporations.
