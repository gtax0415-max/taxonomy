# Foreign-Derived Intangible Income (FDII) Deduction
## Description
Minnesota does not allow the IRC section 250 deduction for foreign-derived intangible income (renamed foreign-derived deduction eligible income under OBBBA). Any FDII deduction taken in arriving at the individual's federal income is added back.
## Notes
Individuals rarely claim section 250 directly; it usually arrives through a pass-through or an individual's section 962 election.
## Prompt
- I have a section 250 deduction from a K-1.
