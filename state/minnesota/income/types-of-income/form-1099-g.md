# Form 1099-G
## Two kinds of Form 1099-G a Minnesotan may get
1. FROM REVENUE — the record of a Minnesota income tax REFUND (for 2026 federal returns: refunds received in 2026, usually from 2025 Form M1)
2. FROM THE MINNESOTA DEPARTMENT OF EMPLOYMENT AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT (DEED) — unemployment compensation (fully taxable federally and in Minnesota; reported on Form M1, Line C)
## Revenue's refund 1099-G
- Sent by January 31 of the year after the refund, if the taxpayer was entitled to a Minnesota income tax refund
- Mailed in some cases; otherwise look it up in the 1099-G Refund System or call 651-296-4444 or 1-800-657-3676
- Informational only; keep it with records
## What amount it shows
Generally the PART of the refund that came from withholding, estimated payments, and the pass-through entity tax credit:
- If withholding + estimated payments + PTE credit ≥ the refund → 1099-G shows the refund
- If less → 1099-G shows withholding + estimated payments + PTE credit − tax; the rest of the refund came from refundable credits (for example, the Renter's Credit or Child Tax Credit) and is not reported as a state tax refund
- Revenue may issue a federal Form 1099-MISC instead for refunds from the Historic Structure Rehabilitation, Enterprise Zone, or Angel Investment credits
## Federal treatment of the state refund
Taxable federally only to the extent the prior-year state income tax deduction on federal Schedule A produced a tax benefit (federal Schedule 1, line 1). Taxpayers who took the federal standard deduction usually owe nothing on it.
## Minnesota treatment
Any state income tax refund included in federal AGI is SUBTRACTED on Form M1, Line 6 (minnesota/deductions/subtractions/other/state-income-tax-refund.md), because Minnesota never allowed a deduction for state income tax.
## Revenue Recapture
A refund applied to a debt (Revenue Recapture) is still a refund for 1099-G purposes; see Revenue's Revenue Recapture page for details.
## Questions
- Did you itemize on your 2025 federal return and deduct state income tax?
- Did you receive unemployment in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Reporting the full Minnesota refund as federal income when the standard deduction was taken
- Forgetting the Form M1, Line 6 subtraction
