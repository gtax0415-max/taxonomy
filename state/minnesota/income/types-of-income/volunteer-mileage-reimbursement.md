# Volunteer Mileage Reimbursement (Income Treatment)
## Federal treatment
Reimbursements from a charity for volunteer driving are tax-free federally only up to the 14-cents-per-mile charitable rate. Any excess is federally taxable income (often reported on Form 1099-MISC).
## Minnesota treatment for 2026
Minnesota lets volunteers SUBTRACT the federally taxable excess, up to 72.5 cents per mile for January 1 to June 30, 2026 and 76 cents per mile for July 1 to December 31, 2026 (Schedule M1M, Line 26). Amounts above those caps stay taxable. Full worksheet: minnesota/deductions/subtractions/other/volunteer-mileage-reimbursement.md.
## Unreimbursed volunteer driving
Itemizers can deduct 14 cents per mile (or actual gas and oil) plus parking and tolls as a charitable contribution on Schedule M1SA.
