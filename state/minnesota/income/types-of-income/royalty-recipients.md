# Royalty Recipients (Mining and Exploration Royalties)
## Withholding
Payers of mining and exploration royalties for the use of Minnesota lands must withhold Minnesota income tax at 6.25%. No withholding on royalties paid to partnerships, S corporations, C corporations, simple trusts that distribute all royalty income (unless they elect withholding), or the State of Minnesota on state-managed mineral lands.
## Claiming the withholding
Report the Minnesota tax withheld on Schedule M1W with Form M1. Do not send the federal Form 1099. If a payer withheld Minnesota tax in error, file Form M1 to get it refunded.
## Nonresidents
Royalties from Minnesota property are Minnesota-source income. A nonresident must file Form M1 with Schedule M1NR if Minnesota gross income (royalties plus other Minnesota-source income) is $15,300 or more for 2026.
## Exemption from withholding
A recipient who owed no Minnesota income tax last year and expects to owe none this year can give the payer Form W-4MN to claim exemption.
## Other royalties
Royalties from other Minnesota property (oil, gas, timber, patents used in a Minnesota business) are Minnesota income for residents and Minnesota-source for nonresidents when the property is located or used in Minnesota.
## Questions
- Do you receive mineral royalties from Minnesota land, and was Minnesota tax withheld?
- Are you a Minnesota resident?
## Common Errors
- Not filing to recover withholding when total Minnesota income is below the filing threshold
## Prompt
- I get iron ore royalties from Minnesota land but live in Arizona.
