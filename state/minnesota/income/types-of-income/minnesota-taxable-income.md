# Minnesota Taxable Income
## Definition
Minnesota taxable income (Form M1, Line 9) = federal AGI + additions − (standard or itemized deduction + dependent exemptions + state income tax refund + subtractions). If zero or less, leave blank.

## Key differences from federal taxable income
- Starts at federal AGI, so federal Schedule 1-A deductions and the federal standard deduction never apply
- Minnesota's own standard deduction ($15,300 / $30,600 / $23,000) and dependent exemption
- Additions for other states' bond interest, 529 K-12 use, bonus depreciation, and 2026 decoupling items
- Subtractions for Social Security, U.S. bond interest, military pay, and more
## Part-year residents and nonresidents
Line 9 is computed on all income; Schedule M1NR then prorates the tax by the Minnesota share of income.
## Negative amounts
Enter negative federal taxable income (Line D) and negative Lines 1, 3, or 13b as negative numbers.
