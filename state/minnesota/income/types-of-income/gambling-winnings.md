# Gambling Winnings
## Taxability
All gambling winnings — including the Minnesota State Lottery, other lotteries, casinos, sports betting, pull-tabs, raffles, and internet gambling — are taxable federally and in Minnesota, even without a Form W-2G.
- RESIDENTS: all winnings, from anywhere in the world
- NONRESIDENTS: winnings from gambling IN Minnesota (Minnesota casinos including tribal casinos, Minnesota lottery tickets bought in Minnesota, lawful/charitable gambling and pari-mutuel wagering in Minnesota). File Form M1 with Schedule M1NR if Minnesota gross income is $15,300 or more
- NOT covered by reciprocity with Michigan or North Dakota
## Losses
- Deductible only as a Minnesota ITEMIZED deduction (Schedule M1SA, Line 26), not more than winnings
- Non-itemizers get no loss deduction
- NOT deductible for Minnesota alternative minimum tax — big winners and losers can owe Minnesota AMT
- Federal difference for 2026: OBBBA limits federal gambling losses to 90%; the 2026 Schedule M1SA allows losses up to winnings (minnesota/deductions/itemized/other-miscellaneous-deductions.md)
## Withholding
Claim Minnesota tax withheld on Form W-2G on Schedule M1W.
## Nonresidents
A nonresident who pays Minnesota tax on Minnesota winnings may get a credit from the home state.
## Questions
- What did you win, and where? Do you have W-2Gs?
- Do you have records of losses, and will you itemize?
## Common Errors
- Netting losses against winnings instead of reporting gross winnings
- Taking losses without itemizing
- Deducting losses for Minnesota AMT
## Prompt
- I won at a Minnesota casino. Do I owe Minnesota tax?
