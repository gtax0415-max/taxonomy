# Individual Use Tax (Form UT1)
## Description
Use tax is the counterpart of sales tax. It is owed on taxable items bought for personal use in Minnesota when Minnesota sales tax was not charged. It is NOT reported on Form M1; the Form M1 instructions remind filers to file Form UT1 separately.
## 2026 figures
EXEMPTION: individuals may buy up to $770 of taxable goods per calendar year without use tax (not indexed). Above $770, use tax is owed on the ENTIRE amount
STATE RATE: 6.875% (same as the state sales tax)
LOCAL USE TAX: also owed where a local sales and use tax applies (Sales Tax Fact Sheet 164)
DUE: April 15, 2027 for 2026 purchases
## When you may owe
- Online or mail-order purchases where the seller did not collect Minnesota sales tax
- Purchases in another state or country that did not collect Minnesota tax
- Purchases where another state's sales tax was collected at a lower rate — owe the difference
## Credit for tax paid elsewhere
Sales tax paid to another state is credited, but only up to the Minnesota tax on the item.
## Other use taxes on Form UT1
- Cannabis use tax (taxable cannabis products received in Minnesota other than from a retailer that paid the tax)
- Liquor gross receipts use tax
Tobacco (Form CT303) and alcoholic beverage (Form LB58) use taxes are filed separately.
## How to file
Online through Revenue's Individual Use Tax Return Online Filing System, or on paper Form UT1. Businesses use the business use tax rules instead.
## Prompt
- I bought furniture online and wasn't charged sales tax.
