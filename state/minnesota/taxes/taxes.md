## Not on Form M1
- use-tax.md — Individual Use Tax (Form UT1), filed separately
Minnesota has no state equivalent of the federal self-employment tax, Additional Medicare Tax, or household employment tax on the income tax return. Minnesota employers of household workers report Minnesota withholding and unemployment insurance separately.
