# Minnesota Alternative Minimum Tax (Schedule M1MT)
## Who must check
- Anyone who owed federal AMT must complete Schedule M1MT
- Minnesota AMT can apply even with no federal AMT — common with large itemized deductions Minnesota AMT disallows, out-of-state municipal bond interest, or large gambling losses
- First complete Part I of federal Form 6251 for Minnesota purposes
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1MT near-final draft, 8/3/2026)
| | MFJ / QSS | MFS | Single / HOH |
|---|---|---|---|
| Exemption (indexed) | $97,470 | $48,740 | $73,100 |
| Exemption phase-out begins | $150,000 | $75,000 | $112,500 |
PHASE-OUT RATE: exemption reduced by 25% of Minnesota AMTI over the phase-out threshold
AMT RATE: 6.75%
MINNESOTA AMT = tentative minimum tax (6.75% × (AMTI − exemption)) minus regular tax (Form M1, Line 10), if positive
## Computing Minnesota AMTI (Schedule M1MT lines 1–20)
START: federal AGI + federal Form 6251 adjustments and preferences
ADD: other additions from Schedules M1M and M1MB; non-Minnesota state and municipal bond interest; intangible drilling costs; depletion
SUBTRACT ONLY THESE DEDUCTIONS:
- Medical and dental (Schedule M1SA line 4)
- Investment interest (M1SA line 13)
- Charitable contributions (M1SA line 21)
- Casualty and theft losses (M1SA line 22)
- Impairment-related work expenses of a person with a disability (part of M1SA line 26)
- Unreimbursed employee business expenses (M1SA line 25)
- State income tax refund (federal Schedule 1 line 1)
- Bonus depreciation subtraction (M1MB line 13)
- Net U.S. bond interest (Schedule M1M — see note)
- Other Minnesota subtractions per the instructions
NOT DEDUCTIBLE for Minnesota AMT: the standard deduction, dependent exemptions, real estate and other taxes, gambling losses, and other M1SA items not listed above.
## 2026 form notes
- Line 3 (federal nonconformity adjustments from the former Schedule M1NC) is intentionally blank for 2026 because Schedule M1NC is discontinued
- Line 17 on the draft says U.S. bond interest comes "from line 14 of Schedule M1M." On the 2026 Schedule M1M the U.S. bond subtraction is Line 16 (Line 14 is Social Security). Likely an un-updated reference; confirm on the final form
## Credit in later years
AMT caused by timing items (for example, depreciation) can generate a Minnesota minimum tax credit in later years (Schedule M1MTC).
## Required Information
- Federal Form 6251 Part I; Schedules M1SA, M1M, M1MB; non-Minnesota municipal bond interest
## Questions
- Did you owe federal AMT?
- Do you have large itemized deductions for taxes, large gambling losses, or out-of-state municipal bond interest?
## Common Errors
- Skipping Schedule M1MT because there was no federal AMT
- Deducting gambling losses or property taxes for Minnesota AMT
- Forgetting to attach Form 6251
## Prompt
- Do I owe Minnesota alternative minimum tax?
