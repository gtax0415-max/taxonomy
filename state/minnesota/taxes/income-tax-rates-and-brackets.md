# Minnesota Income Tax Rates and Brackets
## 2026 — MN Department of Revenue published thresholds
(TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts, 12/1/2025; rates unchanged by the 2026 Tax Bill)
Four brackets: 5.35%, 6.80%, 7.85%, 9.85%. Minnesota taxes capital gains and qualified dividends at these same ordinary rates; there is no preferential rate.

## 2025 — for comparison (from the 2025 Form M1 instructions)
Single brackets began at $32,570 / $106,990 / $198,630; MFJ at $47,620 / $189,180 / $330,410.
## Tax table vs. rate schedules
Below the tax table limit, Form M1 Line 10 comes from the tax table in $100 bands (midpoint method). Above it, use the rate schedules. The 2025 table ran to $86,800; check the 2026 instructions for the 2026 cutoff.
## Part-year residents and nonresidents
Tax is computed on ALL income as if a full-year resident, then multiplied by the Minnesota share from Schedule M1NR (Form M1, Line 13).
## Marriage penalty relief
Married couples where both spouses earn may get the Marriage Credit (minnesota/credits/family-dependent/marriage-credit.md).
## Future rate note
The 2026 Tax Bill created a "fraud tax" whose revenue goes into a tax relief account to reduce the first-tier rate in a future year. It does not change the 2026 rates.
