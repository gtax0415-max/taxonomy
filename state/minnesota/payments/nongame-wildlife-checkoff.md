# Nongame Wildlife Checkoff (Form M1, Line 18)
## Description
A voluntary donation to the Minnesota Department of Natural Resources Nongame Wildlife Fund, which supports species such as bald eagles, loons, and other non-hunted wildlife.
## How it works
- Enter any whole-dollar amount on Form M1, Line 18
- The donation REDUCES the refund or INCREASES the amount owed; it is not a tax
- Direct donations: dnr.state.mn.us/nongame/donate, or a check to DNR Nongame Wildlife Fund, 500 Lafayette Road, Box 25, St. Paul, MN 55155
## Related: State Elections Campaign Fund (top of Form M1)
- Each spouse may designate $5 to a political party or the general campaign fund by entering the party code
- This does NOT reduce the refund or increase tax
- 2026 codes: Republican 11; Democratic/Farmer-Labor 12; Grassroots/Legalize Cannabis 14; Libertarian 16; Legal Marijuana Now 17; Forward Independence 18 (renamed from Independence-Alliance); General Campaign Fund 99
