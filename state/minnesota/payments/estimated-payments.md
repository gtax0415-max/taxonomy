# Estimated Tax Payments
## Form M1, Line 21 — only these three amounts
1. 2026 Minnesota estimated tax payments made in 2026 and 2027
2. The part of the 2025 refund applied to 2026 estimated tax on the 2025 Form M1 (Line 30)
3. Any Minnesota income tax payment made by the regular due date when filing after the due date (extension payment)
## 2026 installment due dates (calendar year)
April 15, 2026; June 15, 2026; September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027. Fiscal-year filers: 15th day of the 4th, 6th, and 9th months of the fiscal year and the 1st month of the next fiscal year.
## Who should pay 2027 estimated tax
- Expect to owe $500 or more after withholding and credits
- No Minnesota withholding on earnings
- Income from pensions, commissions, dividends, or other sources without withholding
- Owners subject to the Minnesota net investment income tax (it is covered by estimated tax rules)
## Safe harbors (2026 Schedule M15)
Pay, through withholding and timely estimates, the LESSER of:
- 90% of the current year's tax (66.7% for farmers and commercial fishermen), or
- 100% of the prior year's tax (110% if prior-year federal AGI was over $150,000). Nonresidents and part-year residents must have had at least $1 of prior-year Minnesota tax liability to use this
Minnesota also counts REFUNDABLE CREDITS as payments when testing underpayment (treated as received evenly).
## Applying a refund (Lines 29–30)
Line 29 = refund to receive; Line 30 = amount applied to 2027 estimated tax; together they equal Line 24. The choice is irrevocable. Not available on an amended return (Form M1X).
## How to pay
Electronically through e-Services (bank account, free), by card (vendor fee), or by check with a voucher from the Payment Voucher System (multiple vouchers can be printed).
