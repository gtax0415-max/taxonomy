# Refund or Amount Owed (Form M1 Lines 23–30)
## Computation
- Line 19 = tax after nonrefundable credits (Line 17) + Nongame Wildlife donation (Line 18)
- Line 23 = withholding (20) + estimated and extension payments (21) + refundable credits (22)
- Line 24 REFUND = Line 23 − Line 19 − Line 27 penalty (and − Line 28 if entered)
- Line 26 AMOUNT YOU OWE = Line 19 − Line 23 + Line 27 (and + Line 28)
## Refund options
- Direct deposit (Line 25): checking or savings; 9-digit routing number; account number up to 17 characters; no hyphens or spaces; NO accounts associated with a foreign bank. Incorrect or rejected numbers → paper check. Revenue may also issue a check if it adjusted the return or recaptured part of the refund
- Paper check
- Apply all or part to 2027 estimated tax (Lines 29–30; irrevocable)
## Refund offsets (Revenue Recapture)
Revenue deducts amounts owed for Minnesota or federal debts, criminal fines, and debts to federal, state, or county agencies, district courts, qualifying hospitals, or public libraries, identified by SSN. Participants in the Senior Citizens' Property Tax Deferral Program have refunds applied to the deferred property tax. Any balance is refunded.
## Refund claim deadline
Generally 3½ years from the original due date; under the 2026 Tax Bill, a claim is also timely within 2 years of paying the tax, penalty, or interest (filing/amend-a-return.md).
## Renter's Credit and other refundable credits
These are part of the income tax refund; they are offset against tax owed or other debts, not paid separately.
## Amount owed
Pay by April 15, 2027 (payments/paying-your-tax.md). If paying with a paper return, include a statement showing any penalty and interest computation. If Line 28 is left blank, Revenue computes penalty and interest and sends a bill.
