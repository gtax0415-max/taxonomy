# Paying Your Individual Income Tax
## Due dates for tax year 2026
| What | Due |
|---|---|
| Tax payment | April 15, 2027 |
| Return | April 15, 2027; NO late filing penalty if filed by October 15, 2027 |
| 2026 estimated tax installments (calendar year) | April 15, June 15, and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027 |
| Fiscal-year filers | 15th day of the 4th month after year-end |
A federal extension does NOT extend the time to PAY Minnesota tax. Combat-zone service members get up to 180 days after leaving the zone to file and pay.
## Payment methods
- ELECTRONIC from a bank account: free, through Revenue's e-Services ("Make a Payment"); choose the date; no foreign bank accounts
- CREDIT OR DEBIT CARD: through a third-party vendor that charges a fee
- CHECK OR MONEY ORDER: create a voucher with the Payment Voucher System and mail it SEPARATELY from a paper return; the check is converted to a one-time electronic transfer
- Can't pay in full: file on time, pay what you can, keep paying by voucher until billed, then request a payment agreement (651-556-3003 or 1-800-657-3909)
## Penalties
| Penalty | Rate |
|---|---|
| Late payment | 4% of unpaid tax. Not charged if the unpaid tax is less than 10% of the total tax AND the return is filed by October 15 |
| Extended delinquency | Additional 5% of unpaid tax if paid 181 days or more after filing |
| Late filing | 5% of unpaid tax if the return is filed after October 15 |
| Underpayment of estimated tax | Schedule M15, Form M1 Line 27 |
| Fraudulently claimed refund | 50% of the fraudulent portion (also 50% of unpaid tax if a fraudulent credit reduced tax) |
| Failure to report federal changes within 180 days | 10% of additional tax |
## Interest
Charged on unpaid tax and penalties after April 15, 2027. The rate is set each calendar year; the 2026 rate is 7%. Use the rate Revenue publishes for 2027 for interest accruing in 2027. Revenue will calculate penalty and interest and bill if Line 28 is left blank.
## Estimated tax for 2027
Make 2027 estimated payments if any apply:
- You expect to owe $500 or more after withholding and credits
- No Minnesota tax was withheld from earnings
- Income includes pensions, commissions, dividends, or other income without withholding
Minnesota net investment income tax is subject to the same estimated tax rules. All or part of a 2026 refund can be applied to 2027 estimated tax (Lines 29–30); the choice cannot be changed later.
## Underpayment penalty (Schedule M15)
May apply if Line 19 exceeds Line 23 by $500 or more, or if a required installment was late even when a refund results. Subtract it from the refund or add it to the amount owed.
## Refund offsets
Refunds can be applied to Minnesota or federal debts, criminal fines, and debts to agencies, district courts, qualifying hospitals, or public libraries, and to deferred taxes under the Senior Citizens' Property Tax Deferral Program.

## Common Errors
- Assuming the October 15 filing grace period also extends payment
- Mailing a check without a voucher, or stapled to the return
- Using a foreign bank account for direct deposit or payment
