# Minnesota Income Tax Withheld (Schedule M1W, Form M1 Line 20)
## What to report
All Minnesota income tax withheld in 2026 from:
- Wages (W-2)
- Pensions, IRAs, annuities, nonemployee compensation, royalties, and other 1099 income
- Gambling winnings (W-2G)
- Nonresident withholding passed through on Schedules KPI, KS, or KF
## Rules
- Schedule M1W is REQUIRED; without it Revenue may disallow the withholding
- Do NOT send W-2s, 1099s, or W-2Gs; keep them in case Revenue asks
- Include any KPI, KS, or KF from which withholding is claimed
- Only MINNESOTA withholding — other states' withholding is not a payment toward Minnesota tax
## Withholding certificates
- Form W-4MN: Minnesota withholding allowances or exemption (employees, and royalty recipients claiming exemption)
- Form MWR: Michigan and North Dakota residents claiming reciprocity exemption from Minnesota withholding (file with the employer each year)
- Royalty payers withhold 6.25% on mining and exploration royalties (income/types-of-income/royalty-recipients.md)
## For estimated tax purposes
Withholding is treated as paid evenly through the year unless you show otherwise (payments/underpayment-penalty.md).
## Common Errors
- Including another state's withholding or local tax
- Forgetting withholding shown on a K-schedule
- Not filing at all when income is below the filing requirement but Minnesota tax was withheld
