## Key 2026 differences from the federal return
- STANDARD DEDUCTION: Minnesota's own amounts ($15,300 / $30,600 / $23,000), lower than federal, and reduced at high income
- ITEMIZING: Minnesota allows itemizing even when the federal return takes the standard deduction. No deduction for state income or sales tax; real estate and personal property tax capped at $10,000 ($5,000 MFS); medical floor 10% of AGI; charitable floor 1% of AGI; casualty losses and unreimbursed employee expenses still allowed
- CHARITABLE SUBTRACTION FOR NON-ITEMIZERS: 50% of contributions above $500 (after the 1%-of-AGI floor)
- EXEMPTIONS: $5,300 per dependent, phased out at high income
- SCHEDULE 1-A: federal deductions for tips, overtime, car loan interest, and the senior deduction are taken below federal AGI, so Minnesota does not allow them
