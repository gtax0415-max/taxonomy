# Standard Deduction (Minnesota)
## Files in this folder
- standard-deduction.md — basic and age-65/blind amounts
- dependent-standard-deduction.md — for someone who can be claimed as a dependent
- standard-deduction-limitation.md — high-income reduction (Worksheets A and B)
## Key point
Minnesota sets its OWN standard deduction (indexed annually). It is not the federal amount. A taxpayer may take the Minnesota standard deduction or itemize on Schedule M1SA, whichever is larger, regardless of what they did federally (except the married-filing-separately rule).
