## Married filing separately — special rules
- Must itemize if the spouse itemizes
- Age 65/disabled subtraction only if the spouses lived apart all year
- Many credits are barred or restricted (see minnesota/credits/)
## Notes
- The 2026 Schedule M1SA standard deduction table prints $34,400 for MFJ with three boxes checked. The arithmetic ($30,600 + 3 × $1,600) gives $35,400; treat $34,400 as a likely draft typo and confirm on the final form
- The QSS column for the 529 subtraction follows the "all other filers" rule ($1,500); only married filing jointly gets $3,000
