# Dependent Exemptions
## 2026 — MN Department of Revenue published figures
(TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts)
EXEMPTION PER DEPENDENT: $5,300
PHASE-OUT BEGINS (AGI over): $366,700 MFJ and QSS / $305,600 HOH / $244,500 single / $183,350 MFS
REDUCTION: 2% for each $2,500 (or part of $2,500) of AGI above the threshold; $1,250 for MFS
FULLY ELIMINATED: when AGI exceeds the threshold by more than $122,500 ($61,250 MFS)
## 2025 — for comparison
$5,200 per dependent; thresholds $358,550 / $298,800 / $239,050 / $179,275.
## Worksheet for Line 5
1. Number of dependents on Schedule M1DQC
2. × $5,300
3. If AGI ≤ threshold → Step 2 is the exemption
4. Otherwise excess = AGI − threshold. If excess > $122,500 ($61,250 MFS) → $0
5. Divide excess by $2,500 ($1,250 MFS), ROUND UP to a whole number, × 2%
6. Exemption = Step 2 − (Step 2 × Step 5)
## Who counts
Same dependents as the federal return (qualifying children and qualifying relatives), listed on Schedule M1DQC. The 2026 Schedule M1DQC adds a checkbox (Row 7) for a non-custodial parent claiming a child under a Form 8332 release.
## Not allowed
- If the taxpayer can be claimed as someone else's dependent, leave Line 5 blank
## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN or ITIN, date of birth, relationship
## Questions
- How many dependents do you claim federally?
- Is your AGI above the phase-out threshold?
## Common Errors
- Claiming an exemption for yourself or your spouse
- Not rounding the $2,500 step up
- Using the 2025 $5,200 amount
