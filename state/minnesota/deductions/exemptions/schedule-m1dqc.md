# Schedule M1DQC, Dependents and Qualifying Children
## Purpose
One schedule that lists dependents and qualifying children for:
- Dependent exemptions (Form M1, Line 5)
- Child and Working Family Credits (Schedule M1CWFC)
- K-12 Education Credit (Schedule M1ED) and K-12 Education Subtraction (Schedule M1M)
- Renter's Credit dependent subtraction (Schedule M1RENT)
- Child and Dependent Care Credit (Schedule M1CD)
Use additional schedules for more than three people.
## 2026 rows (near-final draft, 9/16/2026)
| Row | Item |
|---|---|
| 1–5 | First name, last name, SSN/ITIN/ATIN, date of birth, relationship |
| 6 | Claimed as a dependent (same dependents as the federal return) |
| 7 | NEW: non-custodial parent with the exemption released by federal Form 8332 |
| 8 | Months lived with you (more than half the year but less than 7 → enter 7; born or died in 2026 and lived with you more than half the time alive → 12) |
| 9 | Over 17, under 24, and a full-time student |
| 10 | Permanently and totally disabled in any part of 2026 |
| 11 | Qualifying child (under 18) — Child Tax Credit |
| 12 | Qualifying older child — Working Family Credit add-on |
## Qualifying child steps
1. Relationship: son, daughter, stepchild, foster child, brother, sister, stepbrother, stepsister, half-sibling, or a descendant of any of them; lived with you more than half the year (exceptions for temporary absence, birth, death, kidnapping, adoption or placement during the year)
2. ID: SSN, ITIN, or ATIN issued or applied for by the due date. K-12 Education Credit and subtraction require an SSN
3. Disabled? → row 10
4. Under 18 at year-end → row 11, qualifying child
5. Not under 18 and disabled → row 12, qualifying older child
6. Otherwise: under 24, younger than you (or spouse), over 17, and a full-time student (part of 5 calendar months) → rows 9 and 12
7. Otherwise under 19 → row 12; else not a qualifying child for Minnesota credits or subtractions
## Rules
- A dependent or qualifying child may be claimed on only ONE return
- Row 7 (Form 8332) filers cannot check rows 11 or 12; the custodial parent who released the exemption may still treat the child as a qualifying child
- Use the same months as on federal Schedule EIC if one was filed
## Draft inconsistency
The 2026 Schedule M1CWFC (final draft, 10/1/2026) still counts qualifying children from "Row 10" of Schedule M1DQC, but the renumbered 2026 M1DQC puts qualifying children on row 11. Confirm on the final forms.
