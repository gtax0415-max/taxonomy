# Moving Expenses
## Description
Revenue lists moving expenses under "Deductions and Exemptions," but Minnesota has no separate moving expense deduction for 2026. Minnesota follows the federal rule through federal AGI.
## 2026 rule
- Federally, moving expenses are deductible only by members of the Armed Forces on active duty who move because of a military order (permanent change of station). OBBBA made the suspension for everyone else permanent
- That federal deduction is already in federal AGI, so it flows to Form M1, Line 1 with no Minnesota adjustment
- Non-military taxpayers get no moving expense deduction on either return
## History
For tax year 2018 only, Minnesota had not conformed to the federal suspension and allowed moving expenses on Schedule M1MOVE. That schedule no longer exists.
## Related
Reimbursements excluded federally for military moves stay excluded for Minnesota.
