# Federal Adjustments to Income in Minnesota
## Description
Federal above-the-line adjustments (federal Schedule 1, Part II — educator expenses, HSA contributions, self-employment tax deduction, self-employed health insurance, IRA deduction, student loan interest, alimony for pre-2019 divorces, moving expenses for the military, and others) reduce federal AGI. Because Form M1 starts from federal AGI, they carry into Minnesota AUTOMATICALLY. Minnesota has no separate "adjustments" section.
## Where Minnesota then differs
- Minnesota ADDITIONS can reverse a federal exclusion (minnesota/income/additions/)
- Minnesota SUBTRACTIONS can remove income the federal return taxes (subtractions/)
- Some Minnesota computations use AGI adjusted for the PTE-tax deduction (Adjusted Gross Income Worksheet for PTE Deductions, Form M1 instructions)
