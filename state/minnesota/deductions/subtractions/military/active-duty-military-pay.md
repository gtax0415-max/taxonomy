# Federal Active-Duty Military Pay Subtraction
## Description
Minnesota residents serving in the U.S. armed forces (or United Nations armed forces) subtract all federally taxable active-duty pay, wherever earned. Active Guard Reserve (AGR) pay under Title 32 also qualifies.
## Lines
- LINE 22: Minnesota residents
- LINE 24: residents of another state who must file Minnesota (for example, because of a Minnesota spouse's income or other Minnesota income)
## Not covered
- Civilian employees of the military and state military employees
- Military pensions (use Line 27)
- Federally EXCLUDED pay (combat zone or the newly listed hazardous duty areas) — the subtraction applies only to pay that is federally taxable
## Related
- Residency: Minnesotans who enlist remain residents until they establish domicile elsewhere
- Nonresident military spouses may exempt Minnesota wages under the Military Spouses Residency Relief Act rules
- Combat-zone months qualify for the separate $120-per-month credit (Form M99)
## Questions
- Were you on federal active duty in 2026, and were you a Minnesota resident?
## Common Errors
- Using Line 22 for a nonresident (use Line 24)
- Subtracting both on Line 22 and Line 23 for the same pay
## Prompt
- I am active duty and a Minnesota resident stationed in Texas.
