# Minnesota National Guard and Reserve Pay Subtraction
## Qualifying pay (federally taxable)
- Training: annual training and drill weekends
- State active service: natural disaster response, missing-person searches
- Federally funded state active service: airport security duty, Active Duty Operational Support (ADOS), Title 10 and Title 32 Active Guard Reserve
## Rule
Do not include pay already subtracted on Line 22 as federal active-duty pay.

- I drill with the Minnesota National Guard. Is that pay taxable?
