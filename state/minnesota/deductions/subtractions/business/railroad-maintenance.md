# Railroad Maintenance Expenses
## Description
Taxpayers who claimed the federal Railroad Track Maintenance Credit (IRC 45G, Form 8900) had to reduce depreciable basis federally. Minnesota allows a subtraction for the depreciation lost because of that basis reduction, regardless of when the railroad bought the asset.
## Prompt
- We claimed the federal railroad track maintenance credit.
