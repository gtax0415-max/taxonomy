# Delayed Business Interest
## Description
Minnesota did not follow the CARES Act increase in the IRC 163(j) business interest limit for 2019–2020. Business interest disallowed for Minnesota in 2019–2022 is recovered as a 20% subtraction over five years.
## 2026 rule
- If you filed a 2023 return: subtract the same amount as on the 2023 return
- Otherwise: 20% × (2019 M1NC line 8 + 2020, 2021, 2022 M1NC line 16), plus any amount on a 2026 Schedule KF line 24
- The final 20% is taken in tax year 2027
## Prompt
- I had a Minnesota business interest nonconformity adjustment in 2020.
