# Employee Retention Credit Wage Subtraction
## Description
Federally, wages used to compute the Employee Retention Credit (CARES Act section 2301; TCDTR Act sections 206–207; ARPA section 9651) are not deductible. Minnesota allows a subtraction for those wages not deducted in the current year.
## Notes
Use only wages not deducted federally in the CURRENT taxable year because of the ERC; keep the federal ERC computation as support.
## Prompt
- My business received the Employee Retention Credit.
