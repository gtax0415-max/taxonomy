# Income from a Prior-Year Partnership or S Corporation Sale
## Description
If a gain was ACCELERATED for Minnesota in a prior year (Schedule M1AR) and is recognized federally in 2026 under the installment method, subtract it so it is not taxed twice. Also applies when the gain was accelerated through a composite tax election.
## Prompt
- I already paid Minnesota tax on my installment sale gain when I moved.
