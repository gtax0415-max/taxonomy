# Deferred Foreign Income (IRC Section 965)
## Description
Subtract deferred foreign income recognized on the federal return under IRC section 965 (the 2017 transition tax), which Minnesota does not tax. By 2026 this mostly concerns S corporation shareholders who deferred section 965 income until a triggering event.
## Prompt
- I have section 965 income from an S corporation triggering event.
