# Bonus Depreciation Subtraction (20% Recovery)
## 2026 rule
Subtract 20% of the bonus depreciation ADDITION made on each of the 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, and 2025 Minnesota returns (each reduced by any NOL generated in that year), plus any bonus subtraction received in 2026 as a trust or estate beneficiary (Schedule KF line 18).
## Notes
- The schedule continues even if the asset is sold; there is no acceleration
- Amending a federal return to recapture bonus depreciation requires amending the related Minnesota returns
- Full mechanics, including the 2026 addition, are in the additions file linked above
