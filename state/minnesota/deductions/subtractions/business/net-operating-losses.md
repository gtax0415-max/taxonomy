# Net Operating Losses (Minnesota Differences)
## Description
Individual NOLs normally flow through federal AGI. Minnesota needs adjustments only where it did not follow federal NOL law.
## Line 15 — 2008–2009 losses (WHBA)
Minnesota limited 2008–2009 NOL carrybacks to 2 years. Subtract the Minnesota carryforward used in 2026 (and add back the federal carryforward on Schedule M1MB, Line 5).
## Line 21 — delayed NOL (CARES Act nonconformity)
Minnesota did not follow the CARES Act 5-year carryback and 80%-limit suspension for 2018–2020 losses. Those disallowed losses carry forward up to 20 years from the loss year:
1. Minnesota NOL carried into 2026 (not previously subtracted)
2. Federal taxable income (Form 1040, line 15; 0 if negative)
3. Step 2 × 80%
4. Subtraction = lesser of Step 1 and Step 3
5. Remainder carries to 2027
