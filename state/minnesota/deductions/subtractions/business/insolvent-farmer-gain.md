# Gain from the Sale of a Farm — Insolvent Farmer
## Who qualifies (all required)
- Owned and operated the farm
- Debts exceeded the fair market value of assets immediately before the sale
- The gain is included in federal AGI
- Sale proceeds paid off the mortgage, contract for deed, or lien on the property
## Computation
Subtraction = lesser of (debts − excluded debt forgiveness − FMV of assets) or the gain included in AGI.
## Note
The 2026 instructions refer to a sale "in 2025," apparently an un-updated year reference; the subtraction applies to gain included in 2026 federal AGI. Confirm on the final instructions.
## Prompt
- I sold my farm while insolvent to pay off the mortgage.
