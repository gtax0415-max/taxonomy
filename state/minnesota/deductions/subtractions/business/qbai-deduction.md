# QBAI Deduction Against Net CFC Tested Income (NEW for 2026)
## Description
OBBBA replaced GILTI with "net CFC tested income" (NCTI) and removed the deduction for the 10% deemed return on qualified business asset investment (QBAI). Minnesota restores it: subtract 10% of the U.S. shareholder's pro rata share of QBAI, computed under IRC 951A(b)(2)(A) as amended through May 1, 2023. Effective for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025.
## Worksheet for Line 22
1. Federal net CFC tested income
2. Minus the Step 3 amount from the subpart F addition worksheet
3. Remainder (stop if zero)
4. QBAI (2023 Treas. Reg. 1.951A-1(d)(1))
5. Step 4 × 10%
6. Subtraction = lesser of Step 3 and Step 5
## Prompt
- I have net CFC tested income from foreign subsidiaries.
