# Disallowed Section 280E Expenses — Licensed Cannabis or Hemp Businesses
## Description
IRC section 280E denies federal deductions to businesses trafficking in Schedule I or II controlled substances. Minnesota lets businesses LICENSED by the Minnesota Office of Cannabis Management subtract the ordinary business expenses disallowed federally under 280E.
## Notes
- Requires a current Minnesota license
- Cannabis remains federally illegal for medical expense purposes (not deductible on Schedule M1SA)
## Prompt
- I own a licensed Minnesota cannabis dispensary.
