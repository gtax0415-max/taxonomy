# Service Employees International Union (SEIU) Stipend Payment Subtraction
## Rule
Subtract federally taxable stipend payments from the Minnesota Department of Human Services under the collective bargaining agreement between SEIU Healthcare Minnesota & Iowa and the State of Minnesota (payments on or after June 15, 2025), to the extent reported as federal taxable income.
## Related
The same amount is subtracted in computing household income for the Renter's Credit (Schedule M1RENT, Line 8).
## Prompt
- I am a home care worker who received an SEIU stipend.
