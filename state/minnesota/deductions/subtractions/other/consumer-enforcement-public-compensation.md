# Consumer Enforcement Public Compensation Subtraction
## Rule
Subtract compensation received from the consumer protection restitution account (Minnesota Statutes § 8.37) after being harmed by unlawful conduct as determined by a court order, to the extent included in federal AGI.
## Prompt
- I received restitution from the Minnesota Attorney General's consumer fund.
