# Volunteer Mileage Reimbursement Subtraction
## Description
Mileage reimbursements from a charity above the 14-cents-per-mile charitable rate are federally taxable. Minnesota subtracts the taxable excess up to a cap tied to the business mileage rate.
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1M Worksheet for Line 26)
| Period | Charitable rate (not subtracted) | Cap per mile |
|---|---|---|
| January 1 – June 30, 2026 | 14 cents | 72.5 cents |
| July 1 – December 31, 2026 | 14 cents | 76 cents |
Subtraction = lesser of (reimbursement) or (miles × cap) − (miles × 14 cents), computed for each half-year and each organization (one worksheet if all organizations paid the same rate).
## Rule
Only reimbursements included in federal AGI.
## Prompt
- The food shelf reimburses my volunteer driving and gave me a 1099.
