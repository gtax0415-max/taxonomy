# Coerced Debt Subtraction
## Rule
Subtract debt incurred in your name that a COURT discharged and determined to be coerced debt, to the extent the discharge is in federal AGI. Coerced debt results from use of your personal information without consent, force or threats, or economic abuse.
## Records
Keep the court order; Revenue may ask for proof.
## Prompt
- My abuser ran up debt in my name and a court discharged it.
