# First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account Subtraction
## Description
Interest and dividends earned in 2026 on a designated Minnesota first-time homebuyer savings account are subtracted.
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1HOME)
CONTRIBUTIONS COUNTED: up to $14,000 a year ($28,000 MFJ); lifetime $50,000 ($100,000 MFJ)
SUBTRACTION: all 2026 interest and dividends earned on the account
## Account rules
- Register the account and name a qualified beneficiary on Schedule M1HOME (one schedule per account)
- Beneficiary: a Minnesota resident who has not owned a principal residence in the last three years (and whose spouse has no ownership interest); the account holder may be the beneficiary
- Qualified uses: down payment, closing costs, construction, or construction financing for a Minnesota single-family residence (including condo, townhome, co-op, manufactured or mobile home)
- 10-year limit; nonqualified use triggers an addition plus 10% tax
## Prompt
- I opened a Minnesota first-time homebuyer savings account.
