# Reciprocity Income (Michigan and North Dakota Residents)
## Description
Minnesota has income tax reciprocity with Michigan and North Dakota for personal service income (wages, salaries, tips, commissions, fees, bonuses). A resident of those states who had Minnesota tax withheld files Form M1 only to get it back.
## Who uses Line 20 (all required)
- Permanent resident of Michigan or North Dakota who returned home at least once a month
- Only Minnesota income was wages covered by reciprocity
- Minnesota tax was withheld and a refund is wanted
## How to file
1. Enter federal amounts on Form M1 lines A–F and Line 1
2. Skip Form M1 lines 2–6
3. Enter Form M1 Line 1 on Schedule M1M Line 20 (and Form M1 Line 7); check the state box
4. Include Schedule M1W, a copy of the home-state return, and Form MWR. Do NOT file Schedule M1NR
## 2026 threshold
If Minnesota-assignable gross income OTHER than reciprocity wages is $15,300 or more, the reciprocity subtraction is not available; file Schedule M1NR instead (leave the personal service income out of column B).
## Prevent future withholding
Give the employer Form MWR each year.
## Note
Wisconsin reciprocity ended after 2009 — see minnesota/credits/other-state-tax/.
## Prompt
- I live in North Dakota and work in Moorhead.
