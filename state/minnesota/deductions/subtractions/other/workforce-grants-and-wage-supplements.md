# Workforce Incentive Grants and Nursing Facility Wage Supplements
## Lines
| Line | Payment | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 33 | Long-term service and support (LTSS) workforce incentive grants | Amount included in AGI |
| 34 | Nursing Facility Workforce Incentive Grants | Amount included in AGI |
| 40 | Nursing Facility Workforce Wage Supplement Program payments | NEW for 2026 (2026 Tax Bill); applies only to supplemental wage payments received in 2026 and EXPIRES after tax year 2026 |
## Related
The Line 40 subtraction can also be claimed on the 2026 Form M1PR (Homestead Credit Refund).
## Revenue's list
Revenue's page calls Lines 33–34 "Subtraction for Workforce Incentive Grants." Line 40 is new and not yet on that page.
## Prompt
- I got a workforce incentive grant as a nursing home worker.
