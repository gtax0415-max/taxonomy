# Net Interest from U.S. Bonds (U.S. Government Obligations)
## Description
States cannot tax interest on U.S. government obligations. Minnesota subtracts federally taxable interest from them, NET of related expenses.
## Include
- U.S. bonds, bills, notes, savings bonds, certificates of indebtedness (including accrued market discount)
- Sallie Mae bonds
- Mutual fund dividends attributable to those obligations
- U.S. government interest passed through from partnerships, S corporations, trusts, and estates
## Exclude
Ginnie Mae, Fannie Mae, and Freddie Mac. Other government sources: see Revenue Income Tax Fact Sheet 13.
## Reduce by
Investment interest and other expenses deducted federally that relate to this income.
## Required Information
- 1099-INT Box 3; fund year-end U.S. government percentage; K-1s
## Common Errors
- Including Ginnie Mae, Fannie Mae, or Freddie Mac
- Not netting related expenses
## Prompt
- I have Treasury bill interest. Does Minnesota tax it?
