# Organ Donor Subtraction
## 2026 rule
If the taxpayer, spouse (joint return), or a dependent donated all or part of a liver, pancreas, kidney, intestine, lung, or bone marrow WHILE LIVING to another person, subtract unreimbursed qualified expenses up to $10,000.
## Qualified expenses
Unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages (net of sick pay) due to the transplant.
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother.
