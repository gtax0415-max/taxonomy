# Charitable Contribution Subtraction (Non-Itemizers)
## Who can use it
Taxpayers who do NOT file Schedule M1SA and gave more than $500 to charity.
## 2026 computation (Worksheet for Line 13)
1. Contributions allowable on Schedule M1SA lines 15–16 (same rules and AGI limits)
2. AGI (Form M1, Line 1; 0 if negative)
3. Step 2 × 1% (NEW 2026 floor)
4. Step 1 − Step 3
5. Step 4 − $500
6. Subtraction = Step 5 × 50%
Example: AGI $80,000, cash gifts $3,000 → $3,000 − $800 − $500 = $1,700 × 50% = $850.
## Federal difference
OBBBA's federal non-itemizer charitable deduction ($1,000 / $2,000 MFJ, from 2026) is taken below AGI and does not carry to Minnesota. This Minnesota subtraction is the state equivalent.
## Common Errors
- Forgetting the new 1% floor
- Taking the subtraction and also filing Schedule M1SA
