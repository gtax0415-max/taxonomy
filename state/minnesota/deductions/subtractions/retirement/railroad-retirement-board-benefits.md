# Railroad Retirement Board Benefits
## Description
Federal law bars states from taxing Railroad Retirement Board benefits. Minnesota subtracts every RRB benefit included in federal AGI: retirement (Tier 1 and Tier 2), unemployment, and sick pay.
## Avoid double counting
If federally taxable Tier 1 benefits (Social Security equivalent) are on Form 1040 line 6b and were already subtracted with Social Security on Line 14, do NOT include them on Line 19.
## Related
Expenses deducted federally that relate to RRB income are added back (Schedule M1M, Line 3).
## Prompt
- I get a railroad retirement pension. Does Minnesota tax it?
