## Ordering and overlap rules
- A dollar of pension income can be used for only ONE subtraction. Schedule M1QPEN excludes amounts used for the military pay, military pension, and foreign service subtractions
- Railroad Retirement benefits included on Form 1040 line 6b and subtracted as Social Security (Line 14) are NOT subtracted again on Line 19
- Military pension subtraction bars the Credit for Past Military Service
- There is no general Minnesota subtraction for private pensions, IRAs, or 401(k) distributions
