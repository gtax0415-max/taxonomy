# Foreign Service Retirement Subtraction
## Description
Federally taxable retirement pay from the U.S. Foreign Service is subtracted, prorated to foreign-service years. Introduced for tax year 2025.
## Qualifying plans
- Foreign Service Retirement and Disability System (22 U.S.C. 4041–4069)
- Foreign Service Pension System (22 U.S.C. 4071)
## Worksheet for Line 38
1. Federally taxable foreign service retirement pay
2. Minus any part already used on Schedule M1QPEN line 1
3. Remainder
4. Years in the foreign service
5. Total civil service years credited toward the pension
6. Step 4 ÷ Step 5
7. Subtraction = Step 3 × Step 6
Joint filers with two foreign service pensions use a single combined worksheet.
## Note
The 2026 instructions sometimes refer to "line 36" for this worksheet; the 2026 subtraction line is 38.
## Prompt
- I retired from the State Department Foreign Service.
