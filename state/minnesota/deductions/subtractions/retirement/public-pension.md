# Qualified Public Pension Subtraction (Schedule M1QPEN)
## Description
For public employees who did NOT earn Social Security for their service (so they cannot use the Social Security subtraction for it), Minnesota allows a limited subtraction of taxable public pension income.
## 2026 — MN Department of Revenue published figures
(TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts; 2026 Schedule M1QPEN final draft)
MAXIMUM SUBTRACTION: $13,850 single, HOH, MFS / $27,690 MFJ and QSS
REDUCTION BEGINS (AGI over): $86,410 single and HOH / $110,780 MFJ and QSS / $55,390 MFS
REDUCTION: 10% for each $2,000 (rounded up) of AGI over the threshold
AGI ELIGIBILITY LIMIT (no subtraction above): $105,409 single and HOH / $129,779 MFJ and QSS / $74,389 MFS
## Who qualifies
- Taxable payments or survivor benefits from a QUALIFIED public pension plan
- Based on service as a BASIC member (paid into the pension without paying into Social Security for the same service). Federal CSRS recipients count as basic members; FERS recipients are coordinated members and do NOT qualify
- Members converted from basic to coordinated: only the basic-member share qualifies (months-based worksheet)
## Qualified plans and general basic-to-coordinated conversion dates
PERA (January 1, 1968); Public Employees Police and Fire (all basic); TRA (January 1, 1960); St. Paul Teachers Retirement Fund (July 1, 1978); State agency law enforcement fund (all basic); Local Government Correctional Service Retirement Plan (April 1, 2004); Legislators plan (January 1, 2000); Federal government retirement plan (January 1, 1984); other states' or DC plans that grant reciprocal treatment.
## New 2026 form fields
Pension administrator, payer TIN, occupation, and first year of service for each spouse, plus a spouse column for membership type.
## Not counted here
Pension amounts already used for the active-duty military, National Guard, military pension, or foreign service subtractions.
## Questions
- Which public pension do you receive, and did you pay Social Security tax for that service?
- What is your AGI?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a coordinated (FERS-type) pension
- Not prorating a converted member's pension by basic-member months
- Missing the AGI cutoff
## Prompt
- I am a retired Minnesota teacher. Can I subtract my TRA pension?
