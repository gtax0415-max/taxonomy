# Military Pension Subtraction
## Description
Federally taxable military retirement pay is fully subtractable for Minnesota. No income limit and no dollar cap.
## Qualifying pay
- Retirement pay for service in the active component (10 U.S.C. 1401–1414)
- Reserve component retirement pay (10 U.S.C. 12733)
- Survivor Benefit Plan payments (10 U.S.C. 1447–1455)
Only the amount included in federal AGI.
## Interactions
- Claiming this subtraction BARS the nonrefundable Credit for Past Military Service (Schedule M1C, Line 4)
- Amounts used here are excluded from the public pension subtraction
- VA disability compensation is federally tax-free and never enters the computation
## Questions
- Do you receive military retired pay or SBP annuity payments?
## Common Errors
- Also claiming the Credit for Past Military Service
