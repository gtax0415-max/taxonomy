# AmeriCorps Post-Service Education Award
## Description
Subtract post-service education awards (tuition payments or student loan payments) received in 2026 from the federal government for AmeriCorps National Service, to the extent included in federal AGI.
## Rules
- Only awards received AFTER leaving the program; the living stipend during service does not qualify
- If the award repaid a student loan and the related interest was deducted on federal Schedule 1, line 21, reduce the subtraction by that interest
## Prompt
- I used my AmeriCorps award to pay student loans.
