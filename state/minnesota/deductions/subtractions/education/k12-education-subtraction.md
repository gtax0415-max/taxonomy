# K-12 Education Subtraction
## Description
Subtraction for K-12 education expenses, including PRIVATE SCHOOL TUITION, with no income limit. It complements the refundable K-12 Education Credit: expenses that are not used for the credit (or that do not qualify for it, like tuition) can be subtracted.
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1M instructions)
MAXIMUM per child: $1,625 for grades K–6 / $2,500 for grades 7–12
COMPUTER HARDWARE AND EDUCATIONAL SOFTWARE: $200 per FAMILY (split among children as desired)
INCOME LIMIT: none
## Qualifying child
Your child, adopted child, stepchild, grandchild, or foster child who lived with you in the U.S. more than half the year, was in grades K–12 during 2026, attended a public, private, or home school in MINNESOTA, IOWA, NORTH DAKOTA, SOUTH DAKOTA, or WISCONSIN, and is not claimed as a qualifying child by anyone else.
## Qualifying expenses
Everything that qualifies for the K-12 credit (qualified-instructor tutoring and lessons, academic camps, school supplies and textbooks, musical instruments for school, transportation paid to others, computer costs) PLUS:
- Private school tuition
- Tuition for college courses that satisfy high school graduation requirements
## Not qualifying
Driving your own child, overnight class trips, religious instruction materials, sports camps or lessons, materials for tutoring or enrichment, preschool or post-high-school classes, school lunches, uniforms, monthly internet fees, noneducational software.
## Credit coordination
Complete Schedule M1ED first if eligible for the credit. The same expense cannot be used for both. Married filing separately cannot take the credit but can take the subtraction.
## Required Information
- Each child's name and grade (entered on Line 15) and itemized receipts
## Questions
- Did you pay private school tuition or other K-12 expenses in 2026?
- Did you already use some expenses for the K-12 credit?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the per-child limit or the $200 family computer limit
- Using a school outside the five-state area
- Double-counting expenses used for the credit
