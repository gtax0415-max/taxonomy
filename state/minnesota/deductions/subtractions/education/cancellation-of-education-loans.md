# Discharge of Education Loans (Income-Driven Repayment)
## Description
Subtract student loan debt discharged because the borrower COMPLETED an income-driven repayment program, to the extent the discharged amount is in federal AGI.
## Federal context for 2026
The federal exclusion for student loan discharges under ARPA covered discharges through 2025. Discharges in 2026 at the end of an income-driven plan are generally federally taxable again, which makes this Minnesota subtraction relevant. Death and disability discharges remain federally excluded under OBBBA and never reach Minnesota income.
## Prompt
- My student loans were forgiven after 20 years of income-driven repayment.
