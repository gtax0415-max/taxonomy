# Education Savings Account (529) Contribution Subtraction
## 2026 figures (2026 Schedule M1529 final draft)
SUBTRACTION: net contributions (2026 contributions minus 2026 withdrawals), up to $1,500 ($3,000 married filing jointly)
## Credit or subtraction
Choose ONE: this subtraction or the 529 credit (50% of net contributions up to $500, phased out above $98,410 AGI). The credit usually wins at AGI of $98,410 or less; the subtraction is the only option above $123,410 (single) or $202,140 (MFJ), and for nonresidents and people who can be claimed as a dependent.
## Recapture
Withdrawals not used for Minnesota-qualified expenses trigger recapture of 10% of the subtraction share (Schedule M1529, Line 14) as an additional tax on Form M1, Line 14a.
## Questions
- How much did you contribute to 529 plans in 2026 and withdraw?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and the subtraction
- Not netting withdrawals
