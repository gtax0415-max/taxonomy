# Federal Schedule 1-A Deductions — Not Allowed in Minnesota
## Description
OBBBA created new federal deductions for 2025–2028 claimed on federal Schedule 1-A:
- Qualified tips
- Qualified overtime compensation
- Interest on loans for qualifying new passenger vehicles
- The additional deduction for taxpayers age 65 and older ("senior deduction")
These are subtracted AFTER federal AGI (in arriving at federal taxable income). Form M1 starts from federal AGI, and the 2026 Schedule M1M has NO subtraction line for any of them. So they do NOT reduce Minnesota income.
## What Minnesota offers instead
- Older taxpayers: Social Security subtraction (minnesota/deductions/subtractions/retirement/social-security-subtraction.md), age 65/disabled subtraction (minnesota/deductions/subtractions/retirement/age-65-or-disabled.md), and the higher Minnesota standard deduction for age 65+ ($2,000 / $1,600 per box)
- Vehicle loan interest: no Minnesota deduction (personal interest is not deductible on Schedule M1SA)
- Tips and overtime: fully taxable for Minnesota
## Questions
- Did you claim tips, overtime, car loan interest, or the senior deduction on federal Schedule 1-A?
## Common Errors
- Entering Schedule 1-A amounts as Minnesota subtractions
- Starting the Minnesota return from federal taxable income instead of AGI
## Prompt
- Does Minnesota have no tax on tips?
- Does the federal senior deduction apply to my Minnesota return?
