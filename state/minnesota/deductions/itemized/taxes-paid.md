# Taxes You Paid (Schedule M1SA, Lines 5–10), Including Vehicle License Fees
## 2026 rules
- Line 5 REAL ESTATE TAXES (federal Schedule A line 5b if filed)
- Line 6 PERSONAL PROPERTY TAXES (federal Schedule A line 5c if filed), including part of the Minnesota vehicle license fee
- Line 8 CAP: lesser of Lines 5 + 6 or $10,000 ($5,000 married filing separately)
- Line 9 OTHER TAXES: list type and amount — includes foreign income tax not claimed on federal Form 1116
- NO deduction for state or local INCOME taxes or general SALES taxes (federal Schedule A line 5a has no Minnesota counterpart)
## Vehicle license fee (Revenue's "Deducting Vehicle License Fees")
DEDUCTIBLE PART: the vehicle's REGISTRATION TAX minus $30, for each passenger automobile, pickup truck, or van registered
NOT DEDUCTIBLE: plate fees, filing fees, and other charges
WHERE TO FIND IT: registration renewal notice, or drive.mn.gov (Driver and Vehicle Services: search for registration tax paid)
The same computation applies on federal Schedule A (personal property taxes) if itemizing federally.
## Real estate tax adjustments
- Reduce by any refund or rebate received in 2026, INCLUDING the 2025 Homestead Credit Refund (Form M1PR) received in 2026
- Only taxes actually paid to the taxing authority in 2026 (escrow: what the lender paid)
- Exclude itemized service charges (trash, water, mowing) and assessments for improvements (sidewalks); include charges for maintaining existing facilities
- Home sale: taxes charged to the buyer (Form 1099-S Box 6) are treated as a refund
## Compared with federal 2026
OBBBA raised the federal SALT cap well above $10,000 and includes state income tax. Minnesota keeps a $10,000 cap and excludes income and sales taxes entirely.
## Required Information
- Property tax statements and proof of payment; vehicle registration tax per vehicle; homestead refund received in 2026
## Questions
- What property taxes did you pay in 2026, and did you receive a homestead credit refund?
- How many vehicles did you register, and what was the registration tax on each?
## Common Errors
- Deducting state income tax withheld
- Deducting the full vehicle registration bill instead of registration tax minus $30
- Not reducing property tax by the homestead credit refund
- Exceeding the $10,000 cap
