# Casualty and Theft Losses (Schedule M1SA, Line 22)
## Description
Minnesota allows personal casualty and theft losses WITHOUT the federal restriction to federally declared disasters.
## 2026 rules
- Each separate loss must exceed $100
- Total losses (after the $100 per loss) must exceed 10% of AGI; only the excess is deductible
- Complete Schedule M1CAT for each casualty
## Covered causes
Theft, vandalism, fire, storm and similar events; car, boat, and other accidents; corrosive drywall (repairs to the home or appliances); deposits lost because a financial institution became insolvent or bankrupt.
## Not reduced by the limitation
Casualty losses are excluded from the high-income itemized deduction limitation (Line 28).
## Required Information
- Cost basis, fair market value before and after, insurance reimbursement, and date of each loss
## Questions
- Did you have a theft, accident, storm, or fire loss in 2026 that insurance did not fully cover?
## Common Errors
- Assuming the loss must be a federally declared disaster
- Forgetting the $100 and 10% floors
