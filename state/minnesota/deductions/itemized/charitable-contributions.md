# Charitable Contributions (Schedule M1SA, Lines 15–21)
## 2026 rules (2026 Tax Bill and 2026 Schedule M1SA)
NEW 1% FLOOR: only contributions above 1% of AGI are deductible (Line 19 = AGI × 1%; Line 20 = Lines 15 + 16 − Line 19). The 2026 Tax Bill adopted a 1% floor instead of OBBBA's 0.5% federal floor
CARRYOVER: prior-year excess contributions (Line 17) are added after the floor; generally 5 years to use
AGI LIMITS: 60% of AGI for cash to first-category (public) charities; 50%, 30%, and 20% limits follow IRS rules (Pub. 526)
VOLUNTEER MILEAGE: 14 cents per mile or actual gas and oil, plus parking and tolls
## Lines
- Line 15: cash or check (federal Schedule A line 11), not more than 60% of AGI
- Line 16: other than cash or check (federal line 12) at fair market value
- Line 17: carryover from a prior year
## Substantiation
- Any cash gift: bank record or written record from the charity
- $250 or more (each gift separately): written acknowledgment stating amount and any goods or services received
- Noncash over $500: statement of date, charity, description, value; over $5,000 (or $500 for some clothing and household items): appraisal and Form 8283; vehicles, boats, planes over $500: Form 1098-C
## Not deductible
Political contributions, raffle and bingo tickets, the value of time, blood, club dues, gifts to individuals or for-profit groups, most foreign charities, lobbying groups, civic leagues and chambers of commerce, tuition, and the value of benefits received.
## Non-itemizers
Minnesota allows a SUBTRACTION for taxpayers who do not file Schedule M1SA: 50% of contributions above $500 after the 1% floor (Schedule M1M, Line 13). The federal OBBBA non-itemizer deduction does not carry to Minnesota.
## Questions
- How much did you give in cash and in property, and do you have acknowledgments?
- Do you have a carryover from prior years?
- Are you itemizing on your Minnesota return?
## Common Errors
- Forgetting the new 1% floor or using the federal 0.5% floor
- Claiming the subtraction AND itemizing
- Missing acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more
