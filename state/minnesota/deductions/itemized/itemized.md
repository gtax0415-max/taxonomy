# Minnesota Itemized Deductions (Schedule M1SA)
## Description
Minnesota has its own itemized deduction schedule. It borrows many federal Schedule A amounts but differs in important ways, and it can be used even if the federal return took the standard deduction.

## Minnesota vs. federal itemized deductions, 2026
| Item | Minnesota 2026 | Federal 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Medical floor | 10% of AGI | 7.5% of AGI |
| State and local INCOME or SALES tax | Not deductible | Deductible within the SALT cap |
| Real estate + personal property tax | Capped at $10,000 ($5,000 MFS) | Part of the higher OBBBA SALT cap |
| Charitable floor | 1% of AGI | 0.5% of AGI (OBBBA, from 2026) |
| Casualty and theft losses | Any casualty, $100 and 10%-of-AGI floors | Federally declared disasters (and certain others) only |
| Unreimbursed employee expenses | Allowed above 2% of AGI | Not allowed |
| High-income limitation | 3% / 10% formula, up to 80% (Minnesota thresholds) | OBBBA 2/37 limitation for top-bracket taxpayers |
## When to itemize
Compare Line 29 with the Minnesota standard deduction (minnesota/deductions/standard/). Married filing separately: if one spouse itemizes on the Minnesota return, the other must too.
## Nonresident aliens (Form 1040-NR)
Generally no standard deduction; may itemize charitable contributions, casualty and theft losses, and certain miscellaneous deductions.
