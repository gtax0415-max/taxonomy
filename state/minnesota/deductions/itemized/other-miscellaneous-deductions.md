# Other Miscellaneous Deductions (Schedule M1SA, Line 26)
## Description
A closed list of deductions NOT subject to the 2% floor. List the type and amount of each.
## The only items allowed (2026 Schedule M1SA)
- Gambling losses, up to gambling winnings reported on federal Schedule 1 line 8b (including non-winning bingo, lottery, and raffle tickets)
- Federal estate tax on income in respect of a decedent
- Amortizable bond premium (including carryforwards and pre-October 23, 1986 bonds)
- Repayment of amounts under a claim of right (repayments over $3,000 may instead use the Minnesota credit — see minnesota/credits/other/repaying-taxed-income.md)
- Unrecovered investment in a pension
- Impairment-related work expenses of a person with a disability
- Deductions for personal property used in a short sale (IRC 67(b)(8))
- Cooperative housing tenant-stockholder taxes, interest, and business depreciation (IRC 216)
## Gambling losses — federal difference
The 2026 Schedule M1SA allows gambling losses "to the extent of gambling winnings." OBBBA limits the federal deduction to 90% of losses beginning in 2026, so the Minnesota and federal amounts can differ. Confirm against the final instructions.
## Questions
- Did you have gambling winnings and losses in 2026?
- Did you repay income taxed in a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Deducting gambling losses above winnings
- Listing items not on the closed list (for example, tax preparation or investment fees)
