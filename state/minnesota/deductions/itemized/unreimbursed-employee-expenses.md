# Unreimbursed Employee Business Expenses (Schedule M1SA, Lines 23–25)
## Description
The federal deduction for unreimbursed employee expenses is suspended (made permanent by OBBBA). Minnesota still allows it as an itemized deduction above 2% of AGI.
## 2026 rules
- Expenses must be ordinary and necessary for the job and not reimbursed (and not reimbursable if claimed)
- Only the amount above 2% of AGI is deductible
- Schedule M1UE is REQUIRED if claiming travel, transportation, meals, or entertainment, or if the employer reimbursed any job expenses. On a joint return each spouse completes a separate M1UE
- The 2026 Schedule M1UE adds lines 12a/12b, 13a/13b, and 20a–20c (new mileage and commuting computations)
## Examples
Safety equipment, small tools and supplies, required uniforms not suitable for street wear, protective clothing, required physical exams, professional dues and journals, job-search costs in the same occupation, qualifying home office, certain education costs.
## Not here
Educator expenses already deducted on federal Schedule 1, line 11.
## Questions
- Did you pay job expenses in 2026 that your employer did not reimburse?
## Common Errors
- Claiming expenses the employer would have reimbursed
- Skipping Schedule M1UE when vehicle or travel costs are claimed
