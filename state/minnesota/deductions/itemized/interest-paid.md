# Interest You Paid (Schedule M1SA, Lines 11–14)
## 2026 rules
- Line 11 HOME MORTGAGE INTEREST AND POINTS on Form 1098 (federal Schedule A line 8a if filed), EXCLUDING home equity loan interest included in 8a
- Line 12 MORTGAGE INTEREST AND POINTS NOT ON FORM 1098 (federal line 8b): seller-financed loans (give the seller's name, SSN or FEIN, and address), shared mortgages reported on someone else's 1098, points not reported
- Line 13 INVESTMENT INTEREST (federal line 9); attach a 2026 federal Form 4952 computed for Minnesota unless the simple exceptions apply
- Personal interest (credit cards, auto loans) is not deductible
## Mortgage debt limits
| Loan date | Limit | MFS |
|---|---|---|
| Before December 16, 2017 | $1,000,000 | $500,000 |
| On or after December 16, 2017 | $750,000 (reduced by grandfathered debt) | $375,000 |
| On or before October 13, 1987 | Grandfathered (IRS Pub. 936) | — |
Qualifying home: main or second home with sleeping, cooking, and toilet facilities (house, condo, co-op, mobile home, boat).
## Points
Points to buy a main home are generally deductible when paid; refinancing points are deducted over the loan life (except the portion for improvements).
## Mortgage insurance premiums
The 2026 Schedule M1SA has no separate line for mortgage insurance premiums. OBBBA treats them as deductible mortgage interest federally beginning in 2026; whether they belong on Line 11 for Minnesota should be confirmed in the final instructions.
## Questions
- What mortgage interest and points did you pay, and on what loans and dates?
- Do you have a home equity loan, and what were the proceeds used for?
- Did you pay margin or other investment interest?
## Common Errors
- Including home equity loan interest from federal line 8a
- Exceeding the $750,000 / $1,000,000 debt limits
- Deducting refinance points in full
