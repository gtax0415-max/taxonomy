# Itemized Deduction Limitation (Schedule M1SA, Line 28)
## 2026 figures (TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts; 2026 Schedule M1SA)

## Deductions NOT reduced
Medical and dental (Line 4), investment interest (Line 13), and casualty and theft losses (Line 22).
## Worksheet A (AGI over the first threshold, up to $1,107,750)
1. Line 27 total
2. Lines 4 + 13 + 22
3. Step 1 − Step 2 (reducible deductions); stop if zero or less
4. AGI
5. Lesser of (AGI − $244,400) or $93,400 [MFS: AGI − $122,200, max $46,700]
6. Step 5 × 3%
7. AGI − $337,800 if positive [MFS: − $168,900]
8. Step 7 × 10%
9. Step 6 + Step 8
10. Step 3 × 80%
11. Reduction = lesser of Step 9 and Step 10 → Line 28
## Worksheet B (AGI over $1,107,750)
Reduction = 80% of reducible deductions (Step 3).
