# Family and Dependent Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits tied to children, dependent care, and married couples.
## Credits in this folder
- Child Tax Credit (child-tax-credit.md) — Schedule M1CWFC → Schedule M1REF, Line 2 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable. Shares a combined phase-out with the Working Family Credit (income-based/working-family-credit.md)
- Child and Dependent Care Credit (child-and-dependent-care-credit.md) — Schedule M1CD → Schedule M1REF, Line 1 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable
- Marriage Credit (marriage-credit.md) — Schedule M1MA → Schedule M1C, Line 1 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Credit for Parents of Stillborn Children (parents-of-stillborn-children-credit.md) — Schedule M1PSC → Schedule M1REF, Line 5 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable
- Advance Payments of the Child Tax Credit (advance-child-tax-credit-payments.md) — Schedule M1CWFC Sections 2 and 3; repayment on Form M1, Line 14b
- Credit for Qualifying Older Children (qualifying-older-children-credit.md) — Schedule M1CWFC, Line 5 → Schedule M1REF, Line 2 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable. Legally part of the Working Family Credit
## What changed for 2026
- DEPENDENT CARE CREDIT DECOUPLED from the federal credit by the 2026 Tax Bill. Federal OBBBA raised the federal top rate to 50% for 2026; Minnesota did NOT adopt that and keeps a 35%-to-20% sliding rate with its own phase-out
- The CHILD TAX CREDIT rose to $1,800 per child by inflation indexing (2025: $1,750)
- New checkboxes on Schedule M1CWFC for advance-payment repayment-only filers and text-message updates
