# Child and Dependent Care Credit (Schedule M1CD)
## Description
Refundable Minnesota credit for child or dependent care expenses paid so the taxpayer (and spouse) could work or look for work. Unlike the federal credit, it is REFUNDABLE and targeted to lower incomes through a phase-out.
## Big change for 2026: decoupled from the federal credit
Historically the Minnesota credit equaled the federal Form 2441 credit, made refundable and phased out. The 2026 Tax Bill (Laws 2026, Ch. 128) DECOUPLES the Minnesota credit from the federal credit starting with tax year 2026 and does NOT adopt OBBBA's federal expansion.
- FEDERAL 2026: top rate raised to 50% (max $1,500 / $3,000), with a 20% floor at higher incomes and no income cap
- MINNESOTA 2026: keeps the 35%-to-20% sliding rate on $3,000 / $6,000 of expenses, then phases out by AGI
Do NOT copy the federal Form 2441 credit to Minnesota for 2026. Compute it independently on Schedule M1CD.
## How it works (Schedule M1CD)
1. Qualifying expenses, capped at $3,000 (one qualifying person) / $6,000 (two or more), reduced by excluded dependent care benefits (Part 3/4)
2. Limited to the lower-earning spouse's earned income
3. Multiplied by the decimal from Table 3 based on AGI
4. Plus any prior-year (2025) expenses paid in 2026 (line 7b worksheet)
5. Phased out by 5% of AGI over $65,610

## 2025 — for comparison
AGI limits were under $76,150 (one qualifying person) and under $88,150 (two or more).
## Qualifying person and expenses
Same definitions as the federal credit: a dependent child under 13, or a disabled spouse or disabled dependent, who lived with you more than half the year. Expenses include day care, day camp (not overnight camp), and household services partly for the qualifying person's care. Do not count schooling for kindergarten and above, summer school, or tutoring. When determining dependency, ignore MFIP grants received for the child.
## Special Minnesota rules
- LICENSED FAMILY DAY CARE OPERATORS caring for their own child under 6 may claim a credit using deemed expenses: $3,000 if the child is 16 months or younger; otherwise the rate they would charge, up to $3,000
- CHILD BORN IN 2026: for 2026 the newborn deemed-expense worksheet applies to licensed day care operators whose newborn is enrolled in their facility. The 2025 instructions allowed a newborn credit for parents who did not use a pre-tax dependent care plan; the 2026 final draft restricts it to day care operators. Confirm against the final 2026 instructions
- PRIOR-YEAR EXPENSES: 2025 expenses paid in 2026 can increase the 2026 credit (line 7b), using the 2025 AGI rate
- Part-year residents and nonresidents prorate by earned income taxable to Minnesota
## Married filing separately
Generally barred. Allowed (treated as unmarried) only if the taxpayer lived apart from the spouse for the last 6 months of 2026, the home was the qualifying person's main home for more than half the year, and the taxpayer paid more than half the cost of the home.
## Required Information
- Each care provider's name, address, SSN or FEIN, and amount paid
- Each qualifying person's name, date of birth, SSN, and expenses
- Earned income of each spouse (student or disabled spouse deemed $250/$500 per month)
- Dependent care benefits from W-2 Box 10 or K-1, carryovers, and forfeitures
- AGI
## Questions
- Did you pay someone to care for a child under 13 or a disabled dependent so you could work?
- What was your AGI? (Over $77,610 with one person or $89,610 with two or more means no credit)
- Did you receive dependent care benefits through an FSA at work?
- Are you a licensed family day care operator caring for your own young child?
- Did you pay any 2025 care expenses in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Copying the 2026 federal Form 2441 credit (now up to 50%) onto Schedule M1CD. Minnesota decoupled
- Missing the phase-out above $65,610
- Not reducing expenses by excluded FSA benefits
- Including overnight camp, kindergarten tuition, or tutoring
- Missing care provider ID numbers
- Claiming it while married filing separately without meeting the lived-apart test
