# Credit for Parents of Stillborn Children (Schedule M1PSC)
## Description
Refundable one-time credit for parents who experienced a stillbirth during the year. No federal counterpart. Handle with care when raising it.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: $2,000 per stillborn child (not indexed)
Only one credit per stillbirth.
## Key eligibility rules
- The stillbirth occurred in 2026
- The parent holds a Certificate of Birth Resulting in Stillbirth issued by the MINNESOTA Department of Health, Office of Vital Records (another state's certificate does not qualify)
- The child would have been the taxpayer's dependent had the child been born alive (federal dependent definition)
- Part-year residents and nonresidents prorate by the Schedule M1NR percentage
- A prior-year stillbirth must be claimed on that year's return, not 2026
## Notes
- Joint filers need only one certificate per stillbirth
- Multiple stillbirths in 2026: one Schedule M1PSC per child, totals combined on M1REF Line 5
- If no certificate was requested, the parent can apply to the Office of Vital Records ((651) 201-5970)
## Required Information
- State file number (upper right inside the margin of the certificate)
- Document control number (lower left under the barcode)
- Parent and child names and date of delivery
## Questions
- Did you experience a stillbirth in 2026, and do you have the Minnesota certificate?
- Would the child have been your dependent?
## Common Errors
- Using a certificate from another state
- Leaving off the state file number or document control number
- Claiming for a stillbirth in a different tax year
## Prompt
- Is there a Minnesota tax credit after a stillbirth?
