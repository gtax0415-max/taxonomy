# Tax Credit for Owners of Agricultural Assets (Beginning Farmer Credit for Sale or Rental of Agricultural Assets)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an individual, trust, or pass-through entity that sells or rents agricultural assets (land, livestock, facilities, buildings, machinery used for farming in Minnesota) to an RFA-certified beginning farmer. C corporations and equipment or livestock dealers are not eligible owners.
## 2026 figures
(Credit structure per Legislative Budget Office TERC evaluation, August 19, 2026)
| Agreement | Credit | Maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Sale | 8% of the lesser of sale price or fair market value | $50,000 |
| Sale to a limited land access farmer | 12% of the lesser of sale price or fair market value | $50,000 |
| Cash rent | 10% of gross rental income in each of years 1–3 | $7,000 per year |
| Share rent | 15% of the cash equivalent of gross rental income in each of years 1–3 | $10,000 per year |
ALLOCATION: normally $4 million per year (2024–2030), first come, first served. The 2026 Tax Bill REMOVED the cap for tax year 2026 only
CARRYFORWARD: 15 years
## Key rules
- The RFA must certify both the beginning farmer and the owner's eligibility, and issue the AO- certificate
- The beginning farmer must complete an approved farm business management program (or have a waiver)
- Family-member restrictions apply to rental agreements but no longer to sales
- Pass-through owners: if the certificate was issued to the entity, enter each certificate number; attach a list if more than three
- Nonresidents and part-year residents multiply the credit by the Schedule M1NR line 30 percentage
## Earlier rules (for prior-year carryovers)
Sale credit was previously 5% with a $32,000 maximum. Carryovers from those years keep their original amounts.
## Required Information
- AO- certificate number(s) and amounts
- Agreement type and year of the rental term
- Prior-year unused credits
## Questions
- Did you sell or rent farm assets to a certified beginning farmer, and did the RFA issue you a certificate?
- Is this year 1, 2, or 3 of the rental agreement?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without an RFA certificate
- Claiming a rental credit beyond year 3
- Applying the old 5% / $32,000 sale terms to a current-year sale
## Prompt
- I sold my farm to a beginning farmer.
- I rent cropland to a new farmer on a crop share.
