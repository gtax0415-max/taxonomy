# Agriculture Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
The two Beginning Farmer Program credits, administered by the Minnesota Department of Agriculture's Rural Finance Authority (RFA). Both are nonrefundable, both require an RFA certificate, and neither has a federal counterpart.
## Credits in this folder
- Beginning Farmer Management Credit (beginning-farmer-management-credit.md) — for the beginning FARMER. Schedule M1C, Line 10
- Tax Credit for Owners of Agricultural Assets (owners-of-agricultural-assets-credit.md) — for the SELLER or LANDLORD. Schedule M1C, Line 12
## How the two connect
The beginning farmer must complete an RFA-approved farm business management program for the asset owner to receive the owner credit (waivers exist for those with a completed program, a four-year agricultural degree, or farm-finance experience). The management credit reimburses that program cost.
## 2026 change
The 2026 Tax Bill removed the allocation cap on the owners-of-agricultural-assets credit for tax year 2026 only (normally $4 million a year for 2024–2030).
## Questions
- Are you a beginning farmer enrolled in a farm business management program?
- Did you sell or rent land, machinery, livestock, or buildings to a beginning farmer?
## Prompt
- I rent my farmland to a young farmer. Is there a Minnesota credit?
- I am a beginning farmer taking a farm management class.
