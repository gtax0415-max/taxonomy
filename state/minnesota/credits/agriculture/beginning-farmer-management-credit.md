# Beginning Farmer Management Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a beginning farmer's cost of an RFA-approved farm financial management program. It does NOT require a sale or rental agreement.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: 100% of program enrollment cost, up to $1,500 per year
YEARS: up to three years while enrolled
CARRYFORWARD: 3 years after the year the credit was generated
## Key eligibility rules
- Certified by the RFA as a beginning farmer (generally a Minnesota resident who started farming within the last 10 years and provides the majority of the labor and management on a Minnesota farm)
- Enrolled in an RFA-approved farm financial management program
- Part-year residents multiply the credit by the Schedule M1NR line 30 percentage
## Required Information
- BF- certificate number and credit amount
- Prior-year unused credit
## Questions
- Did the RFA certify you as a beginning farmer and issue a management credit certificate?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without the BF- certificate number
- Claiming more than $1,500 in a year
## Prompt
- I am taking a farm business management course. Is there a credit?
