# State Housing Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributing to Minnesota Housing's State Housing Tax Credit Program and Contribution Fund, which finances affordable housing. Unrelated to the federal Low-Income Housing Tax Credit. Individuals, pass-through entities, corporations, and insurers can participate.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: 85% of the contribution made during the taxable year
CONTRIBUTION RANGE: at least $1,000 and no more than $2,000,000
CARRYFORWARD: unused credit carries forward up to 10 years
2026 AVAILABILITY: Minnesota Housing reports that all state housing tax credits available for 2026 have been issued. Applications opened February 16, 2026
## How it works
1. Apply to Minnesota Housing and be accepted (first come, first served)
2. Contribute; Minnesota Housing issues a Tax Credit Certificate within 30 days
3. Claim the certificate amount on Schedule M1C, Line 15 with the SHTC- certificate number; attach a statement if more than one certificate
## Key rules
- No certificate, no credit. A contribution made without an accepted application does not qualify
- Credit is limited to tax liability; the excess carries forward
- Part-year residents and nonresidents prorate by the Schedule M1NR line 30 percentage
- Pass-through owners report their share from KS, KPI, or KF
## Required Information
- Tax Credit Certificate number(s) and amount(s)
- K-schedules for pass-through shares
- Prior-year unused credit and certificate numbers
## Questions
- Did you contribute to Minnesota Housing's State Housing Tax Credit fund in 2026 and receive a certificate?
- Do you have unused housing credit from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the full contribution instead of 85%
- Omitting the SHTC certificate number
- Losing track of the 10-year carryforward
## Prompt
- I donated to the Minnesota housing tax credit fund.
- I have a State Housing Tax Credit certificate.
