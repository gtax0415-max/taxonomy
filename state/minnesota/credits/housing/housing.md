# Housing Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Relief for the property tax built into rent, and a credit for funding affordable housing.
## Credits in this folder
- Renter's Credit (renters-credit.md) — Schedule M1RENT → Schedule M1REF, Line 4 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable
- State Housing Tax Credit (state-housing-tax-credit.md) — Minnesota Housing certificate → Schedule M1C, Line 15 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable; 85% of a contribution to the affordable-housing fund
## Homeowner refund (separate return, not Form M1)
- homestead-credit-refund.md — HOMESTEAD CREDIT REFUND and SPECIAL PROPERTY TAX REFUND for homeowners (Form M1PR), a separate property tax refund return, not an income tax credit. For refunds based on taxes payable in 2026 the 2026 Tax Bill raised the refund by 14.88% (one time)
- Mobile home owners who rent only the LOT use Form M1PR, not the Renter's Credit
## Notes
- Since tax year 2024 renters claim relief as the Renter's Credit on Form M1, not on Form M1PR
- A renter must file a Minnesota income tax return to get it, even with no filing requirement
