# Homestead Credit Refund (Form M1PR) and Related Homeowner Programs
## Description
A property tax refund for Minnesota HOMEOWNERS, filed on Form M1PR separately from Form M1. (Renters claim the Renter's Credit on Form M1 instead — renters-credit.md.) Listed because the Form M1 instructions promote it and its refunds interact with Schedule M1SA.
## Regular Homestead Credit Refund
- Must own and occupy the home as a homestead on January 2 of the year taxes are payable
- Based on household income and property taxes payable
- 2025 schedule (claims based on 2025 income and taxes payable in 2026): household income under $142,490; maximum refund $3,480
- 2026 Tax Bill: one-time 14.88% increase for refunds based on taxes payable in 2026; Revenue adjusts already-filed 2025 Form M1PR claims automatically
- The 2026 Nursing Facility Workforce Wage Supplement subtraction can also be claimed on the 2026 Form M1PR
## Special Property Tax Refund (Form M1PR, special refund section)
- Net property tax on the homestead increased at least 12% from the prior year AND by at least $100
- Owned and lived in the home on January 2 of both years
- No income limit
## Other programs for homeowners
- Senior Citizens' Property Tax Deferral Program — income tax refunds can be applied to the deferred amount
- Special homestead classification Class 1b for qualifying blind and disabled owners
## Filing
Form M1PR is mailed separately from Form M1 (Minnesota Revenue, Mail Station 0020, 600 N. Robert St., St. Paul, MN 55146-0020) or filed electronically.
## Interaction with income tax
- A homestead credit refund RECEIVED during 2026 reduces the real estate tax deduction on Schedule M1SA (and federal Schedule A)
- Mobile home owners who rent only the lot use Form M1PR, not the Renter's Credit

- I own my home in Minnesota. Can I get a property tax refund?
