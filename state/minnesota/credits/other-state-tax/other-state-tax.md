# Credits for Tax Paid to Other States (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits that prevent a Minnesota resident's income from being taxed by both Minnesota and another state. This is the state-level parallel to the federal Foreign Tax Credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Tax Paid to Another State (credit-for-tax-paid-to-another-state.md) — Schedule M1CR → Schedule M1C, Line 3 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Credit for Tax Paid to Wisconsin (credit-for-tax-paid-to-wisconsin.md) — Schedule M1RCR → Schedule M1C, Line 3 (nonrefundable part) and Schedule M1REF, Line 6 (refundable part) → Form M1, Lines 16 and 22
## Related: reciprocity is not a credit
Minnesota has income tax RECIPROCITY with Michigan and North Dakota for wages. A resident of those states working in Minnesota does not use a credit; they take a SUBTRACTION on Schedule M1M, Line 20 (2026) to recover Minnesota withholding — see minnesota/deductions/subtractions/other/reciprocity-income.md. If their other Minnesota-source gross income is $15,300 or more, they file Schedule M1NR instead. Wisconsin reciprocity ended after 2009, which is why the Wisconsin credit exists.
## Related: pass-through entity tax paid to another state
A credit for PTE tax paid to another state by an entity is reflected through the PTE rules — see business/pass-through-entity-tax-credit.md. The 2026 Tax Bill extended it through tax years beginning before January 1, 2028.
## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to another state, DC, or a Canadian province in 2026?
- Did you work in Wisconsin while living in Minnesota?
- Were you a Michigan or North Dakota resident working in Minnesota?
