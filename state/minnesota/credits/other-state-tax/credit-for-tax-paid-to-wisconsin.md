# Credit for Tax Paid to Wisconsin (Schedule M1RCR)
## Description
Credit for a Minnesota resident who paid Wisconsin income tax on wages or other personal-service income earned in Wisconsin. Because the Minnesota–Wisconsin reciprocity agreement ended after 2009, Minnesota residents working in Wisconsin pay Wisconsin tax and claim this credit. Part of it can be REFUNDABLE, unlike the general other-state credit.
## Key eligibility rules
- Domiciled in Minnesota for all or part of 2026
- Incurred 2026 income tax to BOTH Minnesota and Wisconsin on the same income from professional or personal services performed while a Minnesota resident
## Routing
Schedule M1RCR splits the result: a nonrefundable part goes to Schedule M1C Line 3 (combined with any Schedule M1CR credit) and a refundable part goes to Schedule M1REF Line 6. Follow the line-by-line split on the 2026 Schedule M1RCR rather than estimating it.
## Notes
- File the Wisconsin nonresident return (Form 1NPR) first; the credit uses actual Wisconsin tax liability, not withholding
- Other Wisconsin income (rental, business not tied to personal services) goes through Schedule M1CR rules instead
## Required Information
- Wisconsin return and proof of tax paid
- Wages and personal-service income earned in Wisconsin
- Minnesota tax before credits
## Questions
- Did you live in Minnesota and work in Wisconsin in 2026?
- Have you completed your Wisconsin nonresident return?
## Common Errors
- Using Wisconsin withholding instead of Wisconsin tax liability
- Filing Schedule M1CR instead of M1RCR for Wisconsin wages
- Not carrying the refundable part to Schedule M1REF Line 6
## Prompt
- I live in Minnesota and commute to Wisconsin.
- I paid Wisconsin income tax. Do I get it back from Minnesota?
