# Military Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits for current and former members of the U.S. Armed Forces.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Past Military Service (past-military-service-credit.md) — worksheet in Schedule M1C instructions → Schedule M1C, Line 4 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Credit for Military Service in a Combat Zone (combat-zone-credit.md) — Form M99, filed SEPARATELY from Form M1
## Related subtractions (not credits)
Active-duty pay (Schedule M1M, Line 22; nonresidents Line 24), National Guard and Reserve pay (Line 23), and military pensions (Line 27) are Minnesota SUBTRACTIONS — see minnesota/deductions/subtractions/military/ and minnesota/deductions/subtractions/retirement/military-pension.md. Claiming the military pension subtraction BARS the Credit for Past Military Service.
## 2026 change
Minnesota did NOT adopt OBBBA's hazardous-duty exclusion for service in the Sinai Peninsula, Kenya, Mali, Burkina Faso, and Chad. That pay, excluded federally, is ADDED BACK on Schedule M1M, Line 8 for 2026 — see minnesota/income/additions/individual/hazardous-duty-pay.md.
## Questions
- Are you a veteran with a military pension, 20+ years of service, or a 100% service-connected disability?
- Did you serve in a combat zone or hazardous duty area in 2026?
