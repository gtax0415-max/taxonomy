# Credit for Military Service in a Combat Zone (Form M99)
## Description
Refundable credit for Minnesota-domiciled service members for each month served in a combat zone or hazardous duty area. Claimed on its OWN form, not on Form M1, so it does not appear on Schedule M1REF.
## Figures
CREDIT: $120 for each month (or part of a month) served in a combat zone or hazardous duty area
Months in 2022 through 2026 may be claimed; Form M99 for 2027 service is already in draft.
## Key eligibility rules
- Minnesota is the state of legal residence (domicile) while serving
- Served in a presidentially designated combat zone or a qualified hazardous duty area
## Notes
- File Form M99 with the required documentation; it can also be filed electronically through Revenue's website
- Combat-zone service also extends Minnesota filing and payment deadlines (180 days after leaving the zone or after continuous hospitalization)
## Required Information
- Dates of service in each combat zone or hazardous duty area
- Documentation of domicile and service
## Questions
- Were you a Minnesota domiciliary serving in a combat zone or hazardous duty area in any month of 2022–2026?
## Common Errors
- Looking for it on Schedule M1REF (it is a separate form)
- Missing earlier eligible years
## Prompt
- I was deployed overseas. Does Minnesota pay anything for combat service?
