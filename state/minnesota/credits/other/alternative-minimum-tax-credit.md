# Minnesota Alternative Minimum Tax Credit (Schedule M1MTC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit that recovers Minnesota AMT paid in a prior year (1989–2025) in a year when Minnesota AMT is not owed. Parallel to the federal Form 8801 credit, but computed separately.
## 2026 figures
MINNESOTA AMT EXEMPTION: $97,470 married filing jointly / $73,100 single and head of household / $48,740 married filing separately (TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts)
## Key rules
- Never paid Minnesota AMT → no credit
- Complete Schedule M1MT for 2026 first; if Minnesota AMT is owed in 2026, no credit this year
- Unused credit carries forward
- Minnesota AMT can apply even when federal AMT does not
## Required Information
- Prior-year Schedules M1MT and M1MTC (carryforward amounts)
## Questions
- Did you pay Minnesota alternative minimum tax in any earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal minimum tax credit carryforward instead of the Minnesota amount
## Prompt
- I paid Minnesota AMT a few years ago. Can I get it back?
