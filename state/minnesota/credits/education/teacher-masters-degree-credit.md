# Credit for Attaining Master's Degree in Teacher's Licensure Field (Schedule M1CMD)
## Description
One-time nonrefundable credit for a licensed Minnesota teacher who completes a master's degree in a core content area tied to their licensure field. No federal counterpart.
## 2026 figures
MAXIMUM CREDIT: $2,500 per degree (lesser of $2,500 or tuition, required fees, books, and materials minus scholarships and employer reimbursements, all years combined)
## Key eligibility rules
- Program began after June 30, 2017, and was COMPLETED in 2026
- Held a Minnesota teaching license from PELSB when the program began and when it was completed
- Master of Arts or Science in a CORE CONTENT AREA directly related to the licensure field: reading, English or language arts, mathematics, science, foreign languages, civics and government, economics, arts, history, geography
- The program contains NO pedagogy component. Programs in education, special education, literacy education, curriculum and instruction generally fail this test
- Once per qualifying degree
- Part-year residents and nonresidents prorate by the Schedule M1NR percentage
## Required Information
- PELSB file folder number, licensure information, grade level taught
- Institution, degree program, start and completion dates
- Lifetime costs, scholarships, and employer reimbursements for the program
## Questions
- Are you a licensed Minnesota teacher who finished a master's degree in 2026?
- Is the degree in a core subject area and free of any pedagogy component?
## Common Errors
- Claiming an M.Ed. or curriculum-and-instruction degree
- Claiming in a year other than the completion year
- Not subtracting scholarships or employer reimbursements from all years
## Prompt
- I am a teacher and just finished my master's in math. Is there a Minnesota credit?
