# Student Loan Credit (Schedule M1SLC)
## Description
Nonrefundable Minnesota credit for payments on your OWN qualified postsecondary education loans. No federal credit counterpart (the federal benefit is the student loan interest deduction, already in AGI).
## 2026 figures
(2026 Schedule M1SLC final draft)
MAXIMUM CREDIT: $500 per person ($1,000 on a joint return if both spouses qualify)
## How it works (computed per spouse)
The credit is the SMALLEST of:
1. Loan payments made in 2026 minus 10% of AGI over $10,000
2. Earned income (must be more than zero)
3. 10% of the original loan amount (counting at most $5,000 of loans) PLUS interest paid in 2026 (Form 1098-E)
4. $500
On a joint return, AGI is allocated between spouses by each spouse's share of earned income.
## Key eligibility rules
- Full-year or part-year Minnesota resident (part-year prorates by Schedule M1NR)
- Must have taxable earned income
- Only payments on loans for YOUR OWN postsecondary education. A parent paying a child's loans cannot claim it, regardless of whose name is on the loan
- Loans from a relative or a qualified employer plan do not count
- Payments reimbursed tax-free by an employer under IRC section 127 must be removed
## Federal interaction for 2026
Minnesota did NOT adopt the OBBBA exclusion for employer student loan payments, and freezes the state educational-assistance exclusion at $5,250. Employer-paid student loan amounts excluded federally are ADDED BACK on Schedule M1M (new 2026 line 7 — see minnesota/income/additions/individual/disallowed-educational-assistance.md). The Schedule M1SLC instructions only say that payments reimbursed tax-free under section 127 must be removed and that reimbursements included in AGI may be counted; they do not address payments excluded federally but added back for Minnesota. Treat that case as unresolved until the final instructions or Revenue guidance address it.
## Required Information
- Total 2026 payments (principal and interest) on your own qualified loans, per spouse
- Original loan amounts
- 2026 interest paid (Form 1098-E)
- Earned income per spouse
- Any employer reimbursements
## Questions
- Are you repaying loans for your own college education?
- How much did you pay in 2026, and how much was interest?
- Did your employer reimburse any student loan payments?
## Common Errors
- Claiming payments on a child's loans
- Forgetting the 10%-of-AGI-over-$10,000 reduction
- Including employer-reimbursed, excluded payments
- Claiming it with no earned income
## Prompt
- Is there a Minnesota credit for paying student loans?
- I pay my daughter's student loans. Can I claim anything in Minnesota?
