# Education Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits for K-12 expenses, college savings, student loan payments, and teacher master's degrees.
## Credits in this folder
- K-12 Education Credit (k12-education-credit.md) — Schedule M1ED → Schedule M1REF, Line 3 → Form M1, Line 22. Refundable
- Education Savings Account (529) Contribution Credit (education-savings-account-credit.md) — Schedule M1529 → Schedule M1C, Line 7 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Student Loan Credit (student-loan-credit.md) — Schedule M1SLC → Schedule M1C, Line 9 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
- Credit for Attaining Master's Degree in Teacher's Licensure Field (teacher-masters-degree-credit.md) — Schedule M1CMD → Schedule M1C, Line 8 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
## Credit or subtraction
Two of these have a SUBTRACTION alternative on Schedule M1M (reducing income rather than tax):
- K-12: expenses that do not fit the credit (or households over the income limit) may use the K-12 Education Subtraction
- 529: choose EITHER the credit OR the subtraction ($1,500 / $3,000 MFJ), never both
## Federal relationship
Minnesota has no counterpart to the federal American Opportunity or Lifetime Learning credits. The Student Loan Credit is Minnesota-only. The federal student loan interest deduction flows into AGI and so into Minnesota automatically.
