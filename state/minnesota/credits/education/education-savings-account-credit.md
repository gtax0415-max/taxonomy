# Education Savings Account (529) Contribution Credit (Schedule M1529)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to any state's section 529 plan (including prepaid tuition plans), regardless of who owns the account or who the beneficiary is. Taxpayers choose EITHER this credit OR the 529 subtraction each year.
## 2026 — MN Department of Revenue published figures
(TY2026 inflation-adjusted amounts; 2026 Schedule M1529 final draft)
CREDIT: 50% of net contributions (contributions minus withdrawals), up to $500
FULL $500 AVAILABLE: AGI of $98,410 or less
PHASE-DOWN:
- Single, HOH, MFS: reduced 2% of AGI over $98,410 → zero at $123,410
- Married filing jointly: reduced 1% of AGI over $98,410 down to $250 at $123,410; stays at $250 to $177,140; then reduced 1% → zero at $202,140
ALTERNATIVE SUBTRACTION: up to $1,500 ($3,000 MFJ) of net contributions on Schedule M1M, Line 17 — see minnesota/deductions/subtractions/education/education-savings-account-subtraction.md
## How it works
1. Total 2026 contributions to all 529 accounts
2. Subtract total 2026 withdrawals from accounts you own or contributed to (transfers and rollovers excluded)
3. Net contribution × 50%, limited by the income-based maximum
## Credit or subtraction
- Below $98,410 AGI the credit generally wins
- Above $123,410 (single) or $202,140 (MFJ) only the subtraction is available
- Nonresidents and people who can be claimed as a dependent can take the subtraction but NOT the credit
## Recapture
If 529 money is withdrawn for expenses that are not qualified for Minnesota, part of prior credits and subtractions is recaptured (50% of the credit share and 10% of the subtraction share of the nonqualified amount) as an additional tax on Form M1, Line 14a.
## Minnesota nonconformity
For Minnesota, K-12 tuition and expenses and (starting with payments after July 4, 2025) postsecondary CREDENTIALING expenses are NOT qualified 529 expenses, even though they are federally. If 529 money paid for them, an ADDITION is required on Schedule M1M (lesser of the distribution or earnings on Form 1099-Q).
## Required Information
- Financial institution, account number, and 2026 contribution for each 529 account
- 2026 distributions (Form 1099-Q)
- AGI
- Lifetime totals of prior 529 credits, subtractions, and contributions (needed for recapture)
## Questions
- How much did you contribute to 529 plans in 2026, and did you withdraw anything?
- What was your AGI?
- Did any 529 withdrawal pay K-12 tuition or a credential program?
- Can someone claim you as a dependent, or were you a nonresident?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and the subtraction
- Not netting withdrawals against contributions
- Missing the Minnesota addition for 529 money used for K-12 or credentialing
- Claiming the credit as a nonresident or dependent
- Not keeping lifetime records needed for recapture
## Prompt
- Do I get a Minnesota tax break for 529 contributions?
- Should I take the Minnesota 529 credit or the subtraction?
