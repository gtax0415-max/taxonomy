# Employer Transit Pass Credit (Schedule ETP)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an employer (including a sole proprietor or pass-through owner) that buys transit passes and gives or resells them to employees.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: 30% of the difference between the cost of the passes and the price charged to employees
## Qualifying passes
- A pass, token, farecard, voucher, or similar item for transportation at regular or reduced fares
- Used in Minnesota
- Transportation provided by a public or private mass transit facility, or by a business transporting people for hire in a vehicle seating at least six adults besides the driver
## Required Information
- Cost of passes and amounts recovered from employees
- K-schedule amounts for pass-through shares
## Questions
- Do you buy transit passes for your employees in Minnesota?
## Common Errors
- Computing 30% of total cost instead of cost minus employee charges
## Prompt
- I give my employees Metro Transit passes.
