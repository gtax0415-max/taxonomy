# Historic Structure Rehabilitation Credit
## Description
Refundable, transferable Minnesota credit that parallels the federal Historic Rehabilitation Credit (IRC section 47, Form 3468) for certified rehabilitation of historic buildings in Minnesota.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: generally equals the federal historic rehabilitation credit for the project
PAYOUT: five equal annual installments beginning the year the building is placed in service (applications submitted to SHPO after December 31, 2018)
AVAILABILITY: projects certified by SHPO before July 1, 2031
## Key eligibility rules
- Qualified improvements to a historic property in Minnesota that also qualify for the federal credit
- Applied to SHPO for certification BEFORE starting the project
- SHPO issued a credit certificate at completion
- Only the certificate holder, the assignee, or owners of a pass-through entity that holds it may claim
## Grant in lieu and assignment
- A grant taken in lieu of the credit bars the credit for that amount (a partial grant allows a partial credit)
- The original recipient may assign the certificate ONCE, to ONE taxpayer, before any installment is claimed; notify Revenue within 30 days (Revenue Notice #12-06)
- A taxpayer who assigned the credit cannot claim the assigned portion
## Federal interaction
Ties to federal Form 3468; the Minnesota amount follows the federal credit for the project. Confirm the federal amount on the 2026 Form 3468.
## Required Information
- SHPO certificate and NPS project number
- Installment year (1–5)
- K-schedule amounts for pass-through owners
## Questions
- Did you complete a certified historic rehabilitation in Minnesota, and which installment year is 2026?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the whole credit in one year instead of 1/5
- Missing the NPS project number on Line 7a
- Claiming after assigning the certificate
## Prompt
- I own part of a partnership that renovated a historic building.
