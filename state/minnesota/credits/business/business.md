# Business and Investment Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits that reach individuals through a pass-through entity (Schedules KPI, KS, KF), by assignment, or through a certificate from a state agency. Most require a certificate number on the return.

## General rules
- Part-year residents and nonresidents generally prorate the nonrefundable certificate credits by the Schedule M1NR line 30 percentage
- Prior-year carryovers go on Schedule M1C, Line 17 with the credit name and certificate number
## Questions
- Did you receive a Schedule KPI, KS, or KF showing Minnesota credits?
- Did a state agency issue you a credit certificate, or did you buy an assigned credit?
- Do you have unused credits carried over from prior years?
## Prompt
- My K-1 from a Minnesota partnership shows a credit.
- I received a Minnesota tax credit certificate.
