# Angel Investment Tax Credit (Small Business Investment Credit)
## Description
Refundable 25% credit for qualified equity investments in Minnesota high-technology startups, administered by DEED. Revenue still lists it, but it has NO funding for tax year 2026.
## 2026 status
- No allocation exists for investments in 2025 or 2026. DEED states the application period is closed with no funding committed, and the last certificates (for 2024 investments) were issued February 19, 2025
- A 2025 bill (H.F. 2723) proposed $10 million a year for 2025–2028. Neither Revenue's 2026 tax-law-change summary nor the 2026 forms show a revival
- The 2026 Schedule M1REF Line 9, which carried this credit on the 2025 schedule, is "intentionally left blank"
## Historical terms (for reference)
25% of the qualified investment; maximum $125,000 per person ($250,000 MFJ); refundable; nonresidents could qualify. Credits can be revoked if the investment is not held for the required period.
## What to do for 2026
- New 2026 investments: no Minnesota credit
- A prior-year certificate cannot be claimed on the 2026 return; it belongs on the return for the year of the investment
- Watch for revocation if a prior-year credited investment is sold early
## Questions
- Did you invest in a Minnesota startup and expect an angel credit? (Not available for 2026)
## Common Errors
- Claiming the angel credit on a 2026 return
## Prompt
- Is the Minnesota angel tax credit available this year?
