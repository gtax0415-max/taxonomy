# SEED Capital Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for investing in innovative, DEED-certified businesses in Minnesota border cities next to North and South Dakota.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: 45% of the amount invested
MAXIMUM: $112,500 per year
CARRYFORWARD: up to 4 years
## Key eligibility rules
- Investment in a DEED-certified qualified business located in Breckenridge, Dilworth, East Grand Forks, Moorhead, or Ortonville
- Credits are limited to border-city enterprise zone allocations each year
- The investment must stay in the business for the required holding period, and the business must use it for plant, equipment, R&D, marketing and sales, or working capital
## Required Information
- DEED credit certificate (enclose with Form M1)
## Questions
- Did you invest in a DEED-certified business in one of the five border cities?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without the DEED certificate
- Exceeding $112,500 in a year
## Prompt
- I invested in a startup in East Grand Forks.
