# Credit for Increasing Research Activities (individual pass-through share)
## Description
An individual's share of a pass-through entity's Minnesota research credit. Used first as a nonrefundable credit; the unused CURRENT-YEAR portion can be partly refunded by election.
## How it works (Worksheet for Line 16d)
1. Current-year credit allowed up to Minnesota regular tax remaining after other nonrefundable credits
2. Unused current-year credit → if electing, 25% is refundable (M1REF Line 13, box checked)
3. The other 75% carries forward up to 15 years
4. Prior-year CARRYOVERS cannot be used to compute the refundable portion
## 2026 federal conformity note
Minnesota CONFORMS to OBBBA's immediate expensing of domestic research and experimental expenditures for partnerships, S corporations, estates, trusts, and individuals. It DECOUPLED only for C corporations (80% addition, recovered over four years).
## Required Information
- K-schedules showing the research credit
- Prior-year carryovers
## Questions
- Does a pass-through you own report a Minnesota research credit?
- Do you want to elect the refundable portion?
## Common Errors
- Using carryovers in the refundable computation
- Entering an amount on M1REF Line 13 without checking the election box
## Prompt
- My partnership passed through a Minnesota research credit.
