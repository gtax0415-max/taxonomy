# Film Production Credit
## Description
Assignable, nonrefundable credit for film, video, or digital media productions that spend significantly in Minnesota, allocated by Explore Minnesota Film.
## 2026 figures
CREDIT: 25% of eligible production costs
MINIMUM SPEND: $1,000,000 of eligible costs within 12 consecutive months
PROGRAM CAP: $25 million a year, first come, first served
CARRYFORWARD: 5 years
## Key rules
- Apply through Explore Minnesota Film; claim with the certificate for the year the expenses were incurred
- ASSIGNMENT: the credit may be transferred to another taxpayer before any of it is claimed; send the completed assignment (page 2 of the certificate) to Revenue within 30 days
- Individuals usually receive it through a pass-through entity (KS/KPI line 28, KF line 35) or by assignment
## Required Information
- TAXC- certificate number and amount; assignment confirmation if purchased
## Questions
- Did you receive a film production credit certificate directly, through a pass-through, or by assignment?
## Common Errors
- Claiming part of the credit and then trying to assign the rest
- Not notifying Revenue within 30 days of an assignment
## Prompt
- I bought a Minnesota film tax credit.
