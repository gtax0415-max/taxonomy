# Enterprise Zone Credit (Border-Cities Enterprise Zone)
## Description
Refundable credit for businesses that create investment and jobs in Minnesota's Border-Cities Enterprise Zones. For individuals it reaches sole proprietors, partners, and S corporation shareholders. Allocations are made by the city's enterprise zone administrator and certified by DEED.
## Qualifying locations
Enterprise zone cities of Breckenridge, Dilworth, East Grand Forks, Moorhead, and Ortonville, plus the development zone of Taylors Falls.
## What the program can provide
Income tax credits for debt financing on new construction or expanded facilities and for adding or retaining employees (also property tax credits and a sales tax exemption on construction materials, which are not on Form M1).
## Excluded businesses
Recreation or entertainment facilities; property used by a financial institution; public utility property; fraternal or veterans' organization property; franchised retail food or beverage facilities required to be in Minnesota.
## Key rules
- Apply through the city's enterprise zone administrator before claiming
- Enclose DEED's certification with Form M1
- Include amounts reported on Schedule KS or KPI line 33
## Required Information
- DEED certification and credit amount
## Questions
- Does your business operate in a Minnesota border-city enterprise zone with DEED certification?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without DEED certification
## Prompt
- My business is in Moorhead's enterprise zone.
