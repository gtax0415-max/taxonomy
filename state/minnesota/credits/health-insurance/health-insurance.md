# Health Insurance Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Minnesota credits connected to insurance coverage.
## Credits in this folder
- Long-Term Care Insurance Credit (long-term-care-insurance-credit.md) — Schedule M1LTI → Schedule M1C, Line 2 → Form M1, Line 16. Nonrefundable
## No state premium tax credit
Minnesota has NO state counterpart to the federal Premium Tax Credit on Form M1. Marketplace (MNsure) premium subsidies are reconciled only on the federal Form 8962. The federal enhanced PTC expired after 2025 (see federal/credits/health-insurance/premium-tax-credit.md); that change affects Minnesota only indirectly, through federal AGI.
## MNsure checkbox
Form M1 carries a checkbox ("I do not have health insurance and want to learn what choices I have") that authorizes Revenue to share limited return information (names, household size, dependents' birth dates, address, AGI, email, phone, county) with MNsure so it can contact the filer about free or lower-cost coverage. It is not a credit and does not change tax.
