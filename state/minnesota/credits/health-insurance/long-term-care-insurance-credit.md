# Long-Term Care Insurance Credit (Schedule M1LTI)
## Description
Small nonrefundable credit for premiums paid on a qualified long-term care insurance policy, to the extent the premiums were not already fully deducted as an itemized medical expense on Schedule M1SA. No federal credit counterpart (federally, LTC premiums are only a medical expense deduction, subject to age-based limits).
## 2026 figures (Minn. Stat. § 290.0672 — not indexed)
CREDIT: 25% of qualifying premiums not deducted
MAXIMUM: $100 per person ($200 on a joint return when premiums are paid for both spouses)
## Key eligibility rules
- The policy must qualify for the itemized medical deduction on Schedule M1SA (ignoring the income test)
- The policy must have a LIFETIME long-term care benefit limit of $100,000 or more
- Premiums that produced a full deduction on Schedule M1SA cannot also produce the credit
- Only ONE policy per qualified beneficiary (the taxpayer or spouse) counts
- Part-year residents and nonresidents prorate by the Schedule M1NR percentage
## Required Information
- Premiums paid in 2026 for each insured person
- Policy lifetime benefit limit
- Amount of LTC premiums deducted on Schedule M1SA, if itemizing
## Questions
- Did you pay long-term care insurance premiums in 2026?
- Does the policy have at least a $100,000 lifetime benefit?
- Are you itemizing medical expenses on your Minnesota return?
## Common Errors
- Claiming premiums already fully deducted on Schedule M1SA
- Claiming a policy with a lifetime limit under $100,000
- Claiming more than $100 per person
