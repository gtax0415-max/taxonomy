# Income-Based Credits (Minnesota)
## Description
Credits for low- and moderate-income working Minnesotans where the amount depends mainly on earned income and family size.
## Credits in this folder
- Working Family Credit (working-family-credit.md) — Schedule M1CWFC → Schedule M1REF, Line 2 → Form M1, Line 22. Fully refundable
- The Credit for Qualifying Older Children, which is part of the Working Family Credit by law, has its own file at family-dependent/qualifying-older-children-credit.md
## Notes
- The Working Family Credit is Minnesota's counterpart to the federal Earned Income Credit and follows federal EIC eligibility rules, with two expansions: an ITIN is acceptable in place of an SSN, and childless workers qualify from age 19 (federal: 25)
- It is computed TOGETHER with the Minnesota Child Tax Credit (family-dependent/child-tax-credit.md) on the same schedule, and the two share a single combined phase-out. Always evaluate them together
- For TY2024 returns, the combined child and working family credits cost about $755.5 million; about 20% of that was working family credit (Minnesota House Research, May 2026)
