# AR4 Payer Lists
## Description
Schedule AR4 lists interest and dividends by payer, plus income not taxed by Arkansas. Required when interest or dividends total $1,500 or more.

## Layout
- Part I: taxable interest by payer
- Part II: taxable dividends by payer
- Part III: income not subject to Arkansas tax (Social Security, railroad retirement, ministers' housing allowance, other)
- Columns: (A) primary/joint, (B) spouse (Status 4 only), (C) Arkansas-only (part-year and nonresident)
- Template: ten payer rows each for interest and dividends

## Notes
- Attach when interest or dividends total $1,500 or more
- No 2026 AR4 posted yet; the 2025 layout is used

## Required Information
- Payer names and amounts
- Status 4: which spouse owns each account

## Questions
- Is your total interest or dividends $1,500 or more?
- Who owns each account (you, your spouse, or jointly)?
- Do you have exempt income such as Social Security for Part III?

## Common Errors
- Listing U.S. Treasury interest in Part I
- Putting a jointly owned Status 4 account entirely in one column
- More than ten payers without an attached schedule

## Prompt
- I have accounts at several banks. Do I list each one?
- Do I need Schedule AR4?
