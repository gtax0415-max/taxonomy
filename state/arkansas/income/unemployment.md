# Unemployment Compensation
## Description
Unemployment benefits, including federally funded benefits, are taxable in Arkansas.

## Notes
- Enter all unemployment received; attach Forms 1099-G
- Unemployment-for-sickness payments are excluded
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Every 1099-G box 1 amount
- Arkansas tax withheld on the 1099-G

## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment benefits in 2026, from Arkansas or another state?
- Was Arkansas tax withheld from your benefits?
- Did any payments come for sickness?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out benefits from another state
- Including state tax refunds (1099-G box 2) as unemployment
- Taxing unemployment-for-sickness payments

## Prompt
- I collected unemployment for three months.
- Is unemployment taxable in Arkansas?
