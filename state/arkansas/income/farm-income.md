# Farm Income (Schedule F)
## Description
Net farm income or loss from federal Schedule F.

## Notes
- Enter Schedule F net income or loss; attach Schedule F
- Partnership farm income goes on AR1050 with K-1s
- Federal bonus depreciation is not allowed; adjust on AR-OI
- USDA disaster and Farm Bill extension payments under the American Relief Act of 2025 are exempt (Act 696 of 2025); subtract them on AR-OI
- No other change for 2026

## Required Information
- Schedule F
- USDA payments received and the program
- Arkansas depreciation

## Questions
- Did you farm in 2026?
- Did you receive USDA disaster or relief payments under the American Relief Act of 2025?
- Did you claim bonus depreciation on farm equipment?

## Common Errors
- Taxing exempt American Relief Act payments
- Using federal bonus depreciation
- Reporting partnership farm income here instead of through AR1050

## Prompt
- I run a cattle farm.
- I received USDA disaster payments this year.
