# Military Retirement (Exempt)
## Description
Retirement benefits from the uniformed services are exempt. The gross amount is shown on line 17 for the primary or spouse but not added to income. It interacts with the $6,000 retirement exemption on line 18.

## Notes
- Enter the gross amount in the primary or spouse box, whatever the filing status; attach 1099-Rs
- A retiree cannot claim both the military exemption and a full $6,000 retirement exemption. If the military amount is under $6,000, only the remaining balance of the $6,000 applies on line 18
- Claiming this exclusion bars the Low Income Tax Table; the taxpayer may elect not to use it
- VA disability benefits are exempt separately and not entered here
- Act 508 of 2025 restated the military retirement and disability exemption; no change in reporting for 2026

## Required Information
- DFAS 1099-R for each retiree
- Other pension or IRA distributions for the same person

## Questions
- Do you receive military retired pay or a Survivor Benefit Plan annuity?
- Do you also have a civilian pension or IRA distributions?
- Do you receive VA disability compensation?

## Common Errors
- Adding military retirement into column A
- Claiming a full $6,000 on line 18 on top of the military exemption
- Entering VA disability compensation

## Prompt
- I'm a retired Air Force veteran. Is my pension taxed in Arkansas?
- I get military retirement and a civilian pension.
