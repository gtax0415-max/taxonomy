# Other Gains or Losses (Form 4797 Part II)
## Description
Ordinary gain or loss from selling business property, from federal Form 4797 Part II, adjusted for Arkansas depreciation differences. The capital loss limit does not apply.

## Notes
- Enter the Form 4797 Part II ordinary gain or loss; attach Form 4797 (and AR4684 if applicable)
- No $3,000 capital loss limit
- Gains figured with federal bonus or Section 179 amounts that Arkansas did not allow must be recomputed with Arkansas basis
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Form 4797
- Arkansas depreciation and basis for each property sold

## Questions
- Did you sell business equipment, vehicles, or rental property in 2026?
- Was the property depreciated differently for Arkansas?
- Was the property held more than a year?

## Common Errors
- Using federal basis when Arkansas depreciation differed
- Applying the capital loss limit
- Reporting a capital gain here instead of on AR1000D

## Prompt
- I sold a work truck used in my business.
- I sold rental property equipment at a loss.
