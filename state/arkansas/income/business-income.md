# Business or Professional Income (Schedule C)
## Description
Net profit or loss from federal Schedule C. Arkansas allows no deduction for one-half of self-employment tax and does not follow federal bonus depreciation.

## Notes
- Enter Schedule C net profit or loss; attach Schedule C
- No deduction for half of self-employment tax and no QBI deduction (see federal-only-deductions.md)
- Arkansas has not adopted IRC 168(k) bonus depreciation, including the OBBBA's permanent 100% bonus for 2026. Report the federal-versus-Arkansas difference on AR-OI
- Partnership business income goes on AR1050 with K-1s, not here
- Self-employed health insurance and retirement contributions are on AR1000ADJ lines 9 and 10

## Required Information
- Schedule C for each business
- Federal and Arkansas depreciation schedules
- Status 4: which spouse owns each business

## Questions
- Do you have self-employment or 1099 income?
- Did you claim bonus depreciation federally?
- Do you and your spouse each own a business?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting half of self-employment tax
- Using federal depreciation without the AR-OI adjustment
- Reporting a partnership's income here

## Prompt
- I'm a freelance designer with a Schedule C.
- I bought equipment and took bonus depreciation federally.
