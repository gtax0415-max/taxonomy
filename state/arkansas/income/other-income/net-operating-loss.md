# Net Operating Loss (AR1000NOL)
## Description
Deduction for a carryforward of an Arkansas net operating loss from earlier years, figured on AR1000NOL.

## How the loss is figured
- AGI − deductions = NOL (if negative)
- Add back nontaxable income not normally reported, less expenses incurred to earn it
- Personal credits, capital losses above capital gains, nonbusiness deductions above nonbusiness income, and NOLs from other years are adjusted as on the form
- AR1000NOL tracks each loss year: beginning balance, new loss, deduction claimed, amount expired, carryforward; a separate chart for the spouse's NOL
- Template note: AR1000NOL is not built; enter its result on AR-OI line 13

## Notes
- Carryforward only; Arkansas does NOT allow carrybacks, including for farm losses
- Carryforward period by loss year:
  - Losses in 2021 or later: 10 years
  - 2020 losses: 8 years
  - Losses before 2020: 5 years (now expired)
- Must be used in the first year it can be; a year cannot be skipped
- No 80% income limit is stated in the Arkansas instructions
- Partners and S corporation owners use their share of business losses; the entity itself cannot claim an NOL

## Required Information
- Each loss year, original loss, amounts used, and expired amounts
- Arkansas NOL records by loss year

## Questions
- Did you have a business or farm loss larger than your other income in an earlier year?
- How much of it have you already used?
- Have any of your losses expired?

## Common Errors
- Carrying a loss back
- Using the federal NOL instead of the Arkansas NOL
- Skipping a year when the NOL could have been used

## Prompt
- I had a big business loss in 2024 that I couldn't use.
- Can I carry my Arkansas loss back?
