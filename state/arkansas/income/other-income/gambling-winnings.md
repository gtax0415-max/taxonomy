# Gambling Winnings
## Description
All gambling winnings, other than lottery winnings, are added to Arkansas income. Losses are deductible only as an itemized deduction on AR3.

## Notes
- Enter total gambling winnings, with or without a W-2G; attach W-2Gs
- Lottery winnings go on line 5, not here
- Losses: AR3 line 27, up to winnings (see gambling-losses.md); never netted here
- Mississippi gambling tax paid is deductible on AR3 line 6

## Required Information
- All W-2Gs and other winnings
- Gambling log for winnings without a W-2G

## Questions
- Did you win money gambling in 2026, with or without a W-2G?
- Did you pay gambling tax in another state?
- Did any winnings come from the lottery?

## Common Errors
- Netting losses against winnings
- Including lottery winnings here
- Reporting only W-2G amounts

## Prompt
- I won $3,000 at a casino in Tunica.
- I won at a casino and also lost a lot.
