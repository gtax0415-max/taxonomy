# Federal Depreciation (Addition)
## Description
Arkansas computes depreciation its own way. The federal depreciation deducted on federal schedules is added back here, and the Arkansas depreciation is subtracted on AR-OI line 12.

## Notes
- Add back all federal depreciation claimed on federal schedules for the year; attach Form 4562
- Arkansas has not adopted IRC 168(k) bonus depreciation, including the OBBBA's permanent 100% bonus for property acquired after January 19, 2025, so the gap can be large for 2026
- Pair with state-depreciation.md (line 12)

## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 and asset schedules
- Arkansas depreciation for the same assets

## Questions
- Did you claim depreciation on a business, rental, or farm?
- Did you take bonus depreciation or Section 179 federally?
- Do you have Arkansas depreciation schedules?

## Common Errors
- Adding back federal depreciation without subtracting Arkansas depreciation
- Assuming the 2026 federal bonus carries over
- Adding back depreciation already adjusted on AR1000D for a sale

## Prompt
- I took 100% bonus depreciation on new equipment federally.
- How does Arkansas treat my federal depreciation?
