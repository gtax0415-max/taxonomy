# Other Subtractions
## Description
Any other loss or Arkansas-exempt amount not reported elsewhere. Attach an explanation and schedules.

## Notes
Possible uses for 2026:
- USDA disaster and Farm Bill extension payments under the American Relief Act of 2025 (Act 696 of 2025), if included in Schedule F (confirm the line DFA designates)
- Other income exempted by an Arkansas act but included in federal amounts
- Attach an explanation and schedules

## Required Information
- Each item, amount, and the law that exempts it
- Written explanation for the attached schedule

## Questions
- Do you have income included elsewhere that Arkansas exempts?
- Do you have documents showing the income is exempt?
- Was the income included in federal amounts?

## Common Errors
- Using this line for items that have their own line (depreciation, NOL, PTE, NIL)
- No explanation attached
- Subtracting income that was never included

## Prompt
- I received USDA disaster payments that Arkansas exempts.
- Where do I subtract Arkansas-exempt income?
