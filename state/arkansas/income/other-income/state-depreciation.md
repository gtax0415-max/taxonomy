# State Depreciation (Subtraction)
## Description
The depreciation Arkansas allows, recomputed without federal bonus depreciation. Subtracted to replace the federal depreciation added back on line 1.

## Notes
- Enter Arkansas-allowed depreciation not claimed elsewhere; attach a schedule
- No IRC 168(k) bonus depreciation
- Arkansas's Section 179 limit may differ from the federal 2026 limit; confirm against the 2026 instructions
- Keep a separate Arkansas basis for each asset; it affects gain on sale (AR1000D and Form 4797)

## Required Information
- Arkansas depreciation schedule by asset
- Federal Form 4562 for comparison

## Questions
- Do you keep separate Arkansas depreciation records?
- Did you take bonus depreciation federally?
- Did you sell any depreciated assets?

## Common Errors
- Including bonus depreciation
- Forgetting to carry the Arkansas basis to a later sale
- Using federal Section 179 amounts without checking the Arkansas limit

## Prompt
- How do I figure Arkansas depreciation if I took bonus depreciation federally?
- Do I need separate Arkansas depreciation records?
