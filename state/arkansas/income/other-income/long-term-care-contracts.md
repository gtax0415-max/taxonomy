# Long-term Care Insurance Contracts
## Description
The taxable portion of long-term care insurance benefits is added to Arkansas income.

## Notes
- Add only the taxable portion; attach Forms 1099-LTC
- Reimbursement-type benefits for qualified care are generally not taxable; per diem benefits above the federal per diem limit and actual costs are taxable (figure on federal Form 8853)
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- 1099-LTC and policy type
- Qualified care costs

## Questions
- Did you receive long-term care insurance benefits in 2026?
- Were they paid per day or as reimbursement of costs?
- Did the benefits exceed the care costs?

## Common Errors
- Adding the full benefit instead of the taxable portion
- Not completing federal Form 8853 first
- Treating reimbursement-type benefits as taxable

## Prompt
- My mother's long-term care policy paid her nursing home.
- I receive daily long-term care benefits.
