# Foreign Earned Income Exclusion
## Description
Subtracts foreign earned income excluded on federal Form 2555. Because Arkansas wages start from the W-2, the exclusion has to be subtracted here.

## Amounts
- Federal maximum exclusion: $132,900 (2025: $130,000) (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)
- Enter the amount from Form 2555 line 42; attach Form 2555

## Notes
- The taxpayer must meet the federal bona fide residence or physical presence test
- The housing exclusion follows the federal form

## Required Information
- Federal Form 2555
- Foreign earned income and days abroad

## Questions
- Did you live and work abroad in 2026?
- Did you claim the foreign earned income exclusion federally?
- Do you meet the bona fide residence or physical presence test?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 $130,000 maximum
- Not attaching Form 2555
- Subtracting income that wasn't excluded federally

## Prompt
- I worked in Germany for most of 2026 but I'm still an Arkansas resident.
- I work overseas but kept my Arkansas home.
