# Cancellation of Debt
## Description
Canceled debt is generally taxable and is added on AR-OI, unless an exclusion applies.

## Notes
- Enter the 1099-C amount
- Not taxable in Arkansas: student loans forgiven under Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF) and student loans discharged for total and permanent disability. The list is "including but not limited to"
- The broad federal exclusion for other student loan forgiveness (2021–2025) ended after 2025 federally; confirm Arkansas treatment of 2026 forgiveness with the loan servicer's letter
- Insolvency and bankruptcy exclusions are not addressed in the Arkansas instructions; confirm before excluding

## Required Information
- 1099-C and the reason for cancellation
- Insolvency or bankruptcy records, if an exclusion is claimed

## Questions
- Was any debt forgiven or canceled in 2026?
- Was it a student loan? Under PSLF or for disability?
- Were you insolvent or in bankruptcy?

## Common Errors
- Adding PSLF or disability discharges
- Excluding other debt without support
- Excluding debt under a federal rule Arkansas may not follow without support

## Prompt
- My student loans were forgiven under PSLF.
- A credit card company canceled $4,000 of my debt.
