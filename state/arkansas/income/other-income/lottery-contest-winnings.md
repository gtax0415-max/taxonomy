# Lottery and Contest Winnings
## Description
Lottery winnings and contest prizes are added to Arkansas income on their own line.

## Notes
- Enter lottery and contest winnings; attach W-2Gs
- Noncash prizes at fair market value
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- W-2G / 1099-MISC amounts and prize values
- Fair market value of noncash prizes

## Questions
- Did you win a lottery prize, raffle, or contest in 2026?
- Did you win a noncash prize like a car or trip?
- Was Arkansas tax withheld?

## Common Errors
- Reporting lottery winnings on the gambling line
- Leaving out noncash prizes
- Deducting lottery losses here instead of on AR3

## Prompt
- I won $10,000 in the Arkansas Scholarship Lottery.
- I won a car in a contest.
