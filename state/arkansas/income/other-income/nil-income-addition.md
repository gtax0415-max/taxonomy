# Name, Image and Likeness Income (Addition)
## Description
All income earned under a name, image, and likeness (NIL) agreement is reported here first. The exempt portion paid by a school is then subtracted on line 17.

## Notes
- Enter total NIL income from all sources
- NIL income already on Schedule C should not be counted twice
- Act 839 of 2025 exempts NIL compensation paid to a student-athlete by the institution (including revenue-share payments); subtract that portion on line 17

## Required Information
- All NIL payments by payer
- NIL agreements and 1099s

## Questions
- Did you earn NIL income in 2026?
- Which payments came from your school versus brands or collectives?
- Is any NIL income already on Schedule C?

## Common Errors
- Reporting only the taxable portion here
- Double-counting NIL already on Schedule C
- Leaving out noncash NIL compensation (products, cars)

## Prompt
- I'm a college athlete with NIL deals from my school and a local business.
- How is NIL income taxed in Arkansas?
