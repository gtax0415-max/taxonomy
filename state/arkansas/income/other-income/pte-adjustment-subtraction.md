# Pass-Through Entity Adjustment (Subtraction)
## Description
When a partnership or S corporation ELECTED to pay Arkansas tax at the entity level, the owner's share of the income is exempt to the owner. This line SUBTRACTS that exempt INCOME (excluding capital gains), which is otherwise included through Schedule E and/or line 7.

## Notes
- Subtract exempt income from electing entities, excluding capital gains, from Schedule E and/or line 7
- Attach AR K-1 and/or AR1099PT
- Capital gains from electing entities are adjusted on AR1000D
- No change in the owner's adjustment for 2026

## Required Information
- Each electing entity and the owner's share of income
- AR K-1 or AR1099PT for each electing entity

## Questions
- Do you own part of an entity that paid Arkansas tax at the entity level?
- How much of its income was passed to you on Schedule E?
- Did the entity include capital gains?

## Common Errors
- Paying tax twice on income the entity already paid tax on
- Using this line for losses (exempt LOSSES are added on line 7)
- Including capital gains

## Prompt
- My partnership paid the Arkansas PTE tax. Do I still pay tax on my share?
- My K-1 shows income from an entity that paid Arkansas PTE tax.
