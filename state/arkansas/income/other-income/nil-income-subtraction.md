# Name, Image and Likeness Income (Subtraction)
## Description
Subtracts the NIL compensation that Arkansas exempts: amounts a student-athlete receives FROM AN INSTITUTION OF HIGHER EDUCATION for use of their name, image, or likeness.

## Notes
- Exempt only when paid by the institution (Act 839 of 2025, including revenue-share payments), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- NIL from brands, collectives, or other third parties stays taxable
- Cannot exceed the amount reported on line 9

## Required Information
- Institution-paid NIL amounts
- Total NIL income reported on line 9

## Questions
- How much of your NIL income was paid directly by your college?
- Is the athlete a student at the paying institution?
- Was the full NIL amount reported on line 9?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting third-party NIL
- Subtracting more than was added on line 9
- Subtracting NIL paid by a collective

## Prompt
- My university paid me under a revenue-sharing NIL deal.
- Is my school's NIL revenue share taxable in Arkansas?
