# Other Income (Addition)
## Description
Any other income not reported elsewhere on the return. Attach an explanation and schedules.

## Notes
Examples of other income:
- Reimbursement of prior-year medical expenses that were itemized and gave a tax benefit
- Recovered bad debts
- Other income items with no other line
- Attach an explanation and supporting schedules
- Federal exclusions Arkansas has not adopted are added here; for example, an employer's contribution to a child's Trump Account (W-2 box 12 code TA, IRC 128) excluded under the 2025 federal act
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Each item, amount, and supporting statement
- Written explanation for the attached schedule

## Questions
- Did you receive any income that doesn't fit another line?
- Did you recover a bad debt or prior-year deduction?
- Do you have records of the income?

## Common Errors
- Putting income here that has its own line (gambling, lottery, NIL, canceled debt)
- No explanation attached
- Leaving out income because no form was received

## Prompt
- I got a refund of medical bills I deducted last year.
- I collected a debt I wrote off years ago.
