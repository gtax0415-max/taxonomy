# HSA and MSA Taxable Distributions
## Description
HSA or Archer MSA distributions not used for qualified medical expenses are added to Arkansas income.

## Notes
- Add the taxable portion (distributions not used for qualified medical expenses); attach Forms 1099-SA
- Distributions used for qualified medical expenses are not added
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- 1099-SA amounts
- Qualified medical expenses paid from the account

## Questions
- Did you take money out of an HSA or MSA in 2026?
- Was all of it used for medical expenses?
- Were you over 65 or disabled when you took the distribution?

## Common Errors
- Adding distributions used for medical costs
- Leaving out a nonqualified distribution
- Not keeping receipts for medical expenses paid

## Prompt
- I used HSA money to pay for a vacation.
- I took money out of my HSA for non-medical expenses.
