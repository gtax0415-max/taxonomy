# Scholarships, Fellowships and Stipends
## Description
Only the TAXABLE part of scholarships, fellowships, and stipends is added. Qualified amounts used for tuition, required fees, and required course materials are exempt.

## Notes
- Taxable: amounts used for room, board, travel, or as pay for services (teaching or research)
- Exempt: amounts used for tuition, required fees, and required books and supplies
- Attach the relevant form (W-2, 1042-S, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC, or 1098-T)
- Amounts already in W-2 wages are not added again

## Required Information
- Scholarship amounts and how they were used
- Qualified education expenses

## Questions
- Did you receive a scholarship, fellowship, or stipend in 2026?
- How much went to tuition and required fees versus housing and living costs?
- Were you required to teach or do research for the payment?

## Common Errors
- Adding the full scholarship
- Double-counting a stipend already on a W-2
- Treating amounts for required course materials as taxable

## Prompt
- My scholarship covers tuition and housing.
- I'm a grad student with a research stipend.
