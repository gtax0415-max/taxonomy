# Dividend Income
## Description
All dividends and other distributions on stock. Arkansas has no dividend exclusion and no lower rate for qualified dividends.

## Notes
- Enter all ordinary dividends (1099-DIV box 1a)
- Qualified dividends get no special treatment in Arkansas
- Capital gain distributions (box 2a) go to capital gains (AR1000D), not here
- Exempt-interest dividends from funds holding other states' bonds are taxable as interest in Arkansas
- Attach AR4 when total dividends are $1,500 or more
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Every 1099-DIV by payer
- Status 4: dividends split by spouse

## Questions
- Did you receive dividends from stocks or mutual funds?
- Do any 1099-DIVs show capital gain distributions or exempt-interest dividends?
- Do you own mutual funds that hold other states' municipal bonds?

## Common Errors
- Applying a qualified-dividend rate
- Including capital gain distributions here instead of AR1000D
- Not attaching AR4 at $1,500 or more

## Prompt
- I got dividends from my brokerage account.
- Are qualified dividends taxed differently in Arkansas?
