# Total Income
## Description
The sum of the income lines. Exempt amounts shown only in the primary/spouse boxes (military pay on line 9, military retirement on line 17) are not added.

## Notes
- Line 23 = lines 8 through 22 (column amounts)
- Status 4: computed separately for column A and column B
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Amounts on lines 8 and 10–22 by column
- Status 4: amounts split by spouse

## Questions
- Does every income source appear on a line?
- Are any amounts exempt military pay or retirement?
- Are you filing Status 4?

## Common Errors
- Adding the exempt military amounts
- Status 4: mixing the two columns
- Leaving out an income line

## Prompt
- What is my total income on the Arkansas return?
- Why doesn't my military pay count in total income?
