# Wages, Salaries and Tips
## Description
All wages, salaries, tips, and other employee compensation, with the W-2s attached. Arkansas taxes tips and overtime in full; the 2025 federal deductions for tips and overtime have no Arkansas equivalent.

## Notes
- Enter W-2 box 16 (Arkansas wages) from all W-2s; use box 1 when box 16 differs only because of federal pretax rules that Arkansas follows; attach the W-2s
- Also include tips not reported to the employer and wages received without a W-2 (records of tips, Form 4137, pay stubs)
- Tips and overtime pay are fully taxable in Arkansas (no adoption of IRC 224 or 225)
- Qualified moving-expense reimbursements stay excluded in Arkansas (pre-TCJA rules)
- Dependent care assistance exclusion stays at $5,000 in Arkansas (IRC 129 as of January 1, 2005), not the federal $7,500
- Ministers: the housing allowance is excluded only up to the home's fair rental value; show the excluded amount on AR4 Part III
- Active-duty military pay goes on line 9, not here
- Elective 401(k) and 403(b) deferrals (W-2 box 12 codes D and E) stay excluded, as on the federal return

## Required Information
- Every W-2 for taxpayer and spouse
- Status 4: wages split by spouse

## Questions
- How many W-2s did you and your spouse receive?
- Did you receive tips or overtime?
- Are you a minister with a housing allowance?
- Did your employer give you more than $5,000 of dependent care benefits?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting federal tips or overtime deductions
- Putting active-duty military pay here
- Using federal box 1 when Arkansas box 16 differs (e.g., dependent care over $5,000)
- Not attaching W-2s

## Prompt
- I work two jobs and got two W-2s.
- Are my tips taxed in Arkansas?
