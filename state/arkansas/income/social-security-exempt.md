# Social Security and Railroad Retirement (Exempt)
## Description
Social Security and railroad retirement benefits are not taxed by Arkansas. They are listed on AR4 Part III with other exempt income and counted for the Low Income Tax Table test.

## Notes
- Exempt: Social Security, VA benefits, workers' compensation, and railroad retirement (tier I, tier II, vested dual benefit, supplemental annuity)
- NOT exempt: private railroad pensions reported on a regular 1099-R (line 18)
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- SSA-1099 and RRB-1099 amounts
- Private railroad pension 1099-R, if any

## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security or railroad retirement?
- Do you receive a private railroad pension?
- Are you checking eligibility for the Low Income Tax Table?

## Common Errors
- Adding the federal taxable Social Security amount to Arkansas income
- Treating a private railroad pension as exempt
- Leaving Social Security out of total income for the Low Income Tax Table test

## Prompt
- Is my Social Security taxed in Arkansas?
- Do I report my railroad retirement in Arkansas?
