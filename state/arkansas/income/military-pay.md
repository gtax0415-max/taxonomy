# Military Pay (Exempt)
## Description
Active-duty military pay is exempt from Arkansas income tax. The gross amount is shown on line 9 for the primary taxpayer and spouse but is NOT added to income.

## Notes
- Exempt: active-duty service pay, including active duty in the National Guard and Reserve units
- Enter the gross amount on line 9 in the primary or spouse box, not in column A; attach the W-2s
- A return must be filed to claim the exemption
- Claiming this exclusion bars the Low Income Tax Table; the taxpayer may elect not to use the exclusion to qualify for that table
- Act 508 of 2025 restated the exemption for military compensation and benefits; no change to how it is reported for 2026

## Required Information
- Military W-2s for each service member
- Active-duty periods (Guard/Reserve)

## Questions
- Were you on active duty in 2026, including Guard or Reserve activation?
- Do you have civilian wages as well?
- Would the Low Income Tax Table give you a lower tax?

## Common Errors
- Adding military pay into column A income
- Putting civilian wages on line 9
- Not filing because the pay is exempt (no refund of withholding)
- Using the exclusion when the Low Income Tax Table would be better

## Prompt
- I'm active duty Army. Is my pay taxed in Arkansas?
- I'm in the National Guard and was activated this year.
