# Interest Income
## Description
Taxable interest. Arkansas differs from federal: interest on U.S. obligations is exempt, and interest on bonds of OTHER states and their subdivisions is TAXABLE.

## Notes
- Taxable: bank, credit union, and savings and loan interest; notes and mortgages; corporate bonds; bonds of other states and their cities and counties
- Exempt: interest on U.S. obligations (Treasury bills, notes, bonds, I/EE bonds) and on Arkansas and its political subdivisions
- Attach AR4 when total interest is $1,500 or more
- Early withdrawal penalties are subtracted on AR1000ADJ line 11, not netted here
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Every 1099-INT/1099-OID, split by payer
- Box 3 (U.S. Treasury) and box 8 (tax-exempt) amounts, with the issuing state for box 8
- Status 4: interest split by spouse

## Questions
- Did you receive interest from U.S. Treasury or savings bonds?
- Do you hold municipal bonds from states other than Arkansas?
- Is your total interest $1,500 or more?

## Common Errors
- Taxing U.S. Treasury interest
- Leaving out out-of-state municipal bond interest (federally tax-exempt but taxable in Arkansas)
- Netting the early withdrawal penalty against interest
- Not attaching AR4 at $1,500 or more

## Prompt
- I have interest from savings bonds and a bank account.
- I own Texas municipal bonds. Are they taxable in Arkansas?
