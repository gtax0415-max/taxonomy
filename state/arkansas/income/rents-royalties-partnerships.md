# Rents, Royalties, Partnerships, Estates and Trusts (Schedule E)
## Description
Net rental, royalty, partnership, S corporation, estate, and trust income or loss from federal Schedule E.

## Notes
- Enter the Schedule E total; attach Schedule E
- Federal bonus depreciation is not allowed in Arkansas; adjust on AR-OI
- If an entity elected to pay Arkansas pass-through entity tax, back out the exempt income or loss on AR-OI (lines 7 and 16)
- Passive activity loss limits follow federal Form 8582, including the $25,000 special allowance for active participation (phased out between $100,000 and $150,000 of modified AGI)
- Short-term rental income reported on Form 1099-K is included when the federal return reports it on Schedule E
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Schedule E and all K-1s
- AR K-1 or AR1099PT for any electing entity
- Arkansas depreciation for rental property

## Questions
- Do you own rental property or receive royalties?
- Do you have K-1s from partnerships, S corporations, estates, or trusts?
- Did any entity pay Arkansas pass-through entity tax?

## Common Errors
- Double-taxing income from an electing pass-through entity
- Using federal bonus depreciation
- Leaving out a K-1 from an estate or trust
- Deducting a passive rental loss that federal Form 8582 suspends

## Prompt
- I own two rental houses.
- I received a K-1 from an S corporation.
