# Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
Arkansas AGI: total income less Arkansas adjustments. Used for the IRA and student loan phase-outs and other Arkansas tests. It is NOT the same as federal AGI.

## Notes
- Line 25 = line 23 − line 24
- Status 4: separate for each column
- No change for 2026
- Differs from federal AGI because of exempt military pay and retirement, the $6,000 exemption, the 50% capital gain exclusion, interest differences, no half-SE-tax deduction, and Arkansas-only adjustments

## Required Information
- Total income from line 23
- Adjustments from AR1000ADJ line 19

## Questions
- Are you comparing this with your federal AGI?
- Do you have Arkansas adjustments on AR1000ADJ?
- Do you have exempt military or retirement income?

## Common Errors
- Copying federal AGI
- Using federal AGI for Arkansas phase-out tests
- Subtracting the standard deduction before line 25

## Prompt
- Why is my Arkansas AGI different from my federal AGI?
- How do I figure my Arkansas AGI?
