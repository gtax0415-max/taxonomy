# Alimony and Separate Maintenance Received
## Description
Alimony or separate maintenance received under a court order. Taxable in Arkansas whatever the date of the decree, unlike the federal rule for instruments executed after 2018.

## Notes
- Enter the total received under a court order
- Child support and property settlements are not income
- The payer deducts the same payments on AR1000ADJ line 12
- No change for 2026 (confirm when the 2026 booklet is released)

## Required Information
- Court order and amounts received
- Any part designated as child support

## Questions
- Did you receive alimony or separate maintenance under a court order in 2026?
- Is any of it child support?
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed?

## Common Errors
- Leaving it off because it isn't taxable federally (post-2018 divorce)
- Including child support
- Including a property settlement

## Prompt
- I receive alimony from a 2021 divorce. Is it taxed in Arkansas?
- My ex pays me $1,500 a month in alimony.
