# Head of Household
## Description
Status 3. Gives an extra personal tax credit on line 7A. If the qualifying person is your child but not your dependent, the child's name is entered next to the box.

## Amounts
- Standard deduction: $2,540 (template estimate for 2026; 2025 was $2,470)
- Additional personal tax credit on line 7A: $29 (DFA's 2026 withholding formula still uses $29; confirm in the 2026 booklet)

## Eligibility
- Unmarried or legally separated on December 31, 2026 (married people who lived apart may be treated as unmarried), AND
- EITHER paid more than half the cost of keeping up the main home of a parent you can claim as a dependent (the parent need not live with you)
- OR paid more than half the cost of a home you lived in for more than half the year with a qualifying person: your unmarried child, grandchild, or stepchild; a married child who is your dependent; or another dependent

## Required Information
- Marital status on December 31, 2026
- Qualifying person's name, relationship, and months in the home
- Household costs and the taxpayer's share
- Child's name if the child is not your dependent

## Questions
- Were you unmarried or living apart from your spouse at the end of 2026?
- Who lived with you, and for how many months?
- Did you pay more than half the cost of keeping up the home?
- Is the child claimed as a dependent by the other parent?

## Common Errors
- Claiming HOH while married and living together
- Paying less than half the household costs
- Forgetting the extra HOH credit box on line 7A
- Leaving off the child's name when the child is not your dependent

## Prompt
- I'm a single parent. Can I file head of household in Arkansas?
- I support my mother who lives in her own home.
