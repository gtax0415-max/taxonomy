# Fiscal Year
## Description
The ending date for an individual who files on a fiscal year instead of the calendar year. Rare for individuals; the fiscal year must match the federal return.

## Notes
- Calendar-year 2026 return: leave blank; due April 15, 2027
- Fiscal-year return: due no later than three and one-half months after the year ends
- Template note: the template figures a calendar 2026 return only

## Required Information
- Fiscal year beginning and ending dates (same as federal)
- Federal return showing the fiscal year

## Questions
- Do you file your federal return on a fiscal year rather than January–December?
- When does your tax year end?
- Was this approved for your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Entering a fiscal year end for a calendar-year filer
- Using a fiscal year that differs from the federal return
- Using the calendar-year due date for a fiscal-year return

## Prompt
- My tax year ends June 30. How do I file in Arkansas?
- Can I file Arkansas on a fiscal year?
