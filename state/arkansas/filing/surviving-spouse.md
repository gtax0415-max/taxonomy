# Surviving Spouse with Dependent Child
## Description
Status 6, for a widow or widower with a dependent child in the two years after the spouse's death. Gives an extra personal tax credit on line 7A.

## Amounts
- Standard deduction: $2,540 (template estimate for 2026; 2025 was $2,470)
- Additional personal tax credit on line 7A: $29 (confirm in the 2026 booklet)

## Eligibility
- Spouse died in 2024 or 2025 (enter the year of death)
- Could have filed jointly or separately on the same return for the year the spouse died
- Did not remarry before the end of 2026
- Has a child, stepchild, adopted child, or foster child who is a dependent for 2026
- Paid more than half the cost of keeping up that child's main home for all of 2026 (temporary absences allowed)

## Notes
- If the spouse died in 2026, file jointly (Status 2) or Status 4 for 2026 instead; see deceased-taxpayer.md
- Surviving spouses who died in 2023 or earlier no longer qualify; use Status 1 or 3

## Required Information
- Year of the spouse's death
- Dependent child's name, SSN, and relationship
- Household costs

## Questions
- In what year did your spouse die?
- Have you remarried?
- Does a dependent child live with you all year?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 window (2023 or 2024 deaths) for 2026
- Claiming it in the year of death instead of filing jointly
- Claiming it without a dependent child
- Forgetting the extra credit box on line 7A

## Prompt
- My husband died last year and I have two kids at home.
- Can I still file as a surviving spouse in Arkansas?
