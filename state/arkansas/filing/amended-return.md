# Amended Return
## Description
Arkansas amends on the same AR1000F by checking the amended box. The corrected amounts replace the original entries, and two lines reconcile what was already paid or refunded.

## Lines
- Amended box: top right of page 1
- Line 42 (amended returns only): previous payments, meaning the total paid with the original return, billing notices, and earlier amended returns
- Line 45 (amended returns only): previous refund, meaning total refunds from the original and earlier amended returns
- Line 46 = line 44 (total payments) minus line 45

## Notes
- Do not amend until the original return has been processed
- Attach an explanation and supporting documents for each changed item
- If the IRS changes net taxable income, amend within 180 days of the IRS notice
- Refund claims: within three years of the filing date or two years of paying the tax, whichever is later
- No change for 2026
- Not needed to change an address (Account Change Form) or correct an SSN (contact DFA)
- Withholding and estimated payments still go on their normal lines; line 42 is only amounts PAID with returns or notices

## Required Information
- What changed and why, with documents
- All payments made with the original return and later notices
- All refunds already received
- IRS notice date, if the change came from the IRS

## Questions
- What needs to be corrected on your 2026 Arkansas return?
- Did the IRS change your federal return? When was the notice dated?
- How much did you pay, and how much was refunded, on the original return?

## Common Errors
- Entering only the changed items instead of the full corrected return
- Putting withholding on line 42 instead of line 39A/39B
- Forgetting the previous refund on line 45
- Missing the 180-day deadline after an IRS change
- Amending before the original was processed

## Prompt
- I got a corrected W-2 after I filed.
- The IRS changed my federal return. Do I need to amend Arkansas?
