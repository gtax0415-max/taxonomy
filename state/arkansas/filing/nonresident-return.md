# Part-year and Nonresident Return
## Description
Part-year residents and nonresidents with Arkansas income file AR1000NR. It reports total income in column A (and B for Status 4) and the Arkansas portion in column C; tax is prorated by the Arkansas share of income.

## Notes
- Required for any Arkansas-source gross income, regardless of amount
- Part-year resident: moved into or out of Arkansas during 2026
- Nonresident: domicile outside Arkansas
- Texarkana, Texas residents use the border city exemption on AR1000ADJ line 1
- Out of scope for the template (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year residents with Arkansas income only)

## Required Information
- Dates of moving into or out of Arkansas
- Arkansas-source income by type
- Other state's return

## Questions
- Did you move into or out of Arkansas during 2026?
- Did you earn income in Arkansas while living elsewhere?
- Did you work remotely for an Arkansas employer?

## Common Errors
- Filing AR1000F as a part-year resident
- Putting all income in column C
- Not claiming credit for tax paid to the other state where allowed

## Prompt
- I moved from Arkansas to Texas in June.
- I live in Missouri but work in Arkansas.
