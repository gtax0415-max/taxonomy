# Filer Claimed as a Dependent on Another Return
## Description
A filer (often a student or young worker) who is claimed as a dependent on someone else's return. Arkansas's main effect is on the student loan interest deduction.

## Notes
- NO student loan interest deduction (AR1000ADJ line 6)
- The filer keeps their own personal tax credit ($29) on line 7A and the standard deduction ($2,540 template estimate for 2026), per the template's reading of the instructions (confirm in the 2026 booklet)
- The filer may still need to file: full-year resident single filers with gross income of $14,644 or more (2025 threshold; 2026 not yet published), or to claim a refund of withholding

## Required Information
- Whether anyone can claim the filer as a dependent
- Filer's income and withholding
- Any student loan interest paid

## Questions
- Can your parents or anyone else claim you as a dependent for 2026?
- Did you have Arkansas tax withheld from your pay?
- Did you pay student loan interest?

## Common Errors
- Claiming student loan interest while being someone's dependent
- Not filing when withholding could be refunded
- Claiming a dependent on your own return when you are someone's dependent

## Prompt
- My parents claim me. Do I need to file an Arkansas return?
- I'm a college student with a part-time job in Arkansas.
