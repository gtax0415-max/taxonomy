# Military Spouse and Nonresident Military
## Description
Under the Military Spouses Residency Relief Act, a military spouse whose domicile matches the servicemember's non-Arkansas Home of Record does not pay Arkansas tax on their wages. A nonresident servicemember stationed in Arkansas does not report military pay.

## Notes
- Military spouse: exempt if the servicemember's Home of Record is not Arkansas and the spouse's domicile is the same as the servicemember's
- Check the Military Spouse box and attach AR-MS and the servicemember's W-2 or LES
- The spouse gives ARW-4MS to the employer each year to stop Arkansas withholding
- If Arkansas tax was withheld, the spouse files to get it refunded
- Nonresident servicemember: does not report military income; file AR-NRMILITARY to note no filing requirement
- Out of scope for the template (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year residents with Arkansas income only)

## Required Information
- Servicemember's Home of Record (W-2 or LES)
- Spouse's state of domicile and supporting documents
- Spouse's W-2 and any Arkansas withholding

## Questions
- Is your spouse in the military with a Home of Record outside Arkansas?
- Do you share that home state as your domicile?
- Was Arkansas tax withheld from your wages?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the exemption when the spouse's domicile differs from the servicemember's
- Not filing to recover withheld tax
- Not giving the employer ARW-4MS

## Prompt
- My husband is stationed at Little Rock AFB but we're Texas residents.
- I'm a military spouse working in Arkansas. Do I owe Arkansas tax?
