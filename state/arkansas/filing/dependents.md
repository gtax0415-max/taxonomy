# Dependents
## Description
Each dependent is listed on page 1 and earns a personal tax credit on line 7B. The dependent tests largely mirror the federal tests.

## Amounts
- Credit: $29 per dependent (DFA's 2026 withholding formula still uses $29; confirm in the 2026 booklet)
- Gross income test: under $5,300 for 2026 (2025: $5,200), following the federal qualifying-relative amount

## Eligibility
- The dependent receives over half of their support from you
- Gross income under $5,300, EXCEPT a child under 19 at year-end or a full-time student under 24 (enrolled at least five months)
- Relationship: child, stepchild, parent, grandparent, grandchild, sibling, step-relatives, in-laws, aunt, uncle, nephew, niece, or an unrelated person who lived in your home all year
- A foreign citizen qualifies only if a U.S. resident or a resident of Mexico or Canada
- Not you or your spouse

## Fields
- First and last name, SSN, and relationship for each dependent
- Six lines on the form; attach a schedule for more

## Notes
- A dependent who died during the year still counts for the full credit
- A dependent with developmental disabilities may also qualify for a separate $500 credit (AR1000RC5), outside this file

## Required Information
- Name, SSN, relationship, and date of birth of each dependent
- Student status
- Dependent's gross income and support provided

## Questions
- Who did you support in 2026?
- How old are they, and are any full-time students?
- Did any dependent earn $5,300 or more?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 $5,200 income limit
- Listing yourself or your spouse
- Claiming a child the other parent claims
- Leaving out SSNs

## Prompt
- I have three kids. How many dependent credits do I get?
- My mother lives with me and gets Social Security. Is she my dependent?
