# Full-year Resident
## Description
Determines which return is filed. Full-year residents file AR1000F. Part-year residents and nonresidents with Arkansas income file AR1000NR.

## Definitions
- FULL-YEAR RESIDENT: lived in Arkansas all of 2026, or kept an Arkansas domicile or military Home of Record during the year
- PART-YEAR RESIDENT: established a domicile in Arkansas, or moved out, during 2026
- NONRESIDENT: did not make Arkansas their domicile
- DOMICILE: the permanent home you intend to return to. You have only one, and a temporary move does not change it

## Filing requirement
- Full-year residents file when gross income reaches the threshold for their status (2025: $14,644 single, $24,696 joint with 0–1 dependents; 2026 thresholds not yet published)
- Part-year and nonresidents with ANY Arkansas-source income must file AR1000NR

## Notes
- Template scope: full-year residents with Arkansas income only (team decision 2026-09-30). AR1000NR is out of scope

## Required Information
- Where the taxpayer lived during 2026 and the dates of any move
- State of domicile or military Home of Record

## Questions
- Did you live in Arkansas all of 2026?
- Did you move into or out of Arkansas during the year?
- (Military) Is Arkansas your Home of Record?

## Common Errors
- A part-year resident filing AR1000F
- Assuming a temporary job elsewhere ended Arkansas residency
- A nonresident with Arkansas income not filing

## Prompt
- I moved to Arkansas in July.
- I'm in the military and my Home of Record is Arkansas.
