# Names, SSNs, Address and Contact
## Description
Identifying information at the top of page 1: legal names and SSNs, mailing address, email addresses, and the check boxes that control mailings and extensions.

## Fields
- Primary and spouse legal names and SSNs, exactly as on Social Security cards
- Mailing address; check the box and enter the country name if the address is outside the U.S.
- Primary and secondary email
- Box to have a paper Form 1099-G mailed next year (Arkansas no longer mails 1099-Gs unless asked)
- Box to have a tax booklet mailed next year
- EXTENSION box: check if a federal extension (Form 4868) or an Arkansas extension (AR1055-IT) was filed

## Notes
- Return due April 15, 2027 (next business day if a weekend or holiday)
- A federal extension automatically extends the Arkansas due date to November 15, 2027. The Arkansas extension allows up to 210 days
- An extension does not extend the time to PAY
- The extension box must be checked, or the return gets no credit for the extension
- 2026: no Arkansas act changed the header or due dates
- Do not file an amended return just to change an address; DFA uses an Account Change Form
- An SSN error is corrected by contacting DFA, not by amending

## Required Information
- Legal names and SSNs for taxpayer and spouse
- Current mailing address (foreign country, if any)
- Email addresses
- Whether a federal or Arkansas extension was filed
- Whether the taxpayer wants a paper 1099-G or booklet next year

## Questions
- Has your name or address changed since last year?
- Did you file a federal or Arkansas extension?
- Do you want a paper 1099-G mailed to you?

## Common Errors
- Name not matching the Social Security card (e.g., after marriage)
- Filing on extension without checking the extension box
- Assuming the extension also extends payment

## Prompt
- I moved since I last filed.
- I filed a federal extension. Do I need a separate Arkansas extension?
