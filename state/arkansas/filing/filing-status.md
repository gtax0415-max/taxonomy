# Filing Status
## Description
Arkansas has six filing statuses. Two of them (4 and 5) have no direct federal match, and the Arkansas choice does not have to equal the federal choice for married couples.

## Amounts
- 2026 not yet published; template estimate (indexed, 3% cap): $2,540 per taxpayer; $5,080 for married filing jointly; $2,540 each for Status 4 (2025: $2,470 / $4,940)
- DFA's 2026 withholding formula still uses $2,470; replace the estimate when DFA publishes the 2026 amount

## Statuses
1. Single (unmarried, widowed before 2026, or divorced by year-end)
2. Married filing jointly
3. Head of household
4. Married filing separately on the SAME return (each spouse in its own column)
5. Married filing separately on DIFFERENT returns
6. Surviving spouse with dependent child

## Notes
- Married couples filing jointly federally may file Status 4 in Arkansas, which taxes each spouse's income separately and often lowers tax
- Marital status is determined on December 31, 2026
- See the separate files for Status 3, 4, 5, and 6 rules

## Required Information
- Marital status on December 31, 2026
- Federal filing status
- Household members, dependents, and who paid household costs

## Questions
- Were you married on December 31, 2026?
- Did your spouse die in 2024, 2025, or 2026?
- Do you have a dependent child living with you?
- Would filing separately on the same return lower your Arkansas tax?

## Common Errors
- Copying the federal status without comparing Status 2 and Status 4
- Choosing Status 5 when Status 4 is available and better
- Claiming head of household while married and living together

## Prompt
- We file jointly federally. What should we use in Arkansas?
- What filing status should I use in Arkansas?
