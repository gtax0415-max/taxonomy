# Political Contribution Credit
## Description
Credit for contributions to Arkansas state political candidates, parties, or political action committees.

## Amounts
- Up to $50 per taxpayer; $100 on Status 2 or 4
- Contributions made by April 15, 2027 can count for the 2026 return
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Each contribution's recipient, date, and amount
- Contributions made by April 15, 2027 for the 2026 return

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an Arkansas candidate, party, or PAC?
- How much did you give, and when?
- Are you filing jointly?

## Common Errors
- Claiming federal campaign contributions
- Claiming over $50 per person
- Counting contributions made after April 15, 2027

## Prompt
- I donated $100 to a state legislative candidate.
- Can I get a credit for donating to a state party?
