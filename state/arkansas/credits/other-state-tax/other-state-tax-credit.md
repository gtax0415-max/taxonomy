# Credit for Tax Paid to Another State
## Description
Credit for Arkansas residents who paid income tax to another state on income Arkansas also taxes.

## Amount
- The LESSER of the tax paid to the other state or the Arkansas tax on that income (figured in three steps on AR1000TC)

## Notes
- Residents only; nonresidents cannot claim it
- Attach a signed copy of each other state's return
- Includes a resident shareholder's share of net income tax an S corporation paid to a state that doesn't recognize S status
- Mississippi's gambling winnings tax is not an income tax and doesn't qualify (it can be itemized on AR3)
- Foreign income taxes do not qualify; Arkansas has no foreign tax credit (Form 1099-DIV box 7 foreign tax gives no Arkansas benefit)
- Template note: entered as an amount; not computed

## Required Information
- Other state's return as filed and the tax paid
- Income taxed by both states
- Residency for the full year

## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to another state in 2026?
- On what income?
- Were you an Arkansas resident all year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than the Arkansas tax on that income
- Claiming withholding instead of the other state's actual tax
- Claiming Mississippi gambling tax
- Claiming foreign tax paid through mutual funds

## Prompt
- I live in Arkansas but work in Missouri.
- I paid Oklahoma tax on rental income there.
