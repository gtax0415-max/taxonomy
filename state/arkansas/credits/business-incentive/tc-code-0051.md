# AR1000TC Code 0051: Apprenticeship Program
## Description
Credit for employers who hire qualified youth apprentices (age 16 or older): the lesser of $2,000 or 10% of the apprentice's wages, per apprentice. Unused credit carries forward up to two years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from U.S. Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship or the Arkansas Office of Skills Development (apprenticeship certification), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0051 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business employ youth apprentices in a registered program in 2026?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $2,000 per apprentice
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We hired three high school apprentices this year.
- I have unused Apprenticeship Program credit carried over from last year.
