# AR1000TC Code 0059: Waterways Investments
## Description
Credit for capital improvements to water transportation facilities (ports, terminals, and rail or road access to them). Up to $3 million per taxpayer; 10% of the total credit may be claimed each year; statewide cap of $2.5 million per year; unused credit carries forward nine years. Not available to recipients of the Port, Intermodal, and Waterway Development Grant.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Department of Commerce, limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0059 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Qualifying improvements had to be made between January 1, 2024 and December 31, 2025. For 2026 the credit continues only as annual 10% installments and carryforwards of credits already approved
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business make capital improvements to a port or waterway facility in 2024 or 2025?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than 10% of the total credit in one year
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our river terminal expansion qualified for the waterways credit.
- I have unused Waterways Investments credit carried over from last year.
