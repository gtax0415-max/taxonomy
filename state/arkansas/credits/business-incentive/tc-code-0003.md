# AR1000TC Code 0003: AR Plus
## Description
Investment tax credit for a qualified business that invests in a new or expanded Arkansas facility under a financial incentive agreement. For tax years beginning in 2026, Act 881 of 2025 makes the credit 'up to 10%' of eligible project costs (previously a fixed 10%), lets the business elect a sales and use tax credit instead, and keeps the cap at 50% of annual income tax liability; unused credit carries forward up to 9 years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate or agreement from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual liability cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0003 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Act 881 of 2025 (HB 1922), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026: credit 'up to 10%' of eligible project costs; optional sales and use tax credit instead; 50% liability cap; 9-year carryforward; forfeiture if annual payroll is not timely certified or the credit is not timely claimed
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business invest in an Arkansas facility under an AR Plus (investment tax credit) agreement?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Offsetting more than 50% of the year's income tax; claiming it after electing the sales tax version
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We built a new plant under an AR Plus agreement.
- I have unused AR Plus credit carried over from last year.
