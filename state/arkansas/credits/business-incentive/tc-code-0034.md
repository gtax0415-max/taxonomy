# AR1000TC Code 0034: Waste Reduction, Reuse or Recycle Equipment
## Description
Credit for buying equipment used to reduce, reuse, or recycle solid waste, approved by the Department of Energy and Environment.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Department of Energy and Environment, limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0034 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business buy approved recycling or waste-reduction equipment?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming equipment not on the approval
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We installed a recycling system at our plant.
- I have unused Waste Reduction, Reuse or Recycle Equipment credit carried over from last year.
