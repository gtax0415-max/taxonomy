# AR1000TC Code 0064: Relocating Corporate Headquarters
## Description
NEW for 2026 (Act 881 of 2025). Credit of up to 50% of the payroll for new full-time permanent employees when a business relocates its corporate headquarters to Arkansas. It offsets 100% of income tax in years 1–5, then 80%, 60%, 40%, and 20% in years 6–9, and none from year 10. It cannot be carried forward, sold, or transferred.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0064 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Act 881 of 2025, effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026
- Unclaimed credit is reduced 10% after 12 months and forfeited after 24 months; incentives end if payroll is not certified on time
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business move its corporate headquarters to Arkansas under an AEDC agreement?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Carrying unused credit forward (not allowed)
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We moved our corporate headquarters to Little Rock.
- I have unused Relocating Corporate Headquarters credit carried over from last year.
