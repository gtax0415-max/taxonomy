# AR1000TC Code 0022: Research and Development with Universities
## Description
Credit (33%) for a business that contracts with an Arkansas college or university for research, under a project plan approved by AEDC.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC) (project review by the Division of Science and Technology), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0022 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business pay an Arkansas university to do research under an approved plan?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming research done at an out-of-state university
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We contracted with the University of Arkansas for product research.
- I have unused Research and Development with Universities credit carried over from last year.
