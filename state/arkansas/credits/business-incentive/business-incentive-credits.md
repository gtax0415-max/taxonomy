# Business Incentive Tax Credits
## Description
Credits granted by state agencies for business investment, jobs, research, and other programs, entered by code. Each code has its own file (listed in business-incentive.md).

## Notes
- Enter the credit code, the FEIN of the pass-through entity (if any), and the amount on AR1000TC lines 8A–8F
- Attach copies of the credit certificates or supporting documentation
- Each credit is subject to its own statute's limits and carryforward rules
- Credits cannot exceed the tax
- New for 2026: Relocating Corporate Headquarters (code 0064, Act 881 of 2025); also Modernization and Automation (code 0065, Act 882 of 2025)

## Required Information
- Credit code, amount, and certificate for each credit
- FEIN of the pass-through entity, if the credit came through one
- Carryforward and transfer records

## Questions
- Did you earn or buy an Arkansas business tax credit?
- Was it earned through a partnership or S corporation?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?

## Common Errors
- Missing the certificate or FEIN
- Using the wrong code
- Claiming more than the tax (credits are nonrefundable)

## Prompt
- I have an Arkansas tax credit certificate from AEDC.
- My S corporation passed an Arkansas tax credit through to me.
