# AR1000TC Code 0063: Arkansas Wood Energy Products and Forest Maintenance Expansion
## Description
Wood energy products and forest maintenance credit for an approved EXPANSION project.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0063 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Acts 701 and 709 of 2025 (SB 530), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026: wood byproducts added, higher investment and job requirements, reduced credit amount. Follow the terms on the certificate
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your wood energy project an approved expansion?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using code 0054 for an expansion project (or the reverse)
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our wood products plant expanded under the wood energy credit.
- I have unused Arkansas Wood Energy Products and Forest Maintenance Expansion credit carried over from last year.
