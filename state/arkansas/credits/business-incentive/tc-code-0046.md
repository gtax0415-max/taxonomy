# AR1000TC Code 0046: Recycle-Steel Manufacturer Amendment 82 Project Act 862
## Description
Recycling equipment credit for a steel manufacturer's project financed under Amendment 82 (large economic development projects), under the special rules of Act 862.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Department of Energy and Environment (formerly ADEQ) / Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0046 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your steel project an approved Amendment 82 project?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using this code for a project that isn't an Amendment 82 project
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our Amendment 82 steel mill project bought recycling equipment.
- I have unused Recycle-Steel Manufacturer Amendment 82 Project Act 862 credit carried over from last year.
