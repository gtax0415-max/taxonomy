# AR1000TC Code 0045: Recycle Equipment for Steel Manufacturer
## Description
Waste reduction, reuse, or recycling equipment credit (30% of equipment and installation cost) as it applies to a qualified steel mill; a steel mill that invested more than $200 million can carry the credit forward 14 years instead of 3.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Department of Energy and Environment (formerly ADEQ), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0045 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your business a steel manufacturer that bought certified recycling equipment?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using the 3-year carryforward when the 14-year steel mill rule applies (or the reverse)
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our steel mill installed certified recycling equipment.
- I have unused Recycle Equipment for Steel Manufacturer credit carried over from last year.
