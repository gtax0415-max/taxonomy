# AR1000TC Code 0065: Modernization and Automation
## Description
NEW (Act 882 of 2025). Credit of up to 5% of eligible project costs for an existing Arkansas business (operating at least two years) investing in modernization and automation. AEDC program terms describe a $2 million annual cap per fiscal year; the project must maintain employment, and the credit cannot be combined with other incentives for the same project.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0065 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Act 882 of 2025 (HB 1935); program effective October 1, 2025
- Published summaries differ on the minimum investment ($5 million vs. $25 million); follow the AEDC agreement
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your business investing in automation under an AEDC modernization agreement?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Stacking this credit with another incentive for the same project (causes forfeiture)
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We automated our Arkansas plant under the new modernization credit.
- I have unused Modernization and Automation credit carried over from last year.
