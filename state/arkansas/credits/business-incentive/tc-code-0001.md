# AR1000TC Code 0001: Advantage Arkansas
## Description
Job-creation income tax credit for a business that signs a financial incentive agreement with AEDC and adds new full-time permanent employees. The credit is a percentage of the new employees' payroll, and the percentage depends on the county tier where the jobs are created.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate or agreement from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual liability cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0001 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC code list
- Act 881 of 2025 added a forfeiture rule for Consolidated Incentive Act credits: the credit can be lost if annual payroll is not timely certified or the credit is not timely claimed
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did your business sign an Advantage Arkansas agreement with AEDC and create new jobs?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming payroll for jobs created before the agreement or beyond the certified amount
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We hired 30 new employees under an Advantage Arkansas agreement.
- I have unused Advantage Arkansas credit carried over from last year.
