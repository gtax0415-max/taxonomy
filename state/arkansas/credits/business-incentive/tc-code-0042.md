# AR1000TC Code 0042: Wetland Riparian Zone Conservation
## Description
Credit for conserving existing wetlands or riparian zones under an approved project. Limited to actual out-of-pocket costs, up to $50,000 per project and $5,000 per year, claimed over up to 10 years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Department of Agriculture, Natural Resources Division, limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0042 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- The credit may be claimed in the year the Certificate of Tax Credit Approval is issued; if claimed later, attach the certificate to the first return claiming it
- A project cannot claim both this credit and a Water Resource Conservation credit
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did you protect existing wetlands or streamside land under an approved project?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $5,000 in one year
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- I put a conservation easement on wetlands.
- I have unused Wetland Riparian Zone Conservation credit carried over from last year.
