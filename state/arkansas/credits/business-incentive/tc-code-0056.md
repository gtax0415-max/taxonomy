# AR1000TC Code 0056: Motion Picture
## Description
Credit (20% of qualified production and post-production costs) for a state-certified film or digital production. Carries forward five years; may be sold or transferred. (A separate payroll rebate may also apply.)

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC) (Arkansas Film Commission), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0056 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did you produce a film in Arkansas certified by the Film Commission?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit and a rebate for the same costs
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We filmed a movie in Arkansas this year.
- I have unused Motion Picture credit carried over from last year.
