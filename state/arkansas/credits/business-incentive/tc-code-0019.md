# AR1000TC Code 0019: Low Income Housing
## Description
State low-income housing credit for qualified low-income housing projects allocated credits by ADFA.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate or agreement from Arkansas Development Finance Authority (ADFA), limited by the program's statute (annual liability cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0019 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC code list
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a low-income housing project allocated Arkansas credits?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit after the compliance period or allocation ends
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- My LLC owns a low-income apartment complex with state credits.
- I have unused Low Income Housing credit carried over from last year.
