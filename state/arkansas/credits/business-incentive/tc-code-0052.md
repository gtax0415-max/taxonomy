# AR1000TC Code 0052: Major Historic Rehabilitation
## Description
Historic rehabilitation credit (25% of qualified rehabilitation expenses) for a major rehabilitation project, which carries higher dollar limits than code 0018. The credit may be transferred or sold; unused credit carries forward five years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Heritage / State Historic Preservation Office, limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0052 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your historic rehabilitation certified as a major project?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using code 0018 for a major project (or the reverse)
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We completed a major rehabilitation of a historic downtown block.
- I have unused Major Historic Rehabilitation credit carried over from last year.
