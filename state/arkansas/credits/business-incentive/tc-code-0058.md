# AR1000TC Code 0058: Philanthropic Investment
## Description
Credit for donations to an approved scholarship-granting organization that funds students attending private schools: 100% of the contribution, limited to the tax due. Credits are approved first come, first served under an annual statewide cap. Carries forward three years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas DFA Tax Credits/Special Refunds Section (Philanthropic Investment in Arkansas Kids program), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0058 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Not a charitable deduction: a donation used for this credit cannot also be deducted on AR3
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Did you donate to an approved scholarship-granting organization in 2026?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Also deducting the same donation on AR3
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- I gave $5,000 to an approved private-school scholarship organization.
- I have unused Philanthropic Investment credit carried over from last year.
