# AR1000TC Code 0005: AR Plus 75% Technology-Based
## Description
The AR Plus investment credit for a technology-based enterprise whose wage level lets the credit offset up to 75% of annual income tax liability.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate or agreement from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual liability cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0005 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Act 881 of 2025: DFA's fiscal impact statement for HB 1922 described repeal of the technology-based enterprise credits from 2026, but the enacted text still lists them. Existing agreements are assumed to continue; confirm the code is on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your business a certified technology-based enterprise under an AR Plus agreement at the 75% level?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using the wrong offset percentage for the wage tier
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our tech company has an AR Plus 75% agreement.
- I have unused AR Plus 75% Technology-Based credit carried over from last year.
