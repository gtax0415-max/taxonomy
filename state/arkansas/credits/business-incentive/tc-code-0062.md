# AR1000TC Code 0062: Sustainable Aviation Fuel
## Description
Credit (30%) of the cost of sustainable aviation fuel production and processing equipment bought for use in Arkansas, at a facility certified by AEDC (Act 546 of 2025, Sustainable Aviation Fuel Incentive Act). Facility construction must begin before December 31, 2027.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0062 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Created by Act 546 of 2025; facility construction must begin before December 31, 2027
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Does your business produce sustainable aviation fuel at an AEDC-certified Arkansas facility?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming equipment not used for sustainable aviation fuel production
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We built a sustainable aviation fuel plant in Arkansas.
- I have unused Sustainable Aviation Fuel credit carried over from last year.
