# AR1000TC Code 0044: Biodiesel Incentive Credit
## Description
Credit (5%) of the cost of facilities and equipment a biodiesel supplier uses for wholesale or retail distribution of biodiesel (Biodiesel Incentive Act).

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from written supplier certification, limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0044 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Does your business distribute biodiesel through facilities or equipment it bought?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming equipment not used for biodiesel distribution
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- We installed biodiesel blending tanks at our fuel terminal.
- I have unused Biodiesel Incentive Credit credit carried over from last year.
