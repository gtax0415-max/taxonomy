# AR1000TC Code 0030: Targeted Business Payroll
## Description
Payroll income tax credit for a targeted (technology-based) business under a financial incentive agreement, based on the annual payroll of its employees. The credit may be sold.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0030 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Act 881 of 2025 (effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026): DFA's fiscal impact statement for HB 1922 described repeal of technology-based enterprise credits, but the enacted text still lists them. Existing agreements are assumed to continue; confirm the code is on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your business a certified targeted business with a payroll credit agreement?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming payroll outside the agreement period
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our software company has a targeted business payroll agreement.
- I have unused Targeted Business Payroll credit carried over from last year.
