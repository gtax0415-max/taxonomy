# AR1000TC Code 0054: Arkansas Wood Energy Products and Forest Maintenance
## Description
Credit (30% of the purchase price of qualifying equipment) for a wood energy products and forest maintenance project with at least $50 million of investment; up to $5 million may be redeemed per year; unused credit carries forward until used.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas Economic Development Commission (AEDC), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0054 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- Acts 701 and 709 of 2025 (SB 530), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026: the credit is extended to wood byproducts, investment and job-creation requirements are raised, and the credit amount is reduced. Projects approved under the old rules follow their certificate; confirm the 2026 terms on the certificate
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Is your business an approved wood energy or forest maintenance project?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Redeeming more than the annual limit
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- Our wood pellet plant has a wood energy credit.
- I have unused Arkansas Wood Energy Products and Forest Maintenance credit carried over from last year.
