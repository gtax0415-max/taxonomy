# AR1000TC Code 0057: Retired Law Enforcement Cold Case
## Description
Credit for a retired law enforcement officer or prosecutor who works cold cases: up to $3,500 for 1,000 hours, prorated for fewer hours. Certificates are issued first come, first served up to $25,000 per state fiscal year. Carries forward two years.

## Amount
- The credit amount is the amount on the certificate from Arkansas State Police (Department of Public Safety), limited by the program's statute (annual cap and carryforward period)
- Nonrefundable on the individual return: cannot exceed the tax for the filing status

## Notes
- Enter code 0057 and the amount on one of AR1000TC lines 8A–8F; attach a copy of the credit certificate or supporting documentation
- A credit earned through a pass-through entity also requires the entity's FEIN on line 8
- No 2025 or 2026 Arkansas act changed this credit; confirm the code on the 2026 AR1000TC
- Template note: entered as an amount; the credit is not computed

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and amount for 2026
- Carryforward from earlier years
- Pass-through entity FEIN and the owner's share, if applicable

## Questions
- Are you a retired officer who worked cold cases for the Arkansas State Police in 2026?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from earlier years?
- Was the credit earned through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the full $3,500 with fewer than 1,000 hours
- Claiming more than the tax (the credit is nonrefundable)
- Missing the certificate or the pass-through entity's FEIN

## Prompt
- I'm a retired detective working cold cases with the State Police.
- I have unused Retired Law Enforcement Cold Case credit carried over from last year.
