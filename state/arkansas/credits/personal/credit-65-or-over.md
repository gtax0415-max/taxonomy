# Credit for 65 or Over
## Description
An extra personal credit for each taxpayer age 65 or older.

## Amounts
- $29 per taxpayer 65 or over on January 1, 2027 (born on or before January 1, 1962)
- A taxpayer who died during 2026 qualifies only if they reached 65 before death

## Required Information
- Birth dates of the taxpayer and spouse
- Date of death, if a taxpayer died in 2026
- Filing status (spouse on the same return)

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse born on or before January 1, 1962?
- Did either of you die during 2026?
- Is your spouse on the same return?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 test date
- Claiming it for a deceased taxpayer who died before turning 65
- Claiming it for a dependent parent

## Prompt
- I turned 65 this year. Do I get an extra Arkansas credit?
- My wife is 66 and I'm 63. What credits do we get?
