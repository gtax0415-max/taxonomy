# Dependent Credit
## Description
A personal credit for each dependent listed on page 1.

## Amounts
- $29 per dependent (confirm in the 2026 booklet)
- Dependent tests are in dependents.md (gross income under $5,300 for 2026 unless a child under 19 or a student under 24)

## Notes
- A dependent with developmental disabilities may also qualify for the separate $500 credit on AR1000TC line 7

## Required Information
- Number of dependents listed on page 1
- Each dependent's name, SSN, and relationship

## Questions
- How many dependents do you claim?
- Does any dependent have a developmental disability?
- Do any dependents have income of $5,300 or more?

## Common Errors
- Counting yourself or your spouse
- Using the 2025 $5,200 income test
- Claiming a child the other parent claims

## Prompt
- I have two kids. What's my Arkansas dependent credit?
- My elderly mother lives with me. Can I claim her?
