# 65 Special Credit
## Description
A further credit for a taxpayer 65 or over who does NOT claim the $6,000 retirement exemption on line 18.

## Amounts
- $29 per qualifying taxpayer, in addition to the 65-or-over credit
- Age test: 65 on January 1, 2027

## Notes
- Not allowed for a taxpayer who claims the line 18 retirement exemption
- Determined per person: one spouse may qualify while the other claims the exemption

## Required Information
- Birth dates of the taxpayer and spouse
- Whether each person claims the $6,000 retirement exemption on line 18

## Questions
- Are you 65 or over and NOT taking the $6,000 retirement exemption?
- Do you receive a pension or IRA distributions?
- Is your spouse 65 or over?

## Common Errors
- Claiming both the 65 Special credit and the retirement exemption for the same person
- Claiming it for someone under 65
- Skipping it when no retirement exemption is used

## Prompt
- I'm 70 and have no pension. Is there an extra Arkansas credit?
- I'm 68 and live on Social Security only.
