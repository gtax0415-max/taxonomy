# Credit for Blind or Deaf
## Description
Extra personal credits for a taxpayer or spouse who is blind or deaf at year-end.

## Amounts
- $29 for each taxpayer blind on December 31, 2026
- $29 for each taxpayer deaf on December 31, 2026 (separate box; a person who is both gets both)

## Definitions
- Blind: 20/200 or worse in the better eye with correction, or a field of vision of 20 degrees or less
- Deaf: average speech-frequency hearing loss of 86 dB ISO or worse in the better ear

## Required Information
- Which taxpayer is blind or deaf on December 31, 2026
- Eye doctor's or audiologist's statement

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse legally blind or deaf?
- Do you have a doctor's statement?
- Is the person both blind and deaf?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a dependent (only the taxpayer and spouse)
- Not keeping the medical statement
- Claiming only one box when the person is both blind and deaf

## Prompt
- My spouse is legally blind. Is there an Arkansas credit?
- I'm deaf. Is there an extra Arkansas credit?
