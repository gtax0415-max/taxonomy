# Personal Tax Credits
## Description
Arkansas's flat per-person credits, figured on lines 7A–7C and carried to line 34.

## Amounts
- $29 per box (DFA's 2026 withholding formula still uses $29; confirm in the 2026 booklet)
- Line 7A: one credit each for the taxpayer and spouse (Status 2 or 4), plus one for the head of household or surviving spouse box
- Additional line 7A boxes: 65 or over, blind, deaf, and 65 Special (see the separate files)
- Line 7B: one credit per dependent (dependent-credit.md)
- Line 7C = 7A + 7B → line 34

## Notes
- Not available for children or dependents on line 7A; they go on 7B
- Nonrefundable

## Required Information
- Filing status
- Taxpayer and spouse names and SSNs
- Boxes that apply on line 7A (HOH or surviving spouse, 65+, blind, deaf, 65 Special)
- Number of dependents for line 7B

## Questions
- Are you filing for yourself only, or with your spouse?
- Are you filing as head of household or surviving spouse?
- Are you or your spouse 65 or over, blind, or deaf?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the extra HOH or surviving spouse box
- Counting dependents on line 7A
- Claiming a credit for the spouse on a Status 5 return
