# Child and Dependent Care Credit (AR2441)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit equal to 20% of the child and dependent care credit, figured on AR2441 under the federal rules as Arkansas adopted them (2013 rules), not the current federal rules.

## Amounts
- Expense limit: $3,000 for one qualifying person, $6,000 for two or more
- Decimal: .35 at AGI up to $15,000, falling to .20 at $43,000 and over (2013 federal table)
- Arkansas credit = 20% of that credit
- Part III: dependent care benefits; the Arkansas exclusion stays $5,000 (IRC 129 as of 2005). Taxable benefits go into wages on line 8, marked DCB
- The 2026 federal changes (higher credit rates and the $7,500 benefit exclusion under the OBBBA) are NOT adopted

## Eligibility
- Qualifying person: a child under 13, or a spouse or dependent unable to care for themselves
- Married filing separately only if the unmarried-treatment tests are met

## Notes
- If the child attends an approved early childhood program, the credit can be claimed as a refundable credit on line 43 instead (early-childhood-credit.md); lines 35 and 43 together cannot exceed the AR2441 amount
- Paper filing is required when the early childhood credit is claimed

## Required Information
- Provider details and amounts paid
- Dependent care benefits from W-2 box 10

## Questions
- Did you pay for child care so you could work?
- Did your employer provide dependent care benefits?
- Does your child attend an approved early childhood program?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2026 federal credit rate
- Using the federal $7,500 benefit exclusion
- Claiming the same credit on both line 35 and line 43

## Prompt
- I paid $8,000 for daycare for two kids.
- Does Arkansas match the bigger 2026 federal child care credit?
