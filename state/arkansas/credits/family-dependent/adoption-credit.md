# Credit for Adoption Expenses
## Description
20% of the federal adoption credit allowed on Form 8839.

## Amounts
- Federal maximum for 2026: $17,670 of qualified expenses, of which up to $5,120 is refundable federally (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)
- Arkansas credit = 20% of the federal credit allowed; nonrefundable in Arkansas
- Attach federal Form 8839

## Notes
- The federal credit became partly refundable in 2025. Confirm whether DFA bases the 20% on the total federal credit or only the nonrefundable part

## Required Information
- Federal Form 8839 and the federal credit allowed for 2026
- Child's name, SSN, and adoption finalization date
- Adoption expense receipts

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal adoption credit for 2026?
- Is the adoption final, and in what year?
- Did your employer provide adoption assistance?

## Common Errors
- Entering the full federal credit instead of 20%
- Claiming it without Form 8839
- Claiming it in a year the federal credit isn't allowed

## Prompt
- We adopted a child this year.
- We finalized an international adoption in 2026.
