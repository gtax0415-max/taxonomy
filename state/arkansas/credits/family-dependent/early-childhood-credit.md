# Early Childhood Program Credit (Refundable)
## Description
When the child attends an early childhood program at a facility approved by the Arkansas Department of Education, the AR2441 child care credit can be claimed as a REFUNDABLE credit on line 43.

## Notes
- The facility must be approved for an early childhood program; get the AR1000EC certification from the facility
- Enter the facility's certification number on line 43 and attach AR1000EC and AR2441
- Lines 35 and 43 combined cannot exceed the AR2441 credit
- The return must be filed on paper when this credit is claimed
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Facility's early childhood program certification number
- Form AR1000EC from the facility
- AR2441 credit amount and child care payments

## Questions
- Is your child's program approved by the Arkansas Department of Education?
- Did the facility give you form AR1000EC?
- How much is your AR2441 child care credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it without the certification number
- Claiming the full credit on both line 35 and line 43
- Filing electronically

## Prompt
- My child attends an ABC pre-K program. Can I get a refundable credit?
- Can the Arkansas child care credit be refunded to me?
