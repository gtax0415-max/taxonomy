# Stillborn Child Tax Credit
## Description
"Paisley's Law" credit for parents of a stillborn child.

## Amounts
- $500 for each stillbirth
- Nonrefundable; not more than the tax
- No change for 2026

## Notes
- Claimed only for the tax year the stillbirth occurred
- A Certificate of Birth Resulting in Stillbirth must have been issued; attach a copy

## Required Information
- Date of each stillbirth
- Certificate of Birth Resulting in Stillbirth

## Questions
- Did you experience a stillbirth in 2026?
- Has the certificate been issued?
- How many stillbirths occurred in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it in a later year
- Not attaching the certificate
- Claiming more than the tax

## Prompt
- We lost our baby to stillbirth this year.
- Is there an Arkansas tax credit after a stillbirth?
