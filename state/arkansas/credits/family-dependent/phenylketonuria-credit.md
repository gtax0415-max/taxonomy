# Phenylketonuria Disorder Credit (AR1113)
## Description
Credit for buying medically necessary foods and low-protein modified foods for a dependent child with phenylketonuria (PKU) or another metabolic disorder.

## Amounts
- Up to $2,400 per year, per child
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Dependent child diagnosed with PKU, galactosemia, organic acidemias, or other amino acid metabolism disorders

## Notes
- Template note: AR1113 is not built; enter its result on AR1000TC line 4
- Expenses used here should not also be deducted as medical expenses on AR3

## Required Information
- Child's name, SSN, and diagnosis
- Receipts for medically necessary and low-protein foods by child
- Physician's diagnosis

## Questions
- Does your child have PKU or another metabolic disorder requiring special foods?
- How much did you spend on special foods in 2026?
- How many children have the disorder?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $2,400 per child
- Double-counting the costs on AR3
- Including ordinary groceries

## Prompt
- My son has PKU and needs special low-protein food.
- Is there an Arkansas credit for metabolic formula costs?
