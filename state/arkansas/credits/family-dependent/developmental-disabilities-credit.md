# Credit for Individuals with Developmental Disabilities
## Description
Credit for each dependent with a developmental disability certified on Form AR1000-DD.

## Amounts
- $500 per individual
- Nonrefundable
- No change for 2026

## Notes
- Attach AR1000-DD (certified by a qualified professional) for the first year claimed; it stays on file for later years
- Enter each individual's name, SSN, and amount on AR1000TC lines 7A–7C
- Separate from the $500 adjustment for individuals with permanent disabilities (AR1000ADJ line 13)

## Required Information
- Each individual's name and SSN
- Form AR1000-DD on file (or the certification for the first year)
- Relationship to the taxpayer

## Questions
- Do you have a dependent with a developmental disability?
- Is an AR1000-DD on file with DFA?
- Is the individual your dependent?

## Common Errors
- Confusing this credit with the AR1000ADJ line 13 adjustment
- Not filing AR1000-DD in the first year
- Claiming it for the taxpayer or spouse

## Prompt
- My daughter has a developmental disability.
- Can I get a credit for my son with autism?
