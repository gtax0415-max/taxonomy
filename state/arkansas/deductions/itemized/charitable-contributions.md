# Contributions
## Description
Charitable gifts in four categories: cash, art and literary, other (property), and carryovers.

## Notes
- Line 13 cash/check: list any single donee receiving $3,000 or more
- Line 14 art and literary: allowed only if at least 50% of the donor's income comes from an art-related profession, an approved appraisal is attached, the recipient qualifies in Arkansas; capped at 15% of gross income
- Line 15 other gifts (property): attach federal Form 8283 for noncash gifts of $500 or more. Private school tuition is not deductible
- Driving for a charity: 14 cents per mile for 2026 (DFA proclamation), plus parking and tolls
- Line 16 carryover: contributions above 60% of AGI carry over five years
- The new federal 2026 rules (0.5%-of-AGI floor for itemizers, deduction for non-itemizers) are not printed in the Arkansas instructions; confirm against the 2026 booklet

## Required Information
- Each charity, date, and amount
- Acknowledgments and appraisals
- Carryover from earlier years

## Questions
- Did you give to charities in 2026? How much in cash and how much in property?
- Did any single charity receive $3,000 or more?
- Do you have a contribution carryover?

## Common Errors
- Deducting private school tuition
- Missing Form 8283 for noncash gifts of $500 or more
- Not listing donees of $3,000 or more
- Applying the federal 0.5% floor before Arkansas confirms it

## Prompt
- I donated $5,000 to my church.
- I gave clothes and furniture to Goodwill.
