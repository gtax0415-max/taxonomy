# Other Miscellaneous Deductions
## Description
Other itemized deductions NOT subject to the 2% floor, listed by type and amount with a detailed schedule.

## Notes
- Attach a detailed schedule
- Items that belong on another AR3 line (taxes, interest, contributions, 2% items, gambling) do not go here
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Each item, its type, and support
- Detailed schedule of each item

## Questions
- Do you have deductions not covered by another category?
- Have you checked that the item isn't on another AR3 line?
- Do you have records for each item?

## Common Errors
- Putting 2%-floor items here
- No schedule attached
- Including personal expenses that aren't deductible

## Prompt
- Where do I put a deduction that doesn't fit the other lines?
- I have an impairment-related work expense.
