# Casualty and Theft Losses (AR4684)
## Description
Personal casualty and theft losses using the federal method. Unlike the federal rule, Arkansas does NOT require a federally declared disaster, so ordinary fire, storm, accident, and theft losses can qualify.

## Amounts
- Subtract $100 from each loss
- The loss must exceed 10% of Arkansas AGI
- Net against any casualty gains
- No change for 2026

## Notes
- Reduce the loss by insurance reimbursements received or expected
- A loss in a federal disaster area may be elected on the prior-year return instead (not both years); list the location on line 18
- Attach Form AR4684

## Required Information
- Date, type, and location of the event
- Basis and decline in value
- Insurance reimbursement

## Questions
- Did you lose property to fire, storm, theft, or accident in 2026?
- Was it insured, and what was reimbursed?
- Was it in a federally declared disaster area?

## Common Errors
- Assuming no Arkansas deduction because the federal return requires a declared disaster
- Forgetting the $100 and 10% reductions
- Not subtracting insurance reimbursement
- Claiming the same disaster loss in two years

## Prompt
- My car was stolen and insurance didn't cover all of it.
- A tornado damaged my house.
