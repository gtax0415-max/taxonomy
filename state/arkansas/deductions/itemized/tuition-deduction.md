# Post-secondary Tuition Deduction (AR1075)
## Description
Arkansas-only itemized deduction for tuition paid for the taxpayer, spouse, or a dependent toward an associate, undergraduate, or graduate degree.

## Amounts
Deduction = the smaller of 50% of net tuition paid or 50% of the Arkansas weighted average tuition for that type of school.

Maximums on the 2025 AR1075:
- 4-year college: $5,191
- 2-year college: $2,646
- Technical institute: $1,635

The 2026 maximums are recalculated each year and not yet published; confirm on the 2026 AR1075.

## Notes
- Tuition only: no fees, books, or lodging
- Reduce tuition by scholarships, grants, and fellowships
- Schools outside Arkansas qualify, but the Arkansas averages set the cap
- One AR1075 per student per type of institution; total all forms on AR3 line 19

## Required Information
- Each student, school, and type
- Tuition paid and scholarships

## Questions
- Did you pay college tuition for yourself, your spouse, or a dependent?
- Which type of school (4-year, 2-year, technical)?
- Were there scholarships or grants?

## Common Errors
- Including fees, books, or housing
- Not subtracting scholarships
- Combining two school types on one form
- Using 2025 maximums for 2026

## Prompt
- I paid my daughter's tuition at the University of Arkansas.
- I'm going to community college part time.
