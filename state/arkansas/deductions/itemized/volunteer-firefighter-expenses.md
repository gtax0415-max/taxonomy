# Volunteer Firefighter Expenses
## Description
Arkansas-only itemized deduction for unreimbursed expenses of a volunteer firefighter. Not subject to the 2% floor.

## Amounts
- Up to $1,000 per volunteer firefighter
- Only if the firefighter's total compensation for firefighting was under $5,000
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Department and volunteer status
- Compensation received
- Unreimbursed expenses

## Questions
- Are you a volunteer firefighter?
- Were you paid less than $5,000 for it?
- What did you spend that wasn't reimbursed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $1,000
- Claiming with compensation of $5,000 or more
- Putting it on the 2% line

## Prompt
- I'm a volunteer firefighter and bought my own gear.
- I spent $1,500 on gear as a volunteer firefighter.
