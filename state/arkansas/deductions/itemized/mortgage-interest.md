# Home Mortgage Interest and Points
## Description
Interest on a mortgage for a principal and second residence, interest paid to an individual, and deductible points.

## Notes
- Line 8: interest paid to a financial institution, generally limited to debt up to the cost of the principal and/or second residence plus improvements
- Line 9: interest paid to an individual; enter the lender's name and address
- Line 10: points, fully deductible when for buying or improving the principal residence, customary in amount, and paid from separate funds at closing. Refinance points are amortized over the loan
- The Arkansas instructions state no $750,000 federal debt cap; the limit is tied to the cost of the homes plus improvements
- Home office: only the personal share is deducted here; the business share stays on Schedule C through federal Form 8829
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Form 1098 for each loan
- Seller-financed loan details
- Points and refinance details

## Questions
- Do you have a mortgage on your main home or a second home?
- Is any loan from a private individual?
- Did you buy or refinance in 2026, and did you pay points?

## Common Errors
- Deducting all refinance points in one year
- Leaving out the lender's name and address for a private loan
- Deducting interest on debt above the homes' cost plus improvements

## Prompt
- I paid $9,000 in mortgage interest.
- I refinanced and paid points.
