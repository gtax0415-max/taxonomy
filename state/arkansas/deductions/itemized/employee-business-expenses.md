# Employee Business Expenses (AR2106)
## Description
Form AR2106 figures an employee's ordinary and necessary job expenses, which go to AR3 as a 2% miscellaneous deduction.

## Notes
- Vehicle, travel, lodging, transportation (not commuting; no car loan interest)
- Meals, using actual cost or the federal per diem allowance
- Incidental expenses ($5 per day method if no meals)
- Other job expenses: gifts, education, books, home office, trade publications
- Mileage: 72.5 cents per mile for 2026 (Arkansas business rate set by DFA proclamation of January 7, 2026, matching the IRS rate; 2025 was 70 cents)
- Subtract employer reimbursements not included in W-2 box 1
- Reservist travel over 100 miles is NOT here; it's an adjustment (AR1000ADJ line 15)
- Template note: AR2106 is not built; enter its result on AR3 line 20

## Required Information
- Expenses by category and reimbursements
- Business miles driven and reimbursements received

## Questions
- Did you drive your own car for work (not commuting)?
- Did your employer reimburse you?
- Did you keep a mileage log?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 mileage rate (70 cents) instead of 72.5 cents
- Including commuting miles
- Not subtracting reimbursements
- Putting reservist travel here

## Prompt
- I drive my car for work and my employer doesn't reimburse me.
- Can I deduct my work mileage in Arkansas?
