# Medical and Dental Expenses
## Description
Unreimbursed medical and dental expenses above 10% of Arkansas AGI. Arkansas's floor is HIGHER than the federal 7.5%.

## Amounts
- Floor: 10% of Arkansas AGI (AR1000F lines 25A + 25B)
- Deduction = expenses − 10% of AGI (not below zero)
- Medical travel by car: 20.5 cents per mile for 2026 (DFA proclamation; 2025 was 21 cents), plus parking and tolls
- No change for 2026

## Notes
- Self-employed health insurance already deducted on AR1000ADJ line 9 cannot be counted again
- Premiums paid pre-tax through payroll are not deductible
- Itemizing in Arkansas requires the Regular tax table (not the Low Income Tax Table)

## Required Information
- Medical and dental expenses paid in 2026
- Insurance and other reimbursements
- Arkansas AGI

## Questions
- Did you have large medical or dental bills in 2026?
- Were any reimbursed by insurance or an HSA?
- Did you pay health premiums with after-tax money?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 medical mileage rate (21 cents) instead of 20.5 cents
- Using the federal 7.5% floor
- Including expenses paid from an HSA or reimbursed by insurance
- Double-counting self-employed health insurance

## Prompt
- I had surgery this year and paid $12,000 out of pocket.
- My medical bills were 15% of my income.
