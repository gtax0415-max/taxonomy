# Real Estate Tax
## Description
Real estate tax on non-business property. Arkansas has no SALT cap, since state income tax is not deductible here at all.

## Notes
- Deductible: real estate tax on non-business property paid in 2026
- Not deductible: special assessments and levies, Arkansas income tax, sales tax, federal income tax
- The federal SALT cap ($40,000 for 2026) does not apply in Arkansas
- Taxes on rental or business property belong on Schedule E, C, or F
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Property tax bills and payment dates
- Escrow payments from Form 1098

## Questions
- Did you pay property tax on your home or land in 2026?
- Was it paid through your mortgage escrow?
- Did you buy or sell a home this year?

## Common Errors
- Including special assessments (e.g., sidewalk or sewer improvements)
- Applying the federal SALT cap
- Deducting taxes on rental property here

## Prompt
- I paid $3,000 in property tax on my home.
- Can I deduct the property tax on my lake house?
