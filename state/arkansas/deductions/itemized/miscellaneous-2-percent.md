# Miscellaneous Deductions Subject to 2% of AGI
## Description
Unreimbursed employee expenses and other miscellaneous deductions above 2% of Arkansas AGI. Federal law eliminated these, but ARKANSAS STILL ALLOWS THEM.

## Notes
- Line 20: unreimbursed employee business expenses from AR2106
- Line 21: other items such as union dues, tax preparation fees, safety equipment, tools, brokerage and investment fees (attach a schedule)
- Lines 23–25: subtract 2% of combined Arkansas AGI
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Expense receipts by type
- Employer reimbursements
- Arkansas AGI

## Questions
- Did you pay job expenses your employer didn't reimburse?
- Did you pay union dues, tax prep fees, or investment advisory fees?
- Did you pay tax preparation or investment fees?

## Common Errors
- Skipping it because the federal return doesn't allow it
- Including reimbursed expenses
- Including commuting

## Prompt
- I paid $1,200 in union dues and bought my own tools.
- Can I deduct my tax preparer's fee in Arkansas?
