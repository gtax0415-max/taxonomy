# Gambling Losses
## Description
Gambling losses as an itemized deduction, not subject to the 2% floor. The winnings are reported on AR-OI.

## Notes
- Losses are deductible only up to gambling winnings
- Federal law limits the deduction to 90% of losses starting in 2026 (OBBBA). The Arkansas instructions state only the "not more than winnings" limit; confirm whether Arkansas adopts the 90% limit when the 2026 booklet is released
- Lottery winnings are reported separately on AR-OI

## Required Information
- Total winnings (W-2G and other)
- Documented losses

## Questions
- Did you have gambling winnings in 2026?
- Do you have records of your losses?
- Are your losses more than your winnings?

## Common Errors
- Deducting more than winnings
- Netting losses against winnings on AR-OI instead of itemizing
- No records for losses

## Prompt
- I won $5,000 and lost $4,000 at casinos.
- Can I deduct my lottery ticket losses?
