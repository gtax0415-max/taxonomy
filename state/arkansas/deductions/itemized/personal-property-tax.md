# Personal Property and Other Taxes
## Description
Personal property taxes and certain other taxes, listed by type and amount.

## Notes
- Deductible: personal property tax, city income tax, Mississippi gambling tax, foreign income tax on income taxed on this return
- Not deductible: Arkansas income tax, sales tax, federal income tax, car tags
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Each tax by type and amount paid in 2026
- Tax receipts showing the payment date

## Questions
- Did you pay county personal property tax on vehicles or boats?
- Did you pay tax on gambling winnings in Mississippi?
- Did you pay foreign tax on investments?

## Common Errors
- Deducting car tags or registration fees
- Deducting sales tax
- Deducting Arkansas income tax

## Prompt
- I paid personal property tax on my truck and boat.
- I paid Mississippi tax on casino winnings.
