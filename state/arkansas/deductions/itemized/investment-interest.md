# Investment Interest
## Description
Interest on money borrowed to buy taxable investments, limited to net investment income.

## Notes
- Limited to investment income; the excess carries forward
- Attach federal Form 4952
- No change for 2026
- Investment income for the Arkansas limit should reflect Arkansas amounts (e.g., U.S. Treasury interest is exempt in Arkansas)

## Required Information
- Investment interest paid
- Net investment income
- Prior carryforward

## Questions
- Did you pay margin interest or borrow to buy investments?
- Do you have an investment interest carryforward?
- How much investment income did you have?

## Common Errors
- Deducting more than investment income
- Including interest on loans used for tax-exempt investments
- Counting U.S. Treasury interest as investment income for Arkansas

## Prompt
- I paid margin interest on my brokerage account.
- I borrowed to buy stocks. Is the interest deductible?
