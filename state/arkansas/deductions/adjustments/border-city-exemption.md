# Border City (Texarkana) Exemption
## Description
Exemption for residents of the twin border city of Texarkana. A resident living inside the city limits of Texarkana, Arkansas owes no Arkansas income tax. A resident of Texarkana, Texas may subtract only the income earned inside Texarkana, Arkansas. The income is first reported in full on the return and then removed as an adjustment.

## Amount
- No dollar cap. The adjustment equals the exempt income itself
- Texarkana, ARKANSAS resident (full year): all income → full exemption from Arkansas income tax
- Texarkana, ARKANSAS resident (part year): only income earned while a resident of Texarkana, Arkansas
- Texarkana, TEXAS resident: only income earned within the city limits of Texarkana, Arkansas. All other Arkansas-source income stays taxable
- 2026: no change from 2025. The 2026 rate cut (top rate 3.9% → 3.7%, Act 1 / Act 2 of the 2026 First Extraordinary Session) does not affect this exemption

## Eligibility
- Physical STREET address inside the Texarkana city limits (either side of the state line) entered on the return. A P.O. Box or rural route address disqualifies the claim
- Wage income: the employer's Form AR-TX must accompany the W-2. The employee requests it by giving the employer Form AR4EC
- Non-wage income (interest, dividends, Schedule C, E, F, retirement) does not need an AR-TX, but DFA may request support

## Notes
- The return is still required. Report all Arkansas income first, then subtract it on AR1000ADJ line 1
- A Texarkana, Texas resident files AR1000NR, not AR1000F
- Column (C) of AR1000ADJ applies to part-year and nonresident filers; enter only the Arkansas-attributable amount there

## Required Information
- Street address and dates of residence inside Texarkana city limits
- W-2 and AR-TX for each job
- Income earned inside Texarkana, Arkansas versus elsewhere (Texas residents)
- Part-year residency dates, if any

## Questions
- Did you live inside the city limits of Texarkana, Arkansas or Texarkana, Texas? For the whole year?
- Do you have a street address there (not a P.O. Box or rural route)?
- Did your employer give you Form AR-TX with your W-2?
- (Texas residents) How much of your income was earned inside Texarkana, Arkansas?

## Common Errors
- Using a P.O. Box or rural route address
- Missing the AR-TX for wage income
- A Texarkana, Texas resident subtracting all Arkansas income instead of only income earned in Texarkana, Arkansas
- Not reporting the income first and simply leaving it off the return
- Part-year residents exempting income earned before moving into the city

## Prompt
- I live in Texarkana, Arkansas. Do I owe Arkansas income tax?
- I live in Texarkana, Texas but work in Texarkana, Arkansas.
- I moved into Texarkana, Arkansas in the middle of the year.
