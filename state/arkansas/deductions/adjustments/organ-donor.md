# Organ Donor Deduction (AR1000-OD)
## Description
Arkansas-only, once-in-a-lifetime deduction for the unreimbursed costs of donating an organ as a living donor. No federal counterpart.

## Amount
- Up to $10,000 of unreimbursed expenses
- ONCE PER LIFETIME
- 2026: unchanged

## Eligibility
- Donation made WHILE LIVING by the taxpayer or the taxpayer's dependent
- Donation to another person of all or part of a liver, pancreas, kidney, intestine, lung, or bone marrow
- Claimed for the tax year in which the TRANSPLANT occurred

## Qualified expenses
- Travel
- Lodging
- Medical expenses
- Lost wages
(all only to the extent unreimbursed)

## Required Information
- Donor's name and relationship to the taxpayer
- Transplant date and organ donated
- Unreimbursed expenses by category
- Any reimbursement received

## Questions
- Did you or your dependent donate an organ or bone marrow as a living donor?
- When did the transplant take place?
- What travel, lodging, medical costs, and lost wages were not reimbursed?
- Have you claimed this deduction before?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it a second time (lifetime limit)
- Claiming it in a year other than the transplant year
- Including reimbursed costs
- Claiming a donation made after death or blood/plasma donation

## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother this year.
- My daughter was a bone marrow donor.
