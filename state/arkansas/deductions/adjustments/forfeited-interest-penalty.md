# Forfeited Interest Penalty
## Description
Deduction for the penalty a bank charges when a certificate of deposit or other time deposit is withdrawn before maturity. The interest is reported in full as income, and the penalty is subtracted here.

## Amount
- No dollar cap: the full penalty shown on Form 1099-INT box 2 or 1099-OID box 3
- 2026: no change

## Notes
- Deductible even if the penalty is larger than the interest earned
- Not the 10% additional tax on early IRA or retirement-plan distributions; that is not deductible
- Report the full interest (before the penalty) as interest income

## Required Information
- Form 1099-INT or 1099-OID
- Amount of the penalty

## Questions
- Did you cash in a CD or time deposit before it matured in 2026?
- Does your Form 1099-INT show an amount in box 2?
- Is the penalty larger than the interest earned?

## Common Errors
- Netting the penalty against interest income instead of reporting both
- Treating the IRA early-distribution penalty as forfeited interest
- Overlooking box 2 on the 1099-INT

## Prompt
- I broke a CD early and paid a penalty.
- My 1099-INT shows an early withdrawal penalty.
