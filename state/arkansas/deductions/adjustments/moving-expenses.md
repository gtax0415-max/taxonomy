# Moving Expenses (AR3903)
## Description
Deduction for the cost of moving because of a new job or a change in job location. Federal law has suspended this deduction (except for active-duty military), but ARKANSAS DID NOT CONFORM to the suspension. Arkansas still applies the pre-TCJA rules, so a deduction can exist on the Arkansas return with no federal counterpart.

## Amounts
- No dollar cap; the deduction equals qualifying unreimbursed (or reimbursed and taxed) expenses
- Mileage for driving to the new home: 20.5 cents per mile for 2026 (DFA proclamation of January 7, 2026 under Act 614 of 2025; 2025 was 21 cents)
- 2026: Arkansas confirmed it continues to use the pre-TCJA moving-expense rules for 2026 (no adoption of IRC 217(k) or 132(g)(2))

## Eligibility
- DISTANCE TEST: the new workplace is at least 50 miles farther from the old home than the old workplace was (or at least 50 miles from the old home if there was no prior workplace)
- TIME TEST: full-time work at the new location for at least 39 weeks in the 12 months after the move (78 weeks in 24 months for self-employed)

## Qualified expenses
- Packing, moving, and storing household goods and personal effects
- Travel and lodging for the trip to the new home (not meals)

## Notes
- Qualified employer reimbursements are excluded from Arkansas income. If a reimbursement was included in the W-2, report it as income on the Arkansas return before deducting the expense
- Template note: Form AR3903 is not built in the template; enter its result on AR1000ADJ line 8 and attach AR3903

## Required Information
- Dates of the move and of starting the new job
- Old home, old workplace, and new workplace addresses (for the distance test)
- Expense receipts by type
- Employer reimbursements and how they were reported on the W-2

## Questions
- Did you move in 2026 because of a new job or job transfer?
- How far is the new workplace from your old home, compared to your old workplace?
- Did you (or will you) work full-time at the new location for 39 weeks?
- Did your employer reimburse any moving costs?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 moving mileage rate (21 cents) instead of 20.5 cents
- Assuming no deduction because the federal return doesn't allow one
- Including meals, house-hunting trips, or temporary living expenses
- Deducting expenses the employer reimbursed tax-free
- Failing the 50-mile distance test
- Not attaching Form AR3903

## Prompt
- I moved to Arkansas for a new job.
- My employer relocated me 200 miles. Can I deduct moving costs in Arkansas?
