# ABLE Program Contributions
## Description
Arkansas-only deduction for contributions to a disability savings account under the Achieving a Better Life Experience (ABLE) Program. There is no federal deduction for ABLE contributions.

## Amounts
- Up to $5,000 per taxpayer per year
- CARRYFORWARD: contributions above $5,000 carry forward to the next four succeeding tax years
- 2026: unchanged; no Arkansas act amended this deduction
- Federal context: total annual contributions to one ABLE account are capped at the federal gift-tax exclusion ($19,000 for 2026)

## Notes
- The deduction belongs to whoever made the contribution, not only the beneficiary
- Rollovers from a 529 plan into an ABLE account are not new contributions for this deduction

## Required Information
- 2026 contributions and the contributor
- Excess contributions carried forward from 2022–2025

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an ABLE account in 2026?
- How much, and who made the contribution?
- Do you have excess ABLE contributions from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Deducting more than $5,000 in one year instead of carrying the excess forward
- Forgetting a carryforward from a prior year
- Treating a 529-to-ABLE rollover as a deductible contribution
- Entering ABLE contributions on the 529 line (line 2)

## Prompt
- I contribute to my son's ABLE account.
- I put $7,000 into an ABLE account this year.
