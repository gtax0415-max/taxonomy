# Support for Individuals with Permanent Disabilities
## Description
Arkansas-only deduction of a flat amount for each person with a total and permanent disability whom the taxpayer maintains, supports, and cares for at home. No federal counterpart.

## Amount
- $500 for each qualifying individual
- 2026: unchanged

## Eligibility
- The individual is the taxpayer's natural or adopted child, or a dependent of the taxpayer
- The individual is NOT the taxpayer or spouse
- The taxpayer maintained, supported, and cared for the individual in the taxpayer's home
- A physician has diagnosed a total and permanent disability: unable to engage in any substantial gainful activity because of a medically determinable physical or mental impairment expected to result in death or to last at least 12 continuous months

## Notes
- Form AR1000DC is required for EACH individual and is good for one year, so a new one is attached every year
- This is a deduction, separate from the dependent credits on the return

## Required Information
- Each individual's name, SSN, and relationship
- Physician's diagnosis of total and permanent disability
- Confirmation the individual lived in the taxpayer's home

## Questions
- Do you care for a child or dependent in your home who has a total and permanent disability?
- Has a physician diagnosed the disability?
- How many such individuals live with you?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the taxpayer or spouse
- Claiming an individual who lives in a facility rather than the taxpayer's home
- Not attaching a current-year AR1000DC for each individual
- Claiming a temporary disability

## Prompt
- My adult son has a permanent disability and lives with us.
- We care for two children with severe disabilities at home.
