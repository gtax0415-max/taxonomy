# Teachers Qualified Classroom Investment Expense (AR1000CE)
## Description
Arkansas-only deduction for teachers who spend their own money on classroom items. Separate from, and larger than, the federal educator expense deduction.

## Amounts
- Up to $1,000 per teacher (doubled from $500 by Act 878 of 2025 (HB 1732), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025; continues for 2026)
- Married filing jointly: up to $2,000, ONLY if both spouses are teachers

## Eligibility
- Teacher, instructor, counselor, principal, or aide for students in pre-kindergarten through grade 12
- Employed at least 900 hours during the year at a school certified by the state to provide public preschool, elementary, or secondary education

## Qualified expenses
- Books
- School supplies
- Computer equipment and software
- Athletic equipment
- Food for students
- Clothing for students

## Required Information
- Receipts by category
- Position and school
- Hours worked during 2026
- Which spouse is the teacher (joint returns)

## Questions
- Do you work in a pre-K through 12th-grade public school?
- Did you work at least 900 hours there in 2026?
- How much did you spend out of pocket on your classroom?
- Is your spouse also a teacher?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $500 limit
- Claiming $2,000 on a joint return when only one spouse is a teacher
- Including reimbursed purchases
- Claiming private-school or college teaching positions that don't meet the definition
- Not attaching AR1000CE for each teacher

## Prompt
- I'm a teacher and spent $800 on classroom supplies.
- My spouse and I are both teachers.
