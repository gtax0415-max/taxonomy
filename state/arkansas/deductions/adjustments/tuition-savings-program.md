# Tuition Savings Program (Arkansas 529)
## Description
Arkansas-only deduction for contributions to a 529 college savings plan. There is no federal deduction for 529 contributions, so this adjustment has no federal counterpart line. Qualified withdrawals from an Arkansas plan or another state's plan are exempt from Arkansas income tax.

## Amounts
- Arkansas plan: up to $5,000 per taxpayer ($10,000 married filing jointly when both contribute). Unchanged for 2026
- CARRYFORWARD: Arkansas-plan contributions above $5,000 carry forward to the next four succeeding tax years
- Another state's plan: up to $3,000 per taxpayer ($6,000 joint), only if not deducted on that state's return
- ROLLOVER from another state's plan into an Arkansas plan: up to $7,500 per taxpayer ($15,000 joint) in the year of the rollover, only if the money was not previously deducted in Arkansas
- 2026: no Arkansas act changed these limits; the 2025 limits continue

## Notes
- 529-to-Roth IRA rollovers allowed under SECURE 2.0 are exempt from Arkansas tax (Act 802 of 2025)
- A nonqualified withdrawal, or a rollover OUT to another state's plan, can trigger recapture of amounts previously deducted
- The deduction belongs to the contributor (the account owner or other contributor), not the beneficiary

## Required Information
- Contributions by plan (Arkansas versus another state) and date
- Rollover amount and the prior plan's statement
- Contributions carried forward from 2022–2025
- Whether the out-of-state contribution was already deducted on another state's return

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a 529 plan in 2026? Was it Arkansas's Brighter Future plan or another state's plan?
- Did you roll money from another state's 529 into an Arkansas plan?
- Do you have excess Arkansas-plan contributions from earlier years?
- Did you take any nonqualified withdrawals or roll money out of an Arkansas plan?

## Common Errors
- Applying the $5,000 Arkansas-plan limit to an out-of-state plan (limit is $3,000)
- Forgetting the four-year carryforward of excess Arkansas-plan contributions
- Deducting the same out-of-state contribution in two states
- Looking for this on the federal return (there is no federal 529 deduction)
- Missing recapture after a nonqualified withdrawal

## Prompt
- I put $8,000 into my child's Arkansas 529 plan this year.
- I contribute to another state's 529 plan. Can I deduct it in Arkansas?
- I rolled my old 529 into Arkansas's plan.
