# Federal 2025-Act Deductions, QBI and One-Half of Self-Employment Tax
## Description
Several federal deductions have no Arkansas line because Arkansas builds income line by line and has not adopted them. They should not reduce Arkansas income.

## What does NOT qualify
- Qualified tips deduction (IRC 224)
- Qualified overtime deduction (IRC 225)
- Car loan interest deduction
- Additional $6,000 senior deduction
- Qualified business income (QBI) deduction (IRC 199A)
- Deduction for one-half of self-employment tax

## Notes
- Arkansas confirmed for 2026 that it has no exclusion or deduction for tips or overtime
- Arkansas's own senior benefits are the 65-or-over credit, the 65 Special credit, and the $6,000 retirement exemption

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A, Form 8995/8995-A, and Schedule SE amounts, to confirm none carry to the Arkansas return
- Arkansas income lines, to confirm tips and overtime remain in wages

## Questions
- Did you claim the tips, overtime, car loan, senior, or QBI deduction federally?
- Did you deduct one-half of self-employment tax federally?
- Are you 65 or over and took the federal senior deduction?

## Common Errors
- Carrying any of these federal deductions to the Arkansas return
- Subtracting half of self-employment tax
- Reducing Arkansas wages for tips or overtime

## Prompt
- Do I get the no-tax-on-tips deduction in Arkansas?
- Does Arkansas allow the QBI deduction?
