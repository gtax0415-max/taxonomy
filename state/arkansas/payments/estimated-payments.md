# Estimated Tax and Credit Brought Forward
## Description
2026 estimated tax payments plus any 2025 overpayment applied to 2026.

## Notes
- 2026 installments due April 15, June 15, and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027
- Include the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 estimated tax (2025 AR1000F line 48)
- Do not include a payment made with an extension (line 41) or with the return

## Required Information
- Dates and amounts of each 2026 estimated payment
- 2025 overpayment credited

## Questions
- Did you make Arkansas estimated payments for 2026?
- Did you apply last year's refund to 2026?
- Did you make any payment after January 1, 2027 for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the January 2027 payment
- Forgetting the credit brought forward
- Including extension payments here

## Prompt
- I made four estimated payments to Arkansas.
- Did my estimated payments cover my Arkansas tax?
