# Amount Due
## Description
The balance owed when net tax is more than total payments, plus any estimated tax penalty.

## Notes
- Line 51 = line 38 − line 46, when line 46 is less
- If line 51 is over $1,000, continue to line 52A (AR2210 exception or penalty)
- Line 52C = line 51 + line 52B
- Due April 15, 2027, even with an extension
- Late payment 1% per month, late filing 5% per month (up to 35%), interest 10% per year

## Required Information
- Payment method
- Net tax (line 38) and total payments (line 46)

## Questions
- Will you pay by check, online, or bank draft?
- Is the balance over $1,000?
- Do you need an installment plan?

## Common Errors
- Not completing line 52A when line 51 is over $1,000
- Paying late because of an extension
- Not paying by April 15, 2027

## Prompt
- I owe Arkansas money this year. When is it due?
- How do I pay my Arkansas balance due?
