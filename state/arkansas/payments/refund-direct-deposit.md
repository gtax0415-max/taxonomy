# Overpayment, Refund and Direct Deposit
## Description
Line 47 is the overpayment. Line 50 is the refund after any amount applied to 2027 estimated tax (line 48) and check-off contributions (line 49).

## Notes
- Line 47 = line 46 − line 38, when payments exceed net tax
- Line 50 = line 47 − line 48 − line 49
- Direct deposit to up to two accounts, U.S. banks only
- Refunds for a deceased taxpayer are issued to the estate

## Required Information
- Routing and account numbers, account type
- Amount to apply to 2027 or donate

## Questions
- Do you want your refund by direct deposit? Into one account or two?
- Do you want part of it applied to 2027 or donated?
- Is the account a checking or savings account?

## Common Errors
- Wrong routing or account number
- Using a foreign bank account
- Splitting the refund across more than two accounts

## Prompt
- How do I get my Arkansas refund by direct deposit?
- Split my refund between checking and savings.
