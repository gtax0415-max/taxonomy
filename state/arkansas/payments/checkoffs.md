# Check-off Contributions (AR1000CO)
## Description
Lets the taxpayer donate all or part of an overpayment to state programs, or deposit it into an Arkansas Brighter Future Fund (529) account. Paid ONLY out of a refund; it never increases a balance due.

## Amounts
- Funds 1–9: $1, $5, $10, $20, another amount, or the whole refund
- Brighter Future Fund accounts: $25, $50, $100, another amount, or the whole refund
- The total cannot exceed the overpayment on line 47 (after any amount applied to 2027 estimated tax on line 48)

## Funds
From the 2025 AR1000CO (R 4/25/2025); no 2026 form posted yet:
1. Arkansas Disaster Relief Program
2. Arkansas Game and Fish Foundation
3. Arkansas School for the Blind / School for the Deaf
4. Baby Sharon's Children's Catastrophic Illness Program
5. Organ Donor Awareness Education Program
6. Area Agencies on Aging Program
7. Military Family Relief Program
8. Newborn Umbilical Cord Blood Initiative
9. Law Enforcement Family Relief Trust Fund
10. Arkansas Brighter Future Fund Plan Account (formerly AR529), up to two accounts, each with its account number
11. Total check-off contributions → Form AR1000F, line 49

## Notes
- 2026: no 2026 AR1000CO posted yet. Compare the fund list against the 2026 form when released, since funds are added or removed by law
- A Brighter Future deposit may count as a contribution for the tuition savings adjustment in the year it is deposited
- Example: $10 to the Arkansas Game and Fish Foundation → AR1000CO line 2 and line 11 → AR1000F line 49; refund on line 50 drops by $10

## Required Information
- Funds and amounts chosen
- 529 account number(s) for any Brighter Future deposit

## Questions
- Would you like to donate part of your refund to an Arkansas program?
- Would you like part of your refund deposited into your Arkansas 529 account?
- Which program would you like to support?

## Common Errors
- Choosing contributions larger than the refund
- Missing or wrong 529 account number
- Selecting a check-off on a return with a balance due

## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my Arkansas refund?
- Can my refund go into my child's 529 account?
