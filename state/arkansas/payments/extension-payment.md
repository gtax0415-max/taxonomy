# Payment with Extension
## Description
Tax paid when requesting an extension, with AR1055-IT or by the original due date while on a federal extension.

## Notes
- Enter the amount paid with the extension
- An extension does not extend the time to pay: tax is due April 15, 2027
- Check the extension box on page 1 (see filing/extension.md)

## Required Information
- Date and amount paid
- Whether a federal or Arkansas extension was filed

## Questions
- Did you pay anything when you extended your 2026 return?
- Did you file AR1055-IT or a federal extension?
- Was the payment made by April 15, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Putting the extension payment on line 40
- Paying after April 15, 2027 and expecting no interest
- Counting the payment twice on lines 40 and 41

## Prompt
- I paid $500 when I filed my extension.
- I paid with my extension request.
