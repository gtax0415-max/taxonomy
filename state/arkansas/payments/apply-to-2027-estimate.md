# Overpayment Applied to 2027 Estimated Tax
## Description
The part of the 2026 overpayment the taxpayer chooses to apply to 2027 estimated tax instead of receiving as a refund.

## Notes
- Cannot exceed line 47
- The amount becomes a credit on the 2027 return (line 40 there)
- It reduces the line 50 refund

## Required Information
- Overpayment on line 47
- Amount the taxpayer wants applied to 2027

## Questions
- Do you expect to owe Arkansas tax for 2027?
- How much of your refund should go toward 2027?
- Will your 2027 income be similar to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Applying more than the overpayment
- Forgetting to claim the credit on the 2027 return
- Expecting a refund of the amount applied

## Prompt
- Can I apply my Arkansas refund to next year's taxes?
- Apply my refund to next year.
