# Underpayment of Estimated Tax (AR2210)
## Description
The AR2210 exception number (box 52A) and the penalty amount (line 52B).

## Notes
- Applies when the tax is $1,000 or more and payments missed 90% of the 2026 tax (or the 2025 tax, if less) each quarter
- Exceptions 1–6 entered in box 52A; "6" when AR2210A (annualized) is used
- Penalty: 10% per year by quarter
- Template note: not computed (firm policy, 2026-10-06); the exception number and penalty are entries

## Required Information
- 2026 withholding and estimated payments by quarter
- 2025 Arkansas tax liability
- Reason for any exception (1–6)

## Questions
- Did you pay at least 90% of your 2026 tax, or last year's tax, during the year?
- Does an exception apply?
- Was your income received unevenly during the year?

## Common Errors
- Leaving box 52A blank when an exception applies
- Using the federal 100%/110% prior-year test
- Not attaching AR2210 or AR2210A

## Prompt
- Will I owe a penalty for not paying estimated tax?
- Do I owe a penalty for underpaying estimated tax?
