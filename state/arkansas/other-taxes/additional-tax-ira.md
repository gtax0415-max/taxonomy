# Additional Tax on IRA and Qualified Plan Withdrawals
## Description
Arkansas charges 10% of the federal additional tax on early distributions from IRAs and qualified plans.

## Notes
- Line 32 = 10% of the federal Form 5329 additional tax (Part I early distributions; the template also includes Part II)
- The distribution itself is reported on line 16 or 18
- Applies even when the $6,000 retirement exemption is granted (e.g., certain early distributions)
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Federal Form 5329
- Form 1099-R distribution codes

## Questions
- Did you pay the federal 10% early-withdrawal tax for 2026?
- Did an exception to the federal additional tax apply?
- Was the distribution from an IRA or an employer plan?

## Common Errors
- Entering the full federal additional tax instead of 10% of it
- Forgetting line 32 when the distribution qualified for an exception federally (then there is no federal tax and nothing here)
- Skipping line 32 when the $6,000 exemption was claimed

## Prompt
- I took an early IRA withdrawal. Is there an Arkansas penalty?
- I cashed out my 401(k) at age 40.
