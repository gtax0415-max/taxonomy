# Lump Sum Distribution Averaging (AR1000TD)
## Description
An eligible lump-sum distribution from an employer plan may be taxed separately on AR1000TD using the averaging method.

## Notes
- Use AR1000TD to check eligibility and figure the tax; the result goes on line 31
- A distribution that does not qualify for averaging is reported on line 16 or 18
- Template note: AR1000TD is not built; enter its result on line 31
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- 1099-R and plan details
- Payee's date of birth and years of plan participation

## Questions
- Did you receive your entire balance from an employer plan in one year?
- Was the distribution your entire balance from the plan?
- Were you born before 1936 or otherwise eligible for averaging?

## Common Errors
- Reporting the distribution on line 16/18 and also averaging it
- Using averaging for an IRA distribution
- Not completing AR1000TD

## Prompt
- I took my whole pension as a lump sum.
- Can I use averaging for my pension lump sum in Arkansas?
