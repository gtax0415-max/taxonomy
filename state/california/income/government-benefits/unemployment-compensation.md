# Unemployment Compensation
## Description
Unemployment benefits are taxable federally but NOT taxable in California. Because California starts from federal AGI, the amount must be SUBTRACTED on Schedule CA.

## Amount
- Subtract the full amount included in federal AGI

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Report on the unemployment compensation line (Schedule CA, Part I, Section B, line 7) in column B
- Paid Family Leave benefits use the same line and the same subtraction
- For 2020 only, a portion of federal unemployment was excluded from federal AGI; subtract only what remains in federal AGI

## Required Information
- Form 1099-G, box 1
- Amount included in federal AGI

## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment benefits in 2026?
- Was any federal tax withheld (box 4)?
- Did you also receive Paid Family Leave or disability benefits?

## Common Errors
- Paying California tax on unemployment by skipping the Schedule CA subtraction
- Subtracting more than the amount included in federal AGI
- Forgetting that a 1099-G from the FTB is a state refund, not unemployment

## Prompt
- I collected unemployment from EDD for part of the year.
- Is unemployment taxable in California?
