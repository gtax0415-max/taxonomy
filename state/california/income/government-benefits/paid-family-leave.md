# Paid Family Leave (PFL)
## Description
Benefits from California's Paid Family Leave program, paid by EDD. They are included in federal AGI but EXCLUDED for California, so they are subtracted on Schedule CA on the same line as unemployment compensation.

## What PFL covers
- Birth and care of a new child
- Bonding with an adopted or foster child
- Care of a family member with a serious health condition: spouse/RDP, child, parent or parent-in-law, grandparent, grandchild or sibling

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Subtract on Schedule CA, Part I, Section B, line 7 (the unemployment compensation line), column B
- Subtract only the amount actually included in federal AGI

## Required Information
- Form 1099-G from EDD
- Amount of PFL included in federal AGI

## Questions
- Did you receive Paid Family Leave benefits from EDD in 2026?
- Did your federal return include them in income?

## Common Errors
- Not subtracting PFL on Schedule CA
- Putting the subtraction on the wages line instead of the unemployment line

## Prompt
- I took paid family leave after my baby was born.
- I received PFL benefits to care for my father.
