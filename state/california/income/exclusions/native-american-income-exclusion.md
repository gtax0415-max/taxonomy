# Native American Income Exclusion
## Description
An enrolled tribal member may be exempt from California tax on income earned or received from reservation sources while living in federally recognized California Indian country. Senate Bill 855 expanded these gross income exclusions.

## Eligibility
All of the following:
- Enrolled member of a federally recognized tribe
- Income earned or received from reservation sources
- Lives in federally recognized California Indian country

## Indian country includes
- Reservations
- Dependent Indian communities
- Indian trust allotments

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- A member living in Indian country is still a CALIFORNIA RESIDENT and pays tax on all NONEXEMPT income (for example, off-reservation wages or investment income)
- The optional Enrolled Tribal Member Certification (FTB 3504) declares reservation residency and tax-exempt status and can prevent unnecessary FTB notices
- Members of an Indian tribe also have an exemption from the California health care mandate penalty (code G)

## Required Information
- Tribal enrollment
- Residence address and whether it is in Indian country
- Income by source: reservation vs. off-reservation

## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member of a federally recognized tribe?
- Do you live on a reservation, rancheria or trust allotment in California?
- Where did you earn each type of income?

## Common Errors
- Excluding off-reservation income
- Excluding income while living outside Indian country
- Treating the member as a nonresident of California
- Not filing FTB 3504, which leads to FTB notices

## Prompt
- I'm a tribal member living on a reservation in California and work for the tribe's casino.
- Is my tribal per capita payment taxable in California?
