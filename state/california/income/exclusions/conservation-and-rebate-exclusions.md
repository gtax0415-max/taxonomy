# Conservation, Rebate and Recycling Exclusions
## Description
California excludes several rebates, incentives and conservation payments from income. Subtract any amount included federally on Schedule CA, line 8z, column B.

## Exclusions
- TURF REPLACEMENT water conservation rebates, vouchers or incentives from a public water system, local government or state agency: 2022 through 2026
- WATER OR ENERGY REBATES and vouchers from a local water agency, energy agency or energy supplier for buying and installing water conservation appliances and devices
- SEISMIC IMPROVEMENT incentives (loan forgiveness, grant, credit, rebate or voucher) from the California Residential Mitigation Program or the California Earthquake Authority for earthquake loss mitigation
- GRANTS TO LOW-INCOME INDIVIDUALS to construct or retrofit buildings for energy efficiency (no federal equivalent)
- BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING income
- FOREST LANDOWNER COST-SHARE payments under the California Forest Improvement Act of 1978, or from the USDA Forest Service under the Forest Stewardship Program and Stewardship Incentives Program

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The turf replacement exclusion ends after the 2026 tax year

## Required Information
- Type and amount of each rebate or payment
- Whether included in federal income

## Questions
- Did you get a rebate for replacing your lawn or installing water-saving devices?
- Did you receive an earthquake retrofit grant?
- Did you redeem cans and bottles for recycling income?

## Common Errors
- Reporting excluded rebates as California income

## Prompt
- My water district paid me $2,000 to replace my lawn.
- I got an Earthquake Brace + Bolt grant.
