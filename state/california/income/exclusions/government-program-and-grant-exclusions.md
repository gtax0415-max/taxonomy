# Government Program and Grant Exclusions
## Description
California excludes many state program payments, grants and awards from income. When the amount was included in federal income, subtract it on Schedule CA, line 8z, column B.

## Exclusions and periods
- GUARANTEED INCOME PILOT program payments (grant under Welfare and Institutions Code 18997): June 30, 2022 through June 30, 2026, so only payments before July 1, 2026 qualify on the 2026 return
- CALIFORNIA HOPE FOR CHILDREN TRUST ACCOUNT: deposits, investment returns, interest, and withdrawals by an eligible youth (2023 onward)
- INTERAGENCY COUNCIL ON HOMELESSNESS payments to members (or advisory/working group members) who are or have been homeless (2023 onward)
- DISCHARGED COMMUNITY COLLEGE STUDENT FEES (Education Code 32527): 2022 through 2026
- SMALL BUSINESS AND NONPROFIT COVID-19 SUPPLEMENTAL PAID SICK LEAVE RELIEF GRANT: 2021 through 2029
- SMALL BUSINESS COVID-19 RELIEF GRANT PROGRAM: 2020 through 2029
- CALIFORNIA VENUES GRANT (CalOSBA): September 1, 2020 through 2029
- PPP loan forgiveness and other covered loan forgiveness (2019 onward), with California's expense rules for ineligible entities
- CALIFORNIA NATIONAL GUARD death benefits paid by the state to a surviving spouse/RDP or beneficiary of National Guard, State Military Reserve or Naval Militia members killed in the line of duty
- REWARDS from a government-authorized CRIME HOTLINE (not for hotline employees or sponsors)
- OTTOMAN TURKISH EMPIRE settlement payments (and interest) to persons persecuted 1915–1923, their heirs or estates

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Program, payer and amount
- Whether included in federal income

## Questions
- Did you receive payments from a guaranteed income pilot program?
- Do you have a HOPE trust account?
- Did your business receive a California COVID-19 relief grant or Venues Grant?
- Did you receive a crime hotline reward or a National Guard death benefit?

## Common Errors
- Paying California tax on excluded program payments
- Excluding guaranteed income payments received on or after July 1, 2026

## Prompt
- I'm in a guaranteed income pilot and get $500 a month.
- My small business got a California COVID-19 relief grant.
