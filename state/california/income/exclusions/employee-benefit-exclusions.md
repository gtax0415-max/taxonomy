# Employee Benefit Exclusions (Ridesharing, CQSO)
## Description
Two employee benefits are excluded by California even when they are taxable federally.

## Ridesharing fringe benefits
- California has NO monthly limit on excluding qualified ridesharing and transportation benefits, and its definitions are broader than federal law
- Subtract ridesharing benefits included in federal income on line 1h, column B

## California Qualified Stock Option (CQSO)
Compensation from exercising a CQSO is excluded if ALL apply:
- Earned income from the granting corporation is $40,000 or less
- The market value of the options granted is less than $100,000
- The total number of shares is 1,000 or less
- The corporation designated the stock as a CQSO when the option was granted
Subtract the amount included federally on line 1h, column B.

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Ridesharing benefits included in wages
- CQSO grant designation, shares, value and earned income

## Questions
- Does your employer provide vanpool, transit or ridesharing benefits?
- Did you exercise stock options designated as California Qualified Stock Options?

## Common Errors
- Applying the federal monthly cap to California ridesharing benefits
- Excluding ordinary nonqualified options that were not designated CQSOs

## Prompt
- I exercised California qualified stock options from my startup.
