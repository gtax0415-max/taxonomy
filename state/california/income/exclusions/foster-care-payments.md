# Foster Care Payments
## Description
Qualified foster care payments are EXCLUDED from gross income for personal income tax when paid by a state or local government or a licensed child placement agency.

## Eligibility
- Paid by a state or local government, or by a licensed child placement agency
- Applies to foster individuals of any age at the time of placement

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Normally excluded federally too, so no California adjustment is needed
- If the payments were included in federal AGI, subtract them on Schedule CA

## Required Information
- Payer and amount
- Placement documentation

## Questions
- Did you receive foster care payments in 2026?
- Who paid them: a county, the state, or a licensed agency?
- Did the payments appear in your federal income?

## Common Errors
- Reporting qualified foster care payments as income
- Excluding payments from an unlicensed or private source

## Prompt
- I receive monthly payments from the county for a foster child.
