# Wildfire and Disaster Payment Exclusions
## Description
California excludes several wildfire and disaster settlement payments from income, even when federal law taxes them. A filer who reported a qualifying amount as income in a prior year may amend that year within the normal statute of limitations.

## California exclusions (column B) and their periods
- WILDFIRE DISASTER SETTLEMENTS from a settlement entity for a qualified California wildfire: tax years 2021 through 2029
- FIRE VICTIMS TRUST payments (PG&E bankruptcy trust): tax years beginning before January 1, 2028
- KINCADE FIRE (2019) settlements from PG&E: 2020 through 2027
- ZOGG FIRE (2020) settlements from PG&E: 2020 through 2027
- THOMAS FIRE (2017) and WOOLSEY FIRE (2018) settlements from Southern California Edison: tax years beginning before January 1, 2027
- CALIFORNIA WILDFIRE MITIGATION PAYMENTS (Wildfire Mitigation Financial Assistance Program): 2024 through 2028
- CHIQUITA CANYON elevated temperature landfill event payments: 2024 through 2028

## Federal exclusion California does not follow (column C)
- Qualified WILDFIRE RELIEF PAYMENTS under the Federal Disaster Tax Relief Act of 2023 (federal exclusion for 2020–2025): include in California income if no California exclusion applies. The federal exclusion ends after 2025, so this mainly affects amended prior-year returns

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Payer, fire or event, and amount received
- Whether the amount was included in federal income

## Questions
- Did you receive a wildfire settlement or Fire Victims Trust payment?
- Which fire, and from whom?
- Did you receive a wildfire mitigation grant or a Chiquita Canyon payment?
- Did you pay California tax on such a payment in an earlier year?

## Common Errors
- Paying California tax on an excluded settlement
- Not amending earlier years that taxed an excluded payment
- Excluding a federal-only relief payment for California

## Prompt
- I received a settlement from PG&E for the Kincade Fire.
- I got a payment from the Fire Victims Trust.
