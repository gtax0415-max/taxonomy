# Income Exclusions
## Description
Amounts that are not income for California, mostly subtracted on Schedule CA (540), line 1h or 8z, column B: gifts and inheritances, foster care payments, tribal members' reservation-source income, wildfire and disaster settlements, state program payments and grants, conservation rebates, ridesharing benefits and California Qualified Stock Options.
