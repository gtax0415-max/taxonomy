# Sale of Business Property (Other Gains or Losses)
## Description
Gains and losses from selling business assets (federal Form 4797). Usually no adjustment, but California basis often differs from federal basis because of depreciation differences.

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- When California depreciation differed, the California gain or loss on sale differs too; figure it on Schedule D-1 (540)
- Depreciation recapture follows the California depreciation actually allowed

## Required Information
- Sale price and selling costs
- Federal and California adjusted basis

## Questions
- Did you sell equipment, vehicles, rental buildings or other business property?
- Was California depreciation different from federal?

## Common Errors
- Using federal basis to compute the California gain

## Prompt
- I sold a business vehicle that I depreciated.
