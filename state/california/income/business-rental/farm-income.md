# Farm Income or Loss
## Description
Farm profit or loss from federal Schedule F, adjusted for California differences in depreciation, special credits, NOLs and accelerated write-offs.

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Use FTB 3801 if passive farm activities produce a loss; FTB 3885A for depreciation differences
- Special California recovery periods apply to some vineyard replantings (phylloxera after 1991, Pierce's disease after 1996)
- Non-cash patronage dividends from farmers' cooperatives may differ (FTB Publication 1001)
- Farmers who meet the farm income test may follow special estimated tax rules and use FTB 5805F for the underpayment penalty

## Required Information
- Schedule F and depreciation records
- Passive status

## Questions
- Do you have farm income or loss?
- Did you replant vineyards or take federal bonus depreciation?

## Common Errors
- Using federal depreciation on the California return

## Prompt
- I own an almond orchard in the Central Valley.
