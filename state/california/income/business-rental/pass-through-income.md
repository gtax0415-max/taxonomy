# Partnership, S Corporation, Trust and Royalty Income
## Description
Income or loss from partnerships, LLCs taxed as partnerships, S corporations, estates and trusts, and royalties. It starts from federal Schedule E, but California figures can differ.

## California differences
- The entity reports CALIFORNIA amounts separately (LLCs and LLPs report California distributive items on their California K-1), which can differ from federal K-1 amounts because of depreciation, credits and accelerated write-offs
- PASSIVE ACTIVITY rules: California does not conform to the federal real estate professional exception; use FTB 3801 when passive losses exist
- Accumulation distributions from trusts that did not pay California tax because a beneficiary's interest was contingent may be taxable to the beneficiary (FTB Publication 1001; FTB 5870A)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- PTE elective tax credit from an electing entity: credits/business/pass-through-entity-elective-tax-credit.md
- Rental real estate on Schedule E Part I: income/business-rental/rental-income.md
- Single-member LLCs doing business in California also file Form 568 and pay the LLC annual tax and fee (an entity filing, outside this individual taxonomy)

## Required Information
- All K-1s with California amounts
- Passive or nonpassive status of each activity
- Carryover passive losses

## Questions
- Do you receive K-1s from partnerships, S corporations or trusts?
- Did the entity give you California-specific amounts?
- Do any activities produce passive losses?

## Common Errors
- Using federal K-1 amounts without the California adjustments
- Treating rental activity as nonpassive under the federal real estate professional rule

## Prompt
- I have a K-1 from an S corporation.
- I'm a partner in an LLC that operates in California.
