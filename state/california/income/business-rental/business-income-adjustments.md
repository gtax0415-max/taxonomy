# Business Income Adjustments (Schedule CA, Section B, Line 3)
## Description
Business profit or loss starts from federal Schedule C, but California differs in depreciation, several deductions, and some loan and grant rules.

## Depreciation and basis
- Use FTB 3885A when California depreciation differs: California did not adopt federal ACRS before 1987, and California does not follow federal bonus depreciation and some expensing rules, so recovery periods and basis can differ
- Use FTB 3801 when there are passive activities that produce a loss
- SECTION 179: California limits the expense to $25,000, reduced dollar for dollar once qualifying purchases exceed $200,000 (far below the federal limits); any federal expensing above the California limit is depreciated instead on FTB 3885A
- Basis adjustments include property acquired before becoming a California resident, former Enterprise Zone/LAMBRA/TTA property, and others listed in the Schedule CA instructions

## Deductions California allows but federal law does not (column B)
- Business expenses of a licensed COMMERCIAL CANNABIS business (CA MAUCRSA license)
- Entertainment, on-premises meals and employee transportation fringe benefits that federal law disallows (California does not follow the TCJA limits; enter the difference)
- Wagering expenses of a professional gambler beyond winnings (California does not follow the federal limit)

## Items California disallows or adds (column C)
- Fines or penalties paid by a professional sports franchise owner to its league
- Expenses paid with PPP, other covered loans or shuttered venue grants, if the taxpayer is an "ineligible entity" (publicly traded, or did not meet the 25% gross receipts reduction)
- PPP forgiveness excluded federally under the ARPA expanded eligibility (certain nonprofits and internet-only publishers), which California does not follow
- Deductions for payments to the Edge College and Career Network found unlawful (R&TC 17275.4)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Worker reclassification adjustments: income/business-rental/employee-or-independent-contractor.md

## Required Information
- Schedule C and asset records
- Any cannabis, PPP, grant or fringe benefit items

## Questions
- Do you depreciate business assets, and did you take bonus depreciation federally?
- Is your business a licensed cannabis business?
- Did you receive PPP forgiveness or a shuttered venue grant?

## Common Errors
- Using federal bonus depreciation on the California return
- Missing the cannabis expense deduction
- Not recomputing basis for assets bought before moving to California

## Prompt
- I took bonus depreciation on equipment for my business.
- I run a licensed cannabis dispensary.
