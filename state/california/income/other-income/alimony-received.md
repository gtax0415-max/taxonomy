# Alimony Received
## Description
Spousal support paid under a divorce or separation instrument. California's treatment depends on WHEN the agreement was executed. For 2019 through 2025 agreements, California does NOT follow the federal change, so alimony is taxable in California even though it is not federally.

## Treatment by agreement date
- Executed BEFORE January 1, 2019: taxable federally and in California. Flows through federal AGI
- Executed January 1, 2019 – December 31, 2025: NOT taxable federally, but TAXABLE in California. Add on Schedule CA (column C)
- Executed ON or AFTER January 1, 2026, or executed earlier and modified after December 31, 2025 with the modification expressly adopting the SB 711 (2025–2026) amendments: NOT taxable in California (California now conforms)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- For the 2026 return: payments under an UNMODIFIED 2019–2025 agreement (or one modified without adopting SB 711) are STILL taxable in California and require the Schedule CA addition. Payments under a 2026 agreement are not taxable anywhere
- Child support is never alimony and is never taxable

## Required Information
- Agreement execution date and any modification date
- Whether the modification expressly adopts SB 711
- Amount received in 2026

## Questions
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed?
- Has it been modified, and does the modification reference SB 711?
- How much spousal support did you receive in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Omitting 2019–2025 alimony from the California return because it is not on the federal return
- Treating child support as alimony
- Ignoring the agreement date

## Prompt
- I receive spousal support under a 2021 divorce agreement.
- Is alimony taxable in California?
