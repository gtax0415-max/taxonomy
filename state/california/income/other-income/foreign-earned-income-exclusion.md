# Foreign Earned Income Exclusion
## Description
California does NOT allow the federal foreign earned income exclusion or the foreign housing exclusion or deduction. A California resident working abroad must add the excluded income back.

## Rules
- Add the amount excluded on federal Schedule 1, line 8d to California income (line 8d, column C, as a positive number)
- COMBAT ZONE CONTRACTORS: California also does not follow the federal foreign earned income exclusion for contractors and contractor employees supporting U.S. Armed Forces in designated combat zones (tax years 2018 and later); add it back on line 8d, column C
- The federal foreign HOUSING DEDUCTION is also disallowed (line 24j, column B)
- California does not allow a deduction or credit for foreign income taxes (see deductions/itemized/taxes-paid.md)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Whether the filer is still a California resident while abroad depends on domicile and safe-harbor rules (see filing/residency/part-year-resident-nonresident.md)

## Required Information
- Federal Form 2555 amounts
- Residency status while abroad

## Questions
- Did you exclude foreign wages on your federal return?
- Did you claim the foreign housing exclusion or deduction?
- Were you a California resident while working abroad?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the foreign earned income exclusion in place for California
- Expecting a California credit for foreign income tax paid

## Prompt
- I worked in London and excluded my wages federally.
