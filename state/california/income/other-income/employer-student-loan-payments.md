# Employer Payments of Student Loans
## Description
Federal law excludes up to $5,250 a year of employer payments toward an employee's student loans as part of an educational assistance program (made permanent by OBBBA). California does NOT conform, so these payments are taxable in California.

## Rules
- Add the employer's student loan payments excluded federally on Schedule CA, line 8c, column C

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Amount the employer paid toward the employee's student loans

## Questions
- Did your employer pay any of your student loans?

## Common Errors
- Treating employer student loan payments as tax-free in California

## Prompt
- My employer pays $400 a month toward my student loans.
