# Gambling Winnings
## Description
All gambling winnings are taxable in California, with one major exception: winnings from the CALIFORNIA LOTTERY are NOT taxed by California.

## Taxable winnings include
- Lotteries (other than the California Lottery)
- Raffles
- Horse races
- Casinos

## California Lottery exclusion
- Not taxed: California Lottery games, including SuperLotto, Powerball and Mega Millions bought in California
- Subtract on Schedule CA, Part I, Section B, line 8b, column B
- Lottery winnings from OTHER states remain taxable in California

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Report the full amount of winnings federally; losses are a separate itemized deduction, not a netting
- If California Lottery winnings are subtracted, California Lottery LOSSES are not deductible either (see deductions/itemized/gambling-losses.md)

## Required Information
- All W-2G forms
- Win and loss records by source
- Which winnings came from the California Lottery

## Questions
- Did you win from the California Lottery or from another state's lottery?
- Did you win at casinos, races or raffles?
- Did you receive a W-2G?

## Common Errors
- Paying California tax on California Lottery winnings
- Excluding another state's lottery winnings
- Netting losses against winnings instead of itemizing

## Prompt
- I won $5,000 on a California scratcher.
- I hit a jackpot at a casino in Nevada.
