# Other Income Items (Schedule CA, Lines 8g–8v and 8z)
## Description
The "other income" lines of federal Schedule 1 carry onto Schedule CA. Most need NO California adjustment; the exceptions are noted.

## Items that generally follow federal treatment (no adjustment)
- 8g Alaska Permanent Fund dividends
- 8h JURY DUTY PAY; if turned over to the employer, deduct it on line 24a (same as federal)
- 8i Prizes and awards (California Lottery winnings are on line 8b and are excluded)
- 8j Hobby income (activity not engaged in for profit)
- 8k Stock options (see also California Qualified Stock Options in income/exclusions/employee-benefit-exclusions.md and the AMT on incentive stock options)
- 8l Income from renting personal property for profit (not a rental business); related expenses on line 24b, with any federal/California difference entered there
- 8m Olympic and Paralympic medals and USOC prize money; the federal exclusion (line 24c) applies the same way in California
- 8q Taxable distributions from an ABLE account
- 8r Scholarship and fellowship grants not on a W-2
- 8t Pensions or annuities from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan or nongovernmental 457 plan
- 8u Wages earned while incarcerated
- 8v Digital assets received as ordinary income not reported elsewhere

## Items with California differences (line 8z)
- TAX TREATY exemptions claimed federally: added back in column C unless specifically exempted for state purposes
- ORIGINAL ISSUE DISCOUNT on debt issued in 1985 and 1986: in the year of sale or disposition, holders add the federal/California difference in column C; issuers subtract the deduction difference in column B
- FOREIGN INCOME OF NONRESIDENT ALIENS: restate to California worldwide income (foreign losses in column B, foreign income in column C)
- PARENTS' ELECTION (FTB 3803) to report a child's interest and dividends when the California amount differs from federal: difference on line 8z, column B or C (see filing/tax-computation.md)
- Other California exclusions on line 8z are covered in income/exclusions/

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1, line 8 detail
- Any treaty, OID or nonresident alien items

## Questions
- Did you have jury duty pay, prizes, hobby income, stock options or digital asset income?
- Did you claim a tax treaty exemption?
- Are you a nonresident alien with foreign income?

## Common Errors
- Carrying a treaty exemption to California
- Making adjustments where California follows federal law

## Prompt
- I won a car in a raffle.
- I received crypto as payment for work.
