# Incomplete Gift Non-Grantor (ING) Trust Income
## Description
Since January 1, 2023, income of an incomplete gift non-grantor (ING) trust is taxed to the GRANTOR (the "qualified taxpayer") on their California return, even though the trust is a non-grantor trust federally.

## Reporting
- The qualified taxpayer files a California Fiduciary Income Tax Return (Form 541), checking the box that designates an ING trust
- ING trust income, losses, expenses and deductions are reported on the grantor's Form 540
- NONRESIDENT grantors must file if the ING trust received California-source taxable income: Form 541 (ING box) plus Form 540NR

## Exception (electing ING trusts)
Trust income is NOT included in the grantor's income for a year if ALL apply:
1. The fiduciary timely files an ORIGINAL Form 541 and makes the IRREVOCABLE election (Form 541 box) to be treated as a resident non-grantor trust
2. The trust is a non-grantor trust
3. 90% or more of the trust's distributable net income is distributed, or treated as distributed, to a charitable organization under IRC 501(c)(3)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Charitable remainder trusts are NOT subject to this rule (R&TC section 17082)
- Because ING trusts are non-grantor trusts federally, the income usually is not in the grantor's federal AGI, so it must be added on the California return

## Required Information
- Whether the trust is an ING trust
- Trust income and deductions
- Charitable distributions as a share of DNI
- Whether the election was made on a timely original Form 541
- Grantor's residency

## Questions
- Are you the grantor of an incomplete gift non-grantor trust?
- Did the trust make the resident non-grantor election?
- Did at least 90% of DNI go to 501(c)(3) charities?
- Are you a California resident, or does the trust have California-source income?

## Common Errors
- Leaving ING trust income off the grantor's California return
- Making the election on a late or amended Form 541
- Applying the rule to a charitable remainder trust

## Prompt
- I set up an ING trust in Nevada before moving to California.
