# Wage Adjustments (Schedule CA, Lines 1a–1h)
## Description
Most wage items flow from the federal return with no change. These are the wage-line items where California and federal law differ.

## Subtractions (column B)
- SICK PAY under the Federal Insurance Contributions Act or the Railroad Retirement Act: excluded by California (line 1a or 1h)
- Native American earned income from California Indian country (lines 1a–1h; attach FTB 3504)
- Ridesharing fringe benefits and California Qualified Stock Option exclusion (line 1h; see income/exclusions/employee-benefit-exclusions.md)
- In-Home Supportive Services supplementary payments (line 1d)

## Additions (column C)
- Wages excluded under a U.S. TAX TREATY (unless specifically exempted for state purposes)
- EMPLOYER HSA CONTRIBUTIONS (W-2 box 12, code W): California does not recognize HSAs (line 1h)
- Moving expense reimbursements excluded federally, for non-military filers, from FTB 3913 (line 1h)
- Business gross receipts reclassified as wages when a federal independent contractor is a California employee (line 1a; see income/business-rental/employee-or-independent-contractor.md)

## Usually no adjustment
- Household employee wages not on a W-2 (1b), tip income (1c), taxable dependent care benefits (1e), employer-provided adoption benefits (1f), Form 8919 wages (1g)
- Federal deductions for qualified tips and overtime (OBBBA, 2025–2028) are taken below federal AGI, so they never reach California AGI and need no Schedule CA entry; California has no equivalent deduction

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- All W-2s and the items above
- Treaty and sick pay details

## Questions
- Did your employer contribute to a health savings account?
- Did you receive sick pay under FICA or railroad retirement?
- Did you exclude wages under a tax treaty?
- Did your employer reimburse moving expenses?

## Common Errors
- Leaving employer HSA contributions out of California wages
- Applying a federal treaty exclusion to California
- Taxing FICA sick pay in California

## Prompt
- My employer put $2,000 into my HSA.
- I'm a foreign student and my wages were treaty-exempt federally.
