# In-Home Supportive Services (IHSS) and Medicaid Waiver Payments
## Description
Pay for caring for someone through IHSS or a Medicaid waiver program may be EXCLUDED from income when the provider lives with the person receiving care. California follows the federal exclusion, and it lets the excluded pay still count as EARNED INCOME for CalEITC.

## Eligibility for the exclusion
- Payment is from an IHSS or Medicaid waiver program
- The provider lived with the individual receiving care

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Schedule CA lines: Medicaid waiver payments not on a W-2 go on Section A, line 1d; the nontaxable amount included on federal Form 1040 line 1a or 1d is shown on Section B, line 8s
- IHSS SUPPLEMENTARY PAYMENTS (equal to sales tax the provider paid plus related payroll withholding) included in federal wages are subtracted on line 1d, column B
- Excluded pay can STILL be counted as earned income for CalEITC, which can create or increase CalEITC, YCTC and FYTC
- If a return was already filed and the refund statute of limitations is still open, the filer must AMEND to adjust income and claim credits
- If no return has been filed yet, check CalEITC eligibility and file

## Required Information
- IHSS or Medicaid waiver W-2
- Whether the care recipient lived with the provider, and for which months
- Whether the payment was excluded on the federal return

## Questions
- Are you paid through IHSS or a Medicaid waiver program?
- Does the person you care for live with you?
- Have you claimed CalEITC using this income?
- Are earlier years still open for a refund claim?

## Common Errors
- Paying tax on excludable IHSS pay
- Dropping excluded IHSS pay from CalEITC earned income
- Not amending open prior years to claim CalEITC

## Prompt
- I'm paid by IHSS to care for my mother, who lives with me.
- Does my IHSS income count for the California earned income credit?
