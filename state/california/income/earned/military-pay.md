# Military Pay
## Description
Active duty pay is wages, and California follows most federal rules for it. California's main differences concern WHO is taxed (domicile and duty station) and one combat zone that California does not recognize.

## Rules
- California-domiciled servicemembers include military pay in total income; it is California-source income when stationed in California
- Military pay is NOT California-source income when a servicemember is permanently stationed outside California
- Servicemembers domiciled outside California (and their spouses/RDPs) may exclude the servicemember's military pay when figuring the tax rate on nonmilitary income
- COMBAT PAY: California follows the federal exclusion, EXCEPT for Egypt's Sinai Peninsula. Combat pay excluded federally for Sinai service is added back on Schedule CA, line 1i, column C
- Military compensation of an enrolled tribal member living in California Indian country is treated as reservation-source income (see income/exclusions/native-american-income-exclusion.md)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Residency rules for servicemembers and spouses are in filing/residency/military-servicemember-residency.md and military-spouse-msrra.md
- Military retirement pay is covered in income/retirement/military-retirement-pay-exclusion.md
- FTB Publication 1032 has full guidance

## Required Information
- State of domicile and duty station
- W-2 and combat pay records, including the zone served

## Questions
- Where are you domiciled, and where are you permanently stationed?
- Did you receive combat pay, and in which zone?
- Do you have nonmilitary income?

## Common Errors
- Excluding Sinai Peninsula combat pay for California
- Reporting a nonresident's military pay as California-source income
- A California-domiciled servicemember stationed in California omitting military pay

## Prompt
- I received combat pay for service in the Sinai.
- I'm stationed in California but my home state is Texas.
