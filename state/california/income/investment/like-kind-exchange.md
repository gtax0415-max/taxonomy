# Like-Kind Exchanges (IRC Section 1031)
## Description
A like-kind exchange swaps business or investment property for property of the same kind, deferring gain or loss. California follows federal law, and since 2025 the exchange must involve REAL property only. When California property is traded for property OUTSIDE California, California keeps track of the deferred California-source gain and requires a yearly information return (FTB 3840) until the gain is recognized.

## California rules
- California conforms to the Internal Revenue Code as of January 1, 2025. For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, like-kind exchanges are limited to REAL PROPERTY
- Exchanges initiated after January 10, 2019 and completed before January 1, 2025: California still allowed PERSONAL property exchanges for individuals with AGI under $250,000 ($500,000 for HOH, surviving spouse or MFJ)

## FTB 3840 filing requirement
Required when BOTH occur:
- One or more California properties are exchanged for one or more properties outside California
- Any part of the California-source realized gain or loss is not recognized

Then:
- File for the year of the exchange AND every later year until the California-source deferred gain or loss is recognized
- Attach to the California return, or file it alone as an information return if there is no other California filing requirement
- E-file with the return, or mail to PO Box 1998, Rancho Cordova CA 95741-1998
- Not filing can trigger a Notice of Proposed Assessment for the deferred gain, plus penalties and interest

## Multiple properties
- SOLD replacement property: report the California-source gain; remove the property from FTB 3840 and attach a statement explaining the sale
- EXCHANGED replacement property: remove it from the original FTB 3840 and file a second FTB 3840 listing it as relinquished property, carrying its share of the original California deferred gain, with an explanatory statement
- The obligation continues through later exchanges, even if the new property is also outside California

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Location of each relinquished and replacement property
- Realized and deferred gain, and California-source portion
- Allocation of deferred gain among multiple replacement properties
- Any later sale or exchange of replacement property

## Questions
- Did you exchange California property for property in another state?
- Is the property real property?
- Did you sell or exchange any replacement property this year?
- Have you filed FTB 3840 every year since the exchange?

## Common Errors
- Stopping FTB 3840 filings after moving out of California or after a second exchange
- Treating a personal property exchange as deferred in 2026 (real property only since 2025)
- Not allocating deferred gain among replacement properties
- Not reporting California-source gain when an out-of-state replacement property is sold

## Prompt
- I traded a rental in Los Angeles for one in Texas.
- I did a 1031 exchange a few years ago and just sold the replacement property.
