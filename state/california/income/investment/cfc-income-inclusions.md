# Controlled Foreign Corporation (CFC) Inclusions
## Description
U.S. shareholders of controlled foreign corporations include Subpart F income and GILTI (IRC 951A) federally before any cash is paid. California does NOT conform to these deemed inclusions; it taxes CFC earnings when they are actually distributed as dividends.

## Rules
- Subtract the IRC 951(a) (Subpart F) inclusion: line 8n, column B
- Subtract the IRC 951A(a) (GILTI) inclusion: line 8o, column B
- Add CFC dividends in the year actually distributed: Section A, line 3, column C

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Pass-through shares from partnerships and S corporations are treated the same way
- Basis of the CFC stock can differ between federal and California because of these timing differences

## Required Information
- Subpart F and GILTI amounts included federally
- Actual distributions received

## Questions
- Do you own 10% or more of a foreign corporation?
- Did you report Subpart F income or GILTI federally?
- Did the CFC pay you dividends?

## Common Errors
- Taxing GILTI or Subpart F income in California
- Forgetting to add the CFC dividends when actually paid

## Prompt
- I own 20% of a foreign company and reported GILTI on my federal return.
