# California Credits
## Description
Credits that reduce California tax. Most are NONREFUNDABLE and claimed by credit code on Form 540, lines 43–45 (Schedule P when more than two), or on their own lines. Only CalEITC, YCTC and FYTC (and an elected Program 4.0 film credit) are REFUNDABLE (Form 540, lines 74–77). Schedule P (540), Part III sets the order of use, the tentative-minimum-tax floor and the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation (see credit-limitations-schedule-p.md).
