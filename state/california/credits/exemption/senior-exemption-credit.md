# Senior Exemption Credit
## Description
An additional exemption credit for each filer or spouse/RDP who is 65 or older by the end of the year.

## Amount
- 2026: $158 per person 65 or older (official FTB indexing; 2025 was $153)
- Enter 1 if one spouse qualifies, 2 if both do

## Eligibility
- Age 65 or older by December 31, 2026 (a 65th birthday on January 1, 2027 counts as 65 on December 31, 2026)
- Not available if someone else can claim the filer as a dependent

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: none (exemption credits reduce tax on Form 540, line 32)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- All or part of this credit may be donated to the California Seniors Special Fund (voluntary contribution code 400)
- Subject to the AGI limitation ($6 per $2,500 of federal AGI over the threshold; 2026 thresholds pending FTB release)
- Separate from the Senior Head of Household Credit (code 163)

## Required Information
- Date of birth of filer and spouse

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older by December 31, 2026?
- Can anyone claim you as a dependent?

## Common Errors
- Missing the January 1 birthday rule
- Claiming only one credit when both spouses are 65 or older

## Prompt
- I turned 65 this year. Do I get an extra California credit?
