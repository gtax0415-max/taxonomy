# Dependent Exemption Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for each dependent claimed. Dependents must generally be the same people claimed on the federal return, but California has an alternative identification route for dependents without an SSN or ITIN.

## Amount
- 2026: $491 per dependent (official FTB indexing; 2025 was $475)

## Rules
- List each dependent's name, SSN or ITIN, and relationship; attach a statement for more than three
- Dependents must match the federal return, EXCEPT when FTB 3568 is used for a dependent ineligible for an SSN and an ITIN
- Dependent with no SSN or ITIN: attach FTB 3568 with supporting documents and write "no id" in the SSN field. Prior years back to 2018 can be amended this way (Schedule X), within the refund statute
- Child born and died in the year with no SSN or ITIN: write "Died" in the SSN field and attach a birth certificate, death certificate or hospital records showing a live birth

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: none (exemption credits reduce tax on Form 540, line 32)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Subject to the AGI limitation ($6 per $2,500 of federal AGI over the threshold, per dependent; 2026 thresholds pending FTB release)
- Do not include the filer or spouse/RDP

## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, identifying number and relationship
- Identity documents for dependents without an SSN or ITIN

## Questions
- How many dependents are on your federal return?
- Does each dependent have an SSN or ITIN?
- Did a child pass away in the year of birth?

## Common Errors
- Claiming dependents different from the federal return without FTB 3568
- Leaving the SSN field blank instead of writing "no id" or "Died"
- Not amending prior years when a no-ID dependent was missed
