# Exemption Credits
## Description
California's flat exemption credits (Form 540, lines 7–11 and 32) take the place of federal personal exemptions: personal, blind, senior and dependent. For 2026: $158 per person ($316 joint) and $491 per dependent. They phase out at high federal AGI ($6 per $2,500 over the threshold).
