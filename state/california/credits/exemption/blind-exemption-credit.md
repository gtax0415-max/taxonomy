# Blind Exemption Credit
## Description
An additional exemption credit for a filer or spouse/RDP who is visually impaired.

## Amount
- 2026: $158 per visually impaired filer or spouse (official FTB indexing; 2025 was $153)
- Enter 1 if one is visually impaired, 2 if both are

## Eligibility
- Visually impaired: cannot see better than 20/200 with glasses or contact lenses, OR field of vision is not more than 20 degrees
- Attach a doctor's statement the FIRST year the credit is claimed
- Not available if someone else can claim the filer as a dependent

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: none (exemption credits reduce tax on Form 540, line 32)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Subject to the same AGI limitation as the personal exemption credit ($6 per $2,500 of federal AGI over the threshold; 2026 thresholds pending FTB release)

## Required Information
- Physician's statement (first year)
- Which spouse(s) are visually impaired

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse legally blind or visually impaired?
- Is this the first year you are claiming this credit?
- Can anyone claim you as a dependent?

## Common Errors
- Not attaching the doctor's statement in the first year
- Claiming it when the filer can be claimed as a dependent

- I'm legally blind. Is there a California tax credit?
