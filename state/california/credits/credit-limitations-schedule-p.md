# Credit Limitations and Ordering (Schedule P, Part III)
## Description
Schedule P (540), Part III decides how much of each nonrefundable credit can be used, in what order, and how much carries over. It enforces two limits: the TENTATIVE MINIMUM TAX (TMT) floor, and, for 2024 through 2026, the $5,000,000 BUSINESS CREDIT LIMITATION.

## When Schedule P must be attached
- The filer owes AMT
- The filer has more than two credits
- The filer has any credit that belongs in Section A1, A2 or C
- Certain AMT situations involving negative Part I adjustments

## Sections of Part III
- SECTION A — credits that reduce only "excess tax" (regular tax above TMT); they CANNOT reduce tax below TMT
  - A1: no carryover — Prison Inmate Labor (162), Child and Dependent Care Expenses (232)
  - A2: with carryover — film credits 223 and 237, cannabis (246, 247), Disabled Access (205), Donated Agricultural Products Transportation (204), Homeless Hiring (244), New Donated Fresh Fruits or Vegetables (238), New Employment (234), Prior Year AMT (188), and most repealed-credit carryovers
- SECTION B — credits that MAY reduce tax below TMT
  - B1: no carryover — Joint Custody Head of Household (170), Dependent Parent (173), Senior Head of Household (163), Nonrefundable Renter's
  - B2: with carryover — California Competes (233), Child Adoption (197), College Access (235), Low-Income Housing (172), Natural Heritage Preservation (213), film credits 239, 245 and 248, Research (183), State Historic Rehabilitation (243), and some repealed-credit carryovers
  - B3: Other State Tax Credit (187)
  - B4: Pass-Through Entity Elective Tax Credit (242), applied after the Other State Tax Credit
- SECTION C — credits that may reduce AMT: carryovers of the repealed Solar Energy (180) and Commercial Solar Energy (181) credits
- Not on Schedule P: exemption credits (Form 540, line 32) and the refundable CalEITC, YCTC, FYTC and Program 4.0 film credit (payments, lines 74–77)

## Ordering rules
- Use each credit in the EARLIEST section allowed; complete each section before the next
- The Prior Year AMT credit must be applied before any credit that can reduce tax below TMT
- BUSINESS credits are applied against net tax BEFORE the personal credits that are exempt from the business limitation
- Credits with limited carryover periods are usually best used before credits with unlimited carryovers
- Do not enter the same credit code on more than one line within a section

## Business credit limitation (2024–2026)
- All business credits, including carryovers, may not reduce net tax by more than $5,000,000 per year
- NOT subject to the limitation: EITC, Young Child, Foster Youth, household and dependent care, the elective tax credit under the Small Business Relief Act (PTE), adoption costs, renter's, personal exemption, joint custody head of household and dependent parent, senior head of household, low-income housing, excess unemployment compensation contributions, and the Prior Year AMT credit
- Disallowed amounts carry over, with the carryover period extended by the number of years disallowed (Prison Inmate Labor, which normally has no carryover, can carry over only the disallowed portion)
- REFUND ELECTION: each year, a filer may irrevocably elect on FTB 3870 (with an original, timely return) to receive the disallowed business credits as an annual refundable amount in later years
- Keep a worksheet of disallowed business credits and provide it to FTB on request

## Claiming on Form 540
- Child and Dependent Care Expenses (Section A1) → Form 540, line 40
- Nonrefundable Renter's (Section B1) → Form 540, line 46
- One or two other credits → name, code and amount on lines 43 and 44
- More than two other credits → total of column (b) on line 45

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: all sections (A1–C)
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Each credit's amount and code
- Regular tax, TMT and net tax
- Carryovers and prior FTB 3870 elections

## Questions
- How many credits are you claiming?
- Do any belong in Section A (cannot go below TMT)?
- Is your total of business credits over $5,000,000?
- Do you want to elect a refund of business credits disallowed by the limitation?

## Common Errors
- Letting a Section A credit reduce tax below tentative minimum tax
- Applying personal credits before business credits
- Listing more than two credits on lines 43–44 instead of using line 45
- Missing the FTB 3870 election on an original, timely return
