# Child Adoption Costs Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for 50% of the cost of adopting a child who was in the custody of a California public agency. Much narrower than the federal adoption credit: private, foreign and independent adoptions do not qualify.

## Amount
- 50% of qualifying costs
- Maximum $2,500 per child per tax year
- Excess credit carries over to future years until used

## Eligibility
The child must have been both:
- A U.S. citizen or legal resident
- In the custody of a California public agency or political subdivision

## Qualified costs
- Fees paid to the Department of Social Services or a licensed adoption agency
- Medical costs not covered by insurance
- Travel expenses
- Costs of an unsuccessful adoption attempt that later succeeded, even with a different child

## Notes
- Credit code: 197
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Claimed in the year the adoption decree or order is entered (the adoption is final)
- Not available for a child adopted from another country or another state
- Any deduction for the same expenses must be reduced by the credit (Schedule CA, Part II, line 27)
- NONREFUNDABLE, with an unlimited carryover
- Claimed by entering the credit name, code 197 and amount on line 43 or 44
- Can be claimed in addition to the federal adoption credit; the two have separate rules and limits

## Required Information
- Child's name and custody history with a California public agency
- Qualifying costs by type
- Child's citizenship or legal residency
- Any carryover from prior years

## Questions
- Was the child in the custody of a California county or state agency?
- What fees, uninsured medical costs and travel did you pay?
- Did you have an earlier adoption attempt that failed?
- Do you have unused credit from a prior year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a private, independent or international adoption
- Claiming 100% of costs instead of 50%
- Exceeding $2,500 per child in one year instead of carrying over
- Forgetting the carryover in later years

## Prompt
- We adopted a child through the county foster system.
- I adopted a child in California. Is there a state credit?
