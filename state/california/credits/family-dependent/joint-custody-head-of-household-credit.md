# Joint Custody Head of Household Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an unmarried (or separated) parent who shares custody of a child under a custody agreement but cannot file as head of household because the child lives with them for only part of the year.

## Amount
- 30% of net tax (Form 540, line 35)
- Maximum for 2026: pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $610

## Eligibility
- Joint custody of a child, stepchild or grandchild under a custody agreement
- Not married/RDP at year-end, OR married/RDP but lived apart from the spouse the whole year
- Paid more than half the household expenses of the home
- The home was the child's main home for at least 146 days but NOT more than 219 days

## Notes
- Credit code: 170
- Schedule P (540) section: B1 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; no carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The custody arrangement must be part of a decree of dissolution or legal separation, or a written agreement between the parents where proceedings have started but no decree has been issued
- If the child is married or an RDP, the filer must be entitled to a dependent exemption credit for the child
- Cannot file married/RDP jointly, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse/RDP
- The 146–219 day window is the core test: fewer days fails, more days generally means head of household status instead
- Cannot be claimed together with the Dependent Parent credit
- Form 540NR computes it differently (line 31 minus line 11, plus line 41, times 30%)

## Required Information
- Custody agreement
- Number of days the child lived in the home
- Household expenses paid
- Form 540, line 35 tax

## Questions
- Do you share custody under a written agreement?
- How many days did the child live with you?
- Did you pay more than half your household's costs?
- What filing status are you using?

## Common Errors
- Claiming with fewer than 146 or more than 219 days
- Claiming while also filing head of household
- Claiming without a custody agreement
- Claiming both this and the dependent parent credit

## Prompt
- My ex and I split custody and my son lives with me about half the year.
