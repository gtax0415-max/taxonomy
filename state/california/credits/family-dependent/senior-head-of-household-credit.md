# Senior Head of Household Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a filer 65 or older who lost head of household status because their qualifying person died within the past two years.

## Amount
- 2% of California taxable income (Form 540, line 19)
- Maximum for 2026: pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $1,860

## Eligibility
- 65 or older on December 31 of the tax year
- Qualified as head of household in at least 1 of the past 2 years
- The qualifying person died in the past 2 years
- California AGI below the 2026 threshold (pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $98,652)

## Notes
- Credit code: 163
- Schedule P (540) section: B1 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; no carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- For 2026: qualified as head of household in 2024 or 2025 for a qualifying individual who died in 2024 or 2025 (the 2025 booklet uses 2023 or 2024 for the 2025 return)
- The filer does not need to qualify for head of household status in 2026 to claim the credit
- Built on TAXABLE income (line 19), unlike the joint custody and dependent parent credits, which use line 35 tax
- The window closes two years after the qualifying person's death
- The income test is a cliff (2025: $98,652)

## Required Information
- Filer's date of birth
- Prior-year filing status
- Qualifying person's date of death
- California AGI and taxable income

## Questions
- Were you 65 or older on December 31?
- Did you file as head of household in either of the last two years?
- When did your qualifying person die?
- Is your California AGI under the 2026 threshold (2025: $98,652)?

## Common Errors
- Using line 35 tax instead of 2% of line 19 taxable income
- Claiming more than two years after the death
- Claiming with income at or above the threshold

## Prompt
- I'm 70 and my adult son, who I claimed as head of household, passed away in 2025.
