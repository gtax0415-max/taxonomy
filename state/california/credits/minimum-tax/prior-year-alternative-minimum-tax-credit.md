# Credit for Prior Year Alternative Minimum Tax
## Description
Nonrefundable credit that gives back California AMT paid in a prior year when that AMT arose from timing (deferral) items, which later reverse. Comparable to the federal minimum tax credit on Form 8801.

## Eligibility
Either:
- An AMT credit carryover from last year, OR
- AMT paid last year with adjustments and preference items OTHER THAN exclusion items

## Notes
- Credit code: 188
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover); applied before credits that can reduce tax below TMT
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Schedule P, Section A2; must be applied BEFORE any credit that can reduce regular tax below tentative minimum tax
- Not subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Only AMT caused by deferral items (for example, depreciation timing or incentive stock option spreads) generates the credit; AMT caused by exclusion items does not
- Figured on FTB 3510 and attached to the return
- Unused credit carries forward

## Required Information
- Prior-year AMT paid
- Breakdown of prior-year AMT items into deferral vs. exclusion
- Prior-year carryover amount
- Current-year regular tax and tentative minimum tax

## Questions
- Did you pay California AMT last year?
- What caused the AMT (ISOs, depreciation, other)?
- Do you have an AMT credit carryover?

## Common Errors
- Including AMT from exclusion items
- Not carrying the unused credit forward
- Not attaching FTB 3510

## Prompt
- I paid AMT last year because I exercised incentive stock options.
- I have a California AMT credit carryover.
