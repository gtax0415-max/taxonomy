# College Access Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to the College Access Tax Credit Fund, which funds financial aid for low-income college students. The credit must be reserved through CEFA before the contribution is made.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Available through tax year 2027 (so available for 2026)
- Unused credit carries over up to 6 years

## How to apply (before claiming)
1. Submit the Tax Credit Application Form to CEFA
2. CEFA sends a letter granting a credit amount
3. Pay CEFA by the due date in the letter
4. CEFA issues a College Access Tax Credit Certification

## Notes
- Credit code: 235
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- CAN reduce tax below tentative minimum tax
- Any charitable deduction taken on the FEDERAL return for the same contribution must be ADDED BACK on the California return
- Enter the certification number on FTB 3592 and attach it

## Required Information
- CEFA certification number and allocated credit
- Contribution amount and payment date
- Any federal charitable deduction for the contribution
- Carryover from prior years

## Questions
- Did you apply through CEFA and receive a certification?
- How much did you contribute?
- Did you deduct this contribution on your federal Schedule A?
- Do you have unused credit from an earlier year?

## Common Errors
- Donating without CEFA approval first
- Not adding back the federal charitable deduction
- Claiming 100% of the contribution instead of 50%
- Losing the carryover after 6 years

## Prompt
- I donated to the California College Access Tax Credit Fund.
