# Natural Heritage Preservation Credit
## Description
A credit for a qualified contribution of property donated to the state, a local government, or a nonprofit designated by a local government.

## Amount
- 55% of the fair market value of the qualified contribution

## Notes
- Credit code: 213
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The same contribution cannot also be deducted as a charitable contribution for California

## Required Information
- Appraisal and fair market value
- Recipient and acceptance

## Questions
- Did you donate land or an easement to a California public agency or designated nonprofit?

## Common Errors
- Taking both the credit and a California charitable deduction for the same property

## Prompt
- I donated conservation land to the county.
