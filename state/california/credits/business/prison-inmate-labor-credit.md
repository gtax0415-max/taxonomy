# Prison Inmate Labor Credit
## Description
A credit for wages paid to prison inmates employed under a qualifying joint venture program.

## Amount
- 10% of wages paid to prison inmates

## Notes
- Credit code: 162
- Schedule P (540) section: A1 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; no carryover, except the portion disallowed by the business credit limitation)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- No carryover, EXCEPT the portion disallowed by the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation, which carries over for the number of years it was disallowed

## Required Information
- Program agreement
- Inmate wages paid

## Questions
- Does your business employ inmates through a California joint venture program?

## Common Errors
- Including wages paid outside the qualifying program

## Prompt
- Our company employs inmates through a state joint venture program.
