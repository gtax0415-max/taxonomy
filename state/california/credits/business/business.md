# Business Credits
## Description
Credits for business activity, most claimed on their own FTB form and entered by credit code (Schedule P (540) when more than two). For tax years 2024 through 2026, total business credits are capped at $5,000,000 per year (SB 167); the PTE elective tax credit and low-income housing credit are among the exceptions. Credits blocked by the cap carry forward with an extended period, or an annual FTB 3870 election (on an original, timely return) refunds them in later years. Each file records its Schedule P section (A1/A2 cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; B1–B4 can). Ordering rules: credits/credit-limitations-schedule-p.md.
