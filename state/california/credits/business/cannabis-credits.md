# Cannabis Business Credits
## Description
Two credits for licensed commercial cannabis businesses.

## Credits
- HIGH-ROAD CANNABIS TAX CREDIT (code 246, FTB 3820): for a commercial cannabis business holding a Type-10 (retailer) or Type-12 (micro-business) DCC license. The business must request a TENTATIVE CREDIT RESERVATION from FTB
- CANNABIS EQUITY TAX CREDIT (code 247, FTB 3821): for an equity licensee approved (including contingent approval) for the DCC fee waiver and deferral program

## Notes
- Credit code: 246, 247
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- California also lets licensed cannabis businesses deduct business expenses that federal law disallows (see income/business-rental/business-income-adjustments.md)
- Subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap

## Required Information
- License type and number
- Credit reservation or fee-program approval
- Qualifying expenses

## Questions
- What type of DCC license do you hold?
- Did you obtain a tentative credit reservation?
- Are you an equity licensee?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the High-Road credit without a reservation
- Claiming with an ineligible license type

## Prompt
- I own a licensed cannabis dispensary in California.
