# Donated Agricultural Products Transportation Credit
## Description
A credit for the cost of transporting agricultural products donated to nonprofit charitable organizations.

## Amount
- 50% of the transportation costs paid or incurred

## Notes
- Credit code: 204
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Transportation costs
- Recipient charity and donation records

## Questions
- Did you pay to transport donated crops or farm products to a charity?

## Common Errors
- Including the value of the products instead of the transportation cost

## Prompt
- We trucked donated produce to a food bank.
