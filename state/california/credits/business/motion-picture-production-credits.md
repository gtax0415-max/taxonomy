# California Motion Picture and Television Production Credits
## Description
Credits allocated and certified by the California Film Commission for qualified production spending in California. Several programs exist, each with its own code.

## Programs
- 223: Original California Motion Picture and Television Production credit (2011 onward)
- 237: New California Motion Picture and Television Production credit (2016 onward)
- 239: Program 3.0 (2020 onward)
- 245: Soundstage Filming Tax Credit (2022 onward), for productions at a certified studio construction project with an approved diversity workplan
- 248: Program 4.0 (2025 onward)

## Refundable Program 4.0
- For tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025, a taxpayer may make an IRREVOCABLE election to make the Program 4.0 credit refundable
- The refundable amount is reported as a payment on Form 540, line 74

## Notes
- Credit code: 223, 237, 239, 245, 248
- Schedule P (540) section: 223 and 237: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax); 239, 245 and 248: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax); refundable Program 4.0: Form 540, line 74
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Program 4.0 credit can also be SOLD to an unrelated party (independent films only, FTB 3551), ASSIGNED to an affiliated corporation, or applied against qualified SALES AND USE TAXES
- The refundable election is a one-time irrevocable election, available when the Program 4.0 credit exceeds the tax liability
- Credits must be certified by the California Film Commission (film.ca.gov)
- Subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap unless an exception applies

## Required Information
- Credit certificate and program
- Amount allocated, and whether the refund election was made
- Pass-through share, if from an entity

## Questions
- Which program certified your credit?
- Did you elect to make the Program 4.0 credit refundable?
- Did you receive the credit through a partnership or S corporation?

## Common Errors
- Using the wrong credit code for the program
- Claiming without a Film Commission certificate
- Reporting the refundable Program 4.0 amount as a nonrefundable credit

## Prompt
- I have a California film tax credit certificate.
