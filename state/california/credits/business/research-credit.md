# Research Credit
## Description
Similar to the federal research credit, but limited to qualified research activities conducted IN CALIFORNIA.

## Notes
- Credit code: 183
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Only California research costs count; federal Form 6765 figures must be recomputed
- Unused credit carries forward
- Subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap; amounts blocked by the cap carry forward (with an extended carryover period) or may be partly refunded under the SB 175 election

## Required Information
- California research expenses by type
- Base-period amounts
- Carryovers

## Questions
- Did you perform research and development in California?
- Do you claim the federal research credit?
- Do you have research credit carryovers?

## Common Errors
- Including research performed outside California
- Copying the federal credit amount

## Prompt
- My company does R&D in San Diego. Is there a state credit?
