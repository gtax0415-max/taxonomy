# New Donated Fresh Fruits or Vegetables Credit
## Description
A credit for qualified taxpayers who donate fresh fruits, vegetables or other qualified items to California food banks.

## Amount
- 15% of the qualified value of the donated items, based on the weighted average wholesale price

## Notes
- Credit code: 238
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The older Donated Fresh Fruits or Vegetables credit (code 224) is repealed; only carryovers remain

## Required Information
- Food bank certificate
- Qualified value of donated items

## Questions
- Did you donate fresh produce to a California food bank?

## Common Errors
- Using retail value instead of the weighted average wholesale price
- Using the repealed code 224 for new donations

## Prompt
- Our farm donated vegetables to the local food bank.
