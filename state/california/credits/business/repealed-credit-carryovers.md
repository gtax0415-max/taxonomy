# Repealed Credits with Carryovers
## Description
Many California credits have expired, but unused amounts with carryover provisions can still be claimed until used up (or until a fixed carryover period ends).

## Repealed credits (code, form, Schedule P section)
- A2 (cannot reduce tax below TMT), FTB 3540 unless noted: Agricultural Products (175), Donated Fresh Fruits or Vegetables (224), Employee Ridesharing (194), Employer Child Care Contribution (190), Employer Child Care Program (189), Employer Ridesharing large (191), small (192) and transit passes (193), Energy Conservation (182), Enhanced Oil Recovery (203), Environmental Tax (218), Farmworker Housing (207), Low-Emission Vehicles (160), Main Street Small Business Tax (240) and II (241), Political Contributions (184), Recycling Equipment (174), Residential Rental & Farm Sales (186), Ridesharing (171), Salmon & Steelhead Trout Habitat Restoration (200), Solar Pump (179), Water Conservation (178), Young Infant (161)
- A2, area programs: Local Agency Military Base Recovery Area Hiring and Sales or Use Tax (198, FTB 3807), Manufacturing Enhancement Area Hiring (211, FTB 3808)
- B2 (may reduce tax below TMT): Commercial Solar Electric System (196, FTB 3540), Orphan Drug (185, FTB 3540), Enterprise Zone Hiring and Sales or Use Tax (176, FTB 3805Z), Targeted Tax Area Hiring and Sales or Use Tax (210, FTB 3809)
- B2 and C (may also reduce AMT): Solar Energy (180) and Commercial Solar Energy (181), FTB 3540. Enter the solar carryover on Schedule P, line 16 first, then any remaining carryover in Section C

## Notes
- Credit code: 160–241 (repealed codes)
- Schedule P (540) section: varies by credit (A2, B2 or C; see list)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Only a carryover from earlier years can be claimed; no new credit is generated
- A credit with a fixed carryover period expires at the end of that period
- Carryovers are subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap unless excepted

## Required Information
- Credit name, code and original year
- Carryover balance

## Questions
- Do you have unused credits from earlier California returns?
- Which credit, and from what year?

## Common Errors
- Losing track of carryovers from expired credits
- Claiming an expired credit as a new credit

## Prompt
- I still have an Enterprise Zone hiring credit carryover.
