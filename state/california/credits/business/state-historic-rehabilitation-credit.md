# State Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit
## Description
A credit, allocated by the California Tax Credit Allocation Committee, for rehabilitating certified historic structures and, for individual taxpayers, a qualified residence.

## Notes
- Credit code: 243
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Requires an allocation before the credit can be claimed
- The credit can be RECAPTURED (Form 540, line 63) under the program's rules
- Information: ohp.parks.ca.gov

## Required Information
- Allocation and certification
- Qualified rehabilitation expenditures

## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a certified historic building or a qualified historic residence?
- Did you receive a credit allocation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without an allocation
- Missing recapture on early disposition

## Prompt
- I restored a historic home in California.
