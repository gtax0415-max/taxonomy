# New Employment Credit
## Description
A credit for qualified taxpayers who hire a qualified full-time employee, pay qualified wages, and receive a tentative credit reservation for that employee.

## Requirements
- Hire a qualified full-time employee
- Pay or incur qualified wages
- Receive a tentative credit reservation from FTB for the employee

## Notes
- Credit code: 234
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The credit can be RECAPTURED (Form 540, line 63) if requirements are later not met
- Subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap

## Required Information
- Credit reservation
- Qualified wages per employee

## Questions
- Did you hire new full-time employees in a designated area?
- Did you obtain a credit reservation for each?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a reservation
- Missing recapture

## Prompt
- I hired new full-time staff and reserved the New Employment Credit.
