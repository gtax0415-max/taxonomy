# Homeless Hiring Tax Credit
## Description
A credit for employers who hire eligible individuals experiencing homelessness.

## Requirements
- Obtain a certification of the individual's homeless status from an organization that works with people experiencing homelessness
- Receive a TENTATIVE CREDIT RESERVATION from FTB for that employee

## Notes
- Credit code: 244
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Employee certification
- Credit reservation
- Wages paid

## Questions
- Did you hire someone who was experiencing homelessness?
- Did you get a certification and a credit reservation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a tentative credit reservation

## Prompt
- I hired an employee referred by a homeless services nonprofit.
