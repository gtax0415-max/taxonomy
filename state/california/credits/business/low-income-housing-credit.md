# Low-Income Housing Credit
## Description
Similar to the federal low-income housing credit, but limited to low-income housing located in California.

## Notes
- Credit code: 172
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- EXEMPT from the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap
- The rental real estate passive loss offset dollar limit does not apply to the low-income housing credit program (tax years 2020 and later)

## Required Information
- TCAC allocation
- Investor's share of the credit
- Carryovers

## Questions
- Do you invest in a California low-income housing project?
- Did you receive a K-1 showing a California credit?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal credit amount
- Applying the $5 million cap to this credit

## Prompt
- I have a California low-income housing credit from a partnership K-1.
