# Disabled Access Credit for Eligible Small Businesses
## Description
Similar to the federal disabled access credit for small businesses that make their facilities accessible, but much smaller.

## Amount
- 50% of qualified expenditures that do not exceed $250
- Maximum credit: $125

## Notes
- Credit code: 205
- Schedule P (540) section: A2 (cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The business must be an eligible small business under the federal definition
- Unused credit carries forward

## Required Information
- Accessibility expenditures
- Eligible small business status

## Questions
- Did your small business spend money making the premises accessible to people with disabilities?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal credit amount instead of the California $125 maximum

## Prompt
- I installed a wheelchair ramp at my shop.
