# California Competes Tax Credit
## Description
A negotiated credit, allocated and certified by the California Competes Tax Credit Committee, for businesses that come to California or stay and grow here.

## Notes
- Credit code: 233
- Schedule P (540) section: B2 (may reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; carryover)
- Business credit limitation: Subject to the 2024–2026 $5,000,000 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The credit is tied to an agreement with milestones (hiring, investment); missed milestones can trigger RECAPTURE on Form 540, line 63 (enter only the recaptured amount used)
- Subject to the 2024–2026 $5 million business credit cap
- Information: business.ca.gov

## Required Information
- Credit agreement and allocation
- Milestone results for the year
- Carryovers

## Questions
- Do you have a California Competes agreement?
- Were all milestones met?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before milestones are met
- Missing recapture when milestones fail

## Prompt
- My business received a California Competes tax credit.
