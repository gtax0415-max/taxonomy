# Foster Youth Tax Credit (FYTC)
## Description
Refundable credit for current and former foster youth ages 18 to 25 who qualify for CalEITC and were placed through the California foster care system at age 13 or older.

## Amount
- Maximum credit for 2026: pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $1,189 per eligible individual
- Double if both the filer and spouse/RDP qualify (2025: $2,378)
- Phases out as earned income rises (see FTB 3514, Part IX)

## Eligibility
- Qualify for CalEITC
- Age 18 through 25 at the end of the tax year
- In foster care at age 13 or older, placed through the California foster care system
- Foster care status verified

## Verification
Status is verified in one of three ways:
1. Check the consent box on FTB 3514, line 33 (or the software equivalent), allowing CDSS to verify status from its statewide database
2. Attach the CDSS verification letter (paper) or upload it (e-file, if possible)
3. Otherwise FTB mails a letter requesting verification

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: none (refundable; Form 540, line 77)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Phase-out (2025 booklet): begins when earned income exceeds $27,425 and ends at $32,901; 2026 thresholds pending FTB release
- REFUNDABLE
- Available for 2022 forward by filing or amending
- Placement must have been through CALIFORNIA's system; out-of-state foster care does not qualify
- Questions about foster status go to CDSS (916-651-8848, weekdays 8 AM–5 PM)

## Required Information
- Date of birth for each qualifying individual
- Age at placement and confirmation it was through California foster care
- Verification consent or CDSS letter
- CalEITC eligibility

## Questions
- Were you in California foster care at age 13 or older?
- How old were you on December 31?
- Do you qualify for CalEITC?
- Were both you and your spouse in foster care?

## Common Errors
- Not qualifying for CalEITC first
- Not checking the consent box or providing verification, which delays the credit
- Claiming with placement only before age 13 or outside California
- Missing the second credit when both spouses qualify

## Prompt
- I was in foster care as a teenager and I'm 22 now.
- My spouse and I were both foster youth.
