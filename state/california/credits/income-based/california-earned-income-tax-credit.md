# California Earned Income Tax Credit (CalEITC)
## Description
Refundable credit for low-income working families and individuals. Similar to the federal Earned Income Credit but with its own, much lower income limits. It also acts as the gateway credit: the Young Child Tax Credit and the Foster Youth Tax Credit both require the filer to qualify for CalEITC.

## Amount
- Maximum credit for 2026: pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $3,756
- Earned income limit for 2026: pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $32,900
- Investment income limit: $3,400, FIXED by SB 1435 (2026) and no longer tied to the federal amount
- Amount depends on earned income and number of qualifying children (see the FTB 3514 booklet's credit table)

## Eligibility
- Earned income at or below the 2026 limit (pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 limit was $32,900)
- Investment income of $3,400 or less
- Earned income is wages, salaries, tips and other employee compensation subject to California withholding, or net self-employment income
- Must file a California return and attach FTB 3514 (or follow e-file software instructions)

## Notes
- Schedule P (540) section: none (refundable; Form 540, line 75)
- Business credit limitation: Not subject to the 2024–2026 business credit limitation
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- REFUNDABLE: produces a refund even when no California tax is owed
- Can generally be claimed for up to four prior years by filing or amending a California return
- Qualifying for CalEITC opens eligibility for YCTC (child under 6) and FYTC (former foster youth 18–25)
- California limits differ from the federal EIC; qualifying for one does not guarantee the other

## Required Information
- Earned income by source (W-2 wages, net self-employment income)
- Identifying number for filer, spouse and each qualifying child
- Qualifying child's age, relationship and months lived with the filer
- Investment income (must be $3,400 or less)

## Questions
- Did you have wages or self-employment income in 2026?
- Was your total earned income under the 2026 limit (2025 limit: $32,900)?
- Was your investment income $3,400 or less?
- Do you have a qualifying child, and how many?
- Did you miss claiming CalEITC in any of the last four years?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the federal EIC income limits apply in California
- Using the federal investment income limit instead of California's fixed $3,400
- Not attaching FTB 3514
- Missing the linked YCTC or FYTC after qualifying for CalEITC
- Not amending prior years when the credit was missed

## Prompt
- I earn about $25,000 a year in California. Do I get a state earned income credit?
- I qualified for the federal EIC. Does California have one too?
