# Investment Interest Expense
## Description
Interest on money borrowed to buy taxable investments, limited to net investment income. The California amount can differ from federal because California investment income differs.

## Reasons California differs
- California taxes interest from non-California municipal bonds and exempts U.S. obligation interest, changing net investment income
- California has no special capital gains rate, so the federal election to treat qualified dividends or capital gains as investment income works differently
- Disallowed interest carries forward under California's own computation

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Interest used to buy tax-exempt investments for California is not deductible
- Investment interest is excluded from the itemized deduction limitation

## Required Information
- Investment interest paid
- California net investment income
- California carryover

## Questions
- Did you pay margin interest or borrow to buy investments?
- Do you have an investment interest carryover?

## Common Errors
- Copying the federal Form 4952 amount without FTB 3526

## Prompt
- I paid $8,000 of margin interest.
