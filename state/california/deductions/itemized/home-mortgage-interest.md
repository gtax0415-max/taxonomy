# Home Mortgage Interest
## Description
Itemized deduction for interest on debt used to buy, build or improve a main or second home. California did NOT adopt the federal TCJA limit: the California cap is $1,000,000 of acquisition debt, versus $750,000 federally.

## Amounts
- California: interest on up to $1,000,000 of home acquisition debt
- Federal: interest on up to $750,000 of home acquisition debt
- A loan between $750,000 and $1,000,000 creates extra California interest, added in column C

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- HOME EQUITY INTEREST: federal law suspended the deduction for interest on up to $100,000 ($50,000 MFS) of home equity debt not used to buy, build or improve the home; California still allows it (line 8, column C)
- MORTGAGE INTEREST CREDIT: if the federal deduction was reduced by the federal mortgage interest credit (Form 8396), add that amount back for California (line 8, column C)
- Starts from federal Schedule A, line 8; the extra California interest is an addition on Schedule CA, Part II, line 8
- Interest on a mortgage in excess of the federal limit needs a proration worksheet for each limit
- Since California itemizing is separate, a filer can take the federal standard deduction and still itemize mortgage interest in California

## Required Information
- 1098 interest amount
- Loan balance and acquisition date
- Use of loan proceeds

## Questions
- What is your mortgage balance, and when was the loan taken out?
- Is the balance above $750,000?
- Did you use any loan proceeds for something other than buying or improving the home?

## Common Errors
- Applying the federal $750,000 limit on the California return
- Not itemizing in California when the federal return used the standard deduction

## Prompt
- We have a $900,000 mortgage on our home in San Jose.
- How much mortgage interest can I deduct in California?
