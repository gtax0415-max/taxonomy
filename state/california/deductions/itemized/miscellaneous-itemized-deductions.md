# Miscellaneous Itemized Deductions (2% floor)
## Description
California STILL allows miscellaneous itemized deductions subject to a 2% of federal AGI floor. The federal deduction has been suspended since 2018, so these are California-only deductions entered directly on Schedule CA.

## Amount
- Expenses above 2% of federal AGI

## What qualifies
- Unreimbursed employee business expenses (federal Form 2106 line 10, figured with California amounts)
- Tax preparation and e-filing fees (card convenience fees for paying tax go on a separate line)
- Certain legal and accounting fees
- Investment custodial fees
- Certain casualty and theft losses, and claim-of-right repayments of $3,000 or less

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Because nothing is on federal Schedule A, these are entered on Schedule CA, Part II, lines 19–21
- Useful for employees with large unreimbursed job costs

## Required Information
- Itemized list of expenses with receipts
- Employer reimbursements
- Federal AGI

## Questions
- Did you pay work expenses your employer did not reimburse?
- What did you pay for tax preparation?
- Did you pay investment or legal fees?

## Common Errors
- Skipping these because they are not on the federal return
- Including reimbursed expenses
- Forgetting the 2% of federal AGI reduction

## Prompt
- I paid for my own work tools and uniforms.
- Can I deduct my tax preparation fees in California?
