# Charitable Contributions
## Description
California allows the itemized charitable deduction with its own limits. It does not follow several federal limits and expansions, including the 2026 OBBBA changes.

## California limits (column B reduces the federal amount)
- CASH and other gifts: limited to 50% of federal AGI (federal allows 60% for cash to public charities)
- QUALIFIED CONSERVATION CONTRIBUTIONS: limited to 30% of federal AGI (federal 50%); carryover period 5 years (federal 15 years)
- COLLEGE ACCESS TAX CREDIT contributions: remove the amount used to compute the credit
- Gifts to a postsecondary institution or the Key Worldwide Foundation are disallowed for taxpayers convicted in the specified admissions-fraud cases (R&TC 17275.4)
- Appreciated stock given to a private foundation before January 1, 2002: California carryover uses basis, not fair market value

## Carryovers (line 13)
- California carryover larger than federal: add the difference (column C)
- Expired California conservation carryover: subtract it (column B)

## 2026 federal changes California does NOT follow
- Federal 0.5%-of-AGI floor on itemized charitable deductions (starting 2026): California has no floor, so the California deduction can be larger
- Federal 35% cap on the tax benefit of itemized deductions for the top bracket: no California equivalent
- Federal deduction for NON-ITEMIZERS ($1,000 single / $2,000 joint, cash gifts, starting 2026): taken below federal AGI, so it does not reach California; California offers no non-itemizer deduction

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Gifts by type and recipient
- Federal AGI
- Carryover history

## Questions
- How much did you give to charity in cash and in property?
- Did you donate a conservation easement?
- Did you contribute to the College Access Tax Credit Fund?
- Do you have charitable carryovers?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal 60% AGI limit
- Deducting College Access contributions that generated the credit
- Applying the federal 0.5% floor to California

## Prompt
- We gave $15,000 to charity this year.
- I donated a conservation easement on my ranch.
