# Taxes Paid (Itemized)
## Description
California's deduction for taxes paid differs sharply from federal law: no deduction for state income tax, but NO cap on property taxes.

## Not deductible for California (column B)
- 5a STATE AND LOCAL INCOME TAX, State Disability Insurance (SDI), limited partnership tax, and general sales tax
- 6 FOREIGN INCOME TAXES
- 6 Generation-skipping transfer tax

## Allowed in full (column C where federal law limits)
- 5b Real estate taxes and 5c personal property taxes (including the value-based part of vehicle license fees)
- 5e SALT CAP: California does not adopt the federal cap. Federal 2025: $40,000 ($20,000 MFS). Federal 2026 (OBBBA): $40,400 ($20,200 MFS), phased down toward $10,000 when MAGI exceeds $505,000 ($252,500 MFS). Add back any amount the federal cap disallowed (column C)
- 6 FOREIGN REAL PROPERTY TAXES: suspended federally, allowed by California (column C)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Because state income tax is removed, many California itemizers deduct only property taxes here
- Itemized deductions may be reduced at high income (see deductions/itemized/itemized-deduction-limitation.md)

## Required Information
- Real estate and personal property taxes paid
- State income tax, SDI and sales tax claimed federally
- Foreign taxes paid and their type

## Questions
- How much property tax did you pay on your home and other real estate?
- Did the federal SALT cap limit your deduction?
- Did you pay foreign income or property taxes?

## Common Errors
- Deducting California income tax or SDI on the California return
- Applying the federal SALT cap to California
- Deducting foreign income taxes

## Prompt
- I paid $25,000 in property taxes and $30,000 in state income tax.
