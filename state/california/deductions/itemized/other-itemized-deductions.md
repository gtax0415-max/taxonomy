# Other Itemized Deductions and Adjustments
## Description
The "other" itemized items on federal Schedule A, line 16, plus California-only adjustments on Schedule CA, line 27.

## Line 16 differences
- IMPAIRMENT-RELATED WORK EXPENSES: recompute on Form 2106 with California amounts (pre-1987 depreciation; federal temporary-duty travel for prosecutions over one year is not allowed)
- FEDERAL ESTATE TAX on income in respect of a decedent: NOT deductible for California (column B)
- GAMBLING LOSSES: see deductions/itemized/gambling-losses.md
- CLAIM OF RIGHT repayments over $3,000: deduct, or take a credit instead, whichever gives the lower tax; the credit goes on Form 540, line 78 (see payments/claim-of-right-credit.md). No deduction or credit if California never taxed the amount
- SOCIAL SECURITY REPAYMENTS: California never taxed Social Security, so remove any federal deduction (column B)

## Line 27 California-only adjustments
- ADOPTION expenses deducted federally that also produced the California Child Adoption Costs Credit: enter the credit as a negative number
- Expenses of producing income taxed federally but NOT by California: negative number
- Expenses of producing income taxed by California but NOT federally: positive number
- State legislators' travel expenses: only overnight travel away from home qualifies; enter the difference as a negative number
- INTEREST ON UTILITY COMPANY LOANS used to buy and install energy-efficient equipment (including zone heating) for a California residence: positive number (no federal equivalent)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Each item on federal Schedule A, line 16
- Repayment amount and the year originally taxed
- Federal estate tax on income in respect of a decedent
- Utility loan interest paid and the residence location

## Questions
- Did you repay income you reported in an earlier year?
- Did you pay federal estate tax on inherited income?
- Did you finance energy-efficient equipment through your utility?
- Did you claim the California adoption credit?

## Common Errors
- Deducting federal estate tax on IRD for California
- Taking a claim of right deduction for an amount California never taxed
- Missing the utility loan interest deduction

## Prompt
- I had to repay $5,000 of wages I received last year.
- My utility financed a new heat pump and I pay interest on the loan.
