# Itemized Deductions
## Description
California itemized deductions, starting from federal Schedule A and adjusted on Schedule CA (540), Part II. Key differences: the $1,000,000 mortgage limit, miscellaneous deductions subject to the 2% floor, gambling losses at 100% of winnings, and Governor-declared disasters.
