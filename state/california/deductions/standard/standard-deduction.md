# California Standard Deduction
## Description
California allows every filing status to take a standard deduction, but it is FAR LOWER than the federal one. Many filers who take the federal standard deduction are better off itemizing in California.

## Amounts for 2026 (official FTB indexing, 3.4% inflation)
- Single or married/RDP filing separately: $5,900 (2025: $5,706)
- Married/RDP filing jointly, head of household, or qualifying surviving spouse/RDP: $11,800 (2025: $11,412)

## Filer who can be claimed as a dependent
1. Enter line 2 of the federal Standard Deduction Worksheet for Dependents
2. Minimum standard deduction: $1,350 (2026 federal minimum under Rev. Proc. 2025-32, unchanged from 2025; the 2025 California worksheet used the same amount — confirm in the 2026 Form 540 booklet)
3. Enter the larger of line 1 or line 2
4. Enter $5,900 (single or MFS) or $11,800 (MFJ, HOH, QSS)
5. The smaller of line 3 or line 4 is the California standard deduction

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Choose the LARGER of the standard deduction or California itemized deductions
- California itemizing is independent of the federal choice: a filer can take the federal standard deduction and still itemize in California
- Married/RDP filing separately: if one spouse itemizes, the other must also itemize
- Related 2026 exemption credits (official): personal $158 (single, MFS, HOH), $316 (MFJ, QSS); dependent $491 each

## Required Information
- Filing status
- Whether the filer can be claimed as a dependent
- California itemized deduction total, for comparison

## Questions
- What is your filing status?
- Can someone claim you as a dependent?
- Do your mortgage interest, property tax, medical and charitable totals exceed the California standard deduction?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal standard deduction amount
- Not comparing against California itemized deductions because the federal return used the standard deduction
- Applying the full amount to a dependent filer

## Prompt
- What is the California standard deduction for married filing jointly?
- I took the standard deduction federally. Should I itemize in California?
