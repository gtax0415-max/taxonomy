# Moving Expenses
## Description
Federal law suspended the moving expense deduction for everyone except active-duty military. California did NOT adopt the suspension, so civilians can still deduct qualified moving expenses for California.

## Rules
- Complete FTB 3913 (required for tax years 2021 and later), for both military and non-military filers
- Non-military filers: the result goes to Schedule CA line 14, column C (deduction) or line 1h, column C (taxable reimbursement), depending on FTB 3913, line 5
- The move must meet the distance and time tests of pre-2018 federal law

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Moving costs
- Employer reimbursements
- Old and new home and work locations

## Questions
- Did you move for a new job or job location?
- Did your employer reimburse any moving costs?
- Are you on active military duty?

## Common Errors
- Skipping the California deduction because it is not allowed federally
- Not attaching FTB 3913

## Prompt
- I moved from Seattle to Los Angeles for a new job.
