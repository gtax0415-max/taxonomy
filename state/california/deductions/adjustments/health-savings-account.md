# Health Savings Accounts (HSA)
## Description
California does NOT recognize health savings accounts. HSA money is treated as an ordinary taxable account, so nearly every federal HSA benefit is reversed on the California return.

## California treatment
- CONTRIBUTIONS: no deduction. Add back the federal HSA deduction (line 13, column B)
- EMPLOYER CONTRIBUTIONS (W-2 box 12, code W): taxable wages for California (line 1h, column C)
- EARNINGS: interest, dividends and other earnings inside the HSA are taxable each year as earned (not tax-deferred)
- GAINS on investments sold inside the HSA are taxable (Schedule CA, line 7a adjustment)
- DISTRIBUTIONS for non-medical use, taxed federally: NOT taxable for California because the contributions were already taxed (line 8f, column B); no California additional tax
- MEDICAL EXPENSES paid tax-free from an HSA can be itemized for California above 7.5% of federal AGI (Part II, line 4, column C)
- ARCHER MSA ROLLOVER TO AN HSA: treated as a distribution not used for medical expenses; taxable in California (line 8e, column C) and subject to a 12.5% additional tax on FTB 3805P

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The CARES Act HSA changes were also not adopted
- Archer MSA deductions themselves generally follow federal law (line 23)

## Required Information
- Contributions by the filer and employer
- HSA earnings and gains for the year
- Distributions and their use

## Questions
- Do you or your employer contribute to an HSA?
- Did the HSA earn interest or investment gains this year?
- Did you take HSA distributions, and for what?
- Did you roll an Archer MSA into an HSA?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the HSA deduction or employer contribution in California income
- Not reporting annual HSA earnings
- Taxing nonqualified distributions a second time in California

## Prompt
- I contribute to an HSA through my employer.
- Are HSA withdrawals taxable in California?
