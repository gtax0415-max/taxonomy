# Net Operating Loss (NOL)
## Description
An NOL arises when deductions and losses exceed income from all sources. California computes the NOL much like federal law, but the allowable amounts and carryover periods differ, and California has SUSPENDED the NOL deduction for 2024 through 2026 for higher-income filers.

## Suspension for 2024–2026
- The NOL DEDUCTION is suspended for taxable years 2024, 2025 and 2026 — so the suspension APPLIES to the 2026 return (2026 is the last scheduled year)
- Does NOT apply to individuals with net business income or modified AGI under $1 million
- NOLs can still be COMPUTED and CARRIED OVER during the suspension
- The carryover period is EXTENDED for each year the deduction is suspended
- Disaster loss carryovers are NOT affected

## History
- 2020 and 2021: suspended on the same $1 million terms
- 2022: suspension repealed
- 2013–2018: losses could be carried back 2 years; carrybacks are no longer available

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Schedule CA lines: federal NOL removed on Section B, line 8a (positive, column C); California NOL deduction from FTB 3805V on line 9b2 (column B); NOLs from former Enterprise Zone, LAMBRA or Targeted Tax Area programs (FTB 3805Z, 3807, 3809) on line 9b3
- Extended carryover for NOLs denied by the 2024–2026 suspension: losses from 2025 get one extra year, 2024 two years, and earlier losses three years
- An NOL attributable to a qualified disaster loss can be carried forward (FTB 3805V)
- California NOL amounts differ from federal, so the federal NOL is reversed and the California figure substituted on Schedule CA
- Corporations follow parallel rules with an income-subject-to-tax threshold of $1 million

## Required Information
- Each NOL by year and remaining balance
- Net business income and modified AGI for 2026
- Federal NOL included in federal AGI

## Questions
- Do you have business losses from 2026 or earlier years?
- Is your net business income or modified AGI $1 million or more?
- How much of each prior NOL has been used?

## Common Errors
- Deducting an NOL in 2026 when income is $1 million or more
- Losing track of the extended carryover period
- Using the federal NOL amount for California
- Applying the suspension to a disaster loss carryover

## Prompt
- My business lost money last year. Can I use the loss this year?
- I have an NOL carryover and my income is over $1 million.
