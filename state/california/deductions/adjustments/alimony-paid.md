# Alimony Paid
## Description
Deduction for spousal support paid under a divorce or separation instrument. Because California did NOT follow the 2019 federal change, alimony under 2019–2025 agreements is still deductible in California even though it is not federally.

## Treatment by agreement date
- Executed BEFORE January 1, 2019: deductible federally and in California. Flows through federal AGI
- Executed January 1, 2019 – December 31, 2025: NOT deductible federally, DEDUCTIBLE in California. Subtract on Schedule CA, Part I, Section C, line 19a, column B
- Executed ON or AFTER January 1, 2026, or modified after December 31, 2025 with the modification expressly adopting SB 711 (2025–2026) amendments: NOT deductible in California

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- For the 2026 return: payments under an UNMODIFIED 2019–2025 agreement (or one modified without adopting SB 711) are STILL deductible in California. Payments under a 2026 agreement are not deductible anywhere
- Report the recipient's SSN or ITIN and the agreement date (Schedule CA lines 19b and 19c)
- Child support and property settlements are not alimony

## Required Information
- Agreement execution date and any modification date
- Amount paid in 2026
- Recipient's identifying number

## Questions
- When was your agreement signed, and was it modified?
- How much spousal support did you pay in 2026?
- Do you have the recipient's SSN?

## Common Errors
- Missing the California deduction for a 2019–2025 agreement
- Deducting child support
- Leaving out the recipient's identifying number

## Prompt
- I pay spousal support under a 2020 divorce.
