# Other Adjustments to Income (Schedule CA, Lines 12, 18 and 24)
## Description
The remaining federal above-the-line adjustments. Most follow federal law; the California differences are listed.

## Usually no California adjustment
- 12 Business expenses of reservists, performing artists and fee-basis government officials (adjust only for depreciation differences on Form 2106)
- 18 Penalty on early withdrawal of savings
- 24a Jury duty pay turned over to the employer
- 24b Expenses of renting personal property for profit (adjust only if amounts differ)
- 24c Nontaxable Olympic and Paralympic medals and USOC prize money (California conforms; leave column B blank)
- 24e Repayment of supplemental unemployment benefits under the Trade Act of 1974
- 24h Attorney fees and court costs for unlawful discrimination claims
- 24k Excess deductions of IRC 67(e) expenses from a terminating trust or estate (Schedule K-1 (Form 1041))

## California differences
- 24d REFORESTATION amortization and expenses: California allows only California qualified timber property; subtract the non-California portion (column B)
- 24f Contributions to IRC 501(c)(18)(D) pension plans and 24g contributions by certain chaplains to 403(b) plans: adjust if the California amount differs (FTB Publication 1005)
- 24i Attorney fees and court costs for an IRS WHISTLEBLOWER award: NOT deductible for California (column B)
- 24j Foreign housing deduction: not allowed (see income/other-income/foreign-earned-income-exclusion.md)
- CAPITAL CONSTRUCTION FUND (Merchant Marine Act): a contribution deducted federally reduces California taxable income on Form 540, line 19 (write "CCF" and the amount)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Each federal Schedule 1 adjustment on lines 12, 18 and 24
- Location of any timber property
- Nature of any attorney fees deducted (discrimination claim or IRS whistleblower award)

## Questions
- Did you pay an early withdrawal penalty on a CD?
- Did you deduct attorney fees for an IRS whistleblower award?
- Do you own timberland outside California?

## Common Errors
- Deducting IRS whistleblower attorney fees for California
- Deducting reforestation costs for non-California timber

## Prompt
- I paid a penalty for cashing in a CD early.
