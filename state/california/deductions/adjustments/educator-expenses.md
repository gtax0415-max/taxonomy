# Educator Expenses
## Description
Federal law lets K-12 educators deduct unreimbursed classroom expenses above the line. California does NOT allow this deduction.

## Rules
- Enter the full federal amount from line 11, column A in column B to remove it

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Unreimbursed job expenses may instead qualify as a California miscellaneous itemized deduction (2% floor) on Schedule CA, Part II, line 19 (see deductions/itemized/miscellaneous-itemized-deductions.md)

## Required Information
- Federal educator expense deduction amount
- Unreimbursed job expenses and whether the filer itemizes for California

## Questions
- Did you take the federal educator expense deduction?
- Will you itemize for California?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the federal educator deduction on the California return

## Prompt
- I'm a teacher and bought $300 of classroom supplies.
