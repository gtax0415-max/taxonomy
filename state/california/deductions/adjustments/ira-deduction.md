# IRA Deduction
## Description
Deduction for contributions to a traditional IRA. California generally FOLLOWS federal deduction limits, so the deduction usually flows through federal AGI with no adjustment.

## Contribution limits for 2026
- $7,500 under age 50 (2025: $7,000)
- $8,600 age 50 or older by year-end ($7,500 plus $1,100 catch-up; 2025: $8,000)
- Never more than taxable compensation (example: $1,800 of compensation → $1,800 maximum)
- Contributions can be made up to the return due date, usually April 15, 2027, for the 2026 year

## Deduction phase-outs for 2026 (modified AGI)
NOT covered by a workplace plan:
- Single, HOH, QSS: full deduction at any income
- MFJ, spouse not covered: full deduction at any income
- MFJ, spouse covered: full up to $242,000; partial $242,000–$252,000; none at $252,000+
- MFS, spouse covered: partial under $10,000; none at $10,000+

COVERED by a workplace plan:
- Single or HOH: full up to $81,000; partial $81,000–$91,000; none at $91,000+
- MFJ or QSS: full up to $129,000; partial $129,000–$149,000; none at $149,000+
- MFS: partial under $10,000; none at $10,000+

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Adjust on Schedule CA, Part I, Section C, line 20 only if the California deduction differs (for example, different California compensation, such as for a nonresident or for RDPs recomputing on a pro forma basis)
- Disaster postponements can extend the contribution deadline

## Required Information
- Contributions and the tax year designated
- Workplace plan coverage for filer and spouse
- Modified AGI and taxable compensation
- Age at year-end

## Questions
- How much did you contribute to a traditional IRA for 2026, and when?
- Are you or your spouse covered by a retirement plan at work?
- What is your modified AGI?

## Common Errors
- Contributing more than compensation
- Missing the partial phase-out
- Counting a contribution for the wrong tax year
- Making a Schedule CA adjustment when no difference exists

- I contributed $7,500 to a traditional IRA for 2026.
