# Adjustments to Income
## Description
Above-the-line items on Schedule CA (540), Part I, Section C and line 9b that change California AGI. Key differences: no HSA or educator expense deduction, moving expenses still allowed, alimony under 2019–2025 agreements still deductible, and the California NOL deduction suspended for incomes of $1 million or more through 2026.
