# Refund and Amount Owed
## Description
The bottom of Form 540 nets payments against use tax, the health care penalty and total tax to produce a refund or a balance due.

## Order of netting
1. Payments (line 78) first cover use tax (lines 93–94)
2. Then the Individual Shared Responsibility penalty (lines 95–96)
3. Then total tax (line 97 overpaid or line 100 tax due)

## Overpayment
- Line 98: apply all or part to 2027 estimated tax. The election is BINDING; once made, it cannot later be applied to a deficiency after the return due date
- Line 99 remaining overpayment can fund voluntary contributions (line 110) and is refunded on line 115
- Refunds under $1 require a written request
- DIRECT DEPOSIT into one or two accounts (lines 116–117); each deposit at least $1; if the two amounts do not equal line 115, FTB mails a paper check
- REFUND INTERCEPT: FTB may offset refunds for debts owed to government agencies (including the IRS and California colleges); a joint refund can be applied to either spouse's debts (California has no injured spouse rule)

## Balance due
- Line 111 amount owed, line 112 interest and penalties, line 113 estimated tax penalty, line 114 total due
- Pay by electronic funds withdrawal, Web Pay, credit card (2.30% convenience fee, minimum $1) or check payable to "Franchise Tax Board" (with FTB 3582 voucher if e-filed)
- File by the extended due date even if unable to pay, to avoid the late filing penalty; installment agreements are available

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The 1099-G FTB issues for a refund excludes refundable CalEITC, YCTC and FYTC amounts
- See payments/late-filing-and-payment-penalties.md and payments/underpayment-penalty.md

## Required Information
- Total payments, use tax and penalty amounts
- Amount to apply to 2027 estimated tax
- Bank routing and account numbers
- Payment method for any balance due

## Questions
- Do you want your refund deposited, applied to next year, or partly donated?
- Can you pay the full balance by the due date?

## Common Errors
- Expecting to move an applied overpayment back after the due date
- Mismatched direct deposit amounts

## Prompt
- How do I split my California refund between two bank accounts?
- I can't pay my California tax in full.
