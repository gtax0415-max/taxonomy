# Voluntary Contribution Funds
## Description
An election on the California return to donate to listed charitable funds (for example, funds for wildlife, cancer research, veterans or school supplies). The list changes each year.

## How contributions are paid
- From payments and credits above the tax owed
- From all or part of the refund
- By check or credit card if there is no refund or it does not cover the amount chosen

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Funds listed on the 2025 Form 540 (the 2026 list is set by the 2026 Form 540, expected December 2026; funds are added and dropped each year) (codes): California Seniors Special Fund (400), Alzheimer's Disease and Related Dementia (401), Rare and Endangered Species (403), Breast Cancer Research (405), Firefighters' Memorial (406), Emergency Food for Families (407), Peace Officer Memorial (408), Cancer Research (413), School Supplies for Homeless Children (422), State Parks Protection Fund/Parks Pass Purchase (423), Protect Our Coast and Oceans (424), Prevention of Animal Homelessness and Cruelty (431), Senior Citizen Advocacy (438), Native California Wildlife Rehabilitation (439), Mental Health Crisis Prevention (445), ALS Research Network (447), Pediatric Cancer Research (448), Parkinson's Disease Research (449). The 2026 list may change
- STATE PARKS PASS: a contribution of $195 or more to code 423 gets one Vehicle Day Use Annual Pass per return; smaller amounts are treated as voluntary contributions
- Contributions are paid only after all tax liabilities are covered; if no overpayment is available, the contribution can be disallowed
- Contributions are deductible as a charitable contribution in the year PAID, generally the year the return is filed, not the tax year of the return
- FTB publishes the current funds, annual totals and check-off statistics

## Required Information
- Funds selected and amounts
- Expected overpayment or refund

## Questions
- Do you want to donate part of your refund to a California fund?
- Which funds, and how much?

## Common Errors
- Deducting the contribution on the same return it was made on
- Choosing more than the refund without arranging payment

## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my California refund to charity?
