# Underpayment of Estimated Tax Penalty
## Description
A penalty for not paying enough tax during the year through withholding and estimated payments.

## When it may apply
- Withholding and credits are less than 90% of current-year tax, OR less than 100% of prior-year tax (110% if prior-year AGI exceeded $150,000, or $75,000 MFS)
- The balance due is $500 or more ($250 MFS)
- A required installment was paid late or underpaid (penalty runs from the installment due date to the payment date or the return due date, whichever is earlier)

## Quick tests (booklet)
- Line 100 (tax due) under $500 ($250 MFS): generally no penalty
- Line 100 under 10% of line 48 (excluding tax on lump-sum distributions on line 34): generally no penalty

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- FTB can compute the penalty and bill the filer
- Attach FTB 5805 or 5805F if claiming a waiver, using the annualized income method, or paying under the farmers and fishermen schedule, even if no penalty is owed
- No penalty to the extent the underpayment was created by a law chaptered during and operative for that year

## Required Information
- Withholding and payment dates
- Current and prior-year tax and AGI

## Questions
- Did you pay at least 90% of this year's tax or 100%/110% of last year's?
- Was your income uneven during the year?
- Are you a farmer or fisherman?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal 25%-per-quarter schedule instead of California's 30/40/0/30
- Not using the annualized method when income came late in the year

## Prompt
- I owe $3,000 with my California return. Will I get a penalty?
