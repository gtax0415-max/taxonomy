# Interest and Late Filing and Payment Penalties
## Description
Interest and penalties when the 2026 return is filed or paid late.

## Due dates for the 2026 return
- Return and payment due: April 15, 2027
- Automatic paper-free extension to file: October 15, 2027 (an extension to FILE is not an extension to PAY)

## Late filing penalty
- Charged when the return is filed after the extended due date (October 15, 2027), measured from the original due date
- Maximum: 25% of the unpaid tax
- Minimum for a return more than 60 days late: the smaller of a fixed amount or 100% of the balance due (2025 return: $135; 2026 amount pending)

## Late payment penalty
- 5% of the unpaid tax plus 0.5% for each month or part of a month it remains unpaid
- May be waived for reasonable cause; reasonable cause is presumed if 90% of the tax shown was paid by the original due date

## Interest
- Mandatory; compounds daily; rate adjusted twice a year
- Runs on late penalties from the original due date, and on other penalties from 15 days after the billing notice

## Relief and other penalties
- FIRST-TIME ABATEMENT (tax years 2022 and later) of a timeliness penalty if no prior filing requirement or no prior first-time abatement, all required returns are filed, and all tax due is paid or in a payment arrangement
- Penalties may also apply for returned payments, negligence, substantial understatement and fraud
- Mandatory e-pay noncompliance: 1% penalty
- Use tax: report and pay with a timely return to avoid use tax penalties

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Original and actual filing and payment dates
- Tax paid by April 15, 2027
- Facts supporting reasonable cause
- Prior penalty abatement history

## Questions
- When will you file and pay?
- Did you pay at least 90% of the tax by April 15, 2027?
- Have you ever received a first-time abatement?

## Common Errors
- Treating the October extension as extra time to pay
- Reducing the refund or increasing tax due by penalties instead of entering them on line 112

## Prompt
- I filed my California return late. What penalties apply?
