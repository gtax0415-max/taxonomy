# Withholding on Forms 592-B and 593
## Description
California withholding reported on Form 592-B (payments to nonresidents, pass-through distributions and backup withholding) and Form 593 (sales of California real estate) is claimed on line 73, separately from W-2 and 1099 withholding.

## Rules
- Enter the total California withholding from all Forms 592-B and 593
- Attach copies of the forms to the lower front of Form 540, Side 1 (or follow e-file instructions)
- If filing status changed after escrow closed and before filing, call FTB (888.792.4900) before filing to learn how to claim the withholding

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Do not include withholding from W-2, W-2G or 1099 forms here (line 71)
- Do not include a nonconsenting nonresident member's tax from Schedule K-1 (568), line 15e

## Required Information
- All 592-B and 593 forms received

## Questions
- Did you sell California real estate with tax withheld in escrow?
- Did a partnership or payer withhold California tax on payments to you?

## Common Errors
- Reporting real estate withholding on line 71
- Not attaching the forms

## Prompt
- Escrow withheld 3 1/3% when I sold my rental property.
