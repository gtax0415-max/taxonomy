# Deceased Person (Decedent)
## Description
A final California return must be filed for someone who died during the year if a return would normally be required or if prior-year returns were never filed. Income after the date of death belongs to the estate.

## Who must file
The administrator, executor or beneficiary must:
- File the final return and any past-due returns
- Pay any tax due
- Sign the return, writing "Deceased" and the date of death next to the decedent's name at the top

## Due date
- The final return is due on the date it would have been due had the person lived

## Income and deductions on the final return
- CASH method: report income received before death; deduct only expenses paid before death
- ACCRUAL method: report income accrued or earned before death; deduct expenses the decedent was liable for before death, paid or not

## Surviving spouse/RDP joint return
Allowed if:
- The decedent did not file a return
- The survivor did not remarry in the same year
- No administrator or executor was appointed by the return's due date
Sign with "Surviving spouse/RDP" next to the name.

## Claiming a refund
Must be a surviving spouse/RDP, a surviving relative, the sole beneficiary, or the estate's legal representative.
- Individuals: attach the death certificate and federal Form 1310
- Legal representatives: attach CERTIFIED copies of the death certificate and federal Form 1310

## Probate
- Opened in the superior court of the county where the decedent lived or owned property
- Required when estate assets exceed $166,250; for April 1, 2025 through March 31, 2028 the threshold is $750,000
- Send FTB the Letters of Administration within 90 days after the court first issues them (Probate Code 9202): PO Box 2952, MS 454, Sacramento CA 95812-9974, or fax the FTB Decedent Team at 916-845-0479

## Decedent's estate
- Includes real, tangible personal and intangible property
- The date of death ends the decedent's tax year and starts the estate's tax year
- Resident decedent: report all estate income; nonresident: only California-source income
- Filed on Form 541 (the estate's fiduciary return, outside this individual taxonomy)

## Contact
- FTB decedent line: 916-845-3048, weekdays 8 AM–5 PM

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- Date of death and residency at death
- Income and expenses through date of death
- Accounting method
- Executor or administrator appointment
- Surviving spouse status

## Questions
- When did the person pass away, and were they a California resident?
- Did they file returns for prior years?
- Is there a court-appointed executor or administrator?
- Is there a surviving spouse who wants to file jointly?
- Is a refund expected?

## Common Errors
- Reporting income received after death on the final return
- Not attaching the death certificate and Form 1310 for a refund
- Missing the 90-day deadline to send Letters of Administration
- Filing jointly after an executor has been appointed
