# Registered Domestic Partners (RDP)
## Description
Registered domestic partners file federally as single or head of household, but for California they MUST use the same filing statuses as married couples. The California return is built by combining the partners' separate federal returns.

## Who is an RDP
- Registered with the California Secretary of State (Declaration of Domestic Partnership)
- Generally over age 18
- Same-sex or opposite-sex couples

## Filing rules (tax years 2007 and later)
- Use a married status: married/RDP filing jointly, married/RDP filing separately, head of household (if qualified), or qualifying surviving spouse/RDP
- COMBINE income and deductions from each partner's separate federal return
- CHECK the box in the Filing Status section stating the California status differs from federal
- Before 2007, RDPs could not file jointly in California

## Deceased RDP
- Year of death: the surviving RDP may file married/RDP jointly
- Next two years: may qualify as qualifying surviving spouse/RDP

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- AGI-based limits are recomputed using a federal pro forma joint return
- Update the California withholding certificate with the employer (EDD Form DE 4) to match the RDP filing status
- When a partnership is dissolved, FTB is generally NOT bound by a court order assigning tax debt to one partner; innocent joint filer relief may apply
- FTB Publication 737 has full RDP guidance

## Required Information
- Date of registration
- Both partners' federal returns
- Residency of each partner

## Questions
- Are you registered as domestic partners with the California Secretary of State?
- When did you register?
- Did each of you file a separate federal return?
- Do you want to file jointly or separately in California?

## Common Errors
- Filing single in California because the federal return was single
- Not checking the different-filing-status box
- Not combining both partners' federal figures

## Prompt
- My partner and I are registered domestic partners. How do we file in California?
