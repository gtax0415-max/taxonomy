# Single
## Description
Filing status for a person who, on December 31, was not married or in a registered domestic partnership and does not qualify for head of household or qualifying surviving spouse.

## Who qualifies
On the last day of the year, the filer was:
- Not married and not an RDP, OR
- Divorced or legally separated under a final decree, or had terminated the RDP, OR
- Widowed BEFORE January 1 of the tax year and did not remarry or enter another RDP

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- A spouse who died DURING 2026 does not make the filer single for 2026; the survivor can file jointly
- A filer with a qualifying person may be better off as head of household or qualifying surviving spouse
- Standard deduction for 2026: $5,900; personal exemption credit $158

## Required Information
- Marital or RDP status on December 31, 2026
- Date of any divorce, separation, termination or spouse's death

## Questions
- Were you married or in an RDP on December 31, 2026?
- Is your divorce or separation final?
- Do you have a child or relative living with you?

## Common Errors
- Filing single when a spouse died during the year (joint is allowed)
- Filing single with a pending, non-final divorce
- Missing head of household eligibility

## Prompt
- I'm divorced and have no children. What's my filing status?
