# Head of Household (HOH)
## Description
Lower tax rates and a larger standard deduction than single. FTB actively audits HOH claims, and the Head of Household Filing Status Schedule (FTB 3532) MUST be attached or the status can be denied.

## Who qualifies
All on December 31:
- Unmarried, considered unmarried, or not in an RDP
- Has a qualifying child or qualifying relative
- The qualifying person lived with the filer more than 183 days
- Paid more than half the cost of keeping up the home
- U.S. citizen or legal resident for the whole year

## How to claim
- E-file: follow the software instructions
- Paper: complete and attach FTB 3532

## Most common reasons for denial
- Qualifying relative's gross income is above the limit
- Child is 19–23 and NOT a full-time student
- Child lived with the filer less than 183 days
- Two people claimed the same qualifying person or used the same address
- A married filer claimed a qualifying relative
- FTB 3532 not attached

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Standard deduction for 2026: $11,800 (same as joint); personal exemption credit $158
- FTB Publication 1540 explains the rules in depth
- A married filer who lived apart from the spouse for the last six months with a child may be "considered unmarried"

## Required Information
- Qualifying person's name, relationship, age, student status and income
- Days lived in the home
- Household costs and who paid them
- Marital status

## Questions
- Who lives with you, and how are they related to you?
- How many nights did they live with you in 2026?
- Did you pay more than half the household costs?
- Is the child 19 or older, and a full-time student?
- Are you married?

## Common Errors
- Not attaching FTB 3532
- Claiming a child who lived with the filer less than 183 days
- Claiming a relative whose income is too high
- Two parents claiming the same child

## Prompt
- I'm a single mom with two kids. Can I file head of household?
- My elderly father lives with me and I pay the bills.
