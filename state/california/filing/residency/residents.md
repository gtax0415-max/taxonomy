# California Residents
## Description
A California resident is taxed on ALL income, from every source worldwide. Residency, not where income is earned, drives the California return.

## Who is a resident
Either:
- Present in California for other than a temporary or transitory purpose, OR
- Domiciled in California but outside the state for a temporary or transitory purpose

FTB Publication 1031 lists more factors.

## Form 540 2EZ eligibility
May use Form 540 2EZ if ALL apply:
- Filing status single, MFJ, HOH or qualifying surviving spouse/RDP (not MFS)
- 0–3 dependents
- Taxable income $100,000 or less (single, HOH) or $200,000 or less (MFJ, QSS)
- Income only from wages, salaries and tips; taxable interest, dividends and pensions; mutual fund capital gain distributions (1099-DIV box 2a); taxable scholarships on a W-2; unemployment on a W-2; Paid Family Leave; Social Security; Tier 1 and Tier 2 railroad retirement
- No adjustments to income; standard deduction only
- Payments only California withholding on W-2 and 1099-R
- Exemptions: personal, senior and up to three dependents
- Credits: only nonrefundable renter's credit, CalEITC, YCTC and FYTC

Cannot use 540 2EZ if the filer (or spouse) can be claimed as a dependent AND:
- Has a dependent of their own, OR
- Total income is at or below the 2026 dependent limits (pending FTB release (expected late December 2026); 2025 was $18,956 single, $37,862 MFJ or QSS, $26,762 HOH), OR
- Must use the modified standard deduction for dependents

Otherwise, use Form 540.

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Filing thresholds (2026 pending FTB release) are in filing/filing-requirement.md
- California is a community property state: if one spouse is a resident and the other is not, the resident may have to report income earned outside California
- Tax paid to another state on the same income may qualify for the Other State Tax Credit

## Required Information
- Where the filer lived and was domiciled during 2026
- All income, wherever earned
- Spouse's residency

## Questions
- Did you live in California all of 2026?
- Were you away only temporarily?
- Do you have income from other states or countries?
- Is your spouse also a California resident?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out income earned outside California
- Using Form 540 2EZ with itemized deductions or adjustments
- Treating a temporary absence as giving up residency

## Prompt
- I live in California but work remotely for a company in New York.
- Can I use the short form 540 2EZ?
