# Tax Computation (Rates, Kiddie Tax, Parents' Election)
## Description
California taxes ALL income, including capital gains and qualified dividends, at the same graduated rates (1% to 12.3%). The 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax applies separately above $1,000,000 of taxable income.

## 2026 tax rate schedules (official, FTB Tax News October 2026)

- $0–$11,456: 1% | to $27,157: 2% | to $42,861: 4% | to $59,498: 6% | to $75,197: 8% | to $384,109: 9.3% | to $460,927: 10.3% | to $768,213: 11.3% | over $768,213: 12.3%

## Which method
- Tax Table: taxable income $100,000 or less
- Tax Rate Schedules: taxable income over $100,000
- The full 2026 tax table is due from FTB in late December 2026

## Children with unearned income (FTB 3800)
- Applies to a child 18 or under, or a full-time student under 24, at year-end with unearned income above $2,700 for 2026 (federal Rev. Proc. 2025-32 amount, unchanged from 2025, which FTB 3800 follows; confirm on the 2026 FTB 3800)
- Part of the child's income is taxed at the parents' rate on the child's own Form 540

## Parents' election (FTB 3803)
- Parents may report a child's interest and dividends on their own return (2026: interest and dividends of more than $1,350 but less than $13,500, the federal Form 8814 range that FTB 3803 follows; confirm on the 2026 FTB 3803), even if they do not make the election federally
- File a separate FTB 3803 for each child and add its tax to line 31

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Exemption credits (line 32) and special credits (lines 40–46) reduce the tax computed here

## Required Information
- Taxable income and filing status
- Children's unearned income

## Questions

## Common Errors
- Applying a lower rate to capital gains
- Using the tax table for taxable income over $100,000
