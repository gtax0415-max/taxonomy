# Other Taxes and Penalties
## Description
Taxes added on top of regular income tax (Form 540, lines 34 and 61–63, 91 and 92): alternative minimum tax, the 1% Behavioral Health Services Tax, lump-sum and trust accumulation distribution taxes, additional taxes on retirement and tax-favored accounts, other taxes and credit recapture, use tax, and the individual health care mandate penalty.
