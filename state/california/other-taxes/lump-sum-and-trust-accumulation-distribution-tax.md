# Tax on Lump-Sum Distributions and Trust Accumulation Distributions
## Description
Two special taxes computed separately and added on Form 540, line 34.

## Schedule G-1: lump-sum distributions
- For a QUALIFIED LUMP-SUM DISTRIBUTION from a retirement plan received by a filer born before January 2, 1936
- Special averaging methods may produce less tax than ordinary rates

## FTB 5870A: accumulation distributions
- For accumulation distributions from FOREIGN TRUSTS or certain DOMESTIC TRUSTS, including trusts that did not pay California tax because a beneficiary's interest was contingent
- Figures the additional tax on the distribution

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- Tax on lump-sum distributions on line 34 is excluded when testing for the estimated tax penalty (10% of line 48)

## Required Information
- Filer's date of birth
- Lump-sum distribution amount and plan
- Trust accumulation distribution amount and the trust's tax history

## Questions
- Were you born before January 2, 1936 and did you take a lump-sum plan distribution?
- Did you receive an accumulation distribution from a foreign or domestic trust?

## Common Errors
- Using Schedule G-1 when the birth-date test is not met

## Prompt
- I received an accumulation distribution from a family trust.
