# Behavioral Health Services Tax
## Description
An additional 1% tax on taxable income over $1,000,000 (formerly called the Mental Health Services Tax). Combined with the 12.3% top rate, California's top marginal rate is 13.3%.

## Amount
- 1% of taxable income (Form 540, line 19) above $1,000,000
- The $1,000,000 threshold is not indexed and is the same for every filing status

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- No credits reduce this tax
- The 2026 rate schedules are in filing/tax-computation.md

## Required Information
- Taxable income

## Questions
- Is your California taxable income over $1,000,000?

## Common Errors
- Doubling the threshold for joint filers
- Using AGI instead of taxable income

## Prompt
- I sold my company and have $3 million of taxable income.
