# California Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT)
## Description
California has its own AMT, figured on Schedule P (540). It applies when tax preferences and adjustments push tentative minimum tax above regular tax. Schedule P also limits most credits so they cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax.

## Who may owe it
- Filers with certain deductions, exclusions and credits whose total income exceeds (2025 figures; 2026 pending): $123,667 MFJ or QSS; $92,749 single or HOH; $61,830 MFS
- A child under 19 or a full-time student under 24 if taxable income plus preference items exceeds $9,750 (2025) plus earned income
- EXCLUSION: income, adjustments and preferences from the trades or businesses of a taxpayer with less than $1,000,000 of gross receipts from all trades or businesses are not included

## Common triggers (Schedule P, Boxes A and B)
- Accelerated depreciation in excess of straight-line, intangible drilling costs, depletion, circulation and research expenditures, mining costs, pollution control amortization
- Tax shelter farm and passive activity income or loss; long-term contracts (percentage of completion)
- Investment interest expense; INCENTIVE STOCK OPTION income above the regular tax amount; installment sales of certain property
- Pass-through AMT adjustments from an estate or trust

## Credit limitation (Box C)
- Complete Schedule P if California AGI exceeds (2025): $347,808 single or HOH; $463,745 MFJ or QSS; $231,868 MFS
- Most credits cannot reduce tax below tentative minimum tax; some credits are allowed to (for example, the College Access Tax Credit)

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- AMT EXEMPTION (2025; 2026 pending): $92,749 single or HOH, $123,667 MFJ or QSS, $61,830 MFS; reduced by 25% of AMTI above $347,808 / $463,745 / $231,868, and zero at $718,804 / $958,413 / $479,188
- California Qualified Stock Option compensation excluded for regular tax must be INCLUDED for AMT (Schedule P, line 10), along with incentive stock option spreads
- Since 2024 California disallows PERCENTAGE DEPLETION for coal, oil shale, and oil and gas wells, and the IRC 263(c) deduction for oil and gas intangible drilling costs; geothermal well IDCs remain a preference item
- Installment payments on pre-2013 qualified small business stock sales: 50% of the California excluded gain is an AMT preference (line 13c)
- The itemized deduction limitation does not apply for AMT; the AMT NOL deduction is limited to 90% of AMTI and is also suspended for 2024–2026 at the $1,000,000 threshold
- Tax-exempt interest from private activity bonds is NOT a California AMT preference (Schedule P has no such line): California-issued bond interest stays exempt, and non-California bond interest is already taxed for regular tax
- AMT paid on timing items can generate a later Prior Year Alternative Minimum Tax credit (code 188)

## Required Information
- Preference and adjustment items
- Gross receipts of trades or businesses
- Credits claimed

## Questions
- Did you exercise incentive stock options?
- Do you have large depreciation, depletion or passive activity items?
- Are your business gross receipts under $1,000,000?
- Are you claiming several credits?

## Common Errors
- Missing California AMT on incentive stock options
- Including business preferences when gross receipts are under $1,000,000
- Letting credits reduce tax below tentative minimum tax

## Prompt
- I exercised ISOs this year. Do I owe California AMT?
