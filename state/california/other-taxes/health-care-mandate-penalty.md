# Health Care Mandate (Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty)
## Description
California requires residents, their spouses/RDPs and dependents to have qualifying health coverage (minimum essential coverage) every month, or to claim an exemption. Without either, a penalty is added to the California return. The federal penalty has been $0 since 2019, so this is a California-only charge.

## Penalty amounts for 2026
The 2026 flat amounts, household cap, bronze premium caps and affordability percentage are pending FTB release (expected late December 2026) in the 2026 FTB 3853 instructions. The structure is unchanged; 2025 figures are shown for reference.

The penalty is the LARGER of:
- FLAT AMOUNT (indexed yearly): 2025 was $950 per adult (18 or older) and $475 per child under 18 for a full year without coverage, household flat amount capped at $2,850. Partial years are prorated by uncovered month
- PERCENTAGE: 2.5% of household income above the filing threshold

…but no more than the statewide average bronze plan premium for the household size (2025: $4,524 for 1 person, $9,048 for 2, $13,572 for 3, $18,096 for 4, $22,620 for 5 or more).

## Exemptions (FTB 3853, Part III)
- Income below the filing threshold
- A: Coverage considered unaffordable (required contribution above the annual affordability percentage; 2025 was 7.28%)
- B: Aggregate self-only coverage unaffordable
- C: Short coverage gap of 3 continuous months or less
- D: Citizens living abroad and certain noncitizens
- E: Nonresident or part-year resident (for months outside California)
- F: Member of a health care sharing ministry
- G: Member of an Indian tribe or eligible for Indian health care services
- H: Incarceration after disposition of charges
- I: Household member born or adopted during the year
- J: Household member died during the year
- K: General hardship, L: Religious conscience, M: Unaffordable based on projected income (K, L and M must be granted by Covered California and need an ECN; enter "pending" if the application is still pending)
- N: Certain limited or restricted-scope Medi-Cal coverage

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- If everyone had coverage all year, check the full-year coverage box and skip FTB 3853
- Covered California enrollees also receive a California marketplace statement (FTB 3895), which is used to reconcile California premium assistance
- The 2021 California Premium Assistance Subsidy had its own reconciliation on the 2021 return

## Required Information
- Each household member's coverage by month
- Household income and filing threshold
- Any exemption and ECN

## Questions
- Did you and everyone in your household have health coverage every month of 2026?
- Were there any gaps? How long were they?
- Did Covered California grant you an exemption?
- Did anyone join or leave the household during the year?

## Common Errors
- Not checking the full-year coverage box when everyone was covered
- Missing the short-gap exemption (3 months or less)
- Claiming a K, L or M exemption without an ECN
- Using the federal rule ($0 penalty) on the California return

## Prompt
- I didn't have health insurance for part of 2026.
- Do I owe a California penalty for not having health coverage?
