# Other Taxes and Credit Recapture (Line 63)
## Description
Line 63 collects several additional taxes and recaptured credits.

## Items reported on line 63
- ADDITIONAL TAX ON QUALIFIED PLANS AND TAX-FAVORED ACCOUNTS (FTB 3805P): see other-taxes/retirement-account-penalties.md
- NONQUALIFIED DEFERRED COMPENSATION (IRC 409A): California conforms; a plan that fails 409A owes an additional 5% of the amount included in income, plus interest. Write "NQDC"
- INTEREST ON DEFERRED TAX from installment obligations (IRC 453A). Write "IRC Section 453A interest"
- CREDIT RECAPTURE:
  - FTB 3531 California Competes Tax Credit (only the recaptured amount used)
  - FTB 3540 Credit Carryover and Recapture Summary
  - FTB 3554 New Employment Credit
  - FTB 3835 State Historic Rehabilitation Tax Credit

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)

## Required Information
- 409A failure amounts
- Large installment obligations outstanding
- Credits subject to recapture

## Questions
- Did a deferred compensation plan fail IRC 409A?
- Do you have installment sale notes over the federal threshold?
- Did you previously claim a credit whose requirements are no longer met?

## Common Errors
- Not writing the item name next to the amount
- Missing California recapture after a federal recapture event

## Prompt
- My deferred compensation plan failed 409A.
