# Use Tax
## Description
California use tax applies to items bought from out-of-state or online sellers that did not collect California sales tax, when the items are used in California. Individuals can report it on Form 540, line 91. The line may NOT be left blank.

## How to compute
- USE TAX WORKSHEET (required for any single non-business item of $1,000 or more, and for business purchases by unregistered filers): purchases × the local sales and use tax rate, minus sales tax paid to another state (credit limited to the California tax)
- ESTIMATED USE TAX LOOKUP TABLE: optional for non-business items under $1,000 each; based on California AGI (the 2026 table is pending FTB release in the 2026 Form 540 booklet, expected December 2026; 2025 table: $1 under $20,000, rising to $19 at $175,000–$199,999, then AGI × 0.010%). Using the table means no additional use tax is assessed on those items
- Items brought back from abroad: tax applies to the value above the $800 per-person U.S. Customs exemption (hand-carried items only)
- MFS filers may report half of the use tax and the spouse the other half

## Cannot be reported on Form 540
- Vehicles, vessels and trailers registered with the DMV; mobile homes and commercial coaches; documented vessels; aircraft
- Business purchases of filers who hold (or must hold) a seller's permit, buy more than $10,000 a year subject to use tax, or are otherwise registered with CDTFA

## Notes
- Line numbers follow the 2025 Form 540 and schedules; confirm them on the 2026 forms (expected December 2026)
- The use tax is owed to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA); FTB collects it on the income tax return for convenience
- Do not amend the income tax return to correct a use tax error; contact CDTFA
- Report and pay with a timely return to avoid use tax penalties

## Required Information
- Untaxed out-of-state and online purchases
- Local tax rate
- Tax paid to other states

## Questions
- Did you buy items online or from out-of-state sellers that didn't charge California sales tax?
- Did any single item cost $1,000 or more?
- Did you bring purchases back from a foreign trip?

## Common Errors
- Leaving line 91 blank
- Using the lookup table for an item of $1,000 or more
- Reporting a vehicle purchase on the income tax return

## Prompt
- I bought a $2,000 TV from an out-of-state website with no sales tax.
