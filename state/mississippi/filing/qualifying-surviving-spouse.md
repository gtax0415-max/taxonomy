# Federal Qualifying Surviving Spouse
## Description
Mississippi has NO qualifying surviving spouse status. A federal qualifying surviving spouse (spouse died in 2024 or 2025) files as head of family if a dependent lived in the home all year, otherwise as single.
## Mapping for 2026
- Spouse died in 2026 → married–spouse died (line 2), not this file
- Spouse died in 2024 or 2025, dependent in home all year → head of family (line 4): $9,500 exemption, $3,400 standard deduction
- Otherwise → single (line 5): $6,000 exemption, $2,300 standard deduction
## Example (2026)
- Federal QSS with one child at home: Mississippi head of family. Exemption $9,500 vs. $12,000 federal-joint equivalent; there is no way to keep the married amounts
## Required Information
- Year of the spouse's death
- Whether a dependent lived in the home all year
## Questions
- When did your spouse die?
- Did a dependent child live with you all of 2026?
## Common Errors
- Using the married amounts ($12,000 / $4,600)
- Using married–spouse died for a death before 2026
