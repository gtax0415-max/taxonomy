# Head of Family
## Description
For a single person who maintains a household that is the main home of the taxpayer AND one or more dependents for the whole year.
## Amounts for 2026 (same as 2025)
- $8,000 filing status exemption plus $1,500 for the required dependent = $9,500 (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-21)
- $1,500 for each additional dependent
- Standard deduction $3,400
## Stricter than federal head of household
- The dependent must LIVE IN THE HOME with the taxpayer for the ENTIRE year. Paying for a dependent's separate household does not qualify
- A married person must live apart from the spouse for the ENTIRE year (federal: the last six months)
- The person must be a dependent of the taxpayer
## Required Information
- Each dependent living in the home and for how long
- Marital status and separation dates
## Questions
- Did a dependent live with you all of 2026?
- If married, did you live apart from your spouse all year?
## Common Errors
- Copying federal head of household when the dependent did not live in the home all year
- A married person who separated mid-year using head of family
- Forgetting the $1,500 for the required dependent
## Prompt
- I'm a single mom. What status do I use in Mississippi?
- My parent lives in a nursing home I pay for. Can I be head of family?
