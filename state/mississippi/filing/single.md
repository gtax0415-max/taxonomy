# Single
## Description
For a person who is not married, or is married but legally separated, on the last day of the year.
## Amounts for 2026 (same as 2025)
- Exemption $6,000 (plus $1,500 per dependent, age 65, or blind)
- Standard deduction $2,300
## Also lands here
- Federal head of household filers whose dependent did not live with them all year
- Federal qualifying surviving spouse filers without a dependent living in the home all year
## Example (2026)
- Mississippi AGI $45,000, standard deduction: $45,000 − $2,300 − $6,000 = $36,700 → ($36,700 − $10,000) × 4% = $1,068
## Required Information
- Marital status on December 31, 2026
## Questions
- Were you unmarried or legally separated on December 31, 2026?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal single standard deduction
- Using single when head of family is available

- I'm single with no kids. What are my Mississippi deductions?
