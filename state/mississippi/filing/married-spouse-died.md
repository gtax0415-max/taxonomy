# Married - Spouse Died in Tax Year
## Description
Used when the spouse died in 2026 and the survivor did not remarry in 2026. The surviving spouse is listed FIRST as primary taxpayer.
## Amounts for 2026
- Exemption $12,000 and standard deduction $4,600 (full married amounts)
- Income: the decedent's income up to the date of death plus the survivor's income for all of 2026
- Columns may be used as on a combined return; the negative-column rule applies
## After 2026
- Mississippi has no surviving-spouse status. In 2027 the survivor files head of family (with a dependent in the home all year) or single, even if federal qualifying surviving spouse applies
## Required Information
- Date of death
- The decedent's income through the date of death
## Questions
- Did your spouse die in 2026?
- Did you remarry before the end of 2026?
## Common Errors
- Listing the decedent as primary taxpayer
- Using this status for the year after death
- Leaving out the decedent's income before death
