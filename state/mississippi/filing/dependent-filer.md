# Filer Who Can Be Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
A person (often a student or minor) who is claimed as a dependent on someone else's return but files his or her own Mississippi return.
## Mississippi rule (differs from federal)
- Federal law limits a dependent's standard deduction and allows no personal exemption. Mississippi's instructions give the dependent filer the FULL exemption and standard deduction for the filing status
- Filing requirement for a minor resident: file if gross income exceeds the personal exemption plus the standard deduction ($6,000 + $2,300 = $8,300 for a single filer in 2026)
- Also file if Mississippi tax was withheld, to get a refund
## Notes
- The dependent cannot claim a $1,500 dependent exemption for anyone else and cannot take one for himself or herself
- The parent may still claim the $1,500 dependent exemption for the child
- Confirm this treatment against the 2026 instructions; it is based on the filing-requirement language in the 2025 booklet
## Required Information
- The filer's income and withholding
- Whether a parent claims the filer as a dependent
## Questions
- Can someone else claim you as a dependent?
- Did you have Mississippi tax withheld?
- Was your gross income more than $8,300?
## Common Errors
- Applying the federal limited standard deduction to Mississippi
- Not filing to recover Mississippi withholding
- Claiming a dependent exemption for himself or herself
## Prompt
- I'm a college student and my parents claim me. Do I file in Mississippi?
