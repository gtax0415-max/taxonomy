# Full-year Resident
## Description
A full-year resident files Form 80-105 and reports income from ALL sources, wherever earned.
## Who is a resident
- Keeps a home, apartment, or other place of abode in Mississippi; OR
- Exercises citizenship rights in Mississippi (registered to vote); OR
- Receives a Mississippi homestead exemption
- A resident stays a resident while temporarily away, until a new legal residence is established. Changes in driver's license, vehicle tags, voter registration, and property taxes show intent to change residence
## Special cases
- Military: a Mississippi resident who enters the service keeps Mississippi residency while stationed elsewhere
- Military spouses: may elect the servicemember's state of residence (Military Spouses Residency Relief Act)
- Moved into or out of Mississippi during 2026: part-year resident, Form 80-205
- One spouse resident, the other not: file Form 80-205
## Scope
- Full-year residents are in scope; Form 80-205 cases are out of scope (team decision 2026-09-30)
## Required Information
- Address history for 2026
- Voter registration and homestead status
## Questions
- Did you live in Mississippi all of 2026?
- Do you claim a Mississippi homestead exemption or vote in Mississippi?
- Is your spouse also a full-year resident?
## Common Errors
- Treating a temporary absence as giving up residency
- A resident leaving out income earned in another state
- Filing Form 80-105 when one spouse is a nonresident
## Prompt
- Am I a Mississippi resident?
- I work in Tennessee but live in Mississippi.
