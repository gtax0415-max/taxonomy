# Non-Resident and Part-Year Resident Return
## Description
Nonresidents with Mississippi-source income and people who moved into or out of Mississippi file Form 80-205. Mississippi taxes only Mississippi-source income (or income earned while a resident), but total income must be reported to prorate deductions and exemptions.
## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year residents with Mississippi income only). Kept for routing so the system can recognize and refer these cases
## Key rules
- Deductions and exemptions are multiplied by the ratio of Mississippi income to total income, capped at 100%
- Part-year residents may itemize only expenses incurred while a resident
- A couple where one spouse is a resident and the other is not files Form 80-205
- A nonresident whose only Mississippi income is casino winnings does not file
## Required Information
- Dates of Mississippi residence in 2026
- Mississippi-source income and total income from all sources
- Spouse's state of residence
## Questions
- Did you move into or out of Mississippi in 2026?
- Is your spouse a resident of another state?
## Common Errors
- Filing Form 80-105 for a part-year year
- Not prorating the exemption and deduction
## Prompt
- I moved to Mississippi in June.
- I live in Alabama but work in Mississippi.
