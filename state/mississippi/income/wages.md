# Wages, Salaries, Tips
## Description
All wages, salaries, fees, commissions, tips, and bonuses before deductions. A full-year resident reports wages from EVERY state, not only Mississippi.
## Rules
- Use the STATE wages in W-2 box 16, not federal box 1 (they can differ, for example for certain pre-tax benefits)
- Each W-2 is listed on Form 80-107 and attached; Form 80-107 is required even with no Mississippi withholding
- Taxable fringe items include tips not reported to the employer, meals and lodging the employer provides as a matter of the employee's choice (not for the employer's convenience), and union strike benefits
- On a combined return, each spouse's wages go in that spouse's column
## 2026 point
- The federal deductions for qualified tips and overtime premium (2025–2028) do NOT apply in Mississippi. These amounts stay in Mississippi wages (see other-income-items.md)
- Military pay: National Guard and Reserve pay up to $15,000 is subtracted as an adjustment (line 55), not removed here
## Required Information
- All Forms W-2
- Any wages or tips without a W-2
## Questions
- Did you work in another state during 2026?
- Did you receive tips you did not report to your employer?
- Did you claim the federal tips or overtime deduction?
## Common Errors
- Using federal box 1 instead of state box 16
- Leaving out wages earned in another state
- Subtracting federally deducted tips or overtime
- Not attaching W-2s, which can disallow withholding credit
## Prompt
- Where do I report my W-2 wages in Mississippi?
- Are my tips taxable in Mississippi?
