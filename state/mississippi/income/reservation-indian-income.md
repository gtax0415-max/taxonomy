# Reservation Indian Income Exclusion
## Description
Income earned on the reservation by an enrolled tribal member who also LIVES on the reservation is not subject to Mississippi income tax. The taxpayer attaches the Mississippi affidavit (Form 80-340).
## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year residents with Mississippi income only). Kept for routing so the system can recognize the situation and refer it
## Notes
- Both conditions must be met: living on the reservation AND earning the income there
- Income earned off the reservation remains taxable
## Required Information
- Tribal enrollment, residence, and where the income was earned
## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member who lives and works on the reservation?
## Common Errors
- Excluding income earned off the reservation
- Excluding income of a member who lives off the reservation
## Prompt
- I'm a Choctaw tribal member working on the reservation.
