# Foreign Earned Income Exclusion
## Description
A Mississippi resident working abroad on a temporary basis reports total worldwide income, but may subtract foreign wages that are excluded federally on Form 2555. Form 2555 must be attached.
## Amounts
- 2026 federal exclusion limit: $132,900 per qualifying person (up from $130,000 for 2025), per Rev. Proc. 2025-32
- The Mississippi subtraction equals the federal exclusion actually claimed
## Notes
- Only for someone who stays a Mississippi resident while abroad; a person who has given up Mississippi residence files differently
- The federal foreign housing exclusion follows the same treatment if claimed on Form 2555
## Required Information
- Federal Form 2555
- Foreign wages for 2026
## Questions
- Did you work outside the United States in 2026?
- Did you claim the exclusion on your federal return?
- Do you still keep your Mississippi residence?
## Common Errors
- Leaving foreign wages in Mississippi income when excluded federally
- Using the 2025 limit
- Not attaching Form 2555
## Prompt
- I worked overseas this year. Do I pay Mississippi tax on it?
