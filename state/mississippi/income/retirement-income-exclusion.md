# Retirement Income Exclusion
## Description
Mississippi fully exempts qualified retirement income, at any amount and regardless of age once retirement requirements are met (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-15(4)(k), (l)).
## Exempt
- Pensions and annuities from federal, state, local, military, and private retirement plans
- 401(k), 403(b), 457, and similar plan distributions after meeting the plan's retirement age or service requirements
- IRA distributions (traditional and Roth) that are not early distributions
- Roth conversions (§27-7-15(4)(ff))
- Distributions to a beneficiary after the account owner's death (Miss. Admin. Code 35.III.02.07.105)
## Not exempt
- Early or excess distributions subject to federal additional tax (Form 5329)
- Deferred compensation received before retirement age or service requirements
- Separation pay
## Notes
- No dollar cap and no age floor for qualifying income
- Mississippi tax withheld on a Form 1099-R is still claimed through Form 80-107
## Required Information
- Each Form 1099-R and its box 7 code
- Whether the person met the plan's retirement age or service requirement
## Questions
- What kind of retirement income did you receive?
- Were you under 59½ or did any distribution have a federal penalty?
- Is any of it a payout from an inherited account?
## Common Errors
- Taxing a qualifying pension or IRA distribution
- Excluding an early distribution that had a federal penalty
- Missing the withholding credit on an excluded 1099-R
## Prompt
- Is my pension taxable in Mississippi?
- I converted my IRA to a Roth this year.
