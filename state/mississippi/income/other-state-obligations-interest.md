# Interest on Other States' Obligations
## Description
Interest from bonds of states other than Mississippi and their subdivisions is TAXABLE in Mississippi, even though it is tax-exempt federally (Miss. Admin. Code 35.III.02.04.104).
## Rules
- Include it in total interest on Schedule B, line 1
- Mutual funds holding municipal bonds report the share by state; only the Mississippi share is exempt
- Federal Form 1040 line 2a (tax-exempt interest) is the starting point to find these amounts
## Example (2026)
- National municipal bond fund pays $1,000 of federally exempt dividends; fund statement shows 3% from Mississippi issuers → $970 taxable in Mississippi
## Required Information
- Exempt-interest amounts and issuer state percentages
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds or a municipal bond fund?
- What share came from Mississippi issuers?
## Common Errors
- Leaving out other states' municipal interest because it is federally exempt
- Excluding a whole national fund instead of only the Mississippi share
## Prompt
- I have a tax-free municipal bond fund. Is it taxable in Mississippi?
