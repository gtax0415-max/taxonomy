# Rents, Royalties, Partnerships, S Corporations and Trusts
## Description
Income or loss from rental real estate, royalties, partnerships, S corporations, estates, and trusts, starting from federal Schedule E. Federal Schedule E must be attached.
## Mississippi points
- Pass-through income comes from the MISSISSIPPI K-1, which can differ from the federal K-1 (state depreciation, exempt interest, Mississippi credits)
- Depletion in excess of basis is added back
- Taxes paid by an electing pass-through entity on the owner's behalf are claimed as a payment on line 26 (Form 80-161), not netted here
- Any difference from the federal amount: enter federal on line 41 and adjust on Schedule N, or attach a reconciliation
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule E
- All Mississippi K-1s
- Any electing pass-through entity tax paid for the owner
## Questions
- Do you own rental property or receive royalties?
- Do you own part of a partnership, S corporation, or trust? Did you get a Mississippi K-1?
- Did the entity elect to pay Mississippi tax at the entity level?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal K-1 when a Mississippi K-1 differs
- Leaving out depletion in excess of basis
- Not claiming entity-level tax paid on line 26
## Prompt
- I have a rental house. How do I report it in Mississippi?
- I received a K-1 from a Mississippi partnership.
