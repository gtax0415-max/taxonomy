# Unemployment Compensation
## Description
Unemployment benefits are fully taxable in Mississippi, the same as federally.
## Rules
- Report the amount received in 2026 from Form 1099-G box 1
- List it on Form 80-107, including any Mississippi tax withheld (box 11)
- Benefits from another state are also taxable for a resident
- Repaid benefits follow federal rules
## Required Information
- Form 1099-G
## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment benefits in 2026?
- Was Mississippi tax withheld?
## Common Errors
- Leaving unemployment out because no tax was withheld
- Missing the withholding credit on Form 80-107
## Prompt
- Is unemployment taxable in Mississippi?
