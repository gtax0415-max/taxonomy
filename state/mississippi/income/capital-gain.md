# Capital Gain (Loss)
## Description
Net capital gain or allowed loss computed under federal rules. Mississippi has NO special capital gains rate: gains are taxed at the same 4% (2026) as other income.
## Rules
- Capital loss limit follows federal: $3,000 ($1,500 MFS) against other income, with carryover
- Home sale exclusion follows federal: up to $250,000 / $500,000
- Gains on interests in Mississippi corporations, LPs, LLCs, and Mississippi financial institutions held over one year are exempt (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-9(f)(10)); see ownership-interest-gain-exclusion.md
- Capital gain distributions go here directly if no Schedule D is needed
- Attach federal Schedule D
## Notes
- Gains on Mississippi and U.S. government bonds follow the gain rules here; only the interest is exempt
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule D and Form 8949
- Any sale of an interest in a Mississippi business
## Questions
- Did you sell stock, property, or a business interest in 2026?
- Did you sell your home?
- Do you have a capital loss carryover?
## Common Errors
- Applying a lower capital gains rate
- Deducting more than $3,000 of net loss
- Excluding gain on an out-of-state entity or one held one year or less
