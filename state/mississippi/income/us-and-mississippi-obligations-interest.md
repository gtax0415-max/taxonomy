# U.S. and Mississippi Obligation Interest
## Description
Interest on obligations of the United States, the State of Mississippi, and Mississippi political subdivisions is exempt from Mississippi income tax (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-15(4)(d)).
## What is exempt
- U.S. Treasury bills, notes, bonds, and savings bonds (Series EE and I)
- Other direct U.S. government obligations
- Bonds of the State of Mississippi, its counties, cities, school districts, and other subdivisions
## What is NOT exempt
- Interest on federal refunds and on obligations merely guaranteed by the U.S. (for example many GNMA or FNMA pass-throughs). Check each holding
- Bonds of other states (see other-state-obligations-interest.md)
- Gains on selling these bonds (capital gain rules apply)
## Required Information
- Form 1099-INT box 3
- Issuer of each municipal bond
## Questions
- Do you own Treasury or savings bonds?
- Do you own bonds issued in Mississippi?
## Common Errors
- Leaving U.S. Treasury interest in Mississippi income
- Treating agency-guaranteed mortgage pass-through interest as U.S. obligation interest
## Prompt
- Are I-bond and Treasury interest taxable in Mississippi?
