# Farm Income (Loss)
## Description
Net farm profit or loss from federal Schedule F, including crop-share rentals. Federal Schedule F must be attached.
## Mississippi rules
- FULL-TIME FARMING RULE: a farm LOSS of someone who does not farm full time is allowed only if the person clearly shows the farm is run as a business for profit
- Agricultural disaster program compensation is subtracted as an adjustment (Form 80-105, line 63)
- Depreciation: Mississippi's own 100% bonus depreciation statute applies (see business-income.md)
- Farmers and fishermen: no underestimate interest if two-thirds of gross income is from farming or fishing and the special payment or filing date is met
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule F
- Hours and role in the farming operation
- Any agricultural disaster payments
## Questions
- Do you farm full time?
- Is the farm run to make a profit?
- Did you receive agricultural disaster program payments?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a hobby farm loss
- Leaving disaster program payments in income when the adjustment applies
## Prompt
- I have a small cattle farm on the side that lost money.
