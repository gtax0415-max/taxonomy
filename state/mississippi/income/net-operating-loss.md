# Net Operating Loss
## Description
A Mississippi net operating loss from an earlier year deducted against 2026 income. Mississippi computes and tracks its NOL separately from the federal NOL.
## Mississippi rules (differ from federal)
- CARRYBACK 2 years and CARRYFORWARD 20 years
- The taxpayer may ELECT to waive the carryback and only carry forward; the election is made on Form 80-155
- Federal rules (no carryback for most losses, unlimited carryforward, 80% of taxable income limit) do NOT apply
## Notes
- The Mississippi NOL is computed from Mississippi income, so it can differ from the federal NOL (for example, because retirement income and federal interest are exempt in Mississippi)
- Form 80-155 tracks each loss year, amounts used, and amounts carried forward
- Use the oldest losses first
## Required Information
- Loss year and Mississippi NOL amount
- Amounts used in each later year
- Whether a carryback waiver election was made
## Questions
- Did you have a business, farm, or rental loss larger than your income in an earlier year?
- Did you carry it back, or elect to carry it forward only?
- How much of it has already been used?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal NOL amount
- Applying the federal 80% limit
- Using a loss more than 20 years old
- Not keeping Form 80-155 schedules from earlier years
## Prompt
- I have a loss carryforward from my business. Can I use it in Mississippi?
