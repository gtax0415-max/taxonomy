# Dividend Income
## Description
All dividends received, in cash or property, less any part Mississippi does not tax. Qualified dividends get no lower rate.
## Rules
- Line 4: total dividends (Form 1099-DIV, box 1a)
- Line 5: nontaxable part, mainly the share of a mutual fund dividend that comes from U.S. government obligation interest
- Return-of-capital distributions reduce basis and are not dividend income
- Capital gain distributions go on line 40 (see capital-gain.md)
## Example (2026)
- Fund dividends $2,000, of which 30% came from Treasury interest → line 5 $600 → Mississippi dividends $1,400
## Required Information
- All Forms 1099-DIV
- Fund statements showing U.S. government obligation percentage
## Questions
- Did you receive dividends in 2026?
- Do any funds hold U.S. Treasury or agency bonds?
## Common Errors
- Not subtracting the U.S. obligation share of fund dividends
- Applying a qualified dividend rate
- Reporting capital gain distributions twice
## Prompt
- Are my mutual fund dividends taxable in Mississippi?
