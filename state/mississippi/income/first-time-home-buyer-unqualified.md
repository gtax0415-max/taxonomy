# First-time Home Buyer Account: Unqualified Withdrawals
## Description
Money taken from a Mississippi first-time home buyer savings account for anything other than the down payment or allowable closing costs on a Mississippi single-family home. The withdrawal is added back to income AND charged a 10% penalty.
## The account
- Contributions plus interest are deducted on Form 80-105, line 62
- Annual deduction limit: $2,500 (single, head of family, married filing separate); $5,000 (married filing joint or spouse died)
## No 10% penalty when the withdrawal is due to
- The account holder's death or disability
- A filing under the U.S. Bankruptcy Code
- A transfer to another first-time home buyer savings account
(The amount may still be income; check the 2026 instructions.)
## Example (2026)
- $3,000 withdrawn to pay off a credit card → $3,000 on Schedule N, line 2 and a $300 penalty through Form 80-320
## Required Information
- Account statements showing withdrawals
- Settlement statement if the money was used for a home purchase
## Questions
- Did you withdraw money from a first-time home buyer savings account in 2026?
- Was it used for a down payment or closing costs on a Mississippi home?
## Common Errors
- Reporting the income but not the 10% penalty, or the reverse
- Treating a purchase outside Mississippi as qualified
- Penalizing a transfer to another first-time home buyer account
## Prompt
- I used my first-time home buyer savings for something else.
