# Gain on Sale of an Ownership Interest (27-7-9(f)(10))
## Description
No Mississippi tax on gain from selling certain ownership interests in Mississippi entities held MORE THAN ONE YEAR (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-9(f)(10)).
## Qualifying interests
- Authorized shares of financial institutions domiciled in Mississippi
- Shares of Mississippi domestic corporations
- Partnership interests in Mississippi domestic limited partnerships
- Membership interests in Mississippi domestic limited liability companies
## Loss netting
- Before excluding, the gain is reduced by losses from sales of qualifying interests incurred in the same year or in the two years before or after the gain year
## Notes
- Out-of-state entities (for example a Delaware corporation doing business in Mississippi) do not qualify
- Publicly traded stock of Mississippi-incorporated companies is covered by the statute's wording; confirm treatment before applying
- Not computed in the system: the excluded amount is entered as a Schedule N adjustment with an explanation
## Example (2026)
- Sold a Mississippi LLC interest held 5 years for a $200,000 gain; a $30,000 loss on another Mississippi LLC interest in 2025 → $170,000 excluded on Schedule N
## Required Information
- Entity's state of organization
- Purchase and sale dates
- Qualifying losses in the 5-year window
## Questions
- Was the business organized in Mississippi?
- Did you hold the interest more than one year?
- Did you have losses on other Mississippi entity interests from 2024 through 2028?
## Common Errors
- Excluding gain on an out-of-state entity
- Excluding gain on an interest held one year or less
- Not reducing the exclusion by qualifying losses
## Prompt
- I sold my share of a Mississippi LLC. Is the gain taxable?
