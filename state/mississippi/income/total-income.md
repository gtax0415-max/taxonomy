# Total Income
## Description
The sum of lines 38 through 48 for each column. It is Mississippi income, NOT federal total income.
## Lines summed
- 38 Wages, salaries, tips
- 39 Business income (loss)
- 40 Capital gain (loss)
- 41 Rents, royalties, partnerships, S corporations, trusts
- 42 Farm income (loss)
- 43 Interest income
- 44 Dividend income
- 45 Alimony received
- 46 Taxable pensions and annuities
- 47 Unemployment compensation
- 48 Other income (Schedule N)
## Why it differs from federal total income
- Excludes Social Security, qualifying retirement income, U.S. and Mississippi obligation interest, and Mississippi casino winnings
- Includes other states' municipal interest
- A reconciliation is required when line items differ from the federal return
## Required Information
- All income lines by column
## Questions
- Does your Mississippi total income differ from your federal total income? Why?
## Common Errors
- Copying federal total income
- Adding columns together on a combined return
## Prompt
- Why is my Mississippi income different from my federal income?
