# Business Income (Loss)
## Description
Net profit or loss from a sole proprietorship or profession, from federal Schedule C. Spouses with separate businesses report each in that spouse's column. Federal Schedule C must be attached.
## Mississippi points for 2026
- Start from federal Schedule C, line 31. Report any difference on Schedule N or attach a reconciliation
- The federal Qualified Business Income deduction (§199A, 20%) is taken AFTER federal AGI and is NOT allowed in Mississippi
- Self-employment tax: 50% is deducted as a Mississippi adjustment (Form 80-105, line 61); self-employed health insurance on line 58; SEP/SIMPLE on line 51
- Depreciation: Mississippi provides its own 100% bonus depreciation by statute (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-17, as amended by HB 1733 (2023)). Federal 100% bonus for property acquired after January 19, 2025 (OBBBA) produces the same result for most assets. Confirm Section 179 limits ($2,560,000 / $4,090,000 phase-out for 2026 federally) match for Mississippi before relying on them
- Mixed business and personal expenses: deduct only the business part
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule C for each business, and whose business it is
- Form 4562 and any state depreciation differences
## Questions
- Do you or your spouse own a business?
- Did you place large assets in service in 2026?
- Did you claim the federal QBI deduction?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting the federal QBI deduction
- Putting a spouse's business in the wrong column
- Not reporting depreciation differences on Schedule N
- Not attaching Schedule C
