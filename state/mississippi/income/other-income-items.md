# Other Income and Supplemental Income
## Description
Catch-all for income not reported on another Form 80-105 line, and for differences between federal and Mississippi income. Each item needs a description; amounts in attached statements are not read.
## Common items
- Prizes and awards
- Cancellation of debt (Form 1099-C), following federal exclusions
- Gambling winnings from OUTSIDE Mississippi (Mississippi casino winnings are excluded)
- Wages with no Form W-2 and tips not reported to the employer
- Federal-state differences, entered as a positive or negative adjustment
## 2026 point: federal tips and overtime deductions do NOT carry over
- The federal deductions for qualified tips (up to $25,000) and overtime premium (up to $12,500 / $25,000 joint) for 2025–2028 are taken after federal AGI on Schedule 1-A
- Mississippi builds its own income from W-2 state wages, so these amounts stay TAXABLE in Mississippi
- A 2026 bill to exclude overtime pay (HB 204) was referred to committee with no further action found. Confirm no exclusion was enacted before finalizing
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1 detail
- Gambling winnings split between Mississippi and other states
- Any federal-state difference with an explanation
## Questions
- Did you win prizes or gamble outside Mississippi?
- Did a lender cancel a debt?
- Did you earn wages or tips without a W-2?
## Common Errors
- Reporting Mississippi casino winnings
- Subtracting federally deducted tips or overtime
- Entering an adjustment without a description
## Prompt
- Where do I report gambling winnings from Louisiana on my Mississippi return?
- Are tips and overtime tax-free in Mississippi?
