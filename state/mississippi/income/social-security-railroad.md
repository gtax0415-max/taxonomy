# Social Security and Railroad Retirement
## Description
Social Security benefits and Railroad Retirement Act benefits are exempt from Mississippi tax in total and are not reported.
## Rules
- Exempt regardless of income or age
- Includes Social Security retirement, disability (SSDI), and survivor benefits, and Tier 1 and Tier 2 Railroad Retirement benefits
- The federal senior deduction (2025–2028) is unrelated and does not carry over
## Notes
- Do not count these benefits toward the Mississippi filing requirement
- Supplemental Security Income (SSI) is not taxable federally or in Mississippi
## Required Information
- Form SSA-1099 or RRB-1099 (for confirmation only)
## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits?
## Common Errors
- Carrying the federally taxable part of Social Security (Form 1040 line 6b) to Mississippi
## Prompt
- Is Social Security taxable in Mississippi?
