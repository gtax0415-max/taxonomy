# Catastrophe Savings Account Taxable Distribution
## Description
A withdrawal from a Mississippi Catastrophe Savings Account that was not used for qualified catastrophe expenses is added back to income on Schedule N. It is usually also subject to the separate 2.5% catastrophe savings tax.
## Qualified (not taxable)
- The insurance deductible on the Mississippi legal residence for hurricane, flood, windstorm, or other catastrophe damage
- Uninsured costs after the deductible
- Self-insured losses on the legal residence
## Notes
- The income add-back applies even when the 2.5% tax does not (age 70 or no homestead residence)
- Contributions previously deducted on line 60 are what make the withdrawal taxable
- Losses paid from the account are not also deductible as casualty losses
## Required Information
- Withdrawals and what they paid for
- Insurance claim and deductible records
## Questions
- Did you withdraw from a Catastrophe Savings Account in 2026?
- Was it used for storm or disaster damage to your home?
## Common Errors
- Reporting the 2.5% tax without adding the withdrawal to income
- Treating repairs to a second home or rental as qualified
## Prompt
- I took money out of my catastrophe savings account. Is it taxable?
