# Taxable Pensions and Annuities
## Description
The narrow set of retirement-type payments Mississippi DOES tax. Everything else from retirement plans is exempt (see retirement-income-exclusion.md).
## Taxable in Mississippi
- Early distributions subject to the federal 10% additional tax (Form 5329), such as IRA or 401(k) withdrawals before age 59½ without an exception
- Excess distributions taxed under the Internal Revenue Code
- Deferred compensation paid before the plan's retirement age or service requirement is met
- Separation pay (not retirement income)
## Not here
- Social Security and Railroad Retirement (see social-security-railroad.md)
## Example (2026)
- Age 45 withdraws $10,000 from a 401(k), code 1, federal 10% penalty applies → $10,000 on line 46
- Age 45 withdraws under a federal penalty exception (for example disability, code 3) → generally exempt; confirm the exception
## Required Information
- Forms 1099-R and box 7 codes
- Form 5329, if filed
## Questions
- How old were you when you took the distribution?
- Did a federal early-withdrawal penalty apply?
## Common Errors
- Excluding an early distribution
- Taxing a distribution with a valid federal exception
- Reporting Social Security here
## Prompt
- I took money out of my 401(k) early. Is it taxable in Mississippi?
