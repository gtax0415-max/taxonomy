# Mississippi Gaming Winnings
## Description
Winnings at Mississippi licensed gaming establishments are taxed by a 3% withholding at payout. That withholding is the final tax: the winnings are excluded from Mississippi income and the withholding is NOT refundable or creditable (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-901).
## 2026 change
- Effective January 1, 2026, the slot withholding threshold rose from $1,200 to $2,000 (Department of Revenue Notice 80-25-002), following the federal W-2G threshold change in the One Big Beautiful Bill Act
- The 3% rate is unchanged
## Rules
- Do not report Mississippi casino winnings or include W-2Gs with the return
- Do not claim the 3% withholding as a payment or deduction
- Mississippi casino LOSSES are not deductible (see other-miscellaneous.md)
- Gambling winnings from other states, lotteries, and online sites outside Mississippi are taxable on Schedule N
- A nonresident with only Mississippi casino winnings does not file
## Required Information
- W-2Gs separated between Mississippi casinos and other sources
## Questions
- Did you win at a Mississippi casino?
- Did you have gambling winnings anywhere else?
## Common Errors
- Reporting Mississippi casino winnings as income
- Claiming the 3% casino withholding as a credit
- Leaving out out-of-state gambling winnings
