# Alimony Received
## Description
Alimony and separate maintenance received is taxable in Mississippi only under divorce or separation agreements executed on or before December 31, 2018, following federal law.
## Rules
- Agreement executed on or before December 31, 2018 (and not later modified to adopt the new rule): taxable to the recipient
- Agreement executed or modified after December 31, 2018: NOT taxable
- Child support is never taxable
## Required Information
- Agreement date and any modification date
- Amount received in 2026
## Questions
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed?
- Was it modified after 2018?
- How much of the payment is child support?
## Common Errors
- Reporting alimony under a 2019 or later agreement
- Including child support

- I receive alimony. Is it taxable in Mississippi?
