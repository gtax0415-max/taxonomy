# Consumer Use Tax
## Description
Tax on goods and services bought out of state (including online and catalog purchases and digital downloads) and used, stored, or consumed in Mississippi when no sales tax was paid to ANY state.
## Rates for 2026
- General rate: 7% of the purchase price
- Food and drink for human consumption: 5% (reduced from 7% on July 1, 2025 by HB 1 (2025), which cut the grocery sales tax)
- The 2025 instruction booklet prints only the 7% rate; the 5% food rate follows the sales tax change
## Notes
- If sales tax was paid to another state at a lower rate, generally only the difference is owed (confirm with Mississippi DOR guidance)
- Purchases from sellers that already collected Mississippi tax are not reported
- Examples: books, clothing, computers, electronics, furniture, household items, music, movies, e-books, and software
## Required Information
- List of untaxed out-of-state purchases and amounts
- Which purchases are food for human consumption
- Any sales tax paid to another state
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online or out of state in 2026 without paying sales tax?
- Were any of those purchases groceries?
## Common Errors
- Reporting purchases where the seller already charged Mississippi tax
- Using 7% on qualifying food purchased after June 30, 2025
- Leaving the line blank when untaxed purchases were made
## Prompt
- Do I owe Mississippi use tax on online purchases?
- What is the Mississippi use tax rate on groceries?
