# Ad Valorem Inventory (Code 14)
## Description
Credit for property (ad valorem) taxes paid on business inventory and certain rental equipment by manufacturers, processors, distributors, wholesalers, and retailers (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.5).
## Amount
- Credit is the LESSER of the ad valorem taxes paid or the income tax attributable to each location
- Compute: net income from the entity or Schedule C × effective tax rate
## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Expenses used for the credit must be added back to taxable income
- From a Schedule C business: attach the ad valorem tax bills
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 14 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business pay county property tax on inventory?
- Is the business a pass-through entity or a Schedule C?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Not adding back the taxes used for the credit
- Claiming more than the tax attributable to the location
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My store pays inventory tax to the county.
