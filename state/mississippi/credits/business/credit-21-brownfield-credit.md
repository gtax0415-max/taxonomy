# Brownfield Credit (Code 21)
## Description
Jobs-based credit for commercial or industrial owners who remediate contaminated property under the Mississippi Brownfields Voluntary Cleanup and Redevelopment Act (Miss. Code Ann. §57-73-21(8)).
## Amount
- Follows the basic jobs tax credit rules (tiered percentage of payroll), with NO minimum number of jobs
## Notes
- Cannot be combined with the national/regional headquarters or R&D skills credits
- Requires a Commission on Environmental Quality completion certificate (attach)
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 21 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did the business remediate a brownfield site under an MDEQ agreement?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Combining with the headquarters or R&D credits
- Missing the MDEQ certificate
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We cleaned up a contaminated site and hired workers there.
