# Premium Retaliatory (Code 02)
## Description
Credit tied to premium retaliatory taxes paid by insurance companies. On an individual return it appears only when passed through from an entity that earned it.
## Amount
- Amount is set at the entity level and reported on the owner's Mississippi K-1
## Notes
- Rarely seen on individual returns; usually an insurance company credit
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost (marked * in the Form 80-100 credit code table)
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 02 and amount
## Questions
- Did you receive a Mississippi K-1 showing a premium retaliatory credit?
## Common Errors
- Carrying an unused amount forward (no carryover is allowed)
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a premium retaliatory credit.
