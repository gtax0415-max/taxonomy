# Airport Cargo Charges (Code 22)
## Description
Credit for certain charges paid at Mississippi public airports on the export or import of cargo.
## Amount
- Based on qualifying airport cargo charges paid
## Notes
- HB 961 (2025) reenacted the credit and extended its repeal date to July 1, 2028
- Limitation and carryforward: confirm against the 2026 instructions
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 22 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business ship cargo through a Mississippi public airport?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We ship freight through Jackson airport.
