# Finance Company Privilege (Code 03)
## Description
Credit related to the privilege tax paid by finance companies. Appears on an individual return only through a pass-through entity.
## Amount
- Amount set at the entity level and reported on the Mississippi K-1
## Notes
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 03 and amount
## Questions
- Do you own part of a licensed finance company that passed this credit to you?
## Common Errors
- Carrying an unused amount forward
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My finance company K-1 shows a privilege tax credit.
