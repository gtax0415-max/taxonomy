# New Markets (Code 28)
## Description
Mississippi new markets credit for qualified equity investments in community development entities that invest in low-income communities in Mississippi.
## Amount
- Credit is a percentage of the qualified equity investment, taken over several years (confirm current schedule)
## Notes
- Usually received through a pass-through entity
- Limitation and carryforward: confirm
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 28 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you make a qualified equity investment in a certified community development entity?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a new markets credit.
