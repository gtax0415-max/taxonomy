# Biomass Energy Investment (Code 29)
## Description
Credit for investment in facilities that produce energy from biomass in Mississippi.
## Amount
- Amount and limits: confirm against the statute and the 2026 instructions
## Notes
- Usually received through a pass-through entity
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 29 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions

- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
