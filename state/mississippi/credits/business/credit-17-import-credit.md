# Import Credit (Code 17)
## Description
Credit for port charges paid on cargo IMPORTED through Mississippi ports by qualifying businesses.
## Amount
- Credit equals port charges paid for receiving cargo, handling, and wharfage
## Notes
- Eligibility (per DOR incentive booklet): U.S. headquarters in Mississippi, at least five permanent full-time employees, at least $2 million of Mississippi capital investment; forest products excluded
- Limitation and carryforward: confirm against the 2026 instructions
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 17 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Does your business import cargo through a Mississippi port?
- Is its U.S. headquarters in Mississippi?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Including export charges (separate credit)
- Claiming forest product imports
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We import parts through the Port of Pascagoula.
