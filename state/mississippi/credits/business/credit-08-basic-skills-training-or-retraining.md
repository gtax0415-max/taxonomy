# Basic Skills Training or Retraining (Code 08)
## Description
Credit for businesses that pay for approved basic skills training or retraining of Mississippi employees (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.50 and related).
## Amount
- 50% of qualified training expenses, up to $2,500 per Mississippi employee per year
- Statewide cap of $2.5 million per year
## Notes
- LIMITATION: 50% of Mississippi-attributable income tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Training must be approved (community or junior college / workforce program)
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 08 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business pay for approved employee training in Mississippi?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming training that was not approved
- Exceeding $2,500 per employee
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We sent employees to a community college skills program.
