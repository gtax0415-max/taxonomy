# Inland Water Transportation (Code 38)
## Description
Jobs credit for enterprises primarily engaged in inland water transportation of cargo on lakes, rivers, and intracoastal waterways (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.40).
## Amount
- $2,000 per new full-time Mississippi job that did not exist before January 1, 2019
## Notes
- Can offset up to 100% of income tax
- Must begin claiming within 5 years after the year earned
- Statewide cap $2,000,000 per year
- HB 972 (2025) extended the repeal date to January 1, 2029
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 38 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Does the business move cargo on Mississippi waterways and add new jobs?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My barge company K-1 shows a jobs credit.
