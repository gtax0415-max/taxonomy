# Headquarters Relocation Credit (Code 32)
## Description
Credit for a company that relocates its national or regional headquarters to Mississippi from another state (Miss. Code Ann. §57-73-21(7)).
## Amount
- Equal to actual non-depreciable relocation costs paid
- Minimum 20 new headquarters jobs
## Notes
- LIMITATION: with jobs, headquarters, and R&D skills credits, 50% of income tax liability
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Statewide cap $1,000,000 per fiscal year (excluding carryforwards)
- No credit twice for the same employee within 12 months
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 32 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did the company move its headquarters to Mississippi from another state?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the 50% combined limitation
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- Our company relocated its headquarters from Texas to Mississippi.
