# Manufacturing Investment Tax Credit (Code 23)
## Description
Credit for manufacturers that have operated in Mississippi for at least two years and make a qualifying investment in buildings and equipment for a manufacturing facility.
## Amount
- Generally 5% of eligible investment, with a minimum investment of $1,000,000 (confirm current amounts)
## Notes
- Usually passed through on a Mississippi K-1
- Limitation and carryforward: confirm against the 2026 instructions
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 23 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did the manufacturing business make a major new investment in Mississippi?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My manufacturing company K-1 shows an investment credit.
