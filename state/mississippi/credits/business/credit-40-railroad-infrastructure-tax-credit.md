# Railroad Infrastructure Tax Credit (Code 40)
## Description
Credit for qualified reconstruction, replacement, and new rail infrastructure expenditures by Class II and Class III railroads.
## Amount
- Limited to the income tax due
- Statewide cap $8,000,000 per calendar year
## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Transferable by written agreement during the year earned and the following 5 years
- 2026: SB 2832 extends the credit through January 1, 2029
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 40 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Do you own part of a short-line railroad, or did you buy a transferred railroad credit?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I purchased a railroad infrastructure credit.
