# Jobs Tax (Code 05)
## Description
Credit for eligible manufacturers, processors, distributors, R&D firms, wholesalers, and warehouses that create new full-time jobs in Mississippi (Miss. Code Ann. §57-73-21).
## Amount
- Tier One county: 2.5% of payroll for new jobs (20-job minimum)
- Tier Two county: 5% of payroll (15-job minimum)
- Tier Three county: 10% of payroll (10-job minimum)
- Each year's credit is used for five years
## Notes
- LIMITATION: jobs, national/regional headquarters, and R&D skills credits combined are limited to 50% of the income tax attributable to Mississippi operations
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Usually passed through on a Mississippi K-1 or earned by a Schedule C business
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 05 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business add new full-time jobs in Mississippi?
- In which county tier is the business?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the 50% combined limitation
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My company added 25 jobs in Mississippi.
