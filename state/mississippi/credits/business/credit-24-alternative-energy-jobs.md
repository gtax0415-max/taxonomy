# Alternative Energy Jobs (Code 24)
## Description
Jobs credit for businesses that manufacture or produce alternative energy where at least 50% of the finished product comes from Mississippi resources (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.29).
## Amount
- $1,000 per new full-time job
- Minimum 25 new jobs
- Available for 20 years; must begin using it within 5 years of commercial production
## Notes
- Can offset up to 100% of income tax attributable to the Mississippi project
- Cannot be combined with the basic jobs, headquarters, or R&D skills credits (codes 05, 06, 07)
- A producer affidavit is required
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 24 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Does the business produce alternative energy in Mississippi with 25 or more new jobs?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it together with the jobs, headquarters, or R&D credit
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- Our biofuel plant K-1 shows an alternative energy jobs credit.
