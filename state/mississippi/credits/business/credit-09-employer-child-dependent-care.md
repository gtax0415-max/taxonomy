# Employer Child / Dependent Care (Code 09)
## Description
Credit for EMPLOYERS that provide state-certified dependent care for employees' children or pay a qualifying child care stipend. Not the same as the individual dependent care credit (code 44).
## Amount
- 50% of qualified dependent care expenses
- Stipend credit up to $3,000 per child per year (stipend of at least $2,000)
- Statewide cap of $1,000,000 per calendar year
## Notes
- Can offset up to 100% of income tax
- 2026: SB 2867 reduces the eligible stipend amount, adds certification requirements, and limits total annual credits; confirm the new amounts and effective date
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 09 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business provide child care or a child care stipend to employees?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Confusing it with the individual dependent care credit (code 44)
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My business pays for employees' child care.
