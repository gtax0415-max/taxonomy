# Research and Development Skills (Code 07)
## Description
Credit for each new full-time position that requires research and development skills, such as chemists, engineers, or computer specialists (Miss. Code Ann. §57-73-21(6)).
## Amount
- $1,000 per qualifying position per year, for five years
- No minimum, but total headcount must increase
## Notes
- LIMITATION: combined with jobs and headquarters credits, 50% of Mississippi-attributable income tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 07 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did the business add positions requiring research and development skills?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Counting positions that do not require R&D skills
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We hired three engineers for our Mississippi lab.
