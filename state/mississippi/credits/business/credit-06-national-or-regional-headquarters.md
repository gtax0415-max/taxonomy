# National or Regional Headquarters (Code 06)
## Description
Credit for businesses that establish, transfer, or expand a national or regional headquarters in Mississippi (Miss. Code Ann. §57-73-21(5)).
## Amount
- $500 per new full-time headquarters job per year, for five years
- $1,000 per job if pay is at least 125% of the state average wage; $2,000 if at least 200%
- Minimum 20 new jobs
## Notes
- LIMITATION: combined with jobs and R&D skills credits, 50% of Mississippi-attributable income tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 06 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did the business open or expand a headquarters in Mississippi with 20 or more new jobs?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Combining with the brownfield credit (not allowed)
- Exceeding the 50% combined limitation
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- Our company moved its regional headquarters to Jackson.
