# Bank Share (Code 60)
## Description
Credit related to the bank share tax paid by banks. On an individual return it appears only through a pass-through bank, such as an S corporation.
## Amount
- Amount set at the entity level and reported on the Mississippi K-1
## Notes
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 60 and amount
## Questions
- Do you own shares in an S corporation bank that passed this credit to you?
## Common Errors
- Carrying an unused amount forward
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My bank K-1 shows a bank share credit.
