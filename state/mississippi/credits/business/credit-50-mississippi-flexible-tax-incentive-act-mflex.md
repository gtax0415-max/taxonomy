# Mississippi Flexible Tax Incentive Act (MFLEX) (Code 50)
## Description
Credit for businesses that locate or expand facilities and hire in Mississippi. The business applies to the Mississippi Development Authority (MDA), which certifies the credit amount.
## Amount
- Amount set in the MDA certification
## Notes
- Limited to the income tax liability
- Usable for up to 10 years from the certification date
- May not be used before MDA issues the certification
- Attach the MDA certification letter and a Mississippi Tax Credit Summary Schedule
- Marked * (no carryover) in the booklet credit code table; use is governed by the 10-year certification window
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 50 and amount
## Questions
- Has the business received an MFLEX certification from MDA?
## Common Errors
- Using the credit before certification
- Not attaching the MDA letter
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- Our company received an MFLEX certification.
