# Broadband Technology (Code 19)
## Description
Credit for telecommunications enterprises that invest in equipment used to deploy broadband technologies in Mississippi.
## Amount
- Based on qualifying broadband equipment investment
## Notes
- HB 1644 (2025) revised the definition of qualifying equipment; applies to investments made before July 1, 2030
- Usually reaches an individual through a K-1
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 19 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Do you own part of a telecommunications enterprise deploying broadband in Mississippi?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming equipment placed in service after the eligibility window
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My telecom company K-1 shows a broadband credit.
