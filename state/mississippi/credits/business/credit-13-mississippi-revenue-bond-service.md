# Mississippi Revenue Bond Service (Code 13)
## Description
Credit equal to debt service paid on RED (Regional Economic Development) bonds issued through the Mississippi Business Finance Corporation for a project (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.3; §57-10-401 and -409).
## Amount
- Credit equals the debt service paid on the bonds
## Notes
- LIMITATION: the lesser of 80% of total income tax or the tax attributable to the project
- Carryforward: 3 years
- Available for the life of the bonds
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 13 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Does the business have a financing agreement with the Mississippi Business Finance Corporation?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the 80% limitation
- Carrying forward more than 3 years
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- Our company pays debt service on RED bonds.
