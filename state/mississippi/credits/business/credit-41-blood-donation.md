# Blood Donation (Code 41)
## Description
Credit for EMPLOYERS of $20 for each verified blood donation made by an employee as part of a blood drive.
## Amount
- $20 per verified employee blood donation
## Notes
- Limited to income tax due
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost
- Not available to the individual donor; only to the employer (or its owners through a K-1)
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- 40 verified donations → $800 credit
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 41 and amount
## Questions
- Did your business sponsor a blood drive with employee donations?
## Common Errors
- An individual donor claiming the credit
- Carrying an unused amount forward
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My business held a blood drive.
