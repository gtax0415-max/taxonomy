# Credit for Tax Paid to Another State
## Description
A full-year Mississippi resident taxed on ALL income may take a credit for income tax actually owed to another state on income also taxed by Mississippi (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-77). Not available on the nonresident return.
## Amount: the LEAST of
1. Mississippi income tax on line 17
2. Income tax actually paid to the other state (credits the other state allows do not count as tax paid)
3. Mississippi tax on the income reported to the other state, at the highest applicable Mississippi rate (4% for 2026)
## Notes
- A return MUST be filed with the other state and a copy attached with Form 80-160; W-2 withholding is not proof of tax paid
- Not for taxes paid to foreign countries or for local (city) income taxes, unless the 2026 instructions say otherwise
- Scope: not reached by the current scenarios (residency decision: full-year residents with Mississippi income only), but built
## Example (2026)
- Mississippi resident earns $20,000 in Alabama; Alabama tax paid $800; Mississippi tax on that $20,000 at 4% = $800; line 17 is $2,400 → credit $800
## Required Information
- Other state's return and tax actually paid
- Income taxed by the other state
## Questions
- Did you earn income in another state and file a return there?
- How much tax did you actually owe the other state after its credits?
## Common Errors
- Using withholding instead of tax actually owed
- Not attaching the other state's return
- Claiming more than Mississippi tax on the same income
## Prompt
- I live in Mississippi but work in Tennessee.
- I paid Alabama tax on rental income. Do I get credit in Mississippi?
