# Credit for Tax Paid by an Electing Pass-Through Entity
## Description
When a partnership or S corporation elects to pay Mississippi income tax at the entity level, each owner claims his or her share of that tax as a payment. It works like withholding: it can create a refund.
## Rules
- Total the entity tax shown on each Mississippi K-1 on Form 80-161; line 3D goes to Form 80-105, line 26
- The Mississippi K-1s must be attached
- The owner still reports the pass-through income on line 41 (see rents-royalties-partnerships.md)
## Why entities elect
- The entity deducts the state tax federally, which is not limited by the federal SALT cap ($40,400 for 2026). The One Big Beautiful Bill Act kept this workaround
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 from each electing entity
## Questions
- Do you own part of a partnership or S corporation that elected to pay Mississippi tax for its owners?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the entity-level tax off, or entering it as withholding on line 24
- Reporting the credit but leaving out the K-1 income
- Not attaching the K-1s

- My S corporation paid Mississippi tax for me. Where does it go?
