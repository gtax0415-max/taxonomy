# Dependent Care Credit (Code 44)
## Description
Mississippi credit equal to 25% of the FEDERAL child and dependent care credit, for taxpayers with federal AGI of $50,000 or less.
## Amount
- 25% of the federal credit claimed on Form 2441 (carried to federal Schedule 3)
- Federal AGI must be $50,000 or less (a cliff, not a phase-out)
- Limited to the Mississippi income tax liability
## Notes
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost
- Figured from the facts entered on Client Info
- 2026 federal change (One Big Beautiful Bill Act): the federal credit rate rises (up to 50% of expenses at lower incomes), so the Mississippi credit, which is 25% of the federal credit, increases too
- Must actually claim the federal credit; no federal credit means no Mississippi credit
- Not the same as the employer child care credit (code 09)
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- Federal AGI $42,000, federal Form 2441 credit $1,400 → Mississippi credit $350
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 44 and amount
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal child and dependent care credit for 2026?
- Is your federal AGI $50,000 or less?
## Common Errors
- Claiming with federal AGI over $50,000
- Using 25% of expenses instead of 25% of the federal credit
- Carrying an unused amount forward
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I claimed the federal child care credit and earn $42,000.
- Does Mississippi have a child care credit?
