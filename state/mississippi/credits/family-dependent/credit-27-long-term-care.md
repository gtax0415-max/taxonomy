# Long Term Care (Code 27)
## Description
Credit for premiums paid during the year for qualified long-term care insurance policies (as defined in IRC §7702B).
## Amount
- 25% of premiums paid
- Maximum $500 per policy, or the income tax liability if less
- One policy covering more than one person counts as one policy
## Notes
- NO CARRYOVER: unused credit is lost
- Figured from the facts entered on Client Info
- Premiums used for this credit should be reviewed against the medical expense deduction on Schedule A to avoid double benefit
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- Two separate policies with $2,400 and $1,600 of premiums → $500 + $400 = $900 credit
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 27 and amount
## Questions
- Did you pay premiums on a qualified long-term care insurance policy?
- How many separate policies?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $500 per policy
- Treating a joint policy as two policies
- Carrying an unused amount forward
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I pay for long-term care insurance.
