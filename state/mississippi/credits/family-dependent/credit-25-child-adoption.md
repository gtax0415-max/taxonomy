# Child Adoption (Code 25)
## Description
Mississippi credit for qualified adoption expenses paid or incurred for each child legally adopted. Separate from, and in addition to, the federal adoption credit (Form 8839).
## Amount
- Up to $10,000 per child who resided in Mississippi before the adoption
- Up to $5,000 per child who resided outside Mississippi before the adoption
- Limited to qualified adoption expenses paid or incurred
- Claimed in the tax year the adoption becomes FINAL
## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Figured from the facts entered on Client Info
- Timing differs from the federal credit: the whole Mississippi credit is claimed in the year of finalization
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- Adoption of a child living in Mississippi finalized in 2026; $12,000 of expenses → credit $10,000; with $1,800 of Mississippi tax, $1,800 used and $8,200 carried forward
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 25 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did an adoption become final in 2026?
- Did the child live in Mississippi before the adoption?
- How much did you pay in qualified adoption expenses?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it before the adoption is final
- Using $10,000 for a child who lived outside Mississippi
- Claiming more than actual expenses
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We finalized our adoption this year.
- We adopted a child from another state.
