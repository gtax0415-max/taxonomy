# Business Contributions to Eligible Charitable Organizations (Code 36)
## Description
Credit for BUSINESS enterprises (corporations, LLCs, partnerships, sole proprietorships) that contribute to eligible 501(c)(3) charitable organizations.
## Amount
- Amount follows the approved contribution; limited to 50% of total tax liability
## Notes
- In lieu of the charitable deduction
- Also available against ad valorem tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- A sole proprietor claims it on the individual return; other owners receive it on a K-1
- Taxpayers generally must apply for and receive Department of Revenue approval (credit allocation) before claiming; see dor.ms.gov/charitable-contribution-credits
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 36 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business make a contribution to an eligible charitable organization?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a personal (non-business) gift under this code
- Deducting the same gift
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My LLC donated to a qualifying charity.
