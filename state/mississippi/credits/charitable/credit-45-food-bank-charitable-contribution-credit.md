# Food Bank Charitable Contribution Credit (Code 45)
## Description
Credit for BUSINESS enterprises that make voluntary cash contributions to eligible charitable organizations that buy, warehouse, and deliver food to food pantries or soup kitchens in more than five Mississippi counties each month.
## Amount
- Limited to 50% of total tax liability
## Notes
- In lieu of the charitable deduction
- Also available against ad valorem tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Available to corporations, LLCs, partnerships, and sole proprietorships; a sole proprietor claims it on the individual return
- Taxpayers generally must apply for and receive Department of Revenue approval before claiming; see dor.ms.gov/charitable-contribution-credits
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 45 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did your business donate cash to an eligible Mississippi food bank?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a personal (non-business) gift
- Deducting the same gift on Schedule A or Schedule C
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- My company donated to the Mississippi Food Network.
