# Pregnancy Resource Charitable Contribution Credit (Code 39)
## Description
Credit for voluntary cash contributions to eligible pregnancy resource centers or crisis pregnancy centers (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-22.43).
## Amount
- Limited to 50% of total tax liability
## Notes
- 2026 CHANGE: SB 3124 (signed April 8, 2026) extends the credit to taxpayers that are NOT business enterprises (individuals) and revises organization eligibility. Confirm the tax-year effective date and any individual cap against the 2026 instructions
- Charitable credits approved by DOR are claimed IN LIEU OF the charitable deduction: remove the same gift from Form 80-108, Schedule A, line 5 (see charitable-contributions.md)
- Taxpayers generally must apply for and receive Department of Revenue approval (credit allocation) before claiming; see dor.ms.gov/charitable-contribution-credits
- Also available against ad valorem tax
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 39 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you give cash to an eligible pregnancy resource center?
- Did you receive DOR approval?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and the Schedule A deduction
- Assuming individual eligibility before confirming the effective date
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I donated to a crisis pregnancy center.
