# Qualifying Charitable Contribution Credit Approved by DOR (Code 34)
## Description
Credit for cash contributions to qualifying charitable organizations (501(c)(3) organizations or community action agencies approved by DOR) that serve low-income Mississippians.
## Amount
- Lesser of the contribution or $1,200 (single)
- Lesser of the contribution or $2,400 (married filing joint)
- Married filing separate: each spouse half of the joint amount
## Notes
- Charitable credits approved by DOR are claimed IN LIEU OF the charitable deduction: remove the same gift from Form 80-108, Schedule A, line 5 (see charitable-contributions.md)
- Taxpayers generally must apply for and receive Department of Revenue approval (credit allocation) before claiming; see dor.ms.gov/charitable-contribution-credits
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Also available against ad valorem tax (up to 50% of that liability)
- 2026: HB 1944 adds certain nonprofit special-purpose schools for students with disabilities as eligible organizations, with annual limits; confirm effective date
- Statewide aggregate cap shared with code 35
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- Married couple filing jointly gives $3,000 to an approved organization → credit $2,400
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 34 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you give cash to a DOR-approved qualifying charitable organization?
- Did you receive DOR approval for the credit?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and the Schedule A deduction for the same gift
- Claiming without DOR approval
- Exceeding $1,200 / $2,400
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I donated to a Mississippi charity on the approved list.
