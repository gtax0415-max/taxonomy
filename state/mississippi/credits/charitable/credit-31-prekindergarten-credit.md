# Prekindergarten Credit (Code 31)
## Description
Credit for qualified contributions supporting prekindergarten programs paid to approved providers, lead partners, or collaboratives.
## Amount
- Contributions up to $1,000,000 per contributor per year
- Contributions must be approved by the State Department of Education
## Notes
- Carryforward: 3 years
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 31 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you contribute to an approved prekindergarten program?
- Was the contribution approved by the Department of Education?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a contribution that was not approved
- Carrying forward more than 3 years
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I donated to an early learning collaborative.
