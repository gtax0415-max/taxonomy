# Endowment Fund Charitable Credit (Code 37)
## Description
Credit for charitable gifts to endowed funds held by qualified Mississippi community foundations (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-207).
## Amount
- 25% of the qualified contribution
- Minimum qualified contribution $1,000
- Contribution limits per the instructions: $500,000 maximum per contribution and $200,000 total qualified contributions per taxpayer per year (confirm how these interact)
- Statewide cap $1,000,000 per calendar year
## Notes
- Endowed funds include donor-advised, affiliate, field-of-interest, agency, and designated funds intended to exist in perpetuity
- Community foundation must be recognized by the Mississippi Association of Grantmakers
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- $10,000 to a qualifying endowed fund → $2,500 credit
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 37 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you give at least $1,000 to an endowed fund at a Mississippi community foundation?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Gifts under $1,000
- Gifts to a non-endowed fund
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I gave $10,000 to my community foundation's endowment.
