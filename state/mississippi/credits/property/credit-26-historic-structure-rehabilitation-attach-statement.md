# Historic Structure Rehabilitation (Attach Statement) (Code 26)
## Description
Credit for rehabilitating a certified historic structure or a structure in a certified historic district.
## Amount
- 25% of total qualified rehabilitation costs
- Election: a rebate of 75% of the credit amount instead of the credit, approved by the Department of Archives and History
## Notes
- The Mississippi Department of Archives and History (MDAH) certifies eligible costs; attach the certification
- Claimed in the year the credit is certified
- Not-for-profit entities are not eligible
- If credit was used before electing the rebate, the rebate is reduced by the credit used
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 26 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a certified historic building?
- Do you have MDAH certification?
- Are you choosing the credit or the rebate?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without attaching the MDAH certification
- Claiming both credit and rebate for the same costs
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- We renovated a historic building downtown.
