# Land Donation (Code 18)
## Description
Credit for donating land in Mississippi for qualifying public purposes. Details (qualifying donee, valuation, limits) are not given in the instruction booklet; confirm against the statute before use.
## Amount
- Amount and limits: confirm
## Notes
- Carryforward allowed (not marked as no-carryover in the credit code table)
- A land gift used for this credit should not also be deducted as a charitable contribution unless the statute allows both
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 18 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you donate land to a public entity in Mississippi?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming both the credit and the charitable deduction for the same gift
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I donated land to the county for a park.
