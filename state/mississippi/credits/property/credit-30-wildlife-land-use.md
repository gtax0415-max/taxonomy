# Wildlife Land Use (Code 30)
## Description
Credit for landowners who allow their land to be used as a natural area preserve, wildlife refuge, wildlife management area, or public outdoor recreation area.
## Amount
- $5.50 per acre
## Notes
- Land must first be approved as suitable by the Mississippi Commission on Wildlife, Fisheries and Parks
- Carryforward: 5 years from the close of the year in which the land was approved
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- 500 approved acres → $2,750 credit
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 30 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Is your land approved for public wildlife or recreation use?
- How many acres?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without Commission approval
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I let my land be used as a wildlife management area.
