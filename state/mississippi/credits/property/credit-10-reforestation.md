# Reforestation (Code 10)
## Description
Credit for eligible landowners for approved reforestation practices on Mississippi land. Unlike most codes in this range, it is commonly earned directly by individuals.
## Amount
- Lesser of 50% of actual costs or 50% of the Mississippi Forestry Commission average cost of approved practices
- Annual maximum: lesser of $10,000 or the income tax after other credits (excluding withholding, estimates, and credit for tax paid to another state)
- Lifetime maximum: $75,000
## Notes
- Carryforward: until the $75,000 lifetime limit is reached
- Acreage receiving state or federal cost-share funds generally does not qualify (unless AGI is under the federal EIC level)
- Expenses used for the credit must be added back to taxable income
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401 and attach supporting documents
- When an individual has several credits, they may be used in any order that gives the greatest savings
## Required Information
- Mississippi K-1 or certification showing the credit
- Credit code 10 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you replant timber on land you own in Mississippi?
- Did you receive cost-share funds for the same acreage?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Not adding back the expenses used for the credit
- Claiming on cost-share acreage
- Exceeding $10,000 per year or $75,000 lifetime
- Claiming the credit without the Mississippi K-1 or certification that supports it
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I replanted pine on my land after a harvest.
