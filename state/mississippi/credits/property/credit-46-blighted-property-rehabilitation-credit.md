# Blighted Property Rehabilitation Credit (Code 46)
## Description
Rebate or credit for developing blighted property in Mississippi into an owner-occupied home or commercial building (HB 1201, effective January 1, 2025).
## Amount
- 25% of total rehabilitation costs
- Costs incurred after January 1, 2026 must exceed $50,000 for an owner-occupied dwelling or $100,000 for a commercial structure
- Actual costs must be between 80% and 125% of the Secretary of State-approved estimate
- Statewide caps: $2,000,000 per calendar year and $10,000,000 in total
## Notes
- Carryforward: 10 years
- Program run by DOR with the Secretary of State's office
- New for 2026: costs before January 1, 2026 do not count
- Nonrefundable: limited to Mississippi income tax; enter the code and amount on Form 80-401
## Example (2026)
- Approved owner-occupied rehab with $80,000 of 2026 costs → $20,000 credit, carried forward up to 10 years
## Required Information
- Documents listed in source_doc
- Credit code 46 and amount
- Any carryforward from earlier years
## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a blighted property approved by the Secretary of State?
- Were costs incurred after January 1, 2026, and above $50,000 (home) or $100,000 (commercial)?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from an earlier year?
## Common Errors
- Counting costs incurred before 2026
- Actual costs outside 80%–125% of the approved estimate
- Using more than the credit's limitation or the remaining income tax
- Not entering the two-digit code on Form 80-401
## Prompt
- I restored a blighted house to live in.
