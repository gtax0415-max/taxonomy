# Living Organ Donation Expenses
## Description
Deduction from gross income for unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages of a person who donates one or more organs while living for human transplantation.
## Amounts
- Up to $10,000
- May be claimed ONLY ONCE per donor (lifetime)
- Entered as a negative amount on Schedule N with a description
## Notes
- Covers donation of organs for transplant; reimbursed costs do not count
- Medical costs already deducted on Schedule A cannot be counted again
## Required Information
- Proof of the donation
- Unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages
## Questions
- Did you donate an organ while living?
- Were any of your costs reimbursed?
- Have you claimed this deduction before?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $10,000 or claiming it a second time
- Entering it as a positive amount
- Including reimbursed costs
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother. Is there a Mississippi deduction?
