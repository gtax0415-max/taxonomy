# Agricultural Disaster Program Compensation
## Description
Mississippi subtracts compensation received from an agricultural disaster program. The payments are included in farm income federally (Schedule F), then backed out here.
## Rules
- Only DISASTER program payments qualify (for example, payments for crop or livestock losses from a declared disaster)
- Ordinary farm program payments (price support, conservation) stay taxable
- The payment must be included in income (line 42) for the subtraction to apply
## Required Information
- Form 1099-G and program letters showing which payments are disaster payments
## Questions
- Did you receive USDA or state disaster payments for farm losses in 2026?
- Are they included in your Schedule F income?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting non-disaster farm program payments
- Subtracting a payment not included in income
## Prompt
- I got a USDA disaster payment for my crops.
