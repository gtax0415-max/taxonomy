# Student Loan Interest and Educator Expenses
## Description
Mississippi has no adjustment for the federal student loan interest deduction or the above-the-line educator expense deduction. No enacted provision was found.
## What this means for 2026
- Student loan interest (federal up to $2,500): NOT deductible in Mississippi
- Federal above-the-line educator expenses ($350 per educator for 2026, up from $300): NOT deductible in Mississippi
- NEW for 2026: qualifying educator expenses not deducted on Schedule 1 can be itemized on federal Schedule A, line 17k (not subject to the 2% floor; also covers coaches and athletic administrators). Because Mississippi uses federal Schedule A, these DO reach Mississippi as other miscellaneous deductions when the taxpayer itemizes
## Required Information
- Form 1098-E and educator expense receipts (to recognize and explain the difference)
## Questions
- Did you pay student loan interest?
- Are you a K-12 teacher with classroom expenses?
## Common Errors
- Carrying federal Schedule 1 lines 11 or 21 into Mississippi adjustments
- Missing the itemized educator expense on the Mississippi Schedule A
