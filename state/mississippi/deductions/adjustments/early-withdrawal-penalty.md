# Penalty on Early Withdrawal of Savings
## Description
Interest penalty charged by a bank for withdrawing a time deposit (such as a CD) before maturity. Deductible in full, the same as federally.
## Rules
- Use the amount in Form 1099-INT box 2 (or Form 1099-OID box 3)
- Deduct the full penalty even if it is more than the interest earned
- The related interest is still reported in full on line 43
- Not the 10% penalty on early retirement distributions, which is not deductible
## Required Information
- Forms 1099-INT showing a box 2 amount
## Questions
- Did you cash in a CD early in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Netting the penalty against interest instead of deducting it here
- Deducting the 10% retirement plan penalty
## Prompt
- I broke a CD early and paid a penalty.
