# First-time Home Buyer Savings Account
## Description
Deduction for deposits plus interest in a designated Mississippi first-time home buyer savings account, used to save for the down payment and closing costs on a single-family home in Mississippi. No federal counterpart.
## Amounts for 2026 (statutory, unchanged)
- $2,500 per year: single, head of family, married filing separate
- $5,000 per year: married filing joint, or spouse died
## Notes
- Qualified uses: down payment and allowable closing costs on a Mississippi single-family residence
- Withdrawals for other purposes are added to income and charged a 10% penalty (see first-time-home-buyer-unqualified.md and first-time-home-buyer-penalty.md)
## Required Information
- Deposits and interest for 2026

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the annual limit
- Deducting deposits to an ordinary savings account
