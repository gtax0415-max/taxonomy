# Self-employed SEP, SIMPLE and Qualified Plans
## Description
Contributions a self-employed person makes for himself or herself to a SEP, SIMPLE, or qualified plan (solo 401(k)) are deductible to the same extent as federally. Contributions not deductible federally are not deductible in Mississippi.
## Amounts for 2026 (federal)
- 401(k) elective deferral: $24,500
- SIMPLE deferral: $17,000 ($18,100 for certain applicable plans)
- SEP and total defined contribution limit: $72,000, generally up to 25% of compensation (about 20% of net self-employment earnings)
## Notes
- Employee deferrals through an employer's plan are already excluded from W-2 box 16 state wages; do not deduct them again here
## Required Information
- Plan type and contribution amounts
- Net self-employment earnings
## Questions
- Are you self-employed with a SEP, SIMPLE, or solo 401(k)?
## Common Errors
- Deducting employee deferrals already excluded from wages
- Deducting more than the federal deduction
## Prompt
- I put money into my SEP IRA. Is it deductible in Mississippi?
