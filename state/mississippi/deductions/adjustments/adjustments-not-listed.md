# Adjustments Not Listed on Lines 50 to 64
## Description
Any subtraction from income that does not have its own line (lines 50–64) goes on Schedule N as a NEGATIVE amount with a written description. The system does not read amounts from attached statements.
## Common examples
- Net operating loss carryforward (see net-operating-loss.md)
- Foreign earned income exclusion (see foreign-earned-income.md)
- Living organ donation expenses (see organ-donor-deduction.md)
- Ownership interest gain exclusion (see ownership-interest-gain-exclusion.md)
- Other federal-state differences
## What does NOT go here
- Student loan interest and the above-the-line educator expense deduction — no Mississippi provision (see student-loan-interest-educator.md)
- Federal deductions taken after AGI, such as the 2025–2028 tips, overtime, car loan interest, and senior deductions (Schedule 1-A) — Mississippi does not allow them
## Required Information
- A description and amount for each adjustment
## Questions
- Do you have any deduction from income not on lines 50 to 64?
## Common Errors
- Entering a subtraction as a positive amount
- Putting the amount only in an attached statement
- Carrying federal Schedule 1-A deductions to Mississippi
