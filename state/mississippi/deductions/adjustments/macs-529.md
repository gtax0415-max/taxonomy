# Mississippi Affordable College Savings (MACS)
## Description
Contributions to a Mississippi MACS 529 college savings account are deductible. There is no federal deduction for 529 contributions.
## Amounts for 2026 (statutory, unchanged)
- Up to $10,000 per year for single and other filers
- Up to $20,000 per year for married filing jointly
- Contributions must be made by the return due date (not extensions)
- Only contributions to the Mississippi MACS plan qualify
## Withdrawals
- Withdrawals for unqualified expenses are added back to income on Schedule N
- The One Big Beautiful Bill Act expanded federal qualified uses (K-12 limit to $20,000 from 2026, credential programs). Mississippi's treatment of these new uses is not stated in the instructions; confirm before treating them as qualified
## Required Information
- 2026 contribution amounts and dates
- Forms 1099-Q and how withdrawals were spent
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Mississippi MACS account in 2026?
- Did you take any withdrawals? For what?
## Common Errors
- Deducting contributions to another state's 529 plan
- Exceeding $10,000 / $20,000
- Ignoring non-qualified withdrawals

- Can I deduct my 529 contribution in Mississippi?
