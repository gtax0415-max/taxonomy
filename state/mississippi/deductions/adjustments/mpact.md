# Mississippi Prepaid Affordable College Tuition (MPACT)
## Description
Payments made during the year to the Mississippi Treasury Department under an MPACT prepaid tuition contract for a student beneficiary are deductible. Mississippi has no federal counterpart.
## Rules
- Deduct the contract costs actually paid in 2026
- The instructions state no dollar cap for MPACT (unlike MACS)
- Only Mississippi's MPACT program qualifies; other states' prepaid plans do not
- Payments must be made by the due date of the return (not extensions)
## Notes
- MPACT has been closed to new enrollment, so this mainly applies to existing contracts still being paid (confirm program status)
- Withdrawals for unqualified expenses are added back on Schedule N
## Required Information
- MPACT payment statement for 2026
## Questions
- Are you still making payments on an MPACT contract?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a prepaid plan from another state
- Deducting the same dollars as MACS contributions
## Prompt
- I pay on an MPACT contract for my daughter.
