# National Guard or Reserve Pay
## Description
Mississippi excludes up to $15,000 a year of National Guard or Reserve pay for each taxpayer. The pay is first included in wages, then subtracted here.
## Amounts for 2026 (statutory, unchanged)
- The lesser of the Guard or Reserve pay or $15,000, for EACH taxpayer
- A married couple who both serve can exclude up to $30,000, each in that spouse's column
## Notes
- Applies to Guard and Reserve compensation (drills, annual training)
- Full-time active-duty pay of regular forces does not qualify here
## Example (2026)
- Reservist earns $9,000 in drill pay and $70,000 civilian wages → $9,000 subtracted
## Required Information
- Guard or Reserve pay for each spouse
## Questions
- Are you or your spouse in the National Guard or Reserves?
- How much Guard or Reserve pay did each of you receive?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting more than $15,000 per person
- Excluding pay that was never included in wages
- Putting both spouses' amounts in one column
## Prompt
- I'm in the Mississippi National Guard. Is my drill pay taxable?
