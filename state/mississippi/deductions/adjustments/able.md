# Mississippi ABLE Account
## Description
Deduction from gross income for deposits into a Mississippi ABLE (Achieving a Better Life Experience) savings account for a person with a disability. There is no federal deduction for ABLE contributions; this is a Mississippi benefit.
## Amounts for 2026
- Annual contribution limit per beneficiary (all contributors combined): $20,000 for 2026, per the Mississippi ABLE program. This is now separate from the $19,000 federal gift tax exclusion
- A working beneficiary may contribute more under ABLE-to-work, up to the lesser of compensation or the federal poverty level
- Mississippi's instructions say to enter the amount deposited; no separate state dollar cap is stated. Confirm against the 2026 instructions
## 2026 eligibility change
- Starting January 1, 2026, a person whose disability began BEFORE AGE 46 (up from 26) can have an ABLE account
## Notes
- Withdrawals not used for qualified disability expenses are added back to income in the year withdrawn
- Rollovers from a 529 (MACS) account are not new contributions
## Required Information
- Contributions made in 2026
- Beneficiary's disability onset age
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Mississippi ABLE account in 2026?
- Did the disability begin before age 46?
- Were any withdrawals used for non-disability expenses?
## Common Errors
- Using the $19,000 gift exclusion instead of the $20,000 ABLE limit
- Deducting a 529-to-ABLE rollover as a new contribution
- Missing the age 46 expansion for 2026
## Prompt
- I put money in my son's ABLE account. Is it deductible in Mississippi?
