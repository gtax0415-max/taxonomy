# Alimony Paid
## Description
Alimony and separate maintenance paid is deductible to the same extent as federally: only under agreements executed on or before December 31, 2018 and not later modified to adopt the new rule.
## Rules
- Enter the recipient's name, SSN, state of residence, and date of divorce
- More than one recipient: attach a schedule and enter the total
- Child support and property settlements are not deductible
## Required Information
- Agreement and modification dates
- Amount paid in 2026
- Recipient details
## Questions
- When was your divorce or separation agreement signed or last modified?
- How much did you pay in alimony, separate from child support?
## Common Errors
- Deducting alimony under a 2019 or later agreement
- Leaving out the recipient's SSN
- Including child support
## Prompt
- Can I deduct alimony I pay on my Mississippi return?
