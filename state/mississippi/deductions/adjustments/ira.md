# Payments to an IRA
## Description
Traditional IRA contributions are deductible in Mississippi to the same extent as federally. Use the federal IRA deduction worksheet.
## Amounts for 2026 (federal)
- Contribution limit: $7,500 (up from $7,000), plus $1,100 catch-up at age 50 or older
- Deduction phase-out if covered by a workplace plan: single $81,000–$91,000 MAGI; married filing jointly $129,000–$149,000
- Phase-out if not covered but spouse is: $242,000–$252,000
- Roth IRA contributions are never deductible
## Mississippi notes
- On a combined return, each spouse's deduction goes in that spouse's column
- IRA distributions are generally exempt in Mississippi (see retirement-income-exclusion.md), so the deduction now and the exemption later both apply
## Required Information
- Form 5498 or contribution records
- Whether either spouse is covered by a workplace plan
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a traditional IRA for 2026?
- Are you or your spouse covered by a retirement plan at work?
## Common Errors
- Deducting more than the federal deduction
- Deducting Roth contributions
- Using the 2025 limit of $7,000
