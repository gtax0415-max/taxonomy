# Self-employed Health Insurance
## Description
Enter the self-employed health insurance deduction exactly as claimed on the federal return.
## Rules (federal)
- Premiums for medical, dental, and qualified long-term care insurance for the self-employed person, spouse, and dependents
- Limited to net self-employment profit from the business that established the plan
- Not allowed for any month the person was eligible for an employer-subsidized plan (including through a spouse)
- More-than-2% S corporation shareholders qualify if premiums are included in W-2 wages
## Notes
- Premiums deducted here cannot be counted again as medical expenses on Schedule A
## Required Information
- Federal Form 7206 or worksheet
## Questions
- Are you self-employed and paying your own health insurance?
- Were you eligible for a plan through an employer or spouse?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the same premiums here and on Schedule A
- Claiming months of employer-plan eligibility
## Prompt
- I'm self-employed and pay my own health insurance.
