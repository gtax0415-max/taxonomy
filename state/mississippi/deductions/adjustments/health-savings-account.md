# Health Savings Account
## Description
Mississippi deducts the amount deposited into a health savings account plus accrued interest. Distributions not used for qualified medical expenses or health coverage are added back to income.
## Amounts for 2026 (federal limits)
- Self-only coverage: $4,400
- Family coverage: $8,750
- Catch-up at age 55 or older: $1,000
## 2026 federal changes (One Big Beautiful Bill Act)
- Bronze and catastrophic marketplace plans are treated as HSA-eligible
- Direct primary care arrangements no longer block HSA eligibility
## Notes
- Employer and payroll (cafeteria plan) contributions, W-2 code W, are already left out of state wages; do NOT deduct them again
- Mississippi's wording ("deposited plus accrued interest") differs from the federal deduction; HSA interest is also included. Confirm the exact treatment against the 2026 instructions
## Required Information
- Personal contributions (Form 5498-SA less W-2 code W)
- Interest earned
- Any non-qualified distributions
## Questions
- Did you put money into an HSA yourself, outside payroll?
- Did you use any HSA money for non-medical expenses?
## Common Errors
- Deducting payroll contributions already excluded from wages
- Exceeding the 2026 federal limits
- Not adding back non-qualified distributions
## Prompt
- Is my HSA contribution deductible in Mississippi?
