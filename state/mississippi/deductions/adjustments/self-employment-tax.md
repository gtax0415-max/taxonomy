# Self-employment Tax Deduction
## Description
Deduction equal to 50% of the federal self-employment tax, the same as the federal deduction. Attach federal Schedule SE.
## Amounts for 2026 (federal)
- Self-employment tax: 15.3% on 92.35% of net earnings (12.4% Social Security up to the $184,500 wage base, 2.9% Medicare with no cap)
- The additional 0.9% Medicare tax is not part of the deduction
## Notes
- Each spouse's deduction goes in the column of the spouse with the business
## Example (2026)
- Net Schedule C profit $60,000 → SE tax about $8,478 → deduction about $4,239
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule SE for each self-employed spouse
## Questions
- Did you pay self-employment tax in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the full SE tax instead of half
- Including the additional 0.9% Medicare tax
- Putting the deduction in the wrong spouse's column
