# Moving Expense
## Description
Moving expenses are deductible in Mississippi only to the extent allowable federally. Attach federal Form 3903.
## Who qualifies for 2026
- Active-duty members of the U.S. Armed Forces moving under a permanent change of station order
- NEW for 2026: certain members of the intelligence community moving under a change of station, added by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act
- The One Big Beautiful Bill Act made the suspension permanent for everyone else; civilians cannot deduct moving expenses
## Notes
- Only unreimbursed costs of moving household goods and travel (no meals)
- Reimbursements excluded from wages (W-2 code P) cannot be deducted again
## Required Information
- Form 3903
- PCS or transfer orders
## Questions
- Are you active-duty military or intelligence community moving under orders?
- Were any costs reimbursed?
## Common Errors
- Civilians claiming moving expenses
- Deducting reimbursed costs
## Prompt
- I'm in the Air Force and moved to Keesler this year.
