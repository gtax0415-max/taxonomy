# Standard Deduction
## Description
Flat allowance taken in place of Mississippi itemized deductions. The amounts are set by Mississippi statute (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-17(2)) and are NOT indexed for inflation or tied to the federal standard deduction.
## Amounts
- 2026 amounts are the same as 2025 (no indexing):
  - Single: $2,300
  - Head of family: $3,400
  - Married filing joint or combined return: $4,600
  - Married, spouse died in 2026: $4,600
  - Married filing separate returns: $2,300 EACH
- NO extra standard deduction for age 65 or blindness. Mississippi gives those as $1,500 additional EXEMPTIONS instead
## Notes
- Choose the larger of the standard deduction or Mississippi itemized deductions. The federal choice does NOT bind Mississippi: a taxpayer who took the federal standard deduction may still itemize for Mississippi using federal Schedule A as a worksheet
- Combined return: the $4,600 may be split between Column A and Column B in any manner (see deduction-division.md)
- Separate returns: each spouse claims $2,300; any unused part on one return cannot be moved to the other. If one spouse itemizes, the other must also itemize
- Nonresidents and part-year residents prorate the deduction by the ratio of Mississippi income to total income (Form 80-205, Line 13c, capped at 100%)
## Required Information
- Filing status on the last day of 2026
- For married filers, whether filing joint, combined, or separate
- Whether the spouse died during 2026
- For nonresidents and part-year residents, Mississippi income and total income
## Questions
- What is your filing status for 2026?
- If married, will you file one combined return or two separate returns?
- Will your spouse itemize on a separate return?
- Are your itemized deductions (after removing state income tax and Mississippi gaming losses) larger than your standard deduction?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal standard deduction amount
- Claiming an extra standard deduction for age 65 or blindness
- One spouse taking the standard deduction while the other itemizes on separate returns
- Assuming the federal standard-deduction choice must carry over to Mississippi
- Nonresidents forgetting to prorate
## Prompt
- What is the Mississippi standard deduction for 2026?
- I took the federal standard deduction. Can I itemize on my Mississippi return?
- My wife and I both work. How do we split the standard deduction?
