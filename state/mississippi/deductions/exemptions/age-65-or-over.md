# Age 65 or Over
## Description
Additional $1,500 exemption for the taxpayer and for the spouse who are 65 or older by the end of the year.
## Amounts for 2026 (same as 2025)
- $1,500 for the taxpayer, $1,500 for the spouse (statutory, not indexed)
- A person is treated as 65 on the day BEFORE the 65th birthday, so anyone born on or before January 1, 1962 qualifies for 2026
- Not available for dependents
- Separate from the federal 2025–2028 senior deduction ($6,000 per person), which does NOT carry to Mississippi
## Notes
- Status is judged on the last day of the year, except for a spouse who died during the year
- Mississippi's age benefit is an EXEMPTION, not an extra standard deduction as on the federal return
- Retirement income and Social Security are fully exempt in Mississippi; that is separate from this exemption
## Required Information
- Dates of birth of taxpayer and spouse
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse born on or before January 1, 1962?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a dependent parent
- Missing the person born on January 1, 1962
- Adding an extra standard deduction for age, as on the federal return
- Assuming the federal senior deduction applies in Mississippi
## Prompt
- I turned 65 this year. Do I get anything extra in Mississippi?
