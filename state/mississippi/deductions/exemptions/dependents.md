# Dependents
## Description
Additional $1,500 exemption for each person who qualifies as the taxpayer's dependent for FEDERAL income tax purposes. Mississippi follows the federal dependent definition but keeps its own dollar exemption.
## Amounts for 2026 (same as 2025)
- $1,500 per dependent (statutory, not indexed)
- No age limit and no income phase-out
- A dependency exemption is never allowed for the taxpayer or spouse
## Notes
- Each dependent must be listed by name, relationship, and SSN on line 6
- More than four dependents go on Form 80-491
- Head of family filers count the required dependent here as well (that is how they reach $9,500)
- The federal child tax credit has no Mississippi counterpart; this exemption is the Mississippi benefit
## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN, and relationship
- Confirmation that each person is a dependent on the federal return
## Questions
- Whom did you claim as a dependent on your federal return?
- Does each dependent have an SSN?
- Do you have more than four dependents?
## Common Errors
- Claiming someone who is not a federal dependent
- Claiming yourself or your spouse as a dependent
- Leaving out the dependent's SSN or relationship
- Forgetting Form 80-491 for more than four dependents
## Prompt
- How much is the Mississippi exemption for my children?
- Can I claim my mother as a dependent in Mississippi?
