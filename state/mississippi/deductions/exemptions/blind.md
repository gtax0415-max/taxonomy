# Blind
## Description
Additional $1,500 exemption for the taxpayer and for the spouse who are blind at the end of the year.
## Amounts for 2026 (same as 2025)
- $1,500 for the taxpayer, $1,500 for the spouse (statutory, not indexed)
- Can be combined with the age 65 exemption (up to $3,000 per person)
- Not available for dependents
## Notes
- Mississippi uses the federal definition of blindness: corrected vision no better than 20/200 in the better eye, or field of vision 20 degrees or less
- Status is judged on the last day of the year
## Required Information
- A statement from an eye doctor, kept with records
## Questions
- Are you or your spouse legally blind?
- Do you have a statement from your eye doctor?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a dependent
- Forgetting to also claim age 65 when both apply
## Prompt
- I'm legally blind. Is there a Mississippi exemption?
