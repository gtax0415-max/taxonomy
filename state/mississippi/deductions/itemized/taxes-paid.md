# Taxes Paid Less State Income Taxes
## Description
State and local taxes allowed on federal Schedule A, AFTER the federal SALT cap, minus state income taxes (or general sales taxes elected in lieu) and the 3% tax withheld on Mississippi gaming winnings.
## Amounts
- Federal SALT cap for 2026: $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), up from $40,000 / $20,000 for 2025
- Phase-down for 2026: the cap is reduced by 30% of modified AGI over $505,000 ($252,500 MFS), but never below $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)
- The cap rises 1% a year through 2029 and returns to $10,000 in 2030
- Mississippi line 3a is the CAPPED federal amount; line 3b removes state income taxes
## Notes
- Mississippi's 2025 instructions explicitly adopt the One Big Beautiful Bill Act cap ($40,000 / $20,000). The 2026 amount follows the federal inflation step to $40,400
- What remains deductible for Mississippi is mainly real estate taxes and personal property taxes (such as vehicle ad valorem taxes)
- Federal income tax and Social Security tax are never deductible
- Open item: when the cap binds, the instructions do not say whether line 3b removes the full state income tax paid or only the portion that survived the cap. Confirm against the 2026 instructions
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A, line 7 (taxes after the cap)
- State income tax withheld and paid (or sales tax claimed instead)
- Real estate and personal property tax receipts
- Modified AGI, if above $505,000
## Questions
- Did you deduct state income tax or general sales tax on federal Schedule A?
- How much real estate and vehicle tax did you pay in 2026?
- Is your modified AGI above $505,000?
- Did a Mississippi casino withhold tax from your winnings?
## Common Errors
- Leaving state income tax in the Mississippi deduction
- Using the old $10,000 cap for 2026
- Using 2025's $40,000 cap instead of $40,400
- Deducting Mississippi gaming withholding as a tax paid
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my property taxes in Mississippi?
- Does Mississippi follow the new federal SALT cap ($40,400 for 2026)?
