# Medical and Dental Expenses
## Description
Unreimbursed medical and dental expenses deducted to the extent they exceed 7.5% of FEDERAL AGI, computed exactly as on federal Schedule A.
## Amounts
- Floor for 2026: 7.5% of federal AGI (unchanged; made permanent before 2026)
- The floor uses FEDERAL AGI, not Mississippi AGI
- If the floor exceeds expenses, enter zero on line 2c
## Notes
- Qualifying expenses follow federal rules: doctors, dentists, hospitals, prescriptions, insurance premiums paid with after-tax dollars, long-term care premiums (subject to federal age limits), and medical mileage
- Self-employed health insurance already deducted as an adjustment cannot be counted again
- Expenses paid from an HSA or reimbursed by insurance do not qualify
- Mississippi also has a separate 25% long-term care insurance CREDIT (up to $500 per policy); premiums used for that credit should be reviewed so the same premiums are not over-claimed
## Required Information
- Total unreimbursed medical and dental expenses
- Federal AGI
- Health insurance and long-term care premium statements
- Any reimbursements or HSA distributions
## Questions
- What medical and dental expenses did you pay in 2026 that were not reimbursed?
- Did you pay health insurance premiums with after-tax money?
- Were any expenses paid from an HSA?
- What is your federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Using Mississippi AGI instead of federal AGI for the 7.5% floor
- Including HSA-paid or reimbursed expenses
- Double-counting self-employed health insurance
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my medical bills on my Mississippi return?
- I had a large hospital bill this year.
