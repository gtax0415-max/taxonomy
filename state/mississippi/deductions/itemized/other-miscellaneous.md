# Other Miscellaneous Deductions
## Description
Federal "other itemized deductions" not subject to the 2% floor, minus any losses from Mississippi gaming establishments.
## Why Mississippi gaming losses come out
- Mississippi casino winnings are taxed by a 3% nonrefundable withholding at payout and are EXCLUDED from Mississippi income (Miss. Code Ann. §27-7-901), so the related losses are not deductible
- Losses from gambling outside Mississippi stay deductible, up to non-Mississippi winnings reported on Schedule N
## 2026 changes (One Big Beautiful Bill Act)
- Gambling losses limited to 90% of losses, still capped at winnings
- NEW: qualifying educator expenses not deducted on Schedule 1 can be itemized on federal Schedule A, line 17k (not subject to the 2% floor; also covers coaches and athletic administrators); they flow to this line
- Miscellaneous deductions subject to the 2% floor are permanently eliminated
## Other items on this line
- Impairment-related work expenses of a disabled person
- Estate tax on income in respect of a decedent
- Certain amortizable bond premium and unrecovered annuity investment
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A line 17 detail (17a–17k)
- Gambling winnings and losses split between Mississippi and non-Mississippi
- Educator expense receipts
## Questions
- Did you have gambling losses? Where?
- Are you a K-12 educator with unreimbursed classroom expenses?
## Common Errors
- Deducting Mississippi casino losses
- Ignoring the 90% gambling loss limit for 2026
- Claiming non-Mississippi gambling losses without reporting the winnings on Schedule N
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my casino losses in Mississippi?
- I'm a teacher. Where do my classroom expenses go?
