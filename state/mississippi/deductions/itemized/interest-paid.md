# Interest Paid
## Description
Home mortgage interest, points, and investment interest, deductible for Mississippi in the same amount as for federal purposes. Federal interest limits apply fully.
## Amounts
- Acquisition debt limit: $750,000 ($375,000 MFS) for loans after December 15, 2017; $1,000,000 for older loans. Made permanent by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act
- Home equity interest deductible only if the loan was used to buy, build, or substantially improve the home
- Investment interest limited to net investment income (Form 4952)
## 2026 change
- MORTGAGE INSURANCE PREMIUMS on acquisition debt are again treated as deductible mortgage interest starting in 2026 (subject to the federal income phase-out). Because Mississippi uses the federal Schedule A amount, they flow through
## Notes
- The federal car loan interest deduction (2025–2028) is NOT a Schedule A item and does not appear on this line
## Required Information
- Form 1098 for each mortgage, including box 5 mortgage insurance premiums
- Loan balances and origination dates
- Form 4952 if investment interest is claimed
## Questions
- Did you pay mortgage interest on your main or second home?
- Did you pay private mortgage insurance in 2026?
- Was any home equity loan used for home improvements?
- Did you pay interest on money borrowed to invest?
## Common Errors
- Omitting 2026 mortgage insurance premiums now that they are deductible again
- Including home equity interest used for personal spending
- Putting car loan interest on Schedule A
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my mortgage interest in Mississippi?
- Is PMI deductible for 2026?
