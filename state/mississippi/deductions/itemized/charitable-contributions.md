# Charitable Contributions
## Description
Gifts to religious, charitable, educational, scientific, or literary organizations, subject to the federal percentage limits. Mississippi uses the federal Schedule A amount.
## 2026 changes (One Big Beautiful Bill Act)
- NEW 0.5% floor: itemizers may deduct only contributions above 0.5% of AGI
- The 60% of AGI limit for cash gifts to public charities is made permanent
- The new federal non-itemizer charitable deduction ($1,000 single / $2,000 joint) is NOT a Schedule A item and does not carry to Mississippi
## Mississippi-only rule
- Gifts used for a Mississippi charitable CREDIT are claimed instead of the deduction, not in addition. This covers the qualifying charitable organization credit (up to $1,200 single / $2,400 joint), the foster care charitable credit (up to $1,500 / $3,000), and other credits "in lieu of" the deduction. Remove those gifts from this line
## Required Information
- Receipts and acknowledgments for each gift
- Form 8283 for large noncash gifts
- Federal AGI
- Any gifts claimed for a Mississippi charitable credit
## Questions
- Did you give to charity in 2026?
- Did you claim a Mississippi charitable or foster care credit for any of those gifts?
- Did you give property rather than cash?
## Common Errors
- Ignoring the new 0.5% of AGI floor
- Deducting a gift that was also used for a Mississippi charitable credit
- Carrying the federal non-itemizer charitable deduction onto the Mississippi Schedule A
## Prompt
- Can I deduct my church donations in Mississippi?
- I donated to a qualifying foster care charity. Credit or deduction?
