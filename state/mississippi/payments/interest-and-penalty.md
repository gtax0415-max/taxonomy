# Interest and Penalty
## Description
Interest on underpaid estimated tax, and interest and penalty for paying or filing late.
## Scope
- NOT COMPUTED: left off the return and billed by the Department (firm policy, 2026-10-06). Kept so the system can explain what the taxpayer may be billed
## Rates (statutory)
- Underestimated tax: interest at 1/2% per month on each late or short installment
- Late payment: interest 1/2% per month plus penalty 1/2% per month, penalty capped at 25%
- Late filing: 5% per month, capped at 25%, minimum $100
## Exceptions to underestimate interest
- Farmers or fishermen meeting the two-thirds test and special dates
- Filing requirement first met after a due date
## Required Information
- Dates and amounts of 2026 withholding and estimated payments
- Date the return will be filed and paid
## Questions
- Did you pay enough tax during 2026 through withholding or estimates?
- Will the return be filed and paid by April 15, 2027?
## Common Errors
- Assuming the extension stops interest on unpaid tax
## Prompt
- Will I owe a penalty if I pay late?
