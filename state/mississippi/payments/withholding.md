# Mississippi Income Tax Withheld
## Description
Mississippi income tax withheld from wages, pensions, unemployment, and other payments, listed on Form 80-107 and claimed as a payment.
## Rules
- Add the "MS income tax" amounts from every W-2 (box 17 where box 15 shows MS) and 1099
- Form 80-107 must be completed and attached, even if there is no Mississippi withholding; use additional sheets for more than four W-2s or 1099s
- Attach legible W-2s and any 1099s with Mississippi withholding; the credit may be disallowed without them
- Withholding for OTHER states is not claimed here; it may support the credit for tax paid to another state (Form 80-160)
## Not withholding
- The 3% tax withheld by Mississippi casinos (W-2G) is the final tax on those winnings and is NOT creditable; do not include W-2Gs
- Pass-through entity tax paid for the owner goes on line 26, not here
## Required Information
- All W-2s and 1099s, with state boxes
## Questions
- Did you have Mississippi tax withheld from wages, pensions, or unemployment?
- Did a casino withhold tax from your winnings?
## Common Errors
- Including Mississippi casino W-2G withholding
- Including another state's withholding
- Not attaching W-2s or Form 80-107
- Missing withholding on an excluded pension (still creditable)
## Prompt
- Where do I enter the Mississippi tax withheld from my paycheck?
