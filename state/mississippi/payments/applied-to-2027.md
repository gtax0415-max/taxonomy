# Overpayment Applied to 2027 Estimated Tax
## Description
All or part of the 2026 overpayment can be credited to 2027 estimated tax instead of refunded. The amount is not mailed back.
## Rules
- Cannot exceed the adjusted overpayment on line 31
- The credit counts as a 2027 estimated payment (2027 Form 80-105, line 25)
- The "Farmers or Fishermen" box on the return is checked when two-thirds of gross income is from farming or fishing, which changes the 2027 estimate rules
## Required Information
- Amount to apply
- Whether the farmer or fisherman exception applies
## Questions
- Do you want to apply any of your refund to next year's tax?
- Is at least two-thirds of your income from farming or fishing?
## Common Errors
- Applying more than the overpayment
- Forgetting to count the credit as a 2027 payment next year
## Prompt
- Can I apply my Mississippi refund to next year?
