# First-time Home Buyer Penalty
## Description
10% penalty on money withdrawn from a Mississippi first-time home buyer savings account for unqualified expenses. The withdrawal itself is also added to income (see first-time-home-buyer-unqualified.md).
## Rules
- Penalty = 10% of the Schedule N, line 2 amount
- Flows through Form 80-320 to line 30 (reducing a refund) or line 36 (adding to a balance due)
- No penalty for withdrawals due to death or disability, bankruptcy, or transfer to another first-time home buyer account
## Scope
- Shown as an amount to enter; the template does not add it to line 30 or 36 automatically. Check it is entered when Schedule N line 2 has an amount
## Example (2026)
- $3,000 unqualified withdrawal → $300 penalty
## Required Information
- Unqualified withdrawal amount from Schedule N, line 2
- Reason for the withdrawal (death, disability, bankruptcy, or transfer exceptions)
## Questions
- Did you withdraw from your first-time home buyer account for anything other than buying a Mississippi home?
## Common Errors
- Adding the withdrawal to income but forgetting the penalty
- Penalizing a transfer or a death or disability withdrawal
## Prompt
- Is there a penalty for using my home buyer savings for something else?
