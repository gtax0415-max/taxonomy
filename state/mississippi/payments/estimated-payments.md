# Estimated, Extension and Original Return Payments
## Description
All 2026 tax prepaid other than withholding: quarterly estimates, the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026, payments made with an extension, and (on an amended return) the amount paid with the original return.
## 2026 estimated tax rules
- Required if less than 80% of the 2026 liability is covered by withholding AND the liability is over $200
- Due dates: April 15, 2026; June 15, 2026; September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027
- Paid online through Taxpayer Access Point (TAP) or with Form 80-106 voucher
- Farmers and fishermen (two-thirds of gross income) have a single-payment exception
## Notes
- The 2025 overpayment credited to 2026 counts here, not as withholding
- An extension extends time to FILE, not to PAY
## Required Information
- Date and amount of each 2026 payment
- 2025 overpayment applied to 2026
- Extension payment, if any
## Questions
- Did you make estimated payments for 2026?
- Did you apply last year's refund to 2026?
- Did you pay with an extension?
## Common Errors
- Leaving out the 2025 overpayment credited
- Counting the January 2026 payment (for 2025) as a 2026 payment
- Omitting the original-return payment on an amended return
## Prompt
- I made quarterly payments to Mississippi this year.
