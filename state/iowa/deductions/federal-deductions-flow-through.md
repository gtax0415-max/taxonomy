# OBBBA Deductions That Flow Through Line 2
## Description
The 2025 federal law (OBBBA) created new below-the-line deductions. They reduce federal taxable income, and Iowa has NOT decoupled from them, so they reduce Iowa tax automatically through line 2.

## Deductions for 2026
- No tax on tips: up to $25,000 of qualified tips; phased out above $150,000 MAGI ($300,000 joint). 2025–2028
- No tax on overtime: up to $12,500 ($25,000 joint) of the overtime premium; same phase-out. 2025–2028
- Car loan interest: up to $10,000 on a new vehicle assembled in the U.S., loan after 2024; phased out above $100,000 MAGI ($200,000 joint). 2025–2028
- Enhanced senior deduction: $6,000 per person 65 or older; phased out above $75,000 MAGI ($150,000 joint). 2025–2028
- NEW FOR 2026: non-itemizer charitable deduction for cash gifts, up to $1,000 ($2,000 joint), on new Form 1040 line 12f. Permanent
- Amounts for tips, overtime, car loan interest, and seniors are fixed in the statute for 2025–2028 and are NOT inflation-adjusted, so the 2026 amounts are the same as 2025

## Iowa treatment
- None is added back on Schedule 1. They reduce Iowa taxable income just as federally
- EXCEPTION for the tests only: the senior deduction is entered on line 1f and added back for the low-income exemption, alternate tax, tax reduction, and health insurance tests

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A
- W-2 or employer statements for tips and overtime
- Loan interest statement and VIN
- Charitable receipts

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal tips, overtime, car loan interest, or senior deduction?
- Did you give cash to charity without itemizing?

## Common Errors
- Adding these deductions back on Schedule 1
- Forgetting line 1f for the senior deduction
- Assuming Iowa has its own separate tips or overtime exclusion on top of the federal one (it does not)

## Prompt
- Does Iowa tax my tips?
- I deducted overtime on my federal return. Does Iowa follow it?
