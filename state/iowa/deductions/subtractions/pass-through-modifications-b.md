# Partnership, S Corporation, or Fiduciary Subtractions
## Description
Subtraction for the owner's or beneficiary's share of Iowa modifications that DECREASE income, as computed by a partnership, S corporation, estate, or trust.

## Amount
- Enter your share of the entity's net Iowa SUBTRACTIONS as reported to you
- The entity reports this on its Iowa K-1 schedule (partnership Part II line 19(a), S corporation Part II line 17(a), fiduciary Part II line 9 on the 2025 forms). The Iowa K-1 is a state form, so for this taxonomy treat it as the delivery vehicle; the source is the entity's statement accompanying the federal K-1
- Typical pass-through subtractions: the entity's U.S. government interest, continuing Iowa depreciation on pre-2021 assets, and other nonconformity items

## Notes
- Additions from the same entity go on line 3, column A (see pass-through-modifications-a.md)
- Do not double count: if the entity already reported an item as an Iowa modification, do not also enter it on line 1, 2, 9, or 11
- 2026: OBBBA changes flowing through the federal K-1 generally create no Iowa modification, because Iowa follows the federal code automatically
- Line references follow the 2025 forms; confirm against the 2026 Iowa K-1 and Schedule 1

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule K-1 for each entity
- Entity's Iowa modification statement showing subtractions separately
- Entity name and FEIN

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a partnership, S corporation, estate, or trust?
- Did the entity report Iowa subtractions to you?

## Common Errors
- Entering a subtraction as a negative number in column A
- Double counting U.S. interest already subtracted by the entity
- Ignoring the Iowa statement and using only the federal K-1
