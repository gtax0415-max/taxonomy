# Railroad Unemployment Income
## Description
Subtraction for unemployment benefits paid by the Railroad Retirement Board under the Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act. Federal law bars states from taxing them.

## Amount
- The RRB unemployment benefits included in federal taxable income
- No cap; any age

## Notes
- Federal Schedule 1 line numbers cited here are from the 2025 form; the 2026 Schedule 1 was not available to verify, so confirm them when the final 2026 form is released
- Regular state unemployment benefits from Iowa Workforce Development remain taxable in Iowa
- Railroad retirement annuities are subtracted on line 5 (social security equivalent portion) and line 7 (other portions), not here
- Unchanged for 2026. Line number follows the 2025 Schedule 1

## Required Information
- RRB Form 1099-G

## Questions
- Did you receive unemployment benefits from the Railroad Retirement Board?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting state unemployment benefits
- Putting railroad retirement annuities on this line

## Prompt
- I received railroad unemployment benefits. Are they taxed in Iowa?
