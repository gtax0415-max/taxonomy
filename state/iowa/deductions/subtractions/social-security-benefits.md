# Social Security Benefits
## Description
Iowa does not tax Social Security. Because Iowa starts from federal taxable income, the TAXABLE portion reported federally is subtracted here.

## Amount
- Enter the taxable Social Security benefits from federal Form 1040 line 6b (not the gross benefits on line 6a)
- Includes the social security equivalent part of Tier 1 railroad retirement reported on Form RRB-1099
- Applies at any age; no income limit

## Notes
- The rest of railroad retirement (non-equivalent Tier 1 and Tier 2, on Form RRB-1099-R) is subtracted on line 7, not here
- Social Security disability (SSDI) and survivor benefits on Form SSA-1099 are treated the same way
- The new federal senior deduction (2025–2028) does not exempt Social Security; it is a separate deduction. Taxable benefits on line 6b still need this subtraction
- Social Security on line 6b is added back for the health insurance deduction income test
- Unchanged for 2026. Line number follows the 2025 Schedule 1

## Required Information
- Form SSA-1099 and Form RRB-1099
- Federal Form 1040 line 6b

## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security or railroad Tier 1 benefits in 2026?
- How much of your benefit was taxable on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the gross benefit (line 6a) instead of the taxable amount (line 6b)
- Subtracting Tier 2 railroad retirement here instead of line 7
- Forgetting the subtraction because benefits are thought to be exempt automatically

## Prompt
- Does Iowa tax my Social Security?
- My Social Security was partly taxable on my federal return.
