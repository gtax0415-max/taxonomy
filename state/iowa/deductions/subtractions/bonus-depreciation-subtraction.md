# Bonus Depreciation and Section 179 Subtraction (IA 4562A and IA 4562B)
## Description
Subtraction for the extra Iowa depreciation, or the smaller Iowa gain, on older assets for which Iowa did NOT allow federal bonus depreciation or the full Section 179 deduction when they were placed in service. For 2026 this is a LEGACY adjustment.

## 2026 rule
- Iowa has followed federal bonus depreciation for property placed in service in tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, and federal Section 179 since 2020. OBBBA's 100% bonus depreciation on 2026 purchases flows into Iowa with no adjustment
- A subtraction arises only for assets placed in service BEFORE 2021 (or pre-2020 Section 179 differences, including the special election):
  - Ongoing years: Iowa depreciation exceeds federal because less was deducted for Iowa up front → subtract the difference
  - Disposal: Iowa adjusted basis is higher, so Iowa gain is smaller → subtract the difference in gain
- No Iowa decoupling was enacted in 2026

## Notes
- Additions from the same schedules go on line 9, column A (see bonus-depreciation-addition.md)
- Pass-through entities compute their own adjustment and report it on line 3, not line 9
- Attach IA 4562A and IA 4562B
- Line number follows the 2025 Schedule 1

## Required Information
- Federal and Iowa depreciation schedules for pre-2021 assets
- Disposal details for any pre-2021 assets sold in 2026

## Questions
- Do you still depreciate business or rental assets bought before 2021?
- Did you keep a separate Iowa depreciation schedule?
- Did you sell any of those assets in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Losing the subtraction because the separate Iowa schedule was not kept
- Using federal basis to figure Iowa gain on a pre-2021 asset
- Creating an adjustment for 2026 bonus depreciation

## Prompt
- I bought a tractor in 2019 and Iowa didn't allow bonus depreciation then.
- I sold equipment I bought in 2018; is the Iowa gain different?
