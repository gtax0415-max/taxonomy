# Itemized Deductions Through Line 2
## Description
Iowa has had NO separate itemized deduction schedule since 2023. Federal itemized deductions reach Iowa only through federal taxable income, and Iowa follows the federal choice between standard and itemized.

## Rules for 2026
- Federal itemized deductions, INCLUDING the Iowa income tax deducted as state and local tax, are not added back
- The federal 2026 limits therefore apply for Iowa too:
  - SALT cap: $40,400 for 2026 ($20,200 married filing separately), reduced for MAGI over $505,000, but not below $10,000
  - Charitable: only the amount over 0.5% of AGI is deductible (new for 2026)
  - Overall limit for taxpayers in the 37% federal bracket (new for 2026)
- You cannot itemize for Iowa while taking the standard deduction federally, or the reverse
- The itemized amount is also entered on line 1d for the income tests, limited to line 2

## Interaction with the health insurance deduction
- Premiums included in federal Schedule A line 1 can be deducted in Iowa (Schedule 1 line 15) only for the part disallowed by the 7.5% floor, and only for eligible taxpayers 65 or older

## Notes
- Federal Schedule A line numbers cited here are from the 2025 form. The 2026 Schedule A is being redesigned (two pages, with the new 0.5% charitable floor), so confirm the line numbers on the final 2026 form

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A

## Questions
- Did you itemize on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you include health insurance premiums in your medical deduction?

## Common Errors
- Adding back the state income tax deduction (pre-2023 Iowa rule)
- Trying to itemize for Iowa while taking the federal standard deduction
- Double counting premiums on Schedule A and Iowa Schedule 1 line 15

## Prompt
- Can I itemize on Iowa if I took the standard deduction?
- Does Iowa have its own itemized deductions?
