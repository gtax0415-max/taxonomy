# Iowa Net Operating Loss Carried from Before 2023 (IA 124)
## Description
Subtraction for an Iowa net operating loss that arose in a tax year beginning before January 1, 2023 and is still being carried forward. It replaces the federal pre-2023 NOL that is added back on Schedule 1 line 10.

## Amount
- Enter the Iowa NOL carryforward used in 2026 from IA 124, Part II, line 5
- Carryforward period depends on the loss year: Iowa NOLs from tax years beginning after 2018 carry forward without the old 20-year limit and cannot be carried back; older Iowa NOLs keep the 20-year carryforward. (Confirm the period on the 2026 IA 124 instructions when released)
- Losses arising in 2023 and later are handled entirely through the federal NOL deduction already in federal taxable income; they never appear on this line

## Notes
- Paired with the line 10 addition of the pre-2023 FEDERAL NOL. Both lines are needed, and both are documented on IA 124
- The source of this figure is inherently the taxpayer's own prior Iowa returns, because Iowa computed NOLs separately before 2023. No federal form contains it. This is the one item in this set where a state-prepared record cannot be avoided; it is the taxpayer's own filed return, not a state instruction
- No 2026 Iowa law change. Line number follows the 2025 Schedule 1

## Required Information
- Each pre-2023 loss year and the Iowa NOL generated
- Amounts already used in prior years
- Remaining carryforward at the start of 2026

## Questions
- Did you have a business or farm loss before 2023 that you are still carrying forward?
- Do you have the Iowa returns for the loss years?
- How much of the Iowa loss was used in 2023 through 2025?

## Common Errors
- Entering the pre-2023 federal NOL instead of the Iowa NOL
- Claiming this subtraction without the matching line 10 addition
- Claiming a 2023-or-later loss here
