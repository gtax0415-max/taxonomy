# Retired Farmer Farm Tenancy Income Exclusion (IA 125)
## Description
Subtraction that lets an eligible retired farmer exclude ALL net income from a written farm tenancy agreement covering farmland they own. It is a one-time, irrevocable, lifetime election.

## Eligibility
- RETIRED FARMER: age 55 or older, or disabled, and no longer materially participating in a farming business
- Materially participated in a farming business for 10 or more years in the aggregate
- Held the leased real property for 10 or more years
- Is a party to a WRITTEN farm tenancy agreement as owner-lessor (cash rent, crop-share, flex, and livestock-share leases all qualify)

## Amount
- 100% of the net income from the qualifying lease, as reported on federal Schedule E or Form 4835
- No dollar cap

## Notes
- The election is irrevocable. Electing this exclusion bars the retired farmer capital gain election (IA 100G) and the beginning farmer tax credit in the current and later years, and vice versa
- Pass-through entities: rent earned through a partnership, S corporation, trust, or estate does NOT qualify. Bills to allow it (HF 624 / SF 19, 2025–2026 session) were not enacted. Land held in a single-member LLC disregarded for federal tax can qualify
- A surviving spouse may make the election for a deceased retired farmer within the rule's deadline
- This line was added to the form for 2025; before that it was line 19, code oo, which should no longer be used
- No 2026 Iowa law change. Line number follows the 2025 Schedule 1

## Required Information
- Copy of the written lease
- Net farm rental income for 2026
- Ownership history and years of material participation
- Whether an IA 125 election was made in an earlier year

## Questions
- Are you 55 or older (or disabled) and no longer actively farming?
- Did you farm for at least 10 years and own this land for at least 10 years?
- Is the lease in writing, and is the land owned directly or through an entity?
- Have you previously made a retired farmer capital gain election?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it for rent passed through a partnership, S corporation, or trust
- Claiming it with only an oral lease
- Not realizing the election permanently forfeits the capital gain election
- Still using line 19 code oo

## Prompt
- I retired from farming and cash-rent my land to a neighbor.
- Can I exclude my farm rent from Iowa tax?
