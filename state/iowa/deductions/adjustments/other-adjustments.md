# Other Adjustments (Line 19)
## Description
Catch-all subtraction line. Each item carries a letter code and needs an explanation. All amounts are entered as positive numbers.

## Common codes for 2026
- a. Accrual method: installment gain already fully taxed for Iowa in a prior year
- f. Claim of right: income repaid in 2026 that was taxed on a prior Iowa return (or take the credit instead, not both)
- h. Disability income exclusion: under 65, retired on permanent and total disability, not yet at mandatory retirement age on January 1, 2026. Requires IA 2440 and a doctor's statement every year
- j. First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account contributions: 2025 limit was $2,314 ($4,628 married filing jointly). The SF 2472 fiscal note gives a 2026 maximum of $4,744 (appears to be the joint figure; single would be $2,372 — confirm). No new accounts may be opened on or after July 1, 2026; new contributions go to FirstHome Iowa (see line19-code-fh.md)
- AL. Attorney Loan Repayment Program assistance, up to $10,000 a year for six years (new 2026; see line19-code-al.md)
- FH. FirstHome Iowa contributions, up to $5,500 per beneficiary from July 1, 2026 (new 2026; see line19-code-fh.md)
- p. / q. Contributions to, and grants from, the Injured Veterans Grant Program
- r. State Supplementary Assistance for in-home care of a family member
- v. Organ donor unreimbursed expenses, up to $10,000, once per lifetime
- x. Segal AmeriCorps education awards
- aa. Victim compensation awards, restitution, and civil damages received by a victim
- bb. Additional 65% wage deduction (up to $20,000 per employee) for hiring ex-offenders or persons with disabilities
- ee. Educator expenses: up to $200 more beyond the federal limit ($350 for 2026, was $300), per eligible educator; from 2026 do not include expenses itemized federally under OBBBA
- ii. Iowa ABLE contributions: limit tied to the Iowa 529 per-beneficiary limit, so $6,100 for 2026 ($5,800 for 2025)
- nn. Education savings account payments included in federal income
- pp. Employer payments of an employee's student loans included in federal income

## Notes
- Codes g (529) and oo (farm tenancy) moved to their own lines (14 and 18) starting 2025 and are reserved
- Code dd is only for nonresidents and part-year residents filing IA 126
- Codes and dollar amounts follow the 2025 instructions; confirm the 2026 code list and the homebuyer limit when released

## Required Information
- Documentation for each code claimed
- Iowa homebuyer account designation and annual report
- IA 2440 and physician statement for disability exclusion

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an Iowa ABLE or First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account?
- Are you a teacher with classroom expenses over $350?
- Did your employer pay part of your student loan?
- Did you retire early on permanent disability?

## Common Errors
- Using code g or oo instead of lines 14 and 18
- Using the 2025 ABLE or homebuyer limit for 2026
- Claiming both the claim-of-right deduction and the credit
- Deducting educator expenses already deducted federally

## Prompt
- I put money into an Iowa first-time homebuyer account.
- I'm a teacher and spent $600 on supplies.
