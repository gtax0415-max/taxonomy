# Line 19 Code hh: Rapid Response to State Disasters
## Description
Out-of-state businesses and individuals who come to Iowa to do disaster or emergency-related work are not subject to Iowa income tax or withholding on that work.

## Disaster response period
- Begins 10 days before the state-declared or presidentially declared disaster
- Ends 60 days after the declared disaster or emergency ends

## Scope for this taxonomy
- OUT OF SCOPE: applies to nonresidents and out-of-state businesses filing IA 126. The team's 2026 scope is full-year residents only (team decision 2026-09-30). Keep as a review row
- Related: nonresident electric utility emergency and training work is line 19 code gg

## Required Information
- Disaster declaration, dates, and pay for the work

## Questions
- Are you a nonresident who came to Iowa for disaster cleanup or repair work?

## Common Errors
- A full-year Iowa resident using this code
- Including pay earned outside the disaster response period

## Prompt
- I'm from Missouri and worked Iowa tornado cleanup.
