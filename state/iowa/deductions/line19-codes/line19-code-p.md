# Line 19 Code p: Contributions to the Injured Veterans Grant Program
## Description
Deduction for money paid to the Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs for the Injured Veterans Grant Program.

## Rules for 2026
- Deduct contributions to the extent they are included in federal taxable income, meaning they were NOT already deducted federally
- If the gift was itemized on federal Schedule A, or taken under the new 2026 federal non-itemizer charitable deduction, it is already out of federal taxable income and is not deducted again

## The program
- Grants of up to $10,000 to veterans injured in a combat zone after September 11, 2001

## Required Information
- Contribution receipt
- Whether the gift was deducted federally

## Questions
- Did you donate to the Iowa Injured Veterans Grant Program?
- Did you deduct the gift on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Double-deducting a gift already itemized federally

## Prompt
- I donated to the Iowa Injured Veterans Grant Program.
