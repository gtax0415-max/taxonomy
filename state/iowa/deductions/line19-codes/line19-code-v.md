# Line 19 Code v: Living Organ Donor Expenses
## Description
One-time deduction for a living person who donates all or part of a designated human organ, for unreimbursed expenses of the donation.

## Amount
- Unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages related to the donation
- Maximum $10,000
- Can be claimed only ONCE per lifetime, to the extent the amount is included in federal taxable income

## Rules for 2026
- The donor must be living at the time of donation
- Reimbursed expenses (by the recipient, insurance, or a donor program) do not qualify

## Required Information
- Expense receipts and lost wage records
- Confirmation of the donation
- Whether the deduction was claimed in any earlier year

## Questions
- Did you donate a kidney, part of a liver, or another organ in 2026?
- Were any of your expenses reimbursed?
- Have you claimed this deduction before?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a second time
- Including reimbursed expenses
- Exceeding $10,000

## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother this year.
