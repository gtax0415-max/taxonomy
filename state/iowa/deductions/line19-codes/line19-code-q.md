# Line 19 Code q: Grants from the Injured Veterans Program
## Description
Exclusion for Injured Veterans Grant Program money received by a veteran, to the extent it is in federal adjusted gross income.

## Rules for 2026
- Veterans injured in a combat zone after September 11, 2001 may receive grants of up to $10,000
- Subtract the grant only if it was included in federal income

## Required Information
- Grant award amount and date

## Questions
- Did you receive an Injured Veterans Grant from the Iowa Department of Veterans Affairs in 2026?
- Was it reported as income on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a grant that was not in federal income

## Prompt
- I received an Iowa injured veterans grant. Is it taxable?
