# Line 19 Code nn: Education Savings Account Payments
## Description
Exclusion for payments from Iowa's education savings account (ESA) program, the Students First ESA for nonpublic school expenses, to the extent included in federal taxable income.

## Rules for 2026
- Subtract the ESA payments only if they were included in federal taxable income
- If no federal Form 1099 was issued and nothing was reported federally, there is nothing to subtract

## Notes
- The ESA program is separate from Iowa 529 plans and from federal Coverdell education savings accounts. Coverdell distributions follow federal rules and are not entered here

## Required Information
- ESA statement and federal reporting

## Questions
- Does your child have a Students First education savings account?
- Did you report any ESA payments as income on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Confusing the Iowa ESA with a Coverdell ESA
- Subtracting amounts never included in federal income

## Prompt
- My son's private school is paid through an Iowa ESA. Is that taxable?
