# Line 19 Code ee: Educator Expenses
## Description
Iowa lets eligible educators deduct up to $200 more in out-of-pocket classroom expenses beyond the federal above-the-line limit.

## Amount
- Up to $200 per eligible educator of expenses ABOVE the federal above-the-line limit, which is $350 for 2026 (it was $300 for 2025), so Iowa allows up to $550 in total per educator
- Married filing jointly with two eligible educators: up to $200 each
- Do not include any expense already deducted in federal taxable income

## 2026 change to watch
- Starting in 2026, OBBBA lets eligible educators deduct unreimbursed expenses beyond $350 as a federal ITEMIZED deduction (not subject to the 2%-of-AGI floor). Expenses deducted that way are already in federal taxable income and must not be deducted again here. Code ee is now mainly for educators who take the standard deduction

## Notes
- Federal Schedule 1 line numbers cited here are from the 2025 form; the 2026 Schedule 1 was not available to verify, so confirm them when the final 2026 form is released
- Eligible educator follows the federal definition: K-12 teacher, instructor, counselor, principal, or aide working at least 900 hours in the school year
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Total unreimbursed expenses
- Amount deducted federally (above the line and itemized)

## Questions
- Are you a K-12 educator with more than $350 of classroom expenses?
- Did you itemize educator expenses on your 2026 federal return?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 federal limit of $300 instead of $350
- Deducting more than $200 per educator
- Deducting expenses already itemized federally under the new 2026 rule

## Prompt
- I'm a teacher and spent $600 on supplies.
