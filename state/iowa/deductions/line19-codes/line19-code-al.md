# Line 19 Code AL: Attorney Loan Repayment Program (2026 legislation)
## Description
New subtraction for loan repayment assistance an attorney receives under Iowa's new Attorney Loan Repayment Program, to the extent it is in federal income.

## The program
- Enacted in 2026 (appears as HF 2760 in the bill text and was carried in HF 2770, the justice system budget bill signed June 2, 2026), effective July 1, 2026; administered by the Iowa College Student Aid Commission
- Attorneys licensed in Iowa who agree to practice in Iowa and provide set hours of indigent defense; priority to rural practice
- Up to $10,000 a year for up to six consecutive years, capped at the eligible loan balance; no more than 25 attorneys a year

## Iowa income tax treatment
- Subtract assistance included in income, up to $10,000 per tax year
- Limited to six consecutive tax years in the taxpayer's lifetime
- No double benefit: income used to pay interest that was deducted federally as student loan interest (IRC 221) cannot also be subtracted

## Open items
- Whether any payments reach 2026: the program starts July 1, 2026 and awards depend on the Commission's application cycle. The first-year tax treatment should be confirmed on the 2026 instructions
- The official 2026 code letter has not been published; "AL" is the template's placeholder
- Federal note: employer student-loan payments have a separate federal exclusion; this state program payment is not employer-paid, so check whether it is in federal income at all

## Notes
- Federal Schedule 1 line numbers cited here are from the 2025 form; the 2026 Schedule 1 was not available to verify, so confirm them when the final 2026 form is released

## Required Information
- Program award and payment statements
- Student loan interest deducted federally

## Questions
- Are you an attorney participating in the Iowa Attorney Loan Repayment Program?
- How much assistance was paid in 2026?
- Did you deduct student loan interest on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting more than $10,000
- Claiming beyond six consecutive years
- Subtracting amounts also used for the federal student loan interest deduction

## Prompt
- I'm a rural attorney getting Iowa loan repayment help.
