# Line 19 Code j: First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account Contributions
## Description
Deduction for contributions to an Iowa First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account, a program closing to new accounts in 2026.

## Limit
- 2025: $2,314 per account holder ($4,628 married filing jointly), indexed
- 2026: the SF 2472 fiscal note states a 2026 maximum of $4,744, which appears to be the joint figure (single would be $2,372). Confirm on the 2026 instructions
- The limit is per account holder, not per beneficiary, even with several accounts

## 2026 change (SF 2472, signed May 18, 2026)
- No new First-Time Homebuyer Savings Accounts on or after July 1, 2026
- Existing accounts may transfer into FirstHome Iowa without penalty or Iowa tax
- New-program contributions from July 1, 2026 are deducted under FirstHome Iowa (see line19-code-fh.md), with a $5,500 per-beneficiary limit
- Whether contributions to an EXISTING account after July 1, 2026 remain deductible under code j has not been confirmed

## Other rules
- The account must have been designated with IDR by the April 30 filing deadline following the year it was opened, and an annual report filed with the return
- Interest and earnings are exempt from Iowa tax
- Every withdrawal must be reported within 90 days; non-qualifying withdrawals are added back on line 11 code h with a 10% penalty

## Required Information
- 2026 contributions by date
- Account designation and annual report
- Any transfer into FirstHome Iowa

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an Iowa First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account in 2026, and on what dates?
- Did you move the account into FirstHome Iowa?
- Did you also contribute to a FirstHome Iowa account?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 limit
- Applying the limit per beneficiary
- Deducting the same dollars under both code j and FirstHome Iowa

## Prompt
- I put money into my first-time homebuyer savings account in March.
