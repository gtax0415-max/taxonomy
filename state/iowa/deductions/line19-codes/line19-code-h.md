# Line 19 Code h: Disability Income Exclusion (IA 2440)
## Description
Exclusion for part of the disability pay received by someone under 65 who retired on permanent and total disability.

## Who qualifies (all must be true)
- Received disability pay in 2026
- Under 65 at the end of the tax year
- Retired on disability and was permanently and totally disabled at retirement
- On January 1, 2026, had not yet reached the employer's mandatory retirement age

## How it works
- Figure the exclusion on IA 2440 and attach it. IA 2440 is required to claim it
- A doctor's statement must accompany EACH year's return
- The IA 2440 calculation has its own dollar limit and income reduction; confirm the 2026 figures on the form when released

## Check line 7 first
- Since 2023, retirement benefits received because of disability, from IRAs, 401(k)s, pensions, IPERS and similar plans, are fully excluded on Schedule 1 line 7 (see retirement-income-exclusion.md). Most disability retirement pay is now better excluded there, with no cap
- Code h matters mainly for disability pay that is NOT from a qualifying retirement plan (for example certain employer-paid disability wages). Do not claim the same income on both lines

## Required Information
- Disability pay amounts and payer
- Date of disability retirement and mandatory retirement age
- Physician's statement

## Questions
- Did you retire early because of a permanent and total disability?
- Is your disability pay from a pension or retirement plan, or paid as wages?
- Do you have a current doctor's statement?

## Common Errors
- Claiming code h for income already excluded on line 7
- Missing the annual physician's statement
- Claiming it after turning 65

## Prompt
- I'm 52 and retired on disability. How does Iowa tax my disability pension?
