# Line 19 Code bb: Wages Paid to Certain Individuals (Ex-offenders and Persons with Disabilities)
## Description
Extra Iowa deduction for businesses that hire qualifying ex-offenders or persons with disabilities, on top of the federal wage deduction.

## Amount
- 65% of wages paid during the employee's first 12 months of employment
- Maximum $20,000 per qualifying employee
- The 12 months can span two tax years; claim each year's portion in that year

## Who qualifies
- Ex-offenders: on parole, probation, or in a work release program when hired. Any business type qualifies
- Persons with disabilities: limited to certain small businesses

## Notes
- The federal Work Opportunity Tax Credit can apply to the same employees; the federal wage deduction is reduced by that credit, but the Iowa 65% deduction is computed on wages paid
- Pass-through entities report this through the Iowa K-1 statement (line 3), not here
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Each qualifying employee's hire date, status, and wages in the first 12 months

## Questions
- Did your business hire someone on parole, probation, or work release, or a person with a disability?
- When were they hired, and what were their wages in the first 12 months?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the $20,000 per-employee cap
- Claiming wages after the first 12 months
- A non-qualifying business claiming the disability hiring deduction

## Prompt
- I hired someone on work release. Is there an Iowa tax break?
