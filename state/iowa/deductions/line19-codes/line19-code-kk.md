# Line 19 Code kk: Broadband Infrastructure Grant
## Description
Exclusion for a federal, state, or local grant received by a communications service provider and used to install broadband infrastructure in Iowa's targeted service areas.

## Rules for 2026
- Subtract the grant to the extent included on federal Schedule C line 1
- The infrastructure must deliver service at or above the required download and upload speeds in targeted service areas
- Providers organized as partnerships or S corporations report this through the entity's Iowa statement (Schedule 1 line 3)

## Required Information
- Grant amount and agreement
- Proof of qualifying installation

## Questions
- Is your business a communications service provider that received a broadband grant?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a grant used for something other than qualifying broadband infrastructure
- Subtracting an entity-level grant on the owner's line 19

## Prompt
- My internet business got a state broadband grant.
