# Line 19 Code s: Iowa Veterans Trust Fund Payments
## Description
Exclusion for specific payments from the Iowa Veterans Trust Fund.

## What qualifies
- Travel expenses directly related to follow-up medical care for wounded veterans and their spouses
- Unemployment assistance during unemployment caused by prolonged physical or mental illness or disability resulting from military service

## Rules for 2026
- Subtract only to the extent included in federal taxable income
- Other Trust Fund payments not listed above are not covered by this code

## Required Information
- Payment statements showing purpose and amount

## Questions
- Did you receive money from the Iowa Veterans Trust Fund in 2026, and what was it for?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting Trust Fund payments for purposes not listed

## Prompt
- I got travel reimbursement from the Iowa Veterans Trust Fund for VA appointments.
