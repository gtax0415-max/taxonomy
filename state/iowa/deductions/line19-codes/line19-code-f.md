# Line 19 Code f: Claim of Right Deduction
## Description
Subtraction for income repaid in 2026 that was reported and taxed on an earlier Iowa return because the taxpayer appeared to have a right to it (for example, repaid wages, benefits, or commissions).

## Rules for 2026
- Deduct the amount repaid in 2026, to the extent it was taxed by Iowa in a prior year
- Alternatively, take the Iowa claim-of-right credit on line 26. Use one or the other, not both; compare which gives the larger benefit
- If the repayment was already deducted on the federal return (itemized deduction or IRC 1341), check that the same amount is not deducted again in Iowa

## Notes
- Because Iowa's rate is a flat 3.8% and earlier Iowa rates were higher, the credit (which uses the prior-year tax) can be worth more than the deduction
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Amount repaid and date
- Year the income was originally taxed and the Iowa tax rate then

## Questions
- Did you pay back income in 2026 that you were taxed on in an earlier year?
- Was the repayment already deducted on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Taking both the deduction and the credit
- Double-deducting an amount already in federal taxable income

## Prompt
- I had to repay a bonus my employer paid me last year.
