# Line 19 Code x: Segal AmeriCorps Education Award Payments
## Description
Iowa excludes Segal AmeriCorps Education Award payments from Iowa taxable income, even though they are generally taxable federally.

## Rules for 2026
- Subtract the education award payments included in federal taxable income (paid to schools or lenders on the member's behalf)
- Interest accrual payments made by AmeriCorps on qualifying loans are treated the same way

## Notes
- The federal tax on the award may be offset by federal education credits; that does not change the Iowa subtraction

## Required Information
- Award payments made in 2026
- How they were reported federally

## Questions
- Did you use a Segal AmeriCorps Education Award in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the AmeriCorps living allowance (stipend), which remains taxable

## Prompt
- I used my AmeriCorps education award to pay off student loans.
