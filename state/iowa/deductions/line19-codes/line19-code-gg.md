# Line 19 Code gg: Nonresident Electric Utility Worker Emergency Response and Training
## Description
Excludes pay a NONRESIDENT earns for emergency response work for an electric utility in Iowa under a mutual aid agreement with the worker's home state, and pay for training by an electric utility in Iowa.

## Scope for this taxonomy
- OUT OF SCOPE: applies only to nonresidents (IA 126). The team's 2026 scope is full-year residents only. Keep as a review row
- Related: out-of-state businesses and workers doing state- or federally-declared disaster work in Iowa are not subject to Iowa tax for the disaster period (code hh)

## Required Information
- Dates, location, and pay for the work

## Questions
- Were you a nonresident who came to Iowa to restore power after a storm?

## Common Errors
- A full-year resident using this code

## Prompt
- I'm a lineworker from Minnesota who worked an Iowa storm outage.
