# Line 19 Code r: In-home Health-Related Care Payments
## Description
Exclusion for State Supplementary Assistance payments received for providing unskilled in-home health-related care to a family member.

## Rules for 2026
- Subtract the payments to the extent they are included in federal taxable income
- Many in-home care payments for a person living in the provider's home are already excluded federally as difficulty-of-care payments (IRS Notice 2014-7); only amounts in federal income are subtracted

## Required Information
- Payment statements and amounts
- Whether the payments were excluded federally

## Questions
- Are you paid by the state to care for a family member at home?
- Did you exclude those payments on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting payments already excluded federally
- Subtracting payments for care of a non-family member

## Prompt
- The state pays me to care for my mother at home.
