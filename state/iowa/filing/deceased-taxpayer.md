# Deceased Taxpayer or Spouse
## Description
A final Iowa return is filed for a person who died in 2026. The deceased box is checked and the date of death entered in the signature section.

## Who signs
- SURVIVING SPOUSE on a joint return: write "Filing as a surviving spouse" on the decedent's signature line, check the deceased box, and enter the date of death
- PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE (executor or administrator): signs with their title and attaches the court certificate of appointment
- Anyone else claiming a refund: attaches federal Form 1310. A representative certification must be on file with IDR to act for the decedent

## Rules for 2026
- The final return covers January 1 through the date of death, on the regular 2026 form, due April 30, 2027
- A surviving spouse may file married filing jointly for 2026 if not remarried in 2026
- Iowa follows the federal status, so qualifying surviving spouse is available only for 2027 and 2028 if federal rules are met
- Retirement income exclusions and the 65-or-older credit still apply on the final return

## Required Information
- Date of death
- Who is filing and in what capacity
- Death certificate and appointment papers or Form 1310

## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die in 2026?
- Are you the surviving spouse, the court-appointed representative, or another heir?
- Is a refund expected?

## Common Errors
- Not checking the deceased box or leaving out the date of death
- A non-spouse claiming a refund without Form 1310 or the court appointment
- Treating the final return as a fiscal or short year

## Prompt
- My husband passed away in 2026. How do I file our Iowa return?
- I'm the executor for my mother's final return.
