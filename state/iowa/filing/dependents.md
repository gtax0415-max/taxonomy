# Dependents
## Description
List each dependent claimed on the federal return. Each earns a $40 Iowa dependent exemption credit.

## Rules for 2026
- Iowa follows the federal definition of a dependent (qualifying child or qualifying relative, IRS Publication 501 and Publication 17)
- Enter first name, last name, SSN, and relationship for each dependent
- More than four dependents: check the box and attach a separate page listing the rest
- Credit: $40 per dependent on Step 3 line c; it is not indexed
- Married filing separately: list the same dependents claimed on your own federal return
- Divorced or separated parents: the parent who claims the child federally (including by Form 8332) claims the Iowa credit

## Notes
- The exemption credit total (Step 3 line d) goes to line 8 and is nonrefundable
- A dependent who files their own return still gets their own $40 personal credit
- Unchanged for 2026. Step references follow the 2025 IA 1040

## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, SSN, relationship, and date of birth

## Questions
- Whom did you claim as a dependent on your 2026 federal return?
- Do you have more than four dependents?
- Does the other parent claim any of your children?

## Common Errors
- Listing a dependent not claimed federally
- Both divorced parents claiming the same child
- Forgetting the attached page when there are more than four dependents

## Prompt
- I have three kids and support my mother. Who goes on my Iowa return?
- How much is the Iowa dependent credit?
