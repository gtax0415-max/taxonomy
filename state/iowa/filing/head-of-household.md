# Head of Household
## Description
Status 4 is available only if the taxpayer filed as head of household on the federal return. Iowa applies no separate test.

## Rules for 2026
- Personal credit count: 2 (2 × $40 = $80), the same as married filing jointly
- If the qualifying person is a child who lives with you but is NOT claimed as your dependent (for example, released to the other parent with Form 8332), enter the child's full legal name in Step 2
- Filing threshold: more than $13,500, or more than $32,000 if 65 or older
- Federal 2026 standard deduction for head of household ($24,150) flows into Iowa through federal taxable income

## Notes
- Iowa follows the federal definition of a qualifying person; IRS Publication 501 governs
- Dependents still go on the Step 3 grid and earn $40 each
- Unchanged for 2026. Step references follow the 2025 IA 1040

## Required Information
- Federal filing status
- Qualifying person's name and relationship
- Whether the qualifying child is claimed as your dependent

## Questions
- Did you file as head of household federally?
- Who is your qualifying person, and do you claim them as a dependent?
- Did you sign Form 8332 releasing the exemption to the other parent?

## Common Errors
- Entering only 1 personal credit instead of 2
- Forgetting the child's name when the child is not your dependent
- Using head of household on Iowa without using it federally

## Prompt
- I'm a single parent filing head of household.
- My ex claims our daughter but she lives with me. What do I enter?
