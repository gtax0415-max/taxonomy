# Qualifying Surviving Spouse
## Description
Status 5 follows the federal rules: for 2026 the spouse must have died in 2024 or 2025, the taxpayer has not remarried, and a dependent child lived in the home all year.

## Rules for 2026
- Personal credit count: 1 ($40) — NOT 2, even though federal treats this status like married filing jointly for the standard deduction
- Enter the dependent child in the Step 3 dependents grid ($40 dependent credit)
- If the qualifying child is not your dependent on this return, enter the child's full legal name in Step 2
- Filing threshold: more than $13,500, or more than $32,000 if 65 or older

## Notes
- In the year of death (2026) the survivor normally files married filing jointly, not this status — see deceased-taxpayer.md
- Unchanged for 2026. Step references follow the 2025 IA 1040

## Required Information
- Spouse's date of death
- Qualifying child's name, SSN, and relationship

## Questions
- When did your spouse die?
- Have you remarried?
- Did a dependent child live with you all of 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 2 personal credits instead of 1
- Using this status in the year of death
- Using it when the spouse died more than two years before

## Prompt
- My wife died in 2025 and I have a young son.
- What filing status do I use the year after my husband died?
