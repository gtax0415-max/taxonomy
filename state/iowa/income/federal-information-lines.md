# Federal Information Lines 1a to 1f
## Description
Information lines copied from the federal return. They do NOT change Iowa taxable income; line 2 already includes them. Iowa uses them to rebuild a broader income measure for four tests.

## Lines
- 1a Federal total income (Form 1040 line 9)
- 1b Federal adjustments to income (line 10)
- 1c Federal AGI (line 11a)
- 1d Standard or itemized deduction (line 12e), limited to the amount on line 2 for the income test
- 1e QBI deduction (line 13b on the 2026 Form 1040; it was line 13a on the 2025 form)
- 1f Enhanced deduction for seniors (the senior deduction line of Schedule 1-A; line 37 on the 2025 form — confirm the 2026 line). Iowa treats it as a personal exemption and adds it back for the tests

## The four tests these lines feed
- Low-income exemption / filing requirement: more than $9,000 single and married filing separately ($24,000 if 65 or older); more than $13,500 for other statuses ($32,000 if 65 or older)
- Alternate tax computation
- Tax reduction
- Health insurance deduction $100,000 test

## 2026 notes
- Senior deduction: $6,000 per person age 65 or older for 2025–2028, phased out above $75,000 MAGI ($150,000 joint). Adding it back on 1f can push some seniors over the Iowa thresholds
- Federal line numbers follow the 2026 draft Form 1040 (IRS e-file test scenarios, September 2026). Two changes from 2025: QBI moved to line 13b and Schedule 1-A deductions to line 13a (total from Schedule 1-A line 44); a new line 12f holds the non-itemizer charitable deduction. Iowa line labels follow the 2025 IA 1040; confirm both against the final 2026 forms

## Required Information
- 2026 federal Form 1040 and Schedule 1-A

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse 65 or older and claiming the federal senior deduction?
- Did you claim the QBI deduction?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting these amounts again in computing Iowa taxable income
- Leaving line 1f blank when the senior deduction was taken
- Using the full itemized deduction on 1d when it exceeds line 2 for the income test
