# Other Income Additions (Line 11, Column A)
## Description
Catch-all line for Iowa additions not reported on lines 1–10. Each item is reported with a letter code and an explanation. Most codes can be used only in column A; code bb works in either column and code aa is subtraction-only.

## Addition codes for 2026
- a. Babysitting income not already on federal Schedule C line 1
- d. Iowa 529 / Iowa Advisor 529 non-qualifying withdrawals or cancellations, to the extent previously deducted in Iowa. For 2026 the K-12 tuition limit for a qualifying withdrawal is $20,000 per beneficiary (it was $10,000 for 2025). K-12 tuition must generally be for an Iowa school. A rollover to a non-Iowa 529 plan is treated as non-qualifying. A 529-to-Roth IRA rollover that meets IRC 529(c)(3)(E) is NOT added back
- h. Iowa First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account non-qualifying withdrawals previously deducted, plus a 10% penalty unless an exception applies
- n. Net premium tax credit: the 2025 federal Schedule 3 line 9 credit is reported on the 2026 return to the extent it reimbursed premiums deducted in Iowa in a prior year
- o. Iowa ABLE plan cancellations or non-qualifying withdrawals previously deducted
- x. Other nonconformity adjustments (IA 101)
- y. Business interest expense adjustment (IA 163)
- bb. Other income not in federal taxable income but taxable for Iowa
- dd. Iowa tax credit adjustments: add back charitable contributions also used for the Endow Iowa, School Tuition Organization, Charitable Conservation, Farm-to-Food Donation, or Hoover Presidential Library credits

## Notes
- Code aa (premium pay up to $1,000) is a SUBTRACTION and goes in column B only
- 2026 change: the 529 K-12 limit doubles to $20,000, matching the federal OBBBA change
- Codes are from the 2025 instructions; confirm the 2026 code list when released

## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q and plan statements showing withdrawal purpose
- Prior-year Iowa 529 / ABLE / homebuyer deductions claimed
- 2025 federal Schedule 3 line 9 and prior-year Iowa premium deductions
- Contribution receipts used for Iowa credits

## Questions
- Did you take money out of an Iowa 529, ABLE, or First-Time Homebuyer account for a non-qualified purpose?
- Did you receive a net premium tax credit on your 2025 federal return?
- Did you claim Endow Iowa or School Tuition Organization credits?
- Did you earn babysitting income not reported on Schedule C?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $10,000 K-12 limit for 2026 withdrawals
- Adding back a 529-to-Roth rollover that qualifies
- Forgetting to add back contributions that also produced an Iowa credit
- Putting a subtraction code in column A

## Prompt
- I took a non-qualified withdrawal from my Iowa 529 plan.
- I donated to Endow Iowa and got a tax credit.
