# Bonus Depreciation and Section 179 Adjustment (IA 4562A and IA 4562B)
## Description
Adjustment for the remaining difference between federal and Iowa depreciation on assets for which Iowa did NOT follow federal bonus depreciation or Section 179 in earlier years. For 2026 this is a LEGACY adjustment: it exists only for older assets.

## 2026 rule
- Iowa uses rolling conformity and, since property placed in service in tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2021, follows federal bonus depreciation under IRC section 168(k). OBBBA's permanent 100% bonus depreciation therefore flows into Iowa with NO adjustment for 2026 acquisitions
- Iowa's Section 179 deduction has matched federal for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2020. A 2026 Section 179 deduction flows through unchanged
- A bill to decouple Iowa from federal bonus depreciation (SF 551) was introduced in the 2023–2024 session but was not enacted, and no decoupling was enacted in 2026
- An adjustment is still needed for assets placed in service BEFORE 2021 where Iowa disallowed bonus depreciation, and for pre-2020 Section 179 differences, including the "special election" assets
## How the adjustment works
- In the year of acquisition (pre-2021), Iowa allowed less depreciation than federal, so federal basis became lower than Iowa basis
- In later years, including 2026, Iowa depreciation on those assets is usually HIGHER than federal → a subtraction (column B)
- When such an asset is sold or disposed of, the gain is usually LOWER for Iowa → also a subtraction; if the remaining Iowa depreciation is less than federal, an addition (column A)
- Use column A for net additions and column B for net subtractions (see bonus-depreciation-subtraction.md); report both as positive numbers

## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 and the federal depreciation detail
- A separate Iowa depreciation schedule for pre-2021 assets
- Disposal details (date, sales price, federal and Iowa adjusted basis) for any pre-2021 assets sold in 2026
- IA 4562A and IA 4562B are completed from these records and attached to the return

## Questions
- Do you have business or rental assets placed in service before 2021 that are still being depreciated?
- Did you keep a separate Iowa depreciation schedule for those assets?
- Did you sell or dispose of any pre-2021 business assets in 2026?
- Did you make an Iowa Section 179 special election in 2018 or 2019?

## Common Errors
- Creating an Iowa addback for 2026 bonus depreciation, which Iowa now follows
- Forgetting the subtraction for the continuing Iowa depreciation on pre-2021 assets
- Using the federal basis to compute Iowa gain on the sale of a pre-2021 asset
- Netting the adjustment into one column with the wrong sign

## Prompt
- I took 100% bonus depreciation on equipment this year. Does Iowa follow it?
- I sold equipment I bought in 2018. Is my Iowa gain different?
