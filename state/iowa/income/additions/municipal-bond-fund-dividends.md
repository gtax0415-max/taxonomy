# Dividends from Funds Holding Municipal Bonds
## Description
Addition for exempt-interest dividends paid by mutual funds, investment trusts, or regulated investment companies that hold state and municipal bonds. They are excluded from federal taxable income but taxable in Iowa.

## Amount
- Add ALL exempt-interest dividends from funds investing in state and municipal bonds (Form 1099-DIV box 12)
- Unlike directly held bonds on line 1, the instructions do not carve out an Iowa-issuer portion for fund dividends, so the full box 12 amount is added
- No dollar cap and no phase-out

## Notes
- The related subtraction is Schedule 1 line 2, column B: the portion of a fund's dividends attributable to DIRECT federal securities (Treasury obligations). It is allowed only if the fund provides a statement showing that percentage; attach it to a paper return
- Income from repurchase agreements involving federal securities does not qualify for the subtraction
- Married filing separately: divide by registered ownership; jointly held shares are split equally
- Unchanged for 2026. Line number follows the 2025 IA 1040 Schedule 1; confirm against the 2026 form when released

## Required Information
- Form 1099-DIV box 12 and box 13
- Fund's year-end tax supplement (exempt income by state; U.S. government obligation percentage)

## Questions
- Do you own a municipal bond fund or tax-exempt money market fund?
- Did the fund report exempt-interest dividends in box 12?
- Does any fund you own report a percentage of income from U.S. Treasury obligations?

## Common Errors
- Treating the Iowa-sourced share of a fund as exempt (it is added back in full)
- Reporting fund dividends on line 1 (interest) instead of line 2
- Claiming the federal-obligation subtraction without the fund's percentage statement
- Applying the Treasury percentage to Ginnie Mae or agency mortgage income

## Prompt
- My mutual fund 1099-DIV shows exempt-interest dividends.
- Does Iowa tax my municipal bond fund dividends?
