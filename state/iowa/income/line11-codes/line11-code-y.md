# Line 11 Code y: Business Interest Expense Adjustment (IA 163)
## Description
Adjustment for differences between the federal business interest expense limitation (IRC 163(j)) and Iowa's treatment, figured on IA 163.

## 2026 rule
- Iowa decoupled from the federal 163(j) limitation for earlier years, so differences in disallowed interest and carryforwards can persist
- OBBBA restored the EBITDA-based calculation of adjusted taxable income for tax years beginning after 2024; because Iowa follows federal law automatically, the remaining adjustment for 2026 is mainly the run-off of carryforwards that differ from earlier years
- Column A if Iowa allows less interest than federal, column B if Iowa allows more

## Notes
- Small businesses under the federal gross receipts threshold are exempt from 163(j) and normally have no adjustment
- Attach IA 163
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions; confirm IA 163 for 2026

## Required Information
- Federal Form 8990
- Federal and Iowa disallowed interest carryforwards

## Questions
- Did your business file Form 8990 or have disallowed business interest?
- Do you have a business interest carryforward that differs for Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal carryforward amount for Iowa without checking differences
- Reporting a pass-through entity's adjustment here instead of on line 3

## Prompt
- My business interest was limited federally. Is it the same for Iowa?
