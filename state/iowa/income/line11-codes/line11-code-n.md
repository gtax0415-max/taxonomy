# Line 11 Code n: Net Premium Tax Credit
## Description
Addition that recaptures Iowa health insurance deductions when the federal net premium tax credit later reimburses those premiums. Iowa reports it one year later: the credit on the 2025 federal return goes on the 2026 Iowa return.

## Amount
- The 2025 federal net premium tax credit (Schedule 3 line 9) to the extent it reimbursed premiums deducted on an Iowa return in a prior year
- Column A only; attach an explanation

## Who is affected
- Since 2023 the Iowa health insurance deduction is limited to taxpayers 65 or older with income under $100,000, so this addition is now narrow: mainly older taxpayers on Marketplace coverage
- If no premiums were deducted in Iowa, nothing is added

## Notes
- The related excess advance credit REPAYMENT from the 2025 federal return may be included in the 2026 health insurance deduction (Schedule 1 line 15) for eligible taxpayers
- 2026: the enhanced federal premium tax credit expired December 31, 2025. The House passed a three-year extension (H.R. 1834) in January 2026, but no extension had been enacted as of the latest information found. The 2026 credit itself is reported on the 2027 Iowa return
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- 2025 Form 8962 and Schedule 3 line 9
- Iowa health insurance deduction claimed for the same premiums

## Questions
- Did you receive a net premium tax credit on your 2025 federal return?
- Did you deduct those premiums on an earlier Iowa return?

## Common Errors
- Reporting the 2026 credit instead of the 2025 credit
- Adding back a credit for premiums never deducted in Iowa

## Prompt
- I got a premium tax credit for Marketplace insurance. Does that affect Iowa?
