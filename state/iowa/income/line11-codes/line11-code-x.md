# Line 11 Code x: Other Nonconformity Adjustments (IA 101)
## Description
Adjustment for federal provisions Iowa does NOT follow that have no dedicated Schedule 1 line. The detail is figured on IA 101 and the net goes here: column A for additions, column B for subtractions.

## 2026 status
- Iowa uses rolling conformity, so most federal changes, including OBBBA, flow through automatically
- Items still needing an adjustment are mostly legacy: earlier federal disaster relief acts Iowa did not adopt (for example parts of the 2019 Taxpayer Certainty and Disaster Tax Relief Act), and continuing effects of pre-2023 differences
- No new individual nonconformity items were enacted for 2026 based on the 2026 session. Depreciation (line 9), NOLs (lines 10 and 17), and business interest (code y) have their own lines or codes
- Note: IDR's November 2025 guidance on net CFC tested income (IRC 951A) and SF 2492 (2026) affect corporations and pass-through owners with foreign income; individuals with direct 951A income should check whether an adjustment applies

## Notes
- Attach IA 101
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Each federal item Iowa treats differently and both amounts

## Questions
- Do you have any federal tax item from an earlier year that Iowa treated differently and that still affects 2026?
- Do you report foreign income from controlled foreign corporations?

## Common Errors
- Creating an adjustment for OBBBA items Iowa follows
- Reporting here an item that has its own line (depreciation, NOL, business interest)

## Prompt
- What is an Iowa nonconformity adjustment?
