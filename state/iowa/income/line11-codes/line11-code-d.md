# Line 11 Code d: Iowa 529 Non-qualifying Withdrawals
## Description
Addition that recaptures earlier Iowa 529 deductions when money comes out of ISave 529 or Iowa Advisor 529 for a non-qualifying purpose or the account is cancelled.

## Amount
- Add the non-qualifying withdrawal or cancellation amount, limited to the contributions previously DEDUCTED on Iowa returns
- Column A only; attach an explanation

## Qualifying withdrawals for 2026 (no add-back)
- Qualified higher education expenses (no annual cap)
- K-12 tuition: up to $20,000 per beneficiary per year for tax years beginning after December 31, 2025 (was $10,000). For Iowa, the school must generally be an accredited Iowa school, or an out-of-state school educating a child requiring special education
- Registered apprenticeship expenses
- Student loan payments up to $10,000 (lifetime) for the beneficiary or a sibling
- Rollover to a Roth IRA meeting IRC 529(c)(3)(E): no Iowa add-back since 2024
- A refund of higher-education expenses recontributed to the same Iowa 529 account within 60 days

## Non-qualifying (add back)
- Withdrawals for non-education purposes
- Rollover to another state's 529 plan (Iowa treats it as non-qualifying)
- K-12 tuition above $20,000, or at a non-Iowa school outside the special-education exception

## Notes
- The federal treatment can differ: a withdrawal can be federally qualified but non-qualifying for Iowa (for example out-of-state K-12 tuition)
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions; confirm on the 2026 instructions

## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q and use of funds
- Total Iowa deductions previously claimed for the account

## Questions
- Did you take money out of an Iowa 529 account in 2026, and what was it used for?
- Did you roll an Iowa 529 to another state's plan or to a Roth IRA?
- Was any K-12 tuition paid to a school outside Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $10,000 K-12 limit
- Adding back more than was previously deducted
- Adding back a qualifying 529-to-Roth rollover
- Missing the add-back on a rollover to another state's plan

## Prompt
- I used my Iowa 529 to pay private high school tuition.
- I moved my son's 529 to another state's plan.
