# Line 11 Code aa: Premium Pay Payments
## Description
Subtraction for up to $1,000 of premium pay or retention payments from specific Iowa programs. It can ONLY be a subtraction (column B). The earlier file said "column A or B"; that is corrected.

## Qualifying payments
- Premium pay to certified peace officers designated by the governor as eligible workers
- Premium pay to correctional officers and correctional facility medical staff designated as eligible workers
- Premium pay to teachers at accredited schools or employed by the state, designated as eligible workers
- Teacher retention payments funded from federal pandemic school relief funds
- Teacher retention payments at private or specially accredited schools funded through the state program
- Child care worker recruitment and retention bonuses from the state program

## Amount
- Up to $1,000, to the extent included in federal taxable income

## Notes
- These were pandemic-era programs; payments received in 2026 should be rare. Verify the payment came from one of the listed programs
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Amount and program for each payment

## Questions
- Did you receive premium pay or a retention bonus from an Iowa state program in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Entering it in column A
- Subtracting more than $1,000
- Subtracting an ordinary employer bonus

## Prompt
- I got a teacher retention payment. Is it taxed in Iowa?
