# Line 11 Code h: First-time Homebuyer Savings Account Non-qualifying Withdrawals
## Description
Addition that recaptures earlier Iowa deductions when money is withdrawn from an Iowa First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account for a non-qualifying purpose.

## Amount
- Add the non-qualifying withdrawal to the extent previously deducted, PLUS a penalty equal to 10% of the withdrawal
- No penalty if the withdrawal was because of the account holder's death or another exempt circumstance
- Column A only; attach an explanation

## 2026 change (SF 2472)
- SF 2472 (signed May 18, 2026) creates the new FirstHome Iowa program run by the Treasurer of State. No new First-Time Homebuyer Savings Accounts may be opened on or after July 1, 2026
- Existing balances may be moved into the FirstHome Iowa trust without penalty or Iowa tax if deposited within 30 days. Such a transfer is NOT a non-qualifying withdrawal; a withdrawal not redeposited within 30 days could be
- How non-qualifying withdrawals from new FirstHome Iowa accounts will be added back (code letter, penalty) has not yet been published

## Notes
- After any non-qualifying withdrawal, future contributions to any homebuyer account are not deductible
- Every withdrawal must be reported to IDR within 90 days on the homebuyer withdrawal form
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Required Information
- Withdrawal amounts and dates
- Use of funds
- Deductions previously claimed

## Questions
- Did you withdraw money from an Iowa First-Time Homebuyer Savings Account in 2026?
- Was it used to buy a first home in Iowa?
- Did you move the account into FirstHome Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the 10% penalty
- Treating a transfer into FirstHome Iowa as a non-qualifying withdrawal
- Continuing to deduct contributions after a non-qualifying withdrawal

## Prompt
- I took money out of my first-time homebuyer account but didn't buy a house.
- Can I move my old homebuyer account into FirstHome Iowa?
