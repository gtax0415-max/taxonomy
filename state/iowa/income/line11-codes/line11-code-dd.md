# Line 11 Code dd: Iowa Tax Credit Adjustments
## Description
Addition that prevents a double benefit: when a charitable gift earns an Iowa tax credit and was also deducted on the federal return, the deducted amount is added back.

## Credits that require the add-back
- Endow Iowa Tax Credit
- School Tuition Organization Tax Credit
- Charitable Conservation Contribution Tax Credit
- Farm-to-Food Donation Tax Credit
- Hoover Presidential Library Tax Credit

## Amount
- The contribution amount associated with the credit, to the extent it was deducted in federal taxable income
- Column A only; attach an explanation

## 2026 notes
- If the taxpayer took the federal standard deduction, the gift did not reduce federal taxable income. But the new federal non-itemizer charitable deduction (up to $1,000, or $2,000 joint, starting 2026) can include these gifts, so an add-back may now be needed even for non-itemizers
- The new federal 0.5%-of-AGI floor on itemized charitable gifts (2026) reduces the federally deducted amount; add back only what was actually deducted
- Code letters follow the 2025 instructions

## Notes
- Federal Schedule A line numbers cited here are from the 2025 form. The 2026 Schedule A is being redesigned (two pages, with the new 0.5% charitable floor), so confirm the line numbers on the final 2026 form

## Required Information
- Contributions that generated each credit
- How much of each was deducted federally

## Questions
- Did you claim an Endow Iowa, School Tuition Organization, or other Iowa credit for a donation?
- Did you deduct that donation federally, either itemized or under the new non-itemizer deduction?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the add-back when claiming the credit
- Adding back a gift that was never deducted federally
- Missing the add-back for gifts included in the non-itemizer deduction

## Prompt
- I gave to an Endow Iowa fund and got a tax credit.
- I donated to a School Tuition Organization.
