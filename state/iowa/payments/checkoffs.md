# Checkoff Contributions: Fish/Wildlife and Child Abuse Prevention
## Description
Voluntary donations made on the return. They reduce the refund or add to the balance due, and can be made whether the return shows a refund or an amount owed.

## Amounts
- Fish/Wildlife Fund (Chickadee Checkoff): any amount. Funds the Iowa DNR Wildlife Diversity Program
- Child Abuse Prevention: $1 or more
- No maximum on either checkoff

## 2026 change
- SF 2488 (2026 Iowa Acts, chapter 1164) redirects the child abuse prevention checkoff: amounts designated now go to the new Early Childhood and Family Services (ECFS) System Fund for child abuse prevention instead of the Child Abuse Prevention Program Fund. For the taxpayer the checkoff still works the same way ($1 or more); only the receiving fund and possibly the line label change on the 2026 form

## Notes
- A checkoff contribution is a charitable contribution in the year the return is FILED, not the tax year (a 2026 return filed in 2027 gives a 2027 deduction)
- An amended return cannot add, change, or remove the original contribution
- Line number follows the 2025 IA 1040; confirm on the 2026 form

## Required Information
- Amount for each checkoff

## Questions
- Would you like to donate part of your refund (or add to your payment) for Iowa wildlife or child abuse prevention?

## Common Errors
- Changing or adding a contribution on an amended return
- Deducting the checkoff as a charitable gift on the same year's return
- Entering less than $1 for child abuse prevention

## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my Iowa refund to wildlife programs?
- What are the Iowa tax return checkoffs?
