# Credit for 65 or Older or Blind
## Description
Additional nonrefundable $20 credit for each taxpayer who is 65 or older and each who is blind. A person who is both counts twice.

## Rules for 2026
- Age: 65 or older on or before January 1, 2027 (so a January 1, 2027 birthday counts for 2026, following the federal rule)
- Blind: blind on December 31, 2026
- Married filing jointly: also count the spouse who meets either test
- $20 each; not indexed

## Notes
- This is separate from the federal enhanced senior deduction ($6,000 per person, 2025–2028), which flows into Iowa through federal taxable income
- Age 65 also raises the Iowa filing threshold ($24,000 single, $32,000 other statuses) and opens the health insurance deduction
- Nonrefundable

## Required Information
- Dates of birth for taxpayer and spouse
- Blindness status

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse born before January 2, 1962?
- Is either of you legally blind?

## Common Errors
- Missing a January 1 birthday
- Claiming the spouse's credit on a separate return
- Counting only once for someone both 65 and blind

## Prompt
- I turned 65 in 2026. Is there an Iowa credit?
