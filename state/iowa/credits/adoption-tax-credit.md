# Iowa Adoption Tax Credit (IA 177)
## Description
Refundable credit for unreimbursed adoption expenses for a child placed in Iowa. Separate from, and in addition to, the federal adoption credit.

## Amount
- Up to $5,000 of unreimbursed qualified adoption expenses per child
- No income limit
- Refundable: any excess over tax is paid out
- Married filing jointly: one credit per child. Other adoptive parents may divide it as they agree; total across all years cannot exceed the limit

## Timing
- Claimed only once the adoption is FINAL
- Expenses paid before and in the finalization year are claimed on the finalization-year return
- Expenses paid in later years are claimed in the year paid, if the limit hasn't been reached
- A prior return cannot be amended to claim expenses paid before the finalization year

## 2026 status
- Unchanged for 2026: $5,000 per child. A 2025 bill to raise it to $20,000 (HF 1026) was introduced; no enactment found
- Contrast with the federal credit: $17,670 maximum for 2026, of which up to $5,120 is refundable (OBBBA). Iowa does not follow the federal amount

## Notes
- Part-year residents qualify if they lived in Iowa when the child was permanently placed
- Attach IA 177 and IA 148 for each child; missing schedules can lead to denial
- Expenses reimbursed by an employer or a government program do not qualify

## Required Information
- Each child's name and finalization date
- Unreimbursed expenses by year paid
- Credit claimed for the child in earlier years

## Questions
- Did you finalize an adoption in 2026, or pay expenses after an earlier finalization?
- Was the child placed in Iowa?
- Were any expenses reimbursed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the adoption is final
- Using the federal $17,670 limit instead of Iowa's $5,000
- Claiming the credit twice on joint returns
- Leaving out IA 177 or IA 148

## Prompt
- We finalized our adoption in Iowa this year.
- Does Iowa have its own adoption credit?
