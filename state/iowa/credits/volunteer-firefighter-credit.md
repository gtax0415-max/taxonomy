# Volunteer Firefighter, EMS, and Reserve Peace Officer Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of $250 for a full year of service as a volunteer firefighter, volunteer emergency medical services member, or reserve peace officer in Iowa.

## Amount
- $250 for a full calendar year; prorated by month using the routing table. Any part of a month counts as a full month
- One credit per person, even if serving in more than one volunteer position
- Married filing jointly: each qualifying spouse gets their own credit

## Who qualifies
- Volunteer firefighter: active member of an organized volunteer fire department meeting Fire Service Training Bureau standards
- Volunteer EMS: trained, certified first responder holding an Iowa HHS certificate
- Reserve peace officer: volunteer, non-regular sworn member meeting Iowa Law Enforcement Academy standards
- A paid firefighter or officer who volunteers for a different department qualifies

## 2026 status
- Unchanged at $250. A bill modifying the EMS portion of the credit was introduced in the 2025–2026 session; no enactment found

## Notes
- Keep the supervisor's statement; IDR may request it
- Married filing separately: only the qualifying spouse claims it; it cannot be shifted

## Required Information
- Months of service in 2026 and the supervisor's statement

## Questions
- Did you serve as a volunteer firefighter, EMT, or reserve officer in 2026, and for how many months?

## Common Errors
- Claiming two credits for two positions
- Claiming the full $250 for a partial year

## Prompt
- I'm a volunteer firefighter. Is there an Iowa tax credit?
