# Other Refundable Credits (IA 148 Part II)
## Description
Line 26 total of refundable credits listed by code on IA 148 Part II. Each code has its own file in ia148-codes/.

## How Part II works
- Column I: code; column J: certificate number (unless exempt); column K: current-year amount
- Refundable credits are claimed in full in the year earned or authorized on the certificate; no carryforward
- Part V Section B reduces codes 58 (30%) and 59 and 67 (15%) before entry
- Part IV for pass-through credits

## Codes most relevant to individuals in 2026
- 53 claim of right; 66 adoption; 70 public safety officer moving expenses
- 52, 55, 65 fuel retailer credits (sole proprietors or through entities)
- 56 historic preservation; 76 seed investor; 67, 69, 71, 75 business awards via entities

## 2026 changes affecting Part II
- Research activities (58) repealed for tax years beginning in 2026; supplemental (59) under review
- New: Seed Investor (76), Sustainable Aviation Fuel (77, likely first claimed on 2027 returns), BIG investment (75)
- Codes 62 and 97 (not for individual income tax) and 64 (obsolete) are not in this taxonomy

## Required Information
- Certificates and supporting records for each code

## Questions
- Do you have any refundable Iowa credit certificates, fuel retailer sales, an adoption, or a public safety move?

## Common Errors
- Carrying forward a refundable credit
- Skipping Part V reductions
