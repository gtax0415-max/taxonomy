# Child and Dependent Care Credit
## Description
Refundable Iowa credit equal to a percentage of the federal child and dependent care credit. Taxpayers choose EITHER this credit OR the Early Childhood Development Credit, not both.

## Amount
- Start with federal Form 2441 line 9c (the credit BEFORE the federal tax-liability limit)
- Multiply by the percentage for Iowa taxable income (IA 1040 line 4) shown in the routing table
- Married filing jointly: combined Iowa taxable income
- $90,000 is a cliff: no credit at $90,000 or more
- Refundable: paid even if no Iowa tax is owed

## 2026 change that flows in
- OBBBA raised the FEDERAL credit rate from 2026: up to 50% of expenses at low income, stepping down to 35%, then to 20% at higher incomes (expense limits stay $3,000 / $6,000). Because Iowa takes a percentage of the federal figure, the Iowa credit rises automatically for most filers
- Iowa's own percentages and the $90,000 cliff are unchanged for 2026. Bills to restructure the credit and remove the $90,000 cap (HF 962 / HF 1020) were introduced in 2025; no enactment found
- Federal Form 2441 line numbers may change for 2026; use the line for the credit before the tax limit

## Early Childhood Development Credit (alternative)
- 25% of the first $1,000 of qualifying expenses per dependent aged 3 through 5 (maximum $250 each): preschool, books, lesson materials, educational activities
- Same $90,000 income limit

## Notes
- Married filing separately: the dependent care credit is generally not allowed (follows federal rules); the Early Childhood Development Credit may be claimed by the spouse who claims the dependent
- Attach federal Form 2441

## Required Information
- Federal Form 2441, line 9c
- Iowa taxable income (line 4)
- Provider information and amounts paid

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal child and dependent care credit on Form 2441?
- Is your Iowa taxable income under $90,000?
- Do you have a child aged 3 to 5 with preschool or learning expenses (possible alternative credit)?

## Common Errors
- Using Form 2441 line 11 (after the tax limit) instead of line 9c
- Claiming both this credit and the Early Childhood Development Credit
- Claiming it with Iowa taxable income of $90,000 or more
- Using federal AGI instead of Iowa taxable income to find the percentage

## Prompt
- I pay for daycare and claimed the federal credit. What do I get from Iowa?
- My Iowa taxable income is $35,000 and my federal care credit is $1,200.
