# Iowa Fuel Tax Credit (IA 4136)
## Description
Refundable credit for Iowa motor fuel or undyed special fuel tax paid on fuel used for nonhighway purposes, such as farm machinery, stationary engines, and other off-road equipment.

## Rules for 2026
- Compute on IA 4136 and enter IA 4136 line 9 on line 23. Federal Form 4136 cannot be used for the Iowa credit
- Not for fuel used in on-road vehicles or pleasure boats
- If you filed a fuel tax REFUND claim with a refund permit during the year, you cannot also claim this credit; claiming it invalidates the refund permit
- Certain refunds may still be claimed alongside the credit (casualty losses, transport diversions, pumping credits, reefer units, power takeoff and idle time, blending errors, and similar)

## Pass-through entities
- Partnerships, S corporations, estates, and trusts pass the credit to owners by ownership share; a separate IA 4136 is filed for each pass-through credit

## Notes
- Iowa fuel tax rates for the year are needed to compute the credit; confirm the 2026 rates for gasoline, ethanol blends, diesel, and biodiesel blends on IA 4136
- Dyed diesel is not subject to Iowa fuel tax and earns no credit
- Unchanged for 2026

## Required Information
- Gallons by fuel type and the Iowa tax paid
- Description of the nonhighway use
- Whether a refund permit was used during 2026

## Questions
- Did you buy taxed fuel (not dyed diesel) for off-road use such as farm equipment in 2026?
- Do you hold an Iowa fuel tax refund permit?

## Common Errors
- Using federal Form 4136
- Claiming both a refund and the credit
- Claiming fuel used on the road

## Prompt
- I use taxed gas in my farm equipment. Can I get the Iowa fuel tax back?
