# Early Childhood Development Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for early learning expenses of dependents aged 3 through 5. It is an ALTERNATIVE to the Iowa child and dependent care credit on the same line: a taxpayer claims one or the other.

## Amount
- 25% of the first $1,000 of qualifying expenses per eligible dependent (maximum $250 each)
- Iowa taxable income must be under $90,000 (combined for married filing jointly). The $90,000 is a cliff
- Refundable

## Qualifying expenses
- Preschool services as defined in Iowa Code 237A.1
- Child development books: textbooks, music and art books, teacher's editions, reading books
- Lesson materials: paper, notebooks, pencils, art supplies
- Lesson plans and curricula
- Out-of-home educational activities (drama, art, music, museum) and their entrance fees

## Not qualifying
- Food, lodging, or membership fees for activities

## 2026 change (SF 2231, signed May 19, 2026)
- Removes the exclusion for religious services, materials, or activities from the definition of eligible early childhood development expenses, retroactive to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026. For 2026, materials or activities that teach religious tenets are no longer automatically excluded

## Choosing between the two credits
- The child and dependent care credit is usually larger when there are day care costs and a federal Form 2441 credit; this credit can help families without a federal care credit, for example with a stay-at-home parent and preschool or learning costs
- The 2026 federal Form 2441 increase makes the child care credit more often the better choice

## Notes
- Married filing separately: claimed by the spouse who claims the dependent; the $90,000 test uses only that spouse's income
- Amounts ($1,000 base, 25%, $90,000 limit) are unchanged for 2026; only the religious-expense exclusion changed

## Required Information
- Children aged 3–5 at any time in 2026 and their expenses by type

## Questions
- Do you have a child aged 3, 4, or 5?
- What did you spend on preschool, books, or learning activities?
- Are you also eligible for the child and dependent care credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming both credits
- Counting more than $1,000 per child
- Including activity membership fees
- Still excluding religious materials for 2026 under the pre-SF 2231 rule

## Prompt
- My 4-year-old goes to preschool. Is there an Iowa credit?
