# Iowa Earned Income Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable Iowa credit equal to 15% of the federal earned income credit.

## Rules for 2026
- Only taxpayers who qualify for and claim the federal EITC can claim it
- Amount: federal EITC × 0.15
- Refundable

## Low-income filers
- If the taxpayer owes no Iowa tax because of the low-income exemption, check the box between Steps 4 and 5, skip lines 5 through 19, enter zero on lines 20 and 22, and enter the EITC on lines 25, 30, 31, and 32

## Notes
- Federal EITC amounts and income limits are indexed for 2026; the Iowa credit follows them automatically
- Nonresidents and part-year residents multiply by the IA 126 percentage (out of scope)
- Federal line numbers checked against the 2026 draft Form 1040 (IRS e-file test scenarios, September 2026): line 15 (taxable income) and line 27a (EIC) are unchanged; confirm against the final form
- Unchanged for 2026: 15%

## Required Information
- Federal EITC amount

## Questions
- Did you receive the federal earned income credit for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a federal EITC
- Using a percentage other than 15%
- Not filing an Iowa return because no tax is owed, and missing the refund

## Prompt
- I got the federal EITC. Does Iowa have one too?
