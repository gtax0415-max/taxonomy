# Personal and Dependent Exemption Credits
## Description
Iowa gives flat nonrefundable credits instead of personal exemption deductions: $40 per personal exemption and $40 per dependent. These amounts are set in statute and are not indexed.

## Amounts for 2026
- Personal credit, Step 3 line a: $40 each
  - Count 1: single, married filing separately, qualifying surviving spouse
  - Count 2: married filing jointly, head of household
- Dependent credit, Step 3 line c: $40 for each dependent claimed on the federal return
- Total of lines a, b (65 or older / blind), and c → line d → line 8

## Notes
- A dependent filer (claimed on someone else's Iowa return) still claims their own $40 personal credit
- Nonrefundable: reduces Iowa tax but not below zero
- Married filing separately: each spouse claims the dependents on their own federal return
- Iowa follows the federal dependent definition (IRS Publication 501)

## Required Information
- Federal filing status
- Number and names of dependents

## Questions
- What filing status did you use federally?
- How many dependents did you claim on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 2 personal credits for qualifying surviving spouse
- Dropping the $40 personal credit for a dependent filer
- Claiming dependents not claimed federally

## Prompt
- How much is the Iowa personal exemption credit?
- I'm head of household with two kids. What exemption credits do I get?
