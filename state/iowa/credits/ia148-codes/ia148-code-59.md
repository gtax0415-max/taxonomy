# IA 148 Code 59: Supplemental Research Activities Tax Credit
## Description
Additional research credit awarded to businesses with certain state economic development agreements, on top of the regular research activities credit.

## 2026 status
- It depends on the research activities credit (code 58), which SF 657 repeals for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026. Treat new 2026 claims as a review item; an award certificate that specifically authorizes a 2026 claim should be confirmed with IDR
- Prior years: Part V Section B reduction of 15%

## Required Information
- Certificate number, award year, and amount

## Questions
- Does your business have a supplemental research credit award covering 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a certificate
- Skipping the Part V reduction on earlier-year claims

## Prompt
- We have a supplemental research credit from our HQJ award.
