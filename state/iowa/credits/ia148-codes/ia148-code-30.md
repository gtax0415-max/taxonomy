# IA 148 Code 30: Employer Child Care Tax Credit — ELIMINATED by SF 657
## Description
Credit for employers that provide child care facilities or services for employees, based on the federal Form 8882 credit.

## 2026 status
- SF 657 (signed June 6, 2025) eliminated the Employer Child Care Tax Credit as part of the 2025 tax credit restructuring, generally effective for 2026
- For 2026, use this code only for a valid CARRYFORWARD (5 years) from an earlier year, unless IDR guidance allows otherwise
- Federal side: OBBBA increased the FEDERAL Form 8882 credit from 2026 (up to 40%, 50% for small businesses, higher dollar caps); that does not revive the Iowa credit

## Required Information
- Prior certificate and remaining carryforward

## Questions
- Does your business have unused Iowa employer child care credit from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new Iowa credit for 2026 costs
- Assuming the federal increase applies to Iowa

## Prompt
- We opened an on-site day care. Is there still an Iowa credit?
