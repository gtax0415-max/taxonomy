# IA 148 Code 55: E85 Gasoline Promotion Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable per-gallon credit for Iowa retail dealers who sell E85 (high-ethanol blend) gasoline.

## Rules for 2026
- Compute on IA 135 and attach; no certificate number; not subject to Part V
- The per-gallon rate is set in statute by calendar year; confirm the 2026 rate on IA 135
- The separate E15 promotion credit has its own code

## 2026 legislation
- The earlier file cited "HF 2643 (2026)" as amending this credit. I could not confirm that bill or a change to the E85 credit for 2026. The April 2026 biofuels fiscal note found (HF 2785) addresses the biodiesel credit only. Treat the 2026 E85 rules as unchanged unless IA 135 shows otherwise

## Required Information
- E85 gallons sold by location

## Questions
- Does your station sell E85?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out IA 135
- Including E15 gallons

## Prompt
- We sell E85 at our convenience store.
