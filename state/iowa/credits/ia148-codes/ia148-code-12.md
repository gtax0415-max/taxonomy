# IA 148 Code 12: School Tuition Organization Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to an Iowa school tuition organization that awards tuition grants for accredited nonpublic schools.

## Rules for 2026
- Credit equals 75% of the donation, as shown on the STO certificate
- Carryforward: 5 years
- The donation cannot be designated for the taxpayer's own or dependents' school or college expenses or 529 contributions
- Add back the federally deducted donation on Schedule 1 line 11 code dd
- Nonresidents and part-year residents prorate (out of scope)

## Notes
- The new FEDERAL scholarship-granting-organization credit under OBBBA begins in 2027 and does not affect 2026
- Statewide award caps are set by statute; certificates are issued only up to the cap

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount
- Whether the gift was deducted federally

## Questions
- Did you donate to an Iowa school tuition organization and receive a credit certificate?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the code dd add-back
- Claiming without a certificate

## Prompt
- I donated $2,000 to an Iowa school tuition organization.
