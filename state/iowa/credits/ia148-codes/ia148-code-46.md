# IA 148 Code 46: Third Party Developer Tax Credit (Nonrefundable)
## Description
Credit for a third-party developer that paid Iowa sales tax on a project for a business with an IEDA incentive award.

## Rules for 2026
- IEDA makes the award; the developer applies to the Department of Revenue within one year of contract completion and receives the certificate
- Carryforward: 7 years
- PART V REDUCTION: the claim is reduced by 15% on IA 148 Part V Section A for awards under the agreements described there. The reduction cannot be carried forward
- Racks, shelving, and conveyor equipment use code 47; the refundable version (code 62) applies only to non-individual taxes and is not in this taxonomy

## Required Information
- Certificate number, amount, and agreement type (to know whether Part V applies)

## Questions
- Did your business build a project as a third-party developer for an IEDA-awarded company?

## Common Errors
- Skipping the Part V 15% reduction
- Carrying forward the reduced amount

## Prompt
- We received a third-party developer credit certificate.
