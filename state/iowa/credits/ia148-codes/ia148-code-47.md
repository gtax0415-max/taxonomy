# IA 148 Code 47: Third Party Developer Tax Credit (Racks, Shelving, Conveyor Equipment)
## Description
Same as code 46, but for sales tax paid on racks, shelving, and conveyor equipment in the project.

## Rules for 2026
- Certificate issued by the Department of Revenue after the IEDA award
- Carryforward: 7 years
- PART V REDUCTION: 15% on IA 148 Part V Section A for covered agreements; the reduction cannot be carried forward

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Did the third-party developer credit include racks, shelving, or conveyor equipment?

## Common Errors
- Combining with code 46 on one line
- Skipping the Part V reduction

## Prompt
- Our warehouse project credit covers conveyor equipment.
