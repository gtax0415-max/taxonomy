# IA 148 Code 07: Investment Tax Credit
## Description
Credit awarded through state economic development programs (such as High Quality Jobs) for qualifying new investment in Iowa, usually passed through to owners of the business.

## Rules for 2026
- Carryforward: 7 years
- Enter the certificate number; pass-through credits require IA 148 Part IV with the entity's FEIN
- Claim in the year stated on the certificate (typically when the qualifying property is placed in service)

## Notes
- HF 2799 (2026) extends the MEGA program, whose incentives include an investment tax credit, to January 1, 2030; a new EDGE wage credit program starts with first claims expected in fiscal year 2028

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount
- Entity name and FEIN if passed through

## Questions
- Does your business have an Iowa economic development agreement with an investment tax credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in a year other than the certificate year
- Missing Part IV for pass-through credits

## Prompt
- My S corporation got an Iowa investment tax credit.
