# IA 148 Code 03: Endow Iowa Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for gifts to a permanent endowment fund at a qualified Iowa community foundation.

## Amount for 2026
- 25% of the gift
- Per-taxpayer maximum reduced to $50,000 starting January 1, 2026 (was $100,000), under SF 657 (2025)
- Statewide annual allocation reduced to $3.5 million (was $6 million); credits are awarded first-come
- Gifts to corporate-named endowments no longer qualify from 2026
- Carryforward: 5 years

## Notes
- The gift's federal charitable deduction must be added back on Schedule 1 line 11 code dd
- Claimed in the year the certificate is issued; enter the certificate number
- Received through a pass-through entity: report from the entity's Iowa K-1 statement and complete IA 148 Part IV

## Required Information
- Certificate number, amount, and tax year
- Prior carryforward

## Questions
- Did you give to an Endow Iowa fund and receive a tax credit certificate?
- Did you deduct the gift on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $100,000 cap
- Forgetting the code dd add-back
- Claiming without a certificate

## Prompt
- I gave $40,000 to an Endow Iowa fund. What's my Iowa credit?
