# IA 148 Code 22: Solar Energy System Tax Credit (carryforward only)
## Description
Iowa credit for solar energy systems, generally 50% of the federal residential (25D) or business (48) solar credit, with dollar caps. The Iowa program ended for systems installed after 2021, so in 2026 only CARRYFORWARDS remain.

## Rules for 2026
- No new awards; claim only an unused balance from an earlier certificate
- Carryforward: 10 years
- Enter the original certificate number
- Federal note: the federal residential clean energy credit (25D) itself ended for expenditures after December 31, 2025; that does not affect an existing Iowa carryforward

## Required Information
- Certificate number, original amount, amount used to date

## Questions
- Do you have an unused Iowa solar credit from an earlier installation?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new Iowa solar credit for a 2026 installation
- Claiming an expired carryforward

## Prompt
- I still have an Iowa solar credit carryforward from 2020.
