# IA 148 Code 58: Research Activities Tax Credit — REPEALED for 2026
## Description
Iowa's research activities credit, based on the federal research credit computed on Iowa expenses.

## 2026 status
- SF 657 (signed June 6, 2025) repeals the research activities credit for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026 (removed from the Code January 1, 2027)
- It is replaced by a new IEDA-administered Research and Development Tax Credit for qualified Iowa research expenses, limited to specified business sectors, refundable, with a $40 million annual program cap. That new credit will have its own code
- For calendar-year 2026 returns, code 58 should not be used for new research expenses. Fiscal-year pass-through amounts from a year beginning in 2025 may still flow through

## Prior-law reminders
- Part V Section B reduced the claim by 30% of the amount subject to the limitation for tax years beginning in 2023–2025

## Required Information
- Tax year the research expenses were incurred
- Federal Form 6765 and Iowa expense detail
