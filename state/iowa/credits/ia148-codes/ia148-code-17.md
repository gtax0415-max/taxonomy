# IA 148 Code 17: Agricultural Assets Transfer and Beginning Farmer Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for landowners who lease farmland, equipment, or buildings to, or do custom work for, a qualified beginning farmer under the state's beginning farmer program. Two credits share code 17 and are entered on separate lines.

## Rules for 2026
- Credit equals a percentage of the rent or custom work payments set by the program and shown on the certificate
- Carryforward: 10 years
- Enter the certificate number
- A retired farmer who elects the farm tenancy exclusion or the retired farmer capital gain election cannot claim the beginning farmer credit in that year or later years

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount
- Lease and rent records

## Questions
- Do you rent land or equipment to a certified beginning farmer?
- Have you made a retired farmer farm tenancy or capital gain election?

## Common Errors
- Claiming after electing the farm tenancy exclusion
- Combining the two code 17 credits on one line

## Prompt
- I lease my farmland to a beginning farmer.
