# IA 148 Code 25: Innovation Fund Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for equity investments in a state-certified Iowa innovation fund that invests in early-stage Iowa businesses.

## Rules for 2026
- Credit equals 25% of the equity investment in the certified innovation fund, as shown on the certificate (Iowa Code 15E.52)
- Nonrefundable
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Enter the certificate number; pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- The credit is awarded under a statewide cap

## Notes
- 2026 legislation requires the Regents universities to invest part of their endowments in certified Iowa innovation funds by July 1, 2027; that affects the funds, not the individual credit

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Did you invest in a certified Iowa innovation fund and receive a credit certificate?

## Common Errors
- Confusing this credit with the Angel Investor or Seed Investor credits

## Prompt
- I invested in an Iowa innovation fund.
