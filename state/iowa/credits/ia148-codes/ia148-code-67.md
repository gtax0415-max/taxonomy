# IA 148 Code 67: Refundable Redevelopment Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable version of the brownfield/grayfield redevelopment credit (nonrefundable version: code 21). Refundable ONLY if the Iowa Economic Development Authority specifies it on the award.

## Rules for 2026
- Certificate number required
- PART V REDUCTION: 15% (Section B) before the amount goes to Part II
- Claim in full in the year earned or authorized; no carryforward
- Pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV

## Required Information
- Certificate showing refundable status and amount

## Questions
- Does your redevelopment credit certificate say it is refundable?

## Common Errors
- Skipping the 15% Part V reduction
- Treating a nonrefundable award as refundable

## Prompt
- Our brownfield project credit is marked refundable.
