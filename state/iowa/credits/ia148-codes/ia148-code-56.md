# IA 148 Code 56: Historic Preservation Tax Credit (Refundable)
## Description
Refundable version of the historic preservation credit for qualified rehabilitation of historic property. The nonrefundable version is code 05.

## Rules for 2026
- Claim in full in the year on the certificate; refundable credits have no carryforward
- Certificate number required; pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- No Part V claim reduction for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Is your historic preservation certificate marked refundable?

## Common Errors
- Applying the old Part V reduction
- Reporting a nonrefundable certificate here

## Prompt
- We received a refundable Iowa historic tax credit.
