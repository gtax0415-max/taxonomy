# IA 148 Code 65: E15 Plus Gasoline Promotion Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable per-gallon credit for Iowa retail dealers who sell E15-plus gasoline (blends of at least 15% ethanol that are not E85).

## Rules for 2026
- Compute on IA 138 and attach; no certificate number; not subject to Part V
- The per-gallon rate is set in statute by calendar year; confirm the 2026 rate on IA 138
- E85 gallons go on code 55, not here

## 2026 legislation
- The earlier file cited "HF 2643 (2026)" as amending this credit. I could not confirm that bill or a change to the E15 credit for 2026. Treat 2026 rules as unchanged unless IA 138 shows otherwise

## Required Information
- E15-plus gallons sold by location

## Questions
- Does your station sell E15 or higher ethanol blends?

## Common Errors
- Including E85 gallons
- Leaving out IA 138

## Prompt
- We sell E15 at our gas station.
