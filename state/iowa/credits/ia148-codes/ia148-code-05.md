# IA 148 Code 05: Historic Preservation Tax Credit (Nonrefundable)
## Description
Credit for qualified rehabilitation of a historic property, awarded by the state. Each certificate is either nonrefundable (code 05) or refundable (code 56, Part II).

## Rules for 2026
- Claimed for the tax year on the certificate
- Carryforward: 5 years
- The IA 148 Part V claim reduction no longer applies to this credit for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025
- Enter the certificate number; pass-through credits require IA 148 Part IV

## Required Information
- Certificate number, amount, and whether refundable or nonrefundable

## Questions
- Did you receive or inherit a share of an Iowa historic preservation credit?

## Common Errors
- Reporting a refundable certificate under code 05
- Applying the old claim reduction

## Prompt
- My partnership renovated a historic building and got an Iowa credit.
