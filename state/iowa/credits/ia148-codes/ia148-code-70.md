# IA 148 Code 70: Public Safety Officer Moving Expense Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for public safety officers who move to Iowa for a full-time public safety job.

## Amount
- Unreimbursed moving expenses, up to $2,000
- Expenses reimbursed by the employer or deducted on the federal return do not count

## Who qualifies
- Became an Iowa resident after July 1, 2024, and was not an Iowa resident in the prior 12 months
- Took full-time employment in a qualifying Iowa public safety position (for example sheriff's deputy, city police officer, conservation officer, corrections officer, parole or probation officer) and moved in connection with that job
- Still employed full time and an Iowa resident when claiming

## Timing
- Expenses paid before starting the job or in the year it starts: claim for the year employment begins
- Expenses incurred later: claim in the year incurred

## Notes
- Individuals only; no certificate number; attach IA 176
- Applies for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2024

## Required Information
- Move date, prior state, job start date, expenses and reimbursements

## Questions
- Did you move to Iowa for a full-time police, sheriff, corrections, or similar job?
- Did your employer reimburse any moving costs?

## Common Errors
- Claiming reimbursed expenses
- Claiming after leaving the qualifying job or Iowa

## Prompt
- I moved from Missouri to join an Iowa sheriff's office.
