# IA 148 Code 10: Renewable Energy Tax Credit
## Description
Per-kilowatt-hour (or per-unit) credit for energy produced and sold by an eligible renewable energy facility (Iowa Code chapter 476C), mostly held through partnerships or cooperatives.

## Rules for 2026
- Carryforward: 7 years
- Enter the certificate number; complete IA 148 Part IV for pass-through credits
- Not the same as the federal residential clean energy credit, which ended after 2025

## Required Information
- Certificate number, kilowatt-hours certified, and amount

## Questions
- Do you own part of a wind, solar, biomass, or other renewable energy facility that received Iowa certificates?

## Common Errors
- Confusing it with the federal residential energy credits
- Claiming credits issued in a year whose carryforward has expired

## Prompt
- I have an Iowa renewable energy tax credit from a community wind project.
