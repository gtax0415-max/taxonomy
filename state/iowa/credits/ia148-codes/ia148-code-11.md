# IA 148 Code 11: S Corporation Apportionment Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for a resident shareholder of an S corporation that does business both inside and outside Iowa. It reduces Iowa tax on the share of S corporation income apportioned outside Iowa.

## Rules for 2026
- Computed on IA 134 and attached
- No certificate number; no carryforward
- Cannot be claimed on a corporation income tax return
- Coordinate with the out-of-state tax credit (IA 130) so the same income does not receive two credits

## Source note
- Apportionment data comes only from the S corporation's Iowa statement; no federal form carries it

## Required Information
- Shareholder's share of S corporation income
- Iowa apportionment percentage

## Questions
- Do you own shares of an S corporation that operates in other states as well as Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out IA 134
- Claiming both this credit and IA 130 on the same income

## Prompt
- My S corporation does business in several states.
