# IA 148 Code 31: MEGA Nonrefundable Investment Tax Credit
## Description
Investment tax credit (up to 5% of qualifying investment) for very large projects under the Major Economic Growth Attraction (MEGA) program, usually received through a business entity.

## Rules for 2026
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Enter the certificate number; pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- HF 2799 (signed June 2, 2026) extends the MEGA program's end date from January 1, 2027 to January 1, 2030

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Does your business have a MEGA agreement with an investment tax credit?

## Common Errors
- Missing Part IV for a pass-through credit

## Prompt
- Our company received a MEGA investment credit.
