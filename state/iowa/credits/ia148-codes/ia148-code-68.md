# IA 148 Code 68: Angel Investor Tax Credit (Refundable)
## Description
Refundable version of the angel investor credit for awards made after July 2, 2016 to individuals or fiduciaries.

## 2026 status
- SF 657 (2025) replaced the Angel Investor program with the Seed Investor Tax Credit (code 76)
- A 2026 claim requires a certificate authorizing the 2026 tax year; refundable credits must be claimed in the year earned or authorized and do not carry forward
- Individuals and fiduciaries only

## Required Information
- Certificate number and the tax year it authorizes

## Questions
- Do you hold an angel investor certificate dated for tax year 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new angel investor credit for a 2026 investment (use the Seed Investor credit if awarded)

## Prompt
- I have a refundable angel investor credit certificate.
