# IA 148 Code 26: Farm to Food Donation Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for Iowa food producers who donate food commodities they produced to Iowa food banks or food pantries.

## Rules for 2026
- Credit equals 15% of the value of the food commodities donated during the year, up to $5,000 per year (Iowa Code 190B.106); value follows the federal charitable food rules (IRC 170(e)(3)(C))
- Nonrefundable
- Compute on IA 178 and attach; certificate number required
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Any federal deduction for the same donation must be added back (Schedule 1 line 11 code dd)
- The 2025 IA 178 was simplified (two columns removed)

## Required Information
- Receipts for each donation and value support

## Questions
- Did you donate food you grew or raised to an Iowa food bank in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Missing the code dd add-back
- Leaving out IA 178

## Prompt
- I donated beef from my farm to the local food pantry.
