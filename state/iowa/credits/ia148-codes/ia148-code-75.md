# IA 148 Code 75: BIG Program Investment Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable investment tax credit under IEDA's Business Incentives for Growth (BIG) program, new on the 2025 IA 148. Individuals receive it through a business entity.

## Rules for 2026
- Certificate number required; claim in the year on the certificate
- Pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- HF 2799 (2026) adds a BIG training fund financed by diverting up to 1.5% of withholding; that is not part of this credit

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Does your business have a BIG program agreement with an investment tax credit?

## Common Errors
- Confusing the BIG investment credit with older High Quality Jobs credits (code 07)

## Prompt
- We received a BIG program investment credit.
