# IA 148 Code 76: Seed Investor Tax Credit
## Description
New credit created by SF 657 (signed June 6, 2025) to replace the Angel Investor credit. It rewards equity investments in early-stage Iowa businesses.

## Amount
- 20% of the investment for urban businesses, 35% for rural businesses
- Investments of up to $100,000 per investor per year count
- Qualifying business: value of $10 million or less, with at least two investors and $500,000 committed in total
- Refundable for individual income tax filers
- Shares a $10 million annual program cap with the Innovation Fund credit (code 25)

## Rules for 2026
- Certificate number required; claim in the year on the certificate
- Pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV

## Required Information
- Certificate number, amount, and investment date

## Questions
- Did you invest in an Iowa startup and receive a Seed Investor certificate?
- Is the business rural or urban?

## Common Errors
- Using the old Angel Investor codes (14 or 68) for a Seed Investor certificate
- Claiming without a certificate

## Prompt
- I invested $50,000 in a rural Iowa startup.
