# IA 148 Code 16: Wind Energy Production Tax Credit
## Description
Credit per kilowatt-hour of electricity produced by a qualified wind energy facility (Iowa Code chapter 476B) and sold, available for a ten-year production period after the facility was placed in service.

## Rules for 2026
- Carryforward: 7 years
- Enter the certificate number; pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- Only older facilities that registered under the program still generate credits; most program periods have ended

## Required Information
- Certificate number, amount, and production period

## Questions
- Do you own part of a wind farm that receives Iowa wind energy production credits?

## Common Errors
- Claiming after the facility's ten-year production period
- Confusing it with the renewable energy credit (code 10)

## Prompt
- I have a share of a wind farm's Iowa tax credits.
