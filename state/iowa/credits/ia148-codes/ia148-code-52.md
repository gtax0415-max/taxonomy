# IA 148 Code 52: Biodiesel Blended Fuel Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable per-gallon credit for Iowa retail dealers who sell biodiesel blended fuel.

## Rates (unchanged for 2026)
- Blends of at least 11% but under 20% biodiesel: $0.05 per gallon
- 20% to under 30%: $0.07 per gallon
- 30% or more: $0.10 per gallon

## Rules for 2026
- Compute on IA 8864 and attach; no certificate number; not subject to Part V
- Pass-through dealers allocate to owners

## 2026 legislation
- A 2026 bill (fiscal note on HF 2785, April 2026) extends the retail dealer credit's repeal date from January 1, 2028 to January 1, 2033, with rates unchanged and first effects in tax year 2028. The earlier file cited "HF 2643 (2026)"; I could not confirm that bill number or final enactment. Either way, 2026 claims are unaffected

## Required Information
- Gallons sold by blend level for each Iowa location

## Questions
- Do you operate an Iowa fuel station that sells biodiesel blends?

## Common Errors
- Using the wrong rate for the blend level
- Leaving out IA 8864
