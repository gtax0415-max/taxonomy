# IA 148 Code 77: Sustainable Aviation Fuel Production Tax Credit
## Description
New refundable credit created by SF 657 (2025) for producing sustainable aviation fuel in Iowa.

## Amount
- $0.25 per gallon of SAF produced
- Up to $1 million per business per year and $5 million over the life of the program
- Covers SAF produced January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2035

## Timing — likely NOT on 2026 returns
- Applications for a calendar year's production are accepted February 15 to March 15 of the following year (first window: February 2027 for 2026 production)
- Credits must be claimed for the tax year in which the credit is ISSUED, so 2026 production is expected to be claimed on 2027 returns. Treat a 2026 entry as a review item

## Required Information
- Certificate number, issue date, and amount

## Questions
- Does your business produce sustainable aviation fuel in Iowa?
- When was your SAF credit certificate issued?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit for the production year instead of the issue year

## Prompt
- Our plant started producing SAF in 2026.
