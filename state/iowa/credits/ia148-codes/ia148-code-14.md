# IA 148 Code 14: Angel Investor Tax Credit — Replaced in 2025
## Description
Credit for equity investments in qualifying Iowa small businesses (formerly the Venture Capital credit): 25% of the investment, up to $100,000 per investor.

## 2026 status
- SF 657 (signed June 6, 2025) eliminated this program and replaced it with the Seed Investor Tax Credit Program
- For 2026, code 14 is used mainly for remaining CARRYFORWARDS of previously issued certificates
- Carryforward: 3 years for investments on or after July 2, 2015 (5 years for earlier investments, now expired)
- Certificates awarded after July 2, 2016 to individuals or fiduciaries may be refundable and go under code 68 instead
- New Seed Investor credits will have their own IA 148 code; confirm on the 2026 IA 148

## Required Information
- Certificate number, issue year, amount, and amount used

## Questions
- Do you have an unused Iowa angel investor credit?
- Did you invest under the new Seed Investor program?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new angel investor credit for a 2026 investment
- Putting a refundable certificate under code 14

## Prompt
- I invested in an Iowa startup. Is there still an angel investor credit?
