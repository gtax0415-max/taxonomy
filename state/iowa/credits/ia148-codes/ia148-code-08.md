# IA 148 Code 08: New Jobs Tax Credit — REPEALED in 2026
## Description
Credit for employers with a 260E community college training agreement, based on unemployment insurance taxable wages for new jobs.

## 2026 status
- HF 2799, signed June 2, 2026, repeals the New Jobs Tax Credit against individual and corporate income tax, effective on enactment
- The 260E withholding credit will not be awarded after June 30, 2026; benefits for agreements entered before then remain valid
- Not addressed in the fiscal note: treatment of unused carryforwards and credits earned before repeal. Until IDR guidance is issued, treat existing carryforwards (10-year period) as a review item

## Former amount
- Up to 6% of unemployment insurance taxable wages per new job (2026 maximum $1,224 per job), claimed once per job

## Required Information
- Training agreement, jobs counted, and any carryforward

## Questions
- Does your business have a 260E new jobs training agreement?
- Do you have an unused New Jobs credit carryforward?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 credit after the repeal without IDR confirmation

- Can I still use my Iowa New Jobs tax credit carryforward?
