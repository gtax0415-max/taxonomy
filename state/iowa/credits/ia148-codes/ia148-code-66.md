# IA 148 Code 66: Adoption Tax Credit
## Description
The IA 148 entry for the Iowa adoption credit: up to $5,000 of unreimbursed adoption expenses per child placed in Iowa, refundable, claimed once the adoption is final. Full rules are in adoption-tax-credit.md.

## IA 148 rules for 2026
- One IA 177 and one IA 148 line per child
- No certificate number
- Individuals only; fiduciaries and corporations cannot claim it
- Not subject to Part V
- Unchanged for 2026: $5,000 per child (a 2025 bill to raise it to $20,000, HF 1026, shows no enactment)

## Required Information
- See adoption-tax-credit.md

## Questions
- Did you finalize an adoption of a child placed in Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Combining two children on one IA 148 line
- Leaving out IA 177

## Prompt
- Where does the Iowa adoption credit go on IA 148?
