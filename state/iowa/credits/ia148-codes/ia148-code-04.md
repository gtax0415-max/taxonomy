# IA 148 Code 04: Franchise Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for owners of a financial institution organized as an S corporation or LLC that pays Iowa franchise tax. It prevents double tax on the same income.

## Rules for 2026
- Computed on IA 147 and attached
- No certificate number
- No carryforward: unused credit is lost

## Source note
- The amount comes only from the institution's Iowa statement; no federal form carries it. It is a third-party statement the owner receives, not a state instruction

## Required Information
- Institution name and FEIN
- Share of franchise tax credit

## Questions
- Do you own shares of a bank or other financial institution taxed as an S corporation or LLC in Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out IA 147
- Trying to carry forward an unused amount

## Prompt
- I'm a shareholder of an Iowa S corporation bank.
