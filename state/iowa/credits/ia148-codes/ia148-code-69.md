# IA 148 Code 69: Renewable Chemical Production Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for businesses that produce qualifying renewable chemicals from biomass in Iowa, based on pounds produced. Individuals receive it through a business entity.

## Rules for 2026
- Certificate number required; claimed for the year on the certificate
- Pass-through credits need IA 148 Part IV
- Awarded by IEDA under statutory per-business and program caps

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount

## Questions
- Do you own part of a business that produces renewable chemicals in Iowa?

## Common Errors
- Missing Part IV for a pass-through credit

## Prompt
- Our company received an Iowa renewable chemical credit.
