# Tuition and Textbook Credit (K-12)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for tuition and textbook expenses of dependents attending an accredited Iowa K-12 school or receiving competent private instruction (homeschool) in Iowa.

## Amount
- 25% of the first $2,000 of qualifying expenses per dependent (maximum $500 each)
- Nonrefundable; no income limit

## Qualifying expenses
- Tuition at an accredited, not-for-profit Iowa K-12 school, or for competent private instruction
- Required textbooks and instructional materials, required supply-list items, annual school fees, required extracurricular fees, driver's education, instrument rental for school, required class materials, school transportation and day field trip fees

## 2026 change (SF 2231, signed May 19, 2026)
- Removes the exclusion for sectarian books and for expenses of teaching religious tenets, doctrines, or worship from the definitions of textbooks and tuition, retroactive to tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026. For 2026, such expenses are no longer automatically excluded

## Still excluded
- Expenses paid with Students First education savings account (ESA) funds
- Food, lodging, clothing; tutoring not paid to a school; optional supplies; instrument purchases; overnight trips; yearbooks, class rings, test fees (SAT, ACT), college-credit fees

## Notes
- 529 distributions may pay qualifying expenses and still count (2026 K-12 529 limit is $20,000; see line11-code-d.md for Iowa school rules)
- Divorced parents: only the parent claiming the dependent may claim what that parent paid
- Married filing separately: the spouse claiming the dependent takes the credit

## Required Information
- Each dependent's school or private instruction
- Itemized expenses and any ESA-funded portion

## Questions
- Do your children attend an accredited Iowa private school or receive private instruction?
- What did you pay for tuition, books, and required fees?
- Was any of it paid from a Students First ESA?

## Common Errors
- Including ESA-funded amounts
- Still excluding religious materials for 2026 under the old rule
- Exceeding $2,000 per dependent

## Prompt
- I paid $6,000 tuition at a Catholic school in Iowa.
- What school expenses qualify for the Iowa tuition credit?
