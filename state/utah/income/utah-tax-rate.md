# Utah Tax at 4.45%
## Description
Utah has a single flat income tax rate. Line 8 applies it to Utah taxable income before credits.

## Amount
- Line 8 = TC-40 line 7 × 4.45% (0.0445), not less than zero
- 2025 rate: 4.5%. SB 60 (2026 General Session) lowered it to 4.45% for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026

## Where else 4.45% appears in 2026
Several credits are figured at the same rate, so they all changed with SB 60:
- Social Security Benefits Credit (AN005PT)
- Military Retirement Credit (AN004PT)
- Health Benefit Plan Credit (AN007PT)
- Capital Gain Transactions Credit (AN006PT)
- Gold and Silver Coin Sale Credit (AN011PT)
- ABLE Program Credit (NN018PT)
- Military Survivor Benefits Credit (NN019PT)

## Notes
- No brackets; filing status does not change the rate
- Line 20 Utah income tax = line 8 − line 18 taxpayer tax credit, not less than zero

## Required Information
- Utah taxable income from TC-40, line 7 (federal AGI + additions − state tax refund − subtractions)
- Taxable year begin date (fiscal-year filers use the rate for the year the fiscal year began)

## Questions
- Does your tax year start in 2026 (calendar year, or a fiscal year beginning in 2026)?
- Have you entered all additions and subtractions so line 7 is final?

## Common Errors
- Using 4.5% (2025) on a 2026 return
- Using 4.5% inside a credit worksheet that now uses 4.45%
