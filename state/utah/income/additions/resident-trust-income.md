# Untaxed Income of a Resident Trust
## Description
Closes a gap where a Utah resident trust paid FEDERAL tax on income but no UTAH tax, then distributed it. The Utah resident beneficiary adds that distribution to income so it is taxed by Utah once.

## Amount
- Addition = distributions received from a Utah resident trust of income that was taxed at the trust level federally but NOT taxed at the trust level for Utah
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- You are a Utah resident beneficiary
- The trust is a Utah RESIDENT trust
- The income was federally taxed to the trust but not taxed by Utah at the trust level

## Notes
- Ordinary DNI distributions already in your federal AGI through the K-1 are not added again
- For a NONRESIDENT trust, use code AD010PT instead

## Required Information
- Schedule K-1 (Form 1041)
- Trust's statement of income taxed federally vs. for Utah at the trust level
- Trust's residency

## Questions
- Did you receive a distribution from a trust in 2026?
- Is the trust a Utah resident trust?
- Did the trust pay federal tax but not Utah tax on that income?

## Common Errors
- Adding back ordinary K-1 income already in federal AGI
- Using this code for a nonresident trust

## Prompt
- I received a distribution from a Utah trust that paid federal tax on the income.
