# Child's Income Excluded from Parent's Return
## Description
When a parent elects on federal Form 8814 to report a child's interest and dividends, the first slice of the child's income is excluded from the parent's federal AGI. Utah adds that excluded slice back, minus the federal amount that is never taxed.

## Amount
Per child:
1. Lesser of Form 8814, line 4 or line 5
2. Minus $1,350 (the federal amount not taxed for 2026). If less than zero, enter 0
- Total the result for every child and enter one amount on TC-40_A, Part 5
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- You filed federal Form 8814 for 2026 to include a child's interest and dividends on your return
- Federal Form 8814 limits apply: the child's income must be only interest, dividends and capital gain distributions, and less than $13,500 for 2026 (10 × the $1,350 dependent standard deduction minimum, which is unchanged from 2025)
- Form 8814 line 5 base amount for 2026: $2,700 (2 × $1,350). Of the lesser of line 4 or line 5, the first $1,350 is never taxed and the rest is taxed federally outside AGI, which is the slice Utah adds back

## Notes
- If the child files their own return instead, there is no Utah addition here
- Attach the TC-150 worksheet
- The worksheet is done separately for each child

## Required Information
- Form 8814 for each child, lines 4 and 5
- Number of children for whom Form 8814 was filed

## Questions
- Did you report your child's interest or dividends on your own federal return (Form 8814)?
- How many children did you file Form 8814 for?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to subtract the $1,350 untaxed amount
- Using the greater of line 4 or 5 instead of the lesser
- Combining children into one worksheet calculation
- Using the 2025 figures

## Prompt
- I reported my child's dividends on my return with Form 8814.
- My kids have interest income that I included on my federal return.
