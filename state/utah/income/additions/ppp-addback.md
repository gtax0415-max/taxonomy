# Payroll Protection Program (PPP) Grant or Loan Addback
## Description
Federally, forgiven PPP loans are tax-free AND the expenses paid with them remain deductible. Utah does not allow that double benefit, so it adds back forgiven PPP amounts used for expenses deducted on the federal return.

## Amount
Add back any amount that meets ALL three tests:
1. Forgiven during the 2026 tax year
2. Exempt from federal income tax
3. Used for expenses you deducted on your federal return
- Pass-through owners include their distributed share
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- Rare for 2026: the PPP program ended in 2021 and most forgiveness was finalized years ago. Applies only to a forgiveness decision dated in 2026

## Notes
- Forgiveness that occurred in an earlier year is not added back on the 2026 return
- Pass-through share appears on the Utah K-1 "other income" line received from the entity

## Required Information
- SBA forgiveness notice with date
- Amount of forgiven funds spent on deducted expenses
- K-1 from any entity that received PPP funds

## Questions
- Was a PPP loan forgiven in 2026?
- Were the PPP funds used for expenses you deducted federally?
- Do you own an interest in an entity whose PPP loan was forgiven in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Adding back forgiveness from a prior year
- Adding back amounts used for non-deductible expenses

## Prompt
- My PPP loan forgiveness was finally approved this year.
