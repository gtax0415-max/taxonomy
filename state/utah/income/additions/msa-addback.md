# Medical Care Savings Account (MSA) Addback
## Description
Recapture of a Utah medical care savings account benefit. When money that earned a Utah deduction or credit leaves the account for a non-medical purpose, Utah adds it back to income. This is a Utah-only account; it is NOT a federal HSA or Archer MSA.

## Amount
- Addition = lines 8 + 9 of the administrator's statement (TC-675M)
- CAPPED at the amount previously deducted, or used to figure the MSA credit, on a current or earlier Utah return
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- You own a Utah medical care savings account
- A withdrawal was not used for qualified medical expenses, or the account was otherwise disqualified
- The money withdrawn had produced a Utah tax benefit

## Notes
- No addback for amounts that never produced a Utah deduction or credit
- Part-year and nonresidents: include in TC-40_B column A only to the extent it was previously subtracted from Utah income
- Do not confuse with a federal HSA; HSA distributions are handled entirely on the federal return

## Required Information
- Administrator's statement, lines 8 and 9
- Prior Utah deductions or credits claimed for MSA contributions

## Questions
- Do you have a Utah medical care savings account (not a federal HSA)?
- Did you withdraw money for non-medical purposes in 2026?
- Did you get a Utah deduction or credit for those contributions?

## Common Errors
- Adding back more than the Utah benefit actually received
- Treating a federal HSA distribution as a Utah MSA addback
- Omitting the addback when the administrator statement shows lines 8 or 9

## Prompt
- I took money out of my Utah medical savings account for something non-medical.
- My MSA administrator sent me a statement with an amount on line 8.
