# Untaxed Income of a Nonresident Trust
## Description
Anti-deferral rule for out-of-state trusts. When a nonresident trust accumulated income, paid federal tax on it, but paid NO state tax anywhere, and later distributes that accumulated income to a Utah resident beneficiary, the beneficiary adds it to Utah income.

## Amount
- Addition = distribution of undistributed distributable net income (UNI) from a NONRESIDENT trust, where that income was taxed at the trust level federally but not taxed at the trust level by ANY state
- Distributions come out of the MOST RECENTLY accumulated UNI first
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- You are a Utah resident beneficiary
- The trust is a NONRESIDENT trust (e.g., a trust sited in a no-tax state)
- The distribution is of previously accumulated income, not current-year DNI

## Notes
- Common with trusts sited in Nevada, South Dakota, Delaware, Wyoming and similar states
- If any state taxed the income at the trust level, no addition
- For a Utah resident trust, use code AD009PT

## Required Information
- Schedule K-1 (Form 1041)
- Trust's UNI accumulation records by year
- Proof of whether any state taxed the trust income

## Questions
- Did you receive a distribution from a trust located outside Utah?
- Was it current income or income the trust had accumulated in earlier years?
- Did any state tax that income at the trust level?

## Common Errors
- Ordering the UNI oldest-first instead of most-recent-first
- Adding back current-year DNI already in federal AGI
- Adding back income another state already taxed at the trust level

## Prompt
- My Nevada trust distributed income it had accumulated over several years.
