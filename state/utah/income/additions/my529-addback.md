# my529 Addback
## Description
Recapture of the Utah my529 credit when the money comes out for something other than qualified education, or is rolled to another state's 529 plan. Utah gives a nonrefundable credit (code AN008PT) on contributions, so it takes that benefit back when the account is used outside the rules.

## Amount
- NON-QUALIFIED WITHDRAWAL: add back the amount withdrawn, CAPPED at the amount deducted or used to figure the my529 credit on a current or earlier Utah return
- ROLLOVER OUT OF STATE: the full rollover to another state's 529 plan is added back in the year of the rollover
- Taxed at the 2026 Utah rate of 4.45%

## Eligibility
- Withdrawal not used for qualified education expenses, AND
- Not covered by an exception under IRC §529(c) or §530(d) (death, disability, scholarship, etc.)

## Notes
- Qualified withdrawals, including those for federally allowed K-12 tuition, do not trigger the addback
- A rollover to another my529 account or to a Roth IRA that meets federal rules is not a rollover "to another state's plan"
- Part-year and nonresidents: column A of TC-40_B only to the extent previously subtracted from Utah income

## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q (gross distribution, earnings, basis)
- my529 statement (TC-675H) showing withdrawals and transfers
- Receipts for qualified education expenses
- Prior Utah my529 credits claimed

## Questions
- Did you take money from a my529 account in 2026?
- Was it all spent on qualified education expenses?
- Did you roll money from my529 into another state's 529 plan?
- Does an exception apply (death, disability, scholarship)?

## Common Errors
- Adding back qualified withdrawals
- Missing the addback on a rollover to another state's plan
- Adding back more than the Utah credit actually received on non-qualified withdrawals

## Prompt
- I withdrew money from my529 to buy a car.
- I moved my child's my529 to another state's 529 plan.
