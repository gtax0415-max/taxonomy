# Recapture of Low-Income Housing Credit
## Description
If you recapture part of the FEDERAL low-income housing credit (disposition of the building or an ownership interest, or a drop in qualified basis), you must recapture the same proportion of the UTAH low-income housing credit.

## Amount
1. Federal net recapture tax (Form 8611, line 10)
2. ÷ federal credits subject to recapture (Form 8611, line 3)
3. × total Utah low-income housing credits actually taken over the life of the project on all Utah returns
- Result → TC-40, line 27a

## Eligibility
- Only when federal Form 8611 shows recapture for 2026
- Most investors hold through a partnership; the recapture follows their share

## Notes
- Adds to tax on line 29; not a reduction of credits
- The federal ratio is applied to the Utah credits, not the Utah credit for one year

## Required Information
- Form 8611, lines 3 and 10
- Lifetime Utah credits taken for the project

## Questions
- Did you file federal Form 8611 for 2026?
- How much Utah low-income housing credit have you claimed on this project in all years?

## Common Errors
- Using only the current year's Utah credit
- Recapturing Utah credit when there is no federal recapture

## Prompt
- I sold my interest in a low-income housing partnership and have federal recapture.
