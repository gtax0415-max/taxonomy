# Recapture of Employer-provided Daycare Expenses Credit
## Description
The expenses credit (AN013PT, 10% of the cost to operate an on-site childcare center or provide contracted off-site childcare) must be partly repaid if the employer does not continue the childcare benefit for at least five consecutive years. The whole repayment occurs in the year childcare stops.

## Amount
Total credit taken × repayment percentage:
| Benefit provided | Repay |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 100% |
| 2 but less than 3 years | 75% |
| 3 but less than 4 years | 50% |
| 4 but less than 5 years | 25% |

## Notes
- HB 190 (2026) added contracted off-site childcare and a new 30% Small Business Childcare Expenses Credit (AN014PT). The instructions say AN014PT is subject to the same 5-year recapture "see line 27", but TC-40 line 27 has no separate AN014PT line and TC-150 has no AN014PT worksheet in the 9/29/26 draft — confirm where AN014PT recapture goes before supporting it
- No carryforward for the expenses credit

## Required Information
- Credits taken in each prior year
- Start and stop dates of the benefit

## Questions
- Did you claim the Utah childcare expenses credit in prior years?
- Did you stop providing the childcare benefit in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Using only the last year's credit instead of the total taken
- Putting small-business credit (AN014PT) recapture on line 27c without confirmation

## Prompt
- We ended our employee childcare contract this year.
