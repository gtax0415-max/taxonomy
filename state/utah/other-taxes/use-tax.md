# Utah Use Tax
## Description
Use tax applies to goods and taxable services bought for use in Utah when no sales tax was paid at purchase (common with out-of-state sellers that do not collect Utah tax). Individuals without a Utah sales tax account pay it on the income tax return.

## Amount
TC-150 Use Tax Worksheet, in summary:
- General purchases subject to use tax × the combined sales tax rate for where you live (from tax.utah.gov/sales/rates; the worksheet takes the rate as a percent and multiplies by 0.01)
- Plus grocery food purchases subject to use tax × 3%
- Minus sales tax paid to ANOTHER STATE on those purchases (not a foreign country), not below zero
- Line 28 = worksheet result (one worksheet per state where sales tax was paid; total them)

## Notes
- Holders of a Utah sales tax license report use tax on the sales tax return, not here
- Grocery food rate is 3% (statewide plus local)
- Credit for another state's tax cannot exceed the Utah use tax owed

## Required Information
- Purchases with no sales tax, by type (general vs. grocery)
- Local rate for your address
- Sales tax paid to other states

## Questions
- Did you buy anything in 2026 from a seller that did not charge Utah sales tax?
- Did you pay another state's sales tax on items brought into Utah?
- Do you have a Utah sales tax license?

## Common Errors
- Ignoring use tax on large untaxed online or out-of-state purchases
- Using the general rate for grocery food
- Claiming credit for foreign country VAT
- Reporting on both the income tax and sales tax return

## Prompt
- I bought furniture from an out-of-state seller who didn't charge sales tax.
