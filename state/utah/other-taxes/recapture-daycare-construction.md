# Recapture of Employer-provided Daycare Construction Credit
## Description
The construction credit (AN012PC, 20% of the cost to acquire, build, rehabilitate or expand a qualified on-site childcare center) must be partly repaid if the employer does not operate the center for at least five consecutive years. The recapture is reported in the year the childcare stops.

## Amount
Credit originally claimed × repayment percentage:
| Center operated | Repay |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | 100% |
| 2 but less than 3 years | 75% |
| 3 but less than 4 years | 50% |
| 4 but less than 5 years | 25% |
| 5 years or more | 0% |

## Notes
- The credit itself requires that the employer also claims the federal employer-provided childcare credit (IRC §45F) and does not charge employees for the care
- HB 190 (2026) revised the employer childcare credit qualifications and created a small-business version (AN014PT)
- Unused construction credit carries forward 5 years (AN012PF)

## Required Information
- Credit originally claimed
- Opening and closing dates of the center

## Questions
- Did you claim the Utah childcare construction credit in a prior year?
- Did the center stop operating in 2026? How long did it run?

## Common Errors
- Recapturing on the amount used instead of the amount claimed
- Reporting in a year other than the year operation stopped

## Prompt
- We closed our on-site daycare after three years.
