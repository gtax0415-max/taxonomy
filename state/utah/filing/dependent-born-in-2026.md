# Dependent Born in 2026
## Description
In the year a qualifying dependent is born, Utah allows an ADDITIONAL personal exemption. The child is counted on line 1a and again on line 1c, so the child is worth two exemptions in 2026.

## Amount
- Extra exemption: $2,167 for 2026
- At the 6% taxpayer tax credit rate, worth up to about $130 of extra credit before the phase-out

## Eligibility
- Child born in 2026
- Child qualifies for the federal IRC §24 credit on your 2026 return

## Notes
- The child must appear on BOTH 1a and 1c; 1c alone does not count
- Check the at-home parent credit (NN001PT, $100 per child 12 months or younger at year end): the parent's wages and Schedule C gross income must be $3,000 or less and federal AGI $50,000 or less

## Required Information
- Child's birth date and SSN
- Federal dependent claim

## Questions
- Did you have or adopt a baby in 2026 who you claim as a dependent?

## Common Errors
- Counting the newborn only on 1c, not 1a
- Counting the newborn only on 1a, missing the extra exemption
- Claiming 1c for a child adopted in 2026 but born earlier

## Prompt
- We had a baby in 2026. Is there anything extra on the Utah return?
