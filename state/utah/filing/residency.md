# Full-year Resident (Utah Domicile)
## Description
A resident is a person domiciled in Utah, for the period of domicile (UCA §59-10-136). Full-year residents are taxed on all income; anyone else completes TC-40_B.

## Test 1 — automatic domicile
You are domiciled in Utah if you or your spouse:
1. Claimed an IRC §24 credit for a dependent enrolled in a Utah public K-12 school (not the noncustodial parent unmarried to the custodial parent)
2. Are enrolled as a resident student at a Utah state college or university
3. Voted in Utah during the year and were not registered or voting elsewhere

## Test 2 — facts and circumstances
Even without Test 1, you are domiciled if you have a permanent Utah home you intend to return to and settled here permanently. Factors include: Utah driver's license, residential property exemption, vehicle registration, Utah address on returns and mail, church or club membership, where you earn income, voting in Utah in any of the three prior years, and keeping a place of abode in Utah AND spending 183+ days here.

## Spouses
- If your spouse is domiciled under Test 1, you are too
- If under Test 2, you are too unless you show that in 2026 and the 3 prior years you did not own Utah property, spend over 30 days a year in Utah, earn Utah income, vote in Utah, or hold a Utah license
- Not applicable if legally separated/divorced or both filed MFS federally

## Notes
- Part-year resident: taxed on all income while resident, Utah-source income while nonresident
- Special rules for military (Pub 57), Native Americans, students
- Team scope note: nonresident/part-year handling is out of scope (see nonresident-part-year-tc-40b.md)

## Required Information
- Driver's license state, voting, property exemption, school enrollment, days in Utah

## Questions
- Did you live in Utah all of 2026?
- Do you or your spouse have a Utah driver's license, vote in Utah, or claim the Utah homeowner exemption?
- Do your children attend Utah public schools?

## Common Errors
- Assuming you are not a Utah resident because you spend winters elsewhere
- Ignoring the Test 1 school-enrollment or voting trigger
- Ignoring a spouse's domicile

## Prompt
- I moved to Arizona but still have a house and driver's license in Utah. Am I a resident?
