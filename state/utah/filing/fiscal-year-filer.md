# Fiscal Year Filer
## Description
An individual may file Utah on a fiscal year (12 months ending on the last day of any month except December) ONLY if they are a federal fiscal-year filer. Enter the fiscal year end as mm/yy on page 1.

## Rules
- Use the 2026 forms for a fiscal year BEGINNING in 2026
- Prepare the federal fiscal-year return first
- Due by the 15th day of the fourth month after the fiscal year ends
- Taxpayer tax credit: use the Utah qualified dependents and federal deductions for the fiscal year, but the phase-out amounts for the year the fiscal year BEGAN (2026: $18,696 / $28,045 / $37,392)
- Nonrefundable credits use fiscal-year facts, but caps for the calendar year the fiscal year began (e.g., my529 contributions)
- Use tax: purchases during the fiscal year, at rates for the calendar year it began
- Withholding credits: W-2s and 1099s for the calendar year the fiscal year began

## Notes
- Very rare for individuals
- Utah follows IRS rules for fiscal-year filers (IRS Pubs 17, 505, 538)

## Required Information
- Fiscal year begin and end dates
- Federal fiscal-year return

## Questions
- Do you file your federal return on a fiscal year?
- When does your fiscal year end?

## Common Errors
- Choosing a Utah fiscal year without a federal fiscal year
- Using the forms for the year the fiscal year ENDS
- Using fiscal-year W-2s instead of the calendar-year forms

- My federal return covers July 2026 to June 2027. How do I file in Utah?
