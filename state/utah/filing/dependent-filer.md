# Filer Who Can Be Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
A dependent (often a student) who files their own return has a LIMITED federal standard deduction. Utah's qualified exempt taxpayer test on line 19 uses that limited amount, not the full single standard deduction.

## Amount
- Line 19 worksheet, line 2: enter the standard deduction actually allowed on the federal return
- 2026 federal dependent standard deduction: greater of $1,350 or earned income + $450, but not more than $16,100 (single)
- If Utah taxable income (line 7) minus (that deduction + any federal enhanced senior deduction) is zero or less, the filer is exempt: "X" on line 19 and $0 on line 20

## Notes
- 2026 change: the exemption test is based on Utah TAXABLE income, not federal AGI (HB 60, 2025 session)
- A dependent still uses Utah's own taxpayer tax credit calculation, and the Utah personal exemption is NOT given for the filer themself
- A dependent may need to file just to get a refund of Utah withholding

## Required Information
- Federal standard deduction allowed
- Earned income amount
- Utah withholding (W-2 box 17)

## Questions
- Can someone (like a parent) claim you as a dependent?
- How much did you earn from work in 2026?
- Was Utah tax withheld from your pay?

## Common Errors
- Using the full $16,100 on the line 19 worksheet instead of the limited amount
- Not filing and losing a withholding refund
- Using federal AGI instead of Utah taxable income for the test

## Prompt
- I'm a college student and my parents claim me. Do I owe Utah tax on my part-time job?
