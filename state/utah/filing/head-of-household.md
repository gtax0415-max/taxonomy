# Head of Household
## Description
Follows the federal head of household status. Utah gives HOH its own phase-out base and uses the federal HOH standard deduction in the exempt-taxpayer test.

## 2026 amounts
- Taxpayer tax credit base phase-out (line 15): $28,045
- Line 19 exempt-taxpayer worksheet standard deduction: $24,150 (2026 federal HOH amount)
- TC-150 thresholds for HOH:
  - Child Tax Credit (AN002PT): $49,000 (same as single)
  - Retirement Credit (AN003PT): $32,000 (same as joint)
  - Social Security Benefits Credit (AN005PT): $90,000 (same as joint)
- Adoption credit AGI band: single band ($27,500–$55,000 nonrefundable; under $27,500 refundable)

## Notes
- HOH is treated like single for some Utah tests and like joint for others; check each credit's own table
- Utah does not test HOH eligibility itself — it relies on the federal status

## Required Information
- Federal HOH status
- Qualifying person information (already on the federal return)

## Questions
- Did you file as head of household federally?

## Common Errors
- Using the single ($18,696) or joint ($37,392) base phase-out
- Using the single threshold for the retirement or Social Security credit

## Prompt
- I'm a single parent filing head of household. What Utah amounts apply?
