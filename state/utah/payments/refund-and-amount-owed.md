# Refund and Amount Owed
## Description
Compares total tax with total payments and refundable credits. The difference is a balance due or a refund, and a refund can be split between my529, next year's tax and cash.

## Amount
- Line 29 total tax = lines 24 through 28 (tax after nonrefundable credits, voluntary contributions, amended prior refund, recaptures, use tax)
- Line 35 total payments = lines 30 through 34 (withholding, prepayments, amended prior payments, refundable credits)
- BALANCE DUE: line 36 = line 29 − line 35; line 38 = line 36 + line 37 (penalty and interest)
- REFUND: line 39 = line 35 − line 29

## Refund options
- Line 40a, my529 deposit: deposited after processing (up to 90 days) and after debt offsets. Several accounts under the SSN split it equally; on a joint return both SSNs are matched. Treated as a contribution in the year deposited (normally 2027), so it counts toward that year's my529 credit, not 2026's. Cannot be changed on an amended return
- Line 40b, applied to 2027 Utah tax: becomes a 2027 prepayment. Changing it after filing requires a written request to Taxpayer Resources
- Line 40a + line 40b cannot exceed line 39; the remainder is refunded and may be offset to federal or state debts

## Paying a balance due
- Due April 15, 2027, even under the automatic filing extension to October 15, 2027
- Pay at tap.utah.gov, or by check payable to "Utah State Tax Commission" with "2026 TC-40" and a TC-547 coupon

## Required Information
- Completed return totals
- Amounts to send to my529 or apply to 2027

## Questions
- Would you like part of your refund deposited to my529 or applied to 2027?
- How will you pay any balance due?

## Common Errors
- Line 40 exceeding the refund
- Expecting a 2026 my529 credit for a refund deposit
- Treating the extension as more time to pay

## Prompt
- Am I getting a Utah refund, or do I owe?
- Can I put my refund into my529 or apply it to next year?
