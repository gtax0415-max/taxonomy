# Prepayments (TC-546) and the 2025 Refund Applied
## Description
Utah does not require quarterly estimated payments, but taxpayers may prepay at any time. Line 31 credits all 2026 prepayments and any 2025 refund applied to 2026.

## Amount
- Payments made at tap.utah.gov or mailed with TC-546 (Individual Income Tax Prepayment Coupon) for tax year 2026
- Plus the part of the 2025 refund applied to 2026

## Extension prepayment test
Prepayments count toward the amount that must be paid by April 15, 2027 to avoid the extension penalty:
- 90% of 2026 tax due (line 24 + line 27), or
- 100% of 2025 tax liability (2025 TC-40 line 27 + line 30), or
- 90% of 2026 tax if there was no 2025 Utah liability or this is a first-year filer
Withholding, prior refunds, credits and carryovers also count.

## Notes
- Use the Tax Commission's records if they adjusted the 2025 refund applied
- Payments sent WITH the return (TC-547 coupon) are not prepayments

## Required Information
- Dates and amounts of 2026 prepayments
- 2025 refund applied to 2026

## Questions
- Did you make any Utah tax payments for 2026 before filing?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Including a payment sent with the return
- Using the 2025 refund amount requested rather than the amount actually applied

## Prompt
- I prepaid my Utah taxes when I filed my extension.
