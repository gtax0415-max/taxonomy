# State Income Tax in Federal Itemized Deductions
## Description
Utah does not let state income tax deducted federally boost the Utah taxpayer tax credit. Line 12 removes it from the deduction amount before the 6% credit is figured.

## Amount
- If you claimed the STANDARD deduction: leave blank
- If you deducted state and local INCOME tax on Schedule A, line 5a: Schedule A, line 5e minus lines 5b and 5c, not less than zero
- Do not include any SALES tax you itemized
- If the federal SALT deduction is reduced by the high-income phase-down, include only the state income tax actually deducted — do not include the part phased out

## 2026 context
- Federal SALT cap for 2026: $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately) (2025: $40,000)
- Phase-down: the cap is reduced by 30% of MAGI above $505,000 ($252,500 MFS), but not below $10,000 ($5,000 MFS)
- HB 77 (2026) modified the taxpayer tax credit to align with federal tax changes; the 2026 instruction to exclude phased-out state tax is consistent with that

## Notes
- Line 13 = line 11 − line 12

## Required Information
- Schedule A lines 5a–5e
- Whether income or sales tax was elected

## Questions
- Did you itemize on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you deduct state income tax or sales tax?
- Was your SALT deduction reduced by the income phase-down?

## Common Errors
- Entering an amount when the standard deduction was used
- Including sales tax or property tax
- Including state tax that was phased out federally

- I itemized and deducted my Utah income tax. What goes on line 12?
