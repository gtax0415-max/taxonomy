# Utah Personal Exemption
## Description
Utah's personal exemption is not a deduction from income. It feeds the taxpayer tax credit: line 9 plus the federal standard or itemized deduction, times 6%, is the credit before phase-out.

## Amount
- $2,167 per qualifying dependent on TC-40, line 1 for 2026 (2025: $2,111), indexed under UCA §59-10-1018(1)(g)
- Line 9 = line 1 × $2,167
- Applies only to DEPENDENTS — none for the filer or spouse
- A dependent born in 2026 counts twice (lines 1a and 1c)

## Worked value
- At 6%, each exemption is worth about $130 of credit before phase-out

## Required Information
- Number of qualifying dependents (line 1)

## Questions
- How many dependents did you claim for a federal child tax credit or credit for other dependents?

## Common Errors
- Using $2,111 (2025)
- Including the filer or spouse
- Treating it as a reduction of taxable income
