# Railroad Retirement Income
## Description
Federal law bars states from taxing Railroad Retirement Board benefits: retirement, disability, unemployment and sickness benefits. Utah subtracts whatever part was taxed on the federal return.

## Amount
- The railroad retirement amount included in federal 1040, line 5b (pensions) and/or 6b (Social Security, for Tier 1 SSEB)
- Only the RRB portion if those lines include other income

## Notes
- Only taxable amounts are subtracted; an untaxed amount has nothing to remove
- Tier 1 amounts subtracted here must be backed out of the Social Security Benefits Credit (TC-150, line 2)
- RRB unemployment and sickness benefits reported elsewhere also qualify

## Required Information
- RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R
- Taxable amounts on 1040 lines 5b and 6b

## Questions
- Did you receive Railroad Retirement Board benefits?
- How much was taxable on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the gross benefit instead of the taxable amount
- Subtracting other pensions on line 5b along with RRB
- Also claiming the Social Security credit on the same Tier 1 amount

## Prompt
- I'm a retired railroad worker getting RRB benefits.
