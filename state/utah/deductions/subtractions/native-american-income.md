# Native American Income
## Description
Income of an enrolled tribal member who lives on, and earns the income on, their own tribe's reservation is exempt from Utah tax.

## Eligibility
All three:
1. Enrolled member of a Native American tribe in Utah
2. Lives on that tribe's reservation
3. Earns the income on that tribe's reservation
- Also: enrolled Ute tribe members who work on the Uintah and Ouray Reservation and live on land removed from it under Hagen v. Utah (1994)

## Amount
- The qualifying income included in federal total income
- Enter the enrollment/census number and tribal code for each spouse who qualifies

## Tribal codes
1. Confederated Tribes of the Goshute Reservation
2. Navajo Nation Reservation
3. Paiute Indian Tribe of Utah
4. Skull Valley Band of Goshute Indians
5. Ute Indian Tribe
6. Other tribe

## Notes
- Active-duty military income: see Utah Pub 57
- Social Security benefits included here reduce the Social Security Benefits Credit (TC-150)

## Required Information
- Enrollment number and tribe
- Residence location
- Where the income was earned

## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member of a tribe? Which one?
- Do you live on your tribe's reservation, and did you earn the income there?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting income earned off the reservation
- Living on a different tribe's reservation
- Omitting the enrollment number or tribal code

## Prompt
- I'm an enrolled Navajo member and work on the reservation.
