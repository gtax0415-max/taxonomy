# Nonresident Active Duty Military Pay
## Description
Under the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act (50 USC §4001), a service member who is NOT domiciled in Utah but stationed here does not pay Utah tax on active-duty military pay.

## Status for 2026 — out of scope
OUT OF SCOPE — team decision 2026-09-30 (full-year residents only). A nonresident service member is not a full-year resident; route to review.

## Amount
- Active-duty military pay included in federal AGI

## Notes
- On TC-40_B, do not include this pay in Column A (Utah income)
- Non-military Utah income (e.g., a civilian side job) is still taxable
- See Utah Pub 57, Military Personnel Instructions

## Required Information
- Form W-2 for active-duty military pay
- State of legal residence (DD Form 2058 or Leave and Earnings Statement)
- Any non-military Utah income

## Questions
- Are you an active-duty service member stationed in Utah with legal residence in another state?

## Common Errors
- Utah-domiciled service members subtracting their pay
- Subtracting civilian income

## Prompt
- I'm stationed at Hill AFB but my home of record is Texas.
