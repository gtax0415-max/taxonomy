# FDIC Premiums
## Description
Federal law (IRC §162(r)) disallows part or all of the deduction for FDIC deposit insurance premiums paid by banks with over $10 billion in assets. Utah allows the disallowed premiums as a subtraction (UCA §59-10-114(2)(i)).

## Amount
- FDIC premiums not allowed as a federal deduction under IRC §162(r)

## Eligibility
- Rare for individuals: generally an owner of an S corporation bank, or a sole proprietor bank, subject to the §162(r) limit

## Required Information
- Disallowed premium amount and source

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a bank whose FDIC premiums were partly nondeductible federally?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the full premium rather than the disallowed part

## Prompt
- My S corporation bank couldn't deduct all of its FDIC premiums.
