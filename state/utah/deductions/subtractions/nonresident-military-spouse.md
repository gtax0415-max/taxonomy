# Nonresident Military Spouse Income
## Description
Under the Military Spouses Residency Relief Act (50 USC §4001), income of a service member's spouse is exempt from Utah tax when the spouse is in Utah only because of the service member's orders.

## Status for 2026 — out of scope
OUT OF SCOPE — team decision 2026-09-30 (full-year residents only); route to review.

## Eligibility
The spouse is:
1. Not a Utah resident,
2. Not also a service member, and
3. In Utah solely to be with the service member serving under military orders

## Amount
- Total income of the nonresident spouse on TC-40_A, Part 6
- On TC-40_B, deduct only the spouse's Utah income in Column A, line 33

## Notes
- See Utah Pub 57

## Required Information
- Spouse's W-2s and 1099s, and the Utah portion of that income
- Service member's orders stationing them in Utah
- Proof of the spouse's domicile in another state

## Questions
- Is your spouse a service member stationed in Utah?
- Is your legal residence in another state, and are you in Utah only because of their orders?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it when the spouse established Utah domicile
- Using SU004PT instead

## Prompt
- My husband is stationed in Utah and I work here, but we're Florida residents.
