# Social Security Repayment
## Description
NEW for 2026 (HB 60, 2025 General Session). Subtraction for prior-year Social Security income repaid in 2026, when Utah taxed the income in the earlier year without the Social Security credit. Most filers do not qualify.

## Eligibility
Both:
1. You claimed a credit for the repayment on your 2026 federal return (claim of right, IRC §1341), AND
2. You did NOT claim the Utah Social Security Benefits Credit (AN005PT) in the year you received the income

## Amount
- The prior-year Social Security income repaid in 2026

## Notes
- If the repayment was handled federally as a deduction instead of a credit, this subtraction does not apply
- Amounts subtracted here are backed out of the Social Security Benefits Credit worksheet (TC-150, line 2)

## Required Information
- SSA-1099 repayment amount
- Federal §1341 computation
- Prior Utah return

## Questions
- Did you repay Social Security benefits in 2026 that were received in an earlier year?
- Did you claim the federal repayment credit?
- Did you claim the Utah Social Security credit in the year you got the benefits?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it when the Utah Social Security credit was already taken in the earlier year
- Claiming it when the federal deduction method was used

## Prompt
- Social Security made me pay back benefits from 2024.
