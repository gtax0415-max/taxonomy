# Federal Deductions Utah Does Not Reach
## Description
Utah line 10 takes ONLY the federal standard or itemized deduction from Form 1040, line 12e (1040-NR: line 12). Newer federal deductions taken outside that line never enter the Utah taxpayer tax credit.

## Not reached
- Schedule 1-A: no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, car loan interest (2025–2028)
- The charitable deduction for NON-itemizers (new for 2026: up to $1,000 single / $2,000 joint), which is on its own 1040 line
- Qualified business income (QBI) deduction (§199A)

## Reached only partly
- Federal enhanced senior deduction (Schedule 1-A, line 37): used ONLY in the line 19 qualified exempt taxpayer test, not on line 10

## Effect
- These items lower federal taxable income but not Utah taxable income, and they do not raise the Utah taxpayer tax credit
- Utah starts at federal AGI (line 2), which is figured before any of these deductions

## Notes
- The line number of the 2026 non-itemizer charitable deduction on the final Form 1040 should be confirmed when the IRS releases the final form

## Required Information
- Form 1040 line 12e
- Schedule 1-A amounts

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal deductions for tips, overtime, car loan interest, seniors, or charity without itemizing?

## Common Errors
- Adding Schedule 1-A or non-itemizer charitable amounts to line 10
- Using federal taxable income instead of AGI on line 2

## Prompt
- Does Utah give me the tips and overtime deduction?
- I took the $1,000 charitable deduction without itemizing — does Utah count it?
