# Military Survivor Benefits Credit
## Description
Credit equal to Utah tax on military survivor benefits received by a surviving spouse or dependent child (10 USC §1447) of a member who died while serving (UCA §59-10-1036).

## Amount
- Survivor benefits received in 2026 × 4.45% (2025: 4.5%)
- No carryforward

## Eligibility
Benefits paid because of:
1. Death of an armed forces or reserve member while on active duty, or
2. Death of a reserve member from a service-connected cause during inactive duty training

## Notes
- If the deceased member had RETIRED, use the Military Retirement Credit (AN004PT) instead
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- 1099-R, circumstances of death

## Questions
- Do you receive survivor benefits for a service member who died on active duty or in training?

## Common Errors
- Using this credit for a retiree's survivor annuity
- Using 4.5%

## Prompt
- I receive SBP payments because my wife died on active duty.
