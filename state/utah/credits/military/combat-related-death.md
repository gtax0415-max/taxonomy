# Combat-related Death Credit
## Description
Eliminates Utah tax attributable to a service member who died as a result of service in a combat zone (UCA §59-10-1027).

## Amount
- Single or married filing separately: credit = TC-40 line 20 tax
- Joint (TC-150 worksheet):
  1. Joint federal AGI
  2. Deceased member's federal AGI
  3. Line 2 ÷ line 1
  4. TC-40 line 20
  5. Credit = line 3 × line 4
- Another allocation method may be used if it more accurately reflects the member's tax

## Eligibility
1. Active or reserve member of the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Coast Guard or Space Force
2. Death on or after January 1, 2010
3. Died while serving in a combat zone, or from a wound, disease or injury incurred there
4. Service within the period designated a combat zone by Executive Order

## Required Information
- Casualty report, AGI split

## Questions
- Is this return for a service member who died from combat-zone service?

## Common Errors
- Using the full joint tax on a joint return

## Prompt
- My husband was killed in a combat zone; I'm filing our joint return.
