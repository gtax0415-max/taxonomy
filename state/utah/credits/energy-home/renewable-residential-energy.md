# Renewable Residential Energy Systems Credit
## Description
Credit for the reasonable cost, including installation, of a renewable energy system supplying a Utah residential unit (UCA §59-10-1014). The amount is set by the Office of Energy Development.

## Amount
- The credit amount approved on the OED-stamped TC-40E
- Unused credit carries forward 4 years (NN010PF)

## Status for 2026
- RESIDENTIAL SOLAR: expired for systems after 2023. Unused solar credit carries forward through 2027
- Other residential systems (e.g., wind, geothermal, biomass) remain eligible as approved by OED

## Notes
- A seller of the residence to a non-business buyer may irrevocably transfer an unclaimed credit to the buyer
- Principal portion of lease payments may qualify if the lessor transfers the credit rights
- Not with the credit under UCA §59-10-1029
- Do not attach TC-40E
- Separate from the federal residential clean energy credit (§25D), which ended after 2025

## Required Information
- Approved TC-40E, carryforward history

## Questions
- Did you install a renewable energy system at your Utah home and get OED approval?
- Do you have unused Utah solar credit from 2023 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 solar installation
- Claiming without an approved TC-40E
- Claiming a solar carryforward after 2027

## Prompt
- I installed solar in 2023 and still have Utah credit left.
