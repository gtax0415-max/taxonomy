# Historic Preservation Credit
## Description
Credit for costs to restore a residential certified historic building, certified in advance by the State Historic Preservation Office (UCA §59-10-1006).

## Amount
- The approved amount on the SHPO-certified TC-40H
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years (NN003PF)

## Eligibility
- Residential certified historic building
- SHPO certification verifying approval

## Notes
- Do not send TC-40H; keep it with records
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Certified TC-40H, carryforward history

## Questions
- Did you restore a certified historic home and get State Historic Preservation Office approval?
- Do you have unused credit from 2021–2025?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without SHPO certification
- Entering a carryforward under NN003PC

## Prompt
- We restored our historic home in Salt Lake City.
