# Qualified Sheltered Workshop Cash Contribution Credit
## Description
Credit for CASH contributions to a qualified Utah nonprofit rehabilitation sheltered workshop for persons with disabilities (UCA §59-10-1004).

## Amount
- Lesser of $200 or 50% of total 2026 cash contributions
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
- Workshop holds a current Day Training Provider License or Day Support Provider Certificate from the Department of Human Services
- Contributions claimed on federal Schedule A CANNOT be claimed here
- Enter the workshop's name on TC-40_A, Part 3

## Notes
- Cash only; donated goods do not count
- Maximum benefit reached at $400 of cash gifts

## Required Information
- Receipts, workshop name, licensing status

## Questions
- Did you give cash to a sheltered workshop for people with disabilities in 2026?
- Did you deduct it on Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a gift also deducted federally
- Omitting the workshop name
- Claiming non-cash gifts

## Prompt
- I donated to a workshop that employs people with disabilities.
