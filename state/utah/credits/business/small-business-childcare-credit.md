# Small Business Childcare Expenses Credit
## Description
NEW for 2026 (HB 190). An eligible small business employer may claim 30% of the cost to operate an on-site childcare center or provide childcare at a contracted off-site center (UCA §59-10-1048(3)(a)(i)).

## Amount
- 30% of qualified 2026 costs
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
- "Eligible small business employer" as defined by HB 190 (definition not stated in the 2026 instructions; confirm against UCA §59-10-1048 before supporting)
- Employees are NOT charged
- Use instead of the 10% credit (AN013PT), not in addition

## Recapture
Subject to 5-year recapture like AN013PT. The 2026 draft forms do not show where to report it: TC-40 line 27 lists only 27a–27c, and TC-150 has no AN014PT worksheet. Confirm before supporting.

## Required Information
- Costs, small-business status

## Questions
- Is your business small, and does it provide employee childcare?

## Common Errors
- Claiming 30% without meeting the small-business definition
- Claiming both 10% and 30% credits

## Prompt
- I own a small business and pay for my employees' childcare.
