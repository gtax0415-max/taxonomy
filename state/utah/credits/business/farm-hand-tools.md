# Farm Operation Hand Tools Credit
## Description
Refunds the sales and use tax paid on hand tools bought and used or consumed primarily and directly in a Utah farming operation (UCA §59-10-1105).

## Amount
- The sales and use tax paid on qualifying tools
- Only tools with a purchase price over $250 each
- Refundable

## Notes
- No form; keep receipts
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Receipts with tax amounts, farm use

## Questions
- Did you buy hand tools over $250 each for your Utah farm and pay sales tax?

## Common Errors
- Including tools $250 or less
- Claiming the price instead of the tax paid
- Including non-farm or personal tools

## Prompt
- I paid sales tax on expensive tools for my farm.
