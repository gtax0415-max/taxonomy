# Agricultural Off-highway Gas/Undyed Diesel Credit
## Description
Refunds Utah motor fuel tax on gasoline and undyed diesel bought in Utah and used solely for commercial, non-highway agriculture (UCA §59-13-202).

## Amount
- Gallons × 37.9 cents
- Refundable

## Qualifies
- Fuel bought in Utah in 2026, taxed at purchase
- Used in stationary farm engines and self-propelled farm machinery solely for commercial non-highway agriculture

## Does NOT qualify
- Golf courses, horse racing, boats, highway seeding
- Vehicles registered for highway use
- Hobby or personal farming, other non-agricultural use
- Dyed diesel (untaxed)

## Notes
- Nonapportionable; also available on TC-41 and TC-20

## Required Information
- Gallons and invoices, usage records

## Questions
- Do you run a commercial farm and buy taxed fuel for off-road equipment?

## Common Errors
- Including fuel for registered trucks
- Including dyed diesel
- Including hobby-farm use

## Prompt
- I buy diesel for my tractors on my farm.
