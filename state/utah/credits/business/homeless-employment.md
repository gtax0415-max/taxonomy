# Employing Persons Who Are Homeless Credit
## Description
Credit for employers who hire persons experiencing homelessness, in the amount certified by the Department of Workforce Services (UCA §59-10-1032).

## Amount
- The amount on the DWS certificate
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years (NN013PF)

## Notes
- Do not send the certificate; keep it
- Nonapportionable
- Contact: DWS, wotc@utah.gov

## Required Information
- DWS certificate, carryforward history

## Questions
- Did your business hire a person experiencing homelessness and receive a DWS certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a certificate
- Entering a carryforward under NN013PC

## Prompt
- My business hired someone through a homeless employment program.
