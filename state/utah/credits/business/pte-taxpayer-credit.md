# Pass-through Entity Taxpayer Income Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for the Utah elective entity-level tax a partnership or S corporation paid on the owner's behalf (UCA §59-10-1045). Paired with the AD012PT addition, it lets owners get the federal SALT benefit at the entity level without double Utah tax.

## Amount
- Utah tax paid on your behalf under UCA §59-10-1403.2(2), as shown on the entity's statement
- Unused credit carries forward 10 years (NN006PF)

## 2026 Change
HB 77 (2026, signed March 23, 2026) made the Utah elective pass-through entity tax permanent, applying from 2026.

## Notes
- Only the UTAH elective tax; another state's elective tax is added back (AD012PT) but credited, if at all, through the credit for tax paid to another state (NN008PT)
- Not the same as pass-through WITHHOLDING (TC-40 line 30b)
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Entity statements, carryforward history

## Questions
- Did an entity you own elect to pay Utah tax on your behalf for 2026?
- Do you have unused credit from prior years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit without the AD012PT addition
- Claiming other-state elective tax here
- Treating withholding as elective tax

## Prompt
- My S corp elected to pay Utah PTE tax.
