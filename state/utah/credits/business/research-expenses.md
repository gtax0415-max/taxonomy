# Credit for Research Expenses in Utah
## Description
Utah's volume research credit: 7.5% of current-year qualified research expenses in Utah, with no base amount (UCA §59-10-1012(1)(a)(iii)).

## Amount
- 7.5% of 2026 Utah qualified research expenses
- NO carryforward or carryback — use it or lose it

## Notes
- Can be claimed alongside the incremental credit (NN028PC)
- Because it cannot be carried, apply it before credits that can
- No form; keep records

## Required Information
- Utah QREs, K-1

## Questions
- How much did your business spend on qualified research in Utah in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Carrying forward unused volume credit
- Including non-Utah research

## Prompt
- Does Utah give a credit on all of our R&D spending?
