# Credit for Increasing Research Activities above a Base Amount
## Description
Utah's incremental research credit: 5% of qualified research expenses for research conducted in Utah above a base amount (UCA §59-10-1012(1)(a)(i)). Usually reaches individuals through a pass-through entity.

## Amount
- 5% of Utah qualified research expenses above the base amount
- Unused credit carries forward 14 years (NN028PF)

## Notes
- Utah generally follows federal IRC §41 definitions, limited to research in Utah
- No form; keep records
- Separate from the 7.5% volume credit (NN030PT), which has no carryforward

## Required Information
- Form 6765, Utah allocation, K-1, carryforward history

## Questions
- Does your business (or one you own) do research in Utah?
- Did you claim the federal research credit?

## Common Errors
- Including research done outside Utah
- Entering a carryforward under NN028PC

## Prompt
- My company claims the federal R&D credit for work done in Utah.
