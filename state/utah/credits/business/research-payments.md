# Credit for Payments to an Organization Increasing Research Activities
## Description
5% of certain payments to a qualified organization for basic research in Utah above a base amount (UCA §59-10-1012(1)(a)(ii)). Generally mirrors the federal basic research payment rules.

## Amount
- 5% of qualified Utah basic research payments above the base amount
- Unused credit carries forward 14 years (NN029PF)

## Notes
- Typically paid to a university or research organization
- No form; keep records

## Required Information
- Payment records, Form 6765, carryforward history

## Questions
- Did your business pay a university or research organization for basic research done in Utah?

## Common Errors
- Including payments for research outside Utah
- Entering a carryforward under NN029PC

## Prompt
- Our company funds research at the University of Utah.
