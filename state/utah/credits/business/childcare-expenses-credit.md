# Employer-provided Childcare Expenses Credit
## Description
Credit of 10% of the cost to operate a qualified on-site childcare center, or to provide employees childcare at a contracted OFF-SITE center (UCA §59-10-1048(3)(a)(ii)).

## Amount
- 10% of qualified 2026 costs
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
- Employees are NOT charged for the childcare
- A SMALL business may instead qualify for the 30% Small Business Childcare Expenses Credit (AN014PT)

## 2026 Change
HB 190 (2026) revised the employer childcare credits, including the contracted off-site option, and created AN014PT.

## Recapture
If the benefit is not provided 5 consecutive years, repay 25%–100% of the total credit taken (TC-40 line 27c). See other-taxes/recapture-daycare-expenses.md.

## Required Information
- Operating or contract costs, start date of the benefit

## Questions
- Does your business run an on-site daycare or pay for employees' childcare at an outside center?
- Is your business a small business that might qualify for the 30% credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming both AN013PT and AN014PT on the same costs
- Charging employees

## Prompt
- Our business pays a local daycare for employees' kids.
