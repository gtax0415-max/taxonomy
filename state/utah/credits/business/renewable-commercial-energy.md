# Renewable Commercial Energy Systems Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for a renewable energy system on a commercial unit, or for renewable electricity production, approved by the Office of Energy Development (UCA §59-10-1106).

## Amount
- The amount on the OED-approved TC-40E
- Refundable

## Notes
- Not with the credit under UCA §59-10-1029, and not for the same project as the residential credit (NN010PC)
- Do not attach TC-40E
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Approved TC-40E, K-1

## Questions
- Do you own (directly or through a business) a commercial renewable energy system approved by OED?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without OED approval
- Claiming both the residential and commercial credit for the same system

## Prompt
- My business installed solar on our building.
