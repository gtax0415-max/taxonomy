# Employer-provided Childcare Construction Credit
## Description
Credit of 20% of the cost to acquire, build, rehabilitate or expand a qualified ON-SITE childcare center during the year (UCA §59-10-1048(2)). For individuals with business income (sole proprietors or pass-through owners).

## Amount
- 20% of qualified 2026 costs
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years (AN012PF)

## Eligibility
- The employer claims the FEDERAL employer-provided childcare credit (IRC §45F) for 2026
- Employees are NOT charged for the childcare

## 2026 Change
HB 190 (2026) revised qualifications for the employer childcare credits and added the Small Business Childcare Expenses Credit (AN014PT).

## Recapture
If the center does not operate 5 consecutive years, repay 25%–100% (TC-40 line 27b). See other-taxes/recapture-daycare-construction.md.

## Required Information
- Form 8882, cost invoices, opening date

## Questions
- Did your business build or expand an on-site childcare center for employees in 2026?
- Did you claim the federal childcare facilities credit?
- Do employees pay for the care?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the federal §45F credit
- Charging employees
- Entering a carryforward under AN012PC instead of AN012PF

## Prompt
- Our company built a daycare for employees.
