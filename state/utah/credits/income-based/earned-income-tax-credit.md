# Earned Income Tax Credit
## Description
Utah's EITC is 20% of the federal earned income credit. Unlike the federal credit, Utah's is NONREFUNDABLE and limited to Utah wages reported on W-2s.

## Amount
- Credit = lesser of:
  1. 20% × federal EITC (Form 1040, line 27a), or
  2. Total Utah wages on W-2s (box 16)
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
- Qualify for and claim the federal EITC for 2026
- Have Utah income reported on a W-2

## Notes
- Self-employment income alone does not qualify (no W-2 wages means the limit is $0)
- Apportioned for nonresidents and part-year residents
- Nonrefundable: only helps to the extent of tax after the taxpayer tax credit

## Required Information
- Federal EITC amount
- Utah W-2 wages

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal earned income credit?
- Do you have W-2 wages with Utah wages in box 16?

## Common Errors
- Expecting a refund of the Utah EITC
- Claiming it with only self-employment income
- Using the federal EITC before reductions instead of line 27a

## Prompt
- I got the federal earned income credit. Does Utah have one?
