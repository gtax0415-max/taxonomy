# my529 Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for qualified contributions to a Utah my529 account made during the tax year and not deducted federally (UCA §59-10-1017). my529 computes the credit for the account owner.

## Amount
- Single, head of household, married filing separately, qualifying surviving spouse: TC-675H box 1A
- Married filing jointly: TC-675H box 1B
- 2026 cap per beneficiary: contributions up to $2,560 (single filers) or $5,120 (joint filers) count, at 4.45%, for a maximum credit of $113.92 or $227.84 per beneficiary
- The beneficiary must have been under age 19 when designated on the account
- my529 applies these limits when it prepares TC-675H

## Notes
- No carryforward or carryback
- A grantor trust with more than one TC-675H for the same beneficiary should contact my529
- Non-qualified withdrawals or rollovers out of state later trigger the my529 addback (AD004PT)
- Refunds deposited to my529 via TC-40 line 40a count as contributions in the year deposited
- DRAFT ERROR: the 10/5/26 instructions say to enter this credit "on TC-40_A, Part 3", but AN008PT is an apportionable credit listed in Part 1. Use Part 1

## Required Information
- TC-675H

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Utah my529 account in 2026?
- Are you filing jointly?

## Common Errors
- Using box 1A on a joint return
- Entering in Part 3
- Computing the credit yourself instead of using the my529 statement

## Prompt
- I put money in my529 for my kids this year.
