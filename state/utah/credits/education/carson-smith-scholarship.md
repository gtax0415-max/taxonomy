# Carson Smith Opportunity Scholarship Program Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit equal to 100% of a donation to the Carson Smith Opportunity Scholarship Program, as shown on the program's tax credit certificate (UCA §59-10-1041).

## Amount
- The credit amount on the certificate
- 2026 donations: NO carryforward
- Unused credit from years BEFORE 2026 may carry forward through 2028 under code NN021PF

## 2026 Change
SB 54 (2026 General Session, signed March 19, 2026) modified the program for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.

## Eligibility
- Donation not claimed as a federal itemized deduction
- Credits are issued first-come, first-served under a statewide annual cap

## Notes
- Do not attach the certificate; keep it
- Nonapportionable
- The carryforward code NN021PF does not follow the NN031PT numbering; confirm it on the final 2026 form

## Required Information
- Certificate, Schedule A

## Questions
- Did you donate to the Carson Smith Opportunity Scholarship Program and receive a tax credit certificate?
- Did you deduct the donation on your federal Schedule A?
- Do you have unused credit from before 2026?

## Common Errors
- Also deducting the donation federally
- Carrying forward a 2026 credit
- Claiming more than the certificate shows

## Prompt
- I got a tax credit certificate for a scholarship donation.
