# At-Home Parent Credit
## Description
$100 per infant for a parent who stays home full time to care for a child 12 months old or younger (UCA §59-10-1005).

## Amount
- $100 for each child 12 months or younger on December 31, 2026
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
All of:
1. Child 12 months or younger on December 31, 2026
2. The at-home parent provides full-time care in their residence
3. The child is claimed as a dependent on the at-home parent's return
4. The at-home parent's W-2 wages, tips and compensation PLUS Schedule C line 7 gross income are $3,000 or less
5. Federal AGI (TC-40 line 2) is $50,000 or less
- "Parent" includes biological, step, adoptive and foster parents, legal guardians, and people housing a child placed for adoption

## Notes
- The $3,000 test is the AT-HOME parent's earnings; the $50,000 test is the whole return's AGI
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Child's birth date, parent's earnings, AGI

## Questions
- Do you have a child born in 2026 (or late 2025, still 12 months or younger at year end)?
- Does one parent stay home full time? How much did that parent earn?
- Is your federal AGI $50,000 or less?

## Common Errors
- Using both parents' earnings for the $3,000 test
- Claiming a child who turned one before year end

## Prompt
- I stayed home with our newborn this year.
