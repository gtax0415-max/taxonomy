# Child Tax Credit
## Description
$1,000 nonrefundable credit per young child who qualifies for the federal child tax credit, phased out at 10 cents per dollar of modified AGI above a threshold (UCA §59-10-1047).

## Amount
1. Children who qualify for the federal credit (IRC §24) and are UNDER SIX on December 31, 2026 (age 5 or younger)
2. × $1,000
3. MAGI = TC-40 line 4 − municipal bond interest addition (AD007PT) + federal Form 1040 line 2a tax-exempt interest
4. Threshold for 2026 (HB 290):
   - Married filing separately (and married filing 1040-NR): $30,500
   - Single, head of household: $49,000
   - Married filing jointly, qualifying surviving spouse: $61,000
5. Credit = line 2 − 10% × (MAGI − threshold), not below zero

## 2026 Change
HB 290 (2026), retroactive to January 1, 2026, raised the thresholds from $27,000 / $43,000 / $54,000. The $1,000 amount and the age rule did not change.

## Notes
- No carryforward or carryback
- AGE: the statute says "under six years old on the last day of the taxable year." The 9/29/26 draft TC-150 worksheet says "6 and younger," which conflicts with the statute and the 10/5/26 instructions ("no more than five years old"). Use under six
- A child born in 2026 qualifies

## Required Information
- Children's birth dates and federal CTC eligibility
- TC-40 line 4, AD007PT, 1040 line 2a

## Questions
- Do you have children who were 5 or younger on December 31, 2026?
- Did they qualify for the federal child tax credit?

## Common Errors
- Including a child who turned 6 in 2026
- Using the 2025 thresholds
- Using federal AGI instead of Utah MAGI

## Prompt
- I have a 3-year-old. Is there a Utah child tax credit?
