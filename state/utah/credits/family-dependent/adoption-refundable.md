# Refundable Adoption Expenses Credit
## Description
Refundable Utah credit (UCA §59-10-1114) for adoption expenses of lower-income filers whose adoption was FINALIZED in 2026. Filers with higher AGI use the nonrefundable credit (AN009PC).

## Amount
- Up to $3,500 of adoption expenses, REFUNDABLE
- AGI must be LESS than:
  - $55,000 married filing jointly
  - $27,500 single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse
- Nonresidents and part-year residents multiply by the TC-40_B, line 39 fraction

## Eligibility
Same exclusions as the nonrefundable credit:
- No state or federal assistance received during the year
- Same expenses not claimed under IRC §23 (Form 8839)
- Married filers must file a joint federal return
- Not a stepparent adoption
- Apply through the Department of Workforce Services

## Notes
- No carryforward needed because it is refundable
- Flows to TC-40 line 34, not line 21

## Required Information
- Adoption decree and finalization date
- Itemized adoption expenses
- Federal AGI and filing status
- DWS approval

## Questions
- Was the adoption finalized in 2026?
- Is your federal AGI under $27,500 (or $55,000 joint)?
- Did you receive any state or federal adoption assistance?

## Common Errors
- Using AN009PC when AGI is below the band
- Claiming the same expenses on Form 8839
- Forgetting the apportionment fraction for part-year residents

## Prompt
- We adopted this year and our income is low. Can we get a Utah refund for it?
