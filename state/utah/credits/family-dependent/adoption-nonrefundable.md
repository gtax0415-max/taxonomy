# Nonrefundable Adoption Expenses Credit
## Description
Utah credit (UCA §59-10-1046) for expenses of an adoption FINALIZED during the tax year, for middle-income filers. Lower-income filers get the REFUNDABLE version instead (code AR001PT). The income bands are mutually exclusive, so a filer qualifies for one or the other, not both.

## Amount
- Up to $3,500 of adoption expenses
- AGI band (federal AGI):
  - Married filing jointly: at least $55,000 but less than $110,000
  - Single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse: at least $27,500 but less than $55,000
- Below the band → refundable credit AR001PT. At or above the top → no credit (cliff, no phase-out)
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years (code AN009PF)
- Apportioned for nonresidents and part-year residents

## Eligibility
NOT allowed if any of these apply:
- You received any state or federal assistance during the tax year
- You claimed the SAME expenses for the federal adoption credit (IRC §23, Form 8839)
- You are married but did not file a joint federal return
- Stepparent adoption (adopting your spouse's child)
- Must apply through the Utah Department of Workforce Services

## Notes
- Unlike the federal credit, Utah requires the adoption to be FINALIZED in 2026; there is no pre-finalization timing rule
- The same expense cannot go on both Form 8839 and the Utah credit. Since the federal credit (up to $17,670 for 2026) is much larger, filers with high expenses may allocate up to $3,500 to Utah and the rest to Form 8839
- Married filing separately is not eligible

## Required Information
- Adoption decree and finalization date
- Itemized adoption expenses
- Federal AGI and filing status
- DWS approval
- Federal Form 8839 expenses claimed
- Any state or federal adoption assistance received

## Questions
- Was the adoption finalized in 2026?
- What is your federal AGI and filing status?
- Did you receive any state or federal adoption assistance this year?
- Did you claim the same expenses on federal Form 8839?
- Is this a stepparent adoption?
- Do you have unused Utah adoption credit from 2023–2025?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the same expenses on Form 8839 and the Utah credit
- Using this nonrefundable code when AGI is below the band (should be AR001PT)
- Claiming it as married filing separately
- Claiming a carryforward under AN009PC instead of AN009PF in Part 1cc
- Claiming for an adoption not yet finalized

## Prompt
- We finalized an adoption this year and our income is about $80,000.
- Does Utah have an adoption tax credit?
