# ABLE Program Credit
## Description
Credit of 4.45% of qualified contributions made during the year to an Achieving a Better Life Experience (ABLE) account of a Utah resident (UCA §59-10-1035).

## Amount
- Qualified 2026 contributions × 4.45%
- No carryforward or carryback

## Eligibility
- Contributions made in 2026 to a qualified ABLE program account owned by a Utah resident
- Must have an itemized statement from the program
- Exclude contributions returned to you and amounts already deducted on the federal return

## Notes
- Contributor does not have to be the beneficiary
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Contribution statement, Form 5498-QA

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an ABLE account for a Utah resident in 2026?
- Were any contributions returned?

## Common Errors
- Using 4.5% (2025)
- Including returned contributions

## Prompt
- I contribute to my son's ABLE account.
