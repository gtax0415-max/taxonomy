# Live Organ Donation Expenses Credit
## Description
Credit for up to $10,000 of unreimbursed costs of donating bone marrow, or part of an intestine, kidney, liver, lung or pancreas, for transplant to another person (UCA §59-10-1015).

## Amount
- Qualified costs up to $10,000
- Unused credit carries forward 5 years (NN009PF)

## Qualified costs
- Travel, lodging and lost wages
- Only if NOT reimbursed
- The donor is the filer, spouse, or a dependent

## Notes
- Complete TC-40T; do not attach it
- Nonapportionable

## Required Information
- Donation records, unreimbursed costs

## Questions
- Did you, your spouse or a dependent donate an organ or bone marrow in 2026?
- What travel, lodging or lost-wage costs were not reimbursed?

## Common Errors
- Including reimbursed costs
- Claiming for a donor who is not you, your spouse or dependent

## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother this year.
