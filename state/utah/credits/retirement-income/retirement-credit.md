# Retirement Credit
## Description
A small age-based credit for filers born on or before December 31, 1952 (UCA §59-10-1019). Most retirees get more from the Social Security or Military Retirement credits, and cannot combine them with this one.

## Amount
1. $450 for one qualifying person, $900 if both spouses qualify
2. MAGI = TC-40 line 4 − AD007PT + 1040 line 2a
3. Threshold:
   - Married filing separately (and 1040-NR married): $16,000
   - Single: $25,000
   - Married filing jointly, qualifying surviving spouse, head of household: $32,000
4. Credit = line 1 − 2.5% × (MAGI − threshold), not below zero

## Eligibility
- Born on or before December 31, 1952
- Neither spouse claims the Social Security Benefits Credit (AN005PT) or the Military Retirement Credit (AN004PT)

## Notes
- Enter birth date(s) in TC-40_A, Part 1 too
- No carryforward or carryback
- Compare: Social Security Benefits Credit is 4.45% of taxable benefits with much higher thresholds

## Required Information
- Birth dates
- MAGI inputs

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse born on or before December 31, 1952?
- Do you receive taxable Social Security or military retirement pay?

## Common Errors
- Claiming it together with the Social Security or military credit
- Not comparing which credit is larger
- Omitting birth dates
