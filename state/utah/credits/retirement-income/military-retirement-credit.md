# Military Retirement Credit
## Description
Credit equal to Utah tax on military retirement pay, effectively exempting it (UCA §59-10-1043). No income phase-out.

## Amount
- Per person: taxable military retirement pay × 4.45% (2026 rate)
- Total for both spouses → TC-40_A, Part 1

## Qualifies
- Retirement pay for service in the armed forces, including retirement pay received by a SURVIVOR of a deceased retired service member
- Only amounts included in federal AGI on this return

## Does NOT qualify
- Social Security, 401(k), TSP or IRA distributions
- Non-military federal (civil service) retirement
- Survivor benefits for a member who died on active duty or in training — use the Military Survivor Benefits Credit (NN019PT)

## Eligibility
- Neither spouse claims the Retirement Credit (AN003PT)
- CAN be claimed along with the Social Security Benefits Credit

## Required Information
- 1099-R for military retired pay, per person

## Questions
- Do you or your spouse receive military retirement pay?
- Is any of it Survivor Benefit Plan pay, and did the member die on active duty?

## Common Errors
- Using 4.5% (2025)
- Including TSP distributions
- Combining with the Retirement Credit

## Prompt
- I'm a retired Air Force veteran living in Utah.
