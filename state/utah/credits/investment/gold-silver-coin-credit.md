# Gold and Silver Coin Sale Credit
## Description
Credit equal to Utah tax on capital gain from selling or exchanging gold and silver coins issued by the U.S. government (UCA §59-10-1028, Specie Legal Tender Act).

## Amount
1. Capital gains on all 2026 sales/exchanges of gold and silver coins
2. − capital losses on such sales
3. If line 3 is a loss, no credit
4. Federal Schedule D line 16 (if a loss, no credit)
5. Lesser of line 3 or line 4
6. × 4.45%

## Eligibility
- Coins issued by the U.S. government (e.g., American Gold or Silver Eagles), exchanged for another form of legal tender
- Gain reported on federal Schedule D
- Foreign coins or bullion only if a court or Congress makes them legal tender

## Notes
- Bullion bars and foreign coins do not qualify
- No carryforward

## Required Information
- Coin transactions, Schedule D line 16

## Questions
- Did you sell U.S. gold or silver coins at a gain in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Including bullion or foreign coins
- Not netting coin losses
- Using 4.5%

## Prompt
- I sold some American Silver Eagles at a profit.
