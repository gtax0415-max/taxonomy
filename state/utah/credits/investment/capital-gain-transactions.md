# Capital Gain Transactions Credit
## Description
Credit of 4.45% of short- and long-term capital gain from a transaction whose proceeds were reinvested in a qualified Utah small business corporation (UCA §59-10-1022). Effectively exempts that gain from Utah tax.

## Amount
- Eligible capital gain × 4.45% (2026 rate)

## Eligibility
All of:
1. Transaction occurred on or after January 1, 2008
2. At least 70% of the GROSS proceeds were used to buy stock in a qualified Utah small business corporation within 12 months of the transaction
3. You had no ownership interest in that corporation when you invested

## Notes
- No form; keep all documents
- No carryforward or carryback

## Required Information
- Gain amount and date
- Gross proceeds and reinvestment amount and date
- Corporation's qualified status

## Questions
- Did you sell an asset at a gain and reinvest the proceeds in a Utah small business?
- Did you invest at least 70% of the proceeds within 12 months?
- Did you already own part of that business?

## Common Errors
- Measuring 70% of the gain instead of 70% of gross proceeds
- Investing in a company you already owned
- Using 4.5%

## Prompt
- I sold stock and invested the money in a Utah startup.
