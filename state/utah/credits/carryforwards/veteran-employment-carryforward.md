# Carryforward of Veteran Employment Credit
## Description
The veteran employment credit (UCA §59-10-1031) is no longer available for new hires. Unused credit earned BEFORE 2026 may still be carried forward.

## Amount
- Remaining unused credit, up to the tax liability
- Carryforward period: 5 years, through tax year 2030, or until used up

## Notes
- No new credit can be generated in 2026
- Enter in Part 3cc, not Part 3

## Required Information
- Year earned, amount earned and used

## Questions
- Do you have unused Utah veteran employment credit from before 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming new credit for a 2026 hire
- Using the credit beyond 2030

## Prompt
- I hired veterans in 2024 and still have Utah credit left.
