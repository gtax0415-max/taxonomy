# Apportionable Nonrefundable Credit Carryovers
## Description
Unused apportionable nonrefundable credits from earlier years. For 2026, carryovers have their own seven-character codes and go in Part 1cc, not Part 1.

## Amount
- The unused credit remaining from the original year, limited to the 2026 tax left after current-year credits
- Any amount still unused carries to the next year until its carryforward period ends

## Codes and carryforward periods
- AN009PF — Nonrefundable adoption expenses credit (AN009PC): 3 years. 2026 can use unused credit from 2023, 2024 and 2025
- AN012PF — Employer-provided childcare construction credit (AN012PC): 5 years. 2026 can use unused credit from 2021–2025

## Notes
- Apportionable: nonresidents and part-year residents have these reduced through TC-40_B along with current-year credits
- Current-year credits are entered in Part 1; only the prior-year portion goes in Part 1cc
- Track usage oldest year first so credits do not expire unused

## Required Information
- Year each credit was generated, original amount, and amounts used each later year

## Questions
- Do you have unused Utah adoption or childcare-construction credit from a prior year?
- What year was it generated, and how much is left?

## Common Errors
- Entering a carryover under the current-year code (AN009PC / AN012PC)
- Claiming credit beyond its carryforward period
- Using the old two-character codes

## Prompt
- I couldn't use all of my Utah adoption credit last year.
