# Nonapportionable Nonrefundable Credit Carryovers
## Description
Unused nonapportionable nonrefundable credits from earlier years. For 2026 each carryover has its own seven-character code and goes in Part 3cc, not Part 3.

## Amount
- The unused credit remaining from the original year, limited to the 2026 tax left after current-year credits
- Any amount still unused carries to the next year until its carryforward period ends

## Codes confirmed in the 2026 instructions
| Code | Credit | Carryforward |
|---|---|---|
| NN003PF | Historic preservation | 5 years |
| NN006PF | Pass-through entity taxpayer | 10 years |
| NN009PF | Live organ donation | 5 years |
| NN010PF | Renewable residential energy | 4 years (solar carryforwards end 2027) |
| NN012PF | Veteran employment (credit repealed) | Pre-2026 credit through 2030 |
| NN013PF | Employing persons who are homeless | 5 years |
| NN021PF | Carson Smith scholarship, pre-2026 only | Through 2028 |
| NN028PF | Increasing research above base | 14 years |
| NN029PF | Payments to research organizations | 14 years |

## Unverified
The previous version also listed carryover codes for low-income housing, recycling zone, bond interest, high-cost infrastructure, enterprise zone, alternative energy, guaranty association, clean fuel vehicle and rural jobs, citing a "New Credit Codes" list. Those codes are not in the 10/5/26 instructions and the list is not public. Confirm each code against the Tax Commission's list before supporting it.

## Notes
- Track usage oldest year first so credits do not expire unused
- Credits with no carryforward (e.g., NN030PT research volume, NN008PT other-state tax) never appear here

## Required Information
- Year generated, original amount, amounts used each year

## Questions
- Do you have unused Utah credits from prior years? Which ones and from what year?

## Common Errors
- Entering a carryover under the current-year code
- Carrying a credit past its period
- Using the old two-character codes

## Prompt
- I have leftover Utah historic preservation credit from 2023.
