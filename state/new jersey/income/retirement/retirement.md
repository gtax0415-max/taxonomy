# Retirement Income
## Description
Pensions, annuities, and IRA withdrawals. New Jersey taxes the portion that was not previously taxed; the previously taxed portion (NJ taxes most non-401(k) contributions going in) is excludable.
## Items in this folder
- Pensions, Annuities, and IRA Withdrawals (pensions-annuities-iras.md) — Lines 20a (taxable) and 20b (excludable, informational)
## Not taxed at all
Social Security, Railroad Retirement, U.S. military pensions and survivor benefits, and disability pensions before the year the recipient turns 65.
## Related
- Pension/Retirement Exclusion (Line 28a) and Other Retirement Income Exclusion (Line 28b) — see new-jersey/deductions/retirement-exclusions/
## Questions
- Did you receive a Form 1099-R?
- Did you contribute to the plan with after-tax (NJ-taxed) money?
