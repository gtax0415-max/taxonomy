# Compensation Income
## Description
Wages, salaries, tips, bonuses, commissions, and other employee compensation, taken from W-2 Box 16 (State wages) rather than Box 1 (federal wages).
## Items in this folder
- Wages, Salaries, Tips, etc. (wages.md) — Form NJ-1040, Line 15
## What changed for 2026
Nothing in New Jersey law. The federal tip and overtime deductions (OBBBA, 2025–2028) do NOT apply in New Jersey — tips and overtime remain fully taxable wages.
