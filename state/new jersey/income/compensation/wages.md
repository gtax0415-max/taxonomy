# Wages, Salaries, Tips, etc.
## Description
All compensation from employment inside and outside New Jersey — wages, salaries, tips, bonuses, commissions, fees — whether paid in cash, benefits, digital assets, or property. Reported from W-2 Box 16 "State wages," not Box 1.
## Why Box 16 differs from Box 1
- Contributions to retirement plans OTHER THAN 401(k) — 403(b), 457, SEP, federal Thrift Savings Plan — are TAXED by New Jersey when earned, so they stay in State wages. This is why Box 16 is often HIGHER than Box 1
- 401(k) deferrals up to the federal limit are excluded in both
- Some cafeteria plan benefits (salary-reduction and premium conversion plans) are taxable in NJ
## What can be excluded from W-2 wages
- SICK PAY under an accident or health plan — only if (1) it compensates for wage loss from the employee's own injury or sickness, (2) it is paid under an enforceable contractual obligation, and (3) it is NOT discretionary sick-leave wage continuation. Discretionary initial-period pay (e.g., the first seven days) does NOT qualify. Enclose Form NJ-2440 signed by the employer (N.J.A.C. 18:35-2.3)
- EMPLOYEE BUSINESS EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS included in wages — only if job-related, accounted for to the employer, and reimbursed in the exact amount. Unreimbursed employee business expenses are NEVER deductible in NJ
- MOVING EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENTS included in wages — only the cost of moving household goods and the travel/lodging/meals of the move itself, under the federal rules in effect on December 31, 2017. Moving expenses themselves are not deductible
- MEALS AND LODGING — only if on the employer's premises, for the employer's convenience, and (for lodging) required as a condition of employment. Enclose a signed statement
## Notes
- Federal "statutory employee" status means nothing in NJ; business expenses are deductible only by the self-employed on Schedule NJ-BUS-1
- Part-year residents: include only Box 16 wages earned while a resident; apportion by time if the employer did not separate resident and nonresident wages
- Enclose all W-2s with the return (do not staple)
- Tips and overtime are fully taxable; the 2025–2028 federal deductions have no NJ equivalent
## Required Information
- Every W-2 (Boxes 1, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17)
- Form NJ-2440 from the employer for excludable sick pay
- Records of reimbursed business or moving expenses
- Residency dates if part-year
## Questions
- How many W-2s do you have, and does Box 15 show NJ?
- Do you contribute to a 403(b), 457, or TSP rather than a 401(k)?
- Did you receive sick pay under an employer plan, and can your employer sign Form NJ-2440?
- Did your employer include reimbursements for business or moving expenses in your wages?
- Did you work in another state or city that taxed these wages?
## Common Errors
- Using federal Box 1 wages instead of State Box 16 wages
- Subtracting 403(b)/457/TSP contributions as if they were 401(k) contributions
- Deducting unreimbursed employee business expenses or moving expenses
- Excluding discretionary sick-leave pay or the initial waiting-period pay
- Excluding tips or overtime because of the federal OBBBA deductions
- Part-year residents reporting full-year wages
## Prompt
- My NJ wages on my W-2 are higher than my federal wages.
- I was out sick and got paid under my employer's disability plan.
- Do I pay New Jersey tax on my tips and overtime?
