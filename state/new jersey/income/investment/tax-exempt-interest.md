# Tax-Exempt Interest Income
## Description
Interest New Jersey does not tax, listed on Line 16b for information only. It is NOT added into Line 27.
## What goes here
- Interest on obligations of New Jersey and its political subdivisions
- Interest on direct federal obligations (U.S. Savings Bonds, Treasury bills, notes, bonds)
- The exempt portion of distributions from a NEW JERSEY QUALIFIED INVESTMENT FUND
- Mutual fund distributions attributable to federal-obligation interest
## Not here
- IRA interest — IRAs are handled only when withdrawn (Lines 20a/20b)
- Other states' municipal interest — TAXABLE, Line 16a
## Why it still matters
Line 16b does not change NJ tax, but it is used by programs that count it as income (for example, property tax relief programs and the federal premium tax credit, which counts tax-exempt interest in MAGI).
## Questions
- Do you own New Jersey municipal bonds or a NJ tax-free fund?
- Does your fund provide a year-end NJ-exempt percentage?
## Common Errors
- Adding Line 16b into total income
- Listing out-of-state municipal interest here
- Not enclosing the source listing when over $10,000

- Where do I report my New Jersey muni fund income?
