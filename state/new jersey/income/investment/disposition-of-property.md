# Net Gains or Income From Disposition of Property (Schedule NJ-DOP)
## Description
Net gains from selling, exchanging, or otherwise disposing of real or personal property, tangible or intangible — stocks, bonds, real estate, collectibles, digital assets — netted within this one category.
## How Schedule NJ-DOP works
- Line 1: each NJ-taxable transaction as on federal Schedule D (description, dates, sales price, basis and expense of sale, gain or loss)
- Line 2: capital gain distributions (excluding gains from NJ qualified investment funds attributable to exempt obligations and fund gains attributable to federal obligations)
- Line 3: other net gains not on Lines 1–2
- Line 4: net of Lines 1–3. If a loss, enter zero; nothing goes to Line 19
## Big change for 2026: QSBS exclusion
For dispositions on or after January 1, 2026, gain on qualified small business stock is excluded from NJ gross income to the extent it is excluded federally under IRC §1202. This applies to stock acquired before 2026 as well, and follows the OBBBA enhancements for stock issued after July 4, 2025 (tiered 50%/75%/100% exclusion after 3/4/5 years; per-issuer cap the greater of $10 million — $15 million for stock issued after July 4, 2025 — or 10× basis). Before 2026, this gain was fully taxable in New Jersey.
## Basis rules
- Generally use federal basis, BUT:
- S CORPORATION SHARES: must use NJ adjusted basis
- Partnership interests, sole proprietorships, rental property: may require NJ adjusted basis (NJ depreciation differs — Worksheet GIT-DEP)
- Installment sales: report gain in the same year as federally; if NJ basis differs, compute NJ gain separately
## Other rules
- Sale of main home: taxable to the same extent as federally (the federal §121 exclusion flows through)
- Gains/losses on exempt obligations (NJ municipal, U.S. Treasury) are excluded
- No active/passive distinction; no carryback or carryforward of losses
- Complete liquidation of a pass-through entity: report your share of gain or loss on the asset sale
- Part-year residents: include only transactions while a resident
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule D / Form 8949 and all 1099-B, 1099-DA, 1099-S
- NJ basis for S corporation stock, partnership interests, and depreciated property
- Worksheet GIT-DEP for depreciated assets sold
- QSBS documentation (issue date, holding period, §1202 exclusion computed federally)
## Questions
- Did you sell stocks, real estate, or digital assets in 2026?
- Did you sell S corporation stock or a partnership interest?
- Did you sell stock that qualifies as qualified small business stock?
- Did you sell property on which you claimed §179 or bonus depreciation?
- Did you sell your home?
## Common Errors
- Carrying a federal capital loss carryover onto NJ-DOP (not allowed)
- Using federal basis for S corporation stock
- Omitting the GIT-DEP adjustment on sale of depreciated property
- Missing the 2026 QSBS exclusion — or claiming it for a 2025 sale
- Entering a negative amount on Line 19
