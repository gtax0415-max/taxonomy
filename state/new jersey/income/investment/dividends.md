# Dividends
## Description
Taxable dividends from stocks, mutual funds, and other investments not constituting a trade or business, regardless of where earned.
## How it works
- Qualified and nonqualified dividends are taxed the same — there is no preferential rate in NJ
- Subtract the portion of fund dividends attributable to federal-obligation interest (exempt)
- Return-of-capital (tax-free) distributions reduce basis and are not taxed until basis is recovered
- Insurance company "dividends" are taxable only to the extent they exceed premiums paid; interest on accumulated insurance dividends goes on Line 16a
## Notes
- Part-year residents: include only dividends received while a resident
## Required Information
- All Forms 1099-DIV
- Fund statements showing the percentage from U.S. obligations
## Questions
- Do your mutual funds report a percentage of income from U.S. government obligations?
- Did you receive capital gain distributions?
## Common Errors
- Reporting capital gain distributions on Line 17 instead of Schedule NJ-DOP
- Not excluding the federal-obligation portion of fund dividends
## Prompt
- I have a 1099-DIV from my brokerage.
