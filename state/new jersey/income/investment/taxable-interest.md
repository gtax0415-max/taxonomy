# Taxable Interest Income
## Description
All taxable interest from sources inside and outside New Jersey.
## Taxable in NJ
- Bank, savings and loan, and credit union interest; CDs; savings and checking accounts
- Corporate bonds and notes; Ginnie Mae, Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac; repurchase agreements
- Interest on obligations of OTHER STATES and their subdivisions (exempt federally, taxable in NJ)
- Life insurance dividend interest
- Earnings portion of nonqualified distributions from 529 plans (including NJBEST), 529A ABLE accounts, and Coverdell ESAs
- Grantor trust interest
- Interest on income tax refunds
## Not on this line
- U.S. Treasury and NJ municipal interest → exempt (list on Line 16b)
- Interest earned by a business → Schedule NJ-BUS-1
- Estate/trust interest (non-grantor) → Line 26
## Notes
- The forfeiture penalty for early withdrawal of a time deposit can be subtracted from interest
- If Line 16a + Line 16b differs from federal interest, enclose a statement explaining the difference
- Part-year residents: include only interest received while a resident
## Required Information
- All Forms 1099-INT / 1099-OID with issuer detail for Box 8
- Early withdrawal penalties
- Federal Schedule B if over $1,500
## Questions
- Do any of your 1099-INTs show U.S. Treasury interest in Box 3?
- Do you have tax-exempt interest from bonds issued outside New Jersey?
- Did you pay an early withdrawal penalty?
## Common Errors
- Including U.S. Treasury interest (it is exempt)
- Omitting out-of-state municipal bond interest (it is taxable)
- Reporting business interest here instead of on NJ-BUS-1
## Prompt
- My 1099-INT has interest in Box 3 and Box 8.
- I own New York municipal bonds.
