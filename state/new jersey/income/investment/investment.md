# Investment Income
## Description
Interest, dividends, and gains from selling property held outside a business. Each is its own category.
## Items in this folder
- Taxable Interest (taxable-interest.md) — Line 16a
- Tax-Exempt Interest (tax-exempt-interest.md) — Line 16b, informational
- Dividends (dividends.md) — Line 17
- Net Gains or Income From Disposition of Property (disposition-of-property.md) — Schedule NJ-DOP → Line 19
## What changed for 2026
Gain on QUALIFIED SMALL BUSINESS STOCK sold on or after January 1, 2026 is excluded to the same extent as federally under IRC §1202.
## Key differences from federal
- Interest on OTHER states' municipal bonds is TAXABLE in NJ
- U.S. Treasury and NJ municipal interest (and gains on them) are EXEMPT
- There is no preferential capital gains rate — gains are taxed at ordinary NJ rates
- Capital losses cannot offset interest, dividends, or any other category, and cannot be carried forward
- Capital gain distributions go on Schedule NJ-DOP, not the dividend line
