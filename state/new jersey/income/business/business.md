# Business Income
## Description
Four SEPARATE New Jersey income categories, all summarized on Schedule NJ-BUS-1. A loss in one category cannot offset income in another category, except through the limited Alternative Business Calculation Adjustment (Line 35).
## Items in this folder
- Net Profits From Business (net-profits-from-business.md) — NJ-BUS-1 Part I → Line 18
- Distributive Share of Partnership Income (partnership-income.md) — NJ-BUS-1 Part II → Line 21
- Net Pro Rata Share of S Corporation Income (s-corporation-income.md) — NJ-BUS-1 Part III → Line 22
- Net Gains or Income From Rents, Royalties, Patents, and Copyrights (rents-royalties-patents-copyrights.md) — NJ-BUS-1 Part IV → Line 23
- Depreciation Adjustment (depreciation-adjustment.md) — Worksheet GIT-DEP, adjusts all of the above and Line 19
## What changed for 2026
- The Alternative Business Calculation Adjustment for cross-category business losses is reduced to 25% for gross income over $500,000 up to $1 million and eliminated above $1 million (FY2027 budget) — see new-jersey/deductions/business/alternative-business-calculation-adjustment.md
- New Jersey still does NOT follow the federal §179 limit or bonus depreciation above 30%; the gap is larger now that federal bonus depreciation is 100%
