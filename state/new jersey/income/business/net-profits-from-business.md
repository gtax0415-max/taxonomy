# Net Profits From Business (Schedule NJ-BUS-1, Part I)
## Description
Net profit or loss from a sole proprietorship, trade, or profession, starting from federal Schedule C (or F) and adjusted to New Jersey rules. Multiple businesses are netted together within this category.
## Required adjustments to federal Schedule C/F
1. ADD back any deduction for taxes based on income
2. SUBTRACT interest reported federally that is exempt in NJ (e.g., U.S. obligations)
3. ADD interest from other states' obligations that is exempt federally
4. DEDUCT ordinary business meal and entertainment expenses disallowed federally
5. DEDUCT qualified contributions to a self-employed 401(k) (not above the federal limit). Other self-employed retirement contributions (SEP, SIMPLE, defined benefit) are NOT deductible
6. ADD interest and dividends derived in the conduct of the business
7. ADD or SUBTRACT business rental, royalty, patent, or copyright income/loss
8. ADD or SUBTRACT gains/losses on disposition of business property
9. ADD or SUBTRACT the GIT-DEP adjustment (Part I, Line 7)
10. CANNABIS LICENSEES: deduct ordinary expenses disallowed federally under IRC §280E
## Notes
- Enclose Schedule NJ-BUS-1 and federal Schedule C/F for each business
- Self-employed health insurance is NOT deducted here — it goes on Line 31 through Worksheet F
- Federal statutory employees are treated as employees in NJ and cannot deduct expenses here unless self-employed under NJ law
- Part-year residents: include only amounts while a resident
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule C/F for each business, with name and SSN/EIN
- Detail of state income taxes deducted, interest and dividends, business property sales
- Worksheet GIT-DEP
- Self-employed 401(k) contributions
## Questions
- Did you deduct state income taxes on Schedule C?
- Does the business earn interest, dividends, or rental income?
- Did you sell business equipment?
- Did you claim §179 or bonus depreciation federally?
- Do you contribute to a solo 401(k) or to a SEP/SIMPLE?
## Common Errors
- Reporting a negative amount on Line 18
- Deducting SEP or SIMPLE contributions (only 401(k) contributions are deductible)
- Skipping the GIT-DEP depreciation adjustment
- Deducting self-employed health insurance here instead of on Line 31
## Prompt
- I'm self-employed and file a Schedule C.
- I'm a freelancer with 1099-NEC income.
