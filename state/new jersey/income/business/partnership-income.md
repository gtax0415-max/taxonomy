# Distributive Share of Partnership Income (Schedule NJ-BUS-1, Part II)
## Description
The taxpayer's share of partnership (and multi-member LLC) income or loss, whether or not distributed, computed under New Jersey rules. All partnerships are netted within this category.
## How it works
- Use the NJ amount from Schedule NJK-1, not the federal K-1 amounts
- NJ distributive share already includes the entity's NJ adjustments (including GIT-DEP depreciation)
- Enter each partnership's BAIT share in the separate column; the total goes to Line 63 as a refundable credit
- Guaranteed payments and partnership-level items are folded into the NJ distributive share per NJK-1 — do not split them onto other lines unless the NJK-1 instructs
## Notes
- Enclose Schedule NJ-BUS-1 and each NJK-1 (or federal K-1)
- Part-year residents: prorate by the number of days in the partnership's fiscal year the taxpayer was a resident
- See GIT-9P, Partnership Income
## Required Information
- Schedule NJK-1 for each partnership (or federal K-1 plus GIT-9P Worksheet A)
- Schedule PTE-K-1 for BAIT shares
## Questions
- Did each partnership give you a Schedule NJK-1?
- Did any partnership elect the BAIT and send a PTE-K-1?
## Common Errors
- Using federal K-1 ordinary income instead of the NJK-1 figure
- Netting a partnership loss against wages or S corporation income
- Omitting the BAIT share (and the Line 63 credit)
## Prompt
- I got an NJK-1 from my LLC.
- My partnership lost money this year.
