# Net Gains or Income From Rents, Royalties, Patents, and Copyrights (Schedule NJ-BUS-1, Part IV)
## Description
Net income from rental real estate, royalties, patents, and copyrights held directly. Each property is listed with a type code (1 rental real estate, 2 royalties, 3 patents, 4 copyrights; rental real estate shows the physical address), then netted within this category.
## How it works
- Start from federal Schedule E, then apply the GIT-DEP adjustments from Lines 4 and 6 (depreciation and §179 recapture)
- FEDERAL PASSIVE LOSS LIMITS DO NOT APPLY. Passive losses are deductible in full in the year incurred against other income in THIS category only
- Suspended federal passive losses and NJ losses cannot be carried forward
## Notes
- Enclose Schedule NJ-BUS-1
- Part-year residents: include only amounts while a resident
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule E and 1099-MISC
- Property addresses and type codes
- Worksheet GIT-DEP covering ALL properties in this category on one worksheet
## Questions
- Do you own rental real estate, and where is each property?
- Did you receive royalties or patent/copyright income?
- Did you claim §179 or bonus depreciation on any rental asset?
## Common Errors
- Applying federal passive loss limits or releasing suspended passive losses
- Netting a rental loss against wages
- Omitting the GIT-DEP adjustment
## Prompt
- I own a rental property that lost money.
- I receive book royalties.
