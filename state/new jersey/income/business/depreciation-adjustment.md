# Depreciation Adjustment (Worksheet GIT-DEP)
## Description
New Jersey decoupled from federal bonus depreciation and the federal §179 limit for assets placed in service on or after January 1, 2004. Worksheet GIT-DEP computes the difference and adjusts each affected income category, in the year of purchase, every later year, and on disposition.
## New Jersey rules
- §179 deduction limited to $25,000 total per taxpayer per category (NJ reduced-dollar limitation uses the $25,000 cap; no business income limit; unused amounts are NOT carried forward). New York Liberty Zone property: up to $60,000
- 30% special depreciation allowance: allowed if taken federally
- 50% (and larger) bonus depreciation: NOT allowed
- NJ depreciation uses the SAME method and life as federal, applied to the NJ basis
- Unused depreciation adjustment cannot be carried forward or back or used against another category
- NEW (tax years beginning on/after 1/1/2025): alternate 10-year depreciation for capital costs of new affordable housing developments (construction begun on/after March 20, 2024, at least 20% affordable units, no long-term tax exemption abatement, no construction subsidy). Deductible percentage = (affordable units ÷ total units) × 2
## Why it matters more in 2026
Federal law now allows 100% bonus depreciation permanently and a far higher §179 limit, while NJ's limits are unchanged. The federal-NJ gap on newly placed assets is at its widest, and so is the later reversal on sale.
## Worksheet structure
- Part I: summary and routing (Lines 1–8)
- Part II: NJ basis and NJ depreciation per asset
- Part II(a): affordable housing alternate depreciation
- Part III: NJ adjustment to federal §179 recapture income
- Part IV: NJ adjustment to federal gain or loss on disposition
## Notes
- One GIT-DEP per income category covering ALL assets in that category
- If a required adjustment was never reported (error or no NJ filing requirement at the time), NJ basis equals federal basis going forward
- Keep all worksheets; they are needed every year until the asset is fully depreciated or sold
## Required Information
- Federal Form 4562 detail by asset: placed-in-service date, basis, §179, bonus, method, life
- Prior-year GIT-DEPs
## Questions
- Did you claim §179 or bonus depreciation federally on any asset?
- Did you sell any asset on which NJ depreciation differed?
- Did you build an affordable housing development?
## Common Errors
- Using federal depreciation on the NJ return
- Forgetting the reversal adjustment when the asset is sold
- Applying the $25,000 §179 cap per asset instead of per category
## Prompt
- I took bonus depreciation on my rental. What does New Jersey allow?
- I bought equipment and expensed it under Section 179.
