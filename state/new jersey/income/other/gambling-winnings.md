# Net Gambling Winnings
## Description
Gambling and lottery winnings, NET of gambling and lottery losses from the SAME year. Unlike federal law, NJ nets losses directly against winnings without itemizing.
## Taxable
- All casino and track betting (including online and sports wagering)
- NJ Lottery and multistate games NJ participates in (Powerball, Mega Millions) — ONLY individual prizes over $10,000
- ALL out-of-state lottery winnings, regardless of amount
- Bingo winnings
## How it works
- Subtract same-year gambling losses; if the result is zero or less, leave Line 24 blank
- Losses cannot offset any other category and cannot be carried to another year
## 2026 note
Federal law beginning in 2026 limits deductible wagering losses to 90% for itemizers. That change is in the federal itemized deduction and does NOT alter New Jersey's netting rule on Line 24 as described in the NJ instructions — CONFIRM in the 2026 NJ-1040 instructions.
## Substantiation
Casino win/loss statements, a daily log, canceled checks, losing pari-mutuel tickets, losing lottery tickets. See TB-20(R).
## Questions
- What were your total winnings, and do you have W-2Gs?
- Can you document your gambling losses for the same year?
- Were any lottery prizes from another state?
## Common Errors
- Reporting NJ Lottery prizes of $10,000 or less
- Omitting out-of-state lottery winnings under $10,000
- Netting gambling losses against wages
- Netting without a statement or substantiation
## Prompt
- I won $5,000 in Atlantic City but lost $3,000 over the year.
- I won $600 on a Pennsylvania lottery ticket.
