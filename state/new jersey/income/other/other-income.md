# Other Income
## Description
Catch-all for taxable income with no other line, from legal or illegal sources.
## What goes here
- PRIZES AND AWARDS — raffles, drawings, contests, TV/radio prizes, at fair market value for goods and services
- INCOME IN RESPECT OF A DECEDENT not on the decedent's final return — enclose a listing
- ESTATE AND TRUST distributions from non-grantor trusts (NJK-1 Total Distribution) — enclose the NJK-1 or K-1
- SCHOLARSHIPS AND FELLOWSHIPS — taxable UNLESS all three are true: primary purpose is education; not payment for services under the grantor's direction (e.g., teaching); not for the grantor's benefit
- EMPLOYER-PROVIDED HOUSING — rental value or allowance, unless on business premises, for the employer's convenience, and required as a condition of employment
- Any other taxable income not reported elsewhere (e.g., jury duty fees)
## Questions
- Did you win a prize or award?
- Did you receive a distribution from an estate or trust? Is it a grantor trust?
- Did you receive a scholarship that required teaching or research?
- Does your employer provide your housing?
## Common Errors
- Reporting grantor trust income here instead of in its own category
- Treating a teaching or research assistantship as an excludable scholarship
- Omitting non-cash prizes
## Prompt
- I won a car in a raffle.
- I'm a beneficiary of my mother's trust.
