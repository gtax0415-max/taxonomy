# Other Income
## Description
Gambling winnings, alimony received, and a catch-all line for prizes, estate and trust income, income in respect of a decedent, taxable scholarships, and employer-provided housing.
## Items in this folder
- Net Gambling Winnings (gambling-winnings.md) — Line 24
- Alimony and Separate Maintenance Received (alimony-received.md) — Line 25
- Other Income (other-income.md) — Line 26
## Questions
- Did you have gambling or lottery winnings?
- Do you receive court-ordered alimony?
- Did you receive a prize, an inheritance of untaxed income, or income from an estate or trust?

- I won money at a casino in Atlantic City.
