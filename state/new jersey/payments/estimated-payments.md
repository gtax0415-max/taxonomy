# New Jersey Estimated Tax Payments / Credit From 2025 Return (Line 57)
## Description
All payments made toward 2026 NJ tax other than withholding.
## Include
- 2026 estimated payments (Form NJ-1040-ES)
- Any payment made with an extension request (Form NJ-630 or online)
- The overpayment from the 2025 NJ-1040 that was credited forward (2025 Line 69)
## Do not include
- Refunds from prior years
- Tax paid on the taxpayer's behalf by a partnership
## Who should make estimated payments
Anyone who expects to owe more than $400 in 2026 NJ tax after withholding and credits — typically the self-employed, investors, retirees without withholding, and partners.
## 2026 due dates
| Installment | Due date |
|---|---|
| 1 | April 15, 2026 |
| 2 | June 15, 2026 |
| 3 | September 15, 2026 |
| 4 | January 15, 2027 |
## Notes
- Payments made under a former name or another SSN (marriage, divorce, death of a spouse) need an enclosed statement listing each payment and the name/SSN used
- Part-year residents: include only payments made toward resident-period tax
- A separate payment is required for a 2026 balance due and a 2027 estimate
- See GIT-8, Estimating Income Taxes
## Questions
- Did you make 2026 estimated payments? How much and when?
- Did you pay anything with an extension?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?
## Common Errors
- Forgetting the 2025 overpayment credited forward
- Including a prior-year refund
- Including payments made under a spouse's SSN without a statement
## Prompt
- I made quarterly estimated payments to New Jersey.
