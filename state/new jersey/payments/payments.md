# Payments (Lines 55–66)
## Description
Tax already paid for 2026 — withholding and estimated payments. They sit in the same block as the refundable credits (Lines 56 and 58–65), and all of them add up on Line 66.
## Items in this folder
- New Jersey Income Tax Withheld (withholding.md) — Line 55
- Estimated Payments, Extension Payments, and Prior-Year Overpayment Credit (estimated-payments.md) — Line 57
## Line 66 — Total withholdings, credits, and payments
Add Lines 55 through 65:
- 55 withholding
- 56 property tax credit
- 57 estimated payments / credit from 2025
- 58 NJ EITC
- 59–61 excess UI/WF/SWF, DI, FLI
- 62 Wounded Warrior Caregivers Credit
- 63 BAIT credit
- 64 Child and Dependent Care Credit
- 65 NJ Child Tax Credit
