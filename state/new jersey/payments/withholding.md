# Total New Jersey Income Tax Withheld (Line 55)
## Description
New Jersey gross income tax withheld during 2026 on wages, gambling winnings, pensions, and other payments.
## Where to find it
| Form | NJ withholding box | State box must show NJ |
|---|---|---|
| W-2 | Box 17 | Box 15 |
| W-2G | Box 15 | Box 13 |
| 1099-R | Box 14 | Box 15 |
| 1099-MISC | Box 16 | Box 17 |
| 1099-NEC | Box 5 | Box 6 |
## Notes
- Enclose W-2s and 1099s with the return
- Only NJ income tax — not other states' tax, not NJ UI/DI/FLI contributions, not tax paid by a partnership
- Part-year residents: include only NJ tax withheld while a resident; if the employer combined resident and nonresident periods, include only the resident-period withholding
- To change 2027 withholding, give the employer Form NJ-W4 (or the pension payer Form NJ-W-4P)
## Questions
- Do all your W-2s and 1099s show New Jersey in the state box?
- Were any NJ amounts withheld while you were a nonresident?
## Common Errors
- Including other states' withholding or NJ UI/DI/FLI contributions
- Including partnership-paid tax
- Missing withholding on 1099-R or W-2G
## Prompt
- How much New Jersey tax was withheld from my paycheck?
