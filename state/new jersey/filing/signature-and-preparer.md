# Signature, Preparer, and Other Page 4 Items
## Signature
- Sign and date in blue or black ink; BOTH spouses sign a joint return; photocopied signatures are not accepted
- Unsigned returns are sent back, which can trigger late-filing penalties
## Driver's license number (voluntary)
Driver's license or NJ non-driver ID number helps the Division verify identity and prevent refund fraud.
## Ovals on the signature block
- DEATH CERTIFICATE ENCLOSED — for a deceased taxpayer; enclose the death certificate (and the Surrogate's Short Certificate for an estate) to have the refund issued to the surviving spouse or estate
- NO PAPER FORM NEXT YEAR
- PREPARER AUTHORIZATION — lets the Division discuss the return with the paid preparer who signed it
- NJ-1040-O ENCLOSED — when a preparer subject to the e-file mandate files on paper at the taxpayer's request; both taxpayer and preparer sign Form NJ-1040-O
## Paid preparers
- Must sign and enter an SSN or PTIN, and the firm name and FEIN; $25 penalty per omission
- Preparers who expect to prepare 11 or more NJ resident returns must e-file; $50 penalty per return not e-filed
- Unpaid helpers do not sign
## Gubernatorial Elections Fund
Taxpayer (and spouse on a joint return) may designate $1 each; it does NOT reduce the refund or increase the tax due. See new-jersey/refund-balance-due/gubernatorial-elections-fund.md
## Questions
- Is this a joint return, and will both spouses sign?
- Are you filing for a deceased taxpayer?
- Did a paid preparer prepare the return?
## Prompt
- My husband died this year. How do I sign our return?
