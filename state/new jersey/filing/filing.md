# Filing (Form NJ-1040 Page 1 Header, Lines 1–5, and Signature Block)
## Description
Everything on the NJ-1040 that is not income, deductions, tax, credits, or payments: who must file, residency, filing status, the digital asset question, identification boxes, and the signature and preparer section.
## Items in this folder
- Filing Requirements and Residency (filing-requirements.md) — header boxes, part-year dates, fiscal year, extension oval, county/municipality code, digital assets question
- Filing Status (filing-status.md) — Lines 1–5
- Signature, Preparer, and Other Page 4 Items (signature-and-preparer.md)
## 2026 key dates
- 2026 NJ-1040 due: April 15, 2027
- Extended due date: October 15, 2027 (extension is to FILE, not to pay)
- 2026 estimated payments: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027
