# Low Embodied Carbon Concrete Tax Credit (Form GIT-337)
## Description
Credit for producers of low embodied carbon concrete, or concrete using carbon capture, utilization, and storage, that is either delivered under a State contract (or to a State contractor, at least 50 cubic yards) or has an approved environmental product declaration from DEP.
## Calculation
- Available = current-year certificate amount + carryforward
- Limited to tax after credits (Line 50) minus other off-return credits
- Must first be used in the year the certificate is issued; carries forward SEVEN years
## Questions
- Are you a concrete producer with a DEP-approved credit certificate?
## Prompt
- Our company produces low-carbon concrete for state projects.
