# Film and Digital Media Tax Credit (Form GIT-327)
## Description
Credit for qualified film production and digital media content production expenses incurred between July 1, 2018 and July 1, 2049, approved by the NJ Economic Development Authority. Also usable by purchasers through the EDA tax benefit transfer program.
## Calculation
- Available = current-year certificate amount + carryforward
- Limit = total tax due (Line 54) minus withholdings, credits, and payments (Line 66) and other off-return credits; not below zero
- Allowable = lesser of the two; excess carries forward
- Carryforward: SEVEN years from the tax period for which the certificate was issued
## Notes
- May first be used for the period for which issued, the period in which issued, or any period the project must be maintained in NJ
- Credits may be sold back under the Director's Tax Credit Purchase Program
## Questions
- Do you hold an original or purchased film/digital media credit certificate?
## Common Errors
- Counting carryforward on Line 4 (it goes on Line 5)
- Letting the seven-year window lapse

- I bought New Jersey film tax credits.
