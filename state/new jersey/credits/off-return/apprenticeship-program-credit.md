# Apprenticeship Program Tax Credit (Form GIT-330)
## Description
Credit for documented start-up costs in the INITIAL year of participation in an approved apprenticeship program.
## Amount
- $5,000, plus an additional $5,000 ($10,000 total) for programs in key industries such as manufacturing, construction, healthcare, logistics, pharmaceuticals, transportation, tourism, and renewable energy
## Calculation
- Limited to tax after credits (Line 50) minus other off-return credits
- Must be used in the initial year; NO carryforward
- Costs used here cannot be used for any other credit, exemption, or incentive for the same period
## Questions
- Do you have a DOL approval letter for the Apprenticeship Start-Up Grant Program?
## Prompt
- We started an apprenticeship program this year.
