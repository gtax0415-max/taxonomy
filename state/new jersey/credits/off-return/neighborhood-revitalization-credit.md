# Neighborhood Revitalization State Tax Credit (Form GIT-311)
## Description
Credit for a business that provided funds to a qualified neighborhood preservation and revitalization project approved by the NJ Department of Community Affairs (credits up to 100% of approved assistance).
## Calculation
- Available: lesser of the DCA-approved amount or $1,000,000
- Limited to tax attributable to the earning business: NJ tax (Line 43) × (that business's NJ-BUS-1 income ÷ NJ gross income, 6 decimals). Reduce the business's income by losses from other businesses in the same category
- Allowable = lesser of the two
- NO carryover — unused credit is forfeited
## Eligibility
- Holds a DCA tax credit certificate, and provided the assistance in the same year the certificate was issued
## Questions
- Do you have a DCA certificate issued this year?
## Common Errors
- Expecting to carry unused credit forward
## Prompt
- My company funded a neighborhood revitalization project.
