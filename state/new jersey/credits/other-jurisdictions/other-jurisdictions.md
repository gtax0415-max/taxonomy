# Credits for Taxes Paid to Other Jurisdictions
## Description
New Jersey residents are taxed on income from everywhere. When another state or city also taxes the same income, New Jersey gives a credit so the income is not fully taxed twice.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Income Taxes Paid to Other Jurisdictions (credit-for-taxes-paid-to-other-jurisdictions.md) — Schedule NJ-COJ → Form NJ-1040, Line 44
## Federal comparison
This is the state-level counterpart to the federal foreign tax credit, but with the OPPOSITE scope: New Jersey gives credit only for taxes paid to other U.S. states, their political subdivisions, and D.C. — and NOT for taxes paid to the U.S. Government, Puerto Rico, or any foreign country or territory. The federal foreign tax credit (federal/credits/foreign-tax/foreign-tax-credit.md) has no New Jersey equivalent.
## Questions
- Did you work in, or earn income from, another state or city?
- Did you file a nonresident return in another state?
- Do you work in Pennsylvania or Philadelphia?
## Prompt
- I live in New Jersey and work in New York.
- I paid Philadelphia wage tax.
