# Earned Income Credits
## Description
Refundable New Jersey credit for low- and moderate-income workers, built on the federal Earned Income Credit.
## Credits in this folder
- New Jersey Earned Income Tax Credit (nj-earned-income-tax-credit.md) — Form NJ-1040, Line 58. Refundable
## What changed for 2026
New Jersey law did not change. The NJ credit is 40% of the FEDERAL credit, so it rises automatically with the 2026 federal inflation adjustments (Rev. Proc. 2025-32).
