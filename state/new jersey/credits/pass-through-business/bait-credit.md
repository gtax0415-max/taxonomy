# Pass-Through Business Alternative Income Tax (BAIT) Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for the owner's share of the BAIT paid by an electing New Jersey partnership, LLC taxed as a partnership, or S corporation. Because the entity already paid New Jersey tax on the income, the owner receives a credit that can offset tax and be refunded.
## How it works
- The electing entity pays the BAIT and issues each owner a Schedule PTE-K-1 showing the owner's share of tax
- The owner reports the distributive share of income as usual (Line 21 or 22) and takes the credit on Line 63 through Schedule NJ-BUS-1
- Treated as a PAYMENT — refundable
## Federal interaction for 2026
The entity-level tax is deducted on the entity's federal return, so it reduces the owner's federal income without counting against the individual SALT cap. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act raised the federal SALT cap (with a phase-down for high incomes) but did not eliminate the entity-level workaround, so the BAIT election still produces a federal benefit for many owners. CONFIRM the 2026 federal SALT cap figures before modeling the benefit.
## Eligibility
- Owner of an entity that made a valid BAIT election for the tax year
- Received a Schedule PTE-K-1 (or an NJK-1 from an estate or trust passing through the credit)
## Notes
- Enclose Schedule NJ-BUS-1 and copies of Schedule PTE-K-1, and the NJK-1 or NJ-K-1 as applicable
- Do not include BAIT paid by the entity in NJ withholding (Line 55) or estimated payments (Line 57) — it belongs only on Line 63
- A resident who paid a SIMILAR entity-level tax to another state on the same income should evaluate Schedule NJ-COJ (see new-jersey/credits/other-jurisdictions/credit-for-taxes-paid-to-other-jurisdictions.md)
## Required Information
- Schedule PTE-K-1 for each electing entity
- Schedule NJK-1 / NJ-K-1 income detail
- Schedule NJ-BUS-1
## Questions
- Which of your entities elected the BAIT for 2026?
- Do you have a Schedule PTE-K-1 for each?
- Did any entity pay a similar tax to another state?
## Common Errors
- Reporting the BAIT share as NJ withholding or estimated payments instead of on Line 63
- Omitting Schedule NJ-BUS-1 or the PTE-K-1
- Treating the credit as nonrefundable and leaving the excess unclaimed
## Prompt
- My S corporation elected the BAIT. How do I get credit for it?
- I have a PTE-K-1 showing New Jersey tax paid.
