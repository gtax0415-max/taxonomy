# Pass-Through Business Credits
## Description
Credit for an owner's share of the New Jersey Pass-Through Business Alternative Income Tax (BAIT) paid at the entity level by an electing partnership or S corporation.
## Credits in this folder
- Pass-Through Business Alternative Income Tax Credit (bait-credit.md) — Schedule NJ-BUS-1 → Form NJ-1040, Line 63. Refundable
## Why the BAIT exists
The BAIT is New Jersey's entity-level tax election. The entity pays the tax and deducts it federally, which sidesteps the federal cap on the individual state and local tax (SALT) deduction. The owner then claims a NJ credit for their share so the income is not taxed twice in New Jersey.
