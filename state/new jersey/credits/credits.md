# New Jersey Credits (Form NJ-1040)
## Description
Top-level index of the credits a New Jersey resident claims on Form NJ-1040. New Jersey credits fall into two groups by WHERE they sit on the return, and that location decides whether they are refundable.
- NONREFUNDABLE (reduce tax only): Lines 44 and 46–48. Subtracted from tax on Line 43 before Line 50. They can take tax to zero but never below
- REFUNDABLE (treated as payments): Lines 56 and 58–65. Added together with withholding on Line 66. Any excess over Line 54 becomes a refund on Line 80
## Line numbers — read this first
The 2026 Form NJ-1040 and instructions are not yet published (they are normally released around the turn of the year). Every line number in this taxonomy is taken from the 2025 Form NJ-1040 and its instructions. New Jersey renumbers lines frequently, so CONFIRM every line against the 2026 form when it is released.

## What changed for 2026
- NJ CHILD TAX CREDIT UP 25% for tax years 2026–2028 (S-4531, P.L.2026, c.26, enacted with the FY2027 budget). Top tier rises from $1,000 to $1,250 per child. Reverts to prior amounts in 2029
- FEDERAL-LINKED CREDITS MOVE WITH FEDERAL LAW. The NJ EITC (40% of federal EIC) and the NJ Child and Dependent Care Credit (a percentage of the federal credit) automatically change when the federal credit changes. For 2026 the federal EIC is inflation-adjusted and the federal dependent care credit is enhanced under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, so both NJ credits change even though New Jersey did not amend them
- EXCESS PAYROLL CONTRIBUTION MAXIMUMS RESET. 2026 wage bases are $44,800 (UI/WF/SWF) and $171,100 (DI/FLI); DI and FLI employee rates dropped
## Federal-to-New Jersey crosswalk
| Federal credit | New Jersey counterpart |
|---|---|
| Earned Income Credit | NJ EITC = 40% of federal EIC (Line 58) |
| Child and Dependent Care Credit (Form 2441) | NJ credit = 10%–50% of federal credit (Line 64) |
| Child Tax Credit | SEPARATE NJ credit with its own rules — age 5 or younger, NJ taxable income ≤ $80,000 (Line 65) |
| Foreign Tax Credit (Form 1116) | NO NJ EQUIVALENT. Schedule NJ-COJ excludes taxes paid to the U.S., Puerto Rico, or any foreign country or territory |
| Premium Tax Credit (Form 8962) | NO NJ credit. NJ has its own health coverage mandate instead (Lines 53a–53c, shared responsibility payment) |
| Saver's Credit (Form 8880) | NO NJ credit |
| Education credits (AOTC / LLC) | NO NJ credit. NJ offers deductions instead (NJBEST, NJCLASS, NJ Higher Education Tuition — Lines 37a–37c) |
| Credit for other states' taxes | Federal has none; NJ has Schedule NJ-COJ (Line 44) |
## Part-year residents
Almost every NJ credit is PRORATED by months of New Jersey residence (15 days or more counts as a month). For income-tested credits, several use FULL-YEAR income to test eligibility even though the credit is prorated (Child Tax Credit, Child and Dependent Care Credit, Wounded Warrior Caregivers Credit).
