# Family and Children Credits
## Description
Refundable New Jersey credits for families with young children and for working parents who pay for care.
## Credits in this folder
- New Jersey Child Tax Credit (nj-child-tax-credit.md) — Form NJ-1040, Line 65. Refundable
- Child and Dependent Care Credit (nj-child-dependent-care-credit.md) — Worksheet J → Form NJ-1040, Line 64. Refundable
## What changed for 2026
The NJ Child Tax Credit amounts rise 25% for tax years 2026 through 2028 (S-4531, P.L.2026, c.26). The top credit is now $1,250 per child. The NJ Child and Dependent Care Credit did not change in New Jersey law, but it is a percentage of the FEDERAL credit, and the federal credit is larger for 2026 under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act — so the NJ amount rises automatically for many families.
## Both can be claimed
The Child Tax Credit and the Child and Dependent Care Credit are DIFFERENT credits. A family eligible for both claims both on the same NJ-1040.
