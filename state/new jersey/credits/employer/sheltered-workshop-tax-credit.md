# Sheltered Workshop Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for businesses that employ "qualified persons" (P.L.2005, c.318) who work at or under the supervision of a sheltered workshop.
## Qualifications (Part II)
- Each employee is a "qualified person" under P.L.2005, c.318
- Each employee worked at least 26 WEEKS during the year AND at least 25 HOURS PER WEEK at or under the supervision of a sheltered workshop
## Credit amount (Part III)
- Per qualified person: the LESSER of 20% of wages paid during the year or $1,000
## Limitation (Part IV)
1. Carryforward from prior years + current-year credit = total available
2. Percentage = NJ allocated income from the entity ÷ NJ gross income (6 decimal places)
3. Apportioned tax = NJ tax after the credit for taxes paid to other jurisdictions (Line 45) × percentage
4. Maximum = 50% of apportioned tax, MINUS the NJ Earned Income Tax Credit (Line 58); not below zero
5. Current-year credit = lesser of the maximum or total available
6. Excess carries forward up to SEVEN years
## Notes
- NONREFUNDABLE; enclose Form GIT-317
- The NJEITC reduction is prorated when more than one entity is involved
- Part-year residents: prorate by months of residence
- Figures are from the 2025 GIT-317; CONFIRM on the 2026 form
## Required Information
- Each qualified person's SSN, name, wages, weeks and hours worked
- Entity name, FEIN, NJ allocated income
- NJK-1 for partnership share
- Prior GIT-317 carryforward schedule
## Questions
- Did you employ people placed through a sheltered workshop?
- Did each work at least 26 weeks and 25 hours per week?
- Are you a sole proprietor or a partner?
- Do you have an unused credit from a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $1,000 per person
- Ignoring the 50% apportioned-tax cap and NJEITC reduction
- Letting carryforwards expire after seven years
- Not enclosing Form GIT-317
## Prompt
- I hired employees through a sheltered workshop program.
