# Employer Credits
## Description
Nonrefundable New Jersey credits available to individuals who are EMPLOYERS (typically sole proprietors reporting on Form NJ-1040) for specific hiring or paid-leave practices.
## Credits in this folder
- Sheltered Workshop Tax Credit (sheltered-workshop-tax-credit.md) — Form GIT-317 → Form NJ-1040, Line 46. Nonrefundable
- Credit for Employer of Organ/Bone Marrow Donor (organ-bone-marrow-donor-employer-credit.md) — Form NJ-1040, Line 48. Nonrefundable
## Notes
- Both credits are subtracted on Lines 46–49 and cannot reduce tax below zero
## Questions
- Do you have employees?
- Did you hire people from a sheltered workshop program?
- Did you give an employee extra paid leave to donate an organ or bone marrow?
## Prompt
- I own a business and gave my employee paid leave to donate a kidney.
