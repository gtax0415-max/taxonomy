# Credit for Employer of Organ/Bone Marrow Donor
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an employer that gave an employee PAID time off to donate an organ or bone marrow for transplantation, where that leave was IN ADDITION TO any other paid time off the employee was entitled to.
## How it works
- Credit = 25% of the employee's salary for up to 30 days of missed work, per donation
- NONREFUNDABLE: limited to tax on Line 45
## Eligibility
- Taxpayer is the employer
- Employee missed work to donate an organ or bone marrow for transplantation
- The paid leave was additional to the employee's regular paid time off
## Notes
- The Division may request documentation such as pay statements and a physician's letter; see nj.gov/treasury/taxation/donor.shtml
- The DONOR's own deduction for donation expenses is separate — see new-jersey/deductions/personal/organ-bone-marrow-donation.md (Line 36)
## Required Information
- Employee's salary and days of leave
- Proof the leave was in addition to existing paid time off
- Physician's documentation of the donation
## Questions
- Did you give an employee paid leave to donate an organ or bone marrow?
- Was that leave on top of their regular paid time off?
- How many days, and what was their salary?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for leave taken from the employee's existing PTO bank
- Counting more than 30 days per donation
- Confusing this employer credit with the donor's own deduction
## Prompt
- My employee donated bone marrow and I paid for the time off.
