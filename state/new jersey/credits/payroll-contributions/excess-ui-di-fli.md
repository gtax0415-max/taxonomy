# Excess UI/WF/SWF, DI, and FLI Withheld
## Description
Refundable credit for New Jersey worker contributions to Unemployment Insurance / Workforce Development Partnership Fund / Supplemental Workforce Fund (UI/WF/SWF), state Disability Insurance (DI), and Family Leave Insurance (FLI) that exceed the annual maximum because MORE THAN ONE employer withheld them.
## 2026 maximum employee contributions
| Contribution | 2026 rate | 2026 wage base | 2026 maximum | 2025 maximum |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UI/WF/SWF | 0.425% | $44,800 | $190.40 | $184.02 |
| DI | 0.19% | $171,100 | $325.09 | $380.42 |
| FLI | 0.23% | $171,100 | $393.53 | $545.82 |
CONFIRM these against the 2026 Form NJ-2450 and NJ-1040 instructions.
## How it works
- Add each contribution type across all W-2s on Form NJ-2450
- The excess over the maximum becomes a refundable credit on the matching line
- Each employer's amount counts only UP TO the maximum; anything a single employer withheld above the maximum must be refunded by that employer, not claimed on the return
## Notes
- Enclose Form NJ-2450 and the supporting W-2s with the return; amounts not substantiated by a W-2 are rejected
- JOINT RETURN: each spouse/CU partner files a SEPARATE Form NJ-2450 — the maximums apply per person, never combined
## Required Information
- Every W-2 with NJ Box 14 amounts for UI/WF/SWF, DI, and FLI
## Questions
- How many employers did you have in 2026?
- What does Box 14 on each W-2 show for UI/WF/SWF, DI, and FLI?
- Did any single employer withhold more than the maximum on its own?
## Common Errors
- Using 2025 maximums for 2026 — the DI and FLI maximums DROPPED for 2026
- Claiming excess withheld by a single employer
- Mixing up the three lines (UI/WF/SWF → 59, DI → 60, FLI → 61)
- Not enclosing Form NJ-2450
- Combining both spouses' W-2s on one NJ-2450
## Prompt
- I had two jobs in New Jersey this year.
- My W-2s show too much family leave insurance withheld.
