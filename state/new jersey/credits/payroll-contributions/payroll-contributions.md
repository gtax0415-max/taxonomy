# Payroll Contribution Credits
## Description
Refundable credits for EXCESS employee contributions to New Jersey unemployment, disability, and family leave insurance when two or more employers together withheld more than the annual maximum. New Jersey's counterpart to the federal credit for excess Social Security tax.
## Credits in this folder
- Excess UI/WF/SWF, DI, and FLI Withheld (excess-ui-di-fli.md) — Form NJ-2450 → Form NJ-1040, Lines 59, 60, 61. Refundable
## What changed for 2026
New wage bases ($44,800 UI/WF/SWF; $171,100 DI/FLI) and lower DI and FLI employee rates produce new annual maximums.
