# Property Tax Credits
## Description
New Jersey gives homeowners and tenants EITHER a property tax deduction OR a small refundable property tax credit on the income tax return, based on property taxes paid on their main home (or 18% of rent).
## Credits in this folder
- Property Tax Credit (property-tax-credit.md) — Worksheet H (or Worksheet I with Schedule NJ-COJ) → Form NJ-1040, Line 56. Refundable
## Deduction OR credit — never both
The same property taxes support either the deduction on Line 41 or the $50 credit on Line 56. Worksheet H computes both and picks the better one.
## Not on the income tax return
ANCHOR, Senior Freeze, and Stay NJ are SEPARATE property tax relief programs claimed on their own application (not on Form NJ-1040). The FY2027 budget limits Stay NJ to seniors with household income of $200,000 or less and replaces the flat $6,500 benefit with tiers ranging from $6,500 down to $4,000 by income, effective July 1, 2026.

## Questions
- Did you own or rent your main home in New Jersey during 2026?
- Were property taxes paid on the home, either directly or through rent?
- Are you 65 or older, blind, or disabled?

- I rent an apartment in New Jersey. Do I get anything for my rent?
- I paid property taxes on my house.
