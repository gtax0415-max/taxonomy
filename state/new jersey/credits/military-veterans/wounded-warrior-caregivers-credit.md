# Wounded Warrior Caregivers Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for a taxpayer who cares for a RELATIVE who is a qualifying disabled armed services member. The credit is the LESSER of $675 or the service member's federal disability compensation for the year.
## How it works
- Credit = lesser of $675 or the veteran's federal disability compensation for the year
- If two or more people care for the same veteran, each gets a share based on their percentage of total care expenses
- Refundable — paid even with no NJ tax liability
## Eligibility — the caregiver
- Gross income of $100,000 or less (married filing jointly, head of household, qualifying widow(er)) or $50,000 or less (single, married filing separately)
- Is a RELATIVE of the service member: spouse, parent, child, brother, sister, grandparent, grandchild, aunt, uncle, first cousin, niece, nephew, great-grandparent, great-grandchild, or any of these by marriage (stepchild, in-law, etc.)
## Eligibility — the service member
- Honorably discharged or released under honorable circumstances by the last day of the tax year
- Disability arising from active U.S. military service in a war or conflict ON OR AFTER September 11, 2001
- Either a 100% disability rating, OR individual unemployability benefits (one disability of at least 60%, or two disabilities with a combined rating of at least 70% with one at least 40%)
- Lived with the caregiver in New Jersey for at least six months of the tax year
## Notes
- Enclose a copy of the caregiver approval letter the FIRST time the credit is claimed; additional documentation may be requested
- Part-year residents: use FULL-YEAR income to test the income limit
- The $675 maximum has been unchanged for several years; CONFIRM against the 2026 instructions
## Required Information
- Service member's name, SSN, and relationship to the caregiver
- Service member's federal disability compensation for the year and disability rating
- Discharge status and service period
- Months the service member lived with the caregiver in NJ
- Caregiver approval letter
- Each caregiver's share of care expenses if care was shared
- Caregiver's gross income and filing status
## Questions
- Is the person you care for a relative, and what is the relationship?
- Was their disability incurred in service on or after September 11, 2001?
- Do they have a 100% rating or receive individual unemployability benefits?
- Did they live with you in New Jersey for at least six months?
- Does anyone else also provide care for them?
- Do you have a caregiver approval letter?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for a veteran whose disability predates September 11, 2001
- Claiming $675 when the veteran's federal disability compensation was less
- Not apportioning when multiple caregivers share care
- Omitting the approval letter in the first year
- Caregivers below the filing threshold not realizing they can use Form NJ-1040-HW
## Prompt
- I take care of my brother, a disabled Iraq veteran who lives with me.
- Is there a New Jersey credit for caregivers of veterans?
