# Gold Star Family Counseling Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for licensed mental health care professionals who provide counseling to Gold Star families through New Jersey's Gold Star Family Counseling program. It compensates the practitioner at the TRICARE rate for services provided free to the families.
## How it works
- Credit = hours of counseling provided through the program × the TRICARE rate for the service
- NONREFUNDABLE: it reduces tax (Line 45) but cannot create a refund
## Eligibility
- Mental health care professional who provided counseling through the Gold Star Family Counseling program during the tax year
## Notes
- Part-year residents: include only hours provided while a New Jersey resident
- Unlike most credits in this taxonomy, the claimant is the SERVICE PROVIDER, not the family receiving the benefit
## Required Information
- Hours of counseling provided through the program
- Applicable TRICARE rate for each service type
## Questions
- Are you a mental health professional who counseled through the Gold Star Family Counseling program?
- How many hours did you provide, and at what TRICARE rate?
## Common Errors
- Including hours provided outside the program
- Using the practitioner's standard fee instead of the TRICARE rate
- Expecting a refund from a nonrefundable credit
## Prompt
- I'm a counselor who volunteers with Gold Star families.
