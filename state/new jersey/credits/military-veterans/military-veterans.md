# Military and Veterans Credits
## Description
New Jersey credits connected to military service: one for family members who care for a disabled post-9/11 veteran, and one for mental health professionals who counsel Gold Star families.
## Credits in this folder
- Wounded Warrior Caregivers Credit (wounded-warrior-caregivers-credit.md) — Schedule NJ-WWC → Form NJ-1040, Line 62. Refundable
- Gold Star Family Counseling Credit (gold-star-family-counseling-credit.md) — Form NJ-1040, Line 47. Nonrefundable
## Related
- Veteran exemption ($6,000, Line 9) — see new-jersey/deductions/exemptions/exemptions.md
- Military pension and survivor benefit exclusions — see new-jersey/income/exempt-income.md
## Questions
- Do you care for a relative who is a disabled veteran?
- Are you a licensed mental health professional who counseled through the Gold Star Family Counseling program?
## Prompt
- I take care of my son, who was injured in Afghanistan.
- I'm a therapist who sees Gold Star families.
