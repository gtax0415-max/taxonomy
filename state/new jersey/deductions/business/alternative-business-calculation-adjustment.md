# Alternative Business Calculation Adjustment (Schedule NJ-BUS-2)
## Description
New Jersey's limited relief from the no-cross-category-loss rule. It lets a taxpayer with a loss in one BUSINESS category (Lines 18, 21, 22, 23) receive a partial deduction for the benefit that netting those categories would have produced, and carry unused business losses forward for up to 20 years.
## How Schedule NJ-BUS-2 works
- Column A (regular): the amounts actually reported on Lines 18, 21, 22, 23 (losses are zero)
- Column B (alternative): the NJ-BUS-1 Line 4 amounts INCLUDING losses, plus the prior-year loss carryforward (Line 5b), netted together
- Line 9 BUSINESS INCREMENT = regular business income (Line 7) − alternative business income (Line 8, zero if a loss)
- Line 11 = business increment × adjustment percentage → Line 35
- Line 12: if Column B is a net loss, that loss carries forward
## Big change for 2026
The FY2027 budget (A5323/S4537), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, ties the adjustment percentage to gross income:
| Gross income | Adjustment percentage |
|---|---|
| $500,000 or less | 50% (unchanged) |
| Over $500,000 – $1,000,000 | 25% |
| Over $1,000,000 | 0% — no adjustment |
The 2025 Schedule NJ-BUS-2 hard-codes 0.50 on Line 10; the 2026 schedule should add the income test. CONFIRM on the 2026 schedule: (a) that "gross income" is NJ gross income on Line 29, and (b) how loss carryforwards are tracked for years in which a taxpayer is over $1 million.
## Example
Sole proprietorship profit $80,000 (Line 18); rental loss $30,000 (Line 23 blank). Regular business income $80,000; alternative $50,000; increment $30,000. Adjustment: $15,000 at 50%, $7,500 at 25%, $0 over $1 million.
## Notes
- Only the FOUR business categories participate. Wage, interest, dividend, capital gain, and gambling losses do not
- Keep every NJ-BUS-2 to track carryforwards
- Enclose Schedule NJ-BUS-2
## Required Information
- Schedule NJ-BUS-1 Line 4 for each part, including losses
- Prior-year NJ-BUS-2 Line 12
- NJ gross income
## Questions
- Did any of your business, partnership, S corporation, or rental categories produce a loss this year?
- Do you have a loss carryforward from a prior NJ-BUS-2?
- Is your gross income above $500,000 or $1 million?
## Common Errors
- Using 50% for 2026 when gross income exceeds $500,000
- Forgetting the prior-year carryforward on Line 5b
- Including capital losses or non-business losses
- Not keeping the schedule, losing the carryforward
## Prompt
- My rental property lost money, but my consulting business made a profit.
- I have a partnership loss carryforward from last year.
