# Business Deductions
## Description
Two deductions tied to business income categories.
## Items in this folder
- Health Enterprise Zone Deduction (health-enterprise-zone.md) — Line 34
- Alternative Business Calculation Adjustment (alternative-business-calculation-adjustment.md) — Schedule NJ-BUS-2 → Line 35
## What changed for 2026
The Alternative Business Calculation Adjustment is reduced to 25% for gross income over $500,000 up to $1 million and eliminated above $1 million.
