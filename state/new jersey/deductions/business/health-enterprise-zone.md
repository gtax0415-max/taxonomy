# Health Enterprise Zone (HEZ) Deduction
## Description
Deduction of a percentage of net income from a qualified medical or dental practice that the taxpayer OWNS, located in or within five miles of a designated Health Enterprise Zone, and that provides PRIMARY CARE services.
## Notes
- Eligibility rules and the percentage computation are in Technical Bulletin TB-56
- Do NOT use this line for medical expenses, health insurance premiums, or other personal or business expenses
- Part-year residents: partners and shareholders prorate by days of residence in the entity's fiscal year; sole proprietors use only receipts from the residency period
## Required Information
- NJK-1 or NJ-K-1 from the practice, or the sole proprietor's HEZ computation
- Practice location and HEZ designation
## Questions
- Do you own a primary care medical or dental practice in or near a Health Enterprise Zone?
## Common Errors
- Claiming medical expenses here
- Claiming as an employee rather than an owner
## Prompt
- I own a family practice in a Health Enterprise Zone.
