# Property Tax Deduction
## Description
Deduction of property taxes due and paid on a New Jersey main home, or 18% of rent for tenants, up to $15,000 ($7,500 for married filing separately when spouses share a main home). Reduces taxable income (Line 39) to NJ taxable income (Line 42).
## How it works
1. LINE 40a — property taxes paid (homeowners) + 18% of rent paid (tenants) for the main home. Use Worksheet G if the taxpayer had more than one NJ main home, shared ownership with a non-spouse, owned a multi-unit property, shared rent with a non-spouse, or was both a homeowner and a tenant
2. LINE 40b — homeowner, tenant, or both
3. WORKSHEET H — deduction = lesser of Line 40a or $15,000; then compute tax with and without the deduction. If the deduction saves $50 or more, take it on Line 41; otherwise take the $50 credit on Line 56
## Worksheet G mechanics
- Part I (homeowners): days owned and occupied × ownership share (non-spouse co-owners reduce it) × share of a multi-unit property used as the main home × total taxes. Units are treated as equal size unless the assessor says otherwise
- Part II (tenants): total rent ÷ number of tenants sharing it (spouses count as one) → × 18%
- Part III: total → Line 40a (half if married filing separately with a shared home)
- Days in Parts I and II together cannot exceed the days in the year (365 for 2026)
## Eligibility
Same as the Property Tax Credit: NJ domicile and main home; home subject to property tax (directly or through rent); tenant access to kitchen and bath; owned multi-unit property of no more than four units with at most one commercial unit; income above the filing threshold. NOT for vacation or second homes, 100%-exempt disabled veterans, P.I.L.O.T. payers, or tenants of tax-exempt property (campus, subsidized, nonprofit-owned housing).
## Interactions
- Lowers NJ TAXABLE income (Line 42), which is the income test for the NJ Child Tax Credit and the NJ Child and Dependent Care Credit — a larger deduction can push a family into a better credit tier
- Part-year residents: only taxes/rent due and paid while a resident
- Federal SALT deduction is separate; the federal cap does not affect this NJ deduction
## Required Information
- Property tax bills and proof of payment; rent paid
- Ownership percentages; number of units; co-tenants
- Days at each residence
- PAS-1 base year amount for Senior Freeze participants
## Questions
- Did you own or rent your main home in New Jersey in 2026, and for how many days?
- Do you share ownership or rent with anyone other than your spouse?
- Is your home part of a multi-unit property you own?
- Are you enrolled in Senior Freeze?
- Are you also claiming a credit for taxes paid to another state?
## Common Errors
- Deducting more than $15,000
- Using 100% of rent instead of 18%
- Not reducing for a non-spouse co-owner's share
- Claiming both the deduction and the credit
- Using Worksheet H alone when Schedule NJ-COJ is involved
## Prompt
- I paid $12,000 in property taxes on my house in Bergen County.
- I rent an apartment for $2,000 a month in Jersey City.
- I own a duplex and live in one unit.
