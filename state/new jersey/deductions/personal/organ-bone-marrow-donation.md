# Organ/Bone Marrow Donation Deduction
## Description
Deduction of up to $10,000 of UNREIMBURSED travel, lodging, and lost wages incurred to donate an organ or bone marrow to another person for transplantation.
## Rules
- Available for the taxpayer, spouse (if filing jointly), or any dependent claimed
- Claim in the year of the transplant; expenses in the year before or after are claimed in the year incurred
- Combined deduction across all years cannot exceed $10,000
- Documentation may be requested (pay statements, physician letter) — see nj.gov/treasury/taxation/donor.shtml
## Related
- The donor's EMPLOYER may claim a separate credit — see new-jersey/credits/employer/organ-bone-marrow-donor-employer-credit.md
## Questions
- Who donated, and in what year was the transplant?
- What travel, lodging, and lost wages were not reimbursed?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding $10,000 across years
- Deducting reimbursed or paid-leave amounts as lost wages
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney to my brother and missed three weeks of work.
