# Qualified Conservation Contribution
## Description
The ONLY charitable deduction in New Jersey: a qualified contribution of real property (land) located in New Jersey for conservation purposes — natural habitat, farmland, forest, or open space.
## How it works
- The deduction equals the amount of the contribution allowed in computing FEDERAL taxable income
- Enclose federal Form 8283 if filed
## Notes
- Ordinary charitable contributions (cash, goods, securities) are NOT deductible in New Jersey
- Land outside New Jersey does not qualify
- Part-year residents: only contributions made while a resident
## Questions
- Did you donate land or a conservation easement on New Jersey land?
- What amount was allowed federally?
## Common Errors
- Deducting regular charitable gifts
- Deducting more than the federally allowed amount
