# Alimony and Separate Maintenance Payments Paid
## Description
Court-ordered alimony and separate maintenance paid is DEDUCTIBLE in New Jersey regardless of when the divorce was finalized — unlike federal law, which ended the deduction for divorces executed after 2018.
## Notes
- Child support is not deductible
- The recipient reports it on Line 25 — see new-jersey/income/other/alimony-received.md
- Part-year residents: only payments made while a resident
## Questions
- Do you pay court-ordered alimony or separate maintenance?
- How much of each payment is child support?
## Common Errors
- Skipping the NJ deduction because the federal deduction is unavailable
- Deducting child support or voluntary payments
