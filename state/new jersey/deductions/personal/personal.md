# Personal Deductions
## Description
New Jersey's limited set of personal deductions. There is no standard deduction and no general itemized deduction.
## Items in this folder
- Medical Expenses (medical-expenses.md) — Worksheet F → Line 31
- Alimony and Separate Maintenance Paid (alimony-paid.md) — Line 32
- Qualified Conservation Contribution (qualified-conservation-contribution.md) — Line 33
- Organ/Bone Marrow Donation Deduction (organ-bone-marrow-donation.md) — Line 36
## Questions
- Did you pay large unreimbursed medical costs or self-employed health insurance?
- Do you pay court-ordered alimony?
- Did you donate land for conservation?
- Did you, your spouse, or a dependent donate an organ or bone marrow?
