# New Jersey College Affordability Deductions
## Description
Three deductions for New Jersey-specific college saving, borrowing, and tuition, available when gross income is $200,000 or less.
## Items in this folder
- NJBEST Deduction (njbest-deduction.md) — Line 37a, up to $10,000
- NJCLASS Deduction (njclass-deduction.md) — Line 37b, up to $2,500
- NJ Higher Education Tuition Deduction (nj-higher-education-tuition-deduction.md) — Line 37c, up to $10,000
## Shared rules
- Gross income $200,000 or less
- Married filing separately: COMBINED deductions of both spouses cannot exceed the limits
- Part-year residents: use FULL-YEAR income for the $200,000 test; prorate the deduction by months of residence
- Limits are not indexed; unchanged for 2026
## Federal comparison
New Jersey has no education CREDIT. These deductions are unrelated to the federal American Opportunity and Lifetime Learning credits and the federal student loan interest deduction.
