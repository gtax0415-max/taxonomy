# NJCLASS Deduction
## Description
Deduction of up to $2,500 of PRINCIPAL AND INTEREST paid during the year on an NJCLASS (New Jersey College Loans to Assist State Students) loan, if gross income is $200,000 or less.
## Notes
- Only NJCLASS loans qualify — not federal or private student loans
- Principal counts, not just interest
## Questions
- Do you repay an NJCLASS loan, and how much did you pay in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Deducting federal student loan payments
- Counting only interest and missing principal
## Prompt
- I pay back an NJCLASS loan.
