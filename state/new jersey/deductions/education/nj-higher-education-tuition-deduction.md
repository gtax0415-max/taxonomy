# New Jersey Higher Education Tuition Deduction
## Description
Deduction of up to $10,000 of tuition paid during the year for the taxpayer, spouse, or a dependent enrolled in or attending a NEW JERSEY institution of higher education, if gross income is $200,000 or less.
## Notes
- Out-of-state schools do not qualify
- Tuition only — not room, board, or books
## Questions
- Did you pay tuition to a New Jersey college for yourself, your spouse, or a dependent?
- How much tuition did you pay in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Including out-of-state tuition
- Including room and board
## Prompt
- My son attends Rutgers and I paid his tuition.
