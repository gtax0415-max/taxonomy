# NJBEST Deduction
## Description
Deduction of up to $10,000 of contributions made during the year to an NJBEST (New Jersey Better Educational Savings Trust) 529 account, if gross income is $200,000 or less.
## Notes
- Only NJBEST contributions qualify — not other states' 529 plans
- If a later NONQUALIFIED withdrawal occurs, the earnings AND any portion attributable to previously deducted contributions become taxable
## Questions
- How much did you contribute to NJBEST in 2026?
- Is your gross income $200,000 or less?
## Common Errors
- Deducting contributions to another state's 529 plan
- Exceeding $10,000, or claiming over the income limit
## Prompt
- I put $5,000 into my daughter's NJBEST account.
