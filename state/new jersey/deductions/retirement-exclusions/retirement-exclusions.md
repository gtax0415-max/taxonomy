# Retirement Income Exclusions
## Description
Exclusions that let older and disabled taxpayers remove retirement income — and, in some cases, other income — before arriving at NJ gross income (Line 29).
## Items in this folder
- Pension/Retirement Exclusion (pension-retirement-exclusion.md) — Line 28a
- Other Retirement Income Exclusion, including the Special Exclusion (other-retirement-income-exclusion.md) — Line 28b
- Line 28c = 28a + 28b
## The $150,000 cliff
Both exclusions disappear ENTIRELY when total income on Line 27 exceeds $150,000. Between $100,000 and $150,000 they shrink in steps. One extra dollar at a boundary can cost thousands of dollars of exclusion. The thresholds are NOT indexed and are unchanged for 2026.
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 62 or older, or disabled under Social Security guidelines, on December 31, 2026?
- What is your total income on Line 27?
- Will you ever be eligible for Social Security or Railroad Retirement?
## Prompt
- I'm retired in New Jersey. How much of my pension is excluded?
