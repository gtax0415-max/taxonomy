# Dependent Exemptions (Lines 10–12)
## Description
Exemptions for each dependent who qualifies as the taxpayer's dependent for FEDERAL tax purposes.
## Line 10 — Qualified Dependent Children: $1,500 each
## Line 11 — Other Dependents: $1,500 each
## Line 12 — Dependents Attending College: additional $1,000 each
All must be true:
- Claimed on Line 10 or 11 (never self, spouse, or domestic partner)
- Under age 22 on December 31, 2026 (born 2005 or later)
- Full-time student, as defined by the school
- At school for some part of each of FIVE calendar months
- Accredited college or post-secondary school with regular faculty, curriculum, and student body
- The taxpayer paid HALF OR MORE of tuition and maintenance (financial aid does not count as the taxpayer's payment; the student's work-study earnings count as the student's income)
## Line 14 — Dependent information
- Full name, SSN/ITIN/ATIN (same as on the federal return), and birth year for each dependent; the exemption is denied without a valid number
- Fill in the NO HEALTH INSURANCE oval for any dependent without coverage on the filing date (ties to the NJ health coverage mandate, Lines 53a–53c)
- More than four dependents: enclose a statement
- NJ EITC filers must also list a federal Schedule EIC qualifying child who is not claimed as an NJ dependent

## Questions
- How many dependents do you claim on your federal return?
- Is any dependent a full-time college student under 22, and did you pay at least half of their costs?
- Does every dependent have health insurance?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the college exemption for a student 22 or older, a part-time student, or when financial aid paid most costs
- Missing or mismatched dependent SSNs
- Forgetting the health insurance oval
