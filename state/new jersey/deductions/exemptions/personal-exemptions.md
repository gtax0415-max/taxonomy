# Personal Exemptions (Lines 6–9)
## Description
Exemptions for the taxpayer and spouse/CU partner based on status, age, disability, and military service.
## Line 6 — Regular: $1,000 each
- Self, and spouse/CU partner on a joint return
- Or a DOMESTIC PARTNER registered in NJ by year end who does not file an NJ return; enclose the Certificate of Domestic Partnership the first time
## Line 7 — Senior 65+: $1,000 each
- Age 65 or older on December 31, 2026 (born in 1961 or earlier)
- Self and spouse only — not domestic partners or dependents
- Proof of age the first time claimed
## Line 8 — Blind or Disabled: $1,000 each
- Blind, or totally and permanently disabled, on the last day of the year
- Self and spouse only
- Doctor's certificate or medical records the first time claimed
## Line 9 — Veteran: $6,000 each
- Honorably discharged or released under honorable circumstances from ACTIVE duty before the last day of the year
- Self and spouse only
- Official documentation showing CHARACTER OF SERVICE the first time claimed (see nj.gov/treasury/taxation/military/vetexemption-documentation.shtml)
## Notes
- A person can qualify on several lines at once (e.g., a 70-year-old disabled veteran gets $1,000 + $1,000 + $1,000 + $6,000)
## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older at the end of 2026?
- Are either of you blind or permanently disabled?
- Are either of you a military veteran with an honorable discharge from active duty?
- Have you submitted documentation in a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the senior, blind/disabled, or veteran exemption for a dependent or domestic partner
- Claiming the veteran exemption without first-year discharge documentation (or for reserve service without active duty)
- Box count not matching the ovals
## Prompt
- I'm a veteran. Is there a New Jersey exemption?
- My husband turned 65 this year.
