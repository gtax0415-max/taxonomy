# Exemptions
## Description
Fixed-dollar amounts subtracted from NJ gross income for the taxpayer, spouse, and dependents. Totaled on Line 13 and carried to Line 30. The amounts are NOT indexed for inflation and are unchanged for 2026.
## Items in this folder
- Personal Exemptions (personal-exemptions.md) — Lines 6–9
- Dependent Exemptions (dependent-exemptions.md) — Lines 10–12, with dependent detail on Line 14
## Summary of 2026 amounts
| Exemption | Line | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Regular (self, spouse/CU partner, or domestic partner) | 6 | $1,000 each |
| Senior 65+ (born 1961 or earlier) | 7 | $1,000 each |
| Blind or disabled | 8 | $1,000 each |
| Veteran | 9 | $6,000 each |
| Qualified dependent child | 10 | $1,500 each |
| Other dependent | 11 | $1,500 each |
| Dependent attending college (under 22, born 2005 or later) | 12 | additional $1,000 each |
## Notes
- The NUMBER of exemptions must be entered in the boxes and must match the ovals filled in, or the exemption is disallowed
- Add the dollar amounts (not the box counts) for Line 13
- Part-year residents: prorate Line 13 by months of NJ residence on Line 30
