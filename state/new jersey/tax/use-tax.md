# Use Tax (Line 51)
## Description
Use tax is owed on taxable items and services used in New Jersey when NJ sales tax was not collected, or was collected at less than NJ's 6.625% rate — typically online, mail-order, phone, or out-of-state purchases.
## Worksheet K
- PART I (items under $1,000 each): EITHER exact purchases × 6.625% minus sales tax paid to other states (up to 6.625%; foreign taxes do not count), OR the estimated amount from the chart based on NJ gross income (Line 29)
- PART II (items $1,000 or more each): always exact — purchases × 6.625% minus other states' sales tax
- PART III: Part I + Part II → Line 51
## Estimated Use Tax Chart (2025 instructions — CONFIRM 2026 figures)
| NJ gross income | Estimated use tax |
|---|---|
| Up to $15,000 | $14 |
| $15,001 – $30,000 | $44 |
| $30,001 – $50,000 | $64 |
| $50,001 – $75,000 | $84 |
| $75,001 – $100,000 | $106 |
| $100,001 – $150,000 | $134 |
| $150,001 – $200,000 | $170 |
| $200,001 and over | 0.0852% of income, maximum $494 |
## Notes
- NJ sales and use tax rate for 2026: 6.625%
- Purchases of $1,000 or more cannot use the chart
- See S&U-4 (Sales Tax Guide) and ANJ-7 (Use Tax in New Jersey)
## Questions
- Did you buy taxable items online or out of state without paying NJ sales tax?
- Did you buy any single item costing $1,000 or more without NJ sales tax?
- Did you pay sales tax to another state on those purchases?
## Common Errors
- Leaving Line 51 blank instead of entering 0.00
- Using the estimate chart for large purchases
- Crediting foreign VAT against NJ use tax

- I bought furniture in Delaware and didn't pay sales tax.
