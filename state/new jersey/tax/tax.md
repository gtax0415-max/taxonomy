# Tax Computation and Additional Taxes (Lines 42–54)
## Description
From NJ taxable income to total tax due: the rate schedule, nonrefundable credits, and three additional amounts that ride on the income tax return — use tax, estimated-tax interest, and the health insurance shared responsibility payment.
## Flow
- Line 42 NJ taxable income = Line 39 − Line 41 (property tax deduction)
- Line 43 tax on Line 42
- Line 44 credit for taxes paid to other jurisdictions → Line 45 balance of tax
- Lines 46–48 nonrefundable credits → Line 49 total → Line 50 balance of tax after credits
- Line 51 use tax + Line 52 interest on underpayment + Line 53c shared responsibility payment
- Line 54 TOTAL TAX DUE = Lines 50 through 53c
