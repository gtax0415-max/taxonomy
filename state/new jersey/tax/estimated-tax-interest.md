# Interest on Underpayment of Estimated Tax (Line 52)
## Description
New Jersey income tax is pay-as-you-go. Installment interest may be charged when not enough tax was paid during the year through withholding and estimated payments.
## When it applies
- Tax due (after withholding and credits) is MORE than $400, AND
- Less than 80% of the tax liability was paid on time through withholding and estimated payments
## How it works
- Compute on Form NJ-2210 (which contains the exceptions, such as payments based on prior-year tax); enter Line 19 on NJ-1040 Line 52 and fill in the oval
- Interest runs at 3% above the prime rate; see TB-21(R) for the rate in each period
## 2026 estimated payment due dates
April 15, 2026; June 15, 2026; September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027
## Related
- Estimated payments — see new-jersey/payments/estimated-payments.md
## Questions
- Did you make 2026 estimated payments, and on what dates?
- Did you owe more than $400 when you filed last year?
- Did you have large income without withholding (self-employment, capital gains, retirement distributions)?
## Common Errors
- Not filing NJ-2210 when an exception (e.g., prior-year safe harbor or annualized income) would reduce the interest
- Forgetting the NJ-2210 oval
## Prompt
- I owe New Jersey tax and didn't make estimated payments.
