# Gubernatorial Elections Fund
## Description
The taxpayer — and the spouse/CU partner on a joint return — may each designate $1 to the public financing program for gubernatorial candidates. It does NOT reduce the refund or increase the balance due.
## Notes
- Answer Yes or No for each spouse
- Administered by the NJ Election Law Enforcement Commission (elec.nj.gov)
## Prompt
- Does checking the Gubernatorial Elections Fund box cost me anything?
