# Charitable Fund Contributions (Lines 70–77)
## Description
Voluntary donations to state-designated funds. Each donation REDUCES the refund or INCREASES the balance due. Choose $10, $20, or "Other" for each line. These are not deductible on the NJ return.
## Fixed lines
- 70 N.J. Endangered Wildlife Fund
- 71 N.J. Children's Trust Fund to Prevent Child Abuse
- 72 N.J. Vietnam Veterans' Memorial Fund
- 73 N.J. Breast Cancer Research Fund
- 74 U.S.S. New Jersey Educational Museum Fund
