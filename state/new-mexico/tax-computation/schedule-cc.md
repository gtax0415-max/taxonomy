# Alternative Tax Schedule CC
## Description
An election for a business whose only New Mexico activity is sales: no property or payroll in New Mexico, and gross sales into the state of $100,000 or less. That describes an out-of-state seller, not a full-year resident.

## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE for this taxonomy (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year New Mexico residents only)
- Kept so the line 18 map is complete

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Gross sales into New Mexico
- Confirmation of no New Mexico property or payroll

## Questions
- Are you a full-year New Mexico resident? (If yes, Schedule CC does not apply.)

## Common Errors
- Using Schedule CC on a resident return

## Prompt
- I sell into New Mexico from Arizona with no office here.
