# Additional Tax on Lump-Sum Distributions (Line 19)
## Description
Only for a filer who used the federal Form 4972 10-year averaging option on a lump-sum distribution from a qualified plan. That amount is outside federal AGI, so New Mexico taxes it separately here.

## Computation
- Tax on New Mexico taxable income alone (A)
- Tax on New Mexico taxable income plus 20% of the lump sum (B)
- Line 19 = (B − A) × 5

## Who qualifies federally
- Form 4972 is limited to plan participants born before January 2, 1936 (age 90 or older in 2026) and certain beneficiaries of such participants, so this line is very rare

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Form 4972 and the 1099-R

## Questions
- Did you use federal Form 4972 for a lump-sum retirement distribution?

## Common Errors
- Using this line for an ordinary IRA or 401(k) distribution, which is already in AGI

## Prompt
- I took a lump-sum pension distribution and used Form 4972.
