# Nonresident Gross Royalty Election
## Description
A NONRESIDENT whose only New Mexico income is gross royalties under $5,000 may figure tax directly on those gross royalties instead of completing PIT-B.

## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE for this taxonomy (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year New Mexico residents only)
- The election exists only for nonresidents
- Kept so the line map stays complete

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Royalty statements or 1099-MISC from New Mexico payers
- Residency status for 2026

## Questions
- Are you a full-year New Mexico resident? (If yes, this election does not apply.)

## Common Errors
- Using the election on a resident return

## Prompt
- I live in Texas and get small oil royalties from New Mexico.
