# Federal Net Operating Loss Addback
## Description
New Mexico does not accept the federal NOL deduction. Whatever federal NOL carryforward or carryback reduced federal AGI is added back here as a POSITIVE number, and the separately computed New Mexico NOL carryforward is then deducted on PIT-ADJ line 8.

## Amount
- Equals the federal NOL taken on Form 1040 Schedule 1, line 8a, entered as a positive number (it appears there as a negative)
- No cap on the addback itself

## The New Mexico NOL that replaces it (PIT-ADJ line 8)
- Must flow from a federal NOL; there is no New Mexico NOL without a federal one
- NO carryback for New Mexico
- Losses from tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2013 carry forward 19 years; older losses only 5 years
- New Mexico loss = federal Section 172(c) NOL for the loss year plus U.S. government obligation interest (net of related expenses) reported on the New Mexico return that year
- The federal 80%-of-taxable-income limit does not carry over; the New Mexico schedule tracks its own usage
- Form RPD-41369 (New Mexico NOL carryforward schedule) must be attached or the New Mexico NOL is disallowed

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. SB 151's decoupling from H.R.1 depreciation and interest provisions applies to the CORPORATE income tax only, beginning tax year 2027, so it does not affect individual NOLs for 2026. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Federal NOL deduction on Schedule 1, line 8a
- Year each loss arose and the amount used each year since
- New Mexico NOL carryforward history

## Questions
- Did you deduct a net operating loss on your federal return this year?
- In which year did the loss arise?
- Have you used any of the New Mexico carryforward in earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Entering the addback as a negative number
- Adding back the federal NOL but forgetting the New Mexico NOL deduction on PIT-ADJ line 8
- Carrying a loss back for New Mexico purposes
- Not attaching RPD-41369, which causes the New Mexico NOL to be denied

## Prompt
- I have a federal net operating loss carryforward.
- My business lost money last year; how does New Mexico treat it?
