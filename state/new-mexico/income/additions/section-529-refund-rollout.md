# 529 Contributions Refunded or Rolled Out
## Description
New Mexico lets you deduct contributions to a New Mexico-approved Section 529 education savings plan. If you later take those contributions back out by closing the account, or move them to a plan that is NOT New Mexico-approved, the earlier deduction is recaptured here as an addition.

## Amount
- The CONTRIBUTIONS (principal) refunded or rolled out, limited to contributions that were deducted on an earlier New Mexico return
- Do NOT include earnings refunded or rolled over
- Do NOT include qualified distributions paid for education expenses

## When it applies
- You closed a New Mexico-approved 529 account and received a refund of contributions, or
- You rolled contributions from a New Mexico-approved plan to a non-New Mexico-approved 529 plan

## Notes
- A rollover between two New Mexico-approved plans is not an addition
- The federal tax result (1099-Q box 2 earnings, any 10% penalty) is separate and already in federal AGI
- H.R.1 widened federal qualified 529 expenses (including more K-12 costs and credentialing programs from 2026). New Mexico has not changed this addition; whether New Mexico treats a newly qualified federal expense as qualified for its own purposes should be confirmed in the 2026 instructions

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Form 1099-Q (gross distribution, earnings, basis)
- Amount of contributions in the refund or rollover
- Whether those contributions were deducted on a prior New Mexico return
- Name of the receiving plan for a rollover

## Questions
- Did you close a New Mexico 529 account or move money to another state's plan?
- Did you deduct the contributions on an earlier New Mexico return?
- How much of the distribution was contributions versus earnings?

## Common Errors
- Adding back earnings as well as contributions
- Adding back contributions that were never deducted in New Mexico
- Treating a qualified education distribution as a refund
- Treating a rollover to another New Mexico-approved plan as an addition

## Prompt
- I moved my child's 529 from the New Mexico plan to another state's plan.
- I closed my New Mexico 529 account and got the money back.
