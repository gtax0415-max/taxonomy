# Land Conservation Charitable Deduction Addback
## Description
A donation of land or a conservation easement can produce both a federal charitable deduction and the New Mexico Land Conservation Incentives Credit (credit code L01). New Mexico does not allow both on the same gift, so the federal deduction for that donation is added back.

## Amount
- The charitable deduction for the land donation that was included in federal itemized deductions (Schedule A, line 12, gifts other than cash)
- Only the portion for the donation that earned the New Mexico credit
- Zero if you took the federal standard deduction

## When it applies (both must be true)
- You donated land or an interest in land to a private nonprofit or public conservation agency for conservation purposes and were allowed the New Mexico land conservation credit
- You claimed a federal charitable deduction for the same donation

## Notes
- The related credit is 50% of the donated land's fair market value, up to $250,000 per donation made on or after January 1, 2008, with a 20-year carryforward (see credits/business-investment/land-conservation-incentives-credit.md)
- Federal charitable deduction carryovers matter: add back only the part of the deduction actually included in THIS year's Schedule A
- Unrelated noncash gifts on line 12 stay deductible

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. H.R.1's new 0.5%-of-AGI floor on federal itemized charitable deductions (from 2026) can reduce the federal deduction, and the addback should match the amount actually deducted. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Schedule A, line 12 total and the portion for the land donation
- Form 8283 and appraisal
- EMNRD certification letter and the credit approval number
- Any federal charitable carryover of the land gift

## Questions
- Did you donate land or a conservation easement?
- Were you approved for the New Mexico land conservation credit?
- Did you itemize and deduct the gift federally this year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the credit and keeping the federal deduction
- Adding back the whole Schedule A line 12 total instead of just the land gift
- Adding back a deduction that was not claimed because the standard deduction was used

## Prompt
- I donated a conservation easement and received the New Mexico land conservation credit.
- I gave land to a land trust; how does New Mexico treat the deduction?
