# Cannabis Business Expenses Disallowed by IRC 280E
## Description
Federal law (IRC 280E) bars deductions for a business trafficking in Schedule I or II controlled substances. New Mexico lets a New Mexico-licensed cannabis business deduct, on the state return, the ordinary expenses that 280E disallowed federally.

## Amount
- Equals the expenses that would be federally deductible but for 280E
- No cap
- Cost of goods sold is already allowed federally and is not entered here

## 2026 change: federal rescheduling
- A DEA final rule effective April 28, 2026 moved marijuana covered by a qualifying STATE MEDICAL license to Schedule III. 280E no longer disallows expenses of that medical activity from that date
- Adult-use (recreational) marijuana remains Schedule I, so 280E still applies to it
- Result for 2026: the New Mexico deduction covers (a) all 280E-disallowed expenses before April 28, 2026, and (b) after that date, only those tied to adult-use activity. Expenses that became federally deductible cannot be deducted again here
- Mixed medical/adult-use operations need a defensible allocation

## Eligibility
- The business holds a New Mexico cannabis license
- The expenses were disallowed on the federal return because of 280E

## Status for 2026
No New Mexico law change in the 2026 session; the change is federal. Confirm line numbers and any TRD guidance on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Federal 280E disallowance computation
- License number and type (medical, adult-use, or both)
- Medical versus adult-use allocation, and pre/post April 28, 2026 split

## Questions
- Does your business hold a New Mexico cannabis license?
- Does it sell medical, adult-use, or both?
- How much did 280E disallow on your federal return, before and after April 28, 2026?

## Common Errors
- Deducting cost of goods sold, which was already allowed federally
- Deducting medical-activity expenses that became federally deductible after April 28, 2026 (double deduction)
- Claiming without a New Mexico license

## Prompt
- I own a licensed dispensary in Albuquerque and file Schedule C.
- How does New Mexico treat 280E after marijuana rescheduling?
