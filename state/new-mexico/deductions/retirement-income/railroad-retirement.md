# Railroad Retirement Annuities and Sick Pay
## Description
Federal law bars states from taxing Railroad Retirement Act benefits and Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act sick pay. Any of it included in federal AGI is subtracted in full.

## Amount
- The taxable portion of Railroad Retirement Act annuities and benefits included in federal AGI (tier 1 SSEB on Form 1040 line 6b, tier 2 and other annuity on line 5b)
- Plus Railroad Unemployment Insurance Act sick pay included in federal income
- No income limit and no cap

## Notes
- Attach the RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R to the return
- The tier 1 Social Security Equivalent Benefit is reported federally with Social Security, but it belongs HERE. Do not also count it in the Social Security exemption (PIT-ADJ line 25)
- This exemption has no AGI cliff, unlike the Social Security exemption

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- RRB-1099 box 5 and the taxable amount on the federal return
- RRB-1099-R taxable amount
- RUIA sick pay included in income

## Questions
- Do you receive Railroad Retirement benefits?
- Did you receive railroad sick pay?
- Did you also receive regular Social Security benefits?

## Common Errors
- Counting tier 1 railroad benefits in both this line and the Social Security exemption
- Deducting the gross benefit instead of the taxable amount in federal AGI
- Forgetting to attach the RRB forms

## Prompt
- I receive Railroad Retirement Board benefits.
- Does New Mexico tax my railroad pension?
