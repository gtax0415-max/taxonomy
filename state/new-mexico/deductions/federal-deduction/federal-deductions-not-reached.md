# Federal Deductions New Mexico Does Not Reach
## Description
New Mexico net income is federal AGI minus the IRC section 63 standard or itemized deduction and the section 151 exemption amount (zero). Deductions taken federally AFTER AGI that are not part of that standard or itemized deduction do not reduce New Mexico income.

## Not allowed in New Mexico for 2026
- Qualified tips deduction (up to $25,000)
- Qualified overtime deduction (up to $12,500; $25,000 joint)
- Car loan interest deduction (up to $10,000)
- Enhanced senior deduction ($6,000 per person 65 or older)
- Non-itemizer charitable deduction (new from 2026: up to $1,000; $2,000 joint)
- Qualified business income (QBI) deduction

## Notes
- HB 264 (2026) proposed New Mexico tip and overtime deductions; it was not enacted
- Clients often expect these to carry over. They raise New Mexico taxable income relative to federal taxable income
- New Mexico still has its own age-based relief (PIT-ADJ lines 13 and 25, PIT-RC)

## Status for 2026
No New Mexico change; the federal deductions are new or expanded under H.R.1.

## Required Information
- Federal Schedule 1-A, QBI, and non-itemizer charitable amounts (for explanation only)

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal tips, overtime, senior, car loan interest, or QBI deduction?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting any of these on the New Mexico return
- Entering federal taxable income instead of building from AGI

## Prompt
- Does New Mexico give me the no-tax-on-tips deduction?
- Why is my New Mexico taxable income higher than my federal taxable income?
