# Federal Standard or Itemized Deduction (Line 12)
## Description
New Mexico has no standard deduction of its own. It subtracts the same standard deduction or itemized deductions claimed on the federal return.

## 2026 federal standard deduction
- Single, married filing separately: $16,100
- Head of household: $24,150
- Married filing jointly, surviving spouse: $32,200

- Plus the federal additional amount for age 65 or older or blind, if it is part of the federal standard deduction
- Itemizers enter total itemized deductions and mark box 12a (this triggers the line 10 SALT addback)

## Notes
- Only the standard or itemized deduction carries over; other federal deductions taken after AGI do not (see deductions/federal-deduction/federal-deductions-not-reached.md)
- The federal 2026 Form 1040 renumbers lines in this area; take the standard/itemized amount only, not the total of all deductions below AGI
- HB 93 (2026), which would have raised New Mexico's standard deduction to 205% of federal, was not enacted

## Status for 2026
No New Mexico change. The amounts follow the federal 2026 figures (H.R.1, inflation-adjusted).

## Required Information
- Federal standard deduction or itemized total
- Whether the filer itemized

## Questions
- Did you take the standard deduction or itemize on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Entering the total of all federal deductions, including the tips, overtime, senior, car loan interest, or QBI amounts
- Forgetting box 12a when itemizing
- Using a New Mexico-only itemized amount
