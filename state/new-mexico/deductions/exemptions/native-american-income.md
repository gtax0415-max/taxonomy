# Income of an Enrolled Member Earned on the Member's Own Lands
## Description
Income of an enrolled member of a New Mexico federally recognized Indian nation, tribe, or pueblo is exempt from New Mexico tax if it was earned within the member's OWN reservation, pueblo grant, or Indian country while the member lived there.

## Both tests must be met
- DOMICILE: the member lived within the boundaries of the member's own reservation, pueblo grant, or Indian country when the income was earned
- SOURCE: the income was earned within those same boundaries

## Amount
- All income meeting both tests; no cap
- Armed forces pay: exempt only while the home of record AND domicile are on the member's own lands
- Retirement or pension income: exempt when it came from employment on the member's or spouse's own lands

## Notes
- Income earned OUTSIDE the boundaries is taxable no matter where the member lives
- Income earned on ANOTHER tribe's lands does not qualify
- A non-member spouse is taxable even when living and working on the member spouse's lands, unless the spouse is also a member of a New Mexico nation, tribe, or pueblo
- Combined exemptions on lines 11 and 12 cannot exceed 100% of income
- Amounts exempted here count as income when figuring rebates and credits on PIT-RC

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Name of the member's (and, if applicable, spouse's) nation, tribe, or pueblo
- Address of domicile
- Each income item and where it was earned

## Questions
- Are you an enrolled member of a New Mexico nation, tribe, or pueblo?
- Did you live within your own tribe's lands this year?
- Was the income earned within your own tribe's lands?
- Is your spouse also an enrolled member?

## Common Errors
- Exempting income earned off the reservation or pueblo grant
- Exempting income earned on a different tribe's lands
- Exempting a non-member spouse's income
- Leaving the tribe name blank on 11a or 11b
- Leaving exempt income out of PIT-RC modified gross income

## Prompt
- I am a tribal member and work at my pueblo's health center.
- I live on the Navajo Nation and work in Gallup.
