# Deduction for Certain Dependents (Line 13)
## Description
A New Mexico deduction created when the federal personal exemption went to zero. It gives back part of the lost exemption for larger households.

## Amount
- $4,000 for EACH dependent after the first
- Example: 3 dependents → (3 − 1) × $4,000 = $8,000

## Eligibility
- Filing status married filing jointly or head of household
- The filer is not a dependent of another taxpayer
- Applies only while the federal IRC 151 exemption amount is zero, which H.R.1 made permanent, so it continues for 2026

## Not available
- Single, married filing separately, or surviving spouse

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Filing status
- Number of dependents on the federal return

## Questions
- Are you filing jointly or as head of household?
- How many dependents are on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Counting the first dependent
- Claiming as a surviving spouse or separate filer
- Claiming as someone who is a dependent

## Prompt
- We file jointly with four kids; what is the New Mexico dependent deduction?
