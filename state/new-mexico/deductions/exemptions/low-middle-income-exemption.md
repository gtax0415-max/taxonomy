# Low- and Middle-Income Tax Exemption (Line 14)
## Description
Up to $2,500 per exemption on PIT-1 line 5, phased out as federal AGI rises.

## Amount
- Start with $2,500 per exemption
- Reduce it by a percentage of AGI over a threshold:
- Single: reduction starts above $20,000, rate 15%, fully phased out above $36,667
- Joint, head of household, surviving spouse: reduction starts above $30,000, rate 10%, fully phased out above $55,000
- Married filing separately: reduction starts above $15,000, rate 20%, fully phased out above $27,500

- Not below zero
- Example: single, 1 exemption, AGI $30,000 → $2,500 − 15% × ($30,000 − $20,000) = $1,000

## Notes
- A dependent filer enters 00 on line 5, so this is zero
- The thresholds are fixed in statute and NOT indexed

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm the worksheet on the 2026 PIT-1.

## Required Information
- Federal AGI
- Line 5 exemption count
- Filing status

## Questions
- What is your federal AGI and filing status?
- How many exemptions are on line 5?

## Common Errors
- Using New Mexico taxable income instead of federal AGI
- Claiming it above the cutoff for the filing status
- Claiming it as a dependent filer

- I am single with AGI of $28,000; do I get the New Mexico low- and middle-income exemption?
