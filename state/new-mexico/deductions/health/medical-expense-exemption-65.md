# Medical Care Expense Exemption for Persons 65 or Older
## Description
A flat $3,000 exemption for a household where the taxpayer or spouse is 65 or older and unreimbursed medical care expenses reached $28,000 or more during the year. Qualifying also opens a separate refundable credit on PIT-RC.

## Amount
- $3,000 flat if expenses are $28,000 or more
- $0 below $28,000; it is a cliff, not a phase-in
- Nonresidents with a New Mexico filing requirement may claim it

## What counts
- Care by a physician (MD, DO, dentist, podiatrist, chiropractor, or psychologist licensed in New Mexico) or in a licensed health care facility
- Prescribed drugs, insulin, and oxygen (not over-the-counter drugs or pet prescriptions)
- Qualified long-term care services and long-term care insurance
- Medical insurance premiums, including Medicare Part B
- Licensed nursing services
- Prescribed therapeutic devices: eyeglasses, contact lenses, hearing aids
- Institutional care when medical care is the main reason, including meals and lodging that are part of it
- Expenses for the taxpayer, spouse, and dependents

## What does NOT count
- Cosmetic surgery
- Over-the-counter medicine
- Guide dogs
- Anything reimbursed or compensated

## Notes
- Expenses also deducted on federal Schedule A may still be counted
- Keep receipts; do not send them with the return
- The same expenses feed the refundable medical care credit for persons 65 or older on PIT-RC, so claim both

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. The $28,000 threshold and $3,000 amount are fixed in statute. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Total unreimbursed qualifying medical expenses
- Age of taxpayer and spouse
- Breakdown by type for audit support

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse 65 or older?
- Did your unreimbursed medical costs reach $28,000 this year?
- Did those costs include nursing home or long-term care?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $3,000
- Counting reimbursed expenses or over-the-counter drugs
- Forgetting the matching refundable credit on PIT-RC

## Prompt
- My husband is in a nursing home and we paid $40,000 out of pocket.
- I am 72 with large medical bills; is there a New Mexico break?
