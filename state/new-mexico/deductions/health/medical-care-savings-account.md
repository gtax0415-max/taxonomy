# New Mexico Medical Care Savings Account Exemption
## Description
Exempts qualified amounts in a New Mexico Medical Care Savings Account (NMMSA) that ended up in federal income. An NMMSA is a trust or custodial account set up by a self-employed individual or small employer with a high-deductible health plan to pay employees' unreimbursed medical costs.

## What qualifies (only if included in federal income)
- Principal contributed to the NMMSA
- Interest earned on the NMMSA
- Money paid from the NMMSA for eligible medical expenses
- Money advanced by the employer for eligible medical expenses under the Medical Care Savings Account Act

## What does NOT qualify
- Excess contributions
- Unqualified distributions
- Rollovers into another NMMSA
- Anything already excluded or deducted federally (for example, through a federal HSA)

## Notes
- This is NOT a federal HSA. Most people with health savings accounts have nothing to enter here
- No dollar cap; the amount is whatever qualifying sum was in federal income

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Contributions, interest, and distributions for the year
- Which of those amounts were included in federal income
- Medical expense receipts for distributions

## Questions
- Are you enrolled in an employer-sponsored New Mexico Medical Care Savings Account (not an HSA)?
- Were any contributions, interest, or distributions included in your federal income?

## Common Errors
- Treating a federal HSA as an NMMSA
- Exempting amounts already excluded federally
- Including excess contributions or nonqualified distributions

## Prompt
- My employer set up a New Mexico medical care savings account for me.
- Is my medical savings account taxable in New Mexico?
