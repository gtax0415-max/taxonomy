# Nonresident U.S. Public Health Service Active Duty Pay
## Description
A NONRESIDENT member of the U.S. Public Health Service on active duty in New Mexico can exclude that pay. Full-year residents get no exemption for USPHS pay, and USPHS pay does not qualify for the armed forces active duty exemption (PIT-ADJ line 17).

## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE for this taxonomy (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year New Mexico residents only)
- The deduction applies only to nonresidents, so it never applies to an in-scope return
- Kept so the PIT-ADJ line map stays complete

## Notes
- A person who moved in or out of New Mexico enters only pay earned while a nonresident
- A spouse who moved to New Mexico only to be with a USPHS active duty member may be able to source income to the state of domicile (PIT-B)

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- W-2 for USPHS active duty pay
- Residency status for 2026

## Questions
- Are you a full-year New Mexico resident? (If yes, this line does not apply.)

## Common Errors
- A full-year resident excluding USPHS pay
- Putting USPHS pay on the armed forces active duty line

## Prompt
- I am a USPHS officer stationed in New Mexico but my home is in Maryland.
