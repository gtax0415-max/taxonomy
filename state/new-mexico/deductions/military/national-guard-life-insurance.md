# National Guard Life Insurance Reimbursements
## Description
New Mexico National Guard members can be reimbursed for Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance premiums from a state fund. The reimbursement is taxable federally but exempt in New Mexico.

## Amount
- The full reimbursement received from the National Guard Service Member's Life Insurance Reimbursement Fund, as shown on the 1099-MISC
- No cap

## Notes
- Attach the 1099-MISC to the New Mexico return to support the exemption
- Only the reimbursement is exempt; regular Guard pay is not (unless it is full-time active duty pay, PIT-ADJ line 17)

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- 1099-MISC amount from the Office of Military Affairs

## Questions
- Are you a member of the New Mexico National Guard?
- Did you receive a life insurance premium reimbursement and a 1099-MISC for it?

## Common Errors
- Not attaching the 1099-MISC
- Exempting drill pay along with the reimbursement

## Prompt
- I got a 1099-MISC from the New Mexico National Guard for SGLI reimbursement.
