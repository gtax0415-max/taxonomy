# School Supplies Purchased by a Public School Teacher
## Description
A licensed New Mexico public school teacher can deduct the cost of supplies bought for students' classroom use.

## Amount
- Up to $1,000 per qualifying teacher for 2026 (also $1,000 for 2025, 2027, and 2028; $500 for 2024)
- Two qualifying teachers on a joint return: each may claim up to $1,000

## What counts
- Notebooks, paper, writing instruments, crayons, art supplies, rulers, maps and globes, and similar items used by the teacher's students in the classroom for education

## What does NOT count
- Computers and similar digital devices
- Watches, radios, music players, headphones
- Sporting equipment
- Phones and other electronic communication devices
- Copiers, office equipment, furniture, fixtures

## Eligibility
- Licensed as a teacher under the Public School Code
- Teaches at a New Mexico public school (charter schools that are public schools qualify; private schools do not)

## Notes
- The federal educator expense adjustment is a separate federal item. Whether the same receipts can support both should be confirmed in the 2026 New Mexico instructions

## Status for 2026
$1,000, as scheduled by 2024 HB 252. No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Receipts and total cost
- Teaching license and school

## Questions
- Are you a licensed teacher at a New Mexico public school?
- How much did you spend on classroom supplies?
- Were any of the items electronics or furniture?

## Common Errors
- Claiming over $1,000 per teacher
- Including computers, tablets, or furniture
- Claiming as a private school teacher or as a non-teaching employee

## Prompt
- I teach third grade in Las Cruces and spent $700 on supplies.
- Can a New Mexico teacher deduct classroom supplies?
