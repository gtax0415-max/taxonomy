# 529 Education Savings Plan Contributions
## Description
New Mexico deducts contributions to a New Mexico-approved Section 529 education savings plan. There is no federal 529 deduction, so this is a state-only benefit.

## Amount
- All contributions made during the tax year, for each beneficiary; no dollar cap
- Includes principal AND earnings rolled IN from a non-New Mexico 529 plan
- Married filing separately: each spouse claims half of what would be allowed on a joint return

## Eligibility
- Contribution to a plan established and approved by the New Mexico Education Trust Board
- Contributions to another state's 529 plan do not qualify

## Notes
- If you later close the account and take contributions back, or roll them to a non-New Mexico plan, the deduction is recaptured as an addition (PIT-ADJ line 3)
- H.R.1 broadened federal qualified 529 uses (more K-12 costs and credentialing programs from 2026). That affects federal withdrawals, not this contribution deduction

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Confirm line numbers on the 2026 PIT-ADJ.

## Required Information
- Contributions by date and beneficiary
- Plan name
- Rollover-in amounts and their source plan

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a New Mexico 529 plan this year?
- Did you roll another state's 529 into a New Mexico plan?
- Are you filing married separately?

## Common Errors
- Deducting contributions to an out-of-state plan
- Claiming the full deduction on each of two separate returns
- Forgetting rollover-in earnings, which also count

## Prompt
- I put $5,000 into my grandson's New Mexico Education Plan.
- I rolled my child's 529 from another state into the New Mexico plan.
