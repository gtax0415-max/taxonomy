# Child Income Tax Credit
## Description
A fully refundable credit per qualifying child, scaled by federal AGI. Every income level gets something, but the amount drops sharply as AGI rises.

## 2026 amounts per child
- Federal AGI $0–25,000 (or negative AGI): $656 per child (statutory base $600)
- Federal AGI $25,001–50,000: $437 per child (statutory base $400)
- Federal AGI $50,001–75,000: $218 per child (statutory base $200)
- Federal AGI $75,001–100,000: $109 per child (statutory base $100)
- Federal AGI $100,001–200,000: $82 per child (statutory base $75)
- Federal AGI $200,001–350,000: $54 per child (statutory base $50)
- Federal AGI over $350,000: $27 per child (statutory base $25)

- Married filing separately: each spouse claims half of the joint amount
- The AGI bands are not indexed

## How the 2026 amounts were derived
- NMSA 7-2-18.34 adjusts each base amount by CPI-U for the month ending September 30 of the prior year, divided by CPI-U for September 2022, ROUNDED DOWN to the dollar; never reduced below the prior year
- 2026 factor: 324.800 (September 2025) ÷ 296.808 (September 2022) = 1.09431
- Check: the same method with September 2024 CPI-U (315.301) reproduces the published 2025 amounts exactly ($637 / $424 / $212 / $106 / $79 / $53 / $26)
- When TRD publishes the 2026 PIT-RC, compare its Table 4 with this one

## Eligibility
- New Mexico resident, not a dependent of another taxpayer
- Each child is a qualifying child under IRC 152(c) (also certain minor children or stepchildren in public-assistance situations)
- Available for tax years 2023 through 2031
- No requirement to owe tax

## Notes
- AGI here is federal AGI (PIT-1 line 9), not modified gross income

## Status for 2026
Amounts indexed; no change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Federal AGI
- Number of qualifying children

## Questions
- How many qualifying children live with you?
- What is your federal AGI?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 amounts on a 2026 return
- Using MGI instead of federal AGI
- Not claiming it because no tax is owed (it is refundable)
- Each separate filer claiming the full amount

- I have two kids and make $40,000; what New Mexico child credit do I get?
