# Special Needs Adopted Child Tax Credit
## Description
A refundable credit of $1,500 a year for each adopted child certified as a "difficult to place child." Unlike the federal adoption credit, it repeats every year the child is claimed as a dependent.

## Amount
- $1,500 per qualifying child per year
- Married filing separately: $750 each

## Eligibility
- Child certified as difficult to place by CYFD or a licensed child placement agency
- The child is claimed as a dependent on the federal return (the child may be over 18)
- The filer files a PIT-1 and is not a dependent of another taxpayer
- No modified gross income limit
- Attach the certificate in the FIRST year the credit is claimed

## Notes
- Separate from the federal adoption credit, which applies to adoption expenses in the year of adoption (with a refundable portion of up to $5,120 for 2026). Both can apply

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Certificate for each child
- Each child on the federal dependent list

## Questions
- Did you adopt a child certified as difficult to place?
- Is the child still your dependent on the federal return?
- Is this the first year you are claiming the credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the certification
- Claiming for a child no longer a federal dependent
- Not attaching the certificate in the first year

## Prompt
- We adopted a child with special needs through CYFD.
