# Low Income Comprehensive Tax Rebate
## Description
A refundable rebate for low-income New Mexico households, read from a table by modified gross income and number of exemptions. It is paid even if no tax is owed.

## How the 2026 table was derived
- NMSA 7-2-14 sets base amounts (2021 law) and adjusts each one every year by CPI-U for the month ending September 30 of the prior year, divided by CPI-U for September 2021, ROUNDED DOWN to the dollar; an amount is never reduced below the prior year
- 2026 factor: 324.800 (September 2025) ÷ 274.310 (September 2021) = 1.18406
- Check: the same method with September 2024 CPI-U (315.301) reproduces the published 2025 table exactly ($17 low, $839 high)
- The $36,000 ceiling and the income bands are NOT indexed
- When TRD publishes the 2026 PIT-RC, compare its Table 1 with this one

## Eligibility
- All four PIT-RC qualification statements true (see credits/income-based/rebate-qualifications.md)
- MGI of $36,000 or less

## Status for 2026
Amounts indexed for 2026 as above; no change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Modified gross income
- Exemption count from PIT-RC line 3

## Questions
- Was your total household income, including nontaxable income, $36,000 or less?
- How many exemptions do you have, including the extras for age and blindness?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 table ($839 top amount) for 2026
- Using AGI instead of MGI
- Forgetting the extra exemptions for 65+ and blind
- Taking the full amount on a married-separate return

## Prompt
- I am retired with $20,000 of Social Security; do I get a New Mexico rebate?
