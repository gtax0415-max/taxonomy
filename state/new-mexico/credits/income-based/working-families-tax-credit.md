# Working Families Tax Credit
## Description
New Mexico's earned income credit: 25% of the federal earned income credit, fully refundable.

## Amount
- 25% of the federal EIC (25a), entered on line 25
- Excess over tax is refunded
- Lines 25, 25a, and 25b must all be completed or the credit is denied

## The New Mexico expansion (line 25b)
Filers who were NOT allowed the EIC federally can still claim if they would have qualified except for:
- The federal Social Security number requirement (for example, ITIN filers), or
- The federal minimum age, for workers at least 18 but under 25
Compute the EIC as if federally eligible, enter it on 25a, and mark 25b.

## Eligibility
- New Mexico resident filing a PIT-1
- Eligible for the federal EIC, or eligible under the expansion above

## 2026 notes
- Federal EIC rules and amounts are inflation-adjusted for 2026; H.R.1 did not change the EIC formula
- HB 296 (2026), which would have raised the credit to 50%, was not enacted. The rate stays 25%

## Status for 2026
25% unchanged.

## Required Information
- Federal EIC amount, or a computed EIC for expansion filers
- Earned income, AGI, and qualifying children

## Questions
- Did you claim the earned income credit on your federal return?
- If not, was that only because you have an ITIN or are age 18 to 24?

## Common Errors
- Leaving 25a or 25b blank
- Missing the expansion for ITIN filers or young workers
- Using 20% (the 2021–2022 rate)

## Prompt
- I got the federal earned income credit; what does New Mexico give me?
- I file with an ITIN and work full time.
