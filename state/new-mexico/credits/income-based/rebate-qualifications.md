# Rebate and Credit Qualifications (PIT-RC Section 1)
## Description
A gate for the low-income rebates and credits on PIT-RC. The filer (and on a joint return, each spouse) confirms four statements. If any is false for a person, that person cannot claim the PIT-RC rebates and credits.

## The four statements for 2026
- A: a New Mexico resident during any part of 2026
- B: physically present in New Mexico for at least six months of 2026
- C: not a dependent of another taxpayer for federal purposes
- D: not an inmate of a public institution for more than six months of 2026

## Notes
- For in-scope filers (full-year residents) A and B are normally true; C and D are the real tests
- The Child Income Tax Credit (PIT-RC line 25) has its own rules: New Mexico resident and not a dependent

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Residency and presence for each spouse
- Dependency status
- Any confinement over six months

## Questions
- Can anyone else claim you as a dependent?
- Were you in a prison or other public institution for more than six months in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming PIT-RC rebates as a dependent (for example, a student)
- Marking the statements for only one spouse on a joint return

## Prompt
- Do I qualify for New Mexico's low-income rebates?
