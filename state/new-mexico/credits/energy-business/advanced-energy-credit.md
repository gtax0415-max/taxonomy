# Advanced Energy Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for holders of an interest in a qualified electric generating facility in New Mexico. The credit was REPEALED effective July 1, 2023. For 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD of credit already approved.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code A04.

## Amount (as originally allowed)
- 6% of eligible generation plant costs
- $60,000,000 aggregate limit per qualified facility
- Unused credit carries forward up to TEN years; not refundable

## Status for 2026
- No new credit can be earned
- Approved, unused credit can still be claimed within its ten-year carryforward period
- Not to be confused with the Advanced Energy EQUIPMENT credit (A07), which is active

## Notes
- The right to claim can be allocated among interest owners; an owner claims only the allocated share
- Each approval is a separate PIT-CR line

## Required Information
- Approval letter and credit number
- Allocation notice, if the credit was allocated to you
- Year approved and carryforward used to date

## Questions
- Do you have unused advanced energy tax credit from a facility approved before July 1, 2023?
- Was the credit allocated to you as an interest owner?
- How many years of carryforward remain?

## Common Errors
- Treating it as available for new 2026 investment
- Confusing it with the Advanced Energy Equipment credit (A07)
- Claiming more than your allocated share
- Claiming after the ten-year carryforward expires

## Prompt
- I have a carryforward of the New Mexico advanced energy tax credit.
- Can I still claim the advanced energy tax credit in 2026?
