# Advanced Energy Equipment Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for qualified expenditures on a New Mexico manufacturing facility that produces advanced energy products. HB 154 (2026, signed March 3, 2026) rewrote the definition of "advanced energy product" for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code A07.

## Amount
- The lesser of 20% of qualified expenditures or $25,000,000
- Statewide cap: $25,000,000 a year of certified personal and corporate credits combined
- Unused credit carries forward FIVE years; not refundable

## 2026 change (HB 154)
- Through 2025 the definition borrowed federal Section 45X ("eligible for the federal advanced manufacturing production credit"). Because H.R.1 cut back and phases out 45X, the state credit would have shrunk with it
- From tax year 2026, New Mexico lists the qualifying products itself: components for solar, wind, and batteries, critical minerals, and fusion machines, among others
- Fusion machines are newly eligible

## Eligibility
- Not a dependent of another taxpayer
- Tax year beginning on or after January 1, 2025 and before January 1, 2033
- PRELIMINARY certification from EMNRD (with EDD) BEFORE making the expenditures
- FINAL certification sought within twelve months after production begins
- Claim within ONE year of receiving the final certificate

## Notes
- A certificate can be sold or transferred in pieces of at least $1,000,000 (smaller certificates only in full); TRD must be notified within ten days
- Recapture may apply if the facility closes or stops producing advanced energy products within the required period
- Different from the repealed Advanced Energy Tax Credit (A04)

## Required Information
- Final certificate number and amount
- Date of final certification
- Transfer notice, if acquired
- Carryforward used to date

## Questions
- Did your business build or expand a New Mexico plant making solar, wind, battery, critical mineral, or fusion components?
- Did you get preliminary certification before spending?
- Do you have the final EMNRD certificate, and when was it issued?

## Common Errors
- Spending before preliminary certification
- Claiming more than one year after final certification
- Applying the old federal 45X definition to 2026 production
- Expecting a refund of the excess

## Prompt
- We opened a battery component factory in New Mexico.
- I bought an advanced energy equipment tax credit certificate.
