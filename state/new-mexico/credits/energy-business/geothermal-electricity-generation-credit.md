# Geothermal Electricity Generation Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable production credit for holders of an interest in a geothermal power plant that generates electricity in New Mexico.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code G05.

## Amount
- 1.5 cents ($0.015) per kilowatt-hour generated in New Mexico during the tax year, by the facility the taxpayer holds an interest in
- Statewide cap of $5,000,000 a year (personal and corporate combined); applications over the cap roll to the next calendar year
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Electricity generated on or after January 1, 2025 and before January 1, 2032
- EMNRD certificate before claiming
- Partners and LLC members claim by ownership share

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Certificate number and amount
- kWh generated and the taxpayer's share

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a New Mexico geothermal power plant?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than your ownership share
- Expecting a refund of the excess

## Prompt
- I am an investor in a geothermal power project in New Mexico.
