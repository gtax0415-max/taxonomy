# Agricultural Biomass Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for owners of a dairy or feedlot who transport agricultural biomass (manure) to a facility that uses it to generate electricity or make biocrude or other liquid or gaseous fuel for commercial use.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code A05.

## Amount
- $5 per wet ton of agricultural biomass transported from your dairy or feedlot to a qualifying facility
- Unused credit carries forward up to FOUR consecutive years from the first year it was approved; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Owner of a dairy or feedlot in New Mexico
- Tax year beginning on or after January 1, 2011 and ending before January 1, 2030
- EMNRD certificate of eligibility, then TRD approval

## Notes
- The credit can be transferred; the transferee uses the new credit number TRD assigns
- Each approval is a separate PIT-CR line

## Status for 2026
Active for 2026; the credit window ends with tax years ending before January 1, 2030. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Wet tons transported and destination facility
- EMNRD certificate and TRD credit number
- Any transfer notice and new credit number
- Carryforward used to date

## Questions
- Do you own a dairy or feedlot?
- Did you haul manure to a biomass energy or fuel facility?
- Do you have an EMNRD certificate and TRD approval?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before TRD approval is received
- Using the original credit number after a transfer
- Claiming beyond the four-year carryforward

## Prompt
- My dairy sends manure to a biogas plant.
- I have an agricultural biomass tax credit approval.
