# Renewable Energy Production Tax Credit
## Description
A per-kilowatt-hour credit for producing electricity from solar, wind, or biomass at a qualified generator, for ten consecutive years starting when the generator begins producing. CLOSED to new applications; only previously certified generators can still claim.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code R03.

## Amount
- Set by the EMNRD certificate and the year's production (per-kWh rates depend on resource and start date)
- For generators that first produced on or after October 1, 2007, credit over tax is REFUNDED
- Otherwise, excess carries forward FIVE years
- In the PIT-CR approval-number box, enter the last day of the tax year in which the electricity was produced

## Status for 2026
No new certifications. Existing generators may claim for production within their ten-year eligibility period. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate and any allocation notice
- kWh produced in 2026 and the taxpayer's share

## Questions
- Do you hold an interest in a renewable generator certified for this credit?
- Is 2026 within its ten-year eligibility window?

## Common Errors
- Confusing this with the solar market development credits (S01, S04)
- Claiming after the ten-year period

## Prompt
- I have an allocation of the renewable energy production tax credit from a wind farm.
