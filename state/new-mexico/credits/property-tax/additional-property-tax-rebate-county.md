# Additional Low Income Property Tax Rebate (Bernalillo, Doña Ana, Los Alamos, Santa Fe)
## Description
An extra refundable property tax rebate for low-income HOMEOWNERS in four counties that have adopted it. No age requirement.

## Amount
- Property tax billed on the principal residence × the Table 3 percentage for your MGI
- Not more than $350 ($175 married filing separately: multiply, then halve)

## Table 3 percentages
- MGI $0–8,000: 75%
- MGI $8,001–10,000: 70%
- MGI $10,001–12,000: 65%
- MGI $12,001–14,000: 60%
- MGI $14,001–16,000: 55%
- MGI $16,001–18,000: 50%
- MGI $18,001–20,000: 45%
- MGI $20,001–22,000: 40%
- MGI $22,001–24,000: 35%

## Eligibility
- Own and live in the home in one of the four counties (renters do not qualify)
- Modified gross income $24,000 or less
- All four PIT-RC qualification statements true
- Mark the county box

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- County and property tax billed
- MGI

## Questions
- Do you own your home in Bernalillo, Doña Ana, Los Alamos, or Santa Fe County?
- Was household income $24,000 or less?

## Common Errors
- Claiming as a renter
- Claiming in a county that is not one of the four
- Forgetting to mark the county box

## Prompt
- I own my home in Albuquerque and my income is $18,000.
