# Property Tax Rebate for Persons 65 or Older
## Description
A refundable rebate of part of the property tax on a principal residence, or the property-tax share of rent, for low-income seniors.

## Amount
- Property tax BILLED for 2026 on the principal residence (owners), or 6% of 2026 rent paid (renters)
- Minus the maximum property tax liability from Table 2, which rises with MGI from $20 (MGI $0–1,000) to $180 (MGI $15,001–16,000)
- Not more than $250 ($125 married filing separately); not below zero

## Eligibility
- 65 or older on December 31, 2026 (taxpayer or spouse)
- Modified gross income $16,000 or less
- All four PIT-RC qualification statements true
- Land counts only up to five acres reasonably needed for the home

## Notes
- Rent includes any government subsidy paid to the landlord
- Can be claimed together with the low income comprehensive tax rebate

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Amounts and limits are fixed.

## Required Information
- Property tax billed on the home, or total rent paid
- MGI
- Age

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse 65 or older?
- Do you own or rent your home, and how much property tax or rent did you pay?
- Was household income $16,000 or less?

## Common Errors
- Using taxes paid instead of taxes billed
- Using total rent instead of 6% of rent
- Claiming more than $250

## Prompt
- I am 70, rent an apartment, and my income is $14,000.
