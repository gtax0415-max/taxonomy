# Job Mentorship Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for New Mexico businesses that employed students in a school-sanctioned career preparation program. REPEALED July 1, 2025. For 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code J01.

## Amount (as originally allowed)
- 50% of gross wages paid to up to 10 qualified students, for up to 320 hours each per year
- Maximum $12,000 per tax year; no more than three tax years per student
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years; not refundable
- No credit approval number is needed on PIT-CR

## Status for 2026
- No new credit for 2026 wages
- Unused credit earned before the repeal may still be applied within its three-year carryforward

## Required Information
- Remaining balance and the year it was earned
- School certificates and wage records supporting it

## Questions
- Do you have unused job mentorship credit from before July 1, 2025?

## Common Errors
- Claiming new credit for students employed in 2026
- Claiming past the carryforward period

## Prompt
- Can I still use my job mentorship tax credit in 2026?
