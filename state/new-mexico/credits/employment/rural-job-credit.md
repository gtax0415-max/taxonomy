# Rural Job Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for employers in rural New Mexico that qualify for Job Training Incentive Program (JTIP) assistance, for each qualifying job created after July 1, 2000.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code R01.

## Amount
- 6.25% of the first $16,000 of wages per qualifying job, per 12-month qualifying period (up to $1,000 per job per period)
- Tier 1 rural areas: up to FOUR qualifying periods
- Tier 2 (Alamogordo, Carlsbad, Clovis, Gallup, Hobbs, Roswell): up to TWO qualifying periods
- Usable against personal income tax, or against gross receipts, compensating, withholding, or corporate income tax
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years; not refundable

## What is "rural"
- Excludes Albuquerque, Corrales, Farmington, Las Cruces, Los Alamos County, Los Ranchos, Rio Rancho, Santa Fe, Tijeras, and a 10-mile zone around them

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- TRD approval number
- Jobs, wages, and qualifying periods
- Amount used against other taxes or carried forward

## Questions
- Does your business qualify for JTIP and operate in a rural area?
- How many qualifying jobs did you create, and in which tier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a job in an excluded city or 10-mile zone
- Exceeding the number of qualifying periods for the tier
- Applying the same credit against both gross receipts tax and income tax

## Prompt
- My business in Socorro hired five new employees through JTIP.
