# Foster Youth Employment Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for employers who hired a qualified foster youth. REPEALED July 1, 2025. For 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD of credit already approved.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code F02.

## Amount (as originally allowed)
- Up to $1,000 of wages per qualified foster youth employed at least 20 hours a week in New Mexico, prorated for a partial year
- One year per youth, one employer per youth per year
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years; not refundable; not transferable (can be allocated to owners of a pass-through)

## Status for 2026
- No new credit for 2026 hiring
- Approved, unused credit may still be applied within its three-year carryforward

## Required Information
- Approval number and remaining balance
- Year the credit was approved

## Questions
- Do you have unused foster youth employment credit approved before the July 1, 2025 repeal?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new credit for 2026 hiring
- Claiming past the three-year carryforward

## Prompt
- I have a leftover foster youth employment tax credit.
