# Clean Car Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for buying, or leasing for at least three years, an electric, plug-in hybrid, or fuel cell vehicle from a licensed New Mexico dealer. 2026 is the LAST year at the full amounts; the credit steps down in 2027.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code C03.

## Amounts (by tax year of purchase or lease)
- New electric: $3,000 for 2024–2026; $2,220 for 2027; $1,470 for 2028; $960 for 2029
- New plug-in hybrid or fuel cell: $2,500 for 2024–2026; $1,850 for 2027; $1,225 for 2028; $800 for 2029
- Used electric: $2,500 for 2024–2026; $1,850 for 2027; $1,225 for 2028; $800 for 2029
- Used plug-in hybrid or fuel cell: $2,000 for 2024–2026; $1,480 for 2027; $980 for 2028; $640 for 2029

- One vehicle credit per taxpayer per tax year
- Excess over tax is REFUNDED
- Married filing separately: each spouse claims half

## Eligibility
- Purchase or lease between May 15, 2024 and December 31, 2029 from a dealer licensed by the New Mexico Motor Vehicle Division or a dealer on tribal land in New Mexico. Private-party sales do not qualify
- New vehicle: base MSRP of $55,000 or less
- Used vehicle: market value of $25,000 or less, model year at least two years before the purchase year, dealer-certified, with at least a one-year dealer warranty
- Vehicle registered in New Mexico
- Apply to EMNRD within ONE year of the purchase or lease date
- Claim within THREE tax years after the year EMNRD certifies the credit

## Notes
- The certificate can be sold or transferred, but only in full and only if none of it has been claimed
- A vehicle can earn the credit only twice: once new, once used
- Partners and LLC members may claim in proportion to ownership
- The federal used and new clean vehicle credits ended September 30, 2025; the New Mexico credit is independent and continues

## Status for 2026
Active for 2026 at the top amounts. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and amount
- Purchase or lease date, new or used, vehicle type
- Transfer notice, if the certificate was acquired

## Questions
- Did you buy or lease an EV, plug-in hybrid, or fuel cell vehicle from a New Mexico dealer?
- Was it new or used, and what was the MSRP or value?
- Did you apply to EMNRD within a year, and do you have the certificate?

## Common Errors
- Buying from a private seller or an out-of-state dealer not licensed in New Mexico
- Missing the one-year application deadline
- Using the 2027 step-down amount for a 2026 purchase, or the reverse
- Claiming two vehicle credits in one year

## Prompt
- I bought a new electric car in New Mexico this year.
- I leased a plug-in hybrid for 36 months.
