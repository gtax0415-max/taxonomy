# New Solar Market Development Tax Credit
## Description
A REFUNDABLE credit for buying and installing a solar photovoltaic or solar thermal system on a New Mexico home, business, or farm. More important for 2026: the federal residential clean energy credit (25D) ENDED for expenditures after December 31, 2025, so this state credit is now the main tax incentive for residential solar.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code S04.

## Amount
- 10% of equipment, materials, and labor cost
- Capped at $6,000 (EMNRD's page states the limit as $6,000; confirm on the certificate whether it applies to the credit or the cost)
- Excess over tax is REFUNDED (for credits claimed on or after January 1, 2022)
- Subject to an annual statewide cap administered by EMNRD; complete applications are processed in order

## Eligibility
- Purchased and installed on or after March 1, 2020 and before December 31, 2031
- Property owned by the taxpayer, or since May 15, 2024, held in leasehold from a federally recognized nation, tribe, or pueblo
- EMNRD certificate; the "fund year" follows the date of the successful building code inspection
- Claim within THREE tax years after the year EMNRD certifies the credit
- Certificate may be sold or transferred for its full value; TRD must be notified within ten days

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and amount
- System cost and inspection date

## Questions
- Did you install solar panels or a solar water heater in 2026?
- Has the system passed inspection, and have you applied to EMNRD?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the federal 30% credit still applies to a 2026 installation
- Using S01 (pre-2017 systems) instead of S04
- Claiming before the certificate is issued

## Prompt
- I installed solar panels on my house in Las Cruces this year.
