# Geothermal Ground-Coupled Heat Pump Tax Credit (2024)
## Description
A REFUNDABLE credit for buying and installing a geothermal (ground-source) heat pump in a New Mexico home, business, or farm. Matters more in 2026 because the federal 25D credit for geothermal heat pumps ENDED for expenditures after December 31, 2025.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code G03.

## Amount
- 30% of purchase and installation cost, up to $9,000
- Excess over tax is REFUNDED
- Statewide cap of $4,000,000 a year; EMNRD approves applications in the order received

## Eligibility
- Purchased and installed after May 15, 2024 and before December 31, 2034
- In a residence, business, or agricultural enterprise in New Mexico owned by the taxpayer (or by a partnership or association the taxpayer belongs to)
- Installed by a nationally accredited installer certified by EMNRD
- EMNRD certificate of eligibility before claiming

## Notes
- Partners and LLC members claim by ownership share
- Different from G01 (pre-2021 systems, carryforward only)

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and amount
- Installation date and cost

## Questions
- Did you install a geothermal heat pump in New Mexico in 2026?
- Was the installer EMNRD-certified?
- Did you apply to EMNRD, and was the credit approved under the annual cap?

## Common Errors
- Using a non-certified installer
- Claiming before the EMNRD certificate is issued
- Assuming the federal 25D credit still applies for 2026

## Prompt
- I replaced my furnace with a geothermal heat pump in Santa Fe.
