# Clean Car Charging Unit Tax Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for buying and installing an electric vehicle or fuel cell charging unit in New Mexico.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code C04.

## Amounts
- Level 1 or Level 2 (home or standard) charger: the lesser of cost or $400
- Direct current fast charger: the lesser of cost or $25,000
- Fuel cell charging unit: the lesser of cost or $25,000
- Excess over tax is REFUNDED

## Eligibility
- Not a dependent of another taxpayer
- Charger purchased and installed in New Mexico between May 15, 2024 and December 31, 2029
- EMNRD certificate of eligibility before claiming
- Claim within THREE tax years after the year EMNRD certifies the credit

## Notes
- Cannot be claimed for a charger whose cost was also used for the 2021 Sustainable Building Tax Credit (S05)
- Separate from the Clean Car credit (C03); a buyer can claim both
- Co-owners file one joint application; partners and LLC members claim by ownership share

## Status for 2026
Active for 2026. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and amount
- Charger type, cost, and installation date

## Questions
- Did you install an EV charger at your home or business?
- What type of charger was it?
- Did you claim the same charger under the sustainable building credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming $400 when the charger cost less
- Double-claiming under the 2021 Sustainable Building credit
- Claiming without the EMNRD certificate

## Prompt
- I installed a Level 2 charger in my garage.
- Our business installed a DC fast charger.
