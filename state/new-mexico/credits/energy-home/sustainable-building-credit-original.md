# Sustainable Building Tax Credit (Original)
## Description
The original credit for constructing or renovating a certified sustainable building in New Mexico. It is CLOSED to new applications; for 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD. Current projects use the 2021 Sustainable Building Tax Credit (S05).
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code S02.

## Carryforward
- Up to SEVEN consecutive years from the first year TRD approved the credit; not refundable
- Transferred credits use the new number TRD assigned on the transfer

## Status for 2026
- No new credit
- The 2025 PIT-CR instructions describe it as not available for new applications, carryforward only. The original file's "repealed July 1, 2025" is not stated in those instructions; either way the 2026 treatment is carryforward only

## Required Information
- TRD approval or transfer number
- Year approved and balance used to date

## Questions
- Do you have an unused sustainable building credit from an older project?

## Common Errors
- Confusing S02, S03 and S05
- Claiming past the seven-year carryforward

## Prompt
- I have a carryforward from the original sustainable building credit.
