# 2021 Sustainable Building Tax Credit
## Description
The current sustainable building credit. It covers two kinds of projects:
- ENERGY-CONSERVING PRODUCTS installed in an existing building (from 2021): qualifying heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, windows, doors, insulation, and EV charging equipment
- NEW construction or major renovation (from 2022) of residential or commercial buildings meeting high green-building certification levels, and Energy Star manufactured housing
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code S05.

## Amount
- Set on the EMNRD certificate by product type or by certification level and square footage; higher per-product amounts for low-income residences and affordable housing
- Annual statewide program caps (calendar year): energy-conserving products $2,900,000; new residential $2,000,000; new commercial $1,000,000; manufactured housing $250,000; large commercial renovation $1,000,000
- NONREFUNDABLE in general, but the excess IS refunded to a low-income taxpayer (household AGI at or under 200% of the federal poverty level)

## Eligibility
- EMNRD certificate of eligibility
- Claimable for tax years before January 1, 2028
- For residential certifications awarded on or after January 1, 2022, the owner at certification or a first buyer if no credit was claimed

## 2026 interactions
- The federal home improvement credit (25C) ended for property placed in service after December 31, 2025, so this state credit is the remaining tax incentive for heat pumps, windows, and insulation
- An EV charger claimed here cannot also be claimed under the Clean Car Charging Unit credit (C04)

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and amount
- Low-income status, if seeking a refund

## Questions
- Did you install an energy-efficient heat pump, water heater, windows, or insulation in 2026?
- Did you build a certified green home?
- Is household income at or below 200% of the poverty level?

## Common Errors
- Double-claiming an EV charger under S05 and C04
- Claiming a refund without low-income status
- Applying after the annual cap is reached

## Prompt
- I replaced my furnace with a heat pump in 2026.
- We bought a new Build Green NM home.
