# Solar Market Development Tax Credit (Original)
## Description
The ORIGINAL solar credit for photovoltaic or solar thermal systems purchased and installed after January 1, 2006 and before December 31, 2016. For 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD. New systems use the New Solar Market Development credit (S04).
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code S01.

## Carryforward
- Up to TEN consecutive years from the first eligible year the EMNRD certificate was issued; not refundable
- Because certificates had to be issued before December 31, 2016, 2026 is the LAST possible year to use any remaining balance

## Status for 2026
- No new credit; final carryforward year at the latest
- The 2025 PIT-CR instructions describe it as closed to new applications with carryforward only. The original file's statement that it was "repealed July 1, 2025" is not in those instructions; either way the 2026 treatment is carryforward only

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number and issue date
- Balance used to date

## Questions
- Do you have unused solar credit from a system installed before 2017?

## Common Errors
- Claiming S01 for a newer system (use S04)
- Keeping a balance past its ten-year limit

## Prompt
- I still have leftover solar credit from my 2015 installation.
