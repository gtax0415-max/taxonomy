# Geothermal Ground-Coupled Heat Pump Tax Credit (Original)
## Description
The ORIGINAL geothermal heat pump credit, for systems purchased and installed after January 1, 2010 and before December 31, 2020. For 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD. New installations use the 2024 version (G03).
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code G01.

## Amount (as originally allowed)
- 30% of purchase and installation cost, up to $9,000
- Unused credit carries forward up to TEN consecutive years after the year approved; not refundable

## Status for 2026
- No new credit
- A balance approved in, for example, 2018 can be used through 2028

## Required Information
- EMNRD certificate number
- Year approved and amount used to date

## Questions
- Did you install a geothermal heat pump between 2010 and 2020 and still have unused New Mexico credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming G01 for a system installed after May 15, 2024 (use G03)
- Claiming past the ten-year carryforward

## Prompt
- I have unused credit from my geothermal heat pump installed in 2019.
