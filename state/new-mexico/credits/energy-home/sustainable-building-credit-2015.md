# 2015 Sustainable Building Tax Credit
## Description
A credit for building, renovating into, or permanently installing manufactured housing that is a certified sustainable building (LEED Silver or higher, or the Build Green NM rating). New credits could be claimed for tax years 2017 through 2024 only; for 2026 it exists only as a CARRYFORWARD.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code S03.

## Carryforward
- Up to SEVEN years from the first year TRD approved the credit; not refundable

## Status for 2026
No new credit. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- TRD approval number
- Year approved and balance used to date

## Questions
- Do you have unused 2015 sustainable building credit?

## Common Errors
- Claiming new 2026 credit under S03 (use S05)
- Claiming past seven years

## Prompt
- We built a LEED home in 2022 and still have credit left.
