# Film Production Tax Credit (Legacy)
## Description
The ORIGINAL film credit, for productions that began principal photography BEFORE January 1, 2016. For 2026 it exists only to pay out remaining approved claims. New productions use the New Film Production Tax Credit (F03) or the Film Partner credit (F04).
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code F01.

## Amount (as approved)
- 25% of direct production and postproduction expenditures in New Mexico that are subject to New Mexico tax, plus 5% for certain qualifying productions
- Excludes costs bought with a nontaxable transaction certificate
- Refundable, but payment is subject to the statutory annual limits on film credit payouts, so a refund can be delayed to a later year

## Eligibility
- An approved film production company (New Mexico Film Office, then TRD approval)
- Principal photography began before January 1, 2016

## Status for 2026
No new credit can be generated. No change from the 2026 legislative session; HB 291 (2026) film changes start with tax year 2027 and affect the newer credits.

## Required Information
- TRD approval number and approved amount
- Any portion already paid in earlier years

## Questions
- Do you have an approved film production credit for a production that began before 2016 that has not been fully paid?

## Common Errors
- Using F01 for a production that began after 2015 (use F03 or F04)
- Expecting immediate payment when the annual payout limit has been reached

## Prompt
- I still have an old New Mexico film production credit approval.
