# New Mexico Film Partner New Film Production Tax Credit
## Description
The new film credit (F03) for a "New Mexico film partner": a production company that bought, or signed a ten-year lease on, a qualified production facility and committed to produce in New Mexico.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code F04.

## Amount (as approved)
- Same base and uplifts as F03: 25% base, 5% uplifts for qualifying productions, and the rural uplift for production more than 60 miles outside Bernalillo and Santa Fe counties
- The statewide annual cap that limits other film credits does NOT apply to certifying a film partner's budget
- Higher nonresident talent and crew limits than non-partners
- Refundable

## Eligibility
- Film Partner designation
- Principal photography on or after July 1, 2019
- Registration before principal photography and TRD approval of the final application

## Status for 2026
No change for 2026. HB 291 (2026) film changes take effect for tax years beginning January 1, 2027.

## Required Information
- TRD approval number and amount
- Owner's share, if through a pass-through

## Questions
- Is your production company a designated New Mexico Film Partner?

## Common Errors
- Reporting a Film Partner credit under F03
- Claiming without TRD approval

## Prompt
- Our studio is a New Mexico film partner with an approved credit.
