# New Film Production Tax Credit
## Description
The current film credit for productions that began principal photography on or after July 1, 2019 and are not New Mexico Film Partners. Claimed by the film production company; individuals see it mainly as owners of a pass-through production entity.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code F03.

## Amount (as approved)
- Base: 25% of direct production and postproduction expenditures in New Mexico subject to New Mexico tax (or, under HB 291 from 2027, tribal tax)
- Uplifts of 5% each for qualifying productions (for example, TV series and pilots, qualified production facility use)
- Rural uplift for filming 60+ miles outside Bernalillo and Santa Fe counties. The 2025 PIT-CR instructions describe it as 5%; the 2023 law raised it to 10% for productions starting on or after July 1, 2023. Use the rate on the approval, not either figure from memory
- Nonresident crew wages qualify only within statutory limits
- Refundable, subject to the statewide annual cap; claims over the cap are paid in later years

## Eligibility
- Register with the New Mexico Film Office before principal photography
- Final application to TRD within one year of the last qualifying New Mexico expenditure
- Excludes costs bought with a nontaxable transaction certificate

## Status for 2026
No change for 2026. HB 291 (2026) adds tribal-taxed expenditures and the Navajo Nation as a cooperative partner, effective for tax years beginning January 1, 2027.

## Required Information
- TRD approval number and amount
- Owner's share, if through a partnership or S corporation

## Questions
- Do you own an interest in a film production company with an approved New Mexico film credit?
- What does the approval show as the credit amount?

## Common Errors
- Using F03 for a Film Partner production (use F04)
- Computing the credit yourself instead of using the approved amount
- Claiming before TRD approval
