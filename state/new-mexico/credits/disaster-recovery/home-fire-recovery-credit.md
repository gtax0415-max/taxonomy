# Home Fire Recovery Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for rebuilding a home on the same property after the taxpayer's prior home was destroyed by a WILDFIRE in 2021, 2022, or 2023 (for example, the Hermits Peak/Calf Canyon fire).
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code H02.

## Amount
- Equal to qualified home construction expenditures, up to $50,000 per home
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years after first claimed; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Prior home (lived in most of the year) destroyed by wildfire in calendar years 2021–2023
- New home built on the same property, including a manufactured or modular home
- Rebuilding expenses incurred after May 15, 2024 and before January 1, 2030
- Apply to RLD's Construction Industries Division within twelve months after the calendar year construction is completed
- Claim on the return within twelve months of the certificate being issued
- Partners and LLC members claim by ownership share

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- RLD certificate number and amount, and the date issued
- Construction completion date

## Questions
- Was your home destroyed in a 2021–2023 wildfire?
- Did you rebuild on the same property, and when was it completed?
- When did RLD issue your certificate?

## Common Errors
- Missing the twelve-month claim window after the certificate
- Claiming for a home rebuilt on a different lot
- Expecting a refund of the excess

## Prompt
- We lost our house in the Hermits Peak fire and rebuilt in 2026.
