# Rural Health Care Practitioners Tax Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for licensed health care practitioners who work at an approved practice site in a rural health care underserved area of New Mexico.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code R02.

## Amount
- $5,000: physicians, osteopathic physicians, dentists, psychologists, podiatric physicians, optometrists
- $3,000: pharmacists, dental hygienists, physician assistants, CRNAs, certified nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists, registered nurses, midwives, licensed clinical and independent social workers, professional mental health and clinical mental health counselors, marriage and family therapists, professional art therapists, alcohol and drug abuse counselors, physical therapists
- FULL amount for at least 1,584 hours at the rural site during the year; HALF for 792 to 1,583 hours
- Unused credit carries forward THREE years; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Files a New Mexico return; not a dependent of another taxpayer
- DOH certificate of eligibility for the year

## Looking ahead (not for 2026)
SB 151 (2026) creates a separate $10,000 physician tax credit starting tax year 2027, which can be claimed together with this credit.

## Status for 2026
Active, unchanged for 2026.

## Required Information
- DOH certificate number and amount
- Profession and hours at the approved site

## Questions
- Are you a licensed practitioner working at an approved rural site?
- How many hours did you work there in 2026?
- Has DOH issued your certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the full amount with under 1,584 hours
- Claiming without the DOH certificate
- Adding the new physician credit to a 2026 return

## Prompt
- I am a nurse practitioner at a clinic in rural New Mexico.
