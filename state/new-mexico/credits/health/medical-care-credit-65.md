# Refundable Medical Care Credit for Persons 65 or Older
## Description
A flat, refundable $2,800 credit when the taxpayer or spouse is 65 or older and the household had $28,000 or more of unreimbursed medical care expenses in 2026.

## Amount
- $2,800; married filing separately $1,400 each
- A cliff: $27,999 of expenses gets nothing

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer or spouse 65 or older on December 31, 2026
- Unreimbursed and uncompensated medical care expenses of $28,000 or more for the filer, spouse, and dependents
- Not a dependent of another taxpayer
- No modified gross income limit

## Notes
- The same expenses also qualify for the $3,000 exemption on PIT-ADJ line 18 (see deductions/health/medical-expense-exemption-65.md); claim both
- Qualifying expense types follow that exemption's definitions (physician and facility care, prescriptions, long-term care, insurance premiums, nursing, prescribed devices)

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session. Amounts are fixed.

## Required Information
- Total unreimbursed qualifying expenses
- Ages of taxpayer and spouse

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse 65 or older?
- Did your out-of-pocket medical costs reach $28,000 in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the PIT-ADJ exemption but not this credit, or the reverse
- Counting reimbursed costs or over-the-counter drugs

## Prompt
- My wife is in assisted living and we paid $45,000 out of pocket.
