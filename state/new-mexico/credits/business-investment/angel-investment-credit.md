# Angel Investment Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an accredited investor who makes a qualified equity or convertible debt investment in a New Mexico high-technology or manufacturing business.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code A02.

## Amount
- 25% of the qualified investment
- Maximum $62,500 per investment round
- Investments in no more than FIVE qualified businesses per tax year
- Carryforward: five years for certificates issued in 2015 or later (three years for 2014 and earlier); not refundable

## Eligibility
- Files a New Mexico personal income tax return and is not someone else's dependent
- Accredited investor under federal securities rules
- Investment made on or after January 1, 2007 and on or before December 31, 2030
- Certificate of eligibility obtained before claiming

## Notes
- Each certificate is a separate PIT-CR line with its own certificate number
- The credit goes to individuals only; it is not on the corporate return

## Status for 2026
Active for 2026; investments through December 31, 2030 still qualify. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Certificate number and credit amount for each investment
- Investment date and amount
- Carryforward used in earlier years

## Questions
- Did you invest in a New Mexico high-tech or manufacturing company this year?
- Are you an accredited investor?
- Have you received a certificate of eligibility?
- How many companies did you invest in this year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the certificate is issued
- Claiming more than $62,500 per round or more than five businesses per year
- Applying the old three-year carryforward to post-2014 certificates, or the reverse
- Expecting the unused credit to be refunded

## Prompt
- I invested in a New Mexico startup as an angel investor.
- I have an angel investment credit certificate.
