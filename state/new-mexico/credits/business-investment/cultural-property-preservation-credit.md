# Preservation of Cultural Property Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for restoring, rehabilitating, or preserving a property listed on the official New Mexico Register of Cultural Properties.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code P01.

## Amount
- 50% of approved eligible project costs
- Maximum $25,000 per project
- Maximum $50,000 if the property is inside a state- or municipally-certified arts and cultural district
- Unused credit carries forward FOUR years; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Property listed on the New Mexico Register of Cultural Properties
- The project plan approved by the Cultural Properties Review Committee BEFORE work begins
- The completed work certified as matching the plan

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Log number from the project approval
- Part 2 approval and approved eligible costs
- Arts and cultural district certification, if claiming the $50,000 limit

## Questions
- Is your building on the New Mexico Register of Cultural Properties?
- Was the project plan approved before work started?
- Is the property in a certified arts and cultural district?

## Common Errors
- Starting work before plan approval
- Using the $50,000 limit without district certification
- Claiming past the four-year carryforward

## Prompt
- I restored my historic adobe house listed on the state register.
