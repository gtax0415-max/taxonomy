# Affordable Housing Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for investing land, buildings, materials, cash, or services in an affordable housing project approved by the New Mexico Mortgage Finance Authority. MFA issues the investment voucher; the voucher is the credit.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code A01.

## Amount
- Up to 50% of the value of the qualified investment, as stated on the MFA voucher
- Usable against personal income tax, and also against gross receipts, compensating, withholding, or corporate income tax
- Unused credit carries forward FIVE years; not refundable

## Eligibility
- Investment in an MFA-approved affordable housing project (acquisition, construction, rehabilitation, weatherization, and similar, single- or multi-family)
- Voucher issued by MFA to you, or reissued to you by MFA after a transfer

## Notes
- Vouchers can be sold or transferred only if MFA is notified and reissues the voucher to the new holder; the new voucher number is what goes on the return
- Each voucher is listed separately on PIT-CR with its own number
- The 2026 session's new gross receipts tax deduction for affordable multifamily construction (SB 151) is a separate GRT benefit for sellers of construction materials and labor, starting July 1, 2027. It does not change this credit

## Status for 2026
Active for 2026. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- MFA voucher number(s) and amount
- Any transfer reissue from MFA
- Carryforward used in earlier years and year of issue

## Questions
- Did you receive an MFA affordable housing investment voucher?
- Was it issued to you or transferred to you?
- How much of it have you already used, and when was it issued?

## Common Errors
- Using the voucher number from before a transfer
- Claiming beyond the five-year carryforward
- Combining several vouchers on one PIT-CR line
- Expecting a refund of the excess

## Prompt
- I donated land to an affordable housing project and got an MFA voucher.
- I bought an affordable housing tax credit voucher.
