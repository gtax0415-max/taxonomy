# Land Conservation Incentives Credit
## Description
A nonrefundable credit for donating land, or an interest in land such as a conservation easement, to a private nonprofit or public conservation agency for conservation purposes.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code L01.

## Amount
- 50% of the fair market value of the land or interest donated
- Maximum $250,000 per donation made on or after January 1, 2008 ($100,000 for earlier donations)
- Unused credit carries forward up to TWENTY years after the year of the donation; not refundable
- For donations after January 1, 2008, the credit can be sold or transferred in pieces of $10,000 or more; TRD issues the buyer a new credit number

## Eligibility
- EMNRD certifies the donation as qualified, then TRD approves the credit
- Married filing separately: each spouse claims half of the joint amount

## Interaction
- If the same gift was deducted federally on Schedule A, that deduction is added back on PIT-ADJ line 4 (see income/additions/land-conservation-donation.md)

## Status for 2026
Active. No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- EMNRD letter and TRD credit number
- Year of donation and amount used or sold to date

## Questions
- Did you donate land or a conservation easement, or buy a land conservation credit?
- Did you also deduct the gift on your federal Schedule A?

## Common Errors
- Keeping the federal charitable deduction without the PIT-ADJ addback
- Using the original credit number after a transfer
- Claiming past the twenty-year carryforward

## Prompt
- I put a conservation easement on my ranch.
- I bought New Mexico land conservation tax credits.
