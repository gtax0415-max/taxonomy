# Technology Jobs and Research and Development (Additional) Tax Credit
## Description
The "additional" part of New Mexico's R&D credit, which can be applied against personal income tax. The "basic" part applies only against gross receipts, compensating, and withholding taxes, not income tax.
Claimed on Schedule PIT-CR with credit type code T02.

## Amount
- Basic credit: 5% of qualified R&D expenditures (not claimable on PIT-1)
- Additional credit: another 5%, if annual payroll rises by at least $75,000 for every $1,000,000 of qualified expenditures claimed
- Both double (to 10% each) for R&D in a rural area
- A qualified research and development small business can have part of the additional credit REFUNDED
- Unused additional credit carries forward THREE years

## Eligibility
- Qualified expenditures made on or after January 1, 2016 at a qualified New Mexico facility
- TRD approval before claiming
- A taxpayer claiming the R&D small business tax credit cannot also claim this credit for the same period

## Status for 2026
- HB 291 (2026, technical bill) made minor adjustments to this credit; details should be checked in the 2026 PIT-CR instructions
- HB 27 / SB 97 (2026), which would have extended the carryforward to 10 years and allowed sales of the credit, are not among the enacted tax changes in the Legislative Finance Committee's post-session review; treat as not law unless confirmed

## Required Information
- TRD approval number and approved amounts (basic vs. additional)
- Refundable amount, if a qualified small business

## Questions
- Does your business do research and development at a New Mexico facility?
- Did payroll grow by $75,000 per $1 million of expenditures?
- Is the facility in a rural area?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the basic credit against income tax
- Claiming a refund without small-business qualification
- Combining with the R&D small business credit for the same period

## Prompt
- My startup has an approved New Mexico technology jobs R&D credit.
