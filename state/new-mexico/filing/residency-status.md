# Residency Status
## Description
One letter per spouse: R, F, P, or N. Under the team decision of 2026-09-30, this taxonomy covers only filers who are R (full-year residents). Any other code screens the return out.

## Codes
- R, Resident: domiciled in New Mexico all year, or physically present in New Mexico 185 days or more (except first-year residents and certain military members and spouses)
- F, First-year resident: moved to New Mexico during 2026 intending to stay, and still here on December 31, 2026 (out of scope)
- P, Part-year resident: domiciled in New Mexico for part of the year but not on the last day, and present under 185 days (out of scope)
- N, Nonresident: never domiciled in New Mexico during the year and present under 185 days; also servicemembers temporarily assigned here who keep residency elsewhere (out of scope)

## Notes
- Spouses can have different codes; if either is not R, the return needs PIT-B and is out of scope
- The 185-day presence test can make a person a resident even if domiciled elsewhere

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Domicile for all of 2026, for each spouse
- Days physically present in New Mexico, if domicile is unclear

## Questions
- Did you live in New Mexico for all of 2026?
- Did your spouse also live here all year?
- Did you spend 185 days or more in New Mexico?

## Common Errors
- Marking R for someone who moved in or out during the year
- Forgetting the spouse's code
- Ignoring the 185-day presence rule

## Prompt
- I lived in Albuquerque all year; what residency code do I use?
- My wife moved to New Mexico in September.
