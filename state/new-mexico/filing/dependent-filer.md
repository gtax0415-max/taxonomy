# Filer Who Can Be Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
A student or other person who can be claimed as a dependent on someone else's return still files a PIT-1 if required, but enters 00 on line 5.

## Effects of entering 00
- The low- and middle-income exemption on PIT-1 line 14 is zero, because it is figured per counted exemption
- No deduction for certain dependents on line 13 (only filers who are not dependents may claim it)
- Most PIT-RC rebates and credits are not available to a person who can be claimed as a dependent; check each item on PIT-RC

## Notes
- The test is whether someone CAN claim you, not whether they did
- A dependent filer often files only to get back New Mexico withholding

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Whether another taxpayer can claim the filer
- W-2s and New Mexico withholding

## Questions
- Can your parents or anyone else claim you as a dependent this year?
- Was New Mexico tax withheld from your pay?

## Common Errors
- Entering 01 instead of 00
- Claiming the line 14 exemption or PIT-RC rebates

- I am a college student and my parents claim me; how do I fill out line 5?
