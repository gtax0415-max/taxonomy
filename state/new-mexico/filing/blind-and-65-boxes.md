# Blind and Age 65 or Over Boxes
## Description
Checkboxes on PIT-1 page 1 for the taxpayer (1c, 1d) and spouse (2c, 2d). They are not just information: the Department DENIES the 65-or-blind exemption if the boxes are not marked.

## When to mark
- 1c / 2c: blind for federal income tax purposes
- 1d / 2d: 65 or older on the last day of the tax year (for 2026, born before January 2, 1962)

## What they unlock
- The exemption for persons 65 or older or blind (PIT-ADJ line 13, up to $8,000 each by AGI)
- Age-based items on PIT-RC

## Notes
- The Department may ask for proof of blindness later; do not attach it
- Mark only boxes that match the federal return

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Dates of birth for taxpayer and spouse
- Blindness status

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse 65 or older by December 31, 2026?
- Are either of you legally blind?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the line 13 exemption without marking the boxes
- Marking the spouse's boxes on a separate return

## Prompt
- I turned 65 this year; is there a box to check on the New Mexico return?
