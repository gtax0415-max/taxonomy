# Dependents and Other Dependents
## Description
List each dependent and other dependent claimed on the federal return. New Mexico does not have its own dependency test; it follows the federal return.

## What to enter
- Name, SSN, and date of birth (MM/DD/CCYY) for each
- The first five on line 8; use Schedule PIT-S for the sixth and later

## Why it matters
- Feeds the line 5 exemption count and the low- and middle-income exemption (line 14)
- Feeds the deduction for certain dependents (line 13) for joint and head of household filers
- Feeds dependent-based credits on PIT-RC, such as the child income tax credit

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Name, SSN or ITIN, and date of birth of each dependent
- Whether each is a qualifying child or other dependent federally

## Questions
- Who is listed as a dependent on your federal return?
- Do you have more than five?

## Common Errors
- Listing someone not on the federal return
- Wrong or missing SSN or date of birth
- Forgetting Schedule PIT-S for the sixth dependent and beyond

## Prompt
- I have six children; where do I list them on the New Mexico return?
- Do I list my dependent mother on the New Mexico return?
