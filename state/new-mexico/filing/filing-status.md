# Filing Status
## Description
New Mexico uses the SAME filing status as the federal return. Mark one box on line 7.

## Statuses
1. Single
2. Married filing jointly
3. Married filing separately: enter the spouse's name and SSN on 2a and 2b
4. Head of household: if the qualifying person is not counted as a dependent on the federal return, enter that person's name on 4a
5. Surviving spouse with dependent child

## Why it matters in New Mexico (2026 rate schedules, HB 252 of 2024)
- 1.5%: single $0–5,500; joint, head of household or surviving spouse $0–8,000; married filing separately $0–4,000
- 3.2%: single $5,500–16,500; joint, head of household or surviving spouse $8,000–25,000; married filing separately $4,000–12,500
- 4.3%: single $16,500–33,500; joint, head of household or surviving spouse $25,000–50,000; married filing separately $12,500–25,000
- 4.7%: single $33,500–66,500; joint, head of household or surviving spouse $50,000–100,000; married filing separately $25,000–50,000
- 4.9%: single $66,500–210,000; joint, head of household or surviving spouse $100,000–315,000; married filing separately $50,000–157,500
- 5.9%: single over $210,000; joint, head of household or surviving spouse over $315,000; married filing separately over $157,500

- Head of household and surviving spouse use the JOINT schedule, which is a large benefit
- Status also sets the thresholds for the Social Security exemption, the 65-or-blind table, the low- and middle-income exemption (PIT-1 line 14), and the deduction for certain dependents (line 13)
- Brackets are not indexed for inflation

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session; same brackets as 2025. Confirm against the 2026 rate table when released.

## Required Information
- Federal filing status
- Spouse's name and SSN for married filing separately
- Name of the HOH qualifying person if not a dependent

## Questions
- What filing status are you using on your federal return?
- Are you married but filing separately?

## Common Errors
- Using a different status than on the federal return
- Leaving the spouse's name and SSN blank on a separate return
- Using the single schedule for head of household

## Prompt
- Which filing status do I use on my New Mexico return?
- I am head of household; which New Mexico tax table applies?
