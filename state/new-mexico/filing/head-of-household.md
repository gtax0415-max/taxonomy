# Head of Household
## Description
Available on the New Mexico return only when it is the filing status on the federal return. New Mexico treats head of household like married filing jointly for rates and most thresholds.

## Benefits in New Mexico
- Uses the JOINT rate schedule (1.5% up to $8,000 of taxable income, top 5.9% rate starting above $315,000)
- Joint-level thresholds: Social Security exemption up to $150,000 AGI, low- and middle-income exemption up to $55,000 AGI, 65-or-blind table to $51,000
- Eligible for the deduction for certain dependents on PIT-1 line 13 (available to joint and head of household filers who are not themselves dependents)

## How to report
- Mark box (4) on line 7
- If the person who qualifies you is NOT counted as a dependent on the federal return, write that person's name on 4a

## Notes
- Federal rules decide eligibility (unmarried or considered unmarried, paid more than half the cost of keeping up a home for a qualifying person)

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Federal filing status
- Qualifying person and whether that person is a federal dependent

## Questions
- Are you filing as head of household on your federal return?
- Who is your qualifying person, and are they claimed as your dependent?

## Common Errors
- Claiming head of household in New Mexico without it on the federal return
- Leaving 4a blank when the qualifying person is not a dependent
- Using single-filer thresholds

## Prompt
- I am a single parent filing head of household.
- My qualifying person for head of household is not my dependent.
