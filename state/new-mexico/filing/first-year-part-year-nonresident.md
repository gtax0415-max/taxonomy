# First-Year, Part-Year and Nonresident Returns (PIT-B)
## Description
Filers who were not New Mexico residents all year use Schedule PIT-B to allocate and apportion income. New Mexico tax equals the tax on all income times the New Mexico percentage.

## Scope
- OUT OF SCOPE for this taxonomy (team decision 2026-09-30: full-year New Mexico residents only)
- Kept so the PIT-1 map stays complete and intake can screen these filers out

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Dates of moving into or out of New Mexico
- Income by state

## Questions
- Did you live in New Mexico for all of 2026? (If no, the return is outside this taxonomy.)

## Common Errors
- Treating a first-year or part-year resident as a full-year resident

## Prompt
- I moved to New Mexico in June 2026.
