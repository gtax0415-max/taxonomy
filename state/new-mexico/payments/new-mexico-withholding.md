# New Mexico Income Tax Withheld
## Description
The New Mexico income tax withheld from wages, pensions, gambling winnings, unemployment, and other payments during 2026, as shown on the year-end statements.

## What to include
- Only withholding for NEW MEXICO (check the state code next to each state box)
- W-2 wages, W-2G gambling winnings, 1099-R pensions and IRA distributions, 1099-G unemployment, and any other 1099 showing New Mexico tax withheld

## What does NOT go here
- Oil and gas proceeds withholding (line 28)
- Pass-through entity withholding, composite or entity-level tax (line 29)
- Federal withholding, or tax withheld for another state

## Notes
- Attach the annual statements of income and withholding
- A W-2 with tax withheld for two states lists them on separate rows; take only the New Mexico row

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Every W-2, W-2G and 1099 with a state tax withheld amount, and the state code

## Questions
- Do you have W-2s or 1099s showing New Mexico tax withheld?
- Did any statement show tax withheld for another state?

## Common Errors
- Including another state's withholding
- Entering federal withholding
- Missing a 1099-R or W-2G with New Mexico withholding

## Prompt
- My W-2 shows New Mexico tax withheld.
- My pension withholds New Mexico tax.
