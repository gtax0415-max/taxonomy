# Withholding from Oil and Gas Proceeds
## Description
Tax withheld by an oil and gas remitter from royalty or working-interest payments on New Mexico wells. The owner claims it as a payment and attaches the 1099-MISC or the remitter's statement.

## Who is withheld on
- The withholding requirement applies to payments to NONRESIDENTS
- A full-year resident normally should have NONE. If a remitter withheld anyway (for example, without proof of residency), the resident still claims it here as a credit
- A resident who is being withheld on should ask the remitter to correct its residency records

## Notes
- Do not add oil and gas withholding back to income; that addback applies only to pass-through entity withholding (PIT-ADJ line 5)
- The royalty income itself is in federal AGI (Schedule E)

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Each remitter's 1099-MISC or annual statement showing New Mexico tax withheld

## Questions
- Do you receive oil or gas royalties from New Mexico wells?
- Does your 1099-MISC or remitter statement show New Mexico tax withheld?

## Common Errors
- Putting this withholding on line 27 (wage withholding) or line 29 (pass-through)
- Not attaching the 1099-MISC or statement
- Adding it back to income

## Prompt
- My royalty statement shows New Mexico tax withheld.
