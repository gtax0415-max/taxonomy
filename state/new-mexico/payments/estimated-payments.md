# 2026 Estimated Income Tax Payments
## Description
The total of New Mexico estimated tax paid for tax year 2026, including any 2025 refund the taxpayer chose to apply to 2026.

## What to include
- Quarterly PIT-ES payments for 2026
- The 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 (2025 PIT-1, line 41)
- Do NOT include extension payments or payments made with the return (those go on line 31)

## Who needs to pay estimates
- Generally, anyone whose 2026 New Mexico tax will be $1,000 or more after withholding
- The required annual payment is the LESSER of 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax
- 2026 installments were due April 15, June 15, and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027

## Notes
- Falling short can trigger an underpayment penalty (see payments/underpayment-penalty.md)
- A payment made in January 2027 for the fourth quarter counts for 2026

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each 2026 estimated payment
- 2025 overpayment applied to 2026

## Questions
- Did you make New Mexico estimated payments for 2026?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the applied 2025 overpayment
- Including extension payments here instead of line 31
- Counting a federal estimated payment as a New Mexico payment

## Prompt
- I paid New Mexico estimated tax every quarter this year.
- I applied my 2025 refund to my 2026 taxes.
