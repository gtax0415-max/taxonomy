# Late Filing or Payment Penalty and Interest
## Description
Charges the Department adds when a 2026 return is filed late or tax is paid after April 15, 2027. Reported on PIT-1 lines 36 and 37.

## Firm policy
- NOT SUPPORTED on the return: lines 36 and 37 are left blank and the Department bills any penalty and interest (firm policy, 2026-10-06)
- This file exists so staff can explain a notice

## Penalty (line 36)
- 2% of the unpaid tax for each month or part of a month after the due date
- Capped at 20% of the unpaid tax
- Applies to late filing or late payment; an extension avoids the late-filing penalty but not penalty on late payment

## Interest (line 37)
- Charged on tax unpaid after April 15, 2027, accruing DAILY
- Rate: the federal (IRC) underpayment rate, which changes quarterly and is posted by the Department
- Runs even with an extension, and by law cannot be waived

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Date the return is filed
- Dates and amounts of payments after April 15, 2027

## Questions
- Will you file and pay by April 15, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Assuming an extension prevents penalty or interest on unpaid tax
- Asking for interest to be waived

## Prompt
- What does New Mexico charge if I pay late?
- I got a New Mexico notice for penalty and interest.
