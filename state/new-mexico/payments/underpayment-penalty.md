# Penalty on Underpayment of Estimated Tax
## Description
A penalty for not paying enough tax through withholding and estimates during 2026. Reported on PIT-1 lines 34 and 35.

## Firm policy
- NOT SUPPORTED on the return: lines 34 and 35 are left blank and the Department bills any penalty (firm policy, 2026-10-06)
- This file exists so staff can explain a notice

## When it can apply
- 2026 New Mexico tax of $1,000 or more after withholding, and
- Payments by each installment date fell short of the required annual payment: the LESSER of 90% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax
- The penalty is figured like interest, at the IRC underpayment rate, on each late installment

## Notes
- Line 35 holds a code (1–5) for a special calculation method on RPD-41272, such as annualized income; exceptions are in the PIT-ES instructions
- Leaving line 34 blank does not waive the penalty; it moves the calculation to the Department

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- 2025 New Mexico tax (prior year)
- Dates and amounts of 2026 estimated payments and withholding

## Questions
- Did you owe $1,000 or more when you filed last year?
- Did your withholding and estimates cover 100% of last year's tax?

## Common Errors
- Assuming a refund this year means no penalty (timing of payments matters)

## Prompt
- I got a New Mexico notice for underpayment of estimated tax.
