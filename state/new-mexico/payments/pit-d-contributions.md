# Voluntary Refund Contributions (PIT-D)
## Description
Lets you donate part or all of your New Mexico refund to state funds. It reduces your refund only; it is not a deduction and it does not change tax.

## Funds (2025 PIT-D, lines 2–17)
- New Mexico Housing Trust Fund
- Share With Wildlife
- Veterans' State Cemetery Fund
- New Mexico Substance Abuse Education Fund
- New Mexico Forest Re-Leaf Program
- National Guard Member and Family Assistance
- Kids 'N Parks Transportation Grant Program
- Amyotrophic Lateral Sclerosis Research Fund
- Vietnam Veterans Memorial
- New Mexico Political Parties: $2 per person to one party (1 Democratic, 2 Republican, 3 Libertarian, 4 Green, 5 Better for America, 6 Constitution); the spouse's $2 only on a joint return, so the line is $2 or $4
- Veterans Enterprise Fund
- Lottery Tuition Fund
- Equine Shelter Rescue Fund
- Animal Care and Facility Fund
- Supplemental Senior Services
- Healthy Soil Program

## Amount
- Any amount per fund, except the fixed $2 per person political party checkoff
- The total cannot exceed the overpayment on PIT-1 line 39
- If contributions exceed the refund, NONE are made

## Notes
- Contributions are not refundable, even on an amended return
- Taxpayers who owe tax can give directly to the funds, not through the return
- Refunds intercepted for debts are not available for contributions

## Status for 2026
No new or repealed refund checkoff was found in the 2026 legislative session. The list above is from the 2025 PIT-D; confirm it against the 2026 PIT-D when released. (The prior file's claim that 2025 HB 218 consolidated these distributions could not be verified and has been removed.)

## Required Information
- Overpayment on PIT-1 line 39
- Fund and amount for each contribution
- Party number for the taxpayer and, on a joint return, the spouse

## Questions
- Do you expect a New Mexico refund?
- Would you like to donate part of it to a state fund or a political party?

## Common Errors
- Contributions larger than the overpayment, which voids all of them
- Entering a political party amount other than $2 or $4
- Claiming a spouse's party checkoff on a separate return

## Prompt
- I want to donate part of my New Mexico refund to Share With Wildlife.
- Can I give $2 to a political party on my New Mexico return?
