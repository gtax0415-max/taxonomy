# Other Payments (Including Extension Payments)
## Description
Payments for tax year 2026 that are not withholding or estimated tax, mainly a payment sent with an extension.

## What to include
- Payments made with a 2026 extension
- Other payments made for tax year 2026 before this return is filed, including a payment sent earlier with a PIT-PV voucher

## What does NOT go here
- Quarterly estimated payments and the 2025 overpayment applied (line 30)
- Withholding (lines 27–29)
- A payment mailed with this return to pay the balance due shown on it; that pays the amount owed and is not entered as a payment

## Extensions (time to file, not time to pay)
- Original due date: April 15, 2027 (paper and electronic; the old April 30 e-file date was removed starting with 2025 returns)
- New Mexico honors the federal Form 4868 extension: October 15, 2027
- A New Mexico-only extension must be requested by April 15, 2027
- Mark box 6a and enter the extended due date on 6b; do not attach the extension
- Interest runs from April 15, 2027 on any unpaid tax, extension or not, so pay the expected balance with the extension

## Status for 2026
No change from the 2026 legislative session.

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each extension or other payment
- Whether a federal extension was filed

## Questions
- Did you file a federal or New Mexico extension for 2026?
- Did you pay anything when you requested it?
- Did you make any other payment for 2026 that was not an estimated payment?

## Common Errors
- Double-counting an extension payment on line 30 and line 31
- Entering the balance-due payment sent with this return
- Assuming an extension delays payment
- Leaving box 6a blank on an extended return

## Prompt
- I paid $2,000 with my New Mexico extension.
- I filed a federal extension; do I need a separate one for New Mexico?
