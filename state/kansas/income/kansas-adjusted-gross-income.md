# Kansas Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
Kansas AGI = federal AGI (line 1) plus or minus the net Kansas modification (line 2). Most filers have no modifications and simply copy line 1 to line 3.

## Amount
- Line 2 = Schedule S additions (A1–A8) minus subtractions (A10–A25), carried from line A27
- If the net modification is negative, shade the minus box on line 2
- Line 3 = line 1 plus or minus line 2

## When Schedule S is needed
- Income taxed federally but NOT by Kansas: Social Security benefits, KPERS and federal/military retirement pay, U.S. Treasury interest, state income tax refunds, 529/ABLE/first-time home buyer/adoption savings contributions, and others
- Income NOT taxed federally but taxed by Kansas: other states' municipal bond interest, KPERS contributions, and others (see income/additions/)

## 2026 notes
- Two subtractions enacted in the 2026 session (health care sharing expenses, KDOR Notice 26-07; portable benefit plan contributions, Notice 26-08) apply only to tax years beginning after December 31, 2026. They do NOT apply to 2026 returns
- Kansas AGI is used for the Kansas credits that have income tests and as the base for tax; the medical deduction floor uses FEDERAL AGI instead

## Required Information
- Federal AGI
- Every Schedule S addition and subtraction

## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security, KPERS, military or federal retirement income?
- Do you have bond interest from other states?
- Did you contribute to a Kansas 529, ABLE, first-time home buyer or adoption savings account?

## Common Errors
- Leaving line 2 blank when Social Security is in federal AGI
- Wrong sign on the net modification
- Using Kansas AGI where federal AGI is required (medical floor)

## Prompt
- How do I get from federal AGI to Kansas AGI?
- My Social Security is taxed federally. Is it taxed in Kansas?
