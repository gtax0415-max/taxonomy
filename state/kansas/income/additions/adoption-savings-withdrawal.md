# Unqualified Adoption Savings Account Withdrawals
## Description
Addition that recaptures the Kansas subtraction previously taken for an adoption savings account when the money is NOT used as the law allows. Contributions (up to $6,000, or $12,000 married filing joint, per account) and account earnings are subtracted when deposited or earned; a nonqualifying withdrawal reverses that benefit.

## Amount
Add back the contributions to, and earnings from, the account to the extent that:
- Distributions were not used for adoption expenses or transactions authorized by law, or
- The funds were not held for the minimum time K.S.A. 38-2504 requires, or
- The account holder did not designate a surviving payable-on-death beneficiary (K.S.A. 38-2504)

Unchanged for 2026.

## Notes
- Only amounts that produced a Kansas subtraction need to come back
- Qualified adoption expenses paid from the account are not added back
- Separate from the federal adoption credit (Form 8839) and the Kansas adoption credit; using account money for an expense does not stop that expense from also counting toward those credits under their own rules, but confirm double-benefit limits before claiming both

## Required Information
- Total contributions and earnings subtracted in prior years
- Amount, date and use of each 2026 withdrawal
- Date funds were deposited
- Beneficiary designation

## Questions
- Do you have a Kansas adoption savings account?
- Did you withdraw money in 2026, and was it spent on adoption expenses?
- Did you stop the adoption or close the account?
- Is a payable-on-death beneficiary named?

## Common Errors
- Not adding back money withdrawn for non-adoption uses
- Adding back amounts never subtracted for Kansas
- Confusing this account with a 529 plan or a first-time home buyer account

## Prompt
- We withdrew from our Kansas adoption savings account but the adoption fell through.
- I used my adoption savings account to pay agency fees.
