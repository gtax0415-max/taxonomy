# Other Additions
## Description
Catch-all addition line for items not printed on their own Schedule S line. The total goes on line A8, with a list showing each item.

## Items included
- FEDERAL INCOME TAX REFUND: generally only a refund received in 2026 for a prior year because of a carryback of an investment credit or net operating loss
- PASS-THROUGH ADJUSTMENTS: the owner's share of required addition modifications from a partnership, S corporation, joint venture, estate or trust, as reported by the entity
- COMMUNITY SERVICE CONTRIBUTION CREDIT: charitable gifts deducted federally or on Kansas Schedule A that were used to compute the Kansas community service contribution credit
- 529 NONQUALIFIED WITHDRAWAL: any nonqualified withdrawal from a qualified tuition program, since contributions were earlier subtracted for Kansas
- AMORTIZATION – ENERGY CREDITS: federal amortization claimed on property tied to certain Kansas energy credits, or on carbon dioxide capture equipment and waste heat utilization property
- AD VALOREM TAXES (nonresidents): property taxes paid to another state by a nonresident, if that state does not let Kansas residents deduct Kansas property taxes, to the extent deducted federally
- ABORTION-RELATED FEDERAL CREDITS: federal credits (such as the premium tax credit) allowed for coverage that includes abortion

## Notes
- No new 2026 category was found in 2026 Kansas legislation or KDOR notices; the list follows the most recent (2025) instructions
- OBBBA expanded federal 529 uses for 2026 (e.g., K-12 limit raised to $20,000 and more non-tuition K-12 costs). Whether Kansas treats every newly federal-qualified use as qualified for its add-back should be confirmed against the 2026 KDOR instructions
- Each item must be listed separately on the enclosed list

## Required Information
- Each addition item and amount
- K-1 Kansas modification statements
- 1099-Q and use of 529 funds
- Credit schedules that drove any add-back

## Questions
- Did you receive a K-1 showing Kansas addition modifications?
- Did you take money from a 529 plan for something other than qualified education expenses?
- Did you receive a federal refund from a carryback?
- Did you claim the Kansas community service contribution credit?
- Are you a nonresident who deducted property taxes paid to another state?

## Common Errors
- Entering a regular federal refund (not taxable to Kansas)
- Missing the pass-through entity's addition statement
- Omitting the list of items
- Treating a qualified 529 withdrawal as nonqualified

## Prompt
- My partnership K-1 shows a Kansas addition.
- I withdrew from my 529 plan for a non-school expense.
