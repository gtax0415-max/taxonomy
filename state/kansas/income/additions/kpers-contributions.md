# KPERS Contributions
## Description
Addition for an employee's own contributions to the Kansas Public Employees Retirement System and related state plans. These contributions are excluded from federal wages (W-2 box 1), so Kansas adds them back. Kansas instead exempts the KPERS pension later, when it is paid out.

## Who it applies to
- State employees, teachers, school district employees and other regular and special KPERS members
- Members of the Kansas Police and Firemen's Retirement System (KP&F)
- Members of the Kansas Justices and Judges Retirement System

## Amount
- The full amount contributed from salary in 2026, as shown on the W-2 (usually box 14)
- No cap, no phase-out. Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Retired members receiving KPERS checks make NO entry here unless they also contributed during 2026 (for example, retired mid-year). Their pension is a subtraction on a different Schedule S line
- A member who took a KPERS lump-sum distribution in 2026 still adds back 2026 contributions here; the distribution itself is handled as a subtraction
- Box 14 labels vary by employer ("KPERS", "KPERS 414(h)", "KP&F"); check the pay stub if the W-2 is unclear

## Required Information
- W-2 box 14 amount for each Kansas public employer
- Whether the filer retired or took a lump sum in 2026

## Questions
- Do you work for the State of Kansas, a school district, or another KPERS employer?
- Are you a member of KP&F or the Judges retirement system?
- Did you retire or withdraw your KPERS contributions in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the add-back because the amount is in box 14 rather than box 1
- A retiree adding back contributions when none were made in 2026
- Missing the spouse's W-2 on a joint return

## Prompt
- I am a teacher in Kansas and my W-2 shows KPERS in box 14.
- I retired from the state this year.
