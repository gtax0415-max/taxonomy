# Business Interest Expense Carryforward Deduction
## Description
Addition that pairs with the Kansas subtraction for disallowed business interest. Kansas does not follow the IRC §163(j) limit in the year the interest is paid: it lets the full interest be subtracted that year (Schedule S, line A19). When the disallowed interest is later deducted federally as a carryforward, Kansas adds it back so the same interest is not deducted twice.

## Amount
- Interest paid or accrued in an EARLIER year that is allowed as a federal deduction in 2026 as a §163(j) carryforward
- Interest counts as paid or accrued only in the first year it would have been deductible without §163(j)
- Unchanged Kansas rule for 2026

## Notes
- Only carryforward interest from tax years after December 31, 2020 (when the Kansas subtraction began) should be added back; interest disallowed before Kansas allowed the subtraction was never deducted for Kansas
- OBBBA restored the EBITDA-based adjusted taxable income for tax years beginning after 2024, so more carryforward may be released federally in 2026, which raises this add-back
- Small businesses under the §448(c) gross-receipts test are exempt from §163(j) and normally have nothing here
- Partners: excess business interest is tracked at the partner level through the K-1, so the release year follows the partner's own Form 8990

## Required Information
- Carryforward of disallowed business interest released on the 2026 Form 8990
- Year each released amount was originally paid or accrued
- Whether the interest was subtracted for Kansas in that year

## Questions
- Do you file Form 8990 for a business or as a partner?
- Did you deduct a business interest carryforward on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you subtract the disallowed interest on an earlier Kansas return?

## Common Errors
- Deducting the interest for Kansas in the year paid and again when released, with no add-back
- Adding back carryforward from years before Kansas allowed the subtraction
- Confusing this addition (A5) with the disallowed-interest subtraction (A19)

## Prompt
- My business interest was limited federally and carried forward.
- I deducted a business interest carryforward on my 2026 return.
