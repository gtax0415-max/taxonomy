# Low Income Student Scholarship Contributions
## Description
Addition that prevents a double benefit. A contribution to a Kansas scholarship granting organization can earn the Kansas Low Income Student Scholarship credit AND a federal charitable deduction. Kansas requires the contribution used to figure the credit to be added back to the extent it was deducted on the federal return.

## Amount
- The charitable contribution claimed on the federal return that was used to compute the Kansas scholarship credit
- The instructions tie the add-back to a contribution "claimed on your federal return." If the filer took the federal standard deduction and did not claim the gift, confirm the treatment against the 2026 KDOR instructions when posted
- Itemized deductions do not enter federal AGI, so this add-back works as an offset at the Kansas AGI level rather than reversing an AGI reduction
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- The credit itself is claimed separately as a Kansas credit; this file only covers the add-back
- 2026 Senate Bill 361 makes Kansas a participant in the NEW federal credit for donations to scholarship granting organizations (IRC §25F), but only for tax years beginning after December 31, 2026. It does NOT affect 2026 returns
- The same donation should not be counted as a Kansas Schedule A charitable gift for the portion that produced a state credit

## Required Information
- Amount donated and date
- Name of the scholarship granting organization
- Amount of Kansas credit claimed
- Amount deducted on federal Schedule A

## Questions
- Did you donate to a Kansas scholarship granting organization in 2026?
- Are you claiming the Kansas Low Income Student Scholarship credit for it?
- Did you itemize and deduct the donation on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the Kansas credit without adding back the federal deduction
- Also deducting the same contribution on Kansas Schedule A
- Treating the new federal §25F scholarship credit as available for 2026 (it begins in 2027)

## Prompt
- I gave to a scholarship organization and got a Kansas tax credit.
- Can I deduct a donation that also gave me a state credit?
