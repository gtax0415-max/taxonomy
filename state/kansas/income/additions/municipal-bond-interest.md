# State and Municipal Bond Interest
## Description
Addition for interest on bonds of OTHER states and their local governments. The interest is tax-exempt federally (it never reaches federal AGI), so Kansas adds it back. Interest on Kansas bonds issued after December 31, 1987 stays exempt and is NOT added.

## Amount
- Enter non-Kansas state and municipal bond interest received, credited or earned in 2026
- Reduce it by related expenses directly incurred to buy the bonds (management or trustee fees)
- Mutual funds holding bonds of several states: add back only the non-Kansas share, using the percentage the fund reports
- No dollar cap or threshold. Unchanged for 2026: no 2026 law change affects this line

## What is NOT added back
- Kansas state or local bonds issued after 12/31/1987
- Bonds Kansas law exempts by name: Board of Regents bonds, Electrical Generation Revenue Bonds, Industrial Revenue Bonds, Kansas Highway Bonds, Kansas Turnpike Authority Bonds, Urban Renewal Bonds
- U.S. Treasury and U.S. agency interest (that is a SUBTRACTION, not an addition)

## Notes
- The federal starting point is Form 1040, line 2a (tax-exempt interest), which is informational only and is not in federal AGI
- Exempt-interest dividends from a fund appear in 1099-DIV box 12, not 1099-INT; both must be checked
- Kansas bonds issued on or before 12/31/1987 are NOT exempt and must be added back

## Required Information
- Total tax-exempt interest and exempt-interest dividends
- Issuing state of each bond, or the fund's state percentage
- Issue date of any Kansas bonds (before or after 12/31/1987)
- Related expenses

## Questions
- Did you receive tax-exempt interest or exempt-interest dividends in 2026?
- Which states issued the bonds, or what share did your fund report as Kansas?
- Did you pay fees to manage the bond holdings?

## Common Errors
- Adding back Kansas bond interest that is exempt
- Adding back the full fund distribution instead of only the non-Kansas share
- Missing 1099-DIV box 12 exempt-interest dividends
- Forgetting to reduce by related expenses

## Prompt
- I have a municipal bond fund that pays tax-free interest.
- Do I pay Kansas tax on my Missouri municipal bonds?
