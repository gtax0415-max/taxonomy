# Disallowed Business Meal Expenses
## Description
Kansas lets business filers subtract meal costs that federal law (IRC §274) now disallows, to the extent the expense would have been deductible under §274 as in effect on December 31, 2017. Applies to tax years after 2020.

## Amount
- Meal expenses disallowed in 2026 under §274
- Limited to the portion that was deductible under the §274 rules in effect on December 31, 2017
- Unchanged statute for 2026

## Why it matters in 2026
- From 2026, federal §274(o) DISALLOWS 100% of employer costs for meals provided for the employer's convenience on business premises (on-site cafeterias, de minimis meals). Under 2017 law many of these were fully deductible, so this Kansas subtraction can be significant for employers starting in 2026
- Ordinary business meals: 50% deductible both now and in 2017, so the disallowed 50% generally does NOT qualify
- Entertainment is not a meal expense and does not qualify

## Required Information
- Total meal expenses by type and the federal disallowed amount
- Which costs were deductible under 2017 rules

## Questions
- Do you own a business that provides meals to employees on premises?
- Did your 2026 federal return disallow any business meals beyond the regular 50%?
- Do you receive a K-1 showing meal disallowances?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the standard 50% disallowed half of ordinary business meals
- Subtracting entertainment
- Missing the new 2026 100% disallowance of employer-convenience meals

## Prompt
- My company provides free lunches to employees in our break room.
- Can I deduct the other half of business meals in Kansas?
