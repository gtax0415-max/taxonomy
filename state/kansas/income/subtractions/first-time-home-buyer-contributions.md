# First Time Home Buyer Savings Account Contributions
## Description
Subtraction for money saved in a Kansas first-time home buyer savings account (K.S.A. 58-4903): contributions, plus the income earned in the account. Applies to tax years after 2021.

## Amount
- Contributions up to $3,000 for an individual or $6,000 married filing joint
- PLUS income earned on account assets (interest, dividends) included in federal AGI
- Unchanged for 2026

## Timing election
Contributions made from January 1, 2027 until the 2026 return's due date may be applied to 2026 if elected when filing. A contribution can be used in only one year.

## Notes
- The account must be designated as a first-time home buyer savings account with a financial institution
- Withdrawals not used for an eligible first-home purchase, not held long enough, or with no payable-on-death beneficiary are added back (see income/additions/fthb-unqualified-withdrawal.md)
- Enclose the Kansas first-time home buyer savings account schedule with the return

## Required Information
- Contributions and dates
- Earnings on the account
- Account designation

## Questions
- Do you have a Kansas first-time home buyer savings account?
- How much did you deposit in 2026, and what did it earn?
- Did you deposit between January 1 and April 15, 2027 for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to subtract the account's earnings
- Exceeding the $3,000 / $6,000 limit
- Using a regular savings account not designated for this program
