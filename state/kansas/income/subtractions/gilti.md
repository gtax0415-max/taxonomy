# Global Intangible Low-taxed Income (now Net CFC Tested Income)
## Description
Kansas subtracts 100% of the income a U.S. shareholder includes under IRC §951A from controlled foreign corporations, before the §250 deduction. For tax years beginning after 2025, OBBBA renamed GILTI "net CFC tested income" (NCTI) and changed how it is computed. 2026 Kansas SB 300 (signed April 27, 2026) removed the now-obsolete GILTI wording but KEPT the subtraction for §951A income.

## Amount
- 100% of the §951A inclusion in federal taxable income, before any §250(a)(1)(B) deduction
- For 2026, this is the NCTI inclusion under the amended §951A
- Unchanged in substance for 2026; the 2026 Schedule S line title may change

## Notes
- Rare on individual returns: applies to individuals who own 10% or more of a foreign corporation, directly or through a pass-through entity
- Individuals who made a §962 election should confirm treatment with the 2026 instructions
- The §250 deduction is not available to individuals without a §962 election

## Required Information
- §951A inclusion amount for 2026
- Source (direct ownership or K-1)

## Questions
- Do you own 10% or more of a foreign corporation, directly or through a partnership or S corporation?
- Did your 2026 federal return include a §951A (GILTI/NCTI) amount?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the amount after the §250 deduction instead of 100%
- Assuming the subtraction ended because the GILTI wording was removed
- Missing an inclusion reported on a K-1

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a GILTI inclusion.
- I own part of a foreign company. Does Kansas tax that income?
