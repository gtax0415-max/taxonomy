# Retirement Benefits Exempt from Kansas Tax
## Description
Kansas exempts retirement income from specific public plans. The taxable amount from these plans that is in federal AGI is subtracted. Private pensions, IRAs, 401(k)s and 403(b)s from private employers remain TAXABLE in Kansas.

## Exempt plans
- Federal civil service retirement or disability payments, and other federal employment retirement benefits, including the Thrift Savings Plan
- U.S. Armed Forces retired pay
- U.S. Railroad Retirement Board benefits: tier I, tier II, dual vested and supplemental annuities
- KPERS annuities
- Kansas Police and Firemen's Retirement System pensions
- Overland Park police and fire department retirement plan distributions
- Kansas Teachers' Retirement annuities
- Kansas Highway Patrol pensions
- Kansas Justices and Judges Retirement System annuities
- Board of Public Utilities pensions
- Retirement annuity contracts for faculty and others employed by the State Board of Regents or its institutions (direct or salary-reduction contributions)
- Washburn University retirement plan benefits
- Certain pensions from Kansas first-class cities not covered by KPERS

## Amount
- The taxable amount from these plans included in federal AGI; no cap. Unchanged for 2026
- Do NOT enclose 1099-Rs; keep them for KDOR

## What does NOT qualify
- Social Security (subtracted on A10)
- KPERS lump sums (A11 or A25)
- Private employer pensions, IRAs, 401(k)s, 403(b)s from non-Regents employers
- Other states' public pensions (e.g., Missouri LAGERS) — taxable in Kansas

## Notes
- Military SURVIVOR benefits (SBP) paid from military retirement should be confirmed against the 2026 instructions
- Money rolled from an exempt plan (e.g., TSP) into an IRA: the instructions list the plans, not IRAs, so treat IRA distributions as taxable unless the 2026 instructions say otherwise

## Required Information
- Each 1099-R and the plan that paid it

## Questions
- Do you receive a KPERS, federal civil service, military or Railroad Retirement pension?
- Did you take TSP withdrawals?
- Do you have pensions from other states or private employers?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting private pensions or IRA distributions
- Subtracting another state's public pension
- Subtracting Railroad Retirement here and on the Social Security line

## Prompt
- I am a retired federal employee living in Kansas.
- Is my military retirement taxed in Kansas?
