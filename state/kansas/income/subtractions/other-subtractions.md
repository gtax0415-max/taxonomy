# Other Subtractions
## Description
Catch-all subtraction line for items not printed on their own Schedule S line. Total on line A25, with a list showing each item. Income reported to another state cannot be subtracted.

## Items included
- KANSAS VENTURE CAPITAL, INC. dividends
- KPERS LUMP SUM: withdrawn accumulated contributions (or partial lump sum) taken by a member who left KPERS employment after 7/1/1984, to the extent in federal AGI
- PASS-THROUGH ADJUSTMENTS: the owner's share of required subtraction modifications from a partnership, S corporation, joint venture, trust or estate, as reported by the entity
- S CORPORATION PRIVILEGE ADJUSTMENT: undistributed income from a bank or other financial institution S corporation already taxed under the Kansas privilege tax
- SALE OF KANSAS TURNPIKE BONDS: gain included in federal AGI
- ELECTRICAL GENERATION REVENUE BONDS: gain from sale included in federal AGI
- NATIVE AMERICAN RESERVATION INCOME: income earned on the reservation by a Native American living on their own tribal reservation, to the extent in federal AGI
- AMORTIZATION – ENERGY CREDITS: amortization for property tied to certain Kansas energy credits and for carbon dioxide capture or waste heat utilization property (55% in year one, 5% for each of the next nine years)
- ORGAN DONOR EXPENSES: unreimbursed travel, lodging and medical costs of a living donor (filer or dependent), up to $5,000
- COMPENSATION FRAUDULENTLY OBTAINED: unemployment or other compensation obtained by an identity thief in the filer's name, never received, but included in federal AGI

## Not available for 2026
2026 legislation created two new subtractions — health care sharing ministry expenses (up to $5,000 / $10,000 joint; Notice 26-07) and portable benefit plan contributions for independent contractors (Notice 26-08). Both apply only to tax years beginning after December 31, 2026, so they first appear on 2027 returns.

## Required Information
- Each subtraction item and amount
- K-1 Kansas modification statements
- Supporting records for each item

## Questions
- Did you receive a K-1 showing Kansas subtraction modifications?
- Did you withdraw your KPERS contributions after leaving state employment?
- Did you donate an organ as a living donor in 2026?
- Were you a victim of unemployment benefit identity fraud?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the 2027 health care sharing or portable benefit subtractions on a 2026 return
- Exceeding the $5,000 organ donor limit
- Omitting the list of items
- Subtracting income reported to another state

## Prompt
- My S corporation K-1 shows a Kansas subtraction.
- I donated a kidney this year. Is there a Kansas tax break?
