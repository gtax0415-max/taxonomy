# Kansas Expensing Deduction
## Description
An elective Kansas deduction (K.S.A. 79-32,143a) for business property placed in service IN KANSAS during 2026. It accelerates depreciation for Kansas by giving an extra deduction in year one, computed with a statutory factor. Available to all income tax filers for tax years after 2020.

## Amount
1. Federal depreciable basis
2. Minus federal bonus depreciation (§168(k)) and §179 expense on that property
3. = Net basis × factor from the statutory table (by recovery period and method)
Example: 5-year property, 200% declining balance, net basis $10,000 × 0.116 = $1,160 subtraction

If new expensing is larger than any recapture of earlier expensing, the net goes here; if recapture is larger, the net is an addition (see income/additions/kansas-expensing-recapture.md).

## Qualifying property
- MACRS (§168) tangible property with a recovery period of 25 years or less, placed in service in Kansas
- Off-the-shelf computer software (§197(e)(3)), apportioned by Kansas users
- NOT residential rental or nonresidential real property

## 2026 interaction with federal law
OBBBA permanently restored 100% bonus depreciation for property acquired after January 19, 2025, and raised the §179 limit ($2,560,000 for 2026, phasing out above $4,090,000). When the full cost is expensed federally, the net basis is zero and there is NO Kansas expensing deduction. The deduction mainly helps property where bonus was elected out or does not apply.

## Notes
- The election must be made on a timely filed original return (with extensions) for the year placed in service
- Property used for the expensing deduction cannot also be used for certain Kansas credits (e.g., R&D, HPIP)
- Sale or move out of Kansas within the recovery period triggers recapture
- Enclose federal Form 4562 with the Kansas computation

## Required Information
- Asset list with Kansas location, cost, recovery period and method
- Bonus and §179 claimed

## Questions
- Did you place business equipment or software in service in Kansas in 2026?
- Did you take bonus depreciation or §179 on it federally?
- Will the asset stay in Kansas for its recovery period?

## Common Errors
- Computing on full cost without subtracting bonus and §179
- Claiming on real property
- Missing the original-return election deadline

## Prompt
- I bought equipment for my Kansas business.
- Is there a Kansas depreciation deduction?
