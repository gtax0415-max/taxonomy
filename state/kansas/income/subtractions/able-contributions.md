# ABLE Account Contributions
## Description
Subtraction for contributions to an ABLE account (IRC §529A) for a person with a disability — the Kansas ABLE program or a qualified program of another state. Federal law allows no deduction for ABLE contributions.

## Amount
- Up to $3,000 per beneficiary, or $6,000 per beneficiary for married filing joint
- Unchanged for 2026

## Timing election
Contributions made from January 1, 2027 until the 2026 return's due date may be applied to 2026 if elected when filing. Each contribution can be used in only one year.

## 2026 federal context
- Annual ABLE contribution limit follows the federal gift tax exclusion ($19,000 for 2026)
- From 2026, ABLE eligibility expands to people whose disability began before age 46 (previously 26), so more families can open accounts
- OBBBA made permanent the 529-to-ABLE rollover and the extra contribution for working beneficiaries; a 529 rollover is not a new contribution for this subtraction

## Required Information
- Contributions per beneficiary and dates
- Election for early-2027 contributions

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an ABLE account in 2026?
- Who is the beneficiary, and is it a Kansas or out-of-state plan?
- Did you contribute between January 1 and April 15, 2027 for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the per-beneficiary limit
- Treating a 529-to-ABLE rollover as a contribution
- Using the same contribution in two years

## Prompt
- I contributed to my son's ABLE account.
- Do I get a Kansas deduction for ABLE contributions?
