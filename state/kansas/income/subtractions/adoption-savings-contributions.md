# Adoption Savings Account Contributions
## Description
Subtraction for money saved in a Kansas adoption savings account (K.S.A. 38-2504): contributions, plus the income earned in the account.

## Amount
- Contributions up to $6,000 for an individual or $12,000 married filing joint, PER ACCOUNT
- PLUS income earned on account assets included in federal AGI
- Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Withdrawals not used for adoption expenses, not held for the required time, or with no payable-on-death beneficiary are added back (see income/additions/adoption-savings-withdrawal.md)
- Separate from the federal adoption credit (2026 maximum $17,670, with up to $5,120 refundable) and the Kansas adoption credit
- The 2025 instructions do not print a carryback election (Jan 1–April 15) for this account; confirm with the 2026 instructions before applying early-2027 deposits to 2026

## Required Information
- Contributions per account and dates
- Earnings on the account

## Questions
- Do you have a Kansas adoption savings account?
- How much did you deposit in 2026, and what did it earn?
- Do you have more than one adoption savings account?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to subtract the account's earnings
- Exceeding the per-account limit
- Applying 2027 deposits to 2026 without confirming the election is allowed
