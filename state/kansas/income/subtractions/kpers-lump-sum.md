# KPERS Lump Sum Distributions
## Description
Subtraction for withdrawals from a qualified retirement account (such as an IRA) of money that ORIGINALLY came from a KPERS lump-sum payment at retirement and was rolled over, plus the earnings on it, to the extent included in federal AGI. KPERS retirement benefits are exempt in Kansas, and this keeps that exemption after a rollover.

## Amount
- Withdrawals in 2026, including earnings, from a qualified account to the extent the money:
  1. was originally received as a KPERS lump-sum payment at retirement and rolled over into that account, and
  2. is included in 2026 federal AGI
- Unchanged for 2026

## What does NOT qualify
- Money originally from Regents/education-institution retirement annuity contracts (TIAA-type plans) — those are handled as exempt retirement benefits (A14)
- A pension from a Kansas first-class city not covered by KPERS
- Rolled-over money mixed with non-KPERS contributions: only the KPERS-traceable part

## Notes
- A KPERS lump sum taken directly as cash (not rolled over) by someone who terminated employment after 7/1/1984 is handled under other subtractions (A25), not this line
- Tracing records are essential when the IRA also holds other money

## Required Information
- Withdrawal amount and taxable portion from 1099-R
- Records tracing the KPERS rollover and the account balance attributable to it

## Questions
- Did you roll a KPERS lump sum into an IRA or other retirement account?
- Did you withdraw from that account in 2026?
- Does the account also hold money from other sources?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting withdrawals that are not traceable to a KPERS lump sum
- Using this line for a cash KPERS lump sum (belongs in A25)
- Subtracting the same amount here and on A14

## Prompt
- I rolled my KPERS lump sum into an IRA and took a withdrawal.
- Is my IRA distribution exempt in Kansas because it came from KPERS?
