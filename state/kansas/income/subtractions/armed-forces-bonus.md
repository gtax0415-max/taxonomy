# Armed Forces Recruitment, Sign-up or Retention Bonus
## Description
Subtraction for bonuses received as an incentive to join, enlist or remain in the U.S. armed forces, including the Kansas Army and Air National Guard, and for military-paid education or student loan repayments, to the extent included in federal AGI.

## Amount
- Recruitment, sign-up or retention bonuses included in 2026 federal AGI
- Repayments of education or student loans the member incurred or is obligated on, received because of service in the U.S. armed forces, to the extent in federal AGI
- No cap. Unchanged for 2026

## Notes
- Bonuses earned in a combat zone are excluded federally and are not in federal AGI, so there is nothing to subtract
- Regular military pay is NOT subtracted by Kansas residents (only nonresidents use A15)

## Required Information
- Bonus and loan repayment amounts and whether they are in federal wages

## Questions
- Did you receive an enlistment, reenlistment or retention bonus in 2026?
- Did the military repay any of your student loans?
- Was the bonus earned in a combat zone?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting combat-zone bonuses already excluded federally
- Subtracting regular military pay

## Prompt
- I received a reenlistment bonus from the Kansas National Guard.
- The Army paid off part of my student loans.
