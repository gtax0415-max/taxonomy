# Federal Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
Kansas starts from FEDERAL adjusted gross income. Every income item (wages, interest, dividends, capital gains, business, farm, rental, retirement, Social Security that is federally taxable, unemployment) and every federal "above-the-line" adjustment reaches Kansas through this one number. Kansas follows the Internal Revenue Code as amended (K.S.A. 79-32,109), so 2026 federal law changes flow through automatically unless Kansas modifies them on Schedule S.

## Amount
- Copy federal AGI exactly as reported on the 2026 Form 1040
- If AGI is negative, shade the minus box
- A part-year resident or nonresident still enters TOTAL federal AGI here; the Kansas share is figured on Schedule S, Part B

## 2026 federal changes and what reaches Kansas
- In federal AGI (so in Kansas): items federal law changes ABOVE the line, such as the restored EBITDA-based business interest limit and 100% bonus depreciation for property acquired after January 19, 2025
- NOT in federal AGI (so they do NOT reduce Kansas income): the new OBBBA deductions on Schedule 1-A — qualified tips, qualified overtime, car-loan interest and the $6,000 senior deduction (2025–2028) — and the new non-itemizer charitable deduction. They are subtracted after AGI federally, and Kansas starts before them
- Federal standard and itemized deductions are also below AGI; Kansas uses its own

## Notes
- Taxable Social Security in federal AGI is subtracted on Schedule S (Kansas exempts all Social Security since 2024)
- The 2026 Form 1040 was not final as of October 2026; confirm the AGI line number on the final form
- If the federal return is amended or adjusted, the Kansas return must be amended too (see amended-return.md)

## Required Information
- 2026 federal AGI
- Federal return and schedules

## Questions
- What is the adjusted gross income on your 2026 federal return?
- Did you claim the new federal tips, overtime, car-loan or senior deductions?
- Has your federal return been amended or changed by the IRS?

## Common Errors
- Entering federal TAXABLE income instead of AGI
- Assuming the federal tips/overtime/senior deductions reduce Kansas income
- Entering only Kansas-source income on line 1 for a part-year resident

## Prompt
- Where does my Kansas return start?
- My federal deduction for tips — does Kansas give it too?
