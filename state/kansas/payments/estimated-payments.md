# Estimated Tax Paid
## Description
Total of the 2026 Kansas estimated tax payments, plus any 2025 overpayment the filer chose to credit forward to 2026.

## 2026 due dates (calendar year)
- April 15, 2026
- June 15, 2026
- September 15, 2026
- January 15, 2027

If a due date falls on a weekend or holiday, the next business day applies. A filer who files and pays in full by January 31, 2027 does not need the January 15 payment.

## Who must pay estimates
Both are true:
1. The Kansas balance due after withholding and credits is $500 or more, AND
2. Withholding and credits are less than 90% of the 2026 tax or 100% of the 2025 tax

Farmers and fishers (at least two-thirds of income from farming or fishing) are exempt if they file and pay by March 1, 2027.

## Notes
- Include the 2025 credit forward even though it was not a separate check
- Payments made in 2027 for a 2027 estimate do not belong here
- Underpayment penalty is figured on Schedule K-210 (see penalty-and-interest.md)

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each 2026 payment
- 2025 overpayment applied to 2026

## Questions
- Did you make Kansas estimated payments for 2026? When and how much?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the 2025 credit forward
- Including federal estimated payments
- Including the January 2027 payment for the wrong year

## Prompt
- I made quarterly Kansas estimated payments.
- I applied last year's refund to this year.
