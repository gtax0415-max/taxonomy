# Underpayment and Amount You Owe
## Description
If the tax balance (line 18) is more than total payments and refundable credits (line 27), the difference is the underpayment on line 28. The amount owed on line 32 adds interest, penalty, estimated tax penalty and any voluntary contributions.

## Amount
- Line 28 = line 18 − line 27
- Line 32 = lines 28 + 29 + 30 + 31 + any contributions on lines 35–42
- A balance due under $5 need not be paid

## Due date and payment
- Due April 15, 2027 for the 2026 return (next business day if a weekend or holiday)
- Payment options: direct payment from a bank account when e-filing (the filer chooses the debit date, up to the due date); credit card through an authorized vendor (convenience fee); check or money order with the Kansas payment voucher
- Direct payment can be revoked by calling KDOR by 4:00 PM two business days before the scheduled date
- Returned check fee: $30 plus registered-letter costs
- Unpaid balances after the due date accrue interest and penalty (see penalty-and-interest.md)

## Notes
- Contributions can be made even with a balance due; they are added to the payment

## Required Information
- Bank account details for direct payment
- Payment date chosen

## Questions
- How do you want to pay the balance: direct debit, card, or check?
- Do you want to schedule the payment for the due date?
- Would you like to add any voluntary contributions?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to add contributions to line 32
- Scheduling a debit after the due date
- Mailing a check without the payment voucher

## Prompt
- I owe Kansas tax. How do I pay?
- Can I file now and pay in April?
