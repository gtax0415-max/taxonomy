# Kansas Income Tax Withheld
## Description
Total Kansas income tax withheld in 2026 from wages, pensions, unemployment, gambling winnings and other payments. It is a refundable payment against the tax.

## Amount
- Add every Kansas withholding amount on W-2s and 1099s for the filer (and spouse on a joint return)
- Include only amounts withheld for KANSAS (state code KS); withholding for another state is not entered here

## Notes
- Kansas withholds 5% of gambling winnings (net of the wager) whenever federal withholding is required; it appears on Form W-2G
- KDOR does not require enclosing W-2s or 1099s, but may request them later
- If a W-2 is missing by January 31, 2027, or is wrong, contact the employer
- Tax withheld for another state may support the credit for taxes paid to other states — a separate line, figured from that state's actual tax, not its withholding
- Nonresident whose employer withheld Kansas tax in error: enclose a signed letter on company letterhead stating Kansas wages and withholding
- Withholding counts as paid evenly through the year for the underpayment penalty

## Required Information
- All W-2s and 1099s with Kansas withholding

## Questions
- Do you have W-2s or 1099s showing Kansas tax withheld?
- Was any tax withheld for another state?
- Did you have gambling winnings or unemployment with Kansas withholding?

## Common Errors
- Including another state's or local withholding
- Including federal withholding
- Missing 1099-R or 1099-G withholding

## Prompt
- How much Kansas tax did I have withheld?
- My W-2 shows Missouri and Kansas withholding.
