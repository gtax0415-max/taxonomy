# Overpayment and Refund
## Description
If total payments and refundable credits (line 27) exceed the tax balance (line 18), the difference is the overpayment on line 33. After any credit forward and voluntary contributions, the rest is refunded on line 43.

## Amount
- Line 33 = line 27 − line 18
- Line 43 = line 33 − line 34 − lines 35 to 42
- A refund under $5 is not paid; it may be carried forward to 2027 or donated

## Notes
- REFUND SET-OFF: Kansas applies a refund to debts owed to Kansas agencies, courts and municipalities, the IRS, or the Missouri Department of Revenue. Set-off delays any remaining refund by 10 to 12 weeks. KDOR can only explain Kansas tax debts; other debts go to the debtor set-off office
- Paper returns: allow about 16 weeks. E-filing with direct deposit is faster
- If an examination later reduces the overpayment, contributions are reduced by that amount

## Required Information
- Direct deposit account details
- Amount to credit forward and to donate

## Questions
- Would you like your refund by direct deposit?
- Do you want to apply any of it to 2027 estimated tax or donate to a Kansas fund?
- Do you owe any state agency, court, the IRS, or Missouri?

## Common Errors
- Wrong bank account or routing number
- Forgetting to subtract contributions and credit forward
- Expecting a refund under $5

## Prompt
- How much is my Kansas refund?
- Why was my Kansas refund smaller than expected?
