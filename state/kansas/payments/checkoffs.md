# Voluntary Contributions (Checkoffs)
## Description
Optional donations made on the Kansas return. They reduce the refund or increase the amount owed. They are NOT tax payments and are not refunded.

## Funds for 2026
- Line 35: Chickadee Checkoff (Kansas Nongame Wildlife Improvement Program)
- Line 36: Senior Citizens Meals on Wheels Contribution Program
- Line 37: Kansas Breast Cancer Research Fund
- Line 38: Military Emergency Relief Fund
- Line 39: Kansas Hometown Heroes Fund
- Line 40: Kansas Creative Arts Industry Fund
- Line 41: Local School District Contribution Fund — requires the three-digit school district number; the statute also lets the filer give the ENTIRE refund to the district
- Line 42: Kansas Historic Site Contribution Fund — requires a site number from 1 to 17

No checkoff was added or removed by the 2026 Legislature; the list follows the 2025 K-40. Confirm line numbers against the 2026 K-40 when posted.

## Amount
- $1 or more per fund
- Can be given even when tax is owed; the donation is added to the payment
- Any refund under $5 can be donated instead of carried forward

## Notes
- If an examination later reduces the overpayment, the donations are reduced by that amount; if it increases the overpayment, donations stay the same
- A donation is a charitable gift for the year it is PAID (the year the return is filed), not the tax year of the return

## Required Information
- Amount for each fund
- School district number (line 41)
- Historic site number (line 42)

## Questions
- Would you like to donate any of your refund to a Kansas fund?
- Which school district or historic site do you want to support?

## Common Errors
- Entering a school district or site number with no donation, or a donation with no number
- Treating a donation as an estimated payment
- Deducting the donation on the same year's return it was made on

## Prompt
- Can I donate part of my Kansas refund to the Chickadee Checkoff?
- I want to give to my local school district on my tax return.
