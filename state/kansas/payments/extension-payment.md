# Amount Paid with Kansas Extension
## Description
Money paid to Kansas by the original due date (April 15, 2027) when extending the time to file the 2026 return.

## How the Kansas extension works
- Kansas has NO separate extension form. Filing federal Form 4868 extends the Kansas filing deadline automatically; enclose a copy of the 4868 with the K-40
- An extension of time to FILE is not an extension of time to PAY
- Pay the estimated balance with the Kansas payment voucher marked as an extension payment, or electronically
- If at least 90% of the tax is paid by the original due date, no late-payment penalty applies; interest is still due on any balance
- If a refund is expected, no extension is needed

## Amount
- Total paid with the extension request, as shown on payment records

## Required Information
- Date and amount of the extension payment
- Federal Form 4868

## Questions
- Did you extend your 2026 federal return?
- Did you send a payment to Kansas with the extension?

## Common Errors
- Assuming an extension avoids interest on unpaid tax
- Not enclosing the federal 4868
- Entering the payment again on the estimated tax line

## Prompt
- I filed an extension and sent Kansas a payment.
- Do I need a separate Kansas extension form?
