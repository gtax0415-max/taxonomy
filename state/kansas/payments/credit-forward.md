# Credit Forward to 2027
## Description
The filer may apply all or part of the 2026 overpayment to 2027 Kansas estimated tax instead of receiving it as a refund.

## Amount
- Any amount of $1 or more, up to the overpayment on line 33
- A remaining refund under $5 can be carried forward even if the filer makes no estimated payments

## Notes
- It must be claimed on the 2027 return as an estimated payment (2027 line 20)
- If the 2026 overpayment is later reduced by examination, the credit forward may be reduced

## Required Information
- Amount to apply to 2027

## Questions
- Do you expect to owe Kansas tax for 2027 or need to make estimated payments?
- Would you like to apply part of your refund to 2027?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to claim the credit forward on the next year's return
- Crediting forward more than the overpayment

## Prompt
- Can I apply my Kansas refund to next year?
- I have self-employment income and make estimated payments.
