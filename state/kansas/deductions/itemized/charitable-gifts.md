# Gifts to Charity
## Description
Kansas itemized deduction for charitable contributions that qualify under IRC §170 — gifts to religious, charitable, educational, scientific or literary organizations, and to groups preventing cruelty to children or animals.

## Amount
- Line 10: gifts by cash or check (including out-of-pocket volunteer expenses)
- Line 11: gifts of property other than cash
- Line 12: carryover of gifts that exceeded AGI limits in the prior five years
- Line 13: total
- The federal AGI percentage limits (e.g., 60% for cash to public charities) apply

## 2026 federal changes — Kansas treatment to confirm
- OBBBA adds a FLOOR for 2026: itemized charitable gifts count only above 0.5% of the contribution base (AGI). Kansas allows gifts that "qualify as deductions in section 170"; whether Kansas Schedule A applies the floor is not settled until the 2026 Kansas instructions are published
- OBBBA's new non-itemizer deduction ($1,000 single / $2,000 joint) is taken below federal AGI and is NOT available in Kansas to filers who take the Kansas standard deduction

## What does NOT qualify
- Gifts to the extent a Kansas tax credit was received for them (e.g., community service contribution or scholarship credits — see income/additions/scholarship-contributions-addback.md and other-additions.md)
- Political contributions, dues to fraternal orders, the value of your time
- Payments for the right to buy college athletic tickets

## Required Information
- Each gift: charity name, date, amount
- Acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more
- Noncash gift descriptions and values
- Carryover from earlier years
- Any Kansas credits claimed for gifts

## Questions
- Did you give cash or property to qualified charities in 2026?
- Do you have written acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more?
- Did any gift earn a Kansas tax credit?
- Do you have a charitable carryover from a prior year?

## Common Errors
- Deducting gifts that produced a Kansas credit
- No bank record or receipt for cash gifts
- Missing acknowledgments for gifts of $250 or more
- Assuming the federal non-itemizer charitable deduction reduces Kansas income

## Prompt
- I donated to my church and other charities in 2026.
- Can I deduct my charitable gifts in Kansas if I don't itemize federally?
