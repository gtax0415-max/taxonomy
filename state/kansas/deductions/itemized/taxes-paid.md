# Real Estate and Personal Property Taxes
## Description
Kansas itemized deduction for state and local real estate taxes and personal property taxes (IRC §164). Kansas allows 100% of these taxes with NO dollar cap, even though the federal SALT deduction is capped.

## Amount
- Line 5: real estate taxes on property you own that was not used for business, if assessed uniformly and used for general public purposes
- Line 6: personal property taxes based on value alone and imposed yearly (in Kansas, mainly the value-based part of vehicle registration)
- Line 7: total; no cap

## 2026 SALT cap does not apply
- Federal cap for 2026: $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), phased down for higher incomes under OBBBA
- Kansas ignores the cap for the taxes it allows, so the full property tax goes on Kansas Schedule A

## What does NOT qualify
- State income tax and general sales tax (deductible federally, NOT in Kansas)
- Special assessments for local improvements (sidewalks, sewers)
- Taxes on business or rental property (deducted on the business schedule instead)
- The weight-based or flat part of vehicle registration fees

## Notes
- With escrow, deduct only what the lender actually paid to the county in 2026 (Form 1098, box 10)
- Kansas property taxes are billed in November and half may be paid by December 20 and half by May 10; deduct what was PAID in 2026

## Required Information
- Real estate tax paid in 2026 (by payment date)
- Vehicle registration receipts with the property tax portion

## Questions
- How much real estate tax did you or your lender pay in 2026?
- Do your vehicle registration receipts show a property (ad valorem) tax amount?

## Common Errors
- Including Kansas income tax withheld
- Applying the federal $40,400 cap
- Deducting the full vehicle registration fee instead of the value-based part
- Using the tax billed instead of the tax paid in 2026

## Prompt
- Can I deduct my property taxes in Kansas?
- My federal SALT deduction was capped. What about Kansas?
