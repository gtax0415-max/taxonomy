# Angel Investor Credit
## Description
Credit for an accredited investor's CASH investment in qualified securities of a Kansas business certified by the Department of Commerce (K.S.A. 74-8133). Extended through tax year 2031 by 2026 HB 2464 (KDOR Notice 26-03).

## Amount
- Up to 50% of the cash investment
- Caps: $100,000 of credit per business per investor; $350,000 per investor per year
- Statewide cap: $8 million for 2026
- From 2027, at least 25% of credits are set aside for businesses in counties of 50,000 or fewer people

## Notes
- Cash only; the business must be certified BEFORE the investment
- Unused credit carries forward until used; not refundable
- An investor with no tax liability can receive a transferable credit, which can be transferred once
- Buying the shares is not a federal deduction, so federal tax is unchanged
- The 2026 Schedule K-30 still shows the old sunset; the law change controls

## Required Information
- Commerce certification of the business
- Amount and date of the cash investment
- Credit allocation

## Questions
- Did you invest cash in a Kansas startup certified for the Angel Investor credit?
- How much, and when?
- Do you have unused Angel Investor credit from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a business not certified before the investment
- Claiming for a non-cash investment
- Exceeding the per-business or per-investor cap

## Prompt
- I invested in a certified Kansas startup this year.
- Is the Kansas angel investor credit still available for 2026?
