# Eisenhower Foundation Credit
## Description
Credit for contributions to the Eisenhower Foundation in Abilene (K.S.A. 79-32,274). Extended by 2026 HB 2464 to contributions made before January 1, 2031 (KDOR Notice 26-03).

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Maximum credit: $25,000 for a resident individual
- Statewide cap: $350,000 per fiscal year

## Notes
- No carryforward and no refund: credit above the tax is lost
- Whether non-cash gifts qualify is not stated on the schedule; confirm with the foundation

## Required Information
- Foundation receipt
- Amount and type of the contribution

## Questions
- Did you give to the Eisenhower Foundation in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $25,000
- Expecting to carry the unused part forward

## Prompt
- I donated to the Eisenhower Presidential Library foundation.
