# Kansas Pregnancy Resource Act Credit
## Description
Credit for contributions to a qualified pregnancy resource center (K.S.A. 79-32,316), in effect since 2024.

## Amount
- 70% of the contribution
- No per-taxpayer cap; $5 million of contributions per center and $10 million of credits statewide per year

## Notes
- Apply to KDOR FIRST: the credit is allocated within 30 days, and the gift must be made within 90 days of the allocation or it is cancelled
- Unused credit carries forward five years; not refundable
- The law says "contribution" without limiting it to cash; confirm with the center whether it accepts securities

## Required Information
- KDOR allocation letter
- Center's receipt and contribution details

## Questions
- Did you apply to KDOR for a pregnancy resource credit allocation before giving?
- Was the gift made within 90 days of the allocation?

## Common Errors
- Giving before getting an allocation
- Missing the 90-day window

## Prompt
- I want to give to a pregnancy resource center and get the Kansas credit.
