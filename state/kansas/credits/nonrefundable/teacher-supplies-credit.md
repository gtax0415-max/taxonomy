# Teacher's Purchases of School and Classroom Supplies
## Description
Kansas credit for teachers who buy school and classroom supplies with their own money (K.S.A. 79-32,296). Available for tax years 2022 and later; unchanged for 2026.

## Amount
- Supplies bought in 2026, up to $250 per teacher
- Married couple filing jointly where both are teachers: up to $500
- No carryforward; nonrefundable

## Eligibility
- Kansas resident
- Employed as a teacher at a public or private school

## Notes
- The federal educator expense deduction ($350 for 2026, up from $300; Rev. Proc. 2025-32) is above the line and already reduces federal AGI; whether the same receipts may support both should be confirmed with the 2026 Schedule K-91 instructions
- Unreimbursed purchases only

## Required Information
- Receipts and total cost of supplies
- Teaching position

## Questions
- Are you a public or private school teacher living in Kansas?
- How much did you spend on classroom supplies in 2026?
- Were any purchases reimbursed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than $250 per teacher
- Claiming reimbursed purchases
- Not claiming for both spouses when both teach

## Prompt
- I'm a teacher and bought supplies for my classroom.
- Is there a Kansas credit for teacher expenses?
