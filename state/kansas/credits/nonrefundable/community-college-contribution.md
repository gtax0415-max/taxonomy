# Community College and Technical College Contribution Credit
## Description
Credit for contributions to a Kansas community or technical college for capital improvements, deferred maintenance, or technology and equipment (K.S.A. 79-32,261).

## Amount
- 60% of the contribution
- Up to $250,000 of credit per taxpayer per year; $500,000 per college; $5 million statewide
- 2026 is the last tax year for this credit

## Notes
- Requires a tax credit certificate number from the college
- No carryforward and no refund: credit above the tax is lost
- The gift must be for the listed purposes

## Required Information
- Credit certificate number
- Amount and purpose of the gift

## Questions
- Did you give to a Kansas community or technical college in 2026?
- Did the college issue a tax credit certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without a certificate
- Expecting to carry the unused part forward
- Assuming the credit continues in 2027

## Prompt
- I donated to my local community college's equipment fund.
