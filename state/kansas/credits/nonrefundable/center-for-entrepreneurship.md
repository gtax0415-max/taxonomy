# Center for Entrepreneurship Credit (NetWork Kansas)
## Description
Credit for contributions to the Kansas Center for Entrepreneurship, known as NetWork Kansas, which funds small-business lending (K.S.A. 74-99c09).

## Amount
- 75% of the contribution
- Minimum contribution: $250
- Maximum credit: $100,000 per contributor per year; $2 million statewide per fiscal year

## Notes
- Cash or property qualifies, except used clothing
- NetWork Kansas must approve the application for credits before you claim
- Unused credit carries forward until used; not refundable

## Required Information
- NetWork Kansas approval
- Amount and type of the contribution

## Questions
- Did you give cash or property to NetWork Kansas in 2026?
- Did you receive a credit approval?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for used clothing
- Claiming without the approval

## Prompt
- I donated equipment to NetWork Kansas.
