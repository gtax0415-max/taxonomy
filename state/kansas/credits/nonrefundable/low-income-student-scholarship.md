# Low Income Student Scholarship Credit
## Description
Credit for contributions to an approved Kansas scholarship granting organization that funds private-school scholarships for low-income students (K.S.A. 72-4351 to 72-4357).

## Amount
- 75% of the contribution (tax years after 2022)
- Contributions capped at $500,000 per contributor per year
- Statewide cap: $10 million per year

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward until used; not refundable
- Any part of the contribution deducted on the federal return is ADDED BACK on Schedule S, line A4 (see income/additions/scholarship-contributions-addback.md)
- Do not also deduct it on Kansas Schedule A
- The new federal scholarship credit (IRC §25F) starts in 2027 and is a separate federal item

## Required Information
- Organization name and receipt
- Amount and date given
- Amount deducted federally, if any

## Questions
- Did you give to a Kansas scholarship granting organization in 2026?
- Did you deduct the gift on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Missing the add-back for a federally deducted gift
- Also deducting the gift on Kansas Schedule A
- Treating the 2027 federal scholarship credit as available for 2026

## Prompt
- I gave to a Kansas scholarship organization for low-income students.
- Do I get a Kansas credit for my school scholarship donation?
