# Credit for Taxes Paid to Other States
## Description
A Kansas resident taxed by another state (or a local jurisdiction there) on income that is also in Kansas AGI gets a credit so the same income is not fully taxed twice. Nonrefundable.

## Amount — Worksheet for Residents
1. 2026 income tax actually paid to the other state (including its political subdivisions)
2. Total Kansas income tax (K-40, line 12)
3. Income from the other state included in Kansas AGI
4. Kansas AGI (K-40, line 3)
5. Percentage = line 3 ÷ line 4
6. Maximum credit = line 2 × line 5
7. Credit = the LESSER of line 1 or line 6

More than one state: a worksheet for each, totals combined on line 13.

## Eligibility
- Kansas AGI includes income earned in the other state
- The filer was required to pay income tax to that state on that income
- Tax paid by an S corporation or partnership to another state on income included in the resident owner's Kansas AGI counts as paid by the owner (including other states' pass-through entity taxes)

## Notes
- The tax PAID is the other state's actual liability, NOT the amount withheld
- No credit for income from a state with no income tax
- ENCLOSE a copy of the other state's return and supporting schedules; other-state W-2s alone are not accepted
- Part-year residents filing as nonresidents use a separate worksheet (out of scope)
- Foreign taxes are limited first by the foreign tax worksheet (see foreign-tax-credit.md)

## Required Information
- Other state's return and its tax liability
- Income earned in that state

## Questions
- Did you work or have income in another state in 2026 (for example, Missouri)?
- Did you file that state's return, and what was its tax after credits?
- Do you own a pass-through business that paid tax to another state?

## Common Errors
- Using the other state's withholding instead of its tax liability
- Not enclosing the other state's return
- Claiming credit for a state with no income tax
- Including Kansas City, Missouri earnings tax without a local return or statement

## Prompt
- I live in Kansas and work in Missouri.
- I paid income tax to another state.
