# Gun and Ammunition Storage Credit
## Description
NEW for 2026. Kansas resident individuals may claim a credit for buying lockable storage designed primarily for guns and ammunition. Created by 2026 SB 82 (KDOR Notice 26-06); available for tax years 2026, 2027 and 2028.

## Amount
- 25% of the purchase cost
- Maximum $250 per taxpayer per year (spending $1,000 or more reaches the cap)
- Nonrefundable; unused credit carries forward to later years

## Eligibility
- Kansas resident individual filing a Kansas income tax return
- Lockable storage designed primarily for guns and ammunition (gun safes, lockable cases, ammunition safes)
- Purchased in the tax year claimed

## Notes
- This is an income tax credit, NOT a sales tax exemption; sales tax is still paid at purchase
- KDOR has not yet published the claim schedule or line (as of October 2026); it is expected with the 2026 forms
- The notice does not say whether the $250 limit doubles on a joint return; confirm with the 2026 instructions

## Required Information
- Receipt with date, item and price

## Questions
- Did you buy a gun safe or lockable gun or ammunition storage in 2026?
- What did it cost?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a non-lockable or general-purpose storage item
- Claiming more than $250
- Claiming a purchase from before 2026

## Prompt
- I bought a gun safe this year.
- Is there a Kansas tax credit for gun safes?
