# Other Credit Schedules
## Description
Line 15 totals every other Kansas credit, each figured on its own schedule and enclosed with the return. Most are business, investment or contribution credits; individuals usually reach them through a pass-through entity or a donation.

## Credits covered only by this file
- Pass-through and business credits: apprenticeship (K-24), affordable housing (K-25), aviation (K-26), housing investor (K-27), research and development (K-53), HPIP (K-59), rural opportunity zone (K-89), workforce retention (K-93), and others
- Carryforward-only schedules: K-34, K-55, K-82, K-83

## 2026 law changes
- EXTENDED by 2026 HB 2464 (Notice 26-03): Angel Investor through tax year 2031 (rural set-aside begins 2027); Eisenhower Foundation and Friends of Cedar Crest for contributions before January 1, 2031 ($350,000 annual cap each); Aviation/Aerospace credits sunset moved from December 31, 2026 to December 31, 2031
- REPEALED by 2026 SB 82 (Notice 26-09, effective July 1, 2026): Individual Development Account credit (K-68; never claimed), Owners PEAK credit (K-88; never claimed), agritourism and swine facility credits (corporate only), certain major disasters credit (expired)
- CHANGED by 2026 SB 82 (Notice 26-05): the employer child care credit (K-56) is amended for tax years beginning January 1, 2027 — no change for 2026 returns
- FUTURE: Kansas opts into the federal scholarship granting organization credit (§25F) for tax years after 2026 (Notice 26-10); that is a FEDERAL credit, not a line 15 item

## Notes
- A contribution that earns a Kansas credit generally cannot also be deducted on Kansas Schedule A for the same amount; several credits require an addition modification
- Carryforward rules, caps and refundability differ by credit; follow each schedule
- Credits from carryforward-only schedules (K-34, K-55, K-82, K-83) may still be used up to their carryforward limits

## Required Information
- Credit certificates or approvals
- K-1 credit shares
- Prior-year carryforwards

## Questions
- Did you donate to a program that gives a Kansas tax credit?
- Do you receive Kansas credits from a partnership or S corporation?
- Do you have unused Kansas credits carried forward from earlier years?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the repealed K-68 or K-88 credits
- Missing the required addition modification for a contribution credit
- Not enclosing the credit schedule or approval

## Prompt
- I donated to a Kansas community college foundation.
- My K-1 shows a Kansas tax credit.
