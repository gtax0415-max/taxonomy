# Adoption Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable Kansas credit for Kansas residents equal to 100% of the federal adoption credit (IRC §23) claimed for the year, determined without regard to §23(c) (the federal carryforward rule), plus an extra $1,500 for adopting a special-needs child or a child in the custody of the Kansas Secretary for Children and Families. Current form since tax year 2024 (2024 HB 2465, K.S.A. 79-32,202a; KDOR Notice 24-20).

## Amount
- 100% of the 2026 federal adoption credit claimed (the federal maximum for 2026 is $17,670 per child; up to $5,120 of it is refundable federally)
- PLUS $1,500 for a special-needs child or a child in DCF custody
- Limited to the Kansas tax remaining after other credits

## Points to confirm for 2026
- Whether "the credit claimed" includes the federal REFUNDABLE portion added by OBBBA in 2025 — the Kansas statute predates the refundable portion; check the 2026 Schedule K-47 instructions
- Carryforward of unused credit: confirm on the 2026 Schedule K-47

## Eligibility
- Kansas resident
- Claimed the federal adoption credit for 2026 (same federal timing rules: domestic pre-finalization expenses are claimed the year after paid; foreign adoptions only when final)

## Required Information
- Federal Form 8839
- Special-needs or DCF custody documentation
- Kansas tax after other credits

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal adoption credit for 2026?
- Is the child special needs, or was the child in Kansas DCF custody?
- Were you a Kansas resident?

## Common Errors
- Using the old 25% Kansas rate (tax years before 2024)
- Missing the $1,500 add-on for special needs or DCF custody
- Claiming before the federal timing rules allow

## Prompt
- We adopted a child from Kansas foster care.
- Does Kansas have an adoption credit?
