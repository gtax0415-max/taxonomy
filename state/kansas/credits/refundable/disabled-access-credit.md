# Disabled Access Credit (Individual)
## Description
Credit for an individual's spending to make an existing principal dwelling accessible to people with a disability (K.S.A. 79-32,176a). The dwelling can be the taxpayer's own, or that of a parent, grandparent, child or grandchild. The credit percentage phases down with FEDERAL AGI.

## Amount
Credit = spending × the percentage for federal AGI:
- Single and other filers: 100% up to $40,000 of federal AGI, then 10 points lower for each $10,000, reaching 0% at $130,000
- Married filing jointly: 100% up to $60,000, then 10 points lower for each $10,000, reaching 0% at $150,000

Examples of the bands:
- Joint, federal AGI $60,001–$70,000: 90%
- Single, federal AGI $100,001–$110,000: 30%

Maximum credit: $15,000 base, indexed for inflation ($17,924 on the 2025 schedule; the 2026 figure is not yet published).

## Eligibility
- Work on an EXISTING dwelling (new construction does not qualify)
- Work meets ADA standards
- Once per dwelling every four years
- A small barrier-free unit attached to the dwelling also qualifies

## Notes
- Unused credit carries forward four years
- If the taxpayer's Kansas tax is below an indexed threshold ($4,481 on the 2025 schedule), the credit is refunded in installments: 1/4 the first year, then 1/3, then 1/2, then the rest
- The 50% business version of this credit (K.S.A. 79-32,176) is a different credit
- Home accessibility work gives a non-itemizer no federal benefit

## Required Information
- Invoices for the work and its location
- Whose principal dwelling it is
- Federal AGI and filing status

## Questions
- Did you make your home, or a parent's or child's home, accessible in 2026?
- What was the total cost, and was it an existing home?
- What is your federal AGI?
- Have you claimed this credit for the same dwelling in the last four years?

## Common Errors
- Using 100% without applying the AGI phase-down
- Claiming for new construction
- Using Kansas AGI instead of federal AGI
- Confusing it with the 50% business credit

## Prompt
- We installed a ramp and widened doorways for my mother who lives with us.
- I remodeled my bathroom with grab bars and a walk-in shower.
