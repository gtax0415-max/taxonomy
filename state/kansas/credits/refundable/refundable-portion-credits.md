# Refundable Portion of Tax Credits
## Description
Some Kansas credits (other than the EITC) allow the amount that exceeds tax to be refunded instead of carried forward. That refundable portion goes on line 23, separate from the nonrefundable amount on line 15.

## Amount
- The refundable portion figured on each credit schedule; enclose the schedule
- Not computed in the template beyond the entry: take the figure from the schedule

## Notes
- Most Kansas credits are NONREFUNDABLE (excess carries forward or is lost). Only credits whose schedule provides a refundable portion belong here — for individuals mainly the Community Service Contribution credit (community-service-contribution.md) and the low-tax installments of the individual Disabled Access credit (disabled-access-credit.md)
- The Kansas EITC refundable portion goes on line 22, not line 23
- The tax paid by an electing pass-through entity (K-9) goes on line 25, not line 23
- None of the 2026-new or 2026-amended credits for individuals (gun storage) is refundable

## Required Information
- Each credit schedule showing a refundable portion

## Questions
- Do any of your Kansas credit schedules show a refundable amount?
- Did a partnership or S corporation pass a refundable Kansas credit to you?

## Common Errors
- Putting the nonrefundable carryforward amount here
- Putting the Kansas EITC refund here instead of line 22
- Not enclosing the schedule

## Prompt
- My Kansas credit schedule shows a refundable amount.
- Where do I put a refundable Kansas credit?
