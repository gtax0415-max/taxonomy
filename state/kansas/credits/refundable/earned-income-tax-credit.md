# Earned Income Tax Credit
## Description
Kansas EITC equals 17% of the federal earned income tax credit (K.S.A. 79-32,205). It first reduces Kansas tax, and any excess is REFUNDED. Residents only.

## Amount — EITC worksheet
1. Federal EITC
2. Kansas EITC = line 1 × 17%
3. K-40, line 16 (tax after other credits)
4. Line 2 − line 3
- If line 4 is positive: enter line 3 on K-40 line 17 and line 4 on line 22 (refundable)
- If line 4 is negative or zero: enter line 2 on line 17 and 0 on line 22

## Example
Federal EITC $4,000 → Kansas EITC $680. Kansas tax after other credits $250. Line 17 = $250; line 22 = $430 refunded.

## 2026 federal context
- The 2026 maximum federal EITC is $8,231 (three or more children; Rev. Proc. 2025-32), so the largest Kansas EITC is about $1,399 (17%)
- Kansas rate 17% unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- Full-year Kansas resident (not part-year or nonresident)
- Claimed the federal EITC for 2026
- Valid SSN for the filer, spouse and qualifying children

## Notes
- If the IRS is computing the federal EITC and the result is not back by the Kansas deadline, file without the Kansas credit, pay any tax due, then amend once the IRS figures arrive

## Required Information
- Federal EITC amount
- Residency status

## Questions
- Did you claim the federal earned income credit for 2026?
- Were you a Kansas resident all year?

## Common Errors
- Putting the whole Kansas EITC on line 17 so the refundable part is lost
- Using a percentage other than 17%
- Claiming it as a part-year resident or nonresident

## Prompt
- I get the federal earned income credit. Does Kansas have one?
- How much is the Kansas EITC?
