# Head of Household
## Description
Kansas head-of-household status follows the federal status. Since 2024, a head of household also gets an ADDITIONAL $2,320 exemption (2025 HB 2231, KDOR Notice 25-07), on top of the regular $9,160.

## 2026 amounts
- Exemption: $9,160 + $2,320 = $11,480 (plus $2,320 per dependent)
- Standard deduction: $6,180 (65-or-older or blind: $7,030 for one box, $7,880 for two)
- Brackets: 5.20% up to $23,000, 5.58% above (same as single)

## Eligibility
- Filed as head of household on the 2026 federal return (unmarried or considered unmarried, paid more than half the cost of a home for a qualifying person)
- A federal qualifying surviving spouse also marks head of household on the K-40 (see qualifying-surviving-spouse.md)

## Required Information
- 2026 federal filing status
- Qualifying person's information

## Questions
- Did you file as head of household federally?
- Who is your qualifying person, and did they live with you more than half the year?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the additional $2,320 exemption
- Using the joint bracket thresholds
- Claiming head of household in Kansas without the federal status

## Prompt
- I'm a single parent filing head of household. What is my Kansas exemption?
- What is the Kansas standard deduction for head of household?
