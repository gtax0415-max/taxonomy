# Stillborn Child
## Description
A $2,320 Kansas exemption for a parent of a child who was stillborn. Created by 2025 HB 2062 (K.S.A. 79-32,121(a)(4); KDOR Notice 25-07). There is no federal counterpart; the child is not a federal dependent, so this is a Kansas-only entry.

## Amount
- $2,320 per stillbirth; unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- The pregnancy did not result in a live birth and is a stillbirth as defined in K.S.A. 65-2401
- A certificate of stillbirth was filed as required by law
- The exemption is for the tax year the certificate was issued

## Notes
- If the child was born alive, use child-born-in-tax-year.md instead, even if the child died later in the year
- Handle this topic with care when gathering information

## Required Information
- Certificate of stillbirth and its issue date

## Questions
- Was a certificate of stillbirth filed and issued in 2026?
- Are you filing jointly with the other parent, or separately?

## Common Errors
- Missing the exemption because no federal dependent was claimed
- Claiming it in a year other than the year the certificate was issued
- Claiming it without a filed certificate of stillbirth

## Prompt
- Our baby was stillborn in 2026 and we received a certificate of stillbirth.
- We lost a baby to stillbirth this year. Is there a Kansas tax provision?
