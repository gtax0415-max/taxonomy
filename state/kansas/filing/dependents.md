# Dependents
## Description
A $2,320 Kansas exemption for each dependent the filer is entitled to claim on the 2026 federal return. Kansas does not have its own dependency test.

## Amount
- $2,320 per dependent, not indexed; unchanged for 2026

## Reporting
- Number of dependents in the first box, × $2,320 in the amount box
- For each dependent: name, date of birth (MMDDYYYY), relationship, and SSN. The form has four rows; enclose a separate schedule for more
- Do not list the filer or spouse

## Notes
- A dependent must have a valid SSN for the filer to claim Kansas income tax credits for that dependent
- A filer who is claimed as someone else's dependent enters 0 in the dependents box (see dependent-filer.md)
- A dependent born in 2026 also qualifies for the additional child-born exemption (see child-born-in-tax-year.md)

## Required Information
- Each dependent's name, date of birth, relationship and SSN
- Confirmation they are claimed on the federal return

## Questions
- Who did you claim as a dependent on your 2026 federal return?
- Does each dependent have a valid SSN?
- Was any dependent born in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a dependent in Kansas who was not claimed federally
- Wrong or missing SSN or date of birth
- Listing the spouse as a dependent

## Prompt
- How much do I get for each dependent in Kansas?
- I have five kids. Where do I list them on the K-40?
