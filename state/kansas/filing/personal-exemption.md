# Personal Exemption
## Description
Kansas still allows a personal exemption (the federal one is zero). Amounts were raised by 2024 Special Session SB 1 and are NOT indexed, so 2026 matches 2024 and 2025.

## 2026 amounts
- Married filing joint: $18,320
- Single, head of household, married filing separate: $9,160
- Head of household: additional $2,320
- Each dependent: $2,320
- Additional exemptions ($2,320 each): child born in 2026, stillbirth, 100% disabled veteran

## Example
Married couple filing jointly with two children, one born in 2026: $18,320 + (2 × $2,320) + $2,320 = $25,280 on line 5.

## Notes
- Line 5 (exemptions) plus line 4 (standard or itemized deduction) = line 6, subtracted from Kansas AGI
- The exemption is not phased out at higher incomes

## Required Information
- Filing status
- Number of dependents and any additional exemptions

## Questions
- What is your filing status?
- How many dependents did you claim federally?
- Was a child born, or a stillbirth certified, in 2026?
- Is anyone on the return a 100% disabled veteran?

## Common Errors
- Using the pre-2024 amount ($2,250 per person)
- Using zero because the federal exemption is zero
- Missing the head-of-household additional $2,320

## Prompt
- How much is the Kansas personal exemption for 2026?
- We are married with three kids. What is our Kansas exemption?
