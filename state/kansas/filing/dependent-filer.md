# Filer Who Can Be Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
A student or other person claimed as a dependent on someone else's return still files their own K-40 when required or to recover Kansas withholding. They enter 0 in the number-of-dependents box.

## 2026 treatment
- Dependents box: enter 0
- Personal exemption: the 2025 instructions print no reduction, so the filer enters the normal $9,160 (single)
- Standard deduction: the 2025 instructions print no limit for dependents, so $3,605 (single)
- The 2026 FEDERAL dependent standard deduction limit (greater of $1,350 or earned income + $450; Rev. Proc. 2025-32) does NOT appear in the Kansas instructions
- Confirm both points against the 2026 KDOR instructions when posted; Kansas formerly limited a dependent filer's standard deduction

## Why file
- A return is required to get a refund of Kansas tax withheld, whatever the income
- Filing is required if a federal return is required, or if Kansas AGI exceeds the standard deduction plus exemption ($12,765 single)

## Required Information
- Whether another taxpayer claims the filer
- W-2s and 1099s with Kansas withholding

## Questions
- Can a parent or someone else claim you as a dependent for 2026?
- Did you have Kansas tax withheld from your pay?

## Common Errors
- Entering 1 in the dependents box for yourself
- Not filing and losing a refund of Kansas withholding
- Applying the federal dependent standard deduction limit to Kansas without confirmation

## Prompt
- I'm a college student and my parents claim me. Do I file in Kansas?
- I had a summer job with Kansas withholding.
