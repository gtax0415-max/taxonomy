# Amended Return
## Description
Kansas has no separate amended form. The filer completes a new Form K-40 for 2026, marks the amended box with the reason, and fills in lines 24 and 26 to account for what happened with the original return.

## When an amended return is required
- An error was made on the Kansas return
- Another state's return changed and affects the Kansas credit or income
- The federal return changed (Form 1040-X or an IRS adjustment)

## Reason boxes (mark ONE)
- Amended affects Kansas only
- Amended federal tax return
- Adjustment by the IRS

## Lines
- Line 24: money remitted with the original 2026 return, including any pending scheduled debit
- Line 26: overpayment shown on the original return (refunded or credited forward); entered as a positive number that is subtracted

## Deadlines
- Refund claims: within 3 years of when the original return was filed (including extensions) or 2 years from when the tax was paid, whichever is later
- Federal changes: file within 180 days after the federal adjustment is paid, agreed to, or becomes final, whichever is earlier. If not, Kansas's assessment period stays open
- Filing status cannot be changed from joint to separate after the original due date

## Notes
- If a Schedule S was filed originally, it must be filed again with the amendment even if unchanged
- Enclose a complete copy of Form 1040-X and an explanation when amending because of a federal change; enclose the IRS letter if the 1040-X is adjusted or denied
- Paying the full tax and interest with the amended return avoids the late-payment penalty

## Required Information
- Reason for amending
- Original payments and original refund or credit forward
- Federal 1040-X or IRS notice, if applicable

## Questions
- Why are you amending: a Kansas-only error, a federal amendment, or an IRS adjustment?
- What did you pay with, or receive from, the original return?
- When did the federal change become final?

## Common Errors
- Leaving lines 24 and 26 blank, double-counting payments or refunds
- Missing the 180-day window after a federal change
- Trying to switch from joint to separate after the due date
- Omitting Schedule S because it did not change

## Prompt
- I filed an amended federal return. Do I need to amend Kansas?
- I forgot a W-2 on my Kansas return.
