# Child Born in the Tax Year
## Description
An ADDITIONAL $2,320 exemption for each child born in 2026 whom the filer may claim as a dependent. It is on top of the regular $2,320 dependent exemption, so a newborn is worth $4,640 of exemptions. Created by 2025 HB 2062, which recognizes an unborn child as a dependent (K.S.A. 79-32,121(a)(4); KDOR Notice 25-07).

## Amount
- $2,320 per qualifying child born during 2026; unchanged for 2026

## Eligibility
- The child was born alive during 2026
- The filer may claim the child as a dependent on the 2026 federal return
- For a pregnancy that ended in stillbirth, see stillbirth.md instead

## Example
A single parent with one child born in March 2026: $9,160 + $2,320 (head of household) + $2,320 (dependent) + $2,320 (born in 2026) = $16,120 if filing head of household.

## Required Information
- Child's date of birth and SSN
- Confirmation the child is claimed as a dependent

## Questions
- Was a child born to you in 2026?
- Are you claiming the child as a dependent on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Claiming only the regular dependent exemption and missing the additional one
- Claiming the extra exemption for a child born in 2025
- Claiming it when the other parent claims the child federally

## Prompt
- We had a baby this year. Is there anything extra in Kansas?
- Do I get a Kansas exemption for my newborn?
