# Excess Deductions Against Trade or Business Income (Schedule C-2)
## Description
Normally, Massachusetts 5.0% (Part B) deductions cannot reduce interest, dividends or capital gains. Schedule C-2 is the exception for business owners: if Part B deductions exceed Part B income and the taxpayer files Schedule C or Schedule E, the excess may offset other income that is effectively connected with the active conduct of a trade or business, or other income allowed under IRC 469(d)(1)(B) to offset passive losses.
## Requirements
- The excess deductions must be AGI deductions allowed under MGL ch 62, § 2(d)
- They may offset only business-connected interest, dividends and capital gains (or 469(d)(1)(B) income)
- Applied on Schedule B, Line 8; Schedule B, Line 14; and Schedule D, Line 18
## Notes
- Massachusetts has no NOL carryover; Schedule C-2 is the only way to use a current-year excess against other classes
## Questions
- Do your business losses exceed your wages and other Part B income?
- Do you have interest, dividends or gains connected with the business itself?
## Common Errors
- Using C-2 against personal investment income
## Prompt
- My business lost money and I have capital gains.
