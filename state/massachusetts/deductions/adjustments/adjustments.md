# Massachusetts Deductions on the Face of Form 1
## Description
Two deductions sit directly on Form 1 rather than on Schedule Y. Both reduce only 5.0% Part B income.
## Files in this folder
- Retirement Contributions Deduction (retirement-contributions-deduction.md) — Form 1, Lines 11a and 11b. Up to $2,000 per person
- Rental Deduction (rental-deduction.md) — Form 1, Line 14. 50% of rent, up to $4,000
## Notes
- Form 1, Lines 12 and 13 are "Reserved for future use" — the former deductions for dependents under 12 and dependent care were folded into the Child and Family Tax Credit in 2023

- Did you pay Social Security, Medicare, self-employment tax, or into a government retirement system?
- Do you rent your Massachusetts home?
