# Adoption Agency Fee Exemption
## Description
Massachusetts exemption for the full amount of fees paid during the year to a LICENSED adoption agency in connection with the adoption of a minor child, whether or not the adoption was completed that year. The federal adoption credit has no Massachusetts counterpart; this exemption is the state benefit.
## Notes
- Legal fees, travel and court costs paid directly (not to a licensed agency) do not qualify

- Did you pay fees to a licensed adoption agency in 2026?
## Common Errors
- Including non-agency costs
