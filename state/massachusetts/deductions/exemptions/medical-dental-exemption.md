# Medical and Dental Expense Exemption
## Description
Massachusetts treats medical expenses as an EXEMPTION equal to the amount on federal Schedule A, Line 4 (expenses above the federal AGI floor). Unlike the Massachusetts charitable deduction, it is available ONLY if the taxpayer actually itemized on the federal return.
## Notes
- Because most taxpayers take the federal standard deduction, many with large medical bills get no Massachusetts benefit. Compare the overall federal-plus-state result before choosing to take the federal standard deduction
- Federal: federal/deductions/itemized/medical-expenses.md
## Questions
- Did you itemize deductions on your 2026 federal return?
- What is on Schedule A, Line 4?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it without itemizing federally
- Using total medical expenses instead of the amount above the federal floor
