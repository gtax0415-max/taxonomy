# Schedule DI — Dependent Information
## Description
Lists every dependent (name, SSN or ITIN, date of birth, relationship) and flags which ones qualify for the Earned Income Credit and the Child and Family Tax Credit. Required whenever a dependent exemption, EIC qualifying child, CFTC individual, or head of household qualifying person is claimed. Missing Schedule DI delays processing.
## Notes
- Only one taxpayer (or joint couple) may claim a dependent
- Dependents must be claimed on the U.S. return to get the $1,000 exemption
