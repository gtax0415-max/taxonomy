# Penalty on Early Savings Withdrawal
## Description
Deduction for interest forfeited on early withdrawal of savings, same as federal, provided the related interest was reported on a Massachusetts return.
## Notes
- Report Massachusetts bank interest GROSS on Form 1, Line 5 and deduct the penalty here
## Common Errors
- Netting the penalty against interest
## Prompt
- I broke a CD early and paid a penalty.
