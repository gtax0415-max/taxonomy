# Charitable Contribution Deduction
## Description
Massachusetts deduction equal to the federal charitable deduction, minus household goods and used clothing, available whether or not the taxpayer itemizes federally. Reinstated in 2023.
## 2026: Massachusetts does NOT follow P.L. 119-21
TIR 26-4 confirms Massachusetts does not adopt:
- The 0.5%-of-AGI floor on individual charitable deductions → compute the Massachusetts amount WITHOUT the floor
- The new federal non-itemizer deduction ($1,000 single / $2,000 joint) → not relevant; Massachusetts already allows all filers its full deduction
So the 2026 Massachusetts figure will often be LARGER than the federal Schedule A, Line 14 amount.
## Limits
- Reduces only Part B income (wages, Massachusetts bank interest, business income, etc.) — not dividends, other interest, or capital gains
- Federal percentage-of-AGI limits as they applied before P.L. 119-21
## Required Information
- Receipts and written acknowledgments, as required federally
- Form 8283 for non-cash gifts over $500
- Breakdown of household goods and used clothing
## Questions
- What did you give to charity in 2026, in cash and in property?
- Did any non-cash gifts consist of household goods or used clothing?
- Did you itemize on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Skipping the deduction because you did not itemize federally
- Using the federal amount after the 0.5% floor
- Including donated clothing and household goods
