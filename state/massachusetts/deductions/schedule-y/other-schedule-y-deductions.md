# Other Schedule Y Deductions
## Line 4a — Firefighter or police officer incapacitated in the line of duty
Amounts excludable under MGL ch 41, § 111F that were included in Form 1, Line 3.
## Line 4b — Income exempt under a U.S. tax treaty
Treaty-exempt wages (e.g., qualifying students) included in Form 1, Line 3; fill in the oval.
## Line 5 — Moving expenses for members of the Armed Forces
Federal Schedule 1, Line 14 amount; instructions cover tax years 2018 through 2026.
## Line 9a — Certain federal Schedule 1 deductions
Only these, and only to the extent deductible in Massachusetts: educator expenses (Line 11); jury duty pay given to employer (24a); expenses of renting personal property for profit, only to the extent the income is on Schedule X, Line 4 (24b); Olympic and Paralympic medals and USOC prize money (24c); reforestation (24d); Trade Act supplemental unemployment repayments (24e); chaplains' 403(b) contributions (24g); attorney fees in unlawful discrimination cases (24h) and IRS whistleblower awards (24i); other adjustments (24z) if deductible in Massachusetts. Also Medicaid waiver payments included in Line 3. NOT Schedule 1 Lines 12-23 items or 501(c)(18) plan contributions.
## Line 9b — Certain business expenses
National Guard and Reserve travel more than 100 miles from home, performing artists, fee-basis state or local officials (U.S. Schedule 1, Line 12), and impairment-related work expenses (U.S. Schedule A, Line 16). Not allowed to a taxpayer who files a joint federal return unless a Massachusetts joint return is also filed.
## Line 13 — Contributory pension from another state
Contributory pension income from another state or its subdivisions that does not tax Massachusetts government pensions (TIR 95-9), to the extent included in Form 1, Line 4.
## Line 14 — Claim of right
Income taxed by Massachusetts in a prior year that the taxpayer later repaid because they were not entitled to it (e.g., repaid wages, dividends, commissions, unemployment). Deductible in the year of repayment if not otherwise deductible. See TIR 06-4.
## Line 16 — Human organ donation
Travel, lodging and lost wages (lost wages up to $10,000) of a living donor of bone marrow, liver, pancreas, kidney, intestine or lung. Full-year residents only. See TIR 11-6.
## Questions
- Are you a teacher, reservist, performing artist, or disabled employee with work-related expenses?
- Do you receive a government pension from another state?
- Did you repay income you reported in a prior year?
- Did you donate an organ?
## Common Errors
- Pulling every federal Schedule 1 adjustment into Line 9a
- Claiming organ donation expenses as a part-year resident
## Prompt
- I'm a teacher. Can I deduct classroom expenses in Massachusetts?
- I donated a kidney.
