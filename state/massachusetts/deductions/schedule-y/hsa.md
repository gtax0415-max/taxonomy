# Health Savings Account and Medical Savings Account Deductions
## Description
Massachusetts follows the federal deduction for HSA contributions (Schedule Y, Line 8) and Archer MSA contributions (Schedule Y, Line 6), in the same amounts as federal Schedule 1.
## Notes
- The HSA-eligible high-deductible plan must also meet Massachusetts Minimum Creditable Coverage to avoid the Schedule HC penalty — see other-taxes/health-care-penalty.md
- Federal: federal/deductions/adjustments/hsa.md
