# Self-Employed Health Insurance Deduction
## Description
Same as the federal deduction for health insurance premiums paid by self-employed individuals (U.S. Schedule 1, Line 17).
## Notes
- Interacts with the federal premium tax credit (iterative calculation); Massachusetts takes the final federal number
- Federal: federal/deductions/adjustments/self-employed-health-insurance.md
## Questions
- Are you self-employed and paying your own health insurance?
## Common Errors
- Adding back the federal premium tax credit
## Prompt
- I'm self-employed and pay my own health insurance.
