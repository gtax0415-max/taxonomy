# Prepaid Tuition or College Savings Plan (529) Deduction
## Description
Massachusetts deduction for purchases of, or contributions to, a prepaid tuition program or college savings program established by the Commonwealth (the U.Fund and the U.Plan). Limit $1,000 per return ($2,000 joint); not indexed.
## Recapture
The deduction is recaptured in the year of any distribution or refund for a reason OTHER than:
- Qualified higher education expenses as defined by IRC 529(e)(3)
- The beneficiary's death, disability, or receipt of a scholarship
## 2026 note
TIR 26-4: Massachusetts ADOPTS the P.L. 119-21 expansions of qualified 529 expenses (Sections 70413 and 70414 — additional expenses and postsecondary credentialing) and the extended 529-to-ABLE rollovers. Confirm with the 2026 Schedule Y instructions how these interact with the recapture rule.
## Questions
- Did you contribute to the Massachusetts U.Fund or U.Plan in 2026?
- Did you take any distributions not used for college?
## Common Errors
- Deducting contributions to another state's plan
- Exceeding $1,000 / $2,000
- Missing recapture on a nonqualified withdrawal
