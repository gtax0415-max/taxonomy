# Commuter Deduction
## Description
Massachusetts deduction for commuting costs paid out of pocket.
## Qualifying costs
- Tolls paid through an E-ZPass account
- ALL MBTA fares (not just weekly or monthly passes)
- Regional Transit Authority (RTA) fares
- Bikeshare memberships
- Bicycle purchases (including e-bikes), improvements, repair and storage
- Fares on non-MBTA commuter boats owned, operated or contracted by a public entity
Exclude amounts reimbursed or otherwise deductible (including tax-free employer transit benefits).
## Calculation (per individual)
1. E-ZPass tolls
2. Other commuting costs
3. Total; if $150 or less, no deduction
4. Subtract $150
5. Deduction = lesser of the result or $750
## Notes
- 2026: employer-provided transit and parking exclusions are $335/month in Massachusetts and qualified bicycle reimbursements up to $20/month are excluded (TIR 25-9); those excluded amounts cannot also be deducted here
## Questions
- Did you pay tolls with E-ZPass, ride the T or a regional bus, or buy a bike for commuting?
- Did your employer reimburse any of it?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the first $150
- Combining spouses to exceed $750 for one person
- Including employer-reimbursed passes
## Prompt
- I pay for an MBTA monthly pass.
- I bought an e-bike to get to work.
