# College Tuition Deduction
## Description
Massachusetts deduction for undergraduate tuition paid for the taxpayer or a dependent, to the extent net tuition exceeds 25% of Massachusetts AGI. Full-year residents only.
## Calculation (Schedule Y, Line 11 Worksheet)
1. Tuition paid in 2026 to a qualifying two- or four-year college
2. Less scholarships, grants or financial aid
3. Less insurer reimbursements or refunds (1098-T Box 10)
4. Net tuition (if 0 or less, no deduction)
5. Massachusetts AGI (Line 27 worksheet, Line 7)
6. Line 5 × 25%
7. Deduction = Line 4 − Line 6, if positive
## Qualifying expenses
Tuition and mandatory enrollment fees only, for academic terms beginning in 2026 or the first three months of 2027. NOT room and board, books, supplies, equipment, travel, athletic fees, insurance.
## Notes
- In practice helps mostly lower-income families with high tuition, because of the 25% floor
## Questions
- Is the student an undergraduate? At which college?
- What tuition was paid, and how much aid was received?
- Were you a Massachusetts resident all year?
## Common Errors
- Including room, board or books
- Claiming graduate tuition
- Forgetting the 25% floor
## Prompt
- I paid my daughter's college tuition.
