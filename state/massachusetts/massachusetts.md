## Key 2026 numbers
- Rates: 5.0% (most income and long-term gains); 8.5% short-term gains; 12% collectibles; +4% surtax over $1,107,750; optional 5.85%
- Filing threshold: $8,000 gross income; 2026 return due April 15, 2027
- Exemptions: $4,400 / $6,800 / $8,800; dependents $1,000; age 65+ $700; blind $2,200
- Rental deduction 50% up to $4,000; Social Security/retirement deduction up to $2,000 per person; commuter deduction up to $750 per person
- Massachusetts follows the Internal Revenue Code as of January 1, 2024 for gross income (TIR 26-4); most P.L. 119-21 changes do NOT apply
