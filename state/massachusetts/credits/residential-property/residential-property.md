# Massachusetts Residential Property Credits
## Description
Nonrefundable credits for specific improvements to Massachusetts residential property. None has its own Form 1 line: each is computed on its own schedule, reported on Schedule CMS Section 1, and lands on Form 1, Line 31 ("Other credits").
## Credits in this folder
- Lead Paint Credit (lead-paint-credit.md) — Schedule LP → Schedule CMS (LEDPNT) → Form 1, Line 31. Up to $3,000 per unit. 7-year carryforward
- Septic (Title 5) Credit (septic-credit.md) — Schedule SC → Schedule CMS (SEPTIC) → Form 1, Line 31. 60% of costs, $4,000 per year, $18,000 total. 5-year carryforward
- Solar and Wind Energy Credit (solar-wind-energy-credit.md) — Schedule EC → Schedule CMS (SLRWND) → Form 1, Line 31. 15%, $1,000 lifetime per residence. 3-year carryforward
## Common mechanics
- All three are NONREFUNDABLE; unused amounts carry forward for a limited number of years
- All three require the schedule to be ENCLOSED with the return or the credit is disallowed
- When several are claimed together, each schedule's tax-limit line is reduced by the other credits — use first the credit that expires first
## 2026 federal contrast
The federal residential clean energy credit (Section 25D) ended for expenditures made after December 31, 2025 under Public Law 119-21. The Massachusetts Solar and Wind Energy Credit continues for 2026.
## Questions
- Did you remove or cover lead paint in a Massachusetts home?
- Did you repair or replace a failed septic system or connect to sewer?
- Did you install solar or wind equipment at your Massachusetts principal residence?
