# Solar and Wind Energy Credit (Schedule EC)
## Description
Nonrefundable Massachusetts credit for renewable solar or wind energy source property installed at the taxpayer's Massachusetts principal residence (G.L. c. 62, § 6(d)).
## Amounts (unchanged for 2026; not indexed)
- 15% of net expenditure (cost less any U.S. HUD grant or rebate)
- Maximum $1,000 for the ENTIRE time the taxpayer lives in the same principal residence — a LIFETIME per-residence cap, not annual
- Unused credit carries forward 3 years
## Who qualifies
- Owner OR tenant of Massachusetts residential property who occupies it as principal residence
- Not a dependent of another taxpayer
- Must be the first one to use the item; it must be expected to last 5 years and meet DOR performance standards
- Joint owners share the credit in proportion to ownership
- Condominium and cooperative units may qualify; new construction qualifies for the original owner-occupant
## Qualifying property
Equipment that uses solar energy for heating, cooling, hot water or electricity, or wind energy for electricity or mechanical energy: collectors, rock beds, heat exchangers, windmills, wind-driven generators, power conditioning and storage devices.
## NOT qualifying
- Backup heating or cooling that is not solar or wind
- Storage for nonessential functions (e.g., swimming pools)
- Wood stoves, oil and gas furnaces
- Heat pumps not solely powered by solar or wind
- Structural components
- Insulation, storm windows, caulking, weatherstripping
- Repair or maintenance costs
- Summer or vacation homes; landlord-installed property in a rental unit
## 2026 federal contrast
The federal residential clean energy credit (Section 25D) is NOT available for expenditures made after December 31, 2025 (Public Law 119-21). For 2026 installations, the Massachusetts credit may be the only income tax credit available.
## Timing
Expenditures are generally treated as made when installation is completed.
## Required Information
- Installation cost and completion date
- HUD grants or rebates
- Prior Schedule EC credit taken on the same residence
- Carryforward from 2023-2025 Schedules EC
## Questions
- Did you install solar or wind equipment at your Massachusetts principal residence in 2026?
- When was installation completed, and what did it cost?
- Have you ever claimed this credit for this residence before?
- Did you receive any HUD grant or rebate?
## Common Errors
- Treating the $1,000 as an annual cap
- Claiming a federal 25D credit for a 2026 installation
- Including heat pumps, insulation or windows
- Claiming for a vacation home
## Prompt
- I put solar panels on my house this year.
- Is there still a tax credit for solar in 2026?
