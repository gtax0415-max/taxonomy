# Credit Recapture (Schedule CRS)
## Description
Not a credit — the reverse. When a statute requires a previously claimed credit to be redetermined (e.g., property disposed of before the end of its useful life), the reduction is added back as tax on Form 1, Line 25. Included here because it is the mirror image of the business credits. See DOR Directive 89-7.
## How it works
For each credit (by certificate number, or by type and year):
1. Original credit − revised credit = TENTATIVE RECAPTURE
2. Subtract any portion of the original credit that was NEVER USED
3. The remainder is the addition to tax → Form 1, Line 25
4. Reduce any remaining carryover by the amount used to offset tentative recapture
## Credits commonly recaptured
- Farming and Fisheries — disposition or loss of qualified use before end of useful life, unless in qualified use more than 12 years
- Investment Tax / EOAC — early disposition of property (G.L. c. 63, § 31A(e))
- Sustainable Aviation Fuel (new 2026) — material noncompliance determined by MassDOT
- Certificate-based credits per their statutes
## Notes
- Taxpayers with a recapture calculation must complete Schedule CRS even if no recapture tax results
- Recapture tax is NOT eliminated by No Tax Status and is excluded from the Limited Income Credit computation
## Questions
- Did you sell or stop using property on which you took a Massachusetts credit?
- Has an agency revoked or reduced a credit you claimed?
## Common Errors
- Not filing Schedule CRS when recapture is zero
- Forgetting to reduce the carryover by the offset amount
## Prompt
- I sold farm equipment I took a credit on.
