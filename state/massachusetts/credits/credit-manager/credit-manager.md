# Schedule CMS — Credit Manager Schedule
## Description
The Massachusetts routing schedule for nearly every credit. Unlike the federal return, where many credits have their own line, Massachusetts puts most credits through Schedule CMS. Only FIVE credits bypass it and are claimed directly on Form 1: Limited Income (Line 29), Other Jurisdiction (Line 30), Earned Income (Line 43), Senior Circuit Breaker (Line 44), and Child and Family (Line 46).
## Sections
| Section | What goes here | Total line | Form 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Non-Refundable | Own credits used to reduce tax, passed to members, or shared; plus all available unused credits | 1h | Line 31 |
| 2 Refundable | Own refundable credits for which a refund is requested | 2g | Line 47 |
| 3 Non-Refundable from K-1 | Credits received on a Massachusetts K-1 | 3i | Line 31 |
| 4 Refundable from K-1 | Refundable K-1 credits for which a refund is requested | 4h | Line 47 |
Line 31 = 1h + 3i. Line 47 = 2g + 4h.
## Identification — one or the other, never both
- CERTIFICATE NUMBER (agency-awarded credits): left to right, no hyphens, spaces or symbols. Leave period end date blank
- PERIOD END DATE (credits by operation of law, mm/dd/yyyy, e.g., 12/31/2026). Leave certificate blank
- NON-EXPIRING (indefinite carryover, e.g., Research and Investment Tax credits): fill in the oval, leave both blank, enclose a statement
- Missing identification → credit DISALLOWED and tax adjusted
## Refundable credits: 90% vs. 100%
Section 2/4 columns: balance available → reduction in balance → refundable credit taken. For 90%-refundable credits (Film, Life Sciences, Climatetech Jobs, PTE excise), reducing a balance by $10,000 yields $9,000 refund. See TIR 13-6, Example 3.
## Worked examples (from the 2025 instructions, restated for a 2026 calendar-year return)
- Section 2: FLMCRD certificate 0000000011, $10,000 available, $10,000 reduction, $9,000 refundable; CNSLND certificate 1110000000, $1,000 / $1,000 / $1,000
- Section 1: SEPTIC 12/31/2026, $18,000 available, $4,000 taken; LEAD PAINT 12/31/2026, $1,000 available, $1,000 taken (a credit carried forward from an earlier year keeps its ORIGINAL period end date)
## Rules
- Report ALL available credits, even those not used this year
- A separate schedule required to generate the credit (LP, SC, EC, FAF, EOAC, EDIP, H, RC, VP, RLSC) must also be enclosed
- Brownfields, Film Incentive and Medical Device credits are NEVER reported in Section 3 or 4 (Film refunds go in Section 2)
- Pass-through entities report credits distributed to members in the "credit shared" column
## Required Information
- Every available credit: type code, certificate number or period end date, balance, amount taken, amount shared, amount refunded
- Source entity federal ID for K-1 credits
## Questions
- Which Massachusetts credits do you have, and are any carried over from prior years?
- Did any arrive on a Massachusetts K-1?
- Do you want any refundable credit refunded rather than applied?
## Common Errors
- Not enclosing Schedule CMS (credits delayed or disallowed)
- Entering both a certificate number and a period end date
- Putting K-1 credits in Section 1
- Claiming 100% of a 90%-refundable credit
- Omitting carried-forward credits not used this year
## Prompt
- Where do I put my Massachusetts lead paint credit?
- How do I claim a refundable Massachusetts credit from my K-1?
