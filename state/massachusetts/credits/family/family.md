# Massachusetts Family Credits
## Description
Credits for taxpayers who maintain a household with children, dependents who are elderly or disabled, or a disabled spouse.
## Credits in this folder
- Child and Family Tax Credit (child-and-family-tax-credit.md) — $440 per qualifying individual → Form 1, Line 46. Refundable
## How it compares to federal
- The federal Child Tax Credit is income-tested and phases out; the Massachusetts credit is a FLAT $440 with NO income limit and NO cap on the number of qualifying individuals
- The Massachusetts credit covers people the federal credit does not: dependents 65 or older, disabled dependents, and a disabled spouse
- The child age cutoff is UNDER 13 (federal Child Tax Credit: under 17)
## History
Since 2023 the Child and Family Tax Credit replaced three older benefits: the household dependent credit, the dependent care credit, and the separate deductions for a dependent under 12 and for dependent care. Form 1 Lines 12 and 13 are now "Reserved for future use".
## Questions
- Do you have children under 13?
- Do you support a dependent who is 65 or older or disabled?
- Is your spouse physically or mentally unable to care for himself or herself?
