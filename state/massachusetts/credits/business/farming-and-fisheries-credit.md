# Farming and Fisheries Credit (Schedule FAF)
## Description
Nonrefundable Massachusetts credit for individuals primarily engaged in agriculture, farming or commercial fishing, equal to 3% of the federal cost or other basis of qualifying property acquired, constructed or erected during the year. The personal-income-tax counterpart of the corporate investment tax credit. Effective since 2015.
## Qualifying property
Tangible personal property and other tangible property, INCLUDING buildings and structural components, that is:
- Located in Massachusetts
- Used SOLELY in farming, agriculture or fishing
- Depreciable, with a useful life of at least 4 years
## Calculation
- Owner (Part 1): qualifying property cost × 3%
- Lessee (Part 2): lessor's adjusted basis at start of lease × 3% × (days leased in the year ÷ days in useful life, carried to six decimals)
- Part 3: credit available = current credit + unused prior-year credit; allowed = smaller of that or total tax (Form 1, Line 28) less Limited Income Credit, other-jurisdiction credit and certain other credits
## Lessor vs. lessee
Where the lessee is eligible, the lessor generally is NOT — except equine-based businesses where care and boarding of horses is part of the agricultural activity.
## Carryforward
3 subsequent years. For 2026: unused 2023 credit expires after 2026; 2024 credit usable through 2027; 2025 credit through 2028; 2026 credit through 2029.
## Recapture
If property is disposed of or stops being used in qualified use before the end of its useful life, the difference between the credit taken and the credit allowed for actual use is added back as tax in the year of disposition — unless the property was in qualified use for more than 12 years. Report on Schedule CRS.
## One schedule per business type
File a separate Schedule FAF for agricultural/farming and for commercial fishing.
## Required Information
- Description, cost or basis, date acquired and useful life of each item
- Lease terms (if lessee)
- Carryforward from prior Schedules FAF
- Tax and other credits from Form 1
## Questions
- Are you primarily engaged in farming or commercial fishing?
- What depreciable property did you buy or build in Massachusetts this year?
- Is it used only in the farming or fishing business?
- Do you lease the property?
- Do you have unused credit from 2023-2025?
## Common Errors
- Including property with a useful life under 4 years
- Including property also used outside the farming or fishing business
- Not filing a separate schedule for each business type
- Forgetting recapture when equipment is sold early
- Failing to enclose Schedule FAF (the credit is disallowed)
## Prompt
- I bought a new boat for my fishing business.
- I built a barn on my farm in Massachusetts.
