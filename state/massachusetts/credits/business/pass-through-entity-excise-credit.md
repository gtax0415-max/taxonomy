# Pass-Through Entity Excise Credit
## Description
Refundable Massachusetts credit for individual partners, S corporation shareholders and trust beneficiaries (qualified members) of a pass-through entity that elected to pay the Massachusetts PTE excise (G.L. c. 63D). The entity pays 5% on qualified income; each member gets a credit for 90% of their share. The excise is a federal SALT-cap workaround: the entity deducts the tax federally.
## How it works
- Entity elects and pays 5% excise on members' qualified income (Form 63D-ELT)
- Member receives the share on a Massachusetts K-1
- Member's credit = 90% of their pro-rata share of excise PAID → Schedule CMS, credit type ELTCRD, period end date
- The 10% haircut is the cost of the election
## NEW for 2026: additional 4% excise (G.L. c. 63E)
For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, the FY2026 supplemental budget (St. 2026, c. 101, signed June 12, 2026) lets a PTE elect an ADDITIONAL 4% excise on distributive share income attributable to members whose income exceeds the surtax threshold ($1,107,750 for 2026). DOR's working draft TIR (June 23, 2026) describes the election; confirm how members claim credit for it when final guidance and the 2026 forms are released.
## Interaction with the other-jurisdiction credit
Elective PTE taxes paid to OTHER states affect the Massachusetts other-jurisdiction credit — see DOR Directive 19-1 and other-jurisdiction/credit-for-taxes-paid-to-other-jurisdictions.md.
## Required Information
- Massachusetts K-1 showing the member's share of excise paid
- Entity name and federal ID
- Whether the entity also made the 2026 c. 63E election
## Questions
- Did your partnership or S corporation elect the Massachusetts PTE excise for 2026?
- What share of excise paid is shown on your Massachusetts K-1?
- Did the entity also elect the new 4% additional excise?
## Common Errors
- Claiming 100% instead of 90%
- Claiming excise the entity elected but did not actually PAY
- Reporting in Section 1 instead of Section 3/4 (it is received via K-1)
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows Massachusetts PTE excise paid.
- My S corp elected the Massachusetts SALT workaround.
