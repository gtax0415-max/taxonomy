# Farm Food Donation Credit — NEW for 2026
## Description
New refundable, non-transferable Massachusetts credit for farm businesses that donate food, meals or crops to a nonprofit food distribution organization. Created by the FY2026 supplemental budget (St. 2026, c. 101, signed June 12, 2026). Administered by DOR. Available to both personal income (c. 62) and corporate (c. 63) taxpayers.
## Effective period
Taxable years ending on or after December 31, 2026 and before January 1, 2029 (tax years 2026, 2027, 2028).
## Amount
Fair market value of donated items, capped at $5,000 per year. Excess over tax liability is REFUNDED without interest.
## Key eligibility rules
- Taxpayer is a farm business
- Items are grown, manufactured, packaged or prepared by the farm business
- Recipient nonprofit distributes or serves them FREE, or at a charge covering only handling cost — not used as payment for services or property, not resold above handling cost
- NO federal or state deduction may be claimed for the same donation
- Obtain the nonprofit's written certification
## Notes
- Source: DOR Working Draft TIR (June 23, 2026). The 2026 form, schedule and Schedule CMS code are not yet published
- Compare to the federal enhanced deduction for food inventory — the credit requires giving up any deduction for the same donation; compare value before choosing
## Required Information
- Nonprofit certification(s) with date, quantity and fair market value
- Confirmation no deduction was taken for the same items
## Questions
- Does your farm donate crops or food to a food bank or pantry?
- What is the fair market value of the donations?
- Did you deduct these donations anywhere else?
## Common Errors
- Taking both a deduction and the credit for the same donation
- Missing the nonprofit's written certification
- Exceeding $5,000
## Prompt
- My farm donated produce to the food bank.
