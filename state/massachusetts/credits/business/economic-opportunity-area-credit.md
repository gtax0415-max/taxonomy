# Economic Opportunity Area Credit (Schedule EOAC)
## Description
Massachusetts credit of 5% of the cost of property purchased for business use within an EACC-certified project in an Economic Opportunity Area (EOA). A legacy credit: projects certified on or after January 1, 2010 use EDIP instead.
## Calculation
1. Cost of qualifying property (Line 6) minus any U.S. basis reduction (Line 7)
2. × 5% = current-year credit (Line 9)
3. Allowable = lesser of available credit (current + prior unused) or 50% of (total tax − other credits)
## The 50% limitation
Individuals, trusts and partnerships can use the EOAC to offset at most 50% of tax due after the Limited Income Credit, other-jurisdiction credit and listed credits (Lead Paint, EDIP, Septic, Low-Income Housing, Historic Rehabilitation, Film, Medical Device, Brownfields, Farming and Fisheries, etc.).
## Carryforward
The Schedule EOAC instructions state that individuals may carry unused credit forward 10 years, and that credit unused BECAUSE OF THE 50% LIMITATION may be carried forward for an unlimited period. Track the two categories separately.
## Key rules
- Property must qualify for the 3% investment tax credit under G.L. c. 63, § 31A and be used exclusively in the certified project
- Cannot take both the 5% EOAC and 3% ITC on the same property
- Available to sole proprietors, partnerships, trusts, and corporations
## Required Information
- EACC certification date and project location
- Description, date acquired, life and cost of each item
- Prior-year unused EOAC
- Pass-through entity name and ID, if applicable
## Questions
- When was your project certified by the EACC?
- What property did you buy for the certified project?
- Do you have unused EOAC from prior years?
## Common Errors
- Using Schedule EOAC for a post-2009 project (should be EDIP)
- Ignoring the 50% limitation
- Claiming both EOAC and ITC on the same property
## Prompt
- My business is in an economic opportunity area.
