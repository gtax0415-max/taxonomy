# Certificate-Based Credits (Schedule CMS)
## Description
Catch-all for Massachusetts credits awarded by a state agency and tracked by CERTIFICATE NUMBER. Most are aimed at developers, investors and businesses, but individuals encounter them as sole proprietors, as K-1 recipients, or as BUYERS of transferable credits. Enter the certificate number on Schedule CMS (omit hyphens, spaces and symbols, left to right) — without it the credit is disallowed.

## NEW for 2026: Sustainable Aviation Fuel Credit
- Created by St. 2026, c. 101 (G.L. c. 62, § 6(kk)). Taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026 and ending on or before December 31, 2030
- Nonrefundable, non-transferable; 5-year carryforward; subject to recapture
- Lesser of (i) c. 64J jet fuel excise paid or (ii) gallons of SAF × $1.50, plus $0.015 per additional 1% lifecycle GHG reduction above 50%, max $2.00 per gallon
- Fuel must be for an aircraft departing a Massachusetts airport; authorized by MassDOT Aeronautics Division; $10 million annual cap
- Schedule CMS code not yet published
## Expired / no longer available
- Temporary Authorized Training Tax Credit for Emergency Assistance — no longer available as of January 1, 2026
- Harbor Maintenance (repealed 2022) — carryover only
## Notes
- Report ALL available credits on Schedule CMS even if none is used this year
- Credits indefinitely carried forward under G.L. c. 63, § 32C are reported as "non-expiring" (fill in the oval, leave period end date and certificate blank) with a statement enclosed
- A credit BUYER must hold the transferred certificate; purchase price vs. credit face value may have federal income consequences
## Questions
- Do you hold a Massachusetts credit certificate, or did you buy one?
- Did a credit come to you on a Massachusetts K-1?
- Is the credit refundable, and are you requesting a refund?
## Common Errors
- Omitting or mistyping the certificate number
- Reporting Brownfields, Film or Medical Device credits in Section 3
- Claiming 100% of a 90%-refundable credit
- Not listing unused credits
