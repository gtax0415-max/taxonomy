# Massachusetts Business Credits (Personal Income Tax)
## Description
Credits available to individuals with business, farming, fishing or pass-through income. Every one of them is reported on Schedule CMS. Nonrefundable credits land on Form 1, Line 31; refundable credits being refunded land on Form 1, Line 47.
## Credits in this folder
- Farming and Fisheries Credit (farming-and-fisheries-credit.md) — Schedule FAF → CMS (FRMFSH) → Line 31. 3% of property. Nonrefundable
- Economic Opportunity Area Credit (economic-opportunity-area-credit.md) — Schedule EOAC → CMS (EOACCR) → Line 31. 5% of property. Pre-2010 certified projects
- Economic Development Incentive Program Credit (economic-development-incentive-program-credit.md) — Schedule EDIP → CMS (EDIPCR / EDICRD) → Line 31 or 47. Refundable if authorized
- Pass-Through Entity Excise Credit (pass-through-entity-excise-credit.md) — Massachusetts K-1 → CMS (ELTCRD) → Line 31 or 47. 90% of PTE excise paid. Refundable
- Farm Food Donation Credit (farm-food-donation-credit.md) — NEW for 2026. Refundable, up to $5,000
- Certificate-Based Credits (certificate-based-credits.md) — Historic Rehabilitation, Low-Income Housing, Brownfields, Film, Conservation Land, Dairy Farm, Apprenticeship, Live Theater, Homeownership, Climatetech, Sustainable Aviation Fuel (NEW for 2026), and others
## Identification on Schedule CMS
- CERTIFICATE NUMBER: credits awarded by an agency (EDIP, historic, housing, film, etc.). Enter the number only, no period end date
- PERIOD END DATE: credits arising by operation of law (FAF, EOAC, lead paint, septic, PTE excise, etc.)
- Missing either → the credit is DISALLOWED and tax is adjusted
## Received from a pass-through entity
Partners, S corporation shareholders and beneficiaries receive credits on Massachusetts Schedule 3K-1, SK-1 or 2K-1 and report them in Schedule CMS Section 3 (nonrefundable) or Section 4 (refundable) with the entity's federal ID — they generally do NOT complete the underlying credit schedule.
## Recapture
Several business credits are recaptured if property is disposed of early — see credit-manager/credit-recapture.md.
## Questions
- Do you farm or fish commercially?
- Do you own an interest in a partnership, S corporation or trust that issued a Massachusetts K-1?
- Did a state agency issue you a credit certificate?

- My partnership elected the Massachusetts PTE excise.
- I bought a tractor for my farm.
