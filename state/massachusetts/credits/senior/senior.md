# Massachusetts Senior Credits
## Description
Credits targeted at taxpayers 65 and older. Massachusetts has one income tax credit in this category, aimed at property tax and rent burden.
## Credits in this folder
- Senior Circuit Breaker Credit (senior-circuit-breaker-credit.md) — Schedule CB → Form 1, Line 44 (Form 1-NR/PY, Line 48). Refundable
## Related (not credits)
- Age 65 or over exemption: $700 per person on Form 1, Line 2c (an exemption, not a credit)
- A dependent 65 or older qualifies the taxpayer for the Child and Family Tax Credit — see family/child-and-family-tax-credit.md
- Local property tax exemptions and deferrals (Clause 41A and similar) are administered by cities and towns, not DOR
## Questions
- Are you (or your spouse) 65 or older?
- Do you own or rent your home in Massachusetts?
## Prompt
- I'm a senior and my property taxes are high.
- Is there a Massachusetts credit for seniors who rent?
