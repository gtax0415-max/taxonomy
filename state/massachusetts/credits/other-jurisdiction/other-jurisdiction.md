# Credits for Tax Paid to Other Jurisdictions
## Description
Credit that prevents the same income from being taxed by Massachusetts and by another state. The Massachusetts analog of the federal foreign tax credit — but it covers OTHER STATES, U.S. territories, and Canada, not foreign countries generally.
## Credits in this folder
- Credit for Taxes Paid to Other Jurisdictions (credit-for-taxes-paid-to-other-jurisdictions.md) — Form 1, Line 30 worksheet + Schedule OJC → Form 1, Line 30. Nonrefundable
## Mapping to the federal taxonomy
| Tax paid to | Federal treatment | Massachusetts treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Another U.S. state | Itemized SALT deduction | Line 30 credit |
| U.S. territory (PR, VI, Guam, DC) | Foreign tax credit in some cases | Line 30 credit |
| Canada or a province | Foreign tax credit (Form 1116) | Line 30 credit, REDUCED by the federal foreign tax credit claimed |
| Any other foreign country | Foreign tax credit (Form 1116) | NO Massachusetts credit |
| City or local income tax | — | NO Massachusetts credit |
## Questions
- Did you earn income in another state and file a return there?
- Did you pay tax to Canada or a Canadian province?
- Are you a resident subject to the 4% surtax?
## Prompt
- I live in Massachusetts but work in New Hampshire / Rhode Island / New York.
- I paid tax to another state on the same income.
