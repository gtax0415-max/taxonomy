# Massachusetts Income-Based Credits
## Description
Credits for low- and moderate-income taxpayers where the amount depends on income and family size rather than on a specific expense. Massachusetts has two: one that piggybacks on the federal return and one that is purely state.
## Credits in this folder
- Earned Income Credit (earned-income-credit.md) — 40% of the federal EIC → Form 1, Line 43. Refundable
- Limited Income Credit (limited-income-credit.md) — alternative tax calculation → Form 1, Line 29. Nonrefundable. Paired with No Tax Status (Form 1, Line 27)
## How they differ
- The EIC requires EARNED income and a federal EIC claim. The Limited Income Credit uses total Massachusetts AGI and has NO earned-income test
- The EIC is refundable; the Limited Income Credit can only bring tax to zero
- Both bar MARRIED FILING SEPARATELY (the EIC has narrow exceptions)
- A taxpayer can receive BOTH in the same year
## Notes
- The Child and Family Tax Credit is not income-tested in Massachusetts and lives under family/
- The Senior Circuit Breaker is income-capped but is a housing-cost credit; it lives under senior/
## Questions
- Did you work and have modest income?
- Did you claim the federal Earned Income Credit?
- Was your Massachusetts AGI under about $14,000 (single), $25,200 (head of household) or $28,700 (joint), plus amounts for dependents?
## Prompt
- Do I get the Massachusetts earned income credit?
- I barely made any money this year. Do I owe Massachusetts tax?
