# Foreign Corporation Income of U.S. Shareholder (Schedule FCI)
## Description
Required for any Massachusetts taxpayer — individual or business — who is a U.S. shareholder of a controlled foreign corporation (CFC) or specified foreign corporation and must report CFC income federally. Reports Subpart F income, GILTI, other CFC dividends and the IRC 78 gross-up. For individuals these are dividend-type income taxed on Schedule B.
## Schedule FCI lines (individuals)
1. Subpart F income — U.S. Form 5471, Schedule I, total of Lines 1-4
2. IRC 951A GILTI — U.S. Form 8992, Part II, Line 5
3. Other dividends from CFCs — Form 5471, Schedule I, Lines 5a-5e
4. IRC 78 gross-up for foreign taxes deemed paid
Header: number of foreign corporations reported; copies of every Form 5471 must be enclosed.
## Combined group members only (corporations and financial institutions)
Lines 1-9: total dividends, eliminations, dividends ineligible for the 95% Massachusetts dividends-received deduction (under 15% ownership, RICs, REITs, non-wholly owned DISCs), and the 95% Massachusetts DRD.
## Key Massachusetts rules
- No deduction under IRC 245A (participation exemption), 250 (GILTI/FDII) or 965(c) — the full inclusion and gross-up are taxed
- Federal Forms 5471, 8992 (and Schedule B), 8993, K-2 and K-3 must be submitted with Schedule FCI — they are part of the Massachusetts return
- See TIR 19-11
## 2026 note
P.L. 119-21 renames GILTI as "net CFC tested income" and changes the Section 250 deduction for tax years beginning after 2025. Massachusetts does not adopt the Section 250 change (TIR 26-4) and disallows Section 250 anyway; Massachusetts does adopt the deemed-intangible-income rule (Section 70323). Expect the 2026 Schedule FCI to reference the renamed federal line — confirm when released.
## Questions
- Do you own 10% or more of a foreign corporation?
- Did you file Forms 5471, 8992 or 8993, or receive a K-3 with CFC items?
## Common Errors
- Taking the federal Section 250 deduction in Massachusetts
- Omitting the IRC 78 gross-up
- Not enclosing the federal forms
## Prompt
- I own shares of a foreign company and file Form 5471.
- I have GILTI on my federal return.
