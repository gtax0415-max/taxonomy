# Massachusetts Interest and Dividends
## Description
Massachusetts splits interest into two classes. Interest from Massachusetts banks goes straight onto Form 1 as 5.0% Part B income. All other interest and all dividends go through Schedule B as Part A income — also taxed at 5.0%, but it cannot be reduced by Part B deductions such as the rental or charitable deduction.

## Biggest federal-to-state differences
- Out-of-state municipal bond interest — federally TAX-EXEMPT, Massachusetts TAXABLE
- U.S. Treasury and U.S. agency interest — federally TAXABLE, Massachusetts EXEMPT
- Massachusetts municipal bond interest — exempt for both
