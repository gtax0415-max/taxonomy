# Interest from Massachusetts Banks
## Description
Total interest received or credited on deposit accounts in Massachusetts banks — savings, NOW accounts, term and time deposits, CDs, and savings shares. Taxed as 5.0% Part B income on Form 1, Line 5.
## Why it is separate
Because it is Part B income, Massachusetts bank interest CAN be reduced by Part B deductions (rental deduction, charitable deduction, etc.), unlike interest on Schedule B.
## Notes
- Enter the GROSS amount; forfeited interest and early withdrawal penalties go on Schedule Y, Line 2
- Massachusetts bank interest is also entered on Schedule B, Line 5 and subtracted there, so it is not double-counted
- Online or out-of-state banks are NOT Massachusetts banks, even if the account holder is in Massachusetts
## Required Information
- Forms 1099-INT identifying the bank and its location
## Questions
- Which banks paid you interest, and are they Massachusetts banks?
- Were you charged an early withdrawal penalty?
## Common Errors
- Putting out-of-state or online bank interest on Line 5
- Netting the early withdrawal penalty against the interest
- Double-counting by also leaving it in Schedule B
## Prompt
- Where do I put interest from my local bank?
