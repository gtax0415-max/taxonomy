# Estate, Trust, REMIC and Farm Rental Income (Schedule E-3)
## Description
Income or loss from estates and trusts, REMICs, and farm rental, one Schedule E-3 per entity.
## Key points
- Massachusetts resident trusts and estates pay their own Massachusetts tax on Form 2. Income already taxed on Form 2 is REMOVED here (Line 8) so it is not taxed twice
- Grantor-type trusts and non-Massachusetts estates and trusts are taxed to the beneficiary/grantor here
- Interest and dividends are removed (Line 10) and reported on Schedule B
- A 2K-1 may show a 90% refundable PTE Excise Credit (Line 19e) — enter in the E-3 header and claim on Schedule CMS
## Required Information
- Federal K-1 (1041), Massachusetts 2K-1, U.S. Schedule Q, Form 4835
## Questions
- Is the trust a grantor trust? Is it a Massachusetts trust that files Form 2?
- Do you rent farmland to someone else?
## Common Errors
- Double-taxing income already taxed to a Massachusetts trust on Form 2
- Reporting farm rental on Form 1, Line 6b
## Prompt
- I'm a beneficiary of a trust.
- I rent my farmland to a neighbor.
