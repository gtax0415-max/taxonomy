# Massachusetts Supplemental Income (Schedule E)
## Description
Rental, royalty, partnership, S corporation, estate, trust, REMIC and farm rental income, taxed as 5.0% Part B income. Massachusetts uses a summary schedule plus one sub-schedule PER ENTITY.
## Structure
| Schedule | What | Files per |
|---|---|---|
| E-1 | Rental real estate and royalties | Each property |
| E-2 | Partnerships and S corporations | Each entity |
| E-3 | Estates, trusts, REMICs, farm rental | Each entity |
| E (Reconciliation) | Summary; Line 58 → Form 1, Line 7 | One |

## Common rules
- Interest and dividends inside K-1 income are pulled OUT (Schedule E Lines 33-34, 46) and reported on Schedule B or Form 1, Line 5
- Depreciation, Section 179 and 163(j) follow Massachusetts rules (see income/business/business.md)
- Schedule E filers must generally use software-generated forms (TIR 08-22, 16-9)
- Massachusetts differences (Form 2 trust income, 1987 passive loss differences) → Schedule E, Line 56 with statement
- Abandoned Building Renovation Deduction → Schedule E, Line 57
