# Rental Real Estate and Royalty Income (Schedule E-1)
## Description
Net income or loss from each rental property or royalty, one Schedule E-1 per property, following U.S. Schedule E line-for-line with Massachusetts adjustments.
## Massachusetts differences
- Line 18 depreciation: Massachusetts Section 179 limit (2025: $1,250,000 / $3,130,000) and no 168(n) for 2026
- Line 11 other interest: Section 163(j) under 2024 rules for 2026
- Line 16 other expenses: R&E rules as above
- Lead paint costs taken as the Lead Paint Credit cannot also be expensed
## Notes
- Short-term rentals must be flagged; owners may also owe the state and local room occupancy excise (a separate tax, not income tax)
- Passive loss limits follow the federal rules, subject to Massachusetts differences computed on a pro forma U.S. Form 8582
## Required Information
- Rents, royalties, expenses by category, Massachusetts depreciation, days rented and personal-use days
## Questions
- How many rental properties do you have, and where?
- Is any of it a short-term rental?
- Did you use it personally?
## Common Errors
- One combined E-1 for multiple properties
- Federal depreciation figures carried over
- Not flagging short-term rentals
## Prompt
- I rent out my condo.
- I rent my house on Airbnb.
