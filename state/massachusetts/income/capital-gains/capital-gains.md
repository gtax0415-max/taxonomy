# Massachusetts Capital Gains
## Description
Massachusetts taxes capital gains at THREE rates depending on holding period and asset type, split between Schedule B (short-term and collectibles) and Schedule D (long-term).
## Files in this folder
- Long-Term Capital Gains (long-term-capital-gains.md) — Schedule D → Form 1, Line 24. 5.0%
- Short-Term and Collectible Gains (short-term-and-collectible-gains.md) — Schedule B, Part 2 → Form 1, Lines 23a (8.5%) and 23b (12%)
- Schedule D-IS (schedule-d-is.md) — QSB stock exclusion, 3% Massachusetts start-up rate, 1996-2002 installment sales
## Rates for 2026
| Gain | Rate |
|---|---|
| Long-term (held more than one year), excluding collectibles | 5.0% |
| Short-term (held one year or less) | 8.5% |
| Long-term collectibles (IRC 408(m)) and pre-1996 installment sales | 12% (with a 50% deduction, so effectively 6%) |
| Any taxable income above $1,107,750 | +4% surtax |
## Interaction rules
- Long-term and short-term losses net against each other across the two schedules
- Up to $2,000 of either can offset interest and dividends
- Massachusetts long-term loss carryovers continue indefinitely (Schedule D, Line 23); short-term carryovers via Schedule B, Line 40
- Massachusetts does NOT adopt the 2025 federal expansion of the QSBS exclusion (IRC 1202)
## Questions
- Did you sell stock, mutual funds, real estate, crypto or collectibles?
- How long did you hold each asset?
- Do you have Massachusetts capital loss carryovers?
