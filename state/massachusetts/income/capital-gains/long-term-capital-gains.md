# Long-Term Capital Gains (Schedule D)
## Description
Gains and losses on capital assets held more than one year, EXCLUDING collectibles, taxed at 5.0%. Built from U.S. Schedule D, column h, then adjusted for Massachusetts differences.
## Schedule D flow
1-6. Long-term gains/losses from U.S. Schedule D Lines 8a/8b, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13 (capital gain distributions; or 1040 Line 7a if no U.S. Schedule D)
7. Massachusetts long-term gains/losses from U.S. Form 4797, Part II not included above
8. Massachusetts carryover losses (prior-year Schedule D, Line 23)
9. Combine
10a. Massachusetts adjustments; 10b part-year/nonresident
11. Massachusetts capital gains and losses
12. Remove collectibles and pre-1996 installment sales (to Schedule B, 12%)
13-15. Apply short-term losses against long-term gains and vice versa
16. Up to $2,000 of long-term loss against interest and dividends
18. Schedule C-2 excess trade or business deductions
20. Excess exemptions
21. Taxable long-term gains
22. × 5% (or 5.85% if electing) → Form 1, Line 24
23. Loss available for carryover
## Massachusetts adjustments (Line 10a)
- Pre-1996 installment sales treated as ordinary income in Massachusetts (moved to Schedule X, Line 4)
- Installment sales for federal but not Massachusetts purposes
- BASIS differences: Massachusetts basis reflects Massachusetts depreciation (no Section 168(k) bonus, different Section 179 limits, 168(n) not adopted until 2027), so gain on sale of business or rental property usually differs. Basis increases are entered as negatives
## Notes
- Sale of a principal residence: Massachusetts follows the federal Section 121 exclusion
- Long-term loss carryovers do not expire
- 4% surtax applies to capital gains once total taxable income exceeds $1,107,750 (2026)
- Massachusetts does not adopt the P.L. 119-21 QSBS expansion; check Schedule D-IS rules for qualified small business stock
## Required Information
- Forms 1099-B, 8949, U.S. Schedule D, Form 4797
- K-1 long-term gains
- Massachusetts depreciation and basis records for business/rental property
- Prior-year Massachusetts carryover (Schedule D, Line 23)
## Questions
- Did you sell anything held more than a year?
- Was any of it a collectible (art, coins, metals, gems, stamps, antiques)?
- Did you sell business or rental property on which Massachusetts depreciation differed from federal?
- Do you have Massachusetts long-term loss carryovers?
## Common Errors
- Using federal basis for property with Massachusetts depreciation differences
- Leaving collectibles at the 5.0% rate
- Losing track of the Massachusetts carryover (it differs from the federal one)
- Forgetting the $2,000 offset against interest and dividends
## Prompt
- I sold stock I held for three years.
- I sold my rental property.
