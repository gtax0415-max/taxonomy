# Business or Profession Income (Massachusetts Schedule C)
## Description
Net profit or loss from a sole proprietorship, single-member LLC treated as disregarded, or statutory employee, recomputed under Massachusetts rules on Massachusetts Schedule C. The U.S. Schedule C is NOT an acceptable substitute.
## Key Massachusetts lines
- Line 1-5: Gross receipts and income, EXCLUDING interest (other than Massachusetts bank interest) and dividends
- Line 11: Depreciation and Section 179 — Massachusetts limits. No Section 168(k) bonus; no 168(n). 2025 Massachusetts Section 179 limit $1,250,000, phase-out from $3,130,000; 2026 indexed figure pending DOR
- Line 14b: Other interest — apply Section 163(j) using the 2024 adjusted-taxable-income rules for 2026
- Line 23: Meals — follows federal percentages, including the P.L. 119-21 exceptions (adopted)
- Line 26: Other expenses — for 2026, domestic R&E under Section 174A is ADOPTED
- Line 29: Business use of home
- Line 30: Abandoned Building Renovation Deduction (10% of qualifying renovation costs in an Economic Opportunity Area)
- Lines 32-33: At-risk limits; Line 36: allowable prior-year suspended passive losses
- Line 37 → Form 1, Line 6a
## Notes
- Self-employment tax paid counts toward the $2,000 Social Security/Medicare deduction on Form 1, Line 11
- Massachusetts has NO net operating loss carryover; an unused loss is gone
- The excess business loss is added back on Schedule X, Line 6 and never carried forward
- Filers with Schedule C differences should keep a Massachusetts depreciation schedule; it drives Massachusetts gain on sale
- Filling in the small-business energy sales tax oval is a disclosure, not a deduction
## Required Information
- U.S. Schedule C and all supporting records
- Massachusetts depreciation schedule
- Business interest and dividends to move to Schedule B
- Prior-year suspended passive losses
## Questions
- Did you take bonus depreciation or a large Section 179 deduction federally?
- Did the business earn interest or dividends?
- Do you have research and development costs?
- Did the business have a loss larger than $313,000 single / $626,000 joint (2025 thresholds)?
## Common Errors
- Attaching U.S. Schedule C instead of Massachusetts Schedule C
- Copying federal depreciation
- Leaving interest and dividends in gross receipts
- Expecting a Massachusetts NOL carryforward
## Prompt
- I'm a freelancer. How do I report my income in Massachusetts?
- I took bonus depreciation on equipment.
