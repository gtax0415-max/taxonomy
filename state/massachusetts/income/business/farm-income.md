# Farm Income or Loss
## Description
Income or loss from operating a farm as an individual or cooperative, reported from U.S. Schedule F, Line 34 and taxed as 5.0% Part B income. Massachusetts has no separate farm schedule; differences are shown on a pro forma U.S. Schedule F.
## Notes
- Same depreciation and loss differences as Schedule C: no bonus depreciation, Massachusetts Section 179 limits, no NOL
- Farm rental (landlord, Form 4835) goes to Schedule E-3
- Qualifying farm property may earn the 3% Farming and Fisheries Credit, and 2026 farm food donations may earn the new Farm Food Donation Credit — see credits/business/
## Questions
- Do you operate a farm, or rent farmland to someone else?
- Did you take bonus depreciation federally?
## Common Errors
- Using the federal Schedule F amount without Massachusetts depreciation adjustments
- Reporting farm rental on Line 6b
## Prompt
- I run a small farm in Massachusetts.
