# Massachusetts Business Income
## Description
Sole proprietorship and farm income, taxed as 5.0% Part B income. Massachusetts requires its OWN Schedule C (the U.S. Schedule C is no longer accepted as a substitute) because depreciation, expensing and loss rules differ from federal.

- Farm Income (farm-income.md) — Form 1, Line 6b (pro forma U.S. Schedule F)
## 2026 federal/Massachusetts differences for businesses
| Item | Federal 2026 | Massachusetts 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Bonus depreciation (IRC 168(k)) | 100% | NOT allowed |
| Section 179 | P.L. 119-21 limits ($2.5M / $4M base, indexed) | Pre-P.L. 119-21 limits (2025: $1,250,000 / $3,130,000; 2026 indexed figure pending) — new limits adopted 2027 |
| Qualified production property (168(n)) | 100% | NOT allowed until 2027 |
| Domestic R&E (174A) | Expensed | ADOPTED for 2026; no retroactive or transition relief for 2022-2024 costs |
| Business interest (163(j)) | ATI adds back depreciation | 2024 rules for 2026; adopted 2027 |
| Business meals exceptions | Allowed | ADOPTED |
| Excess business loss | Disallowed, becomes NOL | Disallowed AND lost (no NOL) — Schedule X, Line 6 |
| Net operating loss | Carryforward | NONE |
| QBI deduction (199A) | 20% | NOT allowed |
