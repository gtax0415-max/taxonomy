# Social Security Benefits
## Description
Massachusetts does NOT tax Social Security benefits. Do not report them on Form 1, even if part is taxable on the federal return.
## Where they still matter
- Senior Circuit Breaker (Schedule CB, Line 4): FULL benefits before Medicare premiums count as qualifying income — credits/senior/senior-circuit-breaker-credit.md
- Schedule HC (health care penalty) uses FEDERAL AGI, which includes the federally taxable portion — other-taxes/health-care-penalty.md
- Medicare premiums deducted from benefits are NOT deductible in Massachusetts
## Notes
- The 2026 federal senior deduction (P.L. 119-21) is not adopted by Massachusetts and is irrelevant here because benefits are already exempt
## Questions
- Did you receive Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits?
- Are you claiming the Senior Circuit Breaker?
## Common Errors
- Entering the federally taxable portion on Form 1
- Omitting benefits from Schedule CB income
## Prompt
- Does Massachusetts tax my Social Security?
