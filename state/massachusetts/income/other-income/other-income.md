# Massachusetts Other Income
## Description
Unemployment, lottery winnings, and the catch-all Schedule X items, all taxed as 5.0% Part B income.
## Files in this folder
- Unemployment Compensation (unemployment-compensation.md) — Form 1, Line 8a
- Gambling Winnings (gambling-winnings.md) — Form 1, Line 8b (Massachusetts lottery) and Schedule X, Line 3 (all other)
- Alimony Received (alimony-received.md) — Schedule X, Line 1
- Fees and Other 5.0% Income (fees-and-other-income.md) — Schedule X, Line 4
- PFML Distributions (pfml-distributions.md) — Schedule X, Line 5
- Excess Business Loss Adjustment (excess-business-loss-adjustment.md) — Schedule X, Line 6
- IRA/Keogh/Roth conversion distributions (Schedule X, Line 2) — see income/retirement/ira-keogh-roth-distributions.md
## Schedule X total
Schedule X, Line 7 = Lines 1 through 6, NOT LESS THAN ZERO → Form 1, Line 9. A negative total cannot reduce other income.
## Not income in Massachusetts
- Federal and Massachusetts income tax refunds (interest on them goes to Schedule B)
- Child support
- Social Security benefits
- Net operating losses shown negative on U.S. Schedule 1, Line 9 — not allowed
## Prompt
- I collected unemployment.
- I won money at a casino.
