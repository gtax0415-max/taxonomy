# Gambling and Lottery Winnings
## Description
All gambling winnings are 5.0% Part B income. Massachusetts does NOT adopt the federal gambling loss deduction (IRC 165(d)). Instead it allows a narrow deduction for losses at Massachusetts-licensed venues, only up to winnings from those venues.
## Reporting
| Source | Where | Losses deductible? |
|---|---|---|
| Massachusetts State Lottery | Form 1, Line 8b (winnings minus cost of the winning ticket) | No |
| Casino licensed under MGL ch 23K | Schedule X, Line 3 | Yes, Schedule Y, Line 17, up to qualifying winnings |
| Massachusetts racing / simulcast licensee | Schedule X, Line 3 | Yes, same limit |
| Massachusetts-licensed sports wagering operator (ch 23N, since 2024) | Schedule X, Line 3 | Yes, same limit |
| Out-of-state casinos, lotteries, sports books; raffles, beano | Schedule X, Line 3 | No |
## Worked example (2025 instructions)
Massachusetts lottery winnings $500 → Line 8b. Other winnings $3,100 (Massachusetts casino $800, Las Vegas casino $1,200, Massachusetts sports book $500, Las Vegas sports book $600) → Schedule X, Line 3. Qualifying losses: Massachusetts casino $1,000 and Massachusetts sports book $700, limited to $1,300 of qualifying winnings → Schedule Y, Line 17 = $1,300. Las Vegas losses and lottery scratch-ticket losses are not deductible.
## Notes
- TIR 26-4 lists Massachusetts as conforming to the P.L. 119-21 wagering-loss limitation (IRC 165(d), limiting losses to 90% beginning 2026). That matters where IRC 165(d) governs — professional gamblers in a trade or business. Non-professional Massachusetts taxpayers use only the separate Schedule Y, Line 17 deduction, which is limited to qualifying Massachusetts winnings
- Professional gamblers (a trade or business) follow business rules instead
- DOR matches Line 8b with Lottery Commission records
## Required Information
- W-2Gs, lottery statements, sports wagering account statements, loss records by venue
## Questions
- Where did you gamble — Massachusetts lottery, a Massachusetts casino, a Massachusetts sports app, or out of state?
- Do you have records of losses by venue?
## Common Errors
- Netting losses against winnings as on the federal Schedule A
- Deducting lottery or out-of-state losses
- Putting Massachusetts lottery winnings on Schedule X
## Prompt
- I won on a sports betting app.
- I won the Massachusetts lottery.
- Can I deduct my gambling losses in Massachusetts?
