# Alimony Received
## Description
Taxable alimony and separate maintenance, in the same amount as the federal return (U.S. Schedule 1, Line 2a). Full-year and part-year residents only.
## Notes
- Follows the federal rule: alimony under instruments executed after 2018 is not taxable to the recipient (and not deductible by the payer)
- Payer side: deductions/schedule-y/alimony-paid.md
