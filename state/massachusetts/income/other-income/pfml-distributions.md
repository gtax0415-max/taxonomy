# Paid Family and Medical Leave (PFML) Benefits
## Description
Benefits paid by the Massachusetts Department of Family and Medical Leave, reported on Schedule X, Line 5 from Form 1099-G, Box 1.
## 2026 change
The FY2026 supplemental budget (St. 2026, c. 101) reversed the employee/employer split for 2026:
- Family leave contribution: employer may withhold at most 40% from employees; employers with 25+ employees pay 60%
- Medical leave contribution: may be withheld entirely from employees
Following Revenue Ruling 2025-4:
- FAMILY leave benefits remain FULLY included in Massachusetts gross income
- MEDICAL leave benefits are excluded except to the extent attributable to employer-paid medical contributions — because employees now fund all of the medical contribution, they can generally EXCLUDE the full medical leave benefit
Rely on the amount DOR reports in Box 1 of the Form 1099-G.
## Related
- Excess PFML withholding is refunded on Form 1, Line 49 (not income)
## Questions
- Did you receive family leave, medical leave, or both?
- What does Box 1 of your PFML Form 1099-G show?
## Common Errors
- Reporting PFML benefits as wages or unemployment
- Including excludable medical leave benefits
## Prompt
- I took paid family leave after having a baby.
- I received PFML medical leave benefits.
