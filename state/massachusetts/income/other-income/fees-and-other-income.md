# Fees and Other 5.0% Income (Schedule X, Line 4)
## Description
Catch-all for 5.0% income not reported elsewhere on Form 1, Lines 3-8 or Schedule X, Lines 1-3. Not less than zero.
## Includes
- Fee income: jury duty, election workers, director's fees, executor/administrator compensation
- Commissions or tips not on a W-2
- Bartering income not on Schedule C
- Prizes and awards (quiz shows, drawings, contests) at fair market value; employer awards outside a qualified plan
- Forgiveness of debt, including mortgage forgiveness — check whether Massachusetts follows the federal exclusion (Code as of January 1, 2024)
- Pre-1996 installment sales classified as ordinary income in Massachusetts
- Income from illegal activities
- Union strike or lockout benefits included in federal wages Line 1h
- Other Massachusetts 5.0% income from U.S. Schedule 1, Line 9 not reported elsewhere
## Excludes
- Net operating losses (not allowed in Massachusetts)
- Federal and Massachusetts tax refunds
- Section 965 inclusions (Schedule B via Schedule FCI — income/interest-dividends/foreign-corporation-income.md)
## Related deductions
Jury duty pay turned over to an employer and similar items: Schedule Y, Line 9a (deductions/schedule-y/other-schedule-y-deductions.md).
## Questions
- Did you receive jury duty pay, director's fees, prizes, or a 1099-C?
## Common Errors
- Entering a negative amount
- Omitting cancellation of debt
## Prompt
- I got paid for jury duty.
- A credit card company forgave part of my debt.
