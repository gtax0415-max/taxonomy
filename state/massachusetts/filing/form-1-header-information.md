# Form 1 Header Information
## Identification
- Names as on the federal return / government ID; SSNs on every page; ITIN in the SSN box if you file federally with an ITIN. You must wait for an SSN or ITIN before filing
- Foreign address lines
## Amended-return ovals
Amended return, Federal amendment, Other jurisdiction change (with date), Amended return due to IRS BBA partnership audit — see filing/procedures/amended-returns.md.
## Status ovals
| Oval | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Veteran (OEF, OIF, Noble Eagle, Sinai Peninsula) | Honorably discharged, domiciled in Massachusetts six months before entry; DOR forwards to Veterans' Services and the National Guard to check benefit eligibility. No tax effect |
| Deceased (with date of death) | Final return; see filing/procedures/deceased-taxpayer-refund.md |
| Under age 18 | Under 18 as of January 1 of the filing year (for 2026: January 1, 2027) |
| Name change | Legal name changed since the last Massachusetts return; enclose SS card or government ID |
| Noncustodial parent | Parent of a minor child who does not live with the child (not if parental rights terminated) |
| Custodial parent who released exemption | Head of household who released a dependency exemption (Form 8332 or decree) |
| Filing Schedule TDS | Disclosure of inconsistent filing positions (TIR 06-5) — undisclosed inconsistency penalty equals the tax attributable to it |
| Filing Schedule FCI | Foreign corporation income, including GILTI |
| Reporting digital assets | Received, sold, exchanged or disposed of NFTs, cryptocurrency or stablecoins, or received Form 1099-DA |
## Lines a and b
- a. Total federal income (Form 1040, Line 9)
- b. Federal adjusted gross income (Form 1040, Line 11a)
Used for matching and for Schedule HC. Required — missing entries delay processing.
## State Election Campaign Fund
$1 per spouse; does not change tax or refund — see payments/voluntary-contributions.md.
## Questions
- Did you have any cryptocurrency or NFT transactions in 2026?
- Did your name change?
- Are you a noncustodial parent, or did you release a child's exemption?
## Common Errors
- Leaving lines a and b blank
- Missing the digital assets oval
