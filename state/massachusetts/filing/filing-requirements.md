# Filing Requirements and Due Dates
## Who must file
- RESIDENT: Massachusetts gross income over $8,000, from sources inside or outside Massachusetts → Form 1
- PART-YEAR RESIDENT: moved into or out of Massachusetts and gross income over $8,000 → Form 1-NR/PY
- NONRESIDENT: Massachusetts-source income more than the personal exemption × (Massachusetts income ÷ total income) → Form 1-NR/PY
- Military: Massachusetts domiciliaries remain residents wherever stationed; nonresident service members stationed in Massachusetts report only non-military Massachusetts income
- File even below $8,000 to claim the Earned Income Credit, Child and Family Tax Credit, Senior Circuit Breaker, or a withholding refund
## Residency
- Full-year resident: domiciled in Massachusetts, OR maintains a permanent place of abode in Massachusetts and spends more than 183 days there in the year
- Nonresident: not a resident, but has Massachusetts-source income
- Part-year resident: moved in or out during the year
- Both nonresident and part-year resident in the same year: Schedule R/NR
## Massachusetts gross income
Includes wages, pensions (non-exempt), IRA distributions after basis, alimony, business and pass-through income, rents and royalties, interest and dividends, unemployment, gambling, capital gains, debt forgiveness, taxable scholarships, PLUS out-of-state municipal bond interest and foreign earned income excluded federally.
Excludes U.S. obligation interest, contributory U.S./Massachusetts government pensions, Social Security, public welfare, VA disability, G.I. Bill, certain workers' compensation, gifts, life insurance, certain Holocaust survivor payments, and combat zone pay (to the federal extent).

## Extensions
- Automatic six months for all filers IF at least 80% of the tax ultimately due is paid by the original due date (TIR 16-10); otherwise the extension is invalid
- Extension worksheet: Form 1, Line 32 tax minus Lines 38-40 and 43-47
- Extension payments of $5,000 or more must be made electronically; under $5,000 may use Form M-4868 or pay online
- Extension payments are reported on Form 1, Line 41
## Fiscal year filers
Need Commissioner approval and Form 13; due the 15th day of the fourth month after year-end. Use the form for the calendar year in which the short year began.
## Unable to pay
Pay what you can with the return. For a bill under $10,000, apply for a small payment agreement through MassTaxConnect after the bill is issued.
## Questions
- Were you a resident all year, part of the year, or not at all?
- What was your gross income?
- Will you need an extension, and will 80% of the tax be paid by April 15, 2027?
## Common Errors
- Filing an extension without paying 80%
- A low-income family not filing and missing refundable credits
- A part-year resident filing Form 1
