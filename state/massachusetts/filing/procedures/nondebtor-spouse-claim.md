# Nondebtor Spouse Claim and Allocation for Refund Due (Form M-8379)
## Description
Lets a spouse who filed a joint Massachusetts return recover their share of a joint refund that DOR applied to the OTHER spouse's past-due Massachusetts income tax. Massachusetts counterpart of federal Form 8379 (injured spouse).
## Eligibility — all must be true
- Filed a joint Massachusetts return with an overpayment applied to the spouse's past-due Massachusetts income tax
- Had income
- Made tax payments through withholding or estimated payments
## Not eligible if
- The refund went to a past-due JOINT Massachusetts liability
- You made no payments for the year
- You yourself owe past-due Massachusetts tax
## Allocation (columns: nondebtor spouse / other spouse / joint as filed)
1. Total income (all sources)
2. Adjustments to income
3. Deductions
4. Exemptions
5. Credits (excluding the Limited Income Credit)
6. Taxes withheld (attach W-2s)
7. Tax payments (estimated, with return)
Fill in the oval if the refund should be issued in the nondebtor spouse's name only.
## Questions
- What debt was your joint refund applied to, and whose?
- What income, withholding and payments were yours?
## Common Errors
- Using M-8379 for child support or other-agency offsets
- Allocating payments without supporting W-2s
## Prompt
- My refund was taken for my spouse's old Massachusetts tax bill.
