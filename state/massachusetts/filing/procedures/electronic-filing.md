# Electronic Filing and Payment Forms
## Forms
| Form | Purpose |
|---|---|
| M-8453 | Individual Income Tax Declaration for Electronic Filing — the taxpayer's signature authorization for an e-filed return; kept with the preparer's records per the form's instructions |
| M-9325 | Electronic Filing Information Handout — given by the preparer to the taxpayer confirming the return was e-filed and explaining refund tracking |
| EFO | Personal Income Tax Declaration of Paper Filing — taxpayer's election to have the preparer file on paper despite the e-file mandate |
| PV | Massachusetts Income Tax Payment Voucher — accompanies a check for a balance due |
## E-file requirements
- Preparers who file more than 10 Massachusetts personal income tax returns must e-file (TIR 11-13), subject to the taxpayer opt-out on Form EFO
- Taxpayers subject to the 4% surtax must file and pay electronically
- Schedule E filers must use software-generated forms (TIRs 08-22, 16-9)
- Paper returns from software must carry a 2D barcode
- Free e-filing: MassTaxConnect (prior filers whose forms are supported) and free commercial software for qualifying taxpayers
## Payment options
MassTaxConnect, electronic funds withdrawal with e-filed returns, or check payable to Commonwealth of Massachusetts with Form PV. Payments of $5,000 or more with extensions must be electronic.

## Common Errors
- Paper filing by a preparer without Form EFO
- Surtax filer paying by check
