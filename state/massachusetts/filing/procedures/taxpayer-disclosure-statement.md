# Taxpayer Disclosure Statement (Schedule TDS)
## Description
Massachusetts schedule for making disclosures required by law or DOR guidance, and for explaining positions or federal-to-state differences. Use a SEPARATE Schedule TDS for each issue.
## Header fields
Taxpayer name and ID; tax year beginning and ending; type of return; schedule; line number; statement number.
## When it is used (examples from 2025 instructions)
- Explaining a Massachusetts excess business loss different from federal (Schedule X, Line 6)
- Disclosures under TIR 06-5 (disclosure of positions to avoid penalties), DOR Directive 12-7, and TIR 19-11 (foreign income)
- Explaining differences such as depreciation, Section 179, Section 163(j) and R&E adjustments arising from Massachusetts non-conformity
## Notes
- Adequate disclosure can protect against certain penalties; it does not change the tax
## Questions
- Is any Massachusetts amount on your return different from federal in a way the instructions ask you to explain?
## Common Errors
- Combining several issues on one Schedule TDS
- Omitting the schedule and line reference
## Prompt
- How do I explain a difference between my federal and Massachusetts amounts?
