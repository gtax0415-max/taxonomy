# Application for Relief from Joint Income Tax Liability (Form 84)
## Description
Massachusetts innocent spouse relief under G.L. c. 62C, § 84. Spouses on a joint return are jointly and severally liable; Form 84 asks DOR to relieve one spouse of liability for a substantial understatement attributable to the other spouse.
## Grounds (Line 4)
- Items omitted from gross income
- Erroneous claims of deduction, exemption, credit or basis
- Other
State the amount of relief sought, excluding penalties and interest.
## Timing
- After a Notice of Intent to Assess: within 30 days, with a written conference request
- After a Notice of Assessment: attach Form 84 to Form ABT, within the abatement time limits
## Non-requesting spouse (Form M-12508)
DOR sends the other (non-requesting) spouse Form M-12508, Massachusetts Questionnaire for Non-Requesting Spouse, so that spouse can participate and provide information about the tax years at issue.
## Notes
- DOR must notify the other spouse and will share the application, its attachments and the decision with them — but not the applicant's address, phone numbers or employer
- Filing does NOT stop collection unless and until relief is granted
- Relief applies only to the extent DOR specifies; relief obtained by fraud is void
- The form (Rev. 03/26) refers to "line 6" of Form ABT; the current Form ABT explanation is on Line 5 — write "see attached Form 84" in the explanation
## Required Information
- Tax year(s) of the liability
- Applicant and spouse names, SSNs, addresses
- NIA or NOA copies
- Federal Form 8857 and determination
- Statement of facts
## Questions
- Which tax year is the liability for?
- Have you asked the IRS for innocent spouse relief?
- Did you receive an NIA or an NOA?
- What item caused the understatement, and did you know about it?
## Common Errors
- Entering the refund year instead of the liability year
- Missing the 30-day NIA window
- Expecting collection to stop on filing
## Prompt
- My ex-husband underreported income on our joint return.
