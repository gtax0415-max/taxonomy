# Application for Abatement (Form ABT)
## Description
The Massachusetts form for asking DOR to reduce or cancel a tax, penalty or assessment. One form per tax type, location and account.
## Three prerequisites for a valid application
1. The required return was filed (unless claiming no return is required or disputing a responsible-person determination)
2. Filed within the time limits
3. Fully substantiated with documentation
## Time limits (G.L. c. 62C, § 37) — generally the latest of
- 3 years from the date the return was filed (or from the due date, if filed early)
- 2 years from the date the tax was assessed or deemed assessed
- 1 year from the date the tax was paid
- 2 years from a responsible-person determination
- Any agreed extension under G.L. c. 62C, § 27
## Consent to act after six months
Filing gives DOR consent to act after six months. If you refuse consent (fill in the oval), the application is DEEMED DENIED at six months, which starts the clock for appeal to the Appellate Tax Board.
## Notes
- Fill in the hearing oval to request a hearing with the Office of Appeals
- Collection is generally suspended during the appeal, but interest and penalties keep accruing; paying first stops accrual and any later abatement is refunded with interest
- DOR may abate PENALTIES for reasonable cause but generally cannot abate INTEREST
- Returns that must be e-filed require the abatement to be e-filed
- Power of Attorney section can be used instead of a separate M-2848
- Motor vehicle sales tax: law requires the higher of NADA clean trade-in value or price paid; vehicle condition cannot be considered
## Required Information
- Letter ID and Account ID (no dashes)
- Tax type, periods and amounts in dispute
- Explanation of the issues with statutory references and documentation
## Questions
- What notice did you receive, and what are the Letter ID and Account ID?
- Are you disputing DOR's assessment, or correcting your own return?
- When was the return filed, the tax assessed, and the tax paid?
## Common Errors
- Using Form ABT to amend a return
- Filing without documentation
- Missing the time limits
- Combining several tax types on one form
## Prompt
- I got a bill from Massachusetts DOR that I think is wrong.
- Can Massachusetts waive my late-payment penalty?
