# Statement of Person Claiming Refund Due a Deceased Taxpayer (Form M-1310)
## Description
Used to claim a Massachusetts income tax refund owed to a person who has died. Massachusetts counterpart of federal Form 1310.
## Claimant types
- a. Surviving spouse requesting reissuance of a refund check
- b. Personal representative — attach the court certificate of appointment
- c. Other claimant for the estate — complete Schedule A and attach a death certificate or other proof of death (e.g., Department of Defense notice)
## Schedule A (claimant type c)
1. Did the decedent leave a will?
2a/2b. Has a personal representative been appointed, or will one be? (If yes, the representative should claim)
3. Will the claimant distribute the refund under the law of the decedent's state of domicile? If not checked, DOR withholds payment until authority is shown
## Related
- The final return covers income up to the date of death; fill in the deceased oval and date of death on Form 1
- Health care mandate ends the last day of the last full month the taxpayer was alive (Schedule HC)
## Questions
- What is your relationship to the decedent?
- Has a personal representative been appointed?
## Common Errors
- An heir filing type c when a personal representative exists
- Missing proof of death or court certificate
## Prompt
- My mother died and she is owed a Massachusetts refund.
