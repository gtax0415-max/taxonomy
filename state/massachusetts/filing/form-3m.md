# Form 3M — Income Tax Return for Clubs and Other Organizations Not Engaged in Business for Profit
## Description
Separate Massachusetts return for clubs and similar nonprofit organizations (not individuals) that have taxable income, such as investment income. Listed among DOR's 2025 personal income tax forms; included here so the taxonomy is complete, but it is not part of an individual's Form 1.
## Notes
- Form M-2210 applies to Form 3M filers (no credits line; enter prior-year liability on Line 5)
- Individuals do not file Form 3M
