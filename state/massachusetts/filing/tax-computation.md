## Optional 5.85% rate
Taxpayers may voluntarily pay 5.85% instead of 5.0% on 5.0% income and long-term gains (not on 8.5% or 12% income). The extra goes to the Commonwealth; it is a voluntary election.
## Whole-dollar rounding
Entries must be whole dollars ($1.00-$1.49 → $1; $1.50-$1.99 → $2). Worksheets may round before or after adding, applied consistently.
