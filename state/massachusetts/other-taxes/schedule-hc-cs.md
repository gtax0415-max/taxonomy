# Schedule HC-CS — Health Care Information Continuation Sheet
## Description
Overflow sheet for Schedule HC when the taxpayer or spouse had MCC coverage from more than two insurance companies or administrators during the year (e.g., job changes). Captures insurer name and subscriber number for the third and fourth insurers per person.
## Structure
- Part A — Your health insurance: insurers 3 and 4, with subscriber number from Form MA 1099-HC (or the card ID number if no form)
- Part B — Spouse's health insurance: same, completed even if covered under the same plans
## Notes
- Only needed if Schedule HC Line 3 is Full-year or Part-year MCC
- Available on DOR's website; enclose with Schedule HC
- Main schedule rules: other-taxes/health-care-penalty.md
## Questions
- How many different health insurers did you (and your spouse) have during 2026?
## Common Errors
- Listing older insurers on Schedule HC and newer ones on HC-CS (reverse it)
- Skipping Part B because the spouse was on the same plan
## Prompt
- I changed jobs twice and had three health plans this year.
