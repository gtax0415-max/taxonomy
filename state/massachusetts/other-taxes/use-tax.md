# Use Tax on Internet, Mail-Order and Out-of-State Purchases (Line 34)
## Description
Massachusetts use tax at 6.25% on taxable tangible personal property bought without paying Massachusetts sales tax — online, by catalog, or out of state — and brought into Massachusetts. Reported on the income tax return.
## Safe harbor (items under $1,000 each)
Report an estimate based on Massachusetts AGI (Line 27 worksheet, Line 7) without keeping receipts; DOR will not assess more on audit.
| Massachusetts AGI | Use tax |
|---|---|
| $0 - $25,000 | $0 |
| $25,001 - $40,000 | $20 |
| $40,001 - $60,000 | $31 |
| $60,001 - $80,000 | $44 |
| $80,001 - $100,000 | $56 |
| Over $100,000 | AGI × 0.000625 |
(2025 table; confirm on the 2026 Form 1 instructions.)
## Worksheet
1. Total purchases subject to use tax
2. × 6.25%
3. Credit for sales/use tax paid to another state (lesser of tax paid or 6.25% per item)
4. Line 2 − Line 3 → Line 34
## Commonly affected items
Electronics, computers, software, appliances, furniture, jewelry, books, video games, carpets, artwork, antiques.
## Notes
- Most large online retailers now collect Massachusetts sales tax; use tax applies only where none was charged
- Motor vehicles, boats and aircraft are handled separately (registration), not on Line 34
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online or out of state without being charged Massachusetts sales tax?
- Any single item of $1,000 or more?
## Common Errors
- Using the safe harbor for a $1,000+ item
- Charging use tax on exempt clothing or food
## Prompt
- Do I owe Massachusetts use tax on things I bought online?
