# Form MA 1099-HC (Massachusetts Health Care Coverage)
## Description
Information statement from a Massachusetts health insurer or plan administrator showing, for the subscriber and each covered dependent, whether coverage met Massachusetts MINIMUM CREDITABLE COVERAGE (MCC) for the full year or which months it did. It is the source document for Schedule HC. The state counterpart of federal Forms 1095-B and 1095-C — but it reports MCC, not federal minimum essential coverage.
## Fields
1. Insurance company or administrator name
2. Insurer / administrator FID number
3. Subscriber name; 4. date of birth; 5. subscriber number
6-9. Subscriber address
- Full-year MCC? Yes / No; if No, months with MCC (January through December)
- Corrected indicator
- Dependents (a-d): name, date of birth, subscriber number, full-year MCC, months
## Timing
Insurers must issue Form MA 1099-HC by January 31 of the following year (for 2026 coverage: January 31, 2027).
## Who issues it
Massachusetts-licensed carriers and administrators, including ConnectorCare and other Health Connector plans, and MassHealth. Medicare, U.S. military and other government coverage holders generally do not need it — any month of those coverages ends the Schedule HC inquiry with no penalty.
## Notes
- A plan without an MA 1099-HC (e.g., out-of-state employer plan) may still meet MCC; check the MCC standards in other-taxes/health-care-penalty.md
- Attach Forms MA 1099-HC to Schedule HC
- MassHealth members missing a form call MassHealth; ConnectorCare members call their health plan
## Questions
- Did you receive a Form MA 1099-HC for every plan you had in 2026?
- Does it show full-year MCC? If not, which months?
- Were you covered by more than two insurers?
## Common Errors
- Using a federal 1095 instead of the MA 1099-HC
- Reporting a non-MCC plan as MCC
- Missing a corrected form
