# 4% Surtax ("Fair Share" / Millionaires Tax)
## Description
Additional 4% tax on the portion of annual taxable income above an inflation-indexed threshold, approved by voters in 2022 (Massachusetts Constitution, Article XLIV as amended). Applies to residents on all taxable income and to nonresidents on Massachusetts-source taxable income.
## Thresholds
| Tax year | Threshold |
|---|---|
| 2023 | $1,000,000 |
| 2024 | $1,053,750 |
| 2025 | $1,083,150 |
| 2026 | $1,107,750 |
## Notes
- Applies to ALL classes of taxable income combined — wages, interest, dividends, short-term and long-term gains
- The threshold is per return, not per spouse. Since 2024, couples filing jointly federally must file jointly in Massachusetts
- Surtax filers must file and pay electronically
- NEW for 2026: pass-through entities may elect an additional 4% entity-level excise (G.L. c. 63E) on members' income above the threshold — see credits/business/pass-through-entity-excise-credit.md
- Estimated tax: the surtax must be included in estimated payments
## Questions
- Will your 2026 taxable income exceed $1,107,750, including a one-time sale?
- Can income be spread across years (e.g., installment sale)?
## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 threshold
- Forgetting the surtax in estimated payments for a large one-time gain
