# Additional Tax on Installment Sales (Line 26)
## Description
An interest charge, added to tax, on the Massachusetts tax deferred by reporting large gains on the installment method. Follows the federal IRC 453A and 453(l) rules to the extent practicable.
## When it applies
1. Non-dealer sales over $150,000 where the face amount of such installment obligations arising during the year and outstanding at year-end exceeds $5 million
2. Dealer sales of timeshare rights (six weeks or less), specified campground rights, or residential lots with no dealer improvement obligation
## Notes
- No Tax Status does not eliminate Line 26; it also is excluded from the Limited Income Credit computation
- Massachusetts installment reporting of gains of $1 million or more requires DOR approval in advance (TIR 04-28)
- Installment sales from 1996 through 2002 → Schedule D-IS (income/capital-gains/schedule-d-is.md)
## Questions
- Did you sell property on the installment method for more than $150,000?
- Are your outstanding installment obligations over $5 million?
## Common Errors
- Omitting the Massachusetts interest charge when one applies federally
- Reporting a $1 million+ installment sale without DOR approval
## Prompt
- I sold my business on an installment note.
