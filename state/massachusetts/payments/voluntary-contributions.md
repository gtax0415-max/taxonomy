# Voluntary Contributions (Line 33 and State Election Campaign Fund)
## Line 33 funds (any amount; ADDED to tax)
| Line | Fund | Administered by |
|---|---|---|
| 33a | Endangered Wildlife Conservation (Natural Heritage and Endangered Species Fund) | Division of Fisheries and Wildlife |
| 33b | Organ Transplant Fund | Department of Public Health |
| 33c | Massachusetts Public Health HIV and Hepatitis Fund | Department of Public Health |
| 33d | Massachusetts U.S. Olympic Fund | Supports U.S. Olympic and Paralympic teams |
| 33e | Massachusetts Military Family Relief Fund | Friends of Massachusetts National Guard and Reserve Families |
| 33f | Homeless Animal Prevention and Care Fund | Department of Agricultural Resources |
## State Election Campaign Fund
$1 for the taxpayer and $1 for the spouse (page 1). Provides limited public financing for statewide campaigns. Does not change tax or reduce refund.
## Notes
- Line 33 contributions may be federally deductible as charitable contributions in the year paid (and therefore flow to the Massachusetts charitable deduction the following year)
