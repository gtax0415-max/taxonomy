# Estimated Tax and Extension Payments (Lines 39-41)
## Line 39 — Prior-year overpayment applied
The exact amount of the 2025 overpayment applied to 2026 estimated tax (2025 Form 1, Line 53, or Form 1-NR/PY, Line 56). Not any amount refunded.
## Line 40 — 2026 estimated payments
Total estimated payments for 2026, including a January 15, 2027 payment. Pay through MassTaxConnect or with Form 1-ES.
Required if you expect more than $400 of Massachusetts tax on income not covered by withholding — for example: wages from an employer not withholding Massachusetts tax, interest and dividends (including Massachusetts bank interest), capital gains, business income, estate or trust income not taxed directly, certain pensions, IRA distributions without withholding, rental income, alimony, unemployment with no withholding, SIMPLE distributions, illegal income.
Alternative: increase wage withholding on Form M-4.
## 2026 due dates
April 15, 2026; June 15, 2026; September 15, 2026; January 15, 2027.
## Safe harbors (see payments/underpayment-penalty.md)
Pay the smaller of 80% of 2026 tax or 100% of 2025 tax (12-month return). 4% surtax filers must include the surtax and pay electronically.
## Line 41 — Extension payments
Amount paid with Form M-4868 or electronically on or before April 15, 2027. Payments of $5,000 or more must be electronic.
## Questions
- Did you apply a 2025 overpayment to 2026?
- What estimated payments did you make, and when?
- Did you pay with an extension?
## Common Errors
- Entering the prior-year overpayment on Line 40 (double counting)
- Omitting the January payment
