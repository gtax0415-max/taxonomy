# Refund, Overpayment Applied, and Tax Due (Lines 51-55)
## Lines
- 51 Total payments: Lines 38-42 plus 48-50
- 52 Overpayment: Line 51 − Line 37
- 53 Amount applied to 2027 estimated tax (for the 2026 return). Once elected it cannot be refunded after the due date, applied to additional 2026 tax, or changed on an amended return
- 54 Refund: Line 52 − Line 53. Direct deposit needs a 9-digit routing number (01-12 or 21-32) and account number; otherwise a check is mailed
- 55 Tax due: Line 37 − Line 51, plus interest, penalty and the M-2210 amount
## Mailing addresses (2025 form)
- Refund or no payment: Massachusetts DOR, PO Box 7000, Boston, MA 02204
- With payment: Massachusetts DOR, PO Box 7003, Boston, MA 02204
## Interest and penalties
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Interest on unpaid tax | Federal short-term rate + 4%, compounded daily (2026: 8% / 7% / 8% / 8% by quarter) |
| Late payment penalty | 1% per month (or part), up to 25% |
| Failure to file penalty | 1% per month (or part) of tax due, up to 25% |
| Dishonored payment | Lesser of $30 or the payment amount |
| Underpayment of estimated tax | Form M-2210 — payments/underpayment-penalty.md |
| Inconsistent filing position (undisclosed) | Tax attributable to the inconsistency — filing/procedures/taxpayer-disclosure-statement.md |
DOR can abate penalties for reasonable cause (Form ABT) but generally not interest.
## Notes
- A Massachusetts refund may be federally taxable if state tax was itemized federally
- Refunds can be offset for Massachusetts tax debts, other agency debts or child support — see filing/procedures/nondebtor-spouse-claim.md
- Can't pay in full: file on time anyway; for bills under $10,000, request a payment agreement in MassTaxConnect
## Questions
- Do you want a refund or to apply it to next year?
- Do you want direct deposit?
- Can you pay the balance in full?
## Common Errors
- Wrong routing or account number
- Applying an overpayment forward and later trying to reverse it
