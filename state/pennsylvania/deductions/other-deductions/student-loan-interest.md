# Student Loan Interest Deduction
## Description
CODE S. Up to $2,500 of student loan interest, deductible on PA Schedule O. NEW RELATIVE TO OLDER GUIDANCE — the Department's 2018 Deductions and Credits guide lists student loan interest as "No provision," and that is now wrong.
## Amount
UP TO $2,500. The instructions state the cap without a per-spouse qualifier at this line; the DFO-02 What's New describes it as "up to $2,500 PER FILER." On a joint return with two borrowers, confirm against the current PA Schedule O instructions before claiming more than $2,500 in total.
## What counts as student loan interest
Interest PAID DURING THE TAXABLE YEAR on a qualified student loan, INCLUDING BOTH REQUIRED AND VOLUNTARY INTEREST PAYMENTS.
The voluntary inclusion matters. A borrower who pays extra interest ahead of schedule, or pays interest during a deferment when none is required, may deduct it. Those payments often do not appear on Form 1098-E, so servicer records are needed.
## Qualifying institution
The loan must have been to attend a COLLEGE, UNIVERSITY, VOCATIONAL SCHOOL, OR OTHER POSTSECONDARY EDUCATIONAL INSTITUTION that is ELIGIBLE TO PARTICIPATE IN A STUDENT AID PROGRAM ADMINISTERED BY THE UNITED STATES DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION.
That is the federal eligibility standard. A loan for a non-participating institution, or for K-12 education, does not qualify.
## Documentation
You MAY BE ASKED TO PROVIDE A COPY OF FORM 1098-E. It is not required with the return but should be retained, along with servicer records for any voluntary interest not reflected on the 1098-E.
## The federal comparison
The federal student loan interest deduction is ABOVE THE LINE and reduces federal AGI. Pennsylvania does not start from federal AGI, so the federal deduction does not flow through — PA grants its own, separately claimed, with its own $2,500 cap.
A taxpayer phased out of the federal deduction by income should still check the PA deduction, since the instructions state no PA income phase-out at this line. Confirm against the Schedule O instructions.
## 2026
$2,500, unchanged. The cap is statutory and not indexed.
## Required Information
- Form 1098-E from each servicer
- Servicer records for voluntary interest paid
- Total interest paid during the calendar year
- Confirmation the institution participates in US Department of Education student aid programs
## Questions
- How much student loan interest did you pay during the year?
- Did you make any voluntary interest payments beyond what was required?
- Was the loan for a postsecondary institution eligible for federal student aid?
- Do you have Form 1098-E?
- If married filing jointly, do both spouses have qualifying loans?
## Common Errors
- Assuming PA does not allow it, per the outdated 2018 guide
- Omitting voluntary interest payments not shown on Form 1098-E
- Claiming interest on a loan for a non-participating or K-12 institution
- Claiming more than $2,500 without confirming the per-filer treatment
- Assuming the federal deduction carries over automatically; PA requires its own claim on Schedule O
