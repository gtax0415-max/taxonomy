# Other Deductions (PA-40 Line 10, Schedule O)
## Description
The five deductions Pennsylvania law allows against income, all claimed on PA SCHEDULE O and totalled onto PA-40 LINE 10 with a CODE in the block next to the line description.

## The six codes
| Code | Deduction |
|---|---|
| M | Medical Savings Account contribution deduction |
| H | Health Savings Account contribution deduction |
| S | Student Loan Interest deduction |
| T | Tuition Account Program contribution deduction |
| A | Pennsylvania ABLE Savings Account Program contributions |
| C | COMBINED deduction from two or more contribution deductions |
Five deductions, six codes. USE CODE C whenever two or more apply — do not attempt to enter multiple letters.
## Schedule O is mandatory
You MUST complete PA SCHEDULE O and include the information required for each contribution. The code alone on line 10 is not enough.
## The cap, and the joint-return trap
LINE 10 CANNOT EXCEED LINE 9. Deductions cannot take taxable income below zero, cannot create a loss, and do not carry forward.
ON A JOINT RETURN, line 10 also cannot exceed the Total PA Taxable Income that WOULD HAVE BEEN CALCULATED for that taxpayer or spouse HAD THEY FILED SEPARATELY. A spouse with little or no income cannot have their deduction absorbed by the other spouse's income. Compute the hypothetical separate figure before claiming.
## Two things that are NOT deductible here
- MEDICAL INSURANCE PREMIUMS and ITEMIZED MEDICAL EXPENSES from federal Schedule A. The instructions carry this caution explicitly. Line 10 covers MSA and HSA CONTRIBUTIONS, not medical spending
- UNREIMBURSED EMPLOYEE BUSINESS EXPENSES. Those go on PA SCHEDULE UE against the compensation class at line 1b, not here
## No rollovers, no beneficiary changes
No deduction is allowed for:
- A ROLLOVER from one IRC 529 plan into another
- CHANGING BENEFICIARIES within an IRC 529 account
- A ROLLOVER from one IRC 529A plan into another
- CHANGING BENEFICIARIES within an IRC 529A account
Only NEW CONTRIBUTIONS qualify. Moving existing money does not.
