# Use Tax (PA-40 Line 25)
## Description
The counterpart of sales tax. When you buy items or services subject to sales tax and THE SELLER DOES NOT CHARGE AND COLLECT IT, YOU ARE PERSONALLY RESPONSIBLE for remitting the use tax directly to the PA Department of Revenue.
Line 25 lets an individual report and pay it on the income tax return instead of filing separately.
## IT INCREASES WHAT YOU OWE
LINE 26 TAX DUE = LINE 12 PLUS LINE 25, LESS LINE 24.
Use tax is ADDED to the income tax. It is not a deduction, not a credit, and not subtracted anywhere. A taxpayer expecting a refund can be pushed into a balance due by it.
## The rate is the SALES tax rate, not 3.07%
| Where the item is purchased or used | Rate |
|---|---|
| Pennsylvania generally | 6% state |
| ALLEGHENY COUNTY | 6% + 1% local = 7% |
| PHILADELPHIA | 6% + 2% local = 8% |
This is the only place on the PA-40 where a rate other than 3.07% applies, and the only place the taxpayer's COUNTY changes the arithmetic.
## When use tax is owed
Taxable purchases made:
- OVER THE INTERNET
- Through TOLL-FREE NUMBERS
- From MAIL ORDER CATALOGS
- From OUT-OF-STATE LOCATIONS
- Any other occasion where sales tax was not charged and collected by the seller
Examples of taxable items given by the Department: antiques, paintings, appliances, books, DIGITAL PRODUCTS, stationery, computers, exercise equipment, and similar tangible goods.
## WHAT CANNOT GO ON LINE 25
ONLY INDIVIDUAL CONSUMERS may report use tax on Line 25. This method CANNOT be used for:
- Purchases pursuant to a DOR NOTICE OF POTENTIAL USE TAX DUE
- MOTOR VEHICLES
- WATERCRAFT AND BOATS
- ATVs
- SNOWMOBILES
- IMPORTS TO THE UNITED STATES VIA US CUSTOMS
- CIGARETTES
- BUSINESS PURCHASES
THE VEHICLE EXCLUSION IS THE ONE THAT CATCHES PEOPLE. Someone who buys a car out of state does NOT report the use tax here — it is handled at registration or through a Department notice. Entering it on Line 25 is wrong in both directions: it pays the wrong authority and leaves the vehicle liability open.
BUSINESS PURCHASES likewise belong on a sales and use tax return, not the PA-40.
## No lookup chart
PENNSYLVANIA PROVIDES NO ESTIMATED USE TAX TABLE. The amount must be computed from ACTUAL PURCHASES. There is no safe-harbour figure a filer can enter without tracking what they bought.
LINE 25 MAY BE LEFT BLANK if nothing is owed.
## 2026
No change announced. The 6% state rate and the Allegheny and Philadelphia local additions are statutory.
## Required Information
- Each untaxed purchase, with amount and date
- Where the item was delivered or used, for the local rate
- Confirmation that no item is a vehicle, watercraft, ATV, snowmobile, cigarette, customs import, or business purchase
- Any DOR Notice of Potential Use Tax Due, which is handled separately
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online, by catalog, or out of state without paying PA sales tax?
- Were any purchases delivered to or used in Allegheny County or Philadelphia?
- Did you buy a vehicle, boat, ATV, or snowmobile? Those cannot go on Line 25
- Were any purchases for a business?
- Did you receive a DOR notice about use tax?
## Common Errors
- Reporting vehicle, boat, ATV, or snowmobile use tax on Line 25
- Reporting business purchases on Line 25 instead of a sales and use tax return
- Using the 3.07% income tax rate instead of the 6, 7, or 8 percent sales tax rate
- Ignoring the Allegheny and Philadelphia local additions
- Paying a DOR Notice of Potential Use Tax Due through Line 25
- Treating use tax as a deduction rather than an addition to tax due
