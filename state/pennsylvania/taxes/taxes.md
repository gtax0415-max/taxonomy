# Pennsylvania Taxes
## Description
Taxes computed ON the PA-40 beyond the basic income tax. PA's own tax is a FLAT 3.07% with no brackets, no AMT, and no surtaxes — so this domain is small. The one substantive item is USE TAX.
## Files in this folder
- use-tax.md — PA-40 LINE 25
## Where taxes sit on the PA-40
```
Line 11   Adjusted PA taxable income
Line 12   PA TAX LIABILITY, line 11 x 3.07%
Line 13   Total PA tax withheld
Lines 21-23  Credits
Line 24   TOTAL PAYMENTS AND CREDITS
Line 25   USE TAX
Line 26   TAX DUE, if Line 12 plus Line 25 exceeds Line 24
Line 27   PENALTIES AND INTEREST, with code L, E, B, or X
```
USE TAX IS ADDED TO THE INCOME TAX at line 26, not subtracted. It increases what you owe or reduces the refund.
## The 2026 rate
3.07%, unchanged. Flat, statutory, not indexed. PA has had the same rate since 2004.
## What Pennsylvania does NOT have
| Tax | Federal | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|---|
| Alternative minimum tax | Yes | NO |
| Net investment income tax | Yes federally | NO |
| Additional Medicare tax | Yes federally | NO |
| Self-employment tax | Yes federally | NO |
| Graduated brackets | Yes | NO, flat 3.07% |
| Early distribution penalty | 10% federally | NO |
| RMD shortfall excise tax | 25% federally | NO |
PA's UNIFORMITY CLAUSE in the state constitution requires a single rate on a class of income, which is why no surtax or graduated structure exists.
## Local taxes are OUTSIDE the PA-40
PA has LOCAL EARNED INCOME TAX collected by municipalities and school districts, and the PHILADELPHIA WAGE TAX. NEITHER APPEARS ON THE PA-40, and neither is reported to the Commonwealth. Philadelphia residents file with the City.
No resident credit is available for tax paid to a political subdivision of ANOTHER state.
## Penalties and interest, line 27
Enter penalties and interest for late filing or late payment. If including Form REV-1630 or REV-1630A for estimated underpayment, fill the oval and enter a code:
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L | Late payment, failure to file, or late filing penalty and interest |
| E | Estimated underpayment penalty only |
| B | Both |
| X | No estimated underpayment penalty due |
