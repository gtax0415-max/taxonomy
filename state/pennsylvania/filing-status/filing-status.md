# Pennsylvania Filing Status and Residency
## Description
The ovals at the top of the PA-40. Two separate determinations — RESIDENCY STATUS and FILING STATUS — plus several special-condition ovals that change how the return is processed.
## Files in this folder
- residency-status.md — the R, N, and P ovals
- filing-status-selection.md — the S, J, M, F, and D ovals
- special-ovals.md — Farmers, Final Return, Amended Return, Extension, and date of death
## The two determinations
| Oval | Values |
|---|---|
| RESIDENCY STATUS | R Resident · N Nonresident · P Part-Year Resident, with from and to dates |
| FILING STATUS | S Single · J Married Filing Jointly · M Married Filing Separately · F Final Return · D Deceased |
Note that F and D sit in the FILING STATUS block on the PA-40 even though they describe a circumstance rather than a marital status.
## PA has only three marital filing statuses
| Federal | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|
| Single | S Single |
| Married filing jointly | J Married, Filing Jointly |
| Married filing separately | M Married, Filing Separately |
| HEAD OF HOUSEHOLD | NO EQUIVALENT |
| QUALIFYING SURVIVING SPOUSE | NO EQUIVALENT |
A federal HOH or QSS filer uses SINGLE for Pennsylvania. PA grants no rate benefit, no larger deduction, and no exemption for either status, because it has no rate schedule, no standard deduction, and no exemptions to adjust.
## Filing jointly gives no advantage
PA LAW PROVIDES NO ADVANTAGE TO FILING A JOINT RETURN. It is permitted FOR CONVENIENCE ONLY. Spouses may not offset each other's income or losses, may not use each other's expenses, and are computed separately in every class. See income/class-system-and-losses.md.
## Where residency matters most
| Status | Taxed on |
|---|---|
| RESIDENT | ALL income from every source |
| NONRESIDENT | PA-SOURCE income only |
| PART-YEAR RESIDENT | All income received while a PA resident, plus PA-source income for the rest |
## Also collected at the top of the return
- SCHOOL DISTRICT NAME and SCHOOL CODE where you lived on December 31
- Your occupation and your spouse's occupation
- Daytime telephone number
- Country code, for a foreign address
The school district entry is used for local tax administration, not for the state computation.

## Questions
- Were you a PA resident for the whole year, part of the year, or not at all?
- What is your marital status on December 31?
- Did you or your spouse die during the year?
- Is at least two-thirds of your gross income from farming?
