# Residency Status (R, N, or P)
## Description
FILL IN ONLY ONE OVAL. This single mark determines whether Pennsylvania taxes everything you earned or only what you earned here.
## The three statuses
| Oval | Status | PA taxes |
|---|---|---|
| R | PENNSYLVANIA RESIDENT | ALL income from every source, wherever earned |
| N | NONRESIDENT | PA-SOURCE income only |
| P | PART-YEAR RESIDENT | All income received while a PA resident, plus PA-source income for the nonresident part |
The P oval requires FROM and TO DATES in the format ___/___/2025 to ___/___/2025.
## Residency is domicile plus a statutory test
A PA RESIDENT is someone DOMICILED in Pennsylvania, or someone who MAINTAINS A PERMANENT PLACE OF ABODE in PA and SPENDS MORE THAN 183 DAYS here.
DOMICILE is the place you intend to return to and remain. You may have several residences but ONLY ONE DOMICILE, and it does not change until you establish a new one elsewhere.
## Part-year residents are taxed by PERIOD, not by percentage
PA taxes a part-year resident on income RECEIVED WHILE A PA RESIDENT, plus PA-source income from the nonresident period.
There is no income-percentage method and no as-if-full-year computation. The income is simply divided by residency period, which makes the MOVE DATE the critical fact and makes records fixing it essential.
## PA-source income for a nonresident
- COMPENSATION for services performed in PA
- Income from a BUSINESS carried on in PA
- Income from REAL OR TANGIBLE PROPERTY located in PA
- Gain from the disposition of PA real property
- GAMBLING AND LOTTERY WINNINGS from PA sources
- PA S corporation and partnership income apportioned to PA
NOT PA-source for a nonresident: interest, dividends, and most intangible income.
## The six reciprocal states
INDIANA · MARYLAND · NEW JERSEY · OHIO · VIRGINIA · WEST VIRGINIA
A resident of one of these states whose PA employer withheld PA tax reports ZERO compensation on line 1a and the withholding on line 13, attaching the W-2, a copy of the home-state resident return without schedules, and a statement. This is a refund claim, not an income tax computation.
Reciprocity covers COMPENSATION ONLY. A reciprocal-state resident with PA business, rental, or gambling income files a normal nonresident return for that income.
## The federal contrast
THE FEDERAL RETURN HAS NO RESIDENCY CONCEPT for a citizen or resident alien — worldwide income is taxed regardless of where you live. Residency is purely a state determination with no federal counterpart and no federal figure to carry over.
## Nonresidents and Schedule A
NONRESIDENT TAXPAYERS ARE NOT REQUIRED TO COMPLETE PA SCHEDULE A. Interest is not PA-source income for a nonresident.
## Required Information
- Domicile, and the date it changed if it did
- Move-in and move-out dates with documentation
- Days present in PA, if the statutory test is in play
- Income by source and by residency period
- The other state's return, for a reciprocal-state refund claim
## Questions
- Where was your permanent home, and did it change during the year?
- On what dates did PA residency begin and end?
- Do you have PA-source income while living elsewhere?
- Are you a resident of one of the six reciprocal states?
## Common Errors
- Filling more than one residency oval
- Omitting the from and to dates on a part-year return
- Applying an income-percentage method; PA taxes by period
- A reciprocal-state resident reporting PA compensation instead of claiming a refund
- A nonresident completing PA Schedule A
## Prompt
- I moved to Pennsylvania in June.
- I live in New Jersey and work in Philadelphia.
- I own a rental in PA but live in Florida.
