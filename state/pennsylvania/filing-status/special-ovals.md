# Special Ovals: Amended, Extension, Farmers, Final, Deceased
## Description
Five ovals at the top of the PA-40 that change how the return is processed. Each is easy to overlook and each has a consequence.
## AMENDED RETURN
Fill the oval AND include SCHEDULE PA-40 X. Section III on page 2 of the PA-40 X must EXPLAIN every increase or decrease.
An amended return also requires an AMENDED VERSION OF ANY SCHEDULE THAT CHANGED — Schedule A, B, SP, and so on — each filed with the PA-40 X rather than alone.
An amended PA Schedule SP is required if any information in Sections I, II, or III changed, not only if the credit amount changed.
## EXTENSION
Fill the oval if you filed for an extension.
AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO FILE IS NOT AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO PAY. Tax owed is due April 15, 2026 regardless, and any 2025 EXTENSION PAYMENT made is reported at LINE 16.
## FARMERS
Fill this oval if AT LEAST TWO-THIRDS OF YOUR GROSS INCOME IS FROM FARMING.
The consequence is in the estimated tax rules: a qualifying farmer has a single estimated payment deadline rather than four quarterly ones, and uses FORM REV-1630A rather than REV-1630 to compute any underpayment penalty at line 27.
The two-thirds test is on GROSS income, not net, and is measured for the tax year.
## F — FINAL RETURN
Fill this oval AND INDICATE THE REASON. A final return signals that no further PA-40 will be filed — typically on death, or on permanently leaving Pennsylvania.
## D — DECEASED
Fill this oval and enter the DATE OF DEATH in the block for the taxpayer, the spouse, or both. The PA-40 has separate date-of-death fields for each.
CONSEQUENCE FOR TAX FORGIVENESS: a deceased taxpayer's ELIGIBILITY INCOME MUST BE ANNUALIZED, because the Schedule SP tables assume a full year of income. Divide realized income by days or months lived and multiply by 365 or 12. One-time transactions such as a stock or property sale need NOT be annualized. Describe the method on Schedule SP.
A SURVIVING SPOUSE FILING SEPARATELY must NOT include income in respect of a decedent, income belonging on the estate return, or any of the decedent's income including the decedent's half of joint income received before death.
Schedule SP also has its own DECEASED status oval at line 19a, separate from this one.
## E-FILE OPT OUT
A signature-block oval, not a status oval. Relevant where a paid preparer is subject to the e-file mandate and the taxpayer elects paper.
## The federal contrast
The federal return handles all of these differently: amended returns use Form 1040-X as a standalone filing, the farmer rule is in the estimated tax regulations rather than on the face of the return, and there is no "final return" concept for an individual. PA puts all of them on the first page.
## Required Information
- Date of death for the taxpayer and spouse separately
- Gross income by source, for the farming test
- The originally filed return, for an amendment
- Extension confirmation and any payment made
## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die during the year?
- Is at least two-thirds of your gross income from farming?
- Is this an amended return, and do you have Schedule PA-40 X?
- Did you file an extension and make a payment?
## Common Errors
- Filing an amended return without Schedule PA-40 X or without the Section III explanation
- Amending the PA-40 without amending the schedules that changed
- Treating an extension to file as an extension to pay
- Missing the farmer oval and being assessed a quarterly underpayment penalty
- Failing to annualize a decedent's eligibility income for Tax Forgiveness
- A surviving spouse including the decedent's income on a separate return
## Prompt
- My husband died in March.
- We farm and our income is seasonal.
- I need to amend my PA return.
