# Pennsylvania Income
## Description
Pennsylvania taxes EIGHT SEPARATE CLASSES OF INCOME at a flat 3.07%. The class system is the single most important structural feature of the PA return, and it has NO FEDERAL EQUIVALENT.
PA DOES NOT START FROM FEDERAL ADJUSTED GROSS INCOME. Each class is computed under Pennsylvania rules from Pennsylvania schedules. Federal Schedules C, E, and F are NOT used.
TWO CLASSES ARE THE EXCEPTION. INTEREST and DIVIDENDS start from a specific federal LINE and reconcile on PA Schedules A and B. See federal-to-pa-adjustments.md.
## The eight classes
| Line | Class | Schedule |
|---|---|---|
| 1a-1c | COMPENSATION, gross less allowable expenses | W-2, Schedule UE |
| 2 | INTEREST | PA Schedule A |
| 3 | DIVIDENDS and capital gains distributions | PA Schedule B |
| 4 | NET INCOME OR LOSS FROM A BUSINESS, PROFESSION, OR FARM | PA Schedule C or F |
| 5 | NET GAIN OR LOSS FROM THE SALE, EXCHANGE, OR DISPOSITION OF PROPERTY | PA Schedule D |
| 6 | NET INCOME OR LOSS FROM RENTS, ROYALTIES, PATENTS, OR COPYRIGHTS | PA Schedule E |
| 7 | ESTATE OR TRUST INCOME | PA Schedule J |
| 8 | GAMBLING AND LOTTERY WINNINGS | PA Schedule T |
| 9 | TOTAL PA TAXABLE INCOME | — |

## THE RULE THAT GOVERNS EVERYTHING
LINE 9 ADDS ONLY THE POSITIVE AMOUNTS FROM LINES 1c THROUGH 8. Losses are not subtracted.
YOU MAY NOT OFFSET INCOME IN ONE CLASS WITH A LOSS IN ANOTHER CLASS.
A taxpayer with $50,000 of compensation and a $30,000 business loss has $50,000 of PA taxable income — not $20,000. The loss is simply unusable.
LOSSES MAY ONLY BE REPORTED ON LINES 4, 5, AND 6. The other five classes cannot show a loss at all.
NO CARRYFORWARD AND NO CARRYBACK. Gains and losses cannot be moved to another tax year in either direction.
See class-system-and-losses.md for the full treatment, including the married-couple rules.
## What Pennsylvania does NOT tax
| Item | Federal | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|---|
| Social Security | Partly taxed | NOT TAXED |
| Retirement distributions AFTER qualifying for retirement and retiring | Taxed | NOT TAXED |
| Military pay earned outside PA by a PA resident on active duty | Taxed | NOT TAXED |
| Unemployment compensation | Taxed | NOT TAXED |
| Gifts, bequests, inheritances | Not taxed | NOT TAXED |
| Workers' compensation | Not taxed | NOT TAXED |
This is why PA's income base is so much narrower than the federal one at retirement ages, and why the PA return of a retiree is often blank.
## What Pennsylvania DOES tax that the federal system does not
| Item | Federal | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|---|
| Contributions to an employer retirement plan | EXCLUDED from wages | TAXABLE COMPENSATION |
| EARLY distributions from retirement plans and IRAs | Taxed plus a penalty | TAXABLE, no PA penalty |
| Certain non-employee compensation | Self-employment income | May be COMPENSATION |
The employer plan contribution point catches nearly every PA employee: a 401(k) deferral reduces federal Box 1 wages but is PA-taxable compensation in Box 16.
## No preferential rates
PA LAW DOES NOT DISTINGUISH LONG-TERM FROM SHORT-TERM GAINS. There is no capital gains preference, no qualified dividend rate, and no netting against ordinary income. Everything in a class is taxed at 3.07%.
## 2026
Rate unchanged at 3.07%. The class structure is statutory. The DFO-02 What's New notes SCHEDULE D was updated to add a DIGITAL ASSET QUESTION and to accommodate LIKE-KIND EXCHANGES.
