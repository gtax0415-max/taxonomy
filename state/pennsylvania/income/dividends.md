# Dividends and Capital Gains Distributions from Mutual Funds (PA-40 Line 3)
## Description
Report all dividend income received or credited during the year. A class that CANNOT SHOW A LOSS, and one where PA REROUTES an item the federal system treats as a capital gain.
## CAPITAL GAIN DISTRIBUTIONS ARE DIVIDENDS FOR PA
REPORT CAPITAL GAINS DISTRIBUTIONS FROM YOUR MUTUAL FUNDS FROM BOX 2a OF YOUR 1099-DIV AS PA-TAXABLE DIVIDEND INCOME, EVEN THOUGH YOU REPORT SUCH DISTRIBUTIONS AS CAPITAL GAINS ON FEDERAL SCHEDULE D.
This is a genuine reclassification, not a labelling difference, and it has consequences:
- The amount lands in the DIVIDEND class, which CANNOT take a loss
- IT CANNOT BE OFFSET BY CAPITAL LOSSES, which live in the line 5 class
A taxpayer with a $10,000 Box 2a distribution and $10,000 of realized stock losses nets to zero federally and reports $10,000 of PA dividend income with the losses stranded on line 5.
## You must classify dividends regardless of federal treatment
- PERSONAL dividends → LINE 3
- Dividends from BUSINESS ACCOUNTS and WORKING CAPITAL dividend income → include in determining PROFIT OR LOSS ON THE PA BUSINESS SCHEDULE, not line 3
The classification follows the character of the holding, not the 1099-DIV.
## No preferential rate
PA has no qualified dividend rate. Everything on line 3 is taxed at 3.07%.
## Schedule B and the form substitution rule
See the PA Schedule B instructions for when the schedule must be completed and submitted. YOU CANNOT SUBSTITUTE A FEDERAL SCHEDULE B FOR PA SCHEDULE B.
You do NOT have to submit Forms 1099-DIV and other statements with the return.
## Nontaxable dividends still count for Tax Forgiveness
NONTAXABLE INTEREST AND DIVIDENDS MUST BE INCLUDED AS ELIGIBILITY INCOME ON PA SCHEDULE SP.
## No loss possible
Line 3 cannot be negative. See class-system-and-losses.md.
## Schedule B reconciles to federal line 3b
PA Schedule B STARTS FROM FEDERAL FORM 1040 LINE 3b and adjusts, including an addition for IRC SECTION 965 REPATRIATED FOREIGN INCOME on a cash-receipt basis. See federal-to-pa-adjustments.md for the full line map.
## Required Information
- Form 1099-DIV from each payer, with Box 2a identified
- Classification of each holding as personal, business, or working capital
- Nontaxable dividend amounts, for Schedule SP
## Questions
- Do you have capital gain distributions in Box 2a, which PA treats as dividends?
- Are any dividends from business accounts or working capital?
- Do you have realized capital losses that you expect to offset distributions? They cannot
- Do you have nontaxable dividends, which count for Tax Forgiveness?
## Common Errors
- Reporting Box 2a capital gain distributions on PA Schedule D instead of as dividends
- Expecting capital losses to offset capital gain distributions
- Substituting a federal Schedule B for PA Schedule B
- Reporting business account dividends on line 3
- Omitting nontaxable dividends from Schedule SP eligibility income
## Prompt
- My mutual fund paid a capital gain distribution.
- Are qualified dividends taxed lower in PA?
- Can my stock losses offset my fund distributions?
