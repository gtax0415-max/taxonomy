# Net Income or Loss from a Business, Profession, or Farm (PA-40 Line 4)
## Description
One of only THREE CLASSES THAT CAN SHOW A LOSS. Covers business, farm, SHORT-TERM RENTALS OF LESS THAN 30 DAYS, and business activity from partnership and PA S corporation schedules.
## USE PA SCHEDULES, NOT FEDERAL ONES
YOU MUST USE PA SCHEDULE C to report business or profession income and expenses, or PA SCHEDULE F for a farm. DO NOT USE FEDERAL SCHEDULES C OR F.
FILE A PA SCHEDULE C OR F USING YOUR SEPARATE BOOKS AND RECORDS FOR PA PURPOSES. Net income or loss is determined under ACCEPTED PRINCIPLES AND PRACTICES OF THE ACCOUNTING PROFESSION, not under the Internal Revenue Code.
GENERALLY YOU MUST ADJUST YOUR FEDERAL AMOUNTS FOR PA PURPOSES. Adjustments can INCREASE OR DECREASE PA taxable income, and YOU MUST MAINTAIN APPROPRIATE RECORDS OF THEM.
The detailed differences are in the PA Schedule C and F instructions and the PA Personal Income Tax Guide. Do not assume a federal figure transfers.
## Offsetting within the class
YOU MAY OFFSET YOUR OWN INCOME IN THIS CLASS AGAINST YOUR OWN LOSS OR LOSSES FROM OTHER BUSINESS ACTIVITIES. A profitable shop and a losing consultancy net on line 4.
IF REPORTING A TOTAL NET LOSS, FILL IN THE OVAL next to line 4.
You may NOT offset against another class, and SPOUSES MAY NOT OFFSET EACH OTHER. See class-system-and-losses.md.
## Short-term rentals under 30 days
Rentals of LESS THAN 30 DAYS are BUSINESS ACTIVITY on line 4, not rents on line 6. This is a bright-line PA rule with no federal equivalent, and it moves a large amount of short-term rental activity out of the rental class.
Where the activity is longer-term or passive, see rents-royalties.md and the instructions' discussion of REPORTING RENTAL INCOME AS BUSINESS ACTIVITY ON LINE 4 OR AS RENTS ON LINE 6.
## Pass-through income
Report business income from partnerships, PA S corporations, and LLCs reporting on PA-20S/PA-65 SCHEDULES RK-1 OR NRK-1, or on federal Schedules K-1, on line 4 together with the net profit or loss from all PA Schedules C and F and PA Schedule E line 22.
## Dividends and interest from the business
Dividends from BUSINESS ACCOUNTS and WORKING CAPITAL dividend income are included in determining profit or loss HERE, not on line 3. The same logic applies to business interest.
## Required Information
- PA Schedule C or F prepared from PA books and records
- Every federal-to-PA adjustment, with supporting records
- RK-1 or NRK-1 from each pass-through entity
- The rental period, where short-term rentals are involved
- Which spouse owns each activity
## Questions
- Are you preparing a PA Schedule C, or reusing the federal one?
- What adjustments were made from federal to PA amounts?
- Do you have short-term rentals under 30 days, which belong here?
- Do you have losses in this class, and are they your own rather than your spouse's?
## Common Errors
- Submitting a federal Schedule C or F instead of the PA version
- Transferring federal net income without PA adjustments
- Reporting short-term rentals of under 30 days on line 6
- Offsetting a line 4 loss against compensation or interest
- Offsetting one spouse's loss against the other's profit
- Failing to fill the loss oval
