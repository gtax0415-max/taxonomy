# Net Income or Loss from Rents, Royalties, Patents, or Copyrights (PA-40 Line 6)
## Description
The third and last class that CAN SHOW A LOSS. Rental income is amounts received for the USE OF, OR THE RIGHT TO USE, your real or personal property. Royalty income is amounts received for the EXTRACTION OF COAL, OIL, GAS, OR OTHER MINERALS IN PLACE, and for the use of PATENTS, COPYRIGHTS, SECRET PROCESSES, FORMULAS, GOODWILL, TRADEMARKS, TRADE BRANDS, FRANCHISES, and similar property.
## LINE 6 OR LINE 4? The threshold question
| Situation | Line |
|---|---|
| SHORT-TERM RENTALS OF LESS THAN 30 DAYS | LINE 4, business |
| IN THE BUSINESS of renting property | LINE 4, PA Schedule C |
| Ordinary longer-term rental and royalty income | LINE 6, PA Schedule E |
IF YOU ARE IN THE BUSINESS OF RENTING YOUR PROPERTY, YOU REPORT NET PROFIT OR LOSS ON A PA SCHEDULE C, not Schedule E. The instructions devote a specific discussion to this at "Reporting Rental Income (Loss) as Business Activity on Line 4 or as Rents on Line 6."
The distinction matters because the two classes cannot offset each other.
## PASSIVE LOSSES ARE REPORTED IN FULL
REPORT PASSIVE RENTAL AND ROYALTY LOSS IN FULL, RATHER THAN CARRY IT OVER TO FUTURE YEARS.
There are no PA passive activity loss limitations and no suspended losses. The loss is taken now — and because PA has no carryforward, a loss that exceeds this class's income simply disappears.
This is the reverse of the federal system, where a suspended passive loss survives to a later year.
## Two exclusions from gross rents and royalties
Gross rents and royalties include all gross receipts EXCEPT:
1. Receipts from the SALE, EXCHANGE, OR OTHER DISPOSITION of rental, royalty, and similar property — those go to LINE 5
2. Receipts from operating an oil, gas, or mineral interest AS A BUSINESS, profession, or farm, or otherwise derived in the ordinary course of a business — those go to LINE 4
## Selling mineral interests, patents, or copyrights
IF YOU GAVE UP ALL RIGHTS to mineral interests, patents, or copyrights, the amounts received are PAYMENTS FOR THE SALE OR EXCHANGE OF PROPERTY. REPORT ON PA SCHEDULE D, line 5.
Giving up SOME rights keeps the income here; giving up ALL rights moves it to line 5.
## Rent-to-own contracts
Where a contract is a PURCHASE under accepted accounting principles and practices, the payments received are PAYMENTS OF THE PURCHASE PRICE and are NOT rental income. REPORT ON PA SCHEDULE D.
## Depreciation and sales tax
- DO NOT take depreciation expense on sales tax you ELECTED TO CURRENTLY EXPENSE for PA purposes
- If you currently expense such sales tax, THE PA BASIS DIFFERS FROM FEDERAL and depreciation must be adjusted
- YOU CANNOT EXPENSE THE SALES TAX AND STILL DEPRECIATE IT
- If you do not expense it, add it to the cost basis and depreciate the aggregate
## Capital gains and distributions from other schedules
ADD ANY CAPITAL GAINS AND DISTRIBUTIONS THAT YOU REPORT ON OTHER FEDERAL SCHEDULES where they belong to this class.
YOU MUST SEPARATELY SHOW your share of any rental or royalty gain or loss realized as a PARTNER, a SHAREHOLDER OF A PA S CORPORATION, or other entity.
## Offsetting
You MAY only offset YOUR OWN net income and losses in determining this line. If you realize a TOTAL NET LOSS, FILL IN THE OVAL next to line 6.
The Department's worked example: Mary's $2,000 total loss and Ben's $5,000 total loss are BOTH losses, so they ADD to a $7,000 reported loss. Had one been a gain, the other's loss would have been ignored. See class-system-and-losses.md.
## Report the total from all schedules
Report the TOTAL income or loss from ALL PA Schedules E and all PA Schedules RK-1 and NRK-1 on line 6.
## Required Information
- The rental period for each property, to determine line 4 versus line 6
- Lease and rent records
- Royalty statements by type
- Depreciation schedules with any PA basis adjustment
- Which spouse owns each property
## Questions
- Are any rentals for less than 30 days, which belong on line 4?
- Are you in the BUSINESS of renting, which moves you to Schedule C?
- Did you give up ALL rights to a patent, copyright, or mineral interest?
- Do you have passive losses you expect to suspend? PA takes them now or never
## Common Errors
- Reporting short-term rentals under 30 days on line 6
- Carrying a passive loss forward instead of reporting it in full
- Reporting a sale of rental property on line 6 instead of line 5
- Depreciating sales tax that was currently expensed for PA
- Netting one spouse's rental loss against the other's rental income
- Failing to fill the loss oval
