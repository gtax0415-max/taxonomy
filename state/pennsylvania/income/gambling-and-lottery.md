# Gambling and Lottery Winnings (PA-40 Line 8)
## Description
PA taxes gambling and lottery winnings, INCLUDING PENNSYLVANIA LOTTERY CASH PRIZES PAID ON OR AFTER JANUARY 1, 2016. A class that CANNOT SHOW A LOSS.
## Residents versus nonresidents
| | Reported |
|---|---|
| PA RESIDENT | ALL gambling and lottery winnings FROM ANY SOURCE, INCLUDING OTHER STATES AND COUNTRIES |
| NONRESIDENT | ONLY winnings from PA SOURCES. Do NOT report wagers placed in any other state or country |
## What counts as winnings
Cash; the VALUE OF PROPERTY such as automobiles, jewelry, electronic devices, appliances, and clothes; the VALUE OF THE USE OF PROPERTY such as trips, vacations, airline tickets, and cruises; and other items of value.
ONE EXCEPTION: NONCASH PRIZES OF THE PENNSYLVANIA LOTTERY are not taxable.
## What you may deduct, and what you may not
YOU MAY ONLY DEDUCT YOUR COSTS OF GAMBLING, WAGERING, BETTING, AND PLAYING LOTTERIES FROM YOUR WINNINGS.
YOU MAY NOT DEDUCT any expenses incurred to TAKE PART in these activities — programs, tip sheets, travel, meals, or lodging.
LOSSES CANNOT EXCEED WINNINGS. This class cannot go negative, and no loss carries to another class or another year.
PENNSYLVANIA LOTTERY TICKET COSTS may be deducted only for TICKETS PURCHASED ON OR AFTER JANUARY 1, 2016.
## Powerball and Mega Millions
- A ticket PURCHASED IN PENNSYLVANIA, by a resident or not: any cash prize is PA-taxable if PAID ON OR AFTER JANUARY 1, 2016
- A PA RESIDENT buying in ANOTHER STATE: the prize is PA-taxable income REGARDLESS of the date paid
## Spouses report separately
SPOUSES MUST REPORT THEIR WINNINGS SEPARATELY. One spouse's losses cannot reduce the other's winnings, consistent with the general class rule.
## PA withholding
PA WITHHOLDING IS REQUIRED on all Pennsylvania Lottery cash prizes GREATER THAN $5,000 paid after JULY 12, 2016. Many PA casinos offer VOLUNTARY PA withholding.
If PA tax is withheld in BOX 15 of a federal Form W-2G, report the withholding on PA SCHEDULE T LINE 7, and INCLUDE A COPY OF THE W-2G with your return.
## Documentation: keep it, do not send it
Taxpayers claiming winnings and losses MUST BE ABLE TO DOCUMENT THEM. THE DOCUMENTATION SHOULD NOT BE INCLUDED WITH THE RETURN, but may be requested later.
KEEP AN ACCURATE DIARY OR SIMILAR RECORD of winnings and losses, supported by other documentation:
- Federal Form W-2G
- FEDERAL FORM 5754, Statement by Persons Receiving Gambling Winnings
- Wagering tickets, cancelled checks, substitute checks, credit records, bank withdrawals
- Statements of actual winnings or payment slips from the establishment
SLOT OR ELECTRONIC GAMING: record the MACHINE NUMBER and all winnings BY DATE AND TIME the machine was played, plus establishment statements and payment slips.
HARNESS OR HORSE RACING: record the races, amounts wagered, amounts collected on winning tickets, AND AMOUNTS WAGERED ON LOSING TICKETS, plus the tickets themselves.
For other forms of gambling see IRS Publication 529.
## Contrast with the federal treatment
FEDERALLY, gambling losses are an ITEMIZED DEDUCTION, so a taxpayer taking the standard deduction is taxed on GROSS winnings with no offset.
PENNSYLVANIA NETS WAGERING COSTS AGAINST WINNINGS WITHIN THE CLASS ITSELF on Schedule T, with no itemizing requirement and no need to exceed a standard deduction. For a casual gambler the PA result is more favourable, though still capped at zero.
## Required Information
- All Forms W-2G, with Box 15 PA withholding
- The gambling diary and supporting records
- Costs of wagering, separated from expenses of participating
- Residency, which determines whether out-of-state winnings count
## Questions
- Are you a PA resident, which brings in out-of-state and foreign winnings?
- Do you have a contemporaneous diary of winnings and losses?
- Did you win a noncash PA Lottery prize, which is not taxable?
- Was PA tax withheld in Box 15 of a W-2G?
## Common Errors
- A nonresident reporting out-of-state wagering activity
- Deducting travel, meals, lodging, programs, or tip sheets
- Claiming losses in excess of winnings
- Deducting PA Lottery ticket costs for tickets bought before January 1, 2016
- Netting one spouse's losses against the other's winnings
- Sending the diary and records with the return instead of retaining them
- Omitting the W-2G copy where PA tax was withheld
