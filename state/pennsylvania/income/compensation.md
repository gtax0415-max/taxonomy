# Compensation (PA-40 Lines 1a through 1c)
## Description
The largest class on most returns. BOX 16 OF THE W-2 IS USUALLY THE PA FIGURE, and PA law requires a PA employer to follow PA rules in computing it — which is why BOX 16 OFTEN DIFFERS FROM BOX 1.
## What is PA-taxable compensation
Salaries, wages, tips, gratuities, commissions, bonuses, incentive payments, vacation and holiday pay, EMPLOYER STUDENT LOAN PAYMENTS, STUDENT LOAN DEBT FORGIVENESS for entering certain fields, distributions from NONQUALIFIED deferred compensation plans, CERTAIN EARLY DISTRIBUTIONS FROM RETIREMENT PLANS, and termination pay.
Also taxable, and requiring a STATEMENT DESCRIBING THE AMOUNT with the return:
- Executor or executrix fees from an estate
- Fiduciary fees from a trust
- Honorariums
- JURY DUTY PAY
- Director's fees
- COVENANT NOT TO COMPETE payments
- Damages or settlements FOR LOST WAGES
- EARLY DISTRIBUTIONS from retirement plans or IRAs, traditional or Roth
- Distributions from employee stock ownership plans, if not a PA eligible plan
- Other non-employee compensation for services rendered
## THE RETIREMENT CONTRIBUTION REVERSAL
CONTRIBUTIONS YOU MAKE TO AN EMPLOYER-SPONSORED RETIREMENT PLAN ARE PA-TAXABLE COMPENSATION, EVEN IF NOT TAXABLE FEDERALLY AND EVEN IF EXCLUDED FROM THE STATE WAGES ON YOUR W-2.
A 401(k) deferral reduces federal Box 1 but is PA-taxable. This is the most common reason Box 16 exceeds Box 1, and it catches nearly every PA employee.
PA taxes the money GOING IN and exempts it COMING OUT after retirement. The federal system does the reverse. See retirement-income.md.
## Retirement distributions: taxed only if early
- PAYMENTS RECEIVED AFTER YOU QUALIFY FOR RETIREMENT AND RETIRE: NOT TAXABLE
- ROLLOVERS into another deferred payment program or IRA, where not federally taxable: NOT TAXABLE
- Distributions representing YOUR OWN PREVIOUS CONTRIBUTIONS to a deferred compensation plan: NOT TAXABLE
- Payments to an ESTATE OR DESIGNATED BENEFICIARY on death: NOT TAXABLE
- EARLY DISTRIBUTIONS, before qualifying and retiring: TAXABLE COMPENSATION
EARLY RETIREMENT INCENTIVE PAYMENTS — an inducement to retire early — ARE TAXABLE.
## Military pay
- PA RESIDENT ON ACTIVE DUTY OUTSIDE PA: NOT TAXABLE. Exclude from line 1a
- NONRESIDENT ON ACTIVE DUTY WITHIN PA: NOT TAXABLE
- PA RESIDENT ON ACTIVE DUTY WITHIN PA: TAXABLE. Include Box 16
MILITARY ORDERS MUST BE INCLUDED with the return as proof of active duty outside PA. Where a W-2 mixes taxable and nontaxable military pay, attach a statement showing the amounts and how they were determined.
PA tax withheld from nontaxable military pay still goes on line 13.
## The six reciprocal states
INDIANA, MARYLAND, NEW JERSEY, OHIO, VIRGINIA, WEST VIRGINIA. Generally one state will not tax a resident of the other on compensation SUBJECT TO EMPLOYER WITHHOLDING.
RECIPROCITY DOES NOT APPLY TO:
- Miscellaneous and NON-EMPLOYEE compensation
- Compensation EARNED while a PA resident but RECEIVED while a resident of the reciprocal state
- Compensation paid to an OHIO RESIDENT SHAREHOLDER-EMPLOYEE with a 20 PERCENT OR GREATER interest in a PA S corporation who worked in Pennsylvania
A PA RESIDENT whose employer withheld the other state's tax MUST FILE FOR A REFUND FROM THAT STATE, not claim a PA credit.
A RECIPROCAL-STATE RESIDENT whose employer withheld PA tax reports ZERO on line 1a and the withholding on line 13, attaching the W-2, a copy of the home-state resident return without schedules, and a statement.
## Reimbursements and allowances
REPORT ALL REIMBURSEMENTS AND ALLOWANCES AS COMPENSATION UNLESS ALL THREE apply:
1. The expenses are PA-ALLOWABLE employee business expenses
2. You ACCOUNT for them to your employer
3. Your employer reimburses THE EXACT AMOUNT of the allowable expenses
A fixed mileage allowance, fixed reimbursement, periodic expense allowance, or per-diem NOT EXCEEDING APPLICABLE FEDERAL LIMITS meets these requirements. DO NOT REPORT THOSE EXPENSES ON SCHEDULE UE.
## When Box 16 is not the answer
Complete the PA-40 W-2 RW RECONCILIATION WORKSHEET where Boxes 12 and 14 do not contain enough to reconcile federal and Medicare wages to PA wages, or where you received distributions of previously taxed contributions from a nonqualified deferred compensation plan.
A PA RESIDENT whose W-2 shows another state's withholding and no PA withholding should also complete the W-2 RW.
## When to submit the W-2
PAPER RETURNS: include copies of ALL Forms W-2 and ALL Forms 1099-R regardless of taxability. Include 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC if reported as compensation or showing PA withholding.
E-FILED RETURNS: a W-2 copy and written explanation are required only in TEN listed situations, including line 1a differing from Box 16, a handwritten W-2, withholding above 3.07%, reciprocal-state residency, MEDICARE WAGES IN BOX 5 EXCEEDING PA WAGES IN BOX 16, a PA resident working out of state with no PA withholding, a nonqualified deferred compensation distribution in Box 1, and a W-2 showing another state's income or tax.
## Required Information
- Form W-2 from each employer, Boxes 16 and 17
- A statement describing any of the listed special compensation items
- Military orders, if claiming nontaxable active duty pay
- The W-2 RW worksheet, where reconciliation is needed
- Employer reimbursement records
## Questions
- Does Box 16 differ from Box 1, and do you know why?
- Do you contribute to a 401(k) or similar plan, which is PA-taxable?
- Did you take a retirement distribution, and had you qualified to retire?
- Is any of your pay active duty military earned outside PA?
- Do you live in or work in one of the six reciprocal states?
## Common Errors
- Using Box 1 instead of Box 16
- Omitting employer plan contributions, which are PA-taxable
- Treating an early retirement distribution as nontaxable
- Omitting jury duty pay, director's fees, or executor fees
- Claiming a PA credit for tax withheld by a reciprocal state instead of seeking a refund there
- Reporting per-diem and fixed allowances on Schedule UE
- Omitting military orders when excluding active duty pay
## Prompt
- Why is my PA wage higher than my federal wage?
- I took money out of my 401(k) early.
- I live in PA and work in New Jersey.
