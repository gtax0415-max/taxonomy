# Refund Donation Lines (PA-40 Lines 32 through 36)
## Description
Five lines that let a taxpayer give away part of a PENNSYLVANIA REFUND to a statutorily designated organization. NOT A CREDIT AND NOT A DEDUCTION. Every dollar entered reduces the refund by one dollar and produces NO Pennsylvania tax benefit.
## TEN organizations, FIVE lines
There are TEN ways to donate but only FIVE LINES on the PA-40, so A TAXPAYER MAY CHOOSE AT MOST FIVE IN ONE TAX YEAR. Each of lines 32 through 36 has a CODE BOX.
| Code | Organization |
|---|---|
| A | PA BREAST CANCER COALITION'S Refunds for Breast and Cervical Cancer Research Fund |
| B | WILD RESOURCE CONSERVATION FUND |
| C | MILITARY FAMILY RELIEF ASSISTANCE PROGRAM |
| D | GOVERNOR ROBERT P. CASEY MEMORIAL ORGAN AND TISSUE DONATION AWARENESS TRUST FUND |
| E | JUVENILE (TYPE 1) DIABETES CURE RESEARCH FUND |
| F | PA CHILDREN'S TRUST FUND |
| G | AMERICAN RED CROSS |
| H | PENNSYLVANIA 529 COLLEGE AND CAREER SAVINGS PROGRAM ACCOUNT |
| I | PEDIATRIC CANCER RESEARCH FUND |
| J | VETERANS' TRUST FUND |
Codes are from the 2025 PA-40 instructions, page 25. The list has grown over time — VERIFY THE CODES against the instructions for the filing year rather than carrying them forward.
## CODE H REQUIRES PA SCHEDULE P
A donation to a Pennsylvania 529 College and Career Savings Program account requires PA SCHEDULE P, and SCHEDULE P IS NOT INCLUDED IN THE PA-40 BOOKLET. It must be obtained separately through the Forms Ordering Services.
This is the single most common way a Code H donation fails: the code is entered, no Schedule P is attached, and the donation is rejected.
The account must be an EXISTING PA 529 Guaranteed Savings Plan (GSP) or PA 529 Investment Plan (IP) account. This is the 529 program, sometimes called the Tuition Account Program — NOT PA ABLE, which is a different program and is NOT among the ten codes.
## An overpayment is a PREREQUISITE
YOU MUST HAVE AN OVERPAYMENT. These lines ALLOCATE AN EXISTING REFUND; THEY CANNOT CREATE A PAYMENT. A taxpayer who owes tax cannot donate on the return at all and must send a check directly to the organization.
There is no federal counterpart. The federal return has no mechanism for directing a refund to a charity, and a federal charitable deduction requires an actual contribution by the taxpayer, not a redirected refund.
## The arithmetic constraint
```
Line 29   Overpayment
Line 30   Refund
Line 31   Credit to next year's estimated account
Lines 32-36  Refund donations
THE TOTAL OF LINES 30 THROUGH 36 MUST EQUAL LINE 29
```
Refund, estimated-account credit, and donations together must exhaust the overpayment exactly.
## If you owe tax you may still give
Send a check or money order DIRECTLY to the organization. The addresses are in the PA-40 instructions, pages 44 and 45. Several also accept online contributions:
- MILITARY FAMILY RELIEF: pa.gov/dmva/donate
- CASEY ORGAN AND TISSUE TRUST: apps.pwp.pa.gov/DOHOrganDonations
- AMERICAN RED CROSS: redcross.org
- PA 529: pa529.com
A direct check is a genuine charitable contribution outside the PA return. The instructions describe these as TAX-DEDUCTIBLE, which refers to the FEDERAL return — see below.
## Is it deductible?
NOT ON THE PA RETURN. Pennsylvania allows NO CHARITABLE DEDUCTION OF ANY KIND; the Department's own Deductions and Credits guide lists "Gifts to charity: No provision."
The instructions' repeated phrase "tax-deductible contribution" describes a DIRECT check and points at the FEDERAL return. A donation routed through the refund is a different transaction and should not be assumed federally deductible either.
## The 529 interaction worth checking
A PA 529 CONTRIBUTION IS SEPARATELY DEDUCTIBLE on PA Schedule O, up to the annual federal gift tax exclusion per beneficiary per taxpayer. Whether a refund routed to a 529 through Code H supports that Schedule O deduction, and in which year, is a question for the Schedule O and Schedule P instructions — do not assume it does.
## This is a DONATION, not a credit
It lives in donations/ rather than credits/ because it moves money AWAY from the taxpayer. See state/pennsylvania/donations/donations.md for the full distinction, including the contrast with the EITC and OSTC restricted credits, which return up to 90% of a contribution and live in credits/business-incentive/.
## Notes
- Once filed, the donation cannot be changed even if the Department later adjusts the overpayment
- These lines exist on the PA-40 only. There is no equivalent on the PA-41 fiduciary return
- The Veterans' Trust Fund is also funded through driver's license and vehicle registration donations, so a client may believe they have already given
## Required Information
- Confirmation that Line 29 shows an overpayment
- The organization CODE, A through J, for each choice
- The amount to each, totalling with lines 30 and 31 to exactly Line 29
- For CODE H: a completed PA SCHEDULE P, obtained separately, with the existing PA 529 GSP or IP account details
## Questions
- Do you have a Pennsylvania overpayment to allocate?
- Which organizations, up to five of the ten?
- If donating to a 529, do you have PA Schedule P and an existing GSP or IP account?
- Did you mean the Educational Improvement Tax Credit instead, which is a credit rather than a gift?
## Common Errors
- Entering a donation with no overpayment
- Failing to make lines 30 through 36 total exactly to Line 29
- Attempting more than five organizations
- USING CODE H WITHOUT ATTACHING PA SCHEDULE P, which is not in the booklet
- Assuming Code H covers a PA ABLE account; it does not, and ABLE is not on the list
- Carrying a code forward from a prior year after the list changed
- Expecting a Pennsylvania deduction or credit for the donation
- Confusing these lines with the EITC or OSTC restricted credits, which work in the opposite direction
