# Family and Dependent Credits
## Description
Pennsylvania has exactly ONE family credit: the Child and Dependent Care Enhancement Credit. There is no child tax credit, no adoption credit, and no dependent exemption of any kind.
## Credits in this folder
- Child and Dependent Care Enhancement Credit (child-and-dependent-care-enhancement-credit.md) — PA Schedule DC → PA-40, Line 23. REFUNDABLE
## What Pennsylvania does not have
| Federal provision | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|
| Child tax credit | NO PROVISION |
| Empire State child credit equivalent | NO PROVISION |
| Adoption credit | NO PROVISION |
| Dependent exemption | NO PROVISION — PA has no exemptions at all |
| Household credit | NO PROVISION |
Dependents affect a PA return in only two places: the dependent CHILDREN count on Schedule SP for Tax Forgiveness, and the care expenses on Schedule DC. Nothing else.
## The one credit is unusually generous
At 100% of the federal credit AND REFUNDABLE, PA's care credit can be worth more than the federal credit it is based on. The FEDERAL credit is NONREFUNDABLE, so a taxpayer with no federal liability gets nothing federally and the full amount from Pennsylvania.
