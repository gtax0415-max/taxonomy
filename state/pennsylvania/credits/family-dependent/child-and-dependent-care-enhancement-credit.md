# Child and Dependent Care Enhancement Credit
## Description
A REFUNDABLE credit equal to 100% of the federal child and dependent care credit. Enacted in 2022 and expanded from 30% to 100% for tax years after December 31, 2022. Pennsylvania's only family credit, and an unusually strong one.
## Amount
100% OF THE FEDERAL CREDIT.
| Qualifying persons | Maximum | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| One | $1,050 | $600 |
| Two or more | $2,100 | $1,200 |
The minimum applies provided expenses are at least $3,000 per qualifying person. The cap phases down as income rises, following the federal credit's own phase-down rather than a separate PA schedule.
## 2026: the federal change flows through automatically
PA pays 100% OF THE FEDERAL CREDIT, so a federal change moves the PA credit with NO CHANGE IN PA LAW.
The One Big Beautiful Bill Act raises the federal credit's TOP RATE FROM 35% TO 50% BEGINNING 2026. If the 100% match applies to the new federal amounts, the PA maximum rises above $2,100 automatically. The Department has continued to publish $1,050 and $2,100 in its 2026 filing-season materials, so CONFIRM AGAINST THE 2026 SCHEDULE DC AND ITS INSTRUCTIONS before quoting a figure either way.
The 2026-27 budget signed July 12, 2026 continues the credit, funded at more than $444.5 million across nearly 858,500 credits over four years.
## Beware stale guidance
BEWARE STALE GUIDANCE. The credit began at 30% of the federal amount, and third-party sites and calculators still publish 30%. The Department's current pages state 100% for tax years after December 31, 2022. Use the official figure.
## You must have RECEIVED the federal credit
Pennsylvania requires that you ACTUALLY RECEIVED the federal credit — not merely that you would have qualified for it.
Practical consequence: a taxpayer with no federal tax liability who skipped Form 2441 because the federal credit is nonrefundable and worth nothing to them has ALSO lost the PA credit — which is refundable and would have paid out. File Form 2441 even when it produces no federal benefit.
## Qualifying expenses
Care expenses incurred so you could work or look for work, for:
- A DEPENDENT CHILD UNDER AGE 13
- A SPOUSE physically or mentally incapable of self-care who lived with you more than half the year
- A DEPENDENT incapable of self-care
## Attachments are mandatory
You must attach BOTH:
- Federal Form 2441
- Federal Form 1040 Schedule 3
If these are not included, the Department AUTOMATICALLY REMOVES the credit from Line 23. There is no request for missing documents first. In myPATH the two forms must be uploaded as SEPARATE attachments; combining them into one file blocks submission.
## Married couples must file jointly
The rules of IRC Section 21 apply, and under IRC 21(e)(2) married couples must file a JOINT return to claim the credit. This creates a conflict worth flagging: married taxpayers claiming most Schedule OC restricted credits must file SEPARATELY. A couple with both a restricted credit and care expenses cannot have both.
## How it is applied
The credit is combined with any Schedule OC amounts on Line 23. It may be applied against ALL CLASSES of income. It is applied to the liability first, and ANY REMAINING AMOUNT IS REFUNDED.
## Required Information
- Federal Form 2441 as filed and the credit allowed
- Federal Schedule 3
- Provider name, address, and TIN
- Each qualifying person's name, SSN, and date of birth
- Confirmation the federal credit was actually received
## Questions
- Did you claim AND receive the federal child and dependent care credit?
- Do you have Form 2441 and Schedule 3 to attach?
- Was the care for a child under 13, or a spouse or dependent incapable of self-care?
- Are you married, and are you filing a joint PA return?
- Are you also claiming a restricted credit that would require separate filing?
## Common Errors
- Skipping federal Form 2441 because the federal credit was worth nothing, and losing the refundable PA credit with it
- Omitting the required attachments, which causes automatic removal of the credit
- Uploading Form 2441 and Schedule 3 as a single combined file in myPATH
- Using the superseded 30% rate from older guidance or a third-party calculator
- Married couples filing separately
## Prompt
- I paid for daycare and live in Pennsylvania.
- Does PA have a child care credit?
- My federal child care credit was zero because I owed no tax.
