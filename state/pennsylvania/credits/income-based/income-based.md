# Income-Based Credits
## Description
Two credits keyed to income level. TAX FORGIVENESS is Pennsylvania's signature provision and has no analogue in any other state's personal income tax. The WORKING PENNSYLVANIANS TAX CREDIT is brand new and is PA's first EITC-style credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Tax Forgiveness (tax-forgiveness.md) — PA Schedule SP → PA-40, Line 21. Nonrefundable but can eliminate the entire liability
- Working Pennsylvanians Tax Credit (working-pennsylvanians-tax-credit.md) — PA-40, Line 23. EFFECTIVE TAX YEAR 2025, first claimed in the 2026 filing season. REFUNDABLE
## The two work in opposite directions
Tax Forgiveness is NONREFUNDABLE and capped by the liability; it can only zero out tax. The WPTC is REFUNDABLE and pays out beyond the liability. A low-income working family with children commonly claims BOTH: forgiveness eliminates the 3.07% liability, and the WPTC then produces cash.
## Why Tax Forgiveness exists
PA's flat 3.07% tax has no standard deduction and no personal exemption, so without relief a worker earning $9,000 would owe tax from the first dollar. Tax Forgiveness is the substitute for the zero-bracket that PA's rate structure lacks. It forgives from 10% to 100% of the liability on a sliding scale.
It is why PA's credit list is so short: one broad low-income mechanism does the work that a standard deduction, a personal exemption, a household credit, and an EITC do elsewhere.
## Eligibility income is broader than PA taxable income, but NOT unlimited
Tax Forgiveness uses ELIGIBILITY INCOME on Schedule SP. It starts from PA taxable income and adds back specific nontaxable items — alimony, inheritances, gifts, out-of-state income, scholarships, foster care payments.
IT DOES NOT ADD BACK RETIREMENT INCOME. Social Security, Railroad Retirement, PA-eligible retirement plan distributions after retiring, military pensions, and Civil Service annuities are ALL EXCLUDED by the Schedule SP line 10 instructions. A retired Pennsylvanian can have substantial cash income and almost no eligibility income.
CONTRAST WITH THE FEDERAL POSITION: federally, Social Security is partly taxable and retirement distributions are fully taxable. For PA neither is taxed, and neither counts in the forgiveness test.
## The thresholds are simple arithmetic
Base $6,500 unmarried or $13,000 married, PLUS $9,500 PER DEPENDENT CHILD for 100% forgiveness, then $250 steps down through each 10% band to the 10% figure. Statutory, not indexed.
