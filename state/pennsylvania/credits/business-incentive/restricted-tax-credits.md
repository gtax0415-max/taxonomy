# Restricted Tax Credits (PA-40/PA-41 Schedule OC)
## Description
The shared mechanics for every credit on Schedule OC. SIGNIFICANTLY REDESIGNED FOR TAX YEAR 2025 — revision code EX MOD 08-25. Prior-year versions must no longer be used, and the layout, columns, and line structure all changed.

## The four AWARD SOURCE codes
Every line needs a Credit Description Code:
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| CY | Current-year credit awarded DIRECTLY to the taxpayer |
| PT | Received from a PASS-THROUGH entity |
| PA | PURCHASED from or assigned by another taxpayer |
| CO | CARRIED OVER from a prior year |
If more than one code applies on a line, submit a statement describing the credits.
## The column structure
CREDIT TYPE · AWARD SOURCE · AWARDEE ID TYPE · AWARDEE TAX ID · AWARD NUMBER · AWARD DATE · CLAIM AMOUNT
- AWARDEE ID TYPE: SSN for an individual, FEIN for an entity. OPTIONAL field
- AWARDEE TAX ID: for a business or farm reporting on PA Schedule C or F, the SSN OF THE OWNER. For a purchased credit, the SELLER'S FEIN or SSN. For a pass-through, the FEIN OF THE PASS-THROUGH ENTITY
- For EITC or OSTC passed through MULTIPLE TIERS, enter the FEIN OF THE ORIGINAL entity that first received the credit. Same where a QUALIFIED SUB-CHAPTER S TRUST passes it to its beneficiary
- AWARD NUMBER and AWARD DATE come from the official approval, award, assignment, or certification letter
## ONLY TEN LINES
Schedule OC has lines 1 through 10 and a total on line 11. ATTACH ADDITIONAL FORMS if you have more than ten credits.
LINE 11 GOES TO PA-40 LINE 23, or PA-41 LINE 16. The 2018 Deductions and Credits guide says PA-41 line 14; the current schedule says LINE 16.
## The reconciliation check
If EVERY credit on the schedule is passed through from an entity AND there is no resident credit from another state included, LINE 11 SHOULD EQUAL the sum of Line 9 of your RK-1s or Line 7 of your NRK-1s. A mismatch means something is misrouted or missing.
## An RK-1 entry alone does NOT pass the credit through
THE SINGLE MOST IMPORTANT PROCEDURAL POINT. Listing the credit on an owner's RK-1 or NRK-1 IS NOT SUFFICIENT.
The entity must have contacted the BUREAU OF BUSINESS TAXPAYER ACCOUNTING, ACCOUNT MAINTENANCE CORPORATION TAX DIVISION — formerly the Bureau of Corporation Taxes, Accounting Division — to actually pass the credit through. Verify this with the entity before claiming.
A pass-through form showing the amounts may also be submitted with the PA-40 or PA-41. IF THE CREDIT MOVES THROUGH MULTIPLE TIERS, A SEPARATE PASS-THROUGH FORM IS REQUIRED FOR EACH TIER.
CREDITS MUST BE AWARDED TO EACH OWNER PER THE DISTRIBUTIVE INCOME PERCENTAGE to which the owner is entitled, or THE PASS-THROUGH FORM WILL BE REJECTED.
## Documentation by award source
| Source | What to attach |
|---|---|
| CY, direct award | A COPY OF THE AWARD CERTIFICATION LETTER, with the return |
| PT, pass-through | The ENTITY must send pages 1 and 2 of the award letter to the Bureau BEFORE filing the entity return |
| PA, purchased | Copies of the transfer documentation you received |
| CO, carryover | Documentation of the ORIGINAL award AND a statement showing amounts used in prior years |
The Bureau of Individual Taxes may also request a copy of the entity's awardee documentation. Providing it with every owner's return speeds processing; REDACT SSNs to the last four digits if the same document goes to more than one owner.
## Refund timing: expect the fall, but interest is paid
The Department cannot process the allocation until it has received ALL THREE of:
1. The award from DCED
2. The pass-through form allocating the credit to the owners
3. THE AWARDEE'S OWN TAX RETURN electing to pass the credit through
Most restricted credit donations come through pass-through entities, and those entities often do not file until the fall, on extension or after a compliance check. SO EVEN A TIMELY-FILED PA-40 WILL NOT PRODUCE THE REFUND UNTIL THE FALL. The Department states this plainly and CONFIRMS IT WILL PAY INTEREST on the refund owed.
Tell clients this before they file, not after.
## myPATH: request a Summary of Restricted Credits
Taxpayers and practitioners can ELECTRONICALLY REQUEST a Summary of Restricted Credits through myPATH at any time. It arrives as a PDF in the Letters section THE SAME BUSINESS DAY.
A DETAILED STATEMENT OF ACCOUNT is also available, showing credits applied to liabilities in a given tax period. Use these to verify what the Department believes you hold before claiming.
## The ordering cap
Schedule OC credits are applied LAST:
```
PA tax liability
  minus RESIDENT CREDIT
  minus TAX FORGIVENESS
  = the maximum Schedule OC credit usable
```
The Department's example: a $4,000 liability with a $3,000 resident credit leaves $1,000 of room. A $2,500 restricted credit must be reduced to $1,000. A high Tax Forgiveness percentage can consume the liability entirely and leave a certified credit unusable.
## Married taxpayers must file SEPARATELY
Except for EITC and OSTC, married taxpayers claiming Schedule OC credits MUST FILE SEPARATE RETURNS, even when both are claiming credits. Joint filing is permitted only where the PRIMARY taxpayer claims the credit and the secondary taxpayer has NO taxable income.
THE CONFLICT: the Child and Dependent Care Enhancement Credit requires a JOINT return under IRC 21(e)(2). A married couple cannot claim both a restricted credit and the care credit in the same year. Compare first — the care credit is refundable and can reach $2,100.
## Pass-through restrictions
- The election is IRREVOCABLE. An entity must not pass through more than an owner can use in one year
- An owner must claim a passed-through credit IMMEDIATELY in the year of transfer — no carry-forward, carry-back, refund, resale, or second pass-through
- Except for EITC and OSTC, a credit passed from one pass-through entity to ANOTHER may not then reach that entity's owners
- Credits awarded to or passed to an ESTATE OR TRUST may NOT be passed to beneficiaries
- Married joint owners must be SEPARATELY LISTED on the pass-through request, each with their own tax number, even though they receive one joint RK-1
- Some credits must first be applied to the ENTITY'S OWN corporate tax liability for the award year
## Expense deduction
PA generally allows deducting expenses that qualified you for a credit. NOT for the NEIGHBORHOOD ASSISTANCE PROGRAM or the EDUCATIONAL IMPROVEMENT TAX CREDIT.
## RK-1 mechanics worth knowing
- LINE 8 is the resident credit; LINE 9 is Total Other Credits. Both require a statement
- The statement must carry the entity name, FEIN, tax year, owner names, and owner tax IDs
- An RK-1 must be issued to a C CORPORATION partner IF a Schedule OC credit was allocated, even though RK-1s are otherwise not issued to C corporations
- A $250 NON-FILER PENALTY applies for each missing RK-1 or NRK-1
## Required Information
- Credit type abbreviation and award source code
- Award ID Number and Award Date from the letter
- Awardee Tax ID, per the rules above
- Confirmation the entity actually filed the pass-through with the Bureau
- Remaining liability after the resident credit and Tax Forgiveness
- Filing status
## Questions
- Which credit, and was it awarded directly, passed through, purchased, or carried over?
- Do you have the award letter with the Award ID Number and date?
- Did the ENTITY confirm it filed the pass-through form with the Bureau?
- Does Schedule OC line 11 reconcile to your RK-1 line 9 totals?
- Is any amount on RK-1 line 9 actually an out-of-state credit belonging on Schedule G-L?
- How much liability remains after the resident credit and Tax Forgiveness?
## Common Errors
- Using a prior-year Schedule OC after the 2025 redesign
- Assuming an RK-1 entry passes the credit through without the entity filing with the Bureau
- Putting an RK-1 Line 9 out-of-state credit on Schedule OC instead of Schedule G-L
- Omitting the award certification letter on a directly awarded credit
- Omitting the prior-year usage statement on a carryover
- Using the immediate entity's FEIN instead of the ORIGINAL entity's for a multi-tier EITC or OSTC
- Filing jointly when separate returns are required
- Claiming both a restricted credit and the child care credit as a married couple
- Claiming more than the liability left after the resident credit and Tax Forgiveness
- Promising a client a spring refund on a pass-through credit
- Using PA-41 line 14 instead of line 16
