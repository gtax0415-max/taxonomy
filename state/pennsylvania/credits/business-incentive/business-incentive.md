# Business and Incentive Credits (Restricted Credits)
## Description
Pennsylvania calls these RESTRICTED TAX CREDITS. They are awarded by agency application, certified, and claimed on PA-40 Schedule OC. Most are awarded to PA S corporations and partnerships and passed through to owners, though some go directly to individuals.
This is by far the largest PA credit category, and it is where PA's credit policy actually lives — the Commonwealth runs economic development through restricted credits rather than through personal credits.

## Five rules that govern all of them
1. NONREFUNDABLE, without exception
2. Applied LAST — after the resident credit, then Tax Forgiveness. Capped at whatever liability remains
3. MARRIED TAXPAYERS MUST FILE SEPARATELY, except for EITC and OSTC
4. Claimable against ALL CLASSES of income, unlike most PA provisions which respect class boundaries
5. A credit received by an owner CANNOT be passed through again, sold, assigned, carried back, or refunded
6. AN RK-1 ENTRY ALONE DOES NOT PASS THE CREDIT THROUGH. The entity must file a pass-through form with the Bureau of Business Taxpayer Accounting, Account Maintenance Corporation Tax Division
## The two exceptions worth memorising
EITC and OSTC are exempt from the separate-filing requirement AND from the bar on multi-tier pass-through. Every other restricted credit follows the strict rules. See Informational Notice Miscellaneous Tax 2015-01.
## Expense deduction is usually preserved
PA generally does NOT disallow the deduction of expenses that qualified you for a credit. The exceptions are the NEIGHBORHOOD ASSISTANCE PROGRAM and the EDUCATIONAL IMPROVEMENT TAX CREDIT, where the qualifying expenses are not deductible.
## Refunds on pass-through credits arrive in the FALL
The Department cannot allocate a pass-through credit until it holds the DCED award, the pass-through form, AND the awardee entity's own return electing the pass-through. Entities routinely file in the fall on extension. A timely-filed PA-40 will still wait. The Department PAYS INTEREST on the delayed refund.
