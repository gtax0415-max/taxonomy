# Organ and Bone Marrow Donor Credit
## Description
A credit for an EMPLOYER that continues to pay an employee during leave to donate an organ or bone marrow. It is a restricted credit on Schedule OC, and it reaches an individual PA-40 only by pass-through.
## It is not a donor credit
The donating EMPLOYEE receives nothing. The credit belongs to the employer for the wages and benefits paid during the donation leave.
A PENNSYLVANIA LIVING DONOR HAS NO PA CREDIT AVAILABLE TO THEM PERSONALLY. The credit belongs entirely to the employer who bore the wage cost.
There is no federal organ donation credit either, so a donor has no relief at either level.
## Amount and certification
The credit is based on the compensation and benefits paid during the leave, subject to a statutory per-employee cap and an overall program cap. The amount is fixed by a DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE CERTIFICATION — enter what the certification states, not your own computation.
Unlike most restricted credits, which are certified by the Department of Community and Economic Development, this one is certified by the DEPARTMENT OF REVENUE itself.
## How it reaches a PA-40
- Awarded directly to an individual or fiduciary: enter on PA-40 Schedule OC
- Awarded to a PA S corporation or partnership: reported on PA-20S/PA-65 Schedule OC, then passed through on Schedule RK-1, then entered on the owner's PA-40 Schedule OC
## Restricted credit rules apply
- NONREFUNDABLE
- Capped at the liability remaining AFTER the resident credit and Tax Forgiveness
- MARRIED TAXPAYERS MUST FILE SEPARATE RETURNS to claim it
- Cannot be passed through a second time by an owner who receives it
- A written policy on donation leave is generally required to qualify
## The joint-filing conflict
Because restricted credits require separate filing while the Child and Dependent Care Enhancement Credit requires JOINT filing under IRC Section 21(e)(2), a married couple cannot claim both in the same year. Compare the amounts before choosing.
## Required Information
- Department of Revenue certification letter and amount
- Payroll records for the leave period
- The written donation leave policy
- Schedule RK-1, if received through an entity
- Filing status, given the separate-return requirement
## Questions
- Are you an employer who paid an employee during donation leave?
- Do you have the Department of Revenue certification?
- Did the credit come to you on a Schedule RK-1?
- Are you married, and would separate filing cost you other credits?
## Common Errors
- A donor claiming it personally, when the credit belongs to the employer
- Computing the amount instead of using the certified figure
- Claiming it on a joint return
- Claiming more than the liability left after the resident credit and Tax Forgiveness
- Passing a received credit through again
## Prompt
- I donated bone marrow, is there a PA credit?
- My company paid an employee on organ donation leave.
- What is the PA organ donor credit?
