# Civic and Volunteer Credits
## Description
One credit, for living organ and bone marrow donation. Pennsylvania has no volunteer firefighter credit and NO CHARITABLE DEDUCTION OF ANY KIND.
THE REFUND DONATION LINES ARE NOT HERE. PA-40 lines 32 through 36 give money away rather than reducing tax, so they live in their own domain at state/pennsylvania/donations/. Do not confuse the ORGAN AND BONE MARROW DONOR CREDIT, which pays an employer, with donation CODE D, the Casey Memorial Organ and Tissue Donation Awareness Trust Fund, which is a gift of refund money.
## Credits in this folder

## An employer credit, not a donor credit
The distinction that matters: PENNSYLVANIA'S CREDIT GOES TO THE EMPLOYER who continues paying a donating employee, NOT TO THE DONOR.
A PA resident who donates a kidney gets NOTHING personally from the Commonwealth. Their employer may get a credit. Reaching an individual PA-40 requires the employer to be a pass-through entity and the credit to flow through on a Schedule RK-1.
There is no federal credit for organ donation in either direction, so nothing on a federal return signals that this PA credit may exist.
