# Estimated Payments and Prior-Year Credit (Lines 14 through 18)
## Description
Four lines that gather everything paid toward the year other than withholding.
```
Line 14   CREDIT FROM YOUR 2024 PA INCOME TAX RETURN
Line 15   2025 ESTIMATED INSTALLMENT PAYMENTS — fill the oval if including FORM REV-459B
Line 16   2025 EXTENSION PAYMENT
Line 17   Nonresident tax withheld from NRK-1 — see nonresident-withholding.md
Line 18   TOTAL, add lines 14, 15, 16, and 17
```
## Line 14: the prior-year credit is its own line
An overpayment from the 2024 return that you elected to apply forward appears at LINE 14, SEPARATELY from current-year estimated payments at line 15.
CONTRAST WITH THE FEDERAL RETURN, which folds a prior-year credit into the single estimated tax line. PA keeps them apart, and putting a prior-year credit on line 15 will not reconcile against the Department's records.
The amount must match what the Department actually credited. If the 2024 overpayment was adjusted on review, USE THE ADJUSTED FIGURE.
## Line 15: quarterly installments and Form REV-459B
Estimated payments are made on FORM PA-40 ES vouchers or through myPATH.
FILL THE OVAL IF INCLUDING FORM REV-459B, the Consent to Transfer, Adjust or Correct PA Estimated Personal Income Tax Account. That form moves a payment that was credited to the wrong account or the wrong year — commonly a payment posted to a spouse's account.
BECAUSE SPOUSES ARE SEPARATE TAXPAYERS FOR PA, estimated payments are credited to the SSN that made them. A joint return does not automatically merge two estimated accounts, which is why REV-459B exists.
## Line 16: extension payment
Any payment made with a PA extension request.
AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO FILE IS NOT AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO PAY. Tax remains due April 15, 2026, and a payment made at extension time belongs here rather than at line 15.
## Who must pay PA estimated tax
Generally, a taxpayer expecting more than $8,000 of PA-taxable income not subject to withholding must make estimated payments. The threshold is stated in the PA-40 instructions and should be confirmed for the filing year.
Due dates are the 15th of April, June, September, and January.
FARMERS: a taxpayer with AT LEAST TWO-THIRDS OF GROSS INCOME FROM FARMING has a SINGLE estimated payment deadline rather than four, and fills the FARMERS oval at the top of the return.
## Why PA taxpayers need estimates more than federal taxpayers
PA withholding is a flat 3.07% with NO ALLOWANCES TO ADJUST. A taxpayer with business, rental, or investment income cannot compensate by changing a withholding certificate. Estimated payments are the only lever.
## Required Information
- The 2024 overpayment credited forward, as adjusted by the Department
- Each estimated payment with its date and the SSN it was credited to
- The extension payment
- Form REV-459B, where a payment must be moved
## Questions
- Did you apply a 2024 overpayment forward?
- What estimated payments did you make, and under whose Social Security number?
- Did you make a payment with an extension request?
- Is two-thirds of your gross income from farming?
## Common Errors
- Reporting a prior-year credit on line 15 instead of line 14
- Reporting an extension payment as an estimated installment
- Assuming a joint return merges two spouses' estimated accounts without REV-459B
- Using an unadjusted prior-year overpayment figure
- Treating an extension to file as an extension to pay
- Missing the farmers oval and the single-deadline rule
## Prompt
- I made quarterly payments to Pennsylvania.
- I applied last year's refund to this year.
- My estimated payment went to my wife's account.
