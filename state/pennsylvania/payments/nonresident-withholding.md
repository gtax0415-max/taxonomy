# Nonresident Tax Withheld from Schedule NRK-1 (Line 17)
## Description
NONRESIDENTS ONLY. Pennsylvania requires a partnership or PA S corporation to WITHHOLD PA TAX ON INCOME ALLOCABLE TO ITS NONRESIDENT OWNERS. The amount is reported to the owner on PA SCHEDULE NRK-1 and claimed here.
## Why the line exists
PA cannot rely on a nonresident owner voluntarily filing a PA return for their share of partnership income. The entity withholds at source instead, at the 3.07% rate, and remits on the owner's behalf.
The owner then files a PA-40 as a nonresident, reports the PA-source income, and claims the withholding at line 17 — usually producing a refund where the withholding exceeded the actual liability.
## RK-1 versus NRK-1
| Schedule | Issued to | Withholding line |
|---|---|---|
| PA SCHEDULE RK-1 | RESIDENT owners | No line 17 entry; any withholding is ordinary line 13 |
| PA SCHEDULE NRK-1 | NONRESIDENT owners | LINE 17 |
An entity with both resident and nonresident owners issues both. A PART-YEAR owner may receive BOTH an RK-1 for the resident period and an NRK-1 for the nonresident period.
## Do not confuse line 17 with the other NRK-1 lines
An NRK-1 also carries income by class, and for credits it carries TOTAL OTHER CREDITS at LINE 7, which goes to SCHEDULE OC, not here. Line 17 is withholding only.
## The $250 penalty
An entity that fails to file the PA-20S/PA-65 Information Return may be penalised $250, AND THE SAME $250 APPLIES TO EACH MISSING RK-1 OR NRK-1. An owner who has not received one should ask, because the entity has an enforceable obligation to provide it.
## The federal contrast
THERE IS NO FEDERAL EQUIVALENT. A federal partnership does not withhold on a domestic partner's distributive share; the partner simply reports it. Nonresident withholding is purely a state mechanism, and the federal K-1 carries no corresponding figure.
## Required Information
- PA Schedule NRK-1 from each entity
- The withholding amount and the entity's FEIN
- Confirmation of nonresident status for the period
## Questions
- Are you a nonresident with PA partnership or S corporation income?
- Did you receive a PA Schedule NRK-1?
- Was PA tax withheld and remitted on your behalf?
- Were you a part-year resident, which may produce both an RK-1 and an NRK-1?
## Common Errors
- A RESIDENT using line 17; residents claim withholding at line 13
- Reporting NRK-1 line 7 Total Other Credits on line 17 instead of Schedule OC
- Omitting the line entirely and overpaying, because the withholding was never claimed
- Failing to file a PA-40 at all as a nonresident, leaving the withholding unrecovered
## Prompt
- I am a nonresident partner in a PA partnership.
- My NRK-1 shows PA tax withheld.
- I live out of state but got a PA K-1.
