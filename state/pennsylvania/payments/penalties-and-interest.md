# Penalties and Interest (Line 27)
## Description
All penalties and interest you owe from filing late, paying late, or underpaying estimated tax. Entered on line 27 WITH A CODE in the block provided.
## The four codes
| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| L | LATE PAYMENT penalty and interest, FAILURE TO FILE penalty and interest, or LATE FILING penalty and interest is included |
| E | ONLY the ESTIMATED UNDERPAYMENT PENALTY is included |
| B | BOTH late payment penalty and interest AND estimated underpayment penalty are included |
| X | THERE IS NO ESTIMATED UNDERPAYMENT PENALTY DUE |
CODE X IS THE ONE PEOPLE MISS. It is an affirmative statement that no estimated penalty applies — used where the Department might otherwise compute one, for instance where an annualized method or the prior-year safe harbour applies.
## Fill the oval if including REV-1630 or REV-1630A
FILL IN THE OVAL ON LINE 27 if you are including:
- FORM REV-1630, Underpayment of Estimated Tax by Individuals
- FORM REV-1630A, Underpayment of Estimated Tax by Individual FARMERS
The farmer version exists because a qualifying farmer has a SINGLE estimated payment deadline rather than four. Using REV-1630 for a farmer computes the penalty against quarterly deadlines that do not apply.
## Line 27 sits on BOTH sides of the arithmetic
This is easy to get wrong:
```
Line 26   TAX DUE, if Line 12 + Line 25 exceeds Line 24
Line 27   PENALTIES AND INTEREST
Line 28   TOTAL PAYMENT DUE
Line 29   OVERPAYMENT, if Line 24 exceeds Line 12 + Line 25 + LINE 27
```
LINE 27 IS SUBTRACTED IN THE OVERPAYMENT TEST AT LINE 29. A taxpayer with a small overpayment and an estimated underpayment penalty may end up owing rather than receiving a refund. The penalty is not netted away quietly — it moves the taxpayer across the line.
## Late filing versus late payment
Interest runs on unpaid tax from the original due date regardless of any extension. AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO FILE IS NOT AN EXTENSION OF TIME TO PAY, so a taxpayer who extended and paid late still owes interest from April 15.
## The estimated underpayment penalty
Applies where required estimated payments were not made or were made late. PA's flat 3.07% withholding cannot be adjusted through a withholding certificate, so a taxpayer with business, rental, or investment income who does not make estimates has no other way to avoid this.
Compute on REV-1630, which also carries the annualized income method for taxpayers whose income arrived unevenly during the year.
## The federal contrast
| | Federal | Pennsylvania |
|---|---|---|
| Estimated penalty form | Form 2210 | FORM REV-1630 |
| Farmer variant | Same form, different box | SEPARATE FORM REV-1630A |
| Penalty line on the return | Line 38, after the balance due | LINE 27, INSIDE the overpayment test |
| Code required | No | YES: L, E, B, or X |
The federal return computes the balance due first and adds the penalty after. PA folds the penalty into the test that decides whether there is an overpayment at all.
## Required Information
- Filing date and payment date
- Prior-year PA tax liability, for the safe harbour
- Each estimated payment with its date
- Gross income by source, for the farming test
- REV-1630 or REV-1630A as applicable
## Questions
- Are you filing or paying after April 15?
- Did you make required estimated payments on time?
- Is two-thirds of your gross income from farming, requiring REV-1630A?
- Does your income arrive unevenly, making the annualized method worth using?
## Common Errors
- Leaving the code block blank
- Using REV-1630 for a farmer instead of REV-1630A
- Forgetting the oval when including either form
- Omitting line 27 from the line 29 overpayment test and expecting a refund that does not arrive
- Assuming an extension to file postponed the interest
## Prompt
- I filed my PA return late.
- I did not make estimated payments to Pennsylvania.
- What is code X on line 27?
