# Total PA Tax Withheld (Line 13)
## Description
All Pennsylvania income tax withheld on your behalf during the year, from every source. Enter the total on line 13.
## Where PA withholding appears
| Source | Where |
|---|---|
| Wages | FORM W-2 BOX 17, with PA in Box 15 |
| Retirement distributions | Form 1099-R state withholding |
| Nonemployee compensation | Form 1099-MISC or 1099-NEC state withholding |
| GAMBLING AND LOTTERY | FORM W-2G BOX 15, carried via PA SCHEDULE T LINE 7 |
| Pass-through entities | PA Schedule RK-1 or NRK-1 |
## Withholding is a flat 3.07%
PA WITHHOLDING HAS NO ALLOWANCES, NO EXEMPTIONS, AND NO W-4 EQUIVALENT TO ADJUST. An employer withholds a flat 3.07% of PA-taxable compensation.
CONSEQUENCE: a PA taxpayer cannot fine-tune withholding the way a federal taxpayer can. If the return produces a balance due, the remedy is estimated payments, not a revised withholding certificate.
CONTRAST WITH THE FEDERAL SYSTEM, where Form W-4 controls withholding through filing status, dependents, and other adjustments.
## When withholding exceeds 3.07%
If an employer withheld PA tax AT A RATE HIGHER THAN 3.07% at your request, INCLUDE A STATEMENT FROM THE EMPLOYER verifying the rate and the amount withheld for the year. An unexplained excess is one of the ten conditions that require a W-2 copy with an e-filed return.
## Withholding on nontaxable income
PA tax withheld from income that turns out NOT to be PA-taxable IS STILL CLAIMED ON LINE 13.
Two common cases:
- MILITARY PAY earned on active duty outside PA is not taxable, but any PA tax withheld goes on line 13 and comes back as a refund
- A RECIPROCAL-STATE RESIDENT whose PA employer withheld PA tax reports ZERO compensation on line 1a and the full withholding on line 13. That is a refund claim
## Documentation
PAPER RETURNS: include copies of ALL Forms W-2 and ALL Forms 1099-R regardless of taxability, plus 1099-MISC and 1099-NEC where PA tax was withheld.
E-FILED RETURNS: submit forms only where PA tax withholding is shown, or where one of the ten listed W-2 conditions applies.
A W-2G COPY MUST BE INCLUDED where PA tax was withheld from gambling winnings.
## Local tax is NOT here
LOCAL EARNED INCOME TAX and the PHILADELPHIA WAGE TAX are withheld separately and DO NOT APPEAR ON THE PA-40. They are reported to the local collector or the City, never to the Commonwealth, and they are not claimable on line 13.
A W-2 for a Philadelphia worker shows both PA state withholding and City wage tax withholding. ONLY THE STATE FIGURE GOES ON LINE 13.
## Required Information
- Every W-2 Box 17 amount with PA in Box 15
- 1099-R, 1099-MISC, 1099-NEC state withholding
- W-2G Box 15, routed through Schedule T line 7
- RK-1 or NRK-1 withholding
- An employer statement where the rate exceeded 3.07%
## Questions
- How much PA tax was withheld, across all employers and payers?
- Was PA tax withheld from income that is not PA-taxable?
- Does your W-2 show local wage tax as well, which does not belong here?
- Was any withholding at a rate above 3.07%?
## Common Errors
- Including LOCAL EIT or Philadelphia Wage Tax on line 13
- Omitting W-2G withholding, which routes through Schedule T
- Omitting withholding on nontaxable military pay, forfeiting the refund
- A reciprocal-state resident reporting the compensation instead of claiming the withholding back
- Expecting to adjust PA withholding with a W-4 equivalent
## Prompt
- How much PA tax was withheld from my pay?
- My W-2 shows Philadelphia tax too, does that go on the PA-40?
- I live in New Jersey and my PA employer withheld PA tax.
