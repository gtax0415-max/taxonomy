# Age 65 or Older and Blind Boxes
## Description
Boxes for each spouse who is 65 or older or blind and qualified for the federal additional standard deduction. Missouri uses the federal standard deduction, so each box raises the line 14 amount.

## Amounts for 2026
- Additional amount per condition: $2,050 single or head of household; $1,650 married or qualifying surviving spouse (Rev. Proc. 2025-32; 2025 was $2,000 / $1,600)
- Basic 2026 standard deduction: $16,100 single or MFS; $24,150 head of household; $32,200 married filing combined or qualifying surviving spouse
- Age 65 by December 31, 2026: born before January 2, 1962
- The federal $6,000 senior deduction (2025 federal law) does NOT apply on the Missouri return

## Eligibility
- Qualified for the federal additional standard deduction

## Notes
- There is no separate line; the additional amount goes into line 14 with the basic deduction (2026 form not yet published)
- These boxes do not by themselves qualify a person for the property tax credit or the Social Security subtraction; those have their own age tests (65 and 62)
- MO-1040 also has separate Age 62–64 boxes
- Example: single filer age 66 → $16,100 + $2,050 = $18,150

## Required Information
- Birth dates; blindness status

## Questions
- Were you or your spouse 65 or older or blind at the end of 2026?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 amounts ($2,000 / $1,600)
- Adding the federal $6,000 senior deduction
