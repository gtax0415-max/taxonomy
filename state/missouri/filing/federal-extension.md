# Federal Extension Box
## Description
Box showing an approved federal extension. A federal extension automatically extends the Missouri filing deadline; no separate Missouri extension is needed unless paying.

## Amounts for 2026
- Original due date: Thursday, April 15, 2027
- Extended due date: Friday, October 15, 2027 (computed; 2026 instructions not yet published)
- Interest runs on tax unpaid after April 15, 2027 (7% for 2027 per DOR's posted rate), plus a 5% late-payment addition

## Eligibility
- Approved federal extension

## Notes
- The extension extends the time to file, not to pay
- Maximum extension 6 months (longer only if outside the U.S.)
- Example: filer with a federal extension checks the box and files by October 15, 2027

## Required Information
- Copy of Form 4868

## Questions
- Did you file a federal extension for 2026?
- Do you expect to owe Missouri tax?

## Common Errors
- Not attaching Form 4868
- Assuming the extension delays payment

## Prompt
- Do I need a separate Missouri extension?
