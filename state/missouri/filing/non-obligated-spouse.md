# Non-Obligated Spouse
## Description
Box for a spouse who does not want their share of a combined refund applied to the other spouse's debts owed to Missouri agencies.

## Amounts for 2026
- No amount on the return; DOR splits the refund
- Offsets apply to refunds of $25 or more

## Eligibility
- Combined return where only one spouse owes a debt certified by a Missouri state agency

## Notes
- Covered debts: child support enforced by the Family Support Division, Missouri back taxes, state agency debts (including community colleges and housing authorities), court costs and fines
- IRS debts are excluded
- The DOR instructions describe the split as each spouse's Missouri withholding minus each spouse's tax; the exact method is not fully stated
- The debtor has 30 days after notice to request a hearing

## Required Information
- Which spouse owes the debt

## Questions
- Does your spouse owe Missouri child support, back taxes, or another state debt?

## Common Errors
- Expecting the box to protect against an IRS offset
- Checking the box on a single return

## Prompt
- How do I protect my part of a Missouri refund from my spouse's debt?
