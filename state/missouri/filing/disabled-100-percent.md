# 100% Disabled Box
## Description
Box for a taxpayer or spouse unable to engage in any substantial gainful activity because of a physical or mental impairment expected to result in death or to last at least 12 months. Its main use is qualifying for the property tax credit.

## Amounts for 2026
- No amount
- Property tax credit 2026 limits apply if qualifying through disability

## Eligibility
- Meets the federal "substantial gainful activity" disability definition
- Need not have worked before the disability to qualify for the property tax credit

## Notes
- The statute also lifts the "included in federal AGI and not otherwise deducted" limit on the public pension exemption for people who are 100% disabled under federal guidelines; how the form applies this is not stated
- The age-62 test for the Social Security subtraction is waived for Social Security Disability benefits regardless of this box
- Example: a filer receiving SSDI at 50 checks the box and may qualify for the property tax credit

## Required Information
- Disability documentation and onset date

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse unable to work because of a disability expected to last at least a year?

## Common Errors
- Checking the box for a partial disability rating
- Assuming the box changes the military or railroad retirement subtractions

## Prompt
- What counts as 100% disabled for Missouri?
