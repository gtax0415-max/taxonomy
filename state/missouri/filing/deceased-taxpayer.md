# Deceased Taxpayer or Spouse
## Description
Boxes for a taxpayer or spouse who died during 2026. A combined return may still be filed for the year of death.

## Amounts for 2026
- No amount; the box flags the return

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer or spouse died in 2026

## Notes
- Form MO-1310D (refund claim for a deceased taxpayer) is NOT needed by a surviving spouse filing a combined return, or by a personal representative filing the original return; others claiming the refund file it
- The MO-1040 instructions also list a power of attorney (Form 2827) and the death certificate when a refund is due; this conflicts with MO-1310D's exceptions, so follow the narrower rule only after confirming with DOR
- Age tests for a person who died use the date of death
- Example: spouse died September 14, 2026 → check the spouse's Deceased box and file combined

## Required Information
- Date of death and who is filing

## Questions
- Did you or your spouse die during 2026? On what date?
- Who is filing the return and claiming any refund?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting the box
- Filing MO-1310D when the surviving spouse files combined (not required)
