# Military Personnel and Spouses
## Description
How military pay is treated. A Missouri home of record is presumed Missouri domicile.

## Amounts for 2026
- Line 18: 100% of active duty military pay, including signing bonuses, included in federal AGI
- Line 19: 100% of National Guard and reserve inactive duty and annual training pay (fully phased in since 2024), including Guard and reserve signing bonuses (from 2025)
- Nonresident servicemembers stationed in Missouri subtract their military pay; spouses may subtract their income under the federal spouse residency laws

## Eligibility
- Members of the Armed Forces, National Guard, and reserves

## Notes
- State Active Duty pay is NOT deductible on line 18
- Amounts already subtracted elsewhere cannot be deducted again
- Military retirement is a separate subtraction (MO-A line 10)
- Example: Missouri Guard member deducts drill pay on line 19

## Required Information
- Active and inactive duty pay amounts
- Home of record and duty station

## Questions
- Are you or your spouse in the military, Guard, or reserves?
- What is your home of record, and where are you stationed?

## Common Errors
- Deducting State Active Duty pay
- Treating a Missouri home-of-record member stationed elsewhere as a nonresident without meeting the test

## Prompt
- Is my military pay taxable in Missouri?
