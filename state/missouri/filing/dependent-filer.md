# Claimed as a Dependent
## Description
Status box for a filer whom someone else can claim as a dependent. The standard deduction is the federal dependent standard deduction.

## Amounts for 2026
- Standard deduction: the larger of $1,350 or earned income + $450, but not more than the regular amount ($16,100 single) (Rev. Proc. 2025-32)
- Not eligible for the Working Family Tax Credit

## Eligibility
- Checked "someone can claim you as a dependent" on the federal return

## Notes
- If the federal status is married filing jointly, the Missouri status stays married filing combined
- Example: teenager with $6,000 of wages → $6,000 + $450 = $6,450 standard deduction

## Required Information
- Earned income

## Questions
- Can anyone claim you as a dependent?

## Common Errors
- Taking the full standard deduction
- Claiming the Working Family credit

## Prompt
- I am a dependent; how do I file in Missouri?
