# Filing and Payment Due Dates
## Description
Dates for filing the 2026 return, paying the tax, and making 2026 estimated payments.

## Amounts for 2026
- Return and payment due: Thursday, April 15, 2027
- With a federal or Missouri extension: file by Friday, October 15, 2027; the tax is still due April 15, 2027
- Estimated payments: April 15, June 15, September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027 (from the 2026 Form MO-1040ES)
- Late payment: 5% addition plus 7% interest; late filing: 5% per month up to 25%

## Eligibility
- Every filer

## Notes
- Fiscal-year returns are due the 15th day of the 4th month after the year ends
- Example: a return mailed April 15, 2027 is on time

## Required Information
- Planned filing and payment dates

- When will you file and pay?
- Did you file a federal extension?

## Common Errors
- Paying late because of the filing extension
