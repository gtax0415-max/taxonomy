# Age 62 Through 64 Box
## Description
Box for each spouse who was 62, 63, or 64 on December 31, 2026. The Social Security subtraction requires age 62 (this box or the 65 box).

## Amounts for 2026
- Age 62–64 at year-end: born January 2, 1962 through January 1, 1965 (the federal "day before your birthday" convention; a Missouri statement of that convention was not found)
- Social Security exemption: 100% of taxable benefits at age 62+, regardless of income (since 2024)

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer or spouse aged 62–64 at the end of 2026

## Notes
- Social Security Disability benefits are exempt at any age
- Example: spouse born March 1, 1963 is 63 at the end of 2026 → box checked

## Required Information
- Each spouse's date of birth

## Questions
- What are your and your spouse's dates of birth?

## Common Errors
- Leaving the box blank and losing the Social Security subtraction
