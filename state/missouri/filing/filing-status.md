# Filing Status
## Description
Missouri uses the federal filing status: single, married filing combined, married filing separately, head of household, or qualifying widow(er), plus a "claimed as a dependent" box.

## Amounts for 2026
- Standard deduction: $16,100 single or MFS; $24,150 head of household; $32,200 married filing combined or qualifying widow(er)
- Line 15 additional exemption: $1,400 for head of household and qualifying widow(er)
- Federal tax deduction cap: $5,000 individual, $10,000 combined
- Private pension exemption ($6,000 max) phases out above Missouri AGI of $25,000 single/HOH/QW, $32,000 combined, $16,000 MFS

## Eligibility
- Every filer

## Notes
- A joint federal return can be filed as married filing combined in Missouri; married filing separately also follows the federal choice
- Dependent filers check "claimed as a dependent" instead of single
- Example: federal head of household → Missouri head of household with $1,400 on line 15

## Required Information
- Federal filing status; dependent status

## Questions
- What filing status did you use on your federal return?
- Can someone claim you as a dependent?

## Common Errors
- Choosing married filing separately in Missouri after a joint federal return without meeting the rules
- Missing the $1,400 line 15 exemption

## Prompt
- Can I file separately in Missouri if we filed jointly federally?
