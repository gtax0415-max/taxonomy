# Federal Conformity and 2026 Law
## Description
Missouri starts from federal AGI under the federal Code as currently in effect, so federal changes that affect AGI carry over automatically. Federal deductions taken below AGI do not.

## Amounts for 2026
- Not allowed in Missouri: the 2025 federal deductions for tips, overtime, car-loan interest, and the senior deduction (DOR: they apply only at the federal level)
- Missouri follows current federal bonus depreciation (no decoupling)
- Tax rate: top rate 4.7% (2026 withholding brackets: 0% to $1,348, rising to 4.7% over $9,436; return chart not yet published)
- New for 2026: specie capital gain subtraction (HB 754); property tax credit limits raised (HB 594 & 508 of 2025)
- Continuing from 2025: 100% capital gain subtraction for individuals
- No individual rate or deduction change from the 2026 legislative session

## Eligibility
- Every return

## Notes
- HJR 173 (a constitutional amendment to phase out the income tax) needs voter approval and does not affect 2026
- Example: a server with a $6,000 federal tips deduction has the same Missouri AGI as without it

## Required Information
- None

## Questions
- None

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the federal tips or overtime deduction on the Missouri return

## Prompt
- Does Missouri allow the no tax on tips deduction?
