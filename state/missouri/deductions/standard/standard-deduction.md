# Missouri Standard Deduction
## Description
Missouri's standard deduction is the allowable federal standard deduction, including the additional amounts for age 65 or older and blindness.

## Amounts for 2026
- $16,100 single or married filing separately
- $24,150 head of household
- $32,200 married filing combined or qualifying widow(er)
- Additional per condition: $2,050 single/head of household; $1,650 married/qualifying widow(er)
- Dependent filer: the greater of $1,350 or earned income + $450
- The federal $6,000 senior deduction is NOT allowed

## Eligibility
- Filers who took the federal standard deduction

## Notes
- Must itemize in Missouri if required to itemize federally; cannot itemize in Missouri after taking the federal standard deduction
- Example: head of household → $24,150

## Required Information
- Filing status, age/blind boxes, dependent status

## Questions
- Did you take the standard deduction on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 amounts
- Adding the federal senior deduction
