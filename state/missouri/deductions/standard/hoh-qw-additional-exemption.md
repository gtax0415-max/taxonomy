# Head of Household and Qualifying Widow(er) Additional Exemption
## Description
An extra $1,400 for filers who qualify federally as head of household or qualifying surviving spouse.

## Amounts for 2026
- $1,400 (fixed since 1999; not indexed)

## Eligibility
- Same filing status on the federal return

## Notes
- Added on top of the standard or itemized deduction
- Example: head of household → $1,400 on line 15

## Required Information
- Federal filing status

## Questions
- None (from filing status)

## Common Errors
- Claiming it on a single or married return

## Prompt
- What is the $1,400 exemption on my Missouri return?
