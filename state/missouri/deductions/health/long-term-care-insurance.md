# Long-Term Care Insurance Deduction
## Description
100% of qualified long-term care insurance premiums that were not reimbursed and not already deducted federally.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of nonreimbursed qualified premiums, no dollar cap
- Reduced by the part included in federal itemized medical deductions

## Eligibility
- Paid premiums for a qualified long-term care policy

## Notes
- Death benefits and extended riders do not qualify
- Long-term care premiums do not count toward the health insurance premium subtraction
- Example: standard-deduction filer with $2,400 of premiums → deduct $2,400

## Required Information
- Premiums paid; medical expenses itemized federally

## Questions
- Do you pay long-term care insurance premiums?

## Common Errors
- Including death benefit or rider costs
- Deducting premiums already itemized federally

## Prompt
- Can I deduct long-term care premiums in Missouri?
