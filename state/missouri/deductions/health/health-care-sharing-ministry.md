# Health Care Sharing Ministry Deduction
## Description
Amounts paid as a member of a qualifying health care sharing ministry, to the extent not deducted on the federal return.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of amounts paid during 2026, less any part deducted federally
- No dollar cap; no change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Member of a health care sharing ministry as defined in Missouri law

## Notes
- Sharing ministry payments are not insurance, so they do not go in the health insurance premium subtraction
- Example: $3,600 of ministry shares, nothing deducted federally → deduct $3,600

## Required Information
- Amounts paid in 2026

## Questions
- Are you a member of a health care sharing ministry? How much did you pay in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Deducting amounts already itemized federally
- Also claiming them as health insurance premiums

## Prompt
- Is a health care sharing ministry deductible in Missouri?
