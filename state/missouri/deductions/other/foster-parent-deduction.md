# Foster Parent Tax Deduction
## Description
Deduction for expenses a foster parent paid directly for a child in foster care.

## Amounts for 2026
- Up to $5,000 per return ($2,500 per spouse if married filing separately)
- Full limit requires at least 6 months (183 days) of care during the year; fewer days prorate the limit (days ÷ 183)
- The cap is the same regardless of how many children
- No change for 2026; no sunset

## Eligibility
- Foster parent as defined in Missouri law caring for a child in foster care in 2026

## Notes
- Qualifying expenses: food, clothing, medical costs incurred directly for the child
- Not qualifying: general household costs, utilities, shared electronics, amounts paid by public assistance
- Days do not have to be consecutive
- Example: foster parent with 200 days of care and $6,000 of direct expenses → $5,000

## Required Information
- Expenses by category; days of care

## Questions
- Were you a foster parent in 2026? For how many days?
- What did you spend directly on the foster child?

## Common Errors
- Including household costs or reimbursed amounts
- Not prorating for fewer than 183 days
