# Enterprise Zone or Rural Empowerment Zone Modification
## Description
Exempts income from a DED-approved new business facility: one-half of the Missouri taxable income attributed to the facility in an enterprise zone, or the full amount in a rural empowerment zone.

## Amounts for 2026
- Enterprise zone: 50% of the facility's Missouri taxable income, for a vested period of up to 10 years
- Rural empowerment zone: 100% (per the instructions)
- Effectively a legacy line: DED lists the enterprise zone benefit as closed to new applications, and the rural empowerment zone statutes appear to be repealed

## Eligibility
- Prior DED approval and a benefit period still open in 2026 (unlikely)

## Notes
- Product scope: not computed by the template
- Different from the Enhanced Enterprise Zone tax credit (MO-TC code NEZ)

## Required Information
- DED approval and facility income

## Questions
- Do you hold a DED approval for an enterprise zone modification still in effect in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without DED approval
- Confusing it with the Enhanced Enterprise Zone credit

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri enterprise zone modification?
