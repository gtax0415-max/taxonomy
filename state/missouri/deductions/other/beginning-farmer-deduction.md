# Farmland Sold, Rented, Leased or Crop-Shared to a Beginning Farmer
## Description
Deduction for capital gain from selling farmland, or income from renting, leasing, or crop-sharing farmland, to a certified beginning farmer.

## Amounts for 2026
- 21A sold: 100% of the first $2,000,000 of gain, then 80% / 60% / 40% / 20% of each next $1,000,000; nothing above $6,000,000
- 21B rented or leased and 21C crop-shared: 100% of the income, up to $25,000 per tax year
- No change for 2026; no sunset

## Eligibility
- MASBDA certification for the transaction (application fees $300 sale, $200 lease or crop-share, $100 annual renewal)
- Lease or crop-share agreements of 10 years or less
- Gains received on or after August 28, 2023

## Notes
- Since 2025 the 100% capital gain subtraction may already remove the sale gain; avoid double counting
- Attach the certification, Form 5955, and the federal return
- DOR's FAQ still names an older form number

## Required Information
- Certification, sale gain or rental income, agreement terms

## Questions
- Did you sell, rent, lease, or crop-share farmland to a certified beginning farmer?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without MASBDA certification
- Exceeding $25,000 for rent or crop-share
- Also subtracting the same gain as a capital gain

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri beginning farmer deduction?
