# Federal Income Tax Deduction
## Description
A partial deduction for federal income tax, using a percentage that falls as Missouri AGI rises.

## Amounts for 2026
- Percentage by Missouri AGI (line 6): 35% at $25,000 or less; 25% at $25,001–$50,000; 15% at $50,001–$100,000; 5% at $100,001–$125,000; 0% at $125,001 or more
- Cap: $5,000 ($10,000 married filing combined)
- No change for 2026; line references are from the 2025 federal forms (confirm on 2026 forms)

## Eligibility
- Every filer with federal tax after credits

## Notes
- Federal withholding and estimated payments are not federal tax
- Example: Missouri AGI $58,750, federal tax $4,200 → 15% = $630

## Required Information
- Federal tax lines listed above

## Questions
- What was your federal tax after credits?

## Common Errors
- Entering federal withholding instead of tax
- Not subtracting the earned income credit

## Prompt
- Can I deduct federal taxes on my Missouri return?
