# Contributions to a Qualified 529 Plan
## Description
Contributions to MOST (Missouri's 529 plan) or any other state's qualified 529 plan are subtracted, up to an annual limit per taxpayer.

## Amounts for 2026
- Up to $8,000 per taxpayer; $16,000 on a married-filing-combined return
- Not indexed; unchanged for 2026
- 2026 bills to limit the subtraction to Missouri's own plan (HB 2116 / SB 1440) are not in DOR's 2026 change list and appear not to have been enacted

## Eligibility
- Contributions made in 2026 by the claimant, documented in the claimant's name

## Notes
- Rollovers from another 529 are not new contributions
- A later nonqualified withdrawal must be added back (MO-A line 3)
- A refund deposited to MOST through the return reaches the account in the following year
- Example: a married couple contributes $5,000 → subtract $5,000, split by who contributed

## Required Information
- Contribution dates, amounts, and contributor
- Plan name

## Questions
- How much did you contribute to 529 plans in 2026, and from whose account?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting more than $8,000 per person
- Including rollovers
- Putting a spouse's contribution under the wrong name
