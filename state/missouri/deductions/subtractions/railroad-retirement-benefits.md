# Railroad Retirement Benefits
## Description
Benefits administered by the Railroad Retirement Board that are included in federal AGI. States cannot tax them.

## Amounts for 2026
- All Tier I and Tier II benefits, and RRB sick, disability, and unemployment pay, to the extent in federal AGI
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- RRB benefits included in federal AGI

## Notes
- Reported as a checkbox on MO-A line 11, not a separate line
- Missouri does not tax Social Security either, but that is a separate subtraction
- Example: $9,000 of Tier II benefits in AGI → subtract $9,000

## Required Information
- Taxable amounts from RRB-1099 and RRB-1099-R

## Questions
- Do you receive railroad retirement benefits?

## Common Errors
- Also including the Tier I amount in the Social Security subtraction

## Prompt
- Are railroad benefits taxed in Missouri?
