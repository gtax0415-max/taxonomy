# Missouri Depreciation Adjustment
## Description
Recovery of the federal 30% bonus depreciation that Missouri required taxpayers to add back for property bought July 1, 2002 through June 30, 2003, including the remaining amount when that property is sold.

## Amounts for 2026
- The remaining previously added-back depreciation, recovered over the asset's life or on sale
- Missouri does NOT decouple from current federal bonus depreciation (including the 2025 federal law), so no new adjustment arises for 2026 purchases

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer who added back 2002–2003 bonus depreciation

## Notes
- Rare for 2026; most such property is fully recovered or sold

## Required Information
- Amount previously added back and amount already recovered

## Questions
- Did you add back bonus depreciation on property bought in 2002 or 2003, and do you still own it or sell it in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Making a Missouri adjustment for current-year bonus depreciation

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri depreciation adjustment?
