# Contributions to an ABLE Account
## Description
Contributions to an ABLE account (tax-advantaged savings for a person with a disability).

## Amounts for 2026
- Up to $8,000 per taxpayer, $16,000 on a combined return
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Contributions made in 2026 by the taxpayer
- The 2015 law limited it to Missouri's ABLE program (MO ABLE); whether other states' ABLE plans now qualify is not confirmed

## Notes
- A nonqualified ABLE withdrawal must be added back (MO-A addition line 6)
- Example: $2,500 contribution → subtract $2,500

## Required Information
- Contributions by date and account

## Questions
- Did you contribute to an ABLE account in 2026, and which state's plan?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding $8,000 per person
- Subtracting rollovers that are not new contributions

- Are ABLE contributions deductible in Missouri?
