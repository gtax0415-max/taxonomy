# Business Income Deduction (20%)
## Description
20% of net Missouri-source business income from self-employment, partnerships, S corporations, and farms.

## Amounts for 2026
- 20% of combined business income, if above zero
- At the 20% maximum since 2019; no change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Income from the regular course of business, from Missouri sources
- Counts: Schedule C profit, Schedule E Part II (partnerships and S corporations), Schedule F, Form 4835
- Excluded: Schedule E Part I rents and royalties, wages, and agricultural disaster relief already subtracted

## Notes
- No federal-style limits for specified service businesses or W-2 wages
- Losses net against profits across all schedules; a net loss gives $0
- Computed for each spouse
- Example: Schedule C profit $40,000 → subtract $8,000

## Required Information
- Net income from each qualifying schedule, by spouse

## Questions
- Do you have self-employment, partnership, S corporation, or farm income?

## Common Errors
- Including Schedule E Part I rental income
- Not netting losses
- Including non-Missouri-source income for a nonresident

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri business income deduction?
