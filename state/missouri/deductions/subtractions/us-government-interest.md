# Interest on U.S. Government Obligations
## Description
Interest on direct U.S. obligations that federal law exempts from state tax and that is included in federal AGI.

## Amounts for 2026
- Full interest on U.S. savings bonds, Treasury bills, notes, and bonds
- Mutual funds: only the share of dividends from direct U.S. obligations
- Reduced by related interest and expenses only if they total $500 or more
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Interest included in federal AGI

## Notes
- Each security must be identified; a general description such as "U.S. Government securities" is disallowed
- Not eligible: GNMA, FNMA, and FHLMC interest, and interest on federal tax refunds
- Example: $1,100 of Treasury bill interest → subtract $1,100

## Required Information
- List of each security and its interest
- Fund percentage statements

## Questions
- Did you receive interest on U.S. savings bonds or Treasuries?
- Do you own mutual funds that hold U.S. Treasuries?

## Common Errors
- A general description instead of each security
- Subtracting GNMA or Fannie Mae interest

## Prompt
- Is Treasury interest taxable in Missouri?
