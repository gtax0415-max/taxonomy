# State Income Tax Refund
## Description
A state income tax refund that was included in federal AGI (taxable because the taxpayer itemized in the earlier year).

## Amounts for 2026
- The amount of the refund reported on federal Schedule 1, line 1
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Refund included in 2026 federal AGI

## Notes
- If the refund was not taxable federally (standard deduction last year), there is nothing to subtract
- Example: $640 Missouri refund taxed federally → subtract $640

## Required Information
- Refund amount on Schedule 1, line 1

## Questions
- Did you report a state tax refund on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a refund that was not in federal AGI

## Prompt
- Is my state refund taxable in Missouri?
