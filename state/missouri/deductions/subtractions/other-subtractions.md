# Partnership, Fiduciary, S Corporation and Other Subtractions
## Description
MO-A line 11 collects pass-through subtractions and items without their own line. Checkboxes: Partnership, Fiduciary, S Corporation; Railroad Retirement Benefits; Nonresident Military; Combat Pay; Build America and Recovery Zone Bond Interest; Missouri Public-Private Transportation Act; Net Operating Loss; Business Interest (163(j)); Other.

## Amounts for 2026
- Under "Other": trust accumulation distributions; 25% of gain on a qualifying low-income housing project sale; family development account deposits (up to $2,000) and approved withdrawals; MOHELA bond interest; condemnation gain; 50% of gain on a sale to a Missouri ESOP; IMA employer contributions; 100% of qualifying broadband grants; marijuana business expenses disallowed federally
- No new line 11 items for 2026

## Eligibility
- Each item's own rules

## Notes
- Since 2025 the 100% capital gain subtraction already removes capital gains, so check the low-income housing, ESOP, and condemnation items for double counting
- Railroad retirement and combat pay have their own files
- Example: $2,000 family development account deposit → subtract $2,000 under "Other"

## Required Information
- Item type and amount, with required attachments

## Questions
- Did you receive Missouri subtractions from a partnership, S corporation, or trust?
- Did you have any of the "other" items listed?

## Common Errors
- Claiming without the required certification or attachment
- Subtracting a capital gain here that line 18 already covers
