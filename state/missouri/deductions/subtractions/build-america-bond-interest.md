# Build America and Recovery Zone Bond Interest
## Description
Interest from Build America Bonds and Recovery Zone Bonds that is included in federal AGI.

## Amounts for 2026
- The bond interest included in federal AGI
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Interest from Build America or Recovery Zone Bonds (issued 2009–2010)

## Notes
- The statute exempts only bonds issued by Missouri or Missouri entities; the MO-1040 instructions do not state that limit. Confirm the issuer before subtracting out-of-state bonds
- Attach the 1099-INT
- Example: $700 of Missouri-issued Build America bond interest → subtract $700

## Required Information
- Interest by bond and issuer

## Questions
- Do you own Build America or Recovery Zone Bonds, and who issued them?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting interest from non-Missouri issuers without checking the statute
- Confusing with tax-exempt municipal interest (not in federal AGI)

## Prompt
- Are Build America bonds taxed in Missouri?
