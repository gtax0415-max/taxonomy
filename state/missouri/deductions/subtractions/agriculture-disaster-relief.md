# Agriculture Disaster Relief Payments
## Description
Payments to farmers from federal agricultural disaster programs that are included in federal AGI.

## Amounts for 2026
- Full amount included in federal AGI
- Covers listed programs such as the Livestock Forage Disaster Program, Livestock Indemnity Program, and Noninsured Crop Disaster Assistance Program
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Agricultural producer receiving qualifying disaster payments

## Notes
- Ordinary farm program payments that are not disaster relief do not qualify
- Example: $12,000 disaster payment on Schedule F → subtract $12,000

## Required Information
- Program name and amount for each payment

## Questions
- Did you receive USDA disaster or emergency payments for your farm?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting regular commodity program payments

## Prompt
- Are farm disaster payments taxed in Missouri?
