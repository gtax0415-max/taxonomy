# Capital Gain Subtraction (100%)
## Description
Missouri does not tax individuals' capital gains. 100% of capital gain reported on federal Form 1040 line 7a is subtracted, starting with tax year 2025.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of Form 1040 line 7a (short-term and long-term), if positive
- $0 if line 7a is a loss
- Computed for each spouse

## Eligibility
- Individuals (trusts, corporations, and entities paying pass-through entity tax are excluded)

## Notes
- DOR Letter Ruling 8397 (May 2026): capital losses netted into line 7a cannot be added back, and mutual fund short-term gain distributions reported as ordinary dividends do not qualify
- Gold and silver specie gain is already capital gain on line 7a, so it is covered here (see specie-capital-gain.md)
- Ordinary income such as depreciation recapture is not capital gain
- Example: $6,300 stock gain on line 7a → subtract $6,300

## Required Information
- Form 1040 line 7a amount, split by spouse

## Questions
- Did you report a capital gain on Form 1040 line 7a?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting a capital loss
- Including short-term gain distributions reported as ordinary dividends
- Subtracting specie gain a second time

## Prompt
- Are capital gains taxed in Missouri?
