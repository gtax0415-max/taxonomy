# Military Retirement Benefits
## Description
100% of retirement pay for service in the Armed Forces, including the reserves and the Missouri National Guard, to the extent included in federal AGI.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of taxable military retirement pay
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Retirement benefits from military service

## Notes
- Survivor Benefit Plan (SBP) payments to a surviving spouse are NOT on this line under current law; they go to the public pension exemption on MO-A Part 3 (a 2026 bill to make SBP fully subtractable was not confirmed as enacted)
- VA disability pay is not taxable federally, so there is nothing to subtract
- Separate from the active-duty and inactive-duty military income deductions on MO-1040
- Example: $28,000 military pension → subtract $28,000

## Required Information
- Taxable amount of military retirement on 1099-R

## Questions
- Do you receive military retirement pay?
- Do you receive Survivor Benefit Plan payments?

## Common Errors
- Reporting it again as a public pension on MO-A Part 3
- Putting SBP payments on this line

## Prompt
- Is military retirement taxable in Missouri?
