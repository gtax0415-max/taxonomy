# Capital Gain on Specie (Gold and Silver Legal Tender)
## Description
New for tax years beginning January 1, 2026: a subtraction for capital gain on selling specie (gold and silver bullion products used as currency). For individuals it overlaps completely with the 100% capital gain subtraction.

## Amounts for 2026
- The capital gain on specie included in federal AGI
- Capital gain only; ordinary or dealer gain does not qualify

## Eligibility
- Sale or exchange of specie in 2026

## Notes
- Because individuals already subtract 100% of capital gain on line 7a (including the 28% collectibles gain), this mainly matters for taxpayers who cannot use the 100% subtraction (such as trusts)
- The 2026 MO-A line is not yet published

## Required Information
- Sale proceeds and basis for each specie sale

## Questions
- Did you sell gold or silver coins or bullion in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting the gain twice (here and in the 100% capital gain subtraction)

## Prompt
- Is gain on gold coins taxed in Missouri?
