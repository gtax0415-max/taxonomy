# Kansas City and St. Louis Earnings Taxes
## Description
City earnings taxes stay in the Missouri itemized deduction. Only state income taxes are taken out of federal itemized deductions, so the earnings tax included in Schedule A line 5a is backed out of that subtraction.

## Amounts for 2026
- MO-A Part 2: line 9 state and local income taxes (Schedule A line 5a), line 10 earnings taxes included in line 9, line 11 = line 9 − line 10
- If the federal SALT cap applied, a worksheet prorates the subtraction (2026 federal cap $40,400)
- Both cities' earnings tax rate: 1% (St. Louis renewed by voters April 7, 2026)

## Eligibility
- Itemizers who paid Kansas City or St. Louis earnings tax

## Notes
- Earnings tax is not Missouri withholding (line 37)
- Example: Kansas City itemizer with $900 earnings tax and $4,000 of state and local income tax on line 5a → line 11 subtraction $3,100

## Required Information
- Earnings tax paid (W-2 box 19 and any city returns)

## Questions
- Do you live or work in Kansas City or St. Louis?
- Did you itemize on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Subtracting earnings tax along with state tax
- Entering earnings tax as Missouri withholding

## Prompt
- Do I subtract city earnings tax on my Missouri return?
