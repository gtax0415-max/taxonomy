# Missouri Itemized Deductions
## Description
Federal itemized deductions, plus each spouse's Social Security, railroad retirement, Medicare, and self-employment tax, minus state and local income taxes (except Kansas City and St. Louis earnings taxes).

## Amounts for 2026
- Social Security tax limit: $11,439 per person (6.2% × $184,500 wage base)
- Railroad Tier I: $11,439; Tier II: $6,718 (4.9% × $137,100)
- Medicare tax: no cap (plus 0.9% additional Medicare tax)
- Federal SALT cap for 2026: $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), reduced above $505,000 MAGI; the MO-A worksheet uses the capped figures (2025 form printed $40,000 / $500,000; 2026 form not yet published)

## Eligibility
- Must have itemized on the federal return; must itemize in Missouri if required federally

## Notes
- Compare to the Missouri standard deduction and take the larger only if allowed
- Example: itemized $34,000 + payroll taxes $7,500 − state income tax $3,800 → $37,700

## Required Information
- Schedule A, payroll and SE taxes by spouse, state income taxes deducted, earnings taxes

## Questions
- Did you itemize on your federal return?
- Did you pay Kansas City or St. Louis earnings tax?

## Common Errors
- Itemizing in Missouri after taking the federal standard deduction
- Subtracting earnings tax along with state income tax
- Exceeding the Social Security cap

## Prompt
- Can I itemize on my Missouri return?
