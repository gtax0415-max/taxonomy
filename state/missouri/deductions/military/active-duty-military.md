# Active Duty Military Income Deduction
## Description
100% of pay for active duty in the Armed Forces included in federal AGI.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of active duty pay, including enlistment signing bonuses
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Ordered to federal active duty under Title 10 or Title 32, annual training (typically two weeks), and other active training or schools

## Notes
- Excluded: State Active Duty pay and Inactive Duty for Training (that goes on line 19)
- Attach Leave and Earnings Statements; missing them may disallow the deduction
- Combat-zone pay included in AGI is a separate MO-A subtraction
- Example: $45,000 of active duty pay → deduct $45,000

## Required Information
- Active duty pay and periods

## Questions
- Did you receive active duty military pay in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Deducting State Active Duty pay
- Putting drill pay on line 18 instead of line 19
- Not attaching Leave and Earnings Statements

## Prompt
- Is active duty pay taxed in Missouri?
