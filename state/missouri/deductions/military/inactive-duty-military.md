# Inactive Duty Military Deduction
## Description
100% of National Guard and reserve inactive duty training and annual training pay included in federal AGI, plus Guard and reserve signing bonuses.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% (fully phased in since 2024)
- Includes National Guard and reserve signing bonuses (from 2025)

## Eligibility
- Members of the National Guard or reserve components

## Notes
- State Active Duty pay does not qualify
- Active duty pay goes on line 18
- Example: $6,500 of drill pay → deduct $6,500

## Required Information
- Inactive duty and annual training pay

## Questions
- Are you in the National Guard or reserves?

## Common Errors
- Deducting State Active Duty pay
- Deducting the same pay on both line 18 and line 19

## Prompt
- Is drill pay taxable in Missouri?
