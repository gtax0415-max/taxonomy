# Social Security and Social Security Disability Exemption
## Description
100% of taxable Social Security benefits for a spouse 62 or older at year-end, and taxable Social Security Disability at any age.

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of taxable benefits, regardless of income or filing status (since 2024)

## Eligibility
- Age 62 by December 31, 2026 (age 62–64 or 65 box checked), or receiving Social Security Disability

## Notes
- Figured separately for each spouse
- Reduces that spouse's public pension maximum
- Railroad retirement Tier I is subtracted separately on MO-A line 11
- Example: 66-year-old with $7,020 of taxable benefits → exempt $7,020

## Required Information
- Taxable benefits by spouse; age or disability status

## Questions
- Are you 62 or older and receiving Social Security, or receiving Social Security Disability?

## Common Errors
- Claiming at 61 without disability
- Forgetting the age box
- Also counting railroad Tier I benefits here

## Prompt
- Is Social Security taxed in Missouri?
