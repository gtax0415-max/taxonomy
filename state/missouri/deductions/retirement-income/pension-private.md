# Private Pension Exemption
## Description
Up to $6,000 per spouse of taxable private pensions, annuities, IRAs, and 401(k) distributions, phased out above an income limit.

## Amounts for 2026
- Up to $6,000 per spouse (not indexed)
- Income test: Missouri AGI minus taxable Social Security; the combined exemption is reduced dollar for dollar above $25,000 (single, head of household, qualifying widow(er)), $32,000 (married filing combined), or $16,000 (married filing separately)
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Taxable distributions from private-source retirement plans included in federal AGI

## Notes
- Excludes Roth IRA distributions, rollovers, and government pensions (government pensions use the public pension exemption)
- The $6,000 cap applies per spouse; the phase-out is subtracted from the couple's combined amount
- Example: couple with $30,000 Missouri AGI after Social Security and $8,000 of one spouse's IRA withdrawals → $6,000 exempt (under the $32,000 limit)

## Required Information
- Taxable private retirement income by spouse
- Missouri AGI and taxable Social Security

## Questions
- Do you have taxable IRA, 401(k), annuity, or private pension income?
- Whose account was it?

## Common Errors
- Applying one $6,000 to the couple instead of each spouse
- Including rollovers or Roth IRA distributions
- Using Missouri AGI without subtracting taxable Social Security

## Prompt
- How much of my IRA is exempt in Missouri?
