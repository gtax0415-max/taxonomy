# Public Pension Exemption
## Description
Taxable pension from a federal, state, or local government retirement system, for each spouse, up to an annual maximum, reduced by that spouse's Social Security exemption.

## Amounts for 2026
- Maximum: $48,967 per spouse (DOR pension FAQ; 2025 was $47,633). This is a statutory amount indexed by inflation from $32,500, not the SSA maximum benefit
- No income limit (since 2024)
- Reduced by the same spouse's Social Security exemption

## Eligibility
- Recipient of a public pension included in federal AGI

## Notes
- Military retirement is subtracted separately on MO-A line 10; do not include it here
- Military Survivor Benefit Plan payments go here
- Example: retired teacher with a $40,000 public pension and no Social Security → exempt $40,000

## Required Information
- Taxable public pension and Social Security exemption, by spouse

## Questions
- Do you receive a pension from a government employer?
- Do you also receive Social Security?

## Common Errors
- Including military retirement here and on MO-A line 10
- Not reducing by the Social Security exemption

## Prompt
- Is my state pension taxable in Missouri?
