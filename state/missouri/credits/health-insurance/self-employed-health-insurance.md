# Self-Employed Health Insurance Tax Credit (Form MO-SHC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a self-employed person who could not deduct health insurance premiums federally. It equals the federal income tax caused by including those premiums in income.

## Amounts for 2026
- Federal tax with the premiums minus federal tax without them (short method), or the regular method for itemizers and AMT
- Limited to Missouri tax (MO-1040 line 35)
- Only if Missouri tax before credits (lines 31 + 33 + any lump-sum tax) is under $3,000
- Recompute federal tax using 2026 federal tax rates

## Eligibility
- Self-employed and did not take the federal self-employed health insurance deduction for these premiums

## Notes
- No carryforward or carryback; not transferable
- Cannot also take Missouri's health insurance premium subtraction for the same premiums
- On a combined return, each spouse files a separate MO-SHC
- Sunset: December 31, 2028

## Required Information
- Premiums paid and included in federal AGI
- Federal tax as filed
- Missouri tax before credits

## Questions
- Were you self-employed and unable to deduct your health insurance premiums federally?
- Is your Missouri tax under $3,000?

## Common Errors
- Claiming when premiums were already deducted federally
- Also taking the Missouri premium subtraction

## Prompt
- Is there a Missouri credit for self-employed health insurance?
