# MO Scholars (MO-TC code MES)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for cash contributions to a Treasurer-certified Educational Assistance Organization that funds MOScholars education savings accounts.

## Amount
- 100% of the contribution
- Per-taxpayer limit: 50% of Missouri tax liability
- Statewide: $75 million per calendar year, adjusted yearly by the Treasurer. 2026 adjusted figure NOT YET PUBLISHED; do not reuse a prior-year figure

## Eligibility
- Cash only (check, money order, payroll deduction, electronic transfer); stock, bonds, and property do not qualify since 2024 SB 727
- Cannot designate a student, and cannot benefit your own dependent
- Reserve credits through the Treasurer's portal before contributing (first-come, January 1 – August 1)

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Not transferable
- No sunset (exempt from the sunset act)
- Pass-through allocation to owners not addressed; contribute in your own name

## Required Information
- Reservation number and Treasurer-approved receipt
- Contribution date and amount
- 2026 Missouri tax liability (for the 50% limit)

## Questions
- Did you reserve MOScholars credits before giving?
- Was the gift cash?
- What is your 2026 Missouri tax before credits?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than 50% of liability in one year instead of carrying forward
- Giving stock
- Contributing without a reservation

## Prompt
- I donated to MOScholars.
