# Diaper Bank (MO-TC code DBC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to a qualifying diaper bank. The program lapsed after December 31, 2024 and was revived by 2025 HB 121 effective August 28, 2025.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Minimum contribution: $100
- Per-taxpayer limit: the lesser of tax liability or $50,000 per tax year
- Statewide cap: $500,000 per fiscal year, split equally among qualifying diaper banks; unused amounts roll forward and are reallocated at least quarterly

## Eligibility
- Donation to a diaper bank DSS has certified (national diaper banks qualify from 2025)
- Apply to DSS within 12 months of the donation

## Notes
- Carryforward: next tax year only
- Not transferable
- Sunset: statute says December 31, 2031 (DOR's change list says August 28, 2031). 2026 is covered either way
- Gifts made January 1 – August 27, 2025 fell in the gap before the revival; check with DSS before claiming those

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount
- Prior-year carryforward, if any

## Questions
- Did you donate at least $100 to a qualifying diaper bank?
- When was the donation made?
- Did you receive a DSS certificate?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the credit expired in 2024
- Exceeding the $50,000 per-year limit
- Missing the 12-month application window
- Carrying forward more than one year

## Prompt
- I donated to a diaper bank in Missouri.
- Is the Missouri diaper bank credit still available?
