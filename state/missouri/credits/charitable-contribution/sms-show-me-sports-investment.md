# Show-Me Sports Investment (MO-TC code SMS)
## Description
New 2025 credit for contributions to the MDFB infrastructure development fund for a major-league stadium project (MLB or NFL, $500 million+, 30,000+ seats). A large-contributor credit; rare on an individual return.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Per project: the lesser of 10% of private investment or $50 million, issued over up to 3 years
- Outside the IDC/DRC/EFC $10 million annual cap

## Eligibility
- Board-authorized contribution to a qualifying stadium project
- "Any taxpayer," including individuals in principle

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years after the issuance year
- Transferable at 75%+ of par, written agreement, Board notice within 30 days
- Repayment required if the team relocates
- No sunset stated; refundability not stated (treat as nonrefundable)
- DOR FY26 report shows no activity yet

## Required Information
- Certificate and benefit number
- Issuance year and carryforward

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Missouri stadium infrastructure project, or buy this credit?

## Common Errors
- Coding it as IDC

## Prompt
- I bought a Missouri stadium tax credit.
