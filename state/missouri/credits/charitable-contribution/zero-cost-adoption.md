# Zero-Cost Adoption Tax Credit
## Description
New nonrefundable credit, starting with tax year 2026, for cash contributions to Missouri's Zero-Cost Adoption Fund. The fund pays adoption costs for families adopting children from state care. This is a credit for DONORS, not adoptive parents (adoptive parents use the refundable adoption credit).

## Amounts for 2026
- 100% of the contribution
- Limited to 50% of the taxpayer's Missouri tax liability
- Statewide: $5 million for the first year (later indexed, up to $75 million)
- First-come, first-served January 1 – August 1

## Eligibility
- Cash only (check, money order, payroll deduction, electronic transfer); no securities or property

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; not transferable
- Sunset: 2031 (statute and Revisor differ on the exact date)
- The 2026 MO-TC alpha code and any Treasurer page are not yet published

## Required Information
- Treasurer receipt
- Contribution date and amount
- Missouri tax liability (for the 50% limit)

## Questions
- Did you contribute to the Missouri Zero-Cost Adoption Fund in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than 50% of tax in one year
- Confusing it with the adoption credit for parents

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri zero-cost adoption credit?
