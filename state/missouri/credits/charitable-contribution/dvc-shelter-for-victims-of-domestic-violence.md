# Shelter for Victims of Domestic Violence (MO-TC code DVC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to a domestic violence shelter or rape crisis center that DSS has approved for the fiscal year.

## Amount
- 70% of the contribution
- Minimum: $100 (statute: total in the year; DSS page says per contribution)
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $50,000 per tax year
- No statewide cap since July 1, 2022

## Eligibility
- Shelter or rape crisis center approved by DSS for the year
- Application filed within 12 months of the donation, or the credit is forfeited

## Notes
- Carryforward: next tax year only
- Not transferable
- No sunset; no 2025 or 2026 change

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount

## Questions
- Did you donate at least $100 to a Missouri domestic violence shelter or rape crisis center?
- Was it approved by DSS for that year?

## Common Errors
- Using the old 50% rate
- Missing the 12-month window
- Carrying forward more than one year

## Prompt
- I donated to a domestic violence shelter in Missouri.
