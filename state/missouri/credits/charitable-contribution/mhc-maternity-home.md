# Maternity Home (MO-TC code MHC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to a maternity home that DSS has approved for the fiscal year.

## Amount
- 70% of the contribution
- Minimum contribution: $100 in the tax year
- Per-taxpayer limit: the lesser of tax liability or $100,000 per tax year (raised from $50,000 by 2025 HB 121, effective August 28, 2025)
- No statewide cap for fiscal years beginning on or after July 1, 2022

## Eligibility
- Cash or property donated to a maternity home on the DSS-approved list for that fiscal year
- Taxpayer applies to DSS within 12 months of the donation; late applications are void
- DSS approves within 45 days, first-come, first-served

## Notes
- Carryforward: next tax year only
- Not transferable
- No sunset in statute; covered for 2026
- The donation may also be a charitable deduction; the credit and deduction can interact, so do not double-count on the Missouri itemized side

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount
- Prior-year carryforward, if any

## Questions
- Did you donate at least $100 to a Missouri maternity home?
- Was the home DSS-approved for that fiscal year?
- Did you apply to DSS within 12 months of the gift?

## Common Errors
- Using the old $50,000 cap or the old 50% rate
- Missing the 12-month application window
- Carrying the credit forward more than one year
- Claiming without the DSS certificate

## Prompt
- I donated to a maternity home in Missouri.
- How much Missouri credit do I get for a maternity home donation?
