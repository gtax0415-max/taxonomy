# Champion for Children Tax Credit (Form CFC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to a qualified CASA program, child advocacy center, or crisis care center.

## Amounts for 2026
- 70% of the contribution (raised from 50% for tax years from 2025)
- Credit minimum $50, maximum $50,000 per taxpayer per year (contributions of about $72 to $71,429)
- Statewide: $2.5 million per fiscal year, split equally among the three agency types; prorated if one type is oversubscribed (no penalty or interest if the balance is paid within 60 days of notice)

## Eligibility
- Agency verified by the Department of Social Services
- Claim filed by April 15, 2027 for 2026 gifts

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years
- Not transferable
- Sunset: December 31, 2031
- No Missouri addition required for the federal charitable deduction (none on the MO-A)
- Example: $1,000 gift → $700 credit

## Required Information
- Agency name and type
- Contribution date and amount

## Questions
- Did you give to a CASA program, child advocacy center, or crisis care center?

## Common Errors
- Using the old 50% rate
- Giving to an agency type not covered (such as a general children's charity)

## Prompt
- What is the Champion for Children credit?
