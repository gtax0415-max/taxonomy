# Family Development Account (MO-TC code FDA)
## Description
Credit for a cash contribution to the reserve fund of a DED-approved Family Development Account program, which matches savings of low-income account holders.

## Amount
- Up to 50% of the contribution
- Only the first $50,000 per contributor counts, so at most $25,000 of credit
- Statewide: $300,000 per fiscal year

## Eligibility
- Contributor is not the account holder
- Program approved by DED

## Notes
- Refundability, carryforward, and transferability are not stated in the statute; treat as nonrefundable until confirmed on the certificate
- No sunset; FY2027 cycle opens fall 2026

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount

## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Family Development Account program approved by DED?

## Common Errors
- Counting contributions above $50,000

## Prompt
- I donated to a family development account program.
