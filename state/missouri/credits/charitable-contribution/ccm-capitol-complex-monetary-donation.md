# Capitol Complex – Monetary Donation (MO-TC code CCM)
## Description
Refundable credit for cash, cash-equivalent, or publicly traded securities donated for the Missouri State Capitol Complex. Artifact donations use code CCA (30%, nonrefundable).

## Amount
- 50% of the donation
- No per-donor limit
- Statewide: $10 million per calendar year for all Capitol Complex credits, first-come, first-served

## Eligibility
- Donation to the MDFB Capitol Complex Fund or an eligible 501(c)(3) supporting it
- Individuals, partners, and S-corporation shareholders qualify

## Notes
- Refundable
- Carryforward 4 years; DED says redeem within 5 years of issuance
- Transferable by notarized endorsement filed with DED
- Sunset: August 28, 2027; 2026 donations are covered

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount

## Questions
- Did you give money or securities (not an artifact) to the Capitol Complex?
- Have you received the DED certificate?

## Common Errors
- Coding an artifact gift as CCM
- Listing it before nonrefundable credits on MO-TC

## Prompt
- I donated money to the Missouri State Capitol restoration.
