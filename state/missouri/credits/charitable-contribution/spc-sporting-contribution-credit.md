# Sporting Contribution Credit (MO-TC code SPC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donating to a certified sponsor or local organizing committee that brings amateur, collegiate, or Olympic sporting events to Missouri. The event credit itself (SEC) belongs to the sponsor.

## Amount
- 50% of the donation
- Statewide: $500,000 per fiscal year for credits issued on or after July 1, 2026 (was $10 million)

## Eligibility
- Cash, publicly traded stocks and bonds, or real estate
- Sponsor must be a Sports Events and Tourism Association member
- The sponsor applies and pays DED the credit amount; the donor does not apply

## Notes
- Carryforward 2 years; no carryback
- Transferable by notarized endorsement
- Sunset: August 28, 2032; 2026 is covered

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Donation date, amount, and type

## Questions
- Did you donate to a sporting event sponsor or organizing committee in Missouri?

## Common Errors
- Coding it as SEC
- Carrying forward more than 2 years

## Prompt
- I donated to bring a sporting event to Missouri.
