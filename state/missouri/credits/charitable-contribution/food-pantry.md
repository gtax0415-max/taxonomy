# Food Pantry Tax Credit (Form MO-FPT)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for cash or food donated to a 501(c)(3) food pantry, soup kitchen, or homeless shelter. 2026 is the FINAL year: the credit sunsets December 31, 2026, and no 2026 bill extended it.

## Amounts for 2026
- 50% of the donation
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $2,500 credit (a separate married figure is not stated in statute)
- Statewide: $1.75 million per fiscal year, prorated if exceeded

## Eligibility
- Cash or unexpired food donated by December 31, 2026
- Claim filed July 1 – April 15

## Notes
- Carryforward 3 years (credits earned in 2026 can still be used through 2029)
- Not transferable
- A 2026 House bill to extend it to 2032 did not pass
- Example: $400 cash gift → $200 credit

## Required Information
- Pantry name and address
- Donation dates and amounts
- Whether the gift was itemized federally

## Questions
- Did you donate cash or food to a food pantry, soup kitchen, or shelter in 2026?
- Did you also itemize it on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Itemizing the gift federally without the Missouri add-back
- Expecting the credit for 2027 donations
- Including expired food

## Prompt
- Is there a credit for food pantry donations?
