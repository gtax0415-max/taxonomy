# Neighborhood Assistance (MO-TC code NAC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a business firm's contribution to a DED-approved Neighborhood Assistance Program project.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- 70% for projects in towns under 15,000 people or unincorporated areas (excluding Clay, Jackson, St. Charles, and St. Louis counties)
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $250,000 per year (no limit in impoverished, distressed, or blighted areas)
- DED allocation for the current round: $16 million ($10 million at 50%, $6 million at 70%)

## Eligibility
- Contributor must be a "business firm": sole proprietor, farm operator, individual with Missouri rental or royalty income, or a partner, S-corp shareholder, or LLC member. A purely personal gift does not qualify
- Cash, publicly traded stock, real property, or budgeted in-kind items. Donor-advised funds and private stock do not qualify

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years; no carryback
- Transferability not confirmed in statute; check the certificate
- No sunset; 2026 is covered
- Cannot be claimed until the certificate is issued (after DOR tax clearance)

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount
- Business type that makes you a "business firm"

## Questions
- Did you or your business contribute to a DED-approved NAP project?
- Do you have business, farm, rental, or royalty income in Missouri?
- Was the project in a town under 15,000?

## Common Errors
- Claiming as an individual with only wage income
- Giving through a donor-advised fund
- Using 70% for a project not in an eligible small community

## Prompt
- My business donated to a Neighborhood Assistance Program project.
