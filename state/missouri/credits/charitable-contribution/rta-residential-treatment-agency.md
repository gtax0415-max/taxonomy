# Residential Treatment Agency (MO-TC code RTA)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to a licensed, accredited residential treatment agency for children that contracts with DSS.

## Amount
- 50% of the donation
- No minimum, no per-taxpayer limit, no statewide cap (each agency is limited to DSS payments it received in the prior 12 months)

## Eligibility
- Cash, stock or bonds, or real estate used for direct care of children
- Agency applies and pays DSS 50% of the donation within 12 months

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Transferable by notarized endorsement
- No sunset

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount
- Carryforward used

## Questions
- Did you donate to a Missouri residential treatment agency for children?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the certificate is issued
- Carrying forward more than 4 years

## Prompt
- I donated to a children's residential treatment center in Missouri.
