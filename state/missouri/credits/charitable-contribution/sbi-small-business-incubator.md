# Small Business Incubator (MO-TC code SBI)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributing to the Missouri small business incubators fund or a DED-approved local incubator sponsor.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Statewide: $500,000 per year

## Eligibility
- Any taxpayer, including individuals
- DED accepts applications year-round

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years; no carryback
- Applied after all other credits
- Transferable at 75%+ of par, DED notice within 30 days
- No sunset

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount

## Questions
- Did you donate to a Missouri small business incubator?

## Common Errors
- Listing it ahead of other credits on MO-TC

## Prompt
- I donated to a small business incubator in Missouri.
