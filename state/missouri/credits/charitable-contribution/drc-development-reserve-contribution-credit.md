# Development Reserve Contribution (MO-TC code DRC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributing to the MDFB Industrial Development and Reserve Fund. Same statute and rules as IDC; only the receiving fund differs.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- Statewide: $10 million per year, shared with IDC and EFC (up to $25 million with approval)
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Contribution approved by MDFB before it is made
- Cash, marketable securities, or real property (lower of two MAI appraisals)

## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years; no carryback; must be used within 10 years of the contribution
- Transferable for 75–100% of face value via MDFB Form L
- No sunset; covered for 2026
- Note: the statute is 100.286, not 100.263 (100.263 creates the infrastructure fund used by IDC)
- DOR's FY26 tax credit report shows no separate DRC activity; new 2026 certificates are unlikely

## Required Information
- MDFB certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount
- Carryforward used in prior years

## Questions
- Did you contribute to the MDFB Industrial Development and Reserve Fund?
- In what year, and how much has been used?

## Common Errors
- Coding a DRC certificate as IDC or the reverse
- Using the credit past 5 carryforward years
- Claiming on an amended return

## Prompt
- I have a Missouri development reserve contribution credit.
