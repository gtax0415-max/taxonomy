# Developmental Disability Care Provider (MO-TC code DDC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for donations to a qualified provider that uses the money for direct care of Missouri residents with developmental disabilities.

## Amount
- 50% of the donation
- No minimum, no per-taxpayer limit (other than tax liability), no statewide cap

## Eligibility
- Cash, publicly traded stock or bonds, or real estate
- Provider is accredited (COA, JCAHO, or CARF) or under contract with DSS or DMH
- Provider applies and pays DSS an amount equal to the credit within 12 months of the gift

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Transferable by notarized endorsement
- DSS reduces the credit by any tax the taxpayer owes DOR
- No sunset

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount
- Carryforward used

## Questions
- Did you donate to a developmental disability care provider that offers Missouri tax credits?

## Common Errors
- Claiming before the provider has applied and paid DSS
- Using the 70% rate of DVC/MHC

## Prompt
- I donated to a developmental disability provider in Missouri.
