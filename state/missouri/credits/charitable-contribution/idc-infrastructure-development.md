# Infrastructure Development (MO-TC code IDC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributing cash, marketable securities, or real property to the MDFB Infrastructure Development Fund for an approved public infrastructure project.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution
- No per-taxpayer limit in statute (MDFB policy decides)
- Statewide: $10 million per year, shared with DRC and Export Finance (EFC); may be raised to $25 million with approval of three state officials
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Contribution approved by MDFB before it is made
- Real property valued at the lower of two MAI appraisals; in-kind services do not qualify
- Individuals, estates, trusts, and corporations may contribute

## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years; no carryback; must be used within 10 years of the contribution
- Transferable for 75–100% of face value; file MDFB Form L within 30 days (MDFB does not use Form MO-TF). Sale proceeds are taxable to the seller
- MDFB keeps a 4% fee from the contribution
- MDFB's contributor FAQ says to file on paper and that the credit cannot be claimed on an amended return
- No sunset; covered for 2026
- Separate 2025 special-session credit (RSMo 100.240.6) for stadium/entertainment infrastructure is NOT this code
- Pass-through allocation to owners is not addressed in statute; confirm with MDFB before allocating on a K-1

## Required Information
- MDFB certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount
- Carryforward used in prior years
- Seller, transfer date, and amount for a purchased credit

## Questions
- Did you contribute to the MDFB Infrastructure Development Fund, or buy an IDC credit?
- In what year was the contribution made?
- How much of the credit has already been used?

## Common Errors
- Claiming on an amended return
- Using the credit after 5 carryforward years
- Using Form MO-TF instead of MDFB Form L for a transfer
- Confusing IDC with DRC (different fund, same rules)

## Prompt
- I have an MDFB infrastructure tax credit.
- I bought a Missouri infrastructure development credit.
