# Export Finance (MO-TC code EFC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributing to the MDFB Export Finance Fund. Same statute and rules as IDC and DRC; only the receiving fund differs.

## Amount
- 50% of the contribution (MDFB keeps a 4% fee)
- No per-taxpayer limit in statute
- Statewide: $10 million per year shared with IDC and DRC (up to $25 million with approval)

## Eligibility
- Contribution approved by MDFB before it is made
- Cash, marketable securities, or real property (lower of two MAI appraisals); no in-kind gifts

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years; no carryback; use within 10 years of the contribution
- Transferable for at least 75% of face value via MDFB Form L
- No sunset
- DOR's FY26 report shows no separate EFC activity; new 2026 certificates are unlikely

## Required Information
- MDFB certificate and benefit number
- Contribution year and carryforward used
- Ownership share (pass-through)

## Questions
- Did you contribute to the MDFB Export Finance Fund, or buy an EFC credit?
- In what year, and how much has been used?

## Common Errors
- Coding EFC as IDC or DRC
- Using after 5 carryforward years

## Prompt
- I have a Missouri export finance tax credit.
