# Youth Opportunities (MO-TC code YOC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to DED-approved youth opportunity or violence prevention projects, or for wages paid to youth in approved programs.

## Amount
- 70% of cash contributions for projects approved for 2026 onward (50% for earlier-approved projects)
- 30% of property contributions
- Wage credit: 50% of wages paid to youth under 20, up to $10,000 per youth per year
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $200,000 per tax year
- Statewide: $6 million per year

## Eligibility
- Contribution to a DED-approved project, or youth wages in an approved internship, apprenticeship, or employment project
- Individuals can claim directly (unlike NAC)

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years
- Transferability not addressed in statute
- No sunset; 2026 is covered
- The 70% cash rate is new for 2026-approved projects (2025 legislation)

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date, amount, and type (cash or property)
- Project approval year

## Questions
- Did you donate to a DED-approved youth opportunities project in 2026?
- Was it cash or property?
- When was the project approved?

## Common Errors
- Using 70% for property gifts or pre-2026 projects
- Exceeding $200,000

## Prompt
- I donated to a youth program that gives Missouri tax credits.
