# Pregnancy Resource Center (MO-TC code PRC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for contributions to a pregnancy resource center that DSS has classified as eligible.

## Amount
- 70% of the contribution
- Minimum: $100 total in the year
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $50,000 per tax year
- No statewide cap since FY2022
- No change for 2026 (a 2025 proposal for 100% was not enacted)

## Eligibility
- Cash, stock, bonds, securities, or real property (no furniture, appliances, or clothing)
- Center must have renewed DSS eligibility by June 1 for the fiscal year
- Application filed within 12 months of the donation

## Notes
- Carryforward: next tax year only
- Not transferable
- No sunset
- The regulation still shows 50%; the statute (70%) controls

## Required Information
- DSS certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount

## Questions
- Did you donate at least $100 to a DSS-approved pregnancy resource center?

## Common Errors
- Using the 50% rate from the old regulation
- Missing the 12-month window

## Prompt
- I donated to a pregnancy resource center in Missouri.
