# Miscellaneous Income Tax Credits (Form MO-TC)
## Description
The form that collects every Missouri certificate-based credit and several DOR credits (adoption, Champion for Children, Food Pantry, SALT Parity, Self-Employed Health Insurance, and more). The total goes to MO-1040 line 42.

## Amounts for 2026
- Up to 10 credits per form; attach more MO-TCs if needed
- Column 1 = yourself (or a single filer); Column 2 = spouse on a combined return
- Line 13 cannot exceed the tax on line 12 (MO-1040 lines 35Y/35S) unless the credit is refundable

## Eligibility
- Holding a credit certificate, receipt, or qualifying claim

## Notes
- Credits apply in the order listed, so put nonrefundable credits first and refundable ones last
- Benefit number = last 6 digits of the certificate number
- Both spouses' names must be on a certificate to split it between columns
- New for 2026: Zero-Cost Adoption contribution credit (code not yet published). Expired for 2026: Bank Franchise Tax (BFT), Residential Dwelling Accessibility (DAT), Qualified Beef (QBC). Final year: Food Pantry (FPT)

## Required Information
- Alpha code, benefit number, and amount for each credit
- Which spouse holds each credit

## Questions
- Do you hold any Missouri tax credit certificates or receipts?
- Which spouse's name is on each certificate?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than the tax for a nonrefundable credit
- Listing refundable credits before nonrefundable ones
- Putting a credit in the wrong spouse's column
