# New Generation Cooperative Incentive (MO-TC code NGC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a producer member's cash investment in an eligible new generation cooperative or processing entity.

## Amount
- The lesser of 50% of the investment or $15,000, reduced to the member's share of the project allocation
- Per project: $3 million (employee-qualified) or $1.5 million (large project)
- Statewide: $6 million per fiscal year shared with APU; 10% reserved for small projects

## Eligibility
- Producer member of a MASBDA-approved project
- Investment deadline set per project

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Transferable (notarized endorsement, $50 fee)
- Sunset: December 31, 2033

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Investment amount

## Questions
- Did you invest in a Missouri new generation cooperative as a producer member?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding $15,000

## Prompt
- I invested in a farmer-owned cooperative in Missouri.
