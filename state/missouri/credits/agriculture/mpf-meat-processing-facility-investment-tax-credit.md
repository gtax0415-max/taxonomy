# Meat Processing Facility Investment (MO-TC code MPF)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for modernizing or expanding a Missouri meat processing facility.

## Amount
- 25% of modernization or expansion costs
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $75,000 per year (combined for co-owners)
- Statewide: $2 million per calendar year; credits not issued in a year expire

## Eligibility
- Business has fewer than 500 employees
- Production actually expands within 3 years, or the credit must be repaid
- $100 application fee and 3% issuance fee

## Notes
- Carryforward: 4 years
- Transferable (Form TT, $50 fee)
- Sunset: expenses through December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913 (agency pages still show 2028)
- 2026 application deadline not yet published; the 2025 deadline was November 1 for 2024 credits

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Costs incurred and dates
- Ownership share (pass-through)

## Questions
- Did you invest in a meat processing facility you own in Missouri?
- How many employees does the business have?
- Have you applied to MASBDA?

## Common Errors
- Exceeding the $75,000 combined cap among co-owners
- Using the old 2028 sunset to deny a valid claim
- Ignoring the 3-year expansion recapture

## Prompt
- I expanded my meat processing plant in Missouri.
