# Family Farms Act – Breeding Livestock Loan (MO-TC code FFC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a LENDER that waives the first year of interest on a MASBDA-approved breeding livestock loan to a small farmer. The farmer-borrower does not get the credit.

## Amount
- 100% of the first-year interest waived
- Loan limits: beef or dairy cattle $150,000; swine $70,000; sheep or goats $60,000; up to 90% of cost. Farmer has under $500,000 in gross sales
- Statewide: $300,000 per fiscal year

## Eligibility
- Lender (or a buyer of the credit)
- Application within 60 days of the note date; interest certification within 30 days of the first anniversary

## Notes
- Excess carries forward, up to 3 years total per loan; no carryback
- Transferable by notarized endorsement
- No sunset (not affected by SB 913)

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Interest waived

## Questions
- Are you the lender on a MASBDA breeding livestock loan, or did you buy the credit?

## Common Errors
- Farmer-borrower claiming the credit
- Using beyond 3 years per loan

## Prompt
- I got a family farm breeding livestock loan in Missouri.
