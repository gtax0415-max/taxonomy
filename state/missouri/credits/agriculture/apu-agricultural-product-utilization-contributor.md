# Agricultural Product Utilization Contributor (MO-TC code APU)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a cash contribution to MASBDA that funds grants for rural agricultural business projects.

## Amount
- Up to 100% of the contribution, set competitively; lowest-percentage requests are funded first
- Statewide: $6 million per fiscal year, shared with New Generation Cooperative (NGC)

## Eligibility
- MASBDA approval before contributing; contribution by MASBDA's deadline
- Individuals qualify

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Transferable (notarized endorsement, $50 fee)
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913 (was 2028)
- No credits issued since FY2022 per state budget data; no 2026 round found

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Contribution date and amount
- Carryforward used

## Questions
- Did MASBDA approve your contribution before you made it?

## Common Errors
- Contributing before approval
- Assuming 100% credit when a lower rate was approved

## Prompt
- I contributed to the Missouri agricultural product utilization program.
