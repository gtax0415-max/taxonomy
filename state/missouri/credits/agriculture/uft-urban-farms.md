# Urban Farms (MO-TC code UFT)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for costs to start or improve an urban farm in a Census urbanized area that grows food for sale or donation to the public, or for donating money to such a farm.

## Amount
- 50% of eligible expenses
- $5,000 per taxpayer per farm; $25,000 total per farm across all taxpayers
- Statewide: $200,000 per calendar year; unissued credits expire

## Eligibility
- Labor, marijuana, hemp, and government-funded costs excluded
- $100 application fee; 3% issuance fee ($50 minimum)

## Notes
- Carryforward 3 years; no carryback
- Not transferable
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913
- Application for 2026 costs likely due about November 1, 2027 (pattern; not yet published)

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Eligible costs and dates

## Questions
- Did you start or improve an urban farm in Missouri, or donate to one?

## Common Errors
- Including labor costs
- Exceeding $5,000 per farm

## Prompt
- I started an urban farm in St. Louis.
