# Qualified Beef (MO-TC code QBC)
## Description
EXPIRED. The credit applied only to tax years ending on or before December 31, 2021 and was not renewed (2026 SB 913 did not extend it). No new 2026 credit. Approvals had stopped in 2018, and no credits issued FY2020–FY2022, so leftover carryforward in 2026 is very unlikely.

## Amount
- $0 new credit for 2026
- Former rate: $0.10 or $0.25 per pound of gain on Missouri-born cattle, $15,000 per taxpayer per year

## Eligibility
- Only unused carryforward from a certificate issued for 2021 or earlier

## Notes
- Carryforward was 4 years, so a 2021 credit is last usable in 2025; nothing should remain for 2026
- Do not confuse with Meat Processing Facility (MPF), which runs through 2033

## Required Information
- Original certificate year and amounts used

## Questions
- Do you have an unused Qualified Beef credit from 2021 or earlier?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a new 2026 beef credit
- Using carryforward beyond 4 years

## Prompt
- Is the Missouri qualified beef credit still available?
