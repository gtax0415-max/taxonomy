# Specialty Agriculture Crops Loan (MO-TC code SAC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a LENDER that waives the first year of interest on a MASBDA-approved specialty crop loan to a small family farmer. The farmer-borrower does not get the credit.

## Amount
- 100% of the first-year interest waived
- Loan limits: $35,000 and 90% of cost, one loan per family; farmer has under $100,000 in agricultural sales
- Statewide: $300,000 per fiscal year

## Eligibility
- Lender (or a buyer of the credit)
- Application within 60 days of the note date; interest certification within 30 days of the first anniversary

## Notes
- Excess carries forward, up to 3 years total per loan; no carryback
- Transferable by notarized endorsement filed with MASBDA
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913 (was 2028)

## Required Information
- MASBDA certificate and benefit number
- Interest waived

## Questions
- Are you the lender on a MASBDA specialty crop loan, or did you buy the credit?

## Common Errors
- Farmer-borrower claiming the credit
- Using beyond 3 years per loan

## Prompt
- I got a specialty crop loan through the Missouri Department of Agriculture.
