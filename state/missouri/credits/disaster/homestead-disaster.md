# Homestead Disaster Tax Credit
## Description
Credit for a 2025 homeowner's or renter's insurance deductible on a Missouri homestead damaged in a 2025 disaster (including the May 16, 2025 St. Louis tornado). Only 2025 deductibles qualify; no new credit can be authorized after October 15, 2026. A 2026 deductible earns nothing.

## Amounts for 2026
- No new 2026 credit
- 2025 credit: the deductible, up to $5,000
- Unused 2025 credit can carry forward (29 years)

## Eligibility
- Credit originated on the 2025 return
- Not yet fully used

## Notes
- LITIGATION: SB 3 is before the Missouri Supreme Court (argued September 1, 2026; no decision found). DOR is processing 2025 returns WITHOUT the credit; if SB 3 is upheld, DOR will apply it automatically and send a notice; if struck, no credit
- Until the ruling, any carryforward to 2026 is uncertain
- Transferable; not refundable

## Required Information
- 2025 credit amount and amount used
- Court outcome

## Questions
- Did you claim the homestead disaster credit on your 2025 return?
- Has DOR sent a notice applying it?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a 2026 deductible
- Carrying forward a credit DOR has not allowed

## Prompt
- Is there a credit for my insurance deductible?
