# Refundable Adoption Tax Credit (MO-TC code RAC)
## Description
Refundable Missouri credit for nonrecurring adoption expenses, available for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2024. It replaced the nonrefundable ATC for new adoptions. Any credit above the tax due is refunded.

## Amount
- The lesser of nonrecurring adoption expenses or the annual per-child limit, which is indexed to CPI-U
- 2026 limit: NOT YET PUBLISHED. DOR announces it on the 2026 Form MO-RAC (expected December 2026). Do not reuse the 2025 figure
- Prior-year reference only: $10,318 (2024), $10,616 (2025)
- One credit per child, whether the parent or the parent's employer claims it
- Same limit for special-needs and other children, and the child's state of residence does not matter
- No statewide cap for fiscal years beginning on or after July 1, 2024

## Eligibility
- Nonrecurring adoption expenses (fees, court costs, attorney fees, and other expenses directly related to the adoption)
- Expenses paid with federal, state, or local program money do not count
- The credit is reduced if the child is later placed in state care or a court removes custody (RSMo 135.335)

## Notes
- Timing: 50% at placement, 50% when the adoption becomes final. A placement in 2025 finalized in 2026 splits across two returns
- Refundable, so list it after nonrefundable credits on MO-TC
- Separate from the federal adoption credit (Form 8839); the federal amount is entered on MO-RAC but the two limits are independent
- No sunset; no 2025 or 2026 legislative change
- Related new credit for 2026: Zero-Cost Adoption Fund contribution credit (RSMo 135.315, 2025 HB 121) is for DONORS to the state fund, not adoptive parents. No MO-TC code published yet

## Required Information
- Child's name, date of birth, SSN/ITIN, placement date and final decree date
- Nonrecurring expenses by year paid, net of any government reimbursement
- Children's Division certification (MO-RAC Part D)
- Special-needs certification and physician statement for a special-needs child aged 18 or older (Part E)
- Federal Form 8839 figures

## Questions
- When was the child placed, and when was (or will) the adoption be final?
- What nonrecurring adoption expenses did you pay, and were any reimbursed by a government program?
- Did an employer also claim a credit for this child?

## Common Errors
- Using the 2025 per-child limit on a 2026 return
- Claiming 100% in the placement year instead of 50/50
- Filing a 2024-or-later adoption under ATC
- Including government-reimbursed expenses

## Prompt
- We adopted a child in Missouri this year.
- Is the Missouri adoption credit refundable?
- Our adoption was finalized this year; the child was placed last year.
