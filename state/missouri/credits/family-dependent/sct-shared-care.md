# Shared Care (MO-TC code SCT)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an unpaid caregiver who keeps an elderly person (60+) at home instead of in a care facility.

## Amount
- The lesser of $500 or the caregiver's Missouri tax liability
- No income limit and no statewide cap
- Unchanged for 2026 (statute not amended since 2014)

## Eligibility
- Caregiver registers as a shared care member with DHSS, Division of Senior and Disability Services, EACH YEAR
- The person cared for is 60 or older and lives with the caregiver more than 6 months of the year
- The caregiver receives no payment for the care
- A physician certifies the person cannot live alone and would otherwise need facility care
- The person cannot drive
- The person receives no Medicaid or Social Services Block Grant funding or services

## Notes
- Nonrefundable and cannot be carried forward; unused credit is lost
- Not transferable
- MO-TC lists "Must register each year" in place of a certificate

## Required Information
- 2026 DHSS shared care registration
- Physician's original signature on the certification (entered on Form MO-SCC)
- Age of the person cared for and months lived with you
- Confirmation of no Medicaid / SSBG services

## Questions
- Did you care for someone 60 or older who lived with you more than six months?
- Were you paid for the care?
- Did you register with DHSS for 2026, and will a physician certify the person's condition?
- Does the person receive Medicaid services?

## Common Errors
- Not re-registering with DHSS for the tax year
- Attaching a copy instead of the original signed MO-SCC
- Claiming when the person receives Medicaid-funded services
- Expecting a refund or carryforward of the unused amount

## Prompt
- My elderly mother lives with me and I take care of her.
- How do I claim the Missouri shared care credit?
