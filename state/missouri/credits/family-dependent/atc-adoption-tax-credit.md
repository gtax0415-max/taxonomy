# Adoption Tax Credit (MO-TC code ATC)
## Description
The original, NONREFUNDABLE Missouri adoption credit. For tax year 2026 it is a legacy code: any adoption with a placement on or after January 1, 2024 is claimed on the Refundable Adoption Tax Credit (code RAC, Form MO-RAC). In 2026 the ATC code is used only for unused carryforward from older credits and for credits bought from another taxpayer.

## Amount
- Up to $10,000 of nonrecurring adoption expenses per child (pre-2024 credits)
- Special-needs and non-special-needs children get the same amount for adoptions from January 1, 2022
- No statewide cap for fiscal years beginning on or after July 1, 2024 (the $6 million annual cap applied FY2022–FY2024)
- 2026 creates no NEW ATC credit; the amount used in 2026 is whatever carryforward or purchased credit remains

## Eligibility
- The credit originated from a placement before January 1, 2024, or was purchased from the original holder
- The carryforward period has not run out

## Notes
- Carryforward: 4 years after the year of the credit (5 years in total per child). No carryback. A 2021 credit is therefore last usable in 2025; a 2022 credit is last usable in 2026
- Transferable: may be sold or assigned for at least 75% of face value
- Timing (original rule): 50% claimable at placement, 50% when the adoption becomes final
- Nonrefundable, and MO-TC applies credits in the order listed, so put it ahead of refundable codes such as RAC
- No sunset in statute; no 2025 or 2026 legislative change

## Required Information
- Tax year the credit was first allowed and the original credit amount
- Amount used in each prior year (to compute remaining carryforward)
- For purchased credits: seller's name, transfer date, amount purchased
- Benefit number for the MO-TC line

## Questions
- When was the child placed in your home: before 2024, or 2024 and later?
- Do you have unused Missouri adoption credit from a prior year? Which year was it first claimed?
- Did you buy this credit from someone else?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a 2024-or-later placement under ATC instead of RAC (loses refundability)
- Using carryforward past the 4-year limit
- Leaving out Form MO-TF and the original MO-ATC for a purchased credit
- Expecting a refund of the excess; ATC can only reduce tax to zero

## Prompt
- I have Missouri adoption credit left over from 2022.
- I bought a Missouri adoption tax credit.
- Do I use form ATC or RAC for my adoption?
