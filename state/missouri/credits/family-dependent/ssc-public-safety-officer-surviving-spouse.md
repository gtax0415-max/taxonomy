# Public Safety Officer Surviving Spouse (MO-TC code SSC)
## Description
Refundable credit equal to the property tax paid on the homestead of the surviving spouse of a public safety officer killed in the line of duty.

## Amount
- Equal to the real property tax paid on the homestead in 2026
- Homestead = dwelling plus up to 5 acres, occupied by no more than two families
- No dollar cap and no statewide cap

## Eligibility
- Surviving spouse of a public safety officer killed in the line of duty
- Has not remarried; no credit in the year of remarriage
- Owns and occupies the Missouri homestead

## Notes
- Refundable; excess is treated as an overpayment
- Sunset: December 31, 2027; 2026 is covered
- No 2025 or 2026 legislative change

## Required Information
- Property tax paid in 2026 on the homestead
- Acreage and assessor certification if over 5 acres
- Date of the officer's death and marital status

## Questions
- Are you the surviving spouse of a public safety officer killed in the line of duty?
- Have you remarried?
- How much property tax did you pay on your home in 2026, and is the parcel over 5 acres?

## Common Errors
- Including property tax on land beyond 5 acres
- Claiming in the year of remarriage
- Forgetting to attach the property tax receipt

## Prompt
- My husband was a police officer killed in the line of duty.
- Is there a Missouri credit for surviving spouses of first responders?
