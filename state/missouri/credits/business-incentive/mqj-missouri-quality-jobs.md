# Missouri Quality Jobs (MO-TC code MQJ)
## Description
Jobs incentive replaced by Missouri Works. No new benefits for any project not proposed or approved before August 28, 2013. Most benefits were withholding retention, not income tax credits. A 2026 claim needs a certificate issued for the year under a pre-2013 project, which is very unlikely.

## Amount
- Only the amount on a DED certificate
- Program cap: $80 million per year (historic)

## Eligibility
- Project approved before August 28, 2013 and still within its benefit period (3–5 years in most cases)

## Notes
- Refundable (except job retention credits)
- No carryforward; must be claimed within one year after the close of the tax year it was issued for
- Transferable by notarized endorsement filed with DED
- Statute names the qualified company as recipient; individual use is realistically limited to a purchased certificate

## Required Information
- DED certificate, benefit number, and tax year it was issued for

## Questions
- Do you hold an MQJ certificate issued for 2025 or 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming a certificate more than one year after its tax year
- Treating withholding retention as an income tax credit

## Prompt
- I have a Missouri Quality Jobs tax credit.
