# New Enhanced Enterprise Zone (MO-TC code NEZ)
## Description
Refundable credit for a new or expanded facility in an Enhanced Enterprise Zone. CLOSED to new projects since August 28, 2013. In 2026 only annual certificates still due within a pre-2013 project's 10-year window can be claimed.

## Amount
- The lesser of the DED-authorized amount or the sum of: $400 per new employee, + $400 per employee living in the zone, + $400 per employee paid above the county average wage, + 2% of new investment
- Statewide: $24 million per year
- DOR FY26 report: $0 newly authorized, about $500,000 issued

## Eligibility
- Project approved or proposed before August 28, 2013
- Tax year falls within the 10-year benefit period
- At least 2 new employees and $100,000 new investment

## Notes
- Refundable; no carryforward
- Transferable for at least 75% of face value by notarized endorsement filed with DED
- Pass-through owners: credit allocated by ownership share on the last day of the tax period (135.967.10)
- 2026 SB 994 proposed repealing the program as expired; it does not appear to have been enacted (not in DOR's 2026 changes)

## Required Information
- DED certificate for 2026 and benefit number
- K-1 and ownership percentage (pass-through)

## Questions
- Did you receive a 2026 Enhanced Enterprise Zone certificate from DED?
- When did the project begin operations?
- What was your ownership share on the last day of the entity's tax year?

## Common Errors
- Treating it as available for a new project
- Claiming carryforward (none exists)
- Allocating by average ownership instead of year-end ownership

## Prompt
- I own part of a business in a Missouri enhanced enterprise zone.
