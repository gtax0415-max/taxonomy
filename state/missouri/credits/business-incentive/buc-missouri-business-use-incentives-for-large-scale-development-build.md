# Missouri BUILD (MO-TC code BUC)
## Description
Refundable credit equal to the job development assessment an approved company pays on new-job wages to retire MDFB bonds for a large project. Rarely seen on an individual return; it belongs to the approved company.

## Amount
- Credit = job development assessment actually paid
- Assessment cap: 5% of gross wages of new jobs (10% in a distressed community)
- Statewide: $25 million per year ($950,000 reserved for one world-headquarters project)
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Approved project: at least $15 million investment and 100 new jobs within 3 years (office projects: $10 million and 500 jobs, or 200 in a distressed community)
- Manufacturing, processing, R&D, interstate services, office; retail and professional services excluded

## Notes
- Refundable; excess over income tax is refunded (100.850.6)
- No carryforward or carryback
- Not transferable
- Ends when the project's bonds are retired; no fixed sunset
- On MO-1040 only if the approved company is a sole proprietorship; pass-through allocation to owners is not addressed in statute

## Required Information
- MDFB certificate and benefit number
- Assessments paid during 2026

## Questions
- Is your business an MDFB-approved BUILD company?
- What job development assessments were paid in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming on an owner's return without a statutory basis for flow-through
- Claiming more than the assessments actually paid

## Prompt
- My company is in the Missouri BUILD program.
