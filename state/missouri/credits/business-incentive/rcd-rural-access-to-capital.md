# Rural Access to Capital (MO-TC code RCD)
## Description
Credit for a cash equity investment in a DED-certified rural fund. Credits begin on the third annual credit allowance date, so no credits have issued yet (DOR FY25 and FY26 reports show $0).

## Amount
- 0% on the first two credit allowance dates, then 15% on each of the next four (60% total)
- Statewide: $16 million per calendar year

## Eligibility
- Entity investor (not a foreign entity); individuals only through pass-through allocation

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 years; no carryback
- Cannot be sold; allocation to owners is allowed
- Recapture if the fund misses investment requirements
- Sunset: August 28, 2030

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Allocation share

## Questions
- Do you own part of an entity that invested in a Missouri rural fund?

## Common Errors
- Claiming in the first two years

## Prompt
- My partnership invested in a Missouri rural fund.
