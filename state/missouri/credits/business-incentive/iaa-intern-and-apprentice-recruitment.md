# Intern and Apprentice Recruitment (MO-TC code IAA)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for employers that increase the number of paid interns or registered apprentices above their 3-year average.

## Amount
- $1,500 per qualifying intern or apprentice
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $9,000 per tax year (6 hires)
- Statewide: $1 million per tax year; businesses under 5 years old get priority, then first-come, first-served

## Eligibility
- Paid at least minimum wage, in the taxpayer's line of business
- 2026 count exceeds the average of the prior 3 years
- Intern: at least 60 hours a month for 2 consecutive months, with at least 30 college credit hours
- Apprentice: completed one year of a registered program (144 instruction hours, 2,000 on-the-job hours)

## Notes
- No carryforward or carryback
- Not transferable
- Sunset: December 31, 2029; 2026 is covered
- Statute expressly lets individuals, partners, S-corp shareholders, and LLC members claim

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Number of interns/apprentices in 2026 and prior 3 years
- Ownership share if claimed through a pass-through

## Questions
- Did your business hire paid interns or registered apprentices in 2026?
- How many did you have in 2023–2025?
- Did you apply to DED between July 1 and November 15, 2026?

## Common Errors
- Counting hires that do not exceed the 3-year average
- Claiming more than $9,000
- Missing the November 15 application deadline

## Prompt
- My business hired interns this year in Missouri.
