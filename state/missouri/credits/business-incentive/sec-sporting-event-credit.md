# Sporting Event Credit (MO-TC code SEC)
## Description
Refundable credit for hosting an amateur, collegiate, or Olympic sporting event won by competitive bid. Only the certified sponsor, local organizing committee, or endorsing city or county can claim it. INDIVIDUALS CANNOT CLAIM SEC on Form MO-1040.

## Amount
- From July 1, 2026: $6 per ticket sold or $12 per registered participant (was $5 / $10)
- Statewide: $6 million per year ($5.5 million for events in large charter counties or St. Louis City)

## Eligibility
- Applicant is the certified sponsor, local organizing committee, or endorsing city or county
- Sponsor must be a Sports Events and Tourism Association member (new rule)

## Notes
- Refundable; DOR pays within 90 days of a valid certificate
- Not transferable
- No new support contracts after August 28, 2032
- Keep this file for routing only: individuals who donated belong in SPC (50% credit, nonrefundable, 2-year carryforward, $500,000 annual cap from July 1, 2026)

## Required Information
- None for an individual return

## Questions
- Did you host a sporting event, or donate to one? (A donor uses SPC)

## Common Errors
- Entering SEC on an individual return
- Using the pre-July 2026 per-ticket rate

## Prompt
- I donated to a sporting event committee in Missouri.
