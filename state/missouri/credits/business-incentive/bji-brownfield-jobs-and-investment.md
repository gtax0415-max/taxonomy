# Brownfield Jobs and Investment (MO-TC code BJI)
## Description
Jobs and investment credits for an approved brownfield redevelopment project. No sunset in statute, but DED no longer runs the program and DOR's FY26 report shows no activity. A 2026 claim is very unlikely. Not the same as Brownfield Remediation (code RTC).

## Amount
- $400 per new employee, plus further $400 amounts for jobs above thresholds and for hard-to-employ workers, plus investment credits under the business facility rules
- Limited to the greater of project income or 100% / 50% / 25% of business income
- Benefits last up to 10 consecutive years

## Eligibility
- Approved brownfield project with at least 10 new jobs, 25 retained jobs, or a combination
- Tax year within the 10-year benefit period

## Notes
- No carryforward; transferable only with the project
- Pass-through allocation not addressed in statute

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Project start year

## Questions
- Is your business operating an approved brownfield project still within its 10-year period?

## Common Errors
- Confusing BJI with RTC (remediation)
- Claiming after the 10-year period

## Prompt
- My company redeveloped a brownfield site.
