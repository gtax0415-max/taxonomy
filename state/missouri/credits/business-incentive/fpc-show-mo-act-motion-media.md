# Show MO Act / Motion Media (MO-TC code FPC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for film and series production in Missouri. Most individuals hold it as buyers of transferred credits.

## Amount
- 20% base of qualified Missouri expenses, plus four 5% add-ons (50%+ filmed in Missouri; 15% in rural/blighted areas; Missouri crew advancement; Missouri marketing), up to 40% (Film Office cites 42% with a county bonus; statute says 40%)
- Minimum spend: over $50,000 (under 30 minutes) or $100,000 (over 30 minutes)
- Statewide: $8 million films + $8 million series per year

## Eligibility
- DED-approved production; Missouri apprentice or veteran hiring requirement
- Individuals via K-1 or purchase

## Notes
- Carryforward 5 tax periods; must be used within 10
- Transferable
- Sunset: December 31, 2029; 2026 is covered
- The pre-2013 film credit expired November 28, 2013; any carryforward is past its 10-period limit
- Live concert touring is a different credit (EIJ)

## Required Information
- DED certificate, benefit number, issuance year
- Carryforward used

## Questions
- Did you produce a film or series in Missouri, or buy Show MO credits?

## Common Errors
- Coding a concert tour credit as FPC
- Using past the 10-period limit

## Prompt
- I bought Missouri film tax credits.
