# Qualified Research Expense (MO-TC code REC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for Missouri research spending above the prior 3-year average, using the federal §41 definition of qualified research.

## Amount
- 15% of Missouri QREs above the 3-year average (20% if research is done with a Missouri college or university)
- Spending above 200% of the 3-year average earns nothing
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $300,000 per year
- Statewide: $10 million per year ($5 million reserved until November 1 for minority-owned, women-owned, and small businesses); prorated if over-subscribed, with businesses under 5 years old first

## Eligibility
- Tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2023
- Apply to DED by the end of the following tax period (DED's posted window for 2026 credits not yet published)

## Notes
- Carryforward 12 years
- Transferable (up to 100%) by notarized endorsement
- Sunset: December 31, 2028; 2026 is covered
- Statute includes individuals and partnerships; DED's page names only corporate and financial institution tax. Pass-through claims allocated by ownership on the last day of the year

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Missouri QREs for 2026 and 2023–2025
- University partnership, if any

## Questions
- Did your business do qualified research in Missouri in 2026?
- What were Missouri QREs in each of the prior 3 years?
- Was any research done with a Missouri university?

## Common Errors
- Using total federal QREs instead of Missouri QREs
- Counting spending above 200% of the base
- Exceeding $300,000

## Prompt
- My company claims the federal R&D credit and does research in Missouri.
