# Bank Franchise Tax (MO-TC code BFT)
## Description
Former credit for a bank's franchise tax, applied against the bank tax and then corporate income tax. The statute bars the credit for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2020, so it does not exist for tax year 2026. The code still prints on the MO-TC but should not be used on a 2026 MO-1040.

## Amount
- $0 for 2026
- Former computation: 1/60 of 1% of shares and surplus employed in Missouri above $1 million

## Eligibility
- None for 2026

## Notes
- Was a bank/corporate credit (Form MO-1120), not an individual one
- No carryforward or carryback, so no prior-year amount survives into 2026
- Individuals who own S-corporation bank stock use code BTC (RSMo 143.471), a separate credit

## Required Information
- None

## Questions
- Are you a shareholder in an S corporation that is a bank? (If yes, see BTC)

## Common Errors
- Entering BFT on a 2026 return
- Confusing BFT with BTC

## Prompt
- I own shares in a Missouri bank S corporation.
- Can I claim the Missouri bank franchise tax credit?
