# Bond Enhancement (MO-TC code BEC)
## Description
Credit for a holder of MDFB infrastructure bonds designated credit-eligible, when the borrower defaults. The program is dormant: DOR's FY26 report shows nothing authorized, issued, or redeemed.

## Amount
- 100% of unpaid principal and interest, claimed in the tax year after the year of default
- Replaces any other deduction on those bonds
- Outstanding eligible bonds limited to $50 million principal

## Eligibility
- Holder of an MDFB bond or note marked credit-eligible on its face, or a bank or guarantor providing a credit facility, after a default

## Notes
- Carryforward 10 years; no carryback
- Transferable
- No sunset

## Required Information
- MDFB certificate and benefit number
- Default year and unpaid amounts

## Questions
- Do you hold an MDFB credit-eligible bond that defaulted?

## Common Errors
- Also deducting the bond loss

## Prompt
- An MDFB bond I own defaulted.
