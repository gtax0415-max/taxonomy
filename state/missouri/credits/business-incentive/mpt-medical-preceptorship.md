# Medical Preceptorship (MO-TC code MPT)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an unpaid community-based physician (or other eligible preceptor) who supervises medical student rotations.

## Amount
- $1,000 per qualifying rotation
- Maximum 3 rotations, so $3,000 per tax year
- Statewide: 200 credits / $200,000 per calendar year, first-come, first-served (may exceed $200,000 up to the Medical Preceptor Fund balance)

## Eligibility
- Rotation of at least 120 hours in family medicine, internal medicine, pediatrics, psychiatry, or OB/GYN
- Preceptor is not paid for the teaching
- Not available to taxpayers who employ unauthorized workers

## Notes
- No carryforward, no carryback; unused credit is lost
- Not transferable
- Claimed for the year the rotations were completed
- No sunset; covered for 2026

## Required Information
- DHSS certification
- Number of rotations and hours in 2026

## Questions
- How many medical student rotations did you supervise in 2026, and in what specialty?
- Were you paid for precepting?
- Did you submit documentation to DHSS by January 31, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than 3 rotations
- Missing the January 31 DHSS deadline
- Claiming in a year other than the rotation year
- Expecting carryforward of unused credit

- I precepted medical students this year.
