# Disabled Access (MO-TC code DAC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for an eligible small business that spends to comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act. Missouri picks up where the federal disabled access credit (IRC §44, Form 8826) stops: it applies only to spending ABOVE the federal cap.

## Amount
- 50% of eligible access expenditures that exceed the federal §44 monetary cap ($10,250 on the current MO-8826)
- Per-taxpayer maximum: $5,000 per year
- No statewide cap
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Eligible small business as defined in IRC §44 (gross receipts and employee tests)
- Expenditures to meet ADA access requirements, as defined for the federal credit

## Notes
- Carryforward: until used; no carryback
- Not refundable, not transferable
- No sunset (applies to tax years after December 31, 1999)
- Pass-through owners claim their share using the entity's MO-8826 and the K-1
- Spending up to the federal cap earns the federal credit; only the excess earns the Missouri credit

## Required Information
- Total eligible access expenditures for 2026
- Federal Form 8826 amounts
- Ownership share (pass-through)
- Prior-year Missouri carryforward and amounts used

## Questions
- Is your business an eligible small business for the federal disabled access credit?
- How much did you spend on ADA access improvements in 2026?
- Did you spend more than the federal cap?

## Common Errors
- Applying 50% to all spending instead of only the amount above the federal cap
- Exceeding the $5,000 limit
- Not attaching federal Form 8826

## Prompt
- My small business made ADA accessibility improvements.
- Does Missouri have a disabled access credit like the federal one?
