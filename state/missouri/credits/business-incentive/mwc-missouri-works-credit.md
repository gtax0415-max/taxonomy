# Missouri Works (MO-TC code MWC)
## Description
Refundable jobs credit for a qualified company that creates new jobs. Most Missouri Works benefits are withholding retention, which never reaches a personal return. Only the tax-credit portion appears on MO-TC.

## Amount
- Up to 6% or 3% of new payroll (depending on project type); total benefits no more than 9% of new payroll per year
- Manufacturing credits: $5 million per company, $10 million total per year
- Statewide: $106 million credits per fiscal year, plus $10 million each for infrastructure and manufacturing

## Eligibility
- DED-approved qualified company
- Individuals claim through pass-through allocation or as transferees

## Notes
- Refundable
- No carryforward; must be claimed within one year after the close of the tax year the credit was issued for
- Transferable by notarized endorsement filed with DED
- Sunset: August 28, 2030; 2026 is covered
- The separate 9%-of-payroll credit in 620.2010.7 expired June 30, 2025
- 2026 HB 3231 added a capital-investment credit (620.2012, effective August 28, 2026) that cannot issue for 2026 in practice

## Required Information
- DED certificate, benefit number, and tax year issued for
- Year-end ownership percentage (pass-through)

## Questions
- Did your business receive Missouri Works tax credits (not just withholding retention)?
- What was your ownership share on the last day of the entity's year?

## Common Errors
- Claiming more than one year after the credit's tax year
- Treating withholding retention as a credit

## Prompt
- My company is in the Missouri Works program.
