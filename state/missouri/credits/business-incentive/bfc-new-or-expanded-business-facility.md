# New or Expanded Business Facility (MO-TC code BFC)
## Description
Per-employee and per-investment credit for a new or expanded business facility. Closed to non-headquarters facilities that began operating on or after January 1, 2005. Older projects within their 10-year period and qualifying headquarters projects still produce credits.

## Amount
- Formula based on new employees and each $100,000 of new investment, set by DED on the certificate
- Offset limited to 25%–100% of tax depending on the facility

## Eligibility
- Facility began operating before January 1, 2005, and is within its 10-year period, OR
- Qualifying headquarters (engineering, architecture, or accounting firm headquartered in Missouri 50+ years, 500+ jobs, $20 million+ investment) starting or expanding before January 1, 2031

## Notes
- No carryforward for the original holder
- Headquarters credits only: transferable (at least 75% of face value, DED notice within 30 days), a buyer may carry forward 5 years, and credits beyond tax on business income are refundable for facility use
- Pass-through owners: allocated by ownership on the last day of the tax period

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Year-end ownership percentage

## Questions
- Does your business hold a BFC certificate for 2026?
- Is it a headquarters project?

## Common Errors
- Assuming a post-2004 non-headquarters facility qualifies
- Carrying forward a non-headquarters credit

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a Missouri business facility credit.
