# Brownfield Remediation (MO-TC code RTC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for voluntary cleanup of a contaminated (brownfield) site under a DNR-approved plan. Not the same as Brownfield Jobs and Investment (BJI).

## Amount
- Up to 100% of remediation costs, limited to the least needed for the project to proceed
- Includes up to 4 years of operating and maintenance costs (extendable) and demolition up to the remediation amount
- Up to 75% issued when costs are paid; the rest after DNR's letter of completion

## Eligibility
- Project creates at least 10 new jobs or retains at least 25
- Typically issued to the developer; individuals via K-1 or purchase

## Notes
- Usable over up to 20 years; no carryback
- Transferable with written notice to DED; buyer's use limited to the remaining years
- No sunset; active in FY2026

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Year first issued and amounts used

## Questions
- Did you clean up a brownfield site, or buy remediation credits?

## Common Errors
- Coding as BJI
- Using beyond 20 years

## Prompt
- I bought Missouri brownfield remediation credits.
