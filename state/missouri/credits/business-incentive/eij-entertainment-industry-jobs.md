# Entertainment Industry Jobs (MO-TC code EIJ)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for concert rehearsal and touring activity in Missouri. It is NOT the film/TV credit; that is Show MO (code FPC, RSMo 135.750).

## Amount
- 30% of base investment (qualifying Missouri rehearsal and tour spending)
- Per-taxpayer, per fiscal year: $1 million (base investment under $4M), $2 million ($4M–under $8M), $3 million ($8M or more)
- Statewide: $8 million per fiscal year (DED may add $2 million, reducing the next year's cap)

## Eligibility
- At least $1 million spent with Missouri vendors
- At least 10 rehearsal days at a qualified Missouri facility
- At least 2 Missouri concerts
- Musical performer or tour management company

## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years; no carryback
- Transferable only to another Missouri taxpayer, for at least 60% of face value; notify DED and DOR within 30 days; transfer does not restart the carryforward
- Sunset: December 31, 2030; 2026 is covered
- Pass-through allocation is not specified in statute; buyers of transferred credits can claim

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Year of issue and carryforward used
- Transfer documents if purchased

## Questions
- Did you rehearse and tour in Missouri, or buy this credit?
- When was the credit issued and how much has been used?

## Common Errors
- Coding a film/TV credit as EIJ instead of FPC
- Restarting the 5-year carryforward on a purchased credit

## Prompt
- I bought a Missouri entertainment industry jobs credit.
