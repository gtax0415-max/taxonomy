# Resident Credit for Tax Paid to Other States (Form MO-CR)
## Description
Credit for a Missouri resident who paid income tax to another state or a political subdivision on income Missouri also taxes. It prevents double taxation of the same income.

## Amounts for 2026
- Credit = the smaller of (a) Missouri tax × (income taxed by the other state ÷ Missouri AGI) or (b) the other state's tax after its credits (not its withholding)
- The ratio is capped at 100%
- No change for 2026 for individuals (the 2025 HB 754 / SB 98 change applies to estates and trusts only)

## Eligibility
- Missouri resident (full or part year)
- Paid income tax to another state or political subdivision on income included in Missouri AGI

## Notes
- Cannot exceed Missouri tax, so no refund
- When MO-CR is used, line 32 is 100%
- Pass-through entity tax paid by an entity to another state can count
- Attach each other state's return

## Required Information
- Other state's taxable income from that state and its final tax
- Missouri AGI and Missouri tax

## Questions
- Did you pay income tax to another state or city in 2026?
- Were you a Missouri resident all year?

## Common Errors
- Using the other state's withholding instead of its tax liability
- Not attaching the other state's return

## Prompt
- I live in Missouri but work in Kansas.
