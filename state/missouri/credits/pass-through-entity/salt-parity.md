# SALT Parity Act Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a member's share of Missouri income tax paid at the entity level by a partnership or S corporation that elected to pay pass-through entity tax.

## Amounts for 2026
- The member's direct and indirect pro rata share of the entity tax paid
- No per-taxpayer limit or statewide cap

## Eligibility
- Member of an electing entity who did not opt out

## Notes
- Carryforward without limit (DOR)
- Not transferable
- No sunset, and not tied to the federal SALT cap, so the election remains available for 2026
- The member may still owe a Missouri addition for state income taxes deducted federally (likely through MO-A Part 1, line 2; confirm with the 2026 MO-A)
- Example: a partner's $1,565 share of entity tax → $1,565 credit, limited to tax

## Required Information
- K-1 and entity statement
- Prior carryforward

## Questions
- Did a partnership or S corporation pay Missouri entity-level tax on your share of income?
- Did you opt out of the election?

## Common Errors
- Claiming the entity tax as Missouri withholding instead of a credit
- Missing the state tax add-back
