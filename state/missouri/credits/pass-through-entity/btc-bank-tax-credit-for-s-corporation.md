# Bank Tax Credit for S Corporation (MO-TC code BTC)
## Description
Credit for a shareholder of an S-corporation bank, savings and loan, or credit institution for their share of the Missouri bank tax the institution paid. It prevents the same income from being taxed at both the bank level and the shareholder level.

## Amount
- Shareholder's ownership percentage × the bank tax the S corporation paid under chapter 148
- No per-taxpayer limit and no statewide cap
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Shareholder of a bank, bank holding company, savings and loan, or credit institution with an S election
- Stock held for the whole tax period
- A bank holding company cannot claim it; it passes through to its shareholders

## Notes
- Carryforward: the lesser of 5 years or until used; no carryback
- Not transferable
- Refundability not stated in statute; treat as nonrefundable
- On a joint return, offsets only the tax of the spouse who owns the stock
- No sunset
- Not the same as Bank Franchise Tax (BFT), which has expired

## Required Information
- Ownership percentage and holding period
- Bank tax paid by the institution for 2026
- Carryforward from prior years

## Questions
- Do you own shares in an S corporation that is a bank or savings institution?
- Did you hold the shares for the full year?
- Which spouse owns the shares?

## Common Errors
- Claiming against the non-owner spouse's tax on a joint return
- Using BFT instead of BTC
- Using carryforward past 5 years

## Prompt
- I own stock in a Missouri bank that is an S corporation.
