# Missouri Low Income Housing (MO-TC code LHC)
## Description
State companion to the federal low-income housing credit (IRC §42). Owners of a qualified Missouri project receive state credits each year of the credit period, usually through a partnership allocation.

## Amount
- Set by MHDC, up to the federal credit for the same period
- 2026 MHDC allocation plan: state 9% credit limited to 70% of the federal allocation; up to 50% may use accelerated redemption (state credit in years 1–5 equals the annual federal credit)
- Bond-financed (4%) projects: $6 million per fiscal year statutory cap; MHDC may allocate none
- Standard redemption period: 10 years (MHDC practice)

## Eligibility
- Interest in a Section 42 building in Missouri with an MHDC eligibility statement for the year
- Individual owners, partners, members, shareholders, or transferees

## Notes
- Carryback 3 years, carryforward 5 years
- Transferable; owner files a statement with DOR
- Subject to recapture if the federal credit is recaptured
- No credit for a year without the eligibility statement attached
- No sunset; credits still issuing in FY2026

## Required Information
- MHDC eligibility statement and allocation schedule for 2026
- K-1 allocation amount
- Carryback/carryforward history

## Questions
- Do you invest in a low-income housing partnership in Missouri?
- Did the partnership give you a Missouri allocation schedule for 2026?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the eligibility statement (credit disallowed for the year)
- Confusing LHC with Affordable Housing Assistance (AHC)

## Prompt
- My K-1 shows a Missouri low-income housing credit.
