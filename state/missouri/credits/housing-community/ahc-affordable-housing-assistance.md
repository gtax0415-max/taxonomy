# Affordable Housing Assistance (MO-TC code AHC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a business firm's donation to a nonprofit community-based organization holding an MHDC reservation for affordable housing production or operating assistance. Not the state Low-Income Housing credit (code LHC).

## Amount
- Up to 55% of the donation
- Statewide: $10 million production + $1 million operating ($11 million); unused production credits may shift to operating (2025 HB 754)
- Operating assistance limited to $100,000 per applicant organization

## Eligibility
- Contributor must be a "business firm" (same definition as NAC: sole proprietor, farm operator, Missouri rental or royalty income, partner, S-corp shareholder, or LLC member)
- Gift made between the organization's reservation letter and MHDC's deadline
- Cash, equity, services, or real or personal property

## Notes
- Carryforward 10 years; no carryback
- Fully transferable with MHDC approval
- No sunset; FY2027 rounds open (Round 2: December 1, 2026 – February 1, 2027)

## Required Information
- MHDC certificate and benefit number
- Donation date and amount
- Business type

## Questions
- Did your business donate to a nonprofit with an MHDC affordable housing reservation?
- When was the gift made relative to the reservation?

## Common Errors
- Confusing AHC with LHC
- Claiming as an individual with no business income
- Gift made outside the reservation window

## Prompt
- My business donated to an affordable housing nonprofit in Missouri.
