# Historic Preservation (MO-TC code HPC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for rehabilitating a certified historic structure or a structure in a certified historic district.

## Amount
- 25% of qualified rehabilitation costs
- 35% for costs incurred on or after July 1, 2024 in a "qualifying county" (everywhere except Kansas City and the City of St. Louis); back to 25% if the project also uses state low-income housing credits
- Owner-occupied single-family home: $250,000 per project
- Statewide: $90 million per fiscal year, plus $30 million for projects in qualified census tracts; projects under $275,000 are outside the cap. The caps are raised by CPI only after a year's maximum is fully authorized, and no CPI-adjusted figure has been published for FY2027

## Eligibility
- Rehabilitation costs exceed 50% of the property's basis
- DED approval after SHPO review
- Individuals, businesses, and nonprofits may apply (not government entities)

## Notes
- Carryback 3 years (from the placed-in-service year), carryforward 10 years
- Transferable; seller notifies DED within 30 days
- No sunset
- 2024 HB 2062 created the 35% rate and nonprofit access; a court voided it in November 2025; 2026 HB 3080 re-enacted the same rules, retroactive to applications on or after August 28, 2024
- Pass-through credits allocated pro rata or under a written allocation agreement

## Required Information
- DED final certificate and benefit number
- Year placed in service
- Carryback/carryforward used
- Allocation agreement or K-1 (pass-through)

## Questions
- Did you rehabilitate a historic building, or buy historic credits?
- Where is the property (qualifying county or Kansas City / St. Louis City)?
- When was the project placed in service?

## Common Errors
- Using 35% for a Kansas City or St. Louis City project
- Ignoring the carryback option
- Claiming before the final DED certificate is issued

## Prompt
- I renovated a historic house in Missouri.
- I bought Missouri historic tax credits.
