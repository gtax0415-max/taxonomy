# Neighborhood Preservation (MO-TC code RCN)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for building a new owner-occupied home or rehabilitating a home in an eligible or qualifying area. The code name also mentions the older Rebuilding Communities credit (RSMo 135.535), which has issued nothing since 2013.

## Amount
- New home: 15%, up to $40,000 (qualifying area) or $25,000 (eligible area)
- Rehabilitation: 25%, up to $25,000
- Substantial rehab of a qualifying residence: 35%, up to $70,000
- Caps apply per residence over any 10-year period
- Minimum costs: $10,000 ($5,000 for a qualifying residence)
- Statewide: $16 million per year ($8 million per area type)

## Eligibility
- Owner-occupied residence (rentals excluded)
- Preliminary approval through DED's annual lottery (January – mid-February)

## Notes
- Carryback 3 years, carryforward 5 years
- Transferable by notarized endorsement
- Cannot be combined with other state credits, except Historic Preservation with substantial rehab (then the lesser of 20% of costs or $40,000)
- No sunset; active in FY2026

## Required Information
- DED certificate and benefit number
- Project type and area type
- Carryback/carryforward used

## Questions
- Did you build or rehabilitate a home in a Neighborhood Preservation area?
- Did you get preliminary approval through the DED lottery?

## Common Errors
- Claiming for a rental property
- Stacking with another state credit

## Prompt
- I renovated my house and got a Missouri neighborhood preservation credit.
