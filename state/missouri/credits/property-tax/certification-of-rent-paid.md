# Certification of Rent Paid (Form MO-CRP)
## Description
The renter's worksheet for the property tax credit. It turns rent paid at each location into "rent constituting property tax," which is 20% of rent after a facility percentage.

## Amounts for 2026
- Rent constituting property tax = 20% × (rent paid × facility percentage)
- Facility percentages: apartment, house, mobile home or lot 100%; hotel 100% (50% with meals); boarding or residential care 50%; skilled or intermediate nursing home 45%; low-income housing 100%; shared residence 50%, 33%, or 25%
- The total across all MO-CRPs is capped at $1,055 for 2026 on MO-PTS (was $750)

## Eligibility
- Rent paid to a landlord whose property pays property tax (no credit for tax-exempt facilities)

## Notes
- Count only the rent you paid yourself; subtract housing assistance
- Nursing home residents subtract personal allowances first
- Check the box if related to the landlord
- Example: $6,000 rent in an apartment → $6,000 × 100% × 20% = $1,200 → limited to $1,055

## Required Information
- Landlord name, address, phone
- Rental address, months rented, gross rent
- Facility type and any housing assistance

## Questions
- Where did you rent in 2026, and for how many months?
- How much rent did you pay yourself (not covered by assistance)?
- What type of housing was it?

## Common Errors
- Including food, care, or utility charges billed separately
- Using the old $750 cap
- Counting rent paid by a housing program

- How do I report rent for the Missouri property tax credit?
