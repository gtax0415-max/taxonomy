# Biodiesel Retailer and Distributor (MO-TC code BRD)
## Description
Refundable per-gallon credit for selling biodiesel blends at a Missouri service station or to the final user.

## Amount
- $0.02 per gallon for B5–B10
- $0.05 per gallon for blends over B10 up to B20
- Blends below B5 or above B20 do not qualify
- Statewide: $16 million per fiscal year; if over-subscribed, prorated among claims filed by April 15

## Eligibility
- Retail dealer at a Missouri service station, or distributor selling to the final user

## Notes
- Refundable; not transferable; no carryforward; applied after all other credits
- Pass-through entities claim at the entity level and cannot pass it to owners
- New in 2026 (SB 913): if denied for lack of funds, no penalty or interest if the balance is paid within 60 days
- If reduced by proration, an amended return filed on or after July 1 is considered in the next fiscal year
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 (DOR page still shows 2028)

## Required Information
- Station locations or final-user customers
- Gallons sold by blend level in 2026

## Questions
- Do you sell biodiesel blends in Missouri as a sole proprietor?
- How many gallons of each blend (B5–B10, B11–B20) did you sell?

## Common Errors
- Claiming gallons above B20
- Filing after April 15 and missing the proration pool
- Listing it before nonrefundable credits on MO-TC

## Prompt
- I own a gas station that sells biodiesel blends.
