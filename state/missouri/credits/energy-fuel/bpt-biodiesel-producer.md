# Biodiesel Producer (MO-TC code BPT)
## Description
Refundable per-gallon credit for biodiesel produced in Missouri. No certificate; claimed directly on the return with Form 5875.

## Amount
- $0.02 per gallon of biodiesel produced in Missouri
- Statewide: $5.5 million per fiscal year, first-come, first-served

## Eligibility
- EPA-registered producer that began construction or sales by January 2, 2023

## Notes
- Refundable; not transferable; statute silent on carryforward
- Pass-through entities claim at the entity level (MO-PTE); MO-1065/MO-1120S filers without MO-PTE paper-file. An individual claims it only as a sole proprietor
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913 (DOR page still shows 2028)

## Required Information
- Producer registration date and start of construction or sales
- Gallons produced in 2026

## Questions
- Do you produce biodiesel in Missouri as a sole proprietor?
- How many gallons did you produce in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Passing the credit through to owners instead of claiming at the entity level
- Filing after the fiscal-year cap is reached without expecting proration or denial

## Prompt
- I produce biodiesel in Missouri.
