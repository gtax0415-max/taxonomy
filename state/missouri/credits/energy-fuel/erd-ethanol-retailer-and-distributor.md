# Ethanol Retailer and Distributor (MO-TC code ERD)
## Description
Nonrefundable per-gallon credit for selling higher-ethanol blend fuel (15%–85% ethanol) at a Missouri station or directly to a final user.

## Amount
- $0.05 per gallon of higher-ethanol blend (E15 through E85); one flat rate
- Statewide: $5 million per fiscal year (July 1 start); if over-subscribed, divided equally among claimants

## Eligibility
- Retail dealer selling at its own Missouri service station, or distributor selling directly to the final user in Missouri
- Claim by April 15

## Notes
- Carryforward: 5 years
- Not refundable, not transferable
- Pass-through entities claim at the entity level (MO-PTE); the credit does not flow to owners. Individual only as a sole proprietor
- Sunset: December 31, 2033 under 2026 SB 913 (DOR page still shows 2028)
- Penalty and interest caused by proration are not waived (unlike the biodiesel credit)

## Required Information
- Gallons of E15–E85 sold in 2026
- Station locations or final-user customers
- Prior-year carryforward

## Questions
- Do you sell E15 or higher ethanol blends in Missouri as a sole proprietor?
- How many gallons did you sell in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Claiming E10 gallons
- Passing the credit through to owners
- Filing after April 15

## Prompt
- My gas station sells E15.
