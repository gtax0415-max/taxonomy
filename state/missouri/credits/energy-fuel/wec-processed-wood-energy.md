# Processed Wood Energy (MO-TC code WEC)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a Missouri producer of processed wood fuel (pellets, cubes, flour; charcoal at a multiplier) made from Missouri forest residue.

## Amount
- $5 per ton
- Statewide: $6 million per fiscal year, subject to appropriation
- Claimable for 5 years

## Eligibility
- Missouri wood energy producer; hogged or chipped wood does not qualify
- Cannot also take the federal wood energy credit

## Notes
- Carryforward 4 years; no carryback
- Transferable by assignment certificate filed with DOR
- No new credits after June 30, 2033 (SB 913)
- Sole proprietors claim on MO-1040

## Required Information
- DNR certificate and benefit number
- Tons produced

## Questions
- Do you produce wood pellets or charcoal in Missouri?

## Common Errors
- Including chipped wood tonnage
- Missing the June 30 DNR deadline

## Prompt
- I make wood pellets in Missouri.
