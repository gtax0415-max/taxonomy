# Missouri Working Family Tax Credit (Form MO-WFTC)
## Description
Nonrefundable Missouri credit equal to a percentage of the federal earned income credit. Applied last, after the MO-TC credits (line 42) and the property tax credit (line 43). It cannot be refunded or carried forward.

## Amounts for 2026
- 20% of the federal earned income credit (DOR: 20% for tax year 2024 and forward; the statute's maximum, and nothing in the statute lowers it once reached)
- Limited to MO-1040 line 36 minus lines 42 and 43
- Investment income limit: 2026 figure NOT YET PUBLISHED (DOR has listed $4,050 for 2023, $4,300 for 2024, $4,400 for 2025; it indexes the pre-2021 federal limit, not the current federal figure). Do not reuse the 2025 figure
- Federal EIC is measured under IRC §32 as it existed on January 1, 2021

## Eligibility
- Missouri resident filing single, head of household, qualifying surviving spouse, or married filing combined
- Allowed the federal EIC for 2026
- Not claimed as someone's dependent

## Notes
- Married filing separately cannot claim
- No carryforward; any amount above remaining tax is lost
- Counted in line 45 even though it cannot create a refund

## Required Information
- Federal EIC amount
- Qualifying children (names, SSNs, birth dates)
- Investment income

## Questions
- Did you receive the federal earned income credit for 2026?
- What is your Missouri filing status?

## Common Errors
- Claiming on a married-filing-separately return
- Treating it as refundable
- Using the federal investment income limit instead of Missouri's

## Prompt
- Does Missouri have an earned income credit?
