# Missouri Tax Rate Chart
## Description
Tax for each spouse from the Missouri rate chart, figured separately on each spouse's taxable income and rounded to whole dollars.

## Amounts for 2026
- $0 – $1,348: no tax
- $1,348 – $2,696: 2.0% of the amount over $1,348
- $2,696 – $4,044: $27 + 2.5% over $2,696
- $4,044 – $5,392: $61 + 3.0% over $4,044
- $5,392 – $6,740: $101 + 3.5% over $5,392
- $6,740 – $8,088: $148 + 4.0% over $6,740
- $8,088 – $9,436: $202 + 4.5% over $8,088
- Over $9,436: $263 + 4.7% over $9,436
- (2026 Form MO-1040ES; 2025 top bracket started at $9,191)

## Eligibility
- Every return

## Notes
- Top rate stays 4.7% for 2026; the statute allows a cut to 4.6% only if revenue triggers are met, and none was announced
- Example: taxable income $12,300 → $263 + 4.7% × $2,864 = $397.61 → $398

## Required Information
- Each spouse's line 29

- Figuring one tax on combined income instead of each spouse separately
- Using the 2025 brackets
