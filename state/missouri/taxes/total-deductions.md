# Total Deductions and Subtotal
## Description
Line 25 totals all deductions; line 26 is Missouri AGI minus those deductions.

## Amounts for 2026
- Line 25 = line 8 (pension and Social Security exemptions) + lines 13–24
- Line 26 = line 6 − line 25; if less than zero, enter $0

## Eligibility
- Every return

## Notes
- Lines 20, 22, and 23 are reserved on the 2025 form
- Example: line 6 $80,000, line 25 $36,000 → line 26 $44,000

## Required Information
- Lines 6, 8, 13–24

## Questions
- None (computed)

## Common Errors
- Entering a negative line 26

## Prompt
- How are Missouri deductions totaled?
