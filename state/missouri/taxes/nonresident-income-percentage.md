# Missouri Income Percentage (Form MO-NRI)
## Description
Not a credit in the usual sense: it is the percentage that reduces the tax for a nonresident or part-year resident to the share of income that came from Missouri.

## Amounts for 2026
- Percentage = Missouri-source income ÷ total Missouri AGI, capped at 100% and rounded to a whole percent
- Line 33 = line 30 tax × this percentage
- Enter 100% if not using MO-NRI
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Nonresident with Missouri-source income over $600, or a part-year resident

## Notes
- Part-year residents may use MO-NRI or MO-CR, whichever gives the lower tax
- Missouri-source income includes Missouri wages, Missouri business and rental income, and income earned while a resident

## Required Information
- Residency dates
- Income earned in Missouri or while a resident

## Questions
- Were you a Missouri resident for all of 2026?
- What income did you earn in Missouri?

## Common Errors
- Using MO-NRI and MO-CR together for the same spouse
- Forgetting to enter 100% when MO-NRI is not used

## Prompt
- I moved out of Missouri in the middle of 2026.
