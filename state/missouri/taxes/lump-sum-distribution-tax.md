# Tax on Lump Sum Distributions
## Description
A Missouri resident who used federal Form 4972 (special averaging for a lump-sum retirement distribution) pays Missouri tax equal to 10% of the federal tax on that distribution.

## Amounts for 2026
- 10% of the federal Form 4972 tax on the distribution received in 2026

## Eligibility
- Missouri was the state of legal residence when the distribution was received
- Used federal Form 4972 (generally only participants born before January 2, 1936)

## Notes
- Line 34 has two checkboxes: lump sum distribution and low-income housing recapture
- No change for 2026
- Example: $1,000 of federal Form 4972 tax → $100

## Required Information
- Federal Form 4972 tax amount

## Questions
- Did you use federal Form 4972 for a lump-sum retirement distribution?

## Common Errors
- Including the distribution's ordinary-tax amount instead of the Form 4972 tax

## Prompt
- How is a lump-sum distribution taxed in Missouri?
