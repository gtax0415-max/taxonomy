# Recapture of Missouri Low Income Housing Credit
## Description
When federal low-income housing credit is recaptured, the Missouri credit is recaptured in the same proportion.

## Amounts for 2026
- Missouri recapture = original state credit × (federal recapture ÷ original federal credit)

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer with federal Form 8611 recapture on a building that also received the Missouri credit

## Notes
- Line 34 also holds the lump-sum distribution tax
- No change for 2026

## Required Information
- Federal recapture and original federal credit
- Original Missouri credit

## Questions
- Did you have federal low-income housing credit recapture in 2026?

## Common Errors
- Recapturing the full state credit instead of the proportion

## Prompt
- Do I owe Missouri low-income housing credit recapture?
