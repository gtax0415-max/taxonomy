# Federal Adjusted Gross Income (Line 1)
## Description
Missouri starts from federal adjusted gross income, split between spouses on a combined return.

## Amounts for 2026
- Federal AGI from the 2026 Form 1040 (line 11a on the 2025 form; confirm the line on the 2026 federal form)
- Missouri follows the federal Code as currently in effect, so 2025 federal changes to AGI flow through
- Federal deductions below AGI (tips, overtime, car-loan interest, senior deduction) do not reduce Missouri income

## Eligibility
- Every return

## Notes
- Combined return: each spouse's share is figured on the instructions' worksheet; shares must add to total federal AGI
- Negative federal AGI on a non-combined return → $0
- Example: single filer with federal AGI $52,000 → line 1Y $52,000

## Required Information
- Federal AGI and each spouse's share

## Questions
- What is your federal adjusted gross income?
- On a combined return, how much belongs to each spouse?

## Common Errors
- Starting from federal taxable income
- Using federal line 11b (it repeats 11a for the taxable income calculation)

## Prompt
- Where does my Missouri return start?
