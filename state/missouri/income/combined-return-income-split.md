# Combined Return: Splitting Federal AGI Between Spouses
## Description
On a married-filing-combined return, each spouse reports their own share of federal AGI so Missouri can apply the tax to each spouse's income.

## Amounts for 2026
- Each income item and adjustment is assigned to the spouse who earned or owns it
- Joint accounts follow ownership (generally split equally)
- Social Security follows each spouse's own benefits
- The two columns must add to federal AGI

## Eligibility
- Married filing combined returns

## Notes
- No change for 2026
- Example: joint AGI $174,368 with wages $92,000 and $82,368 → line 1Y $92,000, line 1S $82,368

## Required Information
- Each income item and adjustment by spouse

## Questions
- Whose income is each item? How are joint accounts owned?

## Common Errors
- Putting all joint investment income on one spouse
- Columns that do not add to federal AGI
