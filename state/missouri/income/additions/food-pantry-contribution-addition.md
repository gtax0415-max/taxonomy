# Food Pantry Contributions on Schedule A
## Description
A food pantry gift used for the Food Pantry Tax Credit and also deducted on federal Schedule A is added back, so the gift does not give a double benefit.

## Amounts for 2026
- The amount of the credited gift included in federal itemized deductions
- 2026 is the final year of the Food Pantry credit (sunset December 31, 2026)

## Eligibility
- Taxpayer claimed the Food Pantry credit and itemized the same gift federally

## Notes
- No addition if the taxpayer took the federal standard deduction
- Example: $500 food pantry gift itemized federally and credited → add $500

## Required Information
- Credited gifts and whether they were itemized

## Questions
- Did you claim the food pantry credit and also itemize the same gift on your federal return?

## Common Errors
- Skipping the add-back when itemizing
- Adding back when the standard deduction was taken

## Prompt
- Do I add back food pantry gifts in Missouri?
