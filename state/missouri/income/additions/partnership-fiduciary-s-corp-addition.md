# Partnership, Fiduciary, S Corporation and Other Additions
## Description
Missouri additions passed through from partnerships, trusts, and S corporations, plus other additions with no line of their own. Boxes: Partnership, Fiduciary, S Corporation; Business Interest; Net Operating Loss; Other.

## Amounts for 2026
- Entity additions as reported on the Missouri notice
- Net operating loss: NOL (other than farm losses) carried back more than 2 years or forward more than 20
- Business interest: §163(j) carryforward deducted this year that Missouri already allowed earlier
- Individual Medical Account withdrawals for non-medical use, plus interest
- Family development account deposits withdrawn for a non-qualified use
- No new items for 2026

## Eligibility
- Each item's own rules

## Notes
- The business interest addition reverses a prior-year line 11 subtraction
- Example: K-1 notice of a $400 Missouri addition → add $400 with the partnership box

## Required Information
- Item type and amount, with the entity's notice

## Questions
- Did a partnership, trust, or S corporation report a Missouri addition to you?
- Did you deduct business interest carried forward from an earlier year?

## Common Errors
- Omitting the entity's notice
- Not reversing a prior 163(j) subtraction
