# Nonqualified 529 Distribution
## Description
A 529 withdrawal not used for qualified expenses: contributions previously subtracted on Missouri returns and earnings not already in federal AGI are added back.

## Amounts for 2026
- Previously subtracted contributions + untaxed earnings in the nonqualified withdrawal
- Missouri's definition of qualified expenses follows federal IRC §529 as amended, so K-12 tuition (federal limit $20,000 for 2026) and student loan repayments (up to $10,000 lifetime) are qualified under the statute's wording; DOR has not issued guidance on the 2025 federal expansions

## Eligibility
- Account owner (or beneficiary if the owner has died) who took a nonqualified distribution

## Notes
- Rollovers to another 529 plan are not distributions
- Example: $3,000 withdrawal used for a car → add $3,000 (contributions plus earnings in it)

## Required Information
- Distribution amount and use
- Contributions subtracted in prior years

## Questions
- Did you take money from a 529 plan for something other than education?

## Common Errors
- Adding only the earnings
- Treating qualified K-12 or loan payments as nonqualified
- Treating a rollover as a distribution

## Prompt
- Do I add back a 529 withdrawal in Missouri?
