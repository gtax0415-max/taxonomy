# Nonresident Property Tax
## Description
Nonresidents and part-year residents add back property tax paid to another state that was deducted on federal Schedule A, unless that state allows a subtraction for Missouri property tax.

## Amounts for 2026
- The other-state property tax included in federal itemized deductions
- If the federal SALT cap limited the deduction (Schedule A line 5e), add back only the other-state property tax's proportional share of line 5e
- The 2026 federal SALT cap is $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately) under 2025 federal law

## Eligibility
- Nonresident or part-year resident who itemized
- The other state is not on DOR's list of states that allow a subtraction for Missouri property tax

## Notes
- Full-year residents do not use this line

## Required Information
- Other-state property tax paid and deducted
- Schedule A SALT figures

## Questions
- Were you a nonresident or part-year resident of Missouri in 2026?
- Did you deduct property tax paid to another state?

## Common Errors
- Using it as a full-year resident
- Ignoring DOR's reciprocity list
- Adding back the uncapped amount

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri nonresident property tax addition?
