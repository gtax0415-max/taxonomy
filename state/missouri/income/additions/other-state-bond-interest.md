# Interest on Non-Missouri State and Local Obligations
## Description
Municipal bond interest that is tax-free federally but issued by states and localities other than Missouri is added back.

## Amounts for 2026
- The non-Missouri tax-exempt interest
- Reduced by related expenses only if they total $500 or more
- No change for 2026

## Eligibility
- Interest on obligations of other states and their political subdivisions

## Notes
- Missouri-issued bonds are not added back
- For mutual funds, add only the non-Missouri share (use the fund's state percentage letter)
- Example: $3,200 of Illinois municipal bond interest → add $3,200

## Required Information
- Tax-exempt interest by issuer state

## Questions
- Do you hold municipal bonds or muni funds issued outside Missouri?

## Common Errors
- Adding Missouri bond interest
- Adding a fund's entire exempt dividend instead of the non-Missouri share

## Prompt
- Is out-of-state muni interest taxable in Missouri?
