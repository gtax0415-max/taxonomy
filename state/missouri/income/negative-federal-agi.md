# Negative Federal Adjusted Gross Income
## Description
Missouri does not allow a negative starting amount on line 1.

## Amounts for 2026
- Negative federal AGI on a non-combined return → enter $0
- Combined return with one spouse negative and joint AGI positive → put the full joint AGI on the positive spouse's line and $0 on the other
- If one spouse's Missouri AGI is negative, that spouse's line 7 percentage is 0% and the other's is 100%

## Eligibility
- Returns with a negative federal AGI or a negative spouse share

## Notes
- No change for 2026
- Example: joint AGI $90,000, spouse A $107,000, spouse B −$17,000 → line 1Y $90,000, line 1S $0

## Required Information
- Each spouse's share of federal AGI

## Questions
- Did either spouse have a loss larger than their income?

## Common Errors
- Entering a negative amount on line 1

## Prompt
- What if my AGI is negative on my Missouri return?
