# Farmers Estimated Tax Rule
## Description
A qualified farmer makes a single estimated payment by January 15, or files and pays in full by March 1, instead of four installments.

## Amounts for 2026
- Qualified farmer: farming income is at least two-thirds of Missouri gross income
- Pay by January 15, 2027, or file and pay in full by March 1, 2027
- Required payment: 66⅔% of 2026 tax (or 100% of 2025 tax)

## Eligibility
- Two-thirds farming income test

## Notes
- There is no farmer checkbox on MO-1040 line 55; write "Exempt, farmer" on MO-2210 line 1
- Example: farmer files and pays by March 1, 2027 → no penalty

## Required Information
- Gross farm income and total gross income

## Questions
- Is at least two-thirds of your gross income from farming?
- Will you file and pay by March 1, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Looking for a farmer box on line 55

## Prompt
- Do farmers pay estimated tax in Missouri?
