# Amount Paid with Extension (Form MO-60)
## Description
Payment made with a Missouri extension request by the original due date.

## Amounts for 2026
- Pay by April 15, 2027; the extension moves the filing deadline to October 15, 2027 (inferred; 2026 instructions not yet out)
- The extension does not extend the time to pay: unpaid tax after April 15, 2027 gets a 5% addition plus 7% interest

## Eligibility
- Anyone who files Form MO-60 with a payment

## Notes
- A federal extension (Form 4868) gives an automatic Missouri extension; MO-60 is needed only to pay, or to get a Missouri extension without a federal one
- Enclose a copy of MO-60 with the return
- Example: $500 paid with MO-60 → line 41 $500

## Required Information
- Date and amount of the extension payment

## Questions
- Did you make a payment with a Missouri extension?
- Did you get a federal extension?

## Common Errors
- Counting the extension payment as an estimated payment (line 38)
- Assuming the extension also extends the payment date

## Prompt
- I filed a Missouri extension and paid some tax.
