# Payments for Nonresident Partners or S Corporation Shareholders
## Description
Missouri tax that a partnership or S corporation paid on behalf of a nonresident owner. The owner claims it as a payment on a nonresident MO-1040.

## Amounts for 2026
- Entity withholds at the top individual rate: 4.7%
- Claimed in full on line 39

## Eligibility
- Nonresident individual partner or shareholder who is not included in a composite return
- No withholding is required if the owner signed a nonresident agreement, is on a composite return, has under $1,200 of Missouri-source income from the entity, or received no distributions

## Notes
- Mostly for nonresidents; residents rarely use this line
- MO-NRP shows Missouri-source income for the MO-NRI percentage; the amount withheld comes from MO-2NR

## Required Information
- Tax withheld by each entity
- Missouri-source income from each entity

## Questions
- Are you a nonresident owner of a Missouri partnership or S corporation?
- Did the entity withhold Missouri tax for you?

## Common Errors
- Claiming withholding when the owner was included in a composite return

## Prompt
- My Missouri partnership withheld tax for me as a nonresident.
