# Annualized Income and Other MO-2210 Exceptions
## Description
Exceptions that reduce or remove the penalty for an installment when payments matched one of several tests. Useful when income was uneven during the year.

## Amounts for 2026
- Exception 1 (prior-year tax): 25%, 50%, 75%, 100% of 2025 tax
- Exception 2 (2025 income at 2026 rates): 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%
- Exception 3 (annualized income): 22.5%, 45%, 67.5% of tax on annualized income; NOT available for the fourth installment
- Exception 4 (actual income through each period): 90%, first three installments only

## Eligibility
- Filers using the MO-2210 regular method

## Notes
- No change for 2026 (2026 MO-2210 not yet published)
- Example: a filer with most income in December uses Exception 3 for the first three installments

## Required Information
- Income and deductions through each period

## Questions
- Was your income uneven during 2026?

## Common Errors
- Applying 90% annualized to the fourth installment

## Prompt
- What is the Missouri annualized income exception?
