# Underpayment and Amount Due
## Description
What the taxpayer owes when total tax (line 36) is more than total payments and credits (line 45, or 48 on an amended return), plus any estimated tax penalty.

## Amounts for 2026
- Line 54 = line 36 − line 45 (or 48)
- Line 55 = underpayment of estimated tax penalty from Form MO-2210 (7% for 2026 periods)
- Line 56 = line 54 + line 55
- Due April 15, 2027; an extension to file (October 15, 2027) does not extend the time to pay
- Interest on late payment: 7% (DOR's posted rate for both 2026 and 2027)
- Late-payment addition 5% of unpaid tax; late-filing addition 5% per month up to 25%

## Eligibility
- Every return with line 36 greater than line 45

## Notes
- Payment by check, money order, e-check ($0.50 fee), or credit card (2% + $0.25)
- Example: tax $3,000, payments $2,600 → line 54 $400

## Required Information
- Lines 36 and 45
- MO-2210 result

## Questions
- None (computed)

## Common Errors
- Assuming the filing extension also extends the payment date
