# Overpayment and Refund
## Description
When payments and credits exceed total tax, line 49 is the overpayment. The taxpayer can apply part to 2027, donate to trust funds, or deposit to a MOST 529 account; the rest is refunded on line 53.

## Amounts for 2026
- Line 49 = line 45 (or 48) − line 36
- Line 51 trust fund donations (boxes 51a–51n): minimum $2 ($4 married) for Children's, Veterans, Elderly Home-Delivered Meals, National Guard, and Organ Donor funds; $1 ($2 married) for the others; codes for additional charities $1–$200 each
- Line 53 = line 49 − lines 50, 51, 52

## Eligibility
- Every return with line 45 greater than line 36

## Notes
- Refunds of $100,000 or more must be e-filed with direct deposit
- Paper filers who want direct deposit use DOR's ACH agreement form
- Trust fund donations are irrevocable
- Example: overpayment $800, $25 donation → refund $775

## Required Information
- Donation choices and amounts
- Bank routing and account numbers for direct deposit

## Questions
- Would you like to donate any of your refund to a Missouri trust fund?
- Do you want the refund by direct deposit?

## Common Errors
- Donating less than the fund minimum
- Forgetting donations reduce the refund

## Prompt
- How much is my Missouri refund?
