# Refund Deposit to a MOST 529 Account (Form 5632)
## Description
All or part of the refund deposited directly into existing MOST (Missouri's 529 education plan) accounts.

## Amounts for 2026
- Minimum total deposit: $25
- Maximum accounts: Form 5632 appears to say four (an earlier description said five; confirm on the 2026 form)

## Eligibility
- Open MOST account administered by the Missouri Education Program; other states' 529 plans do not qualify

## Notes
- If the refund is offset or reduced below the requested deposit, the deposit is cancelled and the refund is paid to the taxpayer
- The deposit reaches the account when the refund is paid (in 2027), so it should not be counted as a 2026 contribution for the 529 subtraction (timing rule not confirmed by DOR)
- Example: $500 of the refund to a child's MOST account → line 52 $500

## Required Information
- Each MOST account number and amount

## Questions
- Do you want part of your refund deposited into a MOST 529 account?

## Common Errors
- Depositing into a non-MOST 529 plan
- Also claiming the deposit as a 2026 529 contribution subtraction

## Prompt
- Can my Missouri refund go into a 529?
