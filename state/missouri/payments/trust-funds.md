# Trust Fund Donations (Line 51)
## Description
Donations from the refund to state trust funds and listed charities.

## Amounts for 2026 (2025 list; confirm on the 2026 form)
- 51a Children's Trust Fund; 51b Veterans Trust Fund; 51c Elderly Home-Delivered Meals Trust Fund; 51d Missouri National Guard Trust Fund; 51i Organ Donor Program Fund — minimum $2 ($4 combined)
- 51e Workers' Memorial Fund; 51f Childhood Lead Testing Fund; 51g Missouri Military Family Relief Fund; 51h General Revenue Fund; 51j Kansas City Regional Law Enforcement Memorial Foundation Fund; 51k Soldiers Memorial Military Museum in St. Louis Fund; 51l Missouri Medal of Honor Fund — minimum $1 ($2 combined)
- 51m and 51n, by code: 01 American Cancer Society; 02 American Diabetes Association; 03 American Heart Association; 05 ALS; 07 Muscular Dystrophy Association; 08 March of Dimes; 09 Arthritis Foundation; 10 National MS Society — $1 to $200 each; 14 Foster Care and Adoptive Parents Recruitment and Retention — $1 ($2 combined)

## Eligibility
- Filers with an overpayment

## Notes
- Donations are irrevocable
- Some letter-to-fund assignments came from a partly unreadable PDF; confirm on the printed 2026 form
- Example: $25 to the Veterans Trust Fund on 51b

## Required Information
- Fund and amount for each donation

## Questions
- Would you like to donate part of your refund to a Missouri trust fund or charity?

## Common Errors
- Donating less than the fund minimum
- Donating more than the overpayment
- Using a code not on the list

## Prompt
- Which charities can I donate to on my Missouri return?
