# Overpayment Applied to 2027 Estimated Tax
## Description
Part or all of the 2026 overpayment the taxpayer chooses to apply to 2027 estimated tax instead of receiving as a refund.

## Amounts for 2026
- Any amount up to line 49
- Treated as a 2027 estimated payment made on the date the 2026 return is filed

## Eligibility
- Any return with an overpayment

## Notes
- No interest is paid on the amount applied
- No source confirms the choice can be changed after filing; treat it as final
- On the 2027 return this amount is included on the estimated payments line
- Example: $1,500 applied → 2027 line 38 includes $1,500

## Required Information
- Amount to apply

## Questions
- Do you want part of your refund applied to your 2027 Missouri taxes?

## Common Errors
- Forgetting to include it on next year's estimated payments line

## Prompt
- Can I apply my Missouri refund to next year?
