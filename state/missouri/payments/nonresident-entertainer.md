# Payments for Nonresident Entertainers
## Description
Missouri tax withheld from a nonresident entertainer's pay for performances in Missouri. The entertainer claims it as a payment on the return.

## Amounts for 2026
- Withholding: 2% of compensation when pay is over $300
- Claimed in full on line 40

## Eligibility
- Nonresident entertainer (or the entity) performing in Missouri with tax withheld

## Notes
- Claimed once only, by either the entertainer or the entertainer's corporation
- No Missouri return is needed if every venue withheld and there is no other Missouri income
- 501(c)(3) venues that receive no money from the performance do not withhold

## Required Information
- Compensation and tax withheld per performance
- Performance dates and places

## Questions
- Did you perform in Missouri in 2026 as a nonresident?
- Did the venue withhold Missouri tax?

## Common Errors
- Claiming on both the entity's and the individual's return

## Prompt
- I'm a musician who played shows in Missouri.
