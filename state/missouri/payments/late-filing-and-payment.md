# Late Filing and Late Payment Additions and Interest
## Description
Charges for filing or paying the 2026 return late. DOR computes and bills them; they are not entered on the return (the estimated tax penalty on line 55 is separate).

## Amounts for 2026
- Late payment: 5% of tax not paid by April 15, 2027
- Late filing: 5% per month, up to 25%, of unpaid tax
- Interest: simple interest at 7% (DOR's posted rate for 2026 and 2027), from April 15, 2027 even if an extension was granted

## Eligibility
- Returns filed or paid after the due date

## Notes
- The filing extension (to October 15, 2027) avoids the late-filing addition but not the late-payment addition or interest
- Example: $1,000 unpaid on April 15, 2027 and paid June 15, 2027 with the return filed on time → $50 late-payment addition plus about $12 interest

## Required Information
- Filing and payment dates

## Questions
- Will you file or pay after April 15, 2027?

## Common Errors
- Assuming the extension delays payment
