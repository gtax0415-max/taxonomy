# Underpayment of Estimated Tax Penalty (Form MO-2210)
## Description
Interest-based penalty when estimated payments and withholding fell short of the required amount for any installment.

## Amounts for 2026
- Required annual payment: the smaller of 90% of 2026 tax (66⅔% for qualified farmers) or 100% of 2025 tax; no 110% high-income rule
- No penalty if 2026 tax minus withholding is under $500
- Rate: 7% for days in 2026 and 7% for days in 2027 (DOR's posted statutory rates)

## Eligibility
- Filers who underpaid any installment

## Notes
- Short method: only if payments were made in equal amounts on time and no exception is used
- Regular method: four installments, with exceptions (see annualized-installment-method.md)
- Farmers who file and pay in full by March 1, 2027 are exempt
- Example: tax $2,400 with no payments → penalty figured by installment at 7%

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each payment
- 2025 tax

## Questions
- Did you make Missouri estimated payments? When and how much?

## Common Errors
- Using the federal 110% rule
- Filing MO-2210 when tax minus withholding is under $500

## Prompt
- Do I owe a Missouri underpayment penalty?
