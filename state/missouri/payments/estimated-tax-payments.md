# Missouri Estimated Tax Payments
## Description
Estimated payments made for 2026, plus any 2025 overpayment the taxpayer applied to 2026.

## Amounts for 2026
- Required if expected Missouri tax after withholding and credits is $100 or more
- Due dates: April 15, June 15, and September 15, 2026, and January 15, 2027 (next business day if a weekend or holiday)
- Safe harbor: pay 90% of 2026 tax (66⅔% for farmers) or 100% of 2025 tax; Missouri has no 110% high-income rule
- Underpayment penalty figured on Form MO-2210 at 7% for 2026 periods

## Eligibility
- Any filer expecting $100 or more of tax not covered by withholding

## Notes
- Filing the 2026 return and paying in full by January 31, 2027 (March 1 for farmers) replaces the January payment
- Example: four $750 payments → line 38 $3,000

## Required Information
- Date and amount of each payment
- 2025 overpayment applied to 2026

## Questions
- What Missouri estimated payments did you make for 2026, and when?
- Did you apply part of your 2025 refund to 2026?

## Common Errors
- Leaving out the 2025 overpayment applied
- Including federal estimated payments

## Prompt
- I made quarterly Missouri tax payments.
