# Missouri Tax Withheld
## Description
Missouri income tax withheld from wages, pensions, and other payments during 2026.

## Amounts for 2026
- Sum of Missouri tax withheld on every W-2 and 1099
- 2026 employer withholding used a top rate of 4.7% and the federal standard deduction ($16,100 single, $24,150 head of household, $32,200 married)

## Eligibility
- Any filer with Missouri withholding

## Notes
- Do NOT include federal tax, other states' withholding, local tax, or Kansas City / St. Louis earnings tax (earnings tax belongs in itemized deductions, W-2 box 19)
- Entity-paid nonresident partner tax goes on line 39; entertainer withholding on line 40
- Attach all W-2s and 1099s
- Missouri tax withheld on a W-2G is likely includable but not confirmed in DOR instructions
- Example: W-2 box 17 $2,100 for MO → line 37 $2,100

## Required Information
- Missouri withholding from each W-2 and 1099

## Questions
- How much Missouri tax was withheld on your W-2s and 1099s?
- Was any withholding for another state or a city?

## Common Errors
- Including Kansas City or St. Louis earnings tax
- Including another state's withholding

- Where do I enter Missouri withholding?
