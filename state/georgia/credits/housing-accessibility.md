# Housing and Accessibility Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credits tied to a residence: making a home accessible for a disabled person, and a legacy 2009 homebuyer credit that survives only as carryover. Both are Series 200 individual credits on IND-CR forms routed to Form 500, Line 20.
## Credits in this folder
- Disabled Person Home Purchase or Retrofit Credit (disabled-person-home-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 201 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, up to $500 per residence, 3-year carryforward. Code 201
- Eligible Single-Family Residence Tax Credit (eligible-single-family-residence-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 209 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, legacy carryover only. Code 209
## One active, one legacy
| Credit | Status | Amount | Code |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disabled Person Home Purchase or Retrofit | Active | $500 new home / $125 per retrofit feature, $500 cap | 201 |
| Eligible Single-Family Residence | Legacy (2009 purchase; carryover only) | Lesser of 1.2% of price or $1,800, 1/3 per year | 209 |
## Related
- The disabled-person credit requires a permanent parking permit and one or more of four specific accessibility features
- The single-family residence credit requires having originated the credit on a 2009 return
## Questions
- Are you or your spouse permanently disabled and modifying or buying an accessible home?
- Do you have leftover credit from a 2009 Georgia home purchase?
## Prompt
- I made my home wheelchair accessible.
- I bought a new accessible house.
- I have old carryover from a 2009 Georgia homebuyer credit.
