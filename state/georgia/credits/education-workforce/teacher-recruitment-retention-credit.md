# Teacher Recruitment and Retention Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for teachers designated by the Georgia Department of Education as participating in the teacher recruitment and retention program. Worth $3,000 per qualifying school year for up to five consecutive years.
## Amount
- $3,000 per qualifying school year
- For no more than FIVE school years, which must be CONSECUTIVE
- Cannot exceed the taxpayer's tax liability for the year
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Sections 20-2-251 and 48-7-29.23
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at the tax liability. Unused amounts carry forward for 3 years.
## Eligibility
- Designated by the Georgia Department of Education as a participating teacher under Code Section 20-2-251
- Only teachers who have been designated as qualifying by the DOE should complete Form IND-CR 214
- More information on designation: gadoe.org
## Required Information
- DOE designation as a participating teacher
- Confirmation of the qualifying school year(s) and that they are consecutive
- Any unused carryover from a prior year
## Questions
- Were you designated by the Georgia Department of Education as a participating teacher?
- Which school year(s) are you claiming, and are they consecutive?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without DOE designation
- Claiming non-consecutive school years
- Claiming more than five years
- Overlooking the 3-year carryforward of unused amounts
## Prompt
- I teach in Georgia and was designated for a recruitment and retention program.
- Is there a Georgia tax credit for teachers?
- The state designated me as a participating teacher.
