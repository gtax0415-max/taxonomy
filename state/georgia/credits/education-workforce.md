# Education Workforce Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for teachers designated by the Georgia Department of Education under the teacher recruitment and retention program. A Series 200 individual credit on Form IND-CR 214 routed to Form 500, Line 20.
## Credits in this folder
- Teacher Recruitment and Retention Credit (teacher-recruitment-retention-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 214 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, $3,000 per school year up to 5 consecutive years, 3-year carryforward. Code 214
