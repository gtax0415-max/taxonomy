# Disaster Assistance Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a taxpayer who received disaster assistance during the year from a state or federal disaster agency. It is a flat credit tied to the assistance received, capped at $500.
## Amount
- The lesser of $500 or the actual amount of disaster assistance received
- Cannot exceed the taxpayer's income tax liability
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.4
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at $500 and at the tax liability. Carryforward up to 3 years; no carryback.
## Qualifying assistance
- Grants from the Department of Human Services' Individual and Family Grant Program
- Grants from GEMA/HS (Georgia Emergency Management and Homeland Security Agency) and/or FEMA
- Loans from the Small Business Administration due to disasters declared by the President or Governor
## Required documentation
The approval letter from the disaster assistance agency must be enclosed with the return.
## Required Information
- The agency approval letter
- The date assistance was received
- The amount of disaster assistance received
- Any unused carryover from a prior year
## Questions
- Did you receive disaster assistance from GEMA/HS, FEMA, DHS, or an SBA disaster loan?
- What was the amount, and when did you receive it?
- Do you have the agency's approval letter?
## Common Errors
- Claiming without the agency approval letter attached
- Claiming more than $500
- Claiming assistance from a source outside the qualifying list
- Overlooking the 3-year carryforward
## Prompt
- I received FEMA disaster assistance after a storm.
- Is there a Georgia credit for disaster aid I got?
- I took an SBA disaster loan after a declared disaster.
