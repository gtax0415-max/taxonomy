# Disaster Relief Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for taxpayers who received disaster assistance from a state or federal agency. It is a Series 200 individual credit on Form IND-CR 206 routed to Form 500, Line 20.
## Credits in this folder
- Disaster Assistance Credit (disaster-assistance-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 206 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, lesser of $500 or assistance received, 3-year carryforward. Code 206
## Related
- Georgia also allows income SUBTRACTIONS (not credits) for Hurricane Helene disaster relief payments and federal crop insurance proceeds — those are Schedule 1 subtractions, not credits, and are handled outside this taxonomy
## Questions
- Did you receive disaster assistance from GEMA/HS, FEMA, DHS, or an SBA disaster loan?
- Do you have the agency's approval letter?
## Prompt
- I received FEMA disaster assistance.
- Is there a Georgia credit for disaster aid?
