# Qualified Education Expense Credit — QEE (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for contributions to a qualified Student Scholarship Organization (SSO). Preapproved by the Department of Revenue on a first-come, first-served basis against an annual statewide cap. Claimed on Schedule 2, so a return that includes it must be filed electronically.
## Amount
- Equal to the preapproved contribution to the SSO, subject to per-taxpayer limits set by statute for the year and the statewide cap
- Confirm the current-year contribution limits and cap with the DOR credit summary
## Claiming mechanics
- Preapproval required through the Georgia Tax Center (GTC) before contributing
- Series 100 credit — claimed on Form 500, Schedule 2, carried to Line 21
- ENTER YOUR SSN on Schedule 2, not the FEIN of the SSO
- A return that claims, generates, or includes any Series 100 credit MUST be filed electronically
## Eligibility
- Made a preapproved contribution to a qualified SSO during the year
## Required Information
- DOR preapproval letter and credit amount
- SSO confirmation of the contribution
- Your SSN (for Schedule 2)
## Questions
- Did you get preapproval from DOR before donating to a scholarship organization?
- What was your approved contribution amount?
- Are you filing electronically (required for Series 100 credits)?
## Common Errors
- Contributing before receiving DOR preapproval
- Entering the SSO's FEIN instead of your SSN on Schedule 2
- Paper-filing a return that claims a Series 100 credit
- Assuming last year's contribution limits still apply
## Prompt
- I donated to a Georgia student scholarship organization.
- Is there a Georgia credit for private school scholarship donations?
- I got preapproved for the QEE credit.
