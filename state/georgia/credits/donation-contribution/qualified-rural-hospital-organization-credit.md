# Qualified Rural Hospital Organization Expense Credit — RHO (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for a donation to a Rural Hospital Organization (RHO), allowed on a first-come, first-served basis against an annual cap. Claimed on Schedule 2; e-file required.
## Amount
- Equal to the preapproved donation to the RHO, subject to statutory per-taxpayer limits and the statewide cap for the year
- Confirm current-year limits with the DOR credit summary
## Claiming mechanics
- Preapproval through the Georgia Tax Center (GTC) before donating
- Series 100 credit — claimed on Form 500, Schedule 2, carried to Line 21
- ENTER YOUR SSN on Schedule 2, not the RHO's FEIN
- A return that includes any Series 100 credit MUST be filed electronically
## Eligibility
- Made a preapproved donation to a qualified Rural Hospital Organization during the year
## Required Information
- DOR preapproval letter
- RHO confirmation of the donation
- Your SSN (for Schedule 2)
## Questions
- Did you get preapproval before donating to a rural hospital organization?
- What was your approved donation amount?
- Are you filing electronically?
## Common Errors
- Donating before preapproval
- Entering the RHO's FEIN instead of your SSN on Schedule 2
- Paper-filing a Series 100 credit return
- Using outdated contribution limits
## Prompt
- I donated to a rural hospital in Georgia.
- Is there a Georgia credit for rural hospital donations?
- I got approved for the rural hospital credit.
