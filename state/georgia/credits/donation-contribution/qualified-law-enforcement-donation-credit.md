# Qualified Law Enforcement Donation Credit — QLED (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for donations to qualified law enforcement foundations supporting a local law enforcement unit, for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2023 and ending on or before December 31, 2027. Claimed on Schedule 2; e-file required.
## Amount
- Equal to the preapproved donation to a qualified law enforcement foundation, subject to statutory per-taxpayer limits and the annual cap
- A local law enforcement unit is any agency, office, or department of a county, municipality, or consolidated government
- Confirm current-year limits with the DOR credit summary
## Claiming mechanics
- Preapproval through the Georgia Tax Center (GTC)
- Series 100 credit — Form 500, Schedule 2, carried to Line 21
- ENTER YOUR SSN on Schedule 2, not the foundation's FEIN
- Series 100 credits require electronic filing
## Eligibility
- Made a preapproved donation to a qualified law enforcement foundation (TY2023 through TY2027)
## Required Information
- DOR preapproval letter
- Foundation confirmation of the donation
- Your SSN (for Schedule 2)
## Questions
- Did you get preapproval before donating to a law enforcement foundation?
- What was your approved donation amount?
- Are you filing electronically?
## Common Errors
- Donating before preapproval
- Entering the foundation's FEIN instead of your SSN on Schedule 2
- Paper-filing a Series 100 credit return
- Assuming the credit exists outside the 2023–2027 window
## Prompt
- I donated to a local police foundation in Georgia.
- Is there a Georgia credit for law enforcement donations?
- I got approved for the QLED credit.
