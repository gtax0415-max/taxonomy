# Qualified Foster Child Donation Credit — QFCD (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for donations to qualified foster child support organizations, for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2023. Claimed on Schedule 2; e-file required. Distinct from the adoption credits (208/213), which reward adopting a foster child rather than donating.
## Amount
- Equal to the preapproved donation to a qualified foster child support organization, subject to statutory per-taxpayer limits and the annual cap
- Confirm current-year limits with the DOR credit summary
## Claiming mechanics
- Preapproval through the Georgia Tax Center (GTC)
- Series 100 credit — Form 500, Schedule 2, carried to Line 21
- ENTER YOUR SSN on Schedule 2, not the organization's FEIN
- Series 100 credits require electronic filing
## Eligibility
- Made a preapproved donation to a qualified foster child support organization (TY2023 and later)
## Required Information
- DOR preapproval letter
- Organization confirmation of the donation
- Your SSN (for Schedule 2)
## Questions
- Did you get preapproval before donating to a foster child support organization?
- What was your approved donation amount?
- Are you filing electronically?
## Common Errors
- Donating before preapproval
- Entering the organization's FEIN instead of your SSN on Schedule 2
- Confusing this donation credit with the foster-child adoption credits (208/213)
- Paper-filing a Series 100 credit return
## Prompt
- I donated to a foster care support organization in Georgia.
- Is there a Georgia credit for foster child donations?
- I got approved for the QFCD credit.
