# Qualified Education Donation Credit — QED / PEACH (Georgia)
## Description
Credit for a qualified education donation, effective for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2018, allowed on a first-come, first-served basis. Also known as the PEACH credit. Claimed on Schedule 2; e-file required.
## Amount
- Equal to the preapproved qualified education donation, subject to statutory limits and the annual cap
- Confirm current-year limits with the DOR credit summary
## Claiming mechanics
- Preapproval through the Georgia Tax Center (GTC)
- Series 100 credit — Form 500, Schedule 2, carried to Line 21
- ENTER YOUR SSN on Schedule 2, not the organization's FEIN
- Series 100 credits require electronic filing
## Eligibility
- Made a preapproved qualified education donation during the year (TY2018 and later)
## Required Information
- DOR preapproval letter
- Confirmation of the donation
- Your SSN (for Schedule 2)
## Questions
- Did you get preapproval for a qualified education donation?
- What was your approved amount?
- Are you filing electronically?
## Common Errors
- Donating before preapproval
- Entering the organization's FEIN instead of your SSN on Schedule 2
- Paper-filing a Series 100 credit return
## Prompt
- I made a PEACH education donation in Georgia.
- Is there a Georgia credit for public education donations?
- I got approved for the qualified education donation credit.
