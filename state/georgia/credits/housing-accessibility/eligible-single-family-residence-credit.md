# Eligible Single-Family Residence Tax Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable, legacy credit for the purchase of one eligible single-family residence between June 1 and November 30, 2009. Only unused carryover remains claimable — you must have claimed the credit in 2009 to have any carryover now.
## Amount
- Total credit: the lesser of 1.2% of the purchase price or $1,800
- Annual limit: the credit claimed in a single tax year cannot exceed the lesser of one-third (33.33%) of the total credit or the taxpayer's income tax liability
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.17
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at the tax liability each year. Unused credit can be carried forward (no carryback). The taxpayer must have claimed the credit in 2009 to claim any unused amount now.
## Eligibility (historical)
- Purchase of one eligible single-family residence between June 1, 2009 and November 30, 2009
- Eligible residence types included homes for sale before and after May 11, 2009, foreclosed residences owned by the mortgagor/agent, or owner-occupied residences with acquisition indebtedness in default on or before March 1, 2009
## Required Information
- 2009 IND-CR Part 9, Line 5 (original total credit)
- Amounts of credit used in previous years
## Questions
- Did you buy a qualifying home between June and November 2009?
- Did you claim this credit on your 2009 return?
- How much of the total credit have you used in prior years?
## Common Errors
- Attempting to claim it for a home purchased outside the 2009 window
- Claiming a carryover without having originated the credit in 2009
- Exceeding the one-third-per-year annual limit
## Prompt
- I bought a house in 2009 and got a Georgia credit for it.
- Do I still have carryover from the 2009 homebuyer credit?
- I have leftover credit from an old Georgia home purchase.
