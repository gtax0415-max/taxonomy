# Military and Public Service Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credits for military service. The centerpiece is the National Guard / Air National Guard credit for extended active duty. It is a Series 200 individual credit on Form IND-CR 203 routed to Form 500, Line 20.
## Credits in this folder
- Georgia National Guard / Air National Guard Credit (national-guard-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 203 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, equal to SGLI premiums paid, 3-year carryforward. Code 203
## Related
- Georgia also allows subtractions (not credits) for certain military pay and survivor benefits — those are Schedule 1 subtractions, not in this credits taxonomy
- Public-safety scholarship and grant programs (e.g., Public Safety Memorial Grant) are administered outside the income tax return
## Questions
- Are you a Georgia-resident Guard member who served more than 90 consecutive days?
- How much did you pay in SGLI life insurance premiums?
## Prompt
- I'm in the Georgia National Guard and was activated.
- Is there a Georgia credit for Guard members?
