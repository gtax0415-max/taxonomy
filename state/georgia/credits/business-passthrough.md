# Business and Pass-Through Credits — Index (Georgia)
## Description
Series 100 credits are generated by business activity or allocated to an individual via a K-1 from an S corporation, LLC, LLP, or partnership. They are claimed on Form 500, Schedule 2 (Line 21); refundable ones (Timber) are claimed on Schedule 2B. A return that claims, generates, or includes ANY Series 100 credit must be filed electronically. This index lists them so nothing is lost from the taxonomy; the donation credits that individuals commonly claim have their own full files in donation-contribution/.
## Claiming mechanics common to all Series 100 credits
- Enter entity name, FEIN/SSN, credit code, and certificate number (if preapproved) on Schedule 2
- Purchased credits are included; a credit purchased from a prior year is claimed as a prior-year credit
- Some Series 100 credits are transferable/sellable — see the DOR credit summaries ("Line 10 credits that can be sold")
- Many require preapproval or certification through the Georgia Tax Center or another agency
- Electronic filing is mandatory
## Refundable Series 100 credits (Schedule 2B)
- 145 — Timber Tax Credit (Hurricane Michael 2018 losses); refundable only to the taxpayer who incurred the loss, never if purchased
- 155 — Timber Tax Credit (2025 code), 10-year carryforward; same refundability restriction

## 2025 replacement codes (shortened carryforward)
103 → 157, 114 → 158/159, 148 → 160, 142 → 161, 145 → 155.
## 2026 changes (HB 463 — confirmed repeals)
The Georgia Economic Growth and Tax Relief Act of 2026 (HB 463, Sections 4-1 through 4-9), signed May 11, 2026, REPEALS the following credits effective January 1, 2026 (applicable to tax years beginning on or after that date):
- 114 Port Activity Tax Credit (and its 2025 successor codes 158/159) — repealed
- 131 Alternate Port Activity Tax Credit — repealed
- 119 Electric Vehicle Charger Credit (O.C.G.A. § 48-7-40.16) — repealed
- 147 Personal Protective Equipment Manufacturer Jobs Tax Credit — repealed (medical/PPE manufacturing)
- 148 Life Sciences Manufacturing Job Tax Credit (and 2025 code 160) — repealed (medical equipment/pharmaceutical manufacturing)
- Alternative fuel / low- and zero-emission vehicle credits — repealed
- Teleworking credit — repealed
DOR summarizes the repealed set as "certain port activity, vehicle, teleworking, and manufacturing-related credits" (dor.georgia.gov/repealed-tax-credits). Confirm any specific code against that page, since carryovers of previously earned credits may still be usable even where new generation is repealed.
NEW for 2026: code 162 Georgia Employer Childcare Expense Credit begins TY2026. The donation-credit "Add Back" pool functionality also begins TY2026.
Note: all repealed credits above are business/Series 100 credits; none of the individual-facing credit files (Form 500 Lines 17–20) are affected. Verify the exact repeal list against the Act.
