# Income-Based Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credits that scale with income or that hinge on how income is taxed. Note the SHAPE OF THIS CATEGORY IS DIFFERENT from the federal (and New York) one: Georgia has NO state earned income credit. Its income-driven credit is a small fixed-table Low Income Credit, and the other two entries here are the double-taxation relief credit and the new itemizer credit — all three are claimed directly on Form 500 lines with no code and no supporting schedule.
## Credits in this folder
- Low Income Credit (low-income-credit.md) — Low Income Credit Worksheet → Form 500, Line 17. Nonrefundable. DOR ref. 211
- Other State(s) Tax Credit (other-state-tax-credit.md) — Other State(s) Tax Credit Worksheet → Form 500, Line 18. Nonrefundable. DOR ref. 210
- Georgia Eligible Itemizer Tax Credit (eligible-itemizer-credit.md) — Form 500, Line 19. Nonrefundable. New for 2025
## Three direct-line credits, no codes
| Credit | Amount | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Low Income Credit | $5–$26 per exemption from a table; federal AGI must be under $20,000 | 17 |
| Other State(s) Tax Credit | Lesser of Georgia tax on the doubly-taxed income or the other state's tax | 18 |
| Georgia Eligible Itemizer Credit | Up to $300 per taxpayer (itemizers only) | 19 |
## No Georgia earned income credit
Unlike New York's 30%-of-federal (45% for 2026) refundable EIC, Georgia offers no percentage-of-federal earned income credit. A low earner's only income-scaled relief is the Low Income Credit, which is nonrefundable and tops out at $26 per exemption.

## Questions
- Was your federal AGI under $20,000? (Low Income Credit)
- Did you pay income tax to another U.S. state on income Georgia also taxes? (Other State Credit)
- Did you itemize on your Georgia return? (Eligible Itemizer Credit)
## Prompt
- Does Georgia have an earned income credit?
- I made under $20,000 last year in Georgia.
- I paid tax to two states this year.
