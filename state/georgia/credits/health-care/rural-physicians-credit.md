# Rural Physicians Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable $5,000 credit for physicians who practice and reside in a rural Georgia county. For physicians qualifying after May 15, 2024, the successor program is the Rural Health Care Professional Credit (code 154, Series 100), which also covers dentists.
## Amount
- $5,000 per year
- May be claimed for not more than FIVE years
- No carryover and no carryback
- Cannot exceed the taxpayer's income tax liability
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29; Regulation 560-7-8-.20
## Eligibility
The physician must, on or before May 15, 2024, meet all of the following:
- Started working in a rural county after July 1, 1995 (if they worked in one before that date, at least three years must have elapsed before returning)
- Practice AND reside in a rural county — or, for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2003, practice in a rural county and reside in a contiguous county
- Be licensed to practice medicine in Georgia
- Primarily admit patients to a rural hospital
- Practice in family practice, obstetrics and gynecology, pediatrics, internal medicine, or general surgery
## Definitions
- Rural county: 65 or fewer persons per square mile (2020 U.S. Decennial Census for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2022; see Reg. 560-7-8-.20 for transition rules). A listing of rural counties is available at dor.georgia.gov
- Rural hospital: an acute-care hospital in a rural county with 100 or fewer beds (for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2003)
## Required Information
- County of residence and county of practice
- Type/field of practice
- Medical license
- Date the physician began working in a rural county
## Questions
- Do you practice and reside in a rural Georgia county (or reside in a contiguous county)?
- Is your field family practice, OB/GYN, pediatrics, internal medicine, or general surgery?
- Do you primarily admit patients to a rural hospital?
- Did you qualify on or before May 15, 2024?
## Common Errors
- Claiming beyond the five-year maximum
- Assuming a carryover exists — there is none
- Missing that new qualifiers after May 15, 2024 use code 154 instead
- Misjudging rural-county status against the wrong Census year
## Prompt
- I'm a physician practicing in a rural Georgia county.
- Is there a Georgia tax credit for rural doctors?
- I work at a small rural hospital in south Georgia.
