# Community Based Faculty Preceptor Tax Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for a community based faculty preceptor who conducts uncompensated preceptorship rotations for medical, APRN, or PA students. Applicable for taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2019 and ending on or before December 31, 2026.
## Amount
For a physician (as defined in O.C.G.A. Section 43-34-21):
- $500 for each of the first, second, and third rotations (not to exceed $1,500)
- $1,000 for each of the fourth through tenth rotations (not to exceed $7,000)
- Current-year maximum $8,500
For an advanced practice registered nurse (O.C.G.A. Section 43-26-3) or physician assistant (O.C.G.A. Section 43-34-102):
- $375 for each of the first through third rotations (not to exceed $1,125)
- $750 for each of the fourth through tenth rotations (not to exceed $5,250)
- Current-year maximum $6,375
## Limits
- No more than ten preceptorship rotations in one calendar year
- Cannot be carried forward or carried back
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.22
## Eligibility
- Serve as a community based faculty preceptor conducting qualifying rotations
- You must certify you did NOT receive payment from any source for training the student during the tax year
## Required documentation
Certification from the AHEC Program Office at Augusta University must be enclosed with the return.
## Required Information
- AHEC certification
- Number and type (physician vs APRN/PA) of rotations completed in the calendar year
- Confirmation no payment was received for the training
## Questions
- Did you serve as a preceptor for medical, nurse practitioner, or PA students?
- How many rotations did you complete this calendar year?
- Are you a physician, or an APRN/physician assistant?
- Do you have your AHEC certification?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than ten rotations in a year
- Claiming when you were paid for the training (the certification requires you were not)
- Trying to carry unused amounts forward — there is no carryover
- Filing without the AHEC certification attached
## Prompt
- I precept nursing or medical students in my practice.
- Is there a Georgia credit for being a clinical preceptor?
- I trained PA students for free this year.
