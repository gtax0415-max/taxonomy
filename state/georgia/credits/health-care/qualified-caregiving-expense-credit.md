# Qualified Caregiving Expense Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit equal to 10 percent of the cost of qualified caregiving expenses for a qualifying family member who is elderly or disabled. Capped at $150.
## Amount
- 10% of qualified caregiving expenses
- Cannot exceed $150
- Cannot exceed the taxpayer's income tax liability
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.2; Regulation 560-7-8-.43
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at $150 and at the tax liability. No carryover and no carryback.
## Qualified caregiving expenses
Home health agency services, personal care services, personal care attendant services, homemaker services, adult day care, respite care, or health care equipment and other supplies determined by a physician to be medically necessary. Services must be obtained from an organization or individual NOT related to the taxpayer or the qualifying family member.
## Qualifying family member
- Must be at least age 62, OR determined disabled by the Social Security Administration
- Includes the taxpayer, or an individual related to the taxpayer by blood, marriage, or adoption
## What does not count
Qualified caregiving expenses do NOT include expenses that were subtracted to arrive at Georgia net taxable income, or for which amounts were excluded from Georgia net taxable income.
## Required Information
- Receipts/statements for the qualified caregiving expenses
- The qualifying family member's name, SSN, and relationship
- The family member's age (62+) or date of disability
- Confirmation the provider is unrelated to the taxpayer and the family member
## Questions
- Did you pay for caregiving services for a family member age 62 or older, or SSA-disabled?
- Were the services provided by someone unrelated to you and the family member?
- What did the qualified caregiving expenses total?
## Common Errors
- Claiming more than the $150 cap
- Using a related provider, which disqualifies the expenses
- Double-counting expenses already subtracted or excluded from Georgia income
- Expecting a carryover — there is none
## Prompt
- I pay for home care for my elderly parent.
- Is there a Georgia credit for caregiving expenses?
- I hired a personal care attendant for a disabled family member.
