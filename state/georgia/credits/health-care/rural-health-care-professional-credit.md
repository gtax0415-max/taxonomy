# Rural Health Care Professional Credit (Georgia)
## Description
$5,000 credit for a dentist or physician who practices and resides in a rural county. This is the successor to the Rural Physicians Credit (code 207) and broadens it to include dentists. It is a Series 100 credit claimed on Schedule 2, so a return claiming it must be filed electronically.
## Amount
- $5,000 for a rural health care professional (a dentist or physician who practices and resides in a rural county)
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.26
## Claiming mechanics
- Series 100 credit — claimed on Form 500, Schedule 2, and carried to Line 21
- A return that claims, generates, or includes any Series 100 credit MUST be filed electronically
- Confirm current-year carryforward period against the IT-511 booklet and DOR credit summary
## Eligibility
- Licensed dentist or physician
- Practices and resides in a rural county
## Series 200 vs Series 100
- Physicians qualifying on or before May 15, 2024 generally use the older Rural Physicians Credit (207, IND-CR 207)
- The Rural Health Care Professional Credit (154) is the current program and adds dentists
## Required Information
- Confirmation of licensure as a dentist or physician
- County of practice and county of residence
- Entity documentation if allocated through a pass-through entity
## Questions
- Are you a dentist or physician practicing and residing in a rural Georgia county?
- Are you filing electronically (required for Series 100 credits)?
- Did you qualify under the newer program (154) or the older physicians credit (207)?
## Common Errors
- Paper-filing a return that claims a Series 100 credit
- Confusing this credit (154) with the older Rural Physicians Credit (207)
- Assuming dentists qualify under 207 — they qualify under 154
## Prompt
- I'm a dentist in a rural Georgia county.
- Is there a newer Georgia credit for rural health professionals?
- I'm a rural physician — which credit code applies now?
