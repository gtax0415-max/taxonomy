# Health and Care Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Note the SHAPE OF THIS CATEGORY IS DIFFERENT from the federal one and from New York's. The federal health category is about buying Marketplace insurance; New York's is about paying for long-term care. Georgia's health credits are neither — they are HEALTH-CARE WORKFORCE credits that reward physicians, dentists, and clinical preceptors for practicing or teaching in underserved settings. Georgia has NO premium tax credit, NO long-term care insurance credit, and NO nursing home assessment credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Qualified Caregiving Expense Credit (qualified-caregiving-expense-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 204 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, 10% of caregiving expenses capped at $150, no carryover. Series 200, code 204
- Rural Physicians Credit (rural-physicians-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 207 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, $5,000/year up to 5 years, no carryover. Series 200, code 207
- Community Based Faculty Preceptor Tax Credit (community-based-faculty-preceptor-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 212 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, up to $8,500 (physician) / $6,375 (APRN/PA), no carryover. Series 200, code 212
- Rural Health Care Professional Credit (rural-health-care-professional-credit.md) — Form 500, Schedule 2 → Line 21. Nonrefundable, $5,000, successor to 207, adds dentists. Series 100, code 154
## No premium, LTC, or nursing home credit
- Marketplace health insurance is subsidized by the FEDERAL premium tax credit only; there is no Georgia add-on
- Georgia has no long-term care insurance credit and no nursing home assessment credit (both of which New York offers)
- Medical expenses may still flow through Georgia itemized deductions rather than a credit
## Series 200 vs Series 100 within this folder
- 207 and 212 are Series 200 individual credits on IND-CR forms → Line 20
- 154 is a Series 100 credit on Schedule 2 → Line 21, and forces electronic filing of the whole return
## Related
- Federal premium tax credit has no Georgia analogue — see federal/credits/health-insurance/premium-tax-credit.md
## Questions
- Are you a physician or dentist practicing in a rural Georgia county?
- Did you precept medical, nurse practitioner, or PA students?
- Did you buy insurance through the Marketplace? (federal credit only, no Georgia credit)
