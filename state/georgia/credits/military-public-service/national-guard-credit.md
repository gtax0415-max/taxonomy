# Georgia National Guard / Air National Guard Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for Georgia-resident Guard members on extended active duty, equal to the qualified life insurance premiums they paid. It offsets the cost of SGLI coverage during qualifying service.
## Amount
- Equal to qualified life insurance premiums paid — the premiums paid for coverage through the Servicemembers' Group Life Insurance (SGLI) program administered by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs
- Cannot exceed the qualified premiums paid nor the taxpayer's income tax liability
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.9
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at the premiums paid and the tax liability. Carryforward up to 3 years; no carryback.
## Eligibility
- Georgia resident who is a member of the National Guard or Air National Guard
- On active duty full time in the U.S. Armed Forces, or on active-duty training, for a period of MORE THAN 90 consecutive days
## Which year to claim
The credit is claimed in the year in which the majority of the qualifying days are served. If an equal number of consecutive days falls in two calendar years, claim it in the year in which the 90th day occurs.
## Required Information
- Orders showing the qualifying period of active duty or active-duty training
- Records of qualified SGLI premiums paid
- Any unused carryover from a prior year
## Questions
- Are you a Georgia resident member of the National Guard or Air National Guard?
- Were you on active duty or active-duty training more than 90 consecutive days?
- How much did you pay in SGLI life insurance premiums?
- In which year did the majority of your qualifying days fall?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for a deployment of 90 days or fewer (it must exceed 90 consecutive days)
- Claiming more than the SGLI premiums actually paid
- Claiming in the wrong year when service spans two calendar years
- Overlooking the 3-year carryforward
## Prompt
- I'm in the Georgia National Guard and was activated for several months.
- Is there a Georgia credit for Guard members?
- I paid SGLI premiums during a long deployment.
