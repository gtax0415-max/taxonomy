# Adoption of a Foster Child Credit — Adoptions 2008 through 2020 (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for adopting a qualified foster child, for adoptions that became final in taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2008 and before January 1, 2021. A separate, more generous credit (code 213, IND-CR 213) applies to adoptions on or after January 1, 2021.
## Amount
- $2,000 per qualified foster child per taxable year
- Begins the year the adoption becomes final and continues each year through the year the child attains age 18
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.15
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, cannot exceed the tax liability. Unused credit CAN be carried forward until used (unlimited carryforward).
## Eligibility
- Adoption of a qualified foster child final in a taxable year beginning on or after January 1, 2008 and before January 1, 2021
- Claimed each year from finalization through the year the child turns 18
## Which form applies
- Adoption final BEFORE January 1, 2021: this credit (208)
- Adoption final ON OR AFTER January 1, 2021: use code 213 / Form IND-CR 213 instead
## Required Information
- Date the adoption became final
- Confirmation the child was a qualified foster child
- The child's date of birth
- Any unused carryover from a prior year
## Questions
- When did the adoption become final?
- Was the child a qualified foster child?
- Has the child turned 18?
- Do you have unused credit carried forward from a prior year?
## Common Errors
- Using code 208 for an adoption finalized on or after January 1, 2021 (that is code 213)
- Continuing the credit past the year the child turns 18
- Overlooking the unlimited carryforward and letting unused amounts sit unclaimed
## Prompt
- I adopted a foster child a few years ago.
- Is there a Georgia credit for adopting from foster care?
- My foster adoption was finalized in 2019.
