# Child and Dependent Care Expense Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit equal to a percentage of the federal child and dependent care credit. Georgia piggybacks entirely on the federal IRC Section 21 credit — if you did not claim the federal credit, there is nothing to apply the percentage to.
## Amount
- 50% of the federal child and dependent care expense credit claimed on Form 1040
- The Georgia rate is 50% of the federal credit — increased to 50% beginning tax year 2025 and unchanged for 2026
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.10
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, limited to the tax liability. This credit CANNOT be carried forward — unused amounts are lost.
## Eligibility
- You claimed and were allowed the federal child and dependent care credit under IRC Section 21
- The federal credit amount drives the Georgia figure; no separate Georgia expense computation
## Required Information
- Federal child and dependent care expense credit amount from Form 1040
## Questions
- Did you claim the federal child and dependent care credit?
- What was the federal credit amount on your Form 1040?
## Common Errors
- Claiming the Georgia credit without having claimed the federal one
- Applying an old Georgia percentage instead of the current 50% rate (in effect since 2025)
- Trying to carry unused amounts forward — this credit does not carry over
- Expecting a refund from a nonrefundable credit
## Prompt
- I claimed the federal child and dependent care credit.
- I paid for daycare so I could work — does Georgia have a credit?
- How much of the federal dependent care credit does Georgia give back?
