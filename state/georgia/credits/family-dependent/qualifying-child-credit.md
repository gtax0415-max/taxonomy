# Qualifying Child Credit (Georgia)
## Description
Credit of $250 for each young qualifying child. It appears on the Georgia DOR tax credits list under code 215 but does NOT appear in the 2025 IT-511 IND-CR series (201–214), so its first effective year and its exact form/line routing should be confirmed against the current-year booklet before relying on it.
## Amount
- $250 for each qualifying child
- The child must NOT have attained age six during the taxable year
- "Qualifying child" has the same meaning as IRC Section 24(c)
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.27
## Status flag — verify before filing
This credit is listed by DOR but is not in the 2025 IND-CR forms. Confirm: (1) the first tax year it applies, (2) whether it is refundable, and (3) whether it is claimed on a direct Form 500 line, an IND-CR schedule, or Schedule 2, using the applicable year's IT-511 instructions.
## Eligibility
- Has one or more qualifying children under IRC Section 24(c)
- Each child had not attained age six during the taxable year
## Required Information
- Each qualifying child's date of birth
- Confirmation each child meets the IRC Section 24(c) definition
## Questions
- Do you have a child who was under six for the whole tax year?
- Does each child meet the federal qualifying-child definition?
- Which tax year are you filing for? (confirms whether this credit is available)
## Common Errors
- Claiming it for a tax year before it takes effect
- Claiming it for a child who turned six during the year
- Assuming the form/line routing without checking the current IT-511 booklet
## Prompt
- I have a toddler — does Georgia have a child credit?
- Is there a new Georgia $250 per child credit?
- My child was four all last year.
