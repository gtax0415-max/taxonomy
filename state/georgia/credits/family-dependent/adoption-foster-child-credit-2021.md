# Adoption of a Foster Child Credit — Adoptions on or after January 1, 2021 (Georgia)
## Description
Nonrefundable credit for adopting a qualified foster child, for adoptions final in taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2021. Substantially larger in the early years than the pre-2021 credit (code 208): $6,000 per year for the first five years.
## Amount
- $6,000 per qualified foster child per year for the FIRST FIVE taxable years, commencing with the year the adoption becomes final
- $2,000 per qualified foster child per year for years AFTER the first five
- Continues through the year the child attains age 18
- Statutory basis: O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-29.15
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, cannot exceed the tax liability. This credit CANNOT be carried forward — unlike the pre-2021 version.
## Eligibility
- Adoption of a qualified foster child final in a taxable year beginning on or after January 1, 2021
## Which form applies
- Adoption final ON OR AFTER January 1, 2021: this credit (213)
- Adoption final BEFORE January 1, 2021: use code 208 / Form IND-CR 208 instead
## Required Information
- Date the adoption became final (must be on or after January 1, 2021)
- Number of taxable years since finalization (to apply the $6,000 vs $2,000 step-down)
- Confirmation the child was a qualified foster child
- The child's date of birth
## Questions
- Did the adoption become final on or after January 1, 2021?
- How many years ago was the adoption finalized?
- Was the child a qualified foster child?
- Has the child turned 18?
## Common Errors
- Using code 213 for an adoption finalized before January 1, 2021 (that is code 208)
- Applying $6,000 beyond the first five years instead of stepping down to $2,000
- Trying to carry unused amounts forward — this version does not carry over
- Continuing the credit past the year the child turns 18
## Prompt
- I adopted a child from foster care in 2022.
- Is there a bigger Georgia adoption credit for recent adoptions?
- My foster adoption was finalized last year.
