# Family and Dependent Credits (Georgia)
## Description
Credits tied to caring for or adding children to a family: dependent care, foster-child adoption, and (pending) a young-child credit. The care and adoption credits are Series 200 individual credits routed through the IND-CR forms to Form 500, Line 20.
## Credits in this folder
- Child and Dependent Care Expense Credit (child-dependent-care-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 202 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable. 50% of the federal credit (since 2025, unchanged for 2026). Code 202
- Adoption of a Foster Child Credit, 2008–2020 adoptions (adoption-foster-child-credit.md) — Form IND-CR 208 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, unlimited carryforward. $2,000/child/year. Code 208
- Adoption of a Foster Child Credit, 2021+ adoptions (adoption-foster-child-credit-2021.md) — Form IND-CR 213 → Form 500, Line 20. Nonrefundable, no carryforward. $6,000/child/year first five years, then $2,000. Code 213
- Qualifying Child Credit (qualifying-child-credit.md) — $250 per child under six. Code 215. VERIFY effective year and routing
## Two adoption credits, split by finalization date
| Adoption final | Credit | Amount | Carryforward | Code |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2008 through 2020 | IND-CR 208 | $2,000/child/year | Unlimited | 208 |
| 2021 and later | IND-CR 213 | $6,000/year (first 5 yrs), then $2,000 | None | 213 |
The finalization date, not the current year, decides which form. Both run through the year the child turns 18.
