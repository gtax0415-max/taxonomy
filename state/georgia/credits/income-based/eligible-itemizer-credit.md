# Georgia Eligible Itemizer Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit of up to $300 per taxpayer for full-year and part-year residents who itemize their Georgia deductions. New for tax year 2025, it partially offsets the reduced value of itemizing under Georgia's flat-rate structure.
## Amount
- Up to $300 per taxpayer
- Cannot exceed the tax liability on Form 500, Line 16
- Cannot be carried forward to future returns
## Eligibility
- Full-year OR part-year resident
- Itemized deductions on the Georgia return
- Lived in Georgia 183 days or longer during the year, OR was living in Georgia on the last day of the year
## Cap and carryover
Nonrefundable, capped at $300 per taxpayer and at the Line 16 tax liability. No carryforward.
## Required Information
- Whether you itemized on the Georgia return
- Days of Georgia residency or last-day-of-year residency status
- Georgia tax liability (Form 500, Line 16)
## Questions
- Did you itemize your deductions on your Georgia return?
- Did you live in Georgia at least 183 days, or on the last day of the year?
- What is your Georgia tax liability on Line 16?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it when taking the standard deduction rather than itemizing
- Claiming more than $300 per taxpayer
- Trying to carry unused amounts forward
- Applying it on a pre-2025 return — it begins with tax year 2025
## Prompt
- I itemized my deductions on my Georgia return.
- Is there a new Georgia credit for itemizers?
- I heard Georgia added a $300 itemizer credit.
