# Georgia Net Operating Loss Deduction
## Description
Georgia computes its own net operating loss separately from the federal NOL (the federal NOL carryover is added back on Schedule 1). The Georgia NOL utilized this year is entered on Form 500, Line 15b, reducing Georgia taxable income before NOLs (Line 15a) to Line 15c. Post-2017 NOLs are capped at 80% of income.
## The 80% limitation
NOLs for tax years 2018 and later that are applied to Georgia income cannot exceed 80% of Georgia income before NOLs. Pre-2018 NOLs are not subject to the 80% cap.
## The worksheet
1. NOL carryforward available for the current year from years BEFORE 2018
2. NOL carryforward available for the current year from years 2018 AND LATER
3. Income before Georgia NOL (Form 500, Line 15a)
4. NOL from Line 1 applied to the current year (pre-2018, no 80% cap)
5. NOL from Line 2 applied to the current year (2018+, cannot exceed 80% of Line 3)
6. Total NOL applied = Line 4 + Line 5 → enter on Form 500, Line 15b
## Relationship to the federal NOL
The FEDERAL NOL carryover deducted federally is ADDED BACK on Schedule 1 (see income/additions/net-operating-loss-carryover-addition.md); only the separately computed GEORGIA NOL is deducted here. Do not deduct the federal figure.
## Required Information
- Georgia NOL schedules split by pre-2018 vs 2018-and-later
- Line 15a income before Georgia NOLs
- Prior-year Georgia returns establishing the carryforward
## Questions
- Do you have a Georgia net operating loss carryforward?
- Does any of it arise from years 2018 and later (subject to the 80% cap)?
- What is your Georgia income before NOLs?
## Common Errors
- Deducting the federal NOL instead of the Georgia-computed NOL
- Applying more than 80% of income from post-2017 NOLs
- Failing to add back the federal NOL on Schedule 1
- Mixing pre-2018 and post-2018 carryforwards under one cap
