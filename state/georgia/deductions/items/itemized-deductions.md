# Itemized Deductions (Georgia)
## Description
Georgia itemized deductions START from federal Schedule A, then are ADJUSTED to remove items Georgia does not allow. Claimed on Form 500, Lines 12a–12c. You may itemize for Georgia only if you itemized federally, and you MUST if you itemized federally.
## The three lines
- Line 12a: itemized deductions from federal Schedule A (the federal total)
- Line 12b: adjustments to REMOVE — income taxes paid to states other than Georgia, AND investment interest expense for the production of income that is exempt from Georgia tax
- Line 12c: Line 12a minus Line 12b (the Georgia itemized total that flows to Line 13)
## The SALT $10,000 disallowance formula
If state and local income taxes were limited on the federal return to $10,000 ($5,000 MFS), the disallowed OTHER-STATE income tax on Line 12b is:
other state income taxes ÷ total taxes on Schedule A Line 5d × the lesser of (Schedule A Line 5d amount or $10,000 / $5,000 MFS).
Georgia retains the $10,000 SALT cap for 2026 (it did not adopt the federal increase). Do not include state taxes paid by entities that pay tax at the entity level.
## Federal-conformity lock
If you itemize on your federal return, you MUST itemize on Georgia. If MFS and your spouse itemizes, you must itemize. Include a copy of federal Schedule A with the Georgia return.
## Part-year and nonresident treatment
Part-year residents and nonresidents run the same Line 12b computation, then the resulting itemized amount is prorated using the Schedule 3, Line 9 income ratio.
## Note on NOLs within Line 12a
Post-2017 net operating losses applied to Georgia income cannot exceed 80% of Georgia income before NOLs — see items/georgia-net-operating-loss.md.
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A (required attachment)
- Income taxes paid to non-Georgia states
- Investment interest expense tied to Georgia-exempt income
- Schedule A Line 5d figures (for the SALT disallowance formula)
## Questions
- Did you itemize on your federal return?
- Did you deduct income taxes paid to another state on Schedule A?
- Was your SALT deduction capped at $10,000 federally?
- Are you a full-year, part-year, or nonresident filer?
## Common Errors
- Itemizing for Georgia while taking the federal standard deduction (not allowed)
- Failing to remove non-Georgia state income taxes on Line 12b
- Mis-running the SALT disallowance formula
- Forgetting to attach federal Schedule A
## Prompt
- I itemized on my federal return — how does Georgia handle it?
- Do I subtract my other-state income taxes from my Georgia itemized deductions?
- Does Georgia follow the higher federal SALT cap?
