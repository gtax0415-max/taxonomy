# Estimated Tax (Georgia)
## Description
Taxpayers with significant income not subject to withholding must pay Georgia estimated tax quarterly on Form 500ES. Failure to pay enough triggers an underpayment penalty (Form 500 UET).
## Who must pay
Estimated tax is required for each individual subject to Georgia income tax who reasonably expects gross income during the year exceeding: (1) the exemption for dependents, plus (2) estimated deductions, plus (3) $1,000 of income not subject to withholding.
## Due dates (calendar-year filers)
- April 15 of the taxable year (first installment)
- If the requirement is first met on or after April 1 and before June 1: June 15
- On or after June 1 but before September 1: September 15
- On or after September 1: January 15 of the following year
- Fiscal-year filers use corresponding dates
## Planning tool
The "Schedule for Estimating Georgia Income Taxes" on IT-511 page 35 walks through expected AGI, subtractions, deductions, exemptions, and the tax rate to estimate the year's liability. Amounts are rounded.
## Required Information
- Estimated gross income, deductions, and dependent exemptions
- Records of Form 500ES payments made
## Questions
- Do you have income not subject to Georgia withholding over the threshold?
- How much have you paid in quarterly Form 500ES installments?
- Are you a calendar-year or fiscal-year filer?
## Common Errors
- Not paying estimates on income without withholding, triggering the 500 UET penalty
- Missing a quarterly due date
- Underestimating and relying on withholding alone
