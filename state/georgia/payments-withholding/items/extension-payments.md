# Extension Payments (Georgia)
## Description
An extension gives more time to FILE, not more time to PAY. Tax not paid by the original due date accrues interest and late-payment penalty, so an extension payment (Form IT-560) is used to stop those from running.
## The two forms
- Form IT-303 — request a Georgia extension to file (if you are not relying on a federal extension)
- Form IT-560 — make an extension PAYMENT by the original due date to avoid late-payment penalties and interest; the amount paid is entered on the Form 500 payments section
- You may also use the Georgia Tax Center (GTC) to make the extension payment
## Key rule
An extension of time to file does NOT extend the time to pay. Pay by the original due date (generally April 15) to avoid interest and the 0.5%/month late-payment penalty.
## Required Information
- Federal extension (Form 4868) if relying on it, or Georgia Form IT-303
- Form IT-560 extension payment amount and date
