# Underpayment and Late Penalties and Interest (Georgia)
## Description
Georgia imposes separate penalties for paying late, filing late, underpaying estimated tax, and other failures, plus interest on unpaid tax. Several run at once.
## Late filing and late payment
- Late PAYMENT penalty: 0.5% per month (or fraction of a month) on tax not paid by the statutory due date
- Late FILING penalty: a monthly penalty is also imposed when the return is filed late
- Combined late filing + late payment penalty cannot exceed 25% of the tax not paid by the original due date
- Interest accrues on unpaid tax until paid in full
## Estimated-tax underpayment
- Failure to File Estimated Tax penalty: 9% per year for the period of underpayment
- Use Form 500 UET to calculate the estimated-tax underpayment penalty
## Other penalties
- Frivolous Return penalty: $1,000
- Negligent Underpayment penalty: 5%
- Fraudulent Underpayment penalty (higher; see statute)
## Interest rate
Interest accruing on or after July 1, 2016 accrues at the prime rate plus 3% per year. See dor.georgia.gov/annual-notice-interest-rate-adjustment for the annual rate and dor.georgia.gov/penalty-and-interest-rates for details.
## Required Information
- Tax not paid by the due date and months late
- Underpaid estimated-tax amounts and periods (Form 500 UET)
- The applicable annual interest rate

## Common Errors
- Assuming an extension to file avoids the late-payment penalty
- Not filing Form 500 UET when estimated tax was underpaid
- Overlooking that late filing and late payment penalties stack (up to 25%)
