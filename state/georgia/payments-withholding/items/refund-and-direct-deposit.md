# Refund and Direct Deposit (Georgia)
## Description
An overpayment (total payments exceeding the Line 23 balance) is refunded by direct deposit or paper check. Timing and eligibility rules apply, and the claim window is limited.
## The three-year claim window
A claim for refund must be made within THREE years from the later of the date the tax was paid or the due date of the return (including extensions). Some scenarios (e.g., following a federal change) have their own windows.
## Direct deposit rejects → paper check
A direct-deposit request may be rejected (and a check mailed instead) if:
- Any numbers or letters are crossed out or whited out
- An invalid checking, savings, or routing number is entered
- The financial institution does not allow a joint refund into an individual account
The State is not responsible if a financial institution rejects a direct deposit.
## Paper-check-only filers
You will receive a paper check (even if you request direct deposit) if you are a FIRST-TIME Georgia filer or have not filed in FIVE or more years — for both electronic and paper filers.
## Timing and fraud measures
Allow up to 12 weeks to receive a refund; DOR applies additional fraud-prevention measures. Check status at gtc.dor.ga.gov or 1-877-423-6711.
## Deceased taxpayer
To have a refund check reissued in a deceased taxpayer's name, mail Form GA-5347 with a copy of the death certificate and the information specified on that form.
## Required Information
- Valid account and routing number (for direct deposit)
- The date tax was paid or the return due date (for the claim window)
- Form GA-5347 for a deceased taxpayer's refund
## Questions
- Do you want your refund by direct deposit or check?
- Is this your first Georgia return, or your first in five+ years? (paper check only)
- Is the refund within the three-year claim window?
## Common Errors
- Expecting direct deposit as a first-time or long-absent filer (paper check only)
- Entering an invalid routing/account number
- Missing the three-year refund claim window
