# Georgia Withholding
## Description
Georgia income tax withheld during the year is credited against the tax balance. Only income on which Georgia tax was ACTUALLY withheld is entered in the Income Statement Details; the withholding total then reduces what you owe or increases your refund.
## What to enter
- W-2, 1099, and G2-A income and Georgia withholding on Line 4 (GA Wages/Income)
- For Form G2-RP (withholding on sale of Georgia real property): use the income reported on G2-RP Line 12 or 13
- For Form G2-LP: use the income on G2-LP Line 11
- For Form G2-FL: enter zero for the income amount
- Withholding types recognized: W-2, G2-A, 1099, G2-FL, G2-LP, G2-RP
## Key rule
Only enter income on which Georgia tax was withheld. Income without Georgia withholding does not belong in the Income Statement Details section.
## Adjusting your withholding
Use Form G-4 (or G-4P for pension income) to set withholding allowances. DOR encourages taxpayers who received a large refund to reduce withholding so they keep more of each paycheck.
## Required Information
- W-2 / 1099 / G2-A and other statements showing Georgia withholding
- Employer/payer state withholding ID
- G2-RP / G2-LP / G2-FL figures where applicable
## Questions
- How much Georgia tax was withheld from your pay or distributions?
- Did you have Georgia withholding on a property sale (G2-RP)?
- Do you need to adjust your Georgia withholding (Form G-4)?
## Common Errors
- Entering income that had no Georgia withholding
- Using the wrong line from G2-RP/G2-LP for the income amount
- Omitting the employer/payer state withholding ID
- Claiming withholding without the supporting statement
## Prompt
- How do I report my Georgia tax withheld?
- I sold Georgia property and tax was withheld (G2-RP).
- How do I change my Georgia withholding?
