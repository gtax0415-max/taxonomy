# Payments, Withholding, and Refund (Georgia)
## Description
After the tax balance is set on Line 23, the return applies what the taxpayer has already paid — Georgia withholding, estimated payments, and extension payments — to arrive at a refund or amount due. This branch also covers estimated-tax obligations, underpayment and late penalties, interest, refund mechanics, and the periodic surplus refund.
## Items in this folder
- Georgia Withholding (items/georgia-withholding.md) — W-2/1099/G2-A and other statements; Income Statement Details
- Estimated Tax (items/estimated-tax.md) — Form 500ES, who must pay, due dates
- Extension Payments (items/extension-payments.md) — Form IT-303 (file) and IT-560 (pay)
- Underpayment and Late Penalties and Interest (items/penalties-and-interest.md) — Form 500 UET and late filing/payment penalties
- Refund and Direct Deposit (items/refund-and-direct-deposit.md) — refund claim window, direct deposit, paper-check rules
- Surplus Tax Refund (items/surplus-tax-refund.md) — the periodic one-time surplus refund
## The flow (after Line 23)
- Enter Georgia tax withheld (from W-2s, 1099s, G2-As, and other statements) — only income on which Georgia tax was withheld
- Enter estimated tax paid (Form 500ES) and any extension payment (Form IT-560)
- Compare total payments to Line 23 balance → refund (overpayment) or amount due
- Apply any underpayment penalty (Form 500 UET) and late penalties/interest
## Related
- Withholding credit on the sale of Georgia property (G2-RP) and pass-through withholding (G2-A/G2-LP) feed the Income Statement Details
- The Other State(s) Tax Credit is a credit (Line 18), not a payment — see credits/income-based/other-state-tax-credit.md
