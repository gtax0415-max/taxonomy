# Flat Tax Rate (Georgia)
## Description
Georgia taxes individual income at a single flat rate applied to Georgia taxable income (Line 15c), regardless of filing status or income level. The result is entered on Form 500, Line 16, rounded to the nearest dollar.
## Rate by year
- 2026: 4.99% (HB 463, effective January 1, 2026)
- 2025: 5.19%
- 2024: 5.39% (first flat-tax year under HB 1437; began at 5.49%)
## Future trajectory
Under HB 463, from 2027 the rate steps down 0.125 percentage points per year toward a 3.99% floor, subject to the state meeting revenue and reserve triggers (reductions can be delayed if triggers are not met).
## No brackets, no local income tax
- Georgia replaced its former six-bracket graduated system (top rate 5.75%) with the flat rate in 2024
- No Georgia city or county imposes a local income tax
## Computation
Line 16 = Line 15c × the applicable flat rate, rounded to the nearest dollar.
## Booklet-version caveat
The 2025 IT-511 booklet prints "Multiply Line 15c by 5.19%" on the Line 16 instruction. That is the 2025 figure; a 2026 return uses 4.99%. Confirm the rate for the exact filing year.
## Required Information
- Georgia taxable income (Line 15c)
- The applicable tax-year flat rate
## Questions
- What tax year are you filing?
- What is your Georgia taxable income on Line 15c?
## Common Errors
- Using 5.19% (2025) on a 2026 return instead of 4.99%
- Expecting graduated brackets — Georgia is flat
- Adding a local/city income tax — none exists in Georgia
## Prompt
- What is Georgia's flat tax rate for 2026?
- How much Georgia tax on $50,000 of taxable income?
- Does Georgia have tax brackets?
