# Contributions and Check-offs (Checkoff Georgia)
## Description
Georgia lets a filer donate to designated state funds directly on the Form 500 return by reducing their refund or adding to their payment ("Checkoff Georgia"). These are voluntary CONTRIBUTIONS, not credits or deductions — they reduce the refund dollar-for-dollar and support specific state programs.
## How it works
- On the return's donations section, enter an amount for one or more listed funds
- The total is subtracted from your refund (or added to the amount you owe)
- Contributions are voluntary and irrevocable once the return is processed
- A contribution may be deductible as a charitable gift on a future federal return, but is not a Georgia credit
## The funds (single file)
See items/checkoff-georgia-funds.md for the full list and each fund's purpose:
- Georgia Wildlife Conservation Fund
- Georgia Fund for Children and Elderly
- Georgia Cancer Research Fund
- Georgia Land Conservation Program
- Georgia National Guard Foundation
- Dog and Cat Sterilization Fund
- Saving the Cure Fund (stem cell)
- Realizing Educational Achievement Can Happen (REACH) Program
- Public Safety Memorial Grant Program
- Disabled Veterans' Scholarship Fund
