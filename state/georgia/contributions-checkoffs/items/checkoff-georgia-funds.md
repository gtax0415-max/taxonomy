# Checkoff Georgia Funds (List)
## Description
The designated voluntary contribution funds a filer can donate to on Form 500. Each donation reduces the refund by the amount given. Fund availability and details can change year to year; confirm against the current Form 500.
## The funds
- Georgia Wildlife Conservation Fund — protects and preserves Georgia's nongame wildlife (Dept. of Natural Resources)
- Georgia Fund for Children and Elderly — programs for preschool children with special needs, plus home-delivered meals and transportation for elders (DHS Division of Aging Services; DPH)
- Georgia Cancer Research Fund — supports research on all types of cancer, often as seed money for pilot projects (Georgia Center for Oncology Research and Education)
- Georgia Land Conservation Program — funds land conservation to protect natural resources (Georgia Environmental Finance Authority)
- Georgia National Guard Foundation — emergency relief assistance to soldiers and airmen of the Georgia National Guard
- Dog and Cat Sterilization Fund — spay/neuter program (Dept. of Agriculture)
- Saving the Cure Fund — encourages donation, collection, and storage of stem cells and principled stem cell research
- Realizing Educational Achievement Can Happen (REACH) Program — helps low-income students graduate high school and access college (Georgia Student Finance Authority)
- Public Safety Memorial Grant Program — educational aid to children of public-safety employees disabled or killed in the line of duty (Georgia Student Finance Authority)
- Disabled Veterans' Scholarship Fund — scholarships to disabled veterans (Technical College System of Georgia Foundation)
## Required Information
- The fund(s) you wish to support
- The dollar amount for each
## Questions
- Which fund(s) do you want to contribute to?
- How much do you want to donate from your refund?
## Common Errors
- Confusing a voluntary check-off contribution with a donation tax CREDIT
- Expecting the contribution to reduce tax (it reduces the refund instead)
- Assuming a specific fund is still listed without checking the current Form 500
## Prompt
- What causes can I donate to on my Georgia return?
- I want to give to the Georgia wildlife fund from my refund.
- Is the cancer research check-off still on the Georgia form?
