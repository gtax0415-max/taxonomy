# Additions to Income (Georgia — Schedule 1, Lines 1–6)
## Description
Additions are items that federal adjusted gross income excludes or reduces but Georgia taxes, so they must be ADDED back on Schedule 1. Enter them on Schedule 1, Lines 1–6; the total feeds the net adjustment on Line 14 → Form 500, Line 9. A taxpayer with multiple additions enters the total on Line 5 and attaches a worksheet listing each one.
