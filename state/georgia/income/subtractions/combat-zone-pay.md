# Combat Zone Pay Exclusion (Subtraction)
## Description
Combat zone pay is excluded from Georgia income. To the extent any combat zone pay was included in federal AGI, it may be subtracted. See IT-511 page 14 for the detailed combat zone rules, including filing extensions for service members.
## What is subtracted
- Combat Zone Pay included in federal AGI (see IT-511 page 14)
## Eligibility / applicability
- You are a service member who received combat zone pay that reached federal AGI
## Related filing relief
Service members serving in a combat zone or outside the continental U.S. may have extended time to file — members may file within six months after returning to the continental U.S. with no penalties or interest accruing during that period (see page 14).
## Required Information
- Military orders/pay records identifying combat zone service
- The amount of combat zone pay in federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you receive combat zone pay this year?
- Was any of it included in your federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting combat pay that was already excluded federally (nothing left to subtract)
## Prompt
- I served in a combat zone this year.
- Does Georgia tax combat pay?
