# Path2College 529 Contributions (Subtraction)
## Description
Contributions to the Path2College 529 Plan are deductible as a Georgia subtraction, within a per-beneficiary cap. This is one of the few Georgia-specific savings incentives available to ordinary filers.
## Amount
- Deductible portion of contributions to the Path2College 529 Plan
- Limited to the amount contributed, but cannot exceed $4,000 per beneficiary
- On a married-filing-joint return, cannot exceed $8,000 per beneficiary
## Eligibility / applicability
- You contributed to a Path2College 529 account during the tax year
## Not deductible: ABLE contributions
No subtraction is allowed for contributions to the Georgia ABLE Program or any other state's ABLE program — this is a separate program from Path2College and is specifically non-deductible.
## Required Information
- Contribution statements showing amounts per beneficiary
- List of beneficiaries
- Filing status (for the $4,000 vs $8,000 limit)
## Questions
- Did you contribute to a Path2College 529 plan this year?
- How much did you contribute per beneficiary?
- Are you filing jointly?
## Common Errors
- Exceeding the $4,000 / $8,000 per-beneficiary cap
- Deducting ABLE contributions, which do not qualify
- Deducting more than actually contributed
## Prompt
- I put money in a Georgia 529 plan.
- Does Georgia give a deduction for college savings?
- I contribute to Path2College for my kids.
