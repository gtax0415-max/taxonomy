# Cash Tips Exclusion (Georgia — 2026, NEW)
## Description
NEW for tax year 2026 under HB 463. Georgia allows a temporary exclusion of a portion of qualified cash tips from Georgia taxable income. As with overtime, Georgia decoupled from the federal OBBBA "no tax on tips" provision and enacted its own smaller, capped exclusion.
## Amount
- Up to $1,750 of qualified cash tips may be excluded
- Temporary: available for tax years 2026, 2027, and 2028 only (automatic repeal thereafter)
- Excludable only to the extent included in federal AGI
## Statutory basis and status
- HB 463 (2026), effective for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2026
- Georgia does NOT follow the federal IRC Section 224 no-tax-on-tips deduction; this Georgia exclusion is a separate, reduced amount
- Employer reporting required; DOR rules and the 2026 IT-511 booklet will specify the Schedule 1 line and documentation
## Eligibility / applicability
- You received qualified cash tips included in federal AGI, in tax year 2026, 2027, or 2028
## Required Information
- Employer-reported qualified cash tips amount
- Tip records
- The amount included in federal AGI
## Questions
- Did you earn cash tips in 2026?
- Did your employer report your qualified tips?
- Were the tips included in your federal AGI?
## Common Errors
- Applying the larger federal OBBBA tips figure instead of Georgia's $1,750 cap
- Claiming it for a year outside the 2026–2028 window
- Excluding tips that were never in federal AGI
## Prompt
- Does Georgia tax my tips in 2026?
- Georgia has a new tip exclusion — how much?
- Is Georgia following the federal no-tax-on-tips rule?
