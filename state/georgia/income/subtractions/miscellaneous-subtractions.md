# Miscellaneous Statutory Subtractions (Georgia)
## Description
A group of smaller, narrowly-targeted Schedule 1 subtractions that each apply to specific taxpayers. Collected here so nothing in the statute is lost; a filer typically uses at most one or two.
## Items in this group
- Dependent's unearned income included in the parents' federal AGI (kiddie-tax income reported on the parent return)
- Salaries and wages reduced from federal taxable income because of the Federal Jobs Tax Credit (restores wages Georgia does not require reduced)
- 1981–1986 depreciation difference — SUBTRACTION side (where Georgia depreciation exceeds federal for 1981–1986 assets)
- IRA, Keogh, and SEP withdrawals where tax was already paid to Georgia due to the 1981–1986 Georgia/federal difference (avoids double taxation on the recovered basis)
- Teacher Retirement System of Georgia adjustment for contributions paid between July 1, 1987 and December 31, 1989 that were reported to and taxed by Georgia
- FICA tip credit wages — the amount employers in food and beverage establishments claimed as a federal credit (instead of a deduction) for FICA tax paid on employee cash tips
- 10% of qualified payments to classified subcontractors, or $100,000, whichever is less, per taxable year, by individuals/corporations/partnerships that are party to state contracts (Dept. of Administrative Services; doas.ga.gov)
- Federal tax change adjustments — SUBTRACTION side (where a non-adopted federal change increased federal AGI; see the DOR federal-tax-changes guidance)
## Eligibility / applicability
- You have one of the specific items above included in (or affecting) your federal AGI
## Required Information
- Records supporting the particular item claimed
- The amount included in federal AGI or the Georgia difference
## Questions
- Do you report a child's unearned income on your own return?
- Did the Federal Jobs Tax Credit reduce your deductible wages?
- Are you a retired Georgia teacher with 1987–1989 contributions taxed by Georgia?
- Do you have a state contract with qualified subcontractor payments?
## Common Errors
- Missing the wage restoration when a federal credit reduced deductible wages
- Overlooking the small but real state-contract subcontractor subtraction
- Applying the 1981–1986 items to modern assets
## Prompt
- I have a niche Georgia subtraction that doesn't fit the big categories.
- The Federal Jobs Tax Credit reduced my wage deduction.
- I'm a retired Georgia teacher with old taxed contributions.
