# Federally Exempt Income (Subtraction)
## Description
Income from any fund, program, or system that is specifically exempted by FEDERAL law or treaty is subtracted to the extent it reached federal AGI. This is a catch-all for items Congress or a treaty has designated as exempt from state taxation.
## What is subtracted
- Income from any fund, program, or system specifically exempted from taxation by federal law or treaty
## Eligibility / applicability
- You have income that a federal statute or treaty specifically exempts from state income tax
## Required Information
- The federal law or treaty provision granting the exemption
- Identification of the specific fund/program/system and the amount
## Questions
- Do you have income a federal law or treaty specifically exempts from state tax?
## Common Errors
- Claiming general federal deductions here rather than genuinely exempt income
- Subtracting income exempt by state, not federal, law (belongs to a different provision)
## Prompt
- I have income that's exempt under a federal treaty.
- Federal law says this program's income isn't taxable.
