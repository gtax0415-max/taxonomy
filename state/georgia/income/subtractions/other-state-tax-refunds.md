# Other-State Income Tax Refunds (Subtraction)
## Description
Income tax refunds from states OTHER than Georgia that were included in federal adjusted gross income are subtracted. Georgia income tax refunds are NOT subtracted here.
## What is subtracted
- Income tax refunds from states other than Georgia included in federal AGI
## Important limit
Do NOT subtract Georgia income tax refunds. Only non-Georgia state refunds qualify.
## Eligibility / applicability
- Your federal AGI includes a taxable refund from another state
## Required Information
- 1099-G or state records for the non-Georgia refund
## Questions
- Did you receive a taxable state income tax refund from a state other than Georgia?
## Common Errors
- Subtracting a Georgia refund, which is not allowed here
- Subtracting a non-taxable refund that was never in federal AGI
## Prompt
- I got a state tax refund from another state.
- I moved from another state and got a refund from them.
