# Georgia Build America / Special Municipal Bond Interest (Subtraction)
## Description
Some Georgia municipal bond interest is TAXABLE federally but exempt from Georgia tax, so it is subtracted. This covers Build America Bonds (and equivalents) and Georgia bonds with a special statutory Georgia exemption.
## What is subtracted
1. Federally taxable interest received on Georgia municipal bonds designated as "Build America Bonds" under IRC Section 54AA. Recovery Zone Economic Development Bonds under Section 1400U-2, and any other bond treated as a "Qualified Bond" under Section 6431(f), are considered Build America Bonds for this purpose.
2. Federally taxable interest received on Georgia municipal bonds issued by the State of Georgia and certain Georgia authorities/agencies for which there is a special Georgia-law exemption from Georgia tax on such interest.
## Eligibility / applicability
- You received federally taxable interest on qualifying Georgia bonds
## Required Information
- Bond statements identifying the qualifying Georgia bonds
- The federally taxable interest amount in federal AGI
## Questions
- Do you hold Georgia Build America Bonds or Recovery Zone Economic Development Bonds?
- Do you hold Georgia state/authority bonds with a special Georgia tax exemption?
## Common Errors
- Confusing these (Georgia bonds, taxable federally) with ordinary tax-exempt municipals
- Subtracting non-Georgia Build America Bond interest
## Prompt
- I have Georgia Build America Bond interest.
- My Georgia bond interest was taxable on my federal return.
