# Organ Donation Expense Deduction (Subtraction)
## Description
A living organ or bone-marrow donor may subtract up to $25,000 of unreimbursed costs incurred as a direct result of the donation during the year. Note this is the income-side DEDUCTION; a separate organ donation CREDIT also exists in Georgia's credit system.
## Amount
- Up to $25,000 of unreimbursed travel expenses, lodging expenses, and lost wages
- Incurred as a direct result of the taxpayer's donation of all or part of a kidney, liver, pancreas, intestine, lung, or bone marrow during the taxable year
## Eligibility / applicability
- You were a living donor of a qualifying organ or bone marrow during the tax year
- The expenses were unreimbursed and directly resulted from the donation
## Required Information
- Records of unreimbursed travel, lodging, and lost wages
- Confirmation of the qualifying donation during the year
## Questions
- Did you donate an organ or bone marrow this year?
- Did you have unreimbursed travel, lodging, or lost wages from the donation?
## Common Errors
- Including reimbursed expenses
- Exceeding the $25,000 cap
- Confusing this income deduction with the separate organ donation credit
## Prompt
- I donated a kidney this year.
- Does Georgia help with organ donation expenses?
- I was a living donor and had travel costs.
