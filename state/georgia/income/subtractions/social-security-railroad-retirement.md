# Social Security and Railroad Retirement (Subtraction)
## Description
Georgia does not tax Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits. To the extent they were included in federal AGI, they are subtracted. This is separate from — and must not be folded into — the Retirement Income Exclusion.
## What is subtracted
- Social Security, or Railroad Retirement (Tier 1 and Tier 2) paid by the Railroad Retirement Board, that was included in federal adjusted gross income
## Interaction with the retirement exclusion
Social Security and RRB benefits are excluded here and must NOT be included in the Retirement Income Exclusion calculation base. Keeping them out of the exclusion base preserves more of the exclusion for taxable pension, interest, and dividend income.
## Eligibility / applicability
- Your federal AGI includes taxable Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits
## Required Information
- SSA-1099 / RRB-1099 / RRB-1099-R
- The federally taxable amount included in federal AGI
## Questions
- Do you receive Social Security or Railroad Retirement benefits?
- How much of it was taxable on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Also counting these benefits inside the Retirement Income Exclusion (double benefit, not allowed)
- Subtracting the gross benefit rather than the federally taxable portion
## Prompt
- Does Georgia tax Social Security?
- I get Railroad Retirement — is it taxed in Georgia?
