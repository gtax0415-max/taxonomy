# 1981–1986 Depreciation Difference (Addition)
## Description
For a narrow historical window, Georgia and federal depreciation law diverged. Where federal depreciation on 1981–1986 assets exceeds what Georgia allowed, the difference is added back. This is a legacy item affecting only very old assets still being depreciated or disposed of.
## What is added
- Depreciation differences arising because Georgia and federal law differed during tax years 1981 through 1986
## Eligibility / applicability
- You still hold or recently disposed of assets placed in service 1981–1986 with a remaining Georgia/federal basis difference
## Required Information
- Depreciation schedules showing the 1981–1986 basis difference
## Questions
- Do you have assets from the mid-1980s with a Georgia depreciation difference?
## Common Errors
- Applying this to modern assets — it is limited to the 1981–1986 window
## Prompt
- I have very old depreciable assets with a Georgia adjustment.
## Notes
- The mirror subtraction exists when Georgia depreciation exceeds federal — see subtractions/miscellaneous-subtractions.md
