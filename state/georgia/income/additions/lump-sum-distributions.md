# Lump-Sum Distributions (Addition)
## Description
Lump-sum distributions from employee benefit plans that are reported on IRS Form 4972 receive special averaging treatment federally and are therefore not fully in federal AGI. Georgia taxes them as ordinary income, so the amount is added back.
## What is added
- Lump-sum distributions from employee benefit plans reported on IRS Form 4972
## Eligibility / applicability
- You elected special lump-sum (Form 4972) treatment on a distribution from an employee benefit plan
## Required Information
- IRS Form 4972
- The 1099-R for the distribution
## Questions
- Did you take a lump-sum distribution from a retirement or employee benefit plan?
- Did you file Form 4972 to use special averaging?
## Common Errors
- Overlooking the add-back because the distribution was handled on Form 4972, not the main 1040 income lines
## Prompt
- I took a lump-sum payout from my pension plan.
- I used Form 4972 for a retirement distribution.
