# Credit-Related Deduction Add-Backs (Addition)
## Description
When a taxpayer claims certain Georgia credits for a contribution, the FEDERAL deduction relating to that same contribution must be added back on the Georgia return — you cannot take both a Georgia credit and a Georgia tax benefit for the deduction of the same dollars. This add-back covers the QEE, Land Conservation, Rural Hospital, Qualified Education Donation, Qualified Foster Child Donation, and Qualified Law Enforcement Donation credits.
## What is added (deduction relating to the credit)
- Qualified Education Expense credit (125) — Regulation 560-7-8-.47
- Land Conservation credit (124) — Regulation 560-7-8-.50
- Qualified Rural Hospital Organization Expense credit (136) — Regulation 560-7-8-.57
- Qualified Education Donation credit (140) — Regulation 560-7-8-.60
- Qualified Foster Child Donation credit (151) — Regulation 560-7-8-.68
- Qualified Law Enforcement Donation credit (150) — Regulation 560-7-8-.69
## The state-tax disallowance formula
If the contribution was treated as a deduction of STATE TAXES on federal Schedule A, the disallowed portion is: contribution treated as state income taxes ÷ total taxes on Schedule A Line 5d × the lesser of (Schedule A Line 5d amount or $10,000 / $5,000 MFS). A later federally taxable state refund may then be reduced proportionally.
## Eligibility / applicability
- You claimed one of the listed Georgia credits AND deducted the underlying contribution federally
## Required Information
- Federal Schedule A and the credit preapproval/confirmation
- Schedule A Line 5d state-tax figures (if the contribution was deducted as state taxes)
## Questions
- Did you claim a Georgia donation or education credit?
- Did you also deduct that contribution on your federal Schedule A?
- Was the contribution deducted as a charitable gift or as state taxes?
## Common Errors
- Taking the Georgia credit while leaving the related federal deduction untouched on the Georgia return
- Misapplying the state-tax disallowance formula and its $10,000/$5,000 SALT interaction
## Prompt
- I claimed the QEE credit and deducted the donation federally.
- Do I add back the deduction for my Georgia rural hospital credit?
