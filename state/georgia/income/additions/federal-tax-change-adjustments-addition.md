# Federal Tax Change Adjustments (Addition)
## Description
Georgia adopts the Internal Revenue Code as of a fixed conformity date and does not automatically follow later federal changes. Where a federal change is NOT adopted by Georgia and it reduced federal AGI, an addition restores the Georgia amount. The specific items change year to year and are published by DOR.
## What is added
- Adjustments due to federal tax changes that Georgia has not adopted, as listed in the DOR federal-tax-changes guidance
## Eligibility / applicability
- You benefited federally from a provision Georgia has decoupled from for the year
## Required Information
- The DOR federal-tax-changes guidance for the year
- The specific federal provisions you claimed that Georgia does not follow
## Questions
- Did you claim a recent federal deduction or exclusion that Georgia may not conform to?
## Common Errors
- Assuming full federal conformity — Georgia decouples from selected provisions each year
- Using a prior year's decoupling list
## Prompt
- Does Georgia follow the latest federal tax law changes?
- I claimed a new federal deduction — does Georgia allow it?
## Notes
- There is a mirror SUBTRACTION when a non-adopted federal change increased federal AGI — see subtractions/miscellaneous-subtractions.md
