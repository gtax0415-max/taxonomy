# Unauthorized Employee Payments (Addition)
## Description
Payments of more than $600 in a taxable year to workers who are not authorized employees (and are not excepted by O.C.G.A. Section 48-7-21.1) are added back — Georgia disallows the deduction for such payments. An authorized employee is someone legally allowed to work in the United States.
## What is added
- Payments exceeding $600 in a taxable year made to employees who are not authorized employees and are not excepted by Code Section 48-7-21.1
## Eligibility / applicability
- Your business deducted over-$600 payments to unauthorized workers federally
## Required Information
- Payroll/1099 records of the payments
- Work-authorization documentation
## Questions
- Did your business pay more than $600 to any worker not authorized to work in the U.S.?
## Common Errors
- Overlooking the add-back for contractor payments
- Misapplying the Code Section 48-7-21.1 exception
## Prompt
- My business paid workers who aren't work-authorized.
- Does Georgia disallow wages to unauthorized employees?
