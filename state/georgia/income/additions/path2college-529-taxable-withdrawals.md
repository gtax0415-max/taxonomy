# Path2College 529 Taxable Withdrawals (Addition)
## Description
The taxable portion of withdrawals from the Path2College 529 Plan is added back per Regulation 560-7-4-.04 — for example, non-qualified withdrawals or amounts that recapture a previously deducted Georgia contribution.
## What is added
- Taxable portion of withdrawals on the Path2College 529 Plan (Regulation 560-7-4-.04)
## Eligibility / applicability
- You took a non-qualified or otherwise taxable withdrawal from a Path2College 529 account
## Required Information
- 1099-Q and account statements showing the taxable portion
- Records of prior Georgia contribution deductions
## Questions
- Did you withdraw from a Path2College 529 for a non-qualified purpose?
- Did you previously deduct contributions to that account on your Georgia return?
## Common Errors
- Treating a taxable 529 withdrawal as fully tax-free at the state level
## Prompt
- I took money out of my Georgia 529 plan.
- I made a non-qualified 529 withdrawal.
## Notes
- The mirror subtraction is the 529 CONTRIBUTION deduction — see subtractions/path2college-529-contributions.md
