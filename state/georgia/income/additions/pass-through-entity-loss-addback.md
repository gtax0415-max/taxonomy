# Pass-Through Entity Loss Add-Back (Addition)
## Description
For owners of a pass-through entity that elected to pay Georgia tax at the ENTITY level (the HB 149 election), the owner's allocable share of the loss that was already apportioned and allocated at the entity level is added back on the individual return — because that loss was accounted for at the entity, not the owner, level. Use description PTEADD.
## What is added
- Your allocable share of loss that was apportioned and allocated at the entity level under Regulation 560-7-3-.03(9)(a)
## Mechanics
- Enter on Schedule 1, Line 5 with description PTEADD
- The mirror income item (allocable share of income taxed at the entity level) is a SUBTRACTION with description PTEDED
## Eligibility / applicability
- You own a pass-through entity that made the Georgia entity-level tax election and passed through a loss
## Required Information
- The entity K-1 and entity-level election documentation
- Your allocable share of the entity-level apportioned/allocated loss
## Questions
- Does your partnership or S corporation pay Georgia tax at the entity level?
- Did it pass through a loss to you this year?
## Common Errors
- Using the wrong description (PTEADD for loss add-back vs PTEDED for the income subtraction)
- Failing to add back an entity-level loss on the owner return
## Prompt
- My partnership elected the Georgia entity-level tax.
