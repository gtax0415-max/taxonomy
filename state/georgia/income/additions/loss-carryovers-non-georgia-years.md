# Loss Carryovers From Non-Georgia Years (Addition)
## Description
Loss carryovers that originated in years when you were NOT subject to Georgia income tax must be added back, because Georgia did not participate in the loss when it arose and will not recognize its carryover benefit now.
## What is added
- Loss carryovers deducted federally that arose in tax years when you were not subject to Georgia income tax
## Eligibility / applicability
- You are deducting a federal loss carryover that originated in a year you had no Georgia filing obligation (for example, before moving to Georgia)
## Required Information
- The year the loss originated
- Confirmation you were not subject to Georgia income tax in that year
- The amount of carryover deducted federally this year
## Questions
- Do you have a loss carryover from before you were a Georgia taxpayer?
- In what year did the loss originate?
## Common Errors
- Adding back a carryover that actually arose in a Georgia year
- Forgetting to add back a pre-residency loss carryover
## Prompt
- I have a capital loss carryover from before I moved to Georgia.
- I had losses in a year I didn't file in Georgia.
