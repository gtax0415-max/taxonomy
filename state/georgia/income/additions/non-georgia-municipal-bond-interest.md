# Non-Georgia Municipal Bond Interest (Addition)
## Description
Interest on municipal bonds issued by states other than Georgia is exempt from federal tax but IS taxable to Georgia, so it must be added back. The same applies to mutual fund dividends derived from non-Georgia municipal bonds.
## What is added
- Interest received from non-Georgia municipal bonds
- Dividends received from mutual funds that derived income from non-Georgia municipal bonds
## Allowed reduction
The addition may be reduced ONLY by direct and indirect interest expenses attributable to that income which have not already been deducted in arriving at federal AGI or federal itemized deductions.
## Eligibility / applicability
- You hold municipal bonds or bond funds issued outside Georgia whose interest was excluded from federal AGI
## Required Information
- Issuing state of each municipal bond
- Non-Georgia portion of any mutual fund's tax-exempt interest
- Attributable interest expenses not already deducted federally
## Questions
- Do you own municipal bonds issued by a state other than Georgia?
- Do you own bond mutual funds with out-of-state municipal holdings?
- Did you incur interest expense to carry that investment?
## Common Errors
- Adding back Georgia municipal bond interest (Georgia's own bonds are not added)
- Failing to reduce by attributable interest expense
- Missing the non-Georgia portion buried inside a mutual fund's tax-exempt dividend
## Prompt
- I have tax-exempt interest from out-of-state municipal bonds.
- My bond fund holds municipal bonds from many states.
- Does Georgia tax my muni bond interest?
