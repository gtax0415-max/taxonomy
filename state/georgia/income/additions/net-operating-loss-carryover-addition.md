# Net Operating Loss Carryover (Addition)
## Description
A net operating loss carryover deducted on the FEDERAL return is added back on the Georgia return, because Georgia computes its own NOL separately rather than accepting the federal carryover figure.
## What is added
- Net operating loss carryover deducted on the federal return
## Eligibility / applicability
- Your federal AGI reflects an NOL carryover deduction
## Required Information
- The federal NOL carryover amount deducted this year
- Georgia's separately computed NOL schedules
## Questions
- Did you deduct a net operating loss carryover on your federal return?
## Common Errors
- Failing to add back the federal NOL, then also claiming a Georgia NOL — double counting
- Confusing the federal carryover with the Georgia-computed amount
## Prompt
- I deducted a net operating loss on my federal return.
- I have an NOL carryover — how does Georgia handle it?
