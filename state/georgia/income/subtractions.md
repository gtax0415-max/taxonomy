# Subtractions From Income (Georgia — Schedule 1, Lines 7–13)
## Description
Subtractions are items included in federal adjusted gross income that Georgia does NOT tax, so they are SUBTRACTED on Schedule 1. Enter them on Schedule 1, Lines 7–13; the total feeds the net adjustment on Line 14 → Form 500, Line 9. A taxpayer with multiple subtractions enters the total on Line 12 and attaches a worksheet listing each one. This is where most Georgia tax savings on the income side happen — above all the Retirement Income Exclusion.

## The retirement exclusion dominates
Two files carry most of the value. The general Retirement Income Exclusion (Line 7) and the Military Retirement Income Exclusion (Page 3) each require date of birth (and date of disability if claimed on that basis), each is claimed per spouse on a separate-qualification basis, and each must be prorated by part-year and nonresident filers.
## Related

- ABLE contributions are NOT subtractable (Georgia or any other state's program)
