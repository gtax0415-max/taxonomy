# New York Dependent Exemption
## Description
$1,000 of New York taxable income removed for each dependent. Modest in isolation, but it applies on top of either the standard or the itemized deduction and requires no separate form.
## Amount
- $1,000 PER DEPENDENT for 2026, confirmed from Form IT-2105-I (2026)
- Multiply the number of dependents listed at Item H by 1,000 and enter the result on line 36
- NO exemption for yourself, and none for your spouse
A family with three dependents removes $3,000. At the 2026 top-of-the-middle rate of 5.40%, that is roughly $162 of tax, plus New York City tax if applicable.
## Who counts as a dependent
Anyone you claimed as a dependent on federal Form 1040, PLUS anyone you were ENTITLED to claim but chose not to.
That second group is the one people miss. Form IT-201 instructions give the standard example: you were entitled to claim your college-age child but declined so that the child could claim a federal education credit on their own return. You may still claim them as a dependent for New York and take the $1,000 exemption.
If you did not have to file a federal return, list the dependents you would have been entitled to claim.
## No age cliff
Unlike the Empire State child credit, which stops at age 17, the dependent exemption follows the FEDERAL DEPENDENT DEFINITION. A qualifying relative of any age, a college student under 24, or a dependent parent all count. A family that ages out of the child credit does not age out of this.
## More than seven dependents
Form IT-201 Item H has room for seven. Beyond that, and if filing on paper, attach a separate sheet headed "Form IT-201: Item H continued" with your name and Social Security number at the top and the required information for each additional dependent.
## Married filing separately
If you filed a joint FEDERAL return but must file SEPARATE New York returns, complete Item H as if you had filed separate federal returns. Dependents cannot be claimed twice across the two New York returns.
## Notes
- Line 36 is subtracted from line 35 to produce taxable income at lines 37 and 38
- If line 36 exceeds line 35, leave lines 37 and 38 blank. The excess is not refundable and does not carry forward
- Item H is also the input for the Empire State child credit, the household credit, and the New York City income tax elimination credit. An incomplete Item H damages several claims at once
## Required Information
- Each dependent's first and last name, relationship to you, Social Security number, and date of birth
- Whether anyone else claimed the same dependent
- For dependents you did not claim federally, why you were entitled to
## Questions
- How many dependents did you claim, or could you have claimed, on your federal return?
- Did you decline to claim a child so they could take an education credit?
- Do you support a parent or other relative who qualifies as a dependent?
- Do you have more than seven dependents?
## Common Errors
- Claiming an exemption for yourself or your spouse
- Omitting a dependent you were entitled to claim but did not claim federally
- Assuming the exemption stops when a child turns 17, confusing it with the child credit
- Leaving Item H incomplete, which also damages the child credit and household credit
- Both spouses claiming the same dependent on separate New York returns
## Prompt
- How much is the New York dependent exemption?
- My son is 19 and in college, can I claim him for New York?
- I did not claim my daughter federally so she could get the education credit.
