# New York Exemptions
## Description
New York retained a dependent exemption that the federal system suspended after 2017. It is small but it applies REGARDLESS of whether you itemize, and it comes after the deduction in the computation order.
## Files in this folder
- dependent-exemption.md — $1,000 per dependent
## What New York does not have
There is NO personal exemption for yourself or your spouse. New York eliminated those. Only DEPENDENTS generate an exemption. Form IT-2105-I states it directly: exemptions are not allowed for yourself or, if married, your spouse.
