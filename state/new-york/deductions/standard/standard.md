# New York Standard Deduction
## Description
A flat deduction from New York adjusted gross income, taken instead of itemizing. New York's amounts are set independently of the federal ones and are MUCH SMALLER, which is what makes New York itemizing worthwhile for many people who take the federal standard deduction.

## What follows from the gap
The New York threshold is roughly HALF the federal one, and New York allows deductions the federal system no longer does. Anyone with meaningful property tax, mortgage interest, or unreimbursed work expenses should run Form IT-196 even after taking the federal standard deduction.
