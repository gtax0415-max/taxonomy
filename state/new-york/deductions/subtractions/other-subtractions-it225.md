# Other Subtractions (Form IT-225)
## Description
The catch-all for New York subtractions not given their own line on Form IT-201. Claimed by entering a MODIFICATION CODE and an amount on Form IT-225, which must be submitted with the return. The total from Form IT-225 line 18 goes to Form IT-201 line 31.
## How it works
Each subtraction has a code in the form S-nnn. You enter the code and the amount; there is no free-text option. If no code fits, the subtraction is not available.
Form IT-225 carries ADDITIONS as well, coded A-nnn, which flow the other way to Form IT-201 line 23. Same form, opposite effect — check which side of the form an entry belongs on.
## Subtractions worth checking for
| Code | Subtraction |
|---|---|
| S-118 | Certain combat pay |
| S-121, S-123 | U.S. government obligation income arriving through funds or entities |
| S-124 | Disability income exclusion |
| S-125 | Certain investment income exempted by other New York laws |
| S-129 | Special accruals |
| S-901 | Beneficiary's share of a net fiduciary adjustment |
The full list is in the Form IT-225 instructions and runs to dozens of codes. Read it rather than relying on memory; codes are added and retired.
## Disability income exclusion (S-124)
Up to $100 per week, $5,200 per year, for a taxpayer under 65 who is permanently and totally disabled and retired on disability.
It shares a ceiling with the pension and annuity income exclusion: the two TOGETHER cannot exceed $20,000. See pension-annuity-exclusion.md.
## Partners and S corporation shareholders
The entity reports your modifications to you. Two of them have their OWN LINE on Form IT-201 and must NOT go through Form IT-225:
| Code | Description | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| EA-113 | Interest on state and local bonds and obligations | Line 20 (addition) |
| ES-125 | Interest on U.S. government bonds | Line 28 (subtraction) |
Everything else from the entity goes on Form IT-225. Routing an EA or ES code through Form IT-225 double-counts it.
## Beneficiaries of estates and trusts
Your share of the fiduciary adjustment arrives as a single net figure.
- Net SUBTRACTION: code ES-901 on Form IT-225, line 14
- Net ADDITION: code EA-901 on Form IT-225, line 5
## Special accruals (S-129)
Applies if you were a nonresident on December 31 of the prior year, or will be a nonresident on January 1 of the following year, and have income fixed and determinable in one year but reported federally in another. Anyone moving into or out of New York should check whether accrual applies. The paired addition is A-115.
## Form 4970 interaction
If you filed federal Form 4970 for a trust accumulation distribution, do NOT include the Form 4970 line 1 income on Form IT-201 line 11. Instead include it on Form IT-225 line 5 under addition code A-114, after subtracting New York state and local bond interest included on Form 4970 line 5.
## Required Information
- Schedule K-1 and any entity statement of New York modifications
- The modification code and amount for each item
- For disability income, proof of permanent and total disability and the weekly benefit amount
- Residency dates, if special accruals may apply
## Questions
- Did you receive a K-1 with New York modification codes?
- Are you retired on permanent and total disability and under 65?
- Did you move into or out of New York during this year or the adjacent ones?
- Are you a beneficiary of an estate or trust?
- Did you receive combat pay?
## Common Errors
- Routing EA-113 or ES-125 through Form IT-225 instead of Form IT-201 lines 20 and 28
- Failing to submit Form IT-225 with the return, which invalidates the subtraction
- Entering a subtraction on the addition side of the form
- Claiming the disability exclusion and the pension exclusion to a combined total over $20,000
- Missing special accruals in a move year
## Prompt
- My K-1 has New York modification codes on it.
- I am on disability and under 65.
- I moved out of New York in December.
