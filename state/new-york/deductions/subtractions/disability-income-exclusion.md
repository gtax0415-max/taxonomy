# Disability Income Exclusion
## Description
Excludes disability pay received under an employer plan by someone who RETIRED ON DISABILITY and was PERMANENTLY AND TOTALLY DISABLED. Computed on FORM IT-221 and carried through Form IT-225 code S-124 to line 31.
## Amount
- Up to $100 PER WEEK
- Maximum $5,200 for a full year
- Reduced for weeks in which no disability payment was received
## The shared $20,000 ceiling
The disability income exclusion and the PENSION AND ANNUITY INCOME EXCLUSION share ONE $20,000 ceiling. Their combined total may not exceed $20,000.
For most claimants this is not binding, since $5,200 plus a pension exclusion rarely reaches $20,000. It bites for someone with both a substantial private pension and a full year of disability payments. Compute both before entering either.
## Eligibility
- You were UNDER AGE 65 at the end of the tax year
- You RETIRED ON DISABILITY and were PERMANENTLY AND TOTALLY DISABLED when you retired
- The payments are made under an EMPLOYER'S PLAN as wage continuation
- The payments are included in your federal AGI
The age test is the one that ends the exclusion. At 65 it stops, though the pension and annuity exclusion generally takes over by then.
## Permanently and totally disabled
You cannot engage in any substantial gainful activity because of a physical or mental condition, and a physician has determined the condition has lasted or can be expected to last continuously for at least 12 months, or can be expected to lead to death. Form IT-221 requires the physician's statement.
## What does NOT qualify
- Payments received AFTER you reach the plan's minimum retirement age. At that point the payments become a pension, not disability income
- Social Security disability, which is already fully exempt at line 27 and needs no exclusion here
- Workers' compensation, which is not included in federal AGI in the first place
- Payments from a policy you bought with your OWN after-tax funds, which are not taxable federally either
## Married couples
If both spouses qualify, each may compute the exclusion on their own income. Neither may use the other's unused portion.
## Required Information
- Form IT-221 with the physician's statement
- Weekly disability benefit amount and the number of weeks received
- Date you retired on disability and your age at year end
- Amounts of pension and annuity exclusion claimed, for the shared ceiling
## Questions
- Were you under 65 at the end of the year?
- Did you retire because of a disability, and were you permanently and totally disabled at that time?
- Are the payments made under an employer plan?
- Have you reached the plan's minimum retirement age?
- Are you also claiming the pension and annuity income exclusion?
## Common Errors
- Claiming it at age 65 or older
- Claiming it after reaching the plan's minimum retirement age, when payments are no longer disability income
- Claiming it for Social Security disability, already exempt at line 27
- Exceeding $20,000 combined with the pension and annuity exclusion
- Omitting the required physician's statement on Form IT-221

- I retired on disability and I am 58.
- I get disability payments from my employer's plan.
