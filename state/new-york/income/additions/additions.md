# New York Additions
## Description
Income that is NOT taxable federally but IS taxable by New York. Added to federal AGI at Form IT-201 lines 20 through 23. The mirror image of the subtraction block, and the half people forget, because nobody volunteers additional income.

|---|---|

| 24 | Add lines 19 through 23 |
## Two additions catch most people
LINE 20 catches investors who bought a national municipal bond fund for its federal exemption. New York exempts only ITS OWN bonds. A California or national muni fund produces New York taxable interest that appears nowhere on the federal return.
LINE 21 catches every New York State and New York City public employee. Their 414(h) retirement contributions are excluded from federal wages but taxed by New York. The amount is printed on the W-2 and is easy to overlook because the federal return never uses it.
## The symmetry worth understanding
New York taxes public employee contributions GOING IN (line 21) and exempts the resulting pension COMING OUT (line 26). The federal system does the reverse. Neither system taxes the money twice; they just tax it at different moments. Explaining this usually resolves a public employee's objection to line 21.
## Partners and S corporation shareholders
