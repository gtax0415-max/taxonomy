# Other Additions (Form IT-225)
## Description
The catch-all for New York additions without a dedicated line. Entered as a MODIFICATION CODE plus an amount on Form IT-225, which must be submitted with the return. The total from Form IT-225 line 9 goes to Form IT-201 line 23.
## How it works
Codes take the form A-nnn. Form IT-225 carries SUBTRACTIONS too, coded S-nnn, flowing to line 31. Same form, opposite direction — confirm which side of the form an item belongs on before entering it.
## The one that matters most: the PTET add-back
If you claim a pass-through entity tax credit, you MUST add back the PTET the entity deducted in computing the income reported to you. The credit and the add-back are two halves of one transaction.
Claiming the credit without the add-back understates New York income and reliably produces a notice. Software usually handles it; a manually prepared return often does not. Confirm the current code in the Form IT-225 instructions, since the PTET codes have been revised since the regime began.
See state/new-york/credits/business-incentive/pass-through-entity-tax-credit.md.

The full list runs to dozens of codes and changes between years. Read the current Form IT-225 instructions rather than relying on a prior year's entries.
## Federal Form 4970 (A-114)
If you filed federal Form 4970 for a trust accumulation distribution:
- DO NOT include the Form 4970 line 1 income on Form IT-201 line 11, because the IRC already treats the distribution as part of federal gross income
- DO include it on Form IT-225 line 5 under code A-114, after subtracting any interest on New York State and local bonds that you included on Form 4970 line 5
## Special accruals (A-115)
Applies if you were a NONRESIDENT on December 31 of the prior year, or will be a nonresident on January 1 of the following year, and you have income fixed and determinable in one year but reported federally in another. The paired subtraction is S-129.
Anyone moving into or out of New York should be screened for this. It is the most commonly missed modification in a move year and can be substantial for someone with deferred compensation or an installment sale.
## Net operating loss (A-215)
For New York purposes the NOL deduction is limited to the LESSER of your federal NOL deduction or your federal taxable income computed without the NOL deduction. The difference is an addition.
## Beneficiaries of estates and trusts
Your share of the fiduciary adjustment arrives as one net number:
- Net ADDITION: code EA-901 on Form IT-225, line 5
- Net SUBTRACTION: code ES-901 on Form IT-225, line 14
## Two entity codes that bypass this form
| Code | Description | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| EA-113 | Interest on state and local bonds and obligations | Line 20, not Form IT-225 |
| ES-125 | Interest on U.S. government bonds | Line 28, not Form IT-225 |
## Required Information
- Schedule K-1 and any entity statement of New York modifications
- PTET amounts deducted at the entity level
- Federal Form 4970, if filed
- Residency dates, if special accruals may apply
- NOL computations
## Questions
- Did your partnership or S corporation elect PTET?
- Did you receive a K-1 with New York addition codes?
- Did you move into or out of New York this year or last?
- Are you a beneficiary of an estate or trust?
- Do you have a net operating loss?
## Common Errors
- Claiming a PTET credit without the required add-back
- Entering an addition on the subtraction side of Form IT-225
- Failing to submit Form IT-225 with the return
- Routing EA-113 or ES-125 through Form IT-225 instead of lines 20 and 28
- Missing special accruals in a move year
- Using a prior year's code list
## Prompt
- My K-1 shows New York addition codes.
- Do I need to add back the PTET?
- I moved to Florida in December, what happens to my New York return?
