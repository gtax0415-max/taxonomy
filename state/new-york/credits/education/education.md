# Education Credits
## Description
New York offers ONE education credit, for undergraduate tuition, and it comes with a choice: take it as a credit or as an itemized deduction, but not both.
## Credits in this folder
- College Tuition Credit (college-tuition-credit.md) — Form IT-272 → Form IT-201, Line 68. Refundable
## The election that defines this category
The same tuition can be claimed EITHER as the college tuition credit (Form IT-272, refundable, worth up to $400 per student) OR as the college tuition itemized deduction (Form IT-196, up to $10,000 of tuition per student). You choose one. The deduction only helps if you itemize on your New York return, and New York itemization rules differ from federal ones — plenty of filers take the federal standard deduction and still itemize for New York.
Rough rule: at low and middle incomes the refundable credit usually wins; at higher incomes with large tuition bills and a New York itemized return, the deduction often wins. Run both.
## Not a credit, but adjacent
- NEW YORK'S 529 PLAN gives a SUBTRACTION of up to $5,000 of contributions ($10,000 married filing jointly) on IT-201 line 30, not a credit. Nonqualified withdrawals are added back on line 22
- A refund can be direct-deposited into up to three NYS 529 accounts using Form IT-195
- New York has no equivalent of the American Opportunity or Lifetime Learning credits, and no graduate-level credit

## Questions
- Did you, your spouse, or a dependent pay undergraduate tuition to an eligible institution?
- Are you itemizing deductions on your New York return?
- Did you contribute to a New York 529 plan?
