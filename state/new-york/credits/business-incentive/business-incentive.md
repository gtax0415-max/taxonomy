# Business and Incentive Credits
## Description
Credits earned through business activity, most of them reaching an individual's Form IT-201 by FLOW-THROUGH from a partnership, LLC, or S corporation. This category is much larger in New York than in the federal individual system, because New York runs most of its economic development policy through personal income tax credits.
Nearly all of them require a CERTIFICATE issued by an agency — usually Empire State Development — before any credit exists. No certificate, no credit, regardless of how much qualifying activity occurred.
## Credits in this folder
- Pass-Through Entity Tax Credit (pass-through-entity-tax-credit.md) — Form IT-653 → Form IT-201, Line 71. Refundable. By far the highest-dollar credit in this category

## The MCTMT connection
Self-employed individuals with net earnings allocated to the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation District pay MCTMT on Form IT-201 lines 54a through 54e. For 2025 the district is split into TWO ZONES with separate $50,000 thresholds and separate rates — 0.60% for Zone 1 (the five boroughs) and 0.34% for Zone 2 (the seven suburban counties). Each threshold is tested per individual even on a joint return. This is a tax, not a credit, but it lands in the middle of the credit lines and is easy to miss.
