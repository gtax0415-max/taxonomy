# Family and Dependent Credits
## Description
Credits based on having children or dependents, or on paying for their care. The largest New York credit category by volume and by dollars — the Empire State child credit alone reaches roughly 1.6 million households.
## Credits in this folder
- Empire State Child Credit (empire-state-child-credit.md) — Form IT-213 → Form IT-201, Line 63. Refundable
- Child and Dependent Care Credit (child-and-dependent-care-credit.md) — Form IT-216 → Form IT-201, Line 64. Refundable, state and city
- Household Credit (household-credit.md) — no form, computed on the IT-201 instruction tables → Lines 40 and 48. Nonrefundable
## What changed for 2025
- The EMPIRE STATE CHILD CREDIT DECOUPLED from the federal child tax credit. It is no longer computed as a percentage of the federal credit. New York now sets its own amounts: $1,000 per qualifying child under age four and $330 per qualifying child age four through sixteen
- The under-four amount TRIPLED from $330. The average credit per family roughly doubled
- An SSN or ITIN is required for each child claimed
## What changes for 2026
The credit for children four through sixteen rises from $330 to $500. The under-four amount stays at $1,000.
## The federal linkage that remains
The child and dependent care credit is still computed as a percentage OF THE FEDERAL CREDIT, so federal changes flow straight through. The federal top rate rising from 35% to 50% in 2026 raises the New York credit with no change to New York law.

## Questions
- Do you have children under 17 who lived with you more than half the year?
- How old was each child on December 31 — under four, or four through sixteen?
- Did you pay for childcare or dependent care so you could work or look for work?
- Do you and each child have a valid SSN or ITIN?
## Prompt
- I have kids in New York, what credits can I get?
- I paid for daycare last year and live in Brooklyn.
