# Property and Housing Credits
## Description
Credits tied to owning or renting a home in New York. This category has NO FEDERAL COUNTERPART — the federal system offers a mortgage interest deduction, not property tax credits — so it is one of the places where a New York return produces money a federal return never would.
## Credits in this folder
- Real Property Tax Credit (real-property-tax-credit.md) — Form IT-214 → Form IT-201, Line 67. Refundable, claimable without filing a return
- STAR Credit (star-credit.md) — administered OUTSIDE the income tax return. Refundable
- Historic Homeownership Rehabilitation Credit (historic-homeownership-rehabilitation-credit.md) — Form IT-237 → Form IT-201, Line 71
## Also in this area, not filed separately here
- Historic barn rehabilitation credit — Form IT-212-ATT, nonrefundable, line 42
- Rehabilitation of historic properties credit — Form IT-238, refundable, line 71, for certified historic structures generally rather than owner-occupied homes
- Conservation easement credit — Form IT-242, refundable, line 71, 25% of school district, county, and town real property taxes on easement land, capped at $5,000
- Special additional mortgage recording tax credit — Form IT-256, refundable, line 71
## The renter's credit people miss
The real property tax credit is available to RENTERS as well as owners. Most people assume a property tax credit requires owning property. The income ceiling is low, but a low-income renter aged 65 or older can collect $375 without owing any tax and without otherwise needing to file.
