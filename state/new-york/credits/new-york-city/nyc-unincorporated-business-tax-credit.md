# New York City Unincorporated Business Tax Credit
## Description
Nonrefundable credit against NEW YORK CITY PERSONAL income tax for New York City UNINCORPORATED BUSINESS TAX paid. The UBT is a separate city tax on unincorporated businesses operating in the city, administered by the NYC Department of Finance, not by New York State. This credit relieves part of the resulting double layer.
## Who can claim it
- A New York City resident who filed Form NYC-202 or NYC-202S and paid UBT directly
- A PARTNER in a New York City partnership that filed Form NYC-204 and paid UBT
- A BENEFICIARY of an estate or trust that filed Form NYC-202EIN and paid UBT
You must be a city resident or part-year resident. The credit offsets city tax, so a nonresident with no city personal income tax gets nothing from it.
## Amount
The credit is a percentage of the UBT paid, phasing DOWN as taxable income rises. At lower income levels it can approach full relief; at higher income levels it settles at a smaller fraction. Form IT-219 runs the calculation.
Partners compute the credit on their share of the partnership's UBT, which the partnership reports to them.
## The UBT itself is filed separately
New York State does NOT administer the unincorporated business tax. Do NOT file Form NYC-202, NYC-202S, or NYC-204 with your New York State return — they go to the New York City Department of Finance. Likewise, do not include UBT payments in the estimated tax total on Form IT-201 line 75.
## Notes
- The UBT applies to sole proprietors, partnerships, and LLCs treated as partnerships carrying on a trade or business in the city. Being a self-employed city resident is often enough to trigger it
- A widely used exemption removes taxpayers whose activity is limited to trading for their own account, and there is a small-business exemption below an income floor
- The credit is nonrefundable and reduces city tax only, not state tax
- Freelancers new to the city frequently discover the UBT filing obligation for the first time through this credit
## Required Information
- UBT paid, from the city return or the partnership's report
- Your share, if a partner or beneficiary
- New York City taxable income, for the phase-down calculation
- Months of city residency
## Questions
- Do you operate a business or freelance in New York City?
- Did you file Form NYC-202, NYC-202S, or NYC-204?
- Are you a partner in a city partnership that paid UBT?
- Were you a New York City resident?
- What was your city taxable income?
## Common Errors
- Filing the NYC-202 series with the New York State return
- Including UBT payments in the IT-201 estimated tax line
- Claiming the credit as a city nonresident
- Missing the credit as a partner who never saw the UBT return, only the K-1
- Not realizing self-employment in the city creates a UBT obligation in the first place
## Prompt
- I freelance in New York City and paid unincorporated business tax.
- I am a partner in a Manhattan firm.
