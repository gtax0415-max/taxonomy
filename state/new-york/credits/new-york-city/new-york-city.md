# New York City Credits
## Description
Credits that reduce NEW YORK CITY personal income tax, all claimed on the New York State return. New York City has no separate personal income tax return — city tax is computed on Form IT-201 lines 47 through 53, and city credits land alongside state ones.
## Credits in this folder
- NYC School Tax Credit (nyc-school-tax-credit.md) — Lines 69 and 69a. Refundable, claimable without a return
- NYC Income Tax Elimination Credit (nyc-income-tax-elimination-credit.md) — Form IT-270 → Line 70a. NEW for 2025
- NYC Unincorporated Business Tax Credit (nyc-unincorporated-business-tax-credit.md) — Form IT-219 → Line 53. Nonrefundable

## Who is a New York City resident
City residency uses the same tests as state residency with New York City substituted for New York State: domicile, or a permanent place of abode plus 184 days or more in the city. The Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, and Staten Island are all New York City. Note that Form IT-201 asks for county names, so Brooklyn residents write Kings and Staten Island residents write Richmond.
## Yonkers is different
Yonkers imposes a resident income tax SURCHARGE (16.75% of net state tax) and a nonresident EARNINGS tax, not a full income tax. There are no Yonkers credits as such, only the claim of right credit. Yonkers is handled in new-york/taxes/yonkers.md.
## What changed for 2025
The NEW YORK CITY INCOME TAX ELIMINATION CREDIT was created for tax years beginning on or after January 1, 2025. For a qualifying low-income city resident with dependents it can wipe out the entire remaining city tax liability.
## Questions
- Were you a New York City resident for all, part, or none of the year?
- How many months did you live in the city?
- Do you claim any dependents?
- Did you claim a New York City or New York State PTET credit, which blocks the elimination credit?
