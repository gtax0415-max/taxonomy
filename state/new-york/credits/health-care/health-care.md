# Health and Care Credits
## Description
Credits for long-term care premiums and for nursing home costs. Note the SHAPE OF THIS CATEGORY IS DIFFERENT from the federal one: the federal health category is about buying Marketplace insurance, while New York's is about paying for LONG-TERM CARE. New York has no analogue to the premium tax credit.
## Credits in this folder
- Long-Term Care Insurance Credit (long-term-care-insurance-credit.md) — Form IT-249 → Form IT-201, Line 42. Nonrefundable
- Nursing Home Assessment Credit (nursing-home-assessment-credit.md) — Form IT-258 → Form IT-201, Line 71. Refundable
## Also health-adjacent, filed elsewhere
- Automated external defibrillator credit — Form IT-250, nonrefundable, line 42, up to $500 per unit
- Organ donation credit — see civic-volunteer/
## No New York premium tax credit
Health insurance bought through the NY State of Health Marketplace is subsidized by the FEDERAL premium tax credit only. There is no state add-on credit on Form IT-201. The 2026 reversion to the 400%-of-poverty cliff therefore hits New York filers with no state cushion — the one partial offset is that New York's Essential Plan covers many people who would otherwise face that cliff.
