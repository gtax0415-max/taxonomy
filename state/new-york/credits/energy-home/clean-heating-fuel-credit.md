# Clean Heating Fuel Credit
## Description
Refundable credit for buying BIOHEATING FUEL for residential space heating or hot water in New York State. Small per gallon, but it is refundable and it repeats every year, which makes it one of the few recurring New York credits.
## Amount
- $0.01 per gallon for EACH PERCENT of biodiesel in the fuel
- MAXIMUM $0.20 PER GALLON, reached at B20
- The fuel must be AT LEAST 6% biodiesel (B6) to qualify at all
So B20 fuel earns 20 cents a gallon; B10 earns 10 cents; B5 earns nothing. A household burning 800 gallons of B20 in a season collects $160.
## The percentage is the whole calculation
Everything depends on the biodiesel percentage shown on the delivery invoice. If the invoice does not state it, the credit cannot be substantiated. Ask the dealer to print the blend on every ticket — many New York dealers now do this automatically because of the credit, and downstate deliveries are often B20 by local mandate.
## Eligibility
- The fuel must be used for SPACE HEATING or HOT WATER production
- For RESIDENTIAL purposes
- At a residence in New York State
Both owners and renters can claim it, as long as they bought the fuel. A tenant whose heat is included in the rent has bought nothing and cannot claim it.
## Expiration
The credit has been extended repeatedly and is currently scheduled to sunset. Check the current expiration date on the Form IT-241 instructions for the filing year before relying on it for future planning.
## Notes
- Natural gas, propane, electricity, and straight No. 2 heating oil earn nothing
- Business use is claimed on the same form but under separate rules
- Keep every delivery ticket for the year; the credit is computed on total qualifying gallons
- New York City and Westchester biodiesel blending mandates mean many downstate households already qualify without knowing it
## Required Information
- Total gallons delivered during the year
- Biodiesel percentage for each delivery
- The delivery address
- Whether the use was residential
## Questions
- Do you heat with oil, and is it a biodiesel blend?
- What percentage biodiesel does your invoice show?
- How many gallons did you buy during the year?
- Was the fuel delivered to a New York residence?
- Do you buy the fuel yourself, or is heat included in your rent?
## Common Errors
- Claiming for fuel below B6, which does not qualify
- Not having the biodiesel percentage documented on the invoices
- Claiming for propane, natural gas, or straight heating oil
- A tenant claiming when the landlord buys the fuel
- Missing the credit entirely because the blend is not obvious from the bill
## Prompt
- I heat my house with bioheating oil.
- My fuel invoice says B20, is that worth anything?
- Is there a New York credit for heating oil?
