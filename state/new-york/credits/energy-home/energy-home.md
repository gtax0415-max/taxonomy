# Energy and Home Credits
## Description
Credits for residential energy improvements. THE HEADLINE FOR 2026: the federal residential energy credits are GONE, terminated by the One Big Beautiful Bill Act after December 31, 2025, but THE NEW YORK CREDITS SURVIVE. A solar installation completed in 2026 gets nothing federally and still gets 25% from New York, capped at $5,000.
This reversal makes New York's energy credits far more important than they were, and it is the single most valuable thing to tell a New York homeowner planning energy work.

## Federal versus New York for 2026
| Improvement | Federal | New York |
|---|---|---|
| Solar electric or solar thermal | Terminated after 2025 | 25%, up to $5,000 |
| Geothermal heat pump | Terminated after 2025 | 25%, up to $5,000 |
| Insulation, windows, doors, heat pumps | Terminated after 2025 | No New York credit |
| Bioheating fuel purchases | Never had one | Up to $0.20 per gallon, refundable |
| EV charging equipment | 30C, through June 30, 2026 | Form IT-637, business property |
New York has NO analogue to the federal Section 25C efficiency credit. Insulation, windows, doors, and air-source heat pumps get nothing from New York — those go through NYSERDA rebates and utility programs instead, which are unaffected by federal changes.

## Questions
- What type of system did you install — solar, geothermal, or efficiency improvements?
- When was it placed in service?
- Is it your principal residence in New York State?
- Did you purchase, lease, or sign a power purchase agreement?
## Prompt
- I installed solar panels in 2026, do I get anything now that the federal credit ended?
- I put in a geothermal heat pump.
- I heat with bioheating fuel.
