# Civic and Volunteer Credits
## Description
Credits for public service and donation. There is NO FEDERAL EQUIVALENT to this category — the federal system handles charitable activity through the itemized deduction, not through credits. New York grants flat-dollar credits for specific civic acts, which reach filers who would never itemize.
## Credits in this folder
- Volunteer Firefighters' and Ambulance Workers' Credit (volunteer-firefighters-credit.md) — Form IT-245 → Form IT-201, Line 71. Refundable
- Organ Donation Credit (organ-donation-credit.md) — Form IT-269 → Form IT-201, Line 71. Refundable
## Related credits filed elsewhere
- Contributions to certain funds credit — Form IT-228, nonrefundable, line 42. For contributions to the specific state funds listed on that form. Do not confuse it with the VOLUNTARY CONTRIBUTIONS on Form IT-227, which are donations that INCREASE what you owe, not credits
- Farm donations to food pantries credit — Form IT-649, refundable, line 71
- Automated external defibrillator credit — Form IT-250, nonrefundable, line 42
## The Form IT-227 confusion
Form IT-227 voluntary contributions are the OPPOSITE of a credit. Checking those boxes gives money away and reduces your refund. Form IT-228 is the actual credit. The similar form numbers cause real errors — verify which one is meant whenever a client mentions contributing to a state fund.

## Questions
- Are you a volunteer firefighter or ambulance worker?
- Did you donate an organ during the year?
- Did you receive a property tax exemption for volunteer service, which conflicts with the credit?
## Prompt
- I volunteer with my local fire department.
- I donated a kidney last year.
- Is there a New York credit for volunteering?
