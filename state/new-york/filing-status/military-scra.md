# Military Personnel and the SCRA
## Description
New York conforms to the SERVICEMEMBERS CIVIL RELIEF ACT. For tax years 2023 and after, a servicemember AND their spouse may EACH elect which state's residence to use for income tax purposes — a significant expansion over the prior rules.
## The default rule
A servicemember who was DOMICILED IN NEW YORK when entering the military REMAINS domiciled in New York, even when assigned to duty in another state or country. Military assignment does not change domicile, for the same reason a temporary business assignment does not.
If you are in the armed forces and your permanent home was in New York when you entered, enter your NEW YORK permanent home address on the return regardless of where you are stationed.
## The three SCRA elections
For any tax year of the marriage, and REGARDLESS OF THE DATE OF MARRIAGE, a servicemember and their spouse may elect to use:
1. The RESIDENCE OR DOMICILE OF THE SERVICEMEMBER
2. The RESIDENCE OR DOMICILE OF THE SPOUSE
3. The PERMANENT DUTY STATION of the servicemember
Each spouse elects separately. They need not choose the same one.
## The codes: Item G on Form IT-201
| Code | Who | Election |
|---|---|---|
| M4 | Servicemember | Own state of legal residence or domicile |
| M5 | Servicemember | SPOUSE'S state of legal residence or domicile |
| M6 | Servicemember | State where the PERMANENT DUTY STATION is located |
| M7 | Civilian spouse | Own state of legal residence or domicile |
| M8 | Civilian spouse | The SERVICEMEMBER'S state of legal residence or domicile |
| M9 | Civilian spouse | State where the servicemember's PERMANENT DUTY STATION is located |
M4 through M6 are for the SERVICEMEMBER. M7 through M9 are for the CIVILIAN SPOUSE. Selecting a code from the wrong band invalidates the election.
An election must be entered at Item G. Making the election in substance without the code produces a mismatch the Department will question.
## Why this matters
A New York-domiciled servicemember stationed in Texas may elect the duty station state and escape New York taxation of all income. Before the 2023 expansion the spouse's options were narrower and required matching the servicemember's domicile.
The planning value is highest when the duty station is in a state with NO INCOME TAX — Texas, Florida, Tennessee, Washington, Nevada, and others. The elections apply to the SPOUSE'S OWN income as well, which is where the larger savings often sit.
## Military pay for New York residents
If you are a member of the military AND a New York State resident, the amount of your military pay that is subject to FEDERAL income tax is also subject to NEW YORK tax. There is no general exemption for military pay.
COMBAT PAY receives special treatment — see Form IT-225 subtraction modification code S-118.
## Other military provisions on the return
| Item | Where |
|---|---|
| Combat zone extension of time to file and pay | Item G, code C7 |
| Killed in action in a combat zone, filing on behalf | Item G, code K2 |
| Out of the country, automatic two-month extension | Item G, code E3 |

Military pensions from the United States, including the military, qualify for the FULL government pension exclusion at line 26 — no cap, no age test. See state/new-york/deductions/subtractions/government-pension-exclusion.md.
## Required Information
- Military orders and permanent duty station
- State of legal residence for the servicemember and for the spouse
- Which election each is making, and the corresponding code
- Domicile at the time of entering military service
- Combat zone service dates, if applicable
## Questions
- Were you domiciled in New York when you entered the military?
- Where is your permanent duty station?
- What is your spouse's state of legal residence?
- Which election does each of you want to make?
- Did you serve in a combat zone during the year?
## Common Errors
- Assuming military assignment outside New York changes domicile
- Using a servicemember code (M4 to M6) for the civilian spouse, or the reverse
- Making the election without entering the code at Item G
- Assuming military pay is exempt from New York tax for a resident
- Missing the combat pay subtraction, code S-118
- Overlooking that a military pension is fully exempt at line 26
## Prompt
- I am in the Army and stationed in Texas but I am from New York.
- My husband is active duty and we moved to Florida.
- Does New York tax my military pay?
