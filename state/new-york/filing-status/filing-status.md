# New York Filing Status and Residency
## Description
TWO SEPARATE TOPICS live here. FILING STATUS usually just copies the federal return. RESIDENCY is entirely state law, has no federal analogue, and is the highest-stakes determination on a New York return — it decides which form you file and whether New York taxes all your income or only part of it.

## The three statuses and their forms
| Status | Form | Taxed on |
|---|---|---|
| RESIDENT | IT-201 | ALL income, wherever earned |
| PART-YEAR RESIDENT | IT-203 | Resident-period income in full, plus New York source income for the rest |
| NONRESIDENT | IT-203 | NEW YORK SOURCE INCOME only |
## Residency is determined FOUR TIMES
The same tests are applied separately for each taxing authority:
| Authority | Test |
|---|---|
| New York STATE | Domicile in NY, or permanent place of abode plus 184 days in NY |
| New York CITY | Domicile in the city, or permanent place of abode plus 184 days in the CITY |
| YONKERS | Domicile in Yonkers, or permanent place of abode plus 184 days in YONKERS |
| MCTMT | Not residency at all — based on where self-employment earnings are ALLOCATED |
You can be a New York State resident and a New York City nonresident. You cannot be a city resident without being a state resident, since the city sits inside the state.
## The one asymmetry to memorize
A NONRESIDENT is NOT liable for New York City personal income tax under any circumstances — the city has no nonresident tax. But a nonresident MAY owe the YONKERS NONRESIDENT EARNINGS TAX if they earn wages or carry on a trade or business there, or are a partner in a partnership that does.
## Where residency shows up on Form IT-201
| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| A | Filing status |
| D2 | Living quarters in YONKERS, and months. ALL filers must complete |
| E | Living quarters in NEW YORK CITY, and days in the city. All non-city filers must complete |
| F | Months of NYC residence. City residents and part-year city residents only |
| G | Special condition codes, including the SCRA elections and Y1 |

## Questions
- Where was your permanent home, and where did you actually live?
- How many days were you physically present in New York State, and in the city?
- Did you move into or out of New York during the year?
- Do you work remotely for a New York employer from another state?
- Are you or your spouse in the military?
## Prompt
- Am I a New York resident for tax purposes?
- I moved out of New York but still have an apartment there.
- I work remotely for a New York company from Florida.
