# New York Taxes
## Description
FOUR TAXING AUTHORITIES are computed on one return: New York State, New York City, Yonkers, and the Metropolitan Commuter Transportation Mobility Tax. Each has its own base, its own rates, and its own residency test, and all four land on Form IT-201.

## The four bases are NOT the same
| Tax | Base |
|---|---|
| New York State | New York taxable income, line 38 |
| New York City | NYC taxable income, line 47 — equals line 38 EXCEPT for one adjustment |
| Yonkers resident | NEW YORK STATE TAX ITSELF, not income. A surcharge |
| Yonkers nonresident | WAGES and self-employment earnings sourced to Yonkers |
| MCTMT | NET EARNINGS FROM SELF-EMPLOYMENT allocated to the district |
Understanding that Yonkers taxes the tax, and the MCTMT taxes only self-employment earnings, resolves most confusion about these lines.
## 2026 rate cut
New York lowered its bracket rates for 2026:
| Bracket | 2025 | 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| First | 4.00% | 3.90% |
| Second | 4.50% | 4.40% |
| Third | 5.25% | 5.15% |
| Fourth | 5.50% | 5.40% |
| Fifth | 6.00% | 5.90% |
Rates above the fifth bracket are unchanged: 6.85%, 9.65%, 10.3%, and 10.9%. Confirmed from Form IT-2105-I (2026).
## The recapture above $107,650
When New York AGI exceeds $107,650, the graduated rate schedule is not used directly. Sixteen TAX COMPUTATION WORKSHEETS claw back the benefit of the lower brackets, phased in over $50,000 bands, so that high earners effectively pay a flat rate on all income. The worksheets are selected by filing status and by which income band you fall into.
Above $25,000,000 of New York AGI the result is simply 10.9% of taxable income with no graduation at all.
## New York City rates
Four brackets, 3.078% to 3.876%. Combined with the state top rate, a high-income city resident faces a marginal rate above 14% before the federal return is considered.
NEW YORK CITY HAS NO NONRESIDENT EARNINGS TAX. It was repealed in 1999. Someone who works in Manhattan and lives in New Jersey owes no city income tax. Yonkers, by contrast, still has one.
## Sales and use tax on the income tax return
Line 59 reports use tax on purchases delivered into New York without tax paid, or bought outside New York and brought back. You may use the chart in the IT-201 instructions for items under $1,000 each, or Form ST-141 for exact amounts. If the reported amount is $1,700 or more you must also file Form IT-135.
LINE 59 CANNOT BE LEFT BLANK. Enter 0 if you owe nothing.
