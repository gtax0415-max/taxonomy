# Voluntary Contributions
## Description
Donations to New York State funds, made ON THE RETURN by completing FORM IT-227. THIS IS NOT A CREDIT AND NOT A DEDUCTION. It is a gift. Every dollar entered REDUCES YOUR REFUND OR INCREASES YOUR PAYMENT by exactly one dollar.
Step 7 of Form IT-201, line 60.
## The warning that belongs first
The Form IT-201 instructions state it plainly: your total contributions will reduce your refund or increase your tax payment, AND YOU CANNOT CHANGE THE AMOUNTS AFTER YOU FILE — even if the Tax Department later adjusts your refund or the amount you owe.
There is no undo. A mis-keyed amount is a completed gift.
## The confusion with Form IT-228
Two forms with adjacent numbers do OPPOSITE things:
| Form | What it is | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| IT-227 | VOLUNTARY CONTRIBUTIONS | You GIVE money. Reduces your refund |
| IT-228 | CONTRIBUTIONS TO CERTAIN FUNDS CREDIT | You RECEIVE a credit. Reduces your tax |
This is a genuine and recurring source of error. When someone says they "contributed to a state fund," establish which form applies before entering anything.
## The funds
Roughly forty funds are available, spanning wildlife, health research, veterans, education, libraries, the arts, and public safety. Notable categories:
- HEALTH RESEARCH: breast cancer, prostate and testicular cancer, women's cancers, childhood cancer, leukemia and lymphoma, Alzheimer's, lupus, diabetes, ALS, autism, Lyme and tick-borne disease
- VETERANS AND MILITARY: veterans remembrance and cemetery maintenance, homeless veterans, veterans' homes, military family relief
- PUBLIC SAFETY: missing and exploited children, volunteer firefighting recruitment and retention, firearm violence research, the William B. Hoyt children and family trust
- EDUCATION AND CULTURE: Love Your Library, the state library system, CUNY construction, the Council on the Arts, teen health education, school-based health centers
- OTHER: Return a Gift to Wildlife, the 9/11 Memorial and Museum, food banks, home delivered meals for seniors, organ donation (Life Pass It On), substance use disorder education, retired thoroughbred and standardbred racehorse aftercare
## Funds with FIXED contribution amounts
Most funds accept any amount. Three do not:
| Fund | Required amount |
|---|---|
| U.S. Olympic Committee / Lake Placid Olympic Training Center | $2 per person, $4 if both spouses contribute |
| New York State Campaign Finance Fund | $40 per person, $80 if both spouses contribute |
The Campaign Finance Fund is restricted to NEW YORK STATE RESIDENTS AND PART-YEAR RESIDENTS only.
## Two funds are MATCHED by the state
New York State matches contributions DOLLAR FOR DOLLAR to:
- The Breast Cancer Research and Education Fund
- The Prostate and Testicular Cancer Research and Education Fund
Worth mentioning to a taxpayer who intends to give anyway.
## Rules
- You cannot contribute to a fund BEFORE THE YEAR IT WAS ESTABLISHED. Relevant on an amended or late-filed prior-year return
- Not every fund is available on corporation tax returns; Form CT-227 covers the corporate subset
- Submit Form IT-227 with the return
- If you make no contributions, skip line 60 and go to line 61
## Is it deductible?
A contribution here is a gift to a state fund. Whether it supports a federal charitable deduction depends on the fund and on federal rules; it is NOT automatically deductible, and it produces no New York deduction or credit.
Do not confuse this with the NEW YORK CHARITABLE GIFTS TRUST FUND — the Health Charitable Account and the Elementary and Secondary Education Account — which is a different structure entirely, is reported on Form IT-196 lines 16a and 16b, and triggers a New York City add-back. See state/new-york/taxes/new-york-city/nyc-taxable-income-adjustment.md.
## Required Information
NOTHING IS COLLECTED FROM A THIRD PARTY. Unlike every other item on the return, there is no statement, receipt, or acknowledgment to gather — the decision is made at filing time and the money moves with the return.
- Which funds you want to support, and how much to each
- The FIXED amounts where they apply: $2 Olympic, $40 Campaign Finance
- Residency status, for the Campaign Finance Fund
- Your own record of the amounts entered, since Form IT-227 becomes the only evidence after filing
## Questions
- Do you want to make a voluntary contribution, understanding it reduces your refund?
- Which funds do you want to support?
- Did you mean the IT-228 CREDIT rather than an IT-227 contribution?
- Are you a New York resident, for the Campaign Finance Fund?
## Common Errors
- Confusing Form IT-227 with the Form IT-228 credit and expecting a tax benefit
- Entering an amount without realizing it reduces the refund dollar for dollar
- Using an amount other than $2 or $40 for the fixed-amount funds
- Contributing to a fund for a year before it existed on a prior-year return
- Assuming the contribution is deductible somewhere on the New York return
