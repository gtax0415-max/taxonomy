# Sales or Use Tax
## Description
Use tax on purchases brought into or delivered into New York without New York sales tax paid. Reported at Form IT-201 line 59, in the middle of the income tax return.
## LINE 59 CANNOT BE LEFT BLANK
If you owe nothing, ENTER 0. A blank line 59 is treated as an incomplete return. This is the single most common mechanical error on the whole form.
## When you owe it
- You bought an item or service subject to tax that was DELIVERED TO YOU IN NEW YORK without New York State and local tax being collected, or
- You bought an item or service OUTSIDE New York while a New York resident, subject to tax in New York, and subsequently used it here
Common triggers: internet purchases, catalog orders, television shopping channels, purchases on an Indian reservation other than Oneida Nation purchases, and things bought on a trip to a lower-tax or no-tax state and carried home.
The instructions give two examples: a computer ordered online from an out-of-state company and delivered to Monroe County, and a book bought in New Hampshire and brought back to Nassau County.
## Residency for sales tax is broader
For SALES AND USE TAX purposes, a resident includes anyone with a PERMANENT PLACE OF ABODE in the state. You can be a resident for sales tax while being a nonresident for income tax. A part-year or nonresident income tax filer may still owe use tax.
## The chart method
The instruction chart lets you avoid tracking individual purchases, for items and services costing LESS THAN $1,000 EACH excluding shipping and handling:
| Federal AGI (line 19) | Enter on line 59 |
|---|---|
| Up to $15,000 | $3 |
| $15,001 to $30,000 | $6 |
| $30,001 to $50,000 | $10 |
| $50,001 to $75,000 | $15 |
| $75,001 to $100,000 | $18 |
| $100,001 to $150,000 | $26 |
| $150,001 to $200,000 | $34 |
| $200,001 and above | 0.0175% of income, or $125, whichever is SMALLER |
You may NOT use the chart for purchases related to a BUSINESS, RENTAL REAL ESTATE, or ROYALTY activity, regardless of amount.
If you maintained a permanent place of abode in New York for only part of the year, multiply the chart amount by the months maintained and divide by 12, counting any period over half a month as a full month.
## When Form ST-141 is required
Use Form ST-141 instead of the chart if any of these apply:
- You prefer to compute the exact amount
- You owe tax on an item or service costing $1,000 OR MORE, excluding shipping and handling
- The purchases relate to a business not registered for sales tax, rental real estate, or royalty activity
Enter the Form ST-141 line 7 amount on line 59. DO NOT submit Form ST-141 with the return.
## Form IT-135
If the amount on line 59 is $1,700 OR MORE, you must complete FORM IT-135, Sales and Use Tax Report for Purchases of Items and Services Costing $25,000 or More, and submit it with the return.
## What does NOT go on line 59
- BUSINESS purchases where the business is REGISTERED for sales tax. Those go on the business's sales tax return
- MOTOR VEHICLES, trailers, all-terrain vehicles, vessels, and snowmobiles. Tax is paid directly to the DMV. If you will not register or title it at the DMV, remit directly using Form ST-130 or ST-141
## Credit for tax paid to another state
You may be entitled to a credit for sales tax paid to another state on the same purchase. The exact computation method is in the Form ST-141 instructions. You may also owe ADDITIONAL LOCAL tax if you use the property in a New York locality with a higher rate than the one where you paid.
## Enforcement
The Tax Department conducts routine audits using information from third parties including the U.S. Customs Service and other states. Failure to pay can result in penalty and interest.
## Required Information
- Purchases delivered into New York without tax collected, by amount
- Purchases made out of state and brought back
- Whether any single item cost $1,000 or more
- Whether purchases relate to a business, rental property, or royalty activity
- Sales tax paid to another state, for the credit
- Months you maintained a place of abode in New York
## Questions
- Did you buy anything online, by catalog, or out of state without paying sales tax?
- Did any single purchase cost $1,000 or more?
- Were any purchases for a business, rental property, or royalty activity?
- Did you pay sales tax to another state on the same item?
- Have you entered something on line 59, even if it is zero?
## Common Errors
- Leaving line 59 blank instead of entering 0
- Using the chart for an item costing $1,000 or more
- Using the chart for business, rental, or royalty purchases
- Reporting vehicle or vessel use tax here instead of paying the DMV
- Missing Form IT-135 when the amount reaches $1,700
- Assuming nonresidents never owe it, when the sales tax residency test is broader
## Prompt
- I bought a laptop online and paid no sales tax.
- What is line 59 on the New York return?
- Do I owe New York tax on something I bought in New Jersey?
