# Yonkers Nonresident Earnings Tax
## Description
A tax on WAGES AND NET SELF-EMPLOYMENT EARNINGS earned in Yonkers by people who do not live there. Yonkers is the only New York locality that still imposes one — New York City repealed its equivalent in 1999.
## The threshold question
Were you a Yonkers NONRESIDENT who EARNED WAGES in Yonkers?
- If NO: nothing on line 56
- If YES: complete Form Y-203, Yonkers Nonresident Earnings Tax Return, and submit it with your return
## Rate and exclusion
The rate is 0.50%. An exclusion applies to lower earnings amounts, computed on Form Y-203 itself. Because the exclusion mechanics and any indexing change from year to year, take the figures from the current Form Y-203 and its instructions rather than from memory.
The tax is small in absolute terms — a $100,000 Yonkers salary produces a few hundred dollars — but it is a filing obligation that commuters routinely do not know exists.
## What is taxed
- WAGES for services performed IN YONKERS
- NET EARNINGS FROM SELF-EMPLOYMENT from a trade or business carried on in Yonkers
Income allocated to work performed OUTSIDE Yonkers is not taxed, even by an employee of a Yonkers-based employer. If you work partly in Yonkers and partly elsewhere, allocate by days or by another reasonable method and keep the records.
## What is NOT taxed
- Interest, dividends, capital gains, and other investment income
- Pension and retirement income
- Rental income
- Income of a Yonkers RESIDENT, who pays the resident surcharge instead
The base is EARNINGS ONLY. This is much narrower than the resident surcharge, which reaches all income indirectly through state tax.
## Withholding
Employers with Yonkers operations withhold Yonkers nonresident earnings tax, reported on Form W-2 and summarized on Form IT-2. That withholding is claimed at Form IT-201 line 74, total Yonkers tax withheld.
Withholding is often the first sign a commuter has this obligation. If a W-2 shows Yonkers tax withheld and the filer does not live in Yonkers, Form Y-203 is required.
## Item D2 flags this
Form IT-201 Item D2 must be completed by ALL filers. A Yes on line D2(4) indicates you may be subject to the nonresident earnings tax. Review Item D2 before concluding that line 56 is blank.
## Claim of right
If you anticipate a claim of right credit against the Yonkers nonresident earnings tax, NET the credit against the tax and enter the net amount. Do not report the tax gross and claim the credit separately.
## Residents do not use this line
A Yonkers RESIDENT pays the resident surcharge at line 55, not this tax. A PART-YEAR Yonkers resident may owe BOTH: the surcharge for the resident period via Form IT-360.1 at line 57, and the nonresident earnings tax for the nonresident period if they worked in Yonkers during it.
## Required Information
- Wages earned for services performed in Yonkers
- Days worked in Yonkers versus elsewhere, if allocating
- Net self-employment earnings from business carried on in Yonkers
- Yonkers tax withheld, from Form W-2
- Your Yonkers residency status and any change during the year
## Questions
- Do you work in Yonkers but live somewhere else?
- Does your W-2 show Yonkers wages or Yonkers tax withheld?
- Do you work partly in Yonkers and partly elsewhere?
- Do you carry on a business or profession in Yonkers?
- Did you live in Yonkers for part of the year?
## Common Errors
- Not knowing the tax exists, because New York City has no equivalent
- Reporting all wages rather than only those for work performed in Yonkers
- Including investment, pension, or rental income in the base
- A Yonkers resident filing Form Y-203 instead of computing the line 55 surcharge
- Claiming a claim of right credit separately instead of netting it
- Skipping Item D2, which flags the obligation
## Prompt
- I work in Yonkers but live in the Bronx.
- My W-2 shows Yonkers tax withheld and I do not live there.
- Does Yonkers tax commuters?
