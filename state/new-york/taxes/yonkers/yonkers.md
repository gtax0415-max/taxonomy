# Yonkers Tax
## Description
Yonkers is the ONLY New York locality besides New York City with a personal income tax on the state return, and it works completely differently from the city's. Yonkers does not tax income. It taxes THE STATE TAX ITSELF, as a surcharge on residents, and it taxes WAGES EARNED IN YONKERS by nonresidents.

## There are NO Yonkers income adjustments
This is the direct answer to the question people ask most often here. Yonkers has:
- NO separate additions or subtractions
- NO separate standard or itemized deduction
- NO separate exemption
- NO separate taxable income figure
- NO Yonkers credits, apart from the claim of right credit on Form IT-257
A Yonkers resident's income is computed exactly as any other New York resident's. The surcharge is then applied to the resulting STATE TAX, after most credits. Nothing about the income calculation changes because you live in Yonkers.
## The three lines
| Line | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 55 | Full-year Yonkers RESIDENT | 16.75% surcharge on net state tax |
| 56 | Yonkers NONRESIDENT who earned wages there | Earnings tax, Form Y-203 |
| 57 | PART-YEAR Yonkers resident | Form IT-360.1 |
## Yonkers versus New York City, side by side
| | New York City | Yonkers |
|---|---|---|
| Base | City taxable income | STATE TAX for residents; WAGES for nonresidents |
| Nonresident tax | NONE, repealed 1999 | YES, still imposed |
| Rate | 3.078% to 3.876% of income | 16.75% of state tax; 0.50% of nonresident earnings |
| Own credits | Many | Only claim of right |
| Own income adjustments | One, the Charitable Gifts Trust Fund add-back | None |
A commuter working in Yonkers and living elsewhere DOES owe Yonkers tax. The same commuter working in Manhattan would owe nothing to New York City. This asymmetry surprises nearly everyone.
## Item D2 on Form IT-201
ALL IT-201 FILERS must complete Item D2, not just Yonkers residents. If you or your spouse maintained or had use of an apartment or living quarters in Yonkers during any part of the year — whether or not you personally used them — mark Yes on line D2(1) and enter the number of months.
Living quarters means any dwelling suitable for year-round use that you maintain or pay for, or that is maintained for your primary use by another person, family member, or employer. The instructions give the example of a company leasing an apartment mainly available to one officer: that officer is maintaining living quarters even if others occasionally use it.
A Yes on line D2(4) may make you subject to the nonresident earnings tax.
## Special condition code Y1
If you file jointly and only ONE spouse was a full-year Yonkers resident while the other was a Yonkers nonresident, enter code Y1 at Item G and compute the surcharge on a separate sheet. See the resident surcharge file.
