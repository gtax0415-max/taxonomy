# New York City Tax
## Description
New York City personal income tax is computed ON THE NEW YORK STATE RETURN. There is no separate city income tax return for individuals. Lines 47 through 53 of Form IT-201 carry the whole computation.

## The short answer on city adjustments
NEW YORK CITY HAS ALMOST NO SEPARATE INCOME ADJUSTMENTS. City taxable income at line 47 EQUALS state taxable income at line 38, with exactly one exception: an add-back for New York Charitable Gifts Trust Fund contributions deducted on Form IT-196.
There is no city version of the additions at lines 20 through 23, no city version of the subtractions at lines 25 through 31, no separate city standard deduction, and no separate city itemized deduction. The city rides entirely on the state base.
Where the city DOES diverge is in CREDITS, which are numerous and separate.

## No nonresident earnings tax
The New York City nonresident earnings tax WAS REPEALED IN 1999. A commuter who works in Manhattan and lives in New Jersey, Connecticut, or Westchester owes NO New York City income tax.
This is the single most common misconception about city tax, and it is the sharpest contrast with Yonkers, which still imposes a nonresident earnings tax.
## Who is a city resident
Same tests as state residency with the city substituted: domicile in the city, OR a permanent place of abode in the city plus 184 DAYS OR MORE in the city. Any part of a day counts. Days spent only transiting through the city on a common carrier do not count.
Item E on Form IT-201 collects this for non-city filers; Item F collects months of city residence for city filers.
The five counties: Bronx, Kings (Brooklyn), New York (Manhattan), Queens, and Richmond (Staten Island). Form IT-201 asks for the COUNTY name, so a Brooklyn resident writes Kings and a Staten Island resident writes Richmond.
## Married couples with split city residency
If you file a joint New York return and only ONE spouse was a full-year city resident, do not enter amounts at lines 47a or 48. Instead compute the city tax on a SEPARATE SHEET, as if you had filed separate federal returns, on the city resident's state taxable income, reduced by the city household credit if applicable. Transfer the result to line 51 and submit the sheet with the city resident's name and Social Security number.
If the other spouse was a PART-YEAR city resident, they complete Form IT-360.1 and the two amounts are combined at line 51.
## The unincorporated business tax is separate
NYC UBT is administered by the NYC Department of Finance on Forms NYC-202, NYC-202S, and NYC-204. DO NOT file those with your New York State return, and do not include UBT payments in the estimated tax total at line 75. A credit for UBT paid is available at line 53.
